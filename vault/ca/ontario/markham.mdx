### **Extreme Heat Warning: Seriously, Stay Inside**

A daytime high of 39.4°C is not just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Forget your personal bests, you'll be lucky to avoid heatstroke or exhaustion. Even Environment Canada typically issues heat warnings when temperatures hit 31°C or humidex values reach 40°C for two consecutive days. We're well past that threshold for raw temperature today. If you absolutely *must* leave the house, keep it short, stay in the shade, and question every life choice that led you to believe this was a good idea.
