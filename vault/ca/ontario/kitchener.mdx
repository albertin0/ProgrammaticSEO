<AlertBox type="warning">

Environment Canada might not have an active "heat warning" for today, but a personal warning from someone who's pulled a hamstring in less heat than this: adjust your pace, wear light colours, and for the love of all that is holy, drink more water than you think you need. Consider moving your longer runs to early morning or late evening.

Same goes for Fischer-Hallman Road; there are lane reductions between Huron and Plains that started just yesterday and will be ongoing for months. Forget about setting any personal bests when you're constantly stopping for traffic or trying not to trip over exposed rebar.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Construction Zone Confusion:** There's significant utility work and road reconstruction happening all over. Duke Street West has sections closed off near Victoria and Wellington, and Pioneer Drive is fully closed between Homer Watson and Bechtel for site servicing related to Upper Canada Park upgrades. These aren't just minor inconveniences; they can force you onto busy roads without proper shoulders, or lead to unexpected detours that add mileage and frustration. Plan your route, and then plan a backup route.

So, yeah, enjoy the mostly clear skies, but keep your head on a swivel. And maybe just hit the treadmill for a change, or focus on some strength work at home. Your physical therapist (me) would approve.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is King (or Queen):** Even with moderate pollen, if you're going out, pre-hydrate, carry water, and listen to your body. Headaches, dizziness, or unusual fatigue are not just "pushing through it" — they're signs you need to stop. We're not trying to end up at St. Mary's today.
</AlertBox>
