Alright, Lethbridge, listen up. Today isn't the day to try and outrun a tumbleweed, unless you enjoy getting sandblasted and potentially blown into a coulee.

## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>
    **Gale-force winds:** Environment Canada is calling for wicked wind gusts, hitting 80 to 100 kilometres per hour for southern Alberta, including our beloved Lethbridge. That’s not a breeze, folks, that’s a personal assault.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Moderate pollen:** For those of you whose noses decide to spontaneously combust this time of year, pollen levels are moderate. Could be worse, could be better. Just another Tuesday in Alberta, really.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Muddy misery:** With temperatures hovering around 6°C and overcast skies, our river valley trails are going to be a mucky, slippery mess. Don't be "that guy" tearing up the Coal Banks Trail just because you think your fancy trail shoes can handle it. They can't. And neither can your ankles when you inevitably slip.
  </li>
</BulletList>

You really need to rethink that outdoor run today. Seriously. That 80-100 km/h wind forecast isn't a suggestion, it's a warning. You'll spend more time fighting the wind and trying not to get airborne than actually getting a decent workout. Your lungs will be fine from an AQI standpoint (it's low at 41, dominant pollutant is Ozone/Other), but your joints, specifically your knees and ankles, are going to hate you after battling against those gusts on uneven, likely muddy ground.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Severe Wind Hazard!
  Today, Lethbridge is under a significant wind threat with gusts reaching 80-100 km/h. This isn't just unpleasant; it's genuinely dangerous for outdoor activity. Runners, cyclists, and even walkers risk losing balance, being struck by airborne debris, or being pushed into traffic. Postpone any outdoor runs or rides until these winds die down.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* get out, stick to pavement and stay away from anything exposed, especially near the **High Level Bridge**. That area is always prone to insane winds, and there's a standing warning about the dangers of the river itself near the bridge and weir due to unseen debris. Don't be stupid. No workout is worth a trip to the ER.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Watch for Muddy, Slippery Trails
  Despite recent temperature fluctuations, our coulee trails like the ones off Indian Battle Park are likely soft and muddy today. The City of Lethbridge explicitly warns against using wet trails to prevent damage. Not only will you tear up the trail for others, but you're asking for an ankle sprain. Stick to drier, paved surfaces if you go out.
</AlertBox>

Just because it's not freezing doesn't mean it's good running weather. The "feels like" temperature with that kind of wind chill is going to be significantly colder than the actual 6°C. Plus, you’ll be exerting yourself, sweating, and then that wind will suck the heat right out of you. Hypothermia is no joke, even in what seems like mild temperatures.

Honestly, today is a treadmill day. Or a yoga DVD day. Or maybe a "catch up on that book" day. Your body (and everyone else who uses our trails) will thank you for sitting this one out.
