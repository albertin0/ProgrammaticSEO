<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Advisory: STAY INDOORS!
  The current temperature of 34.1°C is dangerous for outdoor activity. Heatstroke is a serious, life-threatening condition. Symptoms include confusion, dizziness, nausea, rapid pulse, and hot, red skin. Hydration won't save you if you're pushing too hard in this kind of heat. If you *must* go out, keep it minimal, stay in the shade, and drink water like it's your job. But really, don't.
