## Seriously, Stay Indoors (Unless You're Built Different, Which You're Not)

I've run enough Broad Street Runs to know what hot feels like, but this is a different beast. This isn't "push through it" weather; this is "end up in the ER" weather. The city has activated emergency heat programs for a reason.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Advisory: It's Brutal Out There
  Do NOT attempt any strenuous outdoor workouts today. Your body's ability to cool itself is severely compromised when it's this hot and humid. Intense exercise on a sizzling day significantly increases your risk of heat-related illness, extreme fatigue, headache, dizziness, and even death.
</AlertBox>

I saw some folks already trying to get a run in along Kelly Drive earlier. Look, I love that scenic route as much as the next runner, but if you're out there right now, you're playing Russian roulette with your health. The heat isn't just coming from the air; your body generates its own heat during exercise, making it nearly impossible to cool down.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Hydration Won't Save You From Everything
  Yes, drink water. Lots of it. But don't think chugging a gallon means you can go for that 10-miler. Water can't magically lower the ambient temperature or stop your internal organs from cooking. Avoid alcohol entirely if you're going to be outside at all.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* leave your air-conditioned bunker, keep it short, stay in the shade, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. The city has cooling centers, pools, and spraygrounds open. Use them. Call the PCA Heatline if you or someone you know needs help.
