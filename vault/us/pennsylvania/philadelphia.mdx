## Local Lowdown & Trail Talk

Look, it's World Cup season, which means our city's a bit of a circus. The **FIFA Fan Festival at Lemon Hill** is in full swing, turning parts of Fairmount Park and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway into a pedestrian-only zone and a general traffic nightmare. This means if your usual loop takes you past the Art Museum or up to Lemon Hill, be prepared for crowds and detours. Parking around there is a joke, so walk, bike, or take SEPTA if you're headed that way.

Good news for the purists: the **Schuylkill River Trail** itself remains open for us runners and cyclists. Just know that if you're trying to *access* it from areas near the Fan Festival, you might be navigating some extra foot traffic. Pop-up visitor centers along the route from Center City to Lemon Hill are offering water and restrooms, which is a nice touch, I guess, if you can fight through the selfie-takers.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Near I-95:** Heads up if you're running or cycling in Northeast Philly. There's ongoing I-95 bridge construction through June 26, periodically restricting surface streets like Venango Street and Castor Avenue from 6 AM to 3 PM. This means lane closures and flaggers. Keep your head on a swivel and maybe find an alternate route to avoid a close call with a dump truck.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Schuylkill Crossing Update:** The new Schuylkill Crossing at Grays Ferry is still under construction, aiming for a summer 2026 opening. This means that connection between the Grays Ferry Crescent and Bartram's Mile isn't quite ready. Plan your long runs accordingly.
</AlertBox>
