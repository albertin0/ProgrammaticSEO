## Your Lungs & The "Clear" Skies

"Mainly clear" sounds great, doesn't it? Like you can just breathe deep and power through. But for us Philly runners, "clear" often comes with an asterisk. Our beloved city, despite its charms, keeps racking up failing grades for air quality year after year, particularly when it comes to ozone smog and fine particle pollution (PM2.5). So, while the current AQI sits at a "moderate" 55, don't let that lull you into a false sense of security for your lungs. Today's dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which is that nasty stuff that can really dig deep into your respiratory system. If you've got asthma or any other lung sensitivities, be mindful. A shorter, less intense workout might be the smarter play.

## Navigating the Festival Frenzy: Fairmount Park & Parkway

It feels like the entire city is one giant block party these days, and your favorite running routes are paying the price. The **FIFA Fan Festival** at Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park is still in full swing through July 19. This means some prime running real estate is effectively off-limits or seriously altered.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Current Obstacle Course:** The local lanes of the **Benjamin Franklin Parkway** are *still* shut down from Memorial Day until Labor Day. Yes, it's supposed to be "pedestrian friendly," but it funnels everyone into tighter spaces. If you usually run or bike down the Parkway towards the Art Museum, expect congestion.
</AlertBox>

And if you were hoping for a peaceful jaunt around the Waterworks, forget it after 10 AM. **Waterworks Drive** is closed daily at 10 AM for the Fan Festival. So, get your riverside miles in early or be prepared to reroute hard.

Oh, and if you were thinking of hitting up the west side of Fairmount Park, particularly around Belmont Plateau, listen up. The **Roots Picnic**, which just wrapped up this past weekend, caused a bunch of road closures, and **Belmont Mansion Drive** is reportedly *still* closed until 6 PM today. So that scenic loop you had planned? Probably still blocked. Plan an alternate, or brace for some creative dodging of barricades and confused drivers.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Fairmount Park Frustration:** With both the FIFA Fan Festival impacting Lemon Hill/Parkway and residual closures from the Roots Picnic affecting Belmont Plateau, large swaths of Fairmount Park's most accessible running paths are either closed or highly congested. Know your route, or be ready to improvise.
</AlertBox>

The good news? It's cool, clear, and the extreme heat emergency from a few days ago has lifted. Just be smart about where you're putting your feet and what you're breathing in.
