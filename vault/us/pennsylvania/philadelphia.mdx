<BulletList>

**Pollen & AQI Advisory:** With moderate pollen and a "Moderate" AQI (55, with PM2.5 as the primary concern), individuals with respiratory issues like asthma or allergies should proceed with caution. Consider an indoor workout or keeping your outdoor session shorter and less intense. If you're typically sensitive, listen to your body and don't push it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Schuylkill River Trail Alert (Overnight Only):** A section of the Schuylkill River Trail beneath the Martin Luther King Jr. Drive Bridge (between Paine's Park and Fairmount Water Works) is closed for concrete removal. BUT, this is an **overnight closure only**, from 9 p.m. to 5 a.m. So, if you're hitting the trail during daylight hours, you're good. Just don't plan any late-night, ill-advised bridge-dodging sprints. Detour signs are posted for those overnight hours if you're a night owl.
</AlertBox>

Look, the 13°C weather is pleasant enough for a run, even if it feels a little cooler with those gusty winds kicking around. But seriously, those Wissahickon trails are going to be sloppy. Nobody needs a sprained ankle for bragging rights about running in mud season. And that "Mainly Clear" forecast might be optimistic given yesterday's downpour and the "a.m. storms" advisory for Monday. Expect some puddles, especially if your route takes you through any underpasses or low-lying areas in Fairmount Park.

If you're dead set on getting out there, stick to paved paths or hit the treadmill. Your lungs will appreciate the slightly cleaner air indoors, and your joints will thank you for not turning into a mud-covered slip-and-slide participant. Stay safe, Philly.
