## Today's Rundown: The Love Run Takeover & Chilly Winds

Alright, let's cut to the chase. Unless you're one of the thousands running the Love Run Half Marathon today, you're going to have a hard time finding a clear path on your favorite riverside routes. Starting as early as 3 a.m. and continuing until roughly 1 p.m., the race has put a chokehold on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, Eakins Oval, Kelly Drive, and a large section of Martin Luther King Jr. Drive.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Road Closures: Love Run Edition
Seriously, don't even *think* about driving near the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, Eakins Oval, Kelly Drive, or MLK Drive until after 1 p.m. today. If you’re not racing, find an alternate route for your workout, or better yet, stay off those main thoroughfares entirely. Center City roads might reopen around 11 a.m., but the river drives are a no-go for casual movement for much longer. Even MLK Drive is beginning its seasonal weekend closure today, adding to the general chaos.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the skies are clear, which is a nice change of pace from some of the grayer mornings we've had. But don't let that fool you. The mercury is starting out just below freezing at -0.3°C (31.46°F), and while it's supposed to warm up to the mid-50s (Fahrenheit) later, those winds are going to make it feel colder than it is, especially on exposed parts of the trails. Gusts over 25 mph are predicted, which means you'll be fighting the wind both ways.
