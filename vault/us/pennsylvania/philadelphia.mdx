## Environmental Rundown

After a couple of days that felt like running through a swamp, today's weather is a breath of fresh, clear air. The mercury is sitting comfortably at 23.2°C (that's about 74°F for the old school folks) and it's clear skies. We saw a "Heat Health Emergency" declared just yesterday, with heat indices pushing over 100°F, so today is a welcome reprieve. That emergency officially ended last night.

Air quality is moderate with an AQI of 55, driven mostly by PM2.5. It's not perfect, but it's far from the "don't even think about it" days we sometimes get. Pollen is also moderate across the board, which is pretty standard for mid-June.

## Local Hazards & Headaches

Look, the weather might be playing nice, but the city itself is throwing up some roadblocks. This is FIFA World Cup season, people, and Philly is hosting the Fan Festival at Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park from June 11 through July 19. What does that mean for your run? Chaos.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Road Closures & Access Headaches Around Fairmount Park:** Major road closures and parking restrictions are in effect around Lemon Hill, the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, and the Philadelphia Museum of Art due to the FIFA Fan Festival. If your route usually takes you through these areas by car, plan for significant detours. Local lanes of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway are shut down from Memorial Day until Labor Day. And don't even think about driving down Waterworks Drive after 10 AM, because it's closed daily until July 19th for the festival.
</AlertBox>

Here's the silver lining: **the Schuylkill River Trail *itself* is still open for pedestrians and cyclists**. So, if you can navigate *to* the trail, you should be fine once you're on it. Just be aware that access points might be tricky.

Construction also continues to be a constant companion in our fair city. The I-95 CAP project at Penn's Landing means ongoing work on the South Street Pedestrian Bridge and a single-lane reduction on Columbus Boulevard for about a year. So, if you're planning a run along the waterfront, expect some disruptions there. Also, keep an eye out for the Tacony Street Sidepath construction in Northeast Philadelphia, which is ongoing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Upcoming Event (Tomorrow): ODUNDE Festival!** While not directly impacting your workout *today*, the ODUNDE Festival kicks off tomorrow, Sunday, June 14, around 23rd and South Streets, meaning significant road closures and parking restrictions in that area will start early tomorrow morning. Get your runs in today if South Philly is your jam.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for Runners:** Given the moderate pollen and AQI, consider running earlier in the day if you have respiratory sensitivities. Also, with the recent heat, even though today is cooler, stay hydrated! Your body is still recovering from that furnace. And as always, tell someone your route, especially if you're exploring new areas to avoid event congestion.
</AlertBox>
