## Today's Rundown

Right now, it might be clear, but that's just the calm before the meteorological storm. Your initial clear skies at 24°C (75°F) are a deceptive lure. You're going to bake if you head out before those storms hit, and then you're going to get soaked, blown around, and potentially struck by lightning if you're out during them.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Severe Storms and Flash Flood Risk!
The severe weather threat for the afternoon and evening is no joke. Expect heavy downpours, damaging wind gusts, frequent lightning, and even a slim chance for isolated tornadoes. Areas receiving multiple rounds of storms could see flash flooding. Don't be caught out on exposed trails like parts of the **Schuylkill River Trail** when this rolls through. Head indoors, stat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Nasty Heat and Humidity
A Heat Advisory is in effect for Philadelphia until 8 PM. Expect heat index values to push into the upper 90s to 100 degrees. Hydrate *aggressively* if you absolutely must be outside, and keep your efforts light. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, get inside to AC immediately. This isn't the day to push your limits.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Watch Your Step (and Your Route)
If you're still determined to hit the trails, know that parts of the Schuylkill River Trail near the MLK Drive Bridge are closed for construction, and the Market Street Bridge is shut down to cars and bikes until October 2027. One sidewalk remains open on Market Street Bridge for pedestrians, but expect detours and congestion if you're trying to navigate that area. And for you mountain bikers, while Wissahickon Valley Park trails were "PRIME" on Sunday, Pennypack Park was a "STAY OFF" situation. With heavy rain expected, those conditions could change fast.
</AlertBox>
