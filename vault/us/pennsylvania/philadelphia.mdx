## Trail Talk & City Streets

If you're hitting some of the softer trails in the Wissahickon Valley Park today, a general word of caution: even if it's mainly clear, the Wissahickon can get muddy and have standing water or debris after *any* significant rain. We haven't had downpours recently, but uneven terrain is always a thing on those trails. Watch your step.

And for those of you trying to get your miles in near the South Philadelphia Sports Complex, remember that I-95 ramp construction is still active near Front Street until about May 21st. This is more of a traffic headache, but if you're navigating streets in that area, just be prepared for potential detours or extra congestion. It's not a direct trail closure, but it could mess with your flow.

All in all, today's a decent, if slightly hazy, day to get outside. Just listen to your body, especially if you're in the sensitive groups for air quality or have allergies. Hydrate, be smart, and maybe carry a tissue.
