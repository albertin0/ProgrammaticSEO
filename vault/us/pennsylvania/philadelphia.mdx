## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy

Look, the air quality isn't *terrible* today, but "moderate" still means something. We've just survived a month where the Canadian wildfires turned our skies apocalyptic. While those smoky skies are, thankfully, gone for now, that PM2.5 pollutant doesn't magically disappear from its effects on your lungs just because the AQI ticker drops below "unhealthy."

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Airborne Annoyances:** With a Moderate AQI (55) and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, combined with moderate pollen levels, your lungs might feel it. If you're sensitive, this is a day for an easy effort or taking it indoors. Don't push through chest tightness or excessive coughing. That's your body telling you to chill out.
</AlertBox>

### Your Route Just Got a Headache (or Will Soon)

Big news if you frequent the western side of Center City or the Schuylkill River Trail: The Market Street Bridge is undergoing a massive, 14-month reconstruction project. While a pedestrian sidewalk is *supposed* to remain open, anyone who's run near a PennDOT project knows "open" can sometimes mean "labyrinthine detour through a construction site." Prep work is ongoing, and things will get real complicated come August. This is a multi-year, nearly $150 million project, so don't expect it to vanish overnight. Plan your runs across the river accordingly; consider using the Walnut Street or Spring Garden bridges if you want to avoid potential bottlenecks. The Schuylkill River Trail underneath will undoubtedly feel the ripple effects.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Schuylkill River Trail Users, Take Note:** The Market Street Bridge reconstruction, especially impacting the section over the Schuylkill River Trail, I-76, and Amtrak lines near 30th Street Station, is a long-term headache. Even before the full vehicular closure in late August, expect congestion and plan alternate routes if you want a smooth ride or run. Stay vigilant for workers and equipment.
</AlertBox>
