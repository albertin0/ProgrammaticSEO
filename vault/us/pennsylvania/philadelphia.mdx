## The Rundown for October 10, 2026

The air in Philadelphia today is holding at a moderate AQI of 55, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most folks, this is perfectly acceptable, but if you're sensitive, you might feel it. Add in moderate pollen levels, and some of you out there might be reaching for the tissues mid-stride. Hydrate and listen to your body, especially if you're prone to seasonal sniffles.

Speaking of listening to your body, the actual temperature is currently 13.0°C (about 55°F), but it's warming up nicely to the low 70s Fahrenheit (around 21-24°C) with filtered sunshine. Get out there this afternoon, because tomorrow's a different story entirely.

### Weekend Trail Access & City Buzz

For those of us who love our riverside miles, MLK Drive is currently *blissfully* closed to vehicle traffic from the East Falls Bridge down to the MLK Bridge/Eakins Oval. This is part of the regular weekend recreational closure, but it's also Indigenous Peoples Day weekend, which means an extended closure. Enjoy those wide-open lanes! If you're planning a Center City jaunt, Walnut Street from Broad to 19th and 18th Street from Chestnut to Locust are also closed to vehicles for "Open Streets: West Walnut" until 5 PM today, creating a pedestrian-friendly zone for a change.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up for Sunday, October 11th:** Don't get lulled into complacency by today's pleasant weather. The remnants of Hurricane Isaias are rolling in. Expect widespread and heavy rain all day Sunday, with localized flooding becoming a real concern, especially along and southeast of the I-95 corridor. We're talking 1 to 2 inches of rain, possibly more. If you're a fair-weather runner (or even a foul-weather one), you might want to consider the treadmill or an indoor gym for your Sunday miles. Coastal areas of New Jersey and Delaware are under a Gale Watch for strong winds and high seas, and inland gusts of 30-50 mph are possible in our region.
</AlertBox>
