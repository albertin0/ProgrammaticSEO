## The Air: Breathe Smart, Not Hard

That AQI of 77 isn't *Code Purple* wildfire smoke levels like we saw back in July, but "moderate" still means something. It means that for "unusually sensitive" people, there *may* be a risk. And with PM2.5 hanging around, you're inhaling tiny particles that can get deep into your lungs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up, Sensitive Lungs!** If you're prone to respiratory issues or just feel a bit off, consider dialing back the intensity of your outdoor workout today. Listen to your body – it usually knows best, unlike that personal trainer who says "no pain, no gain."
</AlertBox>
