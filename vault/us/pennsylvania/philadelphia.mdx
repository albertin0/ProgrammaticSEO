Today? It's a mixed bag, Philly. You'll need to plan around the crowds, but at least the sky's clear.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
*   Road closures are still a major headache today with multiple events in Center City, making your usual routes a logistical nightmare.
*   While a Freeze Warning for areas north and west of the city expired this morning, temperatures are still starting out chilly, so bundle up if you're an early bird.
*   Air quality is sitting at "Moderate" with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, which means some sensitive folks might feel it.
</BulletList>

## City's a Stage: Events Messing With Your Miles

Look, I get it, we love our events. But today, they're straight-up blocking your path. The **Market Street Run for Blue** is shutting down Market Street, Front Street, Dock Street, and a chunk of 23rd Street until around 10:30 AM. Then, for you West Philly folks, **Open Streets: West Walnut** has Walnut Street (between Broad and 19th) and part of 18th Street locked down until 6 PM.

<AlertBox type="warning">
That "Moderate" AQI with PM2.5 isn't great if you have respiratory issues or are sensitive. It means air quality is acceptable, but there may be a moderate health concern for a very small number of people. Keep an eye on how you feel.
</AlertBox>

## Weather: Crisp and Clear, But Don't Skimp on Layers

The good news? It's "Mainly Clear" out there. The temperature is a brisk 13°C (about 55°F) this afternoon, but remember, we woke up to near-freezing temps in the 30s. Northeast winds were still a bit breezy yesterday, but should be lighter today. So, if you're hitting the pavement before noon, throw on an extra layer or two. You can always shed them later.

<AlertBox type="info">
Remember that Friday's storm system brought some pretty gusty winds earlier this weekend, and a "Small Craft Advisory" was in effect for coastal waters, but those conditions have largely passed for the city itself today.
</AlertBox>

## Construction & Detours

For those of you relying on SEPTA to get to your favorite workout spot, be aware that various bus routes are detoured due to the Center City events today. Always check SEPTA's system status before you head out, especially if your route takes you anywhere near Market Street or Walnut Street.

### The Bottom Line

It's a beautiful, clear fall day, but the city's event schedule is making parts of it a giant obstacle course. If you’re looking for an uninterrupted run, maybe head to somewhere like Fairmount Park or the Schuylkill River Trail *away* from the immediate Center City hubbub. If Center City is your only option, stick to side streets or plan for an indoor workout this morning. Your lungs will be okay, but your joints will thank you for warming up properly in the cooler air.
