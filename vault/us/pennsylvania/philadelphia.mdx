Expect temporary single-lane closures between 9 a.m. and 3 p.m.. They'll have a flagger, but keep your eyes peeled and maybe adjust your timing if you're trying to flow freely through that section.

**Schuylkill River Trail Detour (Norristown Connection):** For those of you who venture further up the Schuylkill River Trail, be aware that a detour where the Chester Valley Trail connects in Norristown began back in January and is expected to last about a year. It's due to some building and site renovation work. It might throw a wrench in your long run plans if you rely on that connection.

**General City Construction:** The city's a constant construction zone. Vine Street, for example, is shut down for months due to an $8.5 million safety project. While not always directly impacting trails, it means more traffic, more detours for getting *to* your workout, and potentially more dusty air. I-95 has ongoing work too, with ramps opening and closing, so stay vigilant if your run takes you near major roadways.

Philly's been a bit bipolar with the temps lately, experiencing some significant swings within 24 hours. This can mess with your body's ability to adapt, so make sure you're hydrated and dressed appropriately, even if it feels mild right now. Also, with strong winds having been reported yesterday, keep an eye out for any lingering debris on trails or sidewalks. A fallen branch is no fun, especially when you're in the zone.
