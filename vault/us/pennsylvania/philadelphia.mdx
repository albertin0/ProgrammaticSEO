<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Underestimate that Breeze
That "gusty northeast breeze" isn't just a turn of phrase, folks. It’s a real thing that will cut through thin layers, especially if you're out on an exposed stretch like parts of the Schuylkill River Trail near the water. Remember, what feels good in the sun can turn chilly in the shade or against the wind.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Coastal Watch (If You're Crazy Enough to Run There Today)
While us inland runners are mostly in the clear, there's a Coastal Flood Advisory in effect until Friday morning for our Jersey and Delaware Shore points, with "strong northeast winds, 6–12 foot surf and multiple rounds of coastal flooding." So if you're thinking of a "beach run" today, maybe stick to the boardwalk and definitely stay out of the water. This isn't your average ripple.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Heads Up for Weekend Closures
While today, Thursday, September 24th, seems to be dodging major event-related trail closures in the city's core, you need to be aware of what's coming. Starting this *weekend*, specifically Saturday, September 26th, and Sunday, September 27th, the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and parts of Kelly Drive will be a mess of road closures and parking restrictions for multiple events. If your long run takes you through Center City or along the Schuylkill on those days, plan alternate routes now. You don't want to get stuck trying to navigate a parade route when you're 15 miles deep and hangry.
</AlertBox>

For now, enjoy the relatively clear conditions. Lace up, hit the pavement or the Schuylkill River Trail, and try not to inhale too much pollen or that lovely PM2.5.
