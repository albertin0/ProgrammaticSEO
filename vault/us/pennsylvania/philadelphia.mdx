Good news for those of you who've been asking: that sinkhole near Race Street and JFK Boulevard that shut down a section of the Schuylkill River Trail for a while back in 2025? It's been fixed and the trail's open. So, no excuses, get out there.

Now, looking ahead, keep an eye on the weekend. The Head of the Schuylkill Regatta is happening Saturday and Sunday (October 24th and 25th). It's not impacting your run *today*, but don't be surprised if your usual Sunday long run gets a little more congested. Also, if you're thinking of a family outing, the Halloween Hikes and Hayrides kick off tomorrow and Saturday at the Schuylkill Center in Roxborough, featuring glowing pumpkins on the Widener Trail. Sounds fun, just not for your serious PR attempt.

Today, though? It's all yours. Enjoy the crisp air before winter truly decides to settle in.
