#### Seriously, Think Twice About Hard Outdoor Workouts Today.

The Philly Health Department issued a warning just yesterday, May 20th, advising residents near the Glenwood Avenue brush fire to "avoid excessive activity" if they had to be outside due to smoke. While the immediate fire might be out, PM2.5 is still the dominant pollutant in our "Moderate" AQI. This city already consistently ranks poorly for particulate pollution and ozone, especially as we head into warmer months. Pushing it hard when the air is iffy is just asking for trouble, particularly for anyone with respiratory issues. Find an indoor option today, or at least keep it incredibly light.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Treachery: Watch for Construction on SRT and MLK Drive.

If you're hitting the Schuylkill River Trail, be aware of ongoing PennDOT bridge rehabilitation around the 30th Street Station area. This project involves the Market Street bridges and will have lane closures and detours that might affect your route until at least June 1st. Also, MLK Drive itself is undergoing asphalt maintenance today, May 21st, between 9:30 AM and 3:30 PM, from Falls Bridge to Sweetbriar Drive. The good news is the *trail* along MLK Drive is supposed to remain open and unaffected by this particular work. Always stay alert in construction zones.
</AlertBox>

#### Just Your Standard Springtime Sneezefest.

"Moderate" pollen is nothing new for us in May. If you're prone to allergies, take your meds, carry tissues, and maybe rinse off after your outdoor activities to minimize exposure. It’s not a showstopper, but it's certainly not helping your lungs feel great when coupled with the particulate matter in the air.
