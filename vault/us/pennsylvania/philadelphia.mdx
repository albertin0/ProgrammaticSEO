### Today's Rundown

This morning, we're looking at decent temperatures around 13°C and mostly clear skies, which is prime running weather for those who hate the heat. But don't get too comfortable, because the weather gods are fickle. Rain showers are expected to move in this afternoon and continue into the evening and overnight. We're talking about gusty winds, potentially 25-30 mph, making any exposed trail feel like a wind tunnel. If you're planning a long session on Forbidden Drive in Wissahickon Valley Park, you'll want to hit it before lunch, or be prepared for slick surfaces and leaves sticking to your face.

### Air Quality & Allergies

The AQI is currently at 55, which is "Moderate." While not officially "unhealthy," let's be real: Philadelphia has a long-standing issue with particulate pollution, and our region consistently gets dinged for poor air quality in national reports. So, even on a moderate day, if you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you're not exactly breathing mountain air. Layer that with a "Moderate" pollen count, and you've got a recipe for a snotty, wheezy workout if you're sensitive.
