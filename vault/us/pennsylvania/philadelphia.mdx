Look, I get it. We've all got miles to log or PRs to chase. But today, August 8th, is simply not the day to push it outdoors in Philadelphia. The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for a reason. We're talking about actual danger here, not just "a bit sweaty." Temperatures are hitting the low 90s, but with the humidity, it'll feel like you're running through a bowl of hot soup. That kind of heat index, especially when sustained, puts a massive strain on your cardiovascular system. Dehydration sets in faster than you can say "water ice," and heat exhaustion or heat stroke become very real possibilities. You might think you're tough, but your kidneys don't care about your Strava segment.

And as if baking alive isn't enough, we've got the delightful prospect of afternoon thunderstorms. These aren't your gentle summer drizzles; we're talking about cells capable of significant wind damage and flash flooding. Imagine being halfway down Kelly Drive, drenched in sweat (or rain, take your pick), when the skies open up and lightning starts flashing. Not ideal. Keep an eye on the radar, because "scattered" means it could pop up anywhere, anytime, after lunch.
