<AlertBox type="info">
  **Runner's Rx: Tackling Moderate Pollen**

  Look, I get it. Allergy season feels like it lasts forever. With moderate pollen today, your usual morning sprint might turn into a snot-filled slog. My advice, from someone who's coughed up half a lung on Kelly Drive more times than I can count:
  *   Pop your antihistamine *before* you head out, not when your eyes start itching. Be proactive, people!
  *   Consider wearing a hat and sunglasses to keep some of that airborne crud out of your face. It's not just for looking cool.
  *   Shower immediately after your workout to wash off any pollen clinging to your skin and hair. Don't track that stuff all over your house.
  *   If you're particularly sensitive, maybe stick to indoor workouts or hit the trails after a good rain washes some of the pollen away (though that's not happening today).
</AlertBox>

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a respectable 55, mainly due to PM2.5. For most of us, that's not going to be an issue. You can breathe easy, literally. <AlertBox type="warning">
  **AQI Watch: Sensitive Groups Take Note**

  While the AQI of 55 is generally good, the dominant pollutant being PM2.5 means fine particulate matter is present. If you're someone who gets winded easily, has a cough that just won't quit, or your doctor's given you "sensitive group" status, pay attention. Consider reducing the intensity or duration of your workout. You don't need to PR every day; sometimes, living to run another day is the real win.
</AlertBox>
