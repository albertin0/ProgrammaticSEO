## Trail & Road Conditions: Don't Be a Moron

### Schuylkill River Trail: Watch Your Step

Remember that deluge we had a few days back? The Schuylkill River was feeling itself, swelling up to levels that put the boardwalk railings under water near 30th Street. While the water's likely receded, don't just blindly sprint onto the **Schuylkill River Trail** expecting pristine conditions. There's a decent chance of lingering muck, debris, and slick spots, especially on the lower-lying sections and the boardwalk. Give it a visual check, and maybe wear those shoes you don't mind getting a little gritty. No heroics, people.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Schuylkill River Trail Alert:** Recent high river levels (as of June 27) mean there could be residual flooding, mud, and debris on sections of the trail, particularly the boardwalks near 30th Street and Locust. Exercise caution and check conditions visually before heading out.
</AlertBox>

### Center City Shenanigans: Evening Workout Headaches

If your plan involves a leisurely evening jog through downtown, particularly around the Midtown Village area, adjust your expectations. "Open Streets: Midtown Village" is happening tonight, Tuesday, June 30th, from 4 PM to 9 PM. That means 13th Street (Chestnut to Walnut), Sansom Street (12th to Juniper), and Drury Street are becoming car-free zones for pedestrians, dining, and general mingling. Great for strolling, terrible for trying to hit your splits. Also, with the Wawa Welcome America Festival kicking off, expect more crowds and potential pop-up disruptions in the Historic District. If you want clear pavement in the evening, head to the parks.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Center City Congestion:** Avoid Midtown Village (13th, Sansom, Drury Streets) between 4 PM and 9 PM due to "Open Streets." Wawa Welcome America Festival events will also increase foot traffic in the Historic District throughout the day. Plan your routes accordingly.
</AlertBox>

### Local Road & Trail Construction (Further Out)

While the big Market Street bridge construction near 30th Street Station is paused until August for the city's 250th anniversary festivities, a heads-up for those venturing a bit further:
*   Periodic weekday lane closures for trail construction are ongoing through June 30 on Ellis Road in Middletown Township from 9 AM to 3 PM.
*   The Dager Road Trail in Lower Gwynedd Township is still closed during daytime hours (7 AM - 3 PM, Mon-Fri) for tree removal.
These aren't exactly in your backyard if you're strictly a city runner, but good to know if your route takes you out of the immediate urban core.
