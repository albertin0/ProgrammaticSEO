## Today's Hot Spots & Hazards

### The Heat is Still On

Even though the severe storm risk is down, the heat isn't going anywhere. The forecast for today is sunny with highs in the 90s, making it the tail end of our sixth heat wave this summer. If you're planning on pushing it after 11 AM, you're just asking for trouble. The hottest part of the day is generally between 11 AM and 4 PM, so plan your workout around that.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Lingering Heat Advisory.** While technically a "Heat Caution" until August 10th, temperatures are still high. Hydrate, take breaks, and consider moving your workout indoors during peak afternoon hours. Don't be a hero, heat stroke is real.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality Check

That moderate AQI isn't terrible, but it's not "crisp mountain air" either. For most of us, it's just a bit muggy. For those with asthma or other respiratory issues, especially if you live or run near industrial zones or major highways like the I-95 corridor, this is a day to be mindful.

### Event Alert: Head for the Cure 5K

If you're heading down to the **Philadelphia Navy Yard** this morning, expect some crowds. The 2nd annual Head for the Cure 5K is happening at the Marine Parade Grounds, with the 5K run/walk kicking off at 8 AM. It's for a good cause, but know that parts of the area might be busy. Maybe find another loop if you're not participating.

### Future Trail Head's Up: Fox Chase Lorimer Trail

Just a heads-up for those who frequent the Fox Chase Lorimer Trail: the Pine Road Bridge over the trail will be closing for reconstruction starting August 17th, through Summer 2027. The trail itself should largely remain open, but expect temporary closures when they start demolishing the bridge later this winter. Plan accordingly for later in the month.
