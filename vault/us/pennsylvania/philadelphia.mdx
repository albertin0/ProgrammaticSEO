### Danger: Extreme Heat & Humidity!
Seriously, this isn't a joke. Highs in the low 90s *feeling like triple digits* means heat stroke is a very real threat. If you *must* be outside, hit it before sunrise, keep it short, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body. Any signs of dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating, get indoors and cool off immediately. And remember those afternoon thunderstorms can pop up fast, bringing high winds and heavy rain with them.
