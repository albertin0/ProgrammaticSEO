<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Air Quality for Sensitive Groups
With a "Moderate" AQI of 55, individuals who are unusually sensitive to air pollution, such as those with asthma, lung, or heart conditions, should consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Keep your inhalers handy if you decide to push it.
</AlertBox>

Looking ahead, don't get too comfortable. Our "nice-ish" weather is on borrowed time. Tomorrow, Thursday, August 20th, the forecast is calling for a significant change with "Showers, storms" and an 85% chance of heavy rain during the day. Some of these storms could even be strong, particularly south of the city. If you're planning a long run on the Schuylkill River Trail, hit it today. Those paths get slick, and parts of the trail have seen closures in the past due to storm damage and sinkholes, so stay aware of conditions.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Key, Even if it Doesn't Feel Extreme
While today isn't an "extreme heat" day, 90-degree weather still warrants proper hydration. Don't wait until you're thirsty to drink water. Electrolytes wouldn't hurt either, especially if you're out there for more than an hour.
</AlertBox>

So, get your miles in, but listen to your body, and maybe check the radar again tonight before setting that alarm for tomorrow's run. We've seen what a little rain can do to this city, and nobody wants to be caught out in a downpour on Kelly Drive.
