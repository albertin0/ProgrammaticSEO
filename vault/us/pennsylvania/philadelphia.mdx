## The Rundown: Breathe Easy-ish, Watch Your Step

The air quality today is clocking in at a **Moderate AQI of 55**, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. That's perfectly acceptable for most of us, but if you're typically huffing and puffing with allergies or have any respiratory issues, you might notice it. Couple that with **Moderate pollen levels**, and you've got a recipe for a slightly congested run for some. If your eyes are itching and your nose is running, you know who you are. Hydrate, consider an antihistamine if you're prone, and don't push the pace too hard if you feel it in your lungs.

## Construction Chaos, Still & Always

Here's the real Philly special: **construction**. It's everywhere, and it's not letting up.

### Penn's Landing & South Street Bridge Mayhem

If you're one of those ambitious souls who likes to run along the Delaware River waterfront, especially around **Penn's Landing**, be ready for some detours. The I-95 Central Access Philadelphia (CAP) Project is still chugging along. They're extending I-95 over Columbus Boulevard and assembling the main span for the new South Street Pedestrian Bridge. This means potential lane shifts on Columbus Boulevard and Front Street, which could mess with your routes. While the existing South Street Pedestrian Bridge is still open with "minor interruptions," the area is an active construction zone. Think about it: steel beams and concrete being hoisted overhead don't exactly scream "peaceful jog." Plan your waterfront runs with an escape route, or just skip that stretch for now.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Unmarked Hazards:** Even if routes are open, construction zones mean uneven surfaces, debris, and sudden changes. Keep your eyes peeled, especially on shorter loops or less-trafficked paths. A twisted ankle is a bad souvenir.
</AlertBox>

### PWD Projects Across the City

It's not just the big interstate projects. The Philadelphia Water Department has a laundry list of water main replacements and green stormwater infrastructure projects kicking off or continuing around town this fall. We're talking East Falls, Roxborough, Overbrook, and even the Neill Drive Stream Restoration in West Fairmount Park, which isn't slated to finish until early 2028. This means localized road closures, sidewalk disruptions, and potential heavy machinery where you least expect it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Local Alerts Before You Go:** Seriously, a quick glance at the PWD construction map or local news can save you a lot of backtracking and frustration. Or just embrace the urban obstacle course, if that's your thing.
</AlertBox>
