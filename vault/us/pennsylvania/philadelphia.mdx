Kelly Drive and MLK Drive should be clear of event closures; those big cycling events and the FIFA Fan Festival on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway wrapped up earlier this month, thankfully. ### Construction & Trail Obstacles

Good news for daytime runners: the major I-95 construction and lighting repair, including on the Girard Point Bridge, is primarily happening overnight, from 9 PM to 5 AM. So, your morning or afternoon sprints shouldn't be impacted by highway lane closures, unless you're an extreme night owl runner, in which case, maybe rethink that route near the interchanges.

We've still got ongoing bridge work, like the Market Street Bridge closure to vehicle traffic (pedestrian access maintained on one sidewalk) and the Pine Road bridge project over the Pennypack Rail/Trail (trail mostly open). These are longer-term projects, so stay aware, but they shouldn't derail your usual route planning too badly.

<AlertBox type="warning">
If you're out running on the Pennypack Rail/Trail near Fox Chase, be aware of the ongoing bridge replacement project on Pine Road. While the trail is mostly open, expect some localized detours or changes in conditions around the construction zone.
