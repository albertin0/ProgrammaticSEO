### Trail & Road Conditions: Don't Get Caught Off Guard

Look, construction is a Philly constant, like potholes and Eagles disappointment. And this weekend is no different.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Schuylkill River Trail (SRT) Overnight Closure:** If you're planning a late-night or early-morning jaunt, be aware that a section of the Schuylkill River Trail (SRT) beneath the Martin Luther King Drive Bridge is closed overnight (9 p.m. to 5 a.m.) through March 24th for concrete removal as part of the Martin Luther King Bridge Rehabilitation Project. This specifically impacts the stretch between Paine's Park and Fairmount Water Works. During the day, it's open, but if you're a nocturnal runner, you'll need to follow the detour behind the Philadelphia Art Museum.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads-Up for Bells Mill Road Bridge:** Maintenance work on the Bells Mill Road Bridge in Wissahickon Valley Park kicks off *tomorrow*, Monday, March 16th. Expect temporary single-lane closures between 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. for about one to two weeks. If you usually run, ride, or even drive through that area, plan for some flagger-directed traffic. It's not a full closure, but it's definitely going to slow things down.
</AlertBox>

We’re also looking at a ton of other construction across the city, because when aren't we? PennDOT alone has nearly 150 active and upcoming projects in the region. So keep your head on a swivel, whether you’re pounding the pavement or navigating a bike lane.

Seriously, enjoy this mild day, but remember those rain clouds are gathering. Monday's shaping up to be a real mess, so if you've got outdoor plans, make today count.
