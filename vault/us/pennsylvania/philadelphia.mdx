## Today's Local Rundown: Thunderstorms, Humidity, and Festival Detours

Don't let the "partly cloudy" morning fool you; the forecast for Philadelphia today, July 9, 2026, includes heavy thunderstorms. If you're planning on hitting the Schuylkill River Trail or any of our other fantastic paths, be ready for sudden downpours and slick surfaces. Rain doesn't just make you wet; it makes every step a potential ankle twist, especially on uneven terrain. The humidity is also high, meaning you'll be sweating buckets even if the air temperature isn't scorching. Your body's ability to cool itself is compromised when the air is already saturated, so that 78°F run will feel more like 90°F. Dehydration is a real risk, even if it's cloudy.

The good news? We're past the extreme heat wave that saw temperatures hitting triple digits and heat health emergencies declared earlier this month. The city isn't under a Code Red for heat, but don't get complacent.

## Watch Out for Closures, Especially Near the Parkway

If you're one of those brave souls determined to get out there, be aware of the ongoing closures around the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and Lemon Hill. The FIFA World Cup Fan Festival is in full swing at Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park until July 19th, which means local lanes of the Parkway are closed until September 7th. Waterworks Drive also gets shut down daily from 10 AM. So, your usual routes might be blocked, forcing you into more congested areas with less shade. Plan accordingly, or better yet, stay home.
