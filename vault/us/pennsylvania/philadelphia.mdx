## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>The weather is actually pretty decent for getting out there. We've got mainly clear skies and a mild 13.0°C (about 55°F) this morning, heading up to a pleasant 26°C (78°F) later on. The strong winds from yesterday have finally decided to chill out. Your joints will thank you for the mild temps.</li>
  <li>However, your lungs might not be so thrilled. We're sitting at a Moderate AQI of 55 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, which means some of you sensitive folks might feel it. Add to that a "Moderate" pollen count, and your allergies might be screaming for mercy. Break out the Zyrtec or wear a Buff, seriously.</li>
  <li>The biggest headache today? The Roots Picnic. Yeah, it's awesome for music, but a nightmare for runners and cyclists who rely on Martin Luther King Drive. That entire stretch is *closed to recreational use* for the festival. So, if your usual Sunday long run or ride involves cruising MLK Drive, you're out of luck on the main road. The side path is technically open, but expect it to be a zoo with everyone else crammed onto it.</li>
</BulletList>

## The Belmont Plateau Shuffle

For those of you planning to hit Fairmount Park, especially around Belmont Plateau, listen up. The Roots Picnic is taking over that whole area. This means massive road closures and traffic headaches, not just on MLK Drive, but also on Belmont Mansion Drive, Chamounix Drive, and Montgomery Drive. If you were planning to park nearby and hit the trails, think again. Your best bet is to either find an alternative route entirely or be prepared for significant detours and crowds.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Roots Picnic Impact:** Martin Luther King Drive is **closed to vehicular traffic AND recreational use** (meaning no running/cycling on the main road) today due to the Roots Picnic at Belmont Plateau. The side path is accessible, but expect it to be crowded. Plan your routes accordingly and avoid driving anywhere near Belmont Plateau.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Benjamin Franklin Parkway:** On a brighter note, the local lanes of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway are closed to vehicular traffic from Memorial Day through Labor Day, making it a pedestrian and cyclist haven. So, if you're looking for open road in the city, that's your spot. Just watch out for other humans.
</AlertBox>
