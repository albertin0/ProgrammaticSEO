## Why today is &lt;em&gt;not&lt;/em&gt; quite the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Early Morning Downpours:** While the skies are clearing to mostly sunny later, Philadelphia started the day with heavy rain and a risk of isolated flash floods through the pre-dawn hours. Expect some lingering wet and potentially slick surfaces, especially on unpaved trails or in low-lying areas.
*   **Philly 10K Road Closures:** This is the big one. The Philly 10K is happening *today*, Sunday, August 23rd, and large sections of South Philadelphia are locked down. Major streets like South Street (4th to 11th), Washington Avenue, and East Passyunk Avenue are closed starting as early as 1-2 AM and will remain so until the race concludes, likely around noon or 2 PM for the festival areas. Good luck navigating that without adding extra miles to your detour.
*   **Moderate Air Quality & Pollen:** The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 54, categorized as Moderate, with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant. Add "Moderate" pollen levels into the mix, and it's not going to feel great on your lungs if you're sensitive, even if it's not explicitly "unhealthy" for most.
</BulletList>

### Local Hazards & Your Workout

If you had plans to hit the streets of South Philly this morning, consider them officially derailed. The Philly 10K means significant street closures are in effect, turning a casual run into an urban maze. Police will try to let traffic and pedestrians cross when safe, but expect major delays and diversions around the course, which snakes through areas including 6th Street, Washington Avenue, and East Passyunk Avenue. Don't be "that runner" trying to weave through barriers or ignore race officials. It's unsafe for everyone.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Philly 10K Gridlock:** Avoid running, cycling, or driving on or near the Philly 10K race course in South Philly (including South Street, Washington Avenue, and East Passyunk Avenue) until at least noon. Expect road closures and pedestrian delays throughout the morning.
</AlertBox>

The good news? The heavy rain that started the day should be giving way to mostly sunny skies and comfortable temperatures in the low 80s Fahrenheit by the afternoon. This means once the race clears out and any lingering moisture evaporates, the afternoon might actually offer some decent conditions, albeit with some humidity.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Slippery Start:** Early morning rain means paved and unpaved surfaces could be slick. Watch your footing, especially on older sidewalks, park paths, and near storm drains where puddles might still be lingering.
</AlertBox>

Looking ahead, get ready for a significant traffic and transit headache starting *tomorrow*. The Market Street Bridge is closing entirely to vehicular traffic for a whopping 14 months for rehabilitation, starting Monday, August 24th. This will undoubtedly shift traffic patterns, potentially impacting adjacent streets and routes for walkers and cyclists trying to cross the Schuylkill. While the Schuylkill River Trail itself is often maintained during such projects, plan for increased congestion on alternative routes and possible detours around the bridge approaches.

<AlertBox type="info">
**AQI & Pollen:** With moderate AQI and pollen levels, sensitive individuals might experience some respiratory discomfort. Consider keeping your effort levels down, especially in the afternoon when ozone levels can sometimes peak. Hydrate well.
</AlertBox>
