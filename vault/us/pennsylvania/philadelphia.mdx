## Local Hazards & Hot Takes

Today is not the day to try and impress anyone with your grit. The weather is actively working against you. That steady rain, combined with a biting wind, means hypothermia is more of a concern than usual if you're out too long and underdressed. Your joints will ache, your hands will freeze, and you'll likely track mud everywhere.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### SLIP HAZARD ALERT!
With persistent rain and cool temperatures, anything from damp leaves to city sidewalks will be slick. Be extra careful, especially on those familiar downhills on the **Kelly Drive** loop or any unpaved sections of the Wissahickon trails. A fall on wet asphalt or muddy trail isn't worth bragging rights.
</AlertBox>

Good news if you like car-free running: Martin Luther King Drive is indeed closed to vehicular traffic between the East Falls Bridge and the MLK Bridge/Eakins Oval for the holiday weekend, including today. So, you'll have the road to yourself, mostly, but don't expect it to be dry or clear of puddles.

<AlertBox type="info">
### MLK Drive is car-free today!
Enjoy the open road on MLK Drive, but remember the rain means you’ll be sharing it with puddles and probably a few less-than-enthusiastic cyclists. The side path remains accessible, but will likely be quite wet.
</AlertBox>

Also, be aware of ongoing construction throughout the city. While no major trail *closures* were reported overnight directly affecting the main running routes, city life means detours and uneven surfaces can pop up. For instance, there's a sinkhole causing a detour for SEPTA Bus 12 near Spruce and 5th until May 25, so if your route takes you through those parts of town, stay vigilant.

If you're one of the masochists signed up for the Tough Mudder Philly in Coatesville today, well, you're getting exactly what you signed up for: "thick mud, river beds, and enough elevation to keep things spicy." Just try not to break anything important out there.

Honestly, today’s a perfect day for the treadmill, some strength work indoors, or just embracing the couch. Your future self (and your joints) will thank you.
