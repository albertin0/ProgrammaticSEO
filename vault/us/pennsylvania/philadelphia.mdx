### Upcoming Disruptions You *Need* To Know About

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up for This Weekend (March 28-29):**

**Saturday, March 28: "No Kings 3" Demonstration**
The "No Kings 3" march and rally is scheduled for Saturday, March 28, and it's going to mess with the city center. Expect significant road closures and parking restrictions around Love Park, City Hall, and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway from late morning through the afternoon. If you usually run in this area, find an alternate route. Seriously, unless you enjoy weaving through crowds and angry motorists, stay clear.

**Sunday, March 29: Love Run Half Marathon & 7K**
Sunday brings the Love Run Half Marathon & 7K to Fairmount. This almost certainly means parts of Kelly Drive and the Schuylkill River Trail will be impacted, if not completely closed, during the event hours (7:30 AM to 12:30 PM). Don't be surprised if your usual long run route along the river is inaccessible. Check the official race maps for precise closures, but consider heading to Wissahickon Valley Park for a trail run instead if you want to avoid the crowds and asphalt.

**Construction Continues:**
While not directly impacting the main running trails *today*, general construction activity is picking up across Philadelphia. These are more regional, but it's a good reminder to always keep an eye out for unexpected detours or hazards, especially if your routes take you beyond the city's core paths.

In short: go run today, enjoy the relative peace. But start thinking about your weekend routes *now* unless you fancy a surprise sprint through a protest or a forced detour off your half marathon.
