*   **Extreme Heat Warning:** We're deep in a dangerous heat wave, with "feels like" temperatures hitting a terrifying 110-115 degrees today. *   **Pop-Up Storm Risk:** As if the heat wasn't enough, there's a slight risk of scattered thunderstorms this afternoon and evening. Any of these could bring gusty, damaging winds, adding another layer of "nope" to your outdoor plans.

You know how I usually tell you to hit the Schuylkill River Trail or run a few loops around the Art Museum steps? Not today, folks. You'd be cooking yourself. Even the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, usually great for a long stride, is a mess with road closures and event preparations for America 250 and the FIFA World Cup. They've set up cooling tents and water stations at the FIFA Fan Festival on Lemon Hill, but that's for spectating, not for setting a new personal best.

### DANGER: Prolonged Extreme Heat!
Seriously, do NOT underestimate this heat. Temperatures are expected to climb to 103-105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values pushing 115 degrees. This is not just "sweaty" weather; it's a "heat stroke waiting to happen" situation. Stay indoors, preferably in air conditioning. Your body won't thank you for pushing it in these conditions. This is the kind of heat that impacts even healthy individuals quickly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Warning: Afternoon Thunderstorms Possible
There's a slight chance of isolated to scattered thunderstorms popping up this afternoon and evening. These storms can bring sudden, locally damaging winds. If you absolutely *must* step outside for any reason, keep an eye on the sky and have a quick exit strategy to shelter.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration and Cooling is Key
If you find yourself stuck outside, drink *constant* water – not just when you're thirsty. Avoid caffeine and alcohol. The city has opened cooling centers and activated its Heatline (215-765-9040). Use them. Check on your elderly neighbors and anyone vulnerable. And for the love of Gritty, keep your pets inside. This heat wave is projected to last through Saturday, so plan your weekend accordingly.
</AlertBox>
