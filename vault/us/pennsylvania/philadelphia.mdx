## The Rundown for Monday, May 4th

Look, if you were planning on hitting the trails bright and early this morning, you probably already know it's a mixed bag out there. The good news: it's a crisp 13.0°C (about 55°F) and "Mainly Clear," so no downpours to contend with. Ideal running weather, truly.

However, let's talk about what's actually in that "clear" air. Our beloved Philadelphia region, bless its industrial heart, isn't doing so hot when it comes to particulate pollution. Recent reports, like the one from May 3rd, put the Philadelphia-Reading–Camden area at the 17th worst in the nation for average particulate pollution. That's a jump from 26th last year, which tells you things are headed in the wrong direction. Today's AQI is 55, squarely in the "Moderate" category, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. This stuff, from "dust, fires, and traffic," can be a real pain for anyone with asthma or other lung conditions, and honestly, it's not great for anyone long-term.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  If you've got sensitive lungs, asthma, or other respiratory issues, today's "Moderate" AQI and PM2.5 dominant pollutant means you might want to dial back the intensity or stick to an indoor workout.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the pollen. It's "Moderate" out there, which for many means itchy eyes and a runny nose. Not exactly conducive to hitting a PR, but probably won't send you to the ER unless you're severely allergic.

On the bright side for runners and cyclists, **Martin Luther King Drive** is still closed to vehicular traffic this morning between the East Falls Bridge and the MLK Bridge/Eakins Oval for its usual weekend recreational closure. That means you've got some glorious car-free miles to enjoy, at least until 7 a.m. when it reopens to cars for the week. So if you're out before then, enjoy the extra space!

In short, it's a "know your body" kind of day. The weather's inviting, but the air isn't doing us any favors, and neither are the trees. Maybe keep that long, hard interval session for a day when the AQI is "Good."
