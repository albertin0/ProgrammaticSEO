### Hazards & Headaches for Today's Philly Workout

<AlertBox type="danger">
**SCHUYLKILL RIVER TRAIL SECTION CLOSED DUE TO SINKHOLE!**
Yeah, you read that right. The Schuylkill River Trail between Race Street and JFK Boulevard is shut down indefinitely for emergency repairs because a sinkhole decided to open up. If that's part of your usual route, you're officially rerouted. No, seriously, don't try to "just squeeze by." It's closed for a reason. This isn't a suggestion, it's a structural integrity issue. Plan an alternate route or prepare to be disappointed (and potentially injured).
</AlertBox>

The good news for trail runners is Wissahickon Valley Park is absolutely *glowing* in peak fall colors right now. No major advisories or closures there for today, just some regular weekend guided hikes yesterday. Go enjoy the foliage on Forbidden Drive, but watch for roots and rocks as those leaves pile up. Don't be "that guy" who twists an ankle because they were too busy admiring the autumn splendor.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Localized Air Quality Concerns Near Port Richmond Cleanup:**
While the city-wide AQI is moderate, remember the Franklin Slag Pile Superfund site in Port Richmond is still undergoing excavation and cleanup. They're running air monitors, and operations stop if dust levels get too high. If you're running nearby on Delaware Avenue, just be aware that localized air quality could be worse, though it's not currently impacting the general city AQI.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Overnight Road Work in Northeast Philly:**
If you're a night owl runner or hitting the roads before sunrise tomorrow, be aware that Byberry Road between U.S. 1 (Roosevelt Boulevard) and Trina Drive has overnight lane closures for pothole repairs starting tonight (Monday, Oct 26) from 9 PM to 5 AM. Not directly a trail issue, but if you're navigating by car to your workout spot in that area, expect delays.
</AlertBox>

Today's forecast is almost too good for late October, so don't let a closed trail or a little pollen stop you entirely. Just pick your path wisely, stay safe, and for the love of all that is holy, don't mess with sinkholes.
