## Trail & Road Hazards

**Pennypack Trail Alert:** If you're heading to the Northeast, be advised: the Pine Road Footbridge over the Pennypack Trail is completely inaccessible as of August 18th. This isn't a temporary thing; the Pine Road Bridge itself is closed for a full replacement expected to last until Summer 2027, with periodic temporary trail closures during demolition. So, if Pennypack is your jam, scout your entry points and prepare for detours around the Pine Road area.

**Market Street Bridge:** Heads up for anyone traversing Center City. A "longer closure" of the Market Street Bridge over the Schuylkill River is slated to begin in "late August." While pedestrians and cyclists "should retain access," the city's already encouraging folks to consider other routes like Chestnut or Walnut Street. Expect construction impacts around this critical artery.
