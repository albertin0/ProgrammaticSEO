**Heads Up: Residual Flooding & Debris!**
Remember those nasty storms that rolled through yesterday, July 11th? They brought heavy downpours, flooding, and knocked down trees across the region. While the Schuylkill River near 30th Street isn't under any active flood advisories as of a couple days ago, it doesn't mean the **Schuylkill River Trail** is pristine. Expect standing water in low-lying areas, mud, and potential tree debris, especially north of the Art Museum or closer to Manayunk. Don't be that person who twists an ankle because you thought you could leap over a log. Take it easy and stay aware of your footing.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Event Gridlock: Lemon Hill & South Philly are a Zoo!**
If your usual route takes you anywhere near Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park, you're going to be swimming upstream. The FIFA Fan Festival is still in full swing through July 19th, drawing massive crowds. Parking restrictions are still in effect for blocks around there, from Fairmount to Strawberry Mansion. Likewise, MLB All-Star Week has South Philly, particularly around Citizens Bank Park, locked down with events and people until today, July 12th. Unless you enjoy dodging tourists and weaving through human traffic, reconsider your usual stomping grounds in these areas. It’s not just inconvenient; it’s genuinely hard to get a consistent pace.
</AlertBox>

Look, the weather's finally decent for running. The stifling heat wave from earlier this month is ancient history. But this isn't an excuse to turn off your brain.
