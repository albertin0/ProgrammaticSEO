### What's Kicking Around Your Lungs

The air quality in Philly today is sitting pretty with an AQI of 55, which is firmly in the "Good" category. Your dominant pollutant is PM2.5, but at this level, unless you've got some serious pre-existing conditions, you're likely fine to push it. No wildfire smoke from out west making its way here, no industrial spills to worry about. Just good, crisp autumn air.

Pollen's still lingering, like that one guest who just won't leave after a party. "Moderate" for ragweed and molds means some of you will still be sniffling and reaching for the tissues, but it's not the suffocating cloud we sometimes get in late spring. If you're sensitive, pop an antihistamine an hour before you head out, and maybe stick to trails with less exposed brush.

### Weather Report: Mostly Not-Trying-to-Kill-You

At 13°C and "Mainly Clear," the weather is as close to ideal as you're going to get for a workout in mid-October in Philadelphia. This isn't that oppressive late-summer humidity, nor is it the biting wind of January. It's the kind of day where you can actually enjoy the effort. Throw on a light long-sleeve or even a short-sleeve if you warm up fast.

<AlertBox type="warning">
No big events are blocking major running routes or closing down sections of the Schuylkill River Trail or Forbidden Drive today. However, always be aware of local park maintenance crews. Sometimes they'll have small sections coned off for tree trimming or path repair, especially around Fairmount Park. Give them space.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
The sun sets around 6 PM these days. If you're planning an evening run, especially on less-lit trails like some of the smaller offshoots in Pennypack Park, make sure you've got reflective gear and a headlamp. Don't rely on your phone's flashlight – that's a recipe for a twisted ankle.
</AlertBox>
