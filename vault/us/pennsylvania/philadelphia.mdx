Today's actually a pretty decent day for a workout if you know where to go and what to expect. We've got clear skies and a comfortable 18.1°C (64.6°F), which is a far cry from the scorching nonsense we saw earlier this month. Your joints will thank you for the mild temps.

However, if your usual route involves the trails around Belmont Plateau in Fairmount Park, you need a new plan. The Roots Picnic brought a whole lot of people and a whole lot of closures. While Montgomery Drive supposedly reopened this morning at 5 AM after being shut down for the festival, don't expect smooth sailing. Event cleanup doesn't just vanish with the sunrise. More importantly, Belmont Mansion Drive, a key artery in that area, remains fully closed until at least Wednesday evening to accommodate venue construction and breakdown. This means major detours and frustration if you're trying to navigate that part of the park. Even if Martin Luther King Drive is now officially open for recreational use again (it was closed for the event over the weekend), getting *to* it might still be a pain if you're coming from the Belmont Plateau side.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Fairmount Park Navigation Alert:** Avoid the Belmont Plateau area of Fairmount Park if you're looking for an uninterrupted run or ride. Significant event-related road closures and cleanup from the Roots Picnic are still impacting the area, especially Belmont Mansion Drive, which is closed until June 3. Plan alternative routes, maybe down along Kelly Drive or explore some of the less-congested spots in Wissahickon Valley Park.
</AlertBox>

Air quality is hovering at a "Moderate" 55 AQI, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the main culprit. It's not terrible, but it's not pristine, either. For most, it's fine, but if you're battling respiratory issues, keep your inhaler handy and listen to your body. Moderate pollen also means your allergies might try to hijack your workout. Pop that antihistamine now, not halfway through your run.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydration & Awareness:** Even with moderate temps, keep that water bottle glued to your hand. And seriously, look up from your phone. There was a fatal hit-and-run in South Philly over the weekend, which is a stark reminder that even on a beautiful day, city streets require your full attention.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's a decent day to get outside, just be smart about your route and recognize that parts of our beloved Fairmount Park are going to be a hot mess for a few more days.
