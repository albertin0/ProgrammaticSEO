## Trail Talk: Conquer MLK Drive

Here's the real kicker for runners and cyclists: Martin Luther King Jr. Drive, stretching from the East Falls Bridge down to the Eakins Oval, is once again car-free this weekend. The city's been doing this every Saturday and Sunday from March through November, and it's always a blessing. So, if you've been wanting to hammer out some miles on a flat, fast course without dodging traffic, this is your golden opportunity. It's truly one of the best stretches of pavement we have for putting in serious work.

  **MLK Drive Weekend Closure:** Take advantage of the car-free Martin Luther King Jr. Drive for your runs and rides this weekend, between the East Falls Bridge and Eakins Oval. It's closed to vehicles every Saturday and Sunday from 7 AM to 7 AM Monday.
