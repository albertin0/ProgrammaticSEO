### Mind the Winds!
Strong winds are no joke. They can sap your energy, make those miles feel longer, and even carry unexpected debris. If you're on a long run, consider routes with more tree cover or buildings for a bit of a windbreak. And seriously, watch your step.
</AlertBox>

### Your Favorite Trails Are Shut Down

Now, for the really annoying part for us regulars. Forget your usual long run or bike ride along **Martin Luther King Drive** today. The city has shut down MLK Drive, not just to cars, but for *recreational use* as well, for the entire weekend due to the Roots Picnic at Belmont Plateau. That means no running, no cycling, no nothing along that stretch. Same goes for Chamounix Drive and Montgomery Drive around that area – completely closed.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Avoid Fairmount Park's Roots Picnic Area!
Seriously, stay away from Belmont Plateau, MLK Drive, Chamounix Drive, and Montgomery Drive this weekend. They are closed to all recreational traffic for the Roots Picnic. Don't waste your time showing up, you'll just get turned around and frustrated. Find an alternate route or another park.
</AlertBox>

On a slightly more active note, if you're in North Philly, the Philly Running Alliance is hosting a 5k Run & 2k Walk in Hunting Park today. The park will be closed to vehicles for the event, making it pedestrian-friendly, but expect crowds. So, if you're looking for a group run or just want to navigate some extra people, that's an option. Otherwise, be prepared for heavier foot traffic if you're just trying to get some solo miles in there.

### Pollen's Still Around
While generally good, we're still dealing with moderate pollen levels. If you're susceptible to allergies, hit up your Zyrtec before you head out, or you'll be sniffling through your miles.
</AlertBox>

In conclusion, it's a decent day to get out, but you need to be smart about *where* you go and prepare for the wind. Avoid the Roots Picnic area of Fairmount Park like it's a marathon water stop with only warm Gatorade.
