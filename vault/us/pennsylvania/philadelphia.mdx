### Today's Rundown

The weather in Philadelphia today is going to be a real joy, folks. We're looking at overcast skies and a comfortable 22.5°C (73°F), which normally would be prime running weather. But don't let that fool you. The National Weather Service has issued a Flood Watch for our region through Monday evening, and for good reason. We've had a significant chance of heavy rain and flash flooding since last night, with "several waves of storms with tropical downpours" expected to bring an additional 2-4 inches of rain to some areas. That means streets could flood quickly, and you absolutely do not want to be caught out in that.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOOD WARNING: Turn Around, Don't Drown!**
Seriously, if you encounter flooded roads or trails, *do not* try to go through them. Less than two feet of water can float your car, and it's even more dangerous on foot. Many of our underpasses, especially around the Schuylkill Expressway, are notorious for collecting water. Find an alternate route or just stay inside.
</AlertBox>

Pollen levels are moderate, so if you're normally sensitive, expect some sniffles even without the rain. Air quality is also sitting at a moderate 55 AQI, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While generally acceptable for most, sensitive groups should still be mindful, especially when combining it with high humidity.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Slippery Surfaces & Debris.**
With the heavy rain and lingering storm risk from overnight, expect wet, potentially slippery surfaces everywhere. Watch out for puddles disguising potholes, storm drains that might be overflowing, and any downed branches or debris, especially on trails like those in Wissahickon Valley Park that are often prone to washouts after heavy rain.
</AlertBox>

Finally, while the massive road closures for the July 4th and America 250 events around the Benjamin Franklin Parkway officially lifted at 6 AM this morning, don't be surprised by residual detours or cleanup activity in the area. If you're planning a run anywhere near Center City, assume things are still a bit messy.
