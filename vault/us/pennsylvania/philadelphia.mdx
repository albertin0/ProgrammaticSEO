## Today's Vitals: Don't Be a Hero

Seriously, folks. The morning might feel deceptively pleasant at 13°C (55.4°F) and "Mainly Clear," but don't let that fool you. The daily forecast for Philadelphia is a completely different beast, with a "NEXT Weather Alert" in effect for extreme heat and thunderstorms. This heat advisory is serious and lasts through at least Saturday evening. If you absolutely *must* get out there, keep it short, stay hydrated, and for the love of all that is holy, don't ignore the warning signs of heat exhaustion.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Heat index values are expected to hit triple digits, making outdoor activity highly risky. Plan for frequent breaks, seek shade, and drink plenty of water. Monitor yourself and others for signs of heat-related illness.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Wissahickon and Beyond

If you were planning a long run on **Forbidden Drive in Wissahickon Valley Park**, be aware that film crews are still in the area through today. While the drive itself remains open, you might encounter vehicles and equipment. Adhere to any posted signs for "equipment ahead" or "slow ahead." It's always a good idea to stay alert, but especially with added distractions and the impending weather.

And for those thinking about the Fox Chase Lorimer Trail, remember that the Pine Road bridge closure for reconstruction starts August 17th and will last through summer 2027. The trail will generally stay open with some temporary closures, but it's not today's issue. The larger concern today is simply not getting fried or struck by lightning on any path you choose.
