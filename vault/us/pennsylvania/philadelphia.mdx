### Pollen Bomb Incoming
Your nose and throat might hate you today. While the overall "pollen level" might be listed as moderate, specific ragweed pollen is currently running high. ### Know Your Air

The Air Quality Index is hovering around 55, which is in the "Moderate" category due to PM2.5. For the average runner, this isn't likely to cause major issues. However, if you're particularly sensitive, have asthma, or other respiratory conditions, you might notice some irritation. Listen to your body – it usually knows best, even if your brain wants to push that extra mile.

## Local Trail Talk: Watch Out by the Schuylkill

If your route involves the Schuylkill River Trail, especially near Center City, be aware of the ongoing headache that is the Market Street Bridge. PennDOT's got that thing shut down to vehicular traffic for a major reconstruction project, which started back in August 2026 and isn't expected to finish until October 2027. While pedestrians and cyclists *should* still have access, you might encounter detours or disruptions, and they're suggesting alternative routes via Chestnut or Walnut Street. Don't assume your usual flow under the bridge will be uninterrupted. It's Philly; there's always a construction cone.

If you're cycling or running on the Schuylkill River Trail near the Museum, double-check your approach to the Market Street Bridge. Expect potential bottlenecks or detours for pedestrian and bike traffic due to the ongoing reconstruction. It’s a mess, but necessary.
