Today, July 25th, Mother Nature is practically rolling out the red carpet for outdoor activity. We're looking at a mainly clear day with temperatures starting around a cool 18°C (64°F) and topping out around 29°C (84°F) later on. No rain, no extreme heat warnings, no wind advisories; just pleasant summer conditions. Your joints will thank you for getting out in this kind of weather.

But here's the catch, and it's a big one for anyone planning a long run or ride: the event schedule is brutal. The **Independence Day Regatta** has shut down a significant stretch of Kelly Drive, specifically from Fountain Green to Strawberry Mansion Drive, and it's staying closed until 6 PM Sunday. So, if your Saturday morning ritual involves cruising along the river, you're going to need a new plan or be prepared for some detours and congestion on whatever routes are left open.

Then, just when you might think the coast is clear, the **Philly Midnight Run** kicks off this evening. Road closures around Eakins Oval and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway begin at 8 PM and last until about 2 AM Sunday. Even Kelly Drive inbound gets detoured to Fairmount Avenue for this one. So, don't even *think* about an evening jaunt around the Art Museum loop or up the Parkway without planning for major interruptions.

While it's a vast improvement from the "Code Purple" days earlier this month when wildfire smoke choked the city, "moderate" still means there's a risk for those unusually sensitive to air pollution.
