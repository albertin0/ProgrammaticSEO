However, it wouldn't be Philly without *some* kind of inconvenience. The big one to watch out for, especially if you're venturing out northeast, is the **Cresheim Trail Fall Foliage Festival.** This isn't some tiny church bake sale; they're blocking off the 6800-6900 blocks of Torresdale Avenue for this shindig through October 25th. If your route involves that stretch or nearby access to the Cresheim Trail in Wissahickon Park, plan your detour now or embrace the festival vibes. Don't be "that guy" trying to argue with a volunteer in a leaf costume about your Strava segment.
