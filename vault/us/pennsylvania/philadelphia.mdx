<AlertBox type="warning">
**Your Lungs & Allergies are on Notice.**
The AQI is currently sitting at 55 with PM2.5 being the main culprit, and pollen is at a moderate level. For most, this is just a nuisance, but if you're one of the folks who turn into a walking allergy commercial this time of year, consider scaling back intensity or hitting the treadmill. Don't push it if your airways are already feeling constricted.

Let's talk about actually *moving* your body outside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Belmont Plateau is Booked.**
If your Saturday ritual involves tearing up the cross-country trails at Belmont Plateau, think again. The Phila NEDA Walk is taking over the trails all day. You'll be dodging a charity event, which is noble, sure, but not ideal for your tempo run. Find another hill, maybe somewhere in Wissahickon Valley Park if you don't mind the drive.
</AlertBox>

Construction season is in full swing, because when is it not? While not all of it will directly block your path, it's worth being aware of. The I-95 Central Access Philadelphia (CAP) Project near Penn's Landing is still chugging along, building a new park and bridge. Expect some minor lane shifts on Columbus Boulevard and Front Street, which could make accessing the waterfront a bit of a headache for pedestrians and cyclists, even if the South Street Pedestrian Bridge is mostly open.

Further afield, if you're a Schuylkill River Trail die-hard, know that there are ongoing construction impacts, especially around the Keim Street Bridge in Pottstown and near Norristown with the Chester Valley Trail connection. You'll find detours and intermittent closures. Don't just blindly follow Strava segments; check local alerts before you head out, or you'll be adding unplanned mileage.

Speaking of detours, the recent parking garage collapse at 30th and Grays Ferry Ave means there are still lane restrictions on Grays Ferry Avenue. If your route takes you through South Philly near that area, plan for some congestion.

<AlertBox type="info">
**MLK Drive is Your Friend Today.**
Good news for runners and cyclists: Martin Luther King Drive is closed to vehicular traffic on weekends from the East Falls Bridge to the MLK Bridge/Eakins Oval. This seasonal closure started in March and is ongoing until November, giving you plenty of car-free space to get your miles in. The side path is also still open.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if you're planning a stroll through Dilworth Park, remember it's Panda Fest today and tomorrow. Expect crowds, food vendors, and pandas (inflatable, presumably). Similarly, FDR Park is hosting free community paddle boating on Edgewood Lake. Fun for the family, maybe less so for trying to find a quiet corner.
