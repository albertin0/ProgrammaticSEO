## The Weather Report: Foggy Start, Warm Day

Woke up to pea soup? That's your dense fog advisory talking, in effect until 10 AM. So if you're out there before then, you're practically invisible. Take heed. After the fog burns off, though, we're looking at a truly unseasonable day. Expect highs to soar into the mid to upper 70s, which is well above average for mid-March. Enjoy it while it lasts, because a strong cold front rolls in by Thursday, ready to drop temperatures by 20-30 degrees and bring some rain, possibly even a wintry mix to the collar counties. Wednesday will also be warm, but clouds will increase ahead of that front.

## Air Quality & Pollen: Breathe (Mostly) Easy, But Watch for the Sneezes

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 55 today, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's in the "Moderate" category, which means it's generally acceptable for most people. However, if you're unusually sensitive to air pollution, you might experience minor respiratory symptoms.

Pollen, on the other hand, is a bit more of a nuisance. It's a moderate day, meaning your seasonal allergies are probably already making their presence known.

## Hazards & Head-Ups

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Early Morning Fog: Seriously, See and Be Seen!
If you're out before 10 AM, that dense fog isn't just atmospheric drama; it's a legitimate visibility hazard. Wear reflective gear, carry a light, and assume no one can see you. Drivers are just trying to get their coffee, not spot your black running tights.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Schuylkill River Trail Users: Plan Ahead for Tomorrow
Good news for daytime runners on the Schuylkill River Trail today: the section beneath the MLK Bridge (between Paine's Park and Fairmount Water Works) is open. But **starting tomorrow, March 11, from 9 PM to 5 AM**, that stretch will be closed for two weeks due to concrete removal as part of the ongoing MLK Bridge Rehabilitation Project. Detours will be posted, directing you behind the Art Museum to Kelly Drive. So if you're a night owl, consider a different route starting tomorrow night.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Logan Circle Construction: Mind Your Step
The innermost travel lane and sidewalk around Logan Circle are currently closed for upgrades, impacting pedestrian flow. Work typically happens weekdays from 7 AM to 3:30 PM. Stay aware of your surroundings and any signage. It’s a busy intersection, and getting ready for the 250th birthday means constant disruption.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated in the Unexpected Warmth
With temperatures pushing into the mid to upper 70s today, it's easy to forget it's still technically early spring. Don't get caught out there dehydrated. Carry water, even if your brain is still telling you it's "cold enough."
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Lingering Snowmelt & Construction Concerns
While direct flooding isn't a top-tier alert today, remember we've had a heavy winter. Snowmelt can lead to localized soggy areas and increase the risk of basement water intrusion for homeowners. Also, ongoing construction projects like those on I-95 and U.S. 202 for sinkhole repairs are a constant fixture on Philly's landscape, so be mindful of potential road changes if your route takes you near these areas.

</AlertBox>
