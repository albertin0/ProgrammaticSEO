### Trail Conditions & Construction

Beyond the sky trying to kill you, there's ground-level annoyance. The ongoing Cobbs Creek Golf Course restoration means flood mitigation efforts are still underway. While this is long-term for flood control, local residents have voiced concerns about construction potentially exacerbating flooding issues. So if you're near Cobbs Creek, stay aware of your surroundings, especially with the severe weather potentially bringing localized heavy downpours.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wissahickon Valley Park Trail Closures:** The Valley Green Run Restoration means sections of the Orange and White trails off Valley Green Road are closed. Don't be that person ignoring the signs. Check the Friends of the Wissahickon website for detours and specific closure information before you head out. This isn't a temporary thing; it's a long-term project.
