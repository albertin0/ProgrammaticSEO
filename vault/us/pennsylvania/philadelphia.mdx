## The Rundown for June 10, 2026

Look, it’s not often we get genuinely pleasant running weather in June, so soak up that 13-degree chill. You won't be sweating through your eyeballs like those poor souls trying to run the Broad Street Run in May a few weeks ago (remember that heat?). The "mainly clear" skies are a bonus, meaning no random summer downpours to turn your Schuylkill River Trail run into a slip-and-slide.

However, let’s be real. It’s Philly, so there’s always *something*. Today, that "something" is a double whammy of mediocre air and a major event turning parts of our beloved Fairmount Park into a temporary pedestrian-only zone.

### Air Quality & Allergies: Don't Breathe *Too* Deeply

Your apps might be telling you it's "moderate" for AQI at 55, and while that's not "stay indoors and hermetically seal your windows" bad, it's not "run a PR" good either. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, those tiny particles that your lungs just *love* to inhale deep down. If you've got even a hint of asthma or other respiratory issues, you'll probably feel it a bit. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and it's a pretty good recipe for a post-run sinus explosion.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate AQI & Pollen:** If you're typically sensitive, consider dialing back your intensity, especially on longer runs. Keep an eye out for any lingering wildfire haze from previous weeks – though not officially reported today, it's always a wildcard in the summer.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Event Chaos: FIFA Fan Fest Edition

Okay, this is the big one today. The FIFA World Cup Fan Festival at Lemon Hill is in full swing, and it's turning the surrounding area into a madhouse. If your usual route takes you anywhere near Kelly Drive, the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, or Lemon Hill itself, be prepared for some serious detours and crowds. The local lanes of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway are already shut down for the long haul, and Waterworks Drive starts its daily 10 a.m. closure tomorrow, June 11th, so plan ahead if you usually park there for a morning run.

The Circuit Trails Coalition is pushing the idea of using the Schuylkill River Trail to access the festival, which is great for foot traffic, but means more bodies on the trail itself. So, if you're looking for a peaceful long run on the SRT near the Art Museum or Boathouse Row, you might want to adjust your expectations (and maybe your pace).

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FIFA Fan Festival Disruptions:** Avoid running directly through the Lemon Hill area if you're trying to hit a specific pace or avoid crowds. Expect significant pedestrian traffic, road closures (especially on Kelly Drive and the Parkway), and limited parking. Your usual route might be totally unrecognizable or blocked.
</AlertBox>

General construction is also always a thing, with ongoing PennDOT projects on I-95 and I-76, and localized PWD work for water mains and sewers across the city. While not typically trail-blocking, they can mean unexpected detours on city streets.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route:** If you're heading out for a longer effort, check live traffic maps and any specific event advisories. Consider trails further afield, like Forbidden Drive in Wissahickon Valley Park, if you want to completely escape the urban crush.
</AlertBox>

Basically, it's a pretty nice day to be outside, but don't assume your favorite running spots are business as usual. Stay aware, be flexible, and maybe pack a nasal spray.
