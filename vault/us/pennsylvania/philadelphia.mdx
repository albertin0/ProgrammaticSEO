Look, I know you're itching to get out there, especially after yesterday's deluge of tornado watches and flash flood warnings that swept through Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. It might feel deceptively calm and cool this morning at 13.0°C (that's a crisp 55.4°F for you Celsius-averse folks), and the main storm threats from Tuesday night *have* been canceled. But don't you dare think that means we're in the clear.

The National Weather Service was tracking severe thunderstorms and the potential for flash flooding through Wednesday. While a broader flood watch for the area was lifted this morning, isolated flash flooding was still a possibility for Wednesday. Even if the skies are currently "mainly clear," that's just a temporary reprieve. Pop-up showers and thunderstorms are expected to develop later today and into the evening, bringing with them a dose of high humidity that'll make even a light jog feel like a slog. Add to that the possibility of patchy fog earlier today, and you've got a recipe for a potentially frustrating, if not outright soggy, workout.

## Air & Allergy Check

Air quality is sitting at a moderate 55 AQI, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Not great, not terrible. If you've got sensitive lungs, you might feel it, but it's probably not the biggest thing trying to put you in an early grave today. Pollen's also hanging around at a moderate level, so plan accordingly if you're prone to seasonal sniffles.
