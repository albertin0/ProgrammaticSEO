## The Rundown: Don't Get Blown Away

Today, you're looking at a cool 13.3°C (around 56°F) and a mostly cloudy sky. This is classic fall running weather, but Mother Nature decided to add a little extra spice with some breezy conditions. Those northeast winds aren't just a gentle whisper; they're going to make you work for it, especially when you're exposed. Think about how those gusts feel coming off the water if you're cruising along the **Schuylkill River Trail** or anywhere along the waterfront.

The good news is the air quality is solid, so you won't be sucking down any nasties. However, with moderate pollen in the mix, any outdoor warriors prone to seasonal allergies should probably pre-dose. Nobody wants to finish a run looking like they went twelve rounds with a tissue box.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Out For Winds & Wetness
The nor'easter brewing offshore means persistent northeast wind gusts up to 20 mph are expected today. Be mindful of falling leaves, debris, and challenging headwind sections. Furthermore, a Coastal Flood Advisory for the tidal Delaware River starts tonight. While major flooding isn't predicted for the city proper during the day, expect potentially higher water levels and slick spots near the river, especially closer to this evening.
</AlertBox>
