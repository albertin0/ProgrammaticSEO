### Trail Status & Event Overload

Look, I love our trails as much as the next runner, but today, you've got to be smart about *where* you go. Kelly Drive, especially near Boathouse Row, is going to be a madhouse with the Stotesbury Regatta. Spectators, rowers, and parents with oversized cameras everywhere. Don't even *think* about trying to PR there today; you'll be dodging more people than potholes on Broad Street.

And remember that sinkhole that ate a chunk of the **Schuylkill River Trail** between Race Street and JFK Boulevard? While some repairs are ongoing and certain sections have reopened, always check local signage or the Schuylkill Banks website before you commit to that segment. We've also got "Art In The Open" happening along the Schuylkill River Banks from the Water Works to Walnut Street, which means more foot traffic than usual.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid Kelly Drive if you value your sanity (and kneecaps).** The Stotesbury Regatta will have that stretch absolutely packed. Find an alternative route unless you enjoy weaving through slow-moving crowds.
</AlertBox>

If you're looking for a quieter long run, maybe hit the Wissahickon Valley Park trails, but keep an eye out for construction on the bridges there – the city's still rehabilitating a few of them. Or, honestly, consider the **Forbidden Drive** in Fairmount Park. It's generally a reliable escape, and you can pre-run some of the "Run to Rebuild" route happening there tomorrow without the actual race-day crowds.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Local Tip: Planning a weekend long run?** Tomorrow (May 16th) is the "Run to Rebuild" 5K and 2-mile walk on Forbidden Drive in Fairmount Park. If you're running that, great! If not, be aware of increased foot traffic in that area tomorrow.
</AlertBox>

So, in short: get your run in, but manage expectations. It's not the worst day, but it's far from perfect. Be aware, be flexible, and for the love of all that is holy, watch where you're going.
