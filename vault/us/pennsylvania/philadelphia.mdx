## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **AQI is moderate (55 PM2.5),** meaning the air itself isn't terrible, but it's not exactly sparkling clean either. For you sensitive types, maybe keep the long-distance stuff indoors.
*   **Pollen is moderate**, which is a standard annoyance this time of year. If you're a sniffer, take your meds.
*   **Weather's a bit of a mixed bag.** The official data pegged it at a crisp 13°C and "Mainly Clear," but current reports show light rain falling this morning with temperatures around 22°C (72°F) and 87% humidity. So, prepare for damp, sticky conditions.
*   **FIFA World Cup & Welcome America Festivities are in full swing.** Forget personal space around Lemon Hill, the Ben Franklin Parkway, and the Philadelphia Museum of Art. The FIFA Fan Festival at Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park, plus a World Cup match at Philadelphia Stadium this evening, means major road closures and crowds. And if that wasn't enough, it's Day 9 of the Wawa Welcome America Festival, culminating in fireworks at RiverRink Summerfest.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Crowds and Closures!
Between the FIFA Fan Festival at Lemon Hill and the Wawa Welcome America events, expect significant congestion, especially around Fairmount Park and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway. While foot and bike traffic along the Schuylkill River Trail is *supposed* to remain accessible, navigating the throngs of people could be its own workout. Parking restrictions are also in effect around Lemon Hill.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Updates: Don't Assume Smooth Sailing

**Wissahickon Valley Park:** If your usual escape is the Wissahickon, prepare for a detour or two. Valley Green Road is shut down to vehicular traffic for about six months due to the Valley Green Run Restoration and Pedestrian Bridge Project. Pedestrians and cyclists get intermittent access, but the Orange and White trails northbound from Valley Green Road are completely closed. Your new best friend? Forbidden Drive.

**Schuylkill River Trail:** Good news for future runs, bad news for *current* ones: there's construction on the Delaware River Trail Extension between Penn Treaty Park and the Battery, which started mid-June and is slated to last about three months. This isn't the whole SRT, obviously, but it's a key waterfront connection to be aware of. Also, while not in Philly proper, upgrades to Reeser's Landing further up the Schuylkill River Trail are set to begin in July and will take 6-8 weeks.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated and Visible
With the light rain and higher humidity reported this morning, staying hydrated is crucial. Visibility might also be reduced, especially near busy event areas, so consider reflective gear if you're out early or late.
</AlertBox>
