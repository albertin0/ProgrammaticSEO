## The Rundown for May 14, 2026

Alright, runners, cyclists, and general outdoor enthusiasts, today's shaping up to be a pretty standard spring day in the city, which, let's be honest, often means a mixed bag. The good news: that chill in the air is actually a blessing. With temperatures hovering around 12.7°C (55°F) and an overcast sky, you won't be battling the oppressive heat we'll undoubtedly see in a few weeks. Humidity is high at 90%, so you might feel a bit sticky, but it's nothing a good post-workout shower can't fix.

Now for the less-than-stellar news. Our air quality is teetering on the edge of "moderate" with an AQI of 55, and PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant. What does that mean for you? Well, the City of Philadelphia Department of Public Health says it's generally acceptable, but if you're sensitive to air pollution – think asthma, other respiratory issues, or even just a general dislike of feeling cruddy after a run – you might experience some minor symptoms. We all know Philadelphia has its challenges with particulate pollution, being ranked 17th worst in the nation for average levels between 2022-2024, so this isn't exactly breaking news. Combine that with moderate pollen levels, and some of you might be reaching for the tissues as well as your running shoes.

### MLK Drive - Know Before You Go

For those of you who typically hit Martin Luther King Drive for a car-free experience, remember that the seasonal weekend closure **does not apply today**. It's only closed to vehicles from Saturday at 7 a.m. to Monday at 7 a.m.. So, if you're out there today (Thursday), expect vehicle traffic as usual between the East Falls Bridge and the MLK Bridge/Eakins Oval. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**MLK Drive is Open to Vehicle Traffic Today!** Don't assume it's your usual weekend running paradise. Share the road and stay alert for cars and bikes.

### West Philly Street Fest Tonight

If your workout takes you through West Philadelphia this evening, be aware that the **Overbrook Night Market** (aka "Rep's Night Out") is happening from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. along North 63rd Street and Lancaster Avenue. Expect road closures, detours, and general pedestrian congestion in that area. Probably not the best spot for a tempo run.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Overbrook Night Market:** Avoid North 63rd Street and Lancaster Avenue in West Philly from 6-10 PM. It'll be a street festival, not a race course.
