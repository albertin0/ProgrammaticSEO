## The Rundown: Your Saturday Sweat Session

Look, after a winter that felt longer than a training run to nowhere, these mild temperatures and clear skies are a gift. Seriously, 13°C (55°F) is prime running weather for our city. But don't get too complacent, because Philly always has a few curveballs.

### Air Quality & Allergens

The air quality being "Moderate" with an AQI of 55 isn't ideal, but it's far from a lockdown. If you've got sensitive lungs, like me on a bad day, you might notice a little extra something in the air. This is mostly thanks to PM2.5, those fine particulate matters that love to hang around. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and some of you might be reaching for the tissues mid-stride. Consider dialing back intensity if you're feeling sniffly or wheezy. Tomorrow (Sunday) is actually forecast to have "Poor" air quality for sensitive groups, so enjoy today's relative calm while you can.

### Trail Alerts & Obstacles

If you're hitting the **Schuylkill River Trail**, especially around Center City, keep your eyes peeled. There are still ongoing overnight closures under the Martin Luther King Jr. Drive Bridge for rehabilitation work. While the trail is open during the day, general construction in the area means you should be aware of your surroundings. They *did* fix that sinkhole near Race Street and JFK Boulevard back in December, so at least we don't have that to contend with anymore.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**SRT Construction:** The Schuylkill River Trail beneath the MLK Jr. Drive Bridge has ongoing construction, meaning potential detours or changed conditions during the day. Stay alert for signage, even if you run this stretch regularly.
</AlertBox>

Heading to **Wissahickon Valley Park**? Embrace the mud, buttercup. It's "mud season" out there, and even with a clear forecast today, any recent rain means those trails will be slick and soft in spots. A guided hike for the Spring Equinox is even happening in Houston Meadow today, which just means more people (and more feet churning up the mud). You might still encounter some residual effects from the Valley Green Run Restoration and Pedestrian Bridge project, so be mindful of potential closures on some of the smaller connector trails, specifically the Orange and White trails northbound from Valley Green Road.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Muddy Wissahickon:** If you're tackling the Wissahickon trails, expect mud and possibly standing water, especially on unpaved sections. Choose appropriate footwear and watch your footing.
</AlertBox>
