<AlertBox type="warning">
### Center City Crowd Control
If your usual loop takes you through Center City, be aware that "Open Streets: West Walnut" is closing portions of Walnut and 18th streets to vehicle traffic today. This is great if you want to run car-free streets, but it also means more pedestrians will be out and about. It's not a hazard, per se, but it's definitely going to slow down your pace if you're trying to hit any PRs. And don't even *think* about doing speed work around Rittenhouse Square; the Fine Art Show is still running through today, so that whole area is going to be packed.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Manayunk Maneuvers

If you're looking for an uninterrupted flow, maybe hit the Schuylkill River Trail instead, or venture deeper into Wissahickon Valley Park where the crowds thin out a bit.
