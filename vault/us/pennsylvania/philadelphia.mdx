### Trail Talk: Where You Can (and Can't) Run Free

Listen up, because this is where good intentions meet reality.

First, your beloved **Martin Luther King Jr. Drive:** It's Friday. That means it's open to traffic, as per usual weekday operations. And a heads-up for the weekend: due to the Roots Picnic on May 30-31, MLK Drive *will not* be car-free this weekend, even though the seasonal closures typically include Memorial Day. The side path will remain accessible, but don't expect to own the road. Plan accordingly if you're used to those car-free weekend sprints.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pennypack Trail Paving:** If the Pennypack Trail is your usual stomping ground, be aware that Welsh Road is currently closed for paving and is expected to reopen by June 2nd. This means detours or access issues near that section. The Moredon Road parking lot will also be closed June 2-5 for more paving. Don't show up expecting to park there next week.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Schuylkill River Trail & Chester Valley Trail:** There are ongoing detours and caution areas where the Chester Valley Trail meets the Schuylkill River Trail in Norristown due to site renovation work. This has been going on since January and is expected to last about a year. AT&T also had some work near the SRT in Norristown and Conshohocken earlier this year, so keep an eye out for lingering disruptions. Construction of the Keim Street Bridge over the Schuylkill River in Pottstown is ongoing until 2028, so further upriver users should be aware.
</AlertBox>
