## Your Daily Dose of Philly Frustrations

Alright, let's talk about the real buzzkills today. While the air *looks* clear, and the temperature is spot on, actually *finding* a clear, uninterrupted path is going to be a challenge, especially if you're a creature of habit.

### Trail & Road Disruptions

The biggest headache for anyone trying to get their miles in near Center City is the ongoing **Schuylkill River Trail closure between Race Street and JFK Boulevard**. There's a proper sinkhole that's decided to make itself at home under the asphalt, and until further notice, that section is a no-go zone. Don't be "that guy" trying to hop fences; it's closed for a reason, likely due to structural instability. Find an alternate route on the street grid if you're heading that way. It's a pain, I know, but better safe than swimming in a sinkhole.

Over in West Philly, if your route takes you near it, the **Market Street Bridge** is still closed to vehicle traffic as part of its massive PennDOT upgrade that started late last month. One pedestrian sidewalk *is* open, but expect more foot traffic than usual if you're trying to cross, and certainly no smooth sailing on two wheels.

And for those in the Northeast, brace yourself for some local detours. **Pine Road in Fox Chase** is still dealing with bridge replacement construction over the Pennypack Rail/Trail. Good news is the trail itself is mostly open, but navigating the surrounding roads might add some unexpected mileage to your warmup. Also, PGW has some delightful construction on **Martins Mill Road**, causing detours until late this week from 9 AM to 3 PM. If you're running that way, keep an eye out for flaggers and sudden lane shifts.
