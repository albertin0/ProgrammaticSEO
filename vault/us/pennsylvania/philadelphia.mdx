### What to Watch Out For

<AlertBox type="info">
The city's "Breathe Philly" initiative with its new network of air quality monitors is a step in the right direction. It's always good to be able to see localized data, especially given Philly's history with getting "failing grades for pollution levels" in recent reports. Keep an eye on the official dashboard if you're particularly sensitive.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
Heads up, early birds and night owls: Several major parks, including sections of **Fairmount Park**, **Wissahickon Valley**, and **Pennypack Park**, are still under **daily curfews from 8:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. until March 31st** for deer control operations. Seriously, don't be that person who gets mistaken for a deer. Stay out of closed park areas during these hours. Your safety, and the deer's, depends on it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
If your route takes you near the **Schuylkill River Trail (SRT)**, specifically under the **Martin Luther King Drive Bridge**, be aware of ongoing construction. While the two-week *overnight* closure (9 p.m. to 5 a.m.) that started March 11th should technically be wrapped up as of yesterday, March 25th, always double-check for lingering equipment or temporary detours during the day. Don't assume everything's perfectly clear the second the official closure window expires. Construction crews aren't exactly known for their punctuality when it comes to packing up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
The **Wissahickon Valley Park** still has some long-standing bike bridge closures due to renovation work that kicked off way back in 2021. If you're planning a ride or run through the Wiss, consult the Friends of the Wissahickon's website for specific bridge status. Don't just blindly follow Strava segments into a construction zone. Your ankles, and my job as a PT, thank you.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a pretty decent day to get outside, as long as you're not trying to set a new personal best for lung capacity while breathing in moderate PM2.5 and pollen. Listen to your body, respect the trail closures, and for the love of all that is holy, don't go running around Fairmount Park in the dark this week.
