## The Air: Not Horrible, But Keep an Eye Out

Alright, so the AQI is sitting at 55, which is "Moderate." That means most of you hard-headed runners will be fine. But let's be real, "moderate" is just a stone's throw from "unhealthy for sensitive groups," and we've seen plenty of those alerts lately. Last year, we had Code Orange warnings from wildfire smoke and ozone that doctors were telling folks to stay indoors for. We also had "unhealthy air" alerts from wildfires back in '23. The weather's cool at 13°C (about 55°F), which usually helps, but the "air quality impacts" mentioned with the recent heat wave are a reminder that the air can still be a bit thick with PM2.5, our dominant pollutant today. If you've got lungs that complain, maybe skip that intense sprint session or stick to the treadmill.

## The Trails: Construction, Closures, and General Annoyance

This is where the real pain lives today. If your plan involved a scenic, uninterrupted long run on the Schuylkill River Trail, you might need a new plan. A significant chunk of the trail, specifically between Plymouth Meeting Creek Culvert and the intersection of Oak Street and Stoddard Avenue, is *still* closed as of today due to ongoing SEPTA construction. They've been at it for a while, and detours are installed, but nobody likes adding unexpected street running to their trail mileage. This kind of construction activity, with intermittent closures and detours, is expected to affect the trail well into 2026. So, if you're out that way, be prepared to navigate some temporary routes or just find a different stomping ground for your run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Schuylkill River Trail Closure Alert!** A section of the SRT near Conshohocken (Plymouth Meeting Creek to Oak Street/Stoddard Ave) is closed *today* for SEPTA construction. Don't plan on cruising through without a detour. Expect construction vehicles and uneven surfaces on alternative routes.
</AlertBox>
