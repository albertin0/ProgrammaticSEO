### Code Red Air Quality Alert!
The air quality today, July 16th, is at Code Red levels due to wildfire smoke. This means high concentrations of fine particulate matter (PM2.5) are in the air, making it unhealthy for everyone. Avoid prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. If you must be outside, keep your activity short and light. Children, older adults, and those with respiratory or heart conditions are especially vulnerable.
</AlertBox>
