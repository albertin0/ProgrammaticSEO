## Local Trail & Road Hazards

Alright, here's where it gets complicated. Philly's always under construction, and today is no different. If you were planning a long run or ride along the Schuylkill, be aware.

The **Market Street Bridge** over the Schuylkill River is slated for a *long-term closure for vehicles starting in late August*, with demolition getting underway. Thankfully, pedestrians and cyclists *should* still have access across the bridge, but don't be surprised if you encounter some confusing detours or are encouraged to use alternative crossings like Chestnut or Walnut Street. This is a multi-year project, folks, so get used to it.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect detours around the Market Street Bridge if you're on the Schuylkill River Trail. While pedestrian access is generally planned, construction zones are unpredictable. Stay alert and follow all signage.
</AlertBox>

Up north, the **Pine Road Bridge over the Fox Chase Lorimer Trail** has been fully closed to all traffic since August 17th, and it's staying that way through Summer 2027. The Fox Chase Lorimer Trail itself remains open for now, but anticipate temporary closures, especially when they start demolition later this winter. Furthermore, a footbridge south of Pine Road on the **Pennypack Trail** is also completely inaccessible until further notice. If Pennypack is your jam, you'll need to use alternative trailheads at Veree, Krewstown, Benton, or Winchester Avenues.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Avoid the Pine Road Bridge area on the Fox Chase Lorimer Trail, and be ready for detours on the Pennypack Trail due to a closed footbridge. Don't be "that guy" trying to sneak through a closed construction zone.
</AlertBox>

On the road front, PennDOT has resurfacing work kicking off today, Monday, August 24, through Friday, August 28. This means lane closures and potential delays on major roads like Girard Avenue, Berkley Street/Roberts Avenue, and Ridge Avenue. If you drive to your workout spot, factor in extra time.
