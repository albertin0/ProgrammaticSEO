### Air Quality & Allergy Woes

Now, for the bad news, or at least the "meh" news. Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 55, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most of you iron-lunged runners, this isn't a showstopper. You'll barely notice. But if you're prone to seasonal allergies or have any kind of respiratory sensitivity, you might feel a bit of a tickle in your throat or a general malaise. Add in a moderate pollen count, and yeah, some post-run sniffles are probably in your future.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up, Allergy Sufferers!**
With moderate pollen and a middling AQI, your lungs and sinuses might not be thrilled with your outdoor ambitions. Consider a shorter, less intense workout, or pop an antihistamine *before* you head out. Don't be that person hacking all the way up the Rocky Steps.
</AlertBox>
