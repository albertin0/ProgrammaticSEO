## Don't Just Stand There, Do Something (Carefully)

Today, March 18, 2026, the temperature is looking pretty decent at 13°C (55°F) and it's "mainly clear" out there. After a frigid start this morning with wind chills in the 30s°F, it's warming up, which is a welcome change. However, if you woke up feeling like someone stuffed your sinuses with cotton, you're not wrong. Pollen is at a moderate level, which for many means a side of sneezing with your strides.

But the real kicker for today is the air. While the official AQI is sitting at 55, which is "moderate," some forecasts are bumping that to "Poor" for sensitive groups. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, those tiny particles that love to mess with your respiratory system. You might feel fine, but your lungs are still doing extra work. If you're someone who usually feels it when the air isn't crisp – kids, older folks, anyone with asthma – maybe skip that PR attempt today.

### What to Watch Out For

**Air Quality Alert:**

<AlertBox type="warning">
Despite the overall "moderate" AQI, sensitive groups should be cautious today due to PM2.5. AccuWeather specifically flags the air as "unhealthy for sensitive groups," suggesting you reduce time outside if you're experiencing symptoms like difficulty breathing or throat irritation. This is less severe than the Code Orange alert we saw earlier in the week, but still enough to warrant a heads-up.
</AlertBox>

**Lingering Wind Issues:**

Remember that mess yesterday? We had severe thunderstorm threats, damaging winds, and even a tornado watch through 7 PM. While today is calmer, those winds, which gusted up to 35-40 mph in some areas, might have left some tree branches or other fun obstacles scattered on your favorite routes. If you're hitting the **Schuylkill River Trail**, especially the less-trafficked sections, keep your eyes peeled. No one needs a face full of fallen branch mid-sprint.
