The Air Quality Index is hanging out at a "Moderate" 55 for PM2.5. For most of us iron-lunged runners, that's generally fine. But if you're rocking asthma or any other respiratory issues, or you're just generally sensitive to the urban haze, you might notice it. Combine that with moderate pollen, and you've got a recipe for a slightly congested, sticky workout. Don't expect any heroic lung performance today. And no, that Old York Road fire from Sunday isn't messing with us anymore; the Health Department says no lingering effects. Good to know our city isn't actively on fire, at least.

While there aren't any major trail-blocking events for us today, you might hit some minor snags. However, some roadwork on Myrtle Street to Beacon Street from last night *could* still be lingering this morning. Be aware, especially if your route takes you through that area early.
