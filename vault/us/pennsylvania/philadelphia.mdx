### Schuylkill River Trail Construction Alert
Heads up, trail runners! While the big sinkhole drama on the Center City section of the Schuylkill River Trail from last fall is long gone and repaired, you're not entirely out of the woods. Ongoing construction on the Keim Street Bridge over the Schuylkill River, particularly impacting the trail along Industrial Highway in Pottstown, means potential detours and intermittent closures that will affect your route through 2026. Additionally, the SRT 422 Connector project in Montgomery County is slated to begin construction around now, so be prepared for potential disruptions there too. Always check local signage and be flexible with your route if you're heading outside the immediate city limits on the SRT.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen & Air Quality Pro-Tips
Even with moderate levels, if you're sensitive, consider timing your outdoor activity for later in the afternoon when pollen counts tend to dip. A quick shower and changing clothes after your workout can help wash off lingering pollen, and keep those inhalers handy if you usually need them. For the AQI, it's not severe enough to warrant extreme measures, but if you're doing a high-intensity workout, just be mindful of how your body feels.
