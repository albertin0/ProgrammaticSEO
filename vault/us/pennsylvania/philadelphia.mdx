Look, I get it. We runners are stubborn. But seriously, pull your head out of the clouds – literally. Yesterday was a Code Purple air quality disaster, which meant *everyone* was at risk from wildfire smoke. Today, while the official AQI might float around "moderate" at 55, the Department of Environmental Protection has issued a **Code Orange Air Quality Alert** for Philadelphia and the surrounding counties. That means if you've got asthma, heart conditions, or just, you know, lungs, you're at risk out there. The PM2.5 particulate matter from distant wildfires is still hanging around, and while rain *might* help later, it's not a guarantee this morning.

And if the dirty air wasn't enough to make you reconsider, how about some actual danger? The National Weather Service has issued a **Flood Watch** for Saturday, running from 10 AM until 2 AM Sunday, covering areas along and northwest of I-95. We're talking scattered to numerous showers and thunderstorms capable of dropping 2-3 inches of rain per hour. That's not a refreshing sprinkle; that's flash flood territory, especially in low-lying urban areas and near creeks. Oh, and did I mention **damaging wind gusts, large hail, and even potential tornadoes**? Your Strava segment isn't worth getting swept away or becoming a human lightning rod.

So, even if you brave the air and the incoming storms, your usual route might be impassable. On top of that, the FIFA World Cup Fan Festival at Lemon Hill in Fairmount Park means Waterworks Drive is closed daily at 10 AM through tomorrow, limiting access and increasing congestion around that area. There's also a Jazz Festival at Saunders Park Greene and a Summer Market at FDR Park today, which might make getting around certain neighborhoods more of a hassle.
