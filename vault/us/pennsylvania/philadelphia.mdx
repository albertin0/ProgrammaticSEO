## Today's Workout Outlook: Dodge the Debris, Navigate the Cones

Alright Philly, listen up. After yesterday's tempest, which even shut down the FIFA Fan Festival at Lemon Hill and delayed a World Cup match, you can bet there's some residual chaos out there. While the skies are looking more agreeable today, the ground might not be. We're talking branches, leaf litter, and maybe even some small flooding remnants on those low-lying sections of your favorite routes.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Post-Storm Scramble:** Expect debris on trails, especially in wooded areas like those around Wissahickon Valley Park. Uneven surfaces and slippery patches are a real possibility. Keep your eyes peeled and maybe dial back the speed on technical terrain.
</AlertBox>

Then there’s the perpetual state of Philadelphia construction. Our beloved **Schuylkill River Trail** always seems to have something cooking. While the big MLK Bridge rehabilitation project wrapped up last year, there's still work underway on the Schuylkill Crossing at Grays Ferry, aiming for a summer 2026 opening to link Grays Ferry Crescent and Bartram's Mile. Expect some ongoing activity there. And if you're thinking of hitting the Delaware River Trail, get ready for detours. Construction on the next segment, connecting Penn Treaty Park to The Battery, kicked off last week and is slated to run for about three months.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead:** Keep an eye out for active construction on sections of the Schuylkill River Trail around Grays Ferry and along the Delaware River Trail between Penn Treaty Park and The Battery. You might encounter detours, narrowed paths, or heavy equipment. Plan your route accordingly.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the temperature is absolutely lovely for moving your body, sitting in the low 20s Celsius (70s Fahrenheit). Humidity will start high but should drop later in the day, which is a blessing after the muggy mess we've had. Just be aware of that "off-and-on rain" that could pop up, making surfaces even more treacherous from yesterday's storm runoff.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Fairmount Park & Parkway Access:** The local lanes of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway are closed from Memorial Day to Labor Day, offering great car-free space for runners and cyclists between Center City and Lemon Hill. However, Waterworks Drive closes daily at 10 AM due to the ongoing FIFA Fan Festival.
</AlertBox>
