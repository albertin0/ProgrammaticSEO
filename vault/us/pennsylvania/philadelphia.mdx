## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   Philly is officially under an **Extreme Heat Warning** and a **Heat Health Emergency** through at least Saturday. We're talking "feels like" temperatures soaring between 110 and 115 degrees today and tomorrow. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Heat is the number one weather-related killer, so take this seriously.
*   On top of the scorching temperatures, we're dealing with a **Code Orange Air Quality Alert** for ground-level ozone across the Philadelphia Metro area. This means air pollution concentrations are unhealthy for sensitive groups – think kids, the elderly, and anyone with asthma, heart, or lung issues. Strenuous outdoor activity is highly discouraged. Don't make your lungs work harder than they have to when they're already fighting bad air.
*   Good luck even finding a clear path! The Wawa Welcome America Festival and other 4th of July shenanigans have locked down significant chunks of Center City. Expect major road closures and detours around the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and the Historic District, affecting everything from your running route to simply getting around. Don't be surprised by unexpected detours if you're trying to hit the streets.
