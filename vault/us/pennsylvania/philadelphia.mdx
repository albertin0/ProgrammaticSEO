## The Rundown

### Pollen Breakdown

| Pollen Level | Dominant Pollen | Other Pollens Present |
| :----------- | :-------------- | :-------------------- |
| Moderate     | Ambrosia        | Grass, Artemisia      |

Ambrosia is doing its thing, and ragweed is particularly high, which is just fantastic news for anyone with seasonal allergies. Prepare for the sneezes and watery eyes, folks. August is peak ragweed season, and it's only going to get worse into September.

### Air Quality Index (AQI)

| AQI | Dominant Pollutant | Category |
| :-- | :----------------- | :------- |
| 55  | PM2.5              | Moderate |

An AQI of 55 isn't terrible, but it's not "crisp mountain air" either. It means the air quality is "acceptable," but if you're sensitive to pollution, you might feel it. Think twice if you've got any underlying respiratory issues or if you're planning on pushing serious mileage.

### Weather Report

| Temperature | Condition    |
| :---------- | :----------- |
| 13.0°C      | Mainly Clear |

Hold onto your hats, or more accurately, *grab an umbrella and a life raft*. While it might be a deceptively clear 13°C (that's about 55°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) this morning, the forecast for *today* is grim. We're under a "NEXT Weather Alert day" with "strong storms" expected, bringing with them "heavy rain," a "flash flooding risk," "frequent lightning," and "damaging winds" particularly in the afternoon and early evening. This isn't just a drizzle; this is the kind of weather that makes you question your life choices if you're caught in it mid-run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Incoming Storms & Flash Flooding Risk!**
Do NOT plan long outdoor workouts, especially in the afternoon or evening. Severe thunderstorms, heavy rain, flash flooding, lightning, and damaging winds are all on the menu for Thursday. Get your outdoor business done early or move it inside. Seriously, you can run on a treadmill for one day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pennypack Trail Footbridge CLOSED Near Pine Road!**
If your route takes you through Pennypack Park, be aware that the footbridge south of Pine Road is completely inaccessible. This isn't a "detour a little bit" situation; it's closed until further notice. Plan your runs accordingly or pick another trail entirely. Don't be "that person" who tries to hop over the barriers. The main Pine Road Bridge over the Fox Chase Lorimer Trail also started long-term reconstruction on August 17th, but the trail *underneath* is mostly open for now, with temporary closures slated for later in the year. Still, it's a construction zone, so keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Allergy Sufferers: Pre-Medicate!**
With ragweed high and general pollen moderate, take your antihistamines *before* you head out, not after your nose starts running like the Schuylkill. Close windows, change clothes after your (likely indoor) workout. Don't suffer needlessly.
</AlertBox>
