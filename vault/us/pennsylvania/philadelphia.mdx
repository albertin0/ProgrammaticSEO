## Pollen & Air Quality Breakdown

Today's air isn't trying to actively kill you, which is a win, I guess. We're sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 55, with PM2.5 being the main culprit. For most of us, this means "go for it." If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you might notice a slight irritation, but it's far from the days where the air actually felt chunky.

Moderate pollen means your allergies might act up, but it's not an all-out assault. Take your meds, pack some tissues, and try not to rub your eyes too much when you're dripping sweat. The upside of overcast skies is usually less direct sun, which can sometimes lessen pollen impact compared to bright, breezy days, but don't count on it being a free pass.

## Weather Wisdom

Honestly, the weather is the best part of today's outdoor workout forecast. 20°C and overcast is basically peak running weather in August for Philadelphia, a city usually known for its oppressive summer humidity. You won't be baking, and the clouds will keep the sun from trying to blind you. Take advantage of it; these days are few and far between before the humidity monster returns.

## Local Lowdown & Trail Talk

If your route involves the Schuylkill River Trail near Center City, be aware of the ongoing construction around the Market Street Bridge. PennDOT's multi-year, $149 million project is a beast. Right now, there's a lane closure on Market Street between Schuylkill Avenue and 30th Street that's been in effect since July 20th and runs through August 20th. While pedestrians and cyclists are *expected* to retain access across the Market Street bridge itself even when the longer vehicular closure starts in late August, navigating that area can still be a pain in the butt with reroutes and heavy equipment. Stay alert and don't assume your usual path is clear. This isn't one of those temporary inconveniences; this bridge project is slated to finish in April 2029. Get used to it.
