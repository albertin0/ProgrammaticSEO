## What's Out There Today, Philadelphia?

If you were planning a glorious long run or ride along the Schuylkill River Trail today, I've got some bad news. The SRT between Race Street and JFK Boulevard remains **closed until further notice** due to emergency repairs for a sinkhole. This isn't some tiny pothole you can hop over; it's a legitimate, ongoing issue that's been impacting folks since last October. So, if your usual route includes that central city stretch, you'll need to reroute. Seriously, don't try to sneak through, it's closed for a reason.

Beyond that prime, but currently problematic, pathway, the general vibe is... fine. The air quality at an AQI of 55 and moderate pollen means your lungs won't be staging a full-scale revolt, though allergy sufferers might still feel a bit of a tickle. It's that sweet spot where you probably won't be gasping for breath, but you won't be doing cartwheels either.
