## Today's Vibe Check: Stay Indoors or Drown in Sweat (or actual water)

Look, I get it. We Philly runners are tough. We power through the Broad Street Run in a downpour and tackle Forbidden Drive coated in ice. But today, September 3, 2026, is a hard pass for outdoor heroics. The city's been under a **Code Red heat alert** for a few days now, and it’s not letting up. While your weather app might show a benign temperature, the *real feel* is what matters, and that's soaring into the **triple digits (100-102°F heat index)** until at least 8 PM. That's not just "uncomfortable," folks, that's **dangerous**.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat & Heat Index
Philadelphia is under a Heat Advisory until 8 PM, with heat index values expected to hit 100-102°F. This kind of heat can lead to heat exhaustion or stroke faster than you can say "Jawn." Stay hydrated *indoors* and avoid any strenuous outdoor activity. The Office of Homeless Services has also issued a Code Red for vulnerable populations.
</AlertBox>

But wait, there's more! If the heat wasn't enough to scare you back to your air-conditioned couch, there's a decent shot of **scattered severe thunderstorms** rolling through. These aren't your gentle summer showers; we're talking damaging winds, potentially large hail, and even a rogue tornado. To add insult to injury, parts of **Southwestern Philadelphia County are under a Flash Flood Warning until 2 PM EDT** because of heavy rain that's already fallen and more on the way. Creeks are high, and urban areas are prone to flooding. So, if you manage to avoid heatstroke, you might just find yourself wading through a newly formed Schuylkill tributary.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Storms & Flooding
Expect scattered severe thunderstorms capable of damaging winds, hail, and isolated tornadoes today. And if you were hoping to just use the **JFK Boulevard Stairs** to get around, guess what? Those are also closed for repairs. There are periodic overnight closures (8 PM - 5 AM) at that junction too.
