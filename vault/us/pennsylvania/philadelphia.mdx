### Weather Woes & Wins

We're kicking off the day crisp, with temperatures around 2.8°C (that's about 37°F for those of you not on the metric system yet) and patchy frost clinging to surfaces until about 10 AM. This is prime time for slipping on unseen ice, so I'm not kidding about watching your footing. It's going to warm up nicely though, with the mercury expected to hit a pleasant 63°F later on, under clear skies. Winds should be light and variable, so no battling a headwind both ways today.

<AlertBox type="info">
Layer up smart for that cold morning start. Think wicking base layers and a light jacket you can shed when the sun finally earns its keep and warms things up. Don't forget gloves and a hat for the first few miles.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergy Annoyances

The Air Quality Index is sitting in the "Moderate" range at 68, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the dominant pollutant. For most folks, that's probably fine for an outdoor workout. However, if you're sensitive to air pollution or have existing respiratory issues, you might notice it a bit. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and some of you might be reaching for the tissues. Listen to your body – if it feels harder to breathe than usual, cut your workout short or take it indoors.

<AlertBox type="warning">
For those with asthma or severe allergies, moderate AQI and pollen mean it's worth considering your workout intensity. Today isn't the day to try for a PR if your lungs are already protesting.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble & Construction Chaos

Now, for the big one that's going to mess with your usual routes. PennDOT's ongoing Market Street Bridge rehabilitation project is causing some significant weekend disruptions. Specifically, the sections of I-76 and Schuylkill Avenue around Center City are impacted, but more importantly for us runners and cyclists, **the Schuylkill River Trail itself is inaccessible to pedestrians and cyclists between 23rd and 30th streets.** This closure started Friday night and won't lift until Monday morning. That means if your Saturday long run or bike ride usually takes you through that prime stretch of the SRT, you need a new plan. Don't just show up and expect a magical bypass; you'll be turning around.

General paving work is also scheduled around the city this week, so always keep an eye out for "No Parking" signs and unexpected detours, especially if you're venturing off the main trails.
