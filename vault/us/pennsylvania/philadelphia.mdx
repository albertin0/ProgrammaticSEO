## The Rundown: Don't Be a Fairmount Park Fool

Today's shaping up to be a pretty decent day to get outside and actually *enjoy* your workout, which, let's be honest, is a rare enough occurrence in this city. The temps are in that sweet spot where you're not sweating through your shirt before mile one, and the sun's out, making those classic Philly routes like the Schuylkill River Trail or Kelly Drive look extra inviting. Just remember, "pleasant" doesn't mean "perfect."

### Air Quality & Allergies: Don't Breath Like You're Running on Cigarettes

While the AQI of 55 isn't going to set off any immediate alarms for most of us, it's still in the "moderate" category, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the main culprit. For those of you with sensitive lungs or existing respiratory issues, like yours truly after one too many late-night cheesesteaks, you might notice it. The Department of Public Health considers AQI between 51 and 100 "acceptable," but warns there "may be a risk for some people, particularly those who are unusually sensitive to air pollution." If you're planning a longer run or a particularly intense session, consider sticking to areas with more green space, or maybe just dial back the effort a notch.

And about that pollen? Oh yeah, it's still hanging around. While tree pollen is low, both ragweed and grass pollen are at moderate levels today, with mold also lingering at low levels. So if you're one of the unfortunate souls whose nose decides to run a marathon of its own this time of year, pop that antihistamine *before* you head out, especially if you're hitting spots like Fairmount Park where the greenery is abundant.

## Looking Ahead: Bundle Up, Buttercup!

### **Weekend Chill is Coming**
While today is mild, a freeze watch is in effect for some of the northern and western suburbs tonight into Saturday morning, with temperatures potentially dropping to near or below freezing. If you're an early bird planning a Saturday morning long run, layer up and consider some wind protection. Your future self will thank you.
