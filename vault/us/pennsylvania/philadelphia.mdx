## What to Watch Out For Today

### **The Air is… Acceptable, But Not Great**

Look, an AQI of 55 isn't exactly "crisp mountain air," is it? While the City of Philadelphia officially labels this as "acceptable," they also admit there might be a risk for "unusually sensitive" folks. My take? If you're prone to seasonal allergies or have any respiratory issues, today's PM2.5 dominant air quality might make that morning shakeout feel a bit heavier than usual. AccuWeather agrees, listing PM2.5 as "Fair." This isn't a stay-inside-and-hermetically-seal-your-windows kind of day, but don't expect to feel like you're breathing pure oxygen, either.

### **Construction & Closures: Waterworks Woes**

If your usual route takes you anywhere near the Philadelphia Museum of Art, specifically past the Waterworks, prepare for a detour. Waterworks Drive is closed from June 11 through July 19. This is due to the FIFA Fan Festival, which is part of the ongoing Welcome America festivities. So, if you're planning to loop around Kelly Drive or hit the Schuylkill River Trail up that way, you'll need to adjust. Don't be that person arguing with the cones; they won't win. Just reroute.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Waterworks Drive Closure:** Heads up, runners and cyclists! Waterworks Drive, right by the Art Museum, is closed until mid-July for the FIFA Fan Festival. Plan your Schuylkill River Trail routes accordingly to avoid getting stuck or having to backtrack.
</AlertBox>

### **The Calm Before the Heat Storm**

Today, we're enjoying a relatively pleasant 20.8°C (around 69°F) and clear skies. It's a prime running temp. But don't let this fool you into thinking it's smooth sailing all week. The entire Central and Eastern US, including Philadelphia, is bracing for a "long and dangerous heat wave" this week, with heat indexes projected to hit 100-110°F (40-43°C). We're talking "impactful to everyone" kind of heat, not just the usual summer sweat-fest. So, while you're out there today, enjoy it, but start hydrating now and rethink your outdoor plans for later in the week.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Upcoming Heat Wave Alert:** Enjoy today's relatively mild conditions. However, a significant heat wave is forecasted for Philadelphia this week, with temperatures and humidity making it feel dangerously hot. Plan ahead for intense hydration and consider moving strenuous outdoor activities to early mornings or evenings, or indoors.
</AlertBox>

There's some ongoing road construction around the Allegheny/Delaware Avenue intersection in Northeast Philly, which is a year-long project, but that's more of a car-traffic headache than a direct trail closure for most runners. No major trail-blocking events were reported for today on the Schuylkill River Trail itself, aside from the Waterworks area. So, for now, dodge the construction, watch your breathing, and enjoy this brief moment of relative calm before Mother Nature decides to crank up the oven.
