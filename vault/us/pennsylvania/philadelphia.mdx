## The Rundown: Breathe Easy? Mostly.

So, the mercury's sitting at a comfortable 12.8°C (that's 55°F for you Celsius-challenged folks), and the sky's mainly clear. Perfect running weather, right? Almost. The Air Quality Index is currently at 55, landing us squarely in the "Moderate" zone with PM2.5 as the main culprit. For most of us, that's just fine, but if you're one of those sensitive types, don't push that tempo run too hard. Your lungs will thank you later. Or at least, they won't stage a full-blown rebellion.

Pollen's also hovering at a moderate level, which means you might be sneezing your way through your miles. Keep those antihistamines handy unless you enjoy looking like you just ran through a pepper spray factory.

## Watch Your Step (and Your Route)

Now for the fun stuff – Philly's perpetual obstacle course.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Good News for Parkway Runners:** The local lanes of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway are actually *closed to vehicle traffic* from Memorial Day straight through Labor Day for the FIFA Fan Festival. This means more room for us runners and cyclists between Center City and Lemon Hill. Enjoy it while it lasts; it's practically a car-free paradise.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Lemon Hill Area Closures:** While the Parkway is chill, several streets directly surrounding Lemon Hill are shut down for the FIFA Fan Festival, running until late July. We're talking Sedgley Drive, Poplar Street, and Lemon Hill Drive. If your usual loop takes you through there, you'll need to find an alternate path. Don't be that person arguing with the barricades.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Overnight Construction Headaches:** PennDOT is busy doing its thing with I-95. They've got overhead bridge construction closing parts of Tacony Street in Northeast Philly from 8 PM to 5 AM through June, and bridge repair closing Ritner Street in South Philly overnight until June 12. If you're a night owl runner or cyclist, plan for detours and stay hyper-aware of changed traffic patterns and heavy machinery.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Long-term Road Work:** The intersection of Allegheny Avenue and Delaware Avenue in Northeast Philadelphia is restricted to a single lane for utility construction all the way through April 2027. While not a trail closure, this is going to be a choke point for traffic in the area, so if you're driving to a workout spot nearby, factor in extra time and frustration.
</AlertBox>

Look, it's Philadelphia. There's always *something* going on. But with mild temperatures and the Parkway giving us a break, today's a solid day to earn those miles. Just keep your head on a swivel, avoid inhaling too much construction dust, and respect those closures. Nobody wants to end up on the evening news for running through a construction zone.
