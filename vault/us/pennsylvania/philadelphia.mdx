Today's the kind of day where the universe is giving you some decent running weather – that 13.0°C (55.4°F) and mainly clear skies? Chef's kiss for a long run. But don't get too comfortable, because the city itself is still doing its thing, and "doing its thing" often means throwing up roadblocks.

First, the good news for your legs: Martin Luther King Jr. Drive is officially closed to vehicular traffic this weekend between the East Falls Bridge and Eakins Oval. That means you've got miles of paved paradise, free from distracted drivers and their honking. Take advantage. Seriously, don't let those car-free miles go to waste.

Now for the reality check. Your lungs might feel decent with an AQI of 55, but "moderate pollen" is still a thing. Don't be surprised if you finish your run feeling like you inhaled a small hedge. Hydrate, and maybe pop an antihistamine *before* you head out, not after you're already wheezing on the Schuylkill River Trail.

### What to Watch Out For

**The Event Gauntlet:**
If you were planning on a casual stroll down South 9th Street, forget it. The Italian Market Festival is in full swing today and tomorrow. Unless you like weaving through crowds and the smell of sausage and peppers clinging to your gear, find another route. The same goes for the Girard Avenue Street Fest and the "Taste of Philly" Food Truck Fest on Lancaster Avenue – these are major event zones today. Avoid them unless your workout involves competitive eating. Also, watch out for lingering setup or teardown around Kelly Drive and the Schuylkill River Banks if you're near the Water Works area; there was a regatta and "Art In The Open" yesterday that might still be impacting access.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Heavy downpours were in the forecast for May 15-16, even if it's clear now, so keep an eye out for slick spots, standing water, or even downed branches, especially on less-maintained trails.
</AlertBox>

**Construction Zones (Because Philly is Always Under Construction):**
While PennDOT has been making strides on I-76 and I-95, you can still expect ongoing road construction in various parts of the city. Specifically, the I-95 reconstruction near the Bridge Street and Betsy Ross Bridge area is ongoing. If your route takes you near major arteries, be aware of potential detours or increased traffic leading to your starting point. Also, Logan Circle was undergoing major upgrades with innermost lanes and sidewalks closed until "spring 2026." It's mid-May, so it *should* be done, but it's Philly; check for barriers before you commit to that particular loop.
