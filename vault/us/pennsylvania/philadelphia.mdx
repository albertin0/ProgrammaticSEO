## Today's Hazards: Breathe at Your Own Risk

The crisp, 13°C (55°F) "mainly clear" weather might look inviting, but don't be fooled by appearances. The air is thick with particulate matter, the kind that digs deep into your lungs and aggravates everything from asthma to a perfectly healthy respiratory system. This isn't just some vague "bad air day"—we're talking Code Purple, folks. Yesterday, some areas, like Fairmount Park, hit an AQI of 211, squarely in the "Very Unhealthy" zone.
