## Local Intel: What Not To Trip On

### Schuylkill River Trail: The Perennial Construction Zone

Look, I love the SRT as much as the next runner trying to avoid traffic, but let's be real, it's always got *something* going on. Today's no different.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Ahead:** A 0.9-mile section of the Schuylkill River Trail, specifically the connection between Industrial Highway and the Route 422 bridge, is an active construction zone and is *closed to the public* until October 2026. If you usually run that stretch, plan for a detour. You'll also encounter intermittent closures and detours along other parts of the SRT near Industrial Highway throughout the year. Keep your head up and follow signage – or risk becoming part of the new bridge infrastructure.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**ATTENTION: Wissahickon Wanderers:** The Yellow Trail near Kitchens Lane has fencing up to protect ground-nesting wildlife and allow forest regeneration. Stay on the designated path, people. This isn't your personal wilderness playground where rules don't apply. And remember, it's "mud season" out there, so expect some soft spots on those trails. Nobody wants to see your face-plant reel on Instagram.
</AlertBox>
