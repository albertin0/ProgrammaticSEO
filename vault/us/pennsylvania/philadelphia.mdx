## Trail Talk & City Streets

The good news is the severe flash flooding we saw yesterday across some Philly suburbs and near the Ben Franklin Bridge on Route 676 in Camden has subsided, and there are no active flood warnings for today. That doesn't mean every single puddle has magically evaporated or every piece of debris has been cleared, especially on less-trafficked paths. Keep an eye out for uneven surfaces if you're venturing off the beaten asphalt.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Detour Dharma:** If your usual route takes you anywhere near the **Benjamin Franklin Parkway** or along **Waterworks Drive** (which, let's be real, affects the Schuylkill River Trail flow), you're going to hit some snags. The local lanes of the Parkway are closed all summer, and Waterworks Drive shutters its doors daily at 10 a.m. until July 19th. Be ready to improvise, or choose another spot. This isn't the day for a PR if you're fighting crowds and construction cones.

And for those of you trying to get *to* your workout via car around the South Philly Sports Complex, expect some general traffic headaches. There's a multi-million dollar traffic improvement plan underway, including new I-76 ramps and AI-powered traffic signals. While it's aimed at reducing congestion for drivers, it won't be a smooth ride getting there.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration & Humidity:** Even with the overcast skies, that 93% humidity is going to make you feel warmer than the thermometer says. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. It's not July in Arizona, but it's still July in Philly, and that means sticky.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, watch your step, maybe grab a kleenex, and get out there before Thursday's thunderstorms roll in to really mess things up.
