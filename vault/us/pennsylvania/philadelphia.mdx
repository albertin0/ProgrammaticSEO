## The Rundown

Alright, listen up, because while it's "Mainly Clear" out there, that doesn't mean everything's peachy. The biggest thing to watch is the air. Sure, the general AQI is sitting at 55, which is technically "Moderate." But let's be real, "moderate" for some folks is "gasping for air" for others. With PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, and AccuWeather forecasting "Poor" air quality for sensitive groups like those with asthma or other respiratory issues, you need to pay attention. If you're in that category, seriously consider moving your workout indoors or scaling way back. The City's own health department warns that between 51-100 AQI, there *may be a risk for some people, particularly those who are unusually sensitive to air pollution*. We've seen this city's air get nasty before, so don't assume a low-ish number means it's a free-for-all.

Weather-wise, we're looking at a decent day if you get out early. The mercury is set to rise from the low 20s Celsius (high 60s Fahrenheit) to nearly 30°C (mid-80s Fahrenheit) later in the day, with clear skies transitioning to mostly cloudy tonight. That afternoon heat, combined with the questionable air, is a recipe for a miserable workout if you're not careful.

Now, for our beloved trails, specifically the **Kelly Drive** loop. While there are no *active* flood warnings in effect today, we had some pretty significant rainfall and flooding earlier this week on Monday, July 6th, which led to closures on both Kelly Drive and Martin Luther King Drive. While these major roads might be clear now, always be on the lookout for lingering damp spots, debris, or slick patches, especially in low-lying areas or near the river. Nobody wants to twist an ankle on a hidden branch or slick mud.

Construction-wise, I-95 continues to be a mess in various spots, but nothing directly impacting the main running trails like the Schuylkill River Trail or Kelly Drive has been reported for today. Just be mindful of traffic if you're driving to your starting point.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydration Reminder:** With temperatures heading into the upper 20s Celsius (mid-80s Fahrenheit) this afternoon, carry plenty of water. Even with clear skies, it's easy to get dehydrated when you're pushing it.
</AlertBox>

So, if you absolutely *must* get outside, go early, keep an eye on how your lungs feel, and stick to well-trafficked, familiar routes where you know the conditions. And for the love of all that is holy, if you start feeling cruddy, stop. It's not worth it.
