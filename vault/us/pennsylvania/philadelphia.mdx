### Potholes, Detours, and General Urban Annoyances

Beyond the weather, Philly's always got something cooking on the construction front. If you're usually navigating the Center City grid, be aware that the **Logan Circle** area is still undergoing upgrades. The innermost travel lane and sidewalk are closed for fountain restoration and sidewalk reconstruction, impacting pedestrian access. Don't be surprised by sudden detours or uneven surfaces around there. And let's not forget the never-ending I-95 reconstruction. While not directly on your favorite running path, it means more traffic, more noise, and general chaos in surrounding areas if you're trying to get to or from your workout spot.

Bottom line: If you're going to get your miles in today, do it early and stay somewhere covered or easily accessible to shelter. The forecast after noon is just asking for trouble.
