#### Heat Hazard Ahead!
Seriously, 90°F is no joke. Running or working out vigorously in this kind of heat puts immense strain on your body. You risk dehydration, heat exhaustion, and even heat stroke. If you *must* exercise outside, keep it short, stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and go during the coolest parts of the day (which today means "early dawn"). This isn't the day to try and outrun your personal best on Kelly Drive. Just slow down.
</AlertBox>
