### Air Quality & Allergy Rundown

Our air quality is hovering in the "moderate" zone at an AQI of 55, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. While some official city reports are calling it "Good" at an AQI of 47, let's be real, anything over 50 means sensitive folks might feel it. If you're asthmatic or have any kind of respiratory sensitivity, this isn't the day to be sucking in lungfuls of fine particulate matter.

And then there's the pollen. The overall forecast might be "moderate," but that's a deceptive blanket statement. AccuWeather is reporting grass pollen as high, which, combined with the moderate ragweed, means your nose and eyes are in for a good time. August is prime time for those weed pollens to start making their presence known, especially along green corridors like our beloved Schuylkill River Trail.
