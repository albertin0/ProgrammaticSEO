## Air Quality Check

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 55, which is in the "Moderate" range, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This isn't cause for panic, but it's not pristine mountain air either.

<AlertBox type="warning">
For those with respiratory sensitivities – think asthma, COPD, or just a bad set of lungs from years of breathing city air – you might notice a slight irritation. Consider an easier pace or shorter distance today. If you're generally healthy, you're likely fine, but don't go trying to set any PRs if it feels off. Back in July, we had some real smoke issues, but today's a mild annoyance by comparison.
</AlertBox>

## Local Road Hazards & Construction

Good news: no major flooding warnings for the Schuylkill River today. Levels are well below action stage, so the Schuylkill River Trail boardwalk should be clear for your morning miles. Also, that sinkhole that closed parts of the Center City SRT back in late 2025? It's been fixed and reopened, so run freely there.

Now for the bad news, or at least the annoying news: construction. PennDOT is still going hard on that I-95 reconstruction, particularly the AF3 project in Northeast Philadelphia between Tioga Street and Wheatsheaf Lane. If you're running or cycling anywhere near that section, especially around the Bridge Street Interchange, be aware of closures and detours. There's an "extensive pedestrian detour" in place on Tacony Street through 2027 related to that work, so don't be surprised if your usual loop is blocked. It's primarily overnight work (9 PM - 5 AM) for bridge demolition on Castor Avenue and Venango Street, but expect lane closures on Wheatsheaf Lane even during the day, 6 AM to 4 PM.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Avoid the I-95 construction zones in Northeast Philadelphia if you can. While much of the heavy lifting is overnight, daytime lane closures on Wheatsheaf Lane and ongoing pedestrian detours on Tacony Street mean it's not a safe or enjoyable place to be exercising. Don't risk it for a few miles. Find an alternate route.
</AlertBox>

Beyond I-95, PennDOT also has resurfacing operations planned across Philadelphia County this week (Oct 12-16). This means lane closures for line striping on streets like Ridge Avenue, Berkley Street/Roberts Avenue, and Girard Avenue, usually between 9 AM and 3 PM, and again from 8 PM to 6 AM. These are primarily traffic disruptions, but if you're running on sidewalks adjacent to these roads or trying to cross them, be extra vigilant. Drivers might be distracted by lane shifts, and pedestrians often get the short end of the stick.

In short: it's a decent day for a workout, especially if you stick to main trails like the SRT. Just be smart about where you're going and keep an eye out for construction zones if you venture into less-trodden city streets.
