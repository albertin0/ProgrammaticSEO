Look, the AQI at 55 isn't *terrible*, but it's not "Good" either. Add "Moderate" pollen on top, and anyone with allergies or respiratory issues might feel it more than usual. This combo means taking it easy isn't a bad idea, especially if you're prone to seasonal sniffles or asthma flare-ups. Don't go trying to set a new PR on the Schuylkill River Trail if you're already feeling congested.
</AlertBox>
