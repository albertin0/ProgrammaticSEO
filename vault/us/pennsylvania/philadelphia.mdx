It's nowhere near Code Orange, Code Red, or Code Purple, which we've seen plague the city recently due to wildfire smoke. So, your lungs can mostly chill today, but if you're asthmatic or sensitive, keep it steady. The air's cleaner, but it's not *clean*.

Temperature at 21.5°C (70.7°F) with mainly clear skies? That's what we call "actually pleasant" in July, which, let's be honest, is a rare blessing. Good for the joints, good for not spontaneously combusting mid-run.

However, if your usual Saturday long run takes you anywhere near Lemon Hill or the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, prepare for some creative rerouting. Today, July 19th, marks the *final day* of the FIFA Fan Festival at Lemon Hill in East Fairmount Park.
