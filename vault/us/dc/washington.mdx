## Your Body is Not a Heat-Resistant Machine

Let's be blunt: 37.3°C (that's 99.1°F for those still in Fahrenheit-land) in early March is insane. Your body isn't acclimated to this. You might think you're tough, but heat stroke doesn't care about your Strava segments. The District usually issues a "Heat Alert" when the heat index hits 95 degrees, and you bet your boots we're way past that.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors!
With temperatures pushing 37.3°C (99.1°F), outdoor workouts are extremely dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, immediate threats. Seriously, rethink that run around the National Mall. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate constantly. Your best bet is to hit the gym or find some AC.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures & Construction Headaches

The Greenbelt Park Perimeter Trail remains closed from mile marker 0.8 to 1.9 due to storm damage and ongoing construction. It's expected to stay that way until at least March 31st, 2026. Don't try to sneak through; it's closed for your safety and to allow the work to proceed. DDOT is also doing its usual citywide dance with pavement markings, sidewalks, and alley restorations. These are often localized disruptions, but they can pop up unexpectedly on your usual routes, so keep an eye out for signs and detours.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route (or Your Indoor Alternative)
Always check for local closures before heading out. The Greenbelt Park Perimeter Trail is a no-go for now. If you're running any city streets, expect potential detours and uneven surfaces due to ongoing DDOT projects. Seriously though, today, the best route is probably to your fridge for a cold drink, or maybe an indoor track.
