Good luck trying to navigate around the Whitehurst Freeway, Potomac River Freeway, and all the main arteries around the Mall. Even if you dodge the puddles, you'll be dodging barricades and bewildered tourists.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### AQI 150: Unhealthy Air Quality

Today's air quality is rated "Unhealthy" due to high PM2.5 levels. This means *everyone* should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. If you have respiratory issues, stay inside. Period. Don't be a hero. Your lungs will absolutely pay for it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Flash Flood Risk

With the heavy rain from Tropical Rainstorm Isaias, there's a significant risk of flash flooding. Streets and underpasses can become submerged quickly. Trails along the Potomac and Anacostia, like sections of the C&O Canal Towpath, are especially vulnerable to standing water and muddy conditions. Even familiar routes can become dangerous.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Detours and Obstacles

Beyond the weather, navigating DC today will be a headache. The Army Ten-Miler has closed numerous streets and bridges, particularly impacting routes through downtown and around the National Mall. Furthermore, a section of the Metropolitan Branch Trail between Brookland and Fort Totten is closed for resurfacing through early November. Check DDOT for updated construction and closures if you *must* go out, but honestly, just don't.
</AlertBox>

If you insist on stepping foot outside, wear waterproof everything, watch your footing like a hawk, and for the love of all that is holy, choose a short route far away from any water or major street closures. Your joints (and lungs) will thank you for rescheduling.
