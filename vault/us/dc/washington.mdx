## Local Hazards & Closures

### Construction Chaos on Nebraska Avenue NW
If your route takes you anywhere near Nebraska Avenue NW, be prepared for some serious headaches. Starting today, July 7th, and running through August 7th, two westbound lanes between Nevada Avenue NW and Connecticut Avenue NW are closed 24/7 for the DC PLUG Feeder 467 project. They've reconfigured the eastbound side to carry two-way traffic, meaning everything is tighter and more congested. This isn't just a driver's problem; pedestrians and cyclists need to be extra vigilant and patient in that area.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect significant pedestrian and traffic delays on Nebraska Avenue NW from Nevada Avenue NW to Connecticut Avenue NW due to 24-hour, two-lane closures through early August. Stay alert and follow all signage.
</AlertBox>

### Metropolitan Branch Trail Bridge Closure (Overnight)
For you early birds or night owls hitting the Metropolitan Branch Trail, know that the pedestrian bridge over the CSX Railroad at Rhode Island Metro Station is closed overnight (11 PM to 5 AM) from July 6th through July 31st for maintenance. No access to the Metro Station from the bridge during those hours, so plan an alternate route if that's part of your commute or late-night run.

### The Ellipse is Still a No-Go
Still can't run around the White House the way you might want to. The Ellipse, including its interior sidewalks, remains closed through July 31st due to US Secret Service security restrictions. If your usual run involved a loop around the President's Park, you'll need to find a different scenic view for a few more weeks.

### Capital Crescent Trail: Still Detoured East of Bethesda
For those up by Bethesda, the Capital Crescent Trail continues to be a mess east of Bethesda due to the Purple Line construction. It's expected to remain closed with a detour through Fall 2026. If you were hoping to finally hit that stretch, you've got a while to wait. The detour is long and "unpleasant in places," so be warned.
