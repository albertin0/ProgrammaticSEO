## Hazards & Head-Ups

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Institute Park Gridlock Alert!**
Forget your usual quiet loops around Institute Park today. The Worcester Firefighters 6K and associated block party are taking over from 11 AM to 5 PM. Seriously, unless you're registered for the race or just want to people-watch, find another spot. Expect crowds, music, and general merriment that will completely disrupt any semblance of a serious workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues**
Worcester's roads are perpetually undergoing "improvements," which means detours and delays are your constant companions. Specifically, watch out for ongoing culvert work on **Salisbury Street**, which will restrict traffic to one lane at a time. The **Ararat Street Bridge** also has eastbound traffic detoured, though pedestrian access is maintained. Plan extra time, be visible, and for Pete's sake, put your phone away. No text is worth getting flattened by a backhoe.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Allergies and Ozone: A Mild Buzzkill**
While the AQI is good for most, that moderate pollen count (oak, ash, grass) means allergy sufferers should pre-dose with antihistamines or consider an indoor session if you're particularly sensitive. The dominant pollutant being Ozone means long-term exposure *could* cause minor symptoms for sensitive groups, even if the overall AQI is low. Listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tick Check: It's Always Tick Season**
Even on a "mainly clear" day with pleasant temperatures, ticks are out there, especially if you're hitting the more wooded trails like those at **Broad Meadow Brook Conservation Center**. Deer tick nymphs are particularly active in the spring. Wear light-colored clothing, tuck your pants into your socks (yes, you'll look silly, but who cares?), use repellent, and do a thorough tick check after every outdoor adventure. Lyme disease is no joke.
</AlertBox>

Don't let these warnings scare you off, just be smart about it. Worcester's got plenty of places to get a good sweat in, even if some of them are a temporary mess.
