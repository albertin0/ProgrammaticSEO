### Today's Rundown

If you woke up thinking of hitting the pavement, check that enthusiasm at the door, because Mother Nature has other plans. Today, Saturday, September 26th, Newark is under a **Wind Advisory** until Sunday morning, with gusts hitting up to 43 mph. Combine that with **heavy rain** and a **Coastal Flood Warning** active through Sunday evening, and you've got yourself a recipe for a soggy, dangerous workout. The weather forecast explicitly calls for rain all day, with a 90 percent chance of precipitation and a high of only 63°F (17°C). So much for that 31.5°C "mainly clear" dream.

The Newark Public Safety Director even put out a traffic advisory yesterday, warning about expected flooding from all this rain and telling drivers to avoid flooded streets. That means your usual routes, especially near low-lying areas or the Passaic River, could be underwater. Don't be that person trying to slog through standing water; you don't know what's underneath, and that's how you twist an ankle or worse.

On the "what the hell is going on where I usually run" front, Branch Brook Park is hosting the CareSparc Community Connections Sustainable Health & Wellness Village Fest today from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. If you were planning on a peaceful long run past the cherry blossoms, expect crowds and possibly restricted areas around the Prudential Concert Grove. It's great for community health, not so great for your PR.
