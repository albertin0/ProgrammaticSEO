## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   **It's practically summer, somehow.** We're talking 37.1°C (a cool 99°F for those clinging to Fahrenheit) out there. In *early March*. This isn't just "warm," it's dangerous, brain-melting, heat-stroke-waiting-to-happen hot.
*   **Wind and dust are having a party, and you're not invited.** The National Weather Service issued a Wind Advisory until 10 PM tonight, with gusts up to 50 mph expected. This isn't just a bad hair day; it means blowing dust, grit in your eyes, and zero fun for your lungs. Clark County even slapped on a dust advisory for the day.
*   **Pollen's still out there, just waiting for the wind to carry it directly into your sinuses.** While the overall AQI is "good" at 39 for general pollutants, that dust and moderate pollen level are going to make it feel a lot worse.
*   **Trail closures are still a thing.** Good luck trying to get a clear run on parts of the Summerlin Parkway Trail, where Crestdale Lane is shut down for three months for tunnel construction. And if you planned on exploring new areas at Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument, some sections south of Grand Teton Drive and east of the Aliante Loop Trail are closed for ongoing construction on the new Tufa Trail.

### The Harsh Reality: Stay Indoors

Look, I love hitting the trails around here as much as the next runner, but today is a hard pass. That 37.1°C temperature is no joke. Your body isn't acclimated to this kind of heat in March, and pushing through it is a fast track to dehydration, heat exhaustion, or worse. We've seen record-breaking early March heat in the valley already, and today is just a continuation of that trend.

The wind isn't helping either. Gusts up to 50 mph turn a simple run into a battle against the elements, and all that kicked-up dust is terrible for anyone with even a hint of respiratory sensitivity. Even if the official AQI is low, blowing dust contains particulate matter you don't want in your lungs.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
**Avoid outdoor activity between 8 AM and 8 PM.** The reported temperature of 37.1°C (nearly 99°F) is life-threatening. Dehydration can happen rapidly. If you *must* be outside, keep it extremely short, stay in the shade, and consume more water than you think you need. Consider rescheduling your workout for another day or finding an indoor alternative.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### High Winds & Dust Advisory!
Expect strong northwesterly winds up to 50 mph. This will kick up significant dust, impairing visibility and air quality, especially near dry lakebeds. Protect your eyes and lungs. Runners should be particularly wary of sudden gusts that can throw you off balance.
</AlertBox>
