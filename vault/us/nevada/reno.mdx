## Current Hazards & What to Do

### Heat: It's a Dry Heat... That Still Kills You

Look, I love our Reno summers as much as the next masochist, but 36.2°C is no joke. We've seen record-breaking temperatures already this month, and while it's not quite at "fry an egg on the pavement" levels of earlier this August, this is still the kind of heat that sneaks up on you. You might *think* you're fine chugging water on the Caughlin Ranch trails, but by the time you're dizzy, it's already too late.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Risk:** With temperatures at 36.2°C (97.16°F) and a forecast high near that mark, the risk of heat-related illness is HIGH. Don't be a hero. Your PR isn't worth passing out in front of the geese at Idlewild Park. Stick to early mornings, late evenings, or better yet, the air-conditioned gym. Hydrate relentlessly if you absolutely *must* be outside.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality: Fire Season's Greatest Hits

The Bug and Stallion fires, unfortunately, are still burning in Washoe County and nearby California, pushing that lovely PM2.5 our way. This means our AQI is in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" category. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just enjoy not coughing up a lung, seriously consider moving your workout indoors. The smoke is affecting areas like Cold Springs and Spanish Springs, and those fine particulate matter particles don't care about city limits.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Poor Air Quality:** An AQI of 101 due to PM2.5 from regional wildfires means sensitive individuals should limit outdoor exertion. Everyone else should shorten and lessen the intensity of outdoor activities. Skip that long run on the Truckee River path today; your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Construction: Never-Ending Obstacle Course

Reno always has something under construction, and this week is no different. If you're planning on cycling or running near I-80, expect lane reductions and shifts between McCarran Boulevard and Keystone Avenue through mid-2026. Also, watch out for ongoing work on Sparks Boulevard, where you'll find 24/7 single lane closures in sections through August. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Construction Zones:** Ongoing roadwork, particularly on I-80 between McCarran and Keystone and long-term lane closures on Sparks Boulevard, means reduced speeds, detours, and potential hazards for runners and cyclists. Be aware, be visible, and for Pete's sake, put down the phone.
</AlertBox>
