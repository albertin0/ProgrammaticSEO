## Today's Vitals: Vegas-Style

It's March 24th, and Mother Nature apparently thinks it's summer already. Seriously, 34.6°C (that's about 94.3°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) under a clear sky? That's not "spring training" weather; that's "stay hydrated or face the consequences" weather. We just broke March temperature records, and while the "Extreme Heat Warning" might have officially expired a couple days ago, the heat didn't get the memo.

Then there's the air. An AQI of 155 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant means it's unhealthy for *everyone*, not just your asthmatic aunt. You'll probably feel it in your chest if you push too hard. While some local reports on March 23rd mentioned PM2.5 being in the "good to moderate" range, that 155 reading is a stark reminder that air quality here can turn on a dime, especially with that constant threat of wildfire smoke drifting in from California.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Alert!** Temperatures are dangerously high for strenuous outdoor activity. Heat-related illnesses spike in these conditions, especially when folks aren't acclimated to such early-season extremes. Even if you're a seasoned desert runner, your body isn't used to this in March. Limit activity, seek shade, and hydrate aggressively. Call 911 for symptoms of heat stroke.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Unhealthy Air Quality!** An AQI of 155 means the air is unhealthy for sensitive groups, and probably not great for anyone else either. Reduce time outdoors, especially high-intensity exercise. If you start coughing or have trouble breathing, get inside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Trail Closures in Effect for Spring Mountains/Mt. Charleston:** If your go-to is a mountain escape, check ahead. Several popular trails, including parts of the Lower Bristlecone Trail and all of the Upper Bristlecone, Mary Jane Falls, and Trail Canyon, are still closed indefinitely due to damage from Tropical Storm Hillary. Don't get stuck or put yourself in an unsafe situation. Stick to marked, open trails, or better yet, hit the gym.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, save your PR attempts for a day when the air isn't trying to actively shorten your lifespan and the sun isn't attempting to bake you like a casserole. Go lift some weights, hit the treadmill in AC, or catch up on that TV show everyone's talking about. Your body will thank you.
