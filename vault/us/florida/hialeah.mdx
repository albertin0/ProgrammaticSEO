## The Lowdown on Today's Outdoor Grind

Look, the temperature at 23.2°C (about 74°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) with "Mainly Clear" skies sounds like prime running weather on paper. It's not the sweltering sauna we usually endure, which is a blessing. Your lungs, for the most part, will thank you. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a respectable 52, which is in the "Moderate" zone, with Ozone being the main culprit, or "Other" if you trust the vague categorization. So, you're probably not choking on exhaust fumes today, which is a win, I guess.

However, don't get too comfortable. That "Moderate" pollen count means if you're one of the many poor souls with seasonal allergies, you might still end up snotting your way through your workout. Grab the antihistamines, you know the drill.

### Watch Your Step, Literally

Here's where things get interesting, and by interesting, I mean potentially annoying. The National Weather Service issued a Coastal Flood Advisory for Miami-Dade County, effective until 11:00 p.m. *tonight*. While Hialeah isn't exactly beachfront property, we all know how quickly our drainage system gets overwhelmed. This isn't just for the fancy folks on the coast; "water accumulation could occur on streets, parks, and parking areas in coastal zones, potentially leading to isolated road closures." Even a few inches of standing water on a corner near, say, Milander Park, can turn your nice, dry run into a sloshy mess or, worse, a twisted ankle if you can't see what's underneath. So, if your route takes you through any low-lying spots, be prepared to get wet or find another path.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Coastal Flood Advisory in Effect:** Expect potential localized street flooding in low-lying areas across Miami-Dade, including Hialeah. Watch for standing water that could obscure potholes or uneven pavement.
