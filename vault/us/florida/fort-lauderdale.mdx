### But let's not get *too* ahead of ourselves. It's still Fort Lauderdale.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Rip Currents & Winds at the Beach.** While the air is lovely, the Atlantic isn't exactly playing nice. There's a dangerous high risk of rip currents today, so if your workout involves getting in the ocean, reconsider. Winds are also breezy, blowing from the east at 10 to 15 mph with gusts up to 20 mph possible. This can make those beach runs a bit more of a slog, and kick up sand.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">

### Local Lowdown: Construction & Future Foresight

Look, we live in a city that's constantly under construction, so expect some detours if you're straying from your usual path.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction at Hugh Taylor Birch State Park:** If you're planning a scenic stroll or run through "Fort Lauderdale's Central Park," be aware that large pavilions, the Banyan restrooms, and North End restrooms have been closed for construction since last September and remain so. Fully accessible restrooms are still available at the Concession Building. You can still get your miles in, just plan your pit stops accordingly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Roadworks on NW 15th Avenue and Sunrise Blvd:** There are ongoing roadway improvements on NW 15th Avenue from West Sunrise Boulevard to Mills Pond Park, with partial closures expected until mid-August 2026. Also, westbound W. Sunrise Boulevard is closed overnight between N. Andrews Avenue and NW 1st Avenue for water main repairs through March 9th, so morning commuters (and early runners) might see lingering impacts. If your route takes you through these areas, be mindful of detours and workers.
</AlertBox>

The City's also designing the new LauderTrail system which will eventually connect 45 parks and 35 neighborhoods with 31 miles of new trails. So while we deal with a few bumps now, there’s better running infrastructure on the horizon. Just keep an eye on city updates for when those new segments actually open up.
