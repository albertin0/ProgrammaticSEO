## Hazards to Watch Out For

### <AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat and Afternoon Thunderstorms:** Today's temperatures are expected to soar into the upper 90s, with heat index values *well above 100 degrees* this afternoon. That's not just uncomfortable; that's dangerous. Couple that with "scattered afternoon and evening thunderstorms" bringing "brief heavy rain, gusty winds, and frequent lightning," and you've got a recipe for a bad time. Don't be that person caught miles from shelter when the sky decides to electrocute everything in sight.
</AlertBox>

### <AlertBox type="warning">
**Ongoing Construction Headaches:** If your route involves downtown, be aware. The Northbank Riverwalk, particularly between Jackson Street and Main Street, is still in various stages of "we're doing stuff, just don't walk here" until fall 2025. This includes the McCoys Creek restoration project. Also, traffic near the Acosta Bridge was already a mess this morning due to a crash. FDOT's I-10 widening project means overnight detours and closures through Thursday on interchanges like Lane Avenue and Cassat Avenue, so if you're out super early or late, be aware. Plan your detours or, better yet, find somewhere else.
</AlertBox>

### <AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** I shouldn't have to say this, but it's Florida in July. You're going to sweat. A lot. Drink more water than you think you need, and then drink some more. Electrolytes too. Headaches, dizziness, confusion? Those aren't signs of a "good workout," they're signs you're an idiot who didn't hydrate. Know the signs of heat illness – heavy sweating, clammy skin, weak pulse are just the start. Cool off immediately if you feel any of this.
</AlertBox>

### <AlertBox type="info">
**Wildfire Smoke - A Lingering Threat (Not Today, But Keep It In Mind):** While the AQI is good today (a solid 24, thanks to whatever breeze we've got), we've seen smoke from wildfires in Lafayette County affect Duval County before, sending our air quality into unhealthy ranges. Florida is a tinderbox, and while there's no active local smoke hazard *today*, stay vigilant on drier, windier days.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, save the heroics for race day, or at least for October.
