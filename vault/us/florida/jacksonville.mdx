## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

You might have heard whispers about wildfire smoke, especially from up north. Good news: our local air quality data shows us at a moderate 59 AQI right now, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. This isn't the choking haze reported in other parts of the country earlier this week. That said, "moderate" still means sensitive groups – asthmatics, those with respiratory issues, and the elderly – should think twice before pushing hard outside. Take it easy, or maybe stick to an indoor workout.

## Mind the Road (Construction, That Is)

If you're planning on driving to your favorite spot, be warned. Our lovely city is a perpetual construction zone. There are ongoing overnight detours and closures, specifically around the I-95 and I-295 North interchange and the Martin Luther King Jr. Parkway/I-95 interchange through at least Wednesday, July 22nd. These are overnight, but traffic snarls can linger. Don't be surprised if your usual quick hop over to the Timucuan Preserve or somewhere north gets rerouted or takes longer than expected, even if the trails themselves are clear. Always check FL511 before you go, or better yet, plan a route that avoids those major arteries if you can.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Around the Interstates:** Expect potential delays and detours if you're traveling near the I-95/I-295 North interchange or the MLK Jr. Parkway/I-95 interchange. These aren't just for overnight anymore; lingering traffic can mess up your morning commute to the trails. Plan extra time or find alternate routes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** Even though it's "only" 76 degrees, this is Florida in July. Humidity is a silent killer. Drink water before, during, and after your workout. Seriously, don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Afternoon Storms:** While today's partly cloudy, typical Jacksonville summer means pop-up thunderstorms are always a possibility later in the day. Keep an eye on the sky and check local radar before you head out, especially for longer runs. Nothing ruins a good workout like getting caught in a torrential downpour or, worse, a lightning storm.
</AlertBox>

No major trail closures reported specifically for our general running paths, but as always, exercise common sense. If a path looks sketchy, or if that AQI is bothering you, live to run another day. Your body will thank you.
