## Air Quality: Don't Be a Hero

Look, I know you want to get your miles in. I get it. But an AQI of 157 isn't something to ignore. This isn't some abstract number; it means there are fine particulate matter (PM2.5) floating around that can get deep into your lungs and bloodstream. We've seen how quickly air quality can decline, whether it's from distant wildfires or local emissions. Pushing hard in this kind of air can lead to coughing, shortness of breath, aggravated asthma, and worse, especially if you have pre-existing conditions.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Unhealthy Air Quality (AQI 157)!** Limit your time outdoors and avoid strenuous activity. This isn't a drill for sensitive groups; it's a red flag for *everyone*. Consider moving your workout inside.
</AlertBox>

## Pollen: A Sticky Situation

Despite what a blanket "Moderate" might imply, weed pollen is currently high, and you know what that means for your sinuses. All that lovely coastal plain and our very own St. Johns River corridor are just breeding grounds for these irritants.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: High Weed Pollen!** If you suffer from allergies, take your meds *before* you even think about stepping out. Expect sneezing, itchy eyes, and general misery if you push it.
</AlertBox>

## Hydration & Common Sense

Even though the heat isn't extreme, you still need to hydrate. You'll be breathing harder if you try to run through that polluted, pollen-filled air. And for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. If you start feeling any irritation in your throat, shortness of breath, or chest discomfort, stop immediately. Your PR isn't worth an emergency room visit.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Check Your Filters!** With PM2.5 and high pollen, your indoor air quality is likely taking a hit too. Make sure your HVAC filters are clean and up to snuff.
</AlertBox>
