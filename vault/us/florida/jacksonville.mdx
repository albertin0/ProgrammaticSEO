## Today's Rundown

Looks like a pretty decent Saturday to get some miles in, assuming you’re not planning on breathing too deeply or enjoying a perfectly smooth ride. The temperature is a sweet 18.9°C (that’s about 66°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit land), and the sky is clear, which is a welcome change from some of the muggier mornings we’ve had. So, visually, it's a win.

However, let's not pretend it's all sunshine and PRs.

### Wildfire Whiffs

Remember that Sargent Fire up by the Florida-Georgia line? Yeah, it's still churning. While it's 50% contained as of yesterday evening, the smoke is definitely drifting down and impacting communities. Don't be surprised if your morning run comes with a side of campfire aroma. If you've got asthma or any other respiratory issues, seriously consider taking it indoors today or sticking to a less strenuous activity. No sense in trading a good workout for an irritated airway.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Smoke Advisory:** That wildfire up north is still producing smoke. If you notice a hazy sky or smell smoke, especially if you're sensitive, modify your outdoor plans. Your lungs aren't meant to filter wildfire particulate matter.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble & Traffic Headaches

Our beloved Emerald Trail is a work in progress, which means less trail and more construction dust in some spots. The LaVilla Link and Hogan Street segments are still actively being built, so expect to navigate around heavy equipment or follow temporary detours. It's great for the future, but a pain in the present. If you're out east, they're also doing improvements on Heckscher Drive, including a new bike path, which can impact your usual routes between Huguenot Memorial Park and the Timucuan Trail.

And if you're driving to your workout spot, be warned: the northbound lanes of the Buckman Bridge are partially closed this weekend for repairs. Plan your commute accordingly, unless you enjoy sitting in traffic contemplating your life choices.

### The Ever-Present Pollen Party

Pollen is at a "Moderate" level today, which means your nose might be running more than you are. Combine that with potential wildfire smoke, and it's a recipe for a snotty, sneezy affair. Hydrate, consider an antihistamine if you're prone to allergies, and maybe save that long run for a day when the air isn't trying to actively attack your sinuses.
