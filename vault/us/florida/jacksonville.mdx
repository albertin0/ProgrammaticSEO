### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Look, I love a good outdoor run as much as the next maniac, but today is not the day to push any PRs when it comes to lung capacity. The AQI is clocking in at 157, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This isn't just a suggestion to "limit outdoor activity" for sensitive folks; it means even healthy individuals can start feeling it if you're out there huffing and puffing for too long. We've had wildfires burning thousands of acres near us, sending smoke our way for a while now, and the city even has a burn ban in effect for Duval County. That smoke isn't just a smell; it's microscopic particles lodging themselves in your airways.

### Pollen & Jazz Fest Jams

As if battling wildfire smoke wasn't enough, we're also dealing with a moderate pollen count. For those of us prone to seasonal sniffles and sneezes, that's just an added layer of misery to an already compromised respiratory situation. Your watery eyes won't appreciate the extra effort.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **WARNING: Moderate Pollen.** If you're an allergy sufferer, expect symptoms. Keep your meds handy, and maybe wear sunglasses to help shield your eyes from irritants.
</AlertBox>

And for you runners who like to hit the urban landscape, particularly around the St. Johns Riverfront or anywhere near the Ford on Bay venue, remember the Jacksonville Jazz Festival is concluding today. While it's a blast, it means more foot traffic, potential road closures (check JSO for real-time updates), and generally less elbow room for your stride. If your usual loop takes you through areas that might be buzzing with festival-goers, plan an alternate route. Trying to weave through a sea of jazz enthusiasts while also trying not to inhale a lungful of PM2.5 sounds like my personal hell.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **TIP: Avoid Downtown Festival Area.** If you're looking for a clear path and less congestion, give the downtown core, especially around Ford on Bay, a wide berth today due to the Jacksonville Jazz Festival.
</AlertBox>
