Look, I know you're itching to get out there, but seriously, exercise some good judgment today. That "clear" sky right now is a total tease. The radar is going to light up, and the humidity will hit you like a wet blanket by lunchtime.

### Air Quality & Allergens

The air quality isn't the worst, but it's not exactly sparkling, either. Our AQI is sitting at a moderate 60, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. For most folks, this is fine, but if you're sensitive to particulates, you might feel it. Pollen is also hanging out at a moderate level, so your seasonal allergies could get a bit testy.
