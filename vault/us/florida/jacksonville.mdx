### River Runs and Downtown Drudgery

If your usual route takes you along the **St. Johns River Greenway**, keep an eye on water levels, especially during high tide. Prolonged onshore winds are known to mess with the river's drainage, so you might encounter some minor tidal flooding in low-lying spots. It's not a full-blown flood warning, but nobody likes soggy shoes or an unexpected detour.

And speaking of detours, Downtown Jacksonville is still a construction zone playground. With a $1.4 billion stadium renovation and other major developments like the RiversEdge project, you can expect ongoing disruptions. If your routine includes the **Downtown Riverwalk**, be prepared for potential closures or re-routes. Check city advisories before you head out, or just assume there's a crane somewhere trying to block your path. Road closures for utility work on San Jose Boulevard and concrete work near Zoo Parkway also mean you might hit unexpected slowdowns on your commute *to* the workout.
