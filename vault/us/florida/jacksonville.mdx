## Your Lungs Are Screaming "No"

Look, I get it. You wake up, see "Mainly Clear" outside, and think it's prime time to hit the pavement. But what you *can't* see is what's truly lurking in the air. Today, Jacksonville's Air Quality Index is clocking in at a whopping 157. [cite: air_quality] That's "Unhealthy" territory, folks, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. [cite: air_quality]

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DO NOT take your workout outside today, seriously.** An AQI of 157 with PM2.5 means *everyone* is at risk of experiencing health effects, not just the "sensitive groups" they always talk about. Minimize outdoor exposure. Run your HVAC. Stay inside. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

This isn't just a mild inconvenience. Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) can burrow deep into your lungs and even hit your bloodstream. Think about that when you're sucking wind on your usual route. AccuWeather is even calling today's air quality "Poor" for Jacksonville. You'd be actively doing more harm than good out there.

## Main Street Bridge? More Like Main Street Bummer.

As if the air wasn't enough, if your route involves crossing the St. Johns River, you're in for a treat. Construction officially kicked off yesterday, August 24, on the Riverfront Plaza Main Street Bridge Nub Removal project. This is not some quick patch job.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**The southbound pedestrian walkway on the Main Street Bridge is CLOSED.** This isn't a suggestion; it's a hard closure that's expected to last until January 30, 2027. You'll need to find an alternate route, like the northbound pedestrian sidewalk, or find another bridge entirely. Traffic on the bridge is also reduced to one southbound lane, so don't expect a peaceful crossing if you're navigating on two wheels.
</AlertBox>

And let's be honest, the "posted detour signs" are rarely as helpful as they sound when you're trying to keep your pace. So, plan ahead if you absolutely *must* traverse the river.
