## It's Not Just Your Lungs, It's the Whole Package

The provided weather data says 26.8°C (about 80°F) and "Mainly Clear," but let's be real, it's Jacksonville in July. The "feels like" temperature is always the real villain. We're talking high humidity that makes 80 degrees feel like 95, turning any serious outdoor activity into a slog that risks heat exhaustion. We've seen folks get hospitalized during warm-weather events here, so don't be a hero. Combine that oppressive humidity with a lung-full of particulate matter, and you're just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Humidity & Heat Stress
While the thermometer might not hit triple digits, the humidity will make it feel like it. Hydrate constantly, and if you *must* be outside for some reason, keep it light and short. Watch for signs of heat stress like dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating.
</AlertBox>
