The thermometer is showing summer vibes long before it should, hitting 26.8°C (80.2°F) and feeling even hotter. This sort of heat, combined with poor air quality, is a recipe for disaster. Forget your long run down the **St. Johns Riverwalk** today; you'll be feeling the heat radiating off that pavement and struggling for breath. Even early morning is going to be warm, and overnight lows haven't provided much relief.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### WARNING: High Heat and Dehydration Risk
With temperatures in the low 80s and feeling warmer, the risk of heat exhaustion and dehydration is significant. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, stick to shaded areas, and drink way more water than you think you need. Seriously, pre-hydrate like your life depends on it, because it kinda does.
</AlertBox>

### Drought & Fire Risk

Florida is deep in an extreme drought, and officials are telling us the peak of wildfire season is here. While no major blazes are actively choking Jacksonville with smoke *today*, the conditions are primed. A burn ban is in effect for Duval County, and for good reason. Be extremely cautious with anything that could spark a fire, because the ground is thirsty and ready to burn.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Pro-Tip: Go Indoors or Adjust Expectations
Today is a fantastic day to try that spin class you've been putting off, or hit the gym. If you're dead-set on outdoor activity, seriously scale back. Short, slow walks, early morning or late evening, and nowhere near heavy traffic. Your lungs and heart will thank you.
