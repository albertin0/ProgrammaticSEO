## Why today isn't the day.

<BulletList>
  - **The Heat is Relentless:** We're under an Extreme Heat Warning, and it's not just hot, it *feels* like the surface of the sun out there. Expect heat index values pushing 112 degrees F this afternoon. Your body will thank you for rescheduling.
  - **Thunderstorms Rolling In:** Clear skies right now are a cruel joke. Later today, we've got a 70% chance of thunderstorms. Don't be that person caught miles from shelter when the lightning starts.
  - **Moderate Pollen & Roadwork Hassles:** While not as deadly as the heat, moderate pollen can still mess with your lungs, and there's enough road construction to make navigating your usual routes a headache.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. We're runners. We like to push. But pushing today is just plain stupid. The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for Jacksonville from 11 AM to 8 PM EDT, with actual high temperatures hitting around 97°F (36°C) and those 'feels like' temperatures soaring into the triple digits. That's not a challenge; that's a one-way ticket to heatstroke. Your lungs might be okay with a moderate AQI of 60, but combine that with air that feels like a wet blanket and you're just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat and Thunderstorm Alert!
  An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for Jacksonville today, with "feels like" temperatures up to 112°F. Coupled with a 70% chance of thunderstorms, staying indoors for intense workouts is strongly advised. Hydration won't save you if you're out in a lightning storm.
</AlertBox>

If you *absolutely* must get some activity in, keep it short, keep it light, and do it early, early this morning before the oven really kicks on. And forget about those long miles on the **Emerald Trail** for now – not only is the heat oppressive, but while segments are being built to connect our neighborhoods, the last thing you need is to get stuck mid-run in a sudden downpour, or worse, a lightning strike.

Speaking of getting stuck, don't forget the ongoing dance with construction. There are overnight ramp closures on **I-295 and U.S. 17** for guardrail repairs until Thursday, and similar overnight work on the **Martin Luther King Jr. Parkway/I-95 Interchange** through Friday. Even the **Buckman Bridge** is still seeing nighttime lane closures for finger joint repairs through Thursday morning, which can impact your early morning commutes or late-night cool-downs. While the big pedestrian walkway closure on the **Main Street Bridge** doesn't start until August 24th, it's just another reminder of the current state of our infrastructure.
