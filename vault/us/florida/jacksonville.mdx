## Local Hazards & Headaches

**Construction Chaos (Still):** FDOT is always busy, and there's plenty of nighttime lane closures on I-10, I-95, and other major arteries. While not directly on pedestrian trails, this can still affect your drive *to* a trail. WOKV also reports ongoing long-term construction across town, from Atlantic Blvd to I-295 and Southside Blvd. So, if your usual running route involves dodging cones, expect more of the same. Luckily, the Florida Trail Association's site isn't showing any specific *trail* closures for today, which is a small victory.

**Events Blocking Your Path:** Today is the first day of the Jacksonville Jazz Festival, running through Memorial Day Weekend. If you planned a scenic run along the Downtown Riverwalk, expect crowds, detours, and general congestion, especially around the main festival areas. Same goes for the North Beaches Art Walk, which typically happens on the third Thursday of the month (that's today, folks) and can clog up the beaches area. Remember that whole Ironman fiasco last weekend? We're still recovering from events messing with our usual routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Downtown & Beaches: Congestion Alert
The Jacksonville Jazz Festival is on, and the North Beaches Art Walk is happening. If your route takes you through downtown or the beaches, prepare for crowds, potential road blocks, and general "event energy" that might not be conducive to a peaceful run. Plan alternative routes or embrace the chaos.
</AlertBox>

## My Two Cents: Get Out Early, or Stay In.

If you *must* get your miles in, hit it before 8 AM or after 7 PM. Seriously. Otherwise, find a treadmill or that sweet, sweet A/C. If you do venture out later, stick to shaded areas where possible. The Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail offers some tree cover and keeps you away from car traffic, which is a smart move today. Remember to hydrate constantly, not just when you feel thirsty.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated & Covered
Wear light, breathable clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, and sunscreen. Carry more water than you think you need. Consider electrolyte tabs or drinks if you're out for anything longer than a casual walk.
</AlertBox>
