## Today's Rundown

Looks like a gorgeous, clear sky out there this morning, but don't let it fool you. We're looking at temperatures climbing to 26.8°C (around 80°F), with highs expected to reach 86°F and a "feels like" of 87°F. The humidity's sitting around 59%, so it won't be quite as oppressive as summer, but it's enough to make you sweat buckets if you're out for anything strenuous.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Hold up on that "good" air quality assumption. While the official AQI might be 35 (which is generally fine), AccuWeather is calling today's air "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups". They're warning about difficulty breathing and throat irritation, especially with prolonged exposure, citing fine particulate matter (PM2.5) as a concern. If you've got any respiratory issues, or even if you don't, cut back on the intensity or just move that workout indoors. Seriously, your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

On top of that, we're still under high fire danger due to drought conditions and some lingering winds. A wildfire in Baker County, though 75% contained, was still causing concerns about smoke and road hazards yesterday. That smoke, even if subtle, isn't doing your lungs any favors, especially when combined with the potential for elevated particulates.

If you're thinking about a run downtown, say around the new Emerald Trail segment near Hogan Street, remember that construction is ongoing with parts of it set to open this year. Always be aware of your surroundings, heavy machinery, and potential detours. Same goes for anything near EverBank Stadium; the "Stadium of the Future" construction has been causing headaches for runners, even impacting the Gate River Run a few weeks back. Don't be surprised by unexpected road blocks or dust.
