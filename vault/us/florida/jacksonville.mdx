## Today's Vitals: Don't Be a Hero

Seriously, today is not the day to push for that PR outdoors. The air quality index is a whopping 157, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Unhealthy" for everyone, not just "sensitive groups." This isn't some abstract number; these are tiny particles that get deep into your lungs, cause inflammation, and can even mess with your heart. We’ve been dealing with this off-and-on for months due to wildfires in surrounding areas, and it seems like July 1st is no different.

You might *think* 26.8°C (about 80°F) and "Mainly Clear" sounds decent, but when your body is already working overtime to filter out crud from the air, adding direct sun and exercise to the mix is just asking for trouble. Hydration is always key in a Jacksonville summer, but it's even more critical when your respiratory system is under assault.

### What to Do (and Not Do)

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid strenuous or prolonged outdoor activity.** I know, I know. But seriously, this isn't a suggestion. Your lungs will thank you. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, low intensity, and ideally, wear an N95 mask to filter out those fine particulates. Cloth masks ain't cutting it for PM2.5.
</AlertBox>

If you were planning a run on a new segment of the Emerald Trail, check for local alerts first. While some parts are opening, there's still a ton of work underway, particularly connecting neighborhoods to downtown and along Hogans Creek and McCoys Creek. You don't want to get halfway through your route only to hit a "Construction Zone - Turn Around" sign and have to backtrack, breathing this mess the whole way.

Consider an indoor workout today. Seriously. Hit up a gym, find some stairs in your apartment building, or pull up a YouTube yoga session. Your lungs, and probably your joints (because let's be real, who feels good running on cruddy air?), will appreciate it. If you're a creature of habit and *have* to get outside, stick to walking, stay in the shade, and keep it brief.
