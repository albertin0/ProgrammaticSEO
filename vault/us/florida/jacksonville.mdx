### Don't Get Dragged Out to Sea, Literally

So, the sun's out, the air's looking decent at an AQI of 45 (that's good, for those of you who don't speak air pollution), and you're probably itching to get that run in. But if your usual route involves the sandy shores of **Jacksonville Beach**, listen up: the National Weather Service is screaming "high risk of rip currents" for our northeast Florida coast through this evening. I don't care how many triathlons you've done or how good you think you are; rip currents are no joke. They'll pull even the most seasoned swimmers into deeper water faster than you can say "I should have just stayed on the Riverwalk." If you insist on getting wet, stick to areas with active lifeguards and pay attention to those warning flags. Or, you know, just don't go in the water. There are plenty of other places to get your miles in without risking a starring role in a Coast Guard rescue video.

### Air Quality & Allergy Woes

On the bright side, your lungs are getting a pretty good deal today. Our Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 45, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. No weird construction dust clouds or smoke from nearby wildfires suffocating us in the city proper, which is a win in my book. There's an active wildfire about 44 miles out, but it's not currently messing with our breathing air here. However, it wouldn't be spring in Florida without some allergens trying to ruin your day. Pollen levels are moderate, so if you're one of the sniffly masses, take your meds *before* you head out, not halfway through your run when you're already feeling like you swallowed a cat.

### Trail Talk & Temperature Check

Now, for those hitting the trails, we're looking good. The temperature is a solid 26.8°C. It's warm, yes, but not in "melt-your-face-off-and-call-911-for-heatstroke" territory. Just keep the hydration up. April in Jacksonville generally hovers around this temperature, so it's what we expect.
