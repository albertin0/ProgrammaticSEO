## AQI is Your Biggest Enemy Today

Seriously, folks. That AQI of 157? That's not "a little hazy," that's "Unhealthy," and your lungs are going to feel it. The dominant pollutant being PM2.5 means tiny particulate matter is floating around, ready to get deep into your respiratory system. Forget about any perceived benefits of that "clear" sky and pleasant 25.3°C (77.5°F) temperature; trying to push hard through this kind of air is just asking for trouble. Inflammation, reduced lung function, aggravated asthma – pick your poison. Even if you don't *feel* it immediately, that junk builds up. So, unless your goal is to add a fresh layer of urban grit to your bronchioles, keep your high-intensity stuff indoors today. AccuWeather even flagged today's air quality as "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups," which, let's be honest, applies to all of us trying to do anything more strenuous than walking to the fridge.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: AQI 157 (Unhealthy) due to PM2.5.** Strenuous outdoor activity is not recommended for *anyone* today. If you must be outside, keep it light and brief. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

## Construction Chaos and Trail Trouble

If you thought you'd escape the bad air by finding a new route, be prepared for some detours. Construction on the **Main Street Bridge** is still ongoing, and you can forget about an easy walk or run across it on the southbound side. The pedestrian walkway there is closed, forcing detours. And let's not forget the extensive work happening around the Riverfront Plaza Project, which means your beloved Riverwalk might not be as accessible as you'd like. Jacksonville's in the middle of a massive construction boom, so expect unexpected closures and reroutes on popular paths. Always check before you head out, especially if your route involves downtown waterways.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Main Street Bridge pedestrian walkway (southbound) is CLOSED due to ongoing construction.** Expect delays and follow posted detours if traversing between the Northbank and Southbank. Riverfront Plaza construction may also impact Riverwalk access.
</AlertBox>

## Fire Risk and Tidal Annoyances

While we're not currently choking on widespread wildfire smoke, the dry conditions are keeping the fire risk at a high level for today and tomorrow. We've seen fires crop up recently, like the one near JTB and Hodges Boulevard a few months back that caused localized smoke. It doesn't take much for smoke to impact your workout, especially when your lungs are already dealing with an "Unhealthy" AQI. Keep an eye out for any plumes, and if you see smoke, turn around. Also, if you're thinking of hitting the beaches or the St. Johns River, News4JAX reported yesterday that minor tidal flooding might still be an issue along the river through this evening's high tide, and rip currents are a moderate risk at the beaches. So, maybe just skip the waterfront efforts for today.
