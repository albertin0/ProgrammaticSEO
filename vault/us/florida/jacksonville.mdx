## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

Look, I get it, you want to get out there. But today, the air isn't doing you any favors. The AQI is sitting at a rough 157, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. This isn't just a "sniffle" day; this is "your lungs will hate you later" day. While there aren't any massive fires burning *in* Duval County right now, smoke from surrounding areas in Florida and even Georgia is drifting our way, blanketing the city in fine particulate matter.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Air Quality Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!
With an AQI of 157, it's unhealthy for sensitive groups, and pushing into unhealthy territory for everyone. People with heart or lung disease, asthma, older adults, and children should seriously reduce time outdoors. For the rest of you, consider making your outdoor activities shorter and less intense, or just take it inside. Don't be a hero; your respiratory system isn't invincible.
</AlertBox>

## Sweat, Thunder, and Construction Detours

Even if the air magically cleared, the weather forecast isn't exactly a runner's paradise. Expect temperatures to climb into the low 90s, but with that lovely Florida humidity, the heat index will easily push into the upper 90s, possibly hitting 100 degrees or more. And because it's July in Jacksonville, we've got a 35% chance of afternoon thunderstorms rolling in, bringing lightning and the potential for brief but strong wind gusts. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because it kind of does.

Beyond the natural elements, our fair city is always in a state of flux. If you're planning a scenic stroll or run on the Northbank Riverwalk, especially near the Hogan Street Cycle Track segment, be aware of ongoing construction. The Hogan Street area is seeing lane reconfigurations, which could impact your route if you're trying to connect to the Riverwalk. Other parts of the Riverwalk are also in various stages of construction, with some segments not expected to fully reopen until later this year or even into 2027/2028. Always check local resources before heading out, unless you enjoy surprise detours and concrete dust with your workout.
