## Today's Rundown: Sweat, Sniffles, and Swerve

Today's actually looking pretty decent by August-in-Florida standards, clocking in at a relatively mild 24.8°C (about 76.6°F) and partly cloudy. So, no immediate extreme heat advisory for *today*, which is a small victory we'll take. But let's not get complacent. This city's still trying to melt us, and the air quality is still a bit hazy with a moderate AQI of 57, thanks to PM2.5. If you've got sensitive lungs, you'll feel it.

Then there's the pollen. Oh, the pollen.

You'll be wiping more than sweat off your brow if you're out there for any sustained period. Keep those tissues handy, and maybe an inhaler if you're prone to seasonal allergies or asthma.

### Construction Chaos: Mind Your Route

Now, for the real fun part: navigating our ever-evolving concrete jungle. If your usual loop takes you downtown, be ready for some serious rerouting.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Detour Alert!** Unplanned maintenance at the Bank of America Tower means Forsyth Street West (from Hogan to Main) and Laura Street North (from Bay to Adams) are both closed until further notice. These are core downtown streets, so plan accordingly or find a different running ground.
</AlertBox>

And looking ahead, if you're a fan of running across the **Main Street Bridge**, know that starting August 24th, the southbound pedestrian walkway is closing for construction on the Riverfront Plaza Main Street Bridge Nub Removal project. Southbound traffic will also be reduced to one lane. It's not today, but it's coming, so get those bridge runs in now if that's your jam, or start scouting alternative routes over the St. Johns.

Out towards the beaches, J. Turner Butler Boulevard (SR 202) is undergoing resurfacing between Belfort Road and Hodges Boulevard. While it's primarily a driving hazard, construction zones often mean dust, uneven surfaces, and distracted drivers, so if you're running nearby, stay alert.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional, Even When "Cooler":** Yes, 76°F feels like a gift in August, but this is Florida. Humidity is still a thing. Drink before, during, and after your workout. Don't be that person collapsed on the Riverwalk.
</AlertBox>

### Weather's Wildcard

Despite today's partly cloudy skies, we've been in a pattern of daily showers and thunderstorms with a risk of localized flooding. It's August, so a pop-up downpour is always a possibility. Keep an eye on the sky and don't assume clear sailing.

So, go get your miles in, but be smart about it.
