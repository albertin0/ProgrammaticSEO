<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Be a Heat Casualty
That 24.7°C (76.5°F) reading might seem manageable, but the humidity cranks the "feels like" temperature way up. We're talking heat index in the triple digits, folks. If you're out there between 10 AM and 4 PM, you're asking for trouble. Keep your runs shorter, slower, and stay in the shade as much as possible. And for the love of all that is holy, drink water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Dehydration is a quick trip to the emergency room, not a PR.
</AlertBox>
