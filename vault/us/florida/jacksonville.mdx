### Weather & Air Quality Breakdown

Today is a prime example of why you can't just glance at the temperature and head out. Yes, 19.6°C (around 67°F) with overcast skies sounds *tempting*, but the real story is in those coastal warnings. We've got a Coastal Flood Warning in effect through Monday for pretty much anywhere near the water in Duval County. That includes your usual scenic routes along the St. Johns River and, definitely, the beaches. This isn't just about wet feet; we're talking about water levels 2 to 3.5 feet above normal around high tide, which means roads, parks, and low-lying areas will be under water. Don't be that person trying to drive or bike through unknown depths; it's foolish and dangerous.

The wind isn't playing nice either. A Wind Advisory is out for coastal areas, with gusts that could knock you sideways. If you were planning a bike ride on the Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail-Trail, you might be okay further inland, but anything exposed is going to feel like you're fighting a small hurricane. Forget trying to get any speed up on the Beach Boulevard bridge.

On the bright side, our Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 42, with Ozone as the dominant pollutant. So, while your lungs might usually be a concern with moderate pollen, today the air itself isn't the primary enemy. That said, Jacksonville is no stranger to wildfire smoke drifting in from surrounding counties or even South Georgia, sometimes pushing our AQI into unhealthy territory. So, while it's clear today, it's always something to keep an eye on in this part of Florida.
