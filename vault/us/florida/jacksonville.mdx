You want to run outside in Jacksonville today? Are you *nuts*? It's July in Florida. Unless your idea of a good time involves sweating through every pore and feeling like you're running inside a convection oven, stay indoors. Seriously.

### Why today is *definitely* not the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Heat, Glorious Heat (Not):** The forecast says a high of 95°F, but the "feels like" temperature is going to be baking between 102-108°F. That's not just uncomfortable, folks, that's straight-up dangerous. We're talking heat exhaustion, heat stroke territory if you're not careful. Your body is working overtime just existing in that.
*   **River's Got Issues:** If you're thinking of cooling off in the St. Johns, think again. The Department of Health issued a blue-green algae health alert for St. Johns River-Watson Island just a few days ago. And let's be real, our river's got a pretty consistent problem with various contaminants and fecal bacteria anyway. Dipping your toes, let alone your whole sweaty self, is a bad call.
*   **Construction Zone Blues:** Downtown is still a maze. While parts of the Emerald Trail, like the Hogan Street Link, are nearing completion or recently opened, much of the Riverwalk is still under some form of construction. Expect detours, uneven surfaces, and a general lack of flow. Not ideal for hitting your stride.
</BulletList>

## The Rundown: What's Hitting You Today

### Weather Report: Fry, Fry Again

It's a "Mainly Clear" morning, which just means the sun gets a direct shot at turning you into a puddle. The air is already sitting at a sticky 80°F (26.8°C), and it's only going up. The National Weather Service is predicting those "feels like" temps of 100-107°F. That's not a suggestion; that's a warning. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because, well, it kinda does.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory Incoming:** "Feels like" temperatures are projected to be well over 100°F today, possibly hitting Heat Advisory levels later this weekend. Take frequent breaks, seek shade, and consider rescheduling outdoor workouts. Heat stroke is a real, potentially fatal risk.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards & Annoyances

#### Construction Chaos

Let's talk about our beloved Jacksonville Riverwalk. If you're planning a run along the Northbank, remember that the Four Seasons Hotel Corridor segment is still under construction, slated for an August completion. And good luck getting a smooth run at Metropolitan Park — that segment isn't expected until 2028. Even if the Emerald Trail's Hogan Street Link is wrapping up, you'll still encounter ongoing work. It's less a scenic run and more an obstacle course these days.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**River Contamination Alert:** A blue-green algae health alert has been issued for parts of the St. Johns River (specifically St. Johns River-Watson Island). Avoid contact with any water showing visible blooms, and keep pets far away. Seriously, don't let Fido drink that stuff.
</AlertBox>

### Events & Detours

No major runs or races are blocking our usual routes today, but there's a Silent Disco at Friendship Fountain tomorrow evening. So, if your Saturday plans involved an evening Riverwalk run near Downtown, you might encounter some headphone-wearing dancers.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable:** With "feels like" temperatures nearing triple digits, carry more water than you think you need. Consider an electrolyte drink, especially if you're out for more than 30 minutes. Your body will thank you, or at least it won't quit on you quite as fast.
</AlertBox>
