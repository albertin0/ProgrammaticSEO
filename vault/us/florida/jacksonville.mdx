**Extreme Caution for Lungs:** An AQI of 157 (Unhealthy) is no joke, especially when PM2.5 is the main culprit. This fine particulate matter gets deep into your lungs and bloodstream, and combined with moderate pollen, today is a prime day for respiratory irritation, asthma attacks, or worse. Just don't. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Watch:** While the immediate danger isn't directly within city limits, the ongoing brush fires in Nassau and Putnam counties are contributing significantly to our poor air quality. Plus, with "near-record highs" and "windy conditions exacerbating wildfire risks" forecast for the weekend, expect this smoky situation to continue, if not worsen. Pay attention to any new alerts, especially if you live near wooded areas.
</AlertBox>

Plan for interruptions and consider if dodging construction cones is really how you want to spend your workout.
