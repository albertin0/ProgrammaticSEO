Alright, Jacksonville, let's talk turkey. Stepping outside this morning means embracing a low-visibility workout, or better yet, heading indoors. The weather data shows a cool 15.1°C (about 59°F) and a thick blanket of fog, particularly prevalent north and west of I-10. That means damp surfaces and drivers who probably haven't had enough coffee yet to spot your reflective gear. If you're out there, run *against* traffic and assume you're invisible.

Then there's the joy that is the Emerald Trail. While we're all looking forward to the completed 30-mile urban oasis, it's currently a patchwork of progress and ongoing disruption. Construction on segments like the Hogan Street Link, which will cut right through Downtown, is a multi-year affair. You might find unexpected closures or reroutes around McCoys Creek and Hogans Creek as they undergo restoration work. This isn't just about inconvenience; it's about legitimate safety hazards near heavy equipment and active work zones.
