## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy Out There

Look, the temperature is decent for a run at 26.8°C (that's about 80°F for those still in the dark ages), and the AQI number is technically "Good" at 39. But let's be real, a quick sniff outside tells you a different story. Smoke from distant wildfires, specifically from places like the Okefenokee, is making its way here. If you have asthma or anything resembling lungs that prefer clean air, you might feel a bit of a burn, regardless of what the "official" numbers claim.

Then there's the wet stuff. Yesterday saw "nuisance flooding" in spots like the Mixontown neighborhood along McCoy's Creek, and roads were blocked near Mayport and Jacksonville Beach. We're looking at a 38% chance of light rain today, jumping to 65% tonight, thanks to that tropical depression brewing out in the Gulf. Don't even *think* about trying to splash through standing water, whether you're driving or on foot. It’s deeper than you think, and it’s gross.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step: Floodwaters are Treacherous.** Recent heavy rains have led to significant nuisance flooding in areas like McCoy's Creek and parts of Atlantic Beach. Expect slick spots, standing water, and potentially closed routes. Turn around, don't drown, and certainly don't run through it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen's Moderate, but Smoke is the Real Buzzkill.** While the pollen count isn't through the roof, that wildfire smoke is going to be the bigger irritant for most. Consider an indoor workout or keeping your outdoor sessions shorter, especially if you're prone to respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Road Closures & Construction are Everywhere.** From the Main Street Bridge's southbound pedestrian closures to ongoing work on Beach Blvd, your usual routes might be blocked or significantly rerouted. Check Jax511.com before you head out, or you'll just be adding frustration to your workout.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today isn't the worst, but it's far from ideal. Choose your path wisely, stay hydrated (even if it's cloudy and rainy, it's still Florida), and maybe just hit the gym.
