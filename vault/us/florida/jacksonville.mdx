### Air Quality: Don't Breathe That In

Look, I know you love the idea of an outdoor run, but unless you're packing an N95 mask (and even then, why risk it?), your lungs are going to hate you today. The pervasive wildfire smoke has made the air genuinely bad. We're talking fine particulate matter that digs deep into your lung tissue. This isn't just a sniffle-and-go situation; it's a "stay indoors, keep your windows closed, and run your A/C with a good filter" situation. Seriously, put the miles on the treadmill or hit up an indoor gym. Your respiratory system will thank you later. This isn't some corporate wellness blog telling you to "listen to your body" – this is a physical therapist telling you that your body is screaming "NO!"

### Weather & Hydration: The Usual Florida Summer Gauntlet

Despite the "mainly clear" descriptor, it's going to be a classic hot and humid Jacksonville day. The feels-like temperature will be significantly higher than the actual thermometer reading. Afternoon thunderstorms are also in the forecast, so don't get caught out in the open. You'll be sweating buckets even standing still. If you absolutely *must* be outside, hydrate like your life depends on it (because, in this heat, it kinda does), wear lightweight clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, find shade whenever possible.
