Alright, Jacksonville, if you're thinking about lacing up your shoes today, it's a mixed bag of Florida sunshine and potential lung irritants.

<LungsJointsScore score={6.5} />

<BulletList>
*   Temps are gorgeous, staying in the mid-70s with mostly clear skies. Perfect running weather, right?
*   Pollen is still hanging around at moderate levels, so if you're sniffly, don't forget your meds.
*   The real kicker? We're still catching some drift from that 1,400-acre South Canal wildfire way down in Lafayette County. Winds are pushing that smoke straight into Duval, especially this morning.
</BulletList>

Look, the AQI might be showing "Good" at 34, which is technically fine for most of us. But I've been running these streets long enough to know that a "Good" AQI doesn't always tell the whole smoky story when there's a wildfire in the region. If you've got asthma, COPD, or you're just generally sensitive to lung-tickling particles, listen to your body. Emergency management officials are actively monitoring the situation and urging folks with health sensitivities to minimize outdoor activity if the smoke thickens. Your run isn't worth an ER visit, so check AirNow.gov for real-time local conditions before you head out, especially if you live further southwest.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Smoke Alert:** While our local AQI is showing good, smoke from the South Canal Fire southwest of us could still be lingering, particularly for sensitive individuals. Minimize outdoor activity if you see or smell heavy smoke.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the air, remember our city is pretty much one giant construction zone. If your route usually takes you near the Buckman Bridge, be prepared for the usual traffic headaches. That project's been a beast for over a year and isn't expected to wrap up until late summer or early fall. It's mostly a car problem, but if you cycle or run near those major arteries, stay sharp.

Then there's Downtown, where it seems like every path and park is getting a facelift. The Northbank Riverwalk, a staple for many, is still seeing significant closures. If you typically cruise from Jackson Street to Main Street, you're going to hit detours thanks to the McCoys Creek restoration and other Riverfront Plaza projects. Phase One of the Riverfront Plaza might be opening early this year, but Phase Two is just getting started, meaning more cranes and more blocked paths through 2027. Don't get me wrong, it's going to be awesome *eventually*, but for now, it's a maze.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Downtown Detours Ahead:** Portions of the Northbank Riverwalk, particularly between Jackson Street and Main Street, remain closed due to ongoing construction for projects like the Riverfront Plaza and McCoys Creek restoration. Plan alternative routes.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the sunshine, but be smart about where you're breathing and where you're stepping.
