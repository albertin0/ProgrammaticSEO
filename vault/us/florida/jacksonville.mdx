### Don't Breathe That In, Seriously

Your air quality data today is flashing bright red for a reason. An AQI of 157 means it's unhealthy for *everyone*, not just your sensitive aunt with asthma. We're talking about fine particulate matter (PM2.5) that gets deep into your lungs and can mess with your heart. Skipping your usual run along the St. Johns River is a no-brainer if you value your cardiovascular health. This isn't a "push through it" day; it's a "stay inside and maybe clean your air filter" day.
