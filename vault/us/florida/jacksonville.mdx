## Local Hazards & Your Knees' Best Friends (or Worst Enemies)

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Unhealthy Air Quality is NO JOKE
That AQI of 157 means the air is straight-up unhealthy, especially if you've got any lung issues like asthma, or if you're over 65, pregnant, or a kid. You're breathing in fine particulate matter (PM2.5), which can burrow deep into your lungs. Forget that long run on the Riverwalk today. You're better off on the treadmill indoors. This isn't just about feeling a little winded; it's about long-term health. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye on the Sky (and the Radar)
While it's clear now, June in Jacksonville means afternoon storms are a given. Yesterday, we saw severe thunderstorms with gusts up to 55 mph, downed trees, and power outages. The St. Johns River was "rocking and rolling," so if you were thinking of a paddle, maybe reconsider. Even if today's storms are "isolated" as predicted, they can turn nasty fast. Heavy rain and lightning are still on the menu.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Trail Construction & Detours
If you're heading out to hit some miles on two wheels or your own two feet, remember that construction is still a thing around town. The Emerald Trail, a fantastic project, has ongoing work. A segment of the Hogan Street Link is expected to be completed by summer 2026, but other parts are still very much under construction or in design. We also had construction start recently on the Timucuan Trail-Fort Caroline Connector, causing some tree removal along Fort Caroline Road. Always check local advisories before you head out, because nobody likes a surprise detour or a construction zone on their planned long run.
</AlertBox>

Look, I love running the trails as much as the next maniac, but today's a day to be smart. That "Unhealthy" air isn't worth bragging about pushing through. Find an indoor option, or at the very least, keep your outdoor time short and easy. Your lungs will thank you.
