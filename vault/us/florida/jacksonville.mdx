Alright, Jacksonville, listen up. If you're planning to hit the pavement or the trails today, March 18th, you'd better bundle up, especially if you're an early bird.

You’re waking up to a sneaky cold one, even for March. The air temp is hovering around 4.1°C (that's about 39°F for those of you not fluent in metric), and parts of inland Northeast Florida are under freeze and frost alerts until this morning. I know, I know, it's Jacksonville, not Siberia, but your lungs will feel that bite if you're out too early on the Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail. Action News Jax is calling for a high of 63°F later today, which is a pretty sweet rebound, but those early hours are no joke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Early Morning Freeze Advisory!
If you're hitting the road or trails before the sun really gets going, especially west of I-95, you could be dealing with temperatures near or below freezing. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; we're talking about a genuine risk of hypothermia if you're not dressed properly, and trust me, that's not how you want to start your Wednesday. The JFRD even put out warnings about cold weather risks back in January.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Dress in Layers, You Goof!
Seriously, I've seen too many people out there looking like they're headed to the beach when it's 40 degrees. Layers are your best friend today. Start with a moisture-wicking base, add an insulating layer, and top it with a wind-resistant shell. You can always shed layers as the day warms up. Don't forget gloves and a hat for those early hours – a lot of heat escapes through your head and hands.
</AlertBox>

While the recent rain threats from earlier in the month seem to have passed for today, let's not forget we're still deep in an extreme drought across the entire area. The fire danger has been significant, and though winds are calm today, the ground is bone dry. Be mindful if you're anywhere near wooded areas, and absolutely no open flames or careless disposal of anything that could spark.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Drought Conditions Persist
Even with calm winds today, extreme drought conditions mean the wildfire risk remains a background threat. Stay vigilant, especially if you're on unpaved trails or near dry brush.
</AlertBox>
