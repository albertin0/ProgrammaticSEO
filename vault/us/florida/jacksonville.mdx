<AlertBox type="danger">
### UNHEALTHY AIR QUALITY!

With an AQI of 157 and PM2.5 being the primary pollutant, outdoor exercise is highly discouraged for *everyone*. This isn't just a sniffle-and-sneeze day; we're talking about particulate matter deep in your lungs. If you absolutely HAVE to be outside, keep it short, easy, and away from heavy traffic or known smoke plumes. This poor air quality is exacerbated by ongoing extreme to exceptional drought conditions fueling regional brush fires.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### High Winds and Hazardous Waters

Those planning a run or ride along the Jacksonville Beach oceanfront or on the Jacksonville Riverwalk need to be mindful of the wind. Gusts up to 40-50 mph are no joke, especially on exposed bridges or near the water. Forget swimming or surfing; the high risk of rip currents and rough seas means the ocean is off-limits.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Alternate Workout Plans

Seriously, this is a prime day for indoor activities. Hit the gym, use your home equipment, or find a class. Your lungs, joints, and frankly, your life, are more important than getting a PR today. Save the outdoor miles for when the air clears up and the Atlantic calms down. If you *must* exercise outside, limit duration and intensity, wear glasses or sunglasses to protect your eyes from particulates, and absolutely avoid strenuous activity.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, the "Mainly Clear" sky isn't much comfort when you're inhaling micro-particles and fighting off a rogue gust of wind. Today is a hard pass for any serious outdoor exertion. Just don't do it.
