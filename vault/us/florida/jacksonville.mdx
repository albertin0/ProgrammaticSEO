But don't get too comfortable. That "mainly clear" sky we're currently enjoying? It's just a temporary lull. "Waves of showers and thunderstorms are expected to push through the region through at least early next week," potentially bringing widespread rainfall and localized flooding. We're talking 1 to 3 inches of rain through Monday, with some spots seeing 4 inches or more. Plus, those storms could pack strong wind gusts near 40 mph.

Pollen-wise, we're at a moderate level. While AccuWeather is reporting low tree and grass pollen for the Beaches, if you're sensitive, you might still feel it. Keep those allergy meds handy.

### Hazard Hotspots & Events

Today is actually pretty busy on the local running scene, so if you're not participating, you'll want to adjust your plans. The "Kindness Matters 5k and Fun Run" is happening at **Seawalk Pavilion in Jacksonville Beach**, and there are also parkruns at **Halsema Trailhead** and **Ed Austin Regional Park**. These will mean crowds and potential path closures in those areas, especially in the morning.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for Runners:** If you're heading out to Jacksonville Beach or one of the parks mentioned, check race schedules to avoid getting caught in the middle of a 5k. Patience, people.
</AlertBox>

On the construction front, our city is always building something. The **Southbank Riverwalk** area, particularly around the Riverfront Plaza, is slated for more development, including new trails and connections to the Main Street Bridge, with construction anticipated to start this year and continue into 2027. While some dune walkover replacements at Jacksonville Beach should have wrapped up by now, always be aware of active work zones. And let's not forget the usual array of road work; Butler Boulevard from Hodges Boulevard to the Intercoastal Waterway has nighttime lane and ramp closures through June 7th. If you're an early bird, those closures might still be active.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Afternoon Storms:** What starts "mainly clear" could quickly turn nasty. Keep an eye on the sky and check radar before and during your workout, especially after lunch. Heavy rain and strong winds are possible.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Lightning is a Real Threat:** Seriously, folks. When thunder roars, go indoors. There's no PR worth getting zapped over. If you see lightning or hear thunder, immediately seek shelter.
</AlertBox>

Stay hydrated, be aware of your surroundings, and maybe pack a small towel for that inevitable humidity. It’s Jacksonville, after all.
