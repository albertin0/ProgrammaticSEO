## The Rundown

Alright, Jacksonville, it's June 12th, and honestly, it could be a whole lot worse. The sun's out, the weather data says we're looking at a clear day around 24.7°C (that's 76.5°F for the Fahrenheit folks). Not exactly cool, but manageable if you get after it early. Just remember, "clear" here often comes with a side of "swampy," as the humidity is already pushing into the high 80s, making it feel closer to 81-83°F. Hydrate like you mean it, even if you're just walking the dog down San Jose.

The official Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting pretty at 42, which is considered "Good." The dominant pollutant listed is "Ozone/Other," which sounds vague but usually isn't as concerning as particulate matter. However, let's not get complacent. Just recently, the city itself was warning about smoke from wildfires in *surrounding* counties leading to "elevated particulate concentrations" and potentially unhealthy air for sensitive groups, even when no fires were burning *in* Duval County. So, while the number looks green, if you start smelling that familiar campfire scent or feel a tickle in your throat, especially near the outer edges of the county, trust your gut over the number.

Moderate pollen means your seasonal allergies might be acting up. Oak, pine, and grass pollen are always lurking in this state. Pop your antihistamines before you head out, and maybe plan your run away from the heaviest wooded areas if you're feeling extra sniffly.

## Trail & Road Hazards

You know the drill. Jax is perpetually under construction, which means your usual routes might have a surprise detour. If you're planning on checking out the progress on the **Emerald Trail**, specifically the Hogan Street Link downtown, be aware that Hogan Street is being narrowed for the pedestrian/cyclist path and is slated for completion in early 2027. The Southbank Riverwalk also has an extension in the works, connecting toward the Fuller Warren Bridge. Don't be that runner who gets annoyed by construction cones; just adjust your route.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Tidal Flooding Risk:** Be mindful of high tide, especially if you're in low-lying areas near the St. Johns River or the Intracoastal. News reports have mentioned tidal flooding as a "main hazard" today, particularly in areas like San Marco. Stick to higher ground to avoid soggy shoes or unexpected swims.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Even with clear skies, the high humidity is going to make you sweat buckets. Carry water, plan routes with water stops, and don't push it too hard, especially if you're doing a longer effort. Early morning is still your best bet to beat the worst of the heat and humidity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Smoke Sensitivity:** Despite a generally good AQI, recent warnings about wildfire smoke from surrounding counties impacting air quality, especially for sensitive groups, are still relevant. If you have asthma, COPD, or other respiratory issues, keep your outdoor activities shorter and less intense, or stick to indoor workouts if you notice any irritation.
</AlertBox>
