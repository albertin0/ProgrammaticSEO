## The Rundown: What's Out There Today

Alright, let's talk real talk for your run. That crisp air is a blessing, especially after some of the hazy days we've had. The low AQI means you won't feel like you're sucking down a campfire, which is always a plus in Duval.

However, don't let the clear skies fool you into thinking it's cool out. We’re deep into May now, and the sun means business. Hydration is non-negotiable. If you’re not thinking about it, you’re already behind. This isn't just a suggestion; it's how you avoid seeing me (your friendly neighborhood PT) for heat exhaustion.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Drought & Burn Ban Still Active:** Don't get complacent just because the skies are clear today. Northeast Florida is still under extreme to exceptional drought conditions, and the burn ban remains in effect across Duval County. While the South Canal Fire showed minimal activity yesterday thanks to some rain, the risk is always there. If you see or smell heavy smoke, especially if it's localized, call 911. Better safe than sorry when the woods are tinder-dry.
</AlertBox>

Good news for our downtown pavement pounders: the **Northbank Riverwalk** officially reopened yesterday with the unveiling of the Riverfront Music Garden. So go enjoy that fresh concrete and the views of the St. Johns. Just be aware that Downtown Jacksonville is a hub of activity, with projects like the new UF campus at West Bay and Lee streets indicating ongoing development. Keep an eye out for any localized construction impacts, though nothing major is reported for foot traffic today.

<AlertBox type="info">
**IRONMAN Impact This Weekend:** Heads up, folks! The IRONMAN Jacksonville race is set for *this Saturday, May 16th*. The U.S. Coast Guard will enforce a temporary safety zone on the St. Johns River from 7 AM to 11 AM that day, restricting vessel traffic. Expect significant traffic impacts all day Saturday along the race routes, which run through San Marco, the Southside, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Downtown, and Riverside. If you usually run or bike these areas on Saturdays, plan your route *now* for a different part of town.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, grab your water bottle (or two), and enjoy the relatively good air while it lasts. Just remember, it’s still Florida in May, and that sun waits for no one.
