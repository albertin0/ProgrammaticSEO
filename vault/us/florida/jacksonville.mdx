## Today's Outdoor Conditions: The "Don't Do It" Edition

Look, I get it. You've got miles to log, gains to make. But today isn't the day to prove your grit outside in Jacksonville. The conditions are lining up to give you a pretty miserable, and potentially unhealthy, experience.

### Air Quality Alert

We're sitting at an AQI of 157, which is firmly in the "Unhealthy" category, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This means that everyone, especially those with respiratory issues, should limit outdoor exertion. Forget what the weather *looks* like (clear skies, woohoo!), because what you *can't see* is far more concerning. News from earlier in the month highlighted how wildfire smoke was already raising air quality issues across Northeast Florida, and while we don't have new wildfire alerts today, this persistent unhealthy air quality is a significant red flag for your lungs.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Unhealthy Air Quality (AQI 157).** Avoid prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. If you have asthma, COPD, or other respiratory conditions, stay indoors. Even healthy individuals are at risk of adverse health effects.
</AlertBox>

### Cold Snap & Wind Chill

A strong cold front blew through yesterday, leaving us with some seriously chilly temperatures for Jacksonville. It's 41°F (5°C) and feels even colder thanks to brisk northwest winds. If you're out on the Fuller Warren Bridge Shared Use Path or any open areas, that wind is going to cut right through you. Yesterday, inland areas even saw freeze warnings. Don't underestimate how quickly hypothermia can set in, even in Florida, when temperatures dip and winds pick up. Your joints are going to scream in protest on that first mile if you're not properly warmed up and layered.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Unexpectedly Cold with Wind Chill.** Layer up significantly if you must go out. Exposed skin is at risk. Protect your hands, ears, and head.
</AlertBox>

### Local Obstacles & Construction

Good news: the major severe weather from yesterday, which included tornado watches in Duval County, has passed. However, construction around the city continues to be a factor. The "Stadium of the Future" construction at EverBank Stadium is causing changes to the Gate River Run route and parking, indicating that the surrounding areas, including the Shipyards, might be less accessible or have detours. Additionally, the Southbank Riverwalk extension, connecting the Baptist Medical Center to the Fuller Warren Bridge Shared Use Path, is ongoing. While these aren't full closures, they mean potential uneven surfaces, equipment, or reroutes on your usual paths. Always stay aware of your surroundings, especially if you're distracted by trying to breathe in this air.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Scope out your route.** If you're planning on running near EverBank Stadium or the Southbank Riverwalk, expect potential construction zones and be prepared for detours.
