## The Air is Thick, Folks

Let's be real, your lungs are not going to thank you for a long run on the Riverwalk today. An AQI of 157 due to PM2.5 means you're breathing in a bunch of tiny particles that can get deep into your lungs and even your bloodstream. If you've got any kind of respiratory issues – asthma, COPD, even just a persistent cough – stay inside. And honestly, even if you feel like a superhero, prolonged exposure to air like this isn't doing your cardiovascular system any favors. AccuWeather is calling today's air quality "Poor," and they're not wrong.

## Wildfire Watch: Don't Be That Guy

The entire First Coast is in an extreme drought. Remember those dry conditions at the Jacksonville Arboretum & Botanical Gardens we heard about? They're still a very real concern across the county. This means dry brush is everywhere, just waiting for a spark. Duval County has a year-round burn ban, but even without active burning, a carelessly tossed cigarette butt or even parking a hot vehicle on dry grass can ignite a serious problem.
