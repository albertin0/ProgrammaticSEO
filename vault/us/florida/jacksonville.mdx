## Today's Vitals (April 6, 2026)

Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a whopping 157, which is straight-up unhealthy. The dominant pollutant? PM2.5. This tiny particulate matter gets deep into your lungs and can mess with your respiratory and cardiovascular systems. Remember that "State of the Air" report from last year? It warned us Jacksonville's air quality was worsening, and today's numbers are a grim reminder. A wildfire near Sanderson yesterday might be contributing to the haze and PM2.5 in the air, even if it's not right on our doorstep.

<AlertBox type="danger">
