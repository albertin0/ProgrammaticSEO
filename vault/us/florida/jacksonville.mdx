## Today's Rundown: The Good, The Bad, and The Muggy

Alright, fellow Jax runners, let's talk about hitting the pavement or trails today, August 4, 2026. The morning actually started off pretty pleasant, sitting around 24.6°C (76.3°F) under clear skies. It's almost deceivingly nice, isn't it? But don't let that fool you. This is Florida, and she always has a surprise up her sleeve.

### Weather Whiplash and Wet Feet

Today's forecast is screaming "classic Florida summer afternoon" louder than a tourist trying to find parking at Jax Beach. We're looking at highs in the low 90s inland and upper 80s to low 90s along the beaches. More importantly, News4JAX is calling for a 40-50% chance of scattered showers and thunderstorms, especially for Northeast Florida, and guess what comes with that? Exactly: heavy rain, localized flooding, damaging winds, and lightning. You know the drill. That inland Flood Watch might have expired yesterday, but tropical moisture is still pushing through, meaning those slow-moving storms could dump enough water to turn your favorite path into a swamp. My advice? Get your miles in early, *before* Mother Nature decides to turn on the sprinklers and throw a lightning show. No PR is worth getting zapped or swimming through a street after a sudden downpour.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Afternoon Storms Are Coming.** Don't try to outrun lightning or navigate flooded streets. Plan your outdoor activities for earlier in the day, and have a backup indoor option. Seriously, it's not a challenge; it's a hazard.
</AlertBox>

### Construction: The Perpetual State of Our City

Our beloved city is constantly under construction, and today is no exception. If you're planning on running along the river, especially near downtown, prepare for detours. The Southbank Riverwalk, bless its heart, is still undergoing improvements, and the Riverfront Plaza (the old Landing site, remember that?) is deep into its second phase of construction. They're talking about a new bike and pedestrian connection to the Main Street Bridge eventually, but for now, it's just more orange cones and blocked paths. Even the RiversEdge area has an amphitheater under construction. And out in Jacksonville Beach, the 4th Street South roadway improvements project, originally slated to wrap up last month, has been riddled with delays, so don't be surprised if that's still a mess too. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Always Check Your Route.** Construction around the Southbank Riverwalk and Riverfront Plaza changes constantly. Look up local road and trail updates before you head out, or embrace the art of spontaneous detours (and added mileage).
</AlertBox>
