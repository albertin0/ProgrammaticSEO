## The Air: A Special Kind of Awful

Let's be blunt: that AQI of 157 is a red flag big enough to wrap around the entire city. When the dominant pollutant is PM2.5, we're talking about tiny particles that bypass your body's defenses and dive straight into your lungs. Think about it. Do you *really* want to be doing maximal exertion, sucking down air that's actively trying to mess with your respiratory system? This isn't just a "sensitive groups" warning; this is a "everyone take it easy" warning. You might not see a thick haze, but the data doesn't lie. Wildfire smoke, even from far-off blazes, has a habit of making its way here without much fanfare, silently elevating those PM2.5 levels.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### UNHEALTHY AIR QUALITY ALERT!
An AQI of 157 (Unhealthy) means significant health effects are likely for the general population. Prolonged outdoor exertion is highly discouraged. Stay indoors if possible.
</AlertBox>

## Flood Watch & Construction Clutter

While the air is the main event, let's not ignore the smaller annoyances that can still ruin your day. The St. Johns River is still at minor flood stage in spots, especially downtown. This means those tempting low-lying sections of our parks near the water might be slick, or worse, unexpectedly deep. Who needs a surprise ankle-deep plunge on their morning run?

Meanwhile, if your route takes you anywhere near the **Main Street Bridge**, you're out of luck on the pedestrian path. Construction is ongoing for the Riverfront Plaza Main Street Bridge Nub Removal project. The southbound pedestrian walkway is closed, and traffic is reduced. So, don't expect a smooth crossing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Flood-Prone Areas & Bridge Closures
Minor tidal flooding along the St. Johns River could affect low-lying roads and park areas. The pedestrian walkway on the southbound side of the Main Street Bridge is closed due to construction.
</AlertBox>
