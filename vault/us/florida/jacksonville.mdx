## Today's Outdoor Rundown: Stay Safe or Stay Inside

Look, I get it. We live in Florida. "Mainly Clear" skies and temps around 70-something degrees in October sound glorious for a run or a paddle. But don't let that fool you today. A cold front is sweeping through, and while it's bringing some cooler, drier air (a welcome change from swamp-ass season, honestly), it's also kicking up a serious fuss, especially near the coast.

If your plans included tackling the surf at **Jacksonville Beach**, forget it. Seriously. There's a high rip current risk and a high surf advisory that'll last through Monday night. Waves are building to 8 to 12 feet. That's not a "fun waves" situation; that's a "don't drown, dummy" situation. Save your beach runs for later this week when things calm down.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Coastal Danger Zone:** Avoid swimming at Jacksonville Beach and along the Atlantic coast today. Dangerous rip currents and high surf (8-12 feet) are expected. Seriously, don't be *that* person who needs rescuing.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the immediate coast, those gusty north-northeast winds are no joke. We're talking 30-40 mph gusts, which can make any outdoor activity a full-body fight against the wind. Even a casual bike ride along the St. Johns River could feel like a full-on struggle. Factor in the moderate pollen and AQI, and your lungs might not appreciate the extra effort to pull air through the bluster.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wind Advisory & Minor Flooding Potential:** Expect strong winds inland, making cycling and exposed trail running challenging. Also, watch for minor tidal flooding along the coast, Intracoastal Waterway, and even parts of the St. Johns River basin. Don't park your car too close to the usual flood zones.
</AlertBox>
