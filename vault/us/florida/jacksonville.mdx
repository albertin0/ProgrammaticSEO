## Why today is *not* your PR day (unless you're in the race)

<BulletList>
*   **Race Day Chaos:** If you're not running the Jacksonville Marine Corps Half Marathon & Freedom 5K today, downtown is a mess. Roads are shut down around Riverfront Plaza, and the course takes over key arteries, including parts of the St. Johns Riverfront and the Acosta Bridge. Unless you like weaving through thousands of competitive and very focused runners, find another route or hit the gym.
*   **Ongoing Construction Annoyances:** The never-ending saga of Jax construction continues. Expect closures on the Northbank Riverwalk, with detours that'll add more miles and frustration than you bargained for. Seriously, plan ahead or stick to familiar, non-downtown turf. If you're near Jacksonville Beach, the westbound lane of Beach Blvd between 1st and 2nd Street is also shut down until Friday.
*   **Moderate Allergies & Air Quality:** We've got moderate pollen out there, and the AQI is sitting at 52 for PM2.5. Not terrible, but enough to make those with sensitivities feel it. No major wildfire smoke issues reported today, but if you're feeling sniffly or a little chesty, take it easy.
</BulletList>

## My Two Cents: Get Out, But Be Smart

Look, the temperature is fantastic. That 19.8°C (67.6°F) is prime running weather for us swamp-dwellers. Enjoy it, but for the love of all that is holy, stay clear of downtown Jacksonville and the Riverfront today unless you're actually pinned a bib to your shirt.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Marine Corps Half Marathon:** This isn't just a 5K around a park. It's a significant event covering major downtown streets, the St. Johns Riverfront, and bridge crossings. If you're running, hydrate and stick to your race plan. If you're not, avoid the area entirely between sunrise and late morning.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Riverwalk Alternatives:** With Northbank Riverwalk detours still a thing, consider hitting up trails further south or east. Places like the Arboretum or even just sticking to your neighborhood roads are probably less headache-inducing right now. If you're set on a longer run, the Jax Beach neighborhoods offer decent, flat miles, but watch for that Beach Blvd closure.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Early Morning Fog (Mostly Cleared):** For those of you who get your miles in before the sun thinks about rising, patchy dense fog was reported across NE Florida through 7 AM. While it should be gone by now, always be mindful of visibility if you're an extreme early bird.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a beautiful day to be outside, but like any good run, you need to plan your route. Don't let a poorly chosen path or an unexpected road closure ruin a perfectly good run in this awesome weather.
