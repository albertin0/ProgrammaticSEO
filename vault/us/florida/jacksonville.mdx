### Trail Talk: The Emerald Trail (Still a Work in Progress)

Look, we all love the vision of the Emerald Trail. A 30-mile loop connecting everything? Amazing. But for today's workout, remember that "work in progress" means actual *work*. The Hogan Street Link segment, running from Riverfront Plaza up to Union Street, is definitely seeing active construction, with some portions anticipated to be complete by early 2027. You might hit detours or find yourself running alongside heavy equipment. The city is pushing to get these downtown segments done first, which is great for the long run, but a headache for your short one. Same goes for some portions around McCoys Creek. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and tries to hop a fence. It's not worth twisting an ankle or worse on rebar or fresh concrete.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beach Runners Beware:** Heading to Jacksonville Beach for a sandy workout? The rip current risk remains moderate today. Those easterly breezes might feel nice, but they're not always your friend in the water. Keep an eye on the flags.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
With the moderate pollen count, even though it's not sky-high, if you're prone to allergies, consider an antihistamine before you head out. Nobody wants a runny nose and itchy eyes adding to the fun of a humid run.
</AlertBox>

In short: Get out there, enjoy the clearer-than-foggy-morning skies, but be smart. Hydrate, watch for those afternoon storms, and remember that some of our urban trails are still literal construction zones.
