### Today's Rundown

Today's shaping up to be one of those "goldilocks" days where the temperature isn't actively trying to melt your face off, and you can actually breathe without tasting a cocktail of pollutants. The AQI is sitting pretty at 41, with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant, but honestly, that's practically pristine for us. Just don't expect to magically become immune to tree and grass pollen; it's still out there, doing its thing at moderate levels.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues!**
If your route takes you anywhere near the Sheridan Street Bridge over Florida's Turnpike, be prepared for potential pedestrian detours and general traffic nastiness. There's also a constant hum of development throughout Hollywood, including new apartment buildings and a massive beachfront high-rise planned for 1301 S. Ocean Drive. These won't directly block your run on the Hollywood Beach Broadwalk today, but expect increased vehicle traffic and localized disruptions around these projects.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the Hollywood Beach Broadwalk, while the general weather is playing nice, remember our coastal waters can be fickle. Earlier this week, we had a Tropical Storm Warning with associated wind gusts and beach erosion. Always be mindful of rip current risks if you decide to dip your toes in the water. Plus, that lovely sargassum seaweed is still making its rounds on South Florida shores.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration Isn't Just for Extreme Heat Anymore**
Even with less oppressive humidity, you're still in South Florida in June. Staying hydrated is always smart, especially during any strenuous outdoor activity. Your body will thank you, and your joints will feel less like rusty hinges.
</AlertBox>
