## Your Monday Morning Rundown

Looks like yesterday's strong winds have calmed down a bit, but that onshore breeze is going to be the main weather story for today, bringing with it those scattered rain chances. For those of you planning to hit up the trails around Lake Eola Park, the conditions should be pretty decent, especially if you get out there earlier. You'll enjoy the clear start to the day.

### Downtown Delays and Detours

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Ahead:** Don't expect smooth sailing if your route takes you through downtown. There's still plenty of long-term construction chewing up roads, especially along Summerlin Avenue. We're talking closures between Livingston Street and Ridgewood Street until tomorrow, and even longer stretches around Hillcrest Street to Colonial Drive until the end of April.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you usually loop around this area, plan an alternate route or just embrace the urban obstacle course. The City of Orlando's got a whole list of these ongoing projects, from the Camping World Stadium reimagining to various utility connections, that can throw a wrench in your flow.

### Coastal Considerations

<AlertBox type="info">
**Beach Day, Not Run Day:** While Orlando itself isn't *on* the coast, a lot of you drive out there for beach runs. Just a heads up, the strong winds from the weekend brought high surf and rip current advisories to coastal areas that were expected to persist. It might be a beautiful day, but maybe save the ocean swimming for later in the week.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today’s a solid day to get outside and move, as long as you're mindful of your allergies and the potential for a mid-workout shower. Stick to your usual spots, but if you're venturing downtown, pull up that road closure map first unless you enjoy sprinting through construction zones.
