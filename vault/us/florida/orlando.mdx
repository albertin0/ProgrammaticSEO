## The Gory Details for Your Orlando Workout

Alright, let's get real. The air quality at an AQI of 48 is fantastic, and the "Moderate" pollen count isn't going to send most of you into a full-blown allergy attack. So, your lungs might actually be okay if you ventured out. But your *entire body* is another story.

### Heat: It's Not a Dry Heat, It's Orlando Heat

We're talking Central Florida in June, people. The term "partly cloudy" sounds benign, but don't let it fool you. While it's 24°C (75°F) this morning, temperatures are climbing fast into the low 90s (around 32°C). But the real kicker? The heat index. That "feels like" temperature is predicted to hit a brutal 100 to 107 degrees Fahrenheit. That's not just uncomfortable; it's straight-up dangerous for sustained physical activity. Your body struggles to cool itself when it's that hot and humid, pushing your heart rate higher and making dehydration a fast track to trouble.

**DANGER: Extreme Heat Index!**
The forecasted heat index of 100-107°F is no joke. Hydration won't save you if you push it too hard. Listen to your body – better yet, listen to me: stay indoors for any high-intensity or long-duration workouts. Early morning might offer a tiny window, but even then, it'll still be muggy.
</AlertBox>

You do NOT want to be caught out on a long stretch of the Cady Way Trail when the sky decides to open up and shoot electrical bolts.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Afternoon Thunderstorms & Lightning!**
