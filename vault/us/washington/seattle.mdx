### The Full Rundown:

Today, May 19, 2026, we're looking at a cool 10.1°C (about 50°F) under an overcast sky. Perfect running weather if you ask me, assuming you don't mind a little dampness and know how to dress. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a decent 52, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "moderate," so most of you can breathe easy, but if you're extra sensitive, maybe stick to that treadmill in your garage.

The real kick in the pants today is the local intel. Forget that easy cruisin' on the **East Lake Sammamish Trail** if you planned on going end-to-end. Word from the county is a section between Louis Thompson Road NE and NE Inglewood Hill Road is officially closed as of May for culvert replacement, and this isn't a "squeeze-by" situation – no detour, folks. If you're out that way, plan to turn around or find an entirely different route. It's a bummer, but necessary work to restore salmon habitat, they say.

Beyond the trails, if your workout involves navigating the city, be aware that Seattle's perpetual construction symphony continues. We still have those charming two northbound lanes closed on I-5 over the Ship Canal Bridge, which means getting north could be a crawl. And if you bike or run along the industrial stretches, SDOT is doing work on E Marginal Way S today, so expect some flaggers and minor traffic revisions. It's not a complete shutdown, but it's enough to throw off your rhythm.

On the bright side, no major wind advisories for Seattle proper, though boaters near the Strait of Juan de Fuca should still be wary of gusty winds up to 30 mph until 5 AM today. So, if your workout involves a kayak, maybe you already know better.
