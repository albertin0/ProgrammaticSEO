Alright, Seattle, if you're thinking of hitting your usual Sunday long run or bike ride, listen up. The weather gods are being *mostly* kind today with those temps and the AQI sitting pretty in the "Moderate" zone. We've got a reported AQI of 59 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, which, let's be real, is a breath of fresh air compared to what we were choking on back in August during the wildfire smoke season. And for those of you with seasonal sniffles, ragweed pollen is at a "Moderate" level, so a quick rinse of the sinuses post-workout might be in order, but it's not a complete red light.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Your Six: Early Darkness & Morning Fog
Daylight Saving Time officially ended today, November 1st. That means the sun is packing it in before 5 PM for the rest of the year. If you're out for an afternoon jog or bike, assume you'll be finishing in the dark. This isn't just a mood killer; it's a safety hazard. Studies have shown a noticeable bump in deadly pedestrian and cyclist crashes when the clocks change. Grab your reflective gear, charge those headlamps, and assume no one can see you. Oh, and Downtown Seattle was under a Dense Fog Advisory until 10 AM, so even if it's cleared now, residual moisture and low visibility could still be a factor, especially near the water or in shaded areas.
</AlertBox>

Now for the real kick in the teeth for anyone trying to get around: traffic.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### I-5 Ship Canal Bridge Shutdown – Expect Chaos
If your workout involves getting across town via I-5, especially northbound, you're in for a world of hurt. WSDOT's "Revive I-5" project has a *weekend-long full closure* of northbound I-5 from I-90 to Northeast 45th Street, running until 5 AM Monday. The express lanes are northbound-only through the weekend to try and mitigate the damage, but let's be honest, it's going to be a mess. This isn't just an inconvenience; it can add significant stress and time to your travel, potentially cutting into your workout or making you rush. Consider routes around the **Ship Canal Bridge** or opt for a workout spot closer to home. Metro also has various bus stop closures and reroutes due to construction, so check your transit plans if that's your jam.
</AlertBox>

Despite the minor environmental annoyances and the major traffic headache, if you can navigate the early darkness and the I-5 blockade, today offers some decent conditions for an outdoor sweat session. Just be smart, be visible, and maybe bring a thermos of something warm for after.
