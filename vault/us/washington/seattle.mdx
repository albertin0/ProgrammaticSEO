<AlertBox type="danger">
### Mount Si Trails CLOSED Due to Bear Activity
Seriously, folks. Two teens were injured earlier this week in a bear encounter on Mount Si. The Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife has closed *all* Mount Si trails. Don't be that person who ignores the signs. Your Strava segment isn't worth a trip to Harborview. Find an alternate route or stick to urban paths today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Pollen is Moderate – Your Nose Might Know
While the air quality is otherwise good, pollen is hanging out at a moderate level. If you're usually sniffling and sneezing this time of year, grab your meds before you head out. Nobody wants a snot rocket mid-stride down Alki.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Juneteenth Park Facility Closures
Since today is Juneteenth, a number of Seattle Parks and Recreation facilities and programs are closed. Most trails remain open, but double-check if you're planning on using a specific community center, pool, or even some public restrooms. Plan accordingly.
