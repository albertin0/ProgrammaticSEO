## Today's Vitals

The air quality, sitting at a "moderate" 55 AQI, might seem okay at a glance, but with ozone as the dominant pollutant, especially during those morning peaks, your lungs are still doing extra work. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and any existing respiratory issues might flare up. Overcast skies and a pleasant 16°C (that's 60.8°F for you Celsius-challenged folks) mean ideal running temps, but it's a calm before the storm.

### The Forecast: Don't Get Complacent

While we're not dealing with a full-blown smoke-ocalypse today, the air's still got that subtle bite thanks to ongoing wildfire season shenanigans across the state. And remember, those afternoon thunderstorms aren't just a nuisance; they carry a risk of localized strong winds and, critically, new fire starts in the Cascades. So, what starts as a mild run could turn into a scramble for shelter, or worse, contribute to later smoke issues.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail & Road Closures:** Beyond the West Seattle Bridge bike path fiasco, expect major highway construction impacts if you're driving to a trailhead. WSDOT has weekend closures on SR 99, I-5 (Ship Canal Bridge still a mess), and SR 520. So, even if your trail is open, getting there might test your patience.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Afternoon Thunderstorms:** The big one for today. Isolated storms are expected this afternoon, moving into the metro area late afternoon/early evening with a chance of strong, gusty winds. Don't be "that guy" trying to PR during a lightning strike. If you see dark clouds rolling in, cut your run short. Your PR isn't worth becoming a lightning rod.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration and Ozone:** Even with overcast skies, stay hydrated. Ozone can exacerbate respiratory symptoms, and adequate hydration helps your body cope. If you're a sensitive individual, consider an indoor workout this afternoon or stick to early morning when ozone levels are typically lower.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. You want to get out there. But today demands a smart approach. Re-route, reschedule, or relocate your workout. Or, you know, do some burpees in your living room – it builds character, and you won't get hit by a rogue gust of wind on the Burke-Gilman.
