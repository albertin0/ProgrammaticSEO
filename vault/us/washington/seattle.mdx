## The Rundown

Look, I know you checked your app and it's probably saying the AQI is "Good" right now, sitting pretty at 26. And yeah, that's technically true for this exact moment in Seattle proper. But don't get too comfortable. The forecast from the pros at NWS Seattle and the Puget Sound smoke blog isn't quite as rosy. They're telling us that wildfire smoke, which has been swirling overhead, is expected to start hitting surface levels in the Puget Sound area *through today and into tomorrow*, potentially bringing us into "Moderate" or even "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" territory. So, that crisp morning air? Enjoy it while it lasts, and maybe hold off on that lung-busting interval session later this afternoon if things start to get hazy.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Smoke Alert:** While AQI is currently good, smoke impacts are *forecasted to increase* today and tomorrow for the Puget Sound region, potentially reaching "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Sensitive individuals should monitor conditions closely and be ready to move workouts indoors.
</AlertBox>

Temperature-wise, we're looking at a cool 13.8°C (around 57°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies. Perfect running weather, honestly, if it weren't for the smoke lurking. Pollen is moderate, which is par for the course in August, so your usual allergy meds should cover it. No extreme heat warnings or anything like that in the city today.

Now, for those of you with bigger ambitions than just pounding pavement around Green Lake, listen up. Our beloved trails east of the Cascades are still feeling the burn. A whopping 50-mile stretch of the **Pacific Crest Trail** between Snoqualmie Pass and Deception Pass is currently shut down because of the Three Queens Fire, among others. And that's just one example. Numerous other trails and recreation areas in the Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest and surrounding regions have closures due to active wildfires. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and puts themselves (and rescuers) at risk. Check the National Forest Service and PCTA websites before you head out. Seriously.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Trail Closures Due to Wildfires:** Multiple trails, including significant portions of the Pacific Crest Trail near Snoqualmie Pass, are closed due to active wildfires. Check official forest service websites before planning any trail runs outside the immediate city. Disregarding closures is dangerous and can lead to fines.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if you're sticking to the concrete jungle, remember that Seattle is always under construction. The "Revive I-5" project is still chugging along, meaning the **Ship Canal Bridge** remains reduced to two lanes northbound until the end of the year. While this is more of a traffic nightmare, it can certainly impact your route if you run or bike near those major arteries.

<AlertBox type="info">
**City Construction:** Ongoing I-5 construction near the Ship Canal Bridge continues to cause lane reductions. Be aware of increased traffic and potential pedestrian/bike detours if your route takes you near these areas.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the run, but keep your head on a swivel.
