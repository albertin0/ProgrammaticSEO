## Today's Workout Outlook: A Cold But Clear Gauntlet

Alright, folks, it's 2.4°C out there this morning with "Mainly Clear" skies. That means you won't be battling rain, but you *will* be battling the urge to stay under the covers. Layer up, people. Think base layer, mid-layer, maybe even a windbreaker if you're hitting the open stretches. Your lungs will thank you for the crisp, clean air, though the "Moderate" pollen count means allergy sufferers should consider a pre-run dose of antihistamines. Don't be that person sniffling their way around Green Lake.

### Trail Updates and Urban Obstacles

Now, let's talk about the real joy of running in this city: the ongoing construction. It seems like every other week a beloved route gets rerouted, and today is no different. If you're usually cruising along the **Burke-Gilman Trail**, be aware that sections are undergoing routine repairs starting *today*, March 30th, and will continue into June. These are fixes for age-related paving issues and root uplift, which is good in the long run, but a pain right now. Keep an eye out for signage and be prepared for potential detours or narrower passages.

And if you’re anywhere near the University District or Eastlake, you’re in for more fun. The RapidRide J Line construction is still at it. Southbound Fairview Ave N between Yale Ave N and Aloha St is closed for another few weeks, with traffic detoured to Eastlake Ave E. This means sidewalks are impacted too, so expect to share a multi-use path on the west side of Fairview. Don't just blindly follow your usual path; look up, pay attention, and assume no clear sailing.

For those up north, starting today, Sound Transit is closing the right-turn lane and sidewalk at the corner of eastbound SR-522 (Bothell Way) and 61st Ave NE in Kenmore. So, if your run takes you that way, adjust accordingly or risk a very un-Zen moment with a bus.
