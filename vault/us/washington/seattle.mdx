### Trail Closures: The Eternal Struggle

If you're a creature of habit and hit the **East Lake Sammamish Trail** for your long rides or runs, prepare to be rerouted or just plain stopped. So, if you hit it, you're turning around. Plan your loops accordingly, or just stick to the Burke-Gilman if you want an uninterrupted ride. The adjacent Parkway is still open, but expect lane restrictions and a bit of a dance with traffic. Typical.
