## The Rundown: Don't Be a Fair-Weather Flop

Look, I get it. The sun *might* peek out later, and those mild morning temps are deceiving. But today's the calm before the storm—literally, with that heat wave barreling down on us.

**Air Quality - A Smoky Forecast:**
Your lungs might be singing a happy tune with today's low AQI of 44, but that's like celebrating before the race is over. Wildfires are still burning hard out east and north, and the winds are looking shifty. Meteorologists are predicting smoke pushing into Western Washington, including Seattle, *today* and through mid-week. If you're planning an evening long run, especially around the Lake Washington loop, keep an eye on those air quality apps. Ozone is the dominant pollutant listed, but that wildfire particulate matter is the real lung-burner. Trust me, I've coughed my way through enough smoky summers to know.

**Roads & Trails - Mind the Mayhem:**
Speaking of the Lake Washington loop, if your route takes you anywhere near Montlake Boulevard or the SR 520 interchange, breathe a sigh of relief that the full weekend closure just lifted this morning. But don't get complacent. Construction is back on Seattle's streets and sidewalks post-World Cup, so watch for new detours and reduced capacity on major routes. For those of you south of the city, 91st Avenue SW is completely shut down between SW Dilworth Road and SW 167th Street today for drainage work, including for bikes and pedestrians. Plan alternate routes now, or you'll be doing an unplanned hill workout just trying to get around.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Upcoming Heat Risk:** While it's overcast and cool now (14.1°C), a Heat Advisory goes into effect tomorrow (Tuesday) for much of the Puget Sound region, with temps expected to reach 85-90 degrees. Today's forecast for Seattle still hits the mid-upper 80s. Hydrate aggressively and consider moving your workouts to early morning or late evening to avoid the worst of the heat.
</AlertBox>
