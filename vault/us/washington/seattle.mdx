Honestly, if you *must* get your miles in outside today, keep your head on a swivel, Seattle. It's a day that screams "stay inside and watch old Mariners games," but I get it, the addiction is real.

Look, the calendar says September, but Mother Nature decided to fast-forward to October this week. We're officially in "fall-like storm" territory. Yesterday (Wednesday) brought the initial blast, and today, Thursday, September 3rd, is a continuation of that. KIRO 7 has even dubbed these "Pinpoint Alert Days," which, for us runners, basically means "don't be an idiot."

We're talking widespread rain with moderate to heavy downpours, especially this afternoon, and winds gusting between 20-35 mph across the lowlands, potentially hitting 40 mph near the coast. Why's this a big deal? Because our trees are still in full summer glory, meaning those gusts are fantastic at pruning weakened branches right onto your head or path. So, if you're out there, actively scan the ground *and* the canopy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Falling Branches & Debris:** High winds combined with full foliage create a real risk of falling branches and other airborne debris. Be hyper-aware, especially near tree-lined routes like the Green Lake loop or any of our urban park trails. Don't run with headphones cranked; you need to hear what's coming down.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, your lungs will thank you. While moderate pollen is a thing, the Air Quality Index is a pristine 27, meaning no smoke from those far-off wildfires is currently bothering us. Thank goodness, because August was a bit of a hazy mess at times. So, if you manage to dodge the falling branches and stay upright on the wet pavement, at least you'll be breathing some good, clean (if damp) air.

For those considering a long run, rethink those technical trails. Anything with exposed roots or loose gravel is going to be extra treacherous with the rain. Stick to well-maintained, wider paved paths if you absolutely must. The Burke-Gilman Trail, for example, is usually a safe bet, but even there, watch for puddles and debris.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Burke-Gilman Trail Construction Heads-Up:** Speaking of the Burke-Gilman, if you regularly hit the section near Sand Point (between NE 65th and NE 77th St), be aware that construction is slated to begin on September 8th and last about three weeks. They'll be repairing damaged sections, so expect temporary gravel ramps and be prepared to dismount your bike and walk through work zones. Not today's problem, but it's coming.
</AlertBox>

Temperature-wise, it's hovering around 14°C (57°F), so dress in layers – a waterproof shell is non-negotiable today. Don't underestimate how quickly that damp chill can get to you once you stop moving. Hydrate, too, even if it doesn't feel hot. Your body is still working hard.

All in all, today's workout forecast is less "runner's paradise" and more "survival challenge." Be smart, be safe, and maybe just hit the elliptical.
