<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Ignore the Air Quality
An AQI of 98, leaning into the "Moderate" category, especially with PM2.5 as the main culprit, is no joke for your lungs. Add "Moderate" pollen on top, and you're actively irritating your respiratory system with every deep breath. If you've got asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, seriously consider an indoor workout today. The Oregon Health Authority recently updated its guidance, basically telling everyone, even healthy adults, to be mindful when air quality is in this range. For kids, they're even stricter. Don't push it just because the sun's out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures are Real Hazards
Portland Parks & Recreation isn't messing around with these closures. Downed trees and landslides, like the one closing a section of the Towhee Trail in Marquam Nature Park, mean unstable ground. Don't try to "just get around" a closure sign. That's how you end up twisted up and calling me for an emergency PT session. Stick to open, maintained trails or hit the pavement.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Even When It's Mild
While today isn't hitting the "extreme heat warning" levels that some parts of the *greater Portland metro area* are seeing this week, the Oregon Health Authority is still pushing for heat preparedness. Hydration is always key, even on cooler days, and with the air quality issues, your body is already working a bit harder. Drink up, before, during, and after.
</AlertBox>
