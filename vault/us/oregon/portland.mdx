## Air Quality & Allergens

The good news is, our air quality is firmly in the "Good" category today, clocking in at an AQI of 50. No wildfire smoke choking us out from Central Oregon or the Cascades, which is a blessing after those August scares. Your lungs can actually enjoy breathing today, a rare treat in recent years. However, if you typically head east, keep an eye out: there's a prescribed burn happening in the North Bank Habitat Management Area (way out near Glide, definitely not here, but still Oregon). Smoke *could* be visible from those communities and might settle in low areas overnight. Not a Portland issue directly, but good to know for regional travel.

Pollen, though, is still hanging around at a "Moderate" level. If you're one of the unfortunate souls who feels every pollen grain personally, you know the drill. It’s not going to ruin your run, but it might make your post-workout shower an exercise in snot removal.

## Local Hazards & Trail Talk

This city, bless its heart, is perpetually under construction. If you're planning on driving anywhere near the freeways, you're going to hit snags. The I-5 southbound full closure between Exit 302A (Broadway) and the I-84 westbound on-ramp, for instance, is still ongoing for another few weeks. That means if you’re trying to get to some of our southern or eastern running spots, factor in extra time and perhaps a few more deep breaths of *frustration*, if not bad air.

More directly impacting your workout choices: **Oxbow Regional Park** is completely shut down for construction until January. Yep, that means no hiking, no river beaches, no boat launches. If you had plans to escape to the Sandy River for a long trail run, you'll need to find an alternate route. That's a significant chunk of our natural playground out of commission.

Also, be aware of ongoing road work. NE Killingsworth Street is seeing pavement repair this week, with lane closures, so expect some minor traffic delays if you're running or cycling there. And for those who love the **Back Cove** loop, keep an eye out for granite stair upgrades starting up "late September to early October." That means sections could be impacted soon.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Oxbow Regional Park Closure:** This is a full closure. Do NOT plan on accessing trails or facilities at Oxbow Regional Park; it's closed through January for water system construction. Find an alternative route or park.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today is a good day to get moving outside, provided you’re mindful of the moderate pollen and plan your route around the known construction and closures. Don't be a hero; if your favorite spot is impacted, find another one. There are plenty of good miles to be had out there.
