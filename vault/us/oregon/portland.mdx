Alright, Portland. Today's your lucky day for getting outside, mostly. Don't expect bluebird skies, but the cooler, overcast weather is a gift in July. Just make sure you know your route, because the city's doing its usual best to close down half your favorite spots.

<BulletList>
*   **AQI is surprisingly decent:** We're sitting pretty at 20, which is "good." Enjoy it before the summer fires truly kick in and turn our skies into a sepia filter. Even with some high-altitude haze rolling in from southern Oregon, your lungs aren't going to get sandblasted today.
*   **Pollen's still lurking:** "Moderate" isn't a death sentence, but if you're one of the sniffly, watery-eyed crowd, consider your antihistamines. Grass pollen is still doing its thing.
*   **It's a construction and festival maze out there:** Between the Waterfront Blues Festival, multiple bridge closures *tomorrow* (but still causing setup impacts and crowds today), and half a dozen trail projects, you'll be dodging more than just slow walkers.

## Hazards & Head-Ups

<AlertBox type="info">
**Smoke Check:** While our ground-level AQI is good, don't be surprised by some haziness overhead. Wildfire smoke from southern Oregon and Northern California is mostly staying high, but it's enough to give the sky that distinct "Oregon summer is coming" glow.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Waterfront Wonderland (or Nightmare):** Tom McCall Waterfront Park is the epicenter of the Waterfront Blues Festival, running through July 4th. Expect crowds, especially later in the day. If you're planning a casual run or ride through, be prepared to navigate a human obstacle course. Also, a local bike group, "The Rocky Ride," is meeting at Salmon Street Springs tonight at 7 PM, so that area will be extra congested.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Trail Closures Galore:** Our city trails are currently a minefield of "nope."
*   **Marquam Nature Park:** The Towhee Trail is closed due to a landslide. Don't even think about trying to navigate unstable slopes.
*   **River View Natural Area:** Parts of Trail 3 and Trail 6 are closed due to catastrophic failures and unstable drainage. Seriously, stay off these.
*   **Forest Park:** The Ridge Trail parking area on NW Bridge Avenue is closed indefinitely for safety reasons. Access the trail on foot, bike, or bus from elsewhere.
*   **Cazadero and Springwater Trails:** PGE construction projects mean intermittent and even full closures (like the Cazadero Trail section between Deep Creek and Highway 212 until mid-August). Keep an eye out for signage and be ready to reroute.
*   **Rocky Butte Road:** Slope erosion repair has this road closed for the summer. Access the park area from the north, at NE Fremont and NE 91st Avenue.
*   **Union Branch Multi-Use Pathway:** This is still an active construction site, not officially opening until September. Do us all a favor and stay off it for now.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Road Work on 82nd Ave:** If you're a cyclist hitting NE/SE 82nd Avenue today, be aware of ongoing paving. You'll be detoured onto NE Siskiyou Street. Expect delays and pay attention to flaggers.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Burn Ban in Effect:** Portland Fire & Rescue put a summer burn ban in place back on June 15th. No recreational campfires, fire pits, or yard debris burning. Keep Portland green, folks.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a perfectly runnable day if you pick your poison. Just be smart about where you're going and don't try to power through a trail that's actively trying to fall into a ravine.
