### Today's Eugene Workout Gauntlet

Look, the sun's out, the air's clean, but don't let that fool you into thinking it's a smooth ride. Our city's in a perpetual state of "improvement," which usually means "detours and frustration" for us runners and cyclists.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Pre's Trail Bridge Closure is Still a Thing!** The Canoe Canal bridge on Pre's Trail, that crucial link in Alton Baker Park, is shut down until April for resurfacing. This means if your route usually takes you through Alton Baker and over that bridge, you're looking at significant rerouting. They've got detours marked near the Emerald Valley BMX track if you're trying to get to the south side, and bike access to Day Island Road is also affected. Plan for extra mileage or just pick a whole new park. Seriously, don't just show up expecting it to be open.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Everywhere Else.** It's not just Pre's Trail. If you're heading towards the south side, the **Ridgeline Trail System** is undergoing expansion, with sections of the South Eugene Meadows Loop Trail and connector to Spencer Butte Park still under construction. They expect to reopen by mid-summer, but for now, you might hit more than a few "Trail Closed" signs. Same goes for **Suzanne Arlie Park**, where new trails and mountain bike paths are still being built, with a grand opening not expected until late 2026.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Road Work Roundup:** The City of Eugene's advisories, last updated March 5th, list a bunch of ongoing road projects. Expect potential sidewalk closures and bike lane merges into traffic on streets like Agate and Moss. There's also limited concrete and electrical work on Empire Park Drive this week. Riverfront Parkway and Millrace Drive have pedestrian and bike closures too. Bottom line: if you're hitting the streets, keep your head on a swivel. Assume drivers aren't looking out for you.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, enjoy the mild weather, but be smarter than the construction cones. Check those detour signs, give yourself extra time, and maybe just hit the treadmill if battling traffic and closed trails sounds like too much work before your *actual* workout.
