## Today's Denton Run-Down: Don't Be That Guy

Okay, so let's be real. Today's not going to be a record-breaker for "most dangerous day to run in Denton," but that doesn't mean you get a pass on common sense.

### Weather Whims and Wet Footing

The early morning chill is no joke. I've seen too many people start out in a tank top because the forecast said "70s" and then spend the first two miles regretting every life choice. Layer up, peel off. Simple.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Morning Chill & Wet Surfaces
It's a cool 7.2°C (45°F) this morning. While it's warming up later, those shaded areas, especially near water or on less-trafficked paths, might still have damp or even slick spots from yesterday's rain. Be extra cautious on bridges or any paved sections that haven't fully dried.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of wet, the "all trails closed due to wet conditions" advisory was for Texas State Parks as of March 7th. While that's not a blanket closure for every single path in Denton, it's a pretty strong hint that any unpaved trails, like some sections along the **Greenbelt Corridor**, are probably still pretty muddy. You'll be tracking that mess everywhere, and frankly, torn ligaments from a slip aren't worth the Instagram photo. Stick to the paved paths if you're not into ankle-deep mud or a twisted knee. Your PT thanks you.

### Pollen Protocol
If you're prone to seasonal allergies, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out. Consider wearing a brimmed cap or sunglasses to keep pollen out of your eyes, especially if you're hitting any more open, grassy areas. And for Pete's sake, don't rub your eyes with your sweaty hands. You'll regret it.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Zone Chaos

Denton County is a perpetual construction zone, and today is no different. Road projects are ongoing, especially around the I-35W interchange and FM 407. If your route takes you near these areas, expect temporary lane closures and shifting traffic patterns. This isn't just an annoyance for drivers; it makes things unpredictable for runners and cyclists who use the shoulders or nearby sidewalks.

Major road construction is impacting areas like the FM 407 and I-35W interchange. If you're running or cycling near these zones, assume drivers are distracted and confused. Wear bright colors, stay alert, and don't assume anyone sees you. Seriously, it's not worth arguing with a two-ton pickup.
