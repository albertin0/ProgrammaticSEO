### Don't Be a Heat Casualty, Austin!
While the morning air is clear and relatively cool, don't get complacent. The National Weather Service is forecasting daily highs in the mid-to-upper 90s, with heat indices frequently climbing between 100°F and 106°F for the beginning of July. AccuWeather agrees, projecting 96° to 100° daily highs this month. This isn't just "uncomfortable," it's "seriously dangerous if you're not careful." Get your workout in early, before the sun decides to personally bake you, and hydrate like your life depends on it – because it kind of does. Even without an official "Advisory," assume you're in one.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Dodge the Detours

If you're heading out to hit the pavement, be ready for some navigational challenges. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, bless its heart, is currently a patchwork of closures and reroutes.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Butler Trail & Shoal Creek Shenanigans
Austin Energy is busy installing underground electrical infrastructure on the north shore of Lady Bird Lake, near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club. This means the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is rerouted in that section, a project expected to run through March 2027. Pay attention to on-site staff; they're probably just as tired of explaining the detours as you are of taking them.

Also, access to the Butler Trail at Willow Street is closed for a whopping 64 months due to the 98 Red River project. And if you typically use the Shoal Creek Trail, be aware that sections, including between Shoal Creek Blvd. and the Janet Fish Bridge, have been undergoing maintenance until late June, with a detour along N. Lamar Blvd. The 5th Street Bridge over Shoal Creek has also been closed for fire repairs, estimated until mid-June. Basically, assume your usual route has a surprise in store.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Wishbone Bridge & I-35 Impacts
Construction for the new Wishbone Bridge at Longhorn Dam officially kicked off yesterday, July 1st. You'll see fencing around Canterbury Park, Longhorn Shores (which is now closed), and Krieg Fields. And if you're trying to cross Lady Bird Lake on the east side of I-35, the pedestrian crossing has been closed since February for bridge construction as part of the I-35 Capital Express Central Project, expected to last about a year. Your best bet for crossing down that way might be the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which opened back in February.
</AlertBox>

Be smart, Austin. Today demands you pay attention to more than just your pace.
