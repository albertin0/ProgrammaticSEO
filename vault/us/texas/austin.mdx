Today's a mixed bag for hitting the Austin trails; grab your water and prepare for some detours.

<BulletList>
*   It's officially the hottest March on record, folks, and today's heat is no joke. Expect temperatures near 83°F (28.4°C) with forecasts showing 80s to 90s for the upcoming week. This isn't your grandma's spring weather.
*   Moderate pollen levels mean your sinuses might feel it, but our air quality is currently "Good" with an AQI of 45. At least your lungs are mostly in the clear from anything but the humidity.
*   While recent updates mean the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail's 10-mile loop is complete with the Wishbone Bridge open and Butler Shores renovations done, the I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still causing major headaches and detours around Lady Bird Lake.

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Heat Advisory: Don't Be a Hero, Austin
It's going to be HOT out there. Seriously. We're on track for the warmest March in Austin's history. Hydration isn't optional, it's essential. Plan your runs or rides for early morning or later in the evening to avoid the worst of the midday sun. This isn't just about comfort; heat exhaustion and stroke are real risks, especially when pushing yourself.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Wildfire Danger is High
Travis County still has an outdoor burn ban in effect. With the hot, dry, and breezy conditions, the Texas A&M Forest Service has warned of "very high" to "extreme" fire danger across Central Texas. Be incredibly careful if you're out on trails near dry brush. One spark is all it takes. Seriously, leave the outdoor grilling for another day, even if it's not directly on a trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
## Lady Bird Lake Trail Detours & What You Need to Know
While The Trail Conservancy has brought us the fantastic news that the full 10-mile loop around Lady Bird Lake is now officially open thanks to the Wishbone Bridge and completed Butler Shores work, don't get too comfortable. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project means significant, ongoing construction.
</AlertBox>

Specifically, the east-side I-35 pedestrian bridge has been closed since early February and is expected to stay that way for about a year. You'll need to use the west-side I-35 sidewalk, or plan to cross the lake at South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge near Pleasant Valley.

Additionally, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029. You'll need to find alternative access points for the Boardwalk. Also, East Avenue under the I-35 bridge is closed for utility work through early 2026, and Willow Street trail access is closed for a whopping 64 months due to nearby construction. These aren't minor reroutes, folks; check your maps before you head out, especially if you have a specific route planned.
