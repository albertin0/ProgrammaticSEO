## Today's Workout Reality: Sweat, Sniffles, and Sidesteps

Let's be real, Austin. It's July, and the heat wants to claim your soul. The clear skies and current comfortable temperature of 26.2°C are a momentary reprieve before the full force of the Texas summer kicks in. This afternoon, you're looking at actual temperatures climbing to the high 90s, with a heat index making it feel like 101°F. That's not "push through it" weather; that's "rethink your life choices" weather.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat & Heat Index
With forecast highs near 98-102°F and a heat index up to 101°F, the risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke is dangerously high. If you insist on being outside, plan for very early mornings or late evenings, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate aggressively. And no, that isn't permission to do an hour-long tempo run at noon.
</AlertBox>

Your lungs might be catching a break from super high AQI (it's a clean 23 today, with ozone as the dominant pollutant), but don't get too comfy. The moderate pollen count means some of you will still be battling sniffles and watery eyes, adding to the general misery of exercising in a sauna.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Moderate Pollen
Cedar fever is mostly a winter thing, but other allergens are still floating around. If you're susceptible, take your meds *before* you head out, or you'll be regretting it halfway down the trail.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: The Butler Trail Hustle

If your plan was a peaceful run or ride around Lady Bird Lake, be aware that tranquility is currently under construction. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is still rerouted on the north shore near the Confluence and the Austin Rowing Club building. This isn't a quick fix; it's a long-term project for electrical infrastructure, running from June 2026 through March 2027.

And if you usually cross I-35 on foot near the lake, you're out of luck. The east-side pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year as they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge as part of the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. You'll need to use the west-side sidewalk or find alternative crossings like South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley (which, thankfully, opened in February).

<AlertBox type="info">
### Heads Up: Trail Detours and Closures
Expect construction activities and reroutes along the north shore of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail. The I-35 pedestrian bridge on the east side is closed. Slow down, pay attention to signage, and be ready to share the road in some areas. The Zilker Eagle mini train is also out of commission for mechanical repairs, so adjust your family outing plans if that was on the agenda.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, today's probably a good day to hit an air-conditioned gym, take a swim at Barton Springs if you can brave the crowds, or just enjoy a frosty beverage on your porch. Your body will thank you.
