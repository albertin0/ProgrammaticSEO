### The Good News: Seriously Pleasant Weather

Look, you don't need me to tell you that 22.9°C and clear skies in late September are pretty damn perfect for a run or ride. We've often suffered through far worse by this point in the year. The humidity is tolerable, there's no immediate threat of flash floods for the weekend, and wildfire smoke isn't an issue around here today, which is a relief given Austin's general wildfire risk. The forecast for Central Texas mentions cold fronts and higher rain chances for *next week*, not today, so enjoy this respite.

### The Bad News: Austin's Permanent Obstacle Course

Now, for the dose of reality. You live in Austin, and that means you're basically training for an urban adventure race every time you step out the door.

### Local Hazards: ACL and the Never-Ending Construction

The real kick in the shorts today comes from the ACL Festival setup. The Austin City Limits Festival isn't until next weekend, but the entire Zilker Park area transforms weeks in advance. While the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and Barton Creek Greenbelt are staying open, *access* is another story. Expect significant parking lot and street closures around Zilker, especially near Barton Springs Road and Stratford Drive, making your usual approach a frustrating detour.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Zilker Park & ACL Festival Prep:** Avoid driving directly to Zilker Park if you can. Parking is a nightmare, and road closures are already in effect for setup. Consider alternative access points or walking/biking to the park if you absolutely must run there.
</AlertBox>

And because Austin can't ever have nice things without tearing something else up, expect ongoing construction disruptions across the city. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is a multi-year beast, and parts of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail are rerouted on the north shore near the Austin Rowing Club due to electrical work, which will continue through March 2027. Plus, the east-side I-35 pedestrian bridge over Lady Bird Lake has been closed since February for new bridge construction. You'll need to cross at South Congress or use the Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley if you're trying to get from north to south shores around that area.

Don't even get me started on Shoal Creek Trail, where parts are still closed for repairs between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge, with other sections near 26th-27th Street still impacted by old landslides. This isn't breaking news, but it's a constant reminder to stay alert for unexpected detours on your route.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Detour Ahead:** If your route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail near the Austin Rowing Club or any part of Shoal Creek Trail, plan for detours. These aren't temporary; they're long-term projects.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, Austin. The weather's great, just be prepared to navigate a city that's constantly under construction and hosting events.
