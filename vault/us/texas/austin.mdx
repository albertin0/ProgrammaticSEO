## Trail Torture & Event Havoc

This is where things get interesting, and by "interesting," I mean "annoying." If your plan involves a long run around Lady Bird Lake, you'd better have a map and a whole lot of patience. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is still an absolute mess in places.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Lady Bird Lake Trail: Prepare for the Labyrinth.**
The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still chewing up the trail. The east-side pedestrian crossing at I-35 has been shut down since February 2026, and it's not expected to reopen until sometime between *2027 and 2029*. Don't even think about trying to navigate the old paths there. Furthermore, a section of the south-shore boardwalk under I-35 had a week-long closure recently, and while that specific closure should be over, access along that part of the trail remains *limited through early 2027*. Seriously, plan your loops accordingly. If you're coming from the north shore near the Austin Rowing Club, you're looking at a reroute until March 2027 due to Austin Energy installing underground lines. Just assume anything near I-35 or Waller Beach is a gamble.
</AlertBox>

Want to escape the downtown chaos? Heading east to the Boggy Creek Trail for an evening workout? Nope. Not tonight.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Boggy Creek Trail: Trick or Treat Takeover!**
Tonight, Friday, October 30th, from 6 PM to 8 PM, the Boggy Creek Trailhead is hosting "Trick or Treat on The Trail." It's a family-friendly, car-free event, which is great for the kids, but terrible for your pace. Expect crowds, spooky decorations, and sugar-fueled mayhem. If you're looking for a serious workout, find another spot for those hours or risk tripping over a tiny zombie.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the trails, construction is still a pervasive Austin soundtrack. Major road projects like parts of Loop 360 continue through late 2026, and even Congress Avenue has ongoing work. While these might not directly block pedestrian paths, expect traffic headaches getting to and from your workout spots.

So, while the weather is giving us a rare gift, the city itself is doing its best to make outdoor exercise a strategic operation. Choose your path wisely, plan for detours, and maybe carry some spare candy if you end up on Boggy Creek.
