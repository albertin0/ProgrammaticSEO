### Hydration is Non-Negotiable

Seriously, I see you out there. You think you're tough. You think you can power through. But heat stroke isn't cool, and neither is passing out face-first on the trail near the Gus Fruh entrance because you thought one water bottle was enough for a 10-mile run. Austin and Travis County have even issued a disaster declaration due to increased wildfire risk, and while that 80-100 acre grass fire in Llano County is mostly contained, it's a stark reminder that things can get spicy out there, fast.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Warning for the Week Ahead
  Temperatures are going to skyrocket. We're talking 90s and 100s, with a heat index that could make it feel like 107°F. This isn't just "sweaty"—it's "dangerous." Plan your runs for dawn or dusk, or better yet, take it inside if you're not properly acclimated. Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke, and don't be afraid to cut your workout short. Cooling centers are available at Austin Public Library and Austin Parks facilities during normal operating hours.
