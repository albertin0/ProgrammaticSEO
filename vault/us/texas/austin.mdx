### Extreme Heat Warning: Heat Index Up To 111°F!
This isn't a drill. The heat index today is forecast to hit as high as 111°F. This level of heat poses a significant risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Limit outdoor activity to the absolute minimum, especially during peak daylight hours. Hydrate aggressively if you must be outside, and keep an eye on vulnerable populations. Your long run can wait.
</AlertBox>
