## Why today is decidedly *not* the day (for most of us)

<BulletList>
*   **Killer Heat:** We're looking at the hottest day of the summer, predicted to hit a blistering 106°F, with a "feels like" temperature soaring up to 109°F. The National Weather Service has slapped a Heat Advisory on us from noon to 8 PM, which means staying hydrated is a full-time job, and strenuous outdoor activity is a bad idea. Your internal cooling system can't keep up when it's this hot and humid.
*   **Moderate Air & Pollen Mix:** The AQI is sitting at 59, which is "Moderate" but still means there's enough PM2.5 floating around to irritate your lungs, especially when you're sucking wind on a run. Combine that with moderate pollen, and you've got a recipe for unnecessary respiratory stress.
*   **Construction Chaos:** Austin is always under construction, and today is no different. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is a mess in spots – narrowed near the Austin Rowing Club and The Confluence for emergency repairs, and the lattice bridge is straight-up closed. Plus, that long-term closure at Willow Street access for the 98 Red River project continues. You'll be dodging cones and reroutes if you're not careful, which is the last thing you need when it's this hot.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, y'all, this isn't a joke. Peak afternoon temperatures are forecast to hit 106°F, with heat index values pushing 108-109°F. Heat stroke is no joke. Limit time outdoors, especially between 12 PM and 8 PM. If you *must* be outside, wear light, loose clothing, hydrate constantly, and take frequent shade breaks. And for the love of all that is holy, do NOT leave kids or pets in your car. Ever.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Lady Bird Lake and I-35 Headaches

If you were planning a long run around Lady Bird Lake, know that TxDOT's I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still causing significant disruptions. That east-side pedestrian crossing over I-35 is closed for about a year to build a new bridge, so you can't cross between the north and south shores there. Also, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along the northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029. Plan your route accordingly, or better yet, hit the treadmill.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Alert: Detours and Closures
Expect delays and detours around the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, especially near the I-35 corridor and The Confluence. The Shoal Creek Trail is also partially closed between Shoal Creek Blvd. and Janet Fish Bridge for repairs, forcing you onto a sidewalk detour along N.
