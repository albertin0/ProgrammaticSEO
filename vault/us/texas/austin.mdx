Hydration is crucial, but it won't save you from a lightning strike.

If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, today is absolutely the day to hit the gym or stay indoors.

### Trail Troubles & Construction Zones

Even if the weather miraculously holds off (it won't), the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake is still a hot mess in parts. Starting this month and continuing through March 2027, there's a significant reroute on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building. Austin Energy is digging around to put in underground electrical infrastructure, so expect detours, slow zones, and general frustration.

And let's not forget the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. The pedestrian bridge on the east side of I-35 has been closed since February, and that's not changing for about a year while they build a new bridge. You'll need to use the west-side sidewalk or head all the way to South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley if you want to cross. Basically, if you're not paying attention, you'll end up rerouting your entire run.
