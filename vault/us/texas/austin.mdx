This isn't just "moderate" or "fair" for everyone; it's straight-up unhealthy if you're a sensitive individual. We're talking about fine particulate matter that gets deep into your lungs, which is exactly what you *don't* want when you're huffing and puffing.

### Trail Closures & Obstacles: The Austin Marathon Pre-Race

The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, bless its heart, is still a maze of construction. If your usual Lady Bird Lake loop is part of your routine, prepare for some navigation. The north shore, near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building, has been rerouted since June for electrical infrastructure and will stay that way until March 2027. Furthermore, access along a portion of the south shore boardwalk, east of I-35, remains limited through early 2027 due to the ongoing pedestrian bridge construction. And don't even think about the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing; it's been shut since February 2026 for a new bridge, a project that could stretch into 2029. You can still cross on the *west* side, but planning is key.

Away from the lake, if you're a fan of climbing some hills out west, portions of the Granite Hiking Trail are currently closed for improvement projects. That started on October 14th and should last about two weeks, meaning it's very much still ongoing today. So, check Travis County Parks before you head out there, unless you enjoy running into a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign after a mile of climbing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**HAZARD: Extensive Trail Closures on Lady Bird Lake Trail and Granite Hiking Trail.** Plan your route meticulously. Expect detours, slow zones, and potential frustration. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail has multiple long-term reroutes and closures in effect.
</AlertBox>
