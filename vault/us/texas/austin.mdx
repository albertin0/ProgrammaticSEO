## Why you should rethink that outdoor workout today:

<BulletList>
*   **Heat, heat, and more brutal heat.** While your weather app might show something vaguely manageable right now, the local news is screaming "dangerous heat" and "near record highs" for today and yesterday, pushing temperatures well over 100°F with heat index values hitting 111°F. That 28.4°C (83°F) you see? That's probably just a fleeting moment before it all goes to hell. This isn't just "sweaty," it's "pass out on the Barton Creek Greenbelt" territory.
*   **Construction chaos on our beloved trails.** If the heat doesn't get you, the detours and closures around town just might test your patience. ACL Fest is still setting up, messing with Zilker access, and the I-35 Capital Express Central Project has significant, ongoing impacts on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake. Don't expect a smooth ride or walk.
*   **Moderate pollen.** For those of us with allergies, "moderate" pollen is just enough to make you sniffle and wheeze, adding another layer of discomfort to an already challenging day.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Seriously, folks. The official forecast for the Austin/San Antonio area for today is calling for near-record highs, pushing into the upper 90s to **106°F**, with heat index values possibly soaring to **104-111°F**. This is not just uncomfortable; it's life-threatening. Even if the sun isn't directly baking you, the ambient heat and humidity are a recipe for heatstroke. Limit your outdoor exposure to early mornings or late evenings, and keep it light.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Lady Bird Lake Trail Headaches:** If you're planning on hitting the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail today, be prepared for some frustrating reroutes. TxDOT's I-35 project means portions west of I-35 are rerouted through September and October, and the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year as they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. You're better off crossing at South Congress or using the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. Oh, and good luck finding parking under the I-35 bridge on the north shore; that's closed until 2033. The Shoal Creek Trail is also closed in a section until November 2026.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Stay Hydrated and Smart:** Given the heat, if you absolutely *must* be outside, drink more water than you think you need. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, slap on that sunscreen, and listen to your body. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, get inside immediately. Also, Travis County is under a burn ban, a general reminder that it's dry out there, so be extra careful with anything that could spark a fire.
</AlertBox>

Stick to the treadmill or the AC-blasted gym today, Austin. Your lungs, joints, and sanity will thank you.
