### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!
Seriously, Austin is under a Heat Advisory today. Highs are pushing well into the triple digits, and the "feels like" temperature is even worse. If you're out there, you're risking heat exhaustion or stroke. Limit your time outdoors, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because it just might. And for the love of all that is holy, don't leave kids or pets in a car.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Detours Aplenty
Your favorite routes are likely disrupted. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail has major reroutes on the north shore around the Confluence due to electrical work, expected to last until March 2027. The Shoal Creek Trail is a mess with sections closed for repairs and due to a landslide. If you're running, biking, or even just walking, be prepared for uneven surfaces, narrower paths, and unexpected detours. Construction zones mean heavy equipment and active work, so stay alert and follow all signage. You'll likely be sharing detoured sidewalks with more people than usual, so slow your roll.
</AlertBox>
