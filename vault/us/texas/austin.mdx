Your weather app might be showing a nice 73°F right now, but that's a lie. Austin hit 102°F yesterday and is set to do it again today. The heat index was pushing 105°F, and the Excessive Heat Warning is still hanging over Central Texas. If you insist on running, lifting, or existing outside past 9 AM, you're playing a dangerous game. Heat stroke is real, and it sneaks up on you. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because it might.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Slippery Slopes & Construction Zones

Yesterday's storms dropped enough rain to close some trails (Government Canyon had to shut down most routes due to mud). While our AQI is good (despite some distant wildfire smoke aloft), leftover moisture combined with loose debris from the wind could make for treacherous footing, especially on less-maintained paths. Factor in the constant construction on major arteries like I-35 and our beloved Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, and you're looking at a frustrating, potentially unsafe workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### When in Doubt, Go Indoors

Seriously. Austin has plenty of air-conditioned gyms, studios, and climbing walls. Or just hit your own living room. It’s August in Texas; embrace the treadmill for another month. Your lungs will appreciate the "Good" AQI, and your joints will thank you for not twisting an ankle on a washed-out trail or cooking yourself in the midday sun. Save the heroics for a cooler, less congested day.
</AlertBox>
