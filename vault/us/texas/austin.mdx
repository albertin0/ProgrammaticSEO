### Air You Can Almost Taste

Look, Austin usually boasts some decent air, but today is not that day. Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a grim 119, firmly in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" category, with PM2.5 being the primary culprit. That's tiny particulate matter getting deep into your lungs. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or even if you just *don't* want to feel like you ran through a smoke detector testing facility, seriously reconsider your outdoor plans. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) even hinted that urban fine particulate matter would be elevated today. Your lungs will thank you for choosing the treadmill.

### Heat and Humidity: A Classic Austin Combo

While it's "only" 28.4°C (83°F) right now, the forecast calls for highs up to 97°F (36°C) later today, with 85% humidity making it feel far worse. Austin Public Health has heat-related illness surveillance active, and for good reason—even a few weeks ago, we were seeing hundreds of heat-related ER visits. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about risking heat exhaustion or worse.

#### AQI & Heat Alert: Stay Indoors if Possible
With an AQI of 119 and afternoon temperatures pushing into the upper 90s with high humidity, today is exceptionally risky for outdoor exercise. Sensitive groups, especially, should avoid all outdoor activity.

### Trail Troubles

If the air and heat weren't enough, don't forget your routes might be messed up. Construction continues to plague sections of our beloved Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail. The north shore, near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, has reroutes in effect through March 2027 for electrical infrastructure. Plus, the east-side I-35 pedestrian bridge is closed for about a year as part of the Capital Express Central Project, meaning fewer options for crossing Lady Bird Lake. You'll be adding miles of detours and dodging construction, which is the last thing you want when you're already fighting the elements.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Ann and Roy Butler Trail Reroutes
Expect detours and potential slowdowns on the north shore of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail due to ongoing construction. The I-35 pedestrian bridge (east side) is also closed. Plan alternate routes or be prepared for disruptions.
</AlertBox>
