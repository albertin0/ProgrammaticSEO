## Air Quality & Allergens

Today's air quality in Austin is sitting at an AQI of 119, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. What does that mean for your run? It means there's enough fine particulate matter floating around to irritate your lungs, especially if you're prone to respiratory issues or pushing a hard effort. This isn't a "stay inside and bubble wrap yourself" alert, but it's definitely a "take it easy and listen to your body" warning. Apparently, we've got some lingering smoke from burnings in the Central Plains and even Mexico, plus the usual urban exhaust contributing to the haze. Light morning winds aren't doing us any favors in dispersing it.

If you're an allergy sufferer, brace yourself. We're still in moderate pollen season.

## Trail Conditions & City Obstacles

Let's talk trails, because they're a mess.

The **Lady Bird Lake Trail** is facing a few long-term headaches. There's an ongoing reroute near The Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, which started back in June and will continue through March 2027, all for some underground electrical work. And if you usually cross Lady Bird Lake via the I-35 pedestrian bridge, forget about it. That's been closed since February for roughly a year while they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge as part of the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. Your best bets for crossing are now the South Congress bridge or the Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley.

Down south, the **Shoal Creek Trail** is also a no-go in some areas. The section between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge is still closed for trail resurfacing and accessibility repairs, and it's looking like it'll be late September before that's cleared up. There's a detour, but it pushes you onto the N Lamar Blvd sidewalk. Good luck with that.

And because it's almost October, **Zilker Park** is already feeling the impacts of ACL Fest setup. Parking lot closures around the park began yesterday, September 14th, with street traffic impacts kicking off next week. While the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and Barton Springs Pool are technically open, expect congestion and limited access around the festival zones.
