### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Alright, Austin, let's talk about the air. While it's a relatively pleasant 14°C (perfect running weather, right?), and the skies are clear, the Air Quality Index is sitting at a grumpy 119. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," folks, with PM2.5 being the main culprit. What does that mean for you? If you have asthma, allergies, or any kind of respiratory issues, you're going to feel this. Even if you don't, sustained heavy exertion in this kind of air isn't exactly a health boon. Don't be a hero trying to PR your usual loop on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail today. You'll just end up coughing your way through Zilker Park later.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **AQI Warning:** With an AQI of 119 driven by PM2.5, folks with lung conditions, heart disease, older adults, and children should seriously consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Everyone else? You might feel some effects too. This isn't just about pollen; it's about particulate matter you're sucking into your lungs.
</AlertBox>

### Local Construction & Events: Still the Same Old Story

No major marathon road closures or surprise festivals blocking your path today, which is a small mercy. However, the eternal I-35 Capital Express Project marches on. You're looking at various lane closures and potential traffic shifts, especially near the downtown corridor and around the 290 East interchange. If your route involves getting anywhere near I-35, check traffic apps before you head out, or just plan a route that keeps you far, far away. We're talking ongoing construction disruptions, not necessarily "hazard" level, but definitely "annoying detour" level. This also means more idling cars, which certainly doesn't help that PM2.5 situation.
