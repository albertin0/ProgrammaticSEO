## The Good, The Bad, and The Detours

Look, the temperature at 10.8°C (about 51°F) and overcast skies means you won't be sweating through your shirt five minutes into your run. It's actually pretty prime running weather if you dress appropriately. And the AQI is a solid 32, which is "Good," meaning your respiratory system won't be fighting any major battles today.

However, we're in Austin, and that means construction. Everywhere. The Ann & Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake is still a minefield of detours thanks to the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. Specifically, the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is shut down for about a year as they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. So if your usual route involves crossing there, forget about it. You're rerouted to South Congress or the shiny new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. And don't even think about the Riverside Drive Pedestrian Ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road; that's closed until 2029. Yeah, you read that right.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger Zone: Lady Bird Lake Trail**
The I-35 Capital Express Central Project has significant, long-term closures and detours impacting the Ann & Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, particularly around the I-35 bridge. Check The Trail Conservancy's site before you head out, and plan for major reroutes, especially if you rely on crossing I-35 on foot. Expect some sections to feel like an obstacle course.
</AlertBox>

On a slightly less catastrophic note, but still annoying for some, portions of Vic Mathias/Auditorium Shores are intermittently closed between 6:30 a.m. and 2 p.m. for fertilizer application. The trails *should* remain open, but be aware of equipment and follow staff instructions. And if you had plans for a frisbee toss or a leisurely stroll on the Zilker Park Polo Field, tough luck. It's closed due to "this morning's rain and forecasted storms." Don't blame me, I just report the news.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Caution: Zilker Park and Lou Neff Road**
The Zilker Park Polo Field is closed today due to recent rain and forecasts. Additionally, if you're trying to access some parts of Zilker, there's a short-term detour (one month, started Feb 16) near Lou Neff Road for maintenance. Trail users are being routed onto the road, so stay alert for traffic.
</AlertBox>
