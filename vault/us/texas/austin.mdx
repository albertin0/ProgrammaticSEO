## EXTREME HEAT ADVISORY!
Seriously, Austin, this isn't a drill. Heat indices are forecast to hit 108-112°F today. Running, cycling, or doing anything strenuous outside is a terrible idea unless you're actively trying to end up in the ER. If you *must* be out, hydrate constantly, wear light-colored, loose clothing, and take frequent, long shade breaks. This heat kills. Don't be a statistic.

<AlertBox type="danger">
## Waterway and Riverfront Trail Ban on Lady Bird Lake!
The City has officially extended the ban on recreational use of Lady Bird Lake, Lake Austin, and the Colorado River downstream of Longhorn Dam until 5 PM today, July 23. Due to ongoing floodgate operations and fast, high flows, the water is dangerous. This means **stay off the water and avoid all riverfront trails along these waterways.** They're unstable and a legitimate hazard. Don't risk it.
</AlertBox>

## Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail & Shoal Creek Trail Detours and Closures
Good luck navigating. There are long-term detours on the **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** near The Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building, expected to last until March 2027 for Austin Energy work. Access at Willow Street is also closed for roughly 64 months due to construction. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project has also got parts of the trail and the pedestrian ramp at Riverside Drive shut down for years. Meanwhile, sections of the **Shoal Creek Trail** between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge are closed for repairs until late July 2026, forcing you onto a N Lamar Blvd sidewalk detour. Other sections are still blocked by landslides. Plan your route or pick another day for a long run on these trails.
</AlertBox>

### Workout Alternatives & General Tips
If you're not swimming in your own sweat by 9 AM, you're doing it wrong... or you're inside. Today's a perfect day for indoor workouts. Keep an eye on that heat index, not just the temp.
