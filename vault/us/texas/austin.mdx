<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads up on the Heat:** Even though it's nice out *right now*, the National Weather Service is predicting continuous above-average heat this week, hitting the mid-90s to low 100s. If you're hitting the pavement or trails after 10 AM, hydrate like it's your job and don't push it. Heat exhaustion is a real thing, and the paramedics have better things to do than pick you up off the side of Barton Springs Road.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Butler Trail Chaos Continues:** Seriously, planning a workout on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail these days requires a scouting mission. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year as they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. If you usually cross there, you'll need to trek to South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. Plus, there are long-term detours on the north shore by the Austin Rowing Club, the Riverside Drive pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk is out until 2029, and the Willow Street access is closed for a whopping 64 months. Just assume you'll hit a wall of orange cones somewhere.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Local "Austin Fire" isn't *our* Austin Fire:** For those of you who saw "Austin Fire" in some news feeds, don't panic about local wildfires. That's referring to fires up in Oregon, and it's not affecting our air quality here in Austin, Texas. So, breathe easy on that front.
</AlertBox>

Overall, if you're an early bird, hit it hard. If you're planning a mid-day session, maybe try the gym or the pool. And if you're hitting the trails, do your homework on closures before you head out, or you'll be adding an unplanned detour to your mileage.
