### Air Quality & Allergies

Let's start with the good news: the AQI is a solid 47, which is "Good." Your lungs can breathe easy today, folks. Dominant pollutant is PM2.5, but at this level, it's not going to be an issue for your outdoor sweat session. The temperature is a mild 25°C (77°F) with overcast conditions, making it perfectly comfortable for a run or ride without the usual Austin inferno.

Pollen's sitting at "Moderate." If you're sensitive, pop an antihistamine, but for most of us, it's not going to ruin your day. No major pollen spikes to report, so your sniffles should be manageable.

Keep an eye out for unexpected workers, and for the love of all that is holy, *slow down*.
