## Today's Austin Workout Rundown: Breathe Easy, Watch Your Step

Alright, folks, it’s April 15, 2026, and Austin is giving us a real mixed bag for getting those miles in. On the one hand, the weather gods are smiling. We’ve got a perfectly temperate 22.6°C (hello, 72.7°F!), partly cloudy skies, and an Air Quality Index sitting pretty at 34. That's "Good," for those keeping score, with Ozone/Other being the dominant, barely-there pollutant. Your lungs are getting a vacation today, especially if you’ve been cooped up inside.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Pointers:** Tree and grass pollen are still doing their thing, but it's only a moderate level. Pop an antihistamine and you might actually make it through your run without resembling a weeping willow.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble & Urban Obstacles

Now, let's talk about the downside, because this is Austin, and there's always a catch. While the air might be clean, the ground underfoot is a different story, especially if you stick to the popular routes. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** around Lady Bird Lake is still a glorious mess of detours and construction thanks to the ongoing I-35 Capital Express Central Project.

If you're planning on crossing Lady Bird Lake on the east side of I-35, forget about it. That pedestrian crossing has been closed since the week of February 9, 2026, and it's staying shut for roughly a year while they build a new bridge. Your options are to hoof it over at South Congress or use the shiny new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which thankfully opened back in February.

And if you usually hit the Boardwalk from Riverside Drive? That pedestrian ramp is closed through 2029. The Boardwalk itself is *mostly* open, but expect intermittent closures. Honestly, just assume anything near I-35 and the lake is a construction zone until further notice – like the I-35 North Shore trail detour that's sticking around until 2033. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on test of your patience and route-finding skills.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Trail Detours:** Construction on the I-35 Capital Express Central Project means significant closures and realignments on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, particularly around the I-35 corridor and Waller Creek. Plan your route, or find a new favorite spot.
</AlertBox>

Further south, the Bergstrom Spur Trail construction is also chugging along, aiming to connect South Austin neighborhoods to East Riverside Drive and US 183. While a welcome addition eventually, it's just more active work zones for now.

### Lingering Wet Spots & Wildfire Whispers

We had some storm warnings for the past weekend and early this week. While today is partly cloudy, there's a chance some of the lesser-maintained trails, especially off the paved paths, could still be a bit muddy or slick from recent rain. If you're heading out to explore the Greenbelt, check ATXFloods before you go for real-time low-water crossing statuses.

Speaking of the Greenbelt, don't let the good weather fool you into complacency about fire risk. Central Texas has been seeing elevated wildfire threats all spring due to dry conditions and new growth drying out quickly. Remember that brush fire near Bluff Springs Road greenbelt area earlier this year? Just be smart, people. No flicking cigarettes, no random campfires.

So, while the weather is inviting, stay sharp, navigate those detours, and respect the natural (and man-made) hazards. Your knees (and lungs) will thank you.
