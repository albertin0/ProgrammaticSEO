<AlertBox type="warning">
**Lady Bird Lake Trail: Still a Construction Zone**
If your usual route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake, be aware that the I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still causing significant reroutes, especially on the north shore. Crews are reconstructing bridges and other infrastructure, meaning permanent closures, temporary bypass lanes, and plenty of noise. Parts of East Avenue under the I-35 bridge are closed through early 2026. Don't expect a serene lakeside run; expect a detour adventure.
</AlertBox>

</AlertBox>
