Today is a truly awful day to be working out outside in Austin; just stay indoors, unless you enjoy extreme heat, smoky lungs, and navigating construction detours.

There's even an Extreme Heat Warning for some eastern areas, where it could feel like 115°F (46°C). This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous for sustained outdoor activity.
*   **Wildfire Smoke & Hazardous Air Quality:** That AQI of 119 for PM2.5? It's not just some random number. Austin's air quality is officially "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups" today. There's a real wildfire, the "Canyonlands Blaze," tearing through West Travis County near Steiner Ranch and Hudson Bend, and the smoke from that beast is categorized as "Hazardous" with high PM2.5 concentrations. Add to that potential smoke plumes drifting from other regional fires, and your lungs are not having a good time out there.
*   **Trail Treachery (Construction & Closures):** Even if you ignored the heat and smoke, good luck finding an uninterrupted run. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is a minefield of detours. There's long-term utility work west of the I-35 bridges through October 2026, rerouting sections of the path. Plus, the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year for bridge construction. And if you were planning on a peaceful stroll on the Shoal Creek Trail, forget about it—sections are closed between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge until at least late August for maintenance.
