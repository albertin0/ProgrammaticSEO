*   Expect temperatures to hit the upper 90s, possibly even 100°F, by afternoon, making those midday miles feel like a forced march through a convection oven. Stay hydrated or stay inside.
*   Air Quality Index is clocking in at 73, firmly in the "Moderate" range with PM2.5 as the main culprit. Your lungs will notice it, especially if you're pushing the pace.
*   Moderate pollen levels mean that if you're an allergy sufferer, you'll be sniffling and wheezing more than usual.

Look, I love running the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail as much as the next sane person, but today is not the day to just blindly head out there. We're looking at scorching temperatures that will hit 98°F to 100°F later today, turning Lady Bird Lake into a shimmering mirage of humidity. That's not just "warm," folks; that's prime dehydration territory if you're not paying attention. Keep your runs to the early morning if you absolutely must hit the pavement, and for the love of all that is holy, bring more water than you think you need.

Then there's the air. The AQI is sitting at 73. That's "Moderate," and it means your lungs are working harder than they should, especially with that delightful PM2.5 floating around. Add in the moderate pollen count, and you've got a recipe for a wheezy, snotty mess if you're prone to allergies. Don't be a hero. If you start feeling it, cut your workout short. Nobody cares about your Strava segment if you're gasping for air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Trouble Ahead: Butler Trail Detours are NOT Optional.**
Seriously, the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is a minefield of construction right now. Expect significant disruptions on the north shore, especially near The Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, due to Austin Water emergency repairs and Austin Energy electrical work. We're talking narrowed sections and full reroutes, with some closures extending through March 2027. Oh, and that I-35 Capital Express Central project means segments near I-35 will continue to see closures through 2033. Don't just charge through; slow down, look for signage, and don't be surprised if your usual route is completely blocked. On the east side of I-35, the pedestrian bridge is closed, so use the west-side sidewalk or plan to cross at South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge. It's not worth getting hit by a construction vehicle or face-planting because you weren't paying attention.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Not a Suggestion.**
With forecast highs pushing triple digits, you should be pre-hydrating, hydrating during your workout, and post-hydrating like it's your full-time job. Water, electrolytes – whatever works for you. And maybe consider moving that long run to an indoor track or hitting the gym. There's no shame in seeking air conditioning when it's this brutal outside.
</AlertBox>
