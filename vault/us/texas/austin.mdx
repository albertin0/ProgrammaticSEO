### Weather Report: Rain, Rain, Go Away (It Won't)

We're sitting under overcast skies at a pleasant 24.7°C (76.5°F), which normally sounds decent for a July morning in Austin. However, don't let that fool you. There's a high chance of rain throughout the day, with scattered thunderstorms predicted. This isn't just a sprinkle; forecasters are talking about tropical moisture pushing in, leading to torrential downpours.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Flash Flooding Expected!** Avoid low-water crossings, underpasses, and drainage areas. Seriously, don't test your luck against rapidly rising water. Rivers and creeks can swell quickly. The flood threat shifts south today, so even if it's not pouring where you are, it could be upstream.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Trail Closures & Detours Aplenty.** If you're heading to the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail today, prepare for unplanned adventures on city streets. The South Shore detour near Barton Creek is temporary, but the I-35 Capital Express Central Project has long-term closures affecting the trail, including the pedestrian crossing near I-35 on the east side of Lady Bird Lake which is shut for about a year. The Riverside Drive pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk is also closed through 2029. Your usual route is probably busted.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergens

The air quality is surprisingly good for all this weather nonsense, with an AQI of 31 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's a "Green" day, folks. So your lungs are mostly fine, assuming you don't inhale a mouthful of rainwater.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for the Drenched:** If you do decide to brave the elements, wear reflective gear. Overcast skies and rain drastically reduce visibility for drivers who are already bad at looking for runners and cyclists. Your bright new Sauconys aren't enough.
</AlertBox>
