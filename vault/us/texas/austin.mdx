## Your Run Today: Hydrate, Navigate, Tolerate

Look, the weather folks are calling for sunny skies and highs in the upper 70s to upper 80s, with tonight cooling down to the 50s. That 83-degree afternoon sun is no joke, especially if you're hitting the pavement for anything longer than a quick dog walk. Drink water before, during, and after. Don't be that person collapsing on the Pfluger Pedestrian Bridge thinking you're invincible. You're not.

### Trail Torture: The Butler Trail Saga Continues

If you're planning on a smooth cruise around Lady Bird Lake, temper your expectations. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is a long-haul beast, slated for eight years of delightful disruptions on and around the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Butler Trail Construction Alert:** The south shore boardwalk near I-35, which was fully closed until October 18, may have reopened but access will remain limited through early 2027 due to ongoing pedestrian bridge construction. Furthermore, expect reroutes on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building through March 2027 as Austin Energy digs around to install electrical infrastructure. Pay attention to signage. Or, you know, just embrace the chaos; it's Austin.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, these aren't just minor inconveniences. These are legitimate reroutes that can throw off your rhythm and add mileage you didn't plan for. And don't even get me started on the partial closure and ongoing construction at the Khabele Bathhouse at Barton Springs. It's like they're trying to keep us on our toes, or just off the trails entirely.

## Pollen & Air Quality: Not The Worst, Not The Best

Air quality is actually pretty good today with an AQI of 37. So your lungs can mostly breathe easy on that front. However, we're still sitting at a "Moderate" pollen level. If you're one of the many sensitive souls in this city, pop an antihistamine before you head out, or you'll be sniffling your way through Zilker Park.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Key:** With temperatures hitting the low 80s, proper hydration is critical. Carry water, even for shorter workouts. Electrolytes aren't just for marathoners; they're for anyone trying not to cramp up on the Barton Creek Greenbelt.
</AlertBox>

No major fire smoke issues from recent Austin-area wildfires, and the flood warnings from earlier in the month are gone, thankfully. So, for today, it's mostly about managing the heat, the pollen, and a healthy dose of trail navigation patience. Good luck out there.
