## The Air: Just Breathe (Carefully)

It's a beautiful, clear day with a comfortable 19.8°C (around 67.6°F) out there, which sounds perfect for a run or ride. Don't let that fool you. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is perched at 119, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," folks, which means if you have asthma, allergies, or just a general preference for not feeling like you ran through a dusty attic, you should seriously scale back your outdoor plans.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Ozone Action Day!
Austin is under an Ozone Action Day. This means ground-level ozone concentrations are high enough to be unhealthy, especially for children, older adults, and anyone with respiratory conditions. Limit strenuous outdoor activities during peak ozone hours (typically afternoon).
</AlertBox>

The TCEQ also noted that light morning winds could stir up urban fine particulate matter, though afternoon breezes might help disperse it a bit. Still, why gamble with your lungs when you could hop on a treadmill or hit an indoor cycling class? Your future self will thank you.

## The Trails: A Labyrinth of Orange Cones

Even if your lungs are bulletproof, your navigation skills will be tested on some of Austin's most popular trails.

The **Shoal Creek Trail** is a hot mess right now. A section between Shoal Creek Blvd. and the Janet Fish Bridge is closed until May 18th for resurfacing. And if you planned to cross the 5th Street Bridge section, forget about it—that's shut down until May 20th due to a recent fire, requiring detours onto surface streets.

Then there's the beloved **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** around Lady Bird Lake. The ongoing I-35 Capital Express Central project means significant reroutes on the north shore that started last September and will continue through *2033*. You'll encounter closures like the one at Willow Street, which is a multi-year affair. Also, that pedestrian bridge on the east side of I-35? Closed for a year since February 9th to build a new one. Your best bet for crossing Lady Bird Lake near there is the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley or South Congress.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Muck and Detours on Lady Bird Lake!
If you're heading to the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, be prepared for significant detours, especially on the north shore due to the massive I-35 Capital Express Central project. You'll be sharing space with construction, so pay attention to signage and staff instructions. A new major detour near the Confluence starts in June and runs through March 2027.
</AlertBox>
