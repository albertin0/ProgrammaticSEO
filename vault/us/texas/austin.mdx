## Trail Closures & Local Headaches

Now, for your joints – and your sanity. If your usual route involves the Shoal Creek Trail, listen up: multiple sections are either closed or just barely reopening today. The stretch between Shoal Creek Blvd. and the Janet Fish Bridge was closed for maintenance until today, May 18th, and it's still closed near the Fifth Street Bridge through May 20th due to fire-related repairs. Plus, that old landslide area between 26th-27th Street is still a no-go. Detours are clearly marked, usually shunting you onto N. Lamar Blvd. sidewalks, but that's hardly the same.

If you're closer to Lady Bird Lake, expect some disruptions on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail. Access at Willow Street is closed for a massive construction project that's slated to last for *years*. Also, detours are in effect near Waller Creek for the Waterloo Greenway's "The Confluence" project, building new pedestrian bridges. And there's new underground electrical infrastructure work along the shoreline between Brazos and Trinity Streets, expected to last a year. Plan for some weaving.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect slick spots on trails and sidewalks today. We got nearly an inch of rain yesterday (May 18), so watch your footing, especially on wooded trails or around creek crossings. Nobody wants a rolled ankle to end their week.
</AlertBox>

## Public Safety Reality Check

And because this isn't some corporate, sugar-coated wellness blog: Austin experienced a string of random shootings over the weekend in parts of South and East Austin. While police have apprehended suspects and the shelter-in-place order was lifted, it’s a stark reminder that staying aware of your surroundings isn't just about traffic. If you're running with headphones, keep the volume low enough to hear what's happening. Stick to well-trafficked areas, especially if you're out early or late.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Be extra vigilant of your surroundings, particularly if running alone or in less-populated areas. Recent events underscore the need to prioritize personal safety.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a mixed bag. The weather's okay, the air's mostly clear, but the trail system is a patchwork of closures and construction, and recent local crime should make you think twice about being complacent. Pick your battles, Austin.
