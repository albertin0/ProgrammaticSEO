## Austin's Perpetual Construction Zone: Trail Edition

Now, for the fun part: dodging the orange cones and temporary fences. The biggest headache right now is still centered around the **Lady Bird Lake Trail** and Zilker Park. The Austin City Limits Music Festival just wrapped its first weekend and its ghost still haunts the park. The Great Lawn is closed until October 18th for setup and takedown. While the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail itself remains *technically* open, expect significant parking lot and street closures around Zilker Park. If you're planning a run or ride through there, consider alternative access points or just pick another route entirely. Barton Springs Pool is also only accessible via the Azie Morton Road entrance on festival weekends.

And let's not forget the I-35 Capital Express Central Project, because why would we ever finish that? You'll encounter "temporary Trail detours near construction areas" on the Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, with specific closures like the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing until 2027 and portions of the south shore boardwalk at I-35 closing later this month for a week. Long-term utility work west of the I-35 bridges on the Butler Trail also means detours through October. Plan accordingly or be prepared to improvise.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Zones: Not Your Personal Obstacle Course
Seriously, respect the barriers. Trying to sneak through a closed construction zone on the Lady Bird Lake Trail or any other affected area isn't just dangerous, it's asking for trouble. You could end up with a twisted ankle, or worse, become a hazard to the workers. Don't be that guy.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Check Before You Trek
Your best bet for avoiding a frustrating dead-end is to hit up the Austin Parks and Recreation Department's closures page or The Trail Conservancy's website before you head out. They usually have the most up-to-date info on what's open and what's not.
</AlertBox>
