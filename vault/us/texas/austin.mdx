## The Rundown

Look, if you're a glutton for punishment or just *have* to get your miles in, pick your poison. The air itself isn't terrible, and we're not dealing with any wildfire smoke choking the skies today, which is a win in itself for Travis County. The pollen count sitting at "Moderate" means most of you allergy sufferers might just get away with it if you pop a pill.

### Heat & Humidity: Your Real Enemy

But let's be real: that 23.4°C (74°F) "actual" temperature is a goddamn lie when you factor in the humidity. We're talking heat index values pushing 37-39°C (upper 90s to low 100s F) by midday. If you're planning a long run, start early or just give up and hit the treadmill. Seriously, your joints and cardiovascular system will thank you. Trying to PR when it feels like you're running through a hot, wet blanket is just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### DANGER: Afternoon Heat Index!
  The "feels like" temperature will soar well into the triple digits this afternoon. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are no joke. Hydrate *before*, *during*, and *after* any outdoor activity. Electrolytes aren't just for fancy recovery drinks; they're essential.
</AlertBox>

### Zilker Park & Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail Shenanigans

Today is the grand finale of Austin City Limits. That means Zilker Park is locked down tight for ticket holders, and the surrounding areas are a chaotic mess of traffic, pedestrians, and general festival spillover.

While the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and Barton Springs Pool *remain technically open*, accessing them near Zilker will be a nightmare due to street closures and parking restrictions. If you're looking to loop Lady Bird Lake, be aware of the ongoing I-35 Capital Express Central Project. There are *long-term detours* on the north shore near I-35 that started way back in 2025 and aren't finishing until 2033. And just to add insult to injury, a section of the Boardwalk on the south shore will close *tomorrow*, October 12th, for a week for construction. Plan your route meticulously, or just choose a different trail entirely.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### WARNING: ACL Festival Gridlock & Trail Detours!
  Avoid the Zilker Park area unless you're attending ACL. Significant road closures and heavy foot traffic will make any workout a frustrating crawl. Furthermore, sections of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail are under long-term construction near I-35, and the Boardwalk will have a new closure starting tomorrow.
</AlertBox>
