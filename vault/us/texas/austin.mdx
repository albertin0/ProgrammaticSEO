## Lungs: Battle the Botanical Blitz

The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 37 today, which usually means clear sailing for your lungs. However, "Good" doesn't mean "allergy-free," not in Austin anyway. We've got scattered burning activities lingering in parts of Central Texas, potentially contributing some light density residual smoke, but thankfully, the major plumes from that Port Arthur refinery incident seem to have largely dispersed.

No, the real villain today is the pollen. Forget "Moderate" – that's a corporate lie. We're talking "Very High" tree pollen, with Ash, Willow, and London Plane leading the charge. Grass pollen is also high, and mold is joining the party too. So, while the air pollution itself isn't a red flag, your respiratory system might still feel like it's running a gauntlet through a cedar chop shop.

## Joints: Mild and Mostly Clear (Except for the Detours)

The weather today is actually a solid win for your joints. We're looking at a comfortable 21.4°C (about 70°F) with plenty of clouds, so no brutal sun beating down on you. Humidity is on the rise overnight, with a chance of patchy fog in the early morning, but that shouldn't impact mid-day runs too much.

However, if your usual route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail, be prepared for some navigational acrobatics. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still causing headaches. The pedestrian bridge on the east side of I-35, which connects the north and south shores of Lady Bird Lake, has been closed since early February and will stay that way for about a year. Your options are to cross at South Congress or use the shiny new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which thankfully opened last month.

And if you're trying to get onto the Boardwalk from Riverside Drive, that pedestrian ramp is also closed through 2029. You'll have to find other access points. Plus, there's a short-term detour near Lou Neff Road routing you onto the street. Basically, always assume there's a detour sign around the lake right now. Construction is also impacting the Shoal Creek Trail north of Pease Park with upcoming repairs, and the Barton Creek trail has a detour near Toomey Road.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect multiple closures and detours on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail, especially around the I-35 corridor and Waller Creek. Plan your routes carefully and add extra time.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
While the Bastrop State Park wildfire from Sunday night is contained, "light density residual smoke" from scattered burning across wider Central Texas is mentioned. Keep an eye (and nose) out for any localized smoke, particularly if you're running east of town.
</AlertBox>
