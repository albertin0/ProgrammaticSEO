### Today's Rundown

Alright, Austin, let's talk turkey. The air is looking pretty good for most of us, with an AQI of 44. AccuWeather calls this "Fair," meaning most can breathe easy, but if you're one of those sensitive types, you might feel some minor irritation. Combine that with the crisp, clear skies, and honestly, you'd be a fool not to lace up. We're talking ideal running temperatures here, folks. Enjoy it before the triple-digits inevitably roll back in.

### Don't Forget Your Allergy Meds

While the air quality is decent, our old pal "Moderate" pollen is still hanging around. If you typically react to spring allergens, hit your antihistamines before you head out. You don't want to be snot-sniffling through your entire run.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble & Crowds

Now, for the fun part: dodging the city's perpetual construction zones and special events. If your usual route involves the **Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake**, listen up, because it's still a hot mess, especially near I-35.

The I-35 Capital Express Central project is a beast, and it's brought some significant trail reroutes and closures. The pedestrian crossing on the east side of I-35 over Lady Bird Lake has been shut down since February 2026 and will be for about a year while they build a new bridge. So, no crossing there, unless you fancy a swim with a crane. You'll need to use the South Congress bridge or the newer Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley if you're trying to get from the north to south shores on that end of the lake.

To make matters worse, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk from Riverside Drive is also closed through 2029. Expect detours, construction noise, and truck traffic along the north shore, particularly near the I-35 bridge, where they were building a massive dock back in November 2025. And if you typically access the trail near East Avenue under I-35, that section's been closed for utility work through early 2026. Basically, if you're running that stretch, prepare to improvise.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### I-35 Corridor on Lady Bird Lake Trail = Construction Zone

Avoid the area directly surrounding the I-35 bridges on the Lady Bird Lake trail if you can. Expect significant detours, reroutes, and closures, including the pedestrian bridge and Riverside Drive ramp. Plan alternate routes or be prepared for interruptions and increased noise.
</AlertBox>

Also, if you're anywhere near **Vic Mathias Shores (Auditorium Shores)** today, you're going to hit the tail end of the Austin Reggae Festival. That means crowds, likely some lingering infrastructure, and general festival vibes that aren't exactly conducive to a personal best. Maybe aim for something a little quieter, like the Shoal Creek Trail (though beware, parts of it are indefinitely closed between 24th and 29th along N. Lamar due to construction) or head deeper into Zilker. Speaking of Zilker, the city is actually looking into lighting upgrades for our urban trails, with a plan hopefully adopted by the end of 2026. So, someday, our early morning runs might be a little less "guess where that pothole is!"

### Get Out There

It's a solid day for running in Austin, despite the minor sniffles and major detours. Just be smart about your route, keep an eye out for construction, and enjoy that sweet, sweet cool air.
