## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

Seriously, AQI 119 with PM2.5 isn't a joke. That's fine particulate matter getting into your lungs. You might not *see* the smoke from some distant wildfire, but these tiny particles are like microscopic sandpaper for your airways. Your body is already working overtime to cool itself in this heat; adding lung irritation to the mix is just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: Heat Index up to 112°F Possible.** Hydration isn't enough when your body can't dissipate heat. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. Check on vulnerable populations.
</AlertBox>
