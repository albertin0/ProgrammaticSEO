### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

That AQI of 119 is officially "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." You might see some reports from yesterday or different parts of town showing "Good" air, but today, for a significant chunk of us, it's not. With PM2.5 being the main culprit, you're looking at tiny particulate matter getting deep into your respiratory system. While there's no active wildfire smoke being reported today that directly blankets Austin, PM2.5 spikes can be from a number of things, including general urban pollution or even distant, diffuse smoke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads up on the air quality, folks.** If you've got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to feeling like you've run through a dusty attic, dial it back or stay inside. Keep an eye on the kids and the elderly too, even if they're just spectating your suffering.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Other Urban Blight

The good news is, most of our beloved Central Texas trails, like the Barton Creek Greenbelt, are predicted to be dry. No mud-bogging to report, which is a blessing after any decent rain around here.

However, Austin is always under construction, and today is no different. The SH 71/Ben White Boulevard to I-35 flyovers are still seeing nightly lane closures as crews work on the I-35 Capital Express projects. While this mostly affects drivers, it's a reminder of the constant churn. More notably, there are plans advancing for residential towers off East Riverside Drive, with a portion of the property adjacent to Lady Bird Lake and its hike-and-bike trail being preserved as open space. That's good for the future, but be mindful of any localized construction noise or dust if you're out on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail today. Downtown, CapMetro reports Night Owl routes are still affected by ongoing construction near Lavaca & 6th. It's not blocking the entire city, but it's part of the scenery.

As for heat, today's overcast 27.2°C is a welcome reprieve from the usual August inferno. We're not in extreme heat territory *today*, though warnings are out for next week, with heat indices potentially hitting 105+ degrees between August 18-21. So enjoy this "cooler" day while it lasts, but remember that even moderate temperatures with high humidity can still sneak up on you. Always hydrate, regardless of the forecast.

**Pro Tip:** If you're really itching to get some miles in despite the air, consider hitting a treadmill indoors or finding a spot with better air circulation, maybe away from major roadways. Your lungs will thank you.
