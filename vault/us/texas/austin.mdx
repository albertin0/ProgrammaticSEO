Look, that 22.3°C (about 72°F) and "Mainly Clear" weather looks inviting, right? Classic Austin spring. Don't let it fool you. While the temps are prime for a run, your lungs might not agree. The Air Quality Index is hitting 119, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) predicts Austin's PM2.5 could hang around the "lower end of Moderate" today, thanks to light morning winds and some lingering fine particulate matter. Meanwhile, AccuWeather is already calling today "Unhealthy" for sensitive groups and advises even healthy individuals might experience throat irritation or difficulty breathing with prolonged exposure. Pick your poison, I guess.

Then there's the pollen. Still "Moderate." So, even if the air quality magically cleared up, your sinuses would still be throwing a party for cedar, oak, and whatever else is trying to kill us this time of year. Bring your meds.

### Trail Closures & Construction Chaos

This is where things get really fun, by which I mean infuriating. Our beloved Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is still under siege by the I-35 Capital Express Central Project.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major I-35 Construction Detours on Lady Bird Lake Trail**
The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail has been *closed since February 9, 2026*, and it's expected to remain that way for about a year while they build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. You simply *cannot* cross between the north and south shores on the east side of I-35. Your options are now South Congress Avenue or the shiny new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley (which opened way back on February 7, 2026).
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Riverside Drive Ramp Closure & Parking Issues**
That pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive? Closed through 2029. The Boardwalk itself is *mostly* open, but expect intermittent closures. Also, don't even *think* about parking under the I-35 bridge on the north shore of Lady Bird Lake; that lot's been closed since September 2025 and isn't coming back until 2033. They’re storing construction equipment there. It's a mess.
</AlertBox>

You'll find detours with 14-foot-wide paths in some areas around the Lady Bird Lake Trail, but it's still a headache to navigate. The city is also still working on "The Confluence" project along Waller Creek, connecting it to the Butler Trail, which is slated for completion sometime this year. Just be aware of active work zones.

And it’s not just downtown:
*   The **Willow Street trail access** is closed for a whopping 64 months due to construction of 98 Red River. Hope you weren't planning on that route.
*   The **Shoal Creek Trail between 24th and 29th along N. Lamar** is *indefinitely closed*. Yeah, indefinitely.

**My PT Tips for Today:**
Given the AQI, stick to indoor workouts if you've got respiratory issues or are just feeling meh. If you brave the outdoors, especially around Lady Bird Lake, be hyper-aware of your surroundings, plan your detours, and accept that your usual route is probably busted. Hydrate like it's your job, and listen to your body – it's not trying to trick you.
