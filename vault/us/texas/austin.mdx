### Today's Rundown

This Sunday morning, while it's "Mainly Clear" and starting a bit cooler, don't let that fool you. The heat is rolling in hard and fast, making those midday and afternoon workouts a gamble with your health. The National Weather Service is flat-out stating "hot and dry weather resumes Sunday and continues through next week," with "moderate heat risk impacts." This isn't just about feeling uncomfortable; it's about genuine heat exhaustion and stroke risk. Remember those new UIL heat safety protocols for high schools that just kicked in yesterday? They're a reminder of how serious this heat is, even for seasoned athletes.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** With temperatures forecast to reach 98-100°F (37-38°C) and heat indices potentially climbing to 109°F, consider rescheduling your outdoor workout to very early morning or late evening. If you must go out, stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Report: Your Patience, Not Just Your Legs, Will Be Tested

If you're planning a long run or ride, especially around Lady Bird Lake, be prepared for diversions. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is a prime example of Austin's perpetual state of construction. There's a significant reroute on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building, ongoing until March 2027, thanks to Austin Energy installing underground electrical infrastructure. Good luck with that.

And don't even get me started on the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. If you usually cross I-35 on foot around Lady Bird Lake, the east-side pedestrian crossing has been closed since February for a new bridge. You'll need to use the South Congress bridge or the shiny new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. The pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along the northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is also closed through 2029. It's a maze out there, so plan your routes carefully, or just accept you'll be adding some "bonus" miles of confusion. Even the Shoal Creek Trail had significant closures recently for maintenance and still has long-term landslide closures north of Pease Park.
