## The Rundown

The weather for your outdoor grind is a bit of a mixed bag. Our official temperature is hitting a warm 28.4°C (83.1°F) with "Mainly Clear" skies, but don't let that fool you. The humidity is through the roof, making it feel a lot warmer and muggier than the number suggests. If you're planning a long run, hydrate like you're training for an Ironman, because Central Texas heat combined with high humidity is a recipe for bonking.

Air quality is sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 57, with ozone and other pollutants being the dominant culprits. For most folks, this isn't a deal-breaker, but if you've got sensitive lungs, you might feel a bit of a tickle.

### Construction & Closures

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major I-35 Construction Impacts!** If your route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, especially near I-35, you *will* encounter closures and detours. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year to build a new bridge. You're advised to use the South Congress bridge or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley to cross Lady Bird Lake instead. Also, the pedestrian ramp along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; plan entirely new routes if these are part of your usual loop. Expect traffic pattern changes around I-35 in general, with some lanes reduced.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beware Isolated Storms!** The National Weather Service mentions a conditional risk of isolated severe storms with potential for large hail, damaging winds, and lightning, primarily west of the city and moving east this evening. While there are no active warnings right now, keep an eye on the sky and your weather app. A clear morning doesn't guarantee a clear afternoon, especially in the Hill Country.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate!** With temperatures in the low 80s and high humidity, heat stress is a real concern. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. Consider moving your longer efforts to earlier in the morning or later in the evening to avoid the peak heat.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, respect the construction and the weather. There are plenty of good miles to be had, but you gotta be smart about where and when you're getting them in today.
