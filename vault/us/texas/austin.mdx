<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!** With PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant and an AQI of 119, anyone with respiratory issues, heart conditions, or allergies should seriously consider keeping their workout indoors today. Even healthy folks might experience shortness of breath, coughing, or throat irritation if they push it too hard outside. Don't play games with your lungs, especially not with that early summer humidity making everything feel heavier.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Lady Bird Lake Trail is a Maze.** If your usual route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, especially the north shore section near the Austin Rowing Club, prepare for detours. Austin Energy is doing some major underground work, rerouting sections until March 2027. Slow down, pay attention to signage, and be ready to share space with construction traffic or navigate temporary paths. It's not the smooth sailing you're used to.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**South Austin Runners, Beware I-35 Closures.** If you typically cross I-35 via the William Cannon Drive bridge, forget about it this weekend. TxDOT has it completely shut down from 10 p.m. tonight until Monday morning. This is part of that never-ending I-35 Capital Express South project. Plan your long run accordingly, or better yet, stay north of the river.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**World Cup Crowds at Auditorium Shores.** The Austin FC Soccer Celebration is going on at Auditorium Shores today, bringing big crowds for FIFA World Cup viewing. If your plan was a peaceful stroll or bike ride through that area of the Butler Trail, expect significant congestion. It's probably best to avoid that section entirely or hit it super early before the fan festivities kick into high gear.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, Austin. There are better days to hit the trails hard. If you absolutely *must* get outside, keep it short, keep it easy, and for the love of all that is holy, bring more water than you think you need. Or, you know, find a gym with good AC and a solid filter. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
