## The Good, The Bad, and The Sweaty

Look, the AQI is holding strong at a respectable 28, which means your lungs won't be taking a beating from pollution today. That's a win, especially with all the talk of drought and wildfire danger that Governor Abbott just renewed for Travis County. However, don't forget that just yesterday, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) was forecasting that our fine particulate matter (PM2.5) could hit the "Moderate" range, thanks to the humidity. It's a subtle reminder that Austin's air can turn on a dime, so if you feel anything iffy, head inside.

Hydration isn't optional, it's a survival mechanism.

## Austin's Trails: A Minefield of Detours

Here's where it gets fun. If you're planning a run or ride on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake, be ready for some navigational challenges. The north shore, specifically near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, is rerouted from *this month* through March 2027. Why? Austin Energy is busy burying electrical infrastructure. So, slow your roll and pay attention to the signs, unless you enjoy an unexpected detour into a construction zone.

And that's not all for the Butler Trail. If you usually access the Boardwalk from the northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive, tough luck. That pedestrian ramp has been closed since February and isn't reopening until 2029. Oh, and the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing? Also closed for about a year as they build a new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. Your best bet for crossing the lake around there is either South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley.

Meanwhile, Barton Springs Pool, bless its heart, is still closed due to recent flooding. Just a friendly reminder that even when the sun's out, the aftermath of our legendary Austin storms can linger. We just had heavy rains and flash flood warnings a couple of days ago on June 15, which closed numerous roads. Governor Abbott even issued a severe storm disaster proclamation for Travis County and others. So, while the skies are clear, keep an eye out for lingering wet spots on lesser-used trails.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Butler Trail & I-35 Construction Chaos:** Significant detours and closures are *active now* on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, especially on the north shore and near I-35.
