## The Real Hazards: Construction and Closures (Again)

Look, I love this city, but sometimes it feels like every trail is under perpetual construction. Today is no different. While Mother Nature is giving us a break, TxDOT and Austin Energy are still hard at work making your usual routes a navigational nightmare.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail: Prepare for Detours!
If your plan involves a chill loop around Lady Bird Lake, you're going to need a new plan or a lot of patience. A significant reroute on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building *started this month* and will run through March 2027. This is for Austin Energy to install underground electrical infrastructure, which is apparently an 8-month endeavor. Slow down, pay attention to the signs, and don't be that person who ignores the cones. Also, the parking lot under the I-35 bridge on the north shore? Closed until 2033. Seriously. Plan accordingly for parking.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Shoal Creek Trail: Still a Mess
Various sections of the Shoal Creek Trail remain closed due to maintenance or prior landslide damage. One section between Shoal Creek Blvd. and the Janet Fish Bridge is closed until late June 2026 for trail maintenance. Another bit near the Fifth Street Bridge was closed through late May and could still be experiencing lingering work. This isn't new news, but it's still impacting your ability to get a smooth run in.
</AlertBox>

### What Else to Watch For

### Local Events
Today's Saturday, and Austin loves a good race. "The First Step - A Celebration of Community" Father's Day 5K is happening this morning (9-11 AM) at The Loop Running Supply, right near the Ann and Roy Butler Trail. Expect some local congestion if you're in that area. Another race, the Lemonade Dash, is happening in Hutto, so that shouldn't impact central Austin too much.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Flash Flood Risk (Even with Overcast Skies)
While we don't have active warnings *right now*, scattered showers and storms are possible this evening and overnight. These can bring heavy rain and localized flooding, especially in low-water crossings. Barton Springs Pool is *still closed* from earlier flooding this week, which tells you how much rain we got. Keep an eye on the forecast if you're planning an evening workout, and absolutely, under no circumstances, should you drive or run through flooded roads. That's just stupid.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the mild weather while it lasts, but be ready to adapt your route and stay aware of your surroundings. Austin always keeps you on your toes.
