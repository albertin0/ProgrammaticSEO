The air quality is surprisingly good today, a welcome change from some of our haze-filled summers. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecasts "Good" air quality for Austin through the weekend. Even with some burning activity around central/eastern Texas and Mexico, the smoke seems to be staying high enough that it's not impacting our ground-level air much. So, if you manage the other hazards, your lungs should be happy.

Now, for the bad news: it's still hotter than a fresh-paved road in July. We're looking at daily highs well into the 90s, potentially cracking 100°F, with heat indices making it feel even worse. Tomorrow is expected to be the peak of this heat wave, so today is just a warm-up, unfortunately. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat Advisory Conditions Expected this Weekend!
Highs approaching 100-103°F with elevated heat indices mean real risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Save your PRs for a cooler day and keep those easy runs easy, or indoors.
</AlertBox>

If you insist on running or biking outside, be prepared for some navigational gymnastics. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is still a construction zone in spots. Due to the never-ending TxDOT I-35 Capital Express Central project, expect significant reroutes on the north shore of Lady Bird Lake through 2033. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing has been closed since February 2026, so you can't cross the lake there. Your best bets for crossing are South Congress or the newer Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. Plus, a section near the Austin Rowing Club building is rerouted for Austin Energy electrical work until March 2027. Good luck out there.

And speaking of traffic, **Zilker Metropolitan Park** is hosting the Zilker Relays this evening, from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Expect extra crowds and potential congestion around the park. While the Hike-and-Bike Trail, Barton Creek Greenbelt, and Zilker Park Disk Golf Course are reportedly staying open through September and October, the general area will see increased activity and likely some parking and street closures related to this and the upcoming ACL Festival setup. Plan your routes accordingly, or just avoid the area.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Zilker Park Traffic & Trail Detours
The Zilker Relays are happening tonight, 6 PM-10 PM, bringing extra crowds and potential for local road congestion around Zilker Park. Additionally, ongoing construction on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail means significant detours and closures, especially on the north shore near I-35. Check the City of Austin's mobility guides before you head out.
</AlertBox>

My advice? Embrace the clear skies this morning, but finish up before the real heat of the day kicks in. Or just stay home, hydrate, and consider an indoor workout. Your body will thank you.
