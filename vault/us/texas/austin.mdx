Today (October 2, 2026) is a terrible day to work out outside in Austin due to heavy rainfall, widespread flooding, and significant event-related closures around Zilker Park. The air quality is good, and the temperature is warm but the risk of injury or being caught in dangerous floodwaters far outweighs any benefits.

  <li>Zilker Park is a no-go zone. Not only is it getting drenched, but the Austin City Limits (ACL) Music Festival is in full swing. Trying to run there is just asking for a headache, or worse, a twisted ankle in the muck.</li>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Widespread Flooding and Hazardous Conditions:** Numerous low-water crossings across Austin are closed. Hamilton Pool was already shut down yesterday due to flooding threats, and you can bet other creek-side trails like portions of the Barton Creek Greenbelt are treacherous. If you see standing water, assume it's moving and dangerous. Don't try to cross it. Just don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **ACL Festival Chaos:** Beyond the rain, the Austin City Limits Festival means major disruptions around Zilker Park and surrounding areas. Barton Springs Road is closed, Stratford Drive has closures, and traffic is a nightmare. Forget trying to run on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail sections near Zilker or under the I-35 bridges, where there are already ongoing construction detours and closures. You'll be dodging barricades and festival-goers, which is hardly a relaxing workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Stay Indoors:** This isn't the day to be a hero or test your luck. Opt for an indoor workout. Hit the gym, do some bodyweight exercises, or just enjoy the fact you're not out there getting soaked and potentially swept away. Your lungs and joints (and common sense) will thank you.
