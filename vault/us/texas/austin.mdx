### DANGER: Active Flash Flood Warning & Flood Watch!
The National Weather Service has issued a Flash Flood Warning for parts of Austin until 10 AM CDT, and a Flood Watch is in effect until 7 PM Tuesday, June 16. Heavy rain, potential for life-threatening flash flooding, and dangerous lightning are all in play. Do not, under any circumstances, attempt to run or bike through standing water. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a slogan for drivers; it applies to you, too. Stay off low-water crossings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### TRAIL ALERT: Lady Bird Lake Trail Reroutes & Other Closures
If you were planning a long run around Lady Bird Lake, be aware of ongoing reroutes on the north shore, affecting the area near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club. This isn't a short-term thing; it's for utility work through March 2027. Factor in extra time for navigation, and be prepared for less-than-ideal running surfaces. Also, the Shoal Creek Trail north of Janet Fish Bridge is closed until late June for maintenance.
</AlertBox>
