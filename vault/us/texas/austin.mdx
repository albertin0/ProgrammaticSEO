## Today's Vitals: Breathe Deeply (or Don't)

Look, I get it, you wanna get out there. The temperature at 15.9°C (that's about 61°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) is pretty decent. If you're a morning person, though, you woke up to a thick blanket of fog. That doesn't just make your Instagram photos moody; it seriously cuts down visibility, especially if you're trying to navigate anything other than a straight, well-lit path. Surfaces are probably slick, too. Think twice before you try to PR on those Greenbelt downhills.

Then there's the air. The AQI is at a lovely 119 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," folks. And let's be honest, who *isn't* sensitive when they're sucking in tiny particulate matter during a hard effort? If you've got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, seriously reconsider that long run. Even for healthy adults, prolonged exposure or high-intensity exercise in this kind of air quality is just dumb.

### The Never-Ending Construction Derby

Remember that I-35 Capital Express Central project? Yeah, it's still eating up parts of our city. If you're planning on hitting up the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake, especially on the north shore or near I-35, expect detours and closures. Austin Energy is busy installing underground electrical infrastructure, rerouting parts of the trail through summer 2027. The I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed through 2027, and the Riverside Drive pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk is out of commission until 2029. So, plan your routes carefully, or just accept that you're going to add an unplanned quarter-mile looking for an open path.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Caution: Reduced Visibility & Slippery Surfaces.** That fog isn't just a pretty picture. Watch your step, especially on trails and sidewalks that might be damp. Your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Local Events & Other Annoyances

Pollen is at a moderate level, which is just enough to make you sniffle and curse, but probably not enough to completely sideline you unless you're one of *those* people whose eyes swell shut when a cedar tree looks at them funny.

There are also a few local events today. If you're heading out to Brushy Creek Lake Park, be aware there's a BarktoberFest 5k happening. While generally contained, local events can mean more people, more cars, and more general chaos in the immediate vicinity. Give yourself extra time or pick another spot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger: AQI 119 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups).** If you have respiratory issues, kids, or just don't like inviting lung inflammation, scale back your outdoor intensity or move your workout indoors. This isn't a "push through it" day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** If you absolutely *must* run outside, consider a less trafficked, unpaved route where car emissions are lower. But honestly, today might be a good day to hit the gym, do some strength work, or embrace the treadmill. Your lungs will send you a thank you card later.
</AlertBox>
