<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heatstroke is Real, People.
Seriously, look at that predicted high of 104°F with a 102°F "RealFeel." You need to be hydrated *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. If you're not sweating like you just ran through a car wash, you're not hydrating enough. Head out before the sun gets serious, stay in the shade, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. That headache and dizziness aren't just "pushing through." That's your body yelling at you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Treachery on Lady Bird Lake
The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is still a bit of a maze in places. There's ongoing construction for Austin Water emergency repairs on the north shore near the Confluence, so expect narrower sections and *slow down* there. Also, remember that whole I-35 pedestrian bridge closure on the east side? That's still a thing until February 2027 while they build a new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. If you're usually crossing there, reroute via South Congress or hit up the Wishbone Bridge. The Riverside Drive pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk is also closed through 2029. Don't be that person surprised by a detour mid-run. Check the city's parks updates, or just plan for the long game around those spots.
</AlertBox>
