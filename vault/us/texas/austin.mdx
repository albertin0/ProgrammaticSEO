Look, the weather folks are calling it overcast and around 14.8°C (that’s about 59°F for the Fahrenheit die-hards), which usually screams "perfect running weather." But don't let that fool you. The real villain today is our air quality. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is hitting a spicy 119, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. For anyone with even a hint of a sensitive respiratory system – or frankly, anyone who enjoys breathing freely – that's a red flag. PM2.5 means tiny particles that love to burrow deep into your lungs and cause trouble. It's not just a sniffle, folks; it's inflammation and reduced lung function waiting to happen. If you’re prone to allergies or have asthma, seriously consider an indoor alternative.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI ALERT: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!**
With an AQI of 119 and PM2.5 in charge, sensitive individuals (asthmatics, kids, the elderly) should drastically reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Everyone else, keep your outdoor time short and moderate. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

On top of the lung-busting air, we've still got our usual springtime "moderate" pollen count. Just enough cedar, oak, and who knows what else floating around to make your eyes water and your nose run, even if the air itself wasn't trying to do you in. Keep those antihistamines handy if you insist on being out there.

Now, let's talk about the Lady Bird Lake Hike-and-Bike Trail. If you were planning a nice, uninterrupted loop, think again. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still wreaking havoc, and it’s not letting up anytime soon. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for construction of a new bridge. If you usually cross there, you'll need to reroute. And don't even get me started on the pedestrian boardwalk ramp along the northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive – that's shut down until 2029. The north shore trail detour isn't going anywhere until at least 2033.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Detours & Navigation Tips:**
The I-35 construction means significant detours on the Lady Bird Lake Hike-and-Bike Trail. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for a new bridge, and the Riverside Drive boardwalk ramp is closed through 2029. Your best bet for crossing the lake if you're east of South Congress is to use the newer Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. Check the Austin Parks and Rec website or The Trail Conservancy for updated maps before you head out, unless you enjoy impromptu detours through downtown traffic.
</AlertBox>

While the Lady Bird Lake trail is the big headache, remember that many of our mountain biking trails, like sections of the Barton Creek Greenbelt, are still predicted "wet" or "observed wet" from recent rains. If you're hitting those, respect the closures and conditions. Riding on wet clay trails just tears them up for everyone else. We've got enough construction, let's not create more.
