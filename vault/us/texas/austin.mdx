## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

Let's just get this out of the way: the Air Quality Index (AQI) today is sitting at a lovely 119, with PM2.5 as our dominant pollutant. That means it's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." And while we might not have a wildfire blazing up 360, this particulate matter often originates from distant wildfire smoke that's decided to grace us with its presence, blanketing regions in hazardous particulate matter.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!** If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or are generally sensitive, stay indoors or keep your outdoor activities extremely light and short. Even healthy individuals might experience some irritation. Today's forecast high is 99°F (37°C), which won't exactly help your lungs either.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Adventures? More Like Trail Detours.

If the air wasn't enough to deter you, the sheer volume of construction around our beloved trails should do the trick. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is basically a maze of orange cones and fencing right now.

Forget a smooth run along the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building. That stretch is rerouted from June 2026 all the way through March 2027 for Austin Energy electrical work. Then there's the ongoing saga of the Longhorn Dam Bridge construction – they just started that beast on July 1st, and it's bringing closures around Canterbury Park, Longhorn Shores, and Krieg Fields. Good luck figuring out where to go there, folks. Plus, access at Willow Street is still closed. And don't even get me started on the I-35 Capital Express Central Project, which has had parts of the pedestrian crossings near Lady Bird Lake closed since February, expected to last a year. The pedestrian ramp at Riverside Drive? Closed through 2029.

And it's not just the Butler Trail taking a hit. If you usually head south, the **Roy G. Guerrero Colorado River Metropolitan Park trail** near the new pedestrian bridge is rerouted until late May 2027. Seriously, just assume there's a detour wherever you go.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Widespread Construction on Popular Trails!** Many sections of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and other city parks have ongoing, long-term closures and detours. Always check the Austin Parks and Recreation website or The Trail Conservancy for specific route changes before you head out. Don't just show up expecting your usual run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider an Indoor Workout:** With high AQI, PM2.5, and moderate pollen, today might be the perfect day to hit a gym with good air filtration, dust off that treadmill, or try a home workout. Give your lungs a break.
</AlertBox>

So, if you absolutely *must* get outside, keep it short, keep it light, and stick to areas you know are clear, if you can find any. Otherwise, embrace the AC and lift something heavy indoors.
