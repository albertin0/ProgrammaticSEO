## The Air Quality Breakdown

That AQI of 119 for PM2.5 isn't just a number; it means there's a significant amount of fine particulate matter floating around. These tiny particles can get deep into your lungs, causing all sorts of trouble, especially if you're asthmatic or have other respiratory issues. And with those winds whipping everything up, it's a double whammy for your airways.

## Trail Talk: Where You *Can't* Go (Easily)

Seriously, if you thought you could just cruise the Lady Bird Lake Trail without interruption, you're dreaming. That I-35 construction is a nightmare. The east-side pedestrian bridge across I-35 is closed for about a year, and the ramp to the Boardwalk near Riverside Drive on the northbound I-35 frontage road is gone until 2029. You're going to be rerouted, dodging construction noise, and generally having a less-than-ideal experience, particularly east of Congress. The "new" Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley is open, at least, if you're out that way.
