### DANGER: Heat Advisory!

Today is not the day to push yourself. A Heat Advisory is in place, with "feels like" temperatures soaring into the middle 90s to lower 100s, potentially hitting 105-110°F in central areas. ## Trail Talk: Austin's Never-Ending Construction Saga

If you're planning on hitting up the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, prepare for some navigational gymnastics. The north shore, specifically near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, has reroutes ongoing until March 2027 for electrical work. And forget about easily crossing the I-35 pedestrian bridge on the east side; it's closed for roughly a year as they build a new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. Your best bet for crossing Lady Bird Lake is still South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley.

Over on the Shoal Creek Trail, it's a hot mess as usual. Sections between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge are closed for maintenance until late September, meaning you'll be detouring onto the sidewalk along N. Lamar Blvd. Plus, that old landslide site between 26th-27th Street is still blocking the trail. Plan accordingly or find another route entirely.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the ACL Fest Zone

While major trails like the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and Barton Creek Greenbelt are *open* during September and October, expect significant parking lot and street closures around the Zilker Park area due to the upcoming ACL Festival. The Great Lawn itself closes September 21st. Access to Barton Springs Pool will also be restricted to the Azie Morton Rd. entrance on festival weekends.
</AlertBox>

## Your PT's Blunt Advice

Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in. But today, the juice isn't worth the squeeze. The heat index alone is enough to send you home with a pounding headache or worse. If you absolutely *must* move your body outside, keep it short, stick to shaded areas, and carry more water than you think you need. Seriously, bring a camelback and chug regularly. Or better yet, find an indoor gym with A/C, or hit up that PhysiYoga workshop at Grounded Performance ATX today. Your body will thank you for not making it fight against 100-degree "feels like" temperatures.
