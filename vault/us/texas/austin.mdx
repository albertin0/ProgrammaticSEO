### Trail Troubles: Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail

If you're dead set on getting outside, be warned: sections of the **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** are still a mess. The north shore, specifically near the Confluence and the Austin Rowing Club building, is narrowed for Austin Water emergency repairs *all month*. And that's not even the big one: a long-term reroute for Austin Energy's underground electrical work means diversions in that same general area (Brazos Street to Trinity Street) are in effect until *March 2027*. Slow down and keep your head on a swivel if you're navigating those sections.
