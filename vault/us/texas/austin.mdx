<BulletList>
*   **Your Lungs Aren't Loving It:** That "Moderate" AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant isn't just a number, it's a real consequence of that Bastrop wildfire that was still burning a few days ago. Your respiratory system is already working harder, even if you can't see the smoke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Muck in the Air
An AQI of 119 with PM2.5 means there's a fair bit of microscopic crud floating around. This stuff gets deep into your lungs. If you have asthma, COPD, or just generally enjoy breathing without a wheeze, consider keeping your run indoors or scaling back the intensity outside. That Bastrop wildfire from earlier this week probably didn't help, even if the visible smoke is gone.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### I-35 Construction is STILL a Nightmare for Runners
Seriously, if your usual route involves the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around the I-35 corridor, be prepared for some serious rerouting. The pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029, and the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year to build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge. You'll be sharing space with traffic or taking lengthy detours. This isn't just an inconvenience; it's a legitimate safety hazard. Stay heads up, wear bright colors, and assume no one in a car sees you. Also, the South Lamar Boulevard corridor project means phased lane closures and detours for you cyclists and pedestrians.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Escape Route
With all the construction messing with the Lady Bird Lake trails, figure out your bail-out points. Know where the open bridges are if you need to cut a loop short. The Wishbone Bridge is open now, which is a blessing, so utilize it if you're trying to complete the full 10-mile loop without swimming. If you're on South Lamar, expect some creative navigation and probably just stick to the sidewalks if possible to avoid the traffic chaos. Don't be a hero; safety over mileage today.
</AlertBox>

Overall, the weather itself is pretty nice and mild, perfect for a long run. But you've got to weigh that against the air quality and the sheer logistical pain-in-the-ass of navigating our city's perpetually-under-construction trails.
