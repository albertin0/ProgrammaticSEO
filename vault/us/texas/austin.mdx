Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in. But if you're planning anything more strenuous than a short, *early* morning stroll, you're flirting with heatstroke. The heat index alone is enough to make a seasoned runner question their life choices. We're talking "near-record highs" and "triple-digit heat index" territory.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Warning!
  Do NOT underestimate today's heat. Forecasted highs around 100°F (38°C) with heat index values up to 110°F (43°C) are dangerous. Limit outdoor activity, especially during peak sun hours (10 AM - 6 PM). Hydrate relentlessly, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. Heat exhaustion and heatstroke are real, and they can put you out of commission for a lot longer than missing one workout.
</AlertBox>

If you insist on hitting the pavement or trails, choose your route wisely. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, while generally a solid choice, has reroutes on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building for electrical work, expected to last through March 2027. Plus, that I-35 pedestrian bridge on the east side has been closed since February for the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge construction. Cross at South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge if you need to switch shores.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Trail & Road Construction Ahead
  Navigating Austin right now is an exercise in patience. Construction is causing detours and closures on major arteries like I-35 and Congress Avenue. Even our beloved Butler Trail has current detours. Be alert for heavy machinery, uneven surfaces, and unexpected reroutes.
</AlertBox>
