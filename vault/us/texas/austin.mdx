## The Rundown: October 13, 2026

Alright, Austin, let's talk about hitting the pavement or the dirt today. The good news is our Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 31, which is "Good." You can breathe easy on that front. The temperature's a balmy 28.4°C (that's about 83°F for those of you clinging to Fahrenheit), under mainly clear skies. Sounds ideal, right? Well, not quite.

### Pollen Pains & Pop-Up Showers

Your lungs might be happy about the AQI, but your nose and throat might have other plans. We're looking at a "Moderate" pollen level today, so if you're prone to seasonal sniffles, you know the drill: pre-medicate, shower post-run, and maybe avoid licking any lampposts.

Also, don't get complacent with the sunshine. While there are no active weather warnings for Austin right now, the forecast mentions isolated to scattered afternoon showers and storms, particularly near and east of I-35, through midweek. Humidity will be a factor, so plan your longer efforts for earlier in the day if you want to dodge the heat *and* potential downpours. A cold front is expected late Thursday into early Friday, which should bring some relief, but for today, it's still sticky and potentially wet.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Watch for Afternoon Storms & Humidity:** Even with clear skies now, scattered showers and thunderstorms are possible this afternoon, especially east of I-35. Hydrate aggressively and be ready for a sudden change in conditions.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Chaos: The I-35 Capital Express Project Strikes Again

Here's where things get *really* interesting, and by interesting, I mean annoying. If your route involves Lady Bird Lake, pay close attention. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still wreaking havoc.

A section of the **Lady Bird Lake south shore boardwalk at I-35 is CLOSED** from October 12-19 to accommodate construction of the new pedestrian bridge. So much for a seamless loop. You *can* still access the trail east of I-35 at South Lakeshore Boulevard or west of I-35. And speaking of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, the north shore section near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building is rerouted through March 2027 for electrical work. The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is also closed for about a year as part of the bridge build-out.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **MAJOR Lady Bird Lake Boardwalk Closure:** The south shore boardwalk at I-35 is closed through October 19. If this is part of your usual route, plan a significant detour or pick another trail. Also, expect ongoing reroutes on the north shore of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail.
</AlertBox>
