### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Inside, Seriously.
Forget that "it's a dry heat" nonsense. Today's forecast is brutal, with highs hitting 104-106°F and heat index values soaring up to 113°F. Even if it felt "only" 28.4°C (83°F) this morning, that's just a cruel joke from Mother Nature. Midday and afternoon runs are a hard pass unless you enjoy debilitating cramps, dizziness, and a trip to the ER. Hydration starts *days* ago, not five minutes before you lace up.

### Trail Troubles: Plan Your Route (and Your Bailout)
If you absolutely *must* hit the trails, be aware that your usual path might be a construction zone. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is a mess in several spots. Long-term utility work west of the I-35 bridges has forced reroutes, which kicked off August 20th and will last about two months. And forget about crossing on the east-side I-35 pedestrian bridge; it's been closed since February 9th and will be for approximately a year. You'll need to use South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley to cross Lady Bird Lake.

Meanwhile, large sections of the **Shoal Creek Trail**, specifically between Shoal Creek Blvd and the Janet Fish Bridge, are closed through late August for repairs. You're rerouted to the N Lamar Blvd sidewalk. There's also an older landslide closure near 26th-27th Street on Shoal Creek. Check AustinTexas.gov/ParkClosures before you head out, unless you enjoy running into orange barricades.

Oh, and there are burn bans in effect for Travis County. So, no casual bonfires on your post-run cool down.
