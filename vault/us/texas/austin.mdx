## The Full Breakdown

### Weather Woes

While the sun might be peeking through *this very moment*, don't get comfortable. Austin's weather is about to shift dramatically. Forecasters are calling for a "wetter weather pattern" starting today, bringing heavy rain and thunderstorms with a significant flood risk for Central Texas. We're talking 70-85% chance of rain, with some areas potentially seeing several inches. Any storms that do pop up could deliver "brief, heavy tropical downpours," making trails and roads slick and potentially hazardous for runners and cyclists. Humidity is going to be high, so even if you manage to avoid a downpour, you'll be swimming through the air.

### Construction Chaos & Trail Closures

This is where your outdoor workout plans really hit a snag. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is a minefield of detours and closures.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Closure Alert: Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail**
Today, July 13, 2026, and tomorrow, a section of the Butler Trail from the pedestrian bridge over Barton Creek to where Barton Creek meets Lady Bird Lake (on the east shore) is CLOSED for tree work. You'll be rerouted onto city streets like Barton Springs Road, Sterzing Street, Toomey Road, and Butler Shores. Expect slower going and watch for vehicle traffic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Another Butler Trail Narrowing!**
The north shore of the Butler Trail, near The Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, is narrowed for Austin Water emergency repairs throughout July. The Confluence lattice bridge, which connects the Waterloo Greenway to the Butler Trail, is also completely shut down. Slow your roll significantly through this area and pay attention to staff instructions.
</AlertBox>

And it doesn't stop there. Long-term construction continues to plague the trail:
*   A multi-month reroute is ongoing on the north shore near The Confluence for Austin Energy electrical work, expected to last until March 2027.
*   The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing is closed for about a year as part of the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge construction. You can still use the west side, but no crossing between north and south shores on the east of I-35.
*   The pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029.

Seriously, if your run depends on hitting a specific segment of the Butler Trail today, double-check your route or just pick another day. It's a mess out there.
