<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory Incoming!**
While the morning feels decent, temperatures are forecast to hit the low 90s, with "feels like" temperatures nearing 100°F today, and even higher into the weekend (101-102°F Friday-Saturday). This isn't the time for personal bests in the midday sun. Hydrate, wear light clothing, and seriously, consider that early morning run or waiting until sundown. If you're out there between 10 AM and 6 PM, you're just asking for trouble. Above-average heat is expected for the next 7 days.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**ACL Festival Prep Around Zilker Park**
Heads up, especially if your usual route takes you through Zilker. The Great Lawn is getting prepped for Austin City Limits and will be closed from September 21st through October 18th. While the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail and the Barton Creek Greenbelt remain open, expect some significant parking and street closures in the Zilker area during the festival weekends (early October). Plan your access points now to avoid driving in circles and adding to your stress levels before you even start moving.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**I-35 Construction Continues to Be Austin's Favorite Pastime**
If your workout involves cycling near I-35 or navigating its underpasses, be aware of ongoing overnight construction. Rolling closures and flyover closures are happening nightly on various sections of I-35, including SH 71 and Ben White Boulevard, and the Stassney Lane bridge is also closing overnight tonight and tomorrow night for work on elevated managed lanes. There's also long-term utility work on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail west of the I-35 bridges, which means reroutes are in effect until early 2027. Stay alert, stay safe, and maybe just stick to the inner trails for a bit.
</AlertBox>

The good news? No active wildfires dumping smoke on us today, and the AQI is holding strong. But honestly, the heat is the real hazard here. Don't push it, Austin. Your body will thank you, and so will I, a PT who's tired of seeing preventable heat exhaustion cases.
