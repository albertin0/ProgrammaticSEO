<AlertBox type="warning">
## Lady Bird Lake Trail Detours & Congestion
If your plan was a serene loop around the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, think again. The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still underway, closing the east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing for the next year. You'll need to use the west-side I-35 sidewalk, or detours via South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley if you want to cross the lake east of downtown. On top of that, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed until 2029. Expect major disruptions and reroutes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
## Plan Your Downtown Runs Carefully
It’s a busy Saturday with the ATX Crawfish Kickoff Festival happening at Republic Square from 3 PM to 10 PM, and an "Adult Field Day" event hitting Zilker Park at 3 PM. Plus, there's a "NO KINGS 3 ATX" protest event scheduled for 1 PM to 4 PM, likely impacting downtown areas. If your route takes you through these popular spots, anticipate significant crowds, potential road or sidewalk closures, and general mayhem. Maybe stick to less central routes or hit the weights today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Moderate Pollen Alert
Ah, spring in Austin. Your eyes are probably already itchy. With moderate pollen levels, if you're susceptible, you'll feel it. Keep those antihistamines handy and maybe opt for an indoor workout if your allergies usually send you into a sneezing fit after a mile. It’s not debilitating for everyone, but certainly not helping your perceived effort in this heat.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. Between the heat, the pollen, and the city actively trying to rebuild itself in every direction, running outside is a logistical puzzle. Be smart, stay safe, and maybe just hit up a local coffee shop instead.
