Get out there, Austin, but know your route because the trails are a hot mess of races and construction today.

*   Pollen is moderate, mostly tree pollen, so grab your antihistamines if you're prone to the cedar fever aftermath or any other spring sniffles.

Today's a mixed bag of glorious weather and logistical nightmares for your outdoor workout. While your lungs will thank you for the clean air, your joints might curse you if you don't plan ahead. It's prime racing season, apparently, because Austin is hosting *two* significant running events today: the "Rogue Trail Series - The Maze," a serious trail run happening in Travis County, and the "Race to Cure Sarcoma" 5K. This means that some of your favorite routes, especially off-road, might be busier or have temporary closures you weren't expecting.

But the races are just the tip of the iceberg. Our beloved **Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail** is perpetually under construction, and today is no different. Expect ongoing detours near Barton Creek, rerouting you through Toomey Road and the baseball diamonds. And don't forget the long-term detour near Lou Neff Road; you'll be sharing the road with vehicles there for another few weeks. If you usually cross Lady Bird Lake on the east side near I-35, tough luck – that pedestrian bridge is closed through at least 2029 for the Capital Express project. Use the South Congress bridge or the newer Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley if you want to get across. Oh, and Shoal Creek Trail north of 24th Street? Still indefinitely closed due to a landslide.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Congestion & Detours**: With two major races (Rogue Trail Series and Race to Cure Sarcoma) happening today, expect higher-than-usual traffic and potential localized closures on various trails and city streets. Always stay aware of your surroundings and any race marshals. Also, the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail has multiple long-term detours in effect, especially near Barton Creek, Lou Neff Road, and the I-35 crossing.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**I-35 Mainlane Closure**: If your route involves getting to or from South Austin, be aware that the Northbound I-35 mainlanes from Slaughter Lane to SH 71/Ben White Boulevard are closed until 9 a.m. this morning for construction. This is a major traffic headache and could impact access to certain areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Precaution**: While the AQI is fantastic, moderate pollen levels might still bother allergy sufferers. Take your medication *before* heading out to keep those sneezes and watery eyes at bay.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, Barton Springs Pool officially reopens today after its extended maintenance closure, so if you want to combine a run with a refreshing dip, that's an option after 9 am!

The bottom line? It's a gorgeous day to be active outside, but you absolutely need to know your intended path. Don't just head out blindly, or you'll be rerouting more than you're running.
