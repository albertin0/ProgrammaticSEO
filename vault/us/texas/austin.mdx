<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Apocalypse: I-35 Construction Edition
Don't even *think* about your usual seamless trot around Lady Bird Lake without checking The Trail Conservancy's detours first. The I-35 Capital Express Central project is a beast, and it's eating up parts of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail. Expect closures, reduced parking, and general chaos, especially around the I-35 bridges and Riverside Drive. The west-side I-35 sidewalk should still be open, but the east-side pedestrian crossing over Lady Bird Lake is a no-go for roughly a year. Plan your route to cross at South Congress or use the newer Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Folks
It's going to be warm. Not inferno-hot, but warm enough that you'll be sweating more than you think. Bring more water than you usually would, especially if you're hitting those longer stretches. Nothing ruins a good workout faster than feeling like a dried-out prune.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Wildfire Watch: The Constant Threat
While Austin itself isn't actively burning today, don't get complacent. Central Texas continues to grapple with an elevated wildfire risk, a lingering issue from ongoing drought conditions earlier in the year. A Community Wildfire Protection Plan meeting is even scheduled for tomorrow, April 8th, highlighting the persistent concern. Keep an eye on local news for any sudden developments. This isn't the day to be tossing lit cigarette butts (seriously, never) or doing anything else that could spark a blaze.
</AlertBox>

Look, the conditions are pretty good for getting outside, especially compared to some of the absolute nightmares we've seen this year. Just be smart about the detours, stay hydrated, and remember that "moderate pollen" in Austin usually means "less likely to make you cry." Enjoy the clear skies while they last.
