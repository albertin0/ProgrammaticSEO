## The Rundown

Alright, Austin, let's cut to the chase. The weather gods are smiling on us today. Clear skies, mild temperatures, and an Air Quality Index of 39, with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant, means your lungs are in for a treat. South winds at 5 to 10 mph will keep things moving without blowing you into Lake Travis. This is basically prime running weather, so no excuses there.

But before you lace up and head out the door, let's talk about the elephant on the trail: **construction**. It's everywhere, and it's making a mess of our beloved paths. If your go-to is the **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake**, prepare for some significant diversions and potential headaches.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### I-35 Crossing Closed
The east-side I-35 pedestrian crossing over Lady Bird Lake has been closed since early February for about a year as part of the I-35 Capital Express Central Project. You won't be able to cross between the north and south shores on the east side of I-35. Your best bets for crossing are the South Congress bridge or the brand-spankin'-new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which thankfully opened just last month. Don't just show up expecting to loop the whole lake unimpeded.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Riverside Drive Ramp Closure
Also impacting the Lady Bird Lake area, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is shut down through 2029. The staircase and the Boardwalk are technically open, but expect intermittent closures. You'll need to use other access points from nearby South Lakeshore Boulevard east of I-35 or the trail entrance west of I-35.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Lou Neff Road Detour
There's a short-term detour near Lou Neff Road that began mid-February for maintenance. Trail users are being routed onto Lou Neff Road itself, and parking in that area is closed. Just be extra cautious sharing the road if you're in that neck of the woods.
</AlertBox>

If you're heading north, the **Shoal Creek Trail** is also undergoing some repairs north of Pease Park, specifically from Gaston Avenue to the Janet Fish Bridge. Expect some localized sidewalk and trail closures there too. It's a good thing, ultimately, for improved trail conditions, but a pain in the short term.

Further afield, the South Lamar Boulevard corridor is a mess of ongoing projects. The Mary Street roundabout should be wrapping up this month, but storm drain improvements continue through 2027. Basically, assume construction is happening anywhere near a major roadway in Austin right now.
