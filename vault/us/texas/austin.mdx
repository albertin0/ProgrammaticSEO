## Lungs: Pollen Bomb & Ozone Haze

Today might *feel* clear and crisp, with temperatures sitting around 15.3°C (that's nearly 60°F for the non-metric crowd), but your respiratory system is in for a shock. While the overall pollen level is officially "Moderate", dig a little deeper, and you'll find tree pollen is absolutely crushing it at "Very High" levels, with oak and alder leading the charge. If you've got even a hint of seasonal sniffles, expect full-blown misery if you push it too hard outside.

The air quality, sitting at an AQI of 61, is also squarely in the "Moderate" category, primarily due to ozone. This means sensitive groups might experience some discomfort, and even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exposure can still make your lungs work harder than they should. Don't be surprised if your usual route feels like you're running through thick soup.

## Joints & Trails: A Maze of Closures and Crowd Control

While the weather itself is pleasant for a run, actually *finding* a clear path is going to be your biggest challenge.

### Lady Bird Lake & Downtown Shenanigans

The I-35 Capital Express Central project is wreaking havoc on our most beloved running spot. The pedestrian bridge over I-35 on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is shut down for the long haul, expected to last until at least 2029. You'll need to reroute, with the South Congress bridge being your best bet for crossing Lady Bird Lake. There's also a new "Wishbone" bridge near Longhorn Dam that recently opened, which might offer some relief on the east side.

Adding to the chaos, SXSW is still consuming downtown Austin, leading to multiple street closures and massive crowds. Forget trying to hit your pace on Red River Street or Sixth Street; they're basically pedestrian-only zones for festival-goers. The Sips & Sounds Music Festival at Auditorium Shores today also means that entire section of the trail will be swamped.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**I-35 Trail Closure:** The I-35 pedestrian bridge over Lady Bird Lake is CLOSED. Plan your Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail route accordingly and expect major detours. You'll be crossing at South Congress or hoping that new Wishbone bridge works out.
</AlertBox>

### Other Notable Nuisances

If you're typically a Barton Springs Pool regular, sorry, Charlie. The pool is closed for maintenance until March 21st. And over on the Shoal Creek Trail, there's a persistent long-term closure between 26th-27th Street due to landslides, diverting traffic to N Lamar Blvd. More construction is happening on the 24th-34th Street stretch, so expect ongoing disruptions there too.

<AlertBox type="info">
**SXSW & Sips & Sounds Impact:** If your usual workout takes you downtown or near Auditorium Shores, find an alternative route. Those areas are teeming with festival crowds and road closures today.
</AlertBox>

So, if you brave the outdoors today, pick your poison: a lung-burning pollenfest on the trails or a congested, detour-filled urban adventure. Good luck, you're going to need it.
