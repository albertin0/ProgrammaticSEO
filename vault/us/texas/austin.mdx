### Trail Chaos on Lady Bird Lake

If the air wasn't enough, let's talk about actually *moving* around this city. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, our beloved urban loop, is currently a patchwork of "nope." TxDOT crews are doing long-term utility work west of the I-35 bridges, which means detours are rerouting folks for the next couple of months. And if you typically cross on the east side of I-35, that pedestrian crossing has been shut down since February 2026 for bridge construction. You'll be hoofing it all the way to South Congress or the Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge if you want to get across the lake. Don't even get me started on the north shore near the Austin Rowing Club; more reroutes there for electrical work, through next March.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect significant detours and potential hazards on the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail. Pay attention to signage (if you can find it) and be prepared for unexpected changes to your route, especially near I-35 and the Austin Rowing Club.
</AlertBox>

Oh, and Zilker's Great Lawn? Parts of it are closed for aeration and fertilizer. Your idyllic picnic spot or dog-walking paradise might be off-limits. Parking lots are open, but watch out for equipment.

### What About the Heat?

It feels decent now at 78°F, but that's just a cruel trick. We're on track for a high of 99°F today, and September is forecast to be warmer than average. No real rain chances, either. While there's talk of some "Austin Fires" out in Oregon, which are spewing smoke and causing air quality alerts there, we don't have direct reports of that smoke choking us out here in Texas today. However, localized fine particulate matter from light morning winds is a concern. Just remember that Austin has a significant wildfire risk, and conditions are ripe.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you absolutely *must* get outside, keep it short, keep it easy, and stick to areas you know are clear. Hydrate like it's your job, and don't push through any respiratory discomfort. Your long run can wait.
</AlertBox>
