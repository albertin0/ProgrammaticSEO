## Why today is/isn't the day.

<BulletList>
  *   **Air Quality is Rough:** Your lungs are not going to thank you today. Austin's Air Quality Index (AQI) is hitting 119, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, putting us firmly in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" category. If you've got any respiratory issues, or even if you don't, you'll feel this. The city's air quality tends to tank in September anyway, so this isn't exactly a shock. Add to that the lingering smoke from brush fires along Onion Creek reported just a few days ago, and you've got a recipe for a cough.
  *   **Trail Treachery:** Good luck trying to get a smooth run on some of our favorite spots. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is a minefield of detours and closures. TxDOT's I-35 Capital Express Central Project has the east-side pedestrian bridge across I-35 closed until next year, forcing you to find alternative crossings at South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge. Plus, a section on the north shore near the Austin Rowing Club building is rerouted for utility work until March 2027. Don't even get me started on the Shoal Creek Trail, which is shut down between Shoal Creek Blvd. and the Janet Fish Bridge until November for maintenance.
  *   **Event Obstacles:** If Walter E. Long Metropolitan Park was on your agenda, forget about it. The Cal Tri Austin event has the park closed for general access from 6 AM to 1 PM today. Good for the triathletes, bad for your morning long run.
</BulletList>

  With an AQI of 119 and PM2.5 as the main culprit, it’s not just a little hazy out there – it's genuinely unhealthy for sensitive groups. <AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Construction Chaos on the Butler Trail
  Seriously, if you're planning on running the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, be prepared for some serious navigational challenges. Multiple sections, particularly around I-35 and on the north shore, are impacted by long-term construction. Expect detours, slow zones, and workers. Pay attention to signage, or better yet, find an alternate route.
</AlertBox>
