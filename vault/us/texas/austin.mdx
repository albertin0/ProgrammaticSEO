### Air Quality & Pollen Rundown

Today's air quality is the main villain. An AQI of 119 due to PM2.5 means "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" is the official designation. This isn't ideal for anyone, but if you're in a sensitive group (kids, older adults, people with heart or lung conditions), seriously reconsider that high-intensity outdoor session. AccuWeather confirms Austin's air quality as "Poor" today, meaning health effects can be felt immediately by sensitive groups, and they should reduce time spent outside. While there are no major wildfire smoke events directly impacting Austin today, persistent pollutants are a real concern.

### Weather Woes (or lack thereof)

Temperature-wise, it's actually not bad for mid-October in Austin. We're looking at a high around 28.4°C (83°F), mainly clear skies, and light winds hovering around 2-5 mph. The cold snap from the weekend is gone, and while we're not quite at "melting on the asphalt" temperatures yet, it's certainly warming up, with forecasts showing near-90-degree temps by next weekend. Hydration is always key, but today, it's more about protecting your lungs than fending off heatstroke.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro-Tip: Hydration Still Matters!** Even with manageable temperatures, you're still in Austin. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Local Annoyances

Alright, let's talk trails. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail is still dealing with its ongoing identity crisis. As of today, October 19, 2026, there's a short-term detour on the south shore between the Pfluger Pedestrian Bridge and the MoPac Colorado River Railroad Bridge. You'll be rerouted onto the W. Riverside Drive sidewalk, so keep an eye out and share the path. And if you're a regular on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club, that long-term detour for electrical work is still active through next March.

The great news? Zilker Park's main areas—including the Barton Creek Greenbelt, Barton Springs Pool, and the disk golf course—are fully open and ready for action, now that the ACL Fest mudbath has, mostly, dried up and packed out. So if you're dodging construction, that's a good escape route.

**Heads Up, Lady Bird Lake Runners!** Expect detours on the south side of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail today, particularly between Pfluger and MoPac. Don't go on autopilot; watch for signage and be prepared for sidewalk running along W. Riverside Drive.
