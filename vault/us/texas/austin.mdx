## Today's Vibe: Hot, Bumpy, and Best Before Sunrise

Alright, Austin, let's cut to the chase. If your idea of a good outdoor workout involves anything beyond a leisurely dog walk, you better have finished it hours ago. The mercury is going to climb like a terrified squirrel up a pecan tree, hitting an actual temperature of 99°F later, but more importantly, that "heat index" is flirting with the triple digits. Your body isn't designed to perform optimally when it feels like a damp sauna, and pushing it in these conditions is a fast track to heat exhaustion, or worse. Hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's a lifeline. And by hydration, I mean water, not that artisanal kombucha you bought at Whole Foods.

### Trail Updates & Roadblocks

Good luck trying to find an uninterrupted stride on some of our most beloved paths. The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** is still dealing with its ongoing identity crisis. Expect reroutes on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building through March 2027 as Austin Energy buries some electrical guts. And if you usually cross the I-35 pedestrian bridge, forget about it. That east-side crossing has been closed since February 2026 for about a year to build the new Lady Bird Lake Bridge as part of the massive I-35 Capital Express Central Project. Your alternatives are South Congress or the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley. Willow Street access to the trail? Closed for construction too, for what feels like an eternity (64 months, to be exact).
