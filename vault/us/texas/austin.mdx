## The Rundown: Breathe Easy, But Watch Your Step

### Weather & Air Quality
It’s a "Mainly Clear" day with the mercury heading towards a toasty 90°F (32°C) this afternoon. That's not "danger zone" territory yet, but it's definitely "carry-extra-water-and-don't-even-think-about-noon-mile-repeats" weather. Humidity is hanging around 67%, so it’ll feel a bit sticky as the day progresses. The good news for your lungs is that the Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a low 27, meaning the air is clean and clear of major pollutants.

<AlertBox type="warning">
That rising heat is no joke, Austin. Get your miles in early or wait until sundown. The CDC recommends avoiding intense activity during the middle of the day when the sun is strongest, and for good reason. Dehydration and heat stress your heart, especially if you’re not used to it.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Trouble Ahead: Construction, Closures, and Reroutes Galore

Here’s where it gets real, folks. The trails, especially around Lady Bird Lake, are a constant puzzle, and today is no exception.

<AlertBox type="danger">
The **Shoal Creek Trail** is still largely a mess. The section near the Fifth Street Bridge is closed through May 29th for pipe repairs after a fire. Plus, the stretch from Shoal Creek Blvd. to the Janet Fish Bridge is shut down until late June, and the 24th to 29th along N. Lamar is closed indefinitely due to a landslide. If Shoal Creek is your jam, you’re out of luck on several fronts.
</AlertBox>

The **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** also has its share of headaches. Access at Willow Street is blocked indefinitely due to the ongoing 98 Red River project. And heads up for next month: starting in June, a significant portion of the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club will be rerouted until March 2027 for Austin Energy's underground electrical work. While the Butler Shores renovation is thankfully complete and those detours are gone, the I-35 Capital Express Central Project has the pedestrian bridge over I-35 closed for about a year (since February). You’ll need to cross Lady Bird Lake at South Congress or use the Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which, good news, opened back in February.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect construction activity just north of the **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** near Brazos Street, starting around Cesar Chavez. Austin Energy crews are working weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., sometimes on weekends, for about a year. While they say it's not a trail reroute, expect heavy equipment and keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
If you’re planning a run on the Butler Trail, check the Trail Conservancy's website for the latest detours. It’s practically a full-time job keeping up with all the changes. And seriously, hydrate more than you think you need to.
</AlertBox>
