## Today's Outdoor Grind: Good Air, Bad Luck for Zilker Runners

Look, the environmental data today is pretty sweet. That 16.3°C (roughly 61°F) and clear skies? Perfection for logging miles. And an AQI of 31 is practically unheard of, meaning your lungs get a well-deserved break from any nasty pollutants. Even wildfire smoke, which usually plagues us this time of year, isn't a factor today. So, if you manage to find an open stretch of pavement or dirt, breathe deep and enjoy it.

However, if your Saturday ritual involves Zilker Park, you're in for a rude awakening. It's the second weekend of Austin City Limits, and that means the entire area around Zilker is a no-go zone for normal recreational activity. Forget finding parking, forget a clear path, and definitely forget keeping your shoes clean. After last weekend's rain, parts of Zilker Park, particularly the Great Lawn, are reportedly a torn-up mud pit. Seriously, if you *must* venture near the festival, prepare for a slog. Barton Springs Pool is still open, but only accessible via the Azie Morton Road entrance, and expect heavy traffic.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**ACL Festival Chaos at Zilker Park!** Today marks Weekend 2 of Austin City Limits. Avoid Zilker Park and surrounding streets like Barton Springs Road unless you have a festival ticket and are prepared for major crowds, traffic gridlock, parking nightmares, and thick mud. Your usual running routes through the park are completely compromised.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the festival frenzy, construction is still our constant companion. The Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, while generally open, has sections west of the I-35 bridges with ongoing utility work causing reroutes. Also, expect reroutes on the north shore near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building due to electrical infrastructure work well into next year. The Shoal Creek Trail is also partially closed for maintenance until November, with detours sending you onto busy N. Lamar.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail & Road Construction Continues.** Expect detours and potential slowdowns on segments of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, especially near I-35 and on the north shore. The Shoal Creek Trail also has a long-term closure in effect. Plan your routes in advance and stay alert for construction signage and active work zones, including some concrete work happening today.
</AlertBox>
