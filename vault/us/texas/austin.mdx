**Air Quality:**

The Air Quality Index (AQI) for Austin is a decent 41 today, which falls squarely in the "Good" category. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5. That's all fine and dandy, but don't get complacent. We're rolling into June, which means those "Ozone Action Day" warnings are going to start popping up with more frequency as the heat builds and light winds trap ground-level ozone. For today, though, your lungs should be pretty happy.

**Weather Check:**

Average temperatures for June in Austin usually hover between 22-33°C (71-89°F), so expect it to keep heating up through the month.

### Local Hazards & Hot Takes

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Reroute on the Butler Trail!** Listen up, Lady Bird Lake regulars: Starting *today*, June 2, 2026, and running all the way through March 2027, a significant portion of the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail on the north shore is being rerouted. This is for Austin Energy to install underground electrical infrastructure. Slow your roll near the Confluence and the Austin Rowing Club building, and for Pete's sake, follow the signs and any on-site staff instructions. Don't be that runner who acts surprised.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**I-35 Construction Continues to Be a Pain:** The I-35 Capital Express Central Project is still a beast, and it's messing with your Lady Bird Lake crossings. The east-side I-35 pedestrian bridge has been closed since February 9, 2026, for a new bridge build, and it'll be out for about a year. If you usually cross there, plan on using the South Congress bridge or hitting up the new Wishbone Pedestrian Bridge at Pleasant Valley, which thankfully opened back in February. Also, the pedestrian ramp to the Boardwalk along northbound I-35 frontage road at Riverside Drive is closed through 2029. This ain't a short-term inconvenience, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pease Park Treehouse Closure:** If you were planning on taking the kiddos (or yourself, no judgment) to the Treehouse at Pease Park today, it's closed. Staff are replacing the top net.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Afternoon Thunderstorms:** Keep an eye on the radar, especially if you're planning a late afternoon or evening run. Scattered thunderstorms mean things can change fast, and nobody wants to be caught in a downpour on the back half of their long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Everywhere:** Downtown Austin is basically one giant construction zone right now with the I-35 reconstruction, Austin Convention Center expansion, and Waterloo Greenway work. This means more traffic, more noise, and general chaos. If your route takes you through downtown, be extra vigilant.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, Austin. Don't let a reroute or a sudden storm derail your workout.
