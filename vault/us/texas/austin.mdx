## Your Run Today: Sunny, Sweaty, and Strategically Detoured

It’s Saint Patrick’s Day, but don’t let the green beer distract you from the fact that Austin is serving up a great day for outdoor activity, temperature-wise. With the mercury pushing 28.4°C and not a cloud in the sky, you'd think it was perfect. And for your lungs, it pretty much is; an AQI of 38 is practically pristine. Just remember to hydrate like it's your job, especially with that sun beating down. Even in March, Austin sun is no joke.

The "Moderate" pollen count means most of you allergy sufferers might just get away with a sniffle or two, especially compared to the full-on cedar fever we often endure. So, that's a win.

However, where Austin always manages to throw a wrench in our plans is with its perpetual state of construction. You'd think we were building Rome in a day around here.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail Detours & Closures

The Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake is still a minefield of detours, thanks to the "Confluence" project near Waller Creek, which isn't slated for completion until *2026*. This means you're contending with active construction zones from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on weekdays, and sometimes on weekends. Don't be surprised by equipment or temporary fencing.

More significantly, the **I-35 pedestrian bridge** is closed as of the week of February 9, 2026, due to the TxDOT I-35 Capital Express project. You absolutely *cannot* cross Lady Bird Lake on the east side of I-35 right now, and this is expected to last until at least 2029. Your best bet for crossing is the South Congress bridge if you're trying to hit both sides of the lake. There's also a detour along Barton Creek that'll reroute you through Toomey Road and some baseball diamonds, also expected to last until 2026. Plan your long runs accordingly, folks.

Also, don't forget the **Shoal Creek Trail** section between 24th and 29th along North Lamar is still indefinitely closed due to landslide damage. Just avoid it entirely unless you're looking for an impromptu obstacle course.
</AlertBox>
