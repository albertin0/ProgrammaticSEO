## Lungs & Joints Breakdown

### Air Quality
The AQI at 53 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant means there's a fair amount of tiny particles floating around. These aren't your lungs' best friends, especially when you're sucking wind on a run. While it's not red alert, it's definitely not pristine mountain air either. Take it easy and listen to your body. Don't push through any unusual chest tightness or coughing.

### Pollen Report

You know the drill. Moderate pollen means some of us are going to be feeling it. Ash, mulberry, and cedar/juniper pollen are often the culprits around here. If your eyes get itchy and your nose starts running just thinking about being outside, consider an indoor workout or stick to shorter, less intense outdoor sessions today.

### Weather Conditions
Temperature-wise, it's a pretty sweet 17.1°C (about 63°F) and "Mainly Clear." Perfect running weather if you ask me, assuming you can handle the air and pollen. Dress in layers, though, because Laredo weather can surprise you faster than a rogue fire ant.
