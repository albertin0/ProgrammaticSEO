Look, the current air quality is registering a "Good" 40 AQI, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. However, before you go thinking you're in the clear, the local air quality forecast is less optimistic. They're talking about light to moderate winds potentially kicking up particulate matter from regional burnings and urban emissions, pushing PM10 into "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" and PM2.5 into "Moderate" ranges in parts of the El Paso area. So, if your lungs are particularly whiny, don't be surprised if they feel it later today, especially if you're huffing it around the dusty areas near the Franklin Mountains.

The real killer today, literally, is the heat. While it's 24.4°C (76°F) this morning, temperatures are projected to climb to 98°F (37°C) today, and we're looking at 100°F+ later this week. This isn't just "a little warm" — it's "your organs might stop working" warm. There are currently no active weather warnings, watches, or advisories for El Paso, but that doesn't mean you should be stupid.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is a Real Problem, Not a Myth
With daytime highs pushing near 100°F, extreme heat is a serious hazard. Hydrate constantly, before, during, and after your workout. Seriously, don't wait until you're thirsty. Consider moving your outdoor activity to the very early morning or late evening. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or your head is pounding, stop immediately and get somewhere cool. This isn't a badge of honor; it's a fast track to the ER.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget the never-ending symphony of jackhammers and detour signs. Construction is still a major pain point. So, depending on how that goes, that area could either stay a mess, or become a different kind of mess.

Meanwhile, if you're trying to get anywhere on the east side, the Horizon/Darrington Reconstruction Project has continuous closures until September. Expect alternating lane closures on Horizon Boulevard and Darrington Road daily from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. And for those navigating I-10, TxDOT has daily lane closures scheduled until Friday, from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., especially around the Transmountain area. Good luck with that commute, let alone your run.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye (and Ear) Out for Construction
Widespread roadwork means unpredictable detours and potential hazards for runners and cyclists. Always check local traffic updates before heading out. Don't assume a route is clear just because it was yesterday. This goes doubly for areas like the I-10 corridors and Horizon Boulevard.
</AlertBox>
