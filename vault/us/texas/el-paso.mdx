### Mind the Moderate Air & Pollen Mix
With a moderate AQI and moderate pollen, this isn't the day to push yourself to a new PR if you're an asthma sufferer or have respiratory issues. Consider scaling back intensity or duration. If you're tackling something like the Resler Canyon Loop, remember that elevation can exacerbate breathing difficulties.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration and Timing are Key
Get your workout in earlier rather than later. By midday, that desert sun is going to be cooking. Drink more water than you think you need, especially if you're out for an extended period. Electrolytes aren't a bad idea either.
</AlertBox>
