### Construction & Trail Troubles

If you were planning on tackling the popular White Rock Trail, know that a significant stretch is currently closed for construction. Crews are working on a wastewater main between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, and that closure is expected to last until September. They've even closed E. Lawther Drive to vehicular traffic in that section to help with trail detours, so expect some routing changes if you insist on going out there. It's always something with these trails, isn't it? Beyond that, TxDOT has plenty of other major road projects happening, like the I-30 Canyon Improvement, so if you're driving to your workout spot, plan for potential delays.

<AlertBox type="warning">
    **White Rock Trail Closure:** Avoid the section of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. Construction is active, and it's simply not open. Look for alternate routes or consider a different park today.
</AlertBox>
