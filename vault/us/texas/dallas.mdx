### Extreme Heat Warning!
An extreme heat warning is in effect until 7 PM today. Forecasted highs for Dallas are still sizzling, with heat indices pushing 110°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Dallas Fire and Rescue has been out on courses for events, checking on runners, and for good reason. Seriously, nobody needs a heat stroke medal.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ozone Action Day in Effect
While the general AQI is "Good" at 46, today is an Ozone Action Day. This means ground-level ozone could reach levels "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" in parts of the DFW area. If you have asthma, COPD, or are just generally sensitive, consider moving your workout indoors or sticking to very early mornings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### White Rock Lake Trail & Fire Risk
Good news for those who frequent the **White Rock Lake Trail**! The construction for the abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road and E. Lawther, which caused closures earlier this summer, was completed ahead of schedule and the trail reopened on August 7th. So, no detours there. However, the extreme dry conditions mean elevated fire concerns persist region-wide. Be extra cautious if you're out in any wilder areas around the lake or other natural trails. Don't be the person who starts a grass fire.
</AlertBox>

Listen, I love hitting the pavement or trails as much as the next runner, but today is not the day to prove anything to anyone. The heat alone is enough to knock you down, and adding an Ozone Action Day into the mix just makes it irresponsible to push hard outdoors. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because it just might. If you absolutely *have* to get moving outside, go before the sun really cranks up, stay in the shade, and keep it short and light. Better yet, find a gym with some A/C. Your lungs and joints will thank you later.
