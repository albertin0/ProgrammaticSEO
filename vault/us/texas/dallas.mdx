## The Rundown: Your Lungs vs. Dallas Air Today

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next runner, but today, your lungs are going to be working overtime. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) slapped us with an Ozone Action Day for the Dallas-Fort Worth area, and that's not a badge of honor. We're talking "Orange" category air quality, meaning it's unhealthy for sensitive groups, including kids, the elderly, and anyone with existing respiratory issues. Even if you think you're tougher than a two-dollar steak, ozone exposure is no joke. It's caused by a lovely cocktail of pollution, high heat, sunshine, and light winds, all of which we've got in spades today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**OZONE ACTION DAY!**
Seriously, stay indoors if you can, especially during peak afternoon hours. If you *must* be outside, keep your activity light and short. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Watch Your Step (and Your Wallet, Probably)

If you're still determined to get outside, be aware of what's happening on the ground. Construction crews are still at it near **White Rock Trail**, specifically working on a wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. It started back in June and it's ongoing, so don't expect a smooth ride if you're trying to bike or run that section. And for anyone thinking of running downtown, remember that the I-30 Canyon project has major road closures, including Cesar Chavez Blvd, Cadiz St, and Akard St, with no pedestrian access in the closed work zones.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Zone Ahead!**
Expect detours and potential hazards on sections of White Rock Trail due to ongoing utility work. If you're near downtown, the I-30 Canyon project has significant road closures and no pedestrian access. Plan your route accordingly.
</AlertBox>
