Let's be blunt: running in Dallas today is a terrible idea for most people. The actual temperature is already a roasting 32.7°C (about 91°F), and the "feels like" temperature is climbing well into the triple digits, expected to hit at least 107°F by this afternoon. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's actively dangerous. We're talking heat exhaustion, heat stroke – the whole grim lineup. The National Weather Service has issued warnings for a reason. This isn't just a weekend thing either; expect this kind of misery to stick around well into next week.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!** Do not underestimate the danger of today's temperatures. Heat index values are dangerously high. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. If you must be outside, stay in the shade, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body. Seriously, this isn't a day for PRs on the Katy Trail.
</AlertBox>

On top of the killer heat, our lungs are dealing with a bit of a Texas special. The Air Quality Index is currently moderate at 57, with PM2.5 as the main offender. But don't let that "moderate" fool you into thinking it's clean air. Forecasts for the Dallas-Fort Worth area today show ozone also reaching the lower to middle end of the moderate range. Plus, there's residual smoke from wildfires far away (Canada, Mexico, East Texas) and some light Saharan dust hanging out, largely aloft but still contributing to fine particulate matter due to elevated humidity. Combine that with gasping for air in 100+ degree weather, and your respiratory system is going to be working overtime.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Trail Section CLOSED:** If your usual route takes you along White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, find a new plan. This section is completely closed for wastewater main construction until September 2026. Detours are posted, but don't just blindly follow your usual path.
</AlertBox>
