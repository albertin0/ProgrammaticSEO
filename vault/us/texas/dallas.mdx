## The Deceptive Calm Before the Storm

Seriously, April 3rd might start off looking like prime running weather, but don't get complacent. The forecast for Friday afternoon and evening is grim, with storms expected to bring strong winds, heavy rain, and the risk of localized flooding across the metroplex. This isn't just inconvenient; it's genuinely hazardous. Running in heavy rain with gusting winds is a recipe for a slip-and-slide straight to my PT office, or worse, a lightning strike. And if you're out on a bike, forget about it. Visibility will be shot, and those crosswinds are no joke.

Air quality-wise, the AQI is a respectable 29, which is good. However, our friends at the American Lung Association consistently remind us that Dallas-Fort Worth is unfortunately one of the most ozone-polluted metro areas in the country in the long term, though today's ozone levels are predicted to be "Good." Still, breathe deep *before* the storm, because those heavy rains might wash out some pollutants, but they'll also make everything slippery.
