## The Full Sweat and Tears Breakdown

Look, I get it. The sun's out, the sky is mainly clear, and your running shoes are calling. But let's be brutally honest: Mother Nature is not on your side today if you're planning a strenuous outdoor workout. We're staring down a heat advisory for North Texas, including our beloved Dallas, until 9 p.m. tonight. The actual temperature is pushing 33°C (around 91°F), but with the humidity, we're talking about heat index values that will make it *feel* like 100 to 105 degrees Fahrenheit this afternoon. That's not just "warm," that's "get-yourself-into-an-air-conditioned-space" territory, especially if you're pushing it. Even the nights aren't providing much relief, meaning your body hasn't had a real chance to cool down. Your joints will thank you for taking it easy, and your heart will thank you for not overtaxing it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!** Heat index values of 100-105°F are expected today. This is a severe risk for heat-related illnesses like heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially between noon and 9 PM.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the air quality is surprisingly good, with an AQI of 24. So your lungs aren't going to be fighting smog, which is a small victory. However, those of you with seasonal allergies might still feel a bit congested, as pollen levels are moderate.

### Trail Troubles & Event Congestion

If your usual loop involves the scenic White Rock Trail, be aware that Dallas Water Utilities is starting major grouting work on an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to the trail and Garland Road. While the full closure for the section between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is slated to begin "the week of June 15," the notices are out, and it's best to assume things could start getting messy, or at the very least, start planning your detours now for the foreseeable future. Don't be that person ignoring the "Trail Closed" signs; it's for your safety (and to avoid getting in the way of heavy machinery).

Meanwhile, down in South Dallas, the Juneteenth 4K Walk & Festival is happening today from 8:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. This means a lot more foot traffic and potential road impacts around the Martin Luther King Jr. Community Center and stretching into Fair Park. If you're running in that area, expect crowds and plan for slower going or reroute entirely. It's a great community event, but not ideal for a fast-paced run. Also, a neighborhood clean-up is scheduled for the SoPac Trail at 8:30 a.m., which might add some minor localized activity.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Trail Construction Imminent:** Major closures for grouting work near Garland Road and White Rock Trail (between E. Lawther Dr. and Winsted Dr.) are commencing "the week of June 15." Check for immediate impacts and plan alternative routes for today and beyond.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** With these heat index values, you need to be drinking water and electrolytes *before, during, and after* any outdoor activity, even if it's just a light walk. Don't wait until you're thirsty. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
</AlertBox>
