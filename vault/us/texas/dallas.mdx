## Morning Aftermath & Afternoon Outlook

Let's be real: this morning was a washout. Severe storms rolled through between 3 AM and 7 AM, dumping rain and kicking up serious winds across the DFW area. If you were out there, I hope you brought your snorkel. For the rest of the day, expect the clouds to *slowly* start clearing out, and temperatures will climb into the low 80s, which is nice, but it doesn't magically dry out every puddle or glue fallen branches back onto trees.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Immediate Danger: Wet, Slick & Flooded Surfaces**
The heavy rainfall from this morning's storms means trails, sidewalks, and low-lying areas will be extremely wet, muddy, and potentially still flooded. Turn around if you see standing water; it could be deeper than it looks and hide unseen hazards. Flash flooding has been an issue in Dallas County recently, even closing parts of I-45 earlier in March, so respect the water.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions: More Than Just Puddles

Even if the skies clear, the ground sure won't. Expect muddy sections, especially on unpaved trails or those near the Trinity River. Those 60+ mph wind gusts don't just disappear; they bring down branches and kick up debris. Your scenic run around White Rock Lake could turn into an obstacle course with fallen limbs and displaced trash. And don't forget that electrical work on the Bachman Trail means the lights are out – great for a late afternoon run when it's already overcast, right? No, that's a recipe for a twisted ankle.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction & Maintenance Headaches**
Portions of the White Rock Lake Trail and Anderson Bonner Park are under construction for gabion wall improvements, which means closed parking lots and equipment storage. Always be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're detouring around these areas. The I-30 Canyon corridor project is still a beast, causing long-term disruptions downtown, though no specific major lane closures are reported for today itself.
</AlertBox>

## Your Lungs & Allergies: The Usual Spring Fun

The air quality (AQI 46) is still in the "Good" range, so your lungs can breathe easy, relatively speaking. But it's spring in Dallas, so "Moderate" pollen is a given. If you're sensitive, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out. Don't add itchy eyes and a runny nose to the list of things to complain about today.
