## Don't Be an Idiot: Heat is the Real Killer Today

Look, I get it. You've got your training plan, your Strava segments, your ego. But today, none of that is worth risking heatstroke or worse. Dallas in July is no joke, and a Heat Advisory means your body is going to struggle to regulate its temperature even at rest. Trying to run or bike hard out there is just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: Heat Index 100-105°F!
Seriously, folks. The NWS is not messing around. Sustained activity outdoors, especially during peak hours (12 PM - 6 PM), can lead to heat exhaustion, heat cramps, and full-blown heatstroke. Symptoms include dizziness, nausea, headaches, confusion, and rapid pulse. If you feel any of this, get inside, hydrate, and cool down immediately. This isn't a challenge to overcome; it's a danger to avoid.
</AlertBox>

### White Rock Lake Trail: Construction Chaos
If you were thinking of a peaceful loop around White Rock Lake, think again. The Dallas Park and Recreation Department has sections closed due to construction. They're grouting an abandoned wastewater main, which sounds about as fun as it is to navigate around. Expect detours, closed lanes on Garland Road, and general annoyance around E. Lawther Drive. Don't try to sneak through; those barriers are there for a reason, and you don't want to explain to a construction crew why you decided to test the structural integrity of a sinkhole repair.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Trail Closures
Portions of the White Rock Lake Trail, particularly between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, are closed for construction until September 2026. Detours are in place, and parts of E. Lawther Drive are closed to vehicular traffic to accommodate trail users. Follow signage and avoid closed sections for your safety and to avoid damaging ongoing work.
</AlertBox>

The air quality, for once, isn't terrible (AQI 42 is good), and we don't have active flood warnings. But don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. The heat is the overriding factor today. If you *must* move, stick to indoor options, hit the gym, or get your run done before the sun has a chance to really cook the pavement, and keep it short. Hydrate constantly. Or better yet, embrace a rest day. Your body will thank you.
