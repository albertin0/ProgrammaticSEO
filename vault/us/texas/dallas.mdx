## The Heat is On (and it's sticking around)

It's nearly October, but Mother Nature clearly missed the memo. We're looking at a high of about 90°F today, and with the humidity, it’s going to feel more like you’re trying to run through a sauna. Yesterday saw similar temps, with a heat index easily cresting 100°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**HEAT STROKE RISK IS HIGH:** If you're out there pushing it, especially after 9 AM, you're risking heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Symptoms include dizziness, nausea, confusion, and a throbbing headache. Don't ignore them. Get inside, cool down, and hydrate *immediately*.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**HYDRATE, HYDRATE, HYDRATE:** Drink more water than you think you need, even if you're just walking the dog. If you absolutely must be outside, go before the sun really gets going, stick to the shade, and keep it short. Otherwise, find a treadmill or a spin class.
</AlertBox>

## Air Quality & Allergens: A Mixed Bag

Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 51, which lands us squarely in the "Moderate" zone, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. For most people, this means acceptable air quality. However, if you're sensitive to ozone, have asthma, or other respiratory issues, you might feel some irritation. The good news is the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) predicts ozone should actually move into the "Good" range for most of the state today, potentially due to the incoming weather system, though some light smoke might linger this morning before dispersing.

Pollen is still at a moderate level. Combined with the moderate air quality, your lungs might not love a strenuous outdoor session.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**LISTEN TO YOUR LUNGS:** If you're unusually sensitive to air pollution or pollen, today is a good day to scale back your outdoor activity or move it indoors. The moderate AQI and pollen can make breathing harder than usual, even without a full-blown "Ozone Action Day" alert.
</AlertBox>
