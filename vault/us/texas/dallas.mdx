<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk!
Seriously, a heat index of 106°F is no joke. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real possibilities, especially if you're pushing it. Limit outdoor activities, stick to the shade, and hydrate constantly. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or get a headache, GET INSIDE IMMEDIATELY. This advisory is active for Dallas County until 8 PM.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Trail Closures
Don't expect your usual unbroken circuit around White Rock Lake. Significant sections of the trail, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, are closed due to ongoing wastewater main construction. There *will not* be a continuous loop. Pay attention to signage and detours around Garland Road. Also, portions of the Elm Fork Trinity River-Upper East at Skyline Trail Bridge area are closed for construction. Choose your route wisely, or better yet, hit the gym today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Key (If You Must Go Out)
If you absolutely *have* to get your workout in today, do it early or late, and carry more water than you think you need. Pre-hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does in this heat). Consider electrolyte drinks. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing. And seriously, listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. We runners are stubborn. But today, the smart move is to hit the treadmill, do some yoga, or just chill out. Dallas in September can be a beast, and today she's roaring.
