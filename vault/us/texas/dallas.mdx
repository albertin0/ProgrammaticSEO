### Heat Check!
Don't underestimate the Dallas heat, even when it's overcast. With temperatures hitting the low 90s today, heat exhaustion is a real threat. Plan your run for early morning or late evening. Wear light, wicking clothing. Carry water, and for Pete's sake, actually *drink* it. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or cramping up, cut your workout short. Nobody wants to be that person wheeled off the Katy Trail.
</AlertBox>

Let's keep the paths safe for everyone, not just those with an engine.
