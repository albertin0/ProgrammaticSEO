## Today's Dallas Workout Reality Check

Look, I get it. The sun is trying to peek through this morning, and the temperature is a pleasant 20.6°C (about 69°F). That sounds inviting, right? Don't let it fool you. We've got a classic North Texas Memorial Day weekend brewing, and that means unpredictable weather. Showers and storms rolled through early Saturday, and while things dried out, another round is expected for Sunday afternoon. This isn't just a drizzle; we're talking about potential lightning and enough rain to cause localized flooding in those usual low-lying spots. Keep an eye on the radar before you head out, and if you hear thunder, it's time to bail. Seriously, no PR is worth getting zapped.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Lightning and Flooding Risk This Afternoon!
Afternoon thunderstorms are likely, bringing with them a risk of dangerous lightning and localized flash flooding. If you see dark clouds or hear thunder, get inside immediately. Roads and underpasses can flood quickly.
</AlertBox>

And if your go-to spot is the scenic loop around Bachman Lake, you might want to reconsider. Not only is the "Texas Triple" marathon taking over the area from Saturday through Monday, guaranteeing crowds and potential detours, but the course itself is undergoing construction. That's right, a marathon on a *construction site*. Bless their hearts. If you do venture out there, expect uneven surfaces, potential bottlenecks, and generally a less-than-ideal experience. It's a reminder that Dallas always has some project going on, and sometimes our favorite running routes pay the price. The City of Dallas and TxDOT have ongoing projects that can impact routes, so always check before you go, especially around major thoroughfares like I-30 or I-635.
