### Trail Troubles: White Rock Lake is Partially Closed

If White Rock Lake is your usual stomping ground, be aware: the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is shut down. Don't try to navigate around construction barriers; they're there for a reason, and you're not special. Find an alternate route or move your workout indoors. Dallas Park and Recreation expects this to continue until September.
