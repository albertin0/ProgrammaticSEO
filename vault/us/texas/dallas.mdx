## Today's Dallas Grind: Heat Hell, Trail Trouble, and a Sigh of Relief on AQI

Look, if you're thinking about a midday run or even a long walk out there in Dallas today, August 13th, 2026, you're either new here or just plain stubborn. The heat is no joke, and frankly, I'm already sweating just typing this.

Our friends at the National Weather Service Fort Worth/Dallas are forecasting triple-digit temperatures with heat index values that'll make it feel like 100 to 108 degrees Fahrenheit. That's not "push yourself" weather; that's "find the nearest AC vent and hug it" weather. Some forecasts even mention heat index values pushing 112 degrees in the urban core. Your body is not designed to perform its best, or even safely, when it's trying to cook itself from the inside out.

### The Sweat, The Smoke, and The Trail Snafus

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Seriously, folks, we're talking about conditions ripe for heat stroke. Temperatures are hitting 100-103°F (38-39°C) and the "feels like" is spiking even higher. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Drink water like it's your job, and don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the oppressive heat, we've got other fun stuff. White Rock Lake, a runner's paradise usually, currently has sections of its trail system *closed*. Specifically, the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is shut down for construction until September 2026. So if you were planning to get your miles in there, prepare for detours and frustration. The city has signage and detours in place, but honestly, who wants to navigate that when you're already battling the sun?

And don't forget, with these hot, dry, and often breezy conditions, the threat of wildfires is elevated across the region. It's another thing to be mindful of, though hopefully, we won't see any significant smoke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Trail Closure:** A significant stretch of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is closed for construction until September 2026. Plan alternate routes or prepare for detours if you usually run this area.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, your lungs might be thanking you (if they can breathe past the heat). The Air Quality Index (AQI) is fantastic today, sitting at a comfortable 29, meaning the air is pretty clean. So, no smog-induced coughing fits, at least. But don't let that lull you into a false sense of security about the heat.
