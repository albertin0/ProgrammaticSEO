### Local Hazards & Hot Spots

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Lake Woes: Construction & Car Break-ins**
Listen up, White Rock regulars: there's ongoing construction along the trail, specifically near Garland Road between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive, where crews are grouting a wastewater main. Expect some detours or narrower sections, so stay alert for heavy equipment and construction workers. More importantly, those car break-ins around White Rock Lake aren't magically gone. Dallas Police confirmed an increase in vehicle burglaries this year, with 58 incidents reported by mid-September. They've upped patrols, but your best defense is to *not* leave valuables visible in your car. "Lock, Take & Hide" is the mantra. Don't be an easy target for opportunistic thieves.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Downtown Gridlock: I-30 Canyon Project**
If your workout involves getting anywhere near downtown Dallas this weekend, plan ahead. I-30 is shutting down in both directions between I-35E and I-45/US 75 starting at 10 p.m. tonight and staying closed until Monday morning. This isn't just a minor lane closure; it's a major artery blocked. While it might not directly close your favorite downtown trail, accessing areas like Klyde Warren Park or the trails along the Trinity River could be a nightmare if you're driving. Factor in significant delays and consider alternate routes or even public transport if you're heading that way.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Reverchon Park Closed for Overseeding**
Heads up for those who use Reverchon Park for field workouts: the baseball field is temporarily closed for winter overseeding. Find an alternate spot for your sprints and drills until it reopens.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the crisp air and clear skies, Dallas. But, as always, be smart about where you go, what you leave behind, and how you get there. Your knees (and your wallet) will thank you.
