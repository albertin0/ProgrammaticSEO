## Current Conditions & Hazards

Let's not sugarcoat it: Mother Nature is not playing nice in Dallas today. We've got a severe thunderstorm watch that was in effect through 11 AM, bringing with it the delightful possibility of scattered damaging wind gusts up to 75 mph, quarter-sized hail, and torrential downpours. If you missed the memo, that's not exactly prime running weather. The National Weather Service even put out a "Slight Risk of Severe Thunderstorms" for our slice of the Southern Plains.

Beyond the immediate thunder and lightning show, the ground is already saturated from recent deluges. Just last week, Dallas saw significant flash flooding with streets like Northwest Highway underwater and Dallas Fire-Rescue scrambling to respond to numerous stranded vehicles. Trying to navigate a flooded street or trail on foot is not just inconvenient, it's downright dangerous. "Turn Around, Don't Drown" isn't just a catchy phrase, it's how you stay alive.

Once the storms decide to take a breather, don't immediately leap for your running shoes. Temperatures are going to climb rapidly, with "feels like" temperatures hitting the lower triple digits this afternoon. Coupled with a "Moderate" AQI due to ozone and other pollutants, your lungs won't thank you for pushing it. A study recently highlighted how wildfire smoke, even if not directly over us today, is generally degrading air quality gains, and while not a direct impact for us today, it underlines the sensitivity of our respiratory systems to what's floating around.
