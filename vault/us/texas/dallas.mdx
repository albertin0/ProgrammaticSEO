## Today's Dallas Rundown: Don't Be a Hero, Be Dry.

Look, I get it. We runners are a dedicated bunch. We see a little cloud cover and think, "Perfect! No blazing Texas sun." But today, the sky is actively plotting against your workout. Meteorologists are calling for scattered to widespread showers and thunderstorms all day, with potential for some seriously heavy rain and localized flash flooding. This isn't just a sprinkle; we're talking about areas possibly getting 2-3+ inches of rain. That concrete jungle out there turns into a slip-and-slide.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Flash Flood Threat is REAL:** Roads and low-lying areas, especially near our creeks and the Trinity River, are primed for significant water accumulation. Running through standing water is not just messy, it's stupid dangerous. You can't see what's under there – potholes, debris, lost dreams, who knows. Turn Around, Don't Drown, and definitely don't sprain your ankle on a submerged curb.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, your lungs actually get a break. The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 42, which is "Good," with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. So, if you manage to find a truly dry, elevated patch of concrete, at least you're breathing clean air while you contemplate your life choices. Pollen is moderate, meaning your allergies might be annoying, but probably not workout-stopping.

### Local Hazards: Trinity Strand Trail & Beyond

Thinking of a scenic run along the **Trinity Strand Trail**? Think again. Any trail near a waterway is going to be a mucky, potentially dangerous mess today. Flash flood warnings are no joke, and those concrete paths can get submerged fast. Don't be that person caught in rapidly rising water. Stick to higher ground, or better yet, stay indoors.

And speaking of staying indoors, while there's always ongoing construction chatter around the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center and the Oak Cliff viaducts, those are more long-term traffic headaches than immediate workout impediments today. No specific trail closures due to construction have been announced for today that would override the primary weather concerns.

**Consider Indoor Options:** Look, I hate the treadmill as much as the next runner, but today might be its day to shine. Or find an indoor track. Or just foam roll and stretch on your living room floor. Your joints will thank you.
</AlertBox>
