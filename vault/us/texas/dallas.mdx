Alright, Dallas, today's the kind of day where your running shoes might be better off on a treadmill in a properly air-conditioned gym, or, at the very least, you need to seriously rethink your outdoor plans.

Look, I love our trails as much as the next idiot who signs up for a marathon, but today's a curveball. The mercury is set to hit 32.7°C (a balmy 91°F) under mainly clear skies. While some news outlets are calling for a "sunny and pleasant" day, anyone who's actually *run* in Dallas when it's north of 90 degrees in October knows "pleasant" is a cruel joke. This kind of heat, especially for sustained effort, is how you end up on a stretcher. We've seen warnings issued for far less severe heat, even leading to cancellations of running club events in the past. Don't be a hero; hydrate relentlessly.

Adding to the fun, Dallas construction projects never sleep. There are ongoing lane closures on the Dallas Parkway (DNT FR) that started last night and are expected to last through tonight, so if your route involves that stretch, plan for traffic headaches. More importantly for us trail warriors, don't forget the seemingly endless "LOOP Trail System" construction. This massive project aims to connect existing trails but means you'll run into detours, uneven surfaces, and potential closures all over the city. Specifically, if you're hitting up the White Rock Trail near Garland Road and E. Lawther Drive, expect to see crews doing some grouting work. Keep your head up and earbuds out; heavy machinery and distracted drivers don't mix with your PR attempt.

Air quality-wise, the AQI is currently sitting at a "Good" 46 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecast for Dallas-Fort Worth today shows "Ozone/PM2.5" conditions, so while it's not terrible, it's not perfectly crisp either. Add to that the moderate pollen count, and if you're prone to allergies, you might feel a bit stuffed up after your run.

### WARNING: Trail & Road Construction
Expect detours and potential hazards. Specifically, watch out for ongoing work around White Rock Lake on the White Rock Trail, and general construction related to the new LOOP Trail System. Road closures on Dallas Parkway will impact access. Always be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're veering off your usual path.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Tip: Hydrate & Plan Smart
If you *must* run outside, do it at dawn or dusk when temperatures are slightly lower. Pre-hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). Carry water, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear, and listen to your body. Cut your workout short if you feel lightheaded, nauseous, or excessively tired.
</AlertBox>
