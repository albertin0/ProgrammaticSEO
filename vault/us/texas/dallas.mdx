## Trail Talk & Safety Hacks

Today's the kind of day where the weather is trying to lull you into a false sense of security. Don't fall for it entirely. While the air itself is clean and the sun's playing peek-a-boo, we still have a few Dallas-specific things to keep an eye on.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Pollen's on the Prowl
Your nose might not agree with the "good" vibe. With moderate pollen levels, those of us prone to seasonal sniffles or asthma need to be mindful. Hit your antihistamines an hour before you head out, carry an inhaler if you need it, and maybe don't plan on breaking any personal bests if your lungs feel like sandpaper.
</AlertBox>

### Katy Trail Calamity (Minor Edition)

Our beloved Katy Trail is still getting its glow-up, which means some detours are in effect. Specifically, the south end near Victory Park and Houston Street is undergoing construction for The Loop Plaza. They're rerouting traffic, which might add a couple of minutes to your journey, but more importantly, it means *new* paths and potential uneven surfaces. Stay alert, watch for signage, and don't assume the path you know is the path that's there today. It’s expected to be completed this summer, so just embrace the temporary inconvenience.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Gap on Katy Trail Construction
Construction for The Loop Plaza is ongoing at the south end of the Katy Trail. Expect reroutes and altered pathways near Houston Street. Stay aware of your surroundings, watch for equipment, and follow all posted signs. That "two-minute delay" they mention can feel like ten if you're not paying attention.
</AlertBox>

There's also some minor work going on at Craddock Park, where a shade canopy is being installed. This might temporarily impact the walking trail near the tennis courts. It's anticipated to be done by May 20th, so you might catch the tail end of it. Just be aware of potential closures or obstructions if you're running there.

Looking ahead, we're not out of the woods for weather this week. While today is mostly dry, the National Weather Service is forecasting increasing chances for thunderstorms starting Monday, with potential for strong storms and even flooding later in the week. So enjoy this pleasant Sunday, but keep an eye on those forecasts if you're planning your long runs mid-week.
