### Heat Warning & Your Body

Look, it's 28°C (about 82°F) right now, but that humidity is cooking us. The National Weather Service isn't kidding around when they say heat index values could approach 110°F today. That's the kind of heat where your body struggles to cool itself, and even the fittest among us can get into trouble fast. By the end of the weekend, we're looking at actual temps hitting 103°F. If you absolutely *must* get outside, think before 8 AM or after 8 PM, and keep it short. Seriously short.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk!
Heat-related illnesses like heat exhaustion and heat stroke are a real threat today. Limit outdoor activity, stay hydrated with plenty of water, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get inside to AC *immediately*. This isn't a game.
</AlertBox>
