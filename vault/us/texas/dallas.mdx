### **Heat Advisory: Don't Be a Hero**
With temperatures pushing into the mid-30s Celsius and high humidity, heat exhaustion and stroke are serious risks. Schedule your runs early morning or late evening. Wear light, breathable clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, carry water. More water than you think you need. Consider your route carefully, especially if it lacks shade.
</AlertBox>
