Even if you're not planning a marathon, a casual walk around White Rock Lake can quickly turn into a bad idea. The combination of heat and moderate air quality, primarily due to PM2.5, means even a leisurely stroll is taxing on your cardiovascular and respiratory systems.

Speaking of White Rock Lake, keep an eye out for construction. The Dallas Park and Recreation Department has ongoing work, including grouting an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and White Rock Trail. Don't assume your usual path is clear, and definitely don't be that person ignoring signage to save 30 seconds.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Alert:** With an AQI of 71 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, individuals with respiratory sensitivities (asthma, COPD) should reconsider outdoor exercise, especially during peak heat hours. Everyone else, take it easy and watch for symptoms.
</AlertBox>
