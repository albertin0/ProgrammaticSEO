## Today's Dallas Rundown: Go, But Be Smart

Look, the temperature is a sweet 21.2°C (about 70°F) and it's mostly clear out there. That's practically perfect running weather in this city, so don't let a little atmospheric funk keep you entirely indoors. Just be smart about it.

### Lung Considerations

Despite the official "Good" AQI of 46, forecasts suggest elevated ozone and fine particulate matter (PM2.5) levels could push into the "Moderate" or even "Poor" range for sensitive folks across the DFW region. If you've got asthma or any kind of respiratory issues, maybe dial back the intensity, especially if you're hitting trails on the north side of town where ozone concentrations are expected to be higher. And seriously, that heavy grass pollen is no joke. If your nose starts running like a faucet, cut your losses and head inside. You're not winning any awards for snot-streaked heroism.

### Joint & Trail Intel

For those of you hitting **White Rock Lake**, you're in for some wildlife viewing with a side of minor inconvenience. A pair of bald eagles has nested with three eaglets, and Dallas Park and Recreation has put up barricades on East Lawther Drive, particularly between the playground and the Dreyfuss split. This is temporarily affecting vehicle access, so expect some delays if you're driving. Cyclists need to slow down in the area, and that playground is temporarily closed. The White Rock Trail itself is open for walking and biking, but be aware that some areas, particularly along the trail and Anderson Bonner Park, are seeing construction for gabion wall improvements, which means portions of parking lots might be closed off for equipment. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Oak Cliff Crowds:** Big news down south! Halperin Park, that new deck park over I-35E in Oak Cliff, is having its grand opening today at 9 AM. Expect crowds, parades, and general Mother's Day festivities if you're in the area. Probably not the day for a PR attempt through that part of town.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's workout forecast is mostly clear with a few scattered chances of you getting annoyed by pollen or detoured by construction. Stay hydrated, keep an eye on your breathing, and enjoy the relatively mild conditions before Mother Nature decides to throw a fit tomorrow.
