### The Good, The Bad, and The Sweaty

For late July in Dallas, this feels like a mirage. The AQI is sitting pretty at 35, with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant, but frankly, that’s almost pristine for us. You won't be doing interval training through a haze of regret today, which is a definite win.

Now, before you lace up and blast out the door thinking it's all sunshine and PRs, let's inject a dose of reality. "Moderate" pollen means something different for everyone. For me, it means my eyes itch just thinking about it. For you, it might mean nothing, or it might mean you're reaching for the Claritin halfway through your loop around White Rock Lake. This isn't a "stay inside" level, but if you're a sniffle-sufferer, be prepared.
