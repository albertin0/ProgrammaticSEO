### Trail Troubles
Beyond the heat, watch your step (and your lungs). The "Moderate" AQI with PM2.5 means your airways will be working harder. Pair that with moderate grass pollen, and anyone with allergies or asthma is in for a rough go.

If you planned a loop around **White Rock Lake**, be aware that construction is still ongoing. Crews are grouting an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. This project kicked off back in June and is certainly still in full swing. Don't be surprised by detours or narrowed sections. Always be aware of your surroundings, especially with machinery in the area.
