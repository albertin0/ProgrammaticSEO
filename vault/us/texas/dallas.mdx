## Today's Vitals: Humidity, Showers, and PM2.5

Temperatures are a pleasant 20.7°C and it's overcast, which usually sounds great for a run. However, the air is thick with humidity, making it feel warmer than the thermometer says. On top of that, we've got a "Moderate" AQI of 55, with PM2.5 being the primary offender. This fine particulate matter can really get into your lungs and bloodstream, especially during strenuous activity. Add in that moderate pollen count, and your respiratory system is working overtime today.

The National Weather Service is tracking tropical moisture bringing scattered showers across North Texas today. While Dallas isn't under the major flood watch impacting West Texas from the Tropical Storm Vance remnants, those scattered showers mean wet pavement and potentially slippery bridges. Nobody needs an unplanned face-plant because they underestimated a damp patch of asphalt.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Watch Your Lungs, Dallas!**
That "Moderate" AQI for PM2.5 isn't just a number. It means sensitive groups can experience symptoms, and sustained exposure can irritate anyone. Combined with moderate pollen, if you're asthmatic or have any kind of respiratory sensitivity, seriously consider an indoor alternative today. The last thing you need is a coughing fit halfway down the Katy Trail.
</AlertBox>
