<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is a Real Threat, Folks
Look, your morning run around White Rock Lake might sound idyllic, but with RealFeel temperatures forecast to hit 101°F today and an extreme UV index of 12, you're flirting with heat exhaustion and heatstroke. This isn't just about feeling a little sweaty; it's about your body literally overheating. Stay hydrated, significantly reduce your intensity, or better yet, stay inside during peak heat hours. Older adults, infants, and those with sensitive medical conditions should minimize outdoor activity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe Deeply – The Air is Cooking
Even with a numerically "good" AQI, don't ignore the warnings. Dallas-Fort Worth is in non-attainment for ozone, and with these sunny, stagnant conditions and high temperatures, ground-level ozone can rapidly form and impact sensitive groups. The forecast also indicates PM2.5 levels could reach the "Moderate" range. If your lungs feel like they're sucking in sandpaper, it's not in your head. Consider an indoor workout today.
</AlertBox>
