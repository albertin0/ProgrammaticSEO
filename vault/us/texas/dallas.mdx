Local forecasters are screaming "dangerous heat" with widespread triple-digit temperatures, peaking around 104-107°F (40-41.6°C) and heat indices up to 105°F (40.5°C) today. Look, I get it. We're runners. We like to pretend we're tough. But pushing through an Excessive Heat Warning in Dallas is less about being tough and more about being, well, stupid. The National Weather Service isn't putting out warnings for fun; they're doing it because people end up in the ER with heat stroke. Your 5k PR isn't worth passing out on the Katy Trail and becoming a news headline.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme, Life-Threatening Heat
Seriously, Dallas is under an Excessive Heat Warning until 9 PM tonight, with actual temperatures expected to hit 104-107°F (40-41.6°C) and heat indices making it feel even hotter. This is not just a "sweaty workout" situation. This is "your body can't cool itself effectively, even if you're hydrated" territory. If you absolutely *must* move, keep it indoors, in the A/C, or stick to a pool.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction & Traffic Everywhere
While not a direct workout hazard on every trail, massive road construction projects are ongoing across the city. The I-635 East Project, I-30 Canyon Improvement Project, and the US 80 interchange project in Mesquite are causing significant disruptions. This means extra traffic, potential detours, and localized dust. If you're running near any of these behemoths, prepare for the added exhaust fumes and less-than-scenic views.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
If you ignore all warnings and decide to brave the heat (which, again, don't), proper hydration is the bare minimum. And I don't mean a tiny water bottle. Think gallon jug. Electrolytes. And for the love of all that is holy, don't wait until you're thirsty. You're already behind. Wear light, loose clothing. Go at sunrise or sunset if you must, but even then, it's still miserably hot.
</AlertBox>
