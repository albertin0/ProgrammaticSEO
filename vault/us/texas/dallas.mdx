### Air Quality: Don't Be a Hero, Check Your Lungs

Look, I love pushing limits as much as the next runner, but today is not the day to play fast and loose with your respiratory system. While the overall AQI might technically be 55, placing it in the "Moderate" category for PM2.5, the big news is that we're under an Ozone Action Day for the DFW area. That means ozone concentrations are elevated, and that's not something to scoff at, especially for those of us with allergies, asthma, or just generally attached to our lungs. Yesterday saw "poor air quality due to high levels of ozone", and today is following suit.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  <h4>Ozone Action Day - Take it Easy!</h4>
  An Ozone Action Day means ground-level ozone is elevated. This can irritate your lungs, causing coughing, throat irritation, and difficulty breathing, particularly during outdoor exertion. If you have respiratory issues, kids, or older adults, seriously consider moving your workout indoors or sticking to lighter activities. Otherwise, try to hit the trails during the early morning or late evening when ozone levels are typically lower.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Local Hot Spots

Good news for the White Rock Lake faithful: the construction along Garland Road and White Rock Trail that was causing closures earlier this year was actually wrapped up ahead of schedule back in August. So, that section of your beloved loop is clear to run.

However, if you're thinking of hitting the Katy Trail for a nice long run, remember that while it's generally clear, the broader Dallas-Fort Worth area is a constant construction zone. There are daily lane reductions on major interstates like I-20 and I-30 for various projects. While these are mostly for drivers, always be aware of increased traffic and potential detours on surrounding streets if your route takes you near these areas. No specific trail closures on the Katy Trail itself for today, but keep your head up.
