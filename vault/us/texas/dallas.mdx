### Sneaky Pollen & Persistent Construction
Don't let the clear skies fool you; pollen is still at a "Moderate" level. If you're one of the lucky ones who gets hit by seasonal allergies, consider popping an antihistamine before you head out, or you'll be snot-rocketing your way through your run.
</AlertBox>

The weather's fantastic for a long run around **White Rock Lake**, but listen up: the ongoing grouting project on that abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road and White Rock Trail (between E. "No pedestrian access is allowed inside the closed work zones" means stay out of those areas. Seriously, a new PR isn't worth a confrontation with a bulldozer or an aggravated assault charge.
