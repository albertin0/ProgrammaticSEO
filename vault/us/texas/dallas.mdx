The average heat index for July in Dallas is a scorching 113°F (45°C), and we're heading straight for that.

### Trail Talk & Construction Chaos

Don't even *think* about doing your usual loop around White Rock Lake without checking for detours. The City of Dallas is still busy grouting an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and White Rock Trail. This project, which kicked off mid-June, has the section of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive *closed* until September. You'll find signs and detours, but frankly, it's a pain. E. Lawther Drive between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way is also closed to vehicular traffic to accommodate trail users.

And if you were hoping for a smooth run around Bachman Lake, think again. The Bachman Lake Loop Trail is undergoing construction/drilling today, July 8th, so expect reroutes on a segment there too.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Navigation:** Always, *always* check local park and recreation websites before heading out. Don't waste your precious motivation driving to a closed trail. Dallas County is also doing striping operations in areas like Cedar Hill, including the Pioneer Trail, so watch out for fresh paint and slow-moving crews if you're in that neck of the woods.
</AlertBox>
