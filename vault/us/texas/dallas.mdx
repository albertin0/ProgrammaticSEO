<AlertBox type="warning">
Today is an **Ozone Action Day** for Dallas-Fort Worth. While the overall AQI for particulate matter (PM2.5) is moderate at 52, ozone pollution can be elevated, making it unhealthy for sensitive groups. If you have asthma, other respiratory conditions, or are just feeling a bit off, consider taking your workout indoors or scaling back your intensity, especially during peak afternoon hours. Carpooling, walking, or biking are encouraged to help reduce ozone pollution, but check your own sensitivity first.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Hazards

Looks like we're mostly clear on the major stuff like wildfires and flooding. No significant wildfire smoke is expected for the next 48 hours, so that's a win. And while we've had some flooding threats earlier in the month, there are no current flood warnings or advisories for today in Dallas County.

However, construction continues to be a thing, because, well, Dallas. If you're planning a run on the ever-popular **Katy Trail**, be aware that there's ongoing construction for an adjacent soft-surface path. Also, "The Loop" Plaza project on the south end of the Katy Trail could mean a short reroute. Keep an eye out for signage. Same goes for parts of the Northaven Trail, with Phase 1B involving bridge construction. Basically, stay aware of your surroundings, don't assume every path is perfectly clear, and respect any construction barriers.

<AlertBox type="info">
With the cool, clear weather, it's easy to get complacent. Remember to stay hydrated even when it's not scorching hot, and if you're a creature of habit hitting the Katy Trail, keep an eye out for those construction detours. They pop up faster than a new high-rise.
</AlertBox>
