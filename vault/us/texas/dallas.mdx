## The Sweat & Suffer Report

### Heat's Coming For You

Yeah, it’s 24.3°C (around 76°F) now and clear, but that’s just the calm before the swelter. A **Heat Advisory** is in effect for Dallas from **12:00 PM to 9:00 PM today, Thursday, June 18th**. This isn't just "a bit warm," it means conditions could be dangerous for strenuous outdoor activity. Forget about running in the direct sun during these hours unless you enjoy feeling like a rotisserie chicken.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Heat Advisory from 12 PM - 9 PM.** If you absolutely *must* get out, do it early, stay in the shade, hydrate aggressively (and not just with coffee, you maniacs), and keep it short. Seriously, heatstroke isn't a badge of honor.
</AlertBox>

### Storm Clouds on the Horizon

Our perfectly clear morning isn't going to last. The National Weather Service DFW office is calling for **thunderstorms, heavy rain, lightning, and gusty winds to return late Thursday** and persist through the weekend. While they're saying "severe weather is not expected," lightning is no joke, and gusty winds can make for a miserable, even unsafe, run or ride.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Impending Thunderstorms.** If you're out late this afternoon or evening, keep an eye on the sky and check radar before heading out. Don't be the idiot caught in a downpour or, worse, under a lightning strike. When thunder roars, go indoors.
</AlertBox>

### White Rock Lake Trail: Grout Expectations

If White Rock Lake is your usual stomping ground, be aware: **a section of the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely closed** for construction. They're grouting an abandoned pipeline, which sounds about as exciting as it is disruptive. This work started this week and isn't expected to finish until September. E. Lawther between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way is also closed to vehicular traffic to help with detours. Plan your route *carefully* and expect detours. Don't just blindly follow Strava if it takes you into a construction zone.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: White Rock Lake Trail Closure.** The trail is closed between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive for ongoing construction. Expect significant detours and plan alternative routes. Seriously, don't try to sneak through a work zone.
</AlertBox>
