## Run Early, Avoid the Fryer

Look, if you're going to get your miles in today, do it *now*. That overcast sky is a temporary reprieve, because by this afternoon, Dallas is going to feel like the inside of a smoker. The National Weather Service is forecasting highs around the mid-90s to 100°F, with heat index values easily hitting or exceeding 105°F. This isn't just "a little warm," folks; this is "your sweat evaporates before it even cools you" territory.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory: Afternoon = Oven.**
Seriously, plan your outdoor workouts for the early morning or late evening. Midday is for AC and large, frosty beverages. Don't be that person needing a medic on the Katy Trail. Hydrate like it's your job, and then hydrate some more.
</AlertBox>

## White Rock Trail: Still a Construction Zone

For all my east Dallas runners, I'm just going to lay it out: forget about large sections of White Rock Trail. Construction crews are still actively working on that wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road, between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. This isn't a "squeeze-by" situation; the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is *closed for the duration of construction*, which isn't slated to wrap until September. Don't be a hero, don't ignore the signage. You're not going to "find a shortcut" through a live construction site, you're going to find a concussion.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Trail Closure: No Jokes.**
The segment between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is SHUT DOWN due to major construction. Plan alternative routes. I'm talking Trinity Levee Trail, Bachman Lake, or even just doing loops around your neighborhood. Just stay clear of the orange cones and heavy machinery.
</AlertBox>

## AQI is Your Friend Today

Surprisingly, the air quality is in the "Good" range with an AQI of 37. While ozone is listed as a dominant pollutant, it's not at levels that should cause widespread concern for most folks today, unlike some of the "Ozone Action Day" warnings we've seen in recent months. Pollen is moderate, so if you're typically susceptible, take your meds, but it shouldn't be a deal-breaker.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep an Eye on the Sky, Especially Later.**
While the morning storms passed and the severe thunderstorm alert from earlier expired before dawn, the National Weather Service mentions a chance of afternoon and evening showers and storms. "A few strong storms may produce gusty winds and frequent lightning." Don't get caught out in the open on the Trinity Levee Trail if the sky starts looking angry.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're determined to get a workout in, go early, go hard, and go somewhere other than White Rock. And for the love of all that is holy, take a water bottle.
