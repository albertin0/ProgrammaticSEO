## Today's Dallas Forecast: Hot, Hazy, and Hella Blocked

Look, it's September, but Mother Nature clearly missed the memo about fall arriving in Dallas. We're in a "hot and dry weather streak," with 57 days and counting since measurable rain at DFW. So, don't expect any sudden downpours to cool things off or wash away the pollen.

### Afternoon Heat: Seriously, Don't Push It

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Afternoon Heat.** Temperatures will climb rapidly. Highs are forecasted for the mid-90s, with a good chance of hitting 100°F (37.7°C). Peak heat indices could reach 105°F (40.5°C) over the weekend. Get your workout in early, stay hydrated, and for the love of your kidneys, know when to bail. Heat stroke is no joke, and you won't look cool passed out on the Katy Trail.
</AlertBox>

### Downtown Debacle: Post-Convention Mess

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Downtown Road & Pedestrian Closures.** The RNC Midterm Convention in Dallas has officially concluded, but the party's over for traffic and clear paths around the American Airlines Center and Victory Park. Expect ongoing road closures and increased security measures through today, Friday, September 11th. Even DART's Victory Station was affected. Tear down and cleanup efforts could impact the area into Saturday. Plan alternative routes and be aware that pedestrian access might be restricted.

### White Rock Trail: Still a Pothole in Your Plan

For those of you who rely on our beloved White Rock Lake loop, remember that the ongoing construction near Garland Road and Lawther Drive is *still* closing a section of the White Rock Trail. They're grouting some old wastewater pipes, and the closure between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is expected to last through September. You'll have to detour. Again.
