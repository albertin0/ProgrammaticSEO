Let's cut to the chase: it's August in Dallas, and that means one thing – it's hotter than Hades. The thermometer is going to be flirting with the triple-digit mark all day, expected to reach 102°F (39°C), and we're looking at heat index values that could spike to a dangerous 105-110°F later this week. I don't care how tough you think you are, this kind of heat can land you in the ER with heat stroke faster than you can say "hydration." The National Weather Service is even warning about heat-related illness symptoms.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning!** Today's temperatures will be 100-102°F (38-39°C), with heat indices expected to rise to 105-110°F later this week. Seriously, adjust your expectations or just stay inside. No PR is worth a trip to the emergency room.

### Trail Trouble: White Rock Lake

If you were planning a nice long run around White Rock Lake, you'll need to adjust your route. There's a section of the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive that's closed down for wastewater main construction. This project kicked off in June and isn't expected to wrap up until September 2026. They've got signage up and detours in place, but it's still a pain in the butt if you're trying to get your mileage in. The city closed E. Lawther Drive between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way to vehicular traffic to help with trail detours, so be aware.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Trail Section Closed!** A portion of the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is closed for construction until September 2026. Expect detours and plan your mileage accordingly.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, at least you won't have any major marathons blocking your downtown routes today. The last big street closures for those were back in 2016. But there's plenty of general construction around I-30 and I-20 with lane closures that could make navigating to your preferred running spot a headache.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** If you *must* run outside, do it before 8 AM or after 8 PM, carry more water than you think you need, and wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing. Seriously, this isn't a suggestion; it's a survival guide.
</AlertBox>
