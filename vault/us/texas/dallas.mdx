## Today's Dallas Workout Rundown

Look, Dallas, we got a mixed bag today. The weather is playing nice – 9.1°C and clear skies are what we're talking about for some decent outdoor activity. Forget that swampy summer heat or the icy grip of winter; this is prime running weather for the next few days, actually.

However, if your usual route involves the scenic loops around White Rock Lake, you're going to want to pay attention. There's been a persistent headache with a massive sinkhole. A portion of the White Rock Lake Trail, specifically between Lawther Drive and the spillway, is *still* closed. This thing is no joke – reportedly at least 14 feet deep – and it's been causing closures off and on for a few days now, especially after some storms rolled through earlier in the week. The city's monitoring it, but until it's properly fixed, consider that section a no-go. Even the Dallas Running Club had to scrap an event because of it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Lake Trail Partial Closure:** Avoid the section of White Rock Lake Trail between Lawther Drive and the spillway due to a large sinkhole. Seriously, turn around. It's not worth the twisted ankle or worse.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, your lungs are catching a break today. The Air Quality Index is a comfortable 38, which is firmly in the "Good" category. That means no smoky skies or nasty pollutants messing with your breathing. The flip side? Pollen is still moderate, and this is Dallas in March, so it's always something. Tree pollen, I'm looking at you. If you usually get sniffly this time of year, don't leave home without your meds.
