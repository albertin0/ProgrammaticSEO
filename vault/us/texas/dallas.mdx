## The Dallas Heatwave is No Joke

Look, nobody needs a meteorologist to tell them July in Dallas is hot. But this isn't just "Dallas summer" hot; we're in a full-blown heatwave. While the temperature provided is already hovering around 91°F, the "feels like" temperature is heading for triple digits and will stay that way through the weekend. News from earlier this week already warned about prolonged triple-digit heat and elevated heat risk across the Central US, including our neck of the woods. Your body isn't fully acclimated to this kind of punishment, even if you think it is. Hospitals in Dallas are seeing a "sharp rise in heat related illnesses" like dizziness, nausea, and dehydration. Seriously, respect the heat, or it will put you on your backside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning for Dallas!** Today's high temperatures and "feels like" index are dangerous. Limit outdoor activity, especially during peak afternoon hours. If you *must* be outside, hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and take frequent breaks in the shade or air conditioning. No PR is worth heatstroke.

## Breathing the Dallas Air

Your lungs are probably already feeling a little thick from the moderate pollen levels. Add to that our AQI of 55, which is mostly ozone, but let's not forget the Saharan dust that's filtering across most of the state today. Plus, there's still some residual, albeit light, wildfire smoke in the mix, even if it's more concentrated in other regions. It's enough to irritate sensitive airways and make that "moderate" rating feel a lot worse when you're pushing hard.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Moderate Air Quality & Pollen.** With ozone, Saharan dust, and moderate pollen levels, your respiratory system is working overtime. Individuals with asthma or other breathing sensitivities should consider indoor workouts. If you're out, pay attention to any shortness of breath or irritation.
</AlertBox>

## White Rock Trail Closure - Still an Issue

For those who love the White Rock Lake Loop, you're out of luck on a section of the main trail. Construction to grout an abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road has closed the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. This isn't a quick fix; it's expected to last until September 2026. Detours are in place, but they're not always ideal for maintaining your stride.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up: White Rock Trail Section Closed.** A portion of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is closed for construction until September. Plan alternate routes or be prepared for detours if you usually run this iconic Dallas path.
</AlertBox>
