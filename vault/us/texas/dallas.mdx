If you're eyeing an outdoor workout today in Dallas, you'd better have some serious puddle-jumping skills or a really good indoor alternative.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
  <li>**Flooding is a legit threat, folks.** We've had a deluge the past two days, with DFW getting over 3 inches of rain on Thursday alone, and more coming down today. The ground is saturated, and the National Weather Service is even talking about "life-threatening flooding" in spots. Unless you're training for an aquathlon, those trails around White Rock Lake are probably going to be a swampy mess, if not completely underwater.</li>
  <li>**Slick surfaces are everywhere.** All that rain means slippery roads, sidewalks, and any outdoor track you can imagine. Your ankles aren't going to thank you for trying to push pace on wet concrete.</li>
  <li>**Construction continues to chew up our city.** Even without the rain, there are ongoing major projects like the I-30 Canyon Improvement and the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center redevelopment that mean detours, closed pedestrian access, and general chaos. If you usually run a route that crosses through downtown or near major highway work, expect disruptions and potentially unsafe conditions.</li>
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. The temperature is a mild 21.9°C, and the air quality index is a respectable 46, which is "good" by any standard. Pollen is only moderate, so your sinuses probably won't spontaneously combust. But those are minor victories when you're risking a broken bone or worse trying to navigate flooded streets and trails.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Flash Flood Warning in Effect:** Multiple reports indicate continuous heavy rainfall from Thursday through today (Saturday), with significant flooding risks across the Dallas area. Many low-lying areas, underpasses, and natural trails are likely impassable or extremely hazardous. Stay clear of flooded roads and trails. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy slogan; it's how you avoid becoming a statistic.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, save your ankles and your dignity. That run along the Trinity Trails might seem appealing with the overcast skies, but I guarantee parts of it are resembling a river right now. Remember when they canceled events at Klyde Warren Park just two days ago due to severe weather? That wasn't for fun. The same storms are still lingering.

We've got plenty of construction headaches too. If you're anywhere near the I-30 corridor, or trying to cross from Oak Cliff into downtown, you're already dealing with endless reroutes and lane closures. Adding a monsoon to that mix makes for a truly miserable and dangerous experience. And don't even get me started on the multi-year project around the convention center. It's a mess, and pedestrians often get the short end of the stick.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Ongoing Construction Zones:** Beyond the immediate flood risk, several major road and infrastructure projects are in full swing across Dallas. Expect unexpected closures and limited pedestrian access around construction areas, especially those related to the I-30 improvements and the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center expansion. Always be aware of your surroundings and obey all signage. Some older alerts from earlier this year even explicitly stated "no pedestrian access is allowed inside the closed work zones" on major streets.
</AlertBox>

Maybe hit the gym. Find a StairMaster. Do some burpees in your living room. Your physical therapist (me, right now) would rather you be bored than broken.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Consider Indoor Options:** With the persistent rain and potential for flooding, today is an excellent day to explore indoor workout alternatives. Many Dallas gyms offer day passes, or you could focus on bodyweight exercises at home.
