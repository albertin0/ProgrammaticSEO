### It's a Scorcher, Even for Texas
Don't let the "partly cloudy" fool you. While the air quality is currently "Good" with an AQI of 46 (dominant pollutant PM2.5), the temperature is sitting around 32.7°C (90.86°F) and feels hotter with the humidity. We've been under a slew of heat advisories and Ozone Action Days recently, and even though today isn't officially one of *those* days, the cumulative effect of this September's record-breaking heat is no joke. You'll burn out faster than a wildfire in August if you don't take it easy.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Out For Sparks (Literally)
With these persistent high temperatures and dry conditions, the risk of grass fires is still elevated, especially west of I-35. If you're out on the trails near dry brush, be extra careful. No discarded cigarettes, no parking on tall grass, and for the love of all that is holy, don't even *think* about outdoor burning right now.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### White Rock Trail Closure Alert
Heads up, White Rock Lake regulars: There's still construction happening on the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. This section has been closed since June for wastewater main work, and while it's *expected* to wrap up this month, it's safer to assume it's still blocked. Check the Dallas Park and Recreation site before you head out, or just plan an alternate route. Nobody wants to add an unexpected detour to their workout when it's already this hot.
</AlertBox>
