Seriously, folks. A section of the White Rock Lake Trail between Lawther Drive and the spillway is *closed due to a sinkhole*. This isn't a suggestion; it's a hard stop. Don't try to get around it. Turn back, find an alternate route, or hit a different trail entirely. Dallas Parks & Recreation isn't messing around with this one, and neither should you.

This is a real pain, especially for those of us who rely on those long loops. You'll need to adjust your plans if White Rock is your usual stomping ground. Maybe hit the Katy Trail for an out-and-back or explore the Trinity Strand Trail, although be aware of ongoing construction there, too.

## Your Daily PT Advice: Adapt and Overcome

Given the sinkhole situation, today is about adapting. If you were planning a long run on White Rock, consider breaking it up or finding another loop. You could still enjoy sections of White Rock, but that full perimeter is out.
