## Today's Dallas Workout Reality Check

Look, I get it. We're runners, we *want* to be outside. But sometimes the city throws you a curveball that’s less "challenging sprint" and more "hospital visit waiting to happen." Today is one of those days. The heat is the absolute killer here. Even if your watch *says* 91°F right now, the ground is hotter, the sun is relentless, and your body is working overtime just to stay cool. Yesterday set records, and today isn't far behind. A heat advisory means dangerous conditions for anyone spending extended time outdoors.

Then there's the air. Our local AQI says "good," and I appreciate the optimism, but with the Ross Fire chewing through Palo Pinto and Jack counties, that smoke *will* drift, especially with those southwesterly winds. "Moderate air quality" has been reported regionally, and the TCEQ has indicated the potential for an Ozone Action Day. If you have any kind of respiratory issues, or even if you don't, your lungs will feel the extra irritation.

And for those of you who love the White Rock Lake loop, planning to hit the pavement for some miles? Think again. The White Rock Trail, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, is completely shut down for wastewater main construction. This isn't a quick detour; it's a "duration of construction" closure expected to last until September. Plan your routes accordingly, or just find a treadmill.
