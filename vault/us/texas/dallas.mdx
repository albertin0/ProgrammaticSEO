### Air Quality & Ozone

Alright, the Air Quality Index (AQI) is looking good at 42, which is nice for your lungs. But let's not get too comfortable. The dominant pollutant is Ozone, and while it's currently in the clear, forecasters are saying conditions today could be ripe for ozone to hit "Moderate" levels in the DFW area by this afternoon and early evening. So, if you *must* be out there, try to get it done earlier in the day before the ozone starts cooking.

### Weather Whining (But Important)

The current temp sits at a decent 23.1°C (73.6°F) and it's mainly clear, which sounds lovely, right? Don't let it fool you. The National Weather Service is calling for highs in the low 90s with heat index values between 95-105°F for today. It’s not just the temperature that gets you; it’s the humidity turning the air into soup.
