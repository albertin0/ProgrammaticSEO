## Trail Talk & Urban Obstacles

If you're planning on hitting the pavement, buckle up, because downtown Dallas is still a construction nightmare. The **I-30 Canyon Project** has various sections of I-30 and surrounding streets like Botham Jean/Lamar Boulevard, Griffin Street, and Cesar Chavez Boulevard closed or restricted through late 2027 and even into 2028. And here’s the kicker: *pedestrian access is not permitted inside closed construction zones*. So, trying to navigate those parts of downtown on foot or bike is not just annoying, it's unsafe and forbidden.

Good news for those of you who love **White Rock Trail** near Garland Road; that wastewater main construction that's been messing with our runs was actually completed back in August. So, that section should be clear for your morning loops. Meanwhile, if you were planning on visiting **Lake Tawakoni State Park**, it officially reopens at 12 PM today after being closed for a youth deer hunt.

<AlertBox type="info">
**I-30 Construction Ahead (and all around you)!** Avoid the downtown I-30 corridor entirely if you're planning a run or ride. Seriously, those closures around Botham Jean, Lamar, Griffin, and Cesar Chavez aren't messing around. Find alternative routes further north or south, or stick to established trails far from the chaos.
</AlertBox>

## Local Lowdown: Deep Ellum Danger

Now, for some grim news that's highly relevant for any evening workout plans. This past Halloween weekend saw a spate of mass shootings in Dallas, primarily centered in the **Deep Ellum** neighborhood. Multiple incidents left people dead and dozens injured. While it's Monday, and things might feel "back to normal," the recent nature of these events means you need to exercise extreme caution.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid Deep Ellum for Evening Workouts.** Following multiple mass shootings in the area over the Halloween weekend, exercising in Deep Ellum, especially after dark, poses a significant safety risk. Prioritize your personal safety and choose a different, well-lit, and populated area for your runs or walks.
</AlertBox>

Finally, for the skaters out there, the **Bachman Skate Park** is experiencing mechanical issues with its main gate, leading to delayed openings around 10:45 a.m. Just a heads-up so you don't show up to locked gates too early.
