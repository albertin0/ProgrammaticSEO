## Today's Dallas Rundown

Let's be real, you *can* get your steps in today, but you need to be smart about it. The weather's doing that classic North Texas "will it or won't it" dance, and you don't want to be caught in the middle of a sudden downpour or, worse, a hailstorm when you're halfway around White Rock Lake.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Storms Brewing
Seriously, keep an eye on the sky and your weather app. Scattered thunderstorms are on the docket for today, and forecasters are talking about the potential for strong winds and hail. Those southerly winds are no joke, hitting up to 25 mph. Headwinds will be a grind, and crosswinds can make cycling tricky. With the rain chances, expect some wet, potentially slick surfaces on paved trails and muddy conditions on natural ones. Watch your footing, especially if you're hitting any inclines or declines.
</AlertBox>
