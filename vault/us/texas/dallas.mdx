## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Ozone Action Day, Y'all:** Forget that "Good" AQI number for a second. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) has officially declared an Ozone Action Day for DFW until Monday evening. This means atmospheric conditions are ripe for producing high levels of ground-level ozone, which is basically a lung irritant. So, your planned run along the Katy Trail might feel less like a workout and more like an asthma attack waiting to happen.
*   **Moderate Pollen:** Adding insult to injury, we've got moderate pollen levels floating around. Combine that with the ozone, and your respiratory system is working overtime before you even tie your shoes.
*   **White Rock Trail is a Hot Mess (Still):** If your go-to is White Rock Lake Trail, get ready for detours or just pick a new spot. Construction crews are still busy grouting an abandoned wastewater main between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, and that section of the trail is completely shut down until at least September. They've closed E. Lawther to cars to help, but seriously, don't expect a clear path.
*   **Heat's Comin' for Ya:** While it's a pleasant 24.2°C (about 76°F) this morning, don't be fooled. Highs are expected to climb into the 100s today, and the "feels-like" temperatures are set to jump to a brutal 105°-110°F starting Tuesday and Wednesday. Today might be okay early, but the overall trend for the week is scorching.

</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ozone Action Day - Limit Outdoor Activity

Seriously, folks. An Ozone Action Day means the air quality is going to suck for your lungs. If you have any respiratory conditions, or even if you don't, consider moving your workout indoors today. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, keep it light, and get out there before the sun really starts baking the ozone. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### White Rock Trail Detours & Future Heat

Planning a long run around White Rock? You'll need to reroute. The closure between E. Lawther and Winsted Drive isn't going anywhere fast, and trying to navigate construction zones isn't worth twisting an ankle. Find an alternate route or embrace the treadmill. Also, keep that brutal heat forecast in mind for the rest of the week. Hydrate *now*, and adjust your workouts accordingly. These "feels like" temps are no joke.
</AlertBox>
