## Local Hazards & Roadblocks

Alright, let's talk about the real buzzkill: construction. While the weather is playing nice, Dallas is still a giant construction zone.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Downtown Mess
Seriously, if you're planning a run that goes anywhere near the heart of the city, especially south of I-30, you need to check for closures. The I-30 Canyon corridor project is still causing major headaches, with long-term closures on Cesar Chavez, Cadiz, and Akard Streets. Pedestrian access is a no-go in those work zones, so plan your alternate routes now. You don't want to get stuck trying to navigate detours when you're just trying to get your miles in. Consider heading to White Rock Lake if you want a guaranteed clear path today.
</AlertBox>
