## Why today is *not* the day to push it:

<BulletList>
*   **Heat is a Real Threat:** While your weather app might only say 28.7°C (83.7°F) right now, the high today is predicted to hit 101°F (38.3°C), and those "feels like" temperatures will hover around 100-105°F this afternoon. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous for sustained outdoor activity. Remember those Mesquite firefighters who got transported for heat exhaustion? That's your body on Dallas August heat if you're not careful.
*   **Your Lungs Aren't Loving It:** We're sitting at a Moderate AQI of 58 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. On top of that, there's an active Air Quality Alert that was issued just a couple of days ago. Pair that with "Moderate" pollen levels, and it's a perfect storm for anyone with even minor respiratory issues. Your lungs will be working overtime just to breathe this thick soup, let alone power a PR.
*   **White Rock Lake Trail is a Construction Zone:** If your usual route takes you around White Rock Lake, be aware that a significant portion of the trail *between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive* is CLOSED. They're grouting an abandoned wastewater main. This project has been ongoing since June and isn't expected to finish until September. Detours are in place, even closing E. Lawther Drive to vehicles in some spots to help trail users. Don't assume you can just power through; you'll hit barricades and likely be forced onto less safe, more convoluted routes. There's also the persistent sinkhole issue near the spillway causing ongoing detours.
</BulletList>

**Extreme Heat Hazard:** Temperatures pushing into the triple digits with high "feels like" values mean heatstroke is a real risk. Reschedule your long runs or intense sessions for early morning or late evening, or better yet, take it indoors. Hydrate like it's your job, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get inside IMMEDIATELY. This isn't a drill.
</AlertBox>

**Air Quality Alert:** Even though the AQI is only "Moderate" right now, the active Air Quality Alert means conditions are ripe for higher pollution and ozone, especially during peak afternoon hours. </AlertBox>
