### Lungs: Breathe Easy (Mostly)

The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 46, which is firmly in the "Good" category. You won't be huffing car exhaust or industrial smog out there today. That's a win. However, it's not all sunshine and clean air for everyone. We're looking at a moderate pollen count. If your nose starts running or your eyes get itchy mid-run, you know who to blame. Keep those allergy meds handy.

**Allergy Advisory:** Moderate tree pollen is the main culprit today. If you're sensitive, pre-dose your antihistamine or consider an indoor alternative if you want to avoid feeling like you ran through a cloud of sneezes.
</AlertBox>

### Joints: Prepare for the Heat Creep

Yesterday saw some scattered showers that kept temperatures in the upper 80s, which for July in Dallas, felt like a miracle. Today, however, we're making a hard pivot back to what we all know and... tolerate. Expect temperatures to climb into the low to mid-90s today, with forecasts predicting a full-blown return to triple digits by early next week. Your body is going to feel that. Even with a "clear" start this morning, the humidity isn't going anywhere, making that "feels like" temperature a real pain.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat's Coming Back:** Don't underestimate the rising temperatures. Hydrate early and often, carry water, and listen to your body. If you're not acclimated, keep your workout shorter and earlier in the day. There's no medal for heat stroke, just a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

### Local Buzzkill: White Rock Lake Trail Closure

Speaking of planning your run, if you're a regular on the White Rock Lake Trail, be aware that a significant section is still closed for construction. Dallas Water Utilities started grouting an abandoned pipeline back in mid-June near Garland Road, and that project isn't wrapping up until September. This means the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is currently shut down. They've got detours posted, but it's a pain, plain and simple. E. Lawther between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way is also closed to vehicle traffic to accommodate the detours. Plan your route accordingly, or better yet, explore a different path today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Lake Trail Section CLOSED:** The White Rock Lake Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive remains closed due to ongoing construction. Expect detours and plan an alternative route to avoid frustration and potential safety hazards around the work zone. This is *not* a temporary inconvenience; it's expected to last until September.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's not a terrible day to get outside, but you've got to be smart about it. Hydrate, know your allergies, and for the love of all that is holy, don't try to power through that closed trail section.
