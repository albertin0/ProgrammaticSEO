<AlertBox type="danger">
### Flash Flood Risk is Real
A Flood Watch is active across North Texas through Sunday afternoon, meaning heavy rainfall could lead to flash flooding. If you see standing water, especially on trails or underpasses, for the love of your ankles (and your car), **TURN AROUND.** That water is deeper than it looks, and currents are no joke. Recent storms even brought quarter-sized hail to Dallas County yesterday, so expect some debris and slick spots if the rain lets up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Lake Trail: A Construction Zone (and potentially a Puddle)
While the big White Rock Trail closure for wastewater work near E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive isn't kicking off until June 15th, there's already ongoing construction around White Rock Lake for gabion wall improvements. Portions of parking lots are being used for equipment. This, combined with any heavy rain, means parts of the trail could be extra muddy, uneven, or have unexpected detours. Don't assume your usual loop is clear.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Gear Up for the Gloom
If you absolutely *must* get outside, wear bright colors, reflective gear, and shoes with good traction. And maybe tell someone where you're going, because slipping and sliding on a muddy trail isn't going to impress anyone, least of all me when you show up in my clinic. Stay hydrated, but mostly, stay out of the floodwaters.
</AlertBox>
