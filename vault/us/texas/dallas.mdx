Look, I get it. It's the Fourth of July, and you're probably feeling all patriotic and ready to run off those barbecue brats before you even eat them. But seriously, Dallas is baking. The National Weather Service is calling for "Hot" conditions with highs around 100°F and heat index values approaching 105°F this afternoon. Do you *really* want to be out there trying to PR your 10k when it feels like running through a wet blanket in an oven? Your body isn't going to thank you for it, and your knees will definitely be sending me hate mail later.

While the air quality is actually quite decent with an AQI of 46 and a "Moderate" pollen count, that's like saying a hot dog cart in July has "acceptable" hygiene standards – it might be true, but it doesn't mean you should dive in headfirst. The real enemy today is the heat. We don't have active warnings for extreme heat *right now* as of early morning on the Fourth, but the forecast clearly indicates we're heading that way. This isn't some surprise pop-up shower; this is predictable, suffocating Texas summer heat.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: Think Twice Before Lacing Up!**
Temperatures are already high and will only climb, with "feels like" temperatures nearing 105°F. This is prime territory for heat exhaustion and heatstroke. If you insist on working out, go *early* – like, before the sun properly wakes up – and keep it short. Hydration is non-negotiable. And for the love of all that is holy, pay attention to your body.
</AlertBox>

If you were planning to hit the scenic White Rock Lake Trail for your Independence Day long run, you might want to reconsider your route. Parts of that popular trail are still under construction, specifically for pipeline repairs and gabion wall improvements. The City of Dallas Park and Recreation department has notices about ongoing work along the trail and Anderson Bonner Park. You'll be dodging cones and equipment, not enjoying the view. Factor in potential detours and added frustration to your already heat-taxed workout.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Construction Ahead: White Rock Lake Detours**
