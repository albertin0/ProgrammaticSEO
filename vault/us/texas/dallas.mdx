## Today's Rundown: What to Watch For

### Post-Rain Potholes and Puddles

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOODING REMNANTS ARE REAL.** Just last night, parts of southeastern Dallas County, including Mesquite, were under a flash flood warning with reports of water rescues. Northeastern Dallas County also saw a flash flood warning, impacting areas around **White Rock Lake** and Garland. While the warnings might be expired, standing water and muddy trails will absolutely persist. Don't be "that guy" who thinks their ankles are waterproof. Turn around if you see standing water; it's usually deeper than it looks and hides all sorts of nastiness. Check local trail group forums or, better yet, eyeball your route first if you're hitting spots prone to flooding.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**DALLAS NORTH TOLLWAY LANE CLOSURES.** If your workout involves driving anywhere near Uptown this weekend, prepare for a slow crawl. The Dallas North Tollway, specifically the stretch near Lemmon Avenue, is down to a single lane in each direction until Monday morning for bridge maintenance. That's a recipe for frustration, so plan your commute to your favorite park or gym accordingly, or just stick to neighborhood runs to avoid the gridlock.
</AlertBox>
