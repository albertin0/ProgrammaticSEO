## Today's Dallas Run Down

Alright, Dallas, if you’re looking to break a sweat today, March 28th, you’ve got a mixed bag. The weather's playing nice, sitting at a cool 12.3°C (that’s about 54°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheitland), under an overcast sky. Don't expect blazing sun, but also don't expect to freeze your extremities off. Light winds are in the forecast, which is a nice change from the gale-force nonsense we sometimes get.

The air quality, bless its heart, is a lovely 36 AQI. That’s "Good" in plain English, so your respiratory system can breathe easy, literally. No wildfire smoke or random industrial funk to worry about today, thankfully.

However, let’s talk pollen, because it’s always something, isn’t it? We’re sitting at a "Moderate" level for the usual suspects: Cedar, Oak, and Elm. If you’re prone to allergies, you know the drill: pre-medicate, shower after, and try not to rub your eyes with your sweaty hands.

### Hazards & Headaches

Here's the real kicker: if White Rock Lake is your usual stomping ground, you might want to find an alternative.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### White Rock Lake Sinkhole: STILL A NO-GO!
Listen up, because this is important: A significant portion of the **White Rock Lake Trail** near the spillway (specifically around the intersection of Garland Road and San Rafael Way) is **CLOSED INDEFINITELY** due to a massive sinkhole. We're talking 20 to 40 feet deep here, folks. They've tried to patch it up, but it keeps causing problems, likely from an abandoned sewer line. Don't be that person trying to sneak around the barriers. It's dangerous, it's not stable, and you'll just piss off the folks trying to fix it. Find another trail. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### White Rock Lake Construction Continues
Beyond the sinkhole, there’s ongoing construction around White Rock Lake and Anderson Bonner Park. Dallas Water Utilities is doing gabion wall improvements, which means parts of parking lots are shut down and you might run into temporary street closures. There's also the "design phase" of the White Rock Lake Dredging Project kicking off this month, which will be ongoing until December 2028. It’s not a full-blown obstacle course, but expect detours and equipment. Stay alert for signs and cones.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Big Cedar Wilderness Trails Events
For you trail runners out there, Big Cedar Wilderness Trails has "The Great Root Trail Race" and a group hike happening today. If you're heading that way, expect more traffic (both foot and vehicle) and potentially some race infrastructure. It's rugged singletrack, so watch your footing even without the crowds.
</AlertBox>
