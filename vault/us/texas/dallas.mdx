### Hazards & Headaches

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone Alert for Sensitive Lungs:** With a moderate AQI driven by ozone, anyone with asthma, COPD, or just generally sensitive respiratory systems should dial back the intensity. Don't plan a PR attempt on your White Rock Lake loop today if you fall into this group. Light activity is fine, but listen to your body before it starts screaming.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Elevated Fire Risk:** The clear, warm, and dry conditions bring an elevated fire risk to the area. Be extremely careful with anything that could spark a fire, especially near our drier green spaces. If you see smoke, report it. Nobody wants to be the person who accidentally incinerates a nature preserve because they weren't paying attention.
</AlertBox>

</AlertBox>

## Your PT's Honest Take

Look, you *can* go outside today. The weather's mostly agreeable. But if you've got allergies, take your meds.
