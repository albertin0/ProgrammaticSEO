### A Word on Dallas Air

While today's AQI for Dallas-Fort Worth is registering in the "Good" range, and the forecast says we're at the upper end of "Good" for ozone, don't get too comfortable. A recent report from the American Lung Association just dropped, and it's a sobering read: the DFW metro area ranked 9th worst in the nation for ozone pollution and 22nd worst for year-round particle pollution (PM2.5) based on 2022-2024 data. It also noted that our air quality has worsened. So, while a single good day is nice, it doesn't erase the underlying issues. Remember that "PM2.5" being the dominant pollutant today is exactly what the Lung Association is sounding the alarm about.

Speaking of trails, it's "Celebrate Trails Day" today! That means a lot of folks will be out enjoying our fantastic trail system. Be courteous, share the path, and enjoy the community vibe.

## Construction & Closures

The never-ending story of Dallas infrastructure continues. Preconstruction is already underway for that massive I-30 rebuild project which is set to kick off in earnest this year and last *five* years. This beast will add lanes and several crossings through a 2.3-mile section of the interstate. While it's not a direct trail closure, if you run or bike anywhere near I-30, expect traffic impacts and potential noise.

More locally, the City Council has approved over $120M in April transportation spending, which includes "trail construction" and "street resurfacing." The Cotton Belt Trail corridor, in particular, is slated for $6.4M in federal construction activity. This means there's a good chance you could encounter unexpected detours or uneven surfaces on less-trafficked paths. Keep your head up and stay aware of your surroundings.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you rely on DART to get to your favorite starting point, be aware of ongoing disruptions. DART Cityplace/Uptown station repairs are causing temporary service changes and weekend work on the northbound track from April 24-27. Check your DART app before you head out, especially if your route involves those stations.
</AlertBox>
