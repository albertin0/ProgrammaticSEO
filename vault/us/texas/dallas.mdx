## The Dallas Rundown: What to Watch Out For

Let's be real, April in Dallas can be a mixed bag. Today, the air quality is fantastic (AQI 37, folks, that's practically pristine with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant). Your lungs will thank you. But for those of us who actually get out there and hit the pavement or the dirt, "pristine" doesn't mean "perfectly safe."

### Trinity River: More Like Trinity *Swim*

Look, I love our city's trails, especially the ones hugging the Trinity River. But after the deluge we saw over the weekend, those low-lying paths are likely underwater. News reports from Saturday indicated the Trinity River was expected to rise above flood stage, with bike trails possibly covered by up to two feet of water. Don't test it. Flash floods are no joke, and you don't want to be that person rescued from a muddy, debris-filled river. Find higher ground.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Immediate Danger: Flooded Trinity River Trails.** Avoid all trails and low-lying areas along the Trinity River. Water levels are likely high and can be extremely dangerous, covering paths and hiding debris.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen Punch

Our environmental data is showing moderate pollen levels. While not extreme, if you’re prone to allergies, you know what that means: watery eyes, runny nose, and feeling like you just ran a marathon before you even started. Take your antihistamines, carry tissues, and maybe opt for a route with less shrubbery if you’re particularly sensitive.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate Pollen Alert.** Allergy sufferers, take your meds *before* you head out. Don't let sneezing fits ruin your rhythm.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Zone Headaches (for Road Warriors)

If your workout involves pounding the pavement on roads rather than trails, be aware of ongoing construction. TxDOT has a laundry list of lane closures and projects. For instance, I-30 is seeing daily lane reductions between FM 2871 and Linkcrest Dr for truck access and pothole repair. While the Dallas North Tollway bridges aren't slated for weekend closures *this* first weekend of April, expect delays if you're driving to your workout spot on any major highway. Keep your head on a swivel if you're running or cycling near these areas.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Road Construction Around Major Highways.** Expect ongoing lane closures and potential traffic near I-30 and other major arteries. Plan your routes and stay extra vigilant if you're exercising on or near roads.
</AlertBox>
