## Today's Dallas Grind: Sweat, Sneezes, and Shady Air

Look, I get it. You've got miles to log, gains to make. But today, September 12, 2026, Dallas is serving up a triple threat: heat, moderate pollen, and a sneaky dive in air quality later on. It’s not the best day for your long run or that grueling interval session you had planned.

### The Heat is Still On

Forget autumn crispness; we're still deep in what feels like August. We're looking at 90-degree heat, and the "feels like" temperature, thanks to our good old friend humidity, is going to hover around 105°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous if you push too hard. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on faster than you think, especially when you're trying to outrun your personal best on the Katy Trail.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Limit strenuous outdoor activity to early morning or late evening. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light, loose-fitting clothing, and pay attention to your body. No PR is worth a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

### Don't Breathe Too Deeply

While the *current* AQI might be sitting pretty at 47, giving us a false sense of security, the forecast isn't as rosy. AccuWeather is predicting our air quality to shift from "Fair" to "Poor" today and into Sunday. That "Ozone/Other" dominant pollutant means your lungs will be working overtime. If you have any respiratory sensitivities, you're going to feel it. Add moderate pollen to that mix, and your sinuses will be staging their own protest.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Advisory:** Sensitive groups should consider postponing outdoor workouts or moving them indoors as the day progresses. Even if you're not sensitive, prolonged exposure isn't doing you any favors with the forecasted "Poor" air quality for the weekend.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Tidbits: White Rock Creek

If you're hitting the trails, especially around White Rock Lake, be aware. The segment of the White Rock Lake Trail along Garland Road that was closed for pipeline grouting back in June? That's actually open now, they finished ahead of schedule in early August. Good news there.

However, White Rock Creek Trail itself has some ongoing maintenance. Crews are still clearing debris and a major downed tree north of the DART rail bridge, and there's surface repair happening near the Royal underpass where creek erosion is an issue. Don't expect perfectly smooth sailing everywhere.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Tip:** When hitting trails like White Rock Creek, always keep an eye out for maintenance crews, debris, and potential erosion spots. A twisted ankle is a real buzzkill. The Katy Trail, however, should be clear and well-lit during its operating hours (5 a.m. to 11 p.m.).
</AlertBox>

So, if you absolutely *must* get out there, go early, hydrate constantly, and pick your route wisely. Otherwise, maybe today’s a good day for some indoor cross-training or simply enjoying a long, air-conditioned nap. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
