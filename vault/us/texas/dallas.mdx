## Dallas, You're Too Hot for Your Own Good

Seriously, Dallas, it's July 22nd, and the city is basically a giant oven with a side of smog. If you're looking to pound the pavement or hit the trails, you need to think twice, then a third, and probably just stay inside. We're under an **Extreme Heat Warning** for a reason. Expect actual temperatures to climb into the low 100s, but the *feels like* temperature? That's going to hit a brutal 112-113°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Physicians at Parkland Memorial Hospital are already seeing a spike in heat-related illnesses this month, urging everyone, especially athletes, to take this seriously. Your body's cooling system gets overwhelmed fast in these conditions.

### Lung-Busting Air Quality

As if the heat weren't enough, it's an **Ozone Action Day** for the Dallas-Fort Worth area. This means atmospheric conditions are ripe for ground-level ozone pollution to reach unhealthy levels. Our general Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a moderate 65, primarily due to PM2.5, but the ozone alert adds another layer of nastiness to the air you'll be sucking in. For anyone with respiratory issues, or honestly, anyone with lungs, this is a terrible combination with strenuous activity. The American Lung Association even highlighted Dallas-Fort Worth's worsening ozone pollution in their 2026 report.

### Trail Trouble

If you thought you could escape the concrete jungle for a nice run around White Rock Lake, think again. There's ongoing construction on the **White Rock Trail** between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. This isn't some quick fix; it's a major project involving an abandoned wastewater main, and it's expected to last until September. That means significant sections are closed, and you'll be forced onto detours that might be less scenic, less shaded, and potentially involve more interaction with traffic. E. Lawther Drive itself is closed to vehicular traffic in a section to accommodate trail detours, so expect some confusion and extra heat if you're navigating that area.
