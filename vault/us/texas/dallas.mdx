## The Rundown: Breathe Easy, Watch Your Step

The air quality in Dallas today is looking good, folks. We're sitting at an AQI of 46, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality even predicts that fine particulate concentrations will have minimal effects today, thanks to low humidity, so your lungs can mostly just do their job without extra drama. That's a win in my book.

Temperatures around 9.7°C this morning mean you'll definitely feel that chill starting out, but don't let it fool you. The forecast shows things warming up into the mid-60s to low 70s Fahrenheit (around 15-22°C) later in the day, with clear skies and light winds. Perfect layering weather, if you ask me.

## Watch Out for the Crowds and Cones

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Trail Construction Alert
If your usual loop involves White Rock Trail near Garland Road between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive, be advised there's ongoing construction. Crews are grouting a wastewater main. It's been going on since June, so it's not new, but always worth a reminder that sections might be a mess or require detours. Don't assume your favorite stretch is clear sailing.
</AlertBox>

Today's a busy one in and around DFW, which means if you're not participating, you might be dodging. The Dallas Heart Walk is happening at Levy Event Plaza in Irving, bringing a good crowd out for 1-mile and 5K routes. Expect some localized congestion if you're in the Las Colinas area. While not directly in Dallas, the Night Nation Run is over in Arlington, and the Spartan Trifecta Weekend is out in Granbury, drawing runners away from the city center but potentially impacting traffic heading out that way.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route Around Events
With multiple runs and events happening, check specific event schedules and maps if you're planning a long run on popular routes. You might find more people than usual, or unexpected detours, especially around areas like Fair Park, where the State Fair of Texas is just wrapping up and could still be causing some lingering traffic.
</AlertBox>

All in all, the weather and air quality are giving us a green light for outdoor exercise. Just be aware of your surroundings, layer up, and maybe pick a less trafficked route if you're trying to set a PR today.
