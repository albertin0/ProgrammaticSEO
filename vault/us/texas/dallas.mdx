Seriously, Dallas, do you *need* to go out there? We're in an "extended stretch of summertime heat and humidity," which is a fancy way of saying it's scorching and sticky. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about risking your health. There's also not much relief overnight, so your body isn't getting a real break from the heat.

Good news on the air front, though: your AQI is sitting pretty at a "Good" 46. So, your lungs aren't going to be fighting pollution *and* the heat. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a perfect day. We're still dealing with moderate pollen, so if you're sensitive, you'll still feel it.

### Local Hazards You Need to Know

**Extreme Heat: It's No Joke.** The official forecast is warning about heat index values that make outdoor activity risky. Don't push through dizziness or nausea.
