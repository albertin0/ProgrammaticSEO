### Temperature Trends: Don't Be a Heat Casualty

Seriously, people, 17.4°C is a lie. The National Weather Service is forecasting high temperatures in the upper 80s to mid-90s across North and Central Texas today and through the weekend. AccuWeather agrees, with daily highs for May 2026 often hitting above 80°F. You think you're going out in a pleasant spring morning, and by noon, you're cooking on the Katy Trail like a forgotten brisket. This is prime time for heat exhaustion if you're not smart.

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Heat Hazard Ahead!
Today's temperatures are climbing fast. If you're out on the White Rock Lake Trail or anywhere exposed, plan your runs for early morning or late evening. Carry more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and don't be too proud to cut your workout short. Nobody wants to be that person passed out by the water fountain.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality: Breathe Easy (Or Try To)

With an AQI of 56 and ozone as the main offender, your lungs aren't getting a free pass. An Ozone Action Day means conditions are ripe for ground-level ozone to reach elevated levels. For those of you with asthma or other respiratory issues, this isn't the day to test your limits. Even if you're a seasoned runner, you might feel a little more winded than usual.

<AlertBox type="info">
## Ozone Awareness
On an Ozone Action Day, try to schedule your outdoor activity for the early morning before ozone levels typically peak in the afternoon. Listen to your body. If you're wheezing or coughing, it's a sign to head indoors.
</AlertBox>
