## Today's Dallas Rundown: Just Stay Dry

Look, I'm all for pushing through, but today feels less like "pushing through" and more like "asking for a sprained ankle or a miserable cold." It's 15.4°C (57°F) and overcast, which usually I'd call a prime running temp, but that 85% chance of rain just kills the vibe. We're talking widespread light to moderate showers, dumping anywhere from 0.25 to 0.75 inches across North Texas. That's enough to turn a pleasant stroll into a slip-and-slide.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step!** With the continuous rain, trails and sidewalks, especially those with uneven surfaces or recent erosion, are going to be slicker than usual. Think twice before trying to set any speed records out on the White Rock Lake Trail; there's already construction on gabion wall improvements that can make things dicey. Adding rain to that mix is just asking for trouble.
</AlertBox>
