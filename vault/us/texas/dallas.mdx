## Today's Dallas Grind Breakdown

Seriously, if you're planning any kind of outdoor workout today, you better be up with the sun and finished before it decides to really flex. The National Weather Service for Fort Worth/Dallas is calling for highs around 100°F and those lovely heat index values pushing 105°F. That's not just "sweaty," that's "risk of heat stroke" territory.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  <h3>Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!</h3>
  An Extreme Heat Warning has been issued for parts of Dallas until Wednesday evening. Limit strenuous activities to early morning or evening. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, do NOT leave kids or pets in unattended vehicles. This isn't a suggestion; it's a matter of survival.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Road Ramblings

That construction on the White Rock Lake Trail near Garland Road and E. Lawther, which closed a section earlier this summer, is now *done* and the trail is officially open again as of Friday. So go enjoy that, but remember the heat.

For those navigating to and from your favorite running spots, keep in mind there are still nightly lane closures on the southbound Dallas North Tollway between Harvest Hill Road and Royal Lane from 9 PM to 5 AM through August 14th. This is primarily for drivers, but if you're trying to hit the Katy Trail early or late, plan accordingly for traffic.
