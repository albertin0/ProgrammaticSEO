Your personal AQI monitor might be showing a moderate PM2.5, but the overall air quality, driven by ozone, is a bigger concern. You might be tough, but your lungs aren't going to thank you for a tempo run when the air is trying to fight back. Yesterday saw similar AQI levels hitting 105 due to ozone.
