## The Dallas Summer Sizzle (and Soak)

Let's cut to the chase: stepping outside for a serious workout in Dallas today is going to be a battle against the elements, primarily the heat and its lingering watery aftermath. While your phone might show a reasonable temperature, the heat index, that "feels like" number, is forecast to hit as high as a brutal 105°F (40.5°C) today. You know as well as I do that North Texas humidity turns even a pleasant 75°F into a sweat lodge, and at 105°F, it's just plain dangerous. Health officials are hammering home the heat safety message this summer for a reason – heat-related illness is no joke.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Index!** "Feels like" temperatures are expected to soar to 105°F or higher today. Limit strenuous outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and listen to your body. Heatstroke is a real, life-threatening risk.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the recent deluge. Dallas-Fort Worth got hammered by flash flooding just a couple of days ago, turning streets into rivers and prompting Dallas Fire-Rescue to respond to countless calls about stranded vehicles. The Trinity River was under a flood warning, with trails along the river expected to be inundated. Even if the sun's out now, those low-lying trails, especially along the Trinity River Greenbelt, are probably still muddy, slick, and potentially closed. You don't want to sprain an ankle or worse trying to navigate what amounts to a mud pit.

## Trail Trouble & Breathing Room

If you were planning a long run around White Rock Lake, think again. Not only has a section been closed since March due to a sinkhole, but major construction work just kicked off this past week on an abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road. That means the trail between E. Lawther and Winsted is shut down until September. Dallas isn't exactly short on construction, and this is another big one impacting a popular running spot. Always check city alerts before heading out.

As for the air, while the overall AQI might seem "Good" at 30, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecasts the daily PM2.5 AQI to hit the "Moderate" range for Dallas-Fort Worth today. AccuWeather also suggests a "Fair" air quality, meaning sensitive groups might experience some symptoms. Given that the American Lung Association recently slammed DFW for worsening air quality and ranking 9th worst for ozone pollution, taking it easy on the lungs today isn't a bad idea.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: White Rock Lake Trail Closures.** A significant portion of the White Rock Lake Trail between E. Lawther and Winsted is closed for construction until September 2026 due to work on a wastewater main. Detours are in place, but expect interruptions.
</AlertBox>
