### Thunderstorm & Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors!
Seriously, lightning kills. Wait until at least 30 minutes *after* the last rumble of thunder before even thinking about heading out. Once it clears, the heat is going to be oppressive. If you absolutely *must* run, do it early, carry more water than you think you need, and pick a shaded route like the Katy Trail or stick to a treadmill. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or confused, stop immediately and get inside. This isn't a day to push your limits, it's a day to survive.
</AlertBox>
