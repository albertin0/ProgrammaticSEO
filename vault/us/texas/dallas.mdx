## Don't Be a Hero, Be Hydrated

Today is one of those days where your ego needs to take a backseat to basic physiology. We're talking about near-record highs, making this one of the hottest weeks all summer. Unless your idea of a good time involves heat stroke or an emergency room visit, stay indoors.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: Noon to 8 PM CDT!
This isn't a suggestion; it's a serious hazard. Actual temperatures could hit 108°F with heat index values pushing 110-120°F. Exercising outdoors during these hours is genuinely dangerous and significantly increases your risk of heat-related illness.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you *must* get your miles in, aim for before 7 AM or after 9 PM. Even then, keep it short, easy, and stay on well-trafficked routes like the White Rock Lake Trail so someone can actually find you if you go down. Bring more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, wear light-colored, breathable clothing. Your "tan lines" are not worth fainting over.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Fire Danger Elevated
The dry conditions and low humidity mean a high fire danger across North Texas, forecast to reach "very high" by Wednesday. While winds are currently light, any spark can become a major problem quickly. Stick to paved trails, away from dry brush.
</AlertBox>
