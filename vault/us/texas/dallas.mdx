## Workout Hazards & Local Alerts

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Afternoon Storms & High Winds Expected
Seriously, don't mess with this. Forecasters are calling for a "First Alert Weather Day" because isolated strong to severe thunderstorms are a real possibility this afternoon and evening. We're talking hail and damaging wind gusts of 30-35 mph. Running or cycling during a Texas thunderstorm with those kind of winds and hail is just dumb. If you're out there, get inside *before* the skies open up. This isn't just a "sprinkle."
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the "Feels Like" Temp
The actual temperature is fine, but the humidity is going to make it feel a lot hotter, especially as the sun tries to peek through. The heat index could climb to near 100°F this afternoon. Hydrate more than you think you need to, and if you feel that familiar Dallas oppressive mugginess hitting you, cut your workout short. Nobody needs to pass out on the Katy Trail because they ignored their body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trinity River Trail Closures & White Rock Lake Construction
If your routine involves the Trinity River trails, be aware that the Charlie 2 Pump Station project means maintenance roads and the levee crest are closed for recreational activities until May 31st. You'll have to find alternate routes, like the Santa Fe Trestle Trail or the Ronald Kirk Pedestrian Bridge. Also, parts of the White Rock Lake Trail and Anderson Bonner Park are seeing construction, with portions of parking lots closed for equipment storage. Expect some detours or tighter spaces around there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan for the Wet Week Ahead
Dallas is looking at 1-3 inches of rain throughout the week, meaning localized flooding is a real possibility, especially from Tuesday onwards. Keep an eye on local news for specific road or trail closures before you head out, particularly if you plan on using underpasses or low-lying areas.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you *must* get out there, do it early this morning. Otherwise, hit the gym or pull out that dusty yoga mat. Don't be a hero.
