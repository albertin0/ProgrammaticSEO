### Trail Report: Don't Get Rerouted to Frustration

Let's talk about getting around, or rather, *not* getting around easily. Dallas Park and Recreation has a few ongoing projects that could impact your run or ride.

**Katy Trail's South End Squeeze:** If you're planning on hitting the iconic **Katy Trail**, be aware there's still construction near the south end for "The Loop Plaza" project. It started back in January 2025 and involves a reroute that adds a couple of minutes to your journey. Signs *should* be up, but this is Dallas, so keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for the Katy Trail:** The reroute is designed to connect to the Design District, West Dallas, and the Trinity Strand Trail. Good for future connectivity, but a minor annoyance today. Just follow the signage and try not to get clipped by an overly eager cyclist.
</AlertBox>

**White Rock Lake Trail Work:** Over at **White Rock Lake**, Dallas Water Utilities has gabion wall improvements scheduled. This means parts of the parking lot and potentially sections of the trail itself near Anderson Bonner Park will be used for equipment and material storage. Don't be surprised if your usual scenic route has a construction cone or two.

**Craddock Park and Courts:** If your jam is smaller neighborhood parks, beware that Craddock Park's walking trail near the tennis courts and playground is undergoing shade canopy installation until at least May 20th. Plus, court improvements are happening across various parks until May 15th, so check for closures if you're hitting the tennis or basketball courts at places like Danieldale Park or Beckley Heights Park.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Motorized Mayhem (or Lack Thereof):** A friendly reminder from Dallas Park and Rec: motorized scooters and electric bicycles are **PROHIBITED** on our parks and trails. Don't be "that guy" trying to pass everyone on the Katy Trail with your e-bike.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's weather is fantastic for getting outside, but the air quality warrants a little caution for sensitive individuals, and you absolutely need to factor in potential construction detours on some of our most popular trails. Plan your route, stay aware, and enjoy the cool air while it lasts.
