### Current Hazards & Your Dumb Ideas

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ## Extreme Heat Warning
  Do NOT attempt any high-intensity or long-duration outdoor workouts, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about heatstroke, dehydration, and potentially collapsing somewhere far from help. If you absolutely MUST be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade (good luck finding enough of that on the Katy Trail), and carry way more water than you think you need. Seriously, half-liter bottles are for chumps today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ## Moderate Pollen Counts
  For those of you with seasonal allergies, today's moderate pollen levels could still trigger sniffles, itchy eyes, or a scratchy throat. It’s not a showstopper for everyone, but if you're prone to it, couple that with heavy breathing in the heat, and you're just adding misery to an already questionable plan. Pop an antihistamine if you're going to brave it.
</AlertBox>

  ## Air Quality is... Okay, For Now

Don't let a "good" AQI lull you into a false sense of security that it's a "good" day to run a PR. It's not.
</AlertBox>
