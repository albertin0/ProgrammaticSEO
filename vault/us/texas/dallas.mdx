## Local Hazards & Gripes

Let's talk brass tacks.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### White Rock Trail Closure!
If your Saturday morning routine involves the **White Rock Trail**, you need a new plan. The section between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely shut down for construction until further notice. This isn't just a lane closure, folks; it's a full-on "stay out" situation. They're grouting an abandoned wastewater main, and it's not a spectator sport. Don't try to "just squeeze by" – you're risking injury and holding up work. Find an alternate trail, like a less crowded section of the Katy Trail (if you can manage the crowds today) or explore a different park.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Race Day Ruckus
Three separate big races are hitting Dallas today: the Golden Harvest Half Marathon, the Oktoberfest Run, and the Run It Forward 10K. This means a lot of eager runners, but it also means potential road closures, crowded sidewalks, and more people than usual vying for space on popular routes. If you're not running one of these, you might want to consider lesser-known paths or hitting the gym to avoid the throngs. Check specific race routes before you head out, because getting stuck behind a half-marathon can really throw off your tempo.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Ozone Action Day Protocol
Given the Ozone Action Day in effect for DFW, it’s not the best day for your hardest efforts outdoors. If you're a sensitive individual (and a lot of us are, even if we don't admit it), consider moving your workout indoors, shortening your run, or sticking to low-intensity activity. Keep an eye out for symptoms like coughing, throat irritation, or chest discomfort. Listen to your body – it's smarter than your ego.
</AlertBox>

And for those of you who regularly traverse downtown, remember the ongoing I-30 Canyon project means no pedestrian access in closed work zones around Cesar Chavez, Cadiz, and Akard Street at I-30. Plan your urban runs accordingly.
