### Dallas Hazards & Heads-Up

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Pollen Bomb at White Rock Lake and Beyond:** With "Very High" tree pollen, your run around White Rock Lake, or any trail with ample foliage, is going to be a gauntlet of sneezes and watery eyes. Mountain Cedar and Elm are the chief culprits. Unless you're trying to prove a point to your sinuses, maybe stick to indoor workouts or load up on the allergy meds *before* you step outside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**DNT Construction Headaches:** If your pre-workout commute takes you anywhere near the Dallas North Tollway (DNT) between Downtown and I-635, be aware. Bridge repairs are ongoing and will cause continuous two-lane closures from 10 PM Friday to 5 AM Monday through May. Work started last Friday around Lemmon Ave. and Wycliff Ave.. While it's not a direct trail closure, it's a traffic nightmare that could cut into your precious running time. Plan your routes, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Allergy Survival Tips:** This isn't groundbreaking, but seriously, keep those windows closed today, especially when the wind picks up. Shower and change your clothes *immediately* after coming inside to wash off that pollen. And if you're hitting the trails, consider a mask, even if you feel silly. Your nasal passages will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Fire Risk Later This Week:** While not a critical alert for *today*, keep an eye on the forecast. Elevated fire danger is a concern in western counties due to dry, low-humidity conditions, and winds are expected to increase on Thursday and Friday. This could potentially affect air quality downwind if conditions worsen, so stay informed.
</AlertBox>

All in all, it’s a beautiful day to move your body in Dallas, but only if you're prepared to battle the airborne allergens. Don't say I didn't warn you.
