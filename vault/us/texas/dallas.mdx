## Your Daily Dallas Rundown

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Discount the Dallas Summer Swelter

The official temperature might hit the upper 90s today, but the heat index, which tells you what it *actually* feels like, will be pushing 88°F (31°C) during the day, and July averages a "burning hot" 113°F (45°C) heat index. This week is likely bringing our first 100-degree days, so consider this a warm-up for the real misery. Pair that with a UV index of 7, which is a high health hazard, and you've got a recipe for heat exhaustion if you're not careful. Get your run in before 9 AM or after 7 PM if you're stubborn, and even then, keep it short.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Lightning Strikes Fast – And It Doesn't Care About Your Strava Segments

Yes, there's a 75% chance of rain today, and with that comes the risk of lightning. Isolated showers and thunderstorms are possible across North Texas this afternoon. These storms can develop quickly, bringing not just a downpour, but also dangerous lightning and gusty winds. If you hear thunder, you're too close. Seriously, it's not worth becoming a statistic just to hit your mileage goal. Head inside immediately.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration, Hydration, Hydration

With the high temperatures and climbing humidity, staying hydrated isn't just a suggestion, it's a command. Drink water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout, even if you don't feel thirsty. Consider electrolytes. And if you're heading out to the White Rock Trail, be mindful of the ongoing construction around the wastewater main near Garland Road. It's a mess, and no one needs an unexpected detour or a close call with heavy machinery.

</AlertBox>
