### Trail Talk & Road Risks

Let's get specific, because nobody likes a surprise mid-workout.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Lake Trail: Still a Construction Zone.**
Looks like Dallas Water Utilities is *still* at it with those gabion wall improvements along parts of White Rock Lake Trail and Anderson Bonner Park. Portions of the parking lot are reportedly closed for equipment storage. This isn't exactly new news, with alerts dating back to last year, but it means you can expect some disruption and potential reroutes. Don't expect a pristine, uninterrupted loop today. You'll likely be navigating around some heavy machinery or blocked-off areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Katy Trail Crossroads: Traffic Signal Upgrades.**
Heads up if your route involves the Katy Trail near Knox Street. There's traffic signal construction happening there, with bid questions due *today*. While it might not be a full trail closure, expect some congestion, detours, or general chaos around that intersection. Stay aware of your surroundings; don't assume drivers are paying attention to you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Afternoon Storm Roulette.**
The National Weather Service is calling for scattered afternoon showers and thunderstorms today, though the severe threat is low. "Low threat" doesn't mean "no threat," especially when you're miles from shelter. Keep an eye on the sky and your weather app. Getting caught in a downpour or, worse, a lightning storm, isn't worth shaving a few seconds off your pace. If you hear thunder, it's time to bail.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're determined to get out there, layer up if you're starting early, hydrate like it's your job, and keep your head on a swivel for both construction barriers and incoming storms. And for the love of all that is holy, stick to the non-motorized rules on the trails. We've seen enough scooters zipping around.
