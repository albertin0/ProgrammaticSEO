### Heat Advisory for Your Dumb Self

Seriously, this heat is no joke. The air quality might be "Good" with an AQI of 30, but that just means you can breathe freely while you're overheating. Last week saw heat indexes over 100°F in North Texas, and while we're *officially* a bit cooler today, 91°F feels like a kick to the face.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk.** 32.7°C (91°F) is prime territory for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. If you're out there, slow down. Seriously. Your PR isn't worth passing out on Mockingbird Lane. Hydrate aggressively, and I mean *before* you feel thirsty. If you're not peeing clear, you're doing it wrong.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Allergy Alert.** Moderate pollen means those lovely airborne irritants are out in force. If you've got seasonal allergies or exercise-induced asthma, you know the drill: pre-medicate, carry your inhaler, and don't push the pace harder than your lungs can handle. You already have enough to deal with in this furnace.
</AlertBox>

### Navigating the Dallas Maze

That I-635 to DNT ramp closure is going to be a mess for anyone driving through North Dallas to get to a trail. If you usually hit the northern end of the Katy Trail or try to access spots near the DNT, plan extra time or find an alternate route. Expect surface streets to be clogged. This isn't just an inconvenience; sitting in traffic before a hot workout just drains you further.

<AlertBox type="info">
**PT's Pro Tip:** Even if you're feeling good, listen to your body. Heat masks perceived exertion, meaning you might be working harder than you think. If you feel any dizziness, nausea, or excessive fatigue, stop, find some shade, and cool down. There's always tomorrow. Unless you get heatstroke today.
</AlertBox>
