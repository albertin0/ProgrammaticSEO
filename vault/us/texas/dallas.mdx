## Today's Dallas Rundown: Don't Get Soaked

So, you woke up ready to hit the Katy Trail or maybe get some hill repeats in at Reverchon Park? Think again, champ. We're looking at heavy thunderstorms throughout the day here in Dallas, with a solid 75% chance of rain and temps hovering around a rather humid 25-26°C (77-78°F). That "partly cloudy" from earlier might have been a cruel joke from Mother Nature. Honestly, unless your idea of fitness involves dodging lightning and sloshing through puddles, you might want to consider the treadmill or some indoor squats.

Our air quality is sitting pretty at an AQI of 39, which is "Good," even with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant. This is a relief, especially since the DFW area just dealt with a few "Ozone Action Days" last week due to those stagnant, hot conditions. So, your lungs will be happy if you *do* brave the elements, but your hair (and probably your sanity) might not be.

### Thunderstorms & Rain: Seriously, Stay Inside
There's a 75% chance of heavy thunderstorms today. This isn't just a light sprinkle; we're talking about potential lightning strikes and localized flooding. Running on wet concrete or slick trails during a storm is a recipe for a twisted ankle, or worse. Re-route your plans to an indoor gym or wait until tomorrow.

### Construction Chaos Continues
Downtown, the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center is undergoing major demolition for its expansion. This means increased noise, dust, and general disruption in that area. If your route takes you anywhere near there, expect detours and keep an eye out for heavy machinery. Also, don't be surprised by closures like the temporary shutdown of Craddock Park for construction. Always check your specific route before heading out.

Stay safe out there, or better yet, stay *in* today. Your knees will thank you.
