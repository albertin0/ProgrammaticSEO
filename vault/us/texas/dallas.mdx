### Trail Talk & Road Rage

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up on I-30:** The I-30 Canyon Improvement Project is a never-ending saga. Expect significant delays and restricted pedestrian access if your route takes you anywhere near I-35E and I-45/U.S. 75. These "active work zones" aren't for the faint of heart, or for anyone who enjoys an uninterrupted run. Find alternative routes if you can.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**White Rock Lake Trail Watch:** Remember that sinkhole that popped up on the White Rock Lake Trail a while back, specifically between East Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive? Well, the city's talking about *another* closure for repairs on a wastewater pipeline in that exact area. The good news? It's *not* closed today, and they're still monitoring it. The bad news? This is Dallas, so "not clear when the closure will begin" could mean tomorrow, next week, or next year. Just be aware that part of your beloved lake loop might require a detour eventually, and keep an eye out for updates. The Loop Dallas is actually working with Greenspace Dallas to make trails safer, so maybe there's hope for actual updates.
</AlertBox>
