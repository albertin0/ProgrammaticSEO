### Extreme Heat & Humidity Alert!
Seriously, Dallas, do you want to melt? With actual temperatures pushing 100°F and a "feels like" up to 105°F, heatstroke is a real, immediate threat. If you *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and consume more water than you think is humanly possible. Electrolytes are your friend. Remember, if you stop sweating, you're in deep trouble – call 911.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Storm Risk Incoming
Later this evening and into Sunday, scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected as a cold front moves in. These could bring heavy downpours, leading to localized flooding, along with gusty winds and small hail. Don't plan any long loops around White Rock Lake, especially if the sky looks angry. "Clear" conditions early this morning don't mean much for later in the day.
</AlertBox>
