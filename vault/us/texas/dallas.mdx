<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning & Ozone Alert!
Dallas-Fort Worth is under an Ozone Action Day for Friday, September 18, 2026. This means the air quality is poor due to elevated ground-level ozone. If you have asthma, COPD, or any other respiratory issues, seriously reconsider outdoor exercise. Even if you don't, you're still breathing in nasty stuff. Couple that with the forecasted "feels like" temperature of 103&deg;F (39.4&deg;C), and you've got a recipe for heat exhaustion or worse.
</AlertBox>

On the ground, literally, there are still issues. If your route includes White Rock Trail, be aware that construction crews are still working on a wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and the trail. The section of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely closed, and it has been for a while. Expect signage and detours, but frankly, it's a pain in the butt. Factor in extra time or plan an entirely different route to avoid that mess.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Trail Closure & Grass Fire Risk
The White Rock Trail closure near Garland Road and E. Lawther Drive is still in effect. Don't be an idiot trying to navigate construction zones; find a proper detour. Also, elevated grass-fire concerns persist, especially west of I-35. Be mindful if you're out on dryer trails or near open fields.
</AlertBox>
