### Watch Out for the Wind and Weekend Shenanigans

That pleasant breeze you might feel this morning is set to get a bit more assertive as the day progresses, with winds hitting 10-20 mph and higher gusts possible. This is just a warm-up act, though, as Sunday is forecast for very strong winds, potentially gusting 40-50 mph, making it a First Alert Weather Day. If you're planning a long ride or a run that involves any bridges or open fields, be prepared to battle the elements today, and seriously reconsider for tomorrow.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**St. Patrick's Day Parade on Greenville Avenue!**
Yes, it's today. If your workout route takes you anywhere near Greenville Avenue or surrounding areas, expect major crowds, road closures, and general mayhem. The Dallas St. Patrick's Day Parade & Festival is back, and it's going to swallow up that part of town. Adjust your plans unless you enjoy dodging green-clad revelers during your sprint intervals.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Lake Trail: Sinkhole Repaired, But Proceed with Caution.**
Good news, folks: the massive 14-foot deep sinkhole on the White Rock Lake Trail near Lawther Drive and the spillway has been repaired and the trail *reopened* to the public on Thursday. However, the city is still monitoring the area for "shifting, depressions, or other ground movement," and future closures are possible for further work. Keep your eyes peeled and don't assume everything is perfectly stable just yet. The Dallas Running Club is even looking into rerouting their April race as a precaution.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration and Sun Protection are Key!**
With temps in the low 80s and clear skies, it's easy to get dehydrated and sunburned. Slather on that sunscreen, wear a hat, and carry more water than you think you need, especially if you're out there for more than an hour.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the sunshine, pick your routes wisely to avoid crowds and potential wind tunnels, and keep an eye on those changing forecasts for Sunday. Your body will thank you.
