### Why today is &lt;em&gt;not&lt;/em&gt; the day for a PR

<BulletList>
*   **Pop-up Thunderstorms & Flash Flood Risk:** While you might see some sun early, expect scattered showers and thunderstorms across North and Central Texas, especially later in the day. The National Weather Service is calling for a Level 1 likelihood of excessive rainfall and minor flooding today, with heavy rain, lightning, and gusty winds all on the menu. Don't get caught out there.
*   **Sweaty & Stuffy:** We're looking at temperatures hitting the upper 80s, pushing into the low 90s, but it's that humidity that'll really bake you. Feels hotter than it is, and you'll be drenched before you hit mile one. You can thank early June for these classic DFW sauna conditions.

## What to Watch Out For

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Storms Aren't Playing Nice

That "mainly clear" snapshot you might have seen? Don't let it fool you. Scattered thunderstorms are forecast through Sunday, with risks of lightning, locally heavy rainfall, and gusty winds. There's even a low chance of some strong to severe storms. For runners, cyclists, or anyone else out on the trails, lightning is a direct threat. If you hear thunder, get inside. Don't wait. Seriously, your Strava segment isn't worth getting zapped. Expect potential for flash flooding, especially in low-lying areas. Turn around, don't drown – it's not just for cars.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### That Dallas Summer Heat is Kicking In

While the real sizzle of 100-degree heat indices is expected next week, today is still plenty hot with temps in the low 90s and high humidity. This combo quickly leads to heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Stay hydrated, lighten your load, and maybe push your run to later this evening *if* the storms clear out. Which, let's be honest, is a big *if*.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Upcoming White Rock Trail Construction & National Trails Day

Heads up, White Rock Lake regulars: major construction is set to kick off the week of June 15th on White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. This means closures and detours will be in effect for months as they grout an abandoned wastewater main. Start planning your alternate routes now, because that stretch will be a no-go zone.

On a brighter note, it's National Trails Day! The Texas Land Conservancy is hosting a volunteer event at **Oak Cliff Nature Preserve** this morning from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM. If you're looking for a way to give back (and maybe dodge the worst of the weather by being done early), grab your closed-toed shoes and a water bottle and head over there. Just be mindful of the weather forecast before you go!
</AlertBox>
