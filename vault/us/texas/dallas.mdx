## The Rundown

Alright, runners, cyclists, and outdoor enthusiasts: it's August in Dallas, which means "hot" is just our default setting. Today is no different, with temperatures pushing 99°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous if you're not careful. Heat-related illnesses are no joke, and the city's own emergency management is telling you to limit outdoor activity. Seriously, listen to them.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory:** With temps near 99°F and an extremely high UV index, heat stroke and exhaustion are real threats. If you *must* work out outside, do it at dawn, not dusk. Stay in the shade as much as possible, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear, and for the love of all that is holy, drink water. And then drink some more. We're talking continuous hydration, not just when you feel thirsty.
</AlertBox>

Good news on the air quality front means your lungs won't be fighting an invisible battle today. However, if you're one of those unlucky souls whose nose starts running at the mere mention of "pollen," take your meds before you head out. "Moderate" pollen can still ruin a good run if you're sensitive.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Dallas Detour Alert:** If you usually run or bike through downtown, especially near I-30, be aware of the ongoing I-30 Canyon Project. Cesar Chavez Boulevard and Cadiz Street are completely closed at I-30, and there's *no pedestrian access*. This isn't a small inconvenience; it's a hard barrier. Find another route entirely. Don't try to navigate through active construction zones. Your run isn't worth a trip to the emergency room.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**White Rock Lake is Open for Business!** For those who've been avoiding the White Rock Trail due to the abandoned pipeline grouting, rejoice! That section along Garland Road and East Lawther Drive reopened last Friday, August 7th, two months ahead of schedule. It's still gonna be blazing out there, so heed all the heat warnings, but at least the path is clear.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, today is one of those days where the treadmill or an indoor gym doesn't sound half bad. But if you're stubbornly dedicated to the outdoors (I get it, I’m one of you), be smart, be safe, and for Pete's sake, tell someone where you're going and when you expect to be back.
