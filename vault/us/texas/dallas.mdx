## Dallas Oven Alert: Stay Off the Trails, People

Look, I love hitting the pavement or the dirt trails as much as the next masochist, but today, Dallas is actively trying to melt you. We're under an **Extreme Heat Warning** through 8 p.m. tonight, and that isn't just some suggestion from the weather folks; it's a legitimate danger. Temperatures are climbing well into the triple digits, likely hitting 105°F or hotter, and while the humidity might be slightly lower, that doesn't make the actual air temperature any less lethal to your kidneys.

### Trail Troubles Beyond the Thermometer

As if the heat wasn't enough, some of our go-to spots are a mess. If you had grand plans for a scenic run or ride around White Rock Lake, you'll be disappointed. Parts of the White Rock Trail, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, are closed for some wastewater main work. This isn't a quick fix either; they're expecting to be done in September. So, plan your loops accordingly, or better yet, just plan to use your treadmill.

Even Cedar Ridge Preserve, a lovely escape, is closing its doors early today at noon due to the heat. They're not letting anyone in after 11:30 a.m., which should tell you all you need to know about how serious this heat is. Respect their call; it's for your own safety.

### Lungs Aren't Loving It Either

While the Air Quality Index is currently sitting pretty at a "Good" 43, it's not a completely clear picture. We've got moderate pollen levels, and there's a light plume of residual smoke from distant wildfires, plus some Saharan dust, floating around aloft. So, even if you manage to avoid heatstroke, your sinuses might still feel like they ran a marathon through a dusty allergy ward.
