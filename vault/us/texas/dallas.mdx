## Today's Dallas Grind: Not Worth the Risk

Seriously, just take a rest day or hit the treadmill. This isn't the day to prove your dedication to the White Rock Lake Trail. The ground is already saturated from previous rainfall, meaning any new downpours are going to make things slick, muddy, and ripe for flash flooding in a hurry. We're still dealing with the after-effects of some pretty brutal heat earlier in the week, and while today offers a "slight break," the humidity is still thick enough to cut with a knife, meaning those "feels like" temperatures are higher than the actual mercury. Combine that with the current thunderstorm threat, and you've got a recipe for a bad time.

### Air Quality & Allergens

Good news on the air quality front, for once. The AQI is low, so your lungs won't be complaining about pollution. Pollen is moderate, which is certainly better than high, but if you're sensitive, it's still enough to cause some irritation. Don't expect crystal clear breathing, especially with the humidity.
