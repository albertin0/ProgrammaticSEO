## The Rundown: Don't Be a Hero, Be Smart

### Air Quality & Your Lungs

Okay, the AQI says "Good" right now, and that's technically true for most of us. But here's the kicker: we're seeing forecasts for ozone pushing into the "Moderate" range by afternoon. Light winds and more sun tend to bake that stuff up. If you've got asthma or any other respiratory shenanigans, consider knocking your intense outdoor session down a notch, especially if you're hitting the trails later in the day. Your lungs will thank you.

### Trail & Road Blocks: Dallas Always Under Construction

Ah, Dallas. Our city flower is the orange cone. You didn't think you'd escape construction, did you?

We're still dealing with the massive, multi-year I-30 Canyon Improvement Project downtown. This isn't just for cars; pedestrian access can be restricted in those active work zones. If your route usually takes you anywhere near I-30 between I-35E and I-45, assume there might be a new detour or an unexpected barrier, even if you’re just walking. Plan extra time and maybe scout your route beforehand, especially if you’re trying to connect through downtown.

Speaking of trails, while the sinkhole closure on a portion of **White Rock Lake Trail** between Lawther Dr and the spillway has reportedly re-opened as of March 9, 2026, keep your eyes peeled for any new barricades or detours. There have been ongoing projects around White Rock, including gabion wall improvements near Anderson Bonner Park that could still impact parking or smaller sections. Just because one thing opened doesn't mean something else hasn't popped up.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydration is Key:** While it's cool today, we're heading into warmer weather quickly. Get in the habit *now* of staying properly hydrated. Your body will appreciate it when we're hitting those unseasonably warm 90s later this week.
</AlertBox>

In short, it's a decent day to run, but bring your common sense. Pay attention to the air if you're sensitive, be extra careful about fire hazards, and for god's sake, watch for construction.
