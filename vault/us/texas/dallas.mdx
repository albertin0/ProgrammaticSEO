<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning

Seriously, stay inside. A Heat Advisory is in effect. We're looking at heat index values up to 110°F today, and highs of 103°F. This kind of heat can lead to heat exhaustion and heatstroke faster than you can say "I thought I was hydrated." If you *must* exercise outdoors, go before the sun is fully up, keep it short, and for the love of all that is holy, drink more water than you think you need. Consider moving your workout indoors to one of the many air-conditioned gyms or your own living room.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### White Rock Trail Closure

Heads up, White Rock Lake regulars! The trail is undergoing some major work on an abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road. The section between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is entirely closed until September. Don't be that person trying to sneak through; it's closed for a reason, primarily your safety. Plan an alternate route or hit a different trail altogether. Navigating detours in this heat is a recipe for a bad day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### General Construction Everywhere

Dallas is a city under perpetual construction, and that means you'll find detours and blocked paths all over the place, not just at White Rock. Always be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're running on roads or in urban areas. TxDOT has various lane closures across the DFW metroplex, so keep an eye out for unexpected traffic patterns if you're biking or running on shoulders. Your "usual" route might have a new surprise.
</AlertBox>
