### Heat Advisory in Full Effect!
A Heat Advisory is in place for North Texas through 8 p.m. tonight. Temperatures are expected to reach a record-breaking 101°F, with heat index values (what it *feels* like) up to 106°F. This level of heat significantly increases the risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Seriously, unless your "workout" involves moving from your couch to the fridge, stay indoors. If you absolutely MUST be outside, keep it short, stay hydrated, and go early or late. Forget about pushing any PRs today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch for Residual Flooding
While the Flash Flood Warning for southwestern Dallas County officially ended this morning, heavy rains (1-4 inches, with more possible) mean some areas, especially near small creeks and streams, roads, and underpasses, are likely still flooded. This isn't just a nuisance; it's a hazard for slips, falls, and who knows what kind of gunk is lurking in that water. Avoid flooded areas, especially if you're out near the Elm Fork Trinity River where other construction is also causing closures.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind Your Route: Construction and Events
Don't plan on an uninterrupted loop around White Rock Lake today. The trail closure between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive for wastewater main work is still in effect and will disrupt your run. Also, if you're trying to get a downtown jog in, beware that the Republican midterm convention around the American Airlines Center means several streets (Victory Avenue, North Houston Street, Nowitzki Way, Payne Street, and parts of Field Street) are closed until Friday, impacting pedestrian access. Check local road closures before you head out, or just hit the treadmill.
</AlertBox>
