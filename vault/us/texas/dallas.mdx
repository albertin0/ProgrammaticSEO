Today, August 7th, is all about survival, not setting new records. The good news is that the much-anticipated reopening of the White Rock Lake Trail along Garland Road is *supposed* to happen today after some pipe-grouting construction. So, if you absolutely *must* get out, at least that section of your favorite loop is back online. Don't go celebrating with a full-speed lap, though.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning
The actual temperature might hover around 101°F today, but the *heat index* is going to be brutal, hitting 100-105°F across the metroplex. We're looking at a solid week of triple-digit highs. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real threats. Your body is working overtime just trying to keep cool.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Lungs Aren't Getting Off Easy
That "moderate" AQI of 60 might not sound terrible, but it's not ideal when combined with the hazy skies. We're getting a delightful blend of wildfire smoke drifting in from the Pacific Northwest and some Saharan dust. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, or just like breathing easily, consider an indoor workout if you're pushing anything beyond a slow stroll.
</AlertBox>

On top of the environmental fun, don't forget that if you were hoping to hit up a city gym for some air-conditioned training, all Dallas recreation centers are closed today due to city furlough days. Aquatic facilities are still open, so maybe go for a swim instead of a run.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Smart Choices for a Sketchy Day
If you *insist* on an outdoor session:
*   **Early bird gets the worm, not heatstroke.** Get out before 8 AM, or wait until after 8 PM. Even then, it's still going to be hot.
*   **Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate.** And then hydrate some more. Water isn't enough; consider electrolytes.
*   **Dial it way back.** Seriously, make that usual 8-mile run a 3-mile walk. This isn't the day for heroics.
*   **Listen to your body.** If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or unusually fatigued, STOP. Get to shade, get water, cool down. Don't push through it.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, or better yet, stay inside. Your body will thank you.
