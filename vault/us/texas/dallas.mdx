## Today's Dallas Workout Hazards

Today is a prime example of why you need to check more than just the temperature before lacing up. We're getting blasted with heat, suffocating under questionable air, and navigating a city that seems perpetually under construction.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Advisory
  It's pushing 100°F out there, folks, and that "Clear" condition just means the sun is beating down with zero mercy. Yesterday, the heat index spiked to 109°F in North and Central Texas, and today isn't much better. Exercising in this kind of heat means your body is working overtime just to keep from cooking. Heat stroke is no joke, and calling 911 when your body starts shutting down is not the "personal best" you're aiming for. Hydrate constantly, and if you must be out, keep it to early morning before 8 AM or after 8 PM, and keep it short. Seriously, *short*.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Ozone Action Day & "Poor" Air Quality
  Alright, Dallas, your lungs are getting a double whammy. Not only is the AQI at 56, putting PM2.5 into the "Moderate" range, but it's also an **Ozone Action Day** for the DFW area. This means ground-level ozone is high enough to be "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" and frankly, not great for anyone else either. If you feel any throat irritation or difficulty breathing, cut your workout immediately. This isn't just for folks with asthma; prolonged exposure can affect anyone.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Road Hazards & Construction Ahead
  If you thought hitting the pavement around downtown or east Dallas would be smooth sailing, think again. The I-30 Canyon Improvement Project continues to cause "major disruptions" and "significant delays" in the downtown area, with closures and reduced lanes on main thoroughfares like Cesar Chavez Boulevard and Good Latimer Expressway. And if you're heading further east, be aware there's planned construction on IH-30 eastbound near the **Lake Ray Hubbard Bridge** starting today and lasting through early September. Expect lane closures and detours. This isn't just a traffic headache; it means less safe conditions for runners and cyclists trying to navigate around heavy machinery and distracted drivers. Stick to known, clear paths if you can find any.
</AlertBox>

  ### Summer Sizzler Race Today
  For those signed up for the "Summer Sizzler" 5K/10K/15K race today, August 28th, know that you'll have company on the roads. Race organizers are usually on top of hydration, but it's still *your* responsibility to listen to your body, especially in this heat. Don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>
