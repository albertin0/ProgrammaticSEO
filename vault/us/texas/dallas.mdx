## Today's Dallas Workout Wisdom

Look, it's Dallas. There's always *something* trying to trip you up, literally or figuratively.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues: Watch Your Step Around White Rock Lake and Bachman Trail**

Don't go thinking your usual loop around White Rock Lake is entirely clear sailing. Dallas Water Utilities is doing their thing with gabion wall improvements along the trail and Anderson Bonner Park. Expect portions of parking lots to be taken over by equipment and materials. It's not a full closure, but it means you might be dodging more than just slow walkers.

And if you're a night owl runner, take note: the lights along the Bachman Trail have been out since March 18th due to electrical work. While today's weather is overcast, if you're out pre-dawn or post-dusk, that trail will be darker than your ex's heart. Run with a headlamp or just choose a different spot unless you enjoy tripping over invisible roots. Seriously, don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep Hydrated, You Sweaty Mess**

Even with the overcast skies, 19.9°C is warm enough to get a good sweat going. Carry water, especially if you're hitting those longer routes or pushing the pace. Dallas humidity still wants a piece of you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress in Layers (It's Still Texas, After All)**

While it's mild now, Dallas weather changes faster than a politician's promise. With the sun expected to peek out, that "overcast" could burn off and suddenly you're baking. Layers are your friend. You can always take them off, but you can't magically conjure a shirt out of thin air.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, watch for those construction detours, and maybe carry some extra tissues for the pollen. It's not perfect, but it's better than January's ice storm, right? Or those severe thunderstorms from earlier this month.
