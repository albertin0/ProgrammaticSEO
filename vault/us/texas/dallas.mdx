## The Rundown

Look, if you're out there hitting the Katy Trail or cruising down the Santa Fe Trail right now, enjoy that sweet, clear air. Our AQI is sitting pretty at 44, which is about as good as it gets around here. And at 24.5°C (that's about 76°F for you non-metric folks) early in the day, it's not half bad.

But this is Dallas in June, so don't be fooled. The temperature is set to climb to a balmy 35°C (95°F) this afternoon, and with that heat comes the fun possibility of scattered thunderstorms across North and Central Texas. We're talking a 20-30% chance of getting caught in something strong, with gusty winds and a light show. So, if your long run starts after lunch, you better have a rain plan or just embrace the humidity.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Lake Closure Alert:** Heads up, my fellow White Rock devotees. A portion of the trail around White Rock Lake is *still* closed. Why? Because a baby bald eagle decided gravity was more fun than flying for a bit and ended up on the ground. Park and Recreation is on baby eagle watch, which means your usual loop might be interrupted until the little guy decides to get airborne again. Don't be "that guy" trying to sneak around the barricades for a Strava segment – respect the wildlife (and the rules).
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Afternoon Storm Risk:** Don't let that clear morning sky fool you. National Weather Service is calling for scattered thunderstorms this afternoon and evening with potential for gusty winds and lightning. If the sky starts looking angry, get indoors. Seriously. That PR isn't worth getting struck by lightning on the Northaven Trail.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the immediate weather, there's always something to keep an eye on. Construction for gabion wall improvements is ongoing along parts of the White Rock Trail and Anderson Bonner Park, which could mean localized parking lot closures and equipment to dodge. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Etiquette & Heat Smartz:** Remember, those trails are for everyone, not just you and your top speed. Dallas Park and Recreation is still reminding folks that motorized scooters and electric dirt bikes are NOT allowed on city trails. And as the temps climb to 95°F this afternoon, hydrate like it’s your job. If you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or like you're cooking from the inside out, back off. There's always tomorrow.
</AlertBox>
