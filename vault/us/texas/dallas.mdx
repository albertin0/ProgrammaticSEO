Let's be real, it's May in Dallas, so heat is always a factor, but today the main headache isn't the oppressive sun, it's the wet stuff falling from the sky and the ground you're trying to run on. Weather reports are all over the map, but the immediate forecast for Saturday, May 23rd, points directly to "Light thunderstorms and rain" with a "20% chance of rain during the day" and "scattered thunderstorms" throughout. Good luck with dry feet on that Katy Trail run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Thunderstorm & Wet Trail Hazard!
Seriously, if you hear thunder, you're too close. Wet trails are slick trails, especially after recent rain in North Texas. Watch for muddy spots, hidden roots, and slippery surfaces. Your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk

If you're dead set on getting those miles in, think about your route. White Rock Lake Trail is always popular, but be aware of the ongoing construction around gabion wall improvements, which means parts of the parking lot could be closed off for equipment. Even worse, the City is still "planning and coordinating work" to shut down a section between East Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive to fix a wastewater pipeline after a sinkhole decided to open up. They say the trail is "open for public use" for now, but they're still monitoring that unstable ground. If you see cones, respect the cones.

Craddock Park also still has some shade canopy installation messing with its walking trail. Just because it's a park doesn't mean it's immune to the city's perpetual state of improvement (read: construction).

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Zones: Keep Your Head on a Swivel
Expect construction activity around White Rock Lake Trail and Craddock Park. Be mindful of potential detours, equipment, and uneven surfaces. Don't be that runner who walks into a ditch because they were too focused on their Strava segment.
</AlertBox>
