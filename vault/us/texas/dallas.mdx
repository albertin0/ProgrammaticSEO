Today is a prime example of why you can't just glance at the temperature and head out the door. We've got a triple threat hitting us in Dallas: lung-scorching ozone, bone-sapping heat, and that general "everything's trying to kill you" vibe.

The TCEQ issued an Ozone Action Day for our neck of the woods, meaning the air quality is going to be rough. While some forecast models tried to paint a rosier picture, the official alert means the atmospheric conditions are ripe for ozone production. Combine that with the "Moderate" pollen levels we're seeing, and your respiratory system is in for a workout it didn't sign up for. Keep those inhalers close if you've got them, or just, you know, stay inside.

Then there's the heat. It's September 17th, and we're still flirting with 100-degree highs. Yesterday had a Heat Advisory until 8 PM, and today's forecast isn't exactly a breath of fresh air, with temps near 97°F (36°C). Dallas-Fort Worth has been stuck in a dry spell for over two months now, hitting 63 consecutive days without rain, which also ramps up fire danger if you're thinking of venturing off paved paths. Don't be that person collapsed on the Katy Trail because you thought a slight breeze made it "fine." Dehydration and heat exhaustion are no joke.

And speaking of trails, that ongoing construction adjacent to Garland Road means a section of the **White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely closed.** It's been like this since June and is expected to continue through September. So if White Rock Lake is your usual stomping ground, be prepared for detours or pick a different spot. Nothing kills a rhythm faster than hitting a barricade halfway through your run.
