## The Heat is No Joke, Y'all

Let's be real, it's July in Dallas. We know heat, but today is not the day to play hero. We're looking at actual temperatures hitting 101-102°F, with the "feels like" temperature, or heat index, climbing to a brutal 106-108°F. Even worse, the National Weather Service had an Excessive Heat Warning earlier this week, with heat index values as high as 115°F. We’re still under a Heat Advisory until at least 8 PM tonight. These aren't just numbers; they're a warning. Your body doesn't recover overnight either, with lows barely dipping below 80-82°F, leaving you dehydrated before you even start.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!
Avoid outdoor exercise between 10 AM and 8 PM. If you must be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate constantly. Heatstroke is a real, potentially fatal risk.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures and Detours

If you planned a nice, straightforward loop around White Rock Lake, think again. Construction on an abandoned pipeline means the White Rock Lake Trail between E. Lawther and Winsted is completely closed, and has been since mid-June. They're expecting work to continue through September, so don't count on it clearing up soon. There are detours, but an unexpected extra mile or two in this oven is the last thing you need. E. Lawther between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way is also closed to vehicular traffic to allow for safe trail user detours. Always check local updates before heading out, especially around White Rock.
