## The Rundown

Look, I get it. You've got that marathon training plan staring you down, or maybe you just need to clear your head. But today in Dallas, pushing an outdoor workout, especially at peak sun, is a one-way ticket to heat exhaustion, or worse. We're already hitting "feels like" temperatures that demand respect. The National Weather Service is talking "Extreme HeatRisk" for a reason. This isn't the day to try and outrun the sun.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, Stay Indoors or Go Early/Late
With the heat index at 100°F today and rising over the weekend, the risk of heat-related illness is *high*. If you absolutely MUST get outside, do it before 8 AM or after 8 PM, and keep it short. Better yet, hit a treadmill or find some AC. This kind of heat can turn deadly fast. Drink water like it's your job, and don't wait until you're thirsty. That's too late.
</AlertBox>

If White Rock Lake Trail is your usual stomping ground, be aware of the ongoing construction. Dallas Water Utilities is working on a wastewater main, meaning parts of the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive are closed. Don't try to navigate around barriers; they're there for your safety. Find an alternative route or just avoid that section entirely. Garland Road also has lane closures impacting traffic near the trail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### White Rock Lake Trail Detours
Construction on White Rock Lake Trail means closures and detours, particularly between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. This isn't a minor inconvenience; it's a full closure in that section for public safety. Save yourself the frustration and find another spot or stick to a different part of the lake.
</AlertBox>

Finally, while the pollen count is moderate, it's the heat that's the real villain today. The good air quality isn't an invitation to push yourself.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Summer Events & Roadblocks

Prioritize your health over your mileage today. There's always tomorrow, assuming you don't end up in an ER from heatstroke.
