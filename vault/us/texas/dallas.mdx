## Dallas Workout Hazards Today

Alright, runners, cyclists, and walkers of Dallas. October 27th is serving up some classic North Texas fall vibes: mild temperatures and clear skies. That's the good news. The bad news? Dallas is always under construction, and today is no different. You've got to be smart about where you push those miles.

First up, let's talk about the air you'll be breathing and the sniffling you might (or might not) be doing.

### Trail and Road Conditions

Now, for the stuff that'll actually trip you up (literally). That beautiful weather means everyone and their dog will be out, so expect some company, and expect to navigate around some serious city improvements.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Heads up, **White Rock Lake Trail** regulars! Construction crews are still at it. Starting back in June, they began grouting a hefty 2,800 feet of an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and White Rock Trail, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. This isn't a new development, but it's *still* happening. Don't assume your usual smooth sailing around that section. Expect potential detours, narrowed paths, and heavy equipment. Stay alert and respect any signage. Nobody wants to end their run face-to-face with a backhoe.
</AlertBox>

If your workout takes you through or near Downtown Dallas, you're going to want to pay extra attention. The ongoing **I-30 Canyon Project** is still a beast, with long-term closures affecting several key thoroughfares. While the full I-30 closure from this past weekend is over, expect lingering impacts.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Avoid any pedestrian access *inside* the closed work zones around the I-30 Canyon Project. Streets like Botham Jean/Lamar Boulevard, Griffin Boulevard, Cesar Chavez Boulevard, Cadiz Street, Akard Street, and Good Latimer Expressway are seeing significant closures and detours, some lasting into late 2027 and even 2030. If your route usually crosses or runs parallel to I-30 in this area, you absolutely need to plan an alternate. These aren't just traffic detours; they're *pedestrian hazards*. Stay safe, stay out of construction zones.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's a solid day for an outdoor workout if you plan smart and stay aware of your surroundings. Enjoy that crisp fall air, but don't let Dallas construction catch you off guard.
