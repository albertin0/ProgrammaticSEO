## The Dallas Inferno is ON

Let's not sugarcoat it: today's forecast for Dallas is just plain hot. The National Weather Service is telling us to expect highs near 99°F, and with the humidity, it's going to feel even worse. This is the kind of heat that doesn't mess around, leading to potential dehydration, heat stroke, and heat exhaustion, especially if you're trying to push yourself. Seriously, if you *must* be out there, keep it short, stick to the absolute earliest hours, and douse yourself in water.

## White Rock Lake Trail Closures

If you were planning on a peaceful loop around White Rock Lake, think again. There's ongoing construction for pipeline grouting near Garland Road, between E. Lawther and Winsted/San Rafael. This means a section of the trail is entirely closed, and has been since mid-June, with work scheduled through September. They've got detours marked, but nobody wants to navigate construction zones when they're trying to hit their stride. Find an alternate route, or hit the treadmill.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Ahead: Alternate Routes & Early Mornings
If you absolutely can't skip your outdoor sweat session, aim for pre-dawn. Seriously, 6 AM is your friend. Stick to shaded areas where possible and carry more water than you think you'll need. Consider alternative routes that aren't impacted by construction, or better yet, find an air-conditioned gym.
</AlertBox>
