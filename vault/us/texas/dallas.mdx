## Why today is... well, it's Dallas in October.

<BulletList>
*   **It's a scorcher, plain and simple.** We're looking at temps hitting 33°C (that's 91°F for those still in the dark ages) in the afternoon. It might feel like autumn in the mornings, but by noon, that "comfortable" feeling will be long gone. Hydration is key unless you like being a dehydrated prune.
*   **Your lungs might protest.** Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 61, with ozone being the main culprit. Throw in moderate pollen levels, and if you're sensitive, you might feel it. Yesterday was an Ozone Action Day, and while today isn't as bad, it's still not pristine breathing air.
*   **Trail headaches continue.** If you're heading to White Rock Lake, keep an eye out. There's an ongoing notice about construction related to an abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road and the trail. They *say* it reopened in August, but the Park and Rec Alert Center still lists a notice from September about crews *starting* work. It's Dallas—assume perpetual construction.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch out for the afternoon heat.** Temperatures will climb, pushing into the low 90s by late afternoon. Plan your outdoor workout for early morning or closer to sunset if you want to avoid feeling like you're running on the surface of the sun. Drink more water than you think you need. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider your route carefully.** While White Rock Lake Trail supposedly reopened after pipeline work, the continued "Construction Notice" on the Dallas Park and Rec site is just *peak Dallas bureaucracy*. Expect the unexpected, or just pick a different spot for your long run today, like Katy Trail, which seems to be (mostly) clear.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**The State Fair of Texas is still running.** If you're anywhere near Fair Park, expect crowds and increased traffic. Not a direct workout hazard, but certainly a motivation killer if you're trying to get a peaceful urban run in.
</AlertBox>

Don't go thinking because it's October you can slack on the hydration and heat precautions. This is Texas, not Portland. Your body doesn't care what the calendar says.
