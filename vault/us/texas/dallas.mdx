## Dallas Workout Weather & Hazards: June 5, 2026

Alright, Dallas runners and outdoor enthusiasts, let's talk about today, June 5th, 2026. If you're looking for that perfect, clear-sky, low-humidity, no-hassle workout, well, you picked the wrong Friday. It's not *terrible*, but it's far from ideal, and you've got some specific obstacles to navigate.

The good news? Our air quality is actually quite decent, hovering at an AQI of 28. So, your lungs should be happy, assuming you're not one of the many who'll be battling moderate pollen levels. The temperature is a mild 24.3°C (around 76°F) and it's overcast, which beats the usual scorching sun we're used to.

But here's where it gets dicey: the weather forecast is screaming "unpredictable Texas summer day." We've got a 35% chance of light rain during the day, escalating to a 75% chance of thunderstorms tonight. The National Weather Service is calling for "isolated to scattered showers and storms across portions of North and Central Texas," with a low severe weather threat, but stronger storms could bring gusty winds, lightning, and heavy rain. Nobody wants to be the tallest thing on the Katy Trail when lightning decides to strike. The humidity's also up there, so even without the sun, you'll still be sweating.

### Trail Trouble Spots

Now, for the really important stuff if you're hitting the pavement or dirt. If your usual route takes you to **White Rock Lake Trail**, listen up.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Trail Closure in Effect!**
Starting the week of June 15th, but with *current closures as of June 3rd*, major construction is underway adjacent to Garland Road. The **White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is CLOSED** for wastewater main work. You'll find signage and detours, and E. Lawther Drive between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way is also shut down to vehicular traffic to help with trail user detours. Don't try to be a hero; respect the closure and find an alternate path.
</AlertBox>

Other projects are ongoing, too. The Trinity Strand Trail Hi-Line Span Project, connecting to the Katy Trail and Circuit Trail, was anticipated for completion this spring. The Loop Plaza project on the Katy Trail also has a rerouted path to ensure safety while work finishes this summer. These might not be full closures, but expect detours and keep your head on a swivel for construction activity.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Thunderstorm & Mud Hazard**
With rain chances high, especially later today and into the weekend, off-road trails like those managed by DORBA (Dallas Off-Road Bicycle Association) are highly susceptible to closure due to muddy conditions. Running or riding on wet, muddy trails damages them for everyone. Check the DORBA website or app before you head out if you're planning an off-road adventure. And seriously, if you see lightning, get inside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Still Key!**
Even with overcast skies and slightly milder temperatures, Dallas humidity can still sneak up on you. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. Keep an eye on the sky, and if you hear thunder, it's time to head indoors. No PR is worth getting struck by lightning.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, Dallas. And maybe just hit the treadmill today if White Rock is your jam.
