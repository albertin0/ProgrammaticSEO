## The Air You Breathe (or Try To)

Let's be real, you noticed it yesterday. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) slapped an Ozone Action Day on us for Thursday, May 28th, meaning conditions were ripe for high ozone levels. While that alert officially ended yesterday evening, you don't just flip a switch on air quality. Today, Friday, May 29th, our AQI is 57, landing us firmly in the "Moderate" category, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That means if you've got asthma, emphysema, or just plain old sensitive lungs, you might feel this one a bit more. Even if you don't, it's not doing you any favors during a strenuous workout.

## Weather Watch: Heating Up Fast

It's clear out, a crisp 22.3°C (72°F) as I write this, which sounds lovely. But don't be fooled by that morning air, rookie. Temperatures are climbing into the low 90s today, and the "feels like" temperature will be pushing higher. By Monday, we're talking triple-digit feel-like temperatures. This isn't just a friendly heads-up; it's a warning.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Today's rising temperatures mean you need to get your workout done early or significantly cut back on intensity later in the day. Proper hydration is critical – if you wait until you're thirsty, you're already behind.
</AlertBox>

## Construction Chaos: Your Daily Dallas Obstacle Course

It wouldn't be Dallas without road work, would it? We've got a couple of major headaches to contend with this weekend.

### Dallas North Tollway (DNT) Bridge Repairs

Starting today, Friday, May 29th, and continuing most weekends through the end of May, the NTTA is working on DNT mainlane bridges from downtown up to I-635. This means lane closures between 10 p.m. Friday and 5 a.m. Monday. While this mostly impacts drivers, if your route involves crossing under the DNT or accessing paths near it, expect disruptions and potential detours.

### White Rock Lake Trail Upgrades

Good old White Rock Lake Trail, always a popular spot, is seeing some action too. Dallas Water Utilities is doing gabion wall improvements along the trail and in Anderson Bonner Park. Expect portions of parking lots to be shut down for equipment. Don't be surprised if your usual entry point is blocked or you have to navigate around heavy machinery.

<AlertBox type="danger">
The I-30 Canyon Improvement Project continues to wreak havoc downtown. Long-term closures affect Cesar Chavez Boulevard, Cadiz Street, and Akard Street at I-30, and pedestrian access is *restricted in active work zones*. If your run takes you through this area, find an alternate route. Seriously, don't play chicken with construction equipment.
</AlertBox>

## The Bottom Line

Today's not a write-off, but it's not exactly a "go wild" kind of day. Moderate air quality and pollen mean your lungs might feel it, so listen to your body. Get your outdoor miles in early before the heat gets oppressive. And for Pete's sake, pay attention to construction zones. There's enough going on around the DNT and near White Rock Lake to make a casual jog unexpectedly adventurous.
