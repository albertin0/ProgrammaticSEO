### Construction & Closures

Looks like the city's always got something cooking, but nothing major is slamming your workout plans *today*.

An extension to the Marni Kaner Trail is expected to be complete by 2027, eventually forming the North Dallas Triangle. Just another reminder that progress is slow, but at least it's happening. No immediate trail closures in the last 24 hours related to this, so your usual routes around places like White Rock Lake should be clear of *new* heavy equipment.
