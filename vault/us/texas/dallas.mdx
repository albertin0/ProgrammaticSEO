## Why today is *definitely* not the day:

<BulletList>
  - **Drowning, not sweating:** Dallas is looking at a near-certain chance of heavy rain today, meaning slick roads, muddy trails, and a real risk of temporary localized flooding. Nobody needs a surprise swim on their morning run.
  - **Pollen's still lurking:** While not extreme, moderate pollen levels coupled with the humidity are a recipe for scratchy throats and itchy eyes for anyone remotely sensitive.
  - **Trail traffic and construction chaos:** If the rain doesn't get you, the crowds or detours might. There's a big event at Bachman Lake Park, and ongoing construction means portions of the White Rock Trail are still impacted.
</BulletList>

### Weather Woes & What to Watch For

It's currently sitting at a mild 21.9°C (around 71°F) under a thick blanket of overcast skies, which sounds lovely until you factor in the near 90% chance of rain today, Sunday, October 4, 2026. And we're not talking light drizzle here; forecasts from yesterday hinted at a "staggering 98% chance of rain" with the possibility of "temporary flooding in roadways and poorly drained areas." Your joints are going to hate that kind of impact, and your lungs won't be thrilled with the added humidity. Forget about setting any speed records unless they involve a kayak.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Flash Flood Advisory!** With such high rain chances, low-lying areas and underpasses can flood quickly. Do NOT attempt to run or drive through standing water. Turn around, don't drown – it's not just for cars.
</AlertBox>

### Air You Breathe (or Don't)

The overall Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a "Good" 46, with Ozone/Other listed as the dominant pollutant. Sounds fine on paper, right? But dig a little deeper, and the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) warns that "light smoke could advect into portions of North Central and East Texas, slightly elevating fine particulate matter." So while it's generally okay, if you've got sensitive lungs, you might still feel it. AccuWeather also labels today's air quality as "Fair," noting that "sensitive groups may experience minor to moderate symptoms from long-term exposure." Best to dial back the intensity if you're out there.

### Local Headaches: Bachman Lake & White Rock

If you were planning a peaceful long run around **Bachman Lake Park** this morning, forget about it. The 5th Annual Di'lolas Walk & Health Fair kicks off at 8:00 a.m. That means a whole lot of extra foot traffic, probably some event setup/takedown, and just general chaos for anyone not participating. Seriously, don't be that person dodging charity walkers in the rain.

And for my **White Rock Trail** stalwarts: remember that ongoing construction near Garland Road? Crews are still busy grouting an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and White Rock Trail, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. Expect potential detours, uneven surfaces, and active work zones. It's not worth twisting an ankle in a construction ditch because you're trying to hit your mileage goal.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Rethink your route!** Today is a prime candidate for indoor workouts. Hit the gym, use the treadmill, or find some stairs. Your body (and your sanity) will thank you.
</AlertBox>
