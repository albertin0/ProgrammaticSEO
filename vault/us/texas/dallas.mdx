## Today's Dallas Danger Zone: That Brutal, Relentless Heat

I've run through my share of Texas summers, but August 19, 2026, is a special kind of hell for outdoor activity. The National Weather Service has slapped an Extreme Heat Warning on North Texas, and it's valid until 8 PM. Forecasts are screaming about actual air temperatures up to 108°F, with heat index values making it feel even hotter.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT:** Do not underestimate this. Heat-related illnesses like heat exhaustion and heat stroke are not jokes. They can kill you. Limit your time outdoors, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. If you *must* be outside, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it does), wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and take frequent breaks in the shade or air conditioning. And for the love of all that is holy, check on your neighbors and *never* leave kids or pets in a vehicle.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Trail Closures: More Obstacles to Your Sanity

Beyond the heat, Dallas is perpetually under construction, and some of it impacts our favorite running routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WHITE ROCK TRAIL SECTION CLOSED:** If your usual route involves the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive, forget about it. Construction crews are currently grouting an abandoned wastewater main, and this section of the trail is closed for public safety. Detours are in place, but frankly, today is not the day to explore them on foot. This project is slated to finish up in September, so we'll get our trail back eventually.
</AlertBox>

There's also ongoing DART Silver Line construction around North Dallas, including temporary lane restrictions on Knoll Trail Drive, and the massive I-30 Canyon Project downtown with closures on Cesar Chavez Blvd, Cadiz St, and Akard St. While these are more traffic-related, the I-30 project specifically bans pedestrian access in closed work zones. Stay clear.
