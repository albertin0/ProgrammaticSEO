Seriously, don't try to power through this. Temperatures will reach 101-102°F, with a heat index up to 104°F. Heatstroke, exhaustion, and dehydration are not badges of honor; they're reasons for an ambulance ride. Even a casual stroll around White Rock Lake will feel like you're running on the sun.

### Ozone Alert: Your Lungs Deserve Better
The AQI might be "moderate" at 41, but the dominant pollutant is ozone, and we've got an air quality alert for high surface ozone. If you have any respiratory issues, or even if you don't, deep breaths of this air during a hard workout are not doing you any favors. Consider an indoor alternative today, especially if you're usually hitting those outdoor miles near busy roadways where ozone levels can be higher.
</AlertBox>

Let's talk about those planned longer runs. You know, the kind where you finally catch up on your podcasts. Today is not the day for it. Keep it short if you absolutely *must* go out, and do it at dawn, not even dusk. The ground retains heat like a brick oven, and those evening temperatures won't drop fast enough to make a real difference.

And a quick note on local infrastructure: While not directly impacting trails, large-scale construction, like the ongoing I-30 Canyon Improvement Project in downtown Dallas, means general road chaos. While you might not be running directly on the freeway, expect increased traffic and detours on surrounding streets. Pedestrian access in some work zones is already restricted, so stick to established paths.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Smart, Stay Hydrated (Indoors)
If you insist on stepping outside, keep it brief, stick to the absolute earliest morning hours, and hydrate like you're a camel preparing for a desert crossing. Better yet, find a treadmill, hit the gym, or do some bodyweight work in your air-conditioned living room. Your lungs, joints, and frankly, your brain, will thank you.
