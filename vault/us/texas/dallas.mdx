## Why today isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   Dallas is staring down the barrel of a scorching Friday, with temperatures hitting a brutal 104°F and "feels like" temperatures that'll top out around 111°F. We've got a Heat Advisory in full effect, and honestly, an Extreme Heat Warning could be on its way. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous.
*   While our current AQI is sitting at a "moderate" 51, the dominant pollutant is ozone. Let's not forget the American Lung Association just dropped a report this year flagging Dallas-Fort Worth for worsening ozone pollution, landing us as the 9th worst in the nation for it. Your lungs already work overtime in this humidity; don't make them fight smog too.
*   If you're eyeing a long run or ride around White Rock Lake, you'll hit a snag. A significant stretch of White Rock Trail between E. ## The Rundown: Your Lungs & Joints Don't Deserve This Today

We're in the thick of a Dallas summer, and today is a prime example of why you need to adjust your expectations. That "Clear" sky sounds nice, but it's a clear shot for the sun to bake us to a crisp. The official temp is 29.0°C (84°F) right now, but that's just a teaser for the triple-digit hellscape coming by afternoon.

The pollen count is moderate, which usually means a few sneezes for some, but nothing too debilitating. Still, combine that with moderate ozone, and you've got a recipe for irritated airways if you're pushing hard outside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat Warning: Don't Be a Hero**
Seriously, folks. Heat index values climbing to 111°F are no joke. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real threats. If you're going to attempt anything outdoors, aim for before 8 AM or after 8 PM, and keep it light. Drink copious amounts of water, electrolytes, and listen to your body. No PR is worth ending up in the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Ozone Levels: Give Your Lungs a Break**
With the DFW metro area consistently ranking poorly for ozone pollution, even "moderate" AQI should give you pause. If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, consider moving your workout indoors today. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **White Rock Trail Construction: Plan Your Route**
Heads up to all you White Rock Lake regulars: that section of the trail is still closed. Don't waste your precious early morning cool-ish hours getting rerouted. Check the Dallas Park and Recreation site for detour info if you absolutely must be out there. Better yet, hit a treadmill or do some strength work inside.
</AlertBox>

We know you're dedicated, Dallas, but sometimes the smartest move is to hit the gym, the pool, or just chill out. This heat wave isn't going anywhere fast, so pace yourself and stay safe.
