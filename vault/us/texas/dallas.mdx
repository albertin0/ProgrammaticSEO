<AlertBox type="warning">
### Dallas Bike Ride: Major City Street Disruptions!
If your usual routine involves pounding the pavement through the Central Business District or along any major city routes, be advised: the Dallas Bike Ride is happening this morning, running from 8 AM to 12 PM. Expect significant road closures and adjusted traffic patterns. Seriously, check the Dallas Bike Ride traffic guide *before* you head out, or you'll be doing a lot more stopping and starting than actual running. This also means popular spots that intersect city streets might be impacted. Consider staying on dedicated trails like the Katy Trail or around White Rock Lake, but even then, be aware of increased vehicle and pedestrian traffic at access points.
</AlertBox>

For those planning to hit the trails or parks, the biggest physical challenge today will be managing the heat and humidity. While winds are expected to be breezy, which can offer some relief, they can also dehydrate you faster.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated and Listen to Your Body
With the rising temperatures and humidity, staying hydrated isn't a suggestion, it's a requirement. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. Don't be a hero trying to push through if you feel lightheaded or excessively fatigued. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are no joke, especially in Texas.
</AlertBox>

As for other hazards, general construction continues across the city. TxDOT has ongoing projects on major arteries like I-635 LBJ East, and there's significant demolition for the Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center expansion downtown. While these generally won't block your favorite running path, they contribute to overall city congestion and can sometimes impact adjacent pedestrian routes. Always keep an eye out for unexpected detours or uneven surfaces when running near construction zones.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind Your Surroundings
Even with clear weather, construction is a constant in Dallas. Watch out for debris, unexpected closures, and traffic changes, particularly if your route takes you near major development sites.
</AlertBox>

In short, today is a good day for an *early* morning workout on an established trail, but the city streets are a mess. Plan smart, hydrate often, and don't assume your usual urban route is clear.
