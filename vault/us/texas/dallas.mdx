## Dallas, Get Your Head in the Game (Indoors)

Alright, Dallas, let's be real. It's August, and anyone who's lived here for more than five minutes knows what that means for outdoor workouts. But today? Today is a special kind of miserable. The National Weather Service has dropped a Heat Advisory on us from 1 PM to 8 PM, with the "feels like" temperature skyrocketing to a brutal 107°F. That's not just uncomfortable; that's actively dangerous for anyone trying to push their limits, or even just take a casual stroll. You think you're tough? Heatstroke is tougher.

### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!
From 1 PM to 8 PM today, Tuesday, August 11, 2026, a Heat Advisory is in effect for Dallas. Expect heat index values up to 107°F. This dramatically increases your risk of heat-related illnesses like heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Seriously, reschedule that long run.

And for those of you who still insist on being out there, hoping to find some solace on the trails, think again. If White Rock Lake is your sanctuary, you're out of luck in a prime section. Construction crews are still messing with an old wastewater main, which means White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely shut down. They've even closed the outside westbound lane of Garland Road in that area. This project has been ongoing, and it's not slated to wrap up until September. So, no scenic loops there for a while. Detour signs are up, but honestly, with this heat, are you really looking for *more* miles on asphalt?

Other construction is popping up too, though less directly impacting major running arteries. There are nightly closures on US 75 northbound lanes up near Sherman, and various roadwork projects around the city. Always keep an eye out, especially if your route takes you near major thoroughfares.

On the bright side, the Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a "Good" 46, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. So your lungs aren't fighting pollution today. But don't let that lull you into a false sense of security with that heat.
