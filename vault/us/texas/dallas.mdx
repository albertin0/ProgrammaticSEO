## The Rundown: Don't Just Breathe Deeply, Check the Air First

Look, I love a crisp October morning in Dallas as much as the next runner. And at a balmy 13.1°C (that's about 56°F for you Fahrenheit die-hards) and clear skies, it *feels* like a dream. Your joints are probably thanking you after yesterday's weird weather. But this isn't just about how it *feels*. It's about what you're actually inhaling.

Despite what some general AQI readings might tell you, several local sources are warning about elevated ozone levels today, and even nitrogen dioxide. That's not good news for your respiratory system, especially if you're pushing hard. One forecast even pegged the AQI at a "poor" 71. So, while the temperature might scream "PR day," your lungs might be screaming "slow down, idiot."

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ozone and NO2 are Up – Lungs Beware!
Don't be fooled by the generally good AQI number; specific pollutants like ozone and nitrogen dioxide are elevated. If you have asthma, allergies, or any respiratory issues, consider scaling back your intensity or moving your workout indoors. The air quality could be "poor" for sensitive individuals today, particularly in the afternoon when ozone typically peaks.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the pollen. It's still hanging around at a "Moderate" level, adding insult to injury for your airways. If you're heading out, consider popping an antihistamine an hour before, unless you enjoy running with watery eyes and a stuffy nose. Nobody looks good sniffling through Klyde Warren Park.

## Local Trail Talk: Watch for Maintenance

Most of our beloved trails are clear, but always check for unexpected hiccups. Specifically, if you were planning to hit the soft-surface trails at **Tenmile Creek Preserve**, be aware that there's a scheduled "Trail Maintenance Workday" this morning between 9 AM and 11 AM. This means potential closures or crews on the trail, so maybe pick a different spot or time if that's your usual haunt.

As for **White Rock Trail**, while some construction related to a pipeline near Garland Road was completed and reopened in August, general construction alerts about that area lingered into September. Always keep an eye out for updated signage around E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive if you're running that stretch, just in case. They're usually pretty good about posting detours, but sometimes you just stumble onto a fresh cone party.
