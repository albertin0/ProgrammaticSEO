## Today's Dallas Rundown: July 3, 2026

If you’re contemplating an outdoor workout in Dallas today, Friday, July 3rd, 2026, my advice is simple: don’t. We are smack dab in the middle of a brutal heatwave that’s got temperatures soaring well past the century mark in real feel, even if the thermometer says 91°F right now. The National Weather Service even put out a heat advisory yesterday, and it’s not easing up. Seriously, even the FIFA World Cup players training here yesterday were struggling, cooling down and hydrating constantly. If elite athletes are having trouble, what makes you think your casual 10k won't put you in the emergency room?

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours!
Dallas is under a dangerous heatwave with actual temperatures expected to exceed 100°F, and heat index values making it feel even hotter, possibly up to 105°F. City officials are explicitly warning residents to avoid outdoor activity between 10 AM and 6 PM. Heat-related illnesses like exhaustion and stroke are serious threats. Hydrate excessively, wear light clothing, and for the love of your kidneys, just hit the gym or wait until very early morning or late evening.
</AlertBox>

### Stormy Surprises
Adding insult to heat injury, there's a low but present chance of isolated afternoon and early evening thunderstorms. These pop-up storms, while perhaps bringing a fleeting moment of slightly cooler air, also bring frequent lightning and strong, gusty winds. You don't want to be caught on an exposed trail when a Texas summer storm decides to roll through. It's like playing Russian roulette with a lightning rod.

### Trail Closures: White Rock Lake Edition
Speaking of trails, if your plan involved a scenic run around White Rock Lake, you might need to reroute. A significant stretch of the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is currently *closed* due to construction on an abandoned wastewater main. This isn't a quick fix; the closure is expected to last until September 2026. So, don't just show up expecting to cruise; you'll be met with detours and road closures on E. Lawther Drive too, which is closed to vehicular traffic for trail users. Check the Dallas Park and Recreation site before you head out, but honestly, with this heat, maybe just admire the lake from your air-conditioned car.
