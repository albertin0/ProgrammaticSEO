### Hazards & Headaches

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Advisory for Sensitive Groups:** With PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant and some reports calling the AQI "poor" for sensitive individuals, anyone with respiratory issues (asthma, COPD, etc.) or just generally sensitive lungs should scale back or move their workout indoors. Today's not the day to test your limits against invisible particles.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Persistent Heat Threat:** Even with a slightly lower current temperature, the Dallas area is still in a "relentless heat wave" context. High humidity makes it feel hotter than it is, and the daily forecast shows temperatures hitting up to 88°F (31°C). Heat-related illnesses are no joke. Seriously, don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**White Rock Trail Section CLOSED:** For the love of your ankles and sanity, avoid the White Rock Trail stretch between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive. It’s shut down for a major construction project. They're grouting an old wastewater main, and this closure is sticking around until September. Look for signage and detours, but frankly, it’s a pain.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable:** If you insist on sweating it out outside, be smarter than the average bear. Drink water like it's your job, and then drink some more. Electrolytes wouldn't hurt either.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep an Eye on the Sky:** Scattered thunderstorms are in the forecast today, with a 40% chance of rain. That means pop-up showers, lightning, and potentially slick surfaces. Don't get caught out there.
