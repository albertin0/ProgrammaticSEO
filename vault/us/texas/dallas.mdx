## Today's Dallas Rundown: You Asked for Spring, You Got Summer's Evil Twin

Seriously, who asked for summer in March? Dallas is boiling today, with forecasters predicting a high of 95°F, possibly breaking a record. The "feels like" temperature, or heat index, is expected to top 100°F, which is a straight-up hazard for any serious outdoor activity. Pair that with breezy south winds gusting up to 30mph, and you're looking at a day that'll dehydrate you faster than you can say "recovery drink."

### Air Quality & Pollen

While the general Air Quality Index might look okay on some apps (user data shows AQI 42, good), the ozone forecast specifically for Dallas-Fort Worth is predicting "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" today. If you've got asthma, allergies, or any respiratory issues, consider that a red flag waving in those hot winds. Speaking of allergies, it’s not just a little sniffle today:

### Trail Talk & Local Headaches

Don't even think about hitting certain parts of your usual routes without checking first.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Rock Lake Trail Partial Closure:** A significant portion of the White Rock Lake Trail is *still* closed due to a sinkhole. Specifically, the area between Lawther Drive and the spillway is affected. The city says it’s stabilized, but it’s closed for a reason. Stay away, respect the barriers, and find another spot. We get it, it's a staple, but your safety is more important than your loop count.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Bachman Trail Light Outage:** If you're planning an early morning or evening run around Bachman Lake, be aware of a reported light outage on the Bachman Trail. Low light plus potential for uneven surfaces or other unexpected obstacles is a recipe for a twisted ankle. Headlamps, people.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Fire Danger West of Dallas:** The dry, windy, and abnormally warm conditions mean there's an elevated fire danger, particularly to the west of the Metroplex. If your workout involves any kind of spark-generating activity (which, hopefully, it doesn't), reconsider. For the rest of us, just know the conditions are primed.
</AlertBox>
