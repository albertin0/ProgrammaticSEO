## Dallas Workout Hazards: July 10, 2026

Alright, Dallas, let's talk turkey. It's Friday, July 10th, and the city is doing its best impression of a convection oven. While this morning might feel *tolerable*, by midday, you're looking at temperatures in the upper 90s, possibly hitting 99°F, and it's only going to get hotter as we head into the weekend. The Texas Department of State Health Services isn't kidding around about heatstroke this month; drink water, find shade, wear light clothes. If you're out there pushing it, your body is working overtime just to stay cool.

Air quality is holding steady in the "Good" range with an AQI of 31, which is a win for a summer day. However, the dominant pollutant is ozone, and the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) predicts those levels will be at the upper end of "Good" in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, especially during the afternoon and early evening. So, if you're sensitive to that, lace up early. We're also seeing moderate pollen levels, mostly grass and weeds, which is just par for the course in North Texas.

### White Rock Trail Closure - Don't Say I Didn't Warn Ya!

Here's the kicker for all you White Rock Lake devotees: A substantial portion of the White Rock Trail is *closed* for construction. Crews are grouting an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road. Specifically, the trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is completely blocked off. This isn't a quick fix; they expect to be at it until September 2026. They've set up detours, and even closed E. Lawther Drive between Garland Road and Winfrey Point Way to cars to make it safer for runners and cyclists. Seriously, don't try to sneak through; it's for your own good. Dallas County is also doing some roadway striping in Cedar Hill, so if your route takes you through there, be extra careful of wet paint and equipment.
