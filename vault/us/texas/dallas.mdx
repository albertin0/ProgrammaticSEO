## Dallas Burns, Air Chokes: Stay Indoors, Seriously.

Look, I love pounding the pavement as much as the next runner, but if you're thinking of hitting the trails today, Monday, July 27th, 2026, you're asking for trouble. Dallas is currently a humid oven with a side of smog, and your lungs and joints will thank you for finding an air-conditioned alternative.

### The Heat is No Joke

The temperature might start out somewhat tolerable at 26.8°C (around 80°F) in the early morning, but don't let that clear sky fool you. We're heading straight into another day of triple-digit misery, with highs projected to hit 101-105°F. Worse yet, the "feels like" temperature, thanks to our delightful Texas humidity, is going to be anywhere from 107°F to a staggering 115°F in some parts of the Metroplex.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT!
A Heat Advisory is currently in effect for North Texas through Monday evening, and dangerous heat is expected to persist throughout the week. Heat-related illnesses like exhaustion and stroke are a real threat. Hydration is critical, but honestly, the best advice is to limit your time outdoors drastically, especially during peak afternoon hours.
</AlertBox>

### Breathe Easy? Not Today.

On top of the scorching temperatures, our air quality isn't doing us any favors. The AQI is sitting at 63, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. AccuWeather is labeling today's air quality as "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) has issued an Ozone Action Day for the Dallas-Fort Worth area, which means conditions are ripe for elevated ground-level ozone. If you've got any kind of respiratory issues, or even if you don't, you'll feel this.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### OZONE ACTION DAY ADVISORY
The air quality is unhealthy for sensitive groups. Reduce time spent outside if you're experiencing symptoms like difficulty breathing or throat irritation. Consider rescheduling intense outdoor workouts for a day when the air isn't trying to choke you.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble

If you were planning a run around White Rock Lake, be aware that you'll be navigating more than just the heat and bad air. There's ongoing construction for a wastewater main replacement project between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive, and specifically, the White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive is *closed* until September 2026. They've got detours, but who needs extra miles of uncertainty when it's this hot and muggy?

<AlertBox type="info">
#### White Rock Trail Construction
Avoid the section of White Rock Trail between E. Lawther Drive and Winsted Drive due to ongoing construction and closures. Plan your route accordingly or choose a different location entirely.
</AlertBox>
