## Today's Dallas Workout Rundown: Monday, March 9, 2026

Alright, Dallas, it's Monday, March 9th, and honestly, it’s not a terrible day to get some miles in, but you gotta be smart about it. We're looking at a clear day, with temperatures climbing from the 60s into the low 80s by the afternoon. That's prime running weather for some of you heathens, but others might find it a bit toasty, especially later in the day. The good news is the serious storm threats aren't hitting *today* for most of the Metroplex, though isolated storms might pop up east of DFW. Just keep an eye on the sky if you're out that way.

Now, for the air you're breathing. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a "Moderate" 51, with PM2.5 as our dominant pollutant. That's not great, not terrible. If you've got sensitive lungs, you'll feel it. Couple that with a "Moderate" pollen level, and your sinuses might decide to stage a protest halfway through your run. Pop that allergy pill *before* you lace up, not after you're already sneezing your way down the Katy Trail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Rock Lake Trail Section CLOSED
Heads up, White Rock Lake regulars! A section of the trail, specifically from Lawther Drive to the Spillway, is still closed due to a sinkhole and the potential risk of collapse. The Dallas Running Club even had to cancel their Saturday event because of it. Don't be "that guy" trying to sneak around the barriers. It's closed for a reason, and nobody wants to pull you out of a mud pit. Plan an alternate route or just stick to the other side of the lake for now. There’s also ongoing general construction around White Rock Lake Trail and Anderson Bonner Park, so keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

Winds are going to be breezy, gusting up to 20 mph at times. Great for a tailwind, not so much for a headwind, or for keeping your hat on. It also means dust and whatever else is loose might be flying around, so combine that with the moderate pollen, and you've got a recipe for irritated eyes and a scratchy throat.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Everywhere, Always.
Dallas is perpetually under construction, right? You know the drill. While there aren't specific *new* closures for runners reported for today, the general sprawl of freeway projects, like the massive I-30 Canyon Improvement Project through downtown, means you need to be aware. Sidewalks get closed, detours pop up, and traffic can be a nightmare for getting to your favorite running spots. Always check local city resources or apps for the latest road and pedestrian closures, especially if your route takes you anywhere near the spaghetti bowl of downtown highways.
</AlertBox>

In short, today isn't a total bust, but it's not perfect. Manage your allergies, be extra vigilant near White Rock Lake, and watch for those breezy conditions. Get your workout in, but don't say I didn't warn you about tomorrow's weather – severe storms are on the horizon.
