If you're hitting the pavement, you're either running before the sun fully commits to its daily assault or you've got a death wish. Seriously, hydrate like it's your job, and don't push anything resembling a personal best after 9 AM.

So if you like your trails uninterrupted, you're out of luck in a few key spots.
