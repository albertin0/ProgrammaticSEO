## Dallas Workout Rundown: September 4, 2026

If you're looking to hit the trails today, the morning is your friend. The current 25.6°C (78°F) is about as good as it gets for September in North Texas, but don't be fooled. The thermostat is climbing to 95°F (35°C) by afternoon, and the entire weekend is forecast to bake us with highs hovering in the mid-90s to low 100s. Hydration isn't a suggestion; it's a non-negotiable, especially if you think you can power through a noon run.

On the lung front, we're not facing any major apocalyptic smoke clouds or dire air quality warnings today. The AQI sits at a "Good" 31, which is a win in my book for Dallas. However, for those of us with allergies, "Moderate" pollen levels mean your sinuses might have a different opinion. Don't be that guy hacking up a lung halfway around White Rock Lake because you forgot your meds.

### Trail Talk: White Rock & Beyond

Good news for White Rock Lake devotees: the section along Garland Road that was closed for pipeline grouting should be good to go. It was slated to reopen by early August, so that particular headache is (hopefully) behind us. That said, Dallas Park and Recreation crews are still out there doing maintenance work on various segments of the White Rock Creek Trail, tackling mud, debris, and surface repairs, especially around the Royal underpass. While Harry Moss Trail is apparently in "fantastic shape" according to recent updates, always be mindful of your footing.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Katy Trail Connection Alert:** If you usually connect from the Katy Trail to the new Hi Line Connector, be aware that The Loop Plaza is currently under construction. This project aims to improve the connection, but for now, it's an active work zone. Plan your route accordingly to avoid any unexpected detours or dead ends.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Afternoon Heat Hazard:** Seriously, don't underestimate the Dallas heat. Even if it feels mild now, by mid-day, those temperatures will spike. A Heat Advisory was in effect just yesterday for heat index values up to 108°F, and while it's officially expired, the underlying conditions for heat-related illness remain. Schedule your outdoor workouts for early morning or late evening. Carry more water than you think you need.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">

Stay safe out there, Dallas. Don't be a hero; be a smart runner.
