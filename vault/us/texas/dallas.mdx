## The Rundown: Your Lungs, Your Joints, and the Dallas Grind

Look, I've run the Katy Trail a thousand times in worse conditions, and so have you. Today isn't one of those "heroic" efforts, but it's not a walk in the park either. That "Mainly Clear" sky might sound inviting, but it's just a green light for the sun to beat down on you. We're creeping into that part of the year where a seemingly innocuous morning run turns into a dehydrating slog if you're not paying attention.

The good news is, no major catastrophe seems to be unfolding, at least not across Dallas trails. No wildfires blanketing the city in smoke, no sudden flash floods turning White Rock Lake Park into a swimming pool. We did have some gnarly storms rip through North Texas just last week, causing power outages and downed trees. While most major arteries are clear, keep an eye out for lingering debris on less-trafficked paths, especially around creeks or wooded areas, just in case some rogue branches are still hanging out.

### Pollen & Air Quality: Your Daily Dose of Annoyance

The air quality is sitting pretty at an AQI of 43, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's actually pretty solid for us. You won't be coughing up a lung from industrial smog today, which is a win in my book.

Pollen, though? Still "Moderate." If you're one of the unfortunate souls who wakes up feeling like you snorted a cactus, you already know your misery. Juniper and Ragweed are often the culprits around here, even if the "moderate" label sounds benign. It means your sinuses might be screaming, but at least your lungs can breathe relatively clean air. Just don't forget the Allegra.

<AlertBox type="warning">
That 25.8°C (78.4°F) is a trap. The sun is out, and it's June in Dallas. Heat stress is a real thing, even on "mild" days. Hydrate *before* you go, *during* your workout, and *after*. And for the love of all that is holy, if you feel dizzy or lightheaded, stop. Nobody cares about your Strava segment if you're passed out on the Santa Fe Trail.
</AlertBox>
