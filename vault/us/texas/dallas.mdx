### Trail Conditions Post-Storm

You know how the trails get after a good Texas gully-washer, and last night was more than just a gully-washer. Reports of "deadly storms" and "widespread tornado watch" across North Texas mean you need to be smart out there. Even if the sun's peeking out now, the ground underneath is likely still saturated. Expect muddy patches, especially on unpaved sections or anywhere water tends to pool. Your ankles will thank you for wearing trail shoes, or at least something with decent traction.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Katy Trail:** While a smooth ride usually, the Katy Trail and similar urban paths can collect windblown debris. Keep an eye out for small branches, leaves, and any larger trash that might have been deposited. Don't assume clear sailing just because it's paved.
</AlertBox>
