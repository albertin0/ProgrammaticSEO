### Today's Workout Hazards in Detail

Let's cut to the chase: the heat is the undisputed champion of "things trying to take you down" today. This isn't just "Dallas hot"; this is the kind of heat that makes you question every life choice that led you to consider an outdoor workout. Multiple sources are forecasting highs well over 100°F, and the heat index will make it feel even worse. If you're planning on joining that "Commit2HIIT Fitness Workout on the Plaza" at Toyota Music Factory today, know what you're getting into and manage your expectations – and your fluid intake.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Advisory
The high temperatures and oppressive "feels like" conditions are a serious health risk. The Texas DSHS is reminding everyone that July is prime heatstroke season, advising plenty of water (and electrolytes!), frequent rest in the shade, and light clothing. If you're out there, watch for signs of heat exhaustion or heatstroke in yourself and others: confusion, dizziness, rapid pulse, and especially a lack of sweating. If you see it, call 9-1-1. This isn't a game.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Worsening Air Quality
While the AQI is currently decent, the forecast points to a climb into the "Moderate" category for PM2.5. This means that sensitive groups – like those with respiratory issues, older adults, or kids – might experience some discomfort or breathing difficulties, especially during the afternoon when pollution typically peaks due to vehicle emissions and lack of wind dispersal. Don't be surprised if your lungs feel a little heavier than usual later in the day.
</AlertBox>
