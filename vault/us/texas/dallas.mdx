## The Rundown: Don't Be a Drowning Statistic

Let's be blunt: if you're planning a long outdoor workout today, especially anywhere near a creek or low-lying areas, you might be asking for trouble. We've got chances for scattered showers and storms continuing throughout Memorial Day weekend, and these aren't just sprinkles. We're talking locally heavy rain and a level 2/4 risk for flooding.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **FLOODING RISK IS REAL.** Seriously, don't mess with it. When you see standing water, *turn around, don't drown.* White Rock Creek, which feeds into our beloved White Rock Lake, is notorious for rising fast and swallowing those nice greenbelt trails when it gets hammered with rain.
</AlertBox>

### White Rock Lake Trail Update: Still Open, But Watch for Future Shenanigans

Remember that sinkhole drama around White Rock Lake Trail back in March? The good news is, the trail is currently open for public use in that area between East Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive, and that confusing "trail closed" sign was removed weeks ago.

However, the city *will* be closing a portion of the trail again at some point for wastewater pipeline repairs, though the exact timeline isn't clear yet. Keep an eye on Dallas Park & Recreation alerts. And while you're out there, just be mindful of any older construction for gabion wall improvements that might still be impacting portions of the parking lot near White Rock Lake Trail and Anderson Bonner Park.

Pay Attention.** AccuWeather is forecasting "Poor" air quality for sensitive groups today and tomorrow, so if you're prone to respiratory issues, consider scaling back your outdoor activity or sticking to indoor options.
