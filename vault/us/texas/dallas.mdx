## The Rundown for September 24, 2026

Alright, Dallas runners, listen up. This morning, we got a brief reprieve with cooler temps around 25.8°C (78.4°F) that made that early start almost bearable. But don't let that fool you. The mercury is heading north, quickly. Expect that 95°F heat to settle in by midday, making any serious outdoor effort a sweat-fest and a potential heat risk. Pair that with an **Ozone Action Day** and moderate PM2.5 levels, and your lungs aren't going to be thanking you for pushing it.

### Air Quality & Heat: The Double Whammy

We're in an Ozone Action Day, folks. That means the air pollution from ground-level ozone is expected to be unhealthy for sensitive groups. Combine that with our standard "Moderate" AQI and PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant, and you've got a recipe for labored breathing if you're out too long. The heat only exacerbates these issues, making your body work harder to cool itself while also dealing with crummy air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Ozone Action Day & Rising Heat Alert:** Avoid strenuous outdoor activities, especially during peak heat hours from late morning through evening. If you absolutely must run, keep it short, easy, and stick to the shadier spots.
</AlertBox>

The **White Rock Trail** still has some ongoing construction. Crews have been grouting an abandoned wastewater main near Garland Road, specifically between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. This has led to trail closures in that section (specifically between E. Lawther Drive and Winstead Drive) for public safety, and while it was expected to be completed in September, always check for current signage. Don't be that person ignoring the detour signs and wondering why you're wading through mud or dodging heavy equipment.
