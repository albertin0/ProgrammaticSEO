## The Rundown: A Chilly, Clear Start with Muddy Underfoot

Alright, Dallas, if you woke up today thinking about hitting the trails, you're in for a mixed bag. The good news? Our notoriously funky air quality is actually looking pretty solid today. With an AQI of 36 and "Good" ratings for ozone, PM2.5, and PM10, your lungs can mostly kick back and relax. That's a rare gift, honestly, so take advantage.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Chilly Start, Dallas!**
It's a crisp 8.6°C (that's about 47°F for those of us still converting) out there, especially this morning. While winds are lighter today after yesterday's bluster, it's still cold enough to require proper layering. Don't be that person shivering two miles into your run.
</AlertBox>

Now for the not-so-great part: while the skies are clear, the ground might not be. We've had some decent rain recently, and that means our unpaved trails are likely a sloppy mess. If your usual go-to is something like Cedar Ridge Preserve, be prepared for some serious mud, or even potential closures to protect the trails. Call ahead or check their social media before you waste a trip – nobody likes caked-on mud in their treads.

## Downtown Dallas: Still a Spartan Aftermath

If you're usually pounding pavement downtown, be aware that the 2026 Dallas Spartan Event, which happened yesterday, is still causing some havoc. Street closures and restricted access around City Hall Plaza and the Central Business District will continue through Tuesday, April 21. Parks like Main Street Garden Park, Harwood Park, Pacific Plaza, and Carpenter Park also have restricted access. So, if your usual loop takes you through that area, plan a different route or prepare for detours.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Pointers**
Pollen levels are moderate today. If you're one of the many Dallasites with seasonal allergies, don't forget your meds before heading out. "Clear" skies don't mean "clear" of allergens for everyone.
</AlertBox>
