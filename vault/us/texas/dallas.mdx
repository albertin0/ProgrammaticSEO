## Current Hazards & What to Do

### Afternoon Pop-Ups & Persistent Heat

<AlertBox type="warning">
We're looking at a 30% chance of isolated afternoon storms today, potentially bringing gusty winds and small hail, especially from 4 PM to 8 PM. On top of that, expect "feels like" temperatures around 100°F. That's not just "warm," that's "your sweat won't evaporate fast enough" territory.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, those pop-up storms can turn a beautiful run into a lightning-dodging sprint in seconds. If you see the sky getting ominous, or hear thunder, cut it short. It’s not worth becoming a statistic or getting pelted by hail. And the heat index? Stay hydrated, folks. Your body is working overtime just to keep cool. Pre-hydrate, carry water, and don't push the pace too hard in the middle of the day.

### Trail & Road Updates

<AlertBox type="info">
The Loop Dallas is still working on its grand plan, with The Loop Plaza, connecting the Katy Trail to Victory Park and the Design District, expected to be finished this summer. If you're running that stretch, expect some minor reroutes or ongoing work in the immediate area. TxDOT also has various I-30 and I-20 lane closures today for maintenance, primarily affecting drivers, but good to know if you're commuting to a trailhead.
</AlertBox>

This isn't major trail-blocking stuff, but it's enough to remind you that even on a "good" day, construction cones are as much a part of the Dallas landscape as blue skies. Keep your head up and stay aware of your surroundings, especially if you're navigating urban trails near these projects.
