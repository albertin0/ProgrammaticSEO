## Dallas Workout Hazards & Heads-Up

Good news, folks: the latest reports from the National Weather Service for Dallas, TX, show no active warnings or advisories in effect as of early this morning. That means no surprise thunderstorms, high wind alerts, or heat domes trying to melt your shoes.

<AlertBox type="info">
### White Rock Lake Trail: Always Check Signage
While there's no *new* news from the last 24 hours regarding fresh catastrophes, runners know White Rock Lake Trail has been a bit of a drama queen lately. Back on March 9th, a section between Lawther Drive and the spillway reportedly reopened after sinkhole repairs. However, as recently as March 11th, local residents were still raising concerns about ongoing erosion and a *new* sinkhole causing temporary closures for investigation. The message here is simple: respect any current trail closure signs you encounter around the lake, especially near the causeway off Garland Road. Don't assume everything's perfectly stable just because it was a few weeks ago.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Bachman Trail Goes Dark
If your workout window extends into the evening, be aware that the lights along Bachman Trail have been out since March 18th due to electrical work related to a dam project. It's not a new hazard, but it's an ongoing one. Plan your sunset runs elsewhere or bring a robust headlamp and exercise extra caution for anything lurking in the shadows – like that rogue sprinkler head.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Road Construction Could Stall Your Start
While not directly on the trails, getting to your favorite workout spot in Dallas can be a workout in itself thanks to ongoing road construction. Expect various lane closures and potential traffic delays on major toll roads like the Chisholm Trail Parkway, Dallas North Tollway, and President George Bush Turnpike this week. For example, southbound lanes on Dallas Parkway from Alpha Road to Interstate 635 have daytime and nightly closures planned through March 30th. Check your commute before you head out, or you might spend more time idling than actual exercising.
</AlertBox>
