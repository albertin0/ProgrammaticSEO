Today’s a mixed bag for hitting the pavement in Dallas – decent air, but Mother Nature might just decide to open the floodgates again this afternoon.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
- Yesterday’s downpour across North Texas has left the ground saturated, meaning any additional rain today could quickly lead to flash flooding.
- While the air quality is currently good and the temperature is a reasonable 24.9°C (77°F) with partly cloudy skies, the forecast calls for a high of 90°F and a significant chance of afternoon thunderstorms, bringing with them the risk of lightning, hail, and strong winds.
- Moderate pollen levels mean seasonal allergy sufferers might still feel a bit congested, even if the air itself is clean.
</BulletList>

The city is still drying out from Monday's heavy rains, which dumped anywhere from 1-3 inches across most of the region, with some western portions of Fort Worth and southern counties seeing 5-9 inches. Although specific flash flood *warnings* from Monday have expired for Dallas County, the National Weather Service indicates that most of North Texas remains under a level 2 out of 4 risk for flash flooding through today. This means it won’t take much more rain to cause problems, especially in areas that got soaked yesterday.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch the Sky, Especially After 10 AM!**
Scattered thunderstorms are expected to redevelop this afternoon, particularly after 10 AM, and these slow-moving systems can bring locally heavy rainfall, hail, and strong winds. Lightning is a serious threat; if you hear thunder, it’s time to head indoors. Don't be "that guy" caught in a downpour miles from shelter.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid Standing Water – Turn Around, Don't Drown!**
With the ground already saturated, flash flooding remains a concern. Even seemingly shallow water can hide hazards or be deep enough to sweep you or your vehicle away. Seriously, don't risk it. If a path or road looks flooded, assume it is and find another route. This applies particularly to low-lying areas and underpasses which are prone to rapid flooding.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Construction Continues – Plan Your Routes**
Don't expect smooth sailing everywhere. Construction continues on sections of our trail system. For instance, there's ongoing grouting work on an abandoned wastewater main adjacent to Garland Road and the **White Rock Trail** between E. Lawther Drive and San Rafael Drive. Always check local park and recreation alerts before heading out, as detours or temporary closures can pop up. Also, be aware of ongoing major road closures in Downtown Dallas and around Fair Park due to the FIFA World Cup events, which could complicate getting to some parks and trails.
</AlertBox>

Stay hydrated, stay aware of changing weather, and be smart about where you choose to run today. Tomorrow is another day for pushing limits; today, it’s about staying safe.
