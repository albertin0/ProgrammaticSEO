## What You Need to Know

Today’s forecast basically screams, "Get it done early, or get on the treadmill." If you insist on outdoor activity, hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's a lifeline. Plan your route carefully, as parts of our beloved greenway system are still undergoing their endless face-lift.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat Index and Dehydration Risks
The heat index could feel well into the 40s (100s°F), creating a moderate to major heat health risk. Seriously, don't mess with this. Heat stroke is real, and it sneaks up on you faster than a runner cutting the course at the Rock 'n' Roll Marathon. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or suddenly get goosebumps, get inside immediately and cool down. Your PR isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Evening Storms & Gusty Winds
If you're planning an evening workout, keep a very close eye on the sky and weather alerts. Isolated storms could pop up, bringing strong winds and enough rain to make some areas briefly hazardous. Gusts up to 50 mph can turn a pleasant stroll into a fight against airborne patio furniture, and cause low-lying areas to flood quickly. Don't be "that guy" trying to cross a flooded street.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Ongoing Trail Closures
Heads up, especially if you usually hit the **Leon Creek Greenway**. A significant portion of the trail near Loop 1604, specifically north of the Valero Trailhead toward The Rim, is still shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Also, parts of the Salado Creek Greenway are seeing periodic closures for construction or maintenance. Always check the city's parks and rec site before you head out, or you'll just be adding frustration to your workout.
</AlertBox>

Remember, listen to your body, not that Instagram influencer telling you to "push through." Today, pushing through might just push you into a really bad spot.
