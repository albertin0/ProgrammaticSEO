### Construction Chaos & Trail Trouble

Now, for the fun part: dodging the orange cones and closed signs. This city runs on construction, and today is no exception.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Major Trail Closures on Leon Creek Greenway!** A significant chunk of the Leon Creek Greenway, specifically north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Seriously, plan a different route or prepare for a long detour. There’s also a section near I-10 by the Marquis Trailhead that’s closed due to a fallen overpass – stay clear of that mess.
</AlertBox>

Other greenways are feeling the pain too. Portions of the Salado Creek Greenway are closed for maintenance or repair work, including south of the Southside Lions Park entrance (possibly reopening today, Oct 9th, but check before you go) and indefinitely from Gembler Road to Houston Street. The Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent trails at Classen-Steubing Ranch Park are also closed through the end of October.

On the roads, TxDOT had some overnight closures on I-35 around Rittiman Road and Eisenhauer Road that wrapped up at 5 a.m. today, October 8th. Also, emergency repairs on IH 10 Westbound main lanes near Crossroads and Vance Jackson Rd were supposed to clear by 5 AM, but emergency work can sometimes linger. If you're driving to your workout spot, expect residual traffic or check those apps before you roll out.

### Events & Crowds

Want to hit up the San Antonio River Walk? Be prepared for some extra foot traffic. The Día de los Muertos Tradition Trail is in full swing through November 2nd, featuring some impressive 5-foot alebrijes and altars along the downtown stretch. It's beautiful, but it's not exactly prime sprinting territory.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **River Walk Wanderer Heads Up:** The Día de los Muertos Tradition Trail means the River Walk will be more crowded than usual. If you're looking for a peaceful, unobstructed run, this might not be your day for a downtown loop. Embrace the cultural immersion, or hit a different spot.
</AlertBox>

Also, the UTSA tailgate and game at Hemisfair Civic Park and the Alamodome this afternoon/evening will likely generate extra traffic and crowds around those areas, even if no specific street closures are planned.
