## Trail Talk: Obstacles & Detours

If you're planning on hitting the greenways, be prepared for some detours and potential frustration. The City's Parks and Recreation department and the San Antonio River Authority have a few ongoing closures you need to be aware of.

For instance, if the Mission Reach is your go-to, remember that parts of the trail near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Concepcion Park are still temporarily closed for "Stem Density efforts." What are "Stem Density efforts," you ask? Probably some folks out there making sure our riparian zones don't get too sparse, but it means you can't just cruise through those sections.

Over on the Leon Creek Greenway, TxDOT is still doing its thing. A chunk of the trail near Loop 1604, specifically north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, remains closed for construction until mid-2027. And don't forget the section near I-10 by the Marquis Trailhead, which is closed thanks to a fallen highway overpass. These aren't temporary inconveniences; they're long-haul closures, so plan your routes accordingly or be ready to bail and reroute.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route, Avoid Surprises
Check the City of San Antonio and San Antonio River Authority websites before you head out. Ongoing closures on popular greenways like parts of the Mission Reach and Leon Creek Greenway mean you might need an alternate plan. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the barricades.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's workout outside isn't entirely off the table, but it's one for the cautious. Keep it shorter, keep it lighter, and prioritize breathing clean air. Maybe hit the treadmill or the indoor track instead. Your lungs will thank you.
