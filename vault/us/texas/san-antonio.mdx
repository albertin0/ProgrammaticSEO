## Today's Rundown: Breathe Easy (or try to)

Let's talk about the air you'll be sucking in. While the temperature is a pleasant 28.1°C (about 82°F) and it's "Mainly Clear," that doesn't tell the whole story. Your friendly neighborhood AQI is clocking in at 95, firmly in the "Moderate" zone, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) dominating the scene. For most of you iron-lunged folks, it might feel okay. But if you're like me and your allergies kick in just looking at a tree, or you've got any underlying respiratory issues, this moderate PM2.5 and pollen combo means you might want to dial back the intensity. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality also forecasted PM2.5 for San Antonio today, so this isn't some rogue sensor reading. You'll feel it if you push too hard.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### AQI and Pollen: Not a Power Couple
With moderate AQI (95) and moderate pollen, sensitive individuals might experience respiratory irritation. Consider moving your run indoors or opting for a lower-intensity outdoor activity, especially if you plan on spending extended time on trails like the Salado Creek Greenway where you might be huffing and puffing.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures: Your Detour is My Headache

If you're planning on hitting some of San Antonio's beautiful greenways, get ready to reroute. There's more orange cone action than a Halloween party at a construction site.

### Salado Creek Greenway & Leon Creek Greenway
Portions of the **Salado Creek Greenway** are currently closed. The segment from Camargo Park to SW Military, west of the New Life Trailhead, is undergoing maintenance and isn't expected to reopen until next week. Likewise, the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is completely out of commission until *winter 2026* for maintenance. Over on the **Leon Creek Greenway**, if you're usually connecting through Culebra Creek Park, that trail's been closed for reconstruction since February 16th and will be for approximately three months. There are also periodic closures under the 1604 overpass north of the Valero Trailhead due to TXDOT construction.

### Mission Reach
Don't even think about the west bank of the **Mission Reach Trail** if you're heading from Lone Star Blvd to the first pedestrian bridge. That whole section is closed for erosion repair work until late July. So, plan to stick to the east bank or find another route.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Chaos
Multiple segments of our beloved greenway trails, including the Salado Creek Greenway, Leon Creek Greenway, and the Mission Reach, are currently closed due to maintenance, reconstruction, or erosion repair. Check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Recreation website before you head out, or you'll be adding unwanted miles to your route just trying to find an open path.
</AlertBox>

## General Trail & Situational Awareness

While the daytime temps aren't scorching today, the low 80s are still plenty warm enough to get you sweating. Stay hydrated, folks. More importantly, always be aware of your surroundings. A recent, somber reminder for those who frequent the **Leon Creek Greenway** near Prue Road: a woman's body was found there on April 10th. While authorities ruled it a suicide with no criminal elements, it's a stark reminder that even in our cherished outdoor spaces, situational awareness is key. Don't zone out with your earbuds too loud.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate & Stay Alert
Even with clear skies, temperatures in the 80s can lead to dehydration. Carry water. And seriously, keep your head up and pay attention to your surroundings, especially if you're running alone.
</AlertBox>
