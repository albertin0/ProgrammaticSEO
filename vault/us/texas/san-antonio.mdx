## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Gale-force winds are no joke.** We're talking sustained winds of 20-35 mph with gusts ripping up to 45-55 mph. That's not just a stiff breeze; that's "your hat is gone and that branch is coming down" wind.
*   **Wind chill is brutal.** Temperatures dropped like a rock overnight, and with these winds, it feels like it's in the 20s and 30s out there. Your joints will hate you.
*   **Red Flag Warning for fire danger.** All that wind, coupled with low humidity and dry ground, means any spark can turn into a real problem, fast. You don't want to be the reason for the next brush fire near the Salado Creek Greenway.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. You've got goals. But pushing through conditions like this isn't "grit," it's just asking for trouble. That cold front that blasted through overnight has turned our usually pleasant running weather into something downright hostile. Yesterday was probably warm, and today you're practically in a wind tunnel. That kind of rapid temperature shift, especially with these sustained winds, is a recipe for hypothermia if you're not dressed like you're climbing Everest.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Wind & Fire Danger!** Seriously, the wind gusts are strong enough to cause structural damage and knock down small trees. Stay clear of anything that looks like it might fly away or fall over. Also, DO NOT do any outdoor burning. A Red Flag Warning is active, and fire conditions are critical.
</AlertBox>
