### Greenways & Trail Headaches

Look, it's November 1st, and if you haven't memorized the permanent "nope" zones around our city, you're just asking for trouble. For instance, a significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway** is still shut down north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim due to TxDOT construction, and a section near I-10 by the Marquis Trailhead is an absolute no-go because of a fallen overpass. That ain't changing until at least mid-2027, folks, so plan your long runs accordingly.

It's not just Leon Creek, either. If you're looking to explore the Mission Reach, be aware that portions around Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Conception Park are still under temporary closure for "Stem Density efforts" as of mid-October. And for those who frequent the Salado Creek Greenway, the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is closed indefinitely for maintenance.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Persistent Trail Closures!** Do NOT ignore the barricades, especially on the Leon Creek Greenway (Valero Trailhead to The Rim, and near Marquis Trailhead) and parts of the Mission Reach. Seriously, respect the closures; they're there for a reason, usually involving heavy machinery or unsafe conditions.
</AlertBox>

### Road Construction & City Scramble

Beyond the trails, city streets are a continuous game of dodge-the-detour. The big one to remember right now is the major water main replacement project on **East Hildebrand Road**. This beast is causing phased road closures and traffic disruptions that are expected to last through March 2027. While McCullough Avenue at Hildebrand *might* reopen this month, it's still a headache if your route takes you through there. Give yourself extra time or find an alternate route to your preferred workout spot. VIA Metropolitan Transit has multiple bus detours in effect too, so if you rely on public transport to get to your start line, double-check those routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Road Work on East Hildebrand!** Expect significant delays and detours on East Hildebrand Road due to ongoing water main construction. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a multi-phase project. Plan accordingly.
</AlertBox>
