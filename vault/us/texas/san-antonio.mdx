## The Rundown

Alright, fellow pavement pounders and trail blazers, let’s talk about today, September 23, 2026. The weather report says it's 24°C (75°F) and clear out there, which sounds like a dream. But the reality is a little more humid, feeling closer to 27°C (81°F) with some partly sunny conditions rolling into cloudy later today, and a 20% chance of rain. So, don’t let that "clear" fool you into thinking it's fall crispness just yet. It’s still San Antonio, after all.

The air quality isn't screaming "stay inside," but it's not exactly pristine, either. Our AQI is sitting at 55, with PM2.5 taking the lead as the dominant pollutant. The forecast shows ozone and PM2.5 both bouncing around the "moderate" range for San Antonio, so if you've got sensitive lungs, take it easy. No official Ozone Action Day, but it's not far off in terms of what your body feels.

## Local Hazards to Dodge

This is where things get interesting, because San Antonio is apparently one giant construction zone lately. Seriously, are they trying to rebuild the entire city at once?

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures Galore!
Several of our beloved greenway trails are still shut down or have significant portions closed. If your usual route takes you through the **Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604** (north of Valero Trailhead to The Rim), you're out of luck until mid-2027. Yes, 2027. Plan an alternate, or just accept that your "long run" might turn into a "long drive to a different starting point." The Culebra Creek Greenway near Tranquil Creek is also still closed for a couple more weeks, and Garza Park Loop Trail is largely inaccessible until January 2027. Seriously, check the city's parks website before you even lace up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Road Work Headaches
Beyond the trails, road construction is a beast. Lookout Road, a pain point for anyone on the Northeast Side, had its closure postponed to *today*. So expect delays if you're driving through there. Also, keep an eye out for lane closures on IH-10 at Flores St. and the US 90 EBFR on-ramp at Loop 410 today. It's enough to make you just want to run in circles in your own neighborhood.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Best Bet
With the moderate AQI and the sheer volume of closures, if you're heading out for anything strenuous, stick to a known open route or hit up your gym's treadmill. If you insist on hitting the trails, verify their status online *before* you leave. The San Antonio River Authority also notes the SASPAMCO Paddling Trail is partially closed due to log jams, so paddlers, be aware. Don’t cross trails under water, either, though thankfully, no major flooding alerts for today. Stay hydrated, be mindful of that lingering humidity, and try not to get too frustrated by all the orange cones.

</AlertBox>
