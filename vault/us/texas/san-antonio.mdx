### The Weather Whiplash is Real

Currently, it's a relatively cool 22.2°C (72°F) and overcast. A nice change, right? Don't get too comfortable. Daily chances for showers and storms are sticking around through the weekend, with the National Weather Service reporting an "uptick in coverage expected particularly from Friday to Sunday." This afternoon, we're looking at random, pop-up downpours, which could bring gusty winds and minor street flooding. And despite the current cool, humidity will crank those "feels like" temperatures into the mid-90s later today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pop-Up Storms & Flash Flooding:** Keep a close eye on the sky and local weather alerts. Flash flooding can happen quickly, especially in low-lying areas or underpasses. If you see standing water, turn around – don't drown. Seriously. Your run isn't worth it.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures & Construction Headaches

Here's where your meticulously planned route might get thrown for a loop. Several spots around town are dealing with closures:

<AlertBox type="danger">
**San Pedro Creek Culture Park & Salado Creek Greenway:** A section of the east bank trail along San Pedro Creek Culture Park is temporarily closed for construction between Cameron Street and two lower trail access points. Additionally, sections of the Salado Creek Greenway are impacted, including periodic closures at the Tobin Park Trailhead and a complete closure of the Gembler Road to Houston Street section until winter 2026 for maintenance. A part of the trail at Tom Slick Park is also slated for closure due to a construction project. Plan your longer runs with alternative routes in mind, or stick to loops you know are clear.
</AlertBox>

General construction and maintenance are also affecting various park playgrounds and parking lots across the city. While these might not directly block your running path, they could impact access or add an unexpected detour. Always pay attention to signage.
