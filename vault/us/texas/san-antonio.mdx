Look, I get it. You want to get outside. But July in San Antonio is no joke, and today is proof. The mercury is climbing to a balmy 96°F (36°C), and while it's "mainly clear," that just means the sun is out there actively trying to cook you. The National Weather Service even warned about elevated heat risk persisting through the weekend. Seriously, hydrate like your life depends on it, because, well, it kinda does. The Texas Department of State Health Services just put out another reminder about preventing heatstroke this month, and they're not wrong.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory
The heat is no joke today. With temperatures pushing 96°F, your risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke is high. Plan your outdoor activities for early morning or late evening. Drink more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and listen to your body. Seriously, if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get inside and cool down immediately.
</AlertBox>

On top of the heat, the air quality isn't doing us any favors. An AQI of 63 is moderate, and with PM2.5 in the air, you might feel some irritation if you’re pushing it too hard. Combine that with moderate pollen, and your lungs might not be thanking you for that PR attempt.

Now, about our trails. If your usual route involves the **Leon Creek Greenway System**, particularly near Loop 1604, listen up: a section north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim is closed until *mid-2027* due to TxDOT construction. That's right, next year. And it's not the only one. Portions of the Salado Creek Greenway are seeing periodic closures for TxDOT work and maintenance, and there are even erosion repairs ongoing on the Mission Reach. It's like a city-wide obstacle course, but without the fun mud pits (usually, unless it rained recently). Speaking of which, while Tropical Storm Bertha's remnants didn't flood us yesterday like some other parts of Texas, remember that rain can make trails muddy and slippery, so proceed with caution if you hit a wet spot.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Construction Headaches
Expect ongoing construction and closures on several major greenway sections, including parts of the Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway. Always check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Recreation website for specific closures before you head out. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the detour signs.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Smart, Stay Safe
Consider hitting the gym today if the outdoor heat feels overwhelming. If you *must* be outside, hydrate, go early, and check current trail conditions. This isn't the day to try a new, remote trail without letting someone know where you are.
</AlertBox>
