Look, I know you want to hit the trails, but unless you're a lizard, today's going to be a rough one out there in San Antonio.

<BulletList>
*   **Heat Warning:** It's August in South Texas, people. The initial 80°F is a cruel deception. We're looking at daily highs hitting the upper 90s to over 100°F later, with heat index values pushing 100-111°F. KSAT's even saying we're probably stringing together over a week of triple digits. Unless you're out before sunrise, you're signing up for a sweat bath with potential heatstroke as a bonus prize.
*   **Trail Blockades:** Good luck finding a clear path. Construction and maintenance are playing Whack-A-Mole with our beloved greenways. Sections of the Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway are shut down for months. The South Salado Creek Greenway near J Street Park is actively getting concrete poured, so forget that. Even the River Walk by Hotel Emma Bridge is facing temporary closures today for crane ops. Plan for detours, uneven surfaces, and probably more road running than you'd like.
*   **Pollen's Mild Annoyance:** At least your allergies aren't getting completely throttled. Moderate pollen isn't ideal, but it's not the primary villain today.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory: This isn't a joke, it's San Antonio in August.**
With heat index values climbing well over 100°F, outdoor exercise, especially during midday, is dangerous. Heatstroke, heat exhaustion, and dehydration are real threats. Adjust your workout times, reduce intensity, and listen to your body. That "push through it" mentality could land you in the ER. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Construction Chaos**
If you're planning a long run on any of our greenways, check the City of San Antonio Parks & Rec page *before* you leave. Expect closures on the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604, sections of the Salado Creek Greenway, and even temporary stoppages on the River Walk near the Pearl. Don't be that runner who gets five miles out only to hit a "trail closed" sign and has to backtrack or navigate sketchy detours. Pickwell Park's parking lot and some playgrounds are also undergoing improvements from bond programs, causing further access issues in some areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Your Only Friend**
If you *must* run outside, do it before the sun fully commits to its daily assault. We're talking 6 AM, maybe 7 AM at the absolute latest. Carry more water than you think you need, or plan routes with known water stops (and check if that Branch River Park water fountain is even working, because it was under repair recently). Electrolytes are your second best friend. And for the love of all that is holy, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear.
</AlertBox>

My recommendation? Hit the gym. Or swim. Or do literally anything indoors that doesn't involve battling triple-digit heat and an obstacle course of construction cones. It's just not worth it today.
