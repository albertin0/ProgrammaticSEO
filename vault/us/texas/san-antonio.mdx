## Trail Talk & Local Lowdown

If you absolutely *must* get out there, here's what you need to know about a few spots:

### McAllister Park
As mentioned, this place is going to be a zoo tonight with the Midsummer Night's Dream Women's Run. Even if you're planning an early morning run, remember those low temperatures are still high (75-80°F) and the humidity will be thick. Don't think because the sun isn't directly scorching you that the heat isn't a problem. It is.

### Leon Creek Greenway
If you usually hit the Leon Creek Greenway, especially the section at Loop 1604 north of the Valero Trailhead, forget about it. It’s closed for TxDOT construction and will be until at least mid-2027. Don't be that person ignoring the signs; those construction zones are active. Other sections of the Salado Creek Greenway are also facing closures or intermittent disruptions for maintenance and construction. Check the City of San Antonio Parks & Rec site before you head out.

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Hydration is Your Only Friend**
If you insist on an outdoor workout today, your water bottle better be super-sized. And it's not just water; replenish those electrolytes. Start hydrating *now*, not an hour before your run. Plan shorter routes, hit the trails at sunrise if you can, and listen to your body. That "push through it" mentality will land you in the ER today.
</AlertBox>

Your provided AQI is 60 (Moderate), but the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecast for San Antonio indicates PM2.5 levels could be at the upper end of "Good" to lower end of "Moderate" particularly in the afternoon and early evening.
