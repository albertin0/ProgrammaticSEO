## The Full Breakdown: What to Expect Today

### Heat and Humidity: Your Worst Enemy

Seriously, the heat is no joke. We’ve been lucky enough to avoid the absolute worst of it this week (remember that 115-degree "feels like" warning we dodged a few days ago?), but 101-103 degrees is still firmly in the "stay inside, fool" category. If you absolutely must get out there, aim for sunrise, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does), and keep it short. Seriously, that long run can wait.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning (Unofficial):** With "feels like" temperatures hitting 101-103°F, heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real threats. Limit outdoor exposure, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality: Not Quite Fresh Hill Country Air

The "Moderate" pollen levels aren't helping, but the real concern for your lungs is the general air quality. Today is forecast to be "Poor" for sensitive groups, and that Saharan dust cloud arriving Monday means things aren't improving soon. If you have asthma or other respiratory conditions, consider swapping the outdoor trails for an air-conditioned gym today.

### Trail Conditions & Closures: Watch Your Step (and Where You Step)

While we haven't had recent heavy rains today, past downpours earlier in June led to significant flooding, and some trails might still be recovering. The City of San Antonio explicitly warns against crossing flooded trails and notes that mud and debris can linger.

Specifically, if your usual route takes you through the **Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 (north of the Valero Trailhead)**, know that a portion is closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. You'll also find the Maverick Creek Trailhead closed for about two weeks for sidewalk and lighting work. These aren't minor detours, folks. Plan alternate routes if these are your stomping grounds.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead:** Major TxDOT construction on Loop 1604 near U.S. 281 means traffic headaches getting *to* some of the northern trails. Plan for delays if you're driving in that direction this weekend.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Key:** Drink plenty of water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Electrolyte drinks aren't a bad idea either.
**Sun Protection:** An extreme UV index means sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses aren't optional. Burn time is fast today.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, San Antonio. Your body will thank you for being smart about it.
