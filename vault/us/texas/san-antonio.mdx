### Trail Troubles: Construction is the Constant
Here's where it gets annoying for us local athletes. San Antonio seems to have a permanent "Under Construction" sign hanging over half its green spaces. If your route involves the **Leon Creek Greenway**, specifically the portion under the 1604 overpass north of the Valero Trailhead, expect periodic closures through April due to TXDOT construction. Similarly, the trail within **Culebra Creek Park** that connects to the Culebra Creek Greenway is closed for reconstruction for approximately three months, having started back in mid-February. The walking trail at Lees Creek Park, which has been closed since December 2025 for bond project improvements, is *scheduled* to reopen today, March 31, 2026. Maybe. Check before you go, because "scheduled" and "actually open" are often two different things in this city.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures Abound:** Don't just show up expecting your usual route to be open. Key segments of Leon Creek Greenway and Culebra Creek Park trails are currently impacted by long-term construction or periodic shutdowns. Always have a backup plan or scout your route online first.
</AlertBox>
