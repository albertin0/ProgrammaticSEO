## Today's Local Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Flash Flood Threat is REAL Tonight Through Friday
Seriously, San Antonio, pay attention. The National Weather Service has issued a Flood Watch from 7 PM tonight (Wednesday) through Friday evening. We're talking 2 to 4 inches of rain across the board, with isolated pockets getting up to a staggering 8 inches. This isn't just "puddles"; this is "your car floats away" territory. Stay off those low-water crossings. If you see water on the road or trail, turn around. It's not worth it. Check BexarFlood.org before you even think about heading out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Construction Messes
That water main break yesterday near Wurzbach and Blanco isn't just a memory; crews were working on it until late last night, and the Salado Creek North trail was a disaster. Beyond that, several sections of our beloved Greenway trails are already closed. Portions of the Salado Creek Greenway are shut down for repairs, including south of Southside Lions Park until October 9 and another section from Gembler Road to Houston Street until further notice. And don't forget the long-term TxDOT construction on the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604, which isn't slated to open until mid-2027. Always check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Trails notices before you head out, because getting caught behind a "Trail Closed" sign in a downpour is just miserable.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Escape (Routes)
With all the road and trail closures, plus the impending flood risk, you need a backup plan for your commute or your workout. TxDOT has various I-35 frontage road closures happening overnight tonight, which could impact your morning plans if you're out early. Even if you're just driving to your starting point, be aware of ongoing construction from the VIA Rapid Green Line project along San Pedro Avenue, which means intermittent lane and sidewalk closures. Leave extra time, or better yet, stay home and do some burpees in your living room.
</AlertBox>
