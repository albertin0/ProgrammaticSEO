## Extreme Heat Advisory: It's a Scorcher, Y'all
Seriously, look outside. We're looking at highs around 101-102°F today, with "feels like" temperatures easily hitting 103°F in the city. The National Weather Service has warned of heat index values between 100°F and 112°F this week for South-Central Texas. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's *dangerous*. Unless you're out before 8 AM or after 7 PM, and even then, staying hydrated is a full-time job. Forget PRs, focus on not ending up in the ER. If you absolutely *must* exercise outdoors, keep it short, stay in the shade, and drink more water than you think you need. Consider moving your workout indoors to avoid heat exhaustion or stroke.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Wildfire Risk: Don't Be That Guy
While our immediate air quality is holding steady at a "Good" AQI of 40, don't get complacent. Texas is under extreme fire danger across a large portion of the state right now, and 157 counties have burn bans in effect. Texas A&M Forest Service was responding to active wildfires as recently as yesterday. We even had an "odor of smoke" incident reported on Oakwell Farms Parkway just a couple of days ago. This means localized smoke could pop up fast, and any spark is a problem. Avoid any activity that could cause a fire. Be vigilant on trails, especially if you're out near dry brush or undeveloped areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
## Trail Closures: Your Favorite Route Might Be a No-Go
Navigating our trails is already a puzzle, and it's even worse right now. A significant chunk of the **River Walk trail between the Hotel Emma Bridge and Brewmeister's Bridge** is closed through Thursday, August 20, for crane operations. So much for a scenic jog near the Pearl. Elsewhere, parts of the Salado Creek Greenway are shut down near Southside Lions Park until October for repairs, and another section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is closed indefinitely. Even a portion of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 is blocked off for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website for current closures before you head out, or you'll be doing an unexpected road run.
</AlertBox>
