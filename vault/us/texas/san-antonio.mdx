### Air Quality & Your Lungs

Today, the Air Quality Index (AQI) is clocking in at 95, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That’s in the “Moderate” range, which sounds benign, but it means if you’ve got sensitive lungs, asthma, or other respiratory issues, you *will* feel it. And let's not pretend San Antonio's air is ever truly pristine; Bexar County regularly exceeds ground-level ozone standards. So, even without an official "Ozone Action Day," your lungs are still doing extra work.

### Trail Closures & Obstacles

If you're planning on a scenic long run, do your homework. Several popular spots have ongoing issues:

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Mission Reach Maintenance:** Crews are still doing "stem-density maintenance" along sections of the Mission Reach, Monday through Friday during daytime hours. That means potential temporary closures on segments, so be prepared for detours or just pick another path. This isn't ending until Spring 2026, so it's a fixture for now.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**San Pedro Creek Culture Park:** The east bank trail along San Pedro Creek is still closed in sections. Don't expect a clear run there if you usually hit that route.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Leon Creek Greenway & Culebra Creek Greenway:** Parts of the Leon Creek Greenway under the 1604 overpass are seeing periodic closures for TXDOT construction. And the Culebra Creek Greenway trail *within Culebra Creek Park* has been shut down for reconstruction for about three months now, so that's definitely out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**River Rest Restroom Closure:** If you're out on Camino Coahuiltecan, the River Rest restroom is closed for maintenance through April 3rd. Plan accordingly, or you'll be doing a very uncomfortable trailside squat.
</AlertBox>
