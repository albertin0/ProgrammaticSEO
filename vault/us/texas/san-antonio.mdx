### Air Quality & Pollen Report: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

While the general AQI for San Antonio today is moderate at 57, with PM2.5 leading the charge, AccuWeather forecasts for both yesterday and today (Saturday and Sunday) are showing the air quality as "Unhealthy" with an AQI of 106. So, while the official numbers might look okay on paper, if you've got any kind of respiratory sensitivity, consider this your personal red flag. Healthy individuals might still experience some throat irritation or breathing difficulty with prolonged exposure, so don't push it too hard. A recent report from IQAir indicated that San Antonio's average annual PM2.5 concentration in 2025 was nearly twice the World Health Organization's guideline, linking it to respiratory and cardiovascular health issues. And yeah, it's still "Moderate" pollen out there, so expect that seasonal sniffle and eye-itch combo.

</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Construction Headaches

Remember those flash flood warnings from earlier this week that canceled Fiesta events and closed roads? Well, the water might have receded in most places, but the San Antonio River Authority still has river access gates closed at Helton Nature Park, Graytown Park, and River Crossing Park due to lingering flood risk. Plus, areas like Camino Coahuilteca and River Front Parkway were closed earlier this week due to high water, and even if they're "reopened," expect mud, debris, and slick spots on low water crossings. Small afternoon storm chances are still in the forecast through Sunday, meaning any pop-up showers could exacerbate localized flooding. Don't be that person who needs rescuing.

And of course, it wouldn't be San Antonio without endless construction. The San Antonio River Authority is dealing with erosion repair along the Mission Reach section of the River Walk, causing closures on the west bank hike and bike trail from Lone Star Blvd to the first pedestrian bridge. The City of San Antonio also lists ongoing closures on various sections of the Salado Creek Greenway and Leon Creek Greenway for maintenance and TXDOT projects, with some lasting until winter 2026. Road closures for VIA Rapid Green Line construction are causing intermittent lane and sidewalk disruptions along the San Pedro Avenue corridor.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  DO NOT cross any flooded roadways or trails, no matter how shallow they look. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase; it's how you stay alive. Areas around creeks and low water crossings are still a gamble after the recent rains.
</AlertBox>

Specifically, if you're planning to hit **Brackenridge Park Trail**, be aware that lighting is currently out along the north portion from the Josephine Street inlet to just past Highway 281 due to an electrical issue. Repairs are expected by tomorrow. If you're out there near dusk or dawn, be extra careful.

<AlertBox type="info">
  Always check the San Antonio River Authority's "Current Conditions and Closures" page and the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation updates before heading out. Local news sites like KENS5 and KSAT are also good for last-minute alerts regarding storm chances and road closures.
</AlertBox>

Today’s a day for cautious outdoor activity. Enjoy the mild air, but keep an eye on your breathing, watch for standing water, and be ready to reroute if construction or trail conditions are worse than advertised.
