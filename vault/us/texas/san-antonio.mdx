## The HeatDome is Real – Stay Indoors or Get Out *Early*

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next guy, but when the National Weather Service is throwing around terms like "dangerous heat" and "heat stroke is an emergency, call 911," you need to listen. We're looking at actual temperatures climbing to 101°F today. That's not just "warm," that's "re-evaluate your life choices" hot for any strenuous activity.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Reschedule Your Strenuous Workout
Seriously. If you absolutely MUST be outside, aim for before 8 AM or after 8 PM, stay in the shade, and hydrate aggressively. The NWS advises rescheduling strenuous activities to early morning or evening. Wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing. Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Don't be that person wheeled off the Salado Creek Greenway by EMS because you thought you were tougher than a Texas August.
</AlertBox>

## Breathing Easy? Mostly.

The good news is our air quality isn't compounding the problem. With an AQI of 43, it's generally good for most folks. But here's the kicker: AccuWeather is still showing our AQI as "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups" today. While other sources are more optimistic, if you've got any kind of respiratory sensitivity, that discrepancy alone should give you pause. Don't push it if you feel any irritation.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Pollen Alert: Ragweed on the Rise
Ragweed is being a jerk today, sitting at moderate levels. If you're an allergy sufferer, expect potential sneezing, runny nose, or itchy eyes. Consider an antihistamine or sticking to indoor workouts to avoid the misery.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures and Other Nuisances

If you're planning a long haul on one of our beloved greenways, you need to check your route. The City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation department has ongoing closures. For example, portions of the **Salado Creek Greenway** are currently closed for repair work south of the Southside Lions Park entrance until October, and another section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is closed indefinitely. There are also periodic closures on the Salado Creek Greenway near the Tobin Park Trailhead due to TxDOT construction.

And for those who like to explore our natural spaces:

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wasp Nests Reported at Confluence Park
Active wasp nests have been sighted near the pavilion at Confluence Park as of yesterday. Exercise extreme caution and avoid disturbing any vegetation in that area. Nobody wants to finish their run with a swarm of angry wasps.
</AlertBox>
