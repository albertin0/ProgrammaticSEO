Listen up, San Antonio. Today is *not* the day to go all-out on your outdoor workout. Unless your idea of "fun" involves inhaling desert dust and sweating through multiple layers of clothing by 8 AM.

<BulletList>
*   **Saharan Dust is Here, Folks:** Expect hazy, milky skies today as a plume of Saharan dust settles over South Central Texas. This isn't just aesthetic; those microscopic silica and quartz particles can trigger respiratory irritation. Consider your lungs warned.
*   **Heat & Humidity Are Back:** While the morning might feel okay, temperatures are set to hit the mid-90s, possibly even touching 100°F in some areas, with heat index values making it *feel* like 100-107°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's a direct route to heat exhaustion if you're not careful.
*   **Ongoing Trail Disruptions:** If your usual route takes you through the Leon Creek Greenway, especially near Loop 1604 or north of the Valero Trailhead, be aware of long-term construction closures. Don't assume your path is clear.
</BulletList>

## Today's Air Quality & Pollen Breakdown

The air quality around San Antonio today is officially "Moderate," with an AQI of 52, and the dominant pollutant being PM2.5. This isn't *terrible* on its own, but combined with the Saharan dust, it means your respiratory system is working harder than usual. The dust alone is enough to make you consider an indoor session, especially if you have any existing lung issues.

Pollen levels are sitting at a "Moderate" rating. No need to panic on that front, but it's another layer for your lungs to deal with.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Seriously, Mind the Heat!
The real feel temperature is going to be brutal this afternoon. Heat index values can easily creep into the triple digits. This isn't just about being hot; it's about heatstroke territory if you're pushing hard without proper hydration and breaks. Don't be a hero. Cut your mileage, shift your workout to early morning (and even then, hydrate like your life depends on it, because it kind of does), or hit the gym.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Saharan Dust: Not Your Friend
That hazy sky isn't just a filter for your sunset photos. The Saharan dust particles are fine enough to get deep into your lungs and cause irritation. If you have asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory sensitivities, stay indoors. Even if you don't, limiting strenuous outdoor activity is a smart move today. Your airways will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Greenway Route (or Find a New One)
Multiple sections of our beloved Greenway Trail system are under construction. The Maverick Creek Trailhead is closed, and a significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 is shut down until mid-2027 due to TxDOT work. Don't show up expecting to run your usual loop only to be met with orange cones and frustration. Check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Trails notices before you head out, or better yet, assume some of your favorite stretches might be impacted and have a backup plan.
</AlertBox>
