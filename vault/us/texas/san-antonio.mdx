## The Rundown

Alright, San Antonio, let's talk about it. Today, May 17, 2026, is a real mixed bag for outdoor enthusiasts. Your lungs are actually getting a break from the usual crud, with an AQI clocking in at a respectable 34. That means less airborne pollution to choke on while you're trying to breathe through those intervals. Good news for your respiratory system, bad news if you were hoping for an excuse to stay on the couch.

However, don't go celebrating just yet. While the air *quality* is good, the *air itself* is a different story. We're looking at 28.1°C (that's pushing 83°F for the Fahrenheit folks) and a humidity level so high, you'll feel like you're swimming through the air instead of running in it. Local forecasts are predicting highs between 76°F and 85°F for today, with a current humidity around 88%, averaging 74% throughout the day. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's a legitimate performance killer and a safety concern. Your body will be working overtime just to cool itself down, making even an easy run feel like a full-on sufferfest. Don't be a hero. Dial back the intensity, slow your pace, and keep your eye on your heart rate.

Then there's the delightful chance of scattered thunderstorms. We're talking about a 20% chance of rain during the day and it jumps to 40% tonight. If you're out on the trails, especially somewhere like the Salado Creek Greenway, those concrete paths can get slick faster than a greased armadillo. Plus, sections of the Salado Creek Greenway, like the part near Gembler Road to Houston Street, are actually closed until winter 2026 for maintenance, and the segment to Tobin Park Trailhead has periodic closures due to TXDOT construction. So, pay attention to signage and don't assume your usual route is clear. The trail within Culebra Creek Park that connects to the Culebra Creek Greenway also has an ongoing closure for reconstruction, if that's in your usual rotation.
