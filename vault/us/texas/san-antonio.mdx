### Trail Conditions & Other Hazards

Remember those torrential downpours from last week? They weren't just a distant memory; the ground is still paying the price. Several areas around town are still reeling from the flooding that was rampant through last Thursday.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOODING CLOSURES!** Don't even *think* about hitting some of your usual spots without checking. Jackson Nature Park is currently closed due to flooding from Cibolo Creek. Furthermore, lower sections of River Crossing Park, Graytown, and Helton are also closed. Unless you're training for an aquathlon, stay out of these areas.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the ongoing saga of San Antonio construction. The city's ever-expanding footprint means more cones, more detours, and more unexpected obstacles.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Zone Ahead:** A significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, is shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Parts of the Salado Creek Greenway are also experiencing intermittent closures. Even if it's not fully closed, you'll encounter uneven surfaces and active work zones. Be smart, stay alert, and don't assume the path is clear just because Google Maps says so. Heavy highway work on Loop 1604 and I-10 has been causing major nightly closures, which can affect access to nearby areas even in the morning.
</AlertBox>

While today's temperature is mild, keep in mind that typical San Antonio summer heat is just around the corner, with forecasts predicting highs in the mid-to-upper 90s later this week. Today might be a temporary reprieve, but don't get complacent.
