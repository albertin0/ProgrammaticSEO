## Watch Out for the Obstacle Course

The air quality is surprisingly good, an AQI of 46, so your lungs are mostly in the clear from pollutants [AQI Data]. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's smooth sailing. The real challenge today isn't the air, it's the actual *ground* you're trying to cover.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beware of Widespread Trail Closures!** If your usual jog is along the Leon Creek Greenway, especially north of the Valero Trailhead under the 1604 overpass, you're out of luck. A significant six-mile stretch is closed through April for TxDOT construction. And that's just *one* example. The Culebra Creek Greenway is also shut down for reconstruction for about three months, and Tobin Park Trailhead is closed indefinitely. Don't even get me started on the intermittent work zones along parts of the Mission Reach for "stem-density maintenance" through Spring 2026. It's a real mess out there, so check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website *before* you head out, or you'll be doing a lot of unexpected detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for the Swing!** Early morning runners, bundle up. That 9.6°C (49°F) feels colder than you think when you're moving. As the day progresses, it warms up nicely, so layers are key if your workout extends past 9 AM. Later in the afternoon should be pretty prime for temperature.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**I-35 North Accident Closure!** If your workout spot requires driving on IH-35 North, be advised there was a "major accident" reported early this morning at Frost Bank Center Drive, causing closures. Expect delays and seek alternate routes if you're heading that way to hit the gym or a less-impacted trail. Traffic sucks enough without adding a major incident to it.
</AlertBox>

Overall, the actual *weather* isn't bad after the initial chill wears off, and the air is clean. But the sheer volume of trail and road construction makes outdoor activity a logistical headache. Good luck out there, you'll need it.
