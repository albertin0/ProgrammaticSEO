## Why today isn't the day (or at least, why you need to be smart)

<BulletList>
*   The Air Quality Index is hovering at a "Nearing Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" 95, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's not doing your lungs any favors, especially if you're pushing hard.
*   We're looking at consistent rain and high humidity across San Antonio all day. What started as "Mainly Clear" apparently changed its mind, because the skies opened up. Expect slick surfaces, reduced visibility, and general damp misery.
*   Don't forget the moderate pollen count, just to add insult to injury. If your sinuses are already protesting the PM2.5, the extra pollen isn't going to help.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Your Step – Rain Makes Everything a Hazard
With the steady rain, trails and sidewalks are going to be slick. Those beautiful limestone sections of the Mission Trails National Historical Park? They turn into ice rinks when wet. And don't even think about trail running off-road unless you enjoy mud baths and twisted ankles. Visibility is also down, so be extra cautious if you're near roads or shared paths.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe Deep (Today, Anyway)
An AQI of 95 with PM2.5 is no joke, especially when you're exerting yourself. If you have asthma, COPD, or any other respiratory issues, seriously reconsider your outdoor plans or keep them very light and short. Even healthy lungs will feel the burn a bit more than usual. Combine that with moderate pollen, and you've got a recipe for a wheezy workout.
</AlertBox>

### Gear Up or Stay In
