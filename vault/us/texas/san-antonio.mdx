If you're looking to crush some miles today, you better have a damn good detour strategy.

<LungsJointsScore score={5} />

<BulletList>
*   **Race Day Chaos:** Unless you're pinning on a bib, Sunday is race day for the Golden Harvest Half Marathon & 5k/10k, meaning route closures and crowds are a guarantee for anyone else trying to get a run in.
*   **Construction Zone City:** Forget serene long runs on parts of the Greenway. TxDOT and city projects have entire sections, like a chunk of the Leon Creek Greenway, shut down for ages. Roads around Loop 1604 and I-10 are a mess until tomorrow morning.
*   **Moderate Pollen Annoyance:** Allergies won't send you to the ER, but a moderate pollen count is enough to make you feel like you've swallowed a cotton ball by mile three.
</BulletList>

Look, the air itself isn't terrible. We're sitting at a comfortable 22.3°C with clear skies, which usually makes for prime running weather. The AQI is a moderate 57, with PM2.5 as the main player, so your lungs aren't going to stage a full-blown rebellion. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's an easy day out there.

Today, San Antonio is a logistical obstacle course. First off, if you’re not signed up for the Golden Harvest Half Marathon, 10k, or 5k, good luck finding an uninterrupted route. Those events mean road closures and detours, especially in the areas they’re running. Seriously, check their course maps before you head out, or you'll be dodging cones and grumpy race marshals.

Then there's the never-ending construction. A significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway, especially north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is basically a hard hat zone until mid-2027. That's not a short-term inconvenience; that's a lifestyle change for anyone who uses those trails. You'll also find closures on sections of the Salado Creek Greenway near Southside Lions Park and between Gembler Road and Houston Street. If you were planning a quiet long run on those routes, you're out of luck. Garza Park's loop trail? Closed until January 2027. Classen-Steubing Ranch Park's Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent are also closed through September.

And don't even get me started on the roads. TxDOT has both eastbound and westbound Loop 1604 frontage roads at the I-10 interchange shut down until 5 AM Monday, September 28. If your workout involves driving anywhere near The Rim, UTSA, or Fiesta Texas, add a solid 20-30 minutes to your commute, or just find another spot entirely.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up for Half Marathoners (and Everyone Else!):** The Golden Harvest Half Marathon & 5k/10k is today. Expect significant road closures and crowds along the race routes. Plan your driving and running accordingly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Disruptions:** Large sections of the Leon Creek Greenway, Salado Creek Greenway, and parks like Garza Park and Classen-Steubing Ranch have ongoing closures due to construction. Check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website for specific closures before you go.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep it Local (and Indoors?):** With all the external chaos, consider hitting the treadmill or exploring a less-trafficked neighborhood park that isn't on any race routes or major construction projects. Or, you know, just embrace the chaos and sign up for that 5k last minute.
</AlertBox>
