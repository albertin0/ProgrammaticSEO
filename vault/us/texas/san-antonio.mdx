### Trail Chaos: It's a Minefield Out There

Seriously, if your workout routine relies on San Antonio's beautiful greenway trails, you need to check specific closures before you lace up. It's not just a little detour here or there; sections of major trails are actively undergoing work.

For instance, the **Mission Reach** is in the middle of "stem-density maintenance" through Spring 2026, meaning temporary closures and crews working on the riverbanks during daytime hours. Don't expect a serene, uninterrupted long run down there. Same goes for stretches of the **Leon Creek Greenway**, where a portion under the Loop 1604 overpass is closed through April for TxDOT construction. Even the loop walking trail at Lees Creek Park is closed until March 31.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Disruptions Ahead!** Multiple sections of the Howard W. Peak Greenway Trails System are impacted by ongoing construction and maintenance. This isn't just an inconvenience; it can mean unexpected detours onto less safe roads or abruptly ending your planned route. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation or San Antonio River Authority websites for the latest updates on closures *before* you go.
</AlertBox>

And if you're thinking of hitting **Brackenridge Park**, be aware there are ongoing lighting issues along the north portion of the trail from Josephine Street to past Highway 281, though repairs are expected by the end of today. The playground is also closed indefinitely, if that's part of your routine.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** With all this going on, now's a good time to explore some of our quieter neighborhood parks or stick to road running if uninterrupted mileage is your goal. If you *must* hit the trails, carry your phone for quick checks and be ready to reroute on the fly. And seriously, watch out for construction equipment and signage; those guys aren't looking out for your PR attempt.
</AlertBox>
