### Heat Advisory: It's a Scorcher!
The temperature is climbing fast, and with high humidity, "feels like" temperatures will hit the triple digits. This isn't just uncomfortable, it's a serious risk for heat exhaustion and heatstroke. If you're out there, stick to the early morning, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body. No PR is worth ending up in the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Hazy Skies & Lung Irritants
Although today's AQI is technically "Good" at 27, we've got a "Moderate" pollen count. On top of that, Saharan dust and even some high-altitude wildfire smoke are expected to cause hazy conditions and could nudge the air quality into the "Moderate" range for fine particulates. If you have respiratory sensitivities, you might want to consider an indoor workout or keeping it very low intensity.
</AlertBox>

### Mind the Closures: Leon Creek Greenway
For those of you who rely on our excellent greenway system, be aware that sections of the Leon Creek Greenway are currently impacted by construction. Specifically, the segment north of the Valero Trailhead at Loop 1604 remains closed due to ongoing TxDOT work, and this isn't expected to clear up until mid-2027. Always check local park and trail updates before heading out to avoid unexpected detours or dead ends. You can't just power through a construction zone, believe me, I've tried.
