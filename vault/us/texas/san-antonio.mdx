Look, let's be real, it's September 14th, 2026, and San Antonio is still very much in summer mode. The weather gods are not granting us any reprieve from the late-season heat wave. Temperatures yesterday, Sunday, hit the upper 90s, pushing close to 100°F, and the "feels like" temperature hovered around a ridiculous 105-107°F. Today is more of the same, with that lovely 107°F heat index sticking around. If you're planning on hitting the pavement or trails, understand that this isn't just "warm," it's "dangerously hot" for sustained activity.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Advisory: It's Hot, Y'all.
A heat advisory was active yesterday, Sunday, September 13th, and similar conditions are expected for Monday, September 14th. With the heat index hitting 107°F, outdoor workouts, especially during peak sun hours (10 AM - 7 PM), are a serious risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Hydration is non-negotiable, and if you're not used to this kind of heat, just stay inside. No PR is worth a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, your lungs are getting a relative break. The Air Quality Index (AQI) for San Antonio is currently a pleasant 26, which is firmly in the "Good" category. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, and while ozone can peak in the mornings around 7-8 AM, today's overall forecast looks clean. So, if you manage to avoid the sun, the air itself isn't actively trying to kill you. Yet.

But even if you find a sliver of shade, you've got to contend with the city's seemingly endless construction projects. For example, **Garza Park on the Westside** has its loop trail, multipurpose field, and softball field #2 completely closed until January 2027 as part of the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail project. That's a long time to be out of commission. Elsewhere, portions of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 are also shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. So, don't just head out hoping for the best; check the San Antonio Parks and Recreation website for current closures before you lace up.

### Smart Moves for Today
If you absolutely *must* get out, do it at sunrise. Seriously. Carry more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and don't push the pace. Listen to your body – if it's screaming for a break, take it. Or better yet, embrace the gym. It's air-conditioned for a reason.
