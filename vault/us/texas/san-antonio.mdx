## Why today is *not* the day to PR

<BulletList>
*   **It's a hot one, folks, no surprise there.** We're looking at temperatures hitting 101°F (38°C) later, with a "RealFeel" pushing 106°F (41°C). The forecast discussion warns of heat index values between 105-110°F through the weekend. While September *should* be bringing some relief, the long-range outlook says we're still leaning warmer than average. Your body isn't going to thank you for baking it.
*   **That "Moderate" AQI isn't exactly inspiring confidence.** With an Air Quality Index of 95, PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant. The light morning winds might elevate fine particulate matter, especially in more populated areas, pushing us into the lower end of the "Moderate" range for PM2.5. On top of that, there are residual smoke plumes over Texas from wildfires up north and even some from seasonal burnings. So you'll be sucking down more than just humid air.
*   **Don't forget the pollen.** It's a moderate pollen day, just enough to make your nose run and your eyes itch mid-stride. What's a little respiratory irritation when you're already breathing wildfire smoke and baking in the sun?
*   **Construction is still a hellscape.** Seriously, if you're trying to hit the Leon Creek Greenway around Loop 1604, forget it. That section is closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. And it's not just the greenways; San Pedro Avenue and various streets along the VIA Rapid Green Line corridor are seeing constant lane and sidewalk closures. Drivers are already fed up with nails in tires and endless detours from the Loop 1604/I-10 overhaul. Don't think your run will be immune.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Save Your Workout for Indoors
With real-feel temperatures hitting 106°F, outdoor exercise is a straight-up bad idea unless you're a lizard. Seriously, this isn't the day to prove anything to anyone. Schedule strenuous activity for the early morning or late evening *if you must*, but honestly, a treadmill or an air-conditioned gym is your best bet today. Hydrate like it's your job, and watch for any signs of heat exhaustion or stroke.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality and Pollen Punch
That moderate AQI (95, PM2.5) combined with moderate pollen means your lungs and sinuses are already working overtime. If you've got asthma, COPD, or are just generally sensitive to environmental crap, stay inside. Even if you don't, you'll feel it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route (Then Plan Again)
If you *insist* on going outside, check the city's park and trail closures before you head out. The Salado Creek Greenway south of Southside Lions Park is closed until October 9th, and portions of the Culebra Creek Greenway are shut for a couple of weeks. You might also hit a "Guided Bike Ride" on the Mission Reach this morning, so expect some company there. Expect the unexpected, and have a backup (or two) for your intended route. Better yet, just hit the gym.
</AlertBox>
