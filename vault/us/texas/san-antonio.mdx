## Workout Wisdom for May 12, 2026

Alright, San Antonio, let's talk turkey. It's a clear, comfortable Tuesday, which sounds like prime running weather on paper. But don't let that pretty blue sky fool you entirely. While the air quality is in the "Good" range and the dominant pollutant, ozone, is low, we're still dealing with the fallout from those nasty Mother's Day storms.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Greenways!**

Those downpours yesterday and over the weekend mean our unpaved trails, especially along places like the **Leon Creek Greenway**, are likely still slick, muddy, and prone to erosion. The City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation department explicitly states that trails can close after rain events for safety and to prevent damage. "Turn around, don't drown" applies to more than just driving; don't try to power through knee-deep mud just to get your miles in. You'll trash the trail, and probably your ankles.
</AlertBox>

Pollen's sitting at "Moderate" today. For some of us, that's a non-issue. For others, it's just enough to trigger a runny nose and itchy eyes. If you're one of the sniffly ones, maybe keep your outdoor efforts moderate and consider a quick rinse-off afterward. Don't push through allergy-induced asthma; that's just dumb.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Construction Zone Ahead – Seriously, It's Everywhere.**

You can't swing a dead cat without hitting a construction zone in this city, and our trails aren't immune. There are ongoing, significant closures affecting several popular spots. Portions of the Salado Creek Greenway (near Tobin Park Trailhead and Gembler Road to Houston Street) and sections of the San Pedro Creek trail are currently impacted by construction. Culebra Creek Park's trail connecting to the Greenway is also shut down for reconstruction for a few months. Seriously, check the City of San Antonio's Parks & Recreation website for specific closures before you head out, especially if your route involves any of the major greenway systems. A lane closure on Huebner Road started yesterday and will impact traffic through Wednesday, so plan your commute to the trails accordingly. Don't be "that guy" trying to hop fences or sneak through active work zones. It's dangerous, and you'll just get yelled at.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Evening Activity at San Pedro Creek:**

There's a Drum Circle scheduled for San Pedro Creek Culture Park this evening from 7 PM to 8 PM. If you're planning an evening stroll or run through that specific area, expect some crowds and rhythmic distractions.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a decent day if you're smart about where you go. Stick to paved paths if you're unsure about trail conditions, stay hydrated, and for the love of all that is holy, pay attention to your surroundings, especially near those construction areas.
