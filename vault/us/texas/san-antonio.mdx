<BulletList>
*   **The Heat is ON:** It's already 25.2°C (77°F) and feels like 28°C (82°F) out there, but don't be fooled by the morning "clear" skies. We're staring down a high of 36°C (97°F) today. <AlertBox type="danger">
### Danger: Extreme Heat Incoming
Don't underestimate that 97°F (36°C) high. Your body's cooling mechanisms will be struggling, especially if you're out during peak afternoon hours. Heat stroke is a real threat. Hydrate *before* you start, carry plenty of water, and consider moving your workout to early morning or late evening. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or get a headache, stop immediately and find some A/C. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Warning: Trail Closures and Downtown Chaos
Runners and cyclists, be aware of ongoing construction impacting trails. The Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 is closed until mid-2027. Also, downtown San Antonio is hosting several pre-July 4th events today. If you're planning to run near the River Walk or Alamo area, expect major congestion, detours, and dense crowds. Avoid the area if you're looking for an uninterrupted workout.
</AlertBox>
