## The Rundown: March 11, 2026

We're looking at a pretty decent day for a workout, especially if you're hitting it early or late. The Air Quality Index is a low 27, which is straight-up excellent, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. The sun's out, and it's going to get warm, pushing into the low 80s Fahrenheit (that's 28.1°C for those keeping track). Good news, your lungs won't be struggling with bad air.

Now, about those joints. They might be fine, but your patience might get a workout navigating closures.

### Weather Watch: More Clear Than Chaos

The severe thunderstorm watch that blanketed some of our surrounding counties until 2 AM this morning has finally expired. So, while you might've heard some rumbles overnight, the immediate threat of hail and damaging winds is behind us. That said, a "Mainly Clear" forecast doesn't mean everything's perfect. Expect those breezy south winds up to 25 mph. Nothing to knock you off your feet, but definitely enough to feel on a long run or ride.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Key in the Heat:** Even with clear skies, 80-degree weather means you need to be smart. Stay hydrated, especially on those longer efforts. And for goodness sake, wear sunscreen.
</AlertBox>

### Local Trail Headaches: A Construction Extravaganza

This is where your run or ride might get complicated. Our fair city loves a good construction project, and they don't seem to care about your Strava segments.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Salado Creek Greenway & Tobin Park Trailhead:** A portion of the Salado Creek Greenway leading to the Tobin Park Trailhead is *intermittently closed* due to a TxDOT project. Don't count on a clear shot through there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Leon Creek Greenway:** If you usually run under the 1604 overpass north of the Valero Trailhead, find a new route. That section of the Leon Creek Greenway is closed through April.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Brackenridge Park Trail Lighting:** Planning an evening run around Brackenridge? Be advised: the lighting along the north portion of the trail, from Josephine Street to just past Highway 281, is out due to an electrical issue. Repairs are supposedly coming by tomorrow, but for tonight, assume it's dark. Use extreme caution or pick a different spot.
</AlertBox>

There's also ongoing "stem-density maintenance" along sections of the Mission Reach through Spring 2026, which means temporary closures could pop up. And the east bank trail at San Pedro Creek Culture Park is also dealing with temporary closures.

Basically, assume you might have to improvise your route today. Don't get tunnel vision on your usual path; be ready to reroute or hit the pavement if your favorite trail is blocked.
