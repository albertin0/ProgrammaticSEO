<AlertBox type="danger">
  **DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory for San Antonio Today!**

  Temperatures are climbing, and the heat index will be dangerously high, reaching up to 106°F. This is a recipe for heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Seriously, reconsider any intense outdoor activity, especially during peak afternoon hours. If you *must* be outside, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does), wear light clothing, and take frequent, long breaks in the shade or AC.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **TRAIL CLOSURES & CONSTRUCTION HEADACHES**

  If you were planning a nice run or ride, you might need a new route. A significant portion of **Garza Park's Loop Trail** and its associated fields are completely closed until January 2027 for the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail Project. Don't be that person who ignores the barricades – large equipment is moving in there. Additionally, parts of the Leon Creek Greenway are shut down near Loop 1604 due to ongoing TxDOT work. Even if your specific trail isn't closed, expect detours and increased traffic if you're anywhere near major highway projects like on Loop 410 or I-35.
</AlertBox>

Also, a major **San Antonio 110 9/11 Memorial Climb** is happening today, which, while not a trail event, will involve a lot of first responders and civilians, potentially impacting traffic or downtown areas.
