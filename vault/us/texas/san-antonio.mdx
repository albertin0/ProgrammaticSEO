### Hot Take: Get Out Early, Or Don't Get Out At All

Look, I love hitting the pavement as much as the next runner who probably has too many half-marathon medals. But today? It's a "get it done before 8 AM or suffer the consequences" kind of day. The heat's no joke, and while there aren't any *official* heat advisories for this specific moment, the long-term forecast has heat index values well over 100°F for the week. That "Mainly Clear" sky sounds nice, but it just means more direct sun beating down on you, raising that body temp faster than you can say "heat stroke."

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Seriously, the forecast for San Antonio is consistently in the mid-90s to near 100°F this week. Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate, and consider moving your workout indoors during the peak heat of the afternoon. Heat exhaustion and stroke are not fun ways to spend your Tuesday.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Concerns for Sensitive Lungs:** With an AQI of 95 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, some sensitive groups might feel the burn, literally. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, take it easy. Distant wildfire smoke and Saharan dust aren't making things any clearer.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail & Road Closures:** Check the City of San Antonio Parks and TxDOT websites before you head out. Nobody likes a surprise U-turn halfway through their run because some ramp got permanently shut down yesterday.

Seriously, if you're not absolutely committed to an outdoor workout today, hit the gym. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
