## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Your Lungs vs. Live Oak:** While the official AQI is sitting pretty at 41 (good for the lungs, generally), the tree pollen situation is a whole other beast. We're talking "Very High" tree pollen levels, especially from those ubiquitous live oaks. This isn't just sniffles for allergy sufferers; the sheer volume can make anyone feel like they've run through a dusty attic. Expect green-yellow dust everywhere, from your car to your shoes.
*   **Sweat and Sniffles:** The temperature is climbing to a balmy 28.1°C (that's 82.6°F, for those keeping score). Combine that heat with the pollen assault, and you've got a recipe for dehydration and increased respiratory irritation, even if you usually handle the heat fine.
*   **Urban Obstacle Course:** Various construction projects continue to make our city trails and sidewalks a pain. From the Mission Reach to major roadworks, you'll need to stay vigilant to avoid closures and detours.
</BulletList>

## Hazards & Hot Takes

### High Heat & Fire Risk

<AlertBox type="warning">
Today's forecast high of 28.1°C (82.6°F) is significant, especially considering the rapid warming trend we're seeing this week. Hydration is paramount. Don't be a hero; carry more water than you think you need. Furthermore, near-critical fire weather conditions are still expected today due to dry air and breezy southerly winds. Bexar County still has a burn ban in effect, so if you see anything suspicious, report it.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen Pummels Your Pavement Pounders

<AlertBox type="danger">
Seriously, folks, the live oak pollen is out to get you. Texas Public Radio reported it's "coating vehicles, sidewalks across San Antonio" and can cause "allergy-like symptoms even in those without an oak allergy". If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, seriously consider an indoor workout. If you *must* run outside, hit the showers and change clothes immediately afterward to wash off the green-yellow menace. Consider saline rinses, too.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Blockages

<AlertBox type="info">
There's ongoing maintenance along sections of the **Mission Reach**, which means crews and equipment are working Monday through Friday during the day, leading to "limited trail closures and intermittent work zones". Similarly, a portion of the Leon Creek Greenway under the Loop 1604 overpass north of Valero Trailhead is closed through April. The VIA Rapid Green Line construction is impacting sidewalks and lanes along San Pedro Avenue from the Airport to downtown, some of which happens at night. Even downtown, the South Alamo Street project, while nearing completion by mid-April, is still causing lane and sidewalk closures. Plan your routes carefully, especially if you're trying to set a PR on a specific segment. You might just hit a barricade.
</AlertBox>
