## Trail Troubles & Wet Woes

Mother Nature can't make up her mind, and neither can the construction crews. We've got scattered rain chances today, and while it's not the major flood threat expected tomorrow, it's enough to make trails slippery and create standing water in low spots.

Beyond the weather, getting a clean run on the **Salado Creek Greenway** is still a game of dodge-the-closure. Portions near the Tobin Park Trailhead are seeing periodic closures thanks to TXDOT construction. And don't even think about running the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street – that sucker's closed until winter 2026 for maintenance. Similar story over at San Pedro Creek Culture Park, where sections of the east bank trail are closed for construction.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Wet Trails & Construction:** Expect muddy, slick spots on unpaved trails. Always watch your footing, especially around creek crossings and areas prone to pooling. Construction closures mean unexpected detours and potentially uneven surfaces. Don't cross trails that are under water.
</AlertBox>

## Downtown Diversions

If you were planning an evening workout downtown, brace yourself for a bit of a party. The city is kicking off "Downtown Thursday" with a Block Party at Legacy Park from 5-7 p.m. This means **Houston Street between Main and Flores will be closed off.** So, if your usual route takes you through there, plan a detour or embrace the festive chaos.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Evening Downtown Workout Tip:** The Downtown Thursday Block Party will close Houston Street between Main and Flores from 5-7 p.m. If you're running, biking, or walking through downtown this evening, reroute around Legacy Park.
