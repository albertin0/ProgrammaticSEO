<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat and Heat Index!

The heat index is hitting dangerous levels, potentially up to 105 degrees Fahrenheit today. This kind of heat can lead to heat exhaustion and heatstroke fast, especially if you're active. San Antonio Metro Health has already advised checking on vulnerable populations during these sustained hot stretches. If you must be outside, keep it short, stick to the early morning, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job. Seriously, chug that water. Consider moving your workout indoors to a gym or pool.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Subpar Air Quality for Sensitive Lungs

The Air Quality Index is in the moderate range, but with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant and reports of haziness from smoke and dust, this is not a day for max effort. If you have asthma, COPD, or any other respiratory issues, seriously reconsider that long run or intense bike ride. Even healthy individuals might notice throat irritation or difficulty breathing with prolonged exposure.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### PSA: Mind the Construction & Trail Closures

Planning to hit your favorite greenway? Good luck with that. The **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** is still shut down for TxDOT construction, probably until mid-2027. And don't even think about a smooth ride on parts of the **Salado Creek Greenway**; there are periodic closures near Tobin Park Trailhead and the Gembler Road to Houston Street section is closed indefinitely for maintenance. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks and Rec site before you head out, or you'll be rerouting and adding miles to an already questionable outdoor session. Maybe today's the day for that long-overdue spin class, or tackling some stairs inside your apartment building. Just saying.
</AlertBox>
