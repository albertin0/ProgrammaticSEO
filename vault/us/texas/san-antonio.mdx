## Why today isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Dangerously Hot Afternoons:** While you might catch a mild breeze this morning, temperatures are set to soar to the triple digits, hitting around 100°F, with "feels like" temperatures potentially climbing even higher later today. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous, and heat advisories are likely on the horizon for mid-week.
*   **Hazy, Dusty Air:** We're dealing with a "moderate" Air Quality Index (AQI) of 95, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The culprit? Saharan dust making its comeback, adding an extra layer of crud to the air you're trying to breathe.
*   **Trail Closure Chaos:** Forget hitting your favorite long routes. Multiple key sections of our greenway system are shut down for construction or repairs, including major portions of the Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway.

</BulletList>

Look, I get it. We love our city, and hitting the trails is practically a religion for some of us. But today, the elements are stacked against you. That 81°F temperature you might see on your phone right now? It's lying to you. By the afternoon, we're talking about actual temperatures nudging 100°F, with heat index values that could make it feel like 105°F or more. Your lungs are already working overtime with that moderate AQI and the Saharan dust floating around. Adding strenuous exercise to that mix when your body is struggling to cool itself is just asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning for Afternoon Workouts
Avoid strenuous outdoor activity between 2 p.m. and 7 p.m. today. Hydrate aggressively if you absolutely must be outside, and watch for signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, which can escalate quickly in this kind of humidity and heat. Seek air-conditioned spaces.
</AlertBox>

And if you thought you'd escape the heat by sticking to the shade on the trails, think again. The City of San Antonio and TxDOT have our greenways looking like a patchwork quilt of "closed" signs. For instance, a significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Want to run by Southside Lions Park? Forget it. A section of the Salado Creek Greenway south of the park entrance is closed until early October for repair work. Even smaller trailheads like Maverick Creek are reportedly closed for two weeks due to sidewalk work. It's a mess, and trying to navigate around closures when you're already fighting the heat and dust is just a recipe for frustration.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality and Respiratory Health
With a moderate AQI of 95 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant (thanks, Saharan dust), those with respiratory sensitivities should seriously consider indoor alternatives. Even healthy individuals might experience irritation during extended outdoor activity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route Ahead
Always check the City of San Antonio's parks and trails notices *before* you head out. Closures are frequent, particularly with ongoing construction projects impacting our beloved greenways. Don't waste your precious workout time getting rerouted.
</AlertBox>

So, seriously. Take a day. Drink some water. Find an air-conditioned gym or just enjoy your perfectly good A/C. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
