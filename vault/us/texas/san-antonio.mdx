## Today's Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Advisory!
A Heat Advisory is active for San Antonio until 7 PM CDT. Temperatures will hit 101-102°F, with the heat index soaring to 106-108°F. This kind of heat is no joke and can lead to heat exhaustion or stroke quickly. If you *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade as much as possible, and hydrate aggressively. The National Weather Service isn't kidding when they tell you to drink plenty of fluids and stay in air-conditioned rooms.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction and Event Congestion
If you're planning a long run or ride, be aware of a few things. First, the Loop 1604 North Expansion Project has major closures all weekend near the I-10 interchange, impacting vehicle access and potentially air quality with dust and exhaust. More directly for trail users, the Tour de Las Misiones is happening today, which means parts of the Mission Trails system will be busy with cyclists and other participants. Expect crowds and plan your route accordingly or find an alternative. Also, a heads-up that Gilbert Garza Park's loop trail is completely shut down until January 2027 for upgrades – find a different spot for your Westside workouts.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Evening Winds
Later this evening, between 7 PM and 10 PM, expect winds to pick up with gusts over 20 mph. This might offer a little relief from the lingering heat, but could also kick up some dust.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, today is a prime day for indoor workouts. Hit the gym, swim laps, or do some strength training in your air-conditioned living room. If you head out, do it early, keep it short, and listen to your body – it's not complaining, it's warning you. This isn't the day to chase that PR or try out a new long route.
