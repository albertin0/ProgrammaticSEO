### Today's Rundown

The air quality is actually pretty good today with an AQI of 49. So your lungs, in theory, should be happy on that front. But let's be real, the heat is the dominant player here. After Saturday's "cold front" (and I use that term loosely for August in Texas) brought some scattered rain and humidity, we're back to that familiar, oppressive heat.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Risk:** Today's temperatures are climbing, and those "feels like" temperatures are going to be brutal. Avoid strenuous activity during peak heat hours (generally 2 p.m. to 7 p.m.) and seek shade. Seriously, heat stroke is no joke. The City of San Antonio has cooling centers, use 'em if you need 'em.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos:** That pothole on Loop 1604 near Von Ormy that shut things down yesterday? It's reportedly fixed and open, but it's a reminder that our roads are always under stress. Also, TxDOT is doing nightly I-10 closures at the Loop 1604 interchange through Monday morning. So if you're driving to a trail after dark tonight or early tomorrow, expect detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route:** If you're heading out to any of the greenway trails, check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation website for the absolute latest on closures. Don't waste your energy driving somewhere only to find a "Trail Closed" sign. Some playgrounds, like at Escondido Creek Parkway, are also closed for maintenance today.
</AlertBox>

Bottom line: If you're running, cycling, or doing anything outside, do it before 9 AM or after 7 PM, and for the love of all that is holy, take water with you. More than you think you need. Stay safe out there.
