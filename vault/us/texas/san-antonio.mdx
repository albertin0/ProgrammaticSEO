### The Heat is No Joke

Seriously, stepping outside after about 9 AM is like walking into a convection oven set to "sweat profusely." The National Weather Service is calling for high temperatures up to 103 degrees with heat index values hitting 100-110°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous. Your body can't cool itself effectively when it's this hot and humid.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard!**
With a forecasted heat index up to 105°F, prolonged outdoor activity is **highly discouraged**. If you *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate aggressively. We're talking more water than you think you need, *before* you even start sweating.
</AlertBox>

### Air You Can Almost Taste (and Sneeze At)

The Air Quality Index is hovering around 59, which is "Moderate," with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. AccuWeather even flags today as "unhealthy for sensitive groups." Add in the moderate pollen, and anyone with allergies or asthma is going to feel it. It's not the worst we've seen, but definitely not ideal for pushing your limits.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Lungs Feeling It?**
If you're prone to respiratory issues or seasonal allergies, scale back any outdoor plans. Mornings *might* be slightly better for ozone, but the heat quickly negates any small benefit.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Event Detours

It's Sunday, so you might think the roads are clear, but think again. The **Summer Meltdown Half Marathon** is running today, August 16, from 7 AM to 11 AM, which means roads and potential trail access will be impacted by the race itself. On top of that, if you were planning a long run on the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, forget it. A significant portion north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim remains closed due to TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Parts of the Salado Creek Greenway are also closed for repairs and other TxDOT projects. You'll spend more time re-routing than actually running.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail & Road Closures:**
Expect major delays and detours around the Summer Meltdown Half Marathon route this morning. Additionally, ongoing TxDOT construction projects continue to impact major thoroughfares like Loop 1604 and sections of the Leon Creek and Salado Creek Greenways. Always check local city parks and TxDOT sites for current closures before you head out.
</AlertBox>
