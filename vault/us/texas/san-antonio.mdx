## Lung & Air Quality Check: Don't Trust That Number

Look, I get it. The app says AQI is 37 and "Good." But the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) and the National Weather Service have both issued an **Ozone Action Day** for San Antonio today. This isn't just a suggestion; it means atmospheric conditions are ripe for high levels of ground-level ozone, which is straight-up bad for your lungs. Ozone can irritate your respiratory system, trigger asthma attacks, and generally make you feel like you're sucking air through a coffee stirrer.

## Trail Updates: Detours and Dim Lighting

If your usual path includes the San Antonio greenways, you might hit a snag or two. Portions of the **Salado Creek Greenway**, specifically the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street, are closed for maintenance until winter 2026. The trail connecting to Tobin Park Trailhead is also seeing periodic closures due to TXDOT construction.

Over on the **San Pedro Creek Culture Park**, the east bank trail is seeing temporary closures for maintenance between Cameron St. and West Houston St. Pay attention to the signage, or you'll be doing extra loops you didn't plan for.

And if you're an evening runner at **Brackenridge Park**, be warned: the trail lighting on the north portion, from Josephine Street to past Highway 281, is out due to an electrical issue. Repairs are expected by the end of today, but until then, it's a dark stretch. Use extreme caution or find an alternative if you're running after sunset.

There are no major events explicitly blocking *trails* today. However, if you're planning for tomorrow, "Race the River 2026 – Mission Reach" is scheduled, which could impact that popular route. Always check local event calendars for your specific running spots.
