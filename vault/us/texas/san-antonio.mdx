## San Antonio Workout Safety: Sunday, July 12, 2026

Alright, San Antonio. This morning's looking deceptively calm, but don't let the "clear" skies fool you entirely – July in Texas always has a few tricks up its sleeve.

Plan alternative routes or prepare for detours.

### Local Hazards & Tips:

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Afternoon Heat & Humidity:** While the morning is tolerable, expect temperatures to rise to 93°F (34°C) this afternoon, with high humidity making it feel hotter. If you're running, cycling, or doing anything strenuous, schedule it for early morning or late evening. Mid-day workouts are for the truly insane, or those with serious access to AC. Drink water before, during, and after. Seriously, don't skimp.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Greenway Grief:** Major parts of the **Leon Creek Greenway** (north of Loop 1604 near the Valero Trailhead) and sections of the **Salado Creek Greenway** (heading toward Tobin Park Trailhead) are still shut down for construction. These aren't minor detours; these are long-term closures. Don't waste your time showing up, and definitely don't try to sneak past construction barriers. You're not special, and you're not getting a PR from a hospital bed.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Road Construction Impact:** Heads up if your commute to the trailhead involves Loop 1604 or I-10. TxDOT has significant weekend-long closures on frontage roads for bridge construction at the interchange, continuing through today. Factor in extra travel time and expect traffic, even early.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Saharan Dust Lingering:** Even with a "Good" AQI, sensitive individuals might notice the fine particulate matter from the Saharan dust plume. If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, pay attention to how you feel. Maybe consider an indoor workout if your lungs are particularly sensitive today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Your Critters:** This is a general Texas reminder from June, but the screwworm alert is still out there. If your furry running buddy has any unusual wounds or maggots, contact the Texas Animal Health Commission immediately. Keep an eye on them.
</AlertBox>
