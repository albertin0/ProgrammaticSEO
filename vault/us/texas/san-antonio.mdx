## The Daily Rundown: What to Watch For

### Weather's Mood Swings
Today's a bit of a mixed bag, folks. We're starting overcast, which is fine, but that doesn't stop the temperature from climbing into the low 90s by the afternoon. Couple that with those irritating south-southeast winds gusting up to 30 mph, and your long run down the Leon Creek Greenway is going to feel like a slog uphill both ways. Hydration isn't a suggestion today; it's survival. Seriously.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat & Wind Advisory:** With temperatures hitting 33.3°C (92°F) and winds up to 48 km/h (30 mph), heat exhaustion is a real threat. Hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Don't be a hero; take breaks in the shade.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the evening. National Weather Service has us at a Level 1 out of 5 risk for isolated severe storms tonight into early Thursday. We're talking heavy rain, damaging wind, and even some hail in spots. The rain chances peak tonight and through the Easter weekend, so if you're planning any late-night outdoor shenanigans, maybe reconsider.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Evening Storm Risk:** Isolated strong to severe storms are expected late Wednesday night into Thursday morning. If you see dark clouds rolling in, cut your workout short and get indoors. Trails can become slick, muddy, and dangerous very quickly after rain.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Trail Closures

It's San Antonio, so you know there's construction. Expect impacts. The Loop 1604 North Expansion Project is still a beast, with various closures. For instance, the eastbound Loop 1604 to westbound Loop 1604 U-turn at the I-10 interchange is closed for at least two weeks, which started Monday. While this is mainly a traffic headache, it can certainly affect how you get to or from your favorite running spots.

More directly impacting your runs:
<BulletList>
*   A section of the Leon Creek Greenway under the 1604 overpass is seeing periodic closures through April for TXDOT construction. You might hit a detour, so plan your route accordingly.
*   The San Antonio River Authority is doing ongoing maintenance on the Mission Reach to manage flood risk, which could mean temporary closures on some segments of the trail through Spring 2026.
*   Don't forget the River Rest restroom along Camino Coahuiltecan is temporarily closed April 1-3. Plan your pit stops.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Maintenance:** Always check local park and trail websites for real-time closures before heading out. Unexpected detours are a pain. And remember, recent flood mitigation efforts mean trails might be a bit squishy or have debris after any rain.
