<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heatstroke is No Joke
With "feels like" temperatures hitting triple digits later today, limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially between noon and 8 p.m. If you're out, drink way more water than you think you need, wear light, loose clothing, and find shade often. This isn't the day to push through.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind Your Lungs and Trails
The moderate AQI, combined with incoming dust and smoke, means sensitive groups should seriously consider an indoor workout or keeping it very light. Also, check trail closures before you head out. The Leon Creek Greenway, especially north of the Valero Trailhead, is undergoing construction until mid-2027, and sections of the Mission Reach are still closed for erosion repair. Don't get stuck doing a detour in this heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Weekend Construction Alert
If you're driving to your workout spot, be aware of TxDOT closures this weekend (starting tonight, Friday at 10 PM) at the Loop 1604 and Interstate 10 interchange as part of the North Expansion Project. Factor in extra time and patience.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, San Antonio heat in late June is nothing to mess with. Be smart, be safe, and maybe hit the gym with the AC on full blast.
