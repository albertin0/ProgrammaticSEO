## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Clear Skies (for now):** The morning and early afternoon are looking pretty sweet. Temps are in the low 70s, heading up to the low 90s, with clear skies. Get out there while you can.
*   **Pollen's Pestering:** We're sitting at "Moderate" pollen levels. Nothing crazy, but if you're sensitive, you know the drill – meds, shower, maybe keep your outdoor sessions a bit shorter.
*   **Air Quality is... Fine-ish:** AQI is technically "Good" at 40, but there's still some lingering particulate matter (PM2.5) from those lovely burnings south of the border, exacerbated by high humidity. Sensitive folks, keep an eye on it, especially if you feel that familiar irritation.
*   **Evening Storms are Brewing:** This is the big one. Severe thunderstorms are rolling in later today, with San Antonio under a "Slight (Level 2 of 5)" risk. We're talking damaging wind gusts, large hail, and heavy downpours between 8 PM and midnight. Don't be that person caught miles from home when the sky opens up.
*   **Trail Turmoil:** Construction and maintenance are still messing with our beloved greenway system. You can expect periodic closures on parts of the Leon Creek Greenway (near the 1604 overpass) and the Salado Creek Greenway (Tobin Park Trailhead). The section of Salado Creek Greenway from Gembler Road to Houston Street is straight-up closed until winter 2026 for maintenance. The east bank trail along San Pedro Creek is also temporarily shut down between Cameron St. and West Houston St. And if you're a night runner who hits Brackenridge Park, the north section of the trail from the Josephine Street inlet to just past Highway 281 has no lighting due to electrical issues. Be careful out there, or just pick another spot for now.
</BulletList>

**Evening Hazard: Severe Thunderstorms inbound!** Starting around 8 PM and going until well after midnight, expect damaging winds, large hail, and heavy rain. Don't risk it. Bring your run inside or push it to tomorrow morning. This isn't just a little drizzle; this is "don't get struck by lightning or pelted by ice chunks" weather.
