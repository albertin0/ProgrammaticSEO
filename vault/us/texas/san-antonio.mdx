## The Scorcher of a Summer Day

Look, it's July in San Antonio, so nobody's surprised by heat. But today, July 9th, is shaping up to be a real beast. We're talking high 90s, pushing that triple-digit "feels like" temperature for hours this afternoon. The City of San Antonio itself advises against strenuous activity between 2 PM and 7 PM, and for good reason. Your body isn't designed to thrive in this kind of oven, so if you're planning a long run or a brutal bootcamp, maybe reschedule for sunrise or sunset, or just hit the gym's AC. Hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's a survival strategy. Drink more water than you think you need, and then drink some more.

### Trail Talk: What's Open, What's Not

If you're heading out to your favorite running spot, be aware that some of our beloved greenways are still under construction or dealing with residual issues. For instance, a portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, remains closed for TxDOT construction, and it's not expected to reopen until mid-2027. Other areas, like parts of the Salado Creek Greenway near Tobin Park Trailhead, are subject to periodic closures. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation site before you head out, especially after any rain – while it's dry today, we had a wet spring, and some trails can stay muddy and have debris.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction & Trail Closures
Ongoing construction projects, including some along the Leon Creek Greenway, mean intermittent closures and detours. Always be mindful of construction zones, signage, and respect any barriers. Don't risk a twisted ankle for a shortcut.
</AlertBox>
