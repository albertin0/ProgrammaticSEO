### Flood Watch is NOT a Suggestion

We live in Flash Flood Alley, people. It's May, which typically means we're in our wettest month, and above-average rainfall is expected. Recent heavy rains have already caused deaths and significant flooding, leading to park and road closures. If you see standing water, especially near low-water crossings, **turn around.** Beitel Creek still has permanently closed low-water crossings and damaged infrastructure from a deadly flood last year, and comprehensive repairs won't even happen until 2027. Don't be a statistic.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**IMMEDIATE DANGER: Flash Flood Risk!** Thunderstorms with locally heavy rain are possible tonight and into the weekend. If the skies open up, creeks, especially along the Salado Creek Greenway, can rise faster than your heart rate on that last hill. Do NOT attempt to cross flooded roads or trails. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase; it's how you stay alive.
</AlertBox>

## Construction Zones & Trail Traps

Seriously, check your routes. The City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation website lists multiple ongoing trail closures for construction and maintenance, including sections of the Salado Creek Greenway and the Leon Creek Greenway. The Oxbow Trail along the Museum Reach is also out of commission until May 14th. If you were planning a long run through Government Canyon State Natural Area later today, know there's a rescheduled Sunset History Hike from 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM, so expect company. Also, TxDOT is still messing with Loop 1604 and I-10, creating overnight closures on IH-10 at Zuehl Road, which started last night. If you're driving to a trailhead, expect delays.
