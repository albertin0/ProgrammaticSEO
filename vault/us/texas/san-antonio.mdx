## Air Quality & Allergens: Take a Deep Breath (Cautiously)

Alright, San Antonio, the air today is what I'd call "aggressively average." We're looking at a Moderate AQI of 95, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant, and ozone also in the mix. This isn't "stay inside unless you absolutely have to" bad, but it's not exactly crisp, clean air, either. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to feeling like you've been breathing exhaust fumes, you might feel this one. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecast indicates we're hitting the lower end of the "Moderate" range for PM2.5. And let's not forget our old friend pollen, also hanging out in the "Moderate" category.

### Construction & Trail Chaos: Welcome to San Antonio, Always Under Construction

If you were planning on a peaceful long run on your favorite trail, you might need to recalibrate. The city's current obsession with orange cones and heavy machinery means you'll be dodging detours and outright closures.

For instance, the City of San Antonio's Parks and Recreation department updated their closures recently, and it's a laundry list. "Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent trails" at Classen-Steubing Ranch Park are closed for construction through October 2026. A significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is shut down for TxDOT work until mid-2027. And get this, a section *under I-10 near the Marquis Trailhead* is closed indefinitely due to a fallen highway overpass. That's not just a detour, that's a "find a whole new route" situation.

And today, October 25th, if you were thinking of hitting up **Olmos Basin Park**, know that the South Texas Off Road Mountain-Bikers (STORM) are doing a trail workday for tree clearing until noon. So unless you're joining the volunteer effort, expect interruptions.

The San Antonio River Authority also warns of Mission Reach closures, with electrical work closing the trail between Roosevelt and Echo Bridge on October 27th, and "Stem Density Efforts" temporarily closing trails near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Conception Park.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Seriously, check your route *before* you head out. The closure list is extensive. The I-10 overpass closure near the Marquis Trailhead is significant and long-term. Don't risk injury trying to bypass barricades; they're there for a reason.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Consider the paved greenway trails that might be less impacted, or stick to a track today. If you're driving to a trailhead, be aware that Loop 410 is seeing nightly lane closures from San Pedro to West Avenue until October 30th, potentially messing with your commute to a park.
</AlertBox>
