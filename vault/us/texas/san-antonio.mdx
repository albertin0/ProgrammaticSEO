Tonight is the **Day of the Dead River Parade** along the Downtown San Antonio River Walk, kicking off at 7 p.m.. This isn't just a quaint little gathering; it's a full-blown spectacle that will pack the River Walk and surrounding downtown streets. While there aren't specific road *closures* for the parade itself, expect monumental traffic congestion and parking shortages. Trying to squeeze in a run along the River Walk this evening would be like trying to run through a mall on Black Friday—futile and probably infuriating.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Downtown Gridlock & Crowds:** Avoid the Downtown San Antonio River Walk and surrounding areas this evening. The Day of the Dead River Parade starts at 7 p.m. and will lead to extreme pedestrian congestion and significant traffic delays. Your "fast-paced world" run will be a slow-motion shuffle.

And because it's San Antonio, there's always construction. TxDOT is at it again, with major closures on Highway 281 and Loop 1604, starting at 9 p.m. tonight and running through Monday morning. Beyond that, if your usual route includes parts of the **Leon Creek Greenway**, know that sections are still closed for TxDOT construction near Loop 1604, expected to continue until mid-2027. Seriously, can't a runner get a break?

<AlertBox type="warning">
