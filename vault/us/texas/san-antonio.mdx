### Afternoon Heat: It's Coming For You
While it's a pleasant 26.7°C (80°F) right now, San Antonio, the afternoon is going to be a brutal beast. Forecasts show temperatures hitting the mid-to-high 30s C (96-98°F), with a "feels like" temperature, or heat index, soaring up to 104°F (40°C). That's not just "warm," that's "stupid hot" for any sustained outdoor activity. If you're running outside, finish *before* the sun gets serious, or stick to shaded routes and keep your pace way down.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures Galore: Construction Never Sleeps
If you're planning a long run on one of our beloved greenway trails, double-check before you head out. A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is completely shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. That's not a short detour, folks. Beyond that, the Salado Creek Greenway south of the Southside Lions Park entrance is closing from tomorrow, August 10th, until October 9th for repairs. Even the Maverick Creek Trailhead is closed for about two weeks for sidewalk and lighting work. It's a patchwork of closures out there, so don't assume your usual loop is open.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Morning Checklist
Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate. Even with decent morning temps, San Antonio humidity is always lurking. Bring plenty of water, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear, and consider anti-chafing balm if you're going long. With moderate pollen, if you're sensitive, pop an antihistamine before you head out. And seriously, don't try to PR anything after 11 AM today. You'll regret it. Also, check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Recreation site for the latest on specific trail closures; things change faster than a Texas summer storm.
</AlertBox>
