## Air Quality: Breathe Easy? Not Today, Pal.

Look, the weather might *look* clear and feel decent at 17.2°C (about 63°F), but don't let that fool you. San Antonio is under an Ozone Action Day, which means the air quality is officially in the "unhealthy for sensitive groups" category, especially if you're out there huffing and puffing. My data says AQI is 51 (Moderate) with PM2.5, but the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) forecast and AccuWeather both put a big red flag on ozone for today, even calling it "Poor" for sensitive folks. That means if you've got asthma, lung issues, or you're pushing a stroller with a little one, seriously, find an indoor track. For the rest of us, it means your lungs are working harder than they need to.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Ozone Action Day in Effect!
Outdoor activity, especially strenuous exercise, is NOT recommended for sensitive groups due to elevated ozone levels. Even healthy individuals should consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Conditions: More Like Obstacle Courses

Remember those "severe storms" and "heavy rainfall" that hit earlier this month, causing all sorts of flooding and closures? While the skies are clear now, those trails don't just magically dry up overnight, especially not in a city with as many creekways as ours. Expect mud, slick spots, and possibly lingering debris. The San Antonio River Walk even saw some flooding yesterday.

And then there’s the perpetual construction. Forget finding solitude on your usual routes. A significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, from the Valero Trailhead north toward The Rim, is still shut down for TxDOT construction, and won't reopen until mid-2027. There's even a section near I-10 by the Marquis Trailhead that's closed indefinitely due to a fallen highway overpass. Seriously, detour around it unless you fancy a swim or climbing over concrete. Various other sections of the Salado Creek Greenway and San Pedro Avenue are also under construction with intermittent closures.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Muddy Conditions
Expect closures and detours on popular greenways like the Leon Creek and Salado Creek Greenways due to ongoing construction and residual mud/debris from recent heavy rains.
</AlertBox>
