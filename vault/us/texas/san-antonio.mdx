## Hazards & Headaches

**Construction Chaos Continues**
San Antonio, the city of endless orange cones, is still at it. There’s widespread construction across the city, and our beloved greenways are feeling the pinch. If you're planning a long run or ride, be aware that you might hit some detours or outright closures.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closure Alert: Lees Creek Park
Heads up if Lees Creek Park is part of your usual route: the loop walking trail is currently closed for improvements related to a 2022 bond project. It's slated to reopen March 31, 2026, but let's be real, "slated" and "actually happens on time" are two different things when it comes to construction. Plan an alternate path until then.
</AlertBox>

Other sections of the **Culebra Creek Greenway** also have closures for reconstruction, so don't assume your favorite stretch is clear. And don't forget the **Maverick Creek Greenway** is getting a new extension near UTSA, which means ongoing construction for the next year. Keep an eye out for equipment and unexpected blockages in those areas.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Pollen is Moderate – Don't Be a Hero
With "moderate" pollen levels, your sinuses might decide to stage a protest. Don't skip your meds if you're prone to seasonal sniffles. It's not worth struggling through a workout when a little antihistamine could make all the difference.
</AlertBox>

**Weather-wise, you're mostly in the clear today.** The overcast skies and mild temperatures are ideal for outdoor activity. There's only a 10% chance of rain, so you probably won't get soaked, but you won't be baking either. Just a heads up for tomorrow, Sunday, March 29, 2026: temperatures will climb into the 80s, and the humidity will increase. Enjoy this cool reprieve while it lasts.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pro Tip: Check City Parks & Rec for Updates
