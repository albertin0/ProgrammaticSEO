### Today's Rundown

It's 9.2°C (about 49°F) and clear out there, which sounds idyllic on paper. The AQI is a stellar 22, meaning you're breathing some quality San Antonio air. But before you lace up, remember that "crisp" air can sneak up on your joints if you're not warmed up properly. And let's be real, anything below 60°F here feels like a polar vortex to some of us.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Layer Up, Buttercup:** While the sun's out, 49°F is chilly. Think layers, especially for your extremities. Gloves and a good hat will make a world of difference, particularly if there's any lingering breeze. Don't let your muscles stiffen up before you even hit your stride.
</AlertBox>

Now for the real kickers: the trail situation. If you're planning on hitting the longer routes, you *need* to be aware.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Sections CLOSED:**
*   The **Medina River Greenway Trail** is currently closed from Applewhite to Pleasanton Rd. This means six miles are out of commission for your Sunday long run.
*   A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway**, specifically near Loop 1604, is shut down for TxDOT construction and won't reopen until mid-2027. If you usually access it north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, you're out of luck.
*   Another section of **Leon Creek Greenway** near the I-10 Marquis Trailhead is also closed due to a fallen highway overpass. Don't try to sneak through; safety first.
*   **Classen-Steubing Ranch Park** has its Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent trails closed through the end of October for construction.
*   And if **Garza Park** is your jam, its loop trail is closed until January 2027 for upgrades.

Seriously, folks, respect the barricades. They're there for a reason, and it's not to mess with your mileage. They're to keep you from accidentally becoming part of a construction project or taking a header into a freshly dug trench.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen's Pestering:** We're sitting at a "Moderate" pollen level. If you're one of the many San Antonians whose nose and eyes decide to declare war this time of year, pop those allergy meds *before* you head out. Clear air doesn't mean clear sinuses.
</AlertBox>

There aren't any major marathons or races actively blocking downtown streets today, which is a small blessing for city runners. Just the usual Sunday traffic, which, in San Antonio, is its own kind of hazard. So, stay vigilant, dress smart, and double-check your routes. It's a day for adaptable runners, not rigid ones.
