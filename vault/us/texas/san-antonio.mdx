## Today's Lung & Joint Breakdown

### The Relentless Heat
Look, I know we're San Antonians, and we *do* summer. But this "Sweat-tember" we're having is pushing records, and today is no different. That "mainly clear" sky means the sun is going to beat down hard, turning our 28.1°C current temp into something far more oppressive by the afternoon. You'll be in the high 90s before you can say "hydration," and those heat indices are going to make it feel like you're running inside a sauna. Don't be a hero. Heat stroke is real, and it doesn't care about your Strava segment.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Warning:** Highs in the mid-to-upper 90s today with heat indices exceeding 100°F. This is dangerous. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity, especially during peak sun hours (10 AM - 6 PM). Hydrate constantly, and if you *must* be out, keep it short, shady, and early.
</AlertBox>

### What's in the Air?
Your air quality is sitting at an AQI of 95, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the main culprit. That's a strong "Moderate," folks. If you're a runner with any kind of respiratory sensitivity, this is your body telling you to stay inside. It's enough to irritate healthy lungs, and it can exacerbate conditions for anyone with asthma or bronchitis. On top of that, moderate pollen levels are just a bonus challenge for your sinuses.

### Construction & Closures: The Usual San Antonio Shuffle
If you thought you could escape the heat by hitting the trails, think again. Our city's endless "improvements" mean prime running spots are constantly impacted. For instance, the loop trail at **Garza Park** on the Westside is completely closed until January 2027 as part of the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail project. That's a long time to reroute. And don't forget that portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** near Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, which is also out of commission until mid-2027 due to TxDOT work. So, even if the air were pristine and the temperature was perfect, you'd still be dodging orange cones and "Trail Closed" signs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Trail Closures:** Multiple greenway and park trails are closed for ongoing construction projects. Check the City of San Antonio's Parks & Recreation site before you head out, especially if you planned on using portions of the Leon Creek Greenway or Garza Park.
</AlertBox>

Yesterday's Great Strides 5K at McAllister Park is done, so at least that area should be clear of event-related crowds today. But frankly, with everything else going on, a clear trail is the least of your worries.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro-Tip:** If you absolutely *must* run outside today, aim for pre-dawn or late evening. Stick to paved surfaces with good shade coverage, and carry more water than you think you need. Or, you know, just don't.
</AlertBox>
