## The Air You Breathe

While the official AQI is sitting pretty at 49, putting us in the "Good" category, don't get too comfortable. That's for today's snapshot, and the dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other. San Antonio consistently surpasses EPA standards for ground-level ozone, especially on warm, sunny days, which can mess with your lungs. And guess what? We're expecting warm temps and afternoon sun, which could push ozone levels up. Elevated humidity is also forecast to raise PM2.5 levels.

On the bright side, any smoke from that wildfire in New Mexico is reportedly getting pushed out by a cold front later today, so at least that's one less thing to worry about.
