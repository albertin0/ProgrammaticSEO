## Today's Outdoor Grind: Heat, Haze, and Hazards

Alright, San Antonio, let's rip the band-aid off. Today is *not* the day for your PR attempt, unless "PR" stands for "Pushed to Retreat." We're looking at a day where your lungs will be working overtime and your body will be screaming for an AC unit.

### Air Quality: Don't Inhale Deeply

Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is clocking in at 95 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This isn't "unhealthy" but it's certainly not "good" either. Expect some Saharan dust to be swirling around, mixing with local particulates and whatever residual smoke is still hanging over from distant agricultural burns. If you've got asthma or any other respiratory issues, seriously, skip the outdoor workout. Even if you don't, you might find yourself wheezing a bit more than usual.

### Weather: The Heat is On (or will be)

While the provided current temperature is 28.1°C and "Mainly Clear," this is South Texas in July. The *real* concern isn't the current thermometer reading, but the heat index that will inevitably soar. Forecasts for this week and month have repeatedly warned of "feels like" temperatures hitting 110-115°F (43-46°C) by the afternoon. That's dangerous territory, folks. High humidity coupled with rising temperatures creates a sauna effect that can quickly lead to heat stroke. Don't push it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Index Warning
Even with a "mainly clear" morning, the heat index will be dangerously high later today. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Hydrate *constantly*. This isn't a suggestion; it's a survival guide.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards: Watch Your Step (and your route)

You know how San Antonio always seems to be under construction? Well, it's not letting up. If your route takes you anywhere near **San Pedro Avenue**, expect delays and detours. The VIA Rapid Green Line project is causing intermittent lane and sidewalk closures from the airport all the way downtown. Pedestrians and cyclists, you'll need to stay extra vigilant and possibly reroute to avoid unexpected barriers.

And let's not forget the lovely EF1 tornado that decided to pay a visit just a couple of days ago on July 15th, causing "significant damage" around the UTSA area, The Rim, and nearby neighborhoods. If you're running or cycling through those areas, keep an eye out for lingering debris, downed branches, or uneven surfaces. Some floodwaters from earlier in the week west of town might still impact lesser-traveled paths too.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction & Debris: Plan Ahead
Check your routes. Especially around San Pedro Avenue and the Loop 1604/I-10 interchange (though nightly closures there are more for drivers). Be aware of potential debris in areas hit by the recent tornado, particularly northside neighborhoods.
</AlertBox>

Look, the Riverwalk is pretty, and the trails are calling, but use your head. If you must get your workout in, do it early, stay hydrated, scale back your intensity, and bring your inhaler if you usually need one. Otherwise, today might be a good day for some indoor cross-training.
