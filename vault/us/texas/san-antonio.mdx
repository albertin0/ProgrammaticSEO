## Current Conditions Breakdown

Here's the lowdown on what's messing with your outdoor plans:

### Weather & Heat

It's clear out there right now, which usually screams "go for a run!" But San Antonio in August is a beast. While the morning might feel okay, by the afternoon, you're looking at "feels like" temperatures well into the triple digits. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's a straight-up health risk if you're pushing it. The National Weather Service and local health officials consistently warn about the dangers of heat exhaustion and heat stroke, especially when the heat index climbs above 100°F.

**HIGH HEAT INDEX ALERT:** Afternoon heat index values are forecast to hit 101-103°F. This is prime time for heat-related illnesses. Stay informed and adjust your plans.

Our AQI of 95 for PM2.5 is on the higher end of "Moderate." Couple that with a moderate pollen count, and anyone with allergies or asthma might be feeling the burn a bit more than usual. There's also some talk of lingering Saharan dust and smoke particles, adding to the hazy atmosphere.

### Trail & Park Closures

As mentioned, portions of the Leon Creek Greenway are still under construction. That means detours or simply choosing a different route if your usual involves that section. Also, remember that a section of the Salado Creek Greenway south of Southside Lions Park entrance will close starting August 10th for repairs, so plan for that next week. Playgrounds at several parks, including Brackenridge, Eisenhower, and Elmendorf Lake Park, are also closed for construction.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**TRAIL ALERT:** The Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604 (north of Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Don't ignore closure signs; they're there for your safety, not to annoy you.
