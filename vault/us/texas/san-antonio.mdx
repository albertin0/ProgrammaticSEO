Alright, San Antonio, let's talk about hitting the pavement today. If you're planning an outdoor workout, you've got some thinking to do.

<BulletList>
*   It's another hot, muggy day out there, with the heat index pushing triple digits. Don't be a hero.
*   Air quality is sitting at a moderate level, primarily due to ozone, which is pretty standard for September around here.
*   Construction is *everywhere*, and it's not just a minor inconvenience – it's actively creating hazards for runners and cyclists.
</BulletList>

Today (Monday, September 28th), we're still basking in that glorious mid-90s heat, feeling more like 100°F with the humidity. The sky's a bit overcast, but don't let that fool you into thinking it's cooler. Your lungs will be working extra hard to pull in that dense, warm air. Add in moderate pollen and an AQI of 53 (that's "Moderate" and driven by ozone, surprise, surprise), and you're not exactly breathing mountain air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory (Unofficial, but you know it):** This isn't the day for setting PRs or doing your long run in the middle of the afternoon. Keep it short, hydrate relentlessly, and listen to your body. Heatstroke isn't worth it.
</AlertBox>

Now, let's talk about the obstacle course that is our fair city. If your route takes you anywhere near Harry Wurzbach Road, you need to be on high alert. Crews are tearing up and repaving sections, which means "small cliffs" between road sections and manholes that look more like speed bumps. They're doing night work, too, so even if you're out there early, the daytime result is a minefield of uneven surfaces and confusing lane shifts. That's a surefire way to twist an ankle or take an unplanned header.

And it's not just Harry Wurzbach. The City's own website lists a bunch of ongoing trail closures. For example, a significant portion of the Salado Creek Greenway south of the Southside Lions Park entrance is closed until at least October 9th for repairs. Other sections, like Gembler Road to Houston Street on the same greenway, are shut down indefinitely. Always check for current closures before you head out, because running into a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign 3 miles into your loop is just plain annoying.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos:** Seriously, watch your step, especially on roads like Harry Wurzbach. Uneven surfaces, sudden drops, and poorly marked detours are a recipe for injury. If you're on a trail, assume there could be closures or recent changes. Check the City's Parks and Rec site for specific Greenway updates.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Incoming Flood Threat Mid-Week:** While today is just hot, forecasters are warning of a "definite pattern change" later this week. A cold front combined with Pacific moisture means heavy rainfall and a significant *flash flood risk* (Level 2 to 3 out of 4) for San Antonio starting Wednesday night and continuing into Thursday. If you're planning any runs mid-week, *do not* mess with flooded roads or trails. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase for tourists; it's how you stay alive.
</AlertBox>
