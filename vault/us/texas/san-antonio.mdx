## The Rundown: Don't Be a Mud Monster

Let's cut right to it. We had some decent rain over the Easter weekend, with "downpours, gusty winds and isolated flooding" hitting South-Central Texas through Sunday. What does that mean for your Monday morning or lunchtime run? It means if you venture onto any unpaved trails, you're likely to find mud, debris, and possibly even standing water. The city's own Parks & Recreation department warns to "use caution on trails before/after rain events" and "Don't cross trails that are under water." Seriously, don't be that person. Your running shoes are not designed to be mud-skis, and a sprained ankle isn't worth proving a point to a puddle.

Temperature-wise, it's a cool 12.2°C (54°F) and overcast, which is actually pretty prime running weather if you can avoid the soggy spots. Winds will be out of the northeast at 10 to 15 mph. Grab a light layer and you'll be comfortable.

## Trail & Construction Headaches

Here's where things get a bit more frustrating for us local runners. There's a fair bit of ongoing work around the city that you need to be aware of:

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Mission Reach Trail Maintenance:** Expect "limited trail closures and intermittent work zones" along sections of the Mission Reach. Crews are performing "stem-density removal and related vegetation maintenance" to manage flood risk, which is important but disruptive. This work has been ongoing since October and is expected to continue through spring. Keep an eye out for signage and equipment.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Culebra Creek Greenway Closure:** A trail within Culebra Creek Park that connects to the Culebra Creek Greenway is closed for reconstruction. This closure started in mid-February and is expected to last approximately three months, so it's definitely still in effect. Find an alternate route if this is part of your usual loop.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Park Playgrounds & Facilities:** If you're planning a family outing, be aware that many park playgrounds are closed for construction or replacement. This includes major closures at parks like Southside Lions Park and Elmendorf Lake Park through April or even May. Normoyle Community Center is also closed for maintenance until May.
</AlertBox>

IH35 Southbound is also seeing nightly lane closures from 9:00 PM to 5:00 AM between North Flores Street and IH35 for joint replacement. This won't affect most daytime workouts, but if you're an early bird or late-night warrior, plan for some traffic slowdowns.

So, while the air is clear and the temperature is pleasant, be smart about your route. Stick to paved paths if you're worried about mud, and always pay attention to posted signs on trails. Don't ignore those construction warnings, they're there for a reason.
