The weather gods are mostly smiling on us today, October 5, 2026. With a clear sky and temperatures hovering around a comfortable 25.6°C (that's 78°F for you Celsius-challenged folks), it's a pretty good day to be outside. Feels like fall is *actually* trying to show up, with a cold front bringing cooler nights and lower humidity this week. But don't get too comfortable, afternoon highs could still flirt with the high 80s or even 90 before dropping to the low 70s tonight.

Your lungs can breathe easy, at least from the big stuff. Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is a blissful 27, putting us squarely in the "Good" category despite San Antonio's ongoing issues with ground-level ozone. So no excuses there. Just remember, "Good" doesn't mean "perfect" – Bexar County is still in "Serious Nonattainment" for ozone, so let's all try to do our part year-round.

However, we've got some recurring headaches. Those breezy northerly winds? They're no joke. They'll be persistent, especially this morning, and could make certain sections of your run or ride feel like you're fighting an uphill battle, even on flat ground.

And then there's the perpetual obstacle course known as San Antonio road and trail construction. Multiple major projects are ongoing, impacting popular spots you might usually rely on. The City of San Antonio and TxDOT have more orange barrels than a pumpkin patch, and they're not going anywhere soon.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Leon Creek Greenway!** A significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway, specifically the stretch north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is currently shut down for TxDOT construction. This closure is expected to last until mid-2027. Don't get caught rerouting unexpectedly; plan a different segment or an entirely new route if this is your usual stomping ground. Other trail sections, like the Salado Creek Greenway near Southside Lions Park and between Gembler Road to Houston Street, also have closures, some until approximately October 9, others indefinitely.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Predicament:** With moderate pollen levels, now's the time to pop those antihistamines if you suffer from seasonal allergies. Don't let a runny nose sabotage your long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wind Advisory (Unofficial):** While no official advisory is out, those 25-30 mph gusts can be strong enough to make you wobble on a bike, knock over loose gear, or even stir up dust if you're on a drier trail. Be aware, especially if you're near open areas or bridges.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a solid day for outdoor activity. Just be smart, check local closure maps before you head out, and don't let that wind blow your spirits (or your hat) away.
