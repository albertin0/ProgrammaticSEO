Today's a mixed bag for outdoor workouts in San Antonio – the air is finally decent, but you'll be dodging puddles and detours if you hit the trails.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>

*   **The Good Air, For Once:** Your lungs are getting a break. Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 27, which is "Good." No smog choking you out today, just nice, clear air.
*   **Ankle-Breaker Alert:** Don't let the clear skies fool you. We just got hammered with some severe weather, including heavy rain and even tornado warnings on Tuesday and Wednesday. That means lingering high water and plenty of mud, especially on low-lying trails. Expect slippery surfaces and debris, particularly around Salado Creek and Leon Creek, where high water was still being reported yesterday. Multiple roadways were underwater as recently as Wednesday morning, especially south of the city. Unless you enjoy impromptu mud runs, stick to pavement or be extra cautious.
*   **Construction Cones Are Your Arch Nemesis:** Beyond the natural hazards, our city loves to tear things up. You'll find active construction impacting several of your favorite routes. If your usual grind takes you along the Salado Creek Greenway, be aware that portions are seeing periodic closures for a TXDOT project, with a section from Gembler Road to Houston Street shut down until winter.

</BulletList>

## The Allergy Report

| Pollen Type | Level    |
| :---------- | :------- |
| General     | Moderate |

"Moderate" pollen is a typical San Antonio nuisance. If you're usually bothered, pop an antihistamine and carry tissues. It's not going to derail most people's workout, but don't expect to skip your daily Zyrtec.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Muck!

Recent heavy rainfall means many trails and low-lying areas are still likely muddy, slippery, or even partially flooded. Don't be a hero; if you see standing water on a trail, turn around. "Turn Around, Don't Drown" isn't just for cars. Your ankles (and laundry machine) will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zone Ahead

If you're planning a run or ride along the Salado Creek Greenway, check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation updates before you head out. Significant construction projects are causing closures and detours, particularly affecting the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street. Don't get halfway through your long run only to hit a "Trail Closed" sign. Plan an alternate route or prepare for some unexpected reroutes.
</AlertBox>
