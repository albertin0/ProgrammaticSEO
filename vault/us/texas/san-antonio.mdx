### Today's Rundown

This morning started out muggy and cloudy at a comfortable 21.4°C, which is great if you got out early. But if you're planning an afternoon sweat session, remember that temps are forecast to hit the mid-80s Fahrenheit, about 15 degrees above average for early March. And for those evening runs, keep an eye on the sky: isolated showers and storms are possible, especially in the Hill Country, but San Antonio itself has a 20-30% chance. Some of these could even bring hail and damaging winds, though the severe risk for our immediate area is lower.

Now, for the real buzzkill: the trails. If you thought construction was bad last year, you haven't seen anything.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Widespread Trail Closures & Construction:** Don't plan on an easy run on many of our beloved greenways.
*   The trail in **Culebra Creek Park** that connects to the greenway? Closed for reconstruction for *three months*. Get used to rerouting.
*   A section of the **Leon Creek Greenway** under the Loop 1604 overpass, north of the Valero Trailhead, is also closed due to TxDOT construction.
*   Parts of the **Salado Creek Greenway** leading to Tobin Park Trailhead are intermittently closed for another TxDOT project.
*   **Mission Reach** has ongoing "stem-density maintenance" from now through spring 2026, meaning crews and equipment will be working, leading to "limited trail closures and intermittent work zones." Hope you like dodging.
*   The east bank trail along **San Pedro Creek Culture Park** is temporarily closed in sections for maintenance.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just trails. If you're running downtown, **South Alamo Street** is still a mess with major construction that won't be "substantially complete" until June 2026. Expect detours and general traffic nastiness. Also, **Perrin Beitel Road** is closed for a pedestrian bridge installation, which could affect access to some greenway areas.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Brackenridge Park Trail Lighting Out:** If you're planning an early morning or evening run through Brackenridge, be advised that lighting is out on the north portion, from Josephine Street to just past Highway 281, due to an electrical issue. Repairs are expected by tomorrow, but proceed with caution.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, it's San Antonio. Expect some natural beauty, a lot of concrete dust, and maybe a scattered thunderstorm to keep things interesting.
