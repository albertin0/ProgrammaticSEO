### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deep

Look, the weather's a pleasant 16.4°C (about 61.5°F) and clear, which usually screams "get out there!" But don't let that fool you. The National Weather Service, at the request of the TCEQ, issued an Air Quality Alert for an Ozone Action Day yesterday for the San Antonio metro area, effective today. We're forecasted for Ozone and PM2.5, which aligns perfectly with that "Moderate" AQI of 95. PM2.5 is the nasty stuff that gets deep into your lungs, so if you're sensitive or planning a long, high-intensity session, dial it back. There's no major wildfire smoke threat hovering over us right now like we've seen in the past, but local air quality is still taking a hit from ground-level ozone and particulate matter.

## Construction & Closures: Your Detour Du Jour

Good luck finding a clear path, seriously. The city's current construction seems more widespread than a bad case of cedar fever.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Major Trail & Park Closures to Dodge:
*   **Classen-Steubing Ranch Park:** If you were eyeing the Prickly Pear Path or Sotol Descent, forget it. Trails here are closed through *all of October* for construction. So much for enjoying the fall foliage without navigating heavy equipment.
*   **Elmendorf Lake Park:** The parking lot and 19th Street entrance are shut down until December for dam repairs. Plan your access accordingly, or just find another spot.
*   **Mission Reach:** The San Antonio River Authority reports ongoing "Stem Density efforts" (whatever that means) are temporarily closing trails near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Conception Park. It's always something.
*   **VIA Rapid Green Line Corridor:** Expect intermittent lane and *sidewalk* closures all along this route. Northbound lane closures on Segment 8 are specifically scheduled from yesterday through early December, with particularly fun turn access restrictions today. If your run takes you near that, you're going to be dodging more than just slow walkers.
</AlertBox>

### What About That River Walk Stroll?

Today is also "Downtown Together: The Great Street Takeover" on West Houston Street, shutting down portions between Soledad and North Flores from 10 AM to 10 PM. And don't forget the Día de los Muertos Tradition Trail on the River Walk, which runs until November 2nd. While it's beautiful, that means crowds. If you're trying to hit your pace, you'll be weaving around tourists and alebrijes, which is less "workout" and more "urban obstacle course."
