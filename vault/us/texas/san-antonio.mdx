### Air Quality & Pollen Rundown

Today's air quality is generally in the "Good" range with an AQI of 31, and ozone is the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) even forecasts "Good" ozone levels for San Antonio. That's a win for your lungs. However, don't let that fool you into thinking you're in the clear. San Antonio has historically struggled with ozone pollution, even ranking 20th most polluted in the nation last year. So, while today's numbers are low, it's always something to keep an eye on, especially as the temperatures climb.

### On the Trails Today

The weather's mostly cooperative right now, but a heads-up: showers and thunderstorms are expected to develop tonight and continue through Saturday evening. That means anything you run this afternoon could be a slippery mess by tomorrow.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Easter Crowds & BBQ Smoke:** Many city parks, including **Brackenridge Park**, **McAllister Park**, and **Woodlawn Lake Park**, are open for overnight Easter camping. Expect significantly more people, potential litter (sadly, it happens), and the distinct aroma of barbecue smoke. If you're sensitive to smoke or just want solitude, hit your run early or choose less popular areas. The San Antonio Fire Department is even reminding people about BBQ fire safety, so keep an eye out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Downtown Detour:** If your route takes you downtown, be aware of "The Passion of the Christ" procession from Travis Park to Main Plaza this morning, 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. Portions of Travis to Jefferson streets will be closed. Plan accordingly or enjoy the spectacle.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Zone Ahead:** Several greenway trails are still under construction. The loop walking trail at Lees Creek Park *should* be open as of April 1st, but always proceed with caution. However, the trail within Culebra Creek Park that connects to the Culebra Creek Greenway is still closed for reconstruction for approximately three months, having started in mid-February. There's also ongoing work on the Zarzamora Creek Trail extension, connecting Tierra Del Sol Park to Ingram Road, with completion anticipated in Q3 2026. If you typically run these routes, double-check local signage.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a decent day to get out, but you'll need to be savvy about where and when you run. Embrace the clouds, dodge the crowds if you can, and for crying out loud, watch for slippery spots tonight and tomorrow morning.
