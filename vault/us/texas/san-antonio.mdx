## Today's Outdoor Grind: Proceed with Extreme Caution (or don't)

Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in, soak up that "fresh air." But "fresh" is a strong word for San Antonio today, June 30, 2026. We're staring down a heat index pushing 100 degrees Fahrenheit, thanks to that stubborn heat dome parked over Texas. It's not just warm; it's the kind of oppressive, humid heat that saps your energy before you even hit the first block. Your body will be working overtime just trying to cool itself down, making any strenuous activity a direct line to heat exhaustion or worse.

### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

On top of the sweltering temps, we've got a Saharan dust plume doing its grand tour across South-Central Texas. That hazy sky isn't just a filter for your sunset photos; it's tiny particulate matter messing with your lungs. Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 95, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) says it's "moderate" for most of us, they also acknowledge it could hit "unhealthy for sensitive groups" in some areas. If you're prone to asthma or have any respiratory issues, this dust-laden air is your personal kryptonite. Even if you're not, you'll feel it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Risk: Skip Peak Afternoon Hours
With the "feels like" temperature hovering around 100 degrees, outdoor workouts between 10 AM and 6 PM are highly discouraged. Heatstroke is no joke, and emergency services don't need extra calls for preventable heat illness. Stay hydrated, but honestly, just stay inside.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Mind the Mess

If you're still determined to brave the elements, be aware that our city's infrastructure isn't exactly rolling out the red carpet. Construction is a perennial beast, and it's still impacting several popular spots. A significant chunk of the Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, is shut down until at least mid-2027 for TxDOT projects. And let's not forget the Maverick Creek Trailhead, which has been closed for a couple of weeks for sidewalk and lighting work. Expect detours, uneven surfaces, and a general lack of flow on many routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Construction Closures on Greenways
Ongoing construction, particularly on sections of the Leon Creek Greenway, means detours and potential hazards. Watch your step, be alert for heavy machinery, and don't assume your usual route is clear.
</AlertBox>
