<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Advisory
The "feels like" temperature is projected to climb into the dangerous range today, potentially exceeding 100°F. Heat-related illnesses like exhaustion and stroke are serious. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 2 PM and 7 PM, drink copious amounts of water, and wear light, loose clothing. Take frequent breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas. Ignoring these warnings is how you end up in the ER, not on the podium.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Air Quality & Allergens
While the AQI is "moderate," several factors like Saharan dust, ozone formation from vehicle emissions, and smoke from distant wildfires are contributing to poor air quality for sensitive individuals. If you're prone to allergies or have any respiratory issues, today is not the day for an intense outdoor session. Keep an eye on any symptoms like coughing or throat irritation.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Trail Closures & Detours
Don't be surprised by unexpected detours. The Loop 1604 North Expansion Project continues to cause major closures on Loop 1604 and I-10, mostly overnight, but access roads can still be a mess. More directly impactful for runners, substantial sections of the Leon Creek Greenway are closed due to TxDOT construction, with some closures expected to last well into 2027. Check the City of San Antonio's Parks and Recreation website before heading out to avoid running into a literal dead end on the Salado Creek Greenway or Huebner Creek Greenway. You might be better off sticking to a treadmill or finding a less congested paved path if you absolutely must get out.
</AlertBox>
