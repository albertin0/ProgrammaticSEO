Now for the fun part: actually finding somewhere to run without hitting a barrier. If you were planning a long run on the **Medina River Greenway Trail**, turn around. Six miles of it, from Applewhite to Pleasanton Road, are closed *today*, October 19th, due to maintenance. Just another Monday, right?

And that's not all. If your usual stomping grounds include the **Mission Reach** area, be aware. Trails near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Concepcion Park are temporarily closed for "Stem Density efforts." Whatever that means, it means you can't run there. The Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604 is also partially closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027, so plan your routes accordingly if you're up north.
