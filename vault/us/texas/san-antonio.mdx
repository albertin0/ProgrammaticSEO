## The Rundown

Alright, San Antonio, April 7th is giving us some classic Texas spring vibes – meaning it's already feeling like summer. The 82.6°F (28.1°C) temperature isn't going to break any records, but it's warm enough to remind you that your midday runs are about to become a miserable slog if you don't adjust your schedule. The air itself is actually quite pleasant, with an AQI of 37, so your lungs can breathe easy, even if the rest of you is sweating through your shirt.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail and Road Hazards: Construction Never Sleeps
If you're planning on hitting some of our beautiful greenway trails, be prepared for detours. A portion of the Leon Creek Greenway, specifically under the 1604 overpass north of the Valero Trailhead, is periodically closed throughout April due to TXDOT construction. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on "find another route" situation. Same goes for parts of the Salado Creek Greenway near the Tobin Park Trailhead.

Speaking of construction, the Loop 1604 North Expansion Project is still a beast. TxDOT announced a major, long-term closure of the eastbound Loop 1604 to westbound Loop 1604 U-turn at the I-10 interchange that started March 30th and will last at least two weeks. While this is primarily a traffic alert, it impacts how you *get* to some of our major trailheads. Plan your routes, people, especially if you’re driving your bike to the trails. This project is ongoing through late 2027, so get used to it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration and Heat: Don't Be a Dummy
With temperatures pushing into the low 80s today, and a forecast for increasing humidity later in the week, hydration isn't just a suggestion, it's mandatory. Bring more water than you think you need, especially if you're out there for more than an hour. The forecast shows temperatures continuing to rise, with chances of rain and storms returning by Friday and into the weekend. So, enjoy this mostly clear day, but keep an eye on that longer-term forecast for your weekend plans.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Past Flood Warnings: Stay Vigilant
While the immediate forecast isn't calling for heavy rain, let's not forget last week's storms that battered the Dellview neighborhood with heavy rain and hail. And who could forget the deadly flash floods we saw in June 2025? San Antonio is prone to flash flooding, and while Bexar County is working on better warning systems, they're not fully deployed. Always, *always* turn around, don't drown. If you see standing water, assume it's deeper and faster than it looks.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, runners. Don't be that person.
