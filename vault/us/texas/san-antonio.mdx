## The Rundown: Don't Be That Guy (or Gal) Who Ignores the Signs

Look, I get it. You had a route planned, you're mentally committed, and a little orange sign isn't going to stop you. But today, those orange signs are more like flashing neon warnings. The City of San Antonio's Parks & Facilities updates are full of ongoing closures, and they're not messing around.

A significant portion of the Salado Creek Greenway is closed for repair work near the Southside Lions Park entrance and further south from Gembler Road to Houston Street. And don't even think about running the Leon Creek Greenway north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim; that's closed until mid-2027 for TxDOT construction, with another section near I-10 blocked by a fallen overpass. That's not a detour, that's a whole-ass different run.

And if you're a road runner, East Hildebrand Avenue is a mess. There's a major water main replacement project underway, shutting down sections, including the intersection of Hildebrand and McCullough avenues until Spring 2027. Trying to squeeze past construction on a busy city street is a recipe for a bad day, and frankly, you're going to annoy everyone else more than you're helping your mileage.

### Air Quality & Allergies: Don't Blame the Mesquite Today

Today's AQI is sitting pretty at 32, which is "Good." The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, but it's at low levels. AccuWeather even calls the air quality "ideal for most individuals." So your lungs should be fine.

However, if you're feeling sniffly, it's probably molds. A cold front passed through last night and this morning, and dampness plus north winds can stir things up.
