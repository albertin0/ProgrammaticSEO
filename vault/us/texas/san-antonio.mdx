**Heads Up for Downtown & Trail Runners!**
The San Antonio Beer Festival is kicking off at Hemisfair Civic Park, and the Tequila & Tacos Festival is at the Tower of the Americas today. While these generally won't close trails like the Mission Reach of the River Walk, expect a surge in foot traffic around downtown, especially later in the day. Keep an eye out for distracted tourists and revelers if you're jogging or cycling through those areas.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid Flat Rock Ranch Today!**
If your weekend plans included hitting the technical trails at Flat Rock Ranch, forget it unless you're part of the Ragnar Trail Texas Hill Country race. That event is running all weekend, October 16-17, and the trails will be packed with racers. You don't want to get in their way, and they don't want you there. Plan for another day or find a different trail system.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Layer Up and Stay Hydrated**
Despite the "clear" conditions, that cool air with lingering breezy stretches (even if less intense than yesterday) can still make it feel colder than the thermometer reads. Dress in layers you can shed, and don't skip the water just because it's not scorching. You're still sweating, even if you don't feel it as much.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, listen to your body today. If you're feeling a bit snotty or wheezy, consider a gentler workout indoors. No point in pushing it when the air isn't doing you any favors.
