## Navigating the Urban Jungle & Natural Trails

Let's talk about getting around, because it's a hot mess out there if you're not paying attention. The temperature is a very pleasant 20.5°C (68.9°F) under overcast skies, which is perfect for a run. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's smooth sailing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Closures, Seriously
The city is doing a lot of work, which is great for the long term, but a pain in the asphalt right now. Expect widespread intermittent lane and sidewalk closures, especially around the VIA Rapid Green Line corridor along San Pedro Avenue. This isn't just a "drive carefully" warning; if you're on a bike or trying to pound pavement, you're going to hit detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Troubles: A Laundry List
Many of our beloved greenway trails are experiencing significant closures. The trail within **Culebra Creek Park** connecting to the Culebra Creek Greenway is shut down for reconstruction for roughly three months. Portions of the **Leon Creek Greenway** under the 1604 overpass and the **Salado Creek Greenway** near the Tobin Park Trailhead are also experiencing intermittent closures due to TxDOT projects. Even the loop walking trail at **Lees Creek Park** is closed until the end of March for improvements. Plan your route meticulously, or better yet, have a backup (or two).
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mission Reach Maintenance
If your go-to is the **Mission Reach**, be aware that the San Antonio River Authority is conducting ongoing stem-density maintenance. This means crews and equipment will be active, and you might encounter limited trail closures and temporary work zones. They're trying to keep our river banks healthy and reduce flood risk, so be patient, but stay vigilant.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Fire Weather Watch
Even with the current overcast conditions, the forecast for Sunday (today) into Monday includes a **Fire Weather Watch** for much of South Texas, including us. A cold front is expected to bring increased winds and combine with dry fuels, significantly elevating the fire danger. If you're heading out to any natural areas with dry brush, be extremely cautious. One spark from anything could turn into a serious problem fast.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Park Events & Playground Closures
If you're planning a family outing or trying to run laps in a park, note that **Martin Luther King (MLK) Park** is one of several parks with playground closures for construction until mid-April. Furthermore, MLK Park hosted the **San Antonio Shamrock Shuffle** on Saturday, so while the main event is over, there might still be some lingering setup or cleanup affecting access.
</AlertBox>
