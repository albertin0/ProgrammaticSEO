## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality's a Bummer:** That AQI is sitting at 95 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Moderate," but it's flirting with "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Your lungs might not appreciate it, especially if you're prone to respiratory issues. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality might be calling it "Good" for today, but honestly, with PM2.5 that high, I'd say use your own judgment.
*   **Pollen and Lingering Mud:** Moderate pollen means sniffles and sneezes for a lot of us. Pair that with the scattered light to moderate rain we've seen on and off, and you're looking at some potentially muddy trails and slick spots. Don't go twisting an ankle out there because you thought a little drizzle meant pristine conditions.
*   **Trail Closureapalooza:** Good luck finding a clear stretch of pavement or dirt. Construction projects are still plaguing our greenways. Portions of the Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604 are shut down for TxDOT construction until mid-2027, and don't even think about a smooth run on the Mission Reach of the San Antonio River Walk; parts of the west bank hike and bike trail are closed for erosion repair. Expect detours, frustration, and probably more asphalt than you'd prefer.

</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Breezy Does Not Mean Cool:** While the "mainly clear" skies and 83-degree temps might sound inviting, remember that San Antonio humidity often makes it feel hotter. We're looking at breezy conditions with winds from the north at 15 to 20 mph, and gusts up to 30 mph are possible. That breeze can be deceptive, drying you out faster than you realize. Hydrate, people, and don't mistake a gust of wind for a cool front.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Watch for Water, Seriously:** Just because the major flood warnings from earlier in the week have (mostly) passed doesn't mean you're in the clear. San Antonio was under a "marginal risk for excessive rainfall" today, and recent heavy rains had over 20 low-water crossings closed across Bexar County, including notorious spots like Old O'Connor Road. If you see standing water, *turn around*. It's not worth it. Period.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**River Walk Crowds:** If you're planning a stroll or light jog along the San Antonio River Walk, be aware that the Día de los Muertos Tradition Trail event means there will be special guided river cruises and installations. This isn't a "hazard" in the typical sense, but if you're looking for an uninterrupted workout, you might find yourself dodging tourists.
</AlertBox>
