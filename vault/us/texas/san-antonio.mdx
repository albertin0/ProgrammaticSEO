## The Heat is On, Y'all. Seriously.

Let's be blunt: August in San Antonio is no joke, and today is proof. While the morning might feel deceptively "mild" in the low 80s (28.1°C), don't be fooled. We're looking at daytime highs pushing 100-102°F (38-39°C), with the "feels like" temperature, or heat index, soaring to a truly dangerous 105-110°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's a legitimate health risk, especially for anyone trying to push their limits.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory:** Temperatures will reach dangerous levels today. Avoid outdoor strenuous activity between 10 AM and 7 PM. If you must be outside, go early, go short, and listen to your body. Heatstroke is no joke and can sneak up on you faster than a tourist on the River Walk.
</AlertBox>

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a "Good" 46, which is a rare win for our lungs this time of year. The dominant pollutant is ozone, but at this level, most people shouldn't have issues. You might recall talk of Saharan dust last week, and while it contributed to some "moderate" AQI days then, its impact today seems minimal.

## Trail and Road Woes

If your workout plans involve the Leon Creek Greenway, especially north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, you're out of luck. That section is still shut down for TxDOT construction, and it's not expected to reopen until mid-2027. Parts of the Salado Creek Greenway also have ongoing closures for repair work, including areas south of the Southside Lions Park entrance and between Gembler Road and Houston Street. Always check local parks and recreation updates before you head out.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Greenway Closures:** Sections of Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway remain closed due to construction and repairs. Plan alternate routes or prepare for detours.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if you're trying to walk or cycle downtown, be aware that Commerce Street (between Flores & Laredo) is reduced to one lane from 9 AM to 3 PM today due to UTSA move-in madness. It's not a major trail, but it will affect downtown mobility.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate or Die-drate:** Seriously, drink more water than you think you need, then drink some more. Electrolytes wouldn't hurt either. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or just generally crappy, get inside and cool down.
</AlertBox>
