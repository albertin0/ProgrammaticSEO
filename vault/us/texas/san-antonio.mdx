### Major Workout Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Persistent Rain and Slick Conditions
Today is forecast for ongoing scattered showers and thunderstorms, especially through the morning and again in the afternoon and evening. While San Antonio itself isn't under a flash flood watch right now, parts of South Central Texas were yesterday, and roads closed due to flooding. Those trails are going to be greasy. Expect slippery surfaces, standing water, and enough mud to rebuild a small village. If you see water covering a path, turn your rear end around. It's not worth getting swept away or becoming a local news story.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Construction & Trail Closures
If you're heading to the North Side, prepare for chaos. The ongoing Loop 1604 North Expansion Project means major overnight closures at the I-10 interchange this weekend (starting tonight at 9 PM). This won't just impact drivers; it'll make accessing trailheads in that vicinity a nightmare.

Specifically, if you were planning to run the **Leon Creek Greenway** near Loop 1604 (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim), forget it. It's closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. And a section under I-10 near the Marquis Trailhead is shut down due to a fallen overpass. Yes, a *fallen overpass*.

Also, word from yesterday was that trails at **Government Canyon State Natural Area** were closed due to heavy rain. Given today's forecast, assume they're still a soggy mess. Always check their specific updates before you trek out there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Your Best Bet?
Stick to pavement, and even then, tread carefully. The cooler, overcast weather is otherwise decent for a run, but the incessant rain and wet conditions are no joke for joint stability or general safety. If you absolutely *must* get your miles in, find a track or hit the treadmill. Seriously, your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>
