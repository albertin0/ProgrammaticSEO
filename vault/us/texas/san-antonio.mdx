### Trail Conditions & Other Hazards

This is where things get gnarly. You want to run the Mission Reach? Great, but be ready for some detours and potential mud.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat: It's Not a Game.** The temperature given is just the start. The heat index is what truly matters, and it's dangerously high today. Symptoms of heat exhaustion and heatstroke are serious. Avoid strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day (typically 2-7 p.m.) and hydrate *constantly*. Don't be that person emergency services have to scrape off the trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Mud, Debris, and Overgrowth:** Recent rains have played havoc with park maintenance schedules. This means trails are likely to be muddy in spots, and you might find overgrown vegetation, even with "stickers and thorns," encroaching on paths like those in Confluence Park. Watch your step, especially if you're hitting lesser-used sections of our greenways. The San Antonio River Authority crews are trying, but Mother Nature (and our rain patterns) isn't cooperating.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Everywhere:** Seriously, it feels like the whole city is a construction zone. While the major weekend closures on Loop 1604 at I-10 wrapped up yesterday, the $1.4 billion expansion project is far from over, meaning new disruptions can pop up without much warning. On top of that, the VIA Rapid Green Line construction has San Pedro Avenue in a chokehold, with lane closures and detours that make running or biking nearby an absolute nightmare. Check City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation or TxDOT websites *before* you head out.
</AlertBox>
