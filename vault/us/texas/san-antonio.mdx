<AlertBox type="warning">
### Allergy Alert: Pollen's Out to Get Ya
Cedar season might be mostly behind us, but "moderate" pollen still means a significant amount of airborne nasties are out there. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, you already know the drill: pre-medicate, wear shades, and maybe stick to indoor activities if a runny nose and itchy eyes ruin your flow.
</AlertBox>

Let's talk about that sunshine. With temperatures hitting 28.1°C (about 82.6°F) and clear skies, it's going to feel warm out there, especially after 9 AM. This is just a preview of the weekend, which is expected to climb into the mid-80s and potentially even the low 90s by Sunday. While not officially "extreme heat" *yet*, treat it like it is. You're losing fluids faster than you think.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
You live in San Antonio, you know this. But seriously, with the mercury creeping up, you need more water than you think. Carry a bottle, plan your route around water fountains (and assume half of them are broken), or stash some drinks in your car for when you finish. Don't be that person who cramps up halfway down the Mission Reach because you thought you were tough enough to skip the water.
</AlertBox>

And then there's the perpetual state of "improvement" in this city. If you're heading out to the Mission Reach, be aware that the San Antonio River Authority has ongoing stem-density maintenance through spring 2026. This means temporary closures on trail segments, usually Monday through Friday during the day. You might also hit detours or unexpected obstacles along the Salado Creek Greenway, with various linear greenway projects under construction or planning across town, including an extension near Hardberger Park. Don't be surprised if your usual loop requires a bit of unplanned navigation.
