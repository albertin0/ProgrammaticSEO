The Air Quality Index is hovering in the "Good" range at 44, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. So, fine particulate matter isn't your biggest concern right now, which is a rare win. However, that "Moderate" pollen isn't going to make anyone with seasonal allergies feel great. It’s enough to trigger the usual watery eyes and congestion, making those long runs feel a little heavier than they should.

## On the Ground: Fog, Mud, and More Construction Headaches

Let's talk about the fog, because it's a real factor this morning. At 16.4°C (about 61.5°F), the temperature is mild, but that dense fog clinging to everything is going to make your outdoor adventure less scenic and more like a game of Marco Polo with traffic. Seriously, if you're out on the road or even a busy section of trail, assume no one can see you. Wear bright colors, bring lights, and for the love of all that is holy, take off those noise-canceling headphones when you're crossing streets.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Visibility is Low:** With morning fog predicted to stick around, wear reflective gear, use headlamps or bike lights, and be extra cautious, especially near roads or intersections.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the ground itself. Yesterday saw scattered showers and even some street flooding on the East Side, so don't expect pristine conditions. Trails, especially in low-lying areas or those known to hold water, are likely slick, muddy, or have standing puddles. This means a higher risk of twisted ankles, falls, and generally disgusting shoes.

### Trail Closures: The Usual Suspects

Now for the real fun: construction. It seems like the city's trying to pave over every natural surface we have, and the Greenway system is currently a minefield of closures. A significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway** under the 1604 overpass is shut down through April due to TxDOT work – that's about six miles out of commission. And don't even think about the Tobin Park Trailhead, because a portion of the **Salado Creek Greenway** leading to it is also intermittently closed. Over on the West Side, the trail within **Culebra Creek Park** is undergoing reconstruction for the next three months. So, if your usual route takes you through any of these spots, you're out of luck. Plan your detours now, or better yet, just embrace the fact that San Antonio views construction as a permanent fixture.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Closures!** Portions of the Leon Creek Greenway (under 1604), Salado Creek Greenway (near Tobin Park Trailhead), and Culebra Creek Park trails are closed due to ongoing construction. Check city parks and recreation updates before you head out.
</AlertBox>

Downtown isn't spared either. The massive S. Alamo Street project is still chugging along, with concrete pouring scheduled today between La Villita and Nueva. While it's mainly a vehicle/pedestrian disruption rather than a running hazard, it means the area around iconic spots like Hemisfair will be a mess if you're trying to integrate some downtown miles.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider an Indoor Backup:** With the fog and trail closures, today might be a good day to hit the treadmill or a gym class. Your lungs and joints (and shoes) will thank you.
</AlertBox>
