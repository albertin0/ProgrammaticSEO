## Why today is *not* the day for a PR

<BulletList>
*   **It's a sauna out there.** The temperature might *start* at 26.9°C (80°F), but expect afternoon highs to hit a scorching 98°F, with a heat index that'll make it feel like 103°F. This isn't "sweat a little," this is "boil your brain" territory.
*   **Your lungs get a moderate workout just breathing.** The Air Quality Index is sitting at 54, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While technically "moderate," some sources are calling it "unhealthy for sensitive groups." Combine that with moderate pollen, and if you've got any kind of respiratory issues, you're rolling the dice.
*   **Construction is still eating our trails.** Don't even think about a long run on the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail or the loop at **Garza Park**. Most of that gem is shut down until January 2027 for upgrades. Same goes for parts of Classen-Steubing Ranch Park and the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604. Your grand plans for a scenic route are likely a construction detour.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Danger: Extreme Heat!
Seriously, that 103°F heat index is no joke. Heat stroke is a real, life-threatening risk. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job. But really, just don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality Advisory for Sensitive Folks
With an AQI of 54 and PM2.5 as the main culprit, if you're asthmatic, have heart conditions, or are just generally not a fan of feeling like you ran through a dusty attic, consider moving your workout indoors. Better safe than wheezing your way through the rest of the week.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route Carefully
Before you head out, check the City of San Antonio's parks and recreation website for current trail closures. There's a lot of essential infrastructure work happening right now, and running into a "Trail Closed" sign halfway through your long run is a quick way to kill your motivation.
</AlertBox>

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next guy, but today is a day to prioritize common sense over PRs. Find a gym with AC, hit the pool, or tackle that home workout you've been putting off. Your lungs, joints, and sanity will thank you.
