## The Weather Rollercoaster Is About to Hit

Today, Thursday, October 15, you're looking at a decent 24.6°C (about 76°F) and partly cloudy skies. It's the kind of San Antonio fall day that tricks you into thinking it'll last. Don't fall for it. Meteorologists are calling this "one more hot day" before "big changes" arrive.

Starting tonight, especially around early Friday morning, a strong cold front is expected to sweep through. Expect a 30% chance of showers between 3 a.m. and 10 a.m. Friday, and prepare for some seriously gusty north winds, with speeds up to 40 mph in the city and potentially 45 mph in the Hill Country. Friday's highs will struggle to hit 70°F, and Saturday morning? We're talking temps near 50°F in San Antonio, dropping into the 40s in the Hill Country, with wind chills that'll feel like the 30s. So, if you're hitting the pavement today, relish it, because tomorrow is going to be a completely different beast.

## Trail Closures: The San Antonio Obstacle Course

Let's be real, navigating San Antonio's trails without hitting a closure sign is becoming an Olympic sport. You want to run the Mission Reach? Parts of it are temporarily closed near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Conception Park for "Stem Density efforts." Whatever that means, it means you're rerouting.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Widespread Trail Closures Ahead!**
Seriously, folks, plan your routes. Many popular spots are currently impacted by long-term construction or maintenance. A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, is closed until mid-2027 due to TxDOT construction, and there's even a section near I-10 closed from a fallen overpass. You'll find similar headaches on parts of the **Salado Creek Greenway** and even at **Classen-Steubing Ranch Park**, where trails like Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent are shut down through October. Don't be surprised if your usual loop is suddenly blocked off.
</AlertBox>
