Look, I love hitting the Mission Reach as much as the next runner, but today is not the day to try and set a personal best down by the San Antonio River. The sun is out, the humidity is up, and your body is going to be screaming at you before you even hit the 5K mark. We've been under heat advisories recently, and while AccuWeather might say there are "no active weather warnings" *right now*, the daily forecast for San Antonio for *today* is explicitly calling for heat index values in the low 100s. Trust me, your internal thermometer is going to agree with the local news on this one, not some generic weather app.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: This Ain't No Joke!
A projected heat index of 99-102°F for today means real danger for outdoor activity. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious. Limit strenuous outdoor exercise to the very early morning or skip it entirely. Hydration is critical, but water alone won't save you from prolonged exposure to this kind of "feels like" temperature.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the heat, if you absolutely must get out, be aware that many of our beloved greenway trails are currently a construction zone obstacle course. **Garza Park** on the Westside is mostly closed until January for a major trail rebuild. Over on the **Salado Creek Greenway**, parts are shut down near the Southside Lions Park entrance and by the Tobin Park Trailhead due to various projects and repairs. The **Leon Creek Greenway** also has a significant closure near Loop 1604, impacting the stretch north of the Valero Trailhead. Trying to navigate these closures in extreme heat? That's just asking for trouble.

### Your Best Bet: Embrace the Indoors (or the Pool)
Seriously, today is a fantastic day for an indoor workout. Hit the gym, find an online yoga class, or just go for a swim. If you're determined to be outside, stick to shaded areas, keep it short, and hydrate aggressively. Pay attention to your body; if you feel lightheaded, nauseous, or excessively tired, get inside immediately.
