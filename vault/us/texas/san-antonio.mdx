## Today's Vibe: Oven with a Side of Allergies

That crisp 27.1°C (80.8°F) you might be seeing on your weather app this morning? Yeah, that's just the overnight low. Don't let it fool you. The actual forecast for San Antonio today, August 24, 2026, is a brutal 100°F+ (37.8°C+) heat wave that isn't letting up until at least Wednesday. We're under a full-blown Heat Advisory for Bexar County, meaning heat indices could hit 110°F (43.3°C). If you're planning anything strenuous outside, you might as well bake yourself a casserole while you're at it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: This is NOT a Drill
Seriously, people. Strenuous outdoor activity during peak sun hours (roughly 10 AM to 7 PM) is a recipe for heat stroke. Keep your runs to before the sun fully commits or after it's dipped. Even then, hydrate like your life depends on it, because it just might.
</AlertBox>

On top of the heat, your lungs aren't getting a break either. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 54 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. What does that mean for your morning jog? You'll be breathing in fine particulate matter, which isn't exactly a performance enhancer. And for those of you with seasonal sniffles, moderate pollen levels are ready to join the party.

### Getting Around (or Not)

Traffic's still a headache thanks to TxDOT's ongoing Loop 1604 North Expansion Project. Expect delays if you're trying to navigate the northwest side. The major closures at the Loop 1604 and I-10 interchange, near The Rim, were scheduled to wrap up at 5 AM this morning, but delays happen, especially with beam setting and bridge demolition. More importantly for us runners, a significant chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is closed until mid-2027. Don't plan your long run through there. And check the City Parks & Rec website for other random trail and park closures due to repairs and maintenance.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Detours
Portions of the Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway are currently closed due to TxDOT construction and other maintenance. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation website for specific closures before you head out, or you'll be hitting an unexpected dead end in this heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Your Best Friend
With temperatures soaring, water isn't enough. Replenish your electrolytes. Think about carrying a water bottle with an electrolyte tab or bringing a sports drink if you insist on pushing it. And seriously, listen to your body – it's not trying to trick you.
</AlertBox>
