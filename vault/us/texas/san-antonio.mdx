### What's Up With the Trails?

Remember last week's deluge? The aftermath is still with us. Several trails, like parts of the Salado Creek Greenway, experienced significant flooding, leading to closures and debris. While many roads have reopened, trail conditions can remain iffy. The City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation department specifically warns to use caution on trails after rain, avoiding those under water and watching for mud and debris. Also, good luck running the Oxbow Trail along the Museum Reach; it's closed until May 1st for project work. And don't even get me started on the ongoing TXDOT construction affecting parts of the Leon Creek Greenway near the 1604 overpass, causing periodic closures. Plan alternative routes, or better yet, hit the gym today.

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Trail Conditions Update
