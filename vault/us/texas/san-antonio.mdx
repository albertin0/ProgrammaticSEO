## The Rundown: San Antonio Outdoors, May 31, 2026

Look, I'm a PT who runs the same damn roads and trails you do, so I'll shoot straight: today isn't bad for a workout in San Antonio, but it's not without its usual local quirks. The good news? Our Lungs & Joints Score is a solid 7 today. The air quality index is a lovely 24, which means your lungs won't feel like you're sucking down exhaust from a Fiesta float, even with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant. And at 23.2°C (around 74°F) and overcast, it's not the usual oven-blast or humidity soup we get here. The "moderate" pollen is almost a relief, right?

But this is San Antonio, where nothing is ever *quite* perfect.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Construction Chaos Continues**: If you're planning a long run or ride, know that our beloved city is basically one giant construction zone.
</AlertBox>

TxDOT is still tearing up major thoroughfares like the US 90 Expansion and the Loop 1604 North Expansion. While a lot of the heavy lane closures are overnight, you'll still be dealing with shifted traffic patterns and construction noise during the day. Don't expect a serene, uninterrupted experience on those routes.

And for those of you who prefer greenways to asphalt, be aware: the **Salado Creek Greenway** near Tobin Park Trailhead is experiencing periodic closures thanks to TxDOT's work. A section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is completely shut down until winter 2026 for maintenance. Same goes for a section around Tom Slick Park. So if those are your usual stomping grounds, you might be detoured into some less-than-ideal terrain or, worse, forced to share space with oblivious drivers. Always, *always* check for local park and trail updates before you head out.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Wet Weather Woes Lingering**: We've had a lot of rain lately, and even though today is clear, the ground is still saturated. Expect muddy patches, especially on unpaved trails or low-lying areas. Your pristine white running shoes? They're about to become "off-road beige."
</AlertBox>

The forecast indicates tropical moisture is coming back next week, meaning more scattered downpours are on the horizon. So enjoy this break, but remember that the ground is primed for more slop.

So, get out there. The air is good, the temperature is cooperative, and the pollen isn't actively trying to kill you. Just be smart about where you go. If you're heading to a park with playgrounds, like Brackenridge Park or Elmendorf Lake Park, know that some facilities are closed for repairs or replacement. Stick to established routes, keep an eye on those TxDOT signs, and don't be surprised if your "scenic" run includes the dulcet tones of a jackhammer.
