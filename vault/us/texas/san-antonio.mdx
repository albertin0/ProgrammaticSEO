## The Heat is No Joke

Look, I get it, you want to get your miles in. But with San Antonio staring down another day of "feels like" temperatures pushing well into the triple digits, you need to be smart, or you'll end up my next PT patient. While the air temperature might be sitting at a somewhat deceptive 27°C (around 80°F) with partly cloudy skies right now, don't let that fool you. The humidity is back, and the heat index is forecasted to hit 103-104 degrees today, with some areas possibly feeling like 105°F or higher. We've been under a Heat Advisory for Bexar and surrounding counties, and that extends through the weekend for the metro area. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about heat exhaustion and stroke. Even Texas schools are now mandated to use Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) readings to modify or cancel outdoor athletic practices, a clear indicator of how serious this ongoing heatwave is for anyone exerting themselves outside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk!
Reschedule strenuous outdoor activity for the *absolute earliest* morning hours or move it indoors. Drink water constantly, even if you don't feel thirsty, and wear light, loose-fitting clothing. Your body is under immense stress just existing out there right now.
</AlertBox>

## What's in the Air?

Beyond the sauna-like conditions, the air itself isn't doing you any favors. Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is at a moderate 53, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. While the current real-time ozone levels might be "good," they've been fluctuating, and historically, ozone often reaches moderate levels in San Antonio, especially with sunlight cooking precursor pollutants. Plus, we've had reports of a "haziness or a milky-looking sky" from suspended smoke particles and a light plume of Saharan dust, which certainly won't help your respiratory system, particularly if you're prone to issues.

### CAUTION: Trail Closures and Construction Ahead
Multiple sections of our beloved Greenway Trail System are currently impacted. A significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway, specifically the stretch north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is closed for ongoing TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Additionally, the Maverick Creek Trailhead is temporarily closed for sidewalk and lighting work, and parts of the Salado Creek Greenway are shut down for repairs and maintenance. Expect detours and potential hazards if you're navigating these areas.

Always check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website or local news for the absolute latest on trail closures and construction before you head out.
