## Trail & Road Conditions: Mind the Muck and the Machines

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Watch Your Step: Muddy Trails Ahead!**
While the rain chances for Halloween itself are low by midday, our trails soaked up a good 2-2.5 inches of rain over the last couple of days. Expect plenty of mud and slick spots, especially on unpaved surfaces. Parks advises extreme caution on trails after rain, reminding us *not* to cross any standing water. You hit the Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604? Expect ongoing closures due to TxDOT's long-term construction project. And don't even think about the Prickly Pear Path or Sotol Descent trails at Classen-Steubing Ranch Park – they're closed for construction all month.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Heat & Humidity: Not Exactly Fall Weather**
That 81°F high for Halloween, coupled with unseasonably muggy conditions, is going to make anything more than a leisurely stroll feel like a slog. This isn't just about comfort; it's about real heat exhaustion risk. Plan your workouts for early morning or late evening if you can.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Downtown Street Closures for Afternoon March**
If you typically pound the pavement downtown, be aware that a "Let's End Homelessness March" is scheduled for 3-5:30 p.m. today, starting at City Hall and ending at San Fernando Cathedral. Expect rolling street closures along its route. Check local traffic advisories before you head out, or just stick to routes away from the city center during those hours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Stay Hydrated, People!**
Even with decent air quality, the heat and humidity demand extra hydration. Drink more water than you think you need, before, during, and after your workout. And for the love of all that is holy, tell someone where you're going if you're hitting those potentially muddy trails solo.
</AlertBox>

Enjoy your Halloween, San Antonio, but don't let it trick you into an unsafe workout.
