## The Good News (and why you should still be careful)

Let's be real, San Antonio weather in March can be a fickle beast. We've had some gnarly storms recently, with warnings for hail and damaging winds just last week, but today? We hit the jackpot. We're looking at a glorious 15.5°C (that's about 60°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheitland), clear skies, and an Air Quality Index (AQI) of 49. Good stuff for your lungs, folks, and definitely makes those miles feel a little easier.

## Your Workout, Brought To You By TxDOT (and SARA, and VIA...)

Alright, here's where my cynical PT brain kicks in. While the skies are clear, the ground beneath your feet might be anything but. San Antonio is currently a giant obstacle course, and it's not just the usual potholes.

### Construction Chaos

You'd think they'd coordinate these things, but apparently, that's too much to ask.
<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Road & Trail Construction:** Be prepared for detours. The I-35 NEX South Project is causing intermittent lane closures, especially near the Frost Bank Center, and that's just the tip of the iceberg. If you're running near downtown, the VIA Rapid Green Line construction along San Pedro Avenue means sidewalk and lane closures, impacting both runners and cyclists. Even the Loop 1604 and Blanco Road area has a new Diverging Diamond Interchange causing traffic shifts and limited pedestrian access. Look alive out there!
</AlertBox>

And it's not just the roads. Our beloved greenways are taking a hit too.
<AlertBox type="danger">
**Greenway Closures & Maintenance:** If your usual route involves the San Antonio River Authority trails, know that the Mission Reach is undergoing "stem-density maintenance" with crews working weekdays, which means temporary trail segment closures through spring. The San Pedro Creek Culture Park's east bank trail is also partially closed. Further out, Lees Creek Park has its loop walking trail closed for improvements until the end of March, and parts of the Leon Creek Greenway under the 1604 overpass are closed through April. The Salado Creek Greenway near the Tobin Park Trailhead also has intermittent closures. Don't assume your go-to trail is open; check local updates before you head out, or you'll be doing a lot of unexpected curb-hopping.
</AlertBox>

### Event Blockages

Today's Saturday, and San Antonio loves an event.
<AlertBox type="info">
**Local Events Today:** If you planned on hitting **Martin Luther King Park** this morning, you might be sharing the space with the San Antonio Shamrock Shuffle 5K, which kicked off at 8:30 AM. Later this evening, the St. Patrick's Day River Parade is happening on the Riverwalk from 5 PM to 9 PM, which means downtown Riverwalk congestion, not ideal for a leisurely cool-down. San Pedro Springs Park is also hosting a "Nature In Our Neighborhoods" event until noon. Plan accordingly to avoid crowds or unexpected diversions.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the beautiful weather, San Antonio. Just make sure you're paying more attention to what's directly in front of you than your split times, or you might end up in a ditch or a traffic jam.
