## Get Out, But Be Smart

The morning looks like prime time for a run or walk, especially if you get out there before the sun really starts doing its thing. However, you've got a few things to contend with:

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ozone Alert in Effect!

Bexar County and surrounding areas are under an Air Quality Alert for ozone until 7 PM CDT. This means that while the AQI might look "Good" early on, ground-level ozone is expected to rise to unhealthy levels for sensitive groups like children, older adults, and people with lung conditions. Plan your outdoor activities for earlier in the day to avoid the worst of it, and listen to your body if you feel any respiratory discomfort.
</AlertBox>

By late morning and afternoon, it’s going to get legitimately hot. That "feels like" temperature is already in the mid-70s at 4 AM, and it's only going up. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because in San Antonio, it often does. Save your long runs for cooler days or move them indoors.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch for Rising Heat

Temperatures are forecasted to hit 91°F later today. This isn't just "warm," it's "set yourself up for heat exhaustion" territory if you're not careful. If you must be outside, go early, stick to shaded routes like some sections of the Salado Creek Greenway, and carry more water than you think you need. Seriously, overdo the hydration.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of trails, if your usual route takes you through **Brackenridge Park**, be advised that the lighting along the north portion of the trail, from the Josephine Street inlet past Highway 281, is currently out due to an electrical issue. They're working on it, with repairs expected by tomorrow evening, but if you're out there near dusk or dawn, keep an eye out and consider an alternative. Also, watch for temporary closures on the east bank trail of **San Pedro Creek Culture Park** due to ongoing construction between Cameron Street and other access points. Always check local park and trail websites for the latest closure info before you head out.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction and Trail Tips

Expect intermittent lane and sidewalk closures across the city due to ongoing projects like the VIA Rapid Green Line corridor work and various TxDOT improvements. If you're running on city streets, be extra vigilant around construction zones. For trail runners, always check for specific trail advisories, as sections of popular greenways can be affected. The section of Salado Creek Greenway from Gembler Road to Houston Street, for instance, is closed until Winter 2026 for maintenance. Don't be that runner who ignores the signs and ends up rerouting mid-workout.
</AlertBox>
