### Weather & Air Quality Rundown

You're looking at a daytime high around 94°F (34.4°C), but that's just the number on the thermometer. With the humidity we've been soaking in, the "feels like" temperature is hitting the triple digits. This oppressive heat isn't just a June fluke; it's sticking around, and an Extreme Heat Warning was even issued for the area recently with feel-like temps up to 115°F. Even though the National Weather Service isn't flagging an official Air Quality Alert for San Antonio right now, our AQI is currently 64, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. AccuWeather's saying the air quality is "Poor" for sensitive groups, so if you're prone to breathing issues, consider this your warning. We've also got some hazy skies thanks to Saharan dust, but thankfully, the impact on air quality from that should be low to moderate.

### Trail Conditions & Closures

Don't even think about just winging it on your favorite paths. We've got river flood warnings still in effect across South Central Texas, especially for downstream sections like the San Antonio River. This means some parks and trails are straight-up closed. Jackson Nature Park, River Crossing Park, Graytown, and Helton are all shut down due to recent flooding from Cibolo Creek and other areas.

On top of the water issues, construction crews are still making a mess of things. A big chunk of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, is closed until mid-2027 for TxDOT work. The Salado Creek Greenway near Tobin Park Trailhead also has periodic closures, and the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is closed indefinitely. Oh, and if you frequent the Maverick Creek Trailhead, that's still closed for another week or so for sidewalk and lighting work. Plus, VIA's Rapid Green Line construction along San Pedro Avenue means intermittent lane and sidewalk closures. Always check the City of San Antonio's Parks & Trails notices before you head out, unless you enjoy running into orange cones and "Trail Closed" signs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### WARNING: Trail Closures & Flooding
Expect significant closures, especially along river-adjacent trails. Parts of the Leon Creek Greenway and Salado Creek Greenway are impacted by long-term construction or recent flooding. Don't risk it by ignoring "Closed" signs; water can still be high, and debris or structural damage might not be visible.
</AlertBox>
