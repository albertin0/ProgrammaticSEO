*   **Morning Delight, Afternoon Fight:** While it's a lovely 23.4°C (that's about 74°F for those still using imperial) right now, don't forget it's still July in South Texas. Expect those temps to climb into the upper 90s, possibly even touching 100°F later today, with the humidity making it feel like you're running through soup. Your 5 AM run might be glorious, but mid-afternoon? That's a different beast.
*   **Construction Chaos Continues:** Our beloved greenway trails are still a patchwork of closures and detours. And don't even get me started on the Salado Creek Greenway near McAllister Park, where a detour is *supposed* to be in place soon due to Wurzbach Parkway construction, but "mid-July" could mean anything in government speak.

### Trail Talk: Leon Creek Greenway Headaches

If the Leon Creek Greenway is your go-to, be prepared for some rerouting. The ongoing TxDOT construction north of the Valero Trailhead means you'll hit a wall if you aren't paying attention. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on closure that forces you onto less-than-ideal surfaces or makes you turn back. Plan your route, or find another spot.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead: Watch Your Step!** Several parks and trails are undergoing renovations. Beyond the major greenway closures, you'll find playgrounds and even parking lots at places like Elmendorf Lake Park and Tom Slick Park are closed for various projects. Stay aware, respect posted signs, and don't try to sneak through closed areas. It's not worth twisting an ankle on some rebar.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Don't Mess with Texas Heat (or Saharan Dust, Even Light)** While the Saharan dust from earlier in the week was thankfully light, the real danger today, and for the foreseeable future, is the heat. The temperature provided is a good start, but forecasts for July consistently point to "above-average temperatures" for Texas. Just because it's clear doesn't mean it's cool. Listen to your body. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or lightheaded, stop immediately. Heatstroke isn't a badge of honor; it's an emergency.
</AlertBox>
