### Don't Be That Runner...

We've seen some brutal heat lately, and even with the current cloud cover, the afternoon temperatures will climb. Hydration isn't optional; it's survival. And let's talk about the air – just because there isn't an "Ozone Action Day" declared for San Antonio by the NWS doesn't mean your lungs aren't working harder. Ozone damages lung tissue, period.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone Outlook is Sketchy:** While the official AQI is "Moderate" (56), conflicting reports show some areas of San Antonio potentially reaching "Unhealthy" levels. If you feel any chest pain, shortness of breath, or wheezing, especially if you have respiratory issues, cut your workout short or take it indoors.
</AlertBox>
