## The Rundown for March 21, 2026

It's currently a pleasant 19.4°C (66.9°F) with clear skies, which sounds idyllic if you're still debating that second cup of coffee. However, San Antonio is slated to hit the low 90s Fahrenheit later today, making those midday miles a truly brutal endeavor. Seriously, if you're not out there by 8 AM, you might as well stay home and hydrate. This isn't your grandma's mild March morning; it's a preview of summer's sweaty embrace.

The good news for your respiratory system is that our Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 36, which is fantastic. The dominant pollutant is Ozone/Other, but at this level, you won't even notice. We've had reports of a smoldering mulch fire in southwest Bexar County near Nelson Road, but fire officials have stated the "light smoke continues intermittently" and does not pose a hazardous condition to the general area or air quality. So, no wildfire smoke choking your lungs today, thank goodness.

However, the high temperatures combined with dry conditions across the region mean fire danger is still a concern, particularly west of I-35. If you're out running in the more rural edges of our fair city, be mindful and avoid anything that could spark a brush fire. We've seen enough of those, thanks.
