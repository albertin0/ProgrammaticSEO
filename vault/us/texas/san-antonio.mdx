### What to Watch Out For

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Brutal Heat Index Approaching
Forget the thermometer, pay attention to the "feels like" temperature. Today, that's pushing into the triple digits, potentially hitting 108°F. The City of San Antonio and health experts are reminding everyone to avoid strenuous activity between 2 PM and 7 PM. If you absolutely *must* get out there, go early, hydrate constantly, and don't push it. Heat stroke is no joke, and we're officially in the "warmest week of the year" territory. This is especially critical for student-athletes whose fall practices are starting.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Moderate Air Quality for Everyone, Not Just "Sensitive Groups"
While the official alert level might be moderate, an AQI of 95, largely due to fine particulate matter, means you *will* feel it if you're working hard outside. You might not get an official "Ozone Action Day" alert from TCEQ today, but that doesn't mean the air is crisp. For sensitive individuals, this could mean minor to moderate symptoms from prolonged exposure. Keep your run short or move it indoors.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Trail & Road Construction Chaos
If your usual escape route is the Salado Creek Greenway, you're in for a rude awakening. Portions south of the Southside Lions Park entrance are completely closed until early October for repair work. And don't even get me started on the ongoing TxDOT projects. The Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604 is closed north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim until mid-2027. Plus, the VIA Rapid Green Line construction means intermittent lane and sidewalk closures all over San Pedro Avenue. This isn't just annoying for drivers; it makes things a headache for runners and cyclists trying to navigate. Plan your routes, then plan your detours, and then probably plan for more detours.
</AlertBox>
