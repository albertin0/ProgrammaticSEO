## Your Joints & Pathways: Construction Chaos and Coming Heat

The temperate 15.1°C (around 59°F) this morning is a trap. By this afternoon, we're hitting 83°F, and the forecast for the weekend is pushing into the low 90s. This is March, people, not July. That quick jump means your body has zero time to adapt, increasing your risk of heat exhaustion, even if you feel fine initially. Stay hydrated, and seriously, don't try to PR in this escalating furnace.

And if you're hitting the trails, prepare for detours. Multiple sections are closed or undergoing maintenance. Don't assume your path is clear.
