If you're looking to hit the trails today, San Antonio, you might want to reconsider or at least dial it way back.

<LungsJointsScore score={3} />

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality is a Dumpster Fire (for your lungs):** While the official AQI is sitting at a "Moderate" 95, AccuWeather is calling it "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups" due to dominant PM2.5 and PM10. Light morning winds and a touch of Saharan dust aren't doing us any favors, and let's not forget that grass fire in south Bexar County yesterday. Your lungs are going to be working overtime.
*   **Heat Index is No Joke:** That 28.1°C (82.6°F) "Mainly Clear" forecast might look innocent, but the "feels like" temperature is reportedly hitting a blistering 109°F today. Even early, the heat and humidity are a serious performance killer and a risk for heat-related illness.
*   **Trail Closures Galore:** Forget a seamless long run. Multiple popular greenways are borked. The Leon Creek Greenway from the Valero Trailhead north to The Rim is still under construction until mid-2027, and apparently, it's getting additional closures this weekend for utilities. The Riverwalk trail between Mission Road and Roosevelt Road is also blocked off because of "bushwhacking" that's deemed "too dangerous."

</BulletList>

Look, I get it. We love our city's trails. But today isn't the day to push it, especially if you've got any respiratory issues. Between the crud in the air and the fact that you can't even get a clean run on the **Leon Creek Greenway** without hitting a construction zone or an impromptu detour, you're better off staying indoors or sticking to very light activity in an air-conditioned gym. Seriously, do you want to run into a closed-off section of the Riverwalk trail because someone's "bushwhacking"? No, you don't.

Hydration will be a full-time job, and any strenuous activity will tax your system like crazy. Save the heroics for a cooler day, which, let's be real, might not be until November in this city.
