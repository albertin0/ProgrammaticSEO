Look, I know you're itching to hit the trails, but today is not the day to channel your inner superhuman. We've got a triple threat of truly miserable conditions out there. First off, the heat index is just plain nasty. Your body thinks it's 115 degrees out there, and that's a recipe for heatstroke faster than you can say "hydration vest." Seriously, if you're out there pushing it, you're flirting with heat exhaustion.

Then there's the rain. "Mainly Clear" might sound nice, but the NWS is forecasting scattered thunderstorms and a flood watch for much of South Central Texas, including Bexar County, through tonight. Areas to our west are looking at *catastrophic* flash flooding. Even if your specific street isn't underwater right now, dozens of low-water crossings are already barricaded. The San Antonio River Authority even closed lower sections of River Crossing Park due to flooding. Thinking about a run along the Leon Creek Greenway? Parts of it are *still* closed for TxDOT construction near Loop 1604 until mid-2027, and any open sections are likely a muddy, possibly flooded mess.

And let's not forget yesterday's tornado on the Northwest Side. That thing caused real damage around the UTSA area, with downed trees and debris scattered. Cleanup crews are still at it. This isn't just about avoiding a little puddle; it's about life-threatening conditions and actual physical hazards.
