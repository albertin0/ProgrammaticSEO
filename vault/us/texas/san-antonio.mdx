## Current Workout Hazards

San Antonio, bless its heart, is always building something. And that something often gets in the way of your something.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Construction, Construction, Everywhere!**
Prepare for detours, folks. The San Antonio River Authority (SARA) has the **Mission Reach** under "stem-density maintenance" through spring 2026. That means crews are out there clearing vegetation to manage flood risk, which can lead to intermittent trail closures and work zones. Don't be surprised if your usual serene stretch along the river suddenly becomes a hard-hat area.

Even worse, parts of the **San Pedro Creek Culture Park's** east bank trail are temporarily closed for construction between Cameron Street and access points near West Houston Street. And if you're out on the greenways, expect trouble near Loop 1604. A portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** under the 1604 overpass, north of the Valero Trailhead, is closed through April due to TxDOT construction. The same goes for sections of the **Salado Creek Greenway** leading to the Tobin Park Trailhead. It's like a city-wide scavenger hunt to find an uninterrupted trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Pro-Tip: Check Before You Go**
Seriously, don't just assume your favorite route is clear. Between SARA's ongoing work on the Mission Reach and various TxDOT projects affecting the Greenways and major roadways like Loop 1604, it's a minefield out there. Look up specific trail conditions on city or county park websites before you head out, or you'll be adding unscheduled cross-training (aka hopping fences or backtracking) to your run. The VIA Rapid Green Line construction along San Pedro Avenue means even sidewalk runs might get interrupted with closures.
</AlertBox>
