## The Heat is On (But No Official "Warning," Yet)

Alright, San Antonio. While we aren't under any *official* extreme heat warnings today, July 2nd, don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. The mercury is still climbing towards a high of 96°F (36°C) with moderate humidity. That's plenty hot enough to cause problems if you're not smart about your outdoor activity. Remember how quickly those "feels like" temps can jump a few weeks ago? It doesn't take much for our South Texas summer to go from "warm" to "oven."

## Trail Troubles: Leon Creek Greenway & Beyond

If your usual route involves one of our beloved greenways, prepare for some frustration. TxDOT and other projects are making a mess of things. A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** is completely shut down for construction, and it's staying that way until at least mid-2027. That's a long time to reroute your runs, folks.

And it's not just Leon Creek. The **Maverick Creek Trailhead** was slated for a two-week closure starting early June, so it might just be reopening, but expect lingering issues or incomplete work. Even the **Tobin Park Trailhead** section of the Salado Creek Greenway is experiencing periodic closures due to TxDOT projects.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Construction Zones are NOT Playgrounds:** Seriously, don't try to sneak through closed sections of the greenway, especially near the Loop 1604 expansion or other TxDOT work. There are heavy machines, uneven terrain, and very real hazards that aren't worth shaving a mile off your run. Just turn around.
</AlertBox>

And remember, we've had some significant rain in the last month that caused serious flooding, even if the immediate warnings are gone now. Trails might still be muddy, uneven, or have debris. So, eyes up, pay attention, and don't assume every path is clear just because the sun's out.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Before You Go:** Before heading out to any of the City's parks or greenways, do yourself a favor and check the San Antonio Parks & Recreation website for the latest on trail closures and maintenance. Don't waste your time driving out just to hit a "Trail Closed" sign.
</AlertBox>
