<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard: Avoid Peak Afternoon Hours!**
San Antonio officials have already been sounding the alarm about dangerous summer heat. Do not, I repeat, *do not* plan any strenuous outdoor activity between 2 p.m. and 7 p.m. Hydration isn't just a suggestion, it's a survival tactic. Carry more water than you think you need, and don't wait until you're thirsty to drink. Watch for signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke in yourself and others. This isn't the time to push for a PR on your Stone Oak loop.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Construction Continues on Leon Creek Greenway**
If you regularly hit the Leon Creek Greenway, especially near Loop 1604, be aware of ongoing TxDOT construction. A portion of the trail north of the Valero Trailhead is still closed and expected to remain so until mid-2027. The Maverick Creek Trailhead is also closed for sidewalk and lighting work for approximately two more weeks. Don't try to navigate closed sections; they're shut for a reason, usually your safety. Find an alternate route or risk a twisted ankle and a very annoyed construction crew.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep an Eye on the Skies and Ground for Rain and Flooding**
While rain chances are spotty today, the forecast favors above-average precipitation for the region from today through next week. Isolated flash flooding is a real concern in San Antonio, as we've seen in previous years. If a trail looks flooded, *turn around*. Don't attempt to cross standing or running water, especially in low-water crossings. You never know what debris is lurking underneath, and it's just not worth it.
</AlertBox>

Overall, if you're going to get your miles in today, make it early. Embrace the dawn patrol, hydrate aggressively, and don't push your limits. Otherwise, enjoy some indoor active recovery.
