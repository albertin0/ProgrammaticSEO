## Why today is *not* the day to hit the trails:

<BulletList>
*   We're currently under a Flash Flood Warning for Bexar County until at least 10:45 AM, with "life-threatening" flooding expected in low-lying areas, creeks, and even major roads. This isn't just puddles; this is dangerous, fast-moving water.
*   More than 30 roads are already closed across Bexar County, with more expected. You're not getting anywhere safely, especially not to your favorite trailhead without risking your vehicle or worse.
*   Even if the rain lets up for a bit, a Flood Watch remains in effect through Tuesday evening. This means conditions can change rapidly, and those flooded areas won't clear up overnight.
</BulletList>

### Hazardous Conditions: Water, Water Everywhere

Seriously, don't be that person. The National Weather Service is forecasting rainfall rates that could hit 2 to 3 inches *per hour*. That's not a gentle spring shower; that's a deluge that turns our familiar streets and greenways into raging rivers. If you ignore the warnings and try to run, you're not just risking a sprained ankle; you're risking being swept away or encountering unseen hazards under murky water. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase, it's a lifeline in San Antonio today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLASH FLOOD WARNING IN EFFECT!** Do NOT attempt to cross flooded roads or low-water crossings. They can be far deeper and swifter than they appear. Seriously, stay home. Check BexarFlood.org for real-time road closures before *any* essential travel.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Park Update: Closed for Business (and Safety)

Beyond the obvious flood risks, several of our favorite running spots are already impacted by ongoing issues or have explicitly posted warnings. Remember that section of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604? Yeah, still closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. The Salado Creek Greenway near the Tobin Park Trailhead also has intermittent closures. And if you were eyeing a River Walk run on the Museum Reach, well, there's a fallen tree blocking the East bank trail between Newell and Camden. It’s a mess out there.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures & Construction:** Expect detours and impassable sections on major greenways like the Leon Creek and Salado Creek Greenways due to both construction and current flood conditions. Don't assume your usual route is clear or safe.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Pollen: The Least of Your Worries (For Once)

Look, usually, I'm harping on about the air, but today, it's pretty much a non-issue compared to the actual *water*. The Air Quality Index is a lovely 19, which is "Good," with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant. The rain we're getting should help keep those particulate levels down. No Ozone Action Day declared, so breathe easy... indoors.

Pollen is currently sitting at a "Moderate" level. Given everything else, if this was your only concern, you'd be golden. But it's not.
