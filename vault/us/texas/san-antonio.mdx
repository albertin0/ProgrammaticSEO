Another Sunday, another chance to get out there, but let's be real, today isn't exactly giving "ideal conditions" vibes.

Look, the AQI is holding strong at 45, which is "Good" by most metrics, so your lungs aren't fighting a full-blown exhaust party today. However, that doesn't mean it's all roses and sunshine for your respiratory system. We've got moderate pollen floating around, and yesterday's house fire on the East Side probably left some lingering funk in the air for anyone running near that area. So, while it's not a lung emergency, don't be shocked if your sinuses put up a fight.

The real kick in the teeth today is the weather. It's a brisk 10.1 degrees Celsius (that's 50 degrees Fahrenheit for the traditionalists), and "Overcast" is being generous. What's worse, those north winds are still blowing at 15 to 20 mph, with gusts reaching over 30 mph. That's a recipe for chilled muscles, stiff joints, and a generally uncomfortable experience. Don't be that hero in shorts and a tank top; you'll regret it halfway through your warm-up.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wind Chill is Real, Folks:** That 50-degree air is going to feel significantly colder with these sustained winds. Expect it to feel more like the low 40s or even upper 30s. Dress in layers, cover your ears, and protect those exposed joints.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of discomfort, let's talk about our beloved greenways. If you're planning a long run on the Leon Creek Greenway, especially near the 1604 overpass or anything south, be prepared for diversions. Construction closures are scattered across the city's trail system, from Culebra Creek to portions of the Salado Creek Greenway and even the Oxbow Trail along the Museum Reach. Plan your route meticulously, or you'll be doing more unexpected road running than actual trail mileage.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Reconnaissance is Key:** Before you head out, check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website for specific closures on your planned route. Don't assume your usual path is open. Better yet, just stick to neighborhood streets today.
</AlertBox>

Rain chances are low for most of the day, around 20% after 9 AM, but "light rain at night" is in the forecast, and scattered showers have been moving through since yesterday evening. This means trails, especially unpaved sections, are likely muddy and slick. Watch your footing to avoid an ankle roll.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Watch Your Step on Wet Surfaces:** Even paved trails can be treacherously slick when wet. With scattered showers and chilly temperatures, black ice isn't out of the question in shaded areas, especially on bridges and overpasses early this morning. Stay vigilant.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're hitting the pavement, keep it shorter, stay bundled, and be mindful of lingering dampness. If you absolutely *must* hit the trails, proceed with extreme caution and be ready for detours. Today's not about speed records; it's about not ending up in my physical therapy office with a strained hamstring or a twisted ankle.
