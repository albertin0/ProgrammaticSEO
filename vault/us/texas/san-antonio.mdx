## The Daily Rundown: What's Kicking in San Antonio

Alright, let's cut to the chase. You've got near-perfect running temperatures this morning, starting in the mid-50s Fahrenheit (around 13°C) and climbing to a warm 85°F (29°C) by noon. The skies are clear, and the humidity is playing nice, so your hair won't stage a revolt. That's the good news.

Now for the "don't be dumb" part. While the weather itself is a green light, the air quality in San Antonio is currently sitting at a "moderate" AQI of 95, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. What does that mean for you? It means most folks will be fine, but if you're one of the "sensitive individuals" – you know who you are, the ones who start coughing just looking at dust – you might experience some mild respiratory symptoms. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and it’s a double whammy for your airways. Consider scaling back your intensity if you're feeling it, or maybe stick to an indoor track today.

### Trail Updates & General Annoyances

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Air Quality & Pollen Alert!** AQI is 95 (moderate, PM2.5 dominant) and pollen is moderate. Sensitive groups should exercise caution and consider shorter, less intense workouts or indoor alternatives. Don't push it if your lungs are complaining.
</AlertBox>

San Antonio's always growing, which means construction, and construction means potential headaches for your usual routes. The San Antonio River Authority (SARA) is reporting several ongoing closures, particularly in the **Mission Reach** area. If you usually run near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, or Conception Park, be aware that trails there are temporarily closed for "Stem Density efforts." Don't try to sneak through; respect the closures. These folks are working to improve our natural spaces, even if it means a temporary detour for your run.

And it's not just the Mission Reach. The Salinas boardwalk in San Pedro Creek Culture Park is shut down for repairs for "a couple of weeks," an alert issued back on October 12th, so it's definitely still active. Also, a section of the trail along the west bank of Lexington Avenue is closed due to City work. Always keep an eye out for posted signs and be ready to reroute.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Check Your Trail!** Various segments of the Mission Reach trails (near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, Conception Park), the Salinas boardwalk on San Pedro Creek Culture Park, and a portion of the Lexington Avenue west bank trail are currently closed. Look for official detour signage or plan an alternate route.
</AlertBox>

While there were recent news reports about a tragic incident involving a rappelling demonstration at the Alamodome over the weekend, this isn't a general outdoor workout hazard for runners or cyclists. Still, it's a somber reminder to always be aware of your surroundings and the specific risks of any activity you undertake.
