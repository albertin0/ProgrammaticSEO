## The Full Breakdown for Your Sweat Session

Look, I get it. We're runners. We like to pretend a little heat won't stop us. But let's be real: San Antonio summers are no joke, and today is another prime example. While the morning might feel deceptively "clear" and a tolerable 25.1°C (77°F), that's just a cruel trick before the sun truly beats down. By noon, you'll be wondering if you accidentally wandered into an oven. Temperatures will be slow to fall even into the evening, hanging around 95°F (35°C) at 8 p.m. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. The city's already seen its first triple-digit day of the year, so we're officially in "don't be a hero" season.

**DANGER: Extreme Heat Index!** The forecast clearly indicates heat index values reaching 100-105°F (38-41°C) this afternoon. This can lead to rapid dehydration, heat exhaustion, and heat stroke. Limit strenuous activity, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Drink water constantly, not just when you're thirsty. Seriously, your body isn't designed to perform at its peak in these conditions.

Beyond the heat, you've got to contend with the usual San Antonio dance around construction. More significantly for the weekend warriors, I-10 at Loop 1604 on the Northwest Side will see full closures from 9 p.m. to 5 a.m. Friday, Saturday, *and* Sunday nights as part of that massive 1604 North Expansion project. While these are primarily road closures, they can impact your drive *to* your favorite trailheads.

And for my trail runners, a heads-up: The Fiesta Night Run is happening today and tomorrow at the Land Heritage Institute. If you're not registered, those trails are going to be busy with race participants and support crews. Maybe pick a different spot today, or embrace the crowds if that's your jam.
