So, while it's not "danger zone" bad, it's enough to notice if you're pushing hard.

### Trail Talk: Closed for (Your) Business

Now, about those trails. If your regular route takes you through **Garza Park** on the Westside, you're out of luck. The Loop Trail, along with Softball Field #2 and the Multi-Purpose Field, is shut down until January 2027 for major improvements related to the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail Project. That’s not a temporary detour; that's a *major* reroute. Don't waste your time showing up, you'll just hit a fence.

And don't even get me started on the Greenways. A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, from north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is closed until mid-2027 for TxDOT construction. That's right, *mid-2027*. Planning is everything if you want to get any continuous mileage in. You'll find similar headaches on parts of the Salado Creek Greenway and even Classen-Steubing Ranch Park still has closures through the end of the month.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Active Construction Zones: Respect the Closures!** Many parks and greenways, including Garza Park and sections of the Leon Creek Greenway, are undergoing heavy construction with large equipment. Ignoring "Trail Closed" signs isn't just rude, it's dangerous. These aren't suggestions; they're for your safety.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Local Tip: Check Before You Go!** Seriously, hit up the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation website or the San Antonio River Authority's current conditions page before you head out. They usually have the most up-to-date info on closures and construction. Don't rely on your memory; things change faster than a Texas thunderstorm.
</AlertBox>

So, today's workout isn't a write-off, but it requires some planning. Pick a route you know is open, maybe stick to sidewalks or a track if air quality is bothering you, and don't try to be a hero on a closed trail. Your lungs, joints, and sanity will thank you.
