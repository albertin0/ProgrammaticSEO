If you're looking to hit the pavement or trails in San Antonio today, you might want to rethink your definition of 'workout' and instead practice your indoor yoga. Seriously, stay inside.

## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Flash Flood Frenzy:** We're under a flood watch until 7 p.m. tonight, folks, and that's not just a suggestion. The National Weather Service is calling for 1 to 2 inches of rain *per hour* in some spots, with localized totals that could hit 6 to 8 inches. This isn't your grandma's drizzle; this is "don't drown trying to get your miles in" rain. Yesterday saw actual high-water rescues, and today could be worse.
*   **Wind and Lightning Woes:** As if drowning wasn't enough, we're talking wind gusts up to 50 mph and frequent lightning strikes. Unless your training plan involves dodging falling branches and becoming a human lightning rod, this isn't conducive to a PR.
*   **Trail Turmoil & Construction Chaos:** Beyond the immediate flooding, many of our beloved greenways are already a mess. Several sections of the Salado Creek Greenway are either flooded and closed or shut down for ongoing maintenance and construction until further notice. The Leon Creek Greenway has its own long-term TxDOT closures, including a section under Loop 1604 and near the Marquis Trailhead due to a fallen overpass. Don't even think about trying to navigate that in current conditions.

## The Grim Details

The air quality, bless its heart, is actually in the "Good" range (AQI 25) with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant. Pollen's sitting at a "Moderate" level. Normally, I'd give you a nod to mask up if you're sensitive, but let's be real: dodging floodwaters and lightning makes pollen utterly irrelevant today. You won't be huffing and puffing from cedar when you're busy trying not to get swept down a swollen creek.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Life-Threatening Flash Flooding and Severe Weather
Multiple news outlets and the National Weather Service confirm that heavy rainfall, high winds (up to 50 mph), and lightning are expected throughout San Antonio today. Roads are already closed, and trails will be impassable and extremely dangerous. DO NOT attempt to run or bike through flooded areas. Turn around, don't drown.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Muddy, Debris-Strewn Trails & Existing Closures
Even if you find a spot that *looks* clear, recent and ongoing heavy rains mean trails are slick, muddy, and likely covered in debris. Furthermore, significant portions of the **Salado Creek Greenway** are either currently flooded or under long-term closure for repairs and maintenance. Check Bexar County's flood map for real-time road closures before even thinking about heading out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### TIP: Embrace the Great Indoors
Today is a prime candidate for a treadmill session, bodyweight circuit in the living room, or honestly, just kicking your feet up and reading a book. Your lungs will appreciate the decent air, and your joints (and life) will thank you for avoiding the very real risks outside.
