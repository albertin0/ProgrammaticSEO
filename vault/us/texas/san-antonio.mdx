#### Flash Flood Threat!
The biggest danger today is the potential for flash flooding. We've got deep tropical moisture moving in, and slow-moving thunderstorms are expected. Creeks, underpasses, and low-water crossings can become deadly in minutes. If you see water over a road or trail, *Turn Around, Don't Drown*. It's not a suggestion; it's how you stay alive. Don't test the currents on the Salado Creek Greenway, for instance.
</AlertBox>

#### Construction and Road Closures
TxDOT has ongoing major weekend closures at the Loop 1604 and I-10 interchange as part of the North Expansion Project, running through early Monday morning. While these are mainly vehicle closures, they can create detours and impact accessibility to certain parks or trailheads if you're driving there. Expect traffic headaches if you're navigating north San Antonio.

Seriously, hit the gym. Or, if you absolutely need fresh air, keep it short, close to home, and away from any drainage areas or greenways. Today is an excellent day for some active recovery, maybe some stretching, or even just planning your workouts for when the skies aren't threatening to open up. Stay safe out there, folks.
