Alright, San Antonio, let's talk real. If you've been eyeing the trails, today's your window, but it's closing fast, and there are still plenty of obstacles. The good news? Your lungs are getting a break. Air quality is holding strong at 41 AQI, with the dominant pollutant being "Ozone/Other" but at levels that won't make you gasp for air on the Museum Reach. No nasty wildfire smoke blowing in, thankfully, though moderate pollen might still tickle some noses.

The weather is playing nice too, for a September day in South Texas. Overcast and 26°C means you won't be roasting like a breakfast taco. But enjoy it while it lasts. Governor Abbott himself warned us on Monday: heavy rains and flash flooding are on the horizon, starting Thursday and potentially sticking around through Friday morning. The National Weather Service is calling for a Level 3 out of 4 risk for flash flooding in San Antonio on Thursday, with rain chances hitting 80-90% from Thursday noon to Friday noon. So, if you've got a long run planned for later in the week, maybe reschedule for a treadmill session or a swim. Or, better yet, just get out there *today*.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Flooding Threat Incoming!**
Heavy rains and flash flooding are forecast for Thursday and Friday in San Antonio. Do NOT run through flooded areas or low-water crossings. Turn around, don't drown. Seriously. Check SARiverFlood.org for real-time conditions.
</AlertBox>

Now, about those trails. While the sky might be clearing (for a bit), the ground is still a mess in spots. The City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation is still listing a laundry list of Greenway trail closures, with some extending well into 2027. Specifically, a significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** is shut down for TxDOT construction until at least mid-2027. Don't count on completing your usual loop without hitting a detour sign or an unexpected pile of dirt. The South Salado Creek Greenway also has closures, including one south of the Southside Lions Park entrance until early October for repair work.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction & Trail Closures Are Still a Headache**
Expect detours and blocked paths, especially on the Leon Creek Greenway and parts of the Salado Creek Greenway. Always check local city parks websites for the latest closures before you head out, and respect all signage.
</AlertBox>

In short, today is your window for a decent outdoor workout, but be smart about where you go and keep an eye on that forecast for later this week. You don't want to get caught literally in the storm.
