## The Full Breakdown

### Weathering the Blaze (and Humidity)

It's July in San Antonio, so are we surprised? Temperatures are set to climb to a high of 99°F (37.2°C) today, but that "RealFeel" temperature of 108°F (42.2°C) is what's going to hit you. That's "Dangerous Heat," folks. Even if you're usually good to go, listen to your body. Dehydration, heat exhaustion, and even heat stroke are serious risks, especially during extended periods or strenuous activity. We had heat advisories last week with similar conditions, and the moderate risk is expected to continue today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Alert!** The RealFeel temperature is hitting 108°F today. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity between 2-7 p.m. Drink water constantly, wear light clothing, and know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Seriously, don't mess with Texas heat.
</AlertBox>

### Breathe Easy? Not So Fast.

Our air quality isn't doing us any favors either. With an AQI of 95, and PM2.5 particles as the main culprit, it's not ideal for everyone. PM2.5 is that fine particulate matter that slips past your body's natural filters and gets deep into your lungs, which is why the EPA tracks it closely. This isn't just a number; it's why your lungs might feel a bit heavier today. The air quality forecast is "Poor" for sensitive groups today, so if you're in that category, think twice before pushing it hard outdoors.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Advisory for Sensitive Groups:** The AQI is 95, and AccuWeather rates today's air quality as "Poor" for sensitive groups. If you have respiratory issues or are very young/old, consider an indoor workout.
</AlertBox>

### Navigating the Obstacle Course

Good luck finding a clear path on some of our favorite spots. Construction is a constant here, and it's impacting our trails. For instance, a portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim) is completely shut down due to TxDOT construction until mid-2027. The Salado Creek Greenway also has periodic closures near Tobin Park Trailhead and an indefinite closure from Gembler Road to Houston Street. And let's not forget the ongoing VIA Rapid Green Line work, which means intermittent lane and sidewalk closures along San Pedro Avenue. Always check local city resources for specific trail closures before you head out, unless you enjoy surprise detours.
