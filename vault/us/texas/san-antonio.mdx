## Watch Your Step, Literally: Construction and Closures

While the skies are clear and the air is good, San Antonio's real obstacle course remains the ongoing construction projects. If you're planning a weekend long run, especially anywhere near the North Side, you need to be acutely aware.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Weekend Road Closures on Loop 1604 & I-10!** Expect major disruptions this weekend. All eastbound lanes and one westbound lane of I-10 at the Loop 1604 interchange were closed from Friday 9 PM to Saturday 4 AM. Both frontage roads of Loop 1604 at the I-10 interchange will be closed from 9 PM Friday until 5 AM Monday. This is not just a driver's headache; it means detours, confused traffic, and generally less safe conditions for anyone on foot or bike in that area. Avoid it if you can.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just the highways. The **VIA Rapid Green Line** project is still chugging along, meaning intermittent lane and sidewalk closures are the norm along San Pedro Avenue from the airport all the way downtown to the Brooks Transit Center. This is going to be a multi-year slog, so factor it into your urban routes.

For our trail runners and greenway enthusiasts, several sections are still under some form of closure or maintenance. Parts of the **Salado Creek Greenway** are seeing intermittent closures near Tobin Park Trailhead due to TXDOT work, and the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street is shut down until winter 2026 for maintenance. That’s a long time to wait for a clear path. Also, if you regularly hit up **Elmendorf Lake Park**, be aware that the playground is closed until May 31st and the parking lot/19th Street entrance until December 2026. Plan accordingly if that's your starting point.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Trail Conditions: Mud & Debris** While we haven't had recent heavy downpours *today*, San Antonio is prone to flash flooding, and just a couple weeks ago, we saw significant high water incidents. Trails can still have residual mud, uneven surfaces, or debris from past events, especially near creeks. Always exercise caution and never cross flooded sections – not even if a robotaxi tries it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Look Ahead to Next Week:** The forecast shows increasing chances of rain and scattered thunderstorms starting Monday and Tuesday. Keep an eye on the weather apps and prepare for potential disruptions if you're planning outdoor activities early next week.
</AlertBox>
