Alright, let's get down to brass tacks. It's August 13th, and while the skies are clear and the air quality index (AQI) is a lovely 30, meaning minimal junk for your lungs, don't let that fool you. The raw temperature might be a modest 26.9°C (around 80°F), but AccuWeather is reporting a "RealFeel®" of a scorching 105°F for parts of San Antonio. KSAT is even forecasting 101°F for today. That's not just "warm," that's "you could fry an egg on the pavement and then pass out from heatstroke trying to eat it" hot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Index
That RealFeel temperature is no joke. Running or intense exercise during peak daylight hours is a recipe for heat exhaustion or worse. If you *must* be outside, go before the sun really gets going or after it dips. Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate, and then hydrate some more. Electrolytes aren't just for fancy sports drinks; they're for staying alive out here. Listen to your body – dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating are not "pushing through it" signals; they're "get inside, now" signals.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, we're not dealing with any active severe weather warnings, wildfires, or major flooding today, which is a win in itself for August in Texas. However, if you're planning on hitting the trails, you're going to need to do some homework. Our sprawling Howard W. Peak Greenway Trail System, while a treasure, is a bit of a minefield right now with construction and maintenance closures.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### CAUTION: Trail Closures Galore
If your usual route involves the Greenway, double-check before you head out. A portion of the Salado Creek Greenway south of the Southside Lions Park entrance is closed for repairs until October. The Tobin Park Trailhead area of the Salado Creek Greenway also has periodic closures due to a TXDOT construction project. Over on the Huebner Creek Greenway Trail, Babcock Road is shut down between Huebner Road and Eckert Road today and tomorrow for pedestrian bridge installation, which will certainly impact access. Even Hendrick Arnold Nature Park is closed for playground development until August 21st. Basically, assume your favorite path is partly blocked and be prepared to improvise.
</AlertBox>
