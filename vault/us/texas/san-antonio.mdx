## Today's Rundown

Let's be real, San Antonio, it's June. And June means heat. Today, June 22nd, 2026, is no exception. While your phone might tell you it's a pleasant 26°C (78.8°F) this morning, don't let that fool you. By midday, we're looking at actual temperatures climbing into the mid-90s, and with our famous South Texas humidity, the heat index is going to soar to a truly miserable 100-110°F. That's not just "uncomfortable," that's "actively dangerous if you're pushing it."

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Heat Index Up to 110°F
The National Weather Service is forecasting heat index values between 100°F and 110°F from noon to 9 PM. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, take frequent breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas, and hydrate like it's your job. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real and can sneak up on you faster than a pop-up shower in August.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the air quality (AQI 36) is in the "Good" range, so you won't be choking on smog in addition to the humidity. However, with a moderate pollen count, those of you with seasonal allergies might still feel a bit congested.

### Local Trail & Road Updates

If you're planning on hitting the trails, know that some of our beloved greenways are still undergoing work. A portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** (north of the Valero Trailhead) is closed for TxDOT construction, and that's not clearing up until mid-2027. So, plan your routes accordingly and don't expect to just power through. Additionally, the **Maverick Creek Trailhead** is still shut down for about another week for elevated sidewalk and path lighting work. Periodic closures are also affecting parts of the Salado Creek Greenway trail to the Tobin Park Trailhead.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ongoing Construction & Trail Closures
TxDOT and city projects mean closures on key greenways like the Leon Creek Greenway and Maverick Creek Trailhead. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation website for the latest updates before you head out, or you'll be adding an unplanned detour (and more heat exposure) to your workout.
</AlertBox>

The River Walk Summer Artisan Show wrapped up yesterday, so you shouldn't run into crowds there today. However, always be mindful of events, especially downtown.
