If you’re running outside in San Antonio today, you better have a good reason and a solid plan, because the elements are not exactly rolling out the red carpet.

*   Heat index values are predicted to hit as high as 111°F in some areas today, even with a seemingly clear morning. Your body will be working overtime just to regulate temperature.

Today, July 30, 2026, San Antonio is a mixed bag of environmental nastiness and persistent urban obstacles. We're looking at a mostly clear morning with temperatures around 28.1°C (82.6°F), but don't let that fool you. The humidity is going to make it feel a lot hotter as the day progresses, with forecast highs up to 103°F and heat index values reaching a brutal 111°F in some spots. That's "Moderate to major heat risk impacts" as far as the National Weather Service is concerned.

<AlertBox type="danger">
<h3>Heat Hazard: Stay Hydrated or Stay Indoors</h3>
The combination of high temperatures and humidity means your body will struggle to cool itself. Hydrate aggressively *before* you start, carry more water than you think you need, and consider pushing your long runs to another day. Heat exhaustion and stroke are no joke, folks.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the air. The AQI hitting 95 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant isn't ideal for anyone, especially those with asthma or other lung conditions. Couple that with a moderate pollen count, and your respiratory system is in for a workout it didn't ask for. If your throat starts feeling scratchy or your chest tightens up, cut it short. It’s not a sign of weakness; it’s common sense.

<AlertBox type="warning">
<h3>Air Quality Alert: Sensitive Lungs Beware</h3>
With AQI at 95 and moderate pollen, anyone with existing respiratory issues or allergies should seriously consider rescheduling or moving their workout indoors. PM2.5 particles are small enough to get deep into your lungs, and you don't need that added stress.
</AlertBox>

As for getting around, good luck. The City of San Antonio is still a maze of road closures and trail maintenance. A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604** remains closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Similar headaches plague the Salado Creek Greenway with periodic closures, including the section from Gembler Road to Houston Street being shut down indefinitely for maintenance. You'll be rerouting, adding mileage, and probably cursing TxDOT under your breath.

Speaking of cursing TxDOT, if you're planning any late-night or early-morning runs near downtown, be advised: I-35 South near Fort Sam Houston has a major closure tonight (Thursday, July 30, 9 PM) through tomorrow morning (July 31, 5 AM) for the I-35 NEX project. Detours will be in effect, adding traffic and general unpleasantness.

<AlertBox type="info">
<h3>Plan Ahead: Construction & Event Check</h3>
Always check the City of San Antonio's closures page before you head out, especially for greenways like the Leon Creek or Salado Creek. And if you're downtown this evening, the Downtown Thursday Concert Series at Travis Park might mean more foot traffic, even if there aren't formal street closures. On the bright side, violent street crime was down last year, so at least that's one less thing to worry about.
</AlertBox>
