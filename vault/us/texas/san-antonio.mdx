## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **It's a hot one, again.** While the mercury might show 28.1°C (about 82.6°F) this morning, the National Weather Service is calling for highs pushing 100°F with a "feels like" temperature of 102-107°F in the afternoon. That's "stay inside and complain about the AC bill" kind of hot, not "personal best" hot.
*   **Breathe deep? Maybe not.** Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 95, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This level is generally acceptable for most folks, but if you've got asthma, heart issues, or just don't like inhaling tiny particles, you might feel some minor to moderate symptoms. There are a couple of wildfires within 100 miles, including the Lonesome Fire 75 miles out, so that PM2.5 isn't just a coincidence.
*   **Your favorite trail might be a construction zone.** Good intentions, terrible timing. Several popular spots are currently impacted by ongoing construction, which means detours or outright closures.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory (Unofficial, but seriously, use common sense)

Look, San Antonio. It's mid-September, but the unseasonably warm conditions are sticking around. Avoid any strenuous outdoor activity between 2 PM and 7 PM. Stay hydrated like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). If you're out there, wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothes. Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke – heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, confusion – and don't be a hero. Get inside and cool down, or call 9-1-1 if things get serious.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Elevated PM2.5 and Moderate Air Quality

With an AQI of 95 and PM2.5 particles making up the bulk of it, those with sensitive respiratory systems or underlying heart conditions should consider keeping their workouts indoors today. These fine particles can easily get into your lungs and bloodstream, aggravating asthma and other issues. If you *must* be outside, keep your effort levels low and pay attention to how you're feeling.
</AlertBox>

## Local Trail & Park Update: Construction Continues

If your regular route takes you through **Garza Park** on the Westside, be aware that the Garza Park Loop Trail, Softball Field #2, and Multi-Purpose Field are all closed for construction related to the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail Project. This closure started on September 8th and is expected to continue until approximately January 2027. They're moving heavy equipment, so don't try to sneak through. Other trail sections, like portions of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604, are also closed for extended periods due to TxDOT projects. Always check the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation website for the latest closures before heading out.
