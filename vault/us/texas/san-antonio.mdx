## The Rundown: Breathe Easy-ish, But Watch Your Step

Look, we finally got a break from that typical San Antonio "fall" where it's 90 degrees and muggy until Thanksgiving. Today, we're looking at a glorious 18.6°C (that's 65°F for you Fahrenheit fundamentalists) under partly cloudy skies with a gentle breeze. Your joints will be singing, no doubt. Seriously, enjoy this while it lasts, because later this week the "tropical moisture" from what's left of Hurricane Vance is supposed to roll in, bringing increasing chances of rain, especially Thursday and Friday.

### Lungs: Moderate Annoyances

Your lungs, however, might have a minor bone to pick. The overall pollen level is clocked at "Moderate," which means if you're one of the unfortunate souls whose sinuses decide to wage war with nature this time of year, you'll feel it. And while there are no major wildfires actively pumping smoke into our airspace (thank god for small miracles), our Air Quality Index (AQI) is at 50 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's technically still in the "Good" range, but it's hugging the line of "Moderate," meaning particularly sensitive individuals might notice some irritation. So, if you're asthmatic or have other respiratory issues, maybe take it easy on those lung-busting hill repeats.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen & PM2.5 Alert:** Moderate pollen combined with PM2.5 at the edge of the "Good" AQI range means sensitive individuals should monitor symptoms. Consider shorter, less intense workouts or sticking to indoors if you're prone to respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

### Joints: Construction Zone Ahead

Now, about those trails. If your favorite running path includes sections of the **Leon Creek Greenway**, particularly north of the Valero Trailhead towards The Rim, you're going to hit a wall. That whole segment is shut down for TxDOT construction, and it's not expected to reopen until mid-2027. Forget that fallen highway overpass section near I-10 by the Marquis Trailhead too – that's a no-go until further notice.

It's not just Leon Creek. The San Antonio River Authority also has sections of the **Mission Reach Trail** near Mission San Juan, Mission Espada, and Conception Park temporarily closed for "Stem Density efforts." Yeah, I don't know what that means either, but it means detours for you. The Museum Reach also has a piece of its west bank sidewalk trail on Lexington Avenue closed for city work.

And for those who drive to their trails, be aware that East Hildebrand Avenue is completely shut down starting today until March 2027 for a SAWS water main project. It's a logistical nightmare, not just for traffic, but for accessing certain parts of the city.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Widespread Trail Closures:** Multiple segments of the Leon Creek Greenway (TxDOT construction, fallen overpass) and the Mission Reach Trail (River Authority work) are closed. Plan your routes carefully and expect detours. Don't blow out your knee trying to hurdle barricades.
</AlertBox>

## Looking Ahead

While today is mostly clear of rain, don't get complacent. Rain chances pick up Tuesday and are forecast to increase through Thursday and Friday, with potential for "tropical downpours" and "isolated flooding" from Tropical Storm Vance remnants. The city parks department explicitly warns to use caution on trails after rain and *never* cross flooded areas. Good advice, unlike some of your buddies trying to get an Instagram photo in a flooded creek. Don't be that guy.
