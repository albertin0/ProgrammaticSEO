## Current Conditions & What to Watch Out For

Let's break down what's hitting us today, September 24, 2026.

### Weather
It's 24.4°C (76°F) out there right now, with clear skies. Sounds lovely, right? Wrong. The temperature is set to hit a scorching 35°C (95°F) by mid-afternoon, and with the humidity, that "feels like" temperature, or heat index, will be pushing 38°C (100°F) between 1 p.m. and 6 p.m. This isn't just "warm," it's "your-body-is-working-overtime-just-to-exist" hot. San Antonio is on track for its hottest September on record, so don't be surprised, just be prepared. Rain chances are a minimal 10-15% today, so don't count on a refreshing shower.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heat Advisory (Unofficial, but you know it)
With the heat index hovering near 100°F this afternoon, heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious risks. If you're running, cycling, or doing anything more strenuous than walking to your fridge, plan your workout for early morning or late evening. Drink more water than you think you need. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergens
Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a moderate 57, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This generally means the air is acceptable, but if you're sensitive to air pollution, or you've got existing lung issues, you might feel some effects.

On the allergy front, it's a real Texas "fall" welcome. While the overall pollen level is "Moderate," that's misleading if you're sensitive to certain species. Fall Elm is hitting hard today, with counts over 600. Mold spores are also moderate. Ragweed is around, but thankfully, its count is light today.

#### Pollen Punch
If you suffer from allergies, especially to Fall Elm or molds, today is not the day for an epic, unfiltered breath of fresh air. Consider an indoor workout or arm yourself with your meds if you insist on hitting the trails.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Hazards
San Antonio is perpetually under construction, and today is no different. Many of your favorite spots are affected.

*   **Garza Park:** The loop trail, softball field #2, and multi-purpose field at Garza Park are completely closed until January 2027 for a major overhaul as part of the Zarzamora Creek Greenway Trail Project. Don't even bother driving over there for a run; you'll just be frustrated.
*   **Leon Creek Greenway:** A section of the Leon Creek Greenway near Loop 1604, specifically north of the Valero Trailhead to The Rim, is closed for TxDOT construction until mid-2027. Plan your routes accordingly to avoid getting rerouted or stuck.
*   **Salado Creek Greenway:** Parts of this popular greenway, particularly south of the Southside Lions Park entrance, are closed until early October for repair work. More intermittent closures are expected near Tobin Park Trailhead.
*   **Classen-Steubing Ranch Park:** The Prickly Pear Path and Sotol Descent trails, plus a section of paved trail, are closed through the end of September.

Road construction isn't just for drivers. If you're cycling or running along city streets, watch out for lane closures on Bandera Road at Wurzbach Road, and SE Military, where crews are working on fiber installation and sign replacement. And if you're near Woodlawn Lake, Hickory Street is basically a construction zone for road overhaul, with traffic restrictions in place after 8:30 a.m.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Check Before You Go
Seriously, before you lace up, pull up the City of San Antonio Parks & Recreation closures page. And if you're cycling, check TxDOT's site too. Nothing's more annoying than planning a 10-mile run only to hit a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign at mile one.
</AlertBox>
