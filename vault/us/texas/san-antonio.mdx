## The Rundown: Don't Melt Out There

Alright, San Antonians. You've got partly cloudy skies and what seems like a decent 75°F (23.8°C) out there right now. If you're out before 10 AM, you might actually enjoy it. But don't get complacent. The National Weather Service is saying we're looking at heat index values—that's the "feels like" temperature, for those of you not paying attention—reaching a nasty 100 to 103 degrees later today. That's not just "uncomfortable," folks, that's "actively dangerous if you're pushing it." Seriously, if you're planning a long run or intense workout, move it indoors or get it done *early*.

### Air Quality & Allergens

The AQI is sitting pretty at 21, which is generally "Fair" for most people. However, we're still dealing with moderate pollen, and the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) predicts our daily PM2.5 might nudge into the lower end of the "Moderate" range in some areas. If you've got asthma or are sensitive to allergens, this isn't the day to try for a PR. Listen to your body and maybe stick to shorter, less intense efforts.

### Trail Conditions & Closures

We had some decent rain earlier in the week, and while the heavy stuff is "no longer in the forecast", that doesn't mean our trails are pristine. Expect mud and debris, especially in low-lying areas.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Heads up, trail runners and cyclists: A section of the **Leon Creek Greenway** at Loop 1604, north of the Valero Trailhead, is still shut down for TxDOT construction and won't reopen until mid-2027. Don't be that person who ignores the signs; find an alternate route. Also, the Maverick Creek Trailhead is closed for about two weeks for sidewalk and lighting work.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **The Heat Index is the Real Threat Today.** With "feels like" temperatures hitting 100-103°F, severe heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real possibilities. The National Weather Service is urging folks to drink plenty of water and limit strenuous outdoor activities. This isn't a suggestion; it's a directive to stay safe.
</AlertBox>
