## The Grind: What You Need to Know

This means if you're sensitive, have asthma, or are just generally not a fan of breathing tiny particulate matter, you might feel it. And let's not forget the ever-present moderate pollen count. ### Trail & Road Conditions: A Construction Zone Special

If you had plans for a serene long run on your favorite trail, you might want to adjust. Construction is absolutely *everywhere*.

A significant portion of the **Leon Creek Greenway** near Loop 1604 is currently shut down for TxDOT construction, and it's not opening until mid-2027. That's right, next year. So, find a new route, or prepare for some creative detouring. The Mission Reach trails also have areas under temporary closure due to "Stem Density efforts" near Missions San Juan, Espada, and Conception Park. Sounds like they're doing some landscaping, which means more obstacles for your run.

And forget about smooth sailing on the roads. TxDOT has major closures impacting Loop 1604, I-10, and Loop 410 this weekend, especially around the Loop 1604/Hwy 281 interchange. While these are mainly traffic closures, they often spill over into pedestrian pathways and make cycling a nightmare.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**SERIOUS WARNING: Trail Closures & Construction.** Do NOT ignore closure signs. A section near I-10 at the Marquis Trailhead is *still* closed due to a fallen highway overpass. Getting around a barrier isn't being "tough," it's being stupid and risking a twisted ankle or worse. Find an open path, or hit the pavement on a quieter street.
</AlertBox>

### Events & Other Annoyances

There's a "Tricks, Treats and Trails 2026" event happening today, which sounds festive but could mean more people than usual out and about in certain areas. Also, the South Texas Off Road Mountain-Bikers (STORM) have a trail workday at **Olmos Basin Park Trails** until noon, clearing trees. Be mindful of volunteers and equipment if you're headed that way.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** If you're determined to get some miles in, consider sticking to areas you know are clear, or embrace shorter loops in neighborhood parks. Brackenridge Park is mostly open, though some playgrounds are closed. Double-check the City of San Antonio's Parks & Rec site before you head out.
</AlertBox>

## Final Word

It's a beautiful day, but San Antonio is clearly under construction season. Keep an eye on your lungs, watch your step, and maybe just hit the treadmill if you're not feeling adventurous enough to navigate the urban obstacle course.
