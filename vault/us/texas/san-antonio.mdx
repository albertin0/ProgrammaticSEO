## The Rundown: Humidity, Ozone, and Closed Trails

Look, I get it. We're runners, we’re out here. But let's be smart about it, shall we? This morning's a relatively "clear" start at 26°C (79°F), but with 86% humidity, it already feels like 29°C (84°F). By afternoon, we're talking about hitting nearly 37°C (99°F) with humidity still in the picture. Hydration isn't optional; it's survival. And no, that little 12-ounce bottle you carry on your pinky finger isn't going to cut it. Drink early, drink often, and if you start feeling fuzzy, find some shade and reconsider your life choices.

The air quality isn't doing us any favors either. While the overall AQI is "Moderate" at 51, the dominant pollutant is ozone. And let's not pretend we don't know what contributes to that. We've got environmental groups raising hell about some West San Antonio data centers and their potential to crank out ozone-forming pollutants. Bexar County is already in "serious nonattainment" for ozone, so every little bit counts. Plus, there’s talk of light smoke plumes drifting up from the Isthmus of Tehuantepec and merging over South Texas. So, if you're feeling a bit more breathless than usual, it's not just your lack of speed work.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Heat & Humidity:** While the temperature isn't hitting triple digits at 9 AM, it's muggy and will only get hotter. Expect "feels like" temperatures to climb. Stick to the early morning or late evening, and carry *plenty* of water. Don't push it. Heat stroke is a real thing, and it's not a fun story to tell at the next group run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Trail Closures & Construction:** Don't assume your usual route is open. Key sections of trails, like a significant portion of the Leon Creek Greenway by Loop 1604, are completely shut down for TxDOT construction until well into next year. Other greenway segments, including parts of the Salado Creek Greenway, have periodic or indefinite closures due to maintenance and other projects. Check the City of San Antonio Parks and Recreation site *before* you head out, unless you enjoy surprise detours onto busy roads.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pet Safety:** Remember that screwworm alert issued for parts of Texas. If you're running with your furry friend, give them a quick once-over for unusual wounds after your run. Better safe than sorry.
</AlertBox>

Bottom line: If you're going to sweat it out today, keep it shorter, keep it hydrated, and check those trail maps. Your body (and your sanity) will thank you.
