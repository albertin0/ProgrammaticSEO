If you're thinking about an outdoor workout today, June 20th, I'd tell you to seriously reconsider anything involving trails or low-lying areas. The ground is probably a soggy mess, and frankly, it's not worth a twisted ankle or worse.

### Why today isn't the day (for most outdoor activities)

<BulletList>
*   **Flash Flood Fallout:** We just had a flash flood warning for much of Bexar County, including downtown, that expired this morning at 6 a.m.. While the heavy downpours are likely past, creeks, streams, and urban areas are still feeling the effects, and "turn around, don't drown" isn't just a suggestion when roads are under water.
*   **Slippery, Closed Trails:** Expect trails to be muddy and potentially littered with debris. The San Antonio River Authority (SARA) has already closed lower river access points at places like River Crossing, Helton Nature Park, and Graytown Park due to rising waters. Also, a portion of the Leon Creek Greenway at Loop 1604 is still closed due to ongoing TxDOT construction until mid-2027, and you might find periodic closures on the Salado Creek Greenway near Tobin Park Trailhead.
*   **Lingering Humidity & Heat:** While the overcast skies (23.8°C / 75°F) might seem mild, humidity is still cranking, and heat indices could still hit 100-102 degrees later today. Don't be fooled by the clouds; San Antonio in June means "very high heat & humidity".
</BulletList>

### **Post-Flood Hazard: Hidden Dangers**
Even if the rain has stopped, many low-water crossings and underpasses could still be flooded. It is *never* safe to drive or run through standing water. Submerged hazards like debris, sharp objects, or even displaced wildlife can pose serious risks. Some areas of the San Antonio River Trail are already closed due to muddy conditions and high river levels. Stick to known dry routes or, honestly, just hit the gym today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Construction Zone Chaos Continues**
If you typically run near major roadways, be aware of significant TxDOT construction. This weekend, from last night until Sunday at midnight, there are major closures impacting Loop 1604 and I-10 around the interchange, specifically the frontage roads and mainlanes. While this primarily affects drivers, it can spill over into surrounding areas and alter usual pedestrian/cyclist routes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Race Day Traffic**
Multiple races are hitting the pavement today, including the Run Juneteenth Day 5K/10K/13.1 and the Sloth Run 5K/10K/13.1, both kicking off around 7:30 AM. If you're out and about, especially downtown or near race routes, expect more foot traffic and plan accordingly. These events are fantastic, but they add another layer to navigating potentially tricky conditions.
</AlertBox>

Look, the air quality is good, which is a rare treat here, and pollen is only moderate, so your lungs are probably fine. But your joints? Your common sense? Those are at risk today. Stick to the treadmill or find a dry, paved route far from any creeks. Today's a day for caution, not for setting new personal bests on a soggy trail.
