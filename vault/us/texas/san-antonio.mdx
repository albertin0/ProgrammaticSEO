The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, but at this AQI, it's largely negligible.

### Construction & Closures: The Usual San Antonio Shuffle

Beyond the wet stuff, construction continues its relentless march across the city. The VIA Rapid Green Line project is active, causing intermittent lane and sidewalk closures, especially along San Pedro Avenue. And if you're navigating around Roosevelt Avenue, know that it's closed between Grove Avenue and Roosevelt Park Drive through this week for construction. Even the restrooms at Camino Coahuilteca (Acequia Park Trail) are closed tomorrow for parking lot improvements. Always check local updates before you head out, because a "shortcut" might just be a detour into a construction zone.

**Construction Everywhere:** From greenways to major thoroughfares, expect closures and detours. Watch for signage, uneven surfaces, and construction vehicles. Plan alternate routes, especially around the 1604 corridor and the San Pedro Avenue area.
