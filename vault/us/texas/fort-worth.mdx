## The Rundown: Fort Worth Outdoor Workout Guide (May 1, 2026)

Today is looking pretty gray and damp, folks. We're talking widespread rain showers through tonight, with the potential for some heavier downpours. While Fort Worth itself isn't under any severe weather warnings right now, areas just south of us in Central Texas are dealing with a Flood Watch due to expected 2-3 inches of rain, and isolated pockets could see up to 4 inches. Don't be a hero and think your trail run is exempt from flash flood common sense, especially if you're near any low-lying areas or creek crossings. Isolated thunderstorms are also on the menu, so don't be surprised by a sudden clap of thunder, some small hail, or a gust of wind trying to knock you off your feet. Temperatures are staying on the cooler side, hovering around 14.1°C (57°F) and everything is currently overcast.

### Trail & Park Obstacles

First off, let's talk about **Trinity Park**. It's Mayfest time, which means the park has been closed since mid-April for setup and won't fully reopen until May 7th. If your go-to route involves this park or significant portions of the Trinity Trails running through it, you're going to need a backup plan. While some bridges remain open to help trail users skirt around the festival grounds, it's not ideal for a focused workout. Dream Park is also inaccessible for maintenance, so those plans are a bust too.

Beyond Mayfest, a portion of the Trinity Trail system over at **Gateway Park** is also closed. Why? Because the Trinity River banks there are eroding due to frequent flooding. This isn't new, but with more rain expected, those conditions aren't getting any better. Avoid that section for your own safety; nobody needs to become an impromptu river-floater.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Watch for Slippery Surfaces & Standing Water:** With continuous rain, paved trails will be slick, and unpaved sections will be muddy and potentially impassable. Be extra careful to avoid slips, falls, and ankle twists.
</AlertBox>

### Road Works & Air Quality

Construction continues to be a never-ending saga in Fort Worth. There are multiple road closures active through today, May 1st, affecting various districts. While these primarily impact vehicular traffic, they can easily spill over to pedestrian and cycling routes, forcing unexpected detours. Always keep an eye out for signs and flaggers, and assume your usual shortcuts might be blocked. The Chisholm Trail Parkway is also undergoing widening, with daytime closures expected this week, so if your route takes you anywhere near that, expect delays.

On the bright side, the air quality is looking pretty good today with an AQI of 29. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, which at this level, isn't much of a concern for your lungs.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Don't Forget the Bug Spray:** All this rain means mosquitoes will be out in force once the sun *might* peek through. Don't let them ruin your workout (or your afternoon).
</AlertBox>
