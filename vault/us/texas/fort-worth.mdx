Planning a peaceful stroll or bike ride along the Trinity Trails? Think again. The Panther Island area is seeing a lot of action with new residential and mixed-use developments starting their "early vertical phases" in 2026. This means potential closures, detours, and heavy machinery near popular routes. Beyond that, significant road construction is ongoing across the city, including I-20 and I-30 frontage roads.
