### Mind the Construction Chaos

Here's where the "deceptively nice" part comes in. While the skies are clear, the ground is, well, under construction. Our beloved city seems to be in a perpetual state of renovation, and today is no different.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### TRAIL CLOSURE: Fort Worth Floodway near Riverside Drive Bridge
Heads up, trail runners and cyclists! Construction on a section of the Fort Worth Floodway at the Riverside Drive Bridge, just east of I-35W, is ongoing and expected to last until at least early December. This means a temporary trail closure and detour are still in effect. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and tries to muscle through; it's dangerous for you and the crews. Find an alternate route or just stick to the west side of the Trinity Trails for a bit. Seriously, those detours are there for a reason.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the trails, expect scattered road closures across the city. There's ongoing work on North Beach Street/North Tarrant Parkway intersection and various street and water main improvements, including sections of N. Main Street seeing full daytime closures this week. If your workout involves road running or cycling through developed areas, check the city's road closure maps before you head out, unless you enjoy impromptu detours and aggressive drivers.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip for Pollen Sufferers:** A moderate pollen count can still be a nuisance. Consider running earlier in the morning before pollen counts peak, or later in the evening when things have settled a bit. A quick shower and change of clothes post-workout can also help minimize exposure.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it’s a good day to get after it, just be smart about your route planning. Don't let a closed trail or an unexpected detour derail your momentum, just adapt. It's Fort Worth, after all; construction is practically a permanent fixture.
