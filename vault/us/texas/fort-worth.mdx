## The Rundown for Fort Worth Runners & Riders

Alright, Fort Worth, let's talk turkey about hitting the pavement or trails today, March 13th. The weather gods are mostly smiling on us, so no excuses about apocalyptic downpours or freezing limbs. We've got clear skies and a comfortable 10.3°C this morning, with the forecast pushing us toward a much warmer afternoon. Perfect for shedding those winter layers and remembering what sunshine feels like.

However, a word to the wise: just because the air *looks* clear doesn't mean it's entirely pristine for everyone. Our current Air Quality Index is a sweet 29, which is firmly in the "Good" category. But the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) is forecasting that ozone levels could creep into the "Moderate" range for the Dallas-Fort Worth area later this afternoon and early evening. For most of us, that's not a deal-breaker, but if you've got sensitive airways, you might want to stick to those morning miles.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Ozone Alert:** Keep an eye on the afternoon if you have respiratory sensitivities. While morning air should be fine, ozone tends to build later in the day, especially when it's sunny and warmer.
</AlertBox>
