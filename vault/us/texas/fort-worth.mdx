The ground is going to be slicker than a politician's promise, and those winds? They're not messing around. If you had plans for a long run on the Trinity Trails, you might want to consider the treadmill or some indoor work instead. Flash flooding wasn't just a threat yesterday; the aftermath is still very real, and those trails could be partially submerged or just plain muddy hazards.
