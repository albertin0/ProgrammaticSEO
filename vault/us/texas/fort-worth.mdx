### Weather & Trail Conditions

Today's temperature is a comfortable 20.7°C (around 69°F) and it's partly cloudy, which sounds inviting on paper. But don't let that fool you. We've got heavy rain showers rolling through this morning, with light rain expected to persist throughout the day. The real kicker is the ground: it's already soaked, and more rain means more runoff and potential for localized flooding.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Flood Watch! Stay Away From Low Water Crossings
Seriously, folks, don't be that person. A Flash Flood Watch is active for Tarrant County through this afternoon. Isolated spots could see up to 6 to 8 inches of rain. Roads can become impassable in minutes. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase; it's how you avoid becoming a statistic. This directly impacts popular spots along the Trinity Trails, especially any underpasses or low-lying sections near the river, like those around Gateway Park where intermittent closures have been ongoing due to TxDOT work on the Riverside Bridge. Even if the trails aren't officially "closed" by the Tarrant Regional Water District, common sense should tell you to avoid flooded areas.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergens

Good news for your lungs (if you can avoid the water): the Air Quality Index (AQI) for Fort Worth is holding strong at 22, which is "Good." The dominant pollutant is ozone, but at this level, it's not a concern for most folks. However, keep in mind there might be some light Saharan dust and residual smoke filtering in from distant burning activities, contributing to fine particulates, even if the overall AQI is good.

The "Citizens for Life March" has downtown Fort Worth tied up today, specifically affecting Main, 4th, Jones, and 2nd Streets. If your usual Sunday long run involves navigating downtown, reroute or hit the treadmill. This isn't a minor street fair; it's a planned event taking over major thoroughfares.
