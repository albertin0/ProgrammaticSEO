Alright, Fort Worth, it’s June 9th, and while the morning started off pretty sweet, don’t let that lull you into a false sense of security for your midday run.

Look, I get it. You woke up, saw that 18.1°C (64.6°F) and thought, "Finally, a break!" But unless you're a vampire or exclusively run pre-dawn, that ain't gonna last. The National Weather Service is screaming about highs in the mid-90s and "RealFeel" heat index values pushing *100 to 105 degrees Fahrenheit* today and Wednesday. That's not just "warm," that's "your-organs-might-start-slow-cooking" territory. This isn't a suggestion; it's a direct warning from someone who's seen too many runners get carted off because they thought they could "power through" a heat advisory.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### IMMEDIATE HEAT HAZARD!
The pleasant morning temperatures are a lie. Expect rapidly rising heat throughout the day with heat index values hitting 100-105°F. Seriously, postpone that long run or move it indoors. This isn't the day to test your limits against North Texas summer.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side for your lungs, the air quality is actually pretty decent. The AQI is sitting at a "Good" 30, and while ozone was a concern earlier in the month, today's forecast for the Dallas-Fort Worth area is generally good for ozone. However, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) mentions some slightly elevated fine particulate matter due to humidity, which can sometimes irritate those with respiratory issues, especially if you're out there gulping down hot, thick air. Pollen is moderate, so if you're usually sneezing your way through spring, you might feel a little reprieve, but it's not zero.

Now, for those of you who like to pound the pavement downtown, specifically around Sundance Square, be aware. While some of the earlier World Cup exhibit closures might be wrapping up, the official "Sundance Square World Cup Celebration" starts June 11th and runs through mid-July, bringing with it ongoing street closures on Main Street (from 2nd to 5th) and parts of 3rd and 4th Streets. If your urban loops typically take you through that area, you're going to be rerouting or dodging more than just traffic. And let's not forget the usual smattering of construction closures across the city; check the City of Fort Worth's road closures if you're running outside the main drag.

For the Trinity Trails crew, you generally have a clear path from what I can see, but keep an eye out for any pop-up events or local maintenance that isn't widely publicized. The real enemy today isn't a detour sign, it's that giant fireball in the sky and the suffocating humidity.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Smart Workout Tips
If you *must* run outside:
*   Hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). Start early and keep it up.
*   Go short. Seriously short. This isn't the day for a half-marathon simulation.
*   Stick to shaded areas, or hit the treadmill. There's no shame in AC.
*   Wear light-colored, moisture-wicking clothing. No cotton.
*   Listen to your body. Any signs of dizziness, nausea, or excessive fatigue mean you STOP.
</AlertBox>

Don't be a hero. It's a Tuesday in June, not the Olympic trials. Stay safe out there.
