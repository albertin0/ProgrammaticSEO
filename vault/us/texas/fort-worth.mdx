### On the Trails & Streets

Let's be real, the best thing about today is the thermometer. Get out there while you can, but don't wander blindly.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Lungs and Knees:** That "Moderate" AQI with PM2.5 isn't ideal, especially if you're asthmatic or have other respiratory issues. And those sustained gusts? They're not just annoying, they can throw off your balance and mess with your gait, putting extra strain on your joints. Pay attention to how your body feels, especially if you're logging serious mileage on the Trinity Trails.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Downtown Dance-Around:** If your workout takes you near downtown, brace yourself for some detours. The FIFA World Cup Celebration has major street closures in effect for Sundance Square from now until mid-July. Main Street, 3rd, and 4th Streets are impacted, so plan your route around those. And if you're thinking of running in the Near Southside this weekend, the Trinity Pride Fest tomorrow (Saturday, June 27th) means more closures on S. Main and West Broadway. Don't get caught jogging into a crowd of soccer fans or parade-goers.
</AlertBox>
