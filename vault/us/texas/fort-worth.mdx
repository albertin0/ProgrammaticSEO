## The Rundown: Don't Get Caught Off Guard

While the mercury is playing nice at a breezy 26.1°C (79°F), don't let it fool you into complacency. Today's fine, but things are about to get wet, real fast. If you're hitting the **Trinity Trails** today, enjoy the reprieve from the heat, but prepare for muddy, possibly impassable conditions very soon. The NWS is talking about "locally heavy rain and flooding" through Friday. That means your favorite riverside loops could turn into murky puddles faster than you can say "hydration vest."

### Air Quality & Allergies: Breathe Easy-ish

Your lungs aren't going to be screaming bloody murder today, but they're not exactly doing a victory lap either. With an AQI of 55, it's considered "Fair" for most, but if you're part of the "sensitive groups" club – and let's be honest, who isn't around here? – you might feel some minor to moderate symptoms if you're out there for a long haul. Add in that 'Moderate' pollen level, and you've got a recipe for some sniffles and maybe a cough, especially as that PM2.5 particulate matter hangs around.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead: Riverside Drive Bridge**
If your route takes you anywhere near the Fort Worth Floodway at the Riverside Drive Bridge, east of I-35W, brace yourself. Construction is ongoing, which means a temporary trail closure and detour. This project, which started back in August, is expected to last for another month or two. Stay alert for signage and don't assume the path is clear. Seriously, don't be *that* person who ignores the cones.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Future Trail Goodness (But Not Today): Bomber Spur Trail**
Good news on the horizon for trail lovers! Construction is underway on the Bomber Spur Trail, which will eventually add a sweet new connection to the Trinity River & Trails system, running from Calmont Avenue to US Route 377. It's not open yet, but it's something to look forward to!
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Incoming Deluge: Prepare for Flooding!**
A Flood Watch is active for Wednesday afternoon through Thursday afternoon. Two to five inches of rain (with isolated amounts up to eight) are expected. This *will* impact many low-lying trails along the Trinity and other waterways. Check local city updates for trail closures before you head out mid-week. "Turn Around Don't Drown" isn't just a catchy slogan; it's how you avoid being rescued by some poor soul in a boat.
</AlertBox>

So, enjoy the run today, Fort Worth. But be smart, be aware, and maybe plan your long run for tomorrow *inside* if you don't fancy a swim.
