## Lungs: Proceed with Caution (and a big "WTF?")

Let's talk about those lungs. The provided data says our Air Quality Index (AQI) is a lovely 29, which is "Good." Fantastic, right? Go forth and breathe deep. Except, hold your horses. AccuWeather, for today, May 7th, is forecasting a "Poor" AQI, explicitly stating the air "has reached a high level of pollution and is unhealthy for sensitive groups." They even suggest reducing time outside if you're experiencing symptoms. Plume Labs calls it "fair" and "moderately polluted," noting that long-term exposure *can* constitute a health risk.

So, what gives? Is Fort Worth gaslighting us? The reality is, air quality can change, and forecasts can differ. As a PT who's seen enough snot-nosed, coughing runners, I'm telling you: trust your body first. If it feels off, it probably is. The official numbers might say "good," but if that forecast for "poor" ozone materializes, especially later in the day, your lungs will be working harder than they need to. DFW is, after all, an EPA "serious" ozone nonattainment area from May to September, so this isn't exactly groundbreaking news for us.

Pollen is still hanging around at a "Moderate" level, so if you're one of the lucky ones who gets hit by every allergen, consider your usual antihistamine routine before lacing up.

## The Bottom Line

Today offers a mixed bag, mostly thanks to confusing air quality reports. The weather itself is nearly perfect for outdoor activity, and that's a rare gift. But you need to pay attention to how your body responds to the air. Don't be a hero; if your chest feels tight or you're wheezing, cut it short. Otherwise, get out there before the summer heat truly kicks in or another storm system decides to grace us with its presence later in the week.
