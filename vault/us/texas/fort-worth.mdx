But today, August 14, 2026, Fort Worth is essentially a giant convection oven with a side of wildfire anxiety. The National Weather Service isn't messing around; we're staring down a Heat Advisory that runs through Saturday. We're talking triple-digit actual temperatures and "feels like" temps that'll make you question all your life choices. Forget those long loops around the Trinity Trails if you're planning on being out mid-day. Early morning or late evening, and even then, keep it short, keep it shaded, and keep that water bottle glued to your hand.
