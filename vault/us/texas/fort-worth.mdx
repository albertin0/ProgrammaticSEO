Yesterday had some "potentially strong storms and flooding" in North Texas, with specific flash flood warnings up until Tuesday evening. You know the drill after a rain: uneven footing is an ankle sprain waiting to happen.

### Pollen & Air Quality: Breathe Easy, But Not Too Easy

That "Moderate" pollen isn't going to kill you, but it's enough to make your run miserable if you're susceptible. And about that air quality: while the general AQI is good, that TCEQ forecast for PM2.5 being "Moderate" due to regional smoke and dust is a real thing. PM2.5 are those tiny particles that get deep in your lungs. If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, maybe keep your distance from busy roads where that stuff concentrates, or just stick to a shorter, less intense workout today.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro Tip for Trail Users:** Before you head out to any specific section of the Trinity Trails, check the City of Fort Worth's website for road and trail closure updates. They've got a whole list of ongoing construction, like the full closures on parts of N. Commerce Street and E. Exchange Avenue that could affect your access downtown. Don't get stuck trying to navigate a construction zone when you're just trying to get your miles in.
</AlertBox>

Stay hydrated, stay aware, and don't forget to stretch. Especially those ankles.
