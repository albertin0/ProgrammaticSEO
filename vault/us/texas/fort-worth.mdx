<AlertBox type="warning">
### Fort Worth Nature Center & Refuge: Trails & Treats Mayhem

If your usual Sunday long run takes you through the Fort Worth Nature Center & Refuge, consider a different route today. It's their annual "Trails and Treats" festival, which means the "discovery trail" will be packed with families, treat stations, and all the associated hubbub. Unless your workout involves dodging small children in superhero costumes, you're better off finding some quieter asphalt. This is a family event, not a PR opportunity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zones: Know Before You Go

Fort Worth is always building something, and some of that "progress" impacts our workout routes. Several major construction projects are ongoing across the city. For example, there's significant work happening on the Everman Parkway Extension in south Fort Worth, including a new 750-foot bridge designed with multiuse trails for us. It's a good thing eventually, but a current headache. Also, keep an eye out for street closures downtown, particularly around E. Exchange Avenue and N. Main Street, as those areas have construction activity and various closures through the coming days. If your route takes you near Harmon Road or Westport Parkway, check the city's road closures page before you head out.
</AlertBox>

All that being said, if you pick your spot wisely and maybe avoid the main drag of the Nature Center, you're in for a good one. Don't let a little moderate pollen or some orange cones stop you from enjoying this rare gift of a weather day.
