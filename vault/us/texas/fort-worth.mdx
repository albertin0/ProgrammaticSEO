## The Rundown:

It's March 18th, and we're looking at a pleasant 18.1°C (about 64.6°F) and mainly clear skies. Perfect running weather, right? Almost. The wind isn't going to knock you over, and there's no immediate flooding risk, which is a nice change from earlier in the month. However, the air you're breathing and the routes you're taking still need a check.

The biggest annoyance for many will be the pollen. If you're susceptible to seasonal allergies, you already know the drill. Take your meds, pack some tissues, and maybe opt for an early morning workout before the pollen count really ramps up, or stick to indoor training today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Ozone on the Horizon:** While our current AQI is "Good," the dominant pollutant is ozone, and we're dancing on the edge of "Moderate." This is especially relevant for outdoor activities. If you have any respiratory conditions, listen to your body. Consider scaling back on high-intensity, long-duration workouts, especially between noon and early evening when ozone levels tend to be higher. Hydration is key to keeping airways moist.
</AlertBox>

Construction is a never-ending saga in our growing city. Don't be surprised by unexpected detours, especially if you're hitting parts of the Trinity Trails that connect to larger projects like the DFW Discovery Trail. Always keep an eye out for signage and be aware of your surroundings. Sharing the trail with unexpected construction vehicles or debris is not how you want to end your run.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Plan Your Route & Stay Alert:** If your usual loop takes you near major development areas, or through Benbrook via Chapin Road, check for alternative paths. The Fort Worth Botanic Garden is also going to be busier than usual with the butterfly exhibit drawing crowds. If you're looking for peace and quiet, maybe aim for a less-trafficked section of the Trinity Trails further from downtown or consider Eagle Mountain Lake Park.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today offers some great opportunities to get moving outside, but be smart about it. Check your allergies, be mindful of the ozone, and don't assume your usual route is clear.
