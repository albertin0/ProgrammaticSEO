## Workout Hazards & Tips

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Storms & Lightning Risk
Expect scattered showers and thunderstorms this afternoon. These aren't just a little sprinkle; they can bring gusty winds and frequent lightning. If you see dark clouds rolling in while you're out on the Trinity Trails, don't try to outrun Mother Nature. Head for cover. Fast.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Even When It's Cooler
Just because it's not 100 degrees doesn't mean you can skip the water. That underlying ozone in the air can still irritate your respiratory system, and staying well-hydrated helps your body cope. Keep that water bottle handy, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Out for Construction & Detours
Fort Worth is always building something, which means unexpected closures. There's ongoing construction that can impact your routes, like the various street closures downtown including E. Belknap St., and the massive I-30 corridor project in West Fort Worth. Check the City of Fort Worth's website for road closures *before* you head out, especially if your route takes you anywhere near downtown or major highway work. Getting rerouted mid-tempo run is annoying at best, dangerous at worst.
</AlertBox>
