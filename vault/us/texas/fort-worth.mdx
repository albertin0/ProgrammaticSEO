### The Heat is On (and On, and On)

Look, it’s August 31st in Fort Worth, so "hot" isn't exactly groundbreaking news. But 101 degrees with a heat index that's going to creep higher as the humidity rolls in? That’s not just "warm," that’s "call your grandma and make sure her AC is working" kind of hot. Even if you're out there at dawn, the ground is already radiating heat, and it won't take long for that sun to beat down. Think carefully about anything more strenuous than a short, slow walk around your block.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Today's forecast calls for temperatures hitting 101°F (38°C) this afternoon. Heat-related illnesses are a serious risk. Limit outdoor activities to early morning or late evening, if at all. Stay hydrated. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

### Don't Forget to Cough – It's Ozone Action Day

Our air quality is sitting at "Fair" with an AQI of 41, thanks to some lovely surface ozone. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) slapped an Ozone Action Day on us for a reason. That means atmospheric conditions are ripe for ozone pollution, which is basically lung irritant soup. If you have asthma, COPD, or really, any breathing apparatus, consider moving your workout indoors today. Even healthy folks can feel the effects with prolonged exposure.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone Action Day:** The Dallas-Fort Worth area is under an Ozone Action Day. Those with respiratory or heart conditions, please limit outdoor exposure. Even healthy individuals should consider scaling back intensity or moving indoors to avoid lung irritation.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Report & Construction Headaches

The Trinity Trails are generally awesome, but if you're planning a long run, remember there's still a reroute at Gateway Park due to riverbank erosion. It's navigable, but it’s an extra mental hurdle when you’re already battling the elements.

As for street runners and cyclists, the City of Fort Worth has a laundry list of ongoing construction projects. Everything from Austin Road to Cantrell Sansom Road to parts of Marine Creek Parkway have lane or full closures, some stretching into late September or even 2027. Always check for current road closures before you head out, especially if your route involves main thoroughfares or crosses under any major bridges. Expect delays, flaggers, and unexpected detours.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trinity Trails Update:** A section of the Trinity Trails at Gateway Park is currently rerouted due to riverbank erosion. Plan your route accordingly, and be mindful of the temporary path.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, today is a day for the treadmill or some heavy lifting indoors. If you absolutely *must* get outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
