## The Weather: Don't Get Caught Off Guard

Sure, it's a comfortable 19.9°C with overcast skies in Fort Worth this morning, which sounds pretty darn good for a run or ride. But don't let the current calm fool you. The National Weather Service predicted a solid 1 to 5 inches of rain for North Texas through today, Friday, May 22nd, bringing with it an increased risk of isolated flooding. We even saw strong thunderstorms with penny-sized hail and 40 mph wind gusts in nearby Wise and Parker counties yesterday, Thursday. "Repeated rounds of rain could lead to additional flooding concerns" through the weekend, so even if the rain isn't hammering down right this second, trails could already be saturated, muddy, and ripe for unexpected closures or slick spots.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Flash Flood Risk:** Seriously, don't mess with floodwaters. Even a few inches can sweep you off your feet or hide hazards. If you encounter any flooded sections on the Trinity Trails or elsewhere, turn around. It's not worth it.
</AlertBox>

## Air Quality & Allergies: Manageable, But Still There

The air quality is actually pretty good today, with an AQI of 59 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's well within the "good" range, so your lungs should be able to breathe easy on that front. However, we're still sitting at a "Moderate" pollen level. For those of us with seasonal allergies, that means you might still experience some sneezing, sniffles, or itchy eyes during your workout.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Allergy Alert:** If you're sensitive to moderate pollen, consider taking your antihistamines *before* you head out. A quick rinse-off after your workout can also help remove pollen from your skin and hair, preventing prolonged exposure.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Conditions & Construction Alerts

Beyond the weather, there are a few local headaches to be aware of. Construction is a constant in Fort Worth, and several projects could impact your route. For example, Phase I of the new Bomber Spur Trail, which will eventually connect the Clear Fork and West Fork trails, broke ground in April, and work is actively underway. While exciting for future connectivity, this means parts of that corridor are active construction zones.

Also, don't forget the ongoing widening project on the Chisholm Trail Parkway. Daytime road closures are happening this week, Wednesday through Friday, May 20-22, on sections like the southbound County Road 920 entrance ramp. If you're driving to a trail access point near these areas, plan for potential detours and delays. Check the City of Fort Worth's road closure maps before you leave.

Finally, a heads-up for tomorrow, Saturday, May 23rd: the "Memorial Day Run 5k" is scheduled for the Trinity Trails. While it's not today, if you're planning an early weekend long run, expect increased traffic and potentially some slower sections around the event start and finish lines.
