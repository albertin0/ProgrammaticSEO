## Today's Fort Worth Workout Hazards: The Nitty-Gritty

### Heatstroke isn't a Badge of Honor

With highs pushing 99°F and a heat index in the triple digits, staying hydrated isn't enough; avoiding the midday sun is critical. If you insist on getting out there, aim for sunrise, not high noon. Even then, cut your workout short. Heat-related illnesses are no joke, and emergency services are already dealing with calls when it gets this hot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Today's "feels like" temperatures of 100-110°F (38-43°C) mean a high risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Limit outdoor activity, stay hydrated, and seek air-conditioned spaces.
</AlertBox>

### Wind vs. Your Willpower

Those 15-25 mph winds, gusting over 40, are going to be a constant battle. They'll dehydrate you faster, make uneven terrain on the **Trinity Trails** more precarious, and generally just sap your energy. If you're planning a bike ride, expect to be working twice as hard in one direction and fighting to control your bike in the other. It's also prime dust-kicking weather, adding another irritant to your respiratory system.

### Air You Don't Want to Breathe

Our AQI might technically be "Moderate" at 52, but AccuWeather is already calling it "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" at 110 due to ozone. Ozone, the dominant pollutant, thrives in hot, sunny conditions, especially with vehicle emissions from corridors like I-820 and I-30. Sensitive individuals will feel it in their lungs. Add in the moderate grass and weed pollen, which thrives in this kind of weather, and your respiratory system is doing overtime.
