### Today's Fort Worth Grind (or lack thereof)

Seriously, do yourself a favor and consider an indoor workout. Treadmill, elliptical, bodyweight circuit in your air-conditioned living room – anything. The National Weather Service isn't messing around when they tell you to "reduce, eliminate, or reschedule strenuous activities" for early morning or late evening. If you must be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job. This isn't the day for a long haul on the Trinity Trails, unless you're planning on being scraped off the pavement.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: This isn't just uncomfortable, it's dangerous.
The heat index in Fort Worth today is expected to reach hazardous levels. Symptoms of heat exhaustion include heavy sweating, weakness, and clammy skin. Heat stroke is a medical emergency with symptoms like a body temperature of 106°F or higher, hot dry skin, and a strong pulse. Call 9-1-1 immediately if you suspect heat stroke.
</AlertBox>

With a moderate AQI dominated by PM2.5, those with respiratory sensitivities might experience irritation. And don't forget the moderate pollen level adding to the fun. Reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors.
