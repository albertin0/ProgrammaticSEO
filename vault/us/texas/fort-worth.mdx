## The Full Rundown: What to Know Before You Go

Look, I love hitting the Trinity Trails as much as the next runner, but if your usual route takes you through Gateway Park, you're going to hit a wall. A significant portion of the Trinity Trail system in Gateway Park is still shut down, thanks to persistent flooding and river bank erosion. It's been an issue, and it's not going away today. For your own safety, just avoid those sections. Don't be "that guy" trying to hop fences; it's not worth a sprained ankle or worse.

On top of that, there's ongoing construction along the Fort Worth Floodway near the Riverside Drive Bridge. This project, which kicked off back in August, has temporary trail closures and detours in place. We're talking 80 to 120 days of work, depending on the weather, so plan your mileage accordingly if that's your usual stomping ground.

Now, about that air. While the temperature is a comfortable 18.1°C (about 64.6°F) and it's mainly clear, the Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 54, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. AccuWeather even indicates an AQI of 52, which they deem "unhealthy for sensitive groups." So, if you've got any respiratory issues, or if you're just generally prone to feeling cruddy on "moderate" air days, maybe dial back the intensity or shorten your workout. It's not worth trading a good run for a wheezy evening.

There are also the usual Fort Worth delights of ongoing road construction on I-20 and I-30 that might affect your commute *to* the trails, but won't directly impact your run unless you're, you know, running on the interstate. Also, don't be surprised by potential railroad crossing delays if you're driving around, but that's a whole other kind of headache.

**Air Quality Alert:** The AQI is moderate due to PM2.5, which could affect sensitive individuals. If you have asthma or other respiratory conditions, consider a shorter workout or sticking to indoor options today. Listen to your body!

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Trinity Trails CLOSED:** Avoid closed sections of the Trinity Trails, especially in Gateway Park due to erosion and flooding. Additionally, expect detours and closures near the Riverside Drive Bridge due to ongoing floodway construction. Don't risk injury for a few extra miles.
</AlertBox>

**Pollen Power:** With moderate pollen levels, if you're prone to allergies, consider taking an antihistamine before you head out, or wear a hat and sunglasses to keep some of it out of your eyes. A shower immediately after your run can also help wash off residual pollen.
