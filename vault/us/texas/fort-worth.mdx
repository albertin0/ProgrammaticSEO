### Heat: It's Not a Suggestion, It's a Threat

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning!** Temperatures will hit 101°F, with the heat index reaching 108°F this afternoon. This is dangerously hot. Limit outdoor activity, especially between noon and 8 PM. Hydrate constantly.
</AlertBox>

Running in this kind of heat puts immense strain on your heart and can quickly lead to heat stroke. Your body struggles to cool itself when the air temperature is higher than your skin temperature, and humidity makes it even worse by preventing sweat from evaporating. If you *must* exercise outdoors, go before 9 AM or after 7 PM. Even then, drastically reduce your pace and distance. Carry more water than you think you need, and don't be afraid to cut your run short if you start feeling lightheaded, dizzy, or overly fatigued. These aren't signs of weakness; they're signs you're not trying to become a statistic.
