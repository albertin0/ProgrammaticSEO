## Fort Worth Forecast: Clear Start, Stormy Finish, and All the Usual Headaches

Alright, Fort Worth, May 16th is serving up a bit of a mixed bag. This morning is looking clear with a comfortable 23°C (73°F), perfect for an early start on the Trinity Trails. Get out there and enjoy it, because the back half of your day could be a whole different story. The National Weather Service is predicting afternoon highs to climb into the upper 80s to lower 90s, which is warm enough on its own. But here's the kicker: we're squarely in the risk zone for severe thunderstorms, complete with potential large hail, damaging winds, and isolated tornadoes from late afternoon into the night. Seriously, this isn't the day to push that long evening run.

### Air Quality & Allergens

Let's talk about what you're breathing. The official Air Quality Index (AQI) might be sitting at a decent 40, which is "Good" by EPA standards, with Ozone as the dominant pollutant. However, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) was forecasting ozone to hit the "lower end of the 'Moderate' range" for Dallas-Fort Worth today. AccuWeather even pegged today as "Poor" for air quality, unhealthy for sensitive groups. News reports from earlier this week highlighted "Ozone Action Days" for our area, urging folks to limit driving to reduce emissions. So, while the number looks okay now, there's clearly some background nastiness in the air that could escalate, especially with those rising temperatures. If you're sensitive, maybe keep it indoors or shorten your outdoor time, especially later in the day when ozone tends to peak.

And for those of us with perpetually running noses, pollen is having a field day. We're at a medium-high level, with grass, osage orange, and oak doing their best to make you sneeze your way through your workout. Stock up on those antihistamines before you hit the door.
