### Weather & Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply, Just Kidding (Mostly)

Today's shaping up to be a pretty sweet day, weather-wise. We're looking at clear skies and a comfortable 18.1°C (about 64°F) which is just about ideal for getting those miles in. No oppressive heat, no biting cold, just right. The National Weather Service even says "severe weather is not expected" for Fort Worth today, despite some earlier warnings for the region. Looks like we dodged a bullet there.

Now, for your lungs: The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 29, which is considered "Good." You'd think that's a green light, right? Well, it is, for most of us. But here's the kicker: Fort Worth, bless its heart, lives in an EPA "serious" ozone nonattainment area. That means between May and September, ground-level ozone regularly exceeds federal health standards. So, even on a "Good" AQI day, if you're someone who gets sensitive to ozone, especially during prolonged exertion, today's "Ozone/Other" dominant pollutant for DFW in the forecast should still keep you mildly on alert. Just something to file away in your "Texas Air Quirks" mental folder.

### Trail Conditions & Construction: Obstacle Course Fort Worth

This is where things get interesting, as usual. If you're planning on hitting the **Trinity Trails**, specifically around Trinity Park, be aware that "parts of Trinity Park are closed for Mayfest setup." While Mayfest itself might be wrapping up or have concluded, teardown and cleanup can keep sections closed longer than you'd expect. Don't assume everything's clear until you see it for yourself. Always a good idea to have a backup route.

And if you're more of a nature-lover, the Fort Worth Nature Center & Refuge has a specific "Trail Closure" for **Forked-Tail Creek**. They're not just closing it for fun, so respect the signs.

On a broader note, Fort Worth is basically one giant construction zone these days. We just passed a massive $845 million bond package for streets, parks, and other improvements, and there's big development happening with the Convention Center expansion and the Panther Island project along the Trinity River. While these aren't all hitting *your specific running path* today, it means detours, heavy equipment, and general disruption are part of the Fort Worth outdoor workout experience. Keep your head up and your ears open.
