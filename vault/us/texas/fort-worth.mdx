## Weather & Local Hazards

The weather is playing nice today. We're looking at a comfortable 25.2°C (that's about 77°F for those of us still converting in our heads) and clear skies. No oppressive humidity, no sudden downpours, no crazy winds trying to turn your run into a sail-boat simulation. It's prime running weather, honestly.

As for local hazards, I checked the usual suspects—wildfires, major construction blocking routes, flash flood warnings—and things are surprisingly quiet. No huge events are shutting down sections of the Trinity Trails that I could find. So you can plan your usual loop without worrying about unexpected detours or getting soaked by an impromptu sprinkler system (unless someone's lawn has a mind of its own).

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** Even though it's clear, always carry water. 25 degrees Celsius might feel comfortable, but the Texas sun still saps your hydration. Especially if you're doing a longer session along the Trinity River where shade can be sparse. Don't be that person collapsed on a bench near the Woodshed, wondering why your water bottle is still in the car.
</AlertBox>

## The Bottom Line

Today's a good day to get out there and log some miles. The temperature is spot on. Just be mindful of that "moderate" air quality if you've got sensitive lungs, and prep for moderate pollen if allergies are your nemesis. Otherwise, enjoy the clear skies and comfortable temps on whatever Fort Worth path you choose to conquer.
