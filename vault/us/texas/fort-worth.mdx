## The Rundown: Don't Get Complacent

Look, the air quality is "Good" with an AQI of 47, which is about as good as it gets in North Texas, so breathe easy on that front. The overcast skies are a nice change from the usual sun-baked mornings, and the 23.2°C (about 74°F) start feels decent. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a breeze out there.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Storms & Rising Heat
The forecast is calling for a 20 percent chance of showers and thunderstorms *after* 10 AM, and temperatures are predicted to hit 92°F today. By Sunday, we're looking at heat index values as high as 96°F. If you're heading out later, be ready for that humidity to smack you in the face, and keep an eye on the sky. Thunderstorms pop up faster than a speed trap on I-35W.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen's Annoying Persistence

Pollen's still hanging around at a "Moderate" level. If you're one of those unlucky souls whose nose runs more than you do, consider an indoor workout or double up on your allergy meds. There's nothing fun about trying to hit a personal best while simultaneously feeling like you're drowning.

## Trail & Road Woes: Fort Worth's Constant Obstacle Course

Now, let's talk about actually *getting* your workout in. The Fort Worth road closure list is a beast. The City has ongoing construction projects all over the place. For instance, sections of Cantrell Sansom Road and Old Denton Road are fully closed for the long haul, and there are numerous lane closures impacting major roads like I-20 and I-30 around areas like Chapel Creek Blvd and Linkcrest Dr for bridge work and pavement maintenance. Trying to navigate these areas, especially during peak traffic, is a workout in itself before you even start running.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trinity Trails: Watch for Muck and Detours
While there aren't any *new* specific trail closures for the glorious 100+ miles of the Trinity Trails themselves directly related to active flooding today, parts of Denton County were under a flood warning until this morning with minor flooding reported. This means some lower-lying sections of our beloved river trails, particularly north of downtown or near creek crossings, could still be muddy, slick, or have lingering standing water. Don't be "that guy" who ruins a good pair of shoes or takes an unexpected dip. Stick to higher ground or well-paved sections if you're unsure.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Downtown Dodging
If you usually run through downtown, be aware that the Sundance Square World Cup Celebration is still going strong until July 19th, bringing with it street closures on Main Street (2nd to 5th Street) and parts of 3rd and 4th Streets (Houston to Commerce). Plan your route to avoid getting rerouted by soccer fans and barricades.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's workout is doable, but it requires a bit more planning and situational awareness than usual. Stay hydrated, be smart about that rising heat, and keep your eyes peeled for construction zones and damp patches.
