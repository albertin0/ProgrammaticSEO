Look, I get it. The 18.1°C (that's about 64.6°F for those who still measure in freedom units) and "Mainly Clear" is looking pretty inviting right now, especially after yesterday's scorcher where the heat index hit a delightful 103 degrees. But don't be fooled. That's a temporary lull before the storm, literally. The National Weather Service is calling for showers and thunderstorms to continue this morning, with 1-2 inches of rain and flooding possible in areas.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Severe Weather Incoming!** Today is a First Alert Weather Day for a reason. Damaging winds, lightning, and heavy rain leading to flooding are all on the table for Fort Worth this afternoon. If you're out on the Trinity Trails and hear thunder, you're already too close. Get inside immediately. Lightning doesn't care about your PR.
</AlertBox>

If you insist on getting your miles in, hit it early. Like, *really* early. Before 10 AM. Even then, keep an eye on the sky and check radar before you step out. Later in the day, the risk just isn't worth it. You twist an ankle avoiding a flooded pothole on Cantrell Sansom Road (which is fully closed in parts anyway), or worse, get struck by lightning, and you'll be seeing me in the PT clinic, not on the trail. And trust me, I'll say "I told you so."

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues:** Don't expect smooth sailing on city streets or sidewalks. From Azle Avenue to North Commerce Street, there are numerous lane and full road closures due to ongoing construction. Even I-35E frontage roads have seen overnight closures that might just be wrapping up this morning. Plan your route meticulously or stick to known, clear paths.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Pestering:** We're still in "moderate" territory for pollen. For many, that means itchy eyes and a runny nose. If you're one of them, maybe double up on your allergy meds, or reconsider that extra long run. Today's humidity won't do you any favors either.
</AlertBox>
