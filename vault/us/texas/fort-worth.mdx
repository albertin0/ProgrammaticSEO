While the weather's playing nice, this city is perpetually under construction. You know the drill.

  **Construction Alert: Chisholm Trail Parkway (CTP):** Expect daytime closures on the southbound entrance ramp for Country Road 920 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. this week. Also, the FM 1902 exit ramp is closed through April 1. If your route involves getting to or from CTP, plan for detours. This isn't directly *on* the trails, but it might mess with your commute to your favorite starting point.
</AlertBox>

There's also ongoing construction listed for parts of the Trinity Trails system, specifically "Phase 2 River Trails Park to River Legacy Park" which was listed in "Construction Phase" as of last year, with a completion date of 2025. And the DFW Discovery Trail alignment is expected to be completed in 2026. This means while major closures might not be active on *every* section, keep an eye out for equipment, temporary path shifts, or muddy areas if you're on these routes. Don't be that runner who acts surprised when a backhoe appears on their favorite segment. Pay attention to signage.

No major events are blocking trails today, and thankfully, we're not dealing with any fresh flooding or wildfire smoke. Those high winds everyone was talking about for mid-week? They're still a day or two out, so enjoy the relatively calm air.

Overall, it’s a pretty good day to lace up and get out there. Just be smart about your route and keep an eye out for construction near major thoroughfares connecting to our excellent trail systems.
