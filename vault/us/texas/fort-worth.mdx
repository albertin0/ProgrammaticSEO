## Today's Fort Worth Workout Reality Check

Look, I'm not going to lie, 18.1°C (that's about 65°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) with "Mainly Clear" skies sounds like a runner's dream. Throw in an AQI of 29, and your lungs are practically throwing a party. So, if you're reading this before noon, lace up and get out there. *Now*. This is your window.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Storms Incoming!
Don't get lulled into a false sense of security by the morning calm. The National Weather Service and CBS Texas are both calling for a risk of severe weather hitting North Texas between 4 p.m. and 8 p.m. today. We're talking scattered thunderstorms, with the potential for large hail, damaging wind gusts, and localized flooding, especially west of I-35. "Mainly Clear" is a morning affair; your afternoon could be a washout or worse. Get caught out there in a hailstorm on the Chisholm Trail Parkway, and you'll regret not taking that early morning spin.
</AlertBox>

### Race Day Reroutes & Road Blocks

Trying to enjoy a peaceful jog on the Trinity Trails today? Good luck. The Heroes and Villains Half Marathon is kicking off right here in Fort Worth, along with a 5K, Quarter Marathon, and Half Marathon. That's a lot of spandex and sweat equity converging on our pathways from 7 a.m. to 11 a.m. You'll likely encounter crowds and potential detours on popular sections, especially near the Marine Creek Parkway area, which is also currently dealing with several lane closures due to ongoing construction. If you weren't registered, consider finding a less-trafficked neighborhood loop, or prepare to be an unscheduled spectator.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Escape Route
If you're still determined to get your workout in later today, stick close to home or have an indoor backup plan. Thunderstorms, hail, and strong winds are no joke, and running on wet, potentially flooded roads or trails is a recipe for a twisted ankle. Also, keep an eye on road closures. The City of Fort Worth has several construction projects affecting traffic and accessibility, including various lane closures along Marine Creek Parkway through October. You don't want to get stuck trying to navigate detours when the sky opens up.
</AlertBox>
