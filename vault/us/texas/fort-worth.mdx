## Today's Fort Worth Grind: Ozone's Back, Y'all

Alright, Fort Worth, May 29th. We've got a "Mainly Clear" sky and a temperature that's a respectable 18.1°C (about 64.6°F) right now, making for a pleasant start to your run or ride. Enjoy it while it lasts. The forecast says we're heading up to 91°F (33°C) later, and that humidity isn't going anywhere fast, currently sitting at 87%.

But let's not pretend this is a perfect day. While the Air Quality Index (AQI) might be sitting at a seemingly benign 29, which is "Good," the dominant pollutant is Ozone/Other. And here's the kicker: the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) has issued an "Ozone Action Day" for the entire Dallas-Fort Worth area, in effect until 6:15 PM today. What does that mean for you? It means atmospheric conditions are ripe for ground-level ozone to build up as the day heats up, making that "Good" AQI a bit of a mirage for later in the afternoon. AccuWeather even calls today's air quality "Fair," warning that sensitive groups might experience minor to moderate symptoms with prolonged exposure. So, if you're prone to lung irritation, asthma, or just generally prefer not breathing in tiny bits of car exhaust, get your workout done and dusted before lunch.

## Fort Worth Trail Updates

Beyond the air, there's always something trying to trip you up on the physical front. Construction, for instance. The City of Fort Worth has "Las Vegas Trail" from Western Hills Blvd to West Freeway under various construction activities until the end of the year. Expect potential lane or full closures there. Also, keep in mind that a new "CentrePort Trail" is under construction to connect Fort Worth to Arlington, starting near the Trinity Railway Express CentrePort Station. While great for future connectivity, these big projects often mean detours and unexpected obstacles on existing routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Out for Construction Zones
If your route takes you near Las Vegas Trail or the areas around the CentrePort TRE Station, be prepared for potential detours or even trail closures. Don't assume your usual path is clear. Check the city's road closure maps before you head out.
</AlertBox>

So, overall, it's a decent day if you're smart about your timing. Get out early, stay hydrated, and don't try to PR on a lung-busting run when the ozone is cooking later.
