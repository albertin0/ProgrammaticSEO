### Ozone Action Day: This ain't a drill.
Today is an Ozone Action Day. This isn't just a suggestion; it means conditions are favorable for high levels of ground-level ozone, which is an irritant to your respiratory system. Sensitive groups – that includes asthmatics, the elderly, and kids – should seriously consider keeping their workouts indoors. Even healthy individuals can experience difficulty breathing and throat irritation with prolonged exposure. A 64-degree day might feel nice, but breathing tainted air isn't worth the personal best.
</AlertBox>
