Today's air quality index is a respectable 49, with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant. That's well within the "Good" range, so your cardio shouldn't feel like you're sucking down car exhaust. However, let's not forget Friday's little incident. An electric vehicle fire in West Fort Worth, near Camp Bowie Boulevard, decided to burn for over 17 hours, spewing a "plume of dark smoke" that was visible for miles. While the smoke has likely dissipated by now, if you're particularly sensitive or were running in that area on Friday, just be mindful.

### Trail Troubles & Road Rage

Here's where things get a bit Fort Worth-y. While there are no severe weather warnings currently active (shocking, I know), the city's perpetual state of construction continues to be your biggest outdoor workout adversary.

Specifically, if you're planning a run or ride along the **Trinity Trails**, be aware of ongoing construction on the Fort Worth Floodway near the Riverside Drive Bridge. This project kicked off back in August and comes with a "temporary trail closure and detour" that's expected to last 80 to 120 days. Don't be that person ignoring the signs and wandering into an active construction zone. Follow the detour, even if it adds a little mileage to your Strava.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trinity Trails Alert:** Construction at the Fort Worth Floodway near the Riverside Drive Bridge means detours. Respect the closure signs; safety first, PR second.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the trails, general road construction is still a thing. TxDOT has ongoing lane and ramp closures on I-20 and I-30, mainly affecting drivers, but it's worth noting if your commute to your favorite park or trail involves these highways. Local road closures for various city projects are also peppered throughout town, so if your route takes you on streets like Marine Creek Parkway or Flagstone Street, check the City of Fort Worth's road closure updates.

No major events are blocking major trails, and honestly, a brush fire in Kaufman County isn't going to affect your run down the Katy Trail today.

So, get out there. The weather's agreeable, the air's mostly clean, just keep an eye out for construction cones and follow those detours. Your knees will thank you for not hurdling barricades.
