### Weather Whiplash Alert

Today starts off "mainly clear" at 18.1°C (about 65°F), which sounds lovely, right? Don't let it lull you into a false sense of security. We're looking at gusty southerly winds up to 35 mph throughout the day, which can kick up dust, debris, and, yes, that moderate pollen count.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Severe Storms Expected Tonight!
Seriously, I'm not kidding. The Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex is under a Level 2 out of 5 risk for severe storms tonight. We're talking damaging winds up to 60 mph, hail, and even isolated tornadoes. Storms are expected to develop to our west around 6 p.m. and hit the metroplex by 11 p.m. If your workout extends into the evening, you need to have an exit strategy. This isn't the night for "one last mile."
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergy Report

Our air quality is sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 60, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most of us, that's just a bit annoying, but if you've got sensitive lungs, you might feel some irritation. Combine that with the moderate pollen level, and those gusty winds become a double-edged sword: they might clear out some stagnant air, but they're also excellent at distributing every tiny bit of allergen directly into your face. Plan accordingly, and maybe consider an indoor option if you're prone to respiratory drama.

### Construction and Other Annoyances

As always, Fort Worth is a city in constant motion, which means construction. The city has over 12,000 projects on file, and daily road closures are just a fact of life. Watch for those "Road Closed" signs, especially around areas like Azle Avenue or N. Main Street, where various construction activities are ongoing this week. Don't be that person trying to squeeze through a coned-off area just to save a minute. Your ankles (and sanity) will thank you.

#### Pro-Tip: Evening Workout Alternative
Given the severe weather threat later tonight, if you absolutely *must* get some exercise in the evening, stick to a treadmill, hit the gym, or do some bodyweight stuff indoors. Trying to outrun a squall line on the Trinity Trails is not a good look, and frankly, it's just asking for trouble.
