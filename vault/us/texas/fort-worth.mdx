## Fort Worth Forecast: Mind the Air, Watch Your Step

Alright, let's talk brass tacks. That crisp 18.1°C (about 65°F) this morning? Chef's kiss for a run. Seriously, soak it in while you can, because the National Weather Service is already talking about heat indices hitting triple digits by Tuesday, a real kick in the teeth after this lovely reprieve. But for *today*, your joints are going to thank you.

Your lungs, though, might have a different opinion. We're sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 64 with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That's not a red alert, but it's enough to notice, especially if you're sensitive or planning a long, hard effort. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) confirms we're dealing with "slightly elevated urban fine particulate levels" across the region, partly thanks to light morning winds keeping everything stirred up, and a plume of Saharan dust just hanging out over most of the state. So, while it looks clear, you're breathing in more than just fresh air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads up, Runners and Cyclists!** The "Red, White and Blue Half Marathon" is happening *today*, July 19th, from 7:00 AM to almost noon, with various distances. While specific trail impacts aren't always crystal clear ahead of time, expect increased congestion and potential temporary closures around popular running routes. Yesterday also saw the "CRC Hell's Half Acre Half Marathon & 5K" rip through Trinity Park and the Trinity Trails, so even if the race itself is done, cleanup crews or lingering participants could still be out.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of Trinity Trails, don't expect smooth sailing everywhere. Phase 2 of the Trinity Trails extension project, running from River Trails Park to River Legacy Park, is still very much in the "Construction Phase" and isn't slated for completion until 2027 (or 2025, depending on which city update you read). And if you frequent the Marine Creek area, Cromwell Marine Creek Road is getting widened, which includes new pedestrian lanes and a new bridge over the creek, but the roundabout there won't be finished until August. Expect intermittent one-lane closures in the meantime. Plus, a new trailhead at Marine Creek Lake Park is under construction. While the six-mile loop trail is open, the parking area for the Ten Mile Bridge section is closed. Keep an eye out for detour signs, uneven surfaces, and construction vehicles.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro-Tip:** If you're on two wheels, remember that while you're dodging construction, Texas law enforcement is currently in "Operation Slowdown" mode until August 2nd, increasing speed enforcement across the state. Even though it's geared towards drivers, being aware of increased police presence and encouraging slower traffic can make your ride safer, especially near road construction zones where fines are doubled.
</AlertBox>
