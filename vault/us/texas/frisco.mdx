## Today's Frisco Workout Reality Check

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next runner, but today is not the day to push it. The environmental data for Frisco on March 8, 2026, is screaming "stay inside."

### The Heat is On (Way Too On)

Let's be blunt: 39.8°C is an insane temperature for any outdoor exertion, let alone a sustained workout. This isn't just "hot," this is "heatstroke is a very real possibility" hot. Even though the sky might be "mainly clear," that sun is going to bake you. Forget your PRs; focus on not becoming a statistic. If you absolutely, positively *must* move your body outside, keep it short, stick to the shade if you can find any, and hydrate like it's your job. Seriously, pre-hydrate, during-hydrate, and post-hydrate. This isn't a suggestion, it's survival. Remember that advice from May 2024 about limiting strenuous activities during extreme heat? It applies tenfold today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** 39.8°C (103.64°F) is dangerously hot. Outdoor exercise can lead to rapid dehydration, heat exhaustion, and heatstroke. Limit time outside, seek shade, and hydrate relentlessly. Do NOT ignore symptoms of heat illness.
</AlertBox>

### What's In The Air?

Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 80, with PM2.5 being the main culprit. For most people, you might just feel a bit sluggish. But if you've got asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, you're going to notice it. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and it's a double whammy for your airways. Your body is already working overtime to cool itself in this heat; don't make it fight for every breath too.
