## The Rundown

Look, let's be real. Houston's never going to win any awards for "most uninterrupted running trails." Today is no exception. While the mercury is finally behaving itself at a comfortable 24.3°C and the skies are partly cloudy, perfect for an outdoor workout, the city's relentless construction is once again playing whack-a-mole with your running plans.

The big one you need to watch out for is the ongoing I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project. Folks trying to hit the **White Oak Bayou Greenway** near Studemont Street and I-45 are still dealing with lane reductions and bridge demolition that's been going on "until further notice". This isn't just a mild inconvenience; it can force you onto less safe routes or add unexpected detours. Don't be that person trying to outsmart a backhoe.

And if you were eyeing the **Brays Bayou Trail**, you're out of luck near Spur 5. That section is still completely shut down thanks to the SH 35 expansion. We're talking "no good detour" territory here, so plan on rerouting your entire session if that's your usual stomping ground. It's frustrating, I know, but running headfirst into a construction zone is generally frowned upon, mostly by your knees.

Air quality-wise, we're in the clear. The AQI is a respectable 39, with "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant, which is a far cry from some of our usual smog-filled mornings. So, no real lung-burn from pollution today. However, the pollen count is sitting at a "Moderate" level, so if you're one of the many Houstonians who considers seasonal allergies their mortal enemy, remember your meds or maybe stick to an indoor track.
