### Air Quality Report

Alright, let's break down what you're breathing today. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a 58, which officially falls into the "Moderate" category. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, those tiny particles that love to get deep into your lungs and irritate everything. For most folks, this isn't going to be a showstopper, but if you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you might feel it more than usual. Don't be surprised if your usual route around Buffalo Bayou Park feels a little heavier today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  With PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant and an AQI of 58, consider scaling back intensity, especially if you're susceptible to respiratory irritation. It's not a "stay inside" day, but it's definitely not a "set a PR" day.
</AlertBox>
