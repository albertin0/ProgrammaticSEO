Look, I get it. You've got runs to do, miles to log. But today isn't a day for PRs on the Buffalo Bayou trails unless your PR involves avoiding heatstroke. We're looking at actual air temperatures in the low to mid-90s Fahrenheit, but with the humidity, it'll *feel* like 101 to 108 degrees Fahrenheit for most of the afternoon. The National Weather Service even has a Heat Advisory slapped across our entire region, urging you to cut back on outdoor time between 10 AM and 6 PM. Your body isn't going to "adapt" to that kind of oppressive mugginess; it's going to struggle.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Caution for Heat-Related Illness:** With heat index values soaring into the triple digits, heat exhaustion and heatstroke are real, immediate threats. If you're out there, hydrate constantly – and I mean *constantly* – and take frequent breaks in the shade. It's not a sign of weakness; it's basic survival.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the air. Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a charming 103, which is "Moderate." The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, those tiny particulate matters that love to burrow deep into your lungs and irritate everything. To make things even more… *Houston*, we've got Saharan dust in the mix, actively contributing to that hazy sky and questionable breathing air for sensitive groups. So, if you've got asthma, COPD, or just enjoy not coughing up a lung, today is not your day for high-intensity intervals.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Breath:** With moderate AQI and PM2.5 as the culprit, coupled with Saharan dust, your respiratory system is under stress. If you're unusually sensitive or have pre-existing conditions, consider moving your workout indoors or scaling it back significantly.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget our old friend, construction. While not a direct air quality issue, Memorial Park is still undergoing its massive "Memorial Groves" project. Construction on a 0.7-mile section of the Seymour Lieberman Trail is underway, with a reroute planned to keep it open. So, if you're navigating the park, keep an eye out for equipment and temporary detours. It’s not a full closure, but it means you need to be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're already feeling fuzzy from the heat.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable:** Seriously. Electrolytes aren't just for Instagram fitness gurus. Drink up *before*, *during*, and *after* any outdoor activity. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and don't be a hero. Cut your workout short if you feel lightheaded, nauseous, or just generally miserable.
</AlertBox>
