### The Air You Breathe (or Wheeze)

Look, it's not an "Ozone Action Day" like last week, thank goodness. Those days back on October 9th and 10th were rough, with air quality hitting "very poor" levels and ozone making sensitive folks cough up a lung. Today, the AQI is holding steady at 55, which technically puts us in the "Moderate" category. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, meaning those fine particulate matter particles are still floating around. So, if you've got asthma or are just generally sensitive, don't pretend like it's mountain air out there.

Then there's the pollen. It's "Moderate" overall, but don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. We're smack in the middle of fall allergy season, and the cold front rolling in tomorrow is actually expected to stir up *more* ragweed. Prepare for the sniffles.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **AQI is Moderate (PM2.5 & Ozone):** If you're susceptible to respiratory issues, keep your longer, harder efforts indoors or adjust your route away from busy roadways. That cold front brewing for tomorrow is going to kick up some pollen too, so allergy sufferers, consider your meds.
</AlertBox>

### Your Trails, Our Construction Zone

This is where my patience wears thinner than an old running sock. If you're planning a run or ride on the White Oak Bayou Greenway, be aware that the section near N Shepherd is still dealing with intermittent daily closures from 7 AM to 5:30 PM. This isn't a new development; it's part of some glorious Harris County Flood Control District work that's expected to drag on for another six months. And let's not forget the stretch of the Little White Oak Bayou trail between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, which is apparently closed until *2028* for I-45/I-10 connector ramps. Seriously, 2028. Just print that on a t-shirt.

Over in Memorial Park, don't even think about the western part of the Outer Loop Trail. It's fenced off until 2027 for the Memorial Groves project. You'd think with all the money floating around, they could at least put up some decent detour signage that isn't three months out of date.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Major Trail Closures!** Both the White Oak Bayou Greenway (near N Shepherd and the Little White Oak section) and sections of Memorial Park's Outer Loop are significantly impacted by long-term construction. Check Houston Bike Guide or local trail groups for the latest detours, or just plan on getting creative with your mileage.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Window: Enjoy it While You Can

Today's actually pretty pleasant, temperature-wise, hovering around 21.5°C (70.7°F) with clear skies. However, get ready for a change. A "real" cold front is blasting through Friday morning, bringing with it a much-needed drop in humidity, but also strong northerly winds, gusting up to 30 mph from Friday afternoon through Saturday. So, enjoy today's relative calm, because tomorrow you'll be fighting a headwind no matter which direction you turn.

### The Lowdown on Local Happenings

No major events are shutting down our usual running haunts today, which is a small blessing amidst the construction chaos. There's a "Natural Disasters Expo USA" happening downtown at the George R. Brown Convention Center, but unless you're planning to run *through* the convention center, it's unlikely to mess with your outdoor routine.
