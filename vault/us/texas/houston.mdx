<BulletList>
*   **Killer Heat Advisory:** We're under a Heat Advisory until 7 PM. You're looking at heat index values between 105 and 110 degrees Fahrenheit. That's not "sweat it out" weather, that's "heatstroke risk" weather, especially if you're out there between noon and 7 PM. Your body just can't cool itself efficiently when the air is this saturated and the sun is beating down.

### Construction & Closures: The Usual Houston Saga

Beyond the White Oak Bayou Greenway mess, the Brays Bayou Trail is still partially shut down at Spur 5 thanks to the SH 35 expansion, impacting access near key areas like Hermann Park. And let's not forget the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project, with ongoing lane reductions and even Studemont Street Bridge demolition. Trying to navigate these detours on a run or bike ride while battling extreme heat and questionable air is just asking for trouble. Save the exploration for a day when the city isn't actively trying to bake or choke you, and when you're not constantly rerouting through traffic.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Stay Informed on Closures:** Before you head out, check for unexpected closures, especially around major bayou trails or highway projects. Houston's a city constantly under construction, and your planned route might be a concrete barrier today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable:** Seriously, I shouldn't have to say this in Houston, but *drink water*. Plenty of it. Not just when you're thirsty. Avoid caffeine and alcohol before and during your workout if you somehow ignore all other warnings and venture out.

If you absolutely HAVE to work out, keep it indoors today. Your lungs, joints, and common sense will thank you.
