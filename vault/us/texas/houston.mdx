### Local Trail Conditions & Construction Headaches

Houston's always building something, which means our beloved trails are constantly playing dodgeball with closures and detours.

Down south, the **Brays Bayou Trail** is still a no-go at Spur 5 thanks to that never-ending SH 35 expansion. You'll need to reroute, so plan accordingly. And if you typically hit **MacGregor Park**, be aware that portions, including sections of the trail, have been closed for renovations since mid-August. Don't expect to find your usual loop entirely open there.

Over at **Memorial Park**, the western portion of the **Outer Loop Trail** remains closed. They're still working on the Memorial Groves project, and it won't be open until 2027. So no full loops on that side for a while.

Meanwhile, a positive note: **Lawrence Park** in the Heights is breaking ground today on some major renovations, including new connections to the MKT Trail. This means more disruption in the short term around that area, but better access down the line. It's the cycle of Houston trail life – one closes, another gets an upgrade.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Construction Closures:** Plan your route carefully, especially around Brays Bayou (Spur 5), MacGregor Park, and Memorial Park's Outer Loop. Check local news or trail apps before you head out.
</AlertBox>
