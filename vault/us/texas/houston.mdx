## Today's Rundown

Don't let that "Mainly Clear" sky fool you; there's more in the air than just sunshine today. Your lungs are getting a double whammy from elevated PM2.5 and some seriously aggressive tree pollen. Anyone with asthma, allergies, or other respiratory issues needs to be extra cautious. Seriously, this isn't the day to push it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### AQI Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!
With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 dominant, your lungs are under attack. Sensitive individuals (that's you, runners with asthma or seasonal allergies) should absolutely limit prolonged outdoor exertion. Consider moving your workout indoors to avoid breathing in that fine particulate matter.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Pollen Bomb in Progress
Our local data pegs pollen at Moderate, but don't get complacent. Tree pollen, especially cedar, is peaking hard. If you're hitting the trails at Memorial Park, expect to be sneezing through your miles. Pop those allergy meds *before* you head out, not after you're already wheezing.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Heat, Humidity, and a Breeze
It's going to be warm and sticky with highs around 83°F (28.2°C). Couple that with gusty southerly winds up to 25 mph, and you've got a recipe for dehydration and fatigue if you're not careful. Stick to early mornings or late evenings if you insist on being outside, and carry more water than you think you need. Even if those winds feel nice, they'll dry you out faster.
</AlertBox>

While there aren't widespread flood warnings for *today*, we've had some heavy rain west and northwest of the city in the past few days. If you're planning on hitting unpaved trails, especially around areas like Jersey Village or Spring, be mindful that some spots might still be muddy or slick, even if the sun's out now.
