## Today's Local Rundown: Don't Assume Your Usual Route Is Open

You might be eyeing that glorious weather and thinking about hitting your favorite stretch, but hold your horses. Houston's in a perpetual state of construction and event madness, and today is no exception.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Downtown & Allen Parkway: Bayou City Art Festival Mayhem
If you're planning a downtown run, consider yourself warned. The **Bayou City Art Festival** kicks off tonight, March 27, and street closures are already starting. Expect major headaches on Bagby, Lamar, and significant chunks of **Allen Parkway** right next to Sam Houston Park from 7 PM tonight through Monday morning. The bike lane on Allen Parkway is specifically closed through March 31. Seriously, just avoid the area or plan a completely different path. This isn't a suggestion; it's a plea to your sanity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### I-10 White Oak Bayou Project: More Closures Tonight
Adding to the fun, the massive I-10 White Oak Bayou Elevation Project is doing a traffic switch tonight, March 27, starting at 10 PM. This means the I-10 westbound frontage road from Studemont Street to Heights Boulevard is entirely closed until Monday morning. And if you usually exit at Heights Boulevard westbound, forget about it; that ramp is closed until mid-2026. This isn't just for cars; it impacts anyone trying to navigate around the **White Oak Bayou** area. Expect delays and plan detours well in advance if you're trying to hit those bayou trails this weekend.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### General Houston Navigation: Always Check Before You Roll
Look, this is Houston. Construction is our state bird. The **Brays Bayou Trail** is still closed at Spur 5, thanks to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. And while the MKT trail closure under the Hogan Street pedestrian bridge was supposed to reopen this spring, don't just blindly assume it's smooth sailing. Always, *always* check Houston Bike Guide or TranStar before you head out. A new water leak hazard near the former Rusk Elementary, causing slippery sidewalks, is *just now* being addressed as of yesterday, March 26. Even seemingly minor issues can trip you up.
</AlertBox>
