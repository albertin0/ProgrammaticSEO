## Today's Sweat Report: Not a Total Loss, But Be Smart

Alright, let's get down to brass tacks. That pleasant 25.6°C (78.1°F) temperature? That's just the ambient air. The National Weather Service in Houston/Galveston has slapped us with a Heat Advisory for a reason. Factor in our notorious Gulf humidity, and it's going to feel like a full-body steam bath, pushing "feels like" temps well into the triple digits, possibly up to 109°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous if you're not paying attention. Hydration is non-negotiable. Don't wait until you're already thirsty.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Heat Advisory in Effect!**
A Heat Advisory is active until 6 PM or 7 PM CDT tonight for many Houston-area counties, including inland Harris and Montgomery. Heat index values (what it *feels* like) are forecast to be between 105-109°F. If you *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and seriously consider an indoor alternative. Heat stroke is no joke, and your PR isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>

On the air front, we're sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 53, largely due to PM2.5. That's not terrible, but it's not pristine either, and combined with "Moderate" pollen, your respiratory system might notice it. Spotty afternoon showers are also on the forecast, mostly south of I-10, so you might get a quick, unexpected cool-down or a sudden dash for cover. They're not expected to be an all-day wash-out, but keep an eye on the sky.
