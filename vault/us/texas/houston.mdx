## Your Lungs Are Already Working Overtime

Our air quality is sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. These tiny particles love to mess with your eyes, nose, and especially your lungs. But here’s the kicker: we’ve got high levels of ozone pollution expected to continue through Sunday evening, with pollution peaking around 8 AM. Harris County consistently gets an "F" for ozone levels from the American Lung Association for a reason. If you have asthma or any other respiratory issues, seriously, keep your outdoor exertion limited to early morning or late evening, if at all.

Adding insult to injury, the east side of the Sam Houston Multi-Use Trailhead is actually *closed* due to a nearby wildfire response that started August 13th. While the main city isn't choked in smoke right now, that's a direct impact of our wildfire season, and it means the air around those areas could be worse. The Texas A&M Forest Service was dealing with almost 3,000 acres burning yesterday across Texas.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Warning: Air Quality Concerns.** Moderate AQI with high ozone levels is a bad combo, especially if you're hitting it hard or have respiratory issues. Wildfire smoke is in the region, actively closing trails.
