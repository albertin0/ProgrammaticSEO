## July 22, 2026: Houston's Heat is No Joke, and the Air Ain't Helping

You want to get outside? I get it, the itch is real. But honestly, today's not the day to be a hero in Houston. We're under an **Extreme Heat Warning** from 11 AM all the way to 8 PM. That means actual temps are climbing to a blistering 102°F, but with our signature humidity, it's going to *feel* like a scorching 115°F. The National Weather Service doesn't throw around "Extreme Heat Warning" lightly; this is serious business for heat-related illnesses. So, if you were planning a long run down the **Buffalo Bayou Trail**, maybe swap it for a treadmill session.

### The Air You Breathe (or Try To)

On top of the heat, we've got an **Ozone Action Day** in effect for Houston, Galveston, and Brazoria. While your overall AQI might technically hover in the "Moderate" range at 72, the elevated ozone levels mean it's unhealthy for sensitive groups. If you've got asthma, heart issues, or you're just not feeling 100%, consider this your official pass to skip the outdoor grind. Even healthy folks might feel that tell-tale scratch in the throat or just a general sluggishness. Don't push it.

### Tropical Troubles Brewing

And just for kicks, Tropical Storm Bertha is moseying in the Gulf. While it's expected to weaken as it heads our way, we're looking at potential strong rainbands later tonight, roughly between 6 PM and 10 PM. There's no major flood threat expected for the city overall, but any single storm cell can dump a ridiculous amount of rain in a short time. So, even if you manage to brave the heat, you might end up drenched.

<AlertBox type="warning">

Ozone levels are elevated today. If you're in a sensitive group, limit your time outside. Everyone else, be aware that air quality is compromised.

### Trail Talk: Major Closures Still in Effect

If you were hoping to hit some green space, good luck. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** (specifically the Little White Oak Bayou Trail section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center) is still shut down for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work, and it's expected to stay that way through 2028. So, plan your detours if you absolutely *must* be out there. There are detours, but frankly, with today's conditions, finding an alternative route just means more exposure.

#### Stay Safe Tips for Today
City cooling centers are open across Houston if you need a place to escape the heat. Drink water like it's your job, wear light, loose clothing, and reschedule any intense outdoor plans. And if you feel dizzy or nauseous, get inside immediately.
