Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next masochist, but today ain't it. We're staring down an AQI of 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality is forecasting PM2.5 to hang out in the "Moderate" range, but other sources are less optimistic, slapping a "Poor" rating on our air quality. That means if you've got asthma, allergies, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, you should probably reconsider that tempo run. Even if you're a seasoned pavement pounder, pushing hard today is just asking for unnecessary inflammation.

The weather report says "Mainly Clear" and 28.2°C (around 83°F), which sounds lovely, right? Except it also means whatever crud is in the air isn't getting blown out anytime soon. We've got scattered thunderstorms on the horizon for Friday and the weekend, but today is just muggy and ripe for air stagnation. Also, remember those burning tanks in east Harris County yesterday? While extinguished, any lingering particulate matter isn't helping our air quality.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch out for ongoing construction closures.** If your route takes you near major thoroughfares, expect traffic and potential detours. IH-10 Katy, IH-45, and US-90A all have active lane closures today, March 5th, per Houston TranStar. The I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project, in particular, is causing long-term closures and traffic headaches.
</AlertBox>

Let's talk about those trails. If you were planning on a peaceful run along the White Oak Bayou Greenway between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, forget about it. That section is closed until *2028* for highway construction. Yes, 2028. You read that right. So much for scenic running. The MKT Trail Hogan Pedestrian Bridge over White Oak Bayou is still undergoing rehabilitation, though the trails *underneath* might theoretically be open by now, the bridge itself won't be fully restored until spring. And don't even get me started on parts of the Brays Bayou Trail, which are also indefinitely impacted by highway expansion.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider indoor alternatives.** Today might be a good day for the gym treadmill, some laps in a pool, or a Pilates class. Your respiratory system will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Avoid strenuous outdoor exercise, especially if you have respiratory issues.** The combination of elevated PM2.5 and moderate pollen is a real lung-burner. Listen to your body and don't push it.
</AlertBox>

Factor in the Houston Livestock Show & Rodeo still going strong at NRG Park, drawing huge crowds and traffic, and the pre-World Baseball Classic street closures downtown, and finding clear space for an outdoor workout is going to be a challenge, even if the air wasn't trying to suffocate you.
