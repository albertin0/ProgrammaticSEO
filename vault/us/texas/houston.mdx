### AQI Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups!
With an AQI of 103 driven by PM2.5, folks with asthma, bronchitis, or other respiratory conditions, as well as the elderly and young children, should absolutely limit prolonged outdoor exertion today. Even healthy individuals might experience some irritation. Don't be a hero; save that lung-busting effort for a clearer day.
</AlertBox>
