### Construction & Closures: The Eternal Houston Saga

This is where things get truly gnarly. It feels like half the city is under construction, and your beloved trails are no exception.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail & Bike Lane Headaches:**
*   The **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5 is shut down due to the ongoing SH 35 expansion. So much for your scenic bayou run.
*   If you rely on the **Austin Street Bike Lane** downtown, it's been demolished between Elgin and Gray. This was a critical north-south connector, and there's no clear timeline for its return.
*   A section of the **Buffalo Bayou Trail** on the north side in Johnny Goyen Park (near UHD) is temporarily closed for safety reasons and slope work. Look for detours, but expect some frustration.
*   The western portion of **Memorial Park's Outer Loop Trail** is closed until 2027 for the Memorial Groves project. Find alternative paths within the park.
*   The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** (Little White Oak Bayou Trail) between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is slated to be closed through 2028 for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. That's a long haul, folks.
*   Early risers might still encounter residual curb lane closures on **Allen Parkway** near Sam Houston Park from the Bayou City Art Festival, though these were scheduled to clear by 3 AM. Always be aware of lingering barriers.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Downtown Dodgeball:** The Houston Astros have a game today at **Daikin Park** (Minute Maid Park), running from 6 AM to midnight. Expect curb lane closures and increased traffic congestion around the ballpark area. Plan your downtown routes accordingly to avoid getting caught in game-day gridlock.
