### DANGER: Extreme Heat & Humidity!
The combination of high temperatures and suffocating humidity means heat exhaustion and heatstroke are serious risks. Symptoms like headaches, dizziness, nausea, and confusion can escalate quickly. If you *must* be outside, hydrate aggressively, wear light clothing, and take frequent, long breaks in air-conditioned spaces. Avoid peak afternoon hours entirely. Never, ever leave kids or pets in a vehicle.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Unhealthy Air Quality
With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 as the primary culprit, sensitive groups should absolutely limit prolonged outdoor exertion. Consider an indoor alternative today, especially if you have existing respiratory or cardiac conditions. Even healthy individuals might experience irritation.
</AlertBox>
