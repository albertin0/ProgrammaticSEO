### Weather & Air Quality Rundown

Today, October 28, 2026, we're looking at a decent 22.2°C (72°F) and "Mainly Clear" conditions. Sounds lovely, right? But this is Houston. There's always a catch.

The air quality index (AQI) is at a moderate 59, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This means that while it's generally acceptable for most, if you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you might feel it. Active children and adults, along with those sensitive to air pollution, should probably ease up on the prolonged outdoor exertion. Don't trust that "Good" reading you saw at 2 AM; we're past that.

### Trail Trouble & Urban Obstacles

If you're planning on hitting the trails, prepare for some frustrating detours and outright blockades. The city's perpetual state of "improvement" means our favorite running and cycling routes are often a hot mess.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### White Oak Bayou Greenway is a Maze, Not a Path.
Expect intermittent daily closures on the **White Oak Bayou Greenway** near N Shepherd, running from 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. for another several months. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a significant disruption on one of our most heavily used greenways. To add insult to injury, there's no signed detour posted. Your best bet is to eyeball nearby street connections, which, let's be honest, aren't exactly designed for runners or cyclists. This is likely due to Harris County Flood Control District work, so it's not going away anytime soon. Another section of the Little White Oak Bayou Trail, between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, has been closed since January 2026 and isn't expected to reopen until 2028 due to I-45/I-10 connector ramp work. Plan accordingly, or suffer the consequences.
</AlertBox>

The Brays Bayou Trail is still closed at Spur 5 thanks to the ongoing SH 35 expansion. If you're hoping for it to open up this year, you're probably out of luck. Even parts of the Buffalo Bayou Trail east of downtown are shut down for improvements, with some sections not seeing completion until summer 2027.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch for Wet Spots & Traffic Hazards.
While the Dense Fog Advisory from this morning has lifted, there's still a chance of light rain making streets wet, especially west and south of the city. Always keep an eye out for slick surfaces, especially around downtown and on the city streets. And as always, watch out for construction-related traffic hazards on major highways like IH-10 Katy and IH-610 South Loop; these are ongoing, and while mostly for cars, they affect getting to and from trailheads.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, enjoy the *mostly* clear skies, but don't be a hero. Be smart, be safe, and maybe stick to the treadmill if you're feeling particularly sensitive today.
