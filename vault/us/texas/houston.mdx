Despite the wildfire smoke, the general Air Quality Index for Houston is sitting pretty at 39, which is considered "Good." The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other. However, if you're in the northeast quadrant of the city, that Anahuac smoke could be a localized irritant.

Pollen is still kicking around at a moderate level. If you're usually bothered, consider an antihistamine before you head out, or stick to indoor workouts if your allergies are particularly sensitive.

### Trail Conditions & Closures

Here's where things get a bit annoying. Houston always seems to be under construction, and our trails are no exception.

The **Buffalo Bayou Trail** near Downtown is still partially closed for safety improvements at Johnny Goyen Park, specifically to fix a blind spot at the confluence of White Oak and Buffalo Bayou. This closure has been in effect since January 31st and is "until further notice." Don't just show up expecting to cruise; check for detours.

Down south, a portion of the **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5 remains closed due to ongoing construction for the SH 35 expansion. This one's expected to drag on through 2026. There are also major upgrades happening along Brays Bayou that started in January and are slated for completion in Summer 2028, so expect localized impacts.

And if you planned on hitting **Fonde Park** for your run, forget about it. It's closed for trail resurfacing until April 20th.
