#### Heat Advisory: It's a Real Oven Out There
The actual temperature might be hovering around 28.2°C (about 83°F), but with Houston's notorious humidity, it's going to *feel* much hotter, pushing into the mid-90s Fahrenheit. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real threats, even for seasoned runners. Hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after* your run. Consider moving your workout indoors or to early morning/late evening, though even then, the air quality and residual heat will be factors.
</AlertBox>

#### Air Quality Alert: Don't Breathe Deep
With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, your lungs are under stress. This level of pollution can exacerbate respiratory issues. People with heart or lung disease, children, and older adults should seriously limit prolonged outdoor exertion. Even healthy individuals might experience some respiratory irritation.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Troubles: Mind the Closures

Just when you thought the heat and air weren't enough, Houston's endless construction projects are still making a mess of our favorite running routes. Don't plan on a clear run on parts of the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center. That stretch has been closed since January 2026 and isn't expected to reopen until 2028 due to IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. There are detours, but who needs the extra mental load of navigating construction cones when you're already fighting the elements? Also keep an eye out around the East Downtown area, as FIFA World Cup 2026 preparations mean ongoing, albeit sometimes paused, construction and temporary road closures impacting pedestrian routes until August.
