## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

Look, it's not just the heat making you wheeze. Houston's got that delightful combination of elevated ozone *and* fine particulate matter (PM2.5) today. Blame it on the light winds keeping urban pollutants stagnant and some scattered burnings out east kicking up smoke that's drifting our way. If you've got asthma or any kind of respiratory issues, do yourself a favor and stay indoors or hit the treadmill. Or, you know, just breathe shallowly.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### AQI Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
With an AQI of 103, today's air quality is considered "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." This includes children, older adults, and anyone with heart or lung conditions. Consider rescheduling outdoor activities or significantly reducing their intensity and duration.
</AlertBox>

## Heat Check: It's Already Summer
The calendar says March 20th, but the thermometer says "Welcome to August." At nearly 83°F and clear skies, you'll be baking out there. Hydration is non-negotiable, and if you're planning a long run, aim for pre-dawn or post-dusk, when the sun isn't actively trying to melt you. Even then, watch out for residual heat radiating off the concrete.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Early Summer Sizzle
Temperatures are unseasonably high, and we're heading for near-record heat this weekend. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real. Listen to your body, slow your pace, and don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>
