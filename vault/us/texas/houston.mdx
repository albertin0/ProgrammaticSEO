### Air Quality Alert

Yeah, the sky looks clear, but don't let that fool you. Our AQI is sitting at 103, and the dominant pollutant is PM2.5. This isn't just a "meh" day; it's the kind of day where running along the White Oak Bayou Greenway could feel more like running through a fine mist of industrial exhaust. If you have asthma, any kind of respiratory issue, or just value your long-term lung function, scaling back is smart. Even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exposure to PM2.5 can irritate your airways and make recovery harder.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is 103 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups) with PM2.5.** If you're running, cycling, or doing anything else strenuous outside, you're actively pulling this stuff deep into your lungs. People with respiratory conditions, the elderly, and children should seriously consider staying indoors. Everyone else? Just dial it way back.
</AlertBox>

### General Hazards

No major floods or wildfires rocking the area right now, which is a small mercy. Construction is always a thing in this city, but nothing is specifically blocking major running routes like the loop around Memorial Park today. However, that elevated temperature means you'll still be sweating buckets. Hydrate, people. It's not optional.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is non-negotiable.** Just because it's not 38°C doesn't mean you can skip the water bottle. With the heat and any potential respiratory irritation, dehydration will only make you feel worse.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
If you absolutely *must* get your miles in, consider moving your workout indoors to a gym or a track with better air filtration. If you're stubborn and staying outside, slow your pace significantly. This isn't the day for PRs. Listen to your body – if you're coughing, wheezing, or feeling unusual shortness of breath, stop immediately. Your pride isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>
