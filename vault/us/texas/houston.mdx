You think today is a good day to hit the trails? Bless your cotton socks. Maybe if you enjoy breathing soup and navigating a scavenger hunt of closed paths.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
- **Your Lungs are in a Tree Pollen Fight Club:** Forget "moderate." The real-time data from sources like AccuWeather and Allermi are screaming "Very High" tree pollen for Houston today, blowing past the "moderate" your app might be whispering. With average winds around 15 mph and gusts up to 30 mph, that lovely fine dust is getting a city-wide tour, right into your airways. Expect itchy eyes, runny noses, and that delightful post-run cough.
- **Damp & Detour-Filled Trails:** While the official AQI is technically "Good" at 36, AccuWeather forecasts "Poor" for sensitive groups today due to air pollution, so don't get too comfortable. Plus, that "light drizzle" means potentially slick surfaces and certainly damp ground. More importantly, several key arteries are still choked. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5? Still closed due to the ongoing SH 35 expansion, forcing some gnarly detours. Same goes for a big stretch of the White Oak Bayou Greenway, which is shut down between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center clear through 2028 for IH-45 connector ramp work. And let's not even start on the Austin Street and Cleburne Street bike lanes, which are also out of commission thanks to ongoing city projects.
- **The Weekend Doesn't Look Much Better:** If you're planning for tomorrow, Saturday, April 4th, expect a 5K/10K race in CityCentre that will fully close Town and Country Blvd for part of the morning. More rain and a flood threat are also on the menu for Saturday evening, potentially making for muddy Easter egg hunts.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Allergy Sufferers, Seriously, Stay Indoors**
Your eyes will stream, your nose will run a marathon of its own, and your throat will feel like sandpaper. "Very High" pollen isn't a suggestion, it's a warning. If you *must* go out, wear a mask, keep your workout short, and hit the showers immediately afterward to wash off those allergens. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Mind the Trail Carnage and Detours**
Houston's always under construction, and right now, it's making a mess of our greenways. With the Brays Bayou Trail effectively cut off at Spur 5 and a big chunk of the White Oak Bayou Greenway closed for years, your usual routes are probably a bust. Don't just blindly follow Strava; double-check the local bike and city websites for *actual* closure signs. Detours often throw you onto busy streets, so stay vigilant and assume drivers aren't looking out for you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Consider an Indoor Alternative**
Look, I'm a runner. I get it. But sometimes the city just isn't cooperating. With the drizzle, wind, and all the trail headaches, today might be the day to hit the gym, jump on the Peloton, or just do some living room yoga. Save those outdoor miles for a day when the air isn't trying to kill your sinuses and the trails aren't a minefield.
</AlertBox>
