**Extreme Heat Advisory:** The forecasted RealFeel of 103-108°F is dangerous. Even experienced runners are at high risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. This isn't a "push through it" day. If you *must* run outside, stick to pre-dawn hours, bring more water than you think you need, and seriously consider an indoor alternative.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead (Everywhere):** That massive IH-10 Katy Freeway project near Studemont and Heights Blvd isn't just for cars. I'm a big fan of hitting the trails at Buffalo Bayou Park, but even its tree cover won't save you from this oppressive heat. Hydrate early, hydrate often, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get inside immediately. This isn't about being weak; it's about being smart enough to avoid ending up in an ER cot. Your PR can wait.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro-Tip from a PT:** Drink water *before* you're thirsty. Thirst means you're already behind. Electrolyte drinks are your friend today, not just plain water, as you'll be losing more than just H2O. Also, don't just pour water on your head; try placing cool towels on your wrists, forearms, and behind your knees where blood vessels are close to the surface. It actually helps.
</AlertBox>
