## Today's Environmental Breakdown

### Weather Watch: Don't Get Fried or Flooded
Forget "mainly clear." As of this morning, we're already seeing moderate to heavy rain, and the forecast for today is packed with scattered thunderstorms. These aren't your quick summer pop-ups; we're talking tropical downpours that can dump inches of rain in a short amount of time, leading to localized street flooding faster than you can say "I-45 traffic jam." Lightning is also a significant concern, so unless you've got a death wish, running outside during a storm is a hard no.

The real killer today, even between the rain, is the heat index. Houston's notorious Gulf Coast humidity makes our 28°C (83°F) feel exponentially worse. Expect "feels like" temperatures to easily reach over 40°C (105°F) in the afternoon. That kind of heat index, combined with high humidity, prevents your body from cooling itself properly, making heat exhaustion and heat stroke a serious threat. Doctors at Houston Methodist have repeatedly warned that humidity can be *more* dangerous than heat alone.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Index & Thunderstorms!** Avoid outdoor workouts, especially between 10 AM and 4 PM. Seek air-conditioned spaces. If you *must* be outside, hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and watch for signs of heat illness (dizziness, nausea, confusion). Do NOT risk being caught outside during lightning or heavy downpours.
</AlertBox>
