## Today's Hazards: A Triple Threat

### The Heat is No Joke

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Houston is under an advisory for dangerously high heat index values. Strenuous outdoor activities should be severely limited or avoided entirely during peak heat hours (late morning through late afternoon). Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real dangers when the RealFeel hits 106°F. Drink plenty of fluids, wear lightweight clothing, and listen to your body – or better yet, stay inside.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. The National Weather Service isn't issuing these warnings for fun. We're talking about a heat index that can cook you faster than a forgotten lunch in a hot car. Even if the actual temperature is only in the high 80s or low 90s, that humidity cranks the "feels like" temp into dangerous territory. If you absolutely *must* get out there, make it a pre-dawn mission or wait until after sunset, and keep it short.

### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deep

Our air quality today is "Moderate," thanks to PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant and high levels of ozone pollution expected. For those of us with even a hint of respiratory sensitivity, this means irritation, coughing, and generally feeling like you're sucking down a mild exhaust pipe.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate Air Quality Alert:** With an AQI of 103 and high ozone levels, individuals with asthma, COPD, or other respiratory issues, as well as children and older adults, should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. PM2.5 particles can irritate eyes, nose, and the respiratory system.
</AlertBox>
