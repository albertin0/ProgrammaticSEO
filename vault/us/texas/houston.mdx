<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### **Immediate Threat: Your Lungs & Your Core Temperature**

  Look, an AQI over 100 for PM2.5 isn't a suggestion, it's a warning. "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" means if you have asthma, heart disease, or are just generally not 20 years old and bulletproof, you're at risk. And even if you *are* young and spry, extended exposure to this garbage air combined with the stifling humidity and heat is a recipe for disaster. Doctors are telling us that heat-related illness symptoms can escalate quickly, so don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### **Watch Your Step: Lingering Wet Spots & Detours**

  Yesterday brought storms and flash flood warnings to parts of the area. While today is mainly clear, there might still be slick spots on trails or soggy patches that can throw off your stride and lead to a twisted ankle. Combine that with the ongoing closure of the White Oak Bayou Greenway, and you're looking at detours that could lead you onto less-safe streets or through un-shaded areas, prolonging your exposure to the cruddy air and heat. Seriously, plan your route *before* you head out, and assume your usual path is probably blocked.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### **Stay Smart Out There (Or Just Stay In)**

  If you absolutely *must* get out, consider dialing back the intensity. Maybe swap that 10k for a leisurely walk, or take it indoors to the gym. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothes, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or get a headache, stop immediately and get somewhere cool. There's always tomorrow, assuming you don't end up a heat-exhausted mess today.
</AlertBox>
