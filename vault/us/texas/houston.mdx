## Why today is *not* the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Houston Summer Has Arrived, Folks.** The "feels like" temperature is already pushing into dangerous territory, and we're only heading further into it. Houston's notorious humidity makes 26.3°C feel a lot closer to 30°C right now, with highs forecast for the mid-90s today and tomorrow. Doctors at Houston Methodist are reminding everyone that humidity can be *more* dangerous than heat alone because your sweat just hangs out, doing nothing to cool you down.
*   **Smoke 'Em If You Got 'Em (Lungs, That Is).** There's a sizable wildfire burning over in the Anahuac National Wildlife Refuge, east of Houston. Expect smoke to be drifting over parts of northeast Houston, and officials anticipate it'll be a smoky day as they battle the blaze. Your lungs don't need that kind of extra stress, especially with a moderate AQI already on the board.
*   **Construction & Closures Continue.** If you're planning a run downtown, good luck. Construction projects mean significant street closures, like Polk Street between Chartres Street and Avenida de las Americas, affecting both vehicle and pedestrian traffic. Don't even get me started on the I-10 White Oak Bayou Project and its associated ramp closures. You'll spend more time re-routing than actually running.
*   **Fourth of July Race Chaos.** It's the Fourth of July weekend, and races are popping up all over. While festive, events like the Houston Fourth Fest in Vintage Park or the Run Wild 5K in Uptown mean increased crowds and potential localized closures. Expect congestion if your route crosses any of these event areas.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning (Basically)

An "Extreme Heat Warning" was in effect through yesterday, and the forecast for today and the weekend suggests more of the same, with "feels like" temperatures potentially hitting 114°F (45.5°C). The Houston Health Department issues alerts for a reason – heat can lead to serious health issues, fast. Limit strenuous exercise to early mornings or late evenings, if at all. Avoid the sun between 10 AM and 4 PM. This isn't just discomfort; it's genuinely dangerous.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Smoke is Lingering

The wildfire in the Anahuac National Wildlife Refuge is producing a fair bit of smoke. While the overall AQI is moderate, "moderate" doesn't mean "good," especially when PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant and localized smoke from a fire is in the mix. If you're prone to respiratory issues, keep your outdoor time short, especially if you're in northeast Houston where the smoke is more pronounced.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable

Seriously, it's Houston in July. You should be constantly hydrating. Water, water, water. Skip the caffeine and alcohol if you plan on being outside for any extended period; they'll just dehydrate you faster. And for the love of your joints, consider moving your workout indoors to avoid the humidity's extra strain.
</AlertBox>
