#### Extreme Heat & Humidity Alert!

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Unhealthy Air Quality for Sensitive Groups!
With an AQI of 103 due to PM2.5, those with respiratory issues, children, and the elderly should significantly reduce or avoid outdoor exertion. Even if you consider yourself healthy, prolonged activity will likely cause some irritation. Consider an indoor workout or a very light, short walk in the early morning if you absolutely must.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Workout Smarter, Not Harder (or Not at All)
If you must be outside, go before 8 AM or after 8 PM, stay in the shade, hydrate *before* you're thirsty (water and electrolytes!), wear light, moisture-wicking clothing, and for goodness sake, listen to your body. Any dizziness, nausea, or excessive fatigue? Stop immediately. Your Strava segment isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>
