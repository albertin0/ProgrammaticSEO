## The Rundown: Houston's Outdoor Gauntlet

Look, I love hitting the pavement or trails as much as the next masochist, but you need to be smart today. That AQI of 103 is a red flag, plain and simple. We're talking about fine particulate matter, PM2.5, getting deep into your lungs. You might think you're tough, but your respiratory system isn't going to thank you for pushing it in this kind of air. Take it from a PT who sees too many runners sidelined by avoidable issues: breathing that crap in while you're sucking wind is asking for trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### AQI: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (103 PM2.5)
Seriously, if you've got asthma, heart conditions, or just sensitive lungs, you should be taking your workout indoors today. Even healthy folks might notice throat irritation or difficulty breathing with prolonged exposure. Prioritize your health over that Strava segment.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the heat. While some forecasts tried to tell us it would be cooler, the mercury is already hovering around 28.2°C (82.8°F) this morning, and it's only going to climb. We're heading into May, which means the "surface of the sun" summer temps are right around the corner. Add in the humidity that's practically a Houstonian birthright, and you've got a recipe for dehydration and heat exhaustion. Don't be that person collapsed on the side of Memorial Drive.

### Trail Torture: Houston's Endless Construction Season

If the air and heat don't get you, the city's commitment to "progress" just might. Houston's trails are a perpetual obstacle course, and today's no different. Heading out towards the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**? Good luck. A significant section of the Little White Oak Bayou Trail, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, has been shut down since January and isn't expected to fully reopen until *2028* due to IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. They've got detours, sure, but "detour" in Houston often means "figure it out yourself and probably get hit by a car."

It's not just White Oak. Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 is also a mess with the SH 35 expansion. And parts of Buffalo Bayou Trail downtown are closed for "safety concerns." Seems like Houston's always digging something up, usually without a clear path for those of us trying to actually *use* the trails. They're also fixing up streets and sidewalks downtown for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, which is great for future crowds but means ongoing construction headaches for us now.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Detour Mayhem
Expect significant disruptions on major greenways. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, Brays Bayou Trail, and parts of Buffalo Bayou are all impacted by long-term construction. Check local trail alerts *before* you head out, and be prepared for poorly marked, possibly unsafe detours.
</AlertBox>

Finally, remember yesterday's deluge? We had hail, heavy rain, and street flooding across the area up until early this morning. While the Flood Watch is over, watch out for lingering puddles, slippery spots, and muddy sections, especially on unpaved trails or low-lying areas. Nobody wants a twisted ankle to go with their irritated lungs and heatstroke.
