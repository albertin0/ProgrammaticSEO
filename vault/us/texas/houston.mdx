## The Down & Dirty Details

It's Tuesday, Election Day, and if you were planning to hit the trails, you might want to reconsider or at least pack a rain jacket. The weather forecast shows a 67% chance of rain today, with scattered showers and thunderstorms making an appearance. This isn't just a light sprinkle; we're talking about conditions that could bring brief, heavy rain and some lightning. It feels like 83 degrees Fahrenheit with almost 90% humidity, so any rain will just make it muggy and slippery.

Then there’s the air. A glorious AQI of 103 for PM2.5 means if you’ve got asthma, heart issues, or you just don’t enjoy breathing what feels like fine dust, stay inside. Even if you're a granite-lunged beast, taking it easy today isn't a bad idea. We've got plenty of industrial sources pumping out PM2.5 in this city, and it's not always pretty.

## Trail Closures and Other Gripes

If the weather and air don't deter you, the perpetual state of Houston construction probably will.

*   **Memorial Park:** Don't even think about the western portion of the Outer Loop Trail. It's been closed since spring 2026 for the Memorial Groves project and isn't expected to reopen until late 2027. The Seymour Lieberman Trail, the park's main three-mile loop, is technically open, but about 0.7 miles of it are being realigned. Expect some detours and general construction annoyance.
*   **White Oak Bayou Greenway:** If you planned on cruising near N Shepherd, forget about smooth sailing. There are intermittent daily closures between 7 AM and 5:30 PM until November 19, 2026. No signed detour means you're on your own to navigate city streets, which, let's be honest, is usually a death wish for cyclists and runners in this town.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Chaos
Always check local park and trail websites before heading out. Projects like the SH 35 expansion affecting Brays Bayou Trail and the Spring Branch Trail West expansion are ongoing. The Buffalo Bayou Trail near downtown also has a blind spot closure. Don't assume your favorite route is clear.
</AlertBox>
