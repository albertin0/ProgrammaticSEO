## The Air: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Alright, Houston, let's talk about that air. The Air Quality Index is currently pegged at 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. If you're sensitive – and let's be honest, who *isn't* sensitive to microscopic crud getting into their lungs? – this is categorized as "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." That means kids, older adults, and anyone with existing heart or lung conditions should seriously reconsider anything more strenuous than walking to the mailbox. PM2.5 particles are tiny enough to burrow deep into your respiratory system, and trust me, you don't want that kind of souvenir from your run. We already have an "F" grade for ozone levels in Harris County; let's not push it further with particulate matter.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: High PM2.5 AQI.** AQI at 103 is "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Limit outdoor activity, especially if you have respiratory issues, are elderly, or are a child. Consider an indoor workout.
</AlertBox>
