<AlertBox type="danger">
  **AQI is in the Red Zone for many.** With an Air Quality Index of 103, driven by PM2.5, sensitive groups (that's anyone with asthma, heart conditions, or just plain lungs that don't love crud in them) should seriously limit outdoor exertion. Even healthy individuals might experience respiratory irritation. This isn't just local exhaust; Saharan dust and lingering wildfire smoke are major contributors.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Woes

Today's forecast is "Mainly Clear" but don't let that fool you. The combination of high temperatures and extreme humidity means heat indices are soaring. We're talking "feels like" temps that can quickly put you in a bad spot.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Extreme Heat Index:** The "feels like" temperature will be well over 100°F (38°C) for most of the day, making any prolonged outdoor activity hazardous. Doctors warn that humidity is often *more* dangerous than the raw temperature because it prevents your sweat from doing its job – cooling you down. Dehydration and heatstroke are serious risks.
</AlertBox>
<AlertBox type="info">
  **Thunderstorm Threat:** There's a 75% chance of thunderstorms today. Pop-up storms in this heat are no joke and can bring sudden heavy rain, lightning, and make already slippery trails even more treacherous. Keep an eye on the sky if you absolutely *must* step outside.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Disruptions

If you were planning on hitting the trails, think again. The city is essentially a maze of closures, especially around our most popular spots.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Buffalo Bayou Park is a Minefield of Closures:** Forget your usual loop around Buffalo Bayou Park. Due to the "Freedom Over Texas" event, major sections of trails are closed. The entire north side between Sabine Street Bridge and Tapley Tributary is shut down through Sunday, July 5th. Starting at 7 PM tonight (Thursday, July 2nd), full closures are in effect around the event site until July 5th. Trying to navigate this will be a frustrating, and potentially unsafe, mess.
</AlertBox>

On top of the event closures, there are ongoing construction projects. The MKT Trail Hogan Pedestrian Bridge over White Oak Bayou, a vital link, has seen continuous delays since January 2025 and is still impacting access, with another reported six-month weekday closure near N. Shepherd. And if you're a Brays Bayou runner, watch out for that closure at Spur 5.

Bottom line: Stay indoors, crank the AC, or find a treadmill. Your body will thank you.
