## The "Joy" of Houston's Early Summer
We're barely into spring and Houston's already decided to skip ahead to May. Today's forecast is flirting with 90 degrees Fahrenheit (32.2 Celsius) which is about 10 degrees above normal and could tie or break a daily record set back in 1907. You think that's just a little sweat? That's your body screaming. This kind of unseasonable heat puts serious strain on your cardiovascular system, especially when you're trying to push a pace. Hydrate, yes, but also question if that outdoor PR is worth a heatstroke today.

And speaking of feeling it in your lungs, the Air Quality Index for Houston is a solid 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," meaning if you've got asthma, allergies, or just lungs that prefer actual clean air, you're going to suffer. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) even mentioned light density smoke and aerosols floating around from burnings. So you'll be breathing in more than just "fresh air," if you can even call it that.
