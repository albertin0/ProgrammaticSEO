## The Full Breakdown for Houston

### Air Quality (AQI: 103 - Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups)

That AQI reading isn't just a number; it's a warning, especially with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This means tiny particles are floating around that can get deep into your lungs. If you're one of those "sensitive groups" – which, let's be real, includes most of us trying to push it in this humidity – you need to seriously reconsider your outdoor plans. Shorten your activity, lower the intensity, or just hit the treadmill. Your lungs will thank you. Harris County consistently struggles with air quality, often earning an "F" grade for ozone levels from the American Lung Association.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **AQI 103 (PM2.5):** This is "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Anyone with lung or heart disease, older adults, and children should avoid prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Everyone else should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion. Basically, don't pretend you're invincible.
</AlertBox>

### Weather & Heat

Our 28.2°C (82.8°F) "mainly clear" skies sound innocent enough, right? Wrong. This is Houston. That temperature, combined with our signature August humidity, means the heat index will be significantly higher. We're talking "feels like" temperatures that quickly push into the mid-90s Fahrenheit (35°C+), making even a moderate workout risky. Dehydration and heat-related illness are no joke. There's also a chance of showers and storms popping up today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Heat Index Hazard:** Even if the thermometer isn't screaming, your body will be. Hydrate constantly, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or have muscle cramps, STOP. Now. Your workout can wait; an ER visit can't.
</AlertBox>
