### Immediate Danger: Thunderstorms & Flooding
Your provided weather indicates thunderstorms right now. This means lightning, heavy rain, and potential for flash flooding. Houston's bayous, like Brays Bayou and White Oak Bayou, have seen significant flooding recently, even shutting down major roads like I-45. Seriously, do not risk it. Even if the rain briefly stops, residual water can hide hazards, and more rain could be on the way.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Lung Burn Warning: Poor Air Quality
The AQI of 103 means the air quality is unhealthy for sensitive groups, and everyone else will likely feel it with prolonged exertion. The primary culprit is PM2.5, fueled by a denser plume of Saharan dust making its way inland. This isn't just a "hazy sky" situation; these fine particles get deep into your lungs and bloodstream. If you have asthma, allergies, or any respiratory issues, today is not your day for outdoor cardio. Period.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Heat & Humidity Hell
Even after the storm passes, that signature Houston humidity isn't going anywhere. Running in these conditions can quickly lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke because your sweat can't evaporate properly to cool you down. Hydrate, yes, but more importantly, listen to your body and avoid peak heat hours. Doctors at Houston Methodist have repeatedly warned that Houston's humidity is often a bigger issue than heat alone.
</AlertBox>
