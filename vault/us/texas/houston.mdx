## The Air: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

You step outside, the sun is shining, 22.8°C (73°F) feels *almost* perfect, and you think, "Great day for a run!" Then you check the actual data, and it's a hard nope for anything strenuous if your lungs aren't bulletproof. We're under an **Ozone Action Day** today, which means ground-level ozone could reach "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels, particularly in central Houston. While the overall AQI might be sitting at a "Moderate" 73 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, don't let that fool you. Ozone is a different beast, and it'll burn. AccuWeather even calls today "Poor" for sensitive groups. So, if you've got asthma, allergies, or just a general aversion to feeling like you ran through a chemical plant, seriously rethink that long outdoor session.

## Trail Closures & Detour Delights (Or Disasters)

Remember those trail closures we've been grumbling about? Yeah, they're still very much a thing.

The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** (specifically the "Little White Oak Bayou Trail" section) between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is locked down through 2028 for that massive IH-45/I-10 connector ramp project. So, if your usual loop involves that stretch, you're going to need to find an alternative. There are detours, but they rarely feel as smooth as the main trail.

Then there's the **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5, still closed thanks to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. They're hoping for a 2026 reopening, "if we're lucky." Don't hold your breath (pun intended, given the AQI).

And let's not forget the **MKT Trail** near the Hogan Street Pedestrian Bridge over White Oak Bayou. While the trail underneath was *supposed* to reopen by the end of 2025, the bridge itself isn't fully restored until "spring 2026." This implies ongoing work, so expect some detours or at least some unsightly construction.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Gaps (and Cones)
Major sections of the White Oak Bayou Greenway and Brays Bayou Trail remain closed due to long-term construction projects. Always check local trail updates before you head out, and be prepared for unexpected detours, uneven surfaces, and increased traffic on alternative routes.
</AlertBox>

## Weather: The Calm Before the Sweltering Storm

Today, May 29th, is actually quite nice, temperature-wise, hovering around 22.8°C (73°F) and clear skies. But Houston in late May? That's a ticking time bomb for heat and humidity. The forecast for the weekend is already showing temperatures climbing into the 90s, potentially hitting 35°C (95°F) by Saturday. Enjoy this brief reprieve, because you'll be swimming through the air again soon.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Smart, Stay Safe
Today's pleasant temperature is fleeting. If you insist on running outside with the Ozone Action Day, go early, go short, and listen to your body. Drink more water than you think you need, especially with the impending heat wave. Consider hitting the treadmill or a gym with good air conditioning to spare your lungs and avoid construction headaches.
</AlertBox>
