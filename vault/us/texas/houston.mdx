Look, I'm all for getting those miles in, but today requires some serious strategizing, or you'll end up feeling like you ran through a swamp full of sawdust. That 28.2°C temperature (that's pushing 83°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) under a clear sky means the sun will be beating down with a vengeance. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because in this heat, it kinda does.

Then there's the air. A "Moderate" AQI of 103 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant isn't exactly prime breathing air. While the ozone itself isn't terrible right now, PM2.5 can still irritate your respiratory system, especially when you're sucking it in deep on a run. Combine that with tree pollen hitting "Very High" levels, and grass pollen at "High," and your lungs are basically entering a boxing match. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, you're going to feel it. Today is probably not the day for that long, unprotected run down the White Oak Bayou Greenway, especially if you're sensitive to what's floating around. The Little White Oak Bayou Trail, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, is still closed anyway, and will be until 2028 due to the IH-45/I-10 connector project. Detours exist, but expect more exposure to traffic.
