### Weather Woes & Wind Advisory

It's 28.2°C (about 83°F) and "mainly clear" right now, but don't let that fool you into thinking it's a nice day for a run. The NWS has slapped a Wind Advisory on all of Southeast Texas from this morning through Monday afternoon for a reason. Gusts up to 40 mph mean you'll be fighting for every step, dodging debris, and potentially getting knocked off balance. That's a good way to twist an ankle or land on your face.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Wind Advisory in Effect!** Expect strong winds and unpredictable gusts today. This isn't just annoying; it's a legitimate safety hazard. Debris can fly, hats can disappear, and keeping your balance on uneven surfaces becomes a real challenge. Be smart, stay inside.
</AlertBox>

### Local Trail Traps & Construction Chaos

As if the wind and air weren't enough, some of our favorite spots are still a mess. If you were thinking of hitting the **MKT Trail** near White Oak Bayou, forget about it. That section, particularly under the Hogan Street pedestrian bridge, is still shut down for that bridge project. It's been closed for a while, and won't reopen until spring. So much for an easy bypass.

And let's not even start with the ongoing I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project. That thing is causing lane reductions and bridge demolition that impacts anyone trying to get around downtown, and it's not letting up until at least mid-2026. If you're running near downtown, you're constantly dodging closures and re-routing through construction zones, which is just asking for trouble.
