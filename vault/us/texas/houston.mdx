<AlertBox type="danger">
### Lung Burn Warning
With an AQI of 103 and high PM2.5, your lungs are working overtime. If you have asthma, COPD, or other respiratory issues, seriously consider an indoor alternative today. Even if you don't, cut your outdoor activity short and don't push the pace. Your respiratory system will thank you.

## Heat and Humidity: The Usual Suspects

It's 28.2°C and mainly clear, which means the sun is out and shining directly on your already struggling body. We're not talking "dangerously hot" by Houston standards, but it's warm enough that you'll be sweating buckets, and that moisture loss combined with the poor air quality can really drain you. Keep that hydration up, people. If you're out there pushing it, your joints are already under stress from impact, don't add dehydration to the mix.

<AlertBox type="info">

Even if you're just doing a quick walk, carry water. Electrolytes wouldn't be a bad idea either. Dehydration creeps up on you, especially when your body is already trying to fight off airborne crud.

## The Bottom Line

Today's not the day for a PR attempt or a long, leisurely run where you just breathe deep and enjoy the fresh air. Because the air isn't fresh. If you must run, keep it short, keep it easy, and be hyper-aware of how your body feels. This isn't about being tough; it's about being smart. Save the heroics for a day when your lungs don't feel like they're being assaulted by microscopic urban grit.
