### Watch Out for the Water, Seriously
Houston's seen "several rounds of heavy rain" recently, and the ground is soaked. More downpours today mean "localized street flooding" is a real possibility. Don't be that person who tries to run through ankle-deep water; you don't know what garbage, or worse, is hiding under there. Turn around, for crying out loud.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Updates (or Lack Thereof)

If you were planning on a nice long run along the **Brays Bayou Trail**, think again. There's a persistent closure at Spur 5 due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. Same goes for parts of the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, which has been shut down since January for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work and won't reopen until 2028. There's also another segment of White Oak Bayou near N Shepherd that's seeing weekday closures. So your usual routes might be rerouted, or just plain gone.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Be a Heatstroke Statistic
We might not be hitting triple-digit air temps *today*, but with that humidity and the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" AQI, your body is under stress. Doctors are constantly reminding us that running in Houston's summer humidity is often *more* dangerous than just heat. If you *must* go outside, stick to early mornings or late evenings when the sun isn't directly cooking the pavement. And don't forget your water bottle; that little plastic friend is your best defense against feeling like death warmed over.
</AlertBox>
