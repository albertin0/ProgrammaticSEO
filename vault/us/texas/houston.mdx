Today is a mixed bag for hitting the trails, so plan your route meticulously or stick to indoor workouts if you're sensitive.

<BulletList>
*   **Warm Weather:** Houston is keeping up its "warm and dry" streak thanks to a stubborn "omega block" in the atmosphere, with temperatures hovering around 28.2°C (83°F). It's not *extreme* heat like last week's warnings, but it's enough to make a midday run feel like a slog.
*   **Moderate Air & Pollen:** Both the Air Quality Index (AQI) at 59 and pollen levels are "Moderate" today. However, reports suggest the AQI is leaning towards the "upper end of the "Moderate" range" due to "elevated urban fine particulate matter and smoke from regional burnings". This, combined with the "warm, dry forecast" potentially keeping allergy symptoms around, means your lungs might feel it.
*   **Trail Treachery (Construction Ahead):** Multiple popular greenways are in various states of closure or disruption. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** is facing "intermittent closures" near Shepherd daily, and another section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center has been shut down since January for ongoing work. Good luck trying to get a continuous long run in there without improvising a detour onto some less-than-ideal streets. The **Brays Bayou Trail** is also affected by construction near Spur 5.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Sweat the Detours (Too Much)
Look, the warm temps mean you'll be sweating, but don't sweat *over* the constant trail closures. If your usual path on the White Oak Bayou Greenway is blocked, know your alternate routes. Sometimes it means hopping onto a sidewalk or a less-trafficked side street. Just keep your head up and stay visible. And for the love of all that is holy, hydrate. You're in Houston, not Antarctica.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures Are Real
This isn't a suggestion; these are actual closures. Planning to cruise down the White Oak Bayou Greenway? There are intermittent closures near Shepherd from 7 AM to 5:30 PM, and a long-term closure between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center. The Brays Bayou Trail also has a section closed at Spur 5. These aren't just minor inconveniences; they can send you unexpectedly onto busy roads if you're not prepared. Check Houston TranStar or local trail partnership sites *before* you head out, or you'll be cursing the concrete.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pollen Predicament
"Moderate" pollen might sound okay, but for us allergy sufferers, it's still enough to make your nose run more than you are. Combine that with "elevated urban fine particulate matter" in the air, and it's a recipe for sniffles and sneezes. Pop an antihistamine before you go, and consider wearing glasses or sunglasses to keep some of that airborne junk out of your eyes. Your lungs will thank you, and your post-run selfie won't look like you've been crying.
</AlertBox>
