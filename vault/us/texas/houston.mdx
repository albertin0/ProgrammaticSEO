## Why today isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   The Air Quality Index is sitting at an "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" 103, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. Your lungs aren't going to thank you for inhaling that junk, especially if you're asthmatic or have other respiratory issues. Healthy individuals might even experience difficulty breathing or throat irritation with prolonged exposure, so limit your time outside.
*   While the actual temperature is a deceptive 24.9°C (around 77°F), the infamous Houston humidity is here to play, pushing the "feels like" temperature well into the 90s. This muggy atmosphere makes any strenuous activity a sweaty, miserable affair that can quickly lead to overheating. We're also hitting our first 90-degree day of 2026, so get ready.
*   If you had plans for a peaceful ride or run, forget it. The I-45 North Houston Highway Improvement Project (NHHIP) is still a beast, with Segment 3 reconstruction of the downtown freeway loop (I-45, I-69, I-10, and SH 288) causing major headaches. On top of that, parts of the Brays Bayou Trail remain closed at Spur 5 due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, is also still shut down due to I-45 connector ramp work, a closure expected to last through 2028. Detours, detours everywhere.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### **AQI Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups**
With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, anyone with lung or heart conditions, older adults, and children should seriously consider keeping their workouts indoors today. Even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exertion in this air can irritate your respiratory system.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### **Watch for Heat Stress, Despite Overcast Skies**
Don't let the "overcast" condition fool you. The combination of the actual temperature and high humidity means the air still feels heavy and hot. Heat index values are high, and the nights won't offer much relief. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and listen to your body if you absolutely must be outside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### **Construction Chaos Continues**
Expect significant traffic and trail disruptions. If your route usually takes you near downtown freeways or major bayou trails like Brays Bayou or White Oak Bayou Greenway, check for updated closures before you head out. Assume you'll need a backup plan or a totally different route.
</AlertBox>
