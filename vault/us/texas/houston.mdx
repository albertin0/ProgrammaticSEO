Alright, Houston, today's one of those days where the weather *looks* inviting, but the city itself is doing its best to trip you up.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
*   That crisp, clear air is hiding some moderate PM2.5, which isn't doing your lungs any favors, especially after yesterday's ozone action.
*   Good luck finding a clear path without rerouting or dodging event crowds, because half our greenways are either closed for construction or hosting a fun run.
*   The temperature is sweet right now at 16.4°C (61.5°F), but it's going to climb fast into the mid-80s (30°C) later, so don't dilly-dally.
</BulletList>

Look, the morning started off pretty enough, sure. Low 60s, clear skies – practically perfect for a long run if you ignore the creeping feeling that something's not quite right. That something, my friends, is our perpetually "moderate" air quality. We're sitting at an AQI of 81 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant today. Remember that Ozone Action Day yesterday? Yeah, your lungs do. While ozone levels might be less of a headache today, fine particulate matter is still hanging around. And let's not even start on the long-term projections about data center construction potentially sending us back to early 2000s air quality. That’s a whole other can of worms, but it means our air is constantly fighting an uphill battle.

Then there's the delightful obstacle course that is our trail system. If you had plans to hit the western portion of the **Memorial Park Outer Loop Trail**, forget it. That section's closed until *2027* for the Memorial Groves project. You can still loop the Seymour Lieberman, but some benches and fitness stations are out of commission. Over on the **White Oak Bayou Greenway** near Shepherd, expect intermittent daily closures for flood control work for the next six months. Good luck finding a posted detour, because, of course, there isn't one. And if you're near **Sims Bayou Greenway Trails** or **Willow Waterhole Greenspace** this morning, prepare for crowds: both are hosting events until around 11 AM.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction & Events Galore:** Major sections of Memorial Park, Brays Bayou Trail, and Terry Hershey Park bridges are under long-term construction or repair. On top of that, Sims Bayou Greenway Trails and Willow Waterhole Greenspace are hosting events this morning, leading to congestion and potential detours. Plan your routes carefully and be prepared to improvise.
</AlertBox>

The good news? Yesterday's wildfire risk due to dry conditions is easing up a bit as humidity is expected to climb. So you likely won't be running through a haze of distant smoke, which is always a plus in this city.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate, Houston!** While the morning is pleasant, temperatures are climbing significantly. If you're heading out for a longer run or later in the day, bring more water than you think you need.
</AlertBox>

Bottom line: If you're going out, go early to beat the heat and the crowds. Pick your paths wisely, wear something breathable, and maybe carry a hanky for when that moderate pollen inevitably decides to introduce itself.
