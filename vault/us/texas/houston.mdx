Listen up, Houston. Forget your PRs today. This isn't the day for it.

Today is a terrible day to be outside for a run, unless you enjoy dodging falling branches and inhaling questionable air.

<BulletList>
- Tropical Storm Edouard is making its grand entrance near the Texas-Louisiana border today. Expect significant rainfall (we're talking 3-6 inches in some spots), elevated winds, and the delightful combo of slick surfaces and potential street flooding. Stay home unless you've got gills and a penchant for danger.
- The air quality is not your friend. While the provided data says AQI 74, news outlets are reporting Houston's overall AQI as high as 129, making it "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" due to elevated ozone levels and PM2.5 from dust and regional burnings. AccuWeather even calls today's forecast "Poor." If you've got lungs, don't punish them.
- Parts of our beloved Brays Bayou Trail, along with sections of Buffalo Bayou Trail, are still shut down for what feels like an eternity of construction and repairs. If you insist on braving the elements, you'll be rerouting through even more hazardous street conditions.
</BulletList>

## Current Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Tropical Storm Edouard is Nearing Landfall.** Expect heavy rains, potentially leading to flash and river flooding, especially along and east of I-45. Winds could gust into the 30-40 mph range along the coast and Galveston Bay, with the rest of us seeing steady 20 mph gusts. This isn't just a nuisance; it's a safety hazard. Several Houston-area school districts have already canceled classes today due to anticipated conditions. Don't become tomorrow's headline by trying to squeeze in a long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Poor Air Quality.** Despite the clear skies, ground-level ozone is elevated, and we've got some West Texas dust mixing in. The air is officially "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" with a dominant PM2.5 pollutant. If you're prone to respiratory issues, or just enjoy breathing, consider moving your workout indoors.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Closures are Ongoing.** Specifically, sections of the Brays Bayou Trail near Spur 5 are still closed for highway expansion, a project that might drag on through the year. The Buffalo Bayou Trail near downtown has also been closed since January for repairs. Don't plan your long run relying on these routes without a solid backup plan and a willingness to navigate detours through wet, possibly flooded, city streets.
</AlertBox>
