## Why today is decidedly *not* the day for a PR attempt:

<BulletList>
*   **That humidity is a beast, making 28°C feel much hotter.** Seriously, it’s already unseasonably warm with "summer-like temperatures" and "elevated humidity" thanks to a delightful "heat dome" parked over us. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because in Houston, it often does.
*   **Your lungs might not thank you for that AQI.** We're sitting at an Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups AQI of 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This could be from regional fire weather threatening parts of Texas, or lingering from that "controlled burn inside Memorial Park" a few days ago that sent "noticeable plumes across nearby neighborhoods." Either way, if you have any respiratory issues, consider that treadmill your best friend today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heat and Air Quality Alert!
With temperatures pushing into the upper 20s Celsius (mid-80s Fahrenheit) and high humidity making it feel even worse, combined with an AQI over 100, your body will be working overtime. Avoid peak sun hours (10 AM - 4 PM). If you're running, keep it short, slow, and stay in the shade. PM2.5 can penetrate deep into your lungs, so pay attention to how you feel. Don't be a hero; listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Construction and Detours
Good luck finding a clear path on some of our favorite routes. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway (Little White Oak Bayou Trail)**, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, is closed for significant IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work and won't be open until 2028. There *is* a detour, but honestly, it’s a pain. Expect other random lane closures and detours on major roadways due to ongoing infrastructure projects, like the I-10 Improvement Project which continues its "march toward a 2028 completion." It's always something.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Timing is Everything
If you insist on getting out there, aim for before sunrise or well after sunset when temperatures are *slightly* cooler and the sun isn't directly baking you. Just remember, the humidity won't disappear, and that AQI isn't magically fixed by darkness. Carry water, tell someone your route, and for the love of all that is holy, don't push it. There's always tomorrow (which will probably be just as hot, let's be real).
</AlertBox>
