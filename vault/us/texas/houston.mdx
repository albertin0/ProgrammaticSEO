## What You Need to Know

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Brutal Afternoon Heat**
Seriously, folks, it’s August in Houston. The "clear" skies mean that sun is going to beat down mercilessly, driving up that heat index well above comfortable levels. If you're running outside after 9 AM, you're asking for trouble. Heat exhaustion and stroke are real. Plan your long runs for dawn or hit the treadmill. And for the love of all that is holy, hydrate *before* you even step out the door.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Air Quality & Allergy Check**
While the AQI is "Moderate" today, remember that means "acceptable for most." If you've got asthma, COPD, or just get generally snotty with seasonal allergies, this combo of moderate pollen and PM2.5 isn't doing you any favors. Listen to your body. If you start wheezing or your throat feels scratchy on your morning jog around Memorial Park, cut it short. It's not worth pushing through for a PR.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Navigate Trail Closures Wisely**
Still hitting the trails? Good for you, but be smarter than the last guy who ignored the signs. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, for instance, has significant, long-term closures due to major highway projects that won't be wrapping up until 2028. Don't assume your usual route is open. Check the Houston Parks Board or city construction updates before you head out. A surprise detour onto a busy street is not the kind of "adventure" you want during your workout.
</AlertBox>

Go get after it, but stay safe out there, Houston. We don't need any more heat casualties or confused runners trying to jump construction barriers.
