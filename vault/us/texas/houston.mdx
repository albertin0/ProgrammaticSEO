<AlertBox type="danger">
#### AQI 103: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
Our air quality is officially "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" today, thanks to PM2.5. This means folks with lung disease, asthma, heart disease, older adults, and children should seriously reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Even if you're a seasoned runner, pushing it when you're inhaling fine particulate matter is just asking for trouble.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Prepare for Street Flooding
Don't be fooled by a momentary break in the clouds. Heavy rainfall is forecast for Tuesday and Wednesday, with widespread storms leading to street flooding. Low-lying areas and places that got hammered yesterday are especially vulnerable. Don't run through standing water – you don't know what's underneath, and it's a quick way to twist an ankle or get sick.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, trying to hit your strides on the Brays Bayou Trail near Spur 5? Forget it. That section's been closed due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion, and it's not looking like it'll be fixed anytime soon, maybe not even by the end of 2026. Even if you find an open path, you're likely running into construction detours, or worse, unexpected flooding.

And for those who think the slightly cooler temperature (around 28°C / 84°F) makes it bearable, remember the humidity. The heat index later this week is going to be brutal, pushing well into the triple digits even if the air temp stays below 100°F. The "heat dome" isn't going anywhere.
