## Houston Weather & Air Quality: A Runner's Delight, Mostly

Alright, Houston, let's be real. Days like today are why we tough out the other 10 months of oppressive humidity. We're looking at a fantastic 10.5°C (around 51°F) this morning, warming up comfortably into the 70s, under crystal-clear skies. The northeast winds, hovering around 5-10 mph, are light enough to be refreshing without fighting you every step of the way. This is prime "get out there and run" weather. Don't waste it indoors.

Air quality is equally stellar, with an AQI of 45 putting us firmly in the "Good" category. Even with some "light smoke... mostly aloft" reported for central and east Texas due to potential scattered fires, it's not expected to hit ground level or impact your lungs. So breathe deep, folks.

## The Sneaky Allergy Attack and Trail Troubles

Now for the buzzkill. While the general air quality is good, if you suffer from allergies, you already know. Pollen levels are still moderate, and specifically, ragweed is "very high." That cold front yesterday didn't bring enough rain to wash it out, so it's just getting blown around. Pop an antihistamine before you head out, or you'll regret it halfway through your long run on White Oak Bayou.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Allergy Alert:** Despite the overall good air quality, ragweed pollen is still high. Prepare with medication if you're sensitive, or stick to indoor workouts if your allergies are severe.
</AlertBox>

And speaking of White Oak Bayou, don't just assume your usual routes are open. The White Oak Bayou Greenway between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is still shut down for I-45/I-10 connector ramp construction and will be through 2028. Seriously, 2028. You'll need to use the posted detours, and let me tell you, "detour" often means "share the road with cars." There are also ongoing daily intermittent closures near N. Shepherd for slope stabilization. Plan your route or face some frustrating dead ends and unsafe improvisations. Same goes for parts of Brays Bayou Trail near Spur 5, which are closed for SH 35 expansion "until 2026, if we're lucky."

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Closures on White Oak Bayou Greenway:** A significant section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is CLOSED through 2028 due to I-45/I-10 construction. Other sections near N. Shepherd also have intermittent daily closures. Expect detours onto city streets, which are NOT ideal for runners. Check maps before you go.
</AlertBox>

Over at Memorial Park, the Western Outer Loop Trail is still closed through 2027. While the Corporate 5K events that caused "heavier traffic on the Seymour Lieberman Trail" for parts of Eastern Glades started Friday and run until Oct 23rd, the main races happen Oct 20-22 in the evenings. So for today, you might still see some increased foot traffic, but nothing like a full-blown race. Just be aware and share the trail.

If you're heading to the coast, maybe for a beach run or just a dip in the Gulf, be extremely careful. There's a high rip current risk in effect through this morning for Gulf-facing beaches, including Galveston Island and the Bolivar Peninsula. These aren't just strong currents; they can literally drag you away from shore. Stay out of the water if you're not an experienced swimmer, and even then, exercise extreme caution.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Coastal Caution:** A High Rip Current Risk is in effect through Sunday morning for Gulf-facing beaches (Galveston Island, Bolivar Peninsula). Stick to dry land activities by the shore.
</AlertBox>
