## Trail Troubles: A Houston Runner's Nightmare

This is where today gets ugly. If you're a regular on the greenways, prepare for disappointment and detours.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Cypress Creek, Cypresswood, and the Anthills Under Threat!

The Harris County Flood Control District is moving forward with plans to remove constructed features from the **Cypress Creek, Cypresswood, and the Anthills trail systems**. This is huge for mountain bikers, trail runners, and hikers in that area. Feature removal is expected to start *soon*. They're not just dirt paths, people; they're vital community assets. If you use these, get involved with GHORBA and email Commissioner Tom Ramsey – this is a big deal.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### White Oak Bayou Greenway & MKT Trail Closures

*   **White Oak Bayou Greenway (Little White Oak Bayou Trail):** The section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is **closed until 2028** due to IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. There's a detour, but it's not ideal.
*   **MKT Trail / Heights Pedestrian Bridge:** That critical pedestrian bridge along the **MKT Trail in the Houston Heights** has been closed since January 2025 and repairs are *still delayed* as of February 2026. This forces cyclists and pedestrians onto less safe surface streets. It's a key link, and its closure is a major pain point.

Plan your routes carefully. These aren't minor inconveniences; they're significant disruptions that can force you onto busy roads.
</AlertBox>
