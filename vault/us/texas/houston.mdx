## The Rundown: Lungs, Joints, and Common Sense

Let's be real, Houston, May is basically pre-summer, and today is giving us a taste of what's to come, but with an extra dose of "ugh."

### Air Quality Alert

You might look outside and see blue, but your lungs are going to be working overtime if you push it. Our Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 103, driven by PM2.5. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," folks. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to feeling like you ran through a smoke detector test, keep your intense workouts indoors. Even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exposure isn't doing you any favors. The stagnant air and building heat we've been seeing aren't helping matters.

### Weather Woes: Heat, Humidity, and H2O (Too Much of It)

While the morning might have been cooler, the forecast for today predicts scattered thunderstorms and highs pushing up to 87°F (31°C) with humidity hovering around 73% (and currently at a whopping 91%). This isn't just "warm," it's "sweat-through-your-socks-before-you-leave-the-house" weather. More critically, a flood watch is in effect through Memorial Day weekend, with additional rainfall of 2 to 3 inches expected for many coastal areas. Some spots south of Houston have already gotten hammered with 5.5 to 7.5 inches of rain this week, leaving the ground saturated. Houston itself has seen 1.5 to 2.5 inches. This means anything from slick spots on trails to outright street flooding, especially if those scattered storms decide to dump heavy rain in one place.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Flood Threat is Real:** The City of Houston is at a Level 2 out of 4 risk for street flooding over the weekend. While today's storms are scattered, conditions can change fast. Avoid underpasses and areas prone to pooling water. Your PR isn't worth getting stranded or worse.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat & Humidity Combo:** Even if you avoid the rain, the heat index will make that 87°F feel a lot hotter. Stay hydrated. Seriously. And consider rescheduling any long, exposed runs for a cooler, drier day.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble: Construction and Closures

If you're planning on hitting the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**, be aware of ongoing issues. The Little White Oak Bayou Trail, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, is closed through 2028 due to work on I-45 and I-10 connector ramps. This isn't a minor detour; it's a significant rerouting. Plan accordingly, or pick a different route entirely.
