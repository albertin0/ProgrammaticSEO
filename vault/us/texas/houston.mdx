## The Rundown

Alright, Houston, let's talk brass tacks. That cold front that finally blew through? It's left us with some pretty sweet running weather. Good AQI, low humidity, and the temperature is going to be pleasant, climbing into the 70s, which is a welcome change from the earlier week's sweat-fest. Don't let the clear skies fool you into thinking it's completely smooth sailing, though.

<AlertBox type="warning">

### Wind Warning!

While the worst of the wind advisories are lifting for most of us, expect some lingering breezy conditions today, especially earlier this morning and closer to the coast. If you're out there, especially on exposed sections of trails or near the bays, you'll be fighting a headwind at some point. Also, remember that combo of dry air and strong winds? It raises the fire danger. So, no bonfires on your post-run cool-down, alright?

</AlertBox>

### Construction & Event Chaos

This city thrives on construction, and unfortunately, that means our beloved trails often pay the price. If your usual loop takes you along the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**, be aware. There are *intermittent daily closures* near N. Shepherd, from 7 AM to 5:30 PM, for flood control work. And get this: no official detour. So, you're on your own figuring out those street connections, which, let's be real, are rarely runner-friendly. Adding insult to injury, a major segment of White Oak Bayou Trail between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center has been shut down since January and isn't expected to reopen until *2028* due to I-45 connector work. That's right, two thousand twenty-eight. Plan alternate routes, people.

Over at **Memorial Park**, the western Outer Loop Trail is still closed until 2027 for the Memorial Groves project. And if you were planning to hit the Seymour Lieberman Trail or the Eastern Glades early today, forget it. The Corporate 5K has those sections locked down until 1 PM. So much for a quiet Saturday morning long run, huh?

<AlertBox type="danger">

### Major Trail Disruptions

Seriously, check your route before you head out, especially if you favor the bayou greenways or Memorial Park. Widespread, long-term construction closures on White Oak Bayou Trail and Brays Bayou Trail (at Spur 5) mean detours are mandatory and often poorly marked. Today's Corporate 5K further limits options in Memorial Park. Don't get stuck doing an unexpected out-and-back because you didn't check.

</AlertBox>
