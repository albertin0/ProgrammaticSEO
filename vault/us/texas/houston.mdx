## Houston's Outdoor Gauntlet: March 13, 2026

So, you want to get outside today, huh? Bless your heart. While the weather's doing its best to lure you out with a "Clear" forecast and a comfortable 10.4°C (51°F) start, peaking around 24°C (75°F) later, the rest of the environment is less enthused about your fitness goals.

Let's talk air. We're sitting at an AQI of 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's a lovely way of saying "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." If you're asthmatic, have any kind of respiratory issues, or just enjoy breathing without feeling like you've chain-smoked a pack, maybe dial it back. That fine particulate matter gets deep into your lungs, and trust me, your future self won't thank you for pushing it.

Then there's the perennial Houston favorite: pollen. It's only "Moderate" today, but for a lot of us, moderate might as well be a full-blown attack when combined with everything else. Keep the antihistamines handy if you're venturing out.

### Trail Closures: A Houston Tradition

Now, for the real kicker: our city's relentless assault on continuous trail running. If you had plans for a long, uninterrupted stride, you better have a backup plan (or three).

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Major Trail & Lane Closures:**
  - **Brays Bayou Trail:** Still shut down at Spur 5 due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. Seriously, that project feels like it started before I was born.
  - **Austin Street Bike Lane:** The city, in its infinite wisdom, demolished a key section from Elgin St to Gray St. Good luck with your downtown connections there.
  - **Cleburne Street Bike Lane:** Another casualty, closed at 288 for the I-45/NHHIP project.
  - **Buffalo Bayou Trail (North Side):** A section near the confluence of White Oak and Buffalo Bayou in Johnny Goyen Park is temporarily closed for safety concerns and modifications. You'll need a detour here.
  - **White Oak Bayou Greenway (Little White Oak Bayou Trail):** Between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is closed until 2028 for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. That's right, 2028.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro-Tip for Navigating Houston's Obstacle Course:** Always check for current detours, especially around Buffalo Bayou and White Oak Bayou. Sometimes it's a quick hop, sometimes it's a full-on street-running adventure. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

Essentially, today is a day for flexibility. If you're determined to get a run in, consider sticking to quieter neighborhood streets, or hitting up a track if you absolutely need to push the pace without inhaling too much PM2.5 or rerouting every five minutes. Or, you know, hit the gym. Nobody's judging (much).
