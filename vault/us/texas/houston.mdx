### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory Coming In Hot
A strong heat dome is settling over Houston, bringing dangerously high temperatures and even higher heat index values. Today's "feels like" temperature will easily hit 106°F, and we're looking at triple digits by tomorrow. This isn't just "uncomfortable"; it's legitimately *hazardous*. Houston Methodist has been explicit: humidity in our city is a massive factor, making it harder for your sweat to do its job and cool you down. ### TRAIL ALERT: Ongoing Construction on Key Greenways
If you're planning a run or ride, be aware of persistent closures. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 remains closed due to the SH 35 expansion. Over on the White Oak Bayou Greenway near Shepherd, expect intermittent closures (7 AM to 5:30 PM) for critical flood control work. Don't assume your usual route is open; check local updates before you head out, especially if you're trying to escape this ridiculous heat on shaded paths.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Smart (Or Just Stay Inside)
With the heat index soaring, seriously consider moving your workout indoors today. Hit the gym, swim laps, or tackle that list of chores. If you absolutely *must* be outside:
*   **Go early, like pre-dawn early.** The 25°C currently registering will vanish fast.
*   **Hydrate constantly.** And I mean constantly. Water, electrolytes, the works.
*   **Listen to your body.** Muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, confusion? Stop immediately and get somewhere cool. This isn't a badge of honor; it's a medical emergency waiting to happen.
</AlertBox>
