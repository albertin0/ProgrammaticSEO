## Houston, Seriously, Stay Inside (or At Least Be Smart)

Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in, hit the trails, feel the burn. But today, June 19, 2026, Houston is actively conspiring against your health. The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our region, with "feels like" temperatures projected to soar well into the triple digits. Yesterday saw extreme heat warnings with heat index values nearing 114 degrees in some spots. That kind of heat isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous, especially when combined with our famous Gulf Coast humidity that prevents your sweat from actually cooling you down. Doctors at Houston Methodist are already warning runners that humidity is often *more* dangerous than heat alone.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: This Ain't a Joke
A Heat Advisory is in effect, and "feels like" temperatures are reaching 109-110°F (43-43.3°C) today. This is prime territory for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Limit outdoor activity to the bare minimum, and if you must be out, go early, go short, and hydrate constantly. Your workout can wait. Your organs? Maybe not so much.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the air. With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, the air quality is squarely in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" category. That means if you've got asthma, heart issues, or even just a pair of lungs you plan on using for a while, prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion is a bad idea. The elevated humidity also helps fine particulate matter form, so it's a double whammy for your respiratory system.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Air Quality Concerns (PM2.5)
An AQI of 103 (PM2.5) puts us in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" range. Consider moving your workout indoors today. If you feel any respiratory discomfort, stop immediately.
</AlertBox>

You'll find sections of the **Brays Bayou Trail** closed at Spur 5 due to ongoing SH 35 expansion. Even the Buffalo Bayou Partnership indicates segments of the Kinder Trail east of downtown are closed for improvements. Don't expect your usual route to be clear sailing.
