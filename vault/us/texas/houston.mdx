## Today's Outdoor Reality Check

Let's be real, it's Sunday, April 19th, and while the mercury's a decent 13.5°C (56°F) under overcast skies, that's about where the good news ends for your outdoor workout. We had "moderate to heavy rain" last night into this morning, and while the chance of more showers today is low (around 14%), the ground isn't going to dry out magically. That means slippery concrete, muddy patches on natural trails, and potential for isolated high water, especially if you're north of I-10. If you saw the East Freeway shut down yesterday at McCarty Street due to high water, you know Houston doesn't mess around with drainage, or lack thereof.

### The Unavoidable Detours

If you're a runner, cyclist, or just trying to get some steps in, you need to be aware of the ongoing urban obstacle course. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** is a perennial favorite, but good luck navigating it seamlessly. That major chunk near I-45 and I-10 is closed off, and their "detour" sign usually just means "good luck, figure it out yourself." And don't forget the MKT Trail near White Oak Bayou, where the Hogan Street pedestrian bridge is still under repair, impacting access. It was supposed to reopen by "Spring 2026," but if you know Houston projects, "Spring" can mean anything from March to July.
