### Stay Smart, Not Sorry

Given the impending storms, localized street flooding is a real concern, especially in areas prone to pooling. Don't be that person trying to run through ankle-deep water; it's a quick way to twist an ankle or get sick. While Houston Transtar reports "no high water locations" right now, that can change faster than a politician's promise once the skies open up.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is still key!** Even with the rain, it's humid as hell. Drink more water than you think you need.
