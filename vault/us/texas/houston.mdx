## Air Quality Breakdown

Houston's AQI at 103 means the air is "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." This includes people with heart or lung disease, older adults, and children. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which can irritate your respiratory system and worsen existing conditions. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) mentioned small, scattered burning activities across parts of Southeast Texas might contribute to these elevated PM2.5 levels. So, that "clear" sky isn't as innocent as it looks.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (103 PM2.5).** If you have any respiratory issues, heart conditions, or are older or very young, seriously consider an indoor workout. Reduce prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion.
</AlertBox>
