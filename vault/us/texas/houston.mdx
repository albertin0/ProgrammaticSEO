<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat & Humidity!
Seriously, today's heat index of up to 110°F means heat-related illnesses are a *very* real threat. Dehydration, heat exhaustion, and even heat stroke can sneak up on you faster than a Texas mosquito. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or get a killer headache, stop immediately. Go indoors, cool down, and drink plenty of water. Forget the sugary sports drinks and alcohol for hydration. Your joints are already working overtime in this humidity, don't make them suffer trying to cool your overheating body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Unhealthy Air for Sensitive Groups
With the AQI at 103 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, people with asthma, heart disease, or other respiratory issues should absolutely reconsider outdoor exercise today. Even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exertion in this kind of air quality is going to be rough on your lungs. Maybe hit the treadmill, or at least keep your outdoor time short and easy.
</AlertBox>
