## The Grim Details: Heat and Haze

Let's be blunt: today's conditions are borderline dangerous for outdoor exertion. That "Mainly Clear" sky isn't a blessing; it just means more direct sun cooking you alive on top of the already oppressive humidity. The city has been under a Heat Advisory, and for good reason. Your body's going to struggle to cool itself, and pushing it too hard out there is a recipe for disaster. This isn't just about feeling a little sweaty; we're talking about legitimate heat exhaustion and stroke risks. Hydration isn't just drinking water; you need electrolytes if you're even *considering* being outside for an extended period.

### Trail Closures & Construction Headaches

As if the weather wasn't enough, some of our favorite spots are still a mess.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**White Oak Bayou Greenway: Intermittent Closures Near Shepherd**
Expect ongoing intermittent closures on the White Oak Bayou Greenway near N Shepherd, typically between 7:00 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. This isn't a quick fix; it's flood control work that's been going on for months and is expected to continue. And get this: there's still no official, clearly marked detour. So if you're relying on this major east-west connector, be prepared to navigate some less-than-ideal street detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">

Still no relief for the Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5. That section remains closed due to the ongoing (and seemingly never-ending) SH 35 expansion.
