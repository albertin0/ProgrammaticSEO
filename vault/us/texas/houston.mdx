  *   **Slippery & Soupy Conditions:** That "overcast" feeling comes with 81% humidity and "feels like" temps in the mid-90s, even with the actual temperature around 83°F. Mix that with wet pavement and potential standing water, and your joints are asking for trouble.

## Today's Rundown

The weather folks are calling for "multiple rounds of rain and storms" through the weekend, and today, Thursday, October 1st, is right in the thick of it. We're looking at 1 to 3 inches of rain in most areas, with isolated spots getting more. That's enough to turn our bayous into angry rivers and low-water crossings into death traps. "Never drive through flooded areas" isn't just a suggestion; it's how you avoid becoming a statistic.

The air quality, surprisingly, is generally "Good" with an AQI of 47. Pollen is moderate, which is a small silver lining if you're stuck indoors with your allergies. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a good day for an outdoor session. The humidity alone is enough to make you feel like you're breathing soup.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Flash Flood Risk is REAL.** With scattered thunderstorms and heavy rainfall predicted, particularly this afternoon and evening, watch out for rapidly rising water. Even a few inches can sweep you off your feet or stall your car. Turn around, don't drown, even if you're on foot.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Construction Chaos on Trails:** If your usual route takes you along the White Oak Bayou Greenway towards downtown, remember that segment's been effectively gone since January 2026 and isn't coming back for years. And the Hogan Pedestrian Bridge rehabilitation? No end in sight since September 2025. It means longer detours and more frustration.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Indoor Options Today:** Seriously, consider the gym, an indoor track, or a living room workout. Your lungs might be okay with the air, but your knees and ankles will thank you for not navigating a watery obstacle course.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, or rather, stay safe *in* there.
