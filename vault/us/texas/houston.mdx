## Trail & Road Hazards

Construction continues to be the unofficial state sport of Houston, and it's impacting our greenways.

*   **White Oak Bayou Greenway Closure:** If you usually run or bike the White Oak Bayou Greenway (specifically the Little White Oak Bayou Trail) between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, brace yourself. It's shut down until 2028 for the IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. There's a detour, but it's not as scenic or smooth. Don't be a hero; use the detour signage.
*   **Brays Bayou Trail:** Still dealing with closures at Spur 5 due to the SH 35 expansion. This one's been dragging on and is expected to continue through 2026. Plan an alternate route if you typically rely on that section.
*   **Downtown Roadwork:** Expect ongoing lane closures on I-10 westbound from IH-45 Gulf/Hogan St to Studemont St as part of the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project. This can impact access to trails in that area, so give yourself extra time if driving to a starting point near there.
