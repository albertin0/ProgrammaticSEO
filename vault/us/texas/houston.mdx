## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality is pushing it:** That AQI of 103? That's firmly in the "Moderate" category, thanks to PM2.5 doing its thing in the air. For those of us who actually *like* breathing, that's not ideal. Sensitive groups, you're looking at "Unhealthy" levels, so maybe skip that long run. Even for us tougher folks, prolonged exposure today might leave you with a scratchy throat. It's that lovely Houston cocktail of everything from industrial emissions (shout out to the persistent issues in Harris County) to just plain stagnant air doing its job.
*   **Pollen's still a punk:** "Moderate" pollen levels mean your nose probably already knows. Allergy sufferers, you're not getting a break. Combine that with the PM2.5 and you've got a delightful recipe for a post-workout sinus party.
*   **Memorial Park is playing hard to get:** If your weekend plans involved cruising the Memorial Loop, you're out of luck, at least during prime daylight hours. The Chevron Championship has those roads locked down for tournament access and parking until 8 PM. So unless your idea of a good time is dodging golf carts and security at midnight, find another route or hit the treadmill.
</BulletList>

### Local Hazards & Heads-Up

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction & Small Road Blocks
If you're downtown, be aware that "Polk Construction" has the north curb lane of Polk Street (between La Branch & Main) closed until 11 PM tonight. Not a massive detour, but annoying if you're trying to weave through for your urban exploration run. Also, a "Neighborhood Community Day" has the 3900 Block of Mulberry St between Sul Ross and Barnard St closed from 1 PM to 5 PM. Plan accordingly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Obviously
It's 28.2°C (that's about 83°F for the Fahrenheit folks) and "mainly clear" out there, so the sun's going to be beating down. Even if the air quality tries to deter you, if you *must* be outside, drink water like it's your job. This isn't exactly "extreme heat" but it's warm enough to get you into trouble if you're not paying attention.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. We're Houstonians. We power through. But today? Maybe keep it shorter, keep it indoors if you're feeling sniffly, or find a less exposed route than your usual. Memorial Park isn't going anywhere, even if you can't run the loop today.
