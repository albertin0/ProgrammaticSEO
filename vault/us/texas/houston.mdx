<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat and Humidity!
This isn't just about feeling a bit sweaty; this is about heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Doctors warn that heat-related illness can escalate quickly. If you *must* be outside, keep it short, stay hydrated with water or electrolyte drinks, and take frequent breaks in the shade or air conditioning. Never leave kids or pets in a car.
</AlertBox>
