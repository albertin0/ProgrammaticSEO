## The Air: Breathe at Your Own Risk (Almost)

So, your phone might say "Mainly Clear" and the sun is out, but that doesn't mean the air is crisp. We're sitting at an AQI of 103, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. That's a solid "Moderate" rating, meaning if you've got any respiratory issues – or just like your lungs to not feel gritty – you might want to scale back that intense run. You're also battling moderate pollen levels. It's not a full-blown "stay inside" day, but it's certainly not doing your airways any favors.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate AQI & Pollen Combo
With PM2.5 in the moderate range and pollen still high, sensitive groups should consider indoor alternatives or reduce outdoor exertion. If you're running, don't push the pace too hard.
</AlertBox>

## Weather: Hot and Potentially Muddy

The temperature is climbing to a balmy 28.2°C (that's about 83°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) under mainly clear skies. While that sounds lovely for a run, remember what happened yesterday: heavy rainfall and localized flooding across the city, particularly north of I-10. Some spots got up to two inches of rain! That means any unpaved trails or low-lying areas are likely waterlogged, muddy, and slick. You might start off dry, but you'll probably finish looking like you wrestled a gator in a swamp.

## Trail Conditions & Roadblocks

Speaking of trails, if your usual route takes you along *Brays Bayou Trail*, be warned: there's ongoing construction at Spur 5 that's keeping parts of it closed. That project isn't going anywhere fast, so plan for detours and frustrations. And if you were hoping to hit up the area around *Sesquicentennial Park* downtown for a scenic jog, forget about it this afternoon. "Bunnies on the Bayou" is taking over from noon until 6 PM, guaranteeing crowds, road closures, and general chaos.

Construction, as always, is a permanent fixture in Houston. The I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project is still chugging along, affecting major roadways and likely sending more cars onto side streets, so keep your head up if you're mixing pavement with your run.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route (and Your Shoes) Wisely
Expect muddy, slick conditions on unpaved trails due to yesterday's heavy rains. Stick to known, paved routes, or be prepared for extra traction. Avoid areas around Sesquicentennial Park this afternoon.
</AlertBox>
