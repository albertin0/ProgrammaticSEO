## The Air: Just Breathe (Indoors)

Today's AQI of 85 is already pushing into "Moderate," but the **Ozone Action Day** takes it up a notch to "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." This isn't just a suggestion; it's a warning. If you have asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, seriously consider moving your workout indoors today. Even healthy individuals might experience throat irritation or difficulty breathing with prolonged exposure.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **OZONE ACTION DAY!** Avoid prolonged outdoor exertion today, especially if you're sensitive to air pollution. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

## The Weather: Swampy & Sneaky Hot

Despite the clear skies, that 25.9°C temperature, climbing to an expected 35°C (95°F) today, combined with the lingering humidity, is a recipe for trouble. Houston's humidity is notorious for making even moderate temperatures feel oppressive and hindering your body's ability to cool itself. Dehydration and heat-related illnesses are a real risk, even for the fittest among us.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Heat + Humidity = Danger.** Even at "moderate" temperatures, Houston's humidity means your body works harder to cool down. Hydrate aggressively, and watch for signs of heat exhaustion.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures: The Eternal Struggle

Remember that smooth stretch of the **White Oak Bayou Trail** near the Heights? Yeah, good luck with that. The section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is locked down tighter than a drum due to I-45/I-10 connector ramp construction and will be until *2028*. It's not a short-term inconvenience; it's a multi-year commitment to detours. And don't forget the daily flood control construction closures near North Shepherd that still might be in effect. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 is also still impacted by construction, with an optimistic "2026 if we're lucky" for reopening. Check Houston TranStar or local trail group pages before you head out, because getting rerouted mid-stride is peak Houston frustration.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Plan Your Route!** Ongoing construction on the White Oak Bayou Trail and Brays Bayou Trail means significant closures. Don't assume your usual path is open.
</AlertBox>
