This isn't just a bit sweaty; it significantly increases your risk for heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Seriously consider moving your long runs indoors, or waiting until the sun is completely gone. And even then, it'll still be hot and muggy.
</AlertBox>

As of September 19, a Flash Flood Warning was issued for Houston until September 22, 4:00 AM. This isn't just about getting your shoes wet; it's about being swept into a bayou or stranded on a suddenly submerged street. If you see standing water, turn around. It's not worth it.
</AlertBox>

</AlertBox>
