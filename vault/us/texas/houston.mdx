## The Lowdown: Don't Let Houston's Charm Trip You Up

Today, the biggest headache for runners and outdoor enthusiasts isn't the weather (for once), but the persistent churn of development and maintenance that keeps our bayou city perpetually under construction. While the sun's out and the temps are reasonable, keep your eyes open.

### Air Quality & Your Lungs

That "Moderate" AQI isn't a red alert, but it's not exactly sparkling mountain air either. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) indicates we've got some Ozone and PM2.5 floating around. This is classic Houston, where we consistently struggle with ozone levels. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just easily irritated lungs, you're in the "sensitive groups" category, and you might experience some minor symptoms with prolonged exposure. It's not a day to push your hardest intervals if your breathing feels off.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone and PM2.5 in the air today means sensitive groups should scale back prolonged or intense outdoor activity.** Pay attention to your body and cut your workout short if you feel any respiratory irritation.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Troubles: Know Before You Go

Houston's green spaces are a godsend, but they're not immune to the city's constant evolution. The **Brays Bayou Trail**, a staple for many of us, is still closed at Spur 5 due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion. This isn't a new closure, but it's easy to forget if it's not on your usual loop. Don't plan on connecting through there without a detour.

Speaking of closures, parts of the **Buffalo Bayou Trail** near Downtown have been closed since January to fix a blind spot, so check the maps if you're heading that way. Also, the **MKT Trail Hogan Pedestrian Bridge** over White Oak Bayou is still completely shut down for rehabilitation, with trails directly underneath it closed. That project started in September of *last year* and isn't expected to finish until the end of December. This means significant detours if you rely on that crossing to connect your run. If you're planning a run through **MacGregor Park**, be aware that large sections, including trails and facilities, are closed for renovations.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Always double-check city and park websites for current trail conditions.** Construction schedules can change, and nobody wants to hit a dead end five miles into a long run.
</AlertBox>

No major extreme weather warnings or city-wide event blockages are affecting workout routes today, apart from minor, localized street closures tonight for "National Night Out" events, which won't impact daytime trail use. So, go ahead and get out there, but keep your head up and breathe easy.
