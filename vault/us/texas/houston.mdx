Look, I get it. The sun's out, and you want to log those miles. But today's a day where your body is going to be fighting a battle on multiple fronts. That AQI of 103 is no joke, especially with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. These tiny particles get deep into your lungs, and coupled with an Ozone Action Day, anyone with asthma, allergies, or even just sensitive airways is going to have a rough time. Even if you consider yourself a "healthy individual," prolonged exertion outside could lead to irritation.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Hazard Alert: Poor Air Quality & Ozone Action Day!** Houston's air is unhealthy for sensitive groups. If you have respiratory issues, kids, or older adults in your household, keep outdoor exposure to a minimum. Everyone else, consider scaling back the intensity and duration of your workout.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget the heat. While 28.2°C (82.8°F) might not sound like a scorcher, Houston's humidity is legendary for making it *feel* much hotter. Expect "feels like" temperatures to climb. Hydration is critical, even more so when your body is already stressed by poor air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Heat Warning: Stay Hydrated!** Even if the thermometer isn't screaming "danger," the humidity will make it feel significantly warmer. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. Plan to run in the early morning or late evening if you absolutely must be outside.
</AlertBox>

On the trails front, our city's never-ending construction saga continues. If your usual route takes you along the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**, be aware that a significant section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is closed for IH-45 and I-10 connector ramp work, and it's expected to stay that way until 2028. There are detours, but they add complexity and often involve less scenic (and sometimes less safe) routes. Also, parts of the Brays Bayou Trail are still affected by the SH 35 expansion near Spur 5, and the Buffalo Bayou Trail in downtown near UH-Downtown has seen recent closures for safety concerns and retaining wall modifications. Don't just assume your path is clear; check for local updates before you head out.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Trail Tips: Know Before You Go.** Construction closures are a constant here. Check the latest updates for trails like the White Oak Bayou Greenway, Brays Bayou Trail, and Buffalo Bayou trails before you lace up. You might find yourself rerouting more than you planned.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, today might be a good day to hit the treadmill, do some strength work indoors, or just embrace the rest day. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
