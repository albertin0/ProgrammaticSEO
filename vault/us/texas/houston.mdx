### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Look, I know 16 degrees Celsius (that's about 61°F for you Celsius-challenged folks) and clear skies sound divine, especially after our usual sauna-like existence. But Mother Nature (or rather, Houston's air) has a cruel joke for us today. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) has slapped an **Ozone Action Day** on us for Thursday, October 22nd. What does that mean for your glorious run? It means atmospheric conditions are basically brewing up ground-level ozone pollution, and the National Weather Service is literally telling us to "restrict outdoor activities to early or late in the day" because it's unhealthy for sensitive groups. Your lungs aren't going to be thrilled. Even our general Air Quality Index is sitting in the "Moderate" range at 66, with PM2.5 as the main culprit, though ozone is the real buzzkill today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Ozone Action Day!
Seriously, if you have asthma, allergies, or just like having functional lungs, consider keeping your hard-core cardio indoors today. If you *must* be outside, keep it light and stick to the early morning or late evening. Your respiratory system will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Updates: The Urban Obstacle Course Continues

Houston construction. Is there any other kind? If you were planning on hitting some of our prime greenways, prepare for detours, closures, and possibly a mild existential crisis.

The **Memorial Park Outer Loop Trail**'s western section is still shut down for the Memorial Groves project, and it's not looking like it'll reopen until 2027. While the Seymour Lieberman Trail is mostly open, parts of it are being shifted, so expect some disruption there too.

Over on the **Brays Bayou Trail**, you're out of luck near Spur 5; that whole section is closed for the ongoing SH 35 expansion. And don't even think about a smooth ride on the **White Oak Bayou Greenway** near Shepherd Drive – they've got intermittent closures daily from 7 AM to 5:30 PM, and it's been going on for months. Another part of the White Oak Bayou Greenway, between Houston Avenue and Castillo Community Center, is also closed until further notice.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Closures, People!
These aren't suggestions; these are actual barriers. Check Houston TranStar for highway work if you're biking on roads, and be prepared for reroutes on our major trails. Don't assume your usual path is open.
</AlertBox>

Oh, and if you're near the **Heights Hike & Bike Trail**, know that the M-K-T complex is hosting their Hike, Bike & Fright Fest today. More people, possibly in costumes, on bikes, looking for candy. Just something to be aware of if you're trying to set a PR.
