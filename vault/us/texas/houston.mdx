## Air Quality: More Than Just a Haze

Don't let those "mainly clear" skies fool you. While it's not a visible smog cloud, the air around Houston today is holding onto fine particulate matter (PM2.5), pushing our AQI into a zone that's not great for anyone, and downright risky for sensitive individuals. This particulate matter can get deep into your lungs, causing irritation, coughing, and shortness of breath. Some forecasts are even nudging that AQI higher, suggesting it could feel even worse. The TCEQ mentioned light winds and possibly "smoke from regional burnings" as contributors to this PM2.5, so it's not just car exhaust.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI (103 - Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups) & PM2.5:** Folks with asthma, heart conditions, or other respiratory issues, seriously consider moving your workout indoors today. Even healthy individuals might feel some irritation or discomfort with prolonged exposure. Less strenuous activities or shorter outdoor durations are highly recommended if you must be out there.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Troubles: Still Houston's Favorite Pastime

It wouldn't be Houston if something wasn't under construction, right? Several major arteries for runners and cyclists are still impacted.

**White Oak Bayou Greenway:** If this is your usual haunt, be aware of intermittent closures near Shepherd, happening daily from 7 AM to 5:30 PM. This is likely due to Harris County Flood Control District work, and last I checked, there wasn't a clearly marked detour. Don't get caught off guard trying to navigate construction zones on the fly.

**Brays Bayou Trail:** Still dealing with that long-term closure at Spur 5 because of the SH 35 expansion. That project's dragging into late 2026, possibly even 2027. Finding alternate routes can be a headache, so plan ahead if you usually run this stretch.

**Memorial Park:** While the Seymour Lieberman Trail (the paved 3-miler) is mostly open, the western portion of the beloved Outer Loop Trail is still closed until late 2027 due to the Memorial Groves project. Stick to the Lieberman loop or explore other areas if you rely on the Outer Loop.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos:** Always check Houston TranStar or local park websites before heading out. Unexpected closures on major thoroughfares like IH-10 also mean more traffic and potentially less safe alternate routes on surface streets.
</AlertBox>
