Forget a glorious morning run, Houston. Today's conditions are a real mixed bag of "meh" and "oof" for your lungs.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
  - The Air Quality Index is hitting 103, squarely in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" range, thanks to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) that you'll be sucking right into your lungs.
  - Tree pollen is absolutely through the roof today, not just "Moderate" as some might say, but "Very High," and those 15-31 mph winds aren't helping keep it grounded.
  - It's going to be a hot one, with temps climbing to nearly 83°F and feeling even warmer, so prepare to sweat it out.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. The sun's out, it's "Mainly Clear," and your running shoes are calling. But let's be honest, Houston, today's not exactly prime outdoor workout weather unless you enjoy coughing up a lung and feeling like you ran through a cottonwood explosion.

The biggest culprit this morning is our good old friend, PM2.5. With an AQI of 103 and fine particulate matter as the dominant pollutant, anyone with even a hint of respiratory issues, or even perfectly healthy folks pushing hard, should seriously reconsider their plans for that long run down the Buffalo Bayou Park trails. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) even mentioned potential lingering light smoke from scattered burning activities across parts of East and Southeast Texas contributing to those PM2.5 levels. So, you're not just breathing city grime, you might be getting a bonus dose of campfire air from a distance.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **AQI 103: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups.** If you have asthma, COPD, or any heart condition, keep your workout indoors today. Even if you don't, prolonged exertion outdoors is a bad idea when PM2.5 is this high. AccuWeather literally said the air quality is "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups," advising to "Reduce time spent outside."
</AlertBox>

Then there's the pollen situation. While the general forecast might say "Moderate," dig a little deeper, and you'll see tree pollen is listed as "Very High" for today. Those steady southerly winds, averaging 15 mph with gusts up to 31 mph, are doing an excellent job of airborne transportation for all that invisible misery. If your eyes are itching and your nose is running just thinking about it, you're not wrong. This isn't the day to try for a PR on the White Oak Bayou Greenway if you want to avoid looking like you just cried through a sad movie.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Allergy Sufferers: Take precautions.** Pop your antihistamines *before* you head out, wear sunglasses to shield your eyes, and consider a quick shower and change of clothes immediately after your workout to get that pollen off you. Don't rub your eyes, no matter how much they itch.
</AlertBox>

Finally, the heat. While 28.2°C (around 83°F) might not scream "extreme heat advisory," it's certainly warm enough to feel it, especially with a RealFeel® of 84°F and a Heat Index of 85°F. Coupled with the humidity we often get, you'll be sweating buckets. Hydration is always key, but today, it's non-negotiable. Don't be a hero; carry water and don't push yourself to heat exhaustion just for the sake of an outdoor sweat session.

Honestly, save your lungs and joints the trouble. Hit the gym, find an indoor track, or do some bodyweight stuff at home. Your body will thank you later.
