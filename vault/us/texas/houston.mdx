## Why your lungs might feel like sandpaper (and your joints like jelly)

<BulletList>

*   **Air Quality Alert is ON for PM2.5:** Your lungs are going to be working overtime today. The AQI is clocking in at 103, which is "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" due to PM2.5. That's not just a suggestion; it means if you've got asthma, heart issues, or even just fancy breathing easily, you need to be cautious. We're also seeing elevated ozone levels, which just adds to the fun.
*   **Houston Heat & Humidity – The OG Workout Hazard:** It's going to hit the mid-90s (around 35°C) with significant humidity. Doctors consistently warn that Houston's humidity is often *more* dangerous than the heat itself because it prevents your sweat from actually cooling you down. Your body's trying its best, but when the air is already saturated, you're essentially marinating in your own sweat, and your core temperature keeps climbing.
*   **Mind the Trail Closures & Race Day Crowds:** If your usual route takes you along the Brays Bayou Trail, specifically near Spur 5, you're out of luck. That section is still closed due to the ongoing SH 35 expansion project, and it's not opening anytime soon. Expect that detour to be a permanent fixture for a while. Also, this morning, several races are happening, like the Sloth Run, Run for Breast Cancer, and the Summer Sizzler at Brazos River Park. If you're near those areas, expect crowds and potential congestion, especially early on.
