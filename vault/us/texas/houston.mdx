### Flood Threat: Your Biggest Obstacle Today

Tropical Storm Edouard's ghost is still dumping rain, and the ground is saturated. Numerous flood watches and flash flood warnings are active across the region. Areas just northeast of Houston, especially in Polk and Liberty counties, have seen devastating rainfall.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DO NOT DRIVE INTO FLOODED ROADS.** It's not just a suggestion; it's how people get hurt, and how rescue teams get put at risk. If you see water on the road or trail, turn around. "Turn Around, Don't Drown" isn't just a catchy phrase; it's a life-saving directive.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Closures

Planning a long run on the **Buffalo Bayou Trail** near Downtown? Forget about it. Sections are still closed due to "blind spot" fixes from back in January, with no clear re-opening date. Meanwhile, the **MKT Trail** also has ongoing impacts from the Shepherd Durham Improvements, with a temporary path as a workaround. Expect slow traffic and potential detours even on major roadways like IH-10 Katy, where lane closures are active for demolition work.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Your Route, Seriously:** Before you head out, verify that your planned trail isn't shut down or riddled with construction detours. Many city parks have ongoing projects.
</AlertBox>

All things considered, this isn't the day to try and earn that Strava badge outdoors. Hit the gym, find an indoor track, or reschedule. Your lungs, joints, and common sense will thank you.
