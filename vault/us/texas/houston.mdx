## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
*   **We're Drowning, Not Running:** Despite any fleeting moments of "mainly clear" skies you *might* have seen, the forecast for Friday is a washout. A Flood Watch is in effect for Harris County until Saturday morning, with "significant rainfall" and "widespread heavy rain" expected to dump up to 4 inches in some spots. Expect street flooding and ponding, especially north of I-10. Running through a shallow lake isn't cross-training, it's asking for leptospirosis and a sprained ankle.
*   **Breathe That In (Don't):** The air quality is sitting at a charming 103 AQI, officially "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," with PM2.5 being the main culprit. This isn't just your imagination; it's likely from agricultural burning down in Mexico, with that smoke drifting north and thickening our already humid air. Combine that with heavy breathing during a workout, and you're just sucking down extra particulate matter. Your lungs deserve better.
*   **Trail? What Trail?:** Forget hitting any long stretches, because between the rain turning everything into mud and ongoing construction, your options are limited anyway. Specifically, a big chunk of the White Oak Bayou Greenway (the Little White Oak Bayou Trail section between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center) is shut down clear through 2028 for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. And if it's not closed, it's probably underwater right now.
</BulletList>

You might have woken up thinking, "Hey, it's only 82 degrees, maybe a quick jog?" (Yes, 28.2°C feels like 82.8°F, and that's already warm enough to make you sweat buckets). But the current weather alerts are screaming "stay inside" for a reason. Those "mainly clear" conditions from earlier? History. We're looking at a day of on-and-off downpours.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOOD WARNING: TURN AROUND, DON'T DROWN.** Seriously, Houston, you know the drill. With several inches of rain expected, roads can become impassable incredibly fast. Do not attempt to run or drive through flooded streets. It's not worth it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**AIR QUALITY ALERT: YOUR LUNGS ARE NOT FANS OF PM2.5.** With an AQI over 100, sensitive groups (asthmatics, older adults, children, and anyone with heart or lung issues) should absolutely avoid outdoor exertion. The rest of us should seriously limit it. If you start feeling any throat irritation or difficulty breathing, cut it short.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**CONSTRUCTION AND CLOSURES ARE STILL A THING.** If you usually run the White Oak Bayou Greenway, remember that major detour is still in effect due to the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project. Always check Houston TranStar for the latest road and trail closures before you head out, though today, the rain makes most of that moot.
</AlertBox>

My professional advice? Find a treadmill, hit a spin class, or do some bodyweight circuits in your living room. Today is a prime example of Houston saying, "Not today, runners. Not today."
