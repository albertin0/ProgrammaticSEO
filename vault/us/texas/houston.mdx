Look, if you're planning anything more strenuous than shuffling from your AC to the fridge today, you're either new to Houston summers or you've got a death wish. Seriously.

<BulletList>

*   **Heat Dome of Doom:** We're currently simmering under a brutal heat dome, pushing "feels like" temperatures into seriously dangerous territory. Air temps are hitting triple digits, but with the humidity, it's going to feel more like 108-110°F, possibly even higher in some spots. Forget about that "dry heat" myth; our Gulf Coast humidity is the real killer, turning your sweat into useless skin decor and making your body work overtime just to stay cool.
*   **Air Quality is Meh, Pollen's Still Around:** Our air quality is hanging out in the "Moderate" zone with an AQI of 54, dominant pollutant being ozone. That's not helping anyone's lungs, especially when you're already gasping for air in this soup. Pollen is also moderate, so your allergies might chime in for the fun too.
*   **Greenway Grief:** If the heat wasn't enough to detour you, good luck navigating some of our prime running spots. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, particularly near North Shepherd, is still experiencing intermittent closures from 7 AM to 5:30 PM due to ongoing flood control work. And don't even think about the section of the Little White Oak Bayou Trail between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center—that's been shut down since January for TxDOT work. So, your usual long run might turn into a frustrating maze.

</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **EXTREME HEAT WARNING: This isn't a drill.**
The city is under a heat advisory, and doctors are screaming from the rooftops to limit strenuous outdoor activity. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on faster than you think, even for seasoned athletes. Don't be that person wheeled into the ER because you "had to get your miles in."
</AlertBox>

### What to do instead of melting on the asphalt:

*   **Early Bird Gets the (Slightly Less Cooked) Worm:** If you absolutely *must* be outside, aim for before 8 AM. Seriously, set your alarm for stupid-early. Even then, the humidity will still be thick enough to chew.
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** And no, your morning coffee doesn't count. Start drinking water hours before you step outside, and keep chugging it. Electrolyte drinks are your friend, especially if you're going to sweat through your shirt just walking to the mailbox.
*   **Dress Like You're Sailing a Yacht, Not Running a Marathon:** Loose-fitting, light-colored, moisture-wicking gear is non-negotiable. Forget cotton; it just gets heavy and traps heat.
*   **Know the Signs:** Dizziness, nausea, excessive thirst, headaches, confusion, or *stopping sweating* are all red flags. Get to shade, get water, cool down, and don't be a hero.
*   **Hit the Gym, Seriously:** This is what indoor treadmills and ellipticals were invented for. Or, you know, find a mall and walk around in the AC. It's not glamorous, but neither is heat stroke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Trail Closure Alert: Mind the Gaps!**
Parts of the White Oak Bayou Greenway and Little White Oak Bayou Trail are closed for construction and flood control. Don't assume your usual route is clear. Check for detours or plan an alternative. These closures can last for months.
</AlertBox>

Don't let Houston's summer get the best of you. There's always tomorrow, when it'll probably be just as hot, but at least you'll have all your wits about you.
