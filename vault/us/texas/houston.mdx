## Hazards to Navigate

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Triple-Digit Heat Index is No Joke
Seriously, Houston heat doesn't mess around. With temperatures climbing into the low to mid-90s and humidity through the roof, the "feels like" temperature will hit triple digits. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous. Hydrate relentlessly, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear, and for the love of your kidneys, listen to your body. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or cramped, *stop*. No PR is worth heatstroke.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Storms & Flooding Risk
Don't let the morning sun fool you. Tropical moisture is here, and scattered thunderstorms are highly probable from midday onwards. These aren't just sprinkles; they can bring sudden, heavy downpours, lightning, and gusty winds. Expect localized street flooding, especially in low-lying areas or where drainage is poor. If the sky starts looking angry, get indoors. Lightning doesn't care about your running streak.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Zone Headaches
If your route takes you anywhere near the I-10 Katy Freeway between Heights Blvd and I-45, or around the White Oak Bayou area, be prepared for disruptions. We're still dealing with major lane closures and even ramp closures due to the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project and Studemont St Bridge demolition. This not only impacts vehicular traffic but can create detours and unsafe crossings for pedestrians and cyclists trying to access paths like the White Oak Bayou Greenway. Stay vigilant, watch for heavy equipment, and assume drivers aren't looking out for you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Moderate Air & Pollen Considerations
Today's AQI is in the moderate range due to PM2.5. While not alarming, combined with moderate pollen levels, those with respiratory sensitivities might feel it a bit more, especially during strenuous activity. It's not a reason to stay home for most, but keep it in mind if you're feeling sniffly or a bit chesty.
</AlertBox>
