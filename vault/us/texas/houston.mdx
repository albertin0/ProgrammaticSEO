## Why today isn't the day to PR

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality's a Bit Gritty:** That AQI is sitting at 72, which is "Moderate," with PM2.5 being the main culprit. Light morning winds can kick up urban particulate matter, and we're even seeing some potential light smoke from burnings in southern Mexico wafting our way. So, your lungs won't exactly be singing.
*   **Pollen's Doing Its Thing:** "Moderate" pollen levels mean those of us with allergies are already sniffly, and adding a workout to that isn't doing anyone any favors.
*   **Bayou Trails are Likely a Soupy Mess:** Remember Tropical Depression Edouard that just rolled through earlier this week? It dumped anywhere from 4 to 12 inches of rain in parts of southeast Texas. Even though the skies are clear today, our bayous are still swollen, and those trails along them, especially the low-lying sections, are probably muddy, slick, and potentially underwater. Your ankles and knees will not thank you for navigating that slop.
*   **Construction Chaos Continues:** Seriously, are they ever *not* working on something here? Several major trail arteries are currently closed. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, a staple for so many, is shut down between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center until 2028 due to the IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. The Brays Bayou Trail is also closed at Spur 5 thanks to the ongoing SH 35 expansion. And don't forget the Buffalo Bayou Trail near downtown, closed since January to fix a "blind spot." These closures funnel runners onto less-safe city streets.
*   **M-K-T Trail Is Hosting a Party:** If you were eyeing the M-K-T Trail, think again during the morning. The "Trail Mix: Fall Edition" is happening today from 10:00 am to 11:00 am on the trailside deck in the Heights, promising crowds, music, and activities. Good for families, bad for setting a pace.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up, Houstonians: Watch for Lingering Water on Trails!**
Despite clear skies today, the recent downpours from Tropical Depression Edouard mean many low-lying areas and underpasses along our bayou trails could still have standing water or thick mud. Expect detours and be prepared to turn around if a path looks unsafe. Don't risk a twisted ankle or worse.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is Moderate: Take It Easy, Especially If You're Sensitive**
With an AQI of 72 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, it's not the day to push lung-busting intervals. If you have respiratory issues or are prone to allergies, consider scaling back your outdoor activity or sticking to indoor workouts. Your airways will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Houston Humidity is Always a Factor**
Even at 79°F, Houston's notorious humidity will make it feel warmer and make it harder for your body to cool itself. Hydrate well before, during, and after your workout, and listen to your body for signs of heat exhaustion. Early morning runs are your best bet.
</AlertBox>
