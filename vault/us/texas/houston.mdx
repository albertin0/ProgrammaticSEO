## Houston's Perpetual Construction Zone: Trails Edition

Let's be brutally honest: finding an uninterrupted stretch of trail in Houston these days is like trying to find a parking spot downtown on a Friday night – nearly impossible. While the weather itself might give you a brief reprieve from the oppressive summer oven, the urban landscape is still fighting against you.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### White Oak Bayou Greenway: Still a Maze
If your usual route involves the White Oak Bayou Greenway, particularly between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, you're out of luck. This section is **closed through 2028** for IH-45/I-10 connector ramp work. That's right, *2028*. Detours are posted, but honestly, it's a headache. Plan an alternate route or prepare for some creative navigation through urban sprawl. There are other sections around Shepherd that also see weekday closures. Don't be surprised by unexpected detours.
</AlertBox>

### What's Up with the Air?

Your AQI is sitting at 76, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) predicts ozone levels could hit the "middle to upper end of the 'Moderate' range" in the Houston area, especially as the day warms up. For the average runner, this means being aware. For those with asthma or other respiratory issues, this is your cue to maybe dial back the intensity or stick to indoor workouts today.

While the "Mainly Clear" forecast might tempt you, remember Houston's humidity is a silent workout killer. Even at 25.9°C (around 79°F), the high moisture content in the air can significantly impede your body's ability to cool itself through sweat evaporation, making heat-related illness a risk even on seemingly mild days. Hydrate early and often, even if you're not feeling parched.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Humidity isn't your friend.
Even without scorching temperatures, Houston's humidity means your sweat just chills on your skin instead of evaporating and cooling you down. You'll overheat faster and put more strain on your heart and lungs. Drink more water than you think you need.
</AlertBox>

### More Trail Closures Than You Can Shake a Stick At
Beyond White Oak Bayou, don't forget the **Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5** is still closed due to that seemingly never-ending SH 35 expansion. Portions of **MacGregor Park** are also under renovation, impacting trails and facilities starting last month. If you're running downtown, expect detours and closures around the Convention District Transformation Project, with **Polk Street** and several others closed to both vehicles and pedestrians. Check Houston TranStar before you head out, or just assume your favorite path is partially eaten by orange cones.
</AlertBox>
