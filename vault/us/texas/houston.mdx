## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>**Your Lungs Aren't Going to Thank You:** With an AQI clocking in at 103 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, the air quality is officially "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." That means if you have asthma, allergies, or just generally enjoy breathing, you're going to feel it. Pushing hard will just shovel more of that particulate matter deep into your system.</li>
  <li>**It's a Hot and Bouncy Mix:** The mercury's climbing to 28.2°C (that's pushing 83°F, for those of us who still think in Fahrenheit).   <li>**Pollen's Piling On:** On top of everything else, we've got a moderate pollen count. So, while you're dodging PM2.5, your body's also trying to fend off whatever airborne flora Houston's throwing at it.</li>

## The Nitty-Gritty: Don't Be Dumb, Houston.

### Air Quality Alert

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **AQI 103 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups) & PM2.5:** This isn't a suggestion; it's a warning. Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) can irritate your eyes, nose, and respiratory system, and long-term exposure can aggravate heart and lung disease. If you're one of the "sensitive groups" (you know who you are), stay inside. If you're not, consider this your body's extra workload. Avoid prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Warnings

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **High Temps & Breezy Conditions:** Today's 28.2°C (82.8°F) with moderate, gusty winds means hydration is paramount. You'll sweat more than you think, and the wind will dry it faster, masking your fluid loss. Drink more water than you think you need, *before* you even step outside. Also, that breeze might feel nice, but it's a perfect vehicle for the airborne crud.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards & Construction

Houston's always building something, which is great for the economy, terrible for uninterrupted workouts.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Brays Bayou Trail & I-10 Construction:** If you're planning a run or ride along the Brays Bayou Trail, be aware there's an ongoing closure at Spur 5 due to the SH 35 expansion project. This one's expected to drag on into 2026, so plan your detours now. Additionally, the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation project and Studemont Street Bridge demolition are causing lane reductions along I-10, which can mean more localized traffic fumes and dust, potentially impacting nearby trail access or creating unpleasant ambient conditions for runners along that corridor.
</AlertBox>
