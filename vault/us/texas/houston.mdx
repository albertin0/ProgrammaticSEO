## Local Hazards & Updates

### Air Quality Alert

Alright, Houston, let's get real about what you're breathing today. Forget what some apps might tell you; the data I'm seeing pegs our AQI at 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's squarely in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" territory. If you've got asthma, emphysema, or just generally enjoy not hacking up a lung, you need to be cautious. Even if you're a seasoned runner, prolonged exposure to these fine particulates can irritate your respiratory system and aggravate existing heart or lung conditions. This isn't just a sniffle-inducing pollen count; this is about tiny particles getting deep into your lungs.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **AQI 103: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups.** Expect irritation, especially for those with respiratory or heart conditions. Consider moving your workout indoors or significantly reducing intensity and duration.
</AlertBox>

On top of that, we've got moderate pollen levels hanging around. Combine that with the PM2.5, and it's a double whammy for anyone prone to allergies or respiratory issues. The mostly clear skies and warm 26.3°C might *look* inviting, but don't let it fool you. The air itself is a bit of a grind today.

### Road & Trail Disruptions

If the air doesn't get you, the concrete might. Today, September 12, 2026, Houston City Hall is hosting the **9/11 Heroes Run 5K**. That means downtown streets are going to be blocked off and packed with people from 8 AM onwards. If your usual downtown loop involves Congress, Smith, Franklin, or Washington, expect major detours and crowds. This isn't the day for a PR through the civic center. Plan your route to entirely avoid the area around City Hall.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **9/11 Heroes Run Downtown:** Avoid the area around Houston City Hall and surrounding streets today, Sept 12, 2026, from 8 AM to noon due to significant road closures and crowds.
</AlertBox>
