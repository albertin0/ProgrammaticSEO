## Today's Outdoor Conditions: The Rundown

Alright, Houston, let's talk about it. The weather app says "Clear," which, sure, technically the sun is out, but that's like saying a muddy puddle is "clear" water. We're sitting at 26.1°C (about 79°F), which isn't oven-hot for us, but combine that with an AQI of 103, and suddenly your "clear" sky feels a lot heavier. ### Hazards & Annoyances

Looks like we dodged any major wildfire smoke this week, thankfully. No hurricane warnings (yet) and the bayous are staying in their banks. However, construction around town is still a beast. Specifically, ongoing infrastructure work continues to impact access and create detours, especially on major routes and green spaces.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Expect detours and potential dust clouds around the Buffalo Bayou Park area due to ongoing construction. If your usual route takes you past the Johnny Steele Dog Park, be prepared to reroute or deal with increased vehicle traffic on alternate paths. Always be aware of your surroundings; a rogue construction vehicle or unexpected barricade can turn a good workout into a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Hydration is always key, but especially when your lungs are already working harder to filter out junk. Don't be a hero; carry water. And maybe skip the intense sprint work today, unless you *enjoy* that burning sensation in your chest that isn't from pushing your limits.
</AlertBox>
