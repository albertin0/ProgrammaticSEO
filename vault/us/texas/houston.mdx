## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Lungs: You'll be breathing in more than just fresh air.** "Moderate" AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant is a classic Houston special. Couple that with "Moderate" pollen, and your airways are in for a good time, especially if you're out there long enough to notice that sticky feeling in your throat. And let's not forget the recent 'Air Quality Alert' for ozone from the NWS just last week. Seems we're always walking that line here. The EPA might *say* we're meeting federal PM2.5 standards, but don't let those folks in D.C. tell you how your lungs feel after a solid 10k.
*   **Joints & Sanity: Good luck finding a clear path, pal.** Between the ongoing Memorial Groves project at Memorial Park closing parts of the Outer Loop Trail until 2027 and the constant intermittent closures on the White Oak Bayou Greenway near Shepherd, you'll feel like you're running a construction zone obstacle course. Oh, and did I mention the long-term closure of a significant chunk of the White Oak Bayou Trail between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center? That's gone until 2028, thanks to the I-10 White Oak Bayou Project. Don't even get me started on the Brays Bayou Trail closure at Spur 5.
*   **Patience: The Corporate 5K is back.** Because apparently, closing off half the city for infrastructure isn't enough, we've got the Chord Energy Houston Corporate 5K taking over Memorial Park, Eastern Glades, from October 20-22. So, if you were hoping for a peaceful jog through one of our premier parks, think again. You'll be navigating a sea of branded t-shirts and awkward team-building exercises. It's like a corporate retreat exploded on the trails.

Avoid the Eastern Glades area of Memorial Park today if you value your personal space or uninterrupted running rhythm. The Chord Energy Houston Corporate 5K is in full swing, and it's going to be a madhouse.

### Persistent Trail Closures & Detour Chaos
Seriously, check your route before you head out. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, especially near Shepherd, is notorious for intermittent closures with *no signed detours*. Ditto for major sections of White Oak Bayou Trail thanks to the I-10 project, impacting connections between Studemont and Downtown. Expect to reroute, get lost, or just give up and run loops around your block.
