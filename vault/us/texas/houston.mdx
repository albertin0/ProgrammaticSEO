Today's a classic Houston summer day, meaning the air is thick, and the heat index is trying to kill you. The official temperature might read a manageable 28.2°C (around 82°F), but with humidity levels peaking around 85%, it'll *feel* closer to 33°C (91°F) by mid-morning, and later in the day, expect heat index values to climb to an even more brutal 100-105°F. Hydration isn't a suggestion; it's a lifeline. Maybe save that long run for tomorrow, or at least go early. Like, *really* early.

## The Air You Breathe (or Choke On)

Let's talk about the air quality. We're sitting at an AQI of 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. For those keeping score at home, that's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." If you've got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, seriously consider moving your workout indoors or keeping it short and low-intensity. Houston's industrial complex along the Ship Channel, coupled with our notorious vehicle emissions and those delightful recirculating sea breezes, often makes for a lovely cocktail of airborne nasties.

## Trail Talk & Construction Chaos

Good news, bad news, same old Houston. The Brays Bayou Trail, a favorite for many of us, is still seeing closures. Specifically, a section at Spur 5 is shut down due to ongoing construction related to the SH 35 expansion. That's a vital artery for runners and cyclists heading to the Texas Medical Center or Hermann Park, so plan your detours carefully. It's not always well-marked out there, folks.

Over on the White Oak Bayou Greenway, expect intermittent closures near Shepherd daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. for flood control district work. And don't even get me started on the Austin Street Bike Lane, which apparently just disappeared between Elgin and Gray Streets thanks to city works. While there's talk of a reinstall, it's not happening today.
