## The Rundown: October 13, 2026, in Houston

Look, the sun's out, which usually fools half the city into thinking it's a good day for a jog or a ride. Don't fall for it. Today, Houston is cooking up a cocktail of environmental nasties that make staying indoors sound pretty appealing.

First off, that "Mainly Clear" sky is a bit of a deception. With an AQI of 103 and PM2.5 as the main culprit, you're looking at air quality that's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." That means your lungs are going to be working overtime, not just from the exercise, but from filtering out the gunk. If you have asthma, COPD, or are just prone to a post-run cough, consider this your warning shot.

Then there's the heat. While 28.2°C doesn't sound like death, factor in that notorious Houston humidity, and you're quickly into "feels like the surface of the sun" territory, well into the low 100s. Dehydration is a real risk, and pushing yourself too hard in these conditions is a fast track to heat exhaustion. Nobody wants to end their run in an ambulance, especially not on the Seymour Lieberman Trail where the crowds are about to get even thicker.

Speaking of which, if Memorial Park is your usual stomping ground, be aware that the western part of the Outer Loop Trail is still shut down until 2027 due to ongoing construction. And while the Picnic Loop just reopened yesterday after the Bayou City Art Festival, you've only got a few days before parts of the Clay Family Eastern Glades and parking lots get taken over for the Chord Energy Houston Corporate 5K from October 16-23. Expect heavier traffic on the Seymour Lieberman Trail then, so plan your runs accordingly.

</AlertBox>
