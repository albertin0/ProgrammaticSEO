### Air Quality Breakdown

Houston's air quality has been less than stellar lately, and today is no exception. That AQI of 103 means it's unhealthy for sensitive groups, and frankly, not ideal for anyone pushing themselves. PM2.5 is the culprit, tiny particles that love to irritate your eyes, nose, and respiratory system. Long-term exposure? It aggravates heart and lung disease. Don't be a hero; listen to your body.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Flash Flood Warning for Houston
Seriously, do not mess with floodwaters. Multiple forecasts are calling for heavy rain and street flooding throughout the day. If you see standing water, especially on trails or underpasses, turn around. It's not worth risking your life, or asking a first responder to risk theirs, just for a run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Closures & Detours Aplenty
Trying to navigate the White Oak Bayou Greenway or parts of the Buffalo Bayou Park trails today is going to be a headache. Expect detours and outright closures due to ongoing construction and drainage projects. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 is still affected by highway expansion, for instance. Your usual route might be a muddy, impassable mess. Check local park websites or Houston TranStar before you head out, though good luck finding real-time updates for *every* trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Gyms are Indoors for a Reason
Given the air quality, flood threat, and general dampness, today might be the perfect day to hit up an indoor gym. If you absolutely *must* get outside, keep it short, stay on well-known paved paths (away from bayous and underpasses!), and pay close attention to air quality alerts and weather updates. And for the love of all that is holy, don't forget to hydrate in this swampy air, even if it's raining.
</AlertBox>
