### Navigating the Urban Obstacle Course

So, you want to run? Good for you. Just know you'll be dodging more than just stray dogs and forgotten e-scooters. The city's in full-on "prep for the FIFA World Cup 2026" mode, which means every other street is torn up.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen Pummels the Pathways:** That "Moderate" pollen rating is a bit misleading. We've got high tree pollen today, especially oak, which can hit you hard. If you're susceptible to seasonal allergies, pop an antihistamine *before* you lace up. Otherwise, expect the full symphony of sneezes, sniffles, and itchy eyes. Don't blame me when you finish your run looking like you wrestled a juniper bush.
</AlertBox>

Let's talk about the **White Oak Bayou Greenway**. It’s a fantastic trail, but the stretch between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is completely shut down until *2028* due to the IH-45 and I-10 connector ramp construction. That's right, 2028. You could have a kid and send them to kindergarten by the time that's clear. There’s a detour, but it’s not ideal. Don't be a hero; use the detour or find another spot. Speaking of I-10, the westbound lanes between I-45 North and Houston Avenue are also reduced to two lanes through mid-2026. This isn't just a headache for drivers; it often means more congestion, noise, and potential detours affecting surrounding streets and pedestrian access points.

And if you were planning on a casual stroll through downtown, be aware that the Main Street Promenade is still under construction, albeit with pedestrian access maintained. The city is pouring money into street and drainage projects across at least 85 blocks downtown, not to mention busy corridors like Montrose Boulevard. So, expect detours, uneven surfaces, and a general air of organized chaos if you're hitting those areas.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Closures: Proceed with Extreme Caution.** The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** closure is extensive and long-term. Likewise, parts of the **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5 are closed for the SH 35 expansion. These aren't minor inconveniences; they're legitimate barriers. Check Houston Bike Guide or Buffalo Bayou Partnership for the latest updates before you head out, or you'll find yourself rerouting mid-run, adding miles and frustration. Seriously, don't assume your usual route is open.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Humidity's Hidden Hand:** While the air temp feels nice and overcast today, the humidity is on the rise. This weekend, expect "feels like" temperatures to climb into the mid-90s. Even with clouds, high humidity makes exertion feel tougher and increases your risk of overheating. Stay hydrated, lighten up your layers, and don't push it too hard. Rain chances are low today but ramp up next week, especially Tuesday and Wednesday, so enjoy the dry-ish air while it lasts.
</AlertBox>

The bottom line: It's a decent day for a workout if you're prepared for pollen, ready to detour, and mindful of rising humidity. Otherwise, maybe just hit the treadmill.
