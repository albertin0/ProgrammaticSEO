### Local Rundown: Buffalo Bayou Trail Closure Continues

Heads up if your usual route takes you along the **Buffalo Bayou Trail**. That section near Downtown has been closed since January 31st to fix a blind spot, and it's still out of commission until further notice. Don't try to navigate around it; you'll just end up frustrated or, worse, on a busy street. Same goes for the Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5, which is still undergoing construction related to the SH 35 expansion and isn't expected to be fully clear until 2026, if we're lucky. Seriously, check the Houston Parks and Rec or Houston Bike Guide sites before you head out.
