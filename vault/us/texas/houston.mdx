### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deep

Look, the temperature is a glorious 21.4°C (about 70°F) and it's clear out, which usually screams "get outside!" But hold your horses, because our air quality today is hitting an AQI of 103, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the main culprit. That's "Moderate" on some scales, but leaning hard into "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" according to AccuWeather and other sources. If you're sensitive—and let's be real, who *isn't* sensitive to microscopic crud clogging their lungs?—you should probably scale back your outdoor plans. This isn't the day for setting a new PR on the **Buffalo Bayou Park** trails, especially if you're trying to push hard near busy roadways. Even the "moderate" rating from Ambee suggests sensitive individuals should "exercise caution in polluted spots." Your lungs will thank you for being smart.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Today's AQI is 103, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Sensitive groups (asthmatics, elderly, children) should limit prolonged outdoor exertion. Everyone else, consider easing up.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Hazards: Houston's Endless Construction Saga

Surprise, surprise, Houston is still under construction. If you were planning a long ride or run, you'll need to be flexible and probably a little annoyed. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** (specifically the Little White Oak Bayou Trail) between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center is closed until 2028 due to freeway connector ramp work. That's not a typo, 2028. There are detours, but plan for extra time and mental fortitude.

The **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5 is also shut down, thanks to the SH 35 expansion, with "2026, if we're lucky" being the hopeful reopening. And let's not forget our perpetually problematic **MKT Trail Hogan Pedestrian Bridge**, which keeps popping up with closure updates. While some sources hoped for a May 2026 reopening for the bridge itself, the phrase "may stay that way for another 2-3 years" also appeared regarding bridges near Studewood and Hogan. Bottom line: expect detours and uncertainty around those areas.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Multiple key trails, including sections of the White Oak Bayou Greenway and Brays Bayou Trail, are experiencing long-term closures. The MKT Trail bridges are also a perpetual headache. Scout your route *before* you head out, or you'll be backtracking more than a confused tourist.
</AlertBox>

In downtown, forget about smooth sailing. Between ongoing general construction and events like "Via Futbol" and "Avenida Programming," you'll find street closures all over the place. Plus, the Astros are playing at Daikin Park today, which means traffic congestion will be a mess around the stadium. Don't even think about trying to navigate through that if you're on foot or bike.

<AlertBox type="info">
Consider an indoor workout today or stick to familiar, open routes. If you do venture out, use navigation apps to check for real-time closures, and stay hydrated. Your body is already working overtime filtering that air.
</AlertBox>
