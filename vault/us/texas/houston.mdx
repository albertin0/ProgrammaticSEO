## The Good, The Bad, and The Gritty

Alright, Houston, today, May 4, 2026, is serving up some "mostly clear" skies and a relatively cool 15.1°C (around 59°F). That's practically sweater weather for us, which means your joints will thank you for getting out there before the humidity inevitably comes back to drown us all. Lower temps mean less heat stress, less risk of cramping up like a cheap suit, and generally a more enjoyable run or ride. So, lace up.

But don't get too giddy. It's still Houston.

### Air Quality: A Hazy Picture

Now, about that air. The data I've got says our Air Quality Index (AQI) is a "Good" 48, with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant. Sounds okay, right? Well, hold your horses. Other reports, like AccuWeather, are calling today "Poor" for sensitive groups, warning of high pollution levels. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) even suggests PM2.5 might hit the "Moderate" range in parts of the city. So, what's a runner to do?

<AlertBox type="warning">
**AQI Discrepancy Alert:** While the initial AQI is "Good," local forecasts suggest "Poor" air for sensitive groups due to elevated ozone and particulate matter. If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, seriously consider a treadmill day or keep your route short and slow. Harris County Public Health has given us an "F" for ozone levels in the past, so this isn't new territory.
</AlertBox>

Pollen is holding steady at "Moderate." Nothing catastrophic, but if you're one of the many Houstonians who feels like their face is trying to escape through their sinuses this time of year, pop an antihistamine before you head out.

### Construction: Forever and Always

Remember that project on the I-10 White Oak Bayou elevation? Yeah, that one. It's still messing with things. The White Oak Bayou Greenway, specifically between Houston Avenue and the Castillo Community Center, is shut down through *2028*. And if you rely on the MKT Trail, particularly around the Hogan Street Pedestrian Bridge, be aware of potential detours or ongoing construction. While the trail underneath was supposed to reopen earlier, the bridge itself wasn't fully restored until spring 2026, and the larger I-10 project continues to impact that area.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route:** If your usual loop takes you anywhere near the White Oak Bayou Greenway, especially where it intersects with major highways like I-10 and I-45, assume there will be closures and detours. Check Houston TranStar before you leave, or be prepared to improvise. Don't be that person ignoring the "Do Not Enter" signs just to save a few minutes. We've all seen how that ends.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the flash flood watches from Friday and Saturday have cleared out. So at least you won't be swimming your intervals today.

Overall, it's a decent day to get outside. Just be smart about your breathing and your route.
