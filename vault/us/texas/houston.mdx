## The Rundown: Don't Assume Your Usual Path Is Open

Look, the temperature is pretty decent this morning, hovering in the low 70s before climbing to the low 90s. It's clear, so at least you won't melt immediately. But "clear" doesn't mean "clear sailing." Those moderate AQI numbers, with PM2.5 in charge, probably mean there's some background smoke in the air, possibly from distant fires or local industrial activity. We had reports of HFD battling a wildfire in northeast Houston recently, and a Fort Worth EV fire just yesterday pumped out plenty of toxic smoke. It's not a crisis for most, but if you're sensitive, your lungs will feel it.

The real headache today is the sheer volume of *stuff* happening. Between the organized chaos of the Space City 10 Miler out by UH Clear Lake and the Bayou City Art Festival taking over Memorial Park, your usual routes might be swarming with people or just outright blocked. Seriously, if you're trying to get a tempo run in at Memorial Park, you'll be weaving through festival-goers and shuttle traffic. Good luck with those splits.
