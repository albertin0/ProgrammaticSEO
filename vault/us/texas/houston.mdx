## Today's Air & Your Body: What You Need to Know

Look, I get it. The sun's out, and it's not August. But that doesn't mean it's a perfect day for interval training. Our air quality index is teetering on "unhealthy for sensitive groups," meaning if you've got any underlying lung issues, you need to be smart about your outdoor time. Couple that with "moderate" pollen counts, and you've got a recipe for sniffles, coughs, and generally feeling like you swallowed a desert.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**AQI Warning for Sensitive Souls:** An AQI of 103 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant means sensitive individuals (kids, elderly, people with heart or lung conditions like asthma) should consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Your lungs are basically doing extra work to filter out crud.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Obstacles Abound

Don't expect a smooth, uninterrupted run on your favorite paths. The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** near N Shepherd is still experiencing intermittent closures daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. for flood control work. They haven't bothered with signed detours, so you're on your own to navigate those lovely adjacent streets. Seriously, Houston, a little signage would be nice for the "most used greenway in the network."

Over at **Memorial Park**, the western portion of the Outer Loop Trail remains entirely shut down until 2027 for the "Memorial Groves" construction project. So much for that scenic route. And if you were planning on a long run on the **Brays Bayou Trail** at Spur 5, forget it—that's still closed due to the never-ending SH 35 expansion.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Mind the Closures:** The **White Oak Bayou Greenway** (near N Shepherd), **Brays Bayou Trail** (at Spur 5), and **Memorial Park's Outer Loop Trail** (western portion) are all impacted by long-term construction. Check local park websites *before* you head out, or you'll be adding unintended street mileage to your route.
</AlertBox>
