## Why today isn't the day (unless you like feeling like you're running through a hot, dusty soup)

<BulletList>
*   **You'll cook.** The actual temperature might say 28.2°C (83°F) but the *feels like* temperature is soaring between 104°F and 108°F today. Your body isn't designed to cool efficiently when it's like this, turning even a light jog into a serious health risk. We're in the thick of Houston's hottest weather of the year.
*   **You'll breathe crud.** An AQI of 103 for PM2.5 means "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." That wildfire smoke from half a continent away is still hanging around, making our already muggy air even less pleasant and potentially harmful to your lungs.
*   **Your route is probably jacked.** Good luck finding a clear path on some of our favorite spots. Construction closures are forcing detours onto less safe street routes.
</BulletList>

### The Heat is the Real Killer (Literally)

Look, I know you're tough, but Houston's summer humidity isn't just "uncomfortable" – it actively sabotages your body's ability to regulate its temperature. When the air is this saturated, your sweat just sits there, unable to evaporate and cool you down. That puts immense strain on your heart and lungs, even if you feel fit. We're seeing "dangerously hot" conditions through today. Forget the heroics; common sense dictates you stay indoors during peak heating hours, which is, well, pretty much all day.

<AlertBox type="danger">
    **DANGER: Extreme Heat Index!**
    Afternoon heat index values are projected to hit 104-108°F. This is dangerous for *everyone* who spends extended time outdoors, not just sensitive groups. Heat-related illnesses can escalate quickly. Stay hydrated, take frequent breaks, and seriously, consider that treadmill today.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality Sucks, Courtesy of Distant Fires

That AQI of 103? The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which is fancy talk for tiny particulate matter that gets deep into your lungs. While Houston itself isn't on fire, residual smoke from wildfires out west and even up in Canada is still drifting our way. So, you're not just breathing hot, humid air; you're breathing hot, humid, smoky air. Lovely.

### Pollen Report: Moderate Annoyance

### Trail Trouble: White Oak Bayou Greenway Edition

If you were planning a nice long run on the White Oak Bayou Greenway near Shepherd, think again. There are intermittent closures daily from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and apparently, no official detour is signed. This isn't some quick fix either; it's likely related to flood control work and could last for months. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 is also closed for ongoing construction, and its detour is reportedly less than ideal. So, you'll be dodging traffic on busy streets if you try to reroute, which adds a whole new layer of hazard to your already ill-advised outdoor adventure.

<AlertBox type="warning">
    **WARNING: Trail Closures & Detours**
    Expect daily closures on the White Oak Bayou Greenway near Shepherd. No official detours are marked, so you'll be sharing space with cars if you try to navigate around it. The Brays Bayou Trail at Spur 5 also remains closed. Plan alternate routes or stick to known safe areas.
</AlertBox>
