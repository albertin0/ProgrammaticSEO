## Why today is *not* the day (or at least, really reconsider it)

<BulletList>
*   **Breathe it in... or don't:** Our Air Quality Index is currently sitting at a lovely 114, which means it's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" thanks to PM2.5 pollution. If you have asthma, COPD, or just like your lungs feeling fresh, this is your warning. Even for the rest of us, long-term exposure carries health risks. Virginia DEQ explicitly tells you to avoid strenuous activity when particle pollution levels are high. So, maybe don't go for that PR on the Dismal Swamp Canal Trail today.
*   **Pollen's back, alright:** It's a "Moderate" pollen day, which, when combined with the delightful PM2.5, means your sinuses and lungs are getting a double-whammy. Expect some extra sniffles and maybe an itchy throat if you push it.
*   **Construction chaos & more:** While not directly blocking trails, there are multiple road closures and lane shifts across Chesapeake this week. Great Bridge Boulevard and Elbow Road have ongoing lane issues through tomorrow, and Poindexter Street is shutting down next week for a few weeks. If your route takes you near these areas, expect detours and potentially higher car exhaust fumes. Also, the State Corporation Commission just approved a new natural gas compressor station near South Military Highway, a project already raising concerns about air and groundwater pollution. Not exactly great news for our overall air quality long-term.
</BulletList>

#### Air Quality Alert: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
With an AQI of 114 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, today's air quality is considered "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." This includes children, older adults, and anyone with respiratory or heart conditions. Seriously, consider moving your workout indoors.
</AlertBox>

#### Mind the Roadwork
