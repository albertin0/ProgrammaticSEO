## The Rundown: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Let's cut to the chase: The air quality in Chesapeake today is a solid AQI 114, with PM2.5 being the primary culprit. That's "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," folks, and if you've got asthma, emphysema, or just plain old human lungs, you fall into that category. You know that wheezy feeling after a long run when the air isn't great? That's your body telling you to take it easy. Running hard when the AQI is this high isn't building character; it's just asking for trouble down the line.

Then there's the pollen. It's at a "Moderate" level today. So, while it's not going to make you miserable if you don't typically suffer, combine it with that PM2.5, and you've got a recipe for irritated airways. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, pop your meds *before* you head out, or better yet, stay inside.

### Weather vs. Reality

The good news? The actual weather is pretty stellar. We're looking at a "Mainly Clear" day with a comfortable 21.6°C (that's around 71°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit). Low winds, no rain expected. If it weren't for the invisible lung irritants and the very visible cones, it'd be perfect.

## Construction Chaos

While there are no major trail closures announced for today on popular spots like the Dismal Swamp Canal Trail (it was closed for an event on Sunday, but should be clear today), the city is still a minefield of road construction.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up, Road Runners!** If your route takes you anywhere near **Albemarle Drive**, be prepared for alternating lane closures between Battlefield Blvd S and Shea Drive. This is for a sidewalk connection project and is ongoing through approximately October 2026, typically from 9 AM to 4 PM. Flaggers will be out there, but seriously, stay alert. Distracted driving is bad enough, don't be a distracted runner.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
Also, **Perry Street** still has sewer pipeline work happening, and it's closed to through traffic with no street parking. They said completion by mid-September, so it could still be an active work zone. If you usually cut through there, find an alternate.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for Trail Lovers:** Since the roads are a mess and the air quality is iffy, consider hitting a trail that's less impacted, or at least a paved path that's away from heavy traffic. The Dismal Swamp Canal Trail might be a decent option, assuming you're not overly sensitive to the air quality. Just check for any last-minute local updates before you head out, as events can pop up.
