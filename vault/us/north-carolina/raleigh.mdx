## The Raleigh Running Report: June 06, 2026

Alright, Raleigh, let's cut to the chase: Today's not your best day for a PR, or even just a long, blissful outdoor ramble. We've got a trifecta of annoyances hitting us – questionable air, surging heat, and enough trail closures and lingering flood zones to make your head spin.

### Air Quality and Heat: Your Lungs and Body Are Already Working Overtime

First up, that Air Quality Index. At 103 with PM2.5 as the main culprit, if you've got asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, seriously consider staying inside. Even if you don't, your lungs are going to be working harder to filter out that crud. Combine that with the mercury shooting into the low 90s, and you're setting yourself up for a miserable, potentially dangerous, workout. Hydration will be absolutely critical if you choose to brave it. Don't be that person collapsing on the Neuse River Greenway.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**High Fire Risk & Potential Smoke:** The fire risk is "High" today due to hot and dry conditions. The Raleigh Fire Department just responded to brush fires along I-540. While the AQI reflects current conditions, keep an eye (and nose) out for localized smoke, which can rapidly worsen air quality.
</AlertBox>

### Greenway Grief: Closures, Detours, and Actual Floodwaters

If you had plans for a nice long run on the greenways, you might need a new plan. Or three.
The City of Raleigh alerts are a battlefield of red and yellow markers.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOODING ALERT:** Large sections of our greenway system, including parts of the **Walnut Creek Trail (Mile Marker 0 to 13.75)** and the **Shelley Lake Trail**, are *not passable* due to standing water and heavy silt from recent rains. Seriously, do not risk it. Floodwaters hide debris and can sweep you off your feet.
</AlertBox>

The **Neuse River Greenway** is a minefield of closures. Multiple segments are shut down for sewer projects, some with no detour. For example, the stretch north of the Skycrest Pedestrian Bridge to River Bend Park is completely blocked. Then there's the ongoing Complete 540 project, which means periodic stoppages between Auburn Knightdale Rd and Battle Bridge Rd.

Over at **Umstead State Park**, the **Turkey Creek Trail** is closed at Mile Marker 16.75 for a bridge replacement, meaning you can't head north into Umstead from the Crabtree Creek Trail there. And speaking of **Crabtree Creek Trail**, a section between Lassiter Mill Road and Rothgeb Drive is closed for streambank restoration. They offer a sidewalk detour, but good luck with Marlowe Road – no sidewalks there, so you'll be dodging cars.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Ahead, Raleigh:** Before you head out, check the City of Raleigh's Greenway Alerts. Assume any section near a creek or river, especially if it's low-lying, might still be underwater or incredibly slick. Wooden bridges? They'll be traction-free zones after all that moisture.
</AlertBox>

In short, today requires extra caution. If you must run outside, stick to known clear paths, stay hydrated, and listen to your body. Your lungs and joints will thank you later.
