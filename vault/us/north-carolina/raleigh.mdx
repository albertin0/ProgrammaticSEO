Seriously, 33.6°C is no joke. Governor Stein has even proclaimed this week (May 18-22) as North Carolina Heat Safety Week for a reason. If you absolutely MUST be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job.

An AQI of 103 means the air is "Moderate" and could be unhealthy for sensitive individuals. With PM2.5 being the main culprit, it could be lingering smoke or other fine particulate matter. </AlertBox>
