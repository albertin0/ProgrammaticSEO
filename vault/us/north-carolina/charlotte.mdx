## The Rundown: Breathe Easy, Watch Your Step

The air quality in Charlotte today is genuinely good for getting outside. Forget those miserable days when the air felt thick enough to chew; at an AQI of 36, you're looking at optimal conditions for your lungs. That's a far cry from the "Code Orange Air Quality Action Day for Ground Level Ozone" that hit other parts of the Piedmont region recently, though thankfully not Charlotte. The temperature is also playing nice, hovering in the low 60s this morning before climbing to a forecasted high in the mid-80s later today, so plan your longer runs for earlier to beat that afternoon warmth.

However, if your usual route includes the **Charlotte Rail Trail** in South End, keep your eyes peeled. There are ongoing construction-related closures due to the South End Station project. While the much-anticipated I-277 Rail Trail Bridge Project had its lane closures postponed recently, meaning less vehicular chaos on that front for now, the general construction around the Rail Trail is a constant. Don't assume your favorite stretch is clear without checking. The City of Charlotte's interactive map is your best bet for up-to-the-minute construction detours if you're really committed to specific urban routes.

No major weather warnings are active for Charlotte right now, which is always a win. We’re not dealing with any immediate severe thunderstorms or flooding *in the city*, though some areas in the southern US are seeing downpours. So, you can leave the rain jacket at home for today, but keep an eye on the forecast for Saturday, as scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected to return to the Carolinas through the week.

Moderate pollen levels mean allergy sufferers should take precautions. Pop that Claritin, grab a tissue, and maybe wear sunglasses to keep some of it out of your eyes. Nobody likes a snotty runner.

### Trail Courtesy & Awareness
Remember to always stay aware of your surroundings, especially if you're hitting lesser-known paths or if you’re running during peak construction hours. Sharing the trail is caring, so make sure you're not hogging the whole thing or darting around blind corners.
</AlertBox>
