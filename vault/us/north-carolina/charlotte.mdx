## The Rundown: Your Lungs vs. The Concrete Jungle

Let's talk air. The general Air Quality Index is sitting at 60, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Moderate." AccuWeather also noted Charlotte's AQI around 66 in recent reports, which they categorize as "unhealthy for sensitive groups." This isn't a Code Red situation like we've seen in the past when wildfire smoke rolled in, nor a Code Orange for ozone, which Mecklenburg County regrettably earned an 'F' for in the American Lung Association's 2022-2024 report. However, "Moderate" still means if you're one of the sensitive types – maybe you've got asthma or just generally fussy lungs – you might feel it during prolonged or heavy exertion. Best to keep your inhaler handy or scale back your intensity.

Then there's the pollen. It's "Moderate" across the board. Expect the usual suspects: oak, pine, and mulberry making their presence known. Your car's already got that lovely greenish-yellow film, so imagine what your airways are dealing with if you're out there pushing hard.

### Mind Your Footing (and Your Detours)

Now for the fun part: trying to actually *move* through this city. Charlotte is in a constant state of "improvement," which usually means "construction."

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Rail Trail Construction Continues:** A major chunk of the **Rail Trail in South End** from Tremont Avenue to Remount Road has been closed for the new South End Station project since April 19th and will remain closed through July. If you rely on that stretch, know your detours: Hawkins Street, Dunavant Street, and South Boulevard are your alternatives. Don't expect a clear run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Little Sugar Creek Greenway Advisory:** If you're hitting the Little Sugar Creek Greenway near Midtown, be aware that adjacent development means scaffolding is hanging over parts of the trail, requiring cyclists to dismount for roughly two years. There could be intermittent closures, so keep an eye out for detour signs on Kings Drive or Kenilworth Drive.
</AlertBox>

And if that wasn't enough to contend with, there's a good cause taking over one of our prime running spots today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**McAlpine Creek Park - Event Alert:** The **Mental Health Matters 5K & Festival** is happening today, May 17th, at McAlpine Creek Park. If McAlpine Creek Greenway is your usual Sunday long run spot, you might want to find an alternative route or wait until later in the afternoon to avoid the crowds and potential course diversions.
</AlertBox>

Overall, the weather's clear and a pleasant 17.8°C (around 64°F), which is fantastic for being outside. Just remember, "clear skies" doesn't always mean "clear sailing" in Charlotte. Acknowledge the moderate air quality, load up on allergy meds, and for the love of all that is holy, check the specific trail closures before you head out. Or just stay inside and lift some weights. Your call.
