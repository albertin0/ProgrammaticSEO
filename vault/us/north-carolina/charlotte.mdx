<AlertBox type="warning">
### Rail Trail Blues: Construction Ahead
Heads up, folks who rely on the **Charlotte Rail Trail** for your miles. Sections of it are officially shutting down, starting this month and lasting through July, all thanks to the South End Station construction. They're building new platforms and adding a pedestrian crossing, which sounds great for 2028, but for now, it means detours. You'll be rerouted along Hawkins Street, Dunevant Street, and South Boulevard. Don't be that person trying to hop barriers; it's not worth the twisted ankle or the angry construction worker. Plan your route, or better yet, find an alternative for a few months. This isn't a small disruption; Blue Line operations are also affected with single-tracking starting April 5th.
</AlertBox>

On the flip side, the environmental factors are practically shouting "Go run!" An AQI of 40 is about as good as it gets for our urban jungle, so breathe deep while you can. That 16.1°C (61°F) weather is what we dream of in the summer, a fleeting moment of perfection before the humidity punches us in the face.

Now, if you're looking for an excuse to get out of South End, today's a packet pick-up day for the New South Trail Marathon over at the **Whitewater Center**. So, expect a bit more traffic and maybe a few extra folks milling around if you're planning on hitting those trails later this afternoon (pick-up runs from 5-7 pm). The actual race isn't until tomorrow, but consider yourselves warned. It's still a good spot, and honestly, the trails there are rarely *empty*, so a few more faces won't ruin your zen.

So, ditch the indoor gym, grab your shoes, and pick your path wisely. Just maybe avoid the Rail Trail if you're not a fan of impromptu urban detours and the smell of fresh concrete.
