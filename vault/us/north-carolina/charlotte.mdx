Forget about that outdoor PR, Charlotte. Today is a disaster.

<BulletList>
*   **Extreme Heat Warning:** We're staring down 100.2°F (37.9°C) with heat indices well over 100°F. The National Weather Service isn't messing around; they issued a heat advisory, explicitly warning that "significant adverse health impacts are possible for all populations without adequate cooling or hydration, and heat illnesses may develop suddenly for those working or exercising outdoors."
*   **Unhealthy Air Quality:** Our AQI is currently 127 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That’s "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," meaning if you have any respiratory issues, you're looking at a bad day. Even if you don't, you're breathing in garbage.
*   **Moderate Pollen:** Adding insult to injury, we've got moderate pollen levels. While not the primary concern today, it's certainly not helping those lungs already fighting off PM2.5.
</BulletList>

Look, I love hitting the pavement or trails as much as the next runner, but today is a hard pass. You’ve got a triple threat of extreme heat, crummy air, and moderate pollen trying to ruin your lungs and cook your core temperature. I'm a physical therapist, not your mom, but even I'm telling you to stay indoors. Seriously, don't be that person collapsed on the Little Sugar Creek Greenway.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Heatstroke is a Real Threat.** With temps pushing past 100°F and a heat advisory in full effect, exercising outside is a direct path to heatstroke. Your body can't dissipate heat efficiently enough in these conditions. Skip it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Don't Breathe That In.** An AQI of 127 means you're inhaling particulate matter that can irritate airways, trigger asthma, and cause other respiratory problems. Even without a Code Orange for ozone today, our air quality has been consistently bad, with recent reports highlighting worsening ozone pollution in the Greater Charlotte area. Your lungs aren't air filters for fun.
</AlertBox>

And if you were planning on a long run down the **Charlotte Rail Trail** in South End, forget it. Large sections are still shut down for that new South End Station construction. This has been ongoing since April and isn't expected to clear up until July. You'll be navigating frustrating detours along Hawkins and Dunavant streets or South Boulevard, likely adding more stress and less enjoyment to an already miserable day for outdoor activity.

<AlertBox type="info">
**PRO TIP: Embrace the Indoors.** Today is the perfect day for some air-conditioned gym time, a swim at a local rec center, or even some bodyweight exercises in your living room. There's no shame in self-preservation. Your lungs and joints will thank you for not punishing them.
</AlertBox>

Do yourself a favor, stay cool, stay hydrated, and try again when Charlotte isn't actively trying to kill your workout buzz.
