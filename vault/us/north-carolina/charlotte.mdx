  ### Extreme Heat Warning & Code Orange Air Quality
  Seriously, people. This isn't just "a little warm." We're talking about a heat index that can cause heat-related illnesses faster than you can say "dehydration." Coupled with a Code Orange AQI for ozone, pushing yourself outside is a direct invitation for trouble. Sensitive groups (that means kids, older folks, and anyone with existing heart or lung conditions) should absolutely stay indoors. Everyone else? Reconsider your life choices if you're planning a long run right now.
</AlertBox>

  ### If You *Must* Go Out (You Rebel)
  Hydration is non-negotiable. We're talking constant water intake. Plan your *very* short, low-intensity workout for the absolute earliest morning hours. And for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. If you stop sweating, feel dizzy, or get nauseous, get inside immediately and cool down. This isn't the day to PR anything. Also, be mindful of those moderate pollen levels; combine that with the poor air quality and you're just asking for a coughing fit.
