## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Humidity is high, rain chances are real:** While not the scorching 37.9°C some might have expected, temperatures are hovering around the low 70s to low 80s Fahrenheit (22-29°C), but with humidity pushing 70-89%, it'll feel heavier than it looks. Expect scattered light rain throughout the day, possibly thunderstorms, with some forecasts showing up to a 75% chance of getting wet. Don't be that person caught miles from your car during a downpour.
*   **Air quality is a mixed bag, lean on the bad:** Your AQI might technically be 53 (Moderate), but reports also indicate fine particulate matter (PM2.5) levels are "just over the Yellow threshold" near Charlotte. AccuWeather even calls today's AQI "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." So, if you've got asthma or dodgy lungs, consider this a yellow light, not a green one.
*   **Pollen's still doing its thing:** It's still a moderate pollen day. Nothing new for us, but combine it with elevated particulates and high humidity, and your respiratory system is going to be working overtime.
*   **Watch for ongoing trail headaches:** Remember those endless CATS South End Station construction closures on the rail trail? Yeah, those are still impacting your routes. Plan your long runs accordingly, especially if you usually rely on seamless connections along sections of the Little Sugar Creek Greenway.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Hydration is paramount, even if it's not blazing sunshine.** High humidity means you're sweating more without realizing how much you're losing. Dehydration combined with PM2.5 and pollen is a recipe for a miserable workout and a PT visit. Drink water BEFORE you start, during, and after.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Thunderstorm risk is real.** With high humidity comes the instability. Keep an eye on the sky and check radar before heading out. Getting caught in a lightning storm on the Four Mile Creek Greenway isn't going to improve your PR, just your chances of being a news story. When thunder roars, go indoors, people.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider indoor alternatives or morning efforts.** Given the humidity, potential rain, and elevated particulates, hitting the gym or getting your miles in early this morning was your best bet. If you're heading out later, shorten your route, slow your pace, and stay vigilant about how your body (especially your lungs) is responding.
</AlertBox>
