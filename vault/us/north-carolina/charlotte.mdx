## Local Hazards You Can't Ignore

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: This Ain't a Dry Heat, Folks.
Seriously, 100.2°F is no joke. Even if you're chasing that '90s heat wave high, you're risking heat stroke, heat exhaustion, and generally feeling like garbage. Your joints are already taking a beating on the pavement, don't ask your organs to do overtime in this oven. Hydrate or die-drate, but better yet, just move your workout indoors.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe That: Unhealthy Air Quality
AQI at 127 and a Code Orange for ozone means your lungs are going to hate you. PM2.5 can get deep into your respiratory system, and ozone aggravates existing conditions. If you have asthma, allergies, or just like to breathe easily, this is your sign to skip the outdoor run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Upcoming Rail Trail Closures – Plan Ahead, Suffer Later (or now)
Starting tomorrow, April 19, significant portions of the **Rail Trail in South End** will be closed for construction on the new South End Station. These closures are expected to last through July. While it officially starts *tomorrow*, expect construction activity and potential confusion around the area today. If the heat and bad air don't get you, the detours might. CATS is putting up signage and suggesting detours via Hawkins Street, Dunevant Street, and South Boulevard, but let's be real, it's going to be a mess.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Risk is Up
With the record-breaking heat and very dry conditions, the risk of wildfires in the area is rising. Don't be the person flicking a cigarette into a dry bush. And if you see smoke, don't run towards it.
</AlertBox>
