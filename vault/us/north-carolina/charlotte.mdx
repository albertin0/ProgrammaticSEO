<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors!
Local health officials and news outlets like WCNC are screaming about "potentially dangerous heat" and a heat advisory is in effect. We're talking about a heat index that can push past 107°F through the weekend. You're not tough for running in this; you're risking heat stroke. The city has even set up cooling stations because it's that bad. Keep your skin, brain, and organs from cooking.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Unhealthy Air Quality
An AQI of 127 is no joke, especially with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That's fine particulate matter directly irritating your lungs. While we're not at the "Code Red" levels we saw a few days ago from wildfire smoke, it's still going to make any vigorous activity feel like breathing through a dirty sock. If you have asthma or any respiratory issues, definitely skip it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Pollen Alert
Grass and mold spores are likely doing their thing. If you absolutely *must* step outside for five minutes, prepare for some sneezes and sniffles on top of everything else.
</AlertBox>

### Local Trail Impact

The Rail Trail in South End is usually packed with people, but today, you'd be a fool to hang out too long. While there's no direct closure reported for the trail itself today, don't forget the I-277 pedestrian bridge project near the Rail Trail. Ramp closures related to that construction start August 17th, which doesn't help with the traffic for those trying to get anywhere.

<AlertBox type="info">
### My Pro-Tips (Because I'm a PT and I See This Stuff)
If you're still determined to move, stick to indoors. Hit a gym, do some bodyweight stuff in your gloriously air-conditioned living room, or find a pool. If you absolutely *must* go outside, keep it extremely short, go at dawn (not even an option anymore today), stay hydrated with electrolytes (water alone won't cut it in this heat), and listen to your body before it throws a wrench in your plans. Heat exhaustion isn't a badge of honor; it's a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>
