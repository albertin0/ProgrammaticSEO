However, it wouldn't be Charlotte without a few quirks to navigate.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Keep Pounding 5K in Uptown
If you're planning a morning loop around Uptown, be aware of the "Keep Pounding 5K" event happening today, Sunday, September 27th. This massive race starts at Levine Cancer Institute and wraps up on the field at Bank of America Stadium, with activities generally running from 7 AM to 1 PM. Expect increased foot traffic and potential detours on surrounding streets. CATS bus routes 5, 6, and 16 will see temporary detours from 6 AM to 9:30 AM. Plan your route accordingly, especially if you're trying to hit the Little Sugar Creek Greenway sections near the stadium.
</AlertBox>

While the major road closures from yesterday's Eucharistic Congress in Uptown should be clearing up, always exercise caution in these high-traffic event areas.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ongoing Greenway & Road Construction
You know the drill, Charlotte's always building something. If you're heading to the **Rail Trail** in South End, be mindful that parts of it are still impacted by the South End station project. While some pedestrian crossings were slated for completion by summer, general construction is an ongoing saga expected to continue through next year. Also, a section of the **Little Sugar Creek Greenway (Midtown)** has scaffolding up for what feels like forever, meaning a mandatory bicycle dismount zone. And don't even get me started on the year-long Sam Wilson Road bridge closure in West Charlotte for the I-485 project; that's a nightmare if you run that way. Detours are often poorly marked, so have a backup plan.
</AlertBox>
