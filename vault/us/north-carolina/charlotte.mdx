Today's one of those days where the air itself is working against you. That AQI of 127 is firmly in the "Code Orange" territory, meaning if you've got asthma, lung disease, or any kind of heart condition, you should seriously limit your time outside. Even if you're a regular iron lung, you're still pulling in a lot of fine particulate matter (PM2.5) with every breath, and that's just not good for long-term health. Don't let that "Mainly Clear" sky fool you; the air itself is hazy with microscopic gunk.

And because Charlotte just can't quit its construction habit, getting a clean, uninterrupted run or ride is tougher than usual. If you were planning to hit the **McAlpine Creek Greenway** near the Johnston Road parking lot, be prepared for intermittent closures. Same goes for portions of the **McDowell Creek Greenway** between Preserve Pond Road and Westmoreland Road, where NCDOT work is causing blockages and detours. Even the mighty **US National Whitewater Center** trails were showing as closed yesterday, October 12th, so definitely check their website before you head out there hoping for a dirt fix.
