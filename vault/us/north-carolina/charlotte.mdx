## Charlotte Outdoor Workout Safety: October 12, 2026

Alright, fellow Charlotte runners, cyclists, and walkers – it's a *gorgeous* Monday out there. We're looking at a crisp 23.5°C (about 74°F) and clear skies, which usually screams "get outside and crush it." But as your friendly neighborhood PT and marathoner, I'm here to tell you that "clear" doesn't always mean "clear sailing."

We just shook off the tail end of Tropical System Isa yesterday, and while the tornado warnings were mostly south of us in places like Anson and Stanley counties, Charlotte felt some of those "strong winds and heavy rains." That means your favorite little singletrack or even some paved greenway sections could be harboring downed branches, wet leaves, or unexpected puddles. Tread carefully, especially on trails like the **Little Sugar Creek Greenway**, where even without recent storms, you often find areas requiring extra attention or dealing with ongoing maintenance. Speaking of, there's been talk of scaffolding near Midtown on that very greenway for "approximately 2 years," which means unexpected dismount zones for cyclists. Keep your eyes peeled and don't just blindly follow that GPS route.

The air quality, for once, is playing nice with an AQI of 35, which is firmly in the "Good" category. No smoke plumes from wildfires or nasty industrial haze to choke on today, so your lungs are getting a break.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Greenway Closures and Detours
Don't plan on a seamless run through the entire Briar Creek Greenway today. A significant section, specifically from Bay Street in Chantilly down to Monroe Road, is still very much a construction site. They're working on it, and while parts might *look* finished, it's explicitly not open to the public yet. Respect the signage and find an alternate route. Trying to sneak through active construction isn't worth a twisted ankle or worse. Check the Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation site for the latest official greenway status updates before you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Out for Road Incidents
Our local roads are already seeing their share of minor pile-ups and blockages this morning. If your run or ride involves mixing with traffic, especially near busy intersections or major arteries like W Sugar Creek Rd & S I-85 HY, be extra vigilant. Drivers are often distracted, and post-storm conditions (even just residual wetness or debris) can make things unpredictable. Wear bright colors, assume no one sees you, and consider sticking to dedicated paths if you can.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Even in Fall
Even with pleasant temperatures, October sunshine can still pack a punch, and residual humidity from yesterday's storm might make it feel warmer than the thermometer says. Keep sipping water, especially if you're out for an extended period. Nobody wants to bonk halfway around Freedom Park.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a good day to get your miles in, but stay sharp. Charlotte always has a few surprises up its sleeve.
