### Local Lowdown: Don't Get Caught Off-Guard

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat is a Serious Threat!**
With temperatures well over 100°F and little relief at night, heat-related illnesses like heat exhaustion and heatstroke are a very real danger. Forget setting a PR today. Your goal should be survival. Seriously, hydrate constantly if you *must* be outside, take frequent breaks, and get indoors into air conditioning if you start feeling anything other than perfectly fine. If you stop sweating, you're in deep trouble.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality: Not Your Friend**
An AQI of 127 for PM2.5 means sensitive groups (kids, elderly, anyone with respiratory or heart conditions) should avoid outdoor exertion entirely. For the rest of us 'tough' runners, expect irritation. Why voluntarily inhale this garbage? Stay inside, use that fancy air purifier you bought during the last wildfire scare, and give your lungs a break.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Charlotte Rail Trail Construction Continues**
If you were planning a long run on the **Charlotte Rail Trail**, be aware that construction on the Pedestrian Bridge over I-277 is still in full swing. Portions of the Rail Trail are experiencing "limited closures" as they rebuild sections and install new crossings, particularly near the track crossing. There are detours, but do you really want to navigate construction zones and unfamiliar streets on a day like this? Stick to the treadmill and avoid the headache (and potential heatstroke).
</AlertBox>
