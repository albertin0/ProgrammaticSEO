## The Charlotte Sweatfest Continues (Indoors, Hopefully)

Look, I get it. You love running the loop at Freedom Park or knocking out some miles on the Little Sugar Creek Greenway. But today, Mother Nature (and a dose of bad air) has other plans for your lungs and joints. We've been under extreme heat advisories all week, and Charlotte officials have been scrambling to get cooling centers open. That tells you everything you need to know.

### Air You Can Almost Chew

That AQI of 127 isn't just a number; it's your lungs screaming for mercy. Ground-level ozone, often the culprit in our Code Orange alerts, reacts with sunlight and emissions, building up throughout the day. Add to that the fine particulate matter (PM2.5) that can sneak deep into your lungs and bloodstream, often linked to smoke from distant wildfires or prescribed burns. This isn't just about feeling a little stuffy; it's about real, measurable health risks. If you've got asthma, heart conditions, or are just generally not a superhuman, stay inside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!
With temperatures well over 37°C (100°F) and a scorching heat index, outdoor activity can quickly lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke. Limit time outside, especially during peak afternoon hours. Seek air-conditioned spaces.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Unhealthy Air Quality (Code Orange)
The AQI is 127, classified as unhealthy for sensitive groups due to PM2.5. This means older adults, children, and people with respiratory or heart conditions should avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion. Keep those windows closed.
</AlertBox>

### South End Shuffle Not Happening

If you were planning on a scenic run through South End, know that ongoing construction projects, particularly for the new South End Station, mean significant sections of the Rail Trail are closed through July. You'll hit detours around areas like Remount Road and the Linea apartment complex. It's an obstacle course you don't need today.

### Tip: Hydrate Like It's Your Job (Because It Is)
If you absolutely *must* step outside, drink way more water than you think you need. Seriously. And remember, Mecklenburg County recreation centers and libraries are open as cooling stations if you need a break from the heat.
</AlertBox>
