## Today's Vibe: Don't Be a Hero, Charlotte

Look, I get it. It's Halloween, the weather's holding at a decent-ish 21°C (about 70°F) after all those summer scorchers we thought were behind us. You want to get out there. But let's be real, your lungs might not thank you for it. We're sitting at an AQI of 127, with PM2.5 being the main culprit. That's "Code Orange," people. If you've got asthma, allergies, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, this isn't your day to set a PR. For sensitive groups, AccuWeather is telling you to reduce time outside if you're feeling symptoms like difficulty breathing or throat irritation. Even healthy folks might notice it with prolonged exposure.

On top of that, we've got a moderate pollen count. So, if the PM2.5 doesn't get you, the tree jizz floating around probably will. Your eyes watering and nose running isn't a good look, even in a zombie costume.
