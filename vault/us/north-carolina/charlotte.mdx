The mercury's sitting at a comfortable 21.4°C, which is a rare treat for a late July morning here. Enjoy it while it lasts. But don't get complacent. We just saw "dangerous heat" warnings and temperatures well over 100°F earlier this month. Hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's a year-round commandment in the Queen City.

Let's talk air. Your weather app might say "clear," but the Air Quality Index (AQI) is at a solid 79, with ozone being the main culprit. That's a 'moderate' level, which means if you're sensitive – think asthma, COPD, or just a seasoned runner whose lungs have seen too many hazy days – you might feel it. We've been under Code Orange Air Quality Alerts for ozone recently, and it's a persistent summer struggle here. So, if you're planning a long haul around Freedom Park, maybe scale back the intensity.

Then there's the beloved Charlotte Rail Trail. For those of you who rely on it for your daily miles or commutes, brace yourselves. Portions in South End are still closed for construction on the new South End station project. They kicked this off back in April, and it's expected to run through July. So, if you're usually cruising from Tremont Avenue to Remount Road, you'll be on detours down Hawkins Street, Dunavant Street, or South Boulevard. It's not the end of the world, but it sure isn't ideal when you're trying to hit your stride.
