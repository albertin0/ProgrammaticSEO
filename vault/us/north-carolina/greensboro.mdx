While some sources might say the air is "Fair" or even "Good" today, the AQI of 114 and the dominant PM2.5 pollutant means your lungs are still dealing with fine particulate matter. This stuff can sneak deep into your lungs and bloodstream, and honestly, who needs that?

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory
Seriously, 38.3°C is no joke. The risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke is critically high. If you must be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate aggressively. Better yet, move your workout indoors. Your body will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Air Quality Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
With an AQI of 114, anyone with respiratory issues (asthma, COPD), heart conditions, or who is pregnant, elderly, or very young should avoid strenuous outdoor activity. Even healthy individuals might experience irritation. Limit your time outside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### PRO TIP: Go Indoors
Plenty of gyms in Greensboro like Workout Anytime on Battleground Avenue or O2 Fitness Friendly Center are open and air-conditioned. Your lungs and joints will appreciate the break from the brutal heat and questionable air. Or just take a rest day; you've earned it.
