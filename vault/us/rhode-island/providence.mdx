## Providence's Outdoor Playground: Proceed with Extreme Caution

If you absolutely, positively *must* get outside today, think short, think low-intensity, and stick to areas you know well. Forget setting any PRs on the **East Bay Bike Path** or doing anything strenuous down by the water, because that air quality is a legitimate concern.

### Air You Don't Want to Breathe

<AlertBox type="warning">
**AQI 128: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups.** This isn't just a suggestion, folks. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or heart conditions, seriously reconsider your outdoor plans. Even healthy individuals should limit prolonged or heavy exertion. Your lungs will thank you later.

That warm weather we're experiencing is great for getting outside, but it also means lingering ice and snow from earlier in the week are probably turning into slush and puddles. The possibility of "minor urban flooding" in low-lying areas, particularly along our rivers, is real. Don't be that person who twists an ankle in a hidden pothole under a sheet of meltwater.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Urban Flooding Risk:** Be vigilant for standing water on roads, sidewalks, and low-lying trails. The unseasonable warmth is a recipe for rapid snowmelt and potential pooling, especially if you're near the Pawtuxet or Blackstone Rivers.
</AlertBox>
