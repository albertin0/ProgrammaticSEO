## Don't Be a Hero

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Risk of Heat-Related Illness is HIGH.
With temperatures hovering near 35°C, heat exhaustion and heatstroke are serious threats. "Heat indices could spike to as high as 110 degrees" during these periods, and even "little respite in the evening either". Listen to your body, or better yet, stay indoors. If you *must* be outside, keep it to minimal activity, stick to the shade, hydrate constantly with water (not that sugary sports drink garbage, unless you're actually exercising for hours, which you shouldn't be today), and wear light clothing. The City of Minneapolis and Hennepin County have been pushing cooling facilities for a reason. Use them. And for Pete's sake, don't leave your pets or kids in the car. Ever.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups"
An AQI of 114 with PM2.5 means if you have asthma, other breathing conditions, are a child, teenager, or just prone to respiratory issues, you should seriously limit or postpone outdoor physical activity. Even healthy folks doing "extended or heavy physical activity" are at higher risk for symptoms like shortness of breath, coughing, or unusual fatigue. Don't push it.
</AlertBox>

## Construction Zones & Trail Headaches

Good luck finding a clear path on some of your usual routes.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Minnehaha Parkway & Franklin Avenue Construction
The Nicollet Avenue bridge over Minnehaha Creek on **Minnehaha Parkway** is closed through Fall 2027 for rehabilitation, impacting bike and pedestrian trails. You'll hit detours there, so plan accordingly or just avoid the area.

Meanwhile, the mess on **Franklin Avenue** continues. As of yesterday, construction has shifted to the south side between Fourth and Chicago avenues. Westbound traffic remains closed in two separate stretches, and some cross streets are shut down. Pedestrian access is maintained, but it's on the *opposite side* of the construction. Watch for signs, watch for traffic, and stay alert. It's not worth getting clipped by a construction vehicle because you were too focused on your splits.
</AlertBox>
