## The Good, The Bad, and The Detours

Alright, Saint Paul, let's talk turkey. If you woke up this morning and thought, "Finally, a day without choking on Canadian wildfires or sweating through my socks by 8 AM," you're mostly right. Our air quality is holding steady at a "Good" AQI of 41 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, a far cry from the "hazardous" mess we dealt with a couple of weeks back. And at a crisp 24.7°C (that's about 76°F for those still using Fahrenheit), with mainly clear skies, the weather's practically begging you to hit the pavement.

But before you lace up those fresh kicks and dream of a blissful, uninterrupted long run, remember where you live. This isn't some pristine wilderness trail; it's Saint Paul. And in Saint Paul, August means one thing: construction. Lots and lots of construction.

### Construction Chaos & Trail Traps

Your morning jaunt down **Grand Avenue** is still a minefield. That whole stretch at Snelling Avenue and between Macalester Street and Snelling Avenue is a mess, and Xcel Energy's apparently pulling weekend shifts, so expect more noise and potential blockages. They *say* pedestrian access is maintained, but "maintained" often means a narrow path next to heavy machinery. Keep your head on a swivel.

If you're a river runner, you're not off the hook. The **I-35E bridge trail over the Mississippi River** is completely shut down through fall 2026. You'll be rerouted over the High Bridge on Smith Avenue South, adding some extra climbs and distance whether you want it or not. Same goes for parts of Shepard Road, which is also closed in sections.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads up, river rats!** The I-35E bridge trail closure means detours. Don't just blindly follow your usual path, or you'll be doing an unexpected river crossing (or more likely, a frustrating U-turn).
</AlertBox>

Then there's the ongoing saga of the **John Ireland Boulevard** bridge, closed until... you guessed it, August 2026. Pedestrians and cyclists are diverted to Kellogg Blvd and the Marion Street bridge. And if you're near the East Side, Arcade Street is a patchwork of closures through late August and even into fall 2025 for some sections.

Remember that Sam Morgan Trail reconstruction we were all waiting for? Good news: work is "substantially complete." Which, translated from city-speak, means you might still hit a snag or two, but it's largely open. Always check before you commit.

While it's a fantastic community event, if your route takes you through the Rondo neighborhood, expect some crowds, potential street closures for the party itself, and generally more people out and about. It's not a hazard, but it's definitely something to be aware of for your pace and people-dodging skills.

<AlertBox type="info">
**My PT Take:** With all the detours and uneven surfaces from construction, pay extra attention to your foot strike and joint alignment. An unexpected curb or loose gravel on a detour path is a quick trip to a twisted ankle. Prioritize stability over speed in these areas.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, you can run. Just be prepared to improvise, and maybe pack a map from 2024 to remind yourself what these streets *used* to look like.
