<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, Mind the Ozone and Heat!
The combination of an Ozone Action Day Alert pushing "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels and a forecasted high of 97°F is not a joke. Ground-level ozone is nasty stuff that can cause acute respiratory problems and trigger asthma attacks. Coupled with intense heat, you're setting yourself up for a bad time. Reduce prolonged or heavy exertion, especially from late morning through the evening.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Cheesman Park is a Party Zone Today
If your workout route typically involves Cheesman Park, be aware it's hosting Diplo's Run Club 5K and a post-race concert. Expect significant crowds, potential detours, and general festive chaos. Maybe pick a different park or trail if you're looking for peace and quiet, or a clear path for a PR.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Stay Hydrated, Adjust Your Expectations
With temperatures soaring and ozone on the rise, proper hydration is non-negotiable. Don't just bring water; consider electrolyte drinks if you're planning any significant activity. And frankly, this isn't the day to push your limits. Listen to your body, cut your workout short if you feel off, and save that high-intensity session for a day when the air isn't actively trying to kill your lungs. Remember, running in this kind of heat and air quality is a performance killer anyway.
</AlertBox>
