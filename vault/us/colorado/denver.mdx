## The Full Breakdown

### Air Quality & Weather

Look, if you're not coughing up a lung just thinking about it, you're probably not paying attention. The Denver metro area is under an **Ozone Action Day Alert** today. That means active children, adults, and anyone with lung disease like asthma should seriously consider scaling back or skipping outdoor exertion, especially between noon and 8 p.m. Our provided AQI is a rough 140, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That tracks with ongoing wildfire smoke impacts causing hazy skies and "occasionally reduced air quality" across the region. Throw in high temps potentially hitting the mid-90s to 100°F (35-38°C) by afternoon, and you've got a recipe for misery, not a PR.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Unhealthy Air & Extreme Heat.** The combination of high ozone, PM2.5 from wildfire smoke, and scorching temperatures makes today a seriously bad day for strenuous outdoor exercise. Keep your workout indoors or postpone it. Your lungs and heart will thank you.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Closures

Just because the air is terrible doesn't mean the trails are clear. The **South Platte River Trail**, a local staple, is still a patchwork of detours and closures. You'll find construction impacting sections between 9th and 13th Avenues, and specifically between S. Huron St. and W. Mississippi Ave., where a retaining wall project is underway. Another stretch near the Broncos pedestrian bridge and the 19th Street pedestrian bridge also has temporary closures. Don't even think about running down South Platte River Drive itself; a paving project has it closed between Santa Fe Drive and Florida Avenue until late August.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: South Platte River Trail Construction.** Multiple sections of the South Platte River Trail remain closed or detoured due to ongoing construction. Check Denver Parks and Recreation for the latest maps before you head out, or just pick another route altogether.
</AlertBox>

## Final Word

Seriously, unless your definition of a good workout involves choking on smoke, battling heatstroke, and navigating construction detours, just hit the gym, lift some weights, or do some yoga indoors. Today is not the day to earn your stripes out on the Denver trails.
