### The Full Rundown: Sweat and Smog

Look, I love hitting the pavement or the dirt trails as much as the next Denver runner, but today is a hard pass for anything strenuous outside. The National Weather Service and local news are screaming "Heat Advisory" until 9 PM tonight, with the mercury pushing into the triple digits. That cool 54F (12.2C) you might have seen this morning was a fleeting moment before the sun decided to try and melt us all. Doctors are already warning people to take precautions. This isn't just about discomfort; we're talking about a legitimate risk of heat exhaustion or worse. Don't be that person wheeled into Denver Health with a core temp of 105.

Adding insult to injury, we've got an Ozone Action Day Alert in place until at least 4 PM. The hot, sunny weather we love so much is cooking up ground-level ozone, making the air "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Even if you don't typically struggle, high ozone levels can irritate your respiratory system, especially during exercise. Remember just last week when Denver's air quality was reportedly "worst in the country" due to ozone and wildfire smoke? While the smoke itself isn't the primary alert today, the ozone is enough to tell you to keep the intense workouts indoors.

### Local Trail & Road Updates

If you absolutely *must* get out, be aware of what's happening on the ground (literally). The full closure of Santa Fe Drive between Evans and Florida Avenues for bridge installation concluded this morning at 5 AM, but traffic and pedestrian patterns around West Jewell Avenue might still be adjusting.

And for our trail enthusiasts, construction continues to impact sections of the **South Platte River Trail** between 9th and 13th avenues, with detours sending you onto Alcott Street. Likewise, Phase 1 improvements at **Governor's Park** have closed the northwest corner and some walking paths through the fall. Don't get caught off guard by unexpected detours or restricted areas.
