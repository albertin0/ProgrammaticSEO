Don't even think about a hard effort outdoors today unless you enjoy huffing wildfire smoke and ozone, Denver.

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality Action Day:** The Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment (CDPHE) has issued a "Multiple Pollutants Action Day Alert" for the Front Range Urban Corridor, including Denver-Boulder, active until at least 4 PM today, August 5, 2026. This means we're dealing with elevated levels of both ground-level ozone and fine particulates (PM2.5), primarily due to wildfire smoke and warming temperatures. Yesterday's ozone hit "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels, and today's forecast is looking similar.
*   **Wildfire Smoke Lingering:** That hazy view isn't just your morning brain fog; it's smoke from out-of-state wildfires (hello, Pacific Northwest, Utah, and Nevada!) making its way into our beautiful state. An Air Quality Health Advisory for Wildfire Smoke was in effect for *all* Colorado counties until 9 AM this morning and the "Multiple Pollutants" alert carries the particulate concern through the afternoon. Even if you're not "sensitive," you'll feel this in your lungs if you push it.
*   **Construction Zone Blues:** There's always some form of construction, and today is no different. A section of the popular South Platte River Trail between S. Huron St. and W. Mississippi Ave. remains closed for retaining wall reconstruction through August 2026, meaning detours are still in play. Also, if you're navigating downtown on foot or bike, be aware of ongoing RTD rail reconstruction around the Colfax at Auraria Station and nearby streets, with changing pedestrian paths.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**SERIOUSLY, TAKE IT EASY OUT THERE!**
The "Multiple Pollutants Action Day Alert" means your lungs are getting a double whammy of ozone and fine particulate matter from wildfire smoke. If you have any heart or lung conditions, are a child, or an older adult, *do not* push yourself outdoors, especially during the afternoon and evening hours. If the smoke is thick enough to reduce visibility to less than 5 miles in your neighborhood, stay indoors. This isn't the day for a PR on the Cherry Creek Path.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**South Platte River Trail Closure!**
Runners and cyclists, be advised that a section of the South Platte River Trail, specifically between S. Huron St. and W. Mississippi Ave., is currently closed due to ongoing construction. There's a detour, but it's a pain. Don't plan your long run through there without checking your route and adding extra mileage for the detour.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Morning is Your (Slightly Less Bad) Window:**
If you absolutely *must* get outside, ozone levels are generally lower in the morning. So, if you're ignoring my advice, at least do it before lunch. Otherwise, hit the gym, jump in a pool, or just chill out inside with some good ventilation. Your respiratory system will thank you.
</AlertBox>
