## Air Quality: Don't Be a Hero

Look, the temperature is pretty decent at 14.9°C (58.8°F) and it's mainly clear, which usually screams "get outside!" But the air is the real culprit today. We've got that overall AQI of 91 with PM2.5 hanging around, but the bigger concern is the **Ozone Action Day Alert**. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a warning. For sensitive groups – that's active kids and adults, and anyone with lung diseases like asthma – you need to reduce prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion, especially from noon to 8 p.m. Ozone is nasty stuff that irritates your respiratory system, and we don't need any more of that.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Ozone Action Day Alert!
An Ozone Action Day Alert is active for Fort Collins until at least 4 p.m. today. Ozone concentrations are expected to reach "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels. Avoid prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion, especially between noon and 8 p.m., if you're in a sensitive group.
</AlertBox>

## Construction Chaos (Maybe Less So Now?)

Today is the official "end date" for a couple of major road and utility projects. Shields Street, between Mountain Avenue and West Mulberry Street, has been fully closed for underground utility work and new bike lanes, and that wraps up today. Same goes for the full closure of Trilby Road at Fossil Creek for a culvert replacement. While the hard closures might be lifting, don't expect smooth sailing immediately. Crews will still be out, detours might still be confusing, and traffic patterns will be adjusting. Always stay alert, because you know how FoCo drivers get when things change. And keep an eye on areas around the **Power Trail** where lane shifts for crossing construction are still ongoing.

## Mountain Manners: Respect the Raptors

For those of you who like to hit the trails higher up, be aware: several raptor nesting sites in the Canyon Lakes Ranger District, including areas around Triple Tier and Boston Peak, are under an area closure until the end of today, July 31st. These closures are put in place to protect nesting birds of prey. Just because it's the last day doesn't mean you get to disregard it. Respect the wildlife and choose another route.
