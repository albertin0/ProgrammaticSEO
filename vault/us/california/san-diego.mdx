## The Heat is No Joke, People

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego. We're spoiled. But this isn't your usual "80 and sunny" kind of heat. This is an unseasonable, record-breaking March heatwave that's got experts worried about what's next. This kind of heat makes even a casual stroll feel like a full-body workout in a sauna. Strenuous activity outside? You're basically asking for heat exhaustion or worse. Overnight lows are barely dipping out of the 70s, so your body isn't getting a break even after sundown.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!** Seriously limit strenuous outdoor activity. Temperatures are hitting 33°C (91.4°F) and higher in some inland areas. Hydrate constantly and listen to your body – it's not complaining, it's warning you.
</AlertBox>

### Your Lungs Aren't Loving It Either

While we don't have active wildfires creating a smoke show right now, the dry conditions from this heatwave are definitely upping the wildfire risk across the county. Our AQI is sitting at a moderate 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. It’s not a red alert, but it's enough to feel heavy when you're sucking wind on a climb, especially coupled with the heat and pollen.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Early Bird or Night Owl Only:** If you absolutely *must* get out, do it at dawn or dusk, and keep it short and light. Stick to shady, flat routes. This is not the day to conquer Cowles Mountain or push the pace. Stay near water sources and have a bailout plan.
</AlertBox>
