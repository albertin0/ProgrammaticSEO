## The Rundown

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego; we want to be outside. But today, the conditions are stacked against you. That 91-degree heat is no joke. The heat advisories aren't for tourists; they're for anyone susceptible to heat exhaustion or stroke, which, last I checked, includes all of us. Even seasoned runners need to drastically scale back their intensity, stick to the shade, and hydrate like you're crossing the Mojave. And honestly, maybe just hit the treadmill. Your ego can take it.

The air quality, while not in the "red zone," is far from pristine. That PM2.5 is irritating your lungs, and when you combine it with moderate pollen, allergy sufferers are going to be feeling it. If you're going to venture out, avoid peak traffic times where car exhaust exacerbates PM2.5 levels. Consider an N95 mask if you're particularly sensitive, but good luck breathing deeply in this heat with one on.
