## Today's Pain Report: May 16, 2026

Look, it's San Diego, so someone's always out there trying to PR something. But let's be realistic about what you're stepping into today. That 33.0°C (91.4°F) isn't some coastal fantasy. That's real, oppressive heat for significant chunks of our county, particularly if you're venturing inland. We've got warnings out there for "unseasonably extreme temperatures" hitting our desert regions, like Anza-Borrego State Park, with inland valleys not far behind. You might think you're tough, but heat stroke doesn't care about your Strava segments.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Risk Inland:** Temperatures are expected to be 10 to 20 degrees above seasonal norms for inland valleys and mountains, and even more severe in the low deserts. This isn't just "warm," it's "stay hydrated or get sidelined" kind of heat. If your route takes you away from the immediate coast, plan accordingly, or just don't go.
</AlertBox>

On the air front, the Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 67, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. The local Air Pollution Control District is calling for "GOOD to MODERATE" conditions today, but don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. Our county was recently ranked fifth-worst in the nation for annual particle pollution. So while today might not be a red-alert smog day, you're still getting a dose of what makes San Diego's air a persistent concern, especially near those portside communities where diesel trucks are constantly rolling. The very board meant to curb these emissions just voted down a key proposal. Your lungs are doing extra work.

Pollen levels are also hanging out in the "Moderate" range. If you're one of the sniffly ones, expect those allergies to kick in. Nothing debilitating, but enough to make that tempo run feel a little less zen and a lot more congested.

### Trail Conditions & Local Annoyances

Don't go planning your long ride through Sweetwater Bike Park today unless you enjoy staring at "Closed for Maintenance" signs. It's shut down until May 20th. Same goes for the nature trail at Guajome Regional Park and parts of the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, including Sherwood and Cathedral Trails. Always a good idea to check those county parks websites before you drive all the way out there just to turn around. Some roads in Cleveland National Forest might also have minor damage from recent storms, and Ortega Highway has weekend closures. Don't be that person surprised by a detour.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures and Maintenance:** Several popular spots like Sweetwater Bike Park and parts of Tijuana River Valley Regional Park are closed for maintenance or repairs. Check local park websites before heading out to avoid wasted trips.
</AlertBox>

For those who enjoy a bit of a breeze, expect some gusty winds this weekend, particularly in the deserts, mountain slopes, and passes. Might cool things down, might just blow sand in your face. Prepare for both.
