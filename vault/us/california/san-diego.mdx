## Today's Deep Dive: Heat, Smoke, and a Side of Sweat

Look, I love pounding the pavement as much as the next San Diegan, but today is a hard NO. We're in the thick of a significant heat wave that's gripping Southern California, and San Diego County is right in the crosshairs. My weather app says 33 degrees Celsius (that’s 91 degrees Fahrenheit for my non-metric friends), and that's just the tip of the furnace. Inland valleys, mountains, and especially the deserts are under extreme heat warnings, with temperatures forecasted to be truly dangerous. We’re talking triple digits that will make your eyeballs sweat. Seriously, even a short, easy jog could put you at risk for heat stroke. Overnight lows are offering little relief, making it tough for your body to recover even indoors.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **EXTREME HEAT WARNING!** Do NOT attempt strenuous outdoor exercise during peak heat hours. The National Weather Service has issued extreme heat warnings and advisories across San Diego County. Heat-related illnesses are a serious, potentially fatal risk. Drink fluids constantly, seek air-conditioned spaces, and check on vulnerable neighbors.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the air. The AQI is sitting at a moderate 67, with PM2.5 being the primary culprit. This isn't terrible on its own, but we've got a smoke advisory in effect for areas near the Creelman Fire in Ramona. While firefighters have done a solid job getting it 60% contained, smoke particles are still floating around. If you're anywhere downwind, expect that moderate AQI to feel a lot worse, especially when you're sucking wind on a run. Combine fine particulate matter with high heat, and your lungs are going to be working overtime.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Smoke Advisory in East County!** If you're in or near areas like San Diego Country Estates, expect reduced air quality due to smoke from the Creelman Fire. Limit outdoor activity, especially if you have respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if you were planning a nice, quiet long run around Montgomery Waller Community Park, think again. The South Bay 24 Hour race is underway, turning those familiar trails, grass, and bike paths into a continuous loop for endurance fanatics. It's a great event, but not for your casual mid-day workout.
