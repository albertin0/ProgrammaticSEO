## Why today is decidedly *not* the day to crush it outdoors.

<BulletList>
  <li>**It's a freaking oven out there.** Seriously, 91 degrees Fahrenheit (33 C)? That's not "exercise" weather, that's "melt on the pavement" weather. Even if the official "Extreme Heat Warning" for some areas like San Gorgonio Pass technically expired last night, the air still feels like a blow dryer set to high. Pushing yourself in this kind of heat isn't heroic, it's just dumb. We saw advisories all over the county just yesterday for temperatures hitting the 90s, even triple digits in the valleys, and coastal highs in the 90s. Just because the official piece of paper is gone doesn't mean the danger disappeared with it.</li>
  <li>**Your lungs are already working too hard.** With an AQI of 67 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, your respiratory system is already trying to filter out more gunk than usual. Add moderate pollen to that equation, and you've got a recipe for wheezing, irritation, and generally feeling like you've been sucking on a tailpipe.</li>
  <li>**Moderate pollen is just extra insult to injury.** If the heat and questionable air quality weren't enough, spring allergies are in full swing. Keep your sneezing and watery eyes indoors where at least the air is filtered and cool.</li>
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Hazard: 91°F is no joke.
  Do NOT attempt any strenuous outdoor activity today. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious, and frankly, completely avoidable if you just use some common sense. Your PR isn't worth an ER visit. The ground is probably hot enough to cook an egg, or at least fry your poor dog's paw pads if you're not careful. Stay indoors, hydrate, and reconsider your life choices if you're even contemplating a lunchtime run in Balboa Park today.
</AlertBox>
