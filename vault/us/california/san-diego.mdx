## Today's Local Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Don't Be a Hero

Look, 33 degrees Celsius (that's 91.4°F) with "mainly clear" skies means the sun is going to beat down on you relentlessly. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about heatstroke, dehydration, and generally making terrible life choices. If you absolutely *must* go out, stick to early morning or late evening, and for the love of all that is holy, hydrate aggressively before, during, and after. We're not in August yet, but the County already closes some desert parks for the summer to keep people from melting. Your body isn't built for sustained effort in this kind of oven.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind Your Air and Your Surroundings

That AQI of 67, while not apocalyptic, means sensitive groups (anyone with asthma, allergies, or just, you know, lungs) will feel it, especially when paired with moderate pollen. Add in the high heat, and you've got a recipe for a truly miserable outdoor experience.

Also, be aware of your surroundings. While not breaking news from the last 24 hours, San Diego has seen a concerning trend of assaults on joggers, even in popular spots like Balboa Park. If you're heading out, consider running with a buddy or joining a group. Safety in numbers isn't just a cliché, it's smart.

And speaking of shared paths: watch out for those e-bikes. The County Public Health Officer just put out an advisory about the rising number of e-bike injuries, especially with younger riders who might not know traffic laws. Don't assume they see you or know what they're doing.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Chaos & Trail Updates

If you usually run or bike down by the waterfront, be advised that the Harbor Drive Trunk Sewer Project has closed the southbound sidewalk and reduced traffic lanes on Harbor Drive from Park Boulevard to Beardsley Street. This is ongoing through mid-2027, so plan your routes accordingly. Further out, if you're hitting up the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, the Cathedral Trail is still closed, and there's always a risk of other closures there, so call ahead if that's your usual stomping ground. And for those north county folks, W Elder Street from S Mission Road to S Pico Avenue is closed until June 15th for grading and paving for a new community. Not ideal for a casual jog.

Stick to indoor workouts or aquatic activities today if you can. Your joints, lungs, and sanity will thank you.
</AlertBox>
