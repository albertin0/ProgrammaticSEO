Seriously, a *tropical storm* in San Diego? It's rare, but Rachel isn't messing around. ### Trail Closures & Construction Updates

If the weather wasn't enough, some of our favorite spots are still a mess.

*   **Tecolote Canyon Natural Park:** Portions of the hiking trail from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue are indefinitely closed 24/7 until summer 2027 due to sewer construction. All trails through the golf course are also a no-go.
*   **Mission Trails Regional Park:** The Father Junipero Serra Trail is still undergoing major pipeline work. While generally open to walkers and cyclists, the vehicle gates are closed 24/7 through late 2028. More importantly, weekday access between Gate 1 (near the Visitor Center) and Gate 10 (Old Mission Dam) is seriously limited from 7 AM to 4 PM until Spring 2027. You'll hit construction sites and have to turn around. Don't make the drive out there only to be disappointed.
