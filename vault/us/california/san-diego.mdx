## Your Daily Grind Breakdown

Today's shaping up to be one of those classic "San Diego is pretty great, but don't get too comfy" days. After Sunday's ridiculous heat, which set records, the mercury is actually dropping to a much more reasonable range. You might even feel a slight breeze, a carry-over from the Santa Ana winds we had over the weekend, though the wind advisory itself has expired. There's even a small chance of scattered showers today, just enough to keep things interesting.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Lungs:** Even with cooler temps, that AQI of 67 means some fine particulate matter (PM2.5) is floating around. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, take it easy. Don't go for that PR today if you feel any tightness. Add in moderate pollen, and it's a double whammy for some of us. Consider an indoor workout if your lungs are particularly finicky.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Status & Local Hazards

Good news for those of you eager to hit up Torrey Pines Natural Reserve: most trails reopened on March 1st after being closed for improvements. Enjoy it while you can, because Phase II of construction is coming in late summer.

However, not all trails are in the clear.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**El Escorpión Trail is Partially Closed:** If your usual route takes you through El Escorpión Trail, be aware that a portion is closed from March to June 2026 for heavy equipment operations and well plugging. Public safety is the reason, so find an alternate route.

**Tijuana River Valley Regional Park:** Several trails here remain closed, including Sherwood Trail, Vireo Trail (west of Hollister St.), and Cathedral Trail. Ongoing repairs mean you might also encounter noise and dust in other areas of the park. Plan your run accordingly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Even with the cool-down, your body is still adjusting from yesterday's oven-like conditions. Don't skimp on the water before, during, and after your workout. Better safe than sorry, especially with that moderate AQI.
</AlertBox>

Remember, construction projects are always popping up around town, like those new transportation contracts starting in 2026, including a connection for the Chollas Creek to Bayshore Bikeway. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours even on your regular routes.

All in all, today's a decent day to get outside, just be smart about where you go and listen to your body, especially those lungs.
