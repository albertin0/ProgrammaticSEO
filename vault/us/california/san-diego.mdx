## Today's Trail Talk: Navigating the Urban & Wild

Today's overcast skies might make for a comfortable run, but don't let the marine layer lull you into complacency. While temperatures are a pleasant 20.3°C (around 68.5°F), it's the smaller irritations and potential long-term issues you need to watch.

First off, let's talk air. With a moderate AQI, predominantly from PM2.5, your respiratory system is working a bit harder than usual. This means your typical pace might feel tougher, and if you've got any underlying lung issues, you absolutely should scale back. It's not worth triggering an asthma attack for a PR on a Friday.

The pollen, too, is hanging around. If you're planning a long loop through, say, Mission Trails Regional Park, remember that those beautiful native plants are also pumping out allergens. Don't be surprised if you finish your run feeling more congested than invigorated.

### Coastal Considerations: More Than Just the Current Swell

While we're not facing an immediate storm, the talk of Kelvin waves heading our way is a reminder that our coastline is in for some changes. These waves could raise sea levels by 6-12 inches, which, when combined with future winter storms and king tides, means increased risk of coastal flooding and beach erosion down the line. This isn't a "today" problem for your run, but it's a good kick in the pants to be aware of what's coming if you frequent the beach paths or bluffs. The City of San Diego is already bracing for an intensified El Niño that will bring heavier rainfall, stronger winds, and coastal impacts this fall and winter. So, enjoy that beach run today, but know that its future might look a little different.
