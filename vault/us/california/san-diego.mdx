Don't expect smooth sailing everywhere you go. If you're planning on hitting some dirt, be aware of what's happening. The **Cathedral Trail in the River Valley** is still closed for construction, and you can expect noise and dust if you're in that general vicinity. So much for a peaceful nature escape, right?

Beyond that specific headache, the County Parks and Rec website always has a laundry list of ongoing issues. While not all of them impact *running* directly, it's a good reminder that our parks are always in various states of repair. For instance, sections of W Elder St in Fallbrook are undergoing grading and paving until mid-June, which could throw a wrench in your cycling or running routes if you're out that way. And if you're looking to hike up in the Cleveland National Forest, remember that the Penny Pines Trailhead still doesn't have water. Always pack extra.
