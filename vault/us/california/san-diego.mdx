<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning

With temperatures pushing into the low 90s across our inland valleys today and higher in the deserts, heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious threats. This isn't just "uncomfortable"; it's legitimately dangerous. If you're going out, stick to the coast, keep it short, and hydrate aggressively. The National Weather Service isn't messing around with these warnings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Smoke Potential

There was a new wildfire, the Capistrano Fire, reported in San Diego County just yesterday, May 30th. While containment details are unknown, any active fire means potential for localized smoke and particulate matter (PM2.5), contributing to that "Moderate" AQI. Keep an eye (and nose) out, especially if you're east of the I-15.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Trail Troubles at Mission Trails

If your go-to is Mission Trails Regional Park, be prepared for ongoing pipeline construction on the Father Junipero Serra Trail. The vehicle gates are still closed, and there are temporary closures and restrictions for bikes and pedestrians. It's been like this since Spring 2025 and isn't letting up until late 2028, so learn your detours or pick another spot. Same goes for parts of Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, where the Cathedral Trail is closed.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, it's not worth pushing it in these conditions. There's always tomorrow, or at least a treadmill.
