## Seriously, It's Hot. And Not Just "San Diego Hot."

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego. We laugh at "cold" weather and scoff at anyone who can't handle a little sunshine. But 33°C is *hot*. Even for us. The heat warning for our desert and inland valley cousins might have officially "expired" from Sunday/Monday, but the NWS is still saying temperatures are elevated through Saturday, even as they "cool" slightly today. Your 91-degree run around Balboa Park isn't doing you any favors, especially after 9 AM. This kind of heat isn't just uncomfortable; it genuinely increases your risk of heat-related illnesses.

### Don't Be a Hero, Stay Hydrated

You think you're tough? Your kidneys don't care. Dehydration is a real threat when you're sweating buckets in this kind of heat. Sip water constantly, not just when you feel thirsty. If you're planning anything longer than a 30-minute stroll, consider postponing it or moving it indoors. Your physical therapist (me, right now) would rather see you on the treadmill than in the ER.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  The National Interagency Fire Center has already flagged the Southwest for an "above normal significant fire potential" this month, directly linking it to the heat spike and low humidity. That means if you see even a wisp of smoke, report it immediately and get out of there. Our trails dry out faster than your excuses for skipping leg day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  With the AQI at 67 and PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant, coupled with moderate pollen, your respiratory system is already under stress. Intense exercise will only exacerbate this, potentially leading to coughing, shortness of breath, or aggravating existing conditions like asthma. Take it easy on the lungs.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  If you absolutely *must* get outside, aim for first light. We're talking 5:30 AM, folks. By 8 AM, the concrete is already radiating enough heat to cook an egg, and by lunch, you'll be one. Stick to shaded routes if possible, and carry more water than you think you need. Or, you know, find a gym with AC.
</AlertBox>
