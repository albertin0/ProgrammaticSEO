## The Full Breakdown

Look, if you're planning on a long run or a strenuous hike today, seriously, reconsider. We're staring down a pretty toasty day. While the coastal breeze might keep things *just* under inferno levels, inland, especially places like Ramona or the trails around Mission Trails Regional Park, it’s going to be brutal. The National Weather Service issued a Heat Advisory for San Diego County mountains and valleys, with temperatures projected near 100°F (38°C). That's not "push through it" weather; that's "find some shade or a treadmill" weather.

Speaking of trails, heads up: County parks like El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, and Mt. Gower Preserve often close for the entire month of August due to extreme heat. While we're still in late July, these closures are an annual reminder of how seriously you need to take the heat out east. Check their specific websites before you drive all the way out there only to find a locked gate.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Heat Stroke is Real, Folks.** With temperatures in the 90s (and pushing 100°F inland), heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious risks. Drink way more water than you think you need, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, don't attempt a new personal best on your long run today.
</AlertBox>

### Air & Allergens

The air quality, reported as an AQI of 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, puts us squarely in the "moderate" category. This means for most of us, it's generally acceptable for outdoor activity, but sensitive groups – kids, older adults, or anyone with respiratory issues – might feel it. San Diego, unfortunately, consistently ranks high for ozone pollution, so even a moderate day still means your lungs are working harder than they should.

## Coastal Chaos

Heading to the beach for a swim or a surf session? Think twice. We've got a Beach Hazards Statement in effect through Wednesday afternoon. That means dangerous ocean conditions: strong rip currents, large waves between 3 to 6 feet, with some sets reaching a gnarly 7 feet on south and southwest-facing beaches. Lifeguards are explicitly telling inexperienced swimmers to stay out of the water. This isn't the day to learn to surf or brave a new spot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Stay Off the Cliffs at Sunset Cliffs!** Lifeguards also issued a warning about cliff jumping. It's illegal, incredibly risky, and a teenager was injured doing it just last weekend. Don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

A few minor construction alerts are floating around, mostly affecting bus stops on University Avenue and around East County Square Walmart until the end of July and October respectively. So, if you're on foot and using public transport, just be aware of potential detours for a few more days.

All in all, if you *must* exercise outside today, stick to shaded routes, go early, keep it short, and stay hydrated. Or just hit the gym, if you can find one that's not trying to steal your money or has working equipment.
