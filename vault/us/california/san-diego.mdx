#### Seriously, Stay Out of the Ocean.
The National Weather Service has issued a hazardous swimming warning for San Diego County's coastline, with life-threatening rip currents and elevated surf. Lifeguards have been busy, and that's not a challenge for your ego, it's a reason to stay on dry land.
</AlertBox>

#### Construction Woes
If your routine takes you anywhere near the I-5 between Solana Beach and Carlsbad, expect delays and possibly some noise. Nighttime work on the SR 52 eastbound connector to northbound I-805 is also ongoing through tonight. Plan your routes accordingly, or just stick to your neighborhood streets.
