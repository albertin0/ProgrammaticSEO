## The Rundown: Hazards & Head-Ups

### Air Quality & Respiratory Irritants

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Smoke Alert:** Even with the "good to moderate" AQI readings, don't ignore the active wildfires. The Border 6 Fire is still kicking up smoke in the southeastern parts of the county, and a new, uncontained Prospect Fire was reported yesterday. If you're anywhere near the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, especially heading towards the Dulzura or Campo areas, expect haze and that distinct smoke smell. Pay attention to your body; if you feel any burning in your throat or lungs, cut your workout short. This isn't the day for setting a PR, especially if you have asthma or other respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Pointers:** Moderate pollen means those seasonal sneezes and sniffles are still on the menu. If you're prone to allergies, consider an antihistamine before you head out, or stick to indoor workouts if you want to avoid feeling generally miserable.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Closures

Planning to hit some trails? A few things to keep in mind:

*   **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park:** While parts of the park are reopening, the **Cathedral Trail in the River Valley is currently closed for repairs**. Don't go trying to skirt around closures, folks; those signs are there for a reason. Plus, with the Border 6 Fire's proximity, consider a different location today if you were eyeing this spot.
*   **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve:** Good news here, the Main Trails and Extension are OPEN. However, remember that many of the steeper trails like Broken Hill, Razor Point, and Beach Trails are often closed *after* rain to prevent erosion. While no significant rain is expected today, with the overcast conditions, keep an eye on trail conditions if you choose a steeper route. Also, the Reserve closes at sunset, which is around 7:30/8 PM this time of year. Don't get caught out there in the dark.
