## Current Conditions: Sweat & Smoke (Barely)

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego; we *expect* sunshine. But today's "Mainly Clear" skies are just a front for a serious oven. The air temperature is already hitting 33°C (91°F) and it's only going up. A Heat Advisory is firmly in place across the county through Sunday evening, especially for our inland valleys and mountains where temps could push past 100°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous.

Yesterday saw a brush fire, the Beaver Fire, kick off near Jamul in Eastern San Diego County. While fire crews got it 50% contained at 20 acres and lifted all evacuations last night, the underlying wildfire risk remains high with these hot, dry conditions. So, even if you don't *see* smoke wafting over your specific running path, the air quality isn't pristine, and the potential for new fires is always simmering.

## Your Workout, My Grim Prognosis

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, today's heat is no joke. With temperatures pushing well into the 90s and potentially triple digits inland, outdoor exertion can quickly lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke. This is why county parks like El Capitan Preserve, Mount Gower, and Hellhole Canyon are closed for the entire month of August. Your favorite trail run isn't worth a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye on Wildfire Conditions
While the Beaver Fire near Jamul is mostly contained, the general high-fire-danger conditions persist. Be aware of your surroundings, and if you see smoke or smell fire, turn around and get out of there. Don't be "that guy" who needs a rescue because you ignored the obvious.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### E-Bike Riders: Helmet Up and Stay Aware
On a related note for those on wheels, health and safety experts held an event yesterday to remind everyone about the serious dangers of e-bikes, especially for kids. With a new city ordinance taking effect soon, this is a good reminder for *all* cyclists: wear a proper helmet, it could literally save your brain.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* get a workout in, hit the gym, or do some early morning yoga in your air-conditioned living room. If you insist on going outside, stick to the absolute earliest hours, seek shade like it's gold, and carry more water than you think you need. Better yet, save the outdoor grind for a day that isn't actively trying to turn you into jerky.
