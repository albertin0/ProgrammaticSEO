Look, if you don't *have* to be outside doing anything strenuous today, maybe don't. Especially if you're planning on running anywhere inland.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
*   **It's a oven out there:** We're hitting 33°C (that's 91°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) and "Mainly Clear" means no marine layer to save your bacon. That's dangerously hot, folks, and definitely not ideal for a long run or intense workout. The heat warnings for our desert areas just expired yesterday, but temperatures in the valleys were still well above average.
*   **Breathing might be a little rough:** With a Moderate AQI of 67 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, your lungs are already working harder than usual. There were reports of a Highway 7 Fire and a Corral Fire in San Diego County burning yesterday, and while it's not a full-blown inferno over the city, that smoke particulate has to go somewhere. If you've got any kind of respiratory issues, seriously reconsider that long-distance push.
*   **Pollen's still doing its thing:** Moderate pollen levels just add another layer of "ugh" to the mix for anyone with allergies. Don't add itchy eyes and a runny nose to potential heat exhaustion.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heatstroke is No Joke!
Today's inland temperatures are flirting with triple digits. If you're out there, stay hydrated with more water than you think you need, seek shade constantly, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. Headaches, dizziness, nausea, confusion – those aren't signs of a "good workout," they're signs you're overheating. Get inside, cool down, and if it's bad, call for help.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, trying to set a PR on a day like this is a recipe for disaster. Especially if you're hitting trails like those around **Mission Trails Regional Park**. The sun beats down pretty hard out there, and dehydration sets in fast when it's this warm. Save the heroics for a cooler, less smoky day.
