## Today’s Rundown: Overcast with a Side of Obstacles

We've got an overcast sky and a comfortable 19.1°C (about 66°F) today. No extreme heat warnings directly for our coastal zones, which is a blessing, especially after that heatstroke incident someone suffered during an outdoor workout recently. But inland, those temperatures are still creeping up, so if your run takes you away from the breeze, remember your limits.

The air quality, while not hitting "unhealthy" levels, is still in the "moderate" category with PM2.5 hanging around. If you’re pushing hard, or you’re one of the many runners who already feels every dust particle, consider adjusting your intensity. And for the allergy sufferers, that moderate pollen isn't doing you any favors; keep those antihistamines handy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  If your usual route includes **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve**, pivot. It's completely closed for utility work for both cars, cyclists, and us humble runners until spring of next year. Don't be "that person" trying to sneak around—it's closed for a reason, and you'll just be wasting your time.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Downtown runners and cyclists: watch out for ongoing construction around the **San Diego Convention Center** on Harbor Drive, where lanes are reduced, and near San Diego International Airport. Your usual smooth ride or run might involve more dodging and weaving than you planned. Check local traffic apps before you head out, or just stick to the Embarcadero for an unobstructed, if less adventurous, path.
</AlertBox>

So, if your workout involves ocean swimming or surfing, maybe just be extra aware of what's sharing the water with you. Probably not a huge concern for your standard morning paddle, but hey, you heard it here first.
