The Pacific Surfliner service is completely shut down today, September 13th, due to "coordinated track closures and infrastructure work" all along the corridor. This includes "double-track improvements, signal optimization, new railroad ties, track surfacing and bridge inspections" right here in San Diego County. While they're not closing pedestrian trails directly, active construction in the area means heavy equipment, potential detours, and general chaos near railroad rights-of-way. Stay clear of active work zones.
