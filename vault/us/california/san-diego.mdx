## The Heat is On (Again)

Look, I love pushing limits, but today's heat is flirting with dangerous. We've seen warnings for inland areas hitting 90s, 100s, and even 110°F earlier this month. Even coastal pockets won't be immune to the general baking happening around us. Your body can only do so much to cool itself, and when the air itself is like a blow dryer, you're fighting a losing battle. Running in this kind of heat isn't tough; it's dumb. Don't be dumb.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** With temperatures pushing into the 90s Fahrenheit (33.0°C), strenuous outdoor activity is highly risky, especially midday. Heatstroke and exhaustion are real. Adjust your plans or stay indoors.
</AlertBox>

## Breathe Easy? Not So Much.

While the AQI of 67 (Moderate) might sound okay, dig a little deeper. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, and there's a specific air quality advisory for Southern Nevada *through today* due to a dense plume of wildfire smoke from Ensenada that drifted across San Diego County. That means tiny, lung-irritating particles are floating around. Don't think just because you can't see a huge smoke cloud that it's not affecting your lungs. Your respiratory system is already working overtime in this heat; don't add smoke to the mix.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Smoke & Air Quality Alert:** Moderate AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant is a concern due to wildfire smoke from across the border. Sensitive groups should limit outdoor exertion; everyone should be mindful of breathing heavy.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Report & Local Annoyances

If you were eyeing Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve, be aware that the Rancho Peñasquitos Adobe Loop, a popular 3.2-mile section, remains closed due to fire risk, a closure that's been in effect since June. The main Los Penasquitos Canyon Trail from the Black Mountain Road staging area is reportedly open, but honestly, with the heat and air quality, why risk it today?

For those trying to squeeze in a neighborhood run, some localized road closures for slurry sealing might pop up on residential streets like Deer Ridge Road or Deer Trail Drive between 7 AM and 4 PM. Minor inconvenience, but just another reason to bail on the outdoor grind today.
