## Today's Outdoor Grind: Think Twice, Sweat Smart

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego. We *want* to be outside. But sometimes, Mother Nature (and our city's event planners) have other ideas. Today is one of those days where a little common sense goes a long way, especially if you're planning on pushing it.

That 33°C (91.4°F) isn't just "warm," it's "pass out on the pavement" territory if you're not careful. This isn't March's heat advisory that just expired, this is *now*. Your body struggles to cool itself efficiently in that kind of heat, leading to everything from dehydration to heat stroke. Don't be that person.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Don't Be a Hero
Seriously, 91.4°F is no joke. If you must be outside, keep it short, stay to the absolute shadiest routes you can find (maybe the tree-lined paths in Balboa Park, but even then, be cautious), and hydrate like it's your job. Reschedule your strenuous stuff for later in the evening, or better yet, hit the gym.
</AlertBox>

Pollen levels are sitting at moderate, which means if you're prone to seasonal sniffles or worse, you'll be feeling it. Couple that with gasping for air in the heat, and your lungs are going to hate you. The Air Quality Index is good at 47, which is a silver lining, but the dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, which sometimes spikes on hot, clear days. Keep an eye on that.

While there aren't widespread smoke advisories for San Diego city itself *today*, a recent Boulevard Fire was reported in San Diego County on April 24th. Always be aware of local conditions, especially if you're closer to the East County or more rural areas.
