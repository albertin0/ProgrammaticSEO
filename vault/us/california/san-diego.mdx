<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, the Heat is No Joke.
That 33.0&deg;C is just the overall San Diego reading. Head five miles inland, and you're cooking. Mountains and deserts are under an Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT tonight, with temperatures projected into the high 90s and even 100s for areas like Ramona. If you're out running in that, you're not a hero, you're an idiot asking for a ride in an ambulance. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke symptoms can come on fast. Drink more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, stick to an air-conditioned gym today if you absolutely *must* get your heart rate up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Keep an Eye on the Skies (and your Air Quality App)
We've got an AQI of 67, which is "Moderate," with PM2.5 as the main culprit. Given the recent Dehesa fires in San Diego County, that PM2.5 isn't just exhaust fumes. These fires are uncontained, so the smoke situation can change faster than a runner trying to beat a red light. If you're prone to respiratory issues, or even if you're not, that smoky air is doing your lungs zero favors. Don't push it on those long runs near the foothills or anywhere the haze looks thicker.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Planning Your Weekend Trail Runs? Check Ahead.
If you're a regular on the Walker Preserve Trail, be aware that county sewer rehabilitation starts on July 13th. The trail won't be fully closed, but sections will be narrowed to a measly 12 feet, and they're planning temporary full closures when they pull pipe liners. Imagine trying to PR with a backhoe for company. Also, keep in mind other ongoing closures for park amenities, like the Cathedral Trail in Tijuana River Valley Regional Park. Always check the County Parks site before you head out, unless you enjoy running into literal roadblocks.
</AlertBox>
