#### Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!
Seriously, folks, this isn't a drill. An Extreme Heat Warning is in place through Saturday evening. Highs could hit 98-108°F (36.6-42.2°C) in some areas, potentially even 110°F (43°C). Heat-related illnesses are a real threat. If you absolutely *must* go out, limit strenuous activities to early morning or late evening, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body. Don't be that person paramedics are hosing down on a trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch Out for Blaze Starters
CAL FIRE is already on alert, and the dry, hot conditions mean fire season is basically here early. Think twice about any activity that could cause a spark, like dragging chains or careless disposal of anything flammable. Air quality could turn from moderate to nasty very quickly if a fire breaks out.
</AlertBox>

If you were planning a nice long run along the **Del Mar Bluffs**, be aware that construction is still ongoing through summer 2026. This project is stabilizing those bluffs, which is great for the long term, but it means periodic work near the seawalls and the southern section of Sea Cliff Park is closed until mid-year. So even if you braved the heat, your scenic route might have detours or dusty patches. Always check local park and trail websites before you head out, because things change faster than my split times on a downhill.
