Today is one of those classic San Diego days where the coastal conditions are fantastic, but you step a few miles inland and things get spicy.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
*   **Wildfire Smoke North County:** The Pipeline Fire is still burning up at Camp Pendleton. While central San Diego's air quality looks decent, areas north of Oceanside, like Fallbrook and Valley Center, are getting smoke. Winds overnight might even push some into Oceanside itself. If you're smelling smoke, assume the air isn't great.
*   **Inland Heat Advisory:** If your usual route takes you anywhere near the San Diego County Deserts, or even the mountains and inland valleys, turn around. An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect through Thursday with temperatures hitting up to 117 degrees in the desert. Even inland valleys are looking at 90s to low 100s. Don't be a hero.
*   **Moderate Pollen with a Vengeance:** The "Moderate" pollen level might sound benign, but San Diego's allergy season is hitting harder than usual this year, thanks to our bizarre weather patterns extending pollen production. If your nose is already running like you just finished a tempo run, be warned.
</BulletList>

Look, the coast is looking pretty much perfect with 17.2°C (around 63°F) and mainly clear skies. That marine layer is doing its job, keeping things cool and breathable right by the water. But you head east, even a little, and it's a completely different ballgame.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning for Desert & Inland Areas:** Seriously, don't mess with the desert this week. An Extreme Heat Warning is active for the San Diego County Deserts until Thursday, with forecasts up to 117 degrees. Mountains and inland valleys will also see dangerously high temps. Stick to the coast or hit the gym if you're not planning a sunrise or sunset run way out west.
</AlertBox>

Up north, the Pipeline Fire on Camp Pendleton is still causing issues. While the general AQI for San Diego County is a good 43, the dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, which can be exacerbated by wildfires. Smoke plumes have been drifting east and northeast towards Fallbrook and Valley Center, and light winds could bring it towards Oceanside today. Keep an eye (and nose) out for smoke, especially if you're hitting trails like those near San Onofre or heading inland from the coast in North County. If you smell smoke, cut your workout short and head indoors.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ongoing Construction at Mission Trails Regional Park:** If you're a regular on the Father Junipero Serra Trail, remember that pipeline construction continues through late 2028. The vehicle gates are closed 24/7, though the walking and bike paths largely remain open. Expect some noise and dust in the area. Also, the Cathedral Trail in Tijuana River Valley Regional Park is still closed for repairs.
</AlertBox>

Good news for those who love Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve: the trails reopened after their temporary closure through February. However, keep in mind Phase II of their improvement project is scheduled for August or September, so enjoy these clear trails while they last!

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Even with cooler coastal temps, the air is dry, and if you *do* venture anywhere inland, you'll need significantly more water than you think. Carry more than you plan to drink.
</AlertBox>
