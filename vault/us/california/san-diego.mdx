The marine layer might be keeping things cool near the beaches, but don't let that fool you into thinking all of San Diego is in the clear. Those inland valleys and mountains are currently baking, and the county isn't messing around when it comes to hiker safety. They've explicitly closed down hotspots like El Capitan County Preserve, Mt. Gower Preserve, and Hellhole Canyon Preserve for all of August. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a hard closure because people routinely get into trouble out there in these conditions, and honestly, who wants to be *that* person needing a helicopter rescue when the county already told you not to go?

### Local Hotspots & Headaches

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat in Inland Areas & Park Closures:** An Extreme Heat Warning is active for San Diego County deserts, mountains, Coachella Valley, and other inland areas through Wednesday evening, with forecasted temperatures between 110-115°F. As a result, El Capitan County Preserve, Mt. Gower Preserve, and Hellhole Canyon Preserve are **closed for the entire month of August**. Stay out of these areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Comic-Con Chaos Downtown:** Harbor Drive, right in front of the San Diego Convention Center, is completely shut down to almost all traffic – that includes bikes, scooters, and skateboards – for Comic-Con 2026. If your workout takes you near the waterfront downtown, expect massive crowds and significant detours. Pedestrian access to the waterfront itself is still allowed, but don't count on moving quickly.
</AlertBox>

So, what's left? Stick to coastal paths, your local gym, or shaded neighborhood routes. Balboa Park might be an option, but be mindful of the crowds and the moderate AQI. Consider moving your workout indoors today if you're susceptible to heat or allergies, or just don't feel like navigating a literal convention-sized obstacle course downtown. Your joints (and lungs) will thank you.
