## The Full Breakdown: Heat, Haze, and Half-Closed Trails

Look, I know St. Patrick's Day usually brings out a bit of extra pep, but unless your celebratory run involves air conditioning, I'd scale back drastically. This isn't just "warm," this is "record-shattering, potential-heatstroke" warm for March. The NWS warnings are for a "long-duration early season heat wave" with "Widespread Major HeatRisk expected" in our deserts and "Moderate HeatRisk" for mountains, valleys, and coast.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** Starting tomorrow (Wednesday) and continuing through the week, dangerously high temperatures are expected across San Diego County. Limit strenuous outdoor activity to early morning or late evening, if at all. Stay hydrated, seek shade, and check on vulnerable neighbors. Car interiors will reach lethal temperatures in minutes.
</AlertBox>

### What to Know Before You Go (Or Don't Go)

This "snow-eater heat wave" isn't just about you feeling hot; it's rapidly drying out vegetation, which means wildfire risk is on the rise. Cal Fire is already talking about an earlier and potentially longer fire season. While there aren't active blazes to report *today* for San Diego, the conditions are ripe for one.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Increased Wildfire Risk:** With soaring temperatures and dry conditions, vegetation is drying out quickly. Be extra vigilant with anything that could spark a fire, and if you see smoke, report it immediately.
</AlertBox>

On the trail front, remember the San Diego River Gorge/Cedar Creek Falls area is still closed per a forest order. Don't push it or assume it's open. While Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve *should* have most trails open again after their February closures, expect ongoing improvement projects there and at other parks like San Dieguito County Park throughout the year. Always check county parks and rec notices before heading out.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Trail Watch:** Expect ongoing construction delays and noise around major projects like the Riverwalk San Diego in Mission Valley or the various road and park upgrades throughout the county. Plan alternative routes and stay informed about local park notices for specific closures.
</AlertBox>
