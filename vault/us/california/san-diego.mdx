Today is a logistical nightmare for outdoor workouts, and your lungs will thank you for keeping it low-key later tonight.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
*   **Fourth of July Chaos:** San Diego is packed tighter than a sardine can today. Expect massive crowds, parades, races, and general holiday gridlock everywhere from Coronado to Mira Mesa. If your workout plan involves pavement, prepare for detours and dodging tourists.
*   **Post-Sunset Smoke Show:** While daytime AQI is hovering around moderate, don't let that fool you. Tonight, with every single "professional" and illegal fireworks display going off across the county, expect significant spikes in particle pollution. Your lungs will take a beating from the smoke, which can linger well into tomorrow morning.
*   **Wildfire Whispers:** We've got a few small, uncontained wildfires currently burning in the county, like the 10-acre Mcp 13 Fire. Combine that with San Diego's year-round fire risk and all those celebratory pyrotechnics, and the air quality situation could get ugly fast, even if the smoke isn't directly from a large blaze right now.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it, it's a holiday, and the partly cloudy, 18°C weather (about 64°F) sounds pretty damn inviting for a run, especially compared to the heat domes baking the rest of the country. But unless your idea of a good time is weaving through throngs of people or choking on smoke, maybe rethink that long effort.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Coronado Crown City Classic & Big Bay Boom Impact

If you were planning on a morning run along Coronado Bay, specifically near Tidelands Park, know that the Crown City Classic 5K and 12K is happening, meaning that whole area will be swarming with runners and spectators. Later, the Big Bay Boom fireworks will draw huge crowds to the waterfront. Forget parking, forget clear paths, just accept the inevitable human traffic jam.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Evening Air Quality Alert: Fireworks Fallout!

Seriously, do *not* plan any strenuous outdoor activity after sunset. The air quality is predicted to take a nosedive to "Unhealthy" or even "Hazardous" in parts of Southern California from fireworks pollution. Even if you don't see thick smoke, those fine particulate matters are doing damage you can't feel until later. Stick to indoor workouts tonight. The San Diego Fire-Rescue Department is already warning about increased fire risk from all the fireworks, legal or otherwise.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pollen Update

Pollen levels are moderate today. If you're sensitive, pop that antihistamine before you head out, because the last thing you need is a runny nose to go with the potential ozone and smoke.
</AlertBox>
