Today's weather might look deceivingly pleasant, but don't let the "June Gloom" lull you into a false sense of security; San Diego's trails are a bit of a minefield right now.

## Why today isn't exactly the day for a PR

<BulletList>
*   **Smoky Skies & Your Lungs:** While the overcast skies might keep temperatures mild at 17.9°C (64°F), there's a good chance you're still breathing in residual particulate matter. The AQI is sitting at 67, which is "Moderate," but the dominant pollutant is PM2.5. We had the Range Fire at Camp Pendleton still 80% contained yesterday and a new Raymond Fire reported in San Diego County, containment unknown. "Contained" doesn't mean "out," and that smoke has to go somewhere. Add in a "Moderate" pollen count, and your respiratory system is working overtime, even if you don't feel it immediately.
*   **Construction Chaos:** If you're thinking of hitting some of our prime canyon trails, think again. The Tecolote Canyon Trunk Sewer Improvement project has shut down the hiking trail from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue until summer 2027, and all trails through the golf course are gone too. You'll find full closures from Balboa to Genesee during work hours. And don't even get me started on Mission Trails Regional Park, where pipeline construction means the Father Junipero Serra Trail's vehicle gates are closed 24/7 through late 2028, and you're dealing with noise, dust, and potential pedestrian detours. It's less a run, more an obstacle course.
*   **Lingering Fire Impacts:** Remember that Sorrento Valley fire back in June? It caused trail closures in Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve. While the immediate blaze is out, these areas aren't always quick to reopen or are still dealing with damage and potential hazards. Running through recently burned areas isn't just about breathing; it's about unstable ground and hidden dangers.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Diversions and Detours are the New Normal:** Expect unexpected closures, heavy equipment, and dusty conditions on many popular routes, particularly around Tecolote Canyon and Mission Trails. Plan your route extensively or stick to known open areas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Keep an Eye on the Air:** While "Moderate" AQI sounds okay, PM2.5 is the stuff that gets deep into your lungs. Given recent and ongoing fires, pay attention to how your body feels. If you have asthma or other respiratory issues, today might be better spent indoors. Running through smoke is not a badge of honor, it's a fast track to feeling like crap.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Key:** Overcast days make us forget, but you're still exerting yourself. With moderate AQI and pollen, staying well-hydrated helps your body filter out irritants and keeps your system happy.
</AlertBox>
