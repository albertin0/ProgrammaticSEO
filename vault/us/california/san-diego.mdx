## The Rundown: What to Watch Out For

### Local Hazards

Look, it's San Diego, there's always *something* happening.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Detours:** If your route takes you near the **San Diego Convention Center**, particularly along Harbor Drive from Park Boulevard to Beardsley Street, be prepared for lane reductions and sidewalk closures. The Harbor Drive Trunk Sewer Project is still churning away, so watch your step and don't get clipped by a cement mixer.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Inland Haze & Smoke:** While the city's air quality is good, there's a "Junction Fire" in the Santa Ysabel area, roughly 75% contained as of yesterday, and a smaller "Jean Fire" also active in San Diego County. If you're planning a trail run deeper inland, like around the eastern edges of Mission Trails Regional Park, keep an eye on official fire updates. It's not impacting downtown breathing right now, but a wind shift can change things fast.
</AlertBox>
