<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heat Stress is Real, Folks!
Even if the official warnings have lifted, 91.4°F is no joke. The City of San Diego was pushing heat precautions all week. Seriously, if you're out there between 10 AM and 6 PM, you're asking for trouble. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke don't care about your Strava segment. Hydrate like it's your job, consider an early morning or late evening run, and *do not* push yourself. Listen to your body – it's probably screaming at you already.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch Your Step (and Your Lungs)
That moderate AQI and pollen count means if you're sensitive, you might feel it. Your lungs are already fighting the heat, so don't give them extra grief. If you have asthma or allergies, make sure your inhaler is topped up, or consider an indoor session today.
</AlertBox>

#### Mission Trails Update
If your route takes you anywhere near the Father Junipero Serra Trail in Mission Trails Regional Park, just know you'll be dealing with ongoing construction until late 2028. It's a pain, but they're improving our sewer infrastructure, so... yay, I guess?
