## San Diego: Hot, Hazy, and Shut Down

Look, I love our sunny San Diego as much as the next runner, but let's be real: today is trying to kill your workout vibe. We're staring down a serious Heat Advisory for our inland valleys, with temperatures expected to climb well past comfortable, potentially hitting 105 degrees in areas like the Inland Empire. Even closer to the coast, your 33°C (91.4°F) isn't exactly "perfect running weather," and the National Weather Service is calling for widespread moderate HeatRisk. You'll be drenched before you even hit your first mile marker, and that's not just a cute saying – it’s a recipe for heat exhaustion or worse. Drink water like it's your job, and frankly, just don't go during peak sun.

On top of the oven-like conditions, the air quality isn't doing you any favors. We're sitting at a Moderate AQI of 67, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. So, while you're roasting, you're also sucking in fine particulate matter. Your lungs aren't going to thank you for that double-whammy, especially when combined with a moderate pollen count that’s just waiting to tickle your sinuses into oblivion.

And if you *were* hoping to escape the concrete jungle for some trail therapy, think again. Good luck finding a clear path. The **Los Penasquitos Canyon Preserve** has all its trails temporarily closed due to the Sorrento Fire. Don't even bother trying the **San Diego River Gorge** or **Three Sisters trails**; those popular (and strenuous) hiking areas in the backcountry are closed for the entire month of August, specifically because of the extreme heat and a concerning number of heat-related rescues. Seriously, people needed to be *rescued* from those trails. Don't be that person. Even downtown, if you're planning a scenic stroll near **Waterfront Park**, know that access is limited for event setup this week.

Looking ahead, the city is also warning about a "very strong" El Niño on the horizon, with officials urging preparation for potential heavy rains and flooding. Not an immediate concern for today's run, but good to know we're cycling through all the natural hazards right now.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, folks. The heat advisory isn't a suggestion. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity between 10 AM and 8 PM, especially inland. If you must go, stay hydrated and listen to your body before you become another rescue statistic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Air Quality
Los Penasquitos Canyon is closed due to fire. San Diego River Gorge and Three Sisters trails are closed due to heat. Check local park websites before you head out. Moderate AQI with PM2.5 means your lungs are under stress – consider an indoor workout or keeping it very mellow.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate or Die-drate
This isn't a joke. With temps this high, you need significantly more water than usual. Pre-hydrate, carry water, and re-hydrate. Seriously.
