## Today's Sweat Session: More Like a Sweat Lodge

Seriously, it's 33 degrees Celsius (that's 91.4°F for those still in the dark ages) and "Mainly Clear" means the sun is going to beat down on you relentlessly. We just tied a record for 90-degree days, so this isn't some rogue warm front, it's a full-blown heat wave hangover. Escondido hit 112°F yesterday; Vista, 105°F. Our City Park Rangers are practically begging people to stay off the Open Space trails during the heat of the day. Listen to them. They know.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning (for your workout)
An official Heat Advisory is in effect until 8 PM tonight for inland valleys, mountains, and even some coastal areas. Heat-related illnesses are a real threat. Don't be a statistic. Your pride isn't worth passing out on a trail.
</AlertBox>

### What's Up With The Air?

That AQI of 67 is "Moderate." It's not "hazardous," but it's not "fresh mountain air" either, especially with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. We've had some minor fire activity in the county recently – a "Turret Fire" a week or so ago, and an "H Canceled Fire" popped up a few days back. Even if they're not raging, that stuff lingers. If you've got sensitive lungs, you'll feel it.

## So, Should You Even Bother Outside?

If you absolutely *must* get out, do it at the crack of dawn, before the real oven kicks on. And forget your usual scenic routes if they involve Torrey Pines; those trails are closed off for construction until at least October. If you're thinking of a run on the beach, well, unless you're way north, the Imperial Beach shoreline is still closed due to sewage. That's a whole other kind of hazard.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures: Torrey Pines is mostly a no-go
The main park road, upper parking lots, and *all Reserve trails* at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve are likely closed to vehicles and pedestrians for improvements. Stick to the South and North Beach parking lots and the Torrey Pines Extension trails if you're determined to be in that area.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration isn't optional, it's life support
If you do venture out, carry way more water than you think you need. Pre-hydrate. Post-hydrate. Bathe in it. Also, maybe leave Fido at home. Pavement and sand are scorching for their paws in this kind of heat.
</AlertBox>

Look, I love running as much as the next masochist, but today's a day to be smart. Hit the gym, find a pool, or just chill out. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
