## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy, San Diego

Look, the sky's blue and the air quality's a "moderate" 57 AQI with PM2.5 as the main culprit, which is fine for most of us. Temperature at a breezy 16.1°C (61°F) is practically perfect. So your lungs are mostly fine, but your joints? And your general safety? That's another story.

### Coastal Chaos

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Coastal Flood Warning in Effect!
Seriously, stay away from low-lying coastal areas. A Coastal Flood Warning is active from 5 AM today through Thursday. High tides are going to be 1 to 1.7 feet above normal, hitting up to 8 feet in some spots. Roads will close, properties will be inundated, and you absolutely *should not* try to run or drive through that water. Six inches of moving water can knock you down, a foot can sweep your car away. It’s not worth it.
</AlertBox>

### Mission Bay Mayhem

If you thought you'd escape the coastal flooding by heading inland to Mission Bay, you're only half-right. You might avoid the flood, but you'll run head-first into thousands of other runners at Crown Point Park. The Crown Point Marathon and associated races are taking over the paved bike paths all morning. Unless you're actually *in* the race, find somewhere else. Those "100% Pavement Bike Path" signs are talking about the *race* course, not your personal training ground today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Crown Point Park is a Race Zone Today
Avoid Crown Point Park and its surrounding bike paths on Mission Bay this morning. A major running event is underway, making it impossible for casual workouts. Plan to hit up some less popular spots or reschedule that long run.
</AlertBox>
