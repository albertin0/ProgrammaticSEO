### Trail Talk: Where Not To Go Today

Seriously, some of our favorite spots are a mess.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Torrey Pines is Basically Closed for Business
Starting *today*, the road into Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve is closed to vehicles, and the Lodge and Kiosk are shut. Then, starting *tomorrow*, September 22, the *entire reserve* – all trails, the Beach Trail Staircase, and both upper parking lots – will be completely closed to *everyone* (pedestrians, bikes, cars) for construction until March 2027. Find another scenic vista, folks. This is a long haul.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Tecolote Canyon's Still a Construction Zone
Don't even think about a full run through Tecolote Canyon right now. Major sections of the hiking trail, specifically from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue, are closed 24/7 until summer 2027 due to sewer improvement construction. And yeah, all trails within the golf course are off-limits too. Stick to established, open paths, or you'll be dodging heavy machinery.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Your Best Bet for Today: Early Morning Coastal Cool Down or Indoors
If you *must* get outside, hit the coast **early** while any marine layer is still lingering and before the sun really starts blasting, especially if that 91-degree reading is what you're seeing. Otherwise, your best bet is an air-conditioned gym. Drink way more water than you think you need, especially if you're dealing with that extreme heat. Forget running long distances. Stick to short, easy efforts.
</AlertBox>
