## Today's Vibe Check: Run Early, Stay Coastal, or Embrace the Treadmill

Look, I love our trails as much as the next idiot who signed up for a full marathon, but today requires some strategic thinking. The immediate coast is sitting pretty at a comfortable 18°C (around 64°F) and partly cloudy. Great for a run along the Embarcadero or a quick loop in Balboa Park.

However, once you leave that sweet, sweet marine layer, things heat up fast. Inland valleys, like those around Mission Trails Regional Park, are already getting toasty, and by Wednesday and Thursday, we're talking about temperatures in the upper 80s to low 90s for those areas. And if you're even *thinking* about heading to the desert, forget it. An extreme heat warning kicks in tomorrow through Thursday with temps up to a scorching 117°F. Seriously, don't be that person.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning for Deserts!** From Tuesday through Thursday, San Diego County deserts are under an extreme heat warning with "dangerously hot conditions" expected. Reschedule any strenuous desert activities to *never*. Seriously, it's not worth it.
</AlertBox>

Air quality is sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. Given the uncontained wildfires in the county, keep an eye on how the air actually *feels* and smells, especially as the day progresses and the marine layer potentially thins out. If you're sensitive, or if you're pushing hard, this could be an issue.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Smoke & Moderate AQI:** With local wildfires active and a moderate AQI from PM2.5, pay attention to any smoke smell or haze. If you have respiratory issues, maybe keep your speed work indoors today.
</AlertBox>

For those of you sticking to your routine, consider shifting your outdoor runs to early morning or later in the evening when temperatures are cooler. And for the love of all that is holy, hydrate. Seriously, carry more water than you think you need.

There are some Caltrans freeway connector closures planned for tonight (westbound SR-54 to southbound I-805, and tomorrow night westbound I-8 to southbound I-5), but those are late-night affairs and shouldn't impact your daytime trail runs. Just be mindful if you're on the roads during those times.
