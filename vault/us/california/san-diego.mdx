## Today's Vibe Check: Heat, Heat, and More Heat

Look, I love pounding the pavement and hitting the dirt as much as the next San Diego runner, but 33 degrees Celsius is no joke. The weather forecast for yesterday mentioned a slight cool-down, but today's actual temp is a stark reminder that we're still deep in August's oven. That kind of heat saps your energy, strains your cardiovascular system, and makes every step feel like you're running through quicksand. Dehydration sets in faster than you can say "electrolyte tabs," and that's when you start risking heat exhaustion or, worse, heatstroke.

### Trail Closures: Listen Up!

The closures for places like Three Sisters and the San Diego River Gorge aren't just for show. These trails are brutal even on a mild day, with their steepest sections hitting you on the return trip when you're already gassed and the sun is at its peak. People have needed helicopter rescues out there because they underestimated the conditions. Don't be that person. Find a different route or, better yet, find a nice air-conditioned gym.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat & Closed Trails!** Do NOT attempt closed trails like Three Sisters Falls or San Diego River Gorge. Violators could face steep fines and even jail time, not to mention putting themselves and rescue personnel at risk.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* get outside, stick to the coast. Early morning runs along Mission Beach might offer a sliver of relief, but even then, the sun gets intense fast. Consider shorter durations and lower intensity. And seriously, hydrate like it's your job.
