## Today's Workout Outlook: Embrace the Marine Layer, Evade the Party

Looks like the marine layer decided to stick around, giving us some prime, cool running weather, especially along the coast. It’s perfect for getting those miles in without feeling like you’re melting into the asphalt. But don't let the comfy temps make you complacent; there are still a few things to keep in mind.

### Air Quality & Allergens

That moderate AQI of 67, with PM2.5 in the air, means if you're sensitive, you might feel it. Same goes for the moderate pollen count. It's not an "stay inside or die" situation, but if you usually get sniffly or have mild asthma, keep your inhaler handy and maybe opt for routes away from heavy traffic or freshly mown areas.

### Fire Watch & Smoke

We've got the Thorn Fire, which as of yesterday, was 1,234 acres and 75% contained, with evacuation and road closures lifted near Boulevard. Good news there, mostly. However, a new Deer Fire was reported yesterday, July 17th, in San Diego County, burning on private land, with no containment status yet. While conditions are overcast and cooler today, wildfires can still impact air quality downwind. Keep an eye on local news for any new developments, especially if you're hitting trails further inland.

### Construction & Road Blocks

Oh, San Diego. The city that never stops building, or digging. If you’re planning on hitting up the waterfront, be aware of the ongoing **Harbor Drive Trunk Sewer Project** south of the Convention Center. Traffic is reduced to one lane, and the southbound sidewalk is closed between Park Boulevard and Beardsley Street. This is an ongoing project through mid-2027, so it's not going away soon.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Trail Construction at County Parks:** William Heise County Park's playground is closed until October 2026 for replacement, and San Dieguito County Park's tot lot is also undergoing improvements. Expect some construction noise if you're camping or visiting nearby areas.
</AlertBox>

### Pride Takes Over!

This is the big one today, folks. **San Diego Pride Weekend** is in full swing. If your workout plans involve Hillcrest or Balboa Park, you need a new plan. Seriously.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Major Road Closures & Crowds!** The San Diego Pride Parade kicks off at 10 AM, marching through Hillcrest, and the Pride Festival is happening at Marston Point in Balboa Park starting at noon. Expect massive crowds, significant road closures, and zero parking in these areas. Even the Pride 5K Run & Walk, which started this morning at University & Richmond Streets in Hillcrest, warned of "VERY LIMITED ACCESS DUE TO CONSTRUCTION" and challenging parking. Just assume everywhere from University Avenue to Balboa Park is a no-go zone for a peaceful workout.
</AlertBox>

### The Verdict: Plan Smart, Run Safe

With cool temps, it's a great day to be outside, but you've got to be smart about *where*. Avoid the Pride zones unless you're there to celebrate, not to set a PR. Head to Mission Bay Park for some flat, unblocked pavement (though check for potential lingering impacts from previous beach hazard statements, even if expired for today, always use caution around water). Or maybe explore a different neighborhood trail system that's less prone to parade detours and construction.
