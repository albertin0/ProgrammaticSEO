## The Air: Breathe Smart, Not Hard

Alright, San Diego, let's talk about the air you're huffing and puffing through. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is clocking in at 67, which is "Moderate," and the main culprit is PM2.5. Now, "moderate" sounds fine, but for anyone with existing respiratory issues – that's asthma, COPD, or even just sensitive lungs – today is not the day to push your limits on a strenuous run. AccuWeather isn't mincing words either, suggesting that for sensitive groups, this level can be "unhealthy," with fine particulate matter potentially leading to "serious health issues" from prolonged exposure. Don't be that person ending up in urgent care because you thought you could "power through" a chest tightness. You know your body; listen to it.

On top of that, we've got a moderate pollen count. So, if you're sneezing and sniffling through your warm-up, that's why. Think twice about that long trail run through Mission Trails Regional Park if you’re prone to allergies; you might just regret it halfway up Cowles Mountain.

## Smooth Moves: Great for the Joints

Now for the good news: your joints are practically throwing a party. With a pleasant 17.1°C (63°F) and partly cloudy skies, it’s not too hot, not too cold.
