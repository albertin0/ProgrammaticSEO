We had multiple new fires reported yesterday in San Diego County, including the "Smokey Fire" and "Broadway Fire," so that PM2.5 isn't a coincidence.

#### Wildfire Smoke & Heat: A Nasty Combo
Four new wildfires sprung up yesterday across San Diego County. While containment details aren't fully clear, the smoke and particulate matter they generate will directly impact our air quality, especially inland. Running in this stuff is asking for trouble, plain and simple. Combine that with the 33.0°C (91.4°F) heat, and you're dramatically increasing your risk for respiratory issues and heat-related illness. Consider indoor workouts or rescheduling.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Gusts & Park Closures
The National Weather Service has issued a Wind Advisory through Sunday, with gusts up to 65 mph in wind-prone passes, and 30-50 mph elsewhere. If you're planning a hike on, say, the trails around Mission Trails Regional Park, those exposed ridgelines are going to be brutal. Furthermore, Waterfront Park is completely shut down today and tomorrow for a special event, so don't even bother trying to get in there for your usual loop. The Cathedral Trail in River Valley is also closed.
</AlertBox>

### What to Do (or Not Do)

Seriously, do you *need* to hit your PR today? No, you don't.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Smart Choices for a Sketchy Day
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Drink more water than you think you need. Pre-hydrate. Re-hydrate. If you're thirsty, you're already behind.
*   **Indoor Options:** Hit the gym. Swim laps at a community pool. Do some yoga in your living room. There are plenty of ways to move without inhaling smoke and baking in the sun.
*   **Early Bird/Night Owl, But Cautiously:** If you absolutely must get outside, aim for the cooler, less windy parts of the day – very early morning or late evening. But even then, keep an eye on the air quality and wildfire reports.
*   **Watch Your Step:** Don't forget those charming sidewalk hazards. A local man just last year suffered serious injuries from protruding bolts at Albatross and Washington where a parking meter used to be. Keep your head up, literally.
*   **Chollas Creek Construction:** The Chollas Creek restoration project on Federal Boulevard just started yesterday. Expect ongoing construction and potential detours in that area if it's part of your usual route.
</AlertBox>
