## The Rundown: Breathe Easy (Mostly) and Pick Your Path Wisely

Let's cut to the chase: that "Clear" weather report at 21.2°C (around 70°F) near the coast sounds inviting, and it is, but don't let it fool you entirely. The air quality index is sitting at 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While the Dehesa 7 Fire, which burned 93 acres, is now 75% contained and not actively spewing smoke over the county, that moderate AQI is something to keep an eye on. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you might feel a little extra burn in your lungs today. Same goes for those of us battling moderate pollen levels.

### Heat Still a Factor for Trail Runners

Here's the kicker for my fellow trail enthusiasts: the National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect until Sunday, August 23rd, for dangerously hot conditions with temperatures up to 113°F in inland areas like Gorgonio Pass. This isn't just a suggestion to drink more water; it's why several popular county parks and trails are *closed* for the entire month of August. Think El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, Mt. Gower Preserve, and the notoriously difficult San Diego River Gorge and Three Sisters trails. If your plan was to bag some serious elevation out east, you're out of luck and probably better off. These closures are for your own safety after a string of heat-related rescues.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Inland Trail Closures!** Do NOT attempt to run trails like the San Diego River Gorge or Three Sisters. They are officially closed through August due to extreme heat and past rescue incidents. Respect the closures and stay safe.
</AlertBox>

For those sticking closer to the urban sprawl, Balboa Park offers some great shaded routes, and the Bayshore Bikeway is always a solid choice for a flatter, coastal run. Just be aware of a few localized events. Today, August 21st, the North Park Farmers Market is active, which means some minor road closures around North Park Way between Granada and Ray Street. No big deal, just be mindful of foot traffic if your route takes you through there. Looking ahead, don't forget that "Bike The Bay 2026" is scheduled for Sunday, August 23rd, so expect significant closures around the San Diego Bay, including the Coronado Bay Bridge.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Localized Traffic:** Farmers markets are happening! Expect minor detours and increased foot traffic if your route goes through areas like North Park (North Park Way between Granada and Ray Street).
</AlertBox>
