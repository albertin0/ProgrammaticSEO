## San Diego Sizzles (and Floods, and Closes Trails)

It’s August 28, 2026, and San Diego is a hot mess, literally. The National Weather Service has an **Extreme Heat Warning** in effect for the entire county until 8 PM tonight. We're talking dangerously hot conditions, with temperatures pushing into the low 90s along the coast and well over 100 degrees in the mountains and deserts. This isn't just about feeling sweaty; heat-related illnesses spike significantly during these events.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for all of San Diego County until 8 PM PDT. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during peak heat hours. Hydrate constantly. Seriously, don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

On top of baking, some parts of our county are drowning. There's a **Flash Flood Warning** for Eastern San Diego County until 4:15 PM PDT. This means creeks and streams could become raging rivers, and roads might turn into swift-moving channels. If you're anywhere near areas like Borrego Springs or Mount Laguna, move to higher ground. This ain't a sprinkle, folks, it's life-threatening.

## Trail Trouble

Adding to the misery, many popular spots are already shuttered. The Cleveland National Forest has closed the **San Diego River Gorge** and **Three Sisters hiking areas** for the entire month of August due to past heat-related rescues. County parks like El Capitan Preserve and Hellhole Canyon Preserve are also closed for the month to keep people safe.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Significant Trail Closures
Several major trails, including San Diego River Gorge and Three Sisters in the Cleveland National Forest, and county preserves like El Capitan, are *closed for the month of August* due to extreme heat. Do not attempt to access them.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget the never-ending construction. If you were hoping for a peaceful run or bike ride through **Tecolote Canyon**, think again. The hiking trail from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue is completely closed 24/7 until summer of *next year*, and all trails within the golf course are also shut down due to sewer improvement and water connection projects. Even Mission Trails Regional Park has ongoing pipeline work, with the Father Junipero Serra Trail closed to vehicles through late 2028 and potential pedestrian impacts.
