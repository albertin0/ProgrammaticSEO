<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning is REAL.
Seriously, folks, this isn't a drill. The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for our inland valleys. Temperatures can easily hit dangerous levels, leading to heat stroke, exhaustion, and worse. If you're out there between 10 AM and 6 PM, you're actively looking for trouble. Coastal breezes offer some relief, but even then, it's not enough to justify a hard effort.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Air Quality: Mind Your Lungs
An AQI of 85 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant isn't terrible for everyone, but if you've got asthma, COPD, or just generally enjoy breathing, take it easy. AccuWeather's "Poor" rating for sensitive groups means you should consider moving your workout indoors today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Gym or Treadmill: Your Best Friends
Given the closures and the overall gnarly conditions, this is a prime day for the gym. Treadmill, spin bike, weights – get it done indoors. If you *must* be outside, keep it extremely short, stick to the shaded, flat routes early in the morning, and for the love of all that is holy, hydrate like your life depends on it. Because today, it just might. And don't even think about hiking El Capitan Preserve; it's closed for a reason.
</AlertBox>
