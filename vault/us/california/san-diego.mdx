<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning!
An Extreme Heat Warning is active across San Diego County through Sunday evening. Temperatures will be dangerously high. Avoid outdoor activity, especially during peak hours (10 AM - 6 PM). This heat can cause rapid dehydration, heat exhaustion, and heatstroke. Don't be a hero; stay cool, stay hydrated, and stay inside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Coastal Hazards!
If you were planning on a beach run or swim, be aware of the coastal flood advisory and high surf. Strong currents and dangerous conditions are expected. It's not the day to "shoot the pier" or try to outrun a rogue wave at Ocean Beach.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Stay Safe Tips
If you absolutely *must* be outside, city park rangers are practically begging you to take precautions. Schedule your outdoor time for the absolute coolest parts of the day – we're talking pre-sunrise or well after sunset, and even then, be smart. Hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, leave your pets at home. Hot pavement and extreme exertion are deadly for them. Check on your neighbors, especially the elderly. There's always tomorrow for that PR, but there might not be if you push it today.
</AlertBox>
