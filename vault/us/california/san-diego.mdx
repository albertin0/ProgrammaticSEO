<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Heatstroke Is No Joke – Dial Back the Intensity**
With temperatures pushing into the 90s, especially further away from the immediate coast, heat-related illness is a very real threat. Your body's ability to cool itself is severely compromised. If you’re planning a run up Cowles Mountain at Mission Trails Regional Park, seriously reconsider or go *extremely* early. Even early morning won't completely escape the accumulated heat. Hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after* any activity. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and if you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or get muscle cramps, stop immediately, find shade, and cool down. Don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Watch Out for Wildfire Smoke**
While some major fires like the Mateo Fire are contained, we still have other uncontained wildfires in the county contributing to that moderate PM2.5 reading. PM2.5 particles are small enough to get deep into your lungs, and even if you don't smell smoke, they can still cause respiratory irritation. If you have asthma or other lung conditions, today might be better spent indoors with a good HEPA filter. The San Diego County Air Pollution Control District's general forecast for "GOOD" air quality might not be fully accounting for localized smoke impacts from these newer, uncontained blazes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **General Workout Wisdom for a Toasty Day**
If you absolutely *must* get out there, aim for before 8 AM or after 7 PM, but even then, the ground will be radiating heat, and the air won't be much cooler. Listen to your body – it's telling you to chill out. Consider swimming, indoor cycling, or anything air-conditioned instead. Your joints will thank you for not pounding them on boiling asphalt, and your lungs will appreciate not filtering extra particulate matter.
</AlertBox>
