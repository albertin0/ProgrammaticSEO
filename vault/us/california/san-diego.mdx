## Today's Outdoor Rundown: Stay Indoors, Seriously

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego. The sun calls to us like a siren. But today, that siren is wailing an emergency. The mercury is rising faster than your heart rate on the Torrey Pines grade, and frankly, it's dangerous out there for any sustained activity. We're staring down the barrel of an intense heatwave across Southern California, with an Extreme Heat Watch in effect today for interior areas. By Wednesday, inland San Diego County valleys and mountains will be under an Extreme Heat Warning, with temperatures projected to hit the high 90s and easily creep into the 100s. Your 91.4°F (33.0°C) reading might sound manageable, but that's likely closer to the coast; head even a few miles east, and it's a whole different ballgame. Expect places like Santee to hit nearly 98°F tomorrow.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning Incoming!
Daytime outdoor recreation is strongly discouraged. You're risking heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Don't be a hero; nobody wants to see you passed out on the side of the road on your way to Mission Trails Regional Park.
</AlertBox>

On top of the sweltering temperatures, your lungs aren't getting a break either. We've got moderate pollen levels mingling with a moderate Air Quality Index of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While there are no active major wildfires *directly* impacting San Diego's air quality with thick smoke today, California is in a busy wildfire season, and this combination with high heat makes for a tougher breathing environment.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Lungs Under Pressure: Moderate AQI & Pollen
The combination of moderate air quality and pollen can trigger respiratory issues, especially when coupled with strenuous activity in the heat. Take it easy or stay inside.
</AlertBox>

And for those craving a coastal cool-down, be warned. Persistent cross-border sewage pollution from Tijuana is causing ongoing beach closures and advisories, particularly in Imperial Beach and surrounding areas. YMCA Camp Surf is literally busing kids to Mission Beach to avoid the contaminated water. So, that ocean breeze might come with an unseen, unsavory guest.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Best Bet: Embrace the Indoors
If you *must* move, stick to air-conditioned spaces or get your workout in before the sun truly commits to scorching the earth (think pre-6 AM). Hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, don't leave pets or people in parked cars. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

A few minor construction alerts are floating around, including some long-term road closures in Fallbrook for new community development, and some county parks have playground renovations underway. However, nothing major is actively blocking primary running trails today. The biggest hazard by far is simply stepping outside.
