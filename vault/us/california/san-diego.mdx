## The "Heat Dome" (or whatever you want to call it) is Real
Forget what the calendar says; summer's decided to arrive with a vengeance today. With temperatures hitting a sweltering 33.0°C (that's 91.4°F for those of you still on Fahrenheit), hitting the pavement or the trails at peak hours is just plain foolish. Heat-related illnesses aren't a joke, and your body will thank you for taking it easy or moving your sweat session indoors. Even a brisk walk can turn dangerous quickly in this kind of heat. If you absolutely must be outside, go before the sun really starts cooking or after it dips, and carry more water than you think you need.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** 91.4°F is dangerously hot for prolonged outdoor activity. Heat exhaustion and heatstroke are serious risks. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and seek shade immediately if you feel unwell.
</AlertBox>

## Wildfires & Air Quality: A Smoky Combination
Just when you thought the heat was enough, we've got active wildfires making an appearance in San Diego County. Both the Fairview 2 Fire and the Moro Fire were reported just yesterday or today. While the Tusil Fire is 100% contained, "full containment doesn't mean the fire is completely out," so smoke could still be lingering in certain areas.

Our Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 67, with PM2.5 being the primary pollutant. For most folks, that's "acceptable," but if you've got any respiratory issues – asthma, allergies (which are moderate today anyway), or just sensitive lungs – you should probably limit your time outside. PM2.5 particles are tiny devils that can dig deep into your lungs and even hit your bloodstream. With active fires, that 67 could climb rapidly depending on wind shifts.

## Trail Talk: Some Closures, Mostly Open
If you're thinking of hitting some dirt, be aware that not all trails are open. For instance, specific sections within **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park**, like Sherwood, Vireo, and Cathedral Trails, are currently closed. Always check the county parks website before heading out to avoid a wasted trip. For popular spots like Mission Trails Regional Park, the Father Junipero Serra Trail is still good to go, but remember that heat applies everywhere.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** If you're running outside today, try to stick to shaded routes or areas with good air circulation, and definitely keep an eye on those wildfire updates. Balboa Park might offer some tree cover, but the heat will still be brutal. Consider the treadmill or a pool day instead.
</AlertBox>
