## Your Body Isn't a Toaster Oven

Let's be blunt: 91 degrees Fahrenheit in San Diego is *hot*. Even if you're down by the coast, that marine layer isn't doing as much heavy lifting as usual. Inland, you're looking at seriously oppressive conditions. The County of San Diego defines an "extreme heat day" at 93.1°F, and we're dancing on that line. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on fast. Your core temperature rises, your body struggles to cool itself, and suddenly, you're in trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Risk Today
Avoid strenuous outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 4 PM. Seek shade, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get immediate medical help. The County's Cool Zones are open for a reason.
</AlertBox>

## Breathing Lessons: Smoke and Allergy Edition

The AQI might be "moderate" at 59 for PM2.5, but don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. We've still got the Border 6 Fire burning near Dulzura and Marron Valley. While some evacuation warnings have been lifted, a smoke advisory is still in effect for folks around Tecate and Campo. Depending on wind shifts, that smoke plume could affect areas further west. If you can smell smoke, you're inhaling particulates that aren't doing your lungs any favors, especially when you're breathing hard.
And on top of that, pollen levels are moderate. So, you might be dealing with a smoky, dusty, allergy-triggering cocktail in the air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Smoke Advisory in East County
If you're planning a run or hike anywhere near the eastern reaches of the county, like the trails around Otay Lakes, be aware of potential smoke exposure. If you're sensitive or have respiratory issues, maybe opt for an indoor workout today. Keep windows closed if you're in an affected area.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk & Roadblocks

Good news for some: the Cox Communications construction on El Tordo, Avenida De Acacias, and Via De Santa Fe is scheduled to wrap up by 4 PM today. So if those were your usual routes, they might open up later. However, general construction on W ELDER ST between S MISSION RD and S PICO AV continues until mid-June.

If you were eyeing a long run through Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, remember that the Cathedral Trail is still closed. And honestly, with the smoke advisory for nearby Tecate and Campo, hitting those particular trails today might not be the smartest move. Stick to paths where you know the air is clearer and you have access to water.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
With these temperatures, you need more water than you think. Carry extra. Plan routes with water stops, or stash bottles along your path if you're doing an out-and-back. And no, that post-run beer isn't hydrating you.
</AlertBox>
