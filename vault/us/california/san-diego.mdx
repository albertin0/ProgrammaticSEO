We're talking widespread moderate to extreme heat risk across the valleys, mountains, and even creeping into the coastal areas. This isn't just about feeling a bit sweaty; we're in "heat illness increases significantly" territory.

Public health officials are telling everyone to drink plenty of fluids and stay out of the sun during the hottest parts of the day for a reason.
