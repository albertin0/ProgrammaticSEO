Pair that with a "Moderate" pollen level, and anyone prone to seasonal allergies should probably preemptively dose up before heading out. So, get out there now while you can actually enjoy the full loop without dodging heavy machinery. You know how quickly those "temporary" closures can turn into an eternity.
