Let's not sugarcoat it: San Diego is hot today. That 33.0°C isn't just a number; it's a serious hazard if you're planning on pounding the pavement or hitting the trails without proper precautions. You might think "mainly clear" means a perfect day, but that high temperature combined with an AQI of 67 (thanks, PM2.5) and moderate pollen means your body is already working overtime before you even start.

<AlertBox type="danger">

Forget your PRs for today, folks. We're under an Extreme Heat Warning for the San Diego County mountains, with temperatures possibly hitting 104°F. Even at the coast, 91°F is no joke. More alarmingly, a tropical storm warning has been issued for the entire county, with rain and winds set to intensify significantly by Saturday. Catastrophic flooding and falling trees are on the table for the weekend. Do NOT get caught far from shelter.

Seriously, that tropical storm isn't playing around. While today might still feel muggy and hot, the forecast for Saturday and Sunday is grim. You do *not* want to be miles into a run up Cowles Mountain when the skies open up and the winds start howling. Stay home, get your gear ready, and make sure your patio furniture is secured.

## Trail Updates: Some Closed, Others Just Asking for Trouble

If you were thinking of a jog through Tecolote Canyon, forget it. Sections of the hiking trail from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue, and all trails within the golf course, are completely shut down 24/7 until summer 2027 for sewer improvement construction. Don't try to sneak through; it's for your safety (and probably the sanity of the construction crew).

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Tecolote Canyon Trail Closures
Avoid Tecolote Canyon altogether. Extensive sections of the trail system are closed for construction until Summer 2027. Save yourself the detour and find another route.
</AlertBox>

Over at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve, expect ongoing disruptions. Phase 2 of their major construction project, which includes utility upgrades and pathway repaving, is scheduled for August or September 2026. While the trails might be technically open in some areas, be prepared for possible closures or construction noise. It's probably best to check their official site before you head out, or just save Torrey Pines for when they're done tearing things up.

For those looking further afield this weekend, keep in mind the Noble Canyon 50K/15K Trail Races are happening tomorrow, September 5th, in the Cleveland National Forest. That means the backcountry trails out there will be bustling with race traffic, so plan accordingly if you were hoping for a quiet long run.
