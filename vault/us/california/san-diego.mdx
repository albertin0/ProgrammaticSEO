With RealFeel temperatures pushing 90°F, heat-related illnesses are a serious threat. Stick to early mornings or late evenings if you must be outside. Otherwise, the gym or an indoor pool is your best friend today. Hydrate like it's your job – and then hydrate some more.
</AlertBox>
