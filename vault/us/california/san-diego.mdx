## Local Hazards & Tips:

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Heat Advisory: Hydration is Non-Negotiable**
With temperatures hitting 33.0°C (91.4°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, the sun is going to beat down hard. If you absolutely MUST get outside, go before 8 AM or after 7 PM. Carry more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. This isn't a game.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Tijuana River Valley Pollution Still a Problem**
While the general AQI might be moderate, if you're anywhere near the Tijuana River Valley, be aware that sewage pipeline collapses caused significant hydrogen sulfide (H2S) spikes and air quality alerts recently. County officials even warned residents to limit outdoor time. The Cathedral Trail in the River Valley is already closed. Don't risk your respiratory health for a run through what smells like rotten eggs. Seriously, just don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Trail Traffic & Construction Ahead**
It's National Trails Day, so expect heavier foot traffic on many of our county trails, especially at places like Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve where a specific hike is planned today. Also, if you're usually navigating the UC San Diego campus area, ongoing construction projects, like the Triton Center work, might impact your usual routes. Check local park and campus notices before you head out, and be extra courteous to fellow trail users.
</AlertBox>
