Look, I love running these hills as much as the next masochist, but that sun is no joke out there today. That 33.0°C temperature isn't just warm, it's straight-up dangerous for sustained activity, especially if you’re not already acclimated to working out in a blast furnace. Your body is going to be working overtime just to keep cool, leaving less in the tank for actual performance and dramatically increasing your risk of heat exhaustion or worse.

And speaking of danger, if you had plans to hit up some of our more challenging spots like the San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters Falls, or even El Capitan Preserve, you can kiss those goodbye. Or rather, don't, because those trails and several other backcountry preserves are officially **closed for the entire month of August** due to the ongoing extreme heat and a concerning number of heat-related rescues. Seriously, federal and county officials aren't messing around; you could face hefty fines or even jail time for violating these closures. They're closed because people were literally collapsing out there, turning a scenic hike into an emergency operation. Stick to the paved paths in more open, shaded areas if you insist on being outside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Danger: Extreme Heat & Closed Trails**
With temperatures pushing 33.0°C (91.4°F), heat illness is a severe risk. Furthermore, popular backcountry trails including San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters Falls, Cedar Creek Falls, El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, and Mt. Gower Preserve are **closed for the entire month of August** due to extreme heat and numerous rescues. Do NOT attempt to use these trails. Violating closures can result in significant fines and legal penalties.
</AlertBox>

On top of the sweltering heat, the Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While the recent Church 2 Fire near Church Road was quickly contained yesterday and all evacuations lifted, any lingering particulate matter isn't going to make your lungs happy. Combine that with a moderate pollen count, and you've got a recipe for irritated airways, especially if you're prone to allergies or asthma.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Warning: Moderate AQI & Pollen**
AQI is Moderate (67, PM2.5). This, combined with moderate pollen levels, can exacerbate respiratory issues and allergies. Monitor your symptoms and consider an indoor alternative if you're sensitive.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* get out there, pick a flat, shaded route, go early or late, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and stay hydrated beyond what you think you need. Or, you know, there’s always the gym. Air conditioning is a beautiful thing.
