## The Local Lowdown: Don't Melt on Torrey Pines

Alright, folks, that heat is no joke, especially if you're planning on hitting the trails at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve. Those exposed trails, like the Beach Trail or even the easy Guy Fleming loop, can feel a lot hotter when the sun's glaring down and there's not a cloud in sight. Remember, those trails are generally closed after rain to prevent erosion, but today it's all about avoiding heatstroke.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Hazard Ahead:** With temperatures hitting 33.0°C, direct sun exposure on trails, especially those without much shade, is a real concern. Plan your workout for early morning or late evening. This isn't March's "extreme heat warning" that had firefighters on high alert, but it's still plenty hot enough to cause problems if you're not smart about it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Not Optional:** You're going to sweat. A lot. Carry more water than you think you need, and don't wait until you're parched to start drinking. Seriously, nobody wants to be *that* person getting rescued off the trail.
</AlertBox>
