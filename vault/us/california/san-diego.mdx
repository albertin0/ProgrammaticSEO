Forget a pleasant spring jog, San Diego. Today is less "runner's high" and more "heatstroke roulette" if you're not careful.

## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>**Scorching Temps:** The mercury is hitting 33°C (91°F) out there. That's dangerously hot, folks, and an open invitation for heat exhaustion. The sun doesn't mess around, even in April.</li>
  <li>**Gusty Winds, Especially Inland:** If the heat doesn't get you, the wind might. A Wind Advisory is still in effect for the mountains and deserts until tomorrow night, with gusts up to 55 mph. Even along the coast and in the valleys, expect persistent breezes from 15 to 30 mph. That's not a refreshing breeze; that's a headwind trying to push you into traffic or off a scenic overlook.</li>
  <li>**Moderate Air Quality & Pollen Combo:** AQI is clocking in at 66 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Mix that with moderate pollen, and your lungs will feel like they're running a gauntlet, even if your legs are doing all the work.</li>
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Risk
  With temperatures pushing into the 90s, the risk of heat-related illness is HIGH. This isn't a drill. Stay indoors, or at the very least, save your workout for before 7 AM or after 7 PM. Hydration isn't an option; it's mandatory. This heat historically elevates wildfire risk, though no *active* smoke advisories are in place today, the dry conditions are always a concern.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Watch Your Step (and Your Hat)
  Those strong westerly winds (25-35 mph, gusts to 55 mph in higher elevations) are no joke. If you're out on exposed trails, say, up around **Mission Trails Regional Park**, you'll be fighting the wind every step of the way. Loose debris, falling branches, and generally poor balance conditions are a real possibility. Consider a treadmill day instead of challenging Mother Nature.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Local Construction Alert: Camino San Diego Road
  Heads up if your route takes you near the University of San Diego campus. Road work to install a storm drain on Camino San Diego Road, near the Belanich Engineering Center, officially kicks off today, April 1st, and runs through May 1st. Expect traffic pattern changes and limited parking in Lot S12. This isn't a trail closure, but it's a headache if you're trying to get around that area on foot or bike. Best to find an alternate route to avoid the mess.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Ongoing Trail Closures
  Remember that some trails in **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park** are still closed due to past storm damage and ongoing repairs, including Sherwood, Vireo (west of Hollister St.), and Cathedral Trail. Don't be that person ignoring signs; it's for your safety (and to keep the park rangers from having to rescue you).
</AlertBox>
