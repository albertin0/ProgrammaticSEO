## Trail Trouble & Coastal Chaos

Here's the real kick in the pants for us local runners:

<AlertBox type="info">
### Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve Trails are CLOSED!
For those of you who planned on hitting the iconic trails at Torrey Pines, tough luck. Major sections of the Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve, including the hiking trails, are closed for extensive utility and accessibility upgrades. This closure started on September 21st and isn't expected to reopen until March 2027. You *can* still hit Torrey Pines State Beach if you park at the North or South Beach lots, but the cliffs and main reserve trails are off-limits. Plan an alternate route, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Hurricane Swells Mean Dangerous Beaches
Don't even think about a blissful beach run or a dip in the ocean. Hurricane Polo, a Category 5 beast off the coast of Mexico, is sending massive swells our way. While the peak isn't until Monday and Tuesday, expect dangerous ocean conditions, including high tides, rip currents, and potential coastal flooding today, especially on south-facing beaches. Stick to the boardwalk if you absolutely must be near the sand, and for the love of all that is holy, stay out of the water. Lifeguards are not your personal Uber.
</AlertBox>

## Heat Advisory: It's a Scorcher

The mercury is hitting 91.4°F (33.0°C) and it's "Mainly Clear" out there. This isn't just a warm day; it's genuinely hot for an intense workout. We've seen plenty of extreme heat warnings in San Diego County this month, with temps often hitting the mid-90s to over 100°F in various areas. Today's temperature is right on the edge of what's considered risky.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Push It in This Heat
Even if you're a seasoned desert runner, 91.4°F is no joke. The risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke is significantly elevated. If you choose to go out, stick to early mornings or late evenings, hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after*, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and for crying out loud, slow your pace way down. This isn't the day for PRs unless your PR is "avoiding an ambulance ride."
</AlertBox>

In short, today is a day for caution, adaptation, and maybe just hitting the gym or doing some gentle yoga in your air-conditioned living room. Save the epic outdoor adventures for a day when the elements aren't actively trying to take you down.
