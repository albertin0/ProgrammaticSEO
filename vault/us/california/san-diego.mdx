## The Breakdown for Your Body

Your lungs are getting a moderate break today with an AQI of 60, but PM2.5 is still the main culprit, likely due to a bit of localized brush fire smoke or general urban haze. Add moderate pollen to the mix, and it's not exactly crisp mountain air, even if the actual mountains were open.

But the real joint-killer and general body-destroyer is the heat. That 33°C is no joke. The National Weather Service had heat advisories and warnings out yesterday, and while they might be "expiring," that doesn't magically cool the air. It just means they're not legally obligated to tell you what common sense already should.

### Hazardous Conditions & What Not To Do

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DO NOT ATTEMPT TO HIKE CLOSED TRAILS!** The San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters, El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon, and Mt. Gower Preserve are all closed for August due to extreme heat and *actual heat-related rescues*. Ignoring these closures puts you and emergency responders at serious risk. Don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Stress & Rhabdomyolysis Risk:** Sustained high temperatures significantly increase your risk for heat exhaustion, heatstroke, and even exertional rhabdomyolysis. This is where your muscles break down and can seriously mess with your kidneys. If you insist on working out outside, keep it short, keep it light, and for the love of all that is holy, stay hydrated.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Thunderstorm Watch:** If you're anywhere near the county mountains or deserts, be aware of afternoon and evening thunderstorms through Wednesday. Heavy rain can lead to flash flooding in canyons and washes, and strong winds can knock down limbs. Don't get caught out there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Coastal Flood Advisory:** High tides between 8-9:30 p.m. this evening could cause minor flooding in low-lying beach areas and parking lots. Your planned sunset beach run might involve more puddle-jumping than expected.
</AlertBox>

### Pro-Tips (Because I know you'll ignore me anyway)

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** Drink water before, during, and after your workout. And I mean *water*, not that sugary energy drink crap. If you're sweating buckets, add some electrolytes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Timing is Everything (or close to it):** If you absolutely *must* run outside, aim for first thing in the morning before the sun really starts baking. The weather will be slightly cooler and clearer.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Listen to Your Body (for once):** If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or lightheaded, STOP. Immediately. Get into the shade, drink water, and cool down. Pushing through "the wall" when it's this hot is just stupid.
</AlertBox>
