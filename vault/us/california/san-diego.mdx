## Workout Hazards for April 25, 2026

If you were planning on hitting those exposed trails along Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve today, or even just doing a long run down the Coast Highway, think again. The National Weather Service San Diego has issued a wind advisory for San Diego County Deserts and San Gorgonio Pass, with west winds between 25 to 35 mph and gusts potentially hitting 50 to 60 mph through the passes, valid through Sunday morning. While the advisory specifically calls out the desert and pass areas, those powerful gusts can still create blustery, unsafe conditions throughout the region, especially in exposed coastal sections. Falling debris and difficulty maintaining balance, particularly for cyclists or those on uneven terrain, are real concerns.

Combine that wind with a scorching 33.0°C (85.4°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, and you're looking at a serious risk of heat stress. Just because we're San Diegans doesn't mean we're immune to dehydration or heat exhaustion. Remember how the County closes backcountry parks like El Capitan and Hellhole Canyon preserves during peak summer heat? While it's not August yet, 85 degrees in April is no joke, and those same heat safety principles apply.
