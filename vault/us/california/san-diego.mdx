## Why today is *not* the day (unless you're a glutton for punishment):

<BulletList>
*   Temperatures are hitting a scorching 33°C (91.4°F) right now. That's not just "warm," folks; that's dangerously hot, especially for March. We're already feeling the start of that "record-breaking March heat" the forecast has been buzzing about for later in the week.
*   The Air Quality Index (AQI) is hanging around 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, putting us squarely in the "Moderate" category. Toss in that lovely moderate pollen count, and your lungs are going to be working overtime, whether you feel it immediately or not.
*   Still got those ongoing trail headaches. If you're planning a run near the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, remember parts of Sherwood, Vireo, and Cathedral Trails are still closed for repairs. Don't waste your time showing up only to turn around.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is a Real Thing, San Diego

Seriously, 33°C is no joke. Running or intense exercise in this kind of heat, especially when your body isn't fully acclimated, is a fast track to heat exhaustion or worse. Even if you're usually a beast, today's the day to take it indoors, hit the pool, or just chill out. Your pride isn't worth a trip to urgent care.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen & Particulates: A One-Two Punch for Your Lungs

| Pollutant | Level      | Notes                                    |
| :-------- | :--------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| PM2.5     | Moderate   | Dominant pollutant, can irritate lungs.  |
| Pollen    | Moderate   | Seasonal allergies, respiratory stress.  |

The air isn't doing you any favors with this combination. Even if you don't typically suffer from allergies, adding fine particulate matter to the mix when you're breathing hard can make for a miserable experience and potentially impact your recovery. The San Diego Air Pollution Control District's forecasts can shift, and today's moderate AQI is a step down from what was predicted for Monday.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration, Hydration, Hydration

If you absolutely *must* get outside, carry way more water than you think you need. Pre-hydrate. Re-hydrate. Over-hydrate. And consider cutting your planned distance or intensity drastically. Dawn patrol is your only real friend right now before the sun fully bakes us. Also, check the San Diego County Parks and Recreation website for specific trail and park closures before you head out.
</AlertBox>
