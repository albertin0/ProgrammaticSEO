Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next masochist, but today's a hard pass for anything strenuous outside. That 33.0°C temperature isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous, especially if you're out there during peak sun. The inland valleys and desert regions are under an *Excessive Heat Warning* because it's going to be north of 100°F. Pushing it in this kind of heat is how you end up with heat stroke, not a new Strava segment record.

Then there's the air. An AQI of 67 is "Moderate," meaning sensitive folks might already feel it, and anyone exercising will be inhaling more particulate matter (PM2.5) than usual. While the marine layer might be helping clear some smoke closer to the coast, remember that yesterday saw a smoke advisory for areas further east, lingering from the Mateo Fire. Combine that with moderate pollen levels, primarily from grass, and your respiratory system is basically running a gauntlet. You're going to feel it in your chest, your sinuses, and probably your mood.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is No Joke
With temperatures soaring and an Excessive Heat Warning inland, severe dehydration and heatstroke are real threats. If you must be outside, go before 8 AM or after 7 PM, hydrate constantly, and keep it light. Seriously, your brain doesn't like being cooked.
</AlertBox>

If you're still determined to get moving outdoors, stick to shaded routes, carry more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. That "moderate" pollen count with high grass pollen means your allergies are likely going to be in full swing, potentially impacting your breathing even if you don't typically struggle.

Also, a heads up if you usually run around Tijuana River Valley Regional Park: the Cathedral Trail is currently closed for repairs. Expect some noise and dust in that area if you're trying to skirt around it.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind Your Lungs
AQI 67 (Moderate, PM2.5) means increased exposure to pollutants during exercise. Sensitive groups should really consider indoor options. For everyone else, keep intense outdoor activity short, or consider a lighter workout. Particle pollution and ozone typically worsen in the late afternoon, so if you're going out, make it early.
</AlertBox>
