Today is not the day for heroics, San Diego. The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning until Thursday evening, and Cal/OSHA is reminding everyone about the dangers of this kind of heat. We're talking seriously dangerous conditions, especially if you're trying to log those miles during peak sun hours. City Park Rangers are already telling people to bail on Open Space trails during this heat and stick to early mornings or evenings if they *must* go out, and for Pete's sake, leave your furry running buddy at home.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!** Temperatures are soaring into the triple digits inland, with coastal areas not far behind. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these events. Hydrate constantly, seek shade, and consider indoor alternatives. Do NOT leave children or pets in unattended vehicles – seriously, don't.
</AlertBox>

If you were planning a long run through **Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve**, be aware there's a "Nature Unplugged Hike-A-Thon" today. Even they've adjusted their start times to avoid the worst of the heat, which should tell you something. Expect more people than usual, and combine that with the heat, it's a recipe for exhaustion.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Gusty Santa Ana Winds!** A Wind Advisory is active for the mountains and foothills, bringing strong easterly winds. These can make running miserable, especially if you're heading into a headwind, and also significantly heighten wildfire danger.
</AlertBox>

And for those who love the scenic trails at **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve**, remember that the Lodge and its hiking trails are still closed for extensive construction and won't be fully reopened until early 2027. So, even if the heat wasn't an issue, your usual route might be a no-go anyway. Best to check county parks websites before heading out, as other playgrounds and facilities are also undergoing maintenance.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep an Eye on the Air.** While not critical, an AQI of 73 with PM2.5 as the main pollutant isn't ideal. If you're sensitive, or if the winds kick up more dust, you might find yourself coughing a bit more than usual.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, just punt today. There are plenty of other days to get your sweat on without risking heatstroke or breathing in extra particulate matter. Hit the gym, do some yoga, or just chill out in the AC. Your body will thank you.
