#### Extreme Heat Warning
An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for parts of San Diego County. Temperatures could reach 109°F in the deserts. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM PDT. Stay hydrated and seek air-conditioned spaces. This isn't a suggestion; it's a safety directive.

#### Watch Out for Wildfire Smoke & Air Quality
While the AQI is "moderate" at 67, and the Harvest Fire is contained, the new Tecate 2 Fire is a developing situation. If you're near Otay Mesa or east county, keep an eye (and nose) out for smoke. Even low levels of particulate matter can irritate your lungs, especially when you're exerting yourself. The ongoing Tijuana River pollution also adds to airborne toxins, particularly for those in south San Diego communities.
</AlertBox>
