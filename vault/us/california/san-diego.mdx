## The Sweat-Fest Continues: Why You Should Stay Indoors (Mostly)

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego, home of "perfect weather." But today, October 5th, 2026, is an exception to that rule. We're currently baking under an Extreme Heat Warning that the National Weather Service issued yesterday and won't lift until Thursday evening. Temperatures are climbing into the high 90s and even hitting triple digits in places like inland valleys. Even at the coast, we're seeing temperatures well above seasonal norms.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT!
Avoid strenuous outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these events. Your body can't cool itself efficiently when it's this hot.
</AlertBox>

On top of the sweltering heat, we've got a moderate Air Quality Index (AQI) of 61. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which, combined with the heat, isn't exactly a recipe for happy lungs. While not "unhealthy," it's definitely not doing you any favors, especially if you're pushing hard. We've seen San Diego rank poorly for particle pollution in recent reports, so don't shrug this off.

### Local Hazards to Avoid

Thinking of tackling Cowles Mountain for a sunrise hike? Think again. The County has previously closed strenuous trails like the San Diego River Gorge and Three Sisters due to similar heat conditions, and for good reason – heat exhaustion rescues are no joke. While Cowles Mountain isn't officially listed as closed today, applying common sense is crucial. The same goes for any significant incline or long-distance outdoor ventures.

Then there's the traffic. Caltrans has southbound I-5 at the Via de San Ysidro off-ramp closed tonight from 11 PM to 5 AM, and northbound I-805 at Palm Avenue from 9 PM to 5 AM for ongoing projects. If you're a night owl cyclist, be aware of these detours around Mission Bay and further south. They're also planning a major I-805 overhaul with future weekend closures and a new bike trail, so expect disruptions there for a while.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WILDFIRE RISK IS ELEVATED!
The combination of high heat and easterly Santa Ana winds creates ripe conditions for fast-moving fires. Be extremely careful with anything that could spark.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Best Bet: Embrace the Indoors (or the Very Early Morning)
If you absolutely *must* get outside, go before 7 AM. Stay on flat, shaded routes, and carry more water than you think you need. Think a leisurely walk, not a sprint. Otherwise, hit the gym or embrace some air-conditioned rest. Your body will thank you.
</AlertBox>
