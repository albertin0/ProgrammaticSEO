### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors, Hydrate, Reschedule!

Today's 33°C (91.4°F) is past "warm" and squarely in "dangerous" territory, especially away from the immediate coast. Prolonged exposure or strenuous exercise during peak heat (10 AM - 6 PM) can lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke. If you're planning a run up Cowles Mountain in Mission Trails Regional Park, you might want to rethink that. Keep your fluids up, wear light-colored, loose clothing, and for the love of your kidneys, avoid caffeine and alcohol.

### Smoke and Air Quality Concerns

While the general AQI is "Moderate" for San Diego, the ongoing Mateo Fire in Camp Pendleton is causing smoke impacts in northern San Diego County. Listen to your body and local advisories. If you're breathing that campfire smell, your lungs are working overtime.
