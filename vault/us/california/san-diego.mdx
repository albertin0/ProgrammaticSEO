## Seriously, Stay Indoors (Or At Least Act Like You Have Common Sense)

I know you want to hit the pavement or the trails, but today is not the day to earn your "Ironman" badge by risking a trip to urgent care. This kind of heat, especially compounded by any lingering smoke, can sneak up on you faster than a tourist trying to snag a parking spot in Little Italy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is No Joke!
With temperatures pushing into the 90s, your body's cooling system gets overwhelmed fast. Limit sun exposure, especially between 10 AM and 4 PM. If you *must* be outside, wear loose, light-colored clothing, hydrate constantly (water and electrolytes, folks, not just your morning coffee), and pay attention to early signs like dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating. These aren't just "toughing it out" symptoms; they're your body screaming for help. Get to shade, get water, cool down.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Closures: Know Before You Go

Even if you brave the heat, some of our favorite spots have issues. The I-805 northbound in the Miramar area is currently closed between State Route 52 and Miramar Road for culvert replacement and pavement repairs, running from Friday night through Monday morning. This isn't just a traffic headache for your commute; it means if you're trying to access trails in that vicinity, you'll be dealing with detours and potential frustration before you even start sweating.

Furthermore, several county parks have ongoing closures. For instance, parts of the **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park** have Sherwood, Vireo (west of Hollister St.), and Cathedral Trails closed due to construction noise and dust. Don't assume your usual route is clear. Always check the County Parks and Rec website before heading out, unless you enjoy running into "trail closed" signs and wasted effort.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Think About Your Lungs
While the AQI is "moderate," the recent brush fire means there could still be local pockets of higher particulate matter. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or just like breathing easily, consider an indoor workout or keeping it very light today. Your long-term lung health is more important than one workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Key, Always.
No, seriously. If you're heading out for anything more than a quick stroll, bring more water than you think you need. Consider an electrolyte drink, especially in this heat. Dehydration impacts performance and, more importantly, can land you in the ER. Don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, San Diego. Or, better yet, stay *in* there.
