<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for various parts of San Diego County, including mountains, inland valleys, and deserts, with temperatures expected to reach into the 90s and even over 100 degrees in some areas through Saturday. Seriously, "outdoor recreation is discouraged during the daytime." If you insist on getting out, do it at sunrise, stay hydrated, and for the love of all that is holy, stick to the shaded, flatter paths. Your body is not a heat-resistant machine. Heat-related illnesses like heat stroke are no joke, and they're preventable.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Park Closures & Trail Maintenance
As mentioned, several county parks including El Capitan Preserve, Mount Gower Preserve, and Hellhole Canyon Preserve are *closed* for all of August specifically due to dangerous heat conditions. Ignoring these closures isn't just stupid, it puts rescue crews at risk. Also, keep an eye out for potential disruptions at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve; Phase 2 of their major construction project is scheduled to kick off in August or September. If you were planning on hitting the Beach Trail or Guy Fleming, double-check the State Parks website before you waste gas.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Air Quality & Allergy Check
Our AQI is sitting at a moderate 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, which is manageable on its own. Pollen is also moderate. If you're sensitive to allergens, pop your meds. Also, watch out for potential hydrogen sulfide emissions in the Tijuana River Valley due to the heat, which can cause a lovely rotten egg smell and irritate your respiratory system. If you're running down there, maybe rethink that route today.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. You want to run. We all do. But today is not the day to push it on the exposed trails. Go for a spin class, hit the treadmill in your AC, or swim laps at the local pool. Your lungs, joints, and frankly, the San Diego Fire-Rescue Department will thank you for not becoming another statistic.
