## The Rundown: Breathe Easy, But Not *Too* Easy

Today's conditions are pretty typical for early May around here. Overcast skies are keeping things cool at a pleasant 16.6°C (61°F). This means your joints won't be screaming from extreme heat, and you won't be fighting any crazy Santa Ana winds tearing through Mission Bay like we saw back in February.

However, let's talk lungs. Our AQI is clocking in at 67 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, putting us firmly in the "Moderate" category. While that's generally acceptable for most folks, AccuWeather's forecast also showed "Poor" air quality for ozone for today and the next couple of days, which is less than ideal for sensitive groups. Couple that with "Moderate" pollen levels, and if you're prone to seasonal allergies or have asthma, you might feel it. Don't go trying to set a new 5K PR if you're already feeling tight.

### Local Hazards – What to Watch For

No major weather warnings or advisories for San Diego proper, which is always nice to hear after some of the intense storms we've had this past winter, which led to flooding around areas like Fashion Valley Road and Hotel Circle South. However, a small vegetation fire was contained yesterday in Vista, up in North County. While it's not directly impacting central San Diego trails, it's a stark reminder that wildfire season is ramping up, and we're officially in that window where risks are elevated. Keep an eye on the news before heading out to any canyon areas, especially as temperatures creep up later in the month.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Caution:** While not a new development, remember that sections of the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park trails remain closed for repairs and due to unauthorized access issues. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the signs. And if you were planning on a dip in the fountain at Waterfront Park after your run, tough luck – the jets are off for repairs until at least June 2026.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today is a decent day to get your workout in, just be mindful of your respiratory health and stick to the officially open trails.
