Today's not terrible, but don't get complacent – San Diego always has a few curveballs.

### The Air You Breathe

Alright, let's talk about the invisible stuff. That "Mainly Clear" sky at 18°C (64°F) isn't telling the whole story. Our AQI is clocking in at 77 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This puts us squarely in the 'moderate' category for air quality. For most of you weekend warriors, it's probably fine, but if you've got sensitive lungs, asthma, or you're pushing serious mileage, you might feel it. Same goes for the moderate pollen count. Just because the wildflowers aren't blooming like crazy doesn't mean your allergies are taking a holiday.

### Wind Watch & Trail Talk

The big news around town earlier this month was the Henderson Fire up near Camp Pendleton. Good news: that beast is 100% contained as of yesterday, October 19th, at 3,260 acres. So, we're not dealing with active wildfire smoke blanketing our trails today, thank goodness. However, we're not entirely out of the woods when it comes to weather changes. The National Weather Service is calling for gusty winds in the mountains and deserts starting today and picking up through Thursday, with some spots seeing gusts up to 40 mph. If you're planning a run or ride in areas like the backcountry near Ramona or out by Cuyamaca Rancho State Park, be prepared for some serious headwinds, potential debris, and definitely secure anything that can blow away.

<AlertBox type="info">
  Heads up for those using the trails at Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve. While they officially reopened after the recent fire, the City's Fire-Rescue crews are still doing work on access roads, which means you could encounter periodic, temporary closures. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the ropes. Stay on marked paths to help the habitat recover.
</AlertBox>

### Roads & Construction

If your workout involves hitting the pavement, there are a couple of things to note, primarily for night owls or early birds. Caltrans has some overnight closures on eastbound SR-78 at South Rancho Santa Fe Road in San Marcos, running from 10 p.m. to 5 a.m. through Thursday. Also, SR-67 from Iron Mountain Drive to Poway Road has one-way controlled traffic for construction during the same night-time hours until October 23rd. During daylight hours, you should be mostly clear, but always keep an eye out for lane shifts or unexpected traffic if you're near these spots.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  If you're in the mountains or deserts, be extra cautious today through Thursday. Strong winds, potentially up to 40 mph, can create hazardous conditions like downed branches or reduced visibility. Consider a safer, more sheltered route.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today’s a decent day to get outside if you manage your lungs and respect the developing wind patterns. Just don't expect pristine conditions.
