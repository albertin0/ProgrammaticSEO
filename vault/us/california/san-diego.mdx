## The Air: Smoky Skies and Pesky Pollen

The overall Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This isn't exactly "crystal clear" air, and we know why. The Sorrento Fire, which sparked up yesterday, is still kicking, albeit with its forward progress stopped and 25% containment as of yesterday evening.

That fire, burning in the eastern Los Peñasquitos Canyon, is still sending smoke southeast towards areas like eastern Mira Mesa, Miramar, Tierrasanta, and the communities around Mission Trails Regional Park. The San Diego County Air District has a smoke advisory in effect, warning that air quality near the fire is "unhealthy for sensitive groups to unhealthy for all individuals." Even in areas with "minor smoke impacts," it's still "moderate to unhealthy for sensitive groups." If you can smell smoke, you should absolutely limit your outdoor activity. This isn't just a sniffle; those fine particulate matters get deep into your lungs.

## The Trails: Hot Closures and Canyon Warnings

Today's weather is overcast and a pleasant 21°C (around 70°F), which *sounds* nice for a run. But don't let that fool you into thinking the backcountry is open season. The county has been explicit: numerous popular trails are closed for the *entire month of August* due to a series of heat-related emergencies and rescues.

We're talking about major spots like El Capitan County Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, Mt. Gower Preserve, the San Diego River Gorge, and the Three Sisters hiking areas – which include the ever-popular Cedar Creek Falls and Three Sisters Falls. These aren't just suggestions; they're official closures aimed at protecting both hikers and the emergency personnel who'd otherwise be hauling your dehydrated self off a mountain. Many of these trails are "reverse-grade," meaning the hardest part of the climb is on the way *out*, usually when the sun's highest and you're already gassed.

Plus, with the Sorrento Fire still being monitored, there's a mandatory evacuation order remaining in place for a specific canyon area (SDC-1444-A). Don't be that person. Stay out of closed canyons; they're blocked off for a reason.

<AlertBox type="danger">
DO NOT attempt to hike or run on any of the closed trails (El Capitan, Hellhole Canyon, Mt. Gower, San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters Falls, Cedar Creek Falls). Violators can face significant fines and endanger themselves and rescue teams.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Stick to coastal routes or well-shaded urban parks today, or hit the gym. If you must be outside, wear an N95 mask if you're in a smoky area, and stay hydrated, even with the cooler, overcast conditions.
</AlertBox>
