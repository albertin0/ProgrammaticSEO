If you're looking for an outdoor workout in San Diego today, August 13th, you'd better have a short memory for all those heat-related rescues we've been seeing.

## Heat Advisory? More Like "Common Sense" Advisory

Seriously, people. Just because the official "extreme heat warning" for some areas expired a few days ago, or the National Weather Service isn't flashing red alerts right this second, doesn't mean it's suddenly pleasant out there. Today's 91-degree forecast is still plenty dangerous for any strenuous activity. We've seen enough choppers pulling dehydrated hikers off the trails this summer to know better.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures are Real, and So Are the Fines
The U.S. Forest Service has explicitly closed the **San Diego River Gorge** and **Three Sisters** hiking areas through the end of August. And if you think you're sneaky, know this: violating these closures can land you a fine up to $5,000, six months in prison, or both. Let that sink in. These aren't suggestions; they're direct orders because people are getting hurt.
</AlertBox>

On top of that, the County of San Diego automatically closes popular spots like El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, and Mt. Gower Preserve every single August. There’s a pattern here. These "reverse-grade trails," where the steepest climb is on the way *out*, become deathtraps when you're already cooked and out of water.

## Air Quality & Allergens

The good news is, our air isn't trying to finish you off today. The AQI is moderate at 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, but local authorities have been forecasting "Good" conditions for San Diego, particularly when it comes to ozone. So, no smoke concerns from any nearby raging infernos today. The pollen count is "Moderate" as well, so if you're sensitive, bring your meds, but it's not the primary villain.

### Construction Zones: Keep Your Head Up
Some county parks are undergoing maintenance. William Heise County Park's playground is closed until October 2026 for a replacement, and you might hear some construction noise there. Dos Picos County Park also has playground upgrades happening until December. The Lakeside River Trail might see some pedestrian traffic limitations due to ongoing improvements. Not a total stop, but be aware of your surroundings and any detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### My Hot Take: Stick to the Coast Path (Early!)
If you absolutely *must* run outside, hit up the Bayshore Bikeway or even the coastal paths around Coronado very early this morning. You might catch a slight marine layer, and it’s flatter, reducing exertion. But seriously, anything inland, especially those gorgeous but brutal canyons and mountains? Save it for October. Or, you know, find a gym with A/C. Your joints, and frankly, your life, will thank you.
</AlertBox>
