### EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT for Inland and Mountain Areas!
Do NOT attempt strenuous outdoor activity in areas like El Capitan Preserve, Hellhole Canyon, or Mount Gower. County officials specifically close some trails in August for heat, and today's conditions are already dangerous. Even if your phone says "overcast," the heat risk is real, especially away from the immediate coast. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these events. Stay hydrated, stay inside with AC, and check on your neighbors. Your run can wait.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Coastal Heat Advisory & Humidity
While the immediate coast might start overcast and feel deceptively cool at 70°F, it's muggy, and that marine layer *will* burn off into warm afternoons. High humidity makes even moderate temperatures feel much hotter and impairs your body's ability to cool itself. If you must run, go before the sun breaks through, carry plenty of water, and keep it short. Seriously, the concrete radiating heat at 10 AM is no joke.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Watch Out for Mission Trails Construction
If you're hitting Mission Trails Regional Park, be aware that pipeline construction is ongoing, with vehicle gates on both ends of the Father Junipero Serra Trail closed to vehicles 24/7 through late 2028. The trail itself is generally open for pedestrians and cyclists, but occasional closures can happen. Expect potential noise and dust. Check their website for the latest impacts before you head out, and remember that section can get brutally hot later in the day, away from the coastal breeze.
</AlertBox>
