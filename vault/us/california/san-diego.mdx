## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy Out There

Let’s be real, the mild 16.6°C (62°F) and overcast skies might *look* inviting, but don't let San Diego trick you. We just got hammered by Post-Tropical Cyclone Rachel, and the city is still shaking it off.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOODING & WATER CONTAMINATION IS REAL.**
San Diego's coastal regions are under a flood advisory until this afternoon. Yesterday saw sudden flooding in areas like **Mission Valley**, Chollas Creek, and parts of the South Bay, leading to road closures and stalled cars. The San Diego Fire-Rescue Department is still on water-rescue status, with teams staged in vulnerable areas. If you're anywhere near a creek, river, or low-lying area, assume it's compromised. Your "shortcut" could be a swift-water rescue waiting to happen.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**STAY OUT OF THE OCEAN, BAY, AND LAGOONS.**
The County Department of Environmental Health and Quality issued a general rain advisory for all coastal beaches and bays. This means urban runoff has likely caused bacteria levels to spike, making water contact (swimming, surfing, diving, or even just wading) unsafe for at least 72 hours *after* the rain stops. Seriously, unless you fancy a nasty infection, keep your distance from that beautiful, murky water.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Park Considerations

If you're thinking of hitting the trails, be aware of what's *actually* open and safe. While there aren't many *new* closures from the last 24 hours affecting runners specifically, ongoing projects mean some spots are still off-limits. The Cathedral Trail in **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park** is closed, and you might encounter noise and dust from campground reopening work there. Also, William Heise County Park's playground is closed until the end of October for replacement. Bottom line: don't just show up expecting business as usual. Check county parks alerts before you leave your house.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Evening Activity in Balboa Park:**
If your workout plans include an evening jog through **Balboa Park**, just know "The Haunted Trail of Balboa Park" is open from 7-11 PM tonight. This means crowds, potential detours, and general Halloween chaos around that area. Plan accordingly, or just avoid it.
</AlertBox>
