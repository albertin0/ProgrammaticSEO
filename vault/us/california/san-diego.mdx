## Today's Vibe Check: Soggy & Sniffly

Look, I get it. It's Halloween, you want to get that run in before the candy corn coma sets in. But San Diego's about to get a good drenching. Local news has been warning us for days that widespread light rain will morph into moderate to heavy stuff tonight into Sunday morning. Even worse, we've got a coastal flood advisory in effect. The Port of San Diego is even telling people to prep for street flooding.

This isn't just about getting wet. Heavy rain means slippery surfaces, reduced visibility, and potential flash flooding in low-lying areas. Remember that CrossFit gym in Mission Valley that got hammered by floods twice? That kind of stuff happens here, and it's not always in January. If you're planning a run down near Mission Beach, be aware that the city was literally handing out sandbags at Rob Field Athletic Center this morning because of anticipated issues. Your trail shoes aren't rain boots, and your ankles aren't designed to navigate hidden curbs in murky water.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **FLOODING HAZARD IMMINENT!**
Seriously, folks. Widespread rain, potentially heavy, is expected to start late this afternoon/evening. Coastal areas are under a flood advisory, meaning minor coastal flooding is possible at high tide. Avoid low-lying areas, underpasses, and coastal paths like those near Mission Beach or along the San Diego River.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Allergies: The Usual Suspects

Beyond the impending deluge, the air quality today is sitting at a moderate AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. It's not "stay indoors and hermetically seal yourself" bad, but it's not doing your lungs any favors, especially when combined with a moderate pollen count. If you've got asthma or any respiratory sensitivities, you're going to feel it more with the damp air and moderate pollutants.

### **Watch Out for Construction & Closures**
Ongoing construction around the San Diego International Airport, particularly on Pacific Highway and Grape Street, means potential lane adjustments and delays. If you're thinking of hitting some dirt, be aware that some Cleveland National Forest trails are subject to closures due to wet weather, and the Cathedral Trail in Tijuana River Valley Regional Park is still closed for repairs. ### **Consider the Treadmill or Later in the Week**
If you absolutely *must* get a workout in today, consider hitting the gym. If that's not your style, then keep it short, stay on well-lit, paved surfaces, and absolutely avoid any areas prone to flooding. Honestly, it looks like early next week will be much drier and warmer, so maybe just enjoy your Halloween candy and hit it hard then.
