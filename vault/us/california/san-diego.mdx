## Stay Home, San Diego. Or At Least Stay Smart.

Look, I get it. We live in a city where "outdoor enthusiast" is practically etched onto our birth certificates. But today, Mother Nature is actively trying to melt us. This isn't just "feel a bit warm" weather; this is "serious heat illness risk" weather, especially for those who aren't used to it.

### Heat is the #1 Enemy

The National Weather Service isn't messing around with these warnings. Temperatures are projected to be historically high for March. If you're planning a lunchtime run through Balboa Park or hitting the trails further inland towards Ramona, you're looking at prime conditions for dehydration, heat exhaustion, or worse. And remember, even if you start early, the heat builds fast and sticks around, limiting overnight relief.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: Today's temperatures pose a significant risk of heat-related illness. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. Stay hydrated, seek shade, and check on vulnerable individuals.
</AlertBox>

### What About the Air and Pollen?

That AQI of 67 might not sound catastrophic, but with PM2.5 as the main culprit and moderate pollen floating around, your lungs are getting a double-whammy. The heat can exacerbate the effects of these pollutants, especially during peak exertion. If you have any respiratory sensitivities or allergies, you're going to feel it.
