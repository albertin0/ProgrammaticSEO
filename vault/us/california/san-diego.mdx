## Hazards to Avoid

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat Exhaustion & Heat Stroke Are Real
Look, 91 degrees is not a game. Heat-related illnesses can sneak up on you faster than a runner cutting the course at Balboa Park. Drink *more* water than you think you need. Seriously. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or suddenly stop sweating, get indoors, cool down, and don't hesitate to call 911. San Diego's deserts are hitting 105-110°F this week, and while our immediate coast is "cooler," those inland valleys are still cooking.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe in Trash
With PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant and moderate pollen, your lungs are already doing overtime. While there are no *new* major wildfires threatening the immediate metro area right now, the effects of those fires that popped up in May, like the Border 4 and Sunset Fires, contribute to the general particulate matter floating around. If you've got allergies or any kind of respiratory sensitivity, today's a great day for an indoor workout or keeping it very low intensity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Trail Troubles & Beach Caution
If your usual route takes you through Mission Trails Regional Park, be aware that the pipeline construction on the Father Junipero Serra Trail is still causing vehicle access closures and general disruption through late 2028. That means dust and noise, even if you're on foot. Planning a beach run or swim? There's a high rip current risk today. Check with lifeguards before you get in the water, or stick to the sand. Also, save the tennis for another day if you were headed to Collier County Park, as courts are closed for maintenance until June 19th.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, or just stay inside. Your choice.
