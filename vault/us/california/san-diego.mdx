## Current Hazards & What to Watch For

**Air Quality & Lingering Smoke:** We had that Thorn Fire kicking up smoke over in Campo just a few days ago, on July 16th. While the official smoke advisory for those East County areas like Boulevard and McCain Valley might have expired by now, that 67 AQI with PM2.5 as the main culprit means there's still something floating around in the air. PM2.5 is the stuff that gets deep into your lungs, so if you’re asthmatic or have any other respiratory issues, take it easy.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Even with the pleasant overcast weather, the Moderate AQI means air quality isn't fantastic for everyone. Consider scaling back intense outdoor efforts, especially if you're sensitive to particulate matter.
</AlertBox>

**Trail Closures & Construction:** This is a big one if you're a regular trail rat. That Tecolote Canyon Trunk Sewer Improvement project is still chugging along. The hiking trail from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue is *closed 24/7 until summer of 2027*, and all trails within the golf course are also out of commission. Seriously, folks, pay attention to those signs. Don't be "that runner" who ignores the barricades and then complains when you're wading through mud or dodging heavy equipment. It's for your safety, not just to annoy you.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  Avoid Tecolote Canyon trails between Mount Acadia Boulevard and Balboa Avenue entirely. They're closed for construction until Summer 2027. Seriously, find another route.
</AlertBox>

**Residual Dampness from Recent Rain:** San Diego County was under a flood advisory on Thursday, July 16th, due to some excessive rainfall. While we've had a few dry days since, some low-lying spots or trails with poor drainage might still be a bit muddy or slippery. Watch your footing, especially if you're hitting canyon trails or areas known for getting swampy. We're not talking full-on floods anymore, but a twisted ankle on a slick patch still sucks.
