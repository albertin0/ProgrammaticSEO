## Why today is/isn't the day for an outdoor workout:

<BulletList>
*   **Inland Oven:** If your idea of fun involves melting your internal organs, head east. Otherwise, stay far away from the backcountry. Several inland preserves, including El Capitan Preserve near Lakeside, Hellhole Canyon, and Mt. Gower, are officially CLOSED for the entire month of August due to the intensifying summer heat. Temperatures on exposed trails out there can easily hit triple digits.
*   **Smoky Skies & Sticky Air:** Even if you're near the coast, we're still dealing with lingering effects from recent wildfires. The Grade Fire near Lake Henshaw was only 45% contained as of yesterday, August 1st, and firefighters are still battling it in over 100-degree heat. While coastal air isn't directly choked, a Moderate AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, combined with moderate pollen levels, means your lungs are working overtime.
*   **Construction Chaos:** Caltrans is starting a massive I-805 rehabilitation project later this year that will bring weekend closures and ramp shutdowns throughout the South Bay. While not a direct running hazard *today*, be aware that your favorite routes might be impacted soon.

</BulletList>

### Weather & Air Quality Breakdown

Today's overcast skies and a pleasant 20.8°C (around 69°F) might make you think it's all clear on the coast. But don't be fooled by the marine layer; that's just a thin veneer over a very hot region. The National Weather Service still has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect for our inland valleys and mountains until 8 PM tonight, extending through Monday night for the deserts.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat & Trail Closures Inland!** Do NOT attempt to hike or run in inland San Diego County today. Seriously. El Capitan Preserve and others are closed for your safety because it's dangerously hot. The US Forest Service also reports an enhanced wildfire risk through today in most of Southern California outside the immediate coastline.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Moderate AQI and Pollen.** Even coastal areas have a Moderate AQI of 67 and moderate pollen levels. If you have respiratory issues, consider keeping your run shorter or moving it indoors. **Tip for Coastal Runners:** If you absolutely *must* get out, stick to the immediate coast, especially early in the morning. Even then, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it might).
