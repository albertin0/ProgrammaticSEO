## Stay Safe, San Diego (If You Absolutely Must Go Out)

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!**
A widespread **Extreme Heat Warning** covers San Diego County today. Forecasts show inland valleys potentially hitting triple digits, with coastal areas experiencing highs in the 90s, creating "moderate HeatRisk" even by the coast. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these events. Seriously, re-think that outdoor workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Park Closures You Need to Know**
Don't even think about hitting trails at **El Capitan County Preserve, Hellhole Canyon Preserve, or Mt. Gower Preserve** – they're all closed for the entire month of August to protect folks from this brutal heat. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a hard closure for your safety. Other desert parks like Agua Caliente Regional Park and Vallecito County Park are also closed for the summer.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate (Like, *Really* Hydrate) and Adjust
If you absolutely insist on getting some miles in, go early, go short, and bring more water than you think you need. We're talking ice water, electrolytes, the whole shebang. Stick to flatter, shaded routes, and consider the treadmill a win today. Your health isn't worth bragging rights on Strava.
</AlertBox>
