## The Full Breakdown

### Heat: It's a Blaze Out There

Seriously, 33.0°C isn't "ideal running weather." It's "stay inside and complain about your electric bill" weather. The air quality, with an AQI of 51, is technically "Good," but that's like saying a warm bath is good for you when you're already in a sauna. Your body is going to struggle to regulate temperature, and that ozone/other pollutant dominant situation won't do your lungs any favors, especially when they're sucking in hot air.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** With temperatures soaring to 33.0°C, the risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke is dangerously high. This isn't just "uncomfortable," it's medically risky. Pace yourself, hydrate excessively (and I mean *excessively*), and consider moving your workout indoors or to the absolute coolest parts of the day. And for the love of all that is holy, don't leave kids or pets in cars.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen Report: Just Enough to Annoy You

We're sitting at a moderate pollen level. Not a full-blown assault, but enough to make those already-stressed lungs feel a little more tight and itchy as you gasp for air in this heat. If you've got seasonal allergies, you're going to feel it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Precaution:** Moderate pollen combined with high heat means your respiratory system is getting a workout whether you want it to or not. If you're sensitive, pre-dose your allergy meds and definitely shower off after your run to get that crud off your skin and out of your hair.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Closed for Your Inconvenience

Your beloved Lonny Brewer Park is closed until noon today for maintenance. So much for that quick loop. If you were planning on heading south, be aware that portions of the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park trails have had a risk of closures lately. It's always a good idea to call the campground office (619-205-1680) before you head out there. Nothing worse than driving all the way out only to find a locked gate and a "Sorry, not today" sign.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Park Closures:** Lonny Brewer Park is closed until noon today, October 22nd. Plan alternate routes. Additionally, trails and roads in Tijuana River Valley Regional Park may be affected by closures; confirm conditions before you go.
</AlertBox>
