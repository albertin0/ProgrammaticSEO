## Local Hazards & Important Updates

### Air Quality & Allergy Alert
That AQI of 67 puts us squarely in the "Moderate" category, largely due to PM2.5. If you're hitting the pavement around high-traffic areas or anywhere near ongoing construction, you're breathing that stuff in. Combine that with "Moderate" pollen, and anyone with allergies or asthma should probably keep their outdoor activities shorter and less intense. You don't want to start wheezing halfway up Cowles Mountain.

### Wildfire Watch (Always, San Diego, Always)
Just a few days ago, on March 8, a brush fire flared up in Mission Valley, fueled by gusty winds. While that particular blaze is hopefully out, it's a stark reminder that even in early spring, San Diego is a tinderbox waiting to ignite. With the incoming heat wave, the "natural curing process where plants start to really lose their moisture" will accelerate, increasing fire risk. Keep an eye on fire alerts, especially if your usual routes take you near dry brush or canyons.

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Torrey Pines Trails Are Open!
  Good news for North County runners: Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve trails officially reopened on March 1st after some construction. Full access is back, so go enjoy those views before the heat makes it unbearable. Just remember, a second phase of construction is planned for later this year.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Trail Obstacles
Caltrans is still rehabilitating sections of I-805, which means potential weekend closures and ramp shutdowns throughout 2026, especially in the South Bay. While this mostly affects drivers, it's worth noting if your route involves crossing under or near the freeway, as unexpected detours could pop up. The MTS Orange Line also has planned weekend track closures for infrastructure upgrades, though today isn't one of them. If you rely on the trolley to get to your favorite workout spot, double-check their schedule for future weekend disruptions.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Del Mar Beach Rules
  Heads up for those who love to get sandy: Del Mar has a new ordinance banning holes deeper than two feet on public beaches. They're citing collapse hazards and risks to emergency vehicles, so save the elaborate sandcastle moats for another city.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Boil Water Advisory at Potrero County Park
  If you're out near Potrero County Park, be aware there's been a Boil Water Order in effect since July 28, 2025. Drinking fountains are closed, and there's no potable water on site. Bring your own hydration if that's your destination, because bacteria in the water source isn't exactly a fun post-workout treat.
</AlertBox>
