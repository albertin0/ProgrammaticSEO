## Hot as Hades, Literally

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego. Sunshine is our brand. But today, "sunshine" is doing a terrible impression of a blast furnace. The forecast is screaming about dangerously hot conditions, with inland valleys potentially hitting triple digits. That's not "push yourself." That's "call 911."

Your body is going to struggle to cool itself in this kind of heat. Sweat isn't as effective when the air around you is cooking. You're risking heat exhaustion, heatstroke, and probably a nasty sunburn even with SPF 50. This isn't just discomfort; it's a legitimate medical risk. Even in the slightly cooler (but still hot) coastal areas, running at 90 degrees is a bad decision.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!** Seriously, stay out of the sun during peak hours. If you absolutely MUST be outside, keep it short, easy, and in the early morning. Hydrate like it's your job, and don't assume a coastal breeze makes it safe.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Closures and Construction Chaos

Don't even bother driving out to Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve today. Or tomorrow. Or any day until next spring. It's completely closed for utility upgrades, and they're not letting anyone in – cars, bikes, or pedestrians. Save yourself the gas and the frustration.

Over at Mission Trails Regional Park, it's a similar story, though less of a blanket closure. There's ongoing pipeline construction along Father Junipero Serra Trail. While the trail is generally open to walkers and cyclists, the vehicle gates are closed 24/7 through late 2028. Expect noise, dust, and potential temporary closures for pedestrians as well. It's not exactly the serene nature experience you're probably hoping for.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Plan Your Route (Indoors):** Given the heat and trail closures, maybe it's a good day for a spin class, an indoor track session, or finally tackling that stack of laundry. Your body (and your laundry pile) will thank you.
