### Know Your Closures, Folks

<AlertBox type="danger">
Listen up: San Diego County *always* shuts down a few of our beloved inland parks for the entire month of August due to extreme heat. So, if you had plans for a brutal hill workout at **El Capitan Preserve**, **Mt. Gower Preserve**, or **Hellhole Canyon Preserve**, they're off-limits. These aren't just "suggestions," they're closures to keep you from ending up a statistic for CAL FIRE. Stay out of there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
Still dealing with construction on the **Escondido Creek Trail**. They’ve been at it for a while now, with temporary closures for crack sealing and general maintenance. If that's your usual route, expect detours and keep an eye out for flaggers. It's not the end of the world, but it *is* an annoyance.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, with the comfortable weather, it's a prime day for cruising along the boardwalk in Mission Beach or tackling the hills around Balboa Park without feeling like you're running on the surface of the sun. Just stick to the designated paths, don't be that person trying to forge a new trail through delicate habitat.
