<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning for Your Workout
Seriously, 91 degrees is not a running temperature, especially not in San Diego where we typically enjoy milder weather. Pushing yourself in this heat can lead to heat exhaustion, cramps, and even heat stroke. Hospitals have been seeing more heat-related illness recently, so don't become another statistic. If you MUST be outside, stick to the absolute coolest parts of the day – we're talking before 7 AM.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye on That Air Quality & Fire Risk
While AQI is currently moderate, the elevated fire weather concerns mean conditions could deteriorate rapidly. If you start smelling smoke, or the air gets hazy, get indoors immediately. Your lungs are not designed to filter wildfire smoke during a hard run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration, Hydration, Hydration!
If you ignore my advice and head out, make sure you're drinking water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. And I don't mean a tiny water bottle. You need a full hydration vest for anything over 30 minutes, or plan routes with water fountains. Electrolytes aren't just for hangovers, folks.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there. Or better yet, just stay inside.
