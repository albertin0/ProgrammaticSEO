<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke Is Real, Folks
Seriously, 91 degrees in April is no joke. Heat-related illnesses spike significantly during these conditions. Don't leave kids or pets in cars, even for a minute. If you *must* train, keep it short, early, or indoors. Hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's self-preservation. You can't just power through this kind of heat without risking serious consequences.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Trust the Tap at Potrero
If your plan involved a long run or ride through Potrero County Park, pack *all* your water. The boil water order from last year is still active, and drinking fountains are closed. Assume any natural water source is also a no-go. Plan your routes to have access to safe, sufficient hydration.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Eyes on the Air, Especially Inland
While the local air quality forecast might lean "good," an AQI of 67 is firmly in the moderate zone. San Diego County has recently been flagged for its particle and ozone pollution, particularly near border regions. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, today might be the day for the elliptical, or at least sticking to coastal areas where the breeze might offer some relief. Even then, watch out for "hazardous ash" if you see or smell smoke, even if the AQI reads green.
</AlertBox>

Look, I love running these trails as much as the next masochist, but today isn't the day to push it. Listen to your body, or better yet, listen to me and find a nice air-conditioned gym. Your joints (and lungs) will thank you.
