<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: This is Not a Drill
Seriously, 33.0°C is dangerous for outdoor exercise. San Diego has seen extreme heat warnings earlier this month, and while that specific advisory might have expired, the physiology doesn't change. Elevated temperatures like this increase your risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Your pace will suffer, your heart rate will be through the roof, and you won't recover well. If you must be outside, keep it short, easy, and stay hydrated with electrolytes. Or, you know, find a gym with air conditioning.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Air Quality & Pollen: Breathe Easy, or Not
An AQI of 67 is "moderate," but with PM2.5 being the main culprit, those fine particulate matters can still irritate your lungs, especially during heavy breathing. Add in the moderate pollen, and you've got a recipe for sniffles, sneezes, and a general feeling of crud. It's not a lung emergency, but it's certainly not ideal.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">

Always check park and trail websites before you head out, because getting rerouted adds miles to your misery.
