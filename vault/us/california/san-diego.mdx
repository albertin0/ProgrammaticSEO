### Extreme Heat & Wind Advisory

It's going to be a seriously hot day, hitting 33.0°C (91.4°F), and to make matters worse, we're looking at strong winds with gusts up to 55 mph starting this afternoon and continuing into the weekend. This combo is a recipe for disaster. Heat exhaustion and stroke are real, and falling branches or flying debris from high winds are no joke. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light clothing, and seriously consider an indoor workout or hitting the trails *only* in the early morning. If you're out later, stay off exposed routes and be hyper-aware of your surroundings.
</AlertBox>
