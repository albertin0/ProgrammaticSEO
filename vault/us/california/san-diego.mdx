Alright, San Diego, March 29th. If you're not out the door by 7 AM, you're gonna regret it.

*   That "partly cloudy" morning is a Trojan horse. We've been sweating through a record-breaking March heatwave, and yesterday saw temperatures rapidly climb into the low 80s even near the coast, with inland spots hitting the low 90s. Today will likely follow suit, so hit it early or stay inside.
*   Air quality is sitting at a "moderate" AQI of 67, mostly thanks to PM2.5. If your lungs are finicky, that's enough to notice.
*   Pollen's also in the moderate zone. Your allergies might not *kill* you, but they'll certainly make you think about it on a long run.

Look, I love our city's trails as much as the next masochist, but you gotta be smart today. Yesterday was still hot as hell across the county, and while the morning fog might feel nice now, don't let it fool you. The ground's still retaining that heat, and once that sun peeks out fully, things are going to warm up fast. Seriously, if you're hitting the trails later in the morning or afternoon, prepare for significantly higher temps than that current 15.6°C (60°F) suggests.

### Trail & Road Rundown

**Tijuana River Valley Regional Park** still has some headaches. If you were planning a long trek down there, be aware that Sherwood Trail, Vireo Trail (west of Hollister St.), and Cathedral Trail are all closed for ongoing work. Don't be "that guy" ignoring closure signs, you're just making more work for the rangers and potentially putting yourself in a sketchy situation.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Hydration – Seriously.** We've had a solid run of unseasonably hot weather all month. Even if today feels cooler, you could still be carrying a hydration deficit. Electrolytes aren't just for Instagram models; they're for runners who don't want to cramp up halfway through their workout. Drink more than you think you need.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Ahead (Literally):** Starting April 1st (Monday, so close enough to plan for), there's road work for a storm drain near the University of San Diego on Camino San Diego Road. Expect temporary traffic pattern changes if you're driving through that area. Also, keep an eye out for ongoing I-805 roadwork in the South Bay; they've been doing weekend closures and ramp shutdowns all year.
</AlertBox>
