### Air Quality & Allergy Annoyances

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a "moderate" 81, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This means that while it's generally okay, if you're prone to respiratory issues or just trying to set a PR, you might feel it. Fine particulate matter from vehicle exhaust and urban activity likes to hang around, especially near busy roads. So, maybe don't sprint directly behind a diesel truck on the 5.

On top of that, pollen is doing its moderate thing. For some of us, that's just background noise. For others, it means watery eyes and a runny nose turning your run into an unplanned interval session of sneezing. You know who you are.

### Coastal Chaos & Trail Troubles

If your idea of a workout involves the Pacific, listen up. The coast has a beach hazards statement in effect until Tuesday morning, warning of 3 to 6-foot surf and dangerous rip currents. I've seen enough tourists get pulled out to sea; stay off the rock jetties and maybe keep your ocean swims shallow or opt for a pool today. This ain't the day to prove your mettle against the ocean.

As for our trails, most are open, but there's a permanent buzzkill in the **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park**: the Cathedral Trail is still closed for some kind of work. Expect noise and dust if you're in the vicinity. Also, be aware of "risk of road closures" on Monument Mesa Road within the park. So, if that's your usual route, plan an alternative or face a dusty detour.
