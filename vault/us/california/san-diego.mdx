## The Rundown

It's Tuesday, June 23, 2026, and San Diego is rocking its usual "June Gloom" vibe with overcast skies and a mild 17.8°C (64°F). Perfect running weather if you ask me, *if* your lungs and sinuses are up for the challenge.

The air quality, while not terrible, isn't sparkling clean either. An AQI of 67 means that some people, particularly those who are unusually sensitive to air pollution, might experience respiratory symptoms. Combine that with a moderate pollen count, and anyone with allergies or asthma should definitely think twice before hitting their usual strenuous pace.

### Local Hazards to Keep in Mind

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Tijuana River Valley Regional Park: Cathedral Trail Closure & Dust**
Don't plan on cruising the Cathedral Trail today. It's closed, and work in the area means you might encounter some dust and noise, especially if you're prone to respiratory irritation. Stick to the open trails or find an alternate route.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**E-Bike Etiquette**
A friendly reminder, especially with the City Council recently discussing a rise in e-bike injuries: stay vigilant on shared paths. Always assume there's a speeding e-bike (or scooter, or regular bike, or stroller) just around the bend. Share the road, folks, and be predictable.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction & Traffic Delays**
While nothing major is directly impacting popular running/biking routes in the immediate city center, drivers heading to trailheads in North County might encounter one-way traffic on SR 76 between Pala and Pauma Valley due to construction, which continues through June 26. Plan accordingly if you're venturing out that way.
</AlertBox>
