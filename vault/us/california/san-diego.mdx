## Why today is *not* the day.

<BulletList>
*   **Scorching Temperatures:** San Diego County is under an Extreme Heat Warning for inland areas and a Heat Advisory for the coast, with current temperatures hitting 33.0°C (91.4°F) and forecast highs well into the 90s, even near the beaches. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerously hot, with overnight lows staying elevated in the 70s.
*   **Moderate Air Quality & Pollen:** Our AQI sits at a moderate 67 (PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant), coupled with moderate pollen levels. While not awful *right now*, the National Weather Service is predicting Santa Ana winds starting tomorrow, which will stir up allergens and particulate matter, making breathing a whole lot less enjoyable very soon.
*   **Elevated Fire Risk:** The combination of hot, dry conditions and the anticipated Santa Ana winds is creating "near-critical fire weather conditions" in the valleys and foothills. Translation: everything's bone dry, and a spark could quickly become a major problem.
</BulletList>

## Current Hazards & What to Do

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat Warning in Effect**
Forget your PRs, folks. An Extreme Heat Warning is active for inland valleys and mountains until Friday evening, with a Heat Advisory for coastal areas. This means significant heat risk for most of the county. Limiting strenuous outdoor activity isn't a suggestion; it's a safety directive. Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate, and if you must be outside, stick to the early morning hours and stay in the shade.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Moderate Air Quality & Impending Santa Ana Winds**
While our AQI is moderate today, it's trending less favorable, and Santa Ana winds are on their way. These winds are notorious for kicking up dust, pollen, and other fine particulate matter, which can exacerbate respiratory issues and allergies. If you have asthma or other lung conditions, consider this your heads-up for potentially worse air quality starting tomorrow.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Trail & Park Closures for Construction/Maintenance**
Planning a run through Tecolote Canyon Natural Park and Reserve? You're out of luck on a major section. The hiking segment from Mount Acadia Boulevard to Balboa Avenue, along with trails inside the golf course footprint, are closed 24/7 until summer 2027 due to ongoing sewer work. Other parks like Tijuana River Valley Regional Park have sections, including Cathedral Trail, closed for repairs. Always check county park websites before you head out, because getting there and finding a "CLOSED" sign is just a punch to the gut.
</AlertBox>
