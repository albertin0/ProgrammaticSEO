Look, I get it. We live in San Diego; we want to be outside. But pushing it today is just asking for trouble. That 33°C (81.4°F) you're seeing for our "Mainly Clear" conditions is just the start, and it's going to climb fast. The NWS is explicitly telling people to "reduce outdoor recreation, especially each afternoon."

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!
  From 10 AM this morning until 8 PM Friday, we are under a Heat Advisory. This isn't just uncomfortable, it's potentially deadly. Temperatures will be 20-30 degrees above normal. Seriously, stay inside during peak heat hours. Save your PR attempts for a cooler day.

Heading out to Mission Trails Regional Park for those elevation gains? Think again. Those exposed trails, especially in the afternoon, will be absolute ovens. The ground temperature alone will radiate heat, and there's little shade once you get moving. Even if you're near the coast, those 80-90 degree highs are no joke when you're exerting yourself.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Air Quality & Activity: Proceed with Caution
  An AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, means that while the air isn't "bad" for everyone, sensitive groups (asthmatics, elderly, children, or anyone with respiratory issues) should be extra careful. Heavy exertion when PM2.5 is elevated can worsen lung function. Don't be a hero; listen to your body.
</AlertBox>
