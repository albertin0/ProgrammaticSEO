## Why today is/isn't the day.

<BulletList>
*   **Scorching Temps:** We're hitting 33.0°C (that's 91.4°F for those still on Imperial) today. While the coastal breeze might offer a slight reprieve, those inland valleys are getting cooked. The NWS predicted 77-86°F for inland valleys, so we're pushing past that. Don't be a hero; heatstroke is a real buzzkill.
*   **Dodgy Air & Smoke Signals:** Our AQI is sitting at 59, which is "Moderate" with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. On its own, not terrible for most, but San Diego County recently got ranked fifth-worst nationally for annual particle pollution, and seventh worst for ozone. Add to that a wildfire, named I 15 Hwy /w Rainbow Valley Bl, reported in San Diego County just a couple of days ago with unknown containment. That's a recipe for some lung irritation.
*   **Pollen Pains:** Moderate pollen levels are just another layer of fun for your respiratory system. If you're sensitive, you'll feel it.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Watch Your Heat Tolerance, Seriously.**
It's 33.0°C out there. That's not "nice and warm," that's "stay hydrated and in the shade" weather. Don't assume your body is acclimatized to this early-season heat. Cal/OSHA even warned about this earlier in the year. Limit strenuous activity, especially during peak sun hours. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or just plain crappy, stop immediately.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Keep an Eye (and Nose) on Air Quality**
That moderate AQI (59) coupled with the potential for wildfire smoke from the I 15 Hwy /w Rainbow Valley Bl fire means those with asthma, COPD, or other respiratory issues should reconsider intense outdoor workouts. Even healthy folks might notice some irritation. Remember that recent report about San Diego's particle pollution problem? Don't push it.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk

While some trails are still seeing closures or maintenance, like certain sections of Tijuana River Valley Regional Park (Sherwood Trail, Vireo Trail, and Cathedral Trail are closed due to ongoing work, and you might encounter dust and noise), there's some good news too.

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Good News: Ruffin Canyon is Open!**
If you're looking for a new spot, the Ruffin Canyon Trail, connecting Mission Valley and Serra Mesa, finally opened last month. It's been a long time coming, offering safe pedestrian and bike access with new footbridges and restored wetlands. So if you're keen to explore, that's a new option, just be mindful of the heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **General Tips for Today**
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** And then hydrate some more. Water is your friend.
*   **Dress Smart:** Light-colored, loose-fitting, moisture-wicking gear. Ditch the cotton.
*   **Timing is Everything:** Early morning is your best bet, or push it to late evening if you absolutely must be outside. Avoid midday.
*   **Listen to Your Body:** This isn't the day to ignore those little warning signs. If you're struggling, cut it short.
*   **Check Park Alerts:** Always double-check county park websites for specific, real-time closures before heading out, especially for lesser-known spots. For instance, Dos Picos County Park had campground restroom closures until today, and Guajome Regional Park's nature trail is still closed from April 18th.
*   **Boil Water Advisory:** Potrero County Park still has a boil water order in effect from way back in July 2025. Don't drink the tap water there without boiling it first.
</AlertBox>
