<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning!
  Today's actual temperature of 33°C (91.4°F) is no joke. Inland areas have been hitting triple digits recently, and even closer to the coast, this heat is abnormal for May. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real. If you're running, cycling, or doing anything strenuous outside, you are putting yourself at serious risk. Hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's mandatory. Seriously, consider pushing your workout to much cooler hours or, dare I say, hitting the gym.
</AlertBox>

### Local Trail Head-Up: Tijuana River Valley Regional Park

If you were planning on a long run or ride through **Tijuana River Valley Regional Park**, be aware there are ongoing impacts. Some trails, like Sherwood, Vireo (west of Hollister St.), and Cathedral Trail, are closed due to campground reopening work. Expect some noise and dust from the construction. It's already hot enough without adding construction debris to your lung workout. Call ahead or check their website if you're planning a trip there today.

  ### Pro Tip: Embrace the Darkness (or the Early Birds)
  If you insist on an outdoor workout today, your *only* sensible options are before the sun fully commits to cooking us alive (think 5 AM) or after it's mostly gone to bed (8 PM or later). And even then, those moderate AQI and pollen levels aren't magically disappearing. Keep your routes shorter, slower, and stay in the shade as much as possible. Carry more water than you think you need. Or, you know, just don't.
