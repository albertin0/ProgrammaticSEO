**Extreme Heat Warning: Avoid Desert Workouts Today!**

Seriously, folks. The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for the Coachella Valley, San Diego County Deserts, and San Gorgonio Pass near Banning. We're looking at dangerously hot conditions with temperatures soaring between 105 and 110 degrees Fahrenheit until 8 PM PDT tonight, May 11th. Unless your idea of a good time involves heat stroke and an ambulance ride, stay far away from trails like those out near Borrego Springs. Stick to air conditioning or very early morning/late evening activities if you *must* be out there.

### Air Quality & Allergies

The AQI is clocking in at 67, which is "moderate," driven by PM2.5. If you're prone to respiratory issues, you might feel it on longer or more intense efforts. Combine that with a "moderate" pollen level, and your sinuses might decide to join the workout, whether you like it or not. Pop those antihistamines if you're usually battling the seasonal sneezes.

### Trail & Park Closures

Heads up, especially for the mountain bikers and trail runners. Sweetwater Bike Park is completely shut down for its annual maintenance until May 20th. So, if you were planning to shred some gnar there, you're out of luck. Also, if you regularly hit Guajome Regional Park, know that a nature trail has been closed since mid-April. And don't forget the Tijuana River Valley Regional Park, where Sherwood, Vireo, and Cathedral Trails are closed for repairs, which means potential noise and dust for any nearby routes. Always check park and trail websites before you head out, because San Diego County always seems to have *something* going on.

While the immediate coast isn't sizzling, stay hydrated. Even moderate temperatures can sneak up on you, especially if you're pushing hard. Carry water, even on shorter runs, and consider electrolyte replenishment if you're out for a while.
