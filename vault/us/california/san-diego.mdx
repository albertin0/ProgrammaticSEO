## Your Cynical Guide to Not Dying Out There

I get it. You want to hit that PR, you want to get your steps in, you want to post that sweaty selfie looking like you conquered something. But today, conquering something probably means conquering the urge to go for a mid-day run.

The National Weather Service has been warning us about dangerously hot conditions for weeks now, and it's still holding true for early April. Your body isn't ready for this sudden furnace, so don't act like it is. Cal/OSHA even put out warnings for *workers* to protect against heat illness because people aren't acclimatized yet. That goes double for your weekend warrior self trying to set a new course record on the Balboa Park trails.

And let's talk about that air. A moderate AQI of 67 with PM2.5 as the main culprit, plus moderate pollen, is a one-two punch for your respiratory system. It's not a full-blown wildfire smoke-out (though new fires were reported in Riverside and Los Angeles counties yesterday, reminding us it's always a risk), but it's enough to make you wheeze more than usual. If you've got even a hint of sensitive lungs, take it inside today. Hit the gym. Do some planks in your air-conditioned living room. Anything.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### CAUTION: Lung and Allergy Annoyance Ahead
With moderate AQI and pollen, if you have asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, you're going to feel this. Consider shorter, less intense workouts, or move them indoors. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

The trails at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve might be beautiful, but today they're just a really hot, slightly dusty place to get dehydrated. Even though they've reopened after some winter maintenance, the heat is your primary enemy. Keep an eye on trail conditions if you insist on going anywhere dusty, as dry conditions can make footing tricky.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pro Tip: Embrace the Indoor Life
Today is a great day to cross-train, lift weights, or do some yoga indoors. Save your outdoor mileage for when the sun isn't actively trying to turn you into a human jerky.
