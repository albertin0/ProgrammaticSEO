<AlertBox type="warning">
**Inland Temps are Spiking**
While the immediate coast might feel okay, forecast models show San Diego County will be experiencing "dangerous temps" throughout the week. That 22.3°C you see for "San Diego" is likely a coastal number and not representative of areas like Poway, Lakeside, or Ramona, where it'll be significantly hotter. Hydrate early and often, and stick to cooler, shaded areas if you insist on being outside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Smoke Check-in:**
The Dehesa 7 Fire is 100% contained, but CAL FIRE was still checking for hotspots and reignition as of August 21st. While the main threat is gone, if you're sensitive or training near the Dehesa area, keep an eye (or nose) out for any lingering smoke. Also, a fire in Riverside County (Wood Fire) is only 25% contained, so smoke drift is a possibility, albeit unlikely to majorly impact central San Diego.
</AlertBox>

Stick to the boardwalk, hit a flat, shaded route in Balboa Park, or just pound some miles on the treadmill today. Your joints, lungs, and the poor folks who'd have to rescue you will thank you.
