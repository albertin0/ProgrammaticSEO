## Today's Local Grind: Dodge the Heat, Mind the Air, and Watch for Closures

Look, San Diego is usually prime for outdoor workouts, but today, the heat is a real buzzkill. That 33°C is no joke, especially if you're hitting the trails later in the day when the sun is beating down. We've seen "Extreme Heat Warnings" issued for our desert areas, and while you might not be running out past Julian, those inland valleys are feeling the burn. Remember last year when Mission Trails Regional Park had signs up reminding folks that hiking in hot weather is not recommended? Take that advice to heart.

And about those lungs: with a moderate AQI of 67 and PM2.5 doing its thing, plus moderate pollen floating around, any deep breaths you take will be grabbing more than just fresh ocean air. Even if it's not outright "unhealthy" for everyone, those with respiratory issues or seasonal allergies are going to feel it. We even had a smoke advisory just a few days ago (June 18th) from the Mateo fire on Camp Pendleton, which reminds us that fire season is always looming and air quality can turn on a dime.

Oh, and if your usual route takes you through Tecolote Canyon, forget about it. Big sections, including all trails within the golf course, are closed 24/7 through at least June 29, 2026, for sewer improvement and water connection work. That means no sneaking through, folks, it's a legitimate safety issue with heavy construction.

### DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk!
With temperatures pushing into the 90s (Fahrenheit), heatstroke is a serious concern. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or confused, or stop sweating, **call 911 immediately**. This isn't just discomfort; it's a medical emergency.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Air Quality & Pollen Combo
AQI is moderate, but combined with moderate pollen, sensitive groups might experience symptoms. If you have asthma or allergies, consider moving your workout indoors or sticking to very early mornings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### PRO TIP: Hydrate Like Your Life Depends On It (Because It Does)
If you absolutely *must* run outside today, go before 8 AM or after sunset. Carry way more water than you think you need, and don't be a hero. Cut your distance, slow your pace, and listen to your body.
