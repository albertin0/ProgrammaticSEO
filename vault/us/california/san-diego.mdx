## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Actual heat closures are in effect.** The Cleveland National Forest has shut down popular spots like San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters, and Cedar Creek Falls for the *entire month* due to extreme heat and way too many heat-related rescues. County parks like El Capitan, Mt. Gower, and Hellhole Canyon Preserves are also closed through August. Don't be "that guy" who thinks they're tougher than triple-digit temperatures.
*   **The "America's Finest City Half Marathon" has taken over.** If your Sunday plans involve running anywhere near Downtown or Point Loma this morning, forget about it between 4 AM and 11 AM. Road closures and detours are in full effect, so unless you're actually *in* the race, find another route or another day.
*   **Moderate air quality and pollen are tag-teaming your respiratory system.** While some forecasts are calling for "Good" air today, the general vibe is "Moderate" with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Mix that with moderate pollen, and you might feel it more than usual, especially when pushing hard.
</BulletList>

### Weather & Air Quality Breakdown:

Today's temperature is a scorching 33.0°C (that's 91.4°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) under mainly clear skies. No marine layer to save us today, folks. This kind of heat is no joke, and it's why officials have wisely closed off some of our most challenging trails.

The Air Quality Index is sitting at 67, which is "Moderate." Nothing catastrophic, but certainly not ideal for a high-intensity session when your lungs are already working overtime. PM2.5, the fine particulate matter, is the main culprit.

### Don't Push Your Luck, San Diego.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Closures: Stay Off Closed Trails!**
Seriously, I can't stress this enough. Trails like the San Diego River Gorge, Three Sisters, and El Capitan are closed for a reason: your safety. Ignoring these closures risks heatstroke, dehydration, and tying up emergency services that could be used for actual fires or other crises. Find a different, safer workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory in Full Effect.**
With temperatures hovering around the low 90s, the risk of heat-related illness is high, especially for strenuous activities. Even if a trail isn't officially closed, use extreme caution. Hydrate *religiously*, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Consider Your Route: America's Finest City Half Marathon**
If you typically run through Downtown or Point Loma in the morning, remember the America's Finest City Half Marathon is happening. Expect significant road closures and crowds until late morning. Even if you're not running it, it's going to mess with your usual routes around those areas. Check MTS detours if you rely on public transport to get to your starting point.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. We live in San Diego; we want to be outside. But today is a day for smart choices. If you absolutely *must* get a workout in, hit the treadmill, or go for a very gentle walk around Balboa Park *early* this morning and stick to the shade. Even places like Mission Trails Regional Park, while not explicitly listed as closed, are still exposed to this brutal heat. Plan for minimal effort and maximum hydration.
