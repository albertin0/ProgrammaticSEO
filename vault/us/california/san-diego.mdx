    ## Extreme Heat Warning!
    With temperatures hitting 33.0°C (91.4°F), this is *not* the day for a lunchtime run or a midday hike. If you absolutely MUST be outside, stick to the early morning before 8 AM or late evening after 7 PM. Drink absurd amounts of water, wear light, loose clothing, and listen to your body. Heat-related illness is no joke. Seriously. Your pride isn't worth passing out on a trail. San Diego County officials have been stressing this for weeks for a reason.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
    ## Breathe Easy (Or Try To)
    The AQI of 67 (Moderate) and Moderate pollen levels mean sensitive groups should scale back outdoor exertion. If you've got asthma, allergies, or other respiratory issues, today is a prime day for an indoor workout. Even if you don't, combine this with the brutal heat, and you're just asking for trouble. Keep an eye out for any localized smoke or dust, which can worsen conditions even if the general AQI looks okay.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
    ## Trail Check-In: Don't Just Assume It's Open
    Speaking of the outdoors, don't just roll up to your favorite spot, expecting clear sailing. While the main trails at **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve** are currently listed as OPEN, areas like Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve still advise checking for closures due to potential damage. Recent rains can lead to erosion and unstable ground, so a quick call to a ranger or a check online can save you a wasted trip – or worse, an injury on a dodgy path.
</AlertBox>
