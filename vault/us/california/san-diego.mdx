## Today's Vibe: Oven-Baked with a Side of Detours

Look, San Diego, I love you, but today isn't exactly prime running weather. That 33.0°C (91.4°F) forecast is no joke, and while there aren't any *official* extreme heat warnings active right now from the National Weather Service, anyone who's actually run here knows that kind of heat demands respect. Factor in the "Mainly Clear" skies, and you're looking at full sun exposure for most of the day.

Your lungs are also getting a moderate workout just by breathing, thanks to an AQI of 67, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Mix that with moderate pollen levels, and if you're sensitive, you might be sniffing and wheezing more than usual. The air quality forecast from the San Diego County Air Pollution Control District confirms "GOOD to MODERATE" levels for today, but your mileage may vary depending on your personal sensitivities and exactly where you are.

### Trail Updates: Patience, My Fellow Pain-Seekers

Now for the real kick in the shorts: trail closures. If you had plans to conquer the epic views at **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve**, pivot immediately. The entire reserve, including its hiking trails, the scenic road, and upper parking lots, is completely *closed* to all visitors – pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles – starting September 22, 2026, and is expected to remain shut until March 2027. They're doing major utility upgrades, accessibility improvements, and replacing that ancient restroom building. So, while the beach itself and its parking lots are open, don't plan on accessing any of the bluff trails. This isn't a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on "find somewhere else" situation.

It's not just Torrey Pines either. Over at **Mission Trails Regional Park**, the Father Junipero Serra Trail is closed to vehicles 24/7 through late 2028 due to ongoing pipeline construction. While the walking/bike path generally remains open, expect temporary lane closures, parking restrictions, and potential dust and noise. Basically, it's a construction zone, so plan for delays and keep your head on a swivel.
