If you're looking for a clear day to hit the trails around San Diego, today's a mixed bag, and honestly, inland folks might want to find an air-conditioned gym.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
*   **Smoke, Smoke, Everywhere Smoke:** The Creelman Fire near Ramona and the Rainbow Fire in extreme Northern San Diego County have smoke advisories out. Depending on which way the marine layer decides to play, parts of our county could be seeing air quality from "unhealthy for sensitive groups" to "unhealthy for all individuals." Seriously, if you smell smoke, don't pretend you're an iron lung. Stay indoors.
*   **Comic-Con Chaos is Upon Us:** If your workout involves navigating downtown, good luck. San Diego Comic-Con kicks off today, bringing with it "packed crowds" and "heavy traffic" until Sunday. Expect major delays around the Convention Center and adjacent areas. Your "fast walk" might turn into a slow shuffle through a sea of costumed heroes and villains.
*   **Pollen and PM2.5 Doing Their Thing:** Moderate pollen levels are standard for this time of year, but when you combine them with a general AQI of 67 (moderate, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant), and then throw in wildfire smoke, your lungs are working overtime.

</BulletList>

Today’s weather around the coast is a clear 19.8°C (about 68°F), which sounds lovely, but don't let that fool you. Inland valleys and deserts are still under an Extreme Heat Warning and Heat Advisory that was issued yesterday, with temperatures potentially reaching into the 90s. If you're planning a long run out towards, say, the eastern trails of Mission Trails Regional Park, be acutely aware of which way the wind is blowing that fire smoke. Even if the immediate park area looks clear, regional advisories mean that particulate matter is in the air.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Wildfire Smoke Advisory:** If you're in an area affected by smoke from the Creelman Fire (Ramona) or Rainbow Fire (Northern San Diego County), seriously limit your outdoor activity. "Unhealthy for sensitive groups" means exactly that. If you smell or see smoke, opt for an indoor workout. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Delays and Crowds:** Trying to squeeze in a run or bike ride through downtown San Diego today? Think again. Comic-Con is afoot, which means heavy pedestrian and vehicle traffic, and potential detours for public transport. Avoid the Gaslamp Quarter and areas around the Convention Center if you value your pace and sanity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Zones:** Always be mindful of ongoing construction. For example, Hillcrest's Pride Promenade project is still causing some disruption, particularly with storm drain work on University Avenue, expected to last until late 2027. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours or uneven surfaces on your usual routes.
</AlertBox>

Overall, if you're hitting the pavement, stick to coastal areas with known good air quality, hydrate like it's your job, and maybe reconsider that deep-tissue breathing exercise until these fires are fully contained and the smoke clears.
