## Today's Outdoor Conditions: Oct 08, 2026

Forget that "fall is coming" feeling, San Diego. Today is a prime example of our "second summer" kicking everyone's butt. The National Weather Service has an **Extreme Heat Warning** plastered across the county, from the beaches to the valleys and mesas, indicating dangerously hot conditions that will stick around until at least 8 PM. We're looking at highs in the upper 90s to over 100°F in some spots, with little cooling overnight. If you absolutely *must* get out there, you're looking at sweating through your socks and risking heat-related illness faster than you can say "hydration."

The air isn't exactly crisp mountain air either. While a 67 AQI isn't apocalyptic, it's still moderate, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) doing most of the dirty work. Coupled with the oppressive heat, your lungs will be working overtime just to keep you cool, let alone process polluted air.

### Trail Specifics: Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve

If you were planning a run through Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve, be aware that while the trails are mostly open after a recent fire, you'll still see some yellow rope fencing guiding you around sensitive regrowth areas. More critically, Fire-Rescue crews might implement periodic, temporary closures on access roads as they continue their work. And obviously, the same extreme heat warnings apply there. Don't even *think* about bringing your dog out there during peak heat; they overheat far quicker than you do.
