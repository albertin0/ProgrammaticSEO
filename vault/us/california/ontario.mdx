## Trail Trouble: Mt. Baldy is Still a No-Go Zone

For those of you who like to trade asphalt for elevation and venture into the San Gabriel Mountains, listen up: a significant portion of the Mt. Baldy area trails remain closed for public safety. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a temporary closure, extended from February 27 through March 20, 2026. We're talking about trails like Mt. Baldy Trail, Baldy Bowl Trail, Devils Backbone Trail, Three T's Trail, Icehouse Canyon Trail, Chapman Trail, and the Ontario Peak Trail itself.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER ZONE: Mt. Baldy Trail Closures!** Do NOT attempt to use the closed trails in the Mt. Baldy area, including Ontario Peak Trail. Forest Orders are in effect until March 20th. Ignoring these isn't just stupid; it could be dangerous and lead to fines.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, these closures are for a reason. Public safety. Don't be "that guy" who thinks the rules don't apply to them. You're not just risking yourself; you're risking the first responders who have to come dig you out when you inevitably get into trouble. There's no specific news of recent fires or anything, but given the time of year and previous seasonal conditions, these closures often stem from lingering hazards or maintenance from winter weather.

## My Two Cents, As a PT Who's Seen It All

Given the moderate AQI and pollen, if you're sensitive, dial back the intensity. Maybe keep that long run to a brisk walk today. Your lungs will thank you. For everyone else, it’s a decent day for shorter, moderate efforts. Stick to the paved paths and keep your mountain ambitions on hold until the Angeles National Forest says it's safe.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip: Check Your Gear.** If you're hitting the Pacific Electric Trail, especially sections that might still be a bit damp from past weather or near landscaping, remember that thorns are a perennial issue for tires. Cyclists, check your pressure and maybe opt for tougher tires. Runners, watch your step to avoid rolling an ankle on any uneven sections.
