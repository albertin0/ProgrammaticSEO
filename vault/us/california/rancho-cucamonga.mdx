### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deep

Look, the AQI sitting at 137 is not a joke. The main culprit today is PM2.5, those tiny particulate matters that love to burrow deep into your lungs and cause all sorts of problems. AccuWeather's forecast also flags the air as "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups," noting high levels of Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) as well. If you're going to push it, expect some throat irritation and maybe a cough that wasn't there before. Your lungs aren't going to thank you for this one.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: High AQI (137) and PM2.5 are serious risks today.**
Limit prolonged outdoor exertion, especially if you have respiratory issues. Consider moving your workout indoors to avoid inhaling unhealthy particulate matter.
</AlertBox>

### Heat: Stay Hydrated, Or Don't Go Out

You might think 87 degrees and clear skies sounds like prime running weather, but combine that with a less-than-stellar air quality, and you're inviting trouble. We've seen heat advisories recently, and today's temperature is definitely in that "take it easy" zone. If you absolutely *must* be outside, stay in the shade, keep it short, and chug water like it's your job. Seriously.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Elevated temperatures increase the risk of heat exhaustion and dehydration.**
If you're exercising outdoors, stick to the coolest parts of the day and hydrate aggressively. Watch for signs of heat-related illness.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen: Just Another Annoyance

The "Moderate" pollen levels, predominantly from Poplar/Aspen/Cottonwood, Oak, and Cedar/Juniper, are just the cherry on top of this lovely environmental sundae. For most, it's an itchy eye or a sniffle. For allergy sufferers, it's going to make that poor air quality feel even worse.

Rancho Cucamonga is always building something, and right now, local street paving projects are causing headaches with parking restrictions and temporary street closures.
