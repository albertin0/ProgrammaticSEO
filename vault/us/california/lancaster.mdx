<BulletList>

Combine that with our already toasty 28.4°C (that's about 83°F, for those of you not on the metric system). Dehydration and heat exhaustion are not good workout partners.
*   **Gusty Winds:** If the heat wasn't enough, we've got a High Wind Warning along the Highway 14 corridor, which means gusts up to 55 mph. That's not a refreshing breeze; that's a direct assault on your balance, energy, and anything not bolted down. Forget that long run at the Antelope Valley California Poppy Reserve; you'll be running *with* the poppies, whether you like it or not.
*   **Moderate AQI & Pollen:** While our Air Quality Index is currently sitting at a "Moderate" 53, and pollen is also "Moderate", these aren't *deal-breakers* on their own. But when you add them to the high heat and ridiculous winds, your lungs are going to be working overtime just to keep you upright.
