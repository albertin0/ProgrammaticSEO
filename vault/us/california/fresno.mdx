Another day, another opportunity to question your life choices, Fresno. Today? You *can* get outside, but you’ll be baking like a Raisinette and probably inhaling some secondhand smoke from our annual fiery season.

<BulletList>
*   It's a hot one, folks. 30.7°C (that’s 87°F for the rest of us) in the shade, and there ain’t much of that in some spots. Plan to be done before noon, or after the sun goes down.
*   That "Good" AQI of 45 is a nice thought, but don't get too comfy. Wildfires are still burning around us, and that smoke has a habit of making its way into the Valley, especially as the day heats up and inversion layers do their thing.
*   Pollen's doing its usual thing – moderate, so your sinuses might throw a mild fit, but your lungs should mostly be clear, assuming the smoke doesn't kick in.

Look, I get it. We live here, we run here. But let's not pretend today is some perfect autumn breeze. Your lungs and joints are going to feel that heat, no matter how much you tell yourself you're "acclimated." Eighty-seven degrees is hot, plain and simple. Head out too late, and you’ll be sucking wind hotter than a car exhaust pipe.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Air Quality & Ozone Spike Risk

While the current AQI is sitting pretty at 45 (Good) for Ozone/Other pollutants, the real threat is what happens later. We've got active wildfires up in Mariposa County, and an Air Quality Alert has been in effect for the entire San Joaquin Valley, specifically warning about particulate matter and potential ozone spikes, especially during afternoon hours when a strong high-pressure system traps emissions. That means your "good" morning air can turn into a hazy, lung-irritating mess by the time you're hitting your afternoon stride. If you start feeling any irritation, cut your run short and get inside.
</AlertBox>

Weeds are the main culprits right now. If you're susceptible, expect the usual itchy eyes and sniffles. Nothing a good shower and a neti pot can't mostly handle, but combine it with smoke, and you've got a recipe for a truly miserable outdoor experience.

The San Joaquin River Parkway is always a popular spot, but consider doing an out-and-back early this morning, or waiting until the sun dips. The river breeze helps, but it won't magically make 87 degrees feel cool. Plus, if any of that wildfire smoke decides to roll in, it'll likely settle over the river basin first.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Chaos Continues

Don't think you can just cruise to your favorite running spot without hitting a detour. High-Speed Rail construction is still a thing, and we're seeing closures along McKinley and West Avenues that have been in place for months and will continue through the end of the year. And don't even get me started on the BNSF Blackstone McKinley Grade Separation project – construction is *supposed* to be in full swing this year, meaning even more traffic headaches near Fresno City College. Plan your routes. Add extra time. Or just accept that you'll be doing some unexpected urban exploration.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated and Heat Aware

Even though it's not a full-blown "heat advisory," 87°F is still plenty hot enough to cause trouble. Drink more water than you think you need, before, during, and after your workout. Seriously. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or your head starts pounding, your body is telling you to stop being a hero and cool down. Find some shade, get some water, or head home. Your PR isn't worth a trip to Saint Agnes.
</AlertBox>
