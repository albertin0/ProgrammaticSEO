## Today's Vitals: Clear, Cool, and Cursed with Construction

Looks like the Bay Area finally shook off that weird "cut-off low" weather pattern that dumped nearly a month's worth of rain on us yesterday. Today's "Mainly Clear" forecast is a welcome sight for sore eyes, but don't be fooled by the sunshine. The city always has a trick up its sleeve. We're looking at a cool 11.7°C (53°F), which is prime running weather if you dress right, especially with a predicted 16 MPH WSW breeze kicking up in the afternoon.

The air quality, bless its heart, is fantastic with an AQI of 28. That's a solid win. However, if your sinuses are already staging a protest, remember we've got moderate pollen levels out there.

## Navigating the Urban Jungle: Closures & Critters

Now for the fun stuff – actually trying to run somewhere.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Twin Peaks Promenade Project is a MESS:** If your usual hill repeats take you up Twin Peaks, you're out of luck. The Promenade Project officially kicked off construction in early May, and the *entire project area* is closed for approximately nine months. That includes the **Crestline Trail**. You'll be diverted north to a connecting trail towards Christmas Tree Point, but honestly, consider an alternate route entirely for a while. This isn't a "minor inconvenience" kind of closure; it's a "find another mountain" kind of closure.
</AlertBox>

Beyond Twin Peaks, the Golden Gate National Parks system has its own array of ongoing headaches. The **Old Springs Trail near Tennessee Valley** has been closed since March for boardwalk replacement and isn't expected to reopen until early August. Upper Fort Mason also has various road and parking area closures that will stretch through Fall 2026. Always check the GGNPC website before heading out to those spots.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Presidio Trail Etiquette (and Safety):** It's coyote pupping season in the Presidio, which runs from April to October. This means segments of the **Park Trail** and **Bay Area Ridge Trail** are closed to dog walkers to prevent conflicts. Keep your dogs on a short leash, clean up after them, and for the love of all that is holy, do NOT feed the wildlife. You're visiting *their* home.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Mind the Muck:** We got some decent rain yesterday, which is great for the parched landscape but potentially less great for your ankles. Expect some trails, especially those with poor drainage or in shaded areas, to be muddy and slick. Keep your eyes peeled and maybe opt for some trail shoes if you're hitting anything unpaved.
</AlertBox>

The city's always a moving target, but with a little planning, you can still get your miles in without running into a literal roadblock.
