<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Muck: Coastal Flooding
The National Weather Service has issued a Coastal Flood Advisory that's still active until 6 PM PDT on Saturday. This isn't just a little puddle; we're talking about up to 1.9 feet of inundation above ground level in low-lying areas around the San Francisco Bay. That means isolated road closures and potential coastal erosion. If your usual route takes you near the water – say, along the Embarcadero or even parts of Crissy Field – be prepared to pivot. Don't risk a twisted ankle (or worse, a dunking) trying to power through water of unknown depth. Turn around. Your dry feet will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Twin Peaks: Closed for Business (and Your Workout)
Heads up, hill seekers. The Twin Peaks Promenade Project construction is well underway, and the entire project area, including the Crestline Trail, is **closed to the public for approximately nine months**. Yeah, nine months. So unless you want to climb over construction fencing and risk a tetanus shot, find another scenic overlook for your vertical challenge. They're diverting folks north to a connecting trail leading to Christmas Tree Point, but it's not the same. Plan accordingly and find an open incline elsewhere.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Weekend Event Prep
While today is Friday, remember that tomorrow is Halloween, and Sunday is the Golden Gate Half Marathon. Expect significant street closures and reroutes around places like Bernal Heights, the Castro, and especially the Golden Gate Bridge, Crissy Field, and Van Ness Avenue on Sunday. If you have big weekend plans that involve these areas, scope out alternatives now. Trying to run a half marathon on the same day as another half marathon (or any major event) is just asking for a bad time.
</AlertBox>
