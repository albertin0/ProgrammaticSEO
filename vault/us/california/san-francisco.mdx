## Local Lowdown

### Golden Gate Park: The Never-Ending Construction Zone

Seriously, what *isn't* under construction in this city? If you're planning a run or ride through Golden Gate Park today, especially around the eastern edge, be aware. The "Stanyan Street Sidewalk Improvements" are still chugging along, promising pedestrian and bicycle detours between Fell and Hayes streets. It’s supposed to wrap up by the end of this month, so we’re in the final stretch of dodging cones and listening to jackhammers. And don't forget the ongoing work on the JFK Promenade; while they're supposedly "nearly complete" with crosswalk upgrades, expect intermittent closures and detours. Just assume you’ll have to improvise your route, because San Francisco just *loves* keeping us on our toes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect detours, shifted traffic lanes, and potential noise around Stanyan Street (between Fell and Hayes) and sections of JFK Promenade in Golden Gate Park due to ongoing construction. Watch for signage and flaggers.
</AlertBox>

### Presidio Pups and Coyote Pups: A Canine Conundrum

For all you dog owners who like to hit the trails, listen up: The Presidio Trust has segments of the Park Trail and the Bay Area Ridge Trail *closed to dog walkers* until October. Why? "Coyote pupping season." Apparently, mama coyotes get a little extra protective of their dens, and frankly, I don't blame them. So, if your four-legged friend usually joins you, make sure you check the Presidio Trust's website for exactly which sections are off-limits. Seriously, don't be that person who ignores the signs and ends up in a standoff with a territorial wild animal. You might think your poodle is tough, but a coyote with pups will absolutely humble them.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads up, dog owners:** Specific sections of the Park Trail and Bay Area Ridge Trail in the Presidio are closed to dogs (on or off-leash) through October due to coyote pupping season. Plan alternate routes to avoid conflicts.
</AlertBox>
