Alright, San Francisco, another Wednesday is upon us, and if you can brave the usual city-wide game of dodge-the-cone, it's a perfectly acceptable day to get your miles in.

They're still tearing up sections of **19th Avenue** with various repaving and barrier work, and don't even get me started on the freeway rehabilitation projects for the Central (US-101) and Bayshore (I-80) viaducts, causing intermittent delays and ramp closures.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Gaps (and the cones)
Construction is omnipresent. Expect lane closures and general chaos, especially if your route takes you near major thoroughfares like 19th Avenue or freeway on/off-ramps. Always assume a driver isn't looking for you, and watch out for uneven surfaces, loose gravel, and surprise detours. Those overnight barrier jobs on US-101? The lingering effects might make your morning commute or ride a bit more exciting than you planned.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Coyote Territory in the Presidio
If you're running through the Presidio with your dog, remember: segments of the Park Trail (from Mountain Lake to the Presidio Promenade) and the Bay Area Ridge Trail (from Rob Hill Campground to the Presidio Golf Course) are closed to dog walkers through October. Coyote parents are extra protective right now, and you do *not* want to find out just how protective. Even without a dog, be aware of your surroundings; these aren't your friendly park squirrels.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Dress for the Marine Layer's Mood Swings
It's overcast and cool, classic San Francisco. That means it could stay that way, or the fog could burn off and suddenly you're sweating. Layers, people, layers! Start with something light, and have a windbreaker handy. Nobody likes being caught out in a chilly drizzle under a thick marine layer in just a tank top.
</AlertBox>

All things considered, the weather's holding up for some decent outdoor activity, provided you're ready to navigate the urban jungle's quirks.
