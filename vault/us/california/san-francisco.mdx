### Air Quality & Allergens

The "Mainly Clear" skies at 14.4°C might look inviting, but don't let the visual fool you entirely. We're sitting at an AQI of 53, pushed into the "Moderate" category primarily by PM2.5 particulate matter. This is a friendly reminder from that Woodside Fire up in Sonoma County that was causing "moderate" air quality in the city just a few days ago. While a Spare the Air alert was in effect through Saturday, the smoke lingers, so those with sensitive respiratory systems might feel it. Combine that with a "Moderate" pollen count, and your lungs might be doing a bit more work than they'd prefer. This isn't the kind of deep breathing that feels refreshing.

### Trail & Road Blocks

Now, for the real fun: navigating the city. If your usual stomping grounds include Golden Gate Park, brace yourself. The Outside Lands Music Festival is officially starting its setup, which means **JFK Drive from Transverse to 36th Avenue is closed starting today, August 3rd.** Don't even think about trying to snake your way through; they mean business. If you planned a long run through the park, find an alternative *now*.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Golden Gate Park Lockdown:** JFK Drive is closed for Outside Lands setup, effective today. Seriously, runners, cyclists, dog walkers – plan a completely different route for the entire park if you want to avoid a headache until after the festival.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the festival frenzy, construction continues its slow march across the city. The **Interior Greenbelt Edgewood Trail** remains closed indefinitely due to a water main break, and they're just starting to install a temporary bypass line this week. Also, if you fancy a run or ride through the Presidio, remember **Fox Trail is closed for maintenance until August 7th** during weekday work hours. Roads and parking at Upper Fort Mason also have ongoing closures for seismic retrofits.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Navigation:** Always check SFMTA updates if your route involves major streets like Howard Street, which has intermittent lane closures for the Transbay Streetscape Project. Assume delays and plan detours.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Perks & Quirks

On the bright side, the temperature is a comfortable 14.4°C (about 58°F) and it's "Mainly Clear." However, don't expect the usual "Fogust" dense marine layer along the coast; El Niño is keeping things a bit clearer and more humid this year. So, while you might get a bit more sun than typical for early August, you might also feel that extra stickiness in the air. Just a heads up for your hydration strategy.

Overall, today's workout demands extra planning. Check those routes, consider your lungs, and maybe hit a neighborhood loop instead of battling park closures.
