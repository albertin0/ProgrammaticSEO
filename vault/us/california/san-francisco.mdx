## Local Hazards & Annoyances

### Watch Your Step (and Your Dog) in the Presidio
Look, I get it, you love your dog. But if you're planning a run or hike in the Presidio, leave Fido at home or stick to designated areas. Segments of the **Park Trail** (from Mountain Lake to the Presidio Promenade) and the **Bay Area Ridge Trail** (from Rob Hill Campground to the Presidio Golf Course) are still strictly off-limits to dog walkers. Why? Coyote pupping season, which runs through October. These coyotes are protective, and you don't want to find out just how protective the hard way.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger: Coyote Pupping Season in Presidio!** Several trails in the Presidio, including parts of the Park Trail and Bay Area Ridge Trail, are closed to dog walkers through October. Coyotes are especially protective of their pups and may act aggressively towards dogs. Leave your canine companions at home or use open dog-friendly trails only.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Traffic Snafus
Our fair city is always under construction, and this week is no different. If your route takes you anywhere near 19th Avenue, especially between Sloat Boulevard and Holloway Avenue, prepare for a headache. The third weekend closure for the 19th Avenue Rehabilitation Project starts Friday, May 22nd, and runs through Monday, May 25th. Two lanes will be shut down, meaning major delays and detours. It's going to be a mess, so plan alternate routes *now*.

Additionally, don't forget the ongoing work on Howard Street between The Embarcadero and 4th Street, which is adding new bike lanes and other "improvements." Expect intermittent delays and lane closures on weekdays. And if you're an early bird or late-night warrior on two wheels, remember the overnight lane closures on the Bay Bridge for repainting and rehabilitation, happening from Sunday nights to Friday mornings.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Warning: Major Construction on 19th Avenue This Weekend!** Starting Friday, May 22nd and running through Monday, May 25th, 19th Avenue between Sloat Blvd and Holloway Ave will have two lanes closed. Expect significant traffic impacts and plan alternative routes.
</AlertBox>

### Don't Be a Victim: Car Break-Ins Still a Thing
It's a broken record, I know, but seriously, don't leave anything visible in your car, especially near popular trailheads and park parking lots. Even if it's just a grocery bag, assume someone wants it. Remove all valuables, lock your doors, and take your phone and electronics with you. A gym locker thief was just arrested who hit multiple fitness studios in the Marina, but the general rule still applies everywhere. Don't make it easy for them.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up: Park Smart.** Always remove all valuables from your car and lock your doors, even for a quick stop at a trailhead or scenic overlook. Auto-thefts remain a risk in park parking areas.
</AlertBox>
