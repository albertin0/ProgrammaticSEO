Now, for the stuff that actually matters on the ground.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wind Gusts on Exposed Routes:** AccuWeather is reporting average winds around 12 mph from the WSW, but gusts could hit 28 mph. If you're planning a run along the Embarcadero or over near Crissy Field, be ready to fight a headwind or get pushed around a bit. It’s not gale-force, but it’s enough to mess with your stride if you're not expecting it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Dreamforce Devastation in SoMa:** Look, if you're anywhere near Moscone Center today, you're going to hit a wall of humanity. The Salesforce Dreamforce Convention is in full swing until Sunday, and today (Thursday, Sept 17) is a peak day. Expect significant traffic and pedestrian congestion, particularly on Howard Street between 3rd and 4th, and on 4th Street from Market Street down to Mission. Trying to run through that gridlock is an exercise in futility and frustration. Just avoid the whole area. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Bernal Heights Trail Closure (Still):** Heads up for you south-city folks. A section of the trail on the southern side of Bernal Heights Park, specifically by Ellsworth Street, is still closed for restoration and construction throughout September. You can still use other parts of the network, but don't count on a seamless loop through that particular section. Plan your detours or find another hill to conquer today.
</AlertBox>

Don't go expecting any "Spare the Air" alerts like we sometimes get with wildfire smoke or stagnant winter air – that’s not the issue today.
