## Local Hazards You Can't Ignore

Look, I get it. You want to get out there. But unless you've trained for obstacle courses involving thousands of revelers, give the Mission District a wide berth today. The **Carnaval San Francisco Festival and Grand Parade** is happening, transforming Harrison Street between 16th and 24th streets into a full-blown party zone from 10 AM to 6 PM. The parade itself kicks off at 10 AM, snaking from 24th and Bryant down Mission to 15th Street. Expect major street closures and enough foot traffic to make a marathon look like a leisurely stroll. Your "PR" today might just be successfully navigating the detours.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Carnaval Chaos in the Mission:** If your route involves Harrison Street or Mission Street in the Mission District, change it. Seriously. Unless your idea of a workout is weaving through a massive festival crowd and parade, you're going to have a bad time. Expect significant street closures and congestion from 10 AM to 6 PM.
</AlertBox>

Want to hit the beach? Think again. We've got a **Beach Hazard Statement** in effect through Monday morning for our west-facing beaches. Translation: strong northerly winds are whipping up rough surf and **dangerous rip and longshore currents**. That "refreshing breeze" is actually a gale warning for coastal waters, with winds up to 50 knots. So, unless you're a professional surfer with a death wish, stay out of the water and off those jetties, piers, and rocks. Don't be "that guy" the Coast Guard has to rescue because you thought a selfie was worth it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Coastal Waters & Beaches Are Dangerous:** A Beach Hazard Statement remains in effect through Monday morning. Strong winds are creating very rough conditions, with dangerous rip and longshore currents along west-facing beaches. A Gale Warning is also active for coastal waters. Stay *out* of the water and *off* coastal structures.
</AlertBox>

For those of you with canine running partners, listen up. The **Presidio Trust** still has parts of the **Park Trail** (from Mountain Lake to the Presidio Promenade) and the **Bay Area Ridge Trail** (from Rob Hill Campground to the Presidio Golf Course) closed to dog walkers. Why? Coyote pupping season. These wild canids are extra protective of their young right now, and they don't appreciate your dog's presence. Avoid conflicts, and find an alternative route for you and your four-legged friend. This closure is expected to last through October, so get used to it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Presidio Coyote Alert:** Sections of the Park Trail and Bay Area Ridge Trail in the Presidio are closed to dog walkers through October for coyote pupping season. Expect protective behavior from coyotes towards dogs. Plan alternate routes.
</AlertBox>

And finally, a perennial favorite: **construction**. Caltrans is still repaving southbound and northbound 19th Avenue between Holloway Avenue and Sloat Boulevard until Monday. Expect lane closures, parking removals, and general traffic headaches if you're trying to traverse that corridor. Plan for delays if your route crosses 19th Avenue.
