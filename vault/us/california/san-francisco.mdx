## Gridlock and Gulls

If you're thinking about a nice long run through **the Presidio**, think again. Not only is the Escape from Alcatraz Triathlon PTO biking and running through there all morning, turning large sections of the park into a closed course, but it's also prime coyote pupping season. Segments of the Park Trail and Bay Area Ridge Trail are already closed to dog walkers, but frankly, if a mama coyote thinks you're too close to her pups, she doesn't care if you've got a leash or not. Seriously, give wildlife a wide berth.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Event Overload:** Multiple events mean major road closures citywide today. The Sikh Day Parade on Market Street, the Union Street Festival on Union Street (Octavia to Fillmore), and the Festa Coloniale Italiana in North Beach (Stockton between Union and Filbert) will shut down huge swaths of the city. Public transit will be rerouted, and driving will be a nightmare. Plan your route meticulously or just stick to your neighborhood sidewalks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Beach Hazards:** If your workout involves Ocean Beach or any coastal swimming, DON'T. There's a beach hazard statement out for "big sneaker waves" and dangerous rip currents that will last through the morning. That cold Pacific water and a strong current are a deadly combo. Stick to the sand, if you must, but honestly, stay away from the water's edge.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Chaos:** While not a direct running hazard unless you're trying to sprint down Howard Street (The Embarcadero to 4th Street), construction for the Transbay Howard Streetscape Project means intermittent delays and lane closures are a daily reality. Just another reason to keep your head up and stay aware of your surroundings, even if you're just walking.
</AlertBox>

Frankly, today might be a good day for a home workout or hitting the gym. If you absolutely must get out there, be prepared for detours, crowds, and don't go near the ocean. You've been warned.
