Let's break it down. Your lungs are getting a treat today, with an AQI of 48 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Good" territory, so breathe deep. Just don't forget that "Moderate" pollen count, which is high enough to make some of us sneeze our way through the Embarcadero. Grab your meds if you're sensitive.

The weather is classic San Francisco for a May morning: cool at 11.8°C (around 53°F) and overcast. Expect that marine layer to hang around, keeping things on the cooler side along the coast and bay shoreline, while inland spots might see a bit more sun later. It'll be breezy, especially by the water, so layer up that windbreaker. No heavy rain on the forecast, just a slight 10% chance, meaning it's mostly dry, if a bit damp from the fog.

Now for the real pain in the glutes: trail access. If your go-to workout involves slogging up Twin Peaks for those "glorious views," you're out of luck, at least for part of it. The popular footpath that climbs from the neighborhood to the hilltops is reportedly closing in *early May* for a nine-month project to transform a roadway into the Twin Peaks Promenade. So, while the city's building something shiny for the future, your present-day hill climbing is getting rerouted. The Marview Trail on the west side of Twin Peaks *is* staying open, thankfully, if you still want a piece of that action.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Twin Peaks Footpath Closure Imminent!**
The main footpath up Twin Peaks is slated to close for approximately nine months starting this month for the Twin Peaks Promenade Project. Check local updates and plan alternative routes if this is part of your running or hiking routine.
</AlertBox>

For those of you running with canine companions, listen up. It's coyote pupping season in the Presidio, which means some trails are off-limits to dog walkers until October. Specifically, the Park Trail from Mountain Lake to the Presidio Promenade and the Bay Area Ridge Trail from Rob Hill Campground to the Presidio Golf Course are closed to dogs. These wild critters get territorial when they've got pups, so respect the closures to keep everyone safe.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Coyote Pupping Season in the Presidio!**
Sections of the Park Trail and Bay Area Ridge Trail are temporarily closed to dog walkers through October. Keep your furry friends on a short leash and on open trails, and report any aggressive coyote behavior.
</AlertBox>

Other ongoing construction includes the Old Springs Trail near Tennessee Valley, closed for boardwalk replacement until early August. McLaren Park's trail improvement project is "very close to completing," but you should still stick to designated paths. There's also freeway off-ramp construction overnight, which might snarl your drive *to* a trailhead tonight or tomorrow morning, but won't impact your run directly.

**Dress for the San Francisco Chill!**
With temps hovering around 12°C (53°F) and breezy, overcast conditions, layers are your friend. A light jacket or windbreaker is a good call, especially if you're hitting coastal paths or open hills.
