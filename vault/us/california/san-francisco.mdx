## The Bay's Got Issues: High Tides and Wet Trails

Alright, San Francisco. Let's get real about today's outdoor grind. Your lungs are in for a treat with an Air Quality Index of a stellar 40, thanks to "Ozone/Other" being the dominant pollutant, which is essentially a non-issue at this level. No wildfire smoke choking us out, so breathe easy on that front. Pollen's moderate, so if you're usually a sneezin' mess, maybe pop an antihistamine, but it's nothing to write home (or your doctor) about.

Weather-wise, it's a classic San Francisco morning: cool at 13.7°C (about 57°F) and overcast. Don't get complacent, though; the clouds are expected to burn off for a sunnier afternoon. You'll also be dealing with some onshore winds, blowing 10 to 20 mph with gusts up to 30 mph in spots, particularly near SFO. Layer up, because that wind off the water can be sneaky cold.

### Coastal Flood Advisory: Don't Get Your Feet Wet

Here's the kicker for anyone planning a run or ride along the water: a Coastal Flood Advisory is in effect for bayshore locations around San Francisco, San Pablo, and Monterey bays. This advisory kicked off at 11 a.m. today and runs through Thursday, October 1st at 5 p.m. We're talking about unusually high tides pushing up to 1.2 feet of water above ground level in low-lying areas near shorelines and tidal waterways. This isn't about rain, it's about the bay trying to reclaim some turf.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger: Coastal Trails and Beaches May Be Inaccessible!** Expect significant inundation on waterfront paths, low-lying roads, and bayside parks. If your route takes you anywhere near the water, particularly along the Embarcadero or other low-lying bayfront areas, you *will* encounter standing water or entirely submerged sections. Turn around, find an alternate route, or resign yourself to a very wet run. Seriously, don't risk it.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Zone Headaches

Beyond the natural hazards, our fair city is always under some form of renovation.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Warning: Construction Closures in Effect.** There's intermittent road closure today on Field Road near Battery Wallace in the Presidio until 4:30 p.m. for tree removal. Also, if you were eyeing a workout near China Beach, forget about it for a while. The bathhouse, lower terrace, and picnic areas are undergoing renovations all fall and winter, plus the SFPUC is messing with Sea Cliff Avenue for waterline replacement, impacting access. Expect ongoing delays and lane closures on Howard Street between The Embarcadero and 4th Street for the Transbay Streetscape Project, usually weekdays 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Plan your detours now.
</AlertBox>
