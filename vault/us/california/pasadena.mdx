## What to Watch Out For

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Afternoon Heat
A **Heat Advisory** is in effect for Pasadena from 10 AM to 6 PM. Temperatures will soar into the high 80s and low 90s. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. If you're out there pushing it, you're risking heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and dehydration. Seriously, nobody needs that kind of bragging rights.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Lingering Winds & Debris
Even if the main Wind Advisory for Pasadena expired yesterday, areas in the nearby mountains are still under an advisory until 3 PM today with gusts up to 70 mph. Expect some lingering gusty winds, especially in the foothills or more exposed areas like parts of the Rose Bowl Loop. Watch your footing and any falling debris. Unsecured objects can become projectiles, and nobody wants a rogue trash can to end their run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Best Bet for a Workout
If you absolutely *must* run outside today, get it done early. Like, sunrise early. Once that sun starts climbing, those temperatures are going to rocket. Carry more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, breathable clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or like you're cooking from the inside out, stop. Go inside. Drink some water. Your workout can wait. Also, consider indoor alternatives or water-based activities during the heat advisory.
</AlertBox>
