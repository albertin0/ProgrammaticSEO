### Coastal Chaos: The Peninsula's Peril

Listen up, my fellow pavement pounders and trail blazers, especially if your usual route takes you anywhere near the water. The National Weather Service issued a Coastal Flood Advisory and a High Surf Advisory that are both active until 5 PM PDT today, Tuesday, September 29th. We're talking moderate coastal flooding expected and breaking waves of 4 to 7 feet with dangerous rip currents.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DO NOT underestimate the ocean today.** Stay clear of the water and be extremely cautious on beaches and any low-lying coastal paths. Those rip currents will drag you out faster than you can say "Long Beach Marathon isn't for another few weeks." Seriously, save the heroics for race day.
</AlertBox>

Specifically, the Long Beach Peninsula is still recovering from some recent hurricane-charged swells that tore up the boardwalk and exposed the bulkhead. The city's been trying to shore it up, but it remains vulnerable. So, if your usual run takes you along the Peninsula's coastline, reconsider that route today. You don't want to become part of the emergency repair budget.

### Inland Intel: Construction and Clear Skies

Away from the immediate coast, things are looking pretty good. The air quality index (AQI) is sitting at a moderate 51, with Ozone being the dominant pollutant. This is on the lower end of "moderate," so most of you shouldn't feel much impact. Pollen levels are also moderate. The 11.4°C (that's about 52.5°F for you Fahrenheit diehards) and mainly clear skies are actually perfect running weather. Layer up a bit, and you'll be golden.

However, don't forget the eternal Long Beach construction. The Shoemaker Bridge replacement project means ongoing lane and ramp closures that can mess with your commute or bike route. Keep an eye out for work crews and unexpected detours, especially if you're navigating around Downtown or the Port area. Also, there's some buzz about an Anaheim Street Corridor Improvement Project, so expect potential disruptions there down the line too.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect detours and potential traffic delays if your route involves the Shoemaker Bridge or surrounding downtown/Port area roadways. Always yield to construction vehicles and follow posted signage.
</AlertBox>
