<AlertBox type="danger">
#### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: This isn't a "push through it" day.
With temperatures soaring to 36.5°C, the risk of heat stroke, heat exhaustion, and severe dehydration is extremely high. Your body's ability to cool itself is severely compromised at these temperatures, especially with high exertion. Forget your personal best; you're risking an ambulance ride. Even short, easy walks can be dangerous in this kind of heat. If you *must* go outside, stick to the absolute earliest morning hours or after sunset, and even then, be hyper-aware.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Moderate AQI with PM2.5 - Your Lungs Will Thank You for Staying Inside
An AQI of 128, with PM2.5 as the main offender, means there's enough microscopic junk in the air to irritate your respiratory system. When you're breathing heavily during a run or bike ride, you're essentially vacuuming that stuff deep into your lungs. People with asthma or other respiratory conditions, you already know the drill: stay indoors. For everyone else, consider this a prime day for indoor cardio or strength training.
</AlertBox>
