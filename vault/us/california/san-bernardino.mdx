Today is a prime example of why you can't just glance at the temperature and call it a good day to push your limits outside.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
*   **Wildfire Smoke's a Sneaky Bastard:** There's a fast-moving wildfire, the Springs Fire, burning east of Moreno Valley. While it's in Riverside County, the smoke is spreading and we've got a wind advisory, meaning that crud is absolutely getting blown our way. Even if our AQI isn't screaming "DANGER!" just yet, don't be a hero and breathe in that particulate matter. Your lungs will hate you later.
*   **Wind Advisory is No Joke:** The National Weather Service issued a wind advisory for San Bernardino and Riverside County valleys through Saturday afternoon, with gusts up to 50 mph expected. That's not a gentle breeze for your run; that's "tree limbs could be blown down and power outages may result" territory. It's also "fall on your face and sprain an ankle trying to hold your stride" territory.
*   **Pollen's Doing Its Thing:** While not the main event, the moderate pollen count (from the provided data, though some local forecasts are calling it high) is just another irritant when combined with wildfire smoke.
</BulletList>

Look, the mercury might be sitting pretty at 20.6°C (a pleasant 69°F for those still using imperial units), and the sky's "mainly clear" from a superficial glance. But real clear skies don't come with wildfire plumes on the horizon and gusts trying to redecorate your backyard with your patio furniture.

Forget trying to tackle anything serious out in the San Bernardino National Forest today. Not only do you have the fresh wildfire smoke making things questionable, but parts of the forest, including areas around Gray's Peak, are still under various closures from past storm damage, ongoing eagle habitat protection, and previous fire activity (like the Line Fire closure order that was active in late 2024). The last thing you need is to be out there dealing with unexpected trail conditions or breathing in that smoke when you're already huffing and puffing.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Springs Fire Smoke & High Wind Advisory!
The Springs Fire is generating significant smoke, and a Wind Advisory with gusts up to 50 mph is in effect for San Bernardino County. Visibility could drop, and air quality can deteriorate rapidly, especially for PM2.5, which is already our dominant pollutant. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity until conditions improve. Strong winds also mean unstable footing and potential flying debris.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Air Quality for Sensitive Groups
Our AQI is sitting at 65. While technically "moderate," the presence of PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant combined with wildfire smoke and high winds means sensitive groups (that's you, asthma sufferers and anyone with respiratory issues) should seriously consider moving their workout indoors. Even if you're tough, why challenge your lungs unnecessarily?
</AlertBox>

#### Stay Hydrated (Indoors)!
Even if you're just sitting on the couch watching reruns, stay hydrated. Your body works harder in smoky, windy conditions, even if you're not exerting yourself directly. And if you *do* brave a short, low-intensity outdoor walk, make sure that water bottle is full.

But sometimes the smartest thing you can do for your fitness is to skip the outside misery and hit the treadmill or the stationary bike, or just do some bodyweight stuff in your living room. There will be better days, San Bernardino. Today's just not one of 'em for a full-on outdoor grind.
