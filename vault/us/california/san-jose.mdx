### Local Hazards & Hot Takes

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, It's Hot. Think Indoors.
A Heat Advisory means business. We're talking mid-90s in some inland areas today, which is prime for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. If you *must* be outside, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does), wear light-colored, loose clothing, and stick to the early morning or late evening. Midday workouts are for people who enjoy calling 911. Don't leave your dog out there, don't leave your kids in the car. Just use common sense, people.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Crowds and Closures
Good luck finding solitude today. If you were planning a peaceful long run, you might be out of luck. Hellyer County Park is hosting the 4th Annual Picnic by the Lake from 10 AM to 3 PM, so expect significant crowds and potential congestion in and around the park. Similarly, Calero County Park has an organized hike this afternoon.

Also, if you use **Ewert Road** to get to the Guadalupe River Trail, be aware that a planned October 1st closure for airport-related work likely means that route is still impacted. This could force cyclists and pedestrians onto less safe alternatives. And let's not forget the city's massive street paving project for 2026; if your favorite running street is on the "in progress" list, expect lane closures and detours. Always check local signage, because nobody wants to run into a fresh layer of asphalt.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### E-Bikes and Shared Paths: New Rules, Same Courtesy
California just passed some new e-bike laws, limiting youth access and clarifying rules, largely in response to a rise in collisions. This is a good thing for shared paths like the Los Gatos Creek Trail or the Coyote Creek Trail. While these new rules are rolling out, remember your trail etiquette: slow down, announce yourself, and for the love of all that is holy, don't ride like you're in the Tour de France on a crowded path. The laws might be changing, but basic human courtesy shouldn't.
</AlertBox>
