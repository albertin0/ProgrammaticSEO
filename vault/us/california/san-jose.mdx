## The Heat Is No Joke

Look, I get it. The sky is "Mainly Clear" and the sun feels great on your skin for about five minutes. But 31.0°C (87.8°F) for an outdoor workout in San Jose is borderline idiotic, especially mid-day. Your body is going to be working overtime just to keep itself cool. That's less energy for your actual workout and more for avoiding heat exhaustion or, worse, heatstroke.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, folks, 31.0°C is dangerously hot for strenuous outdoor activity. Dehydration will hit you faster than a cyclist on Stevens Creek Canyon Road. Plan your run for dawn, or just hit the gym. Or, you know, a pool.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* run outside, you need to adjust. Go early, *before* the sun really starts to bake the pavement, or wait until after dusk, if it cools down enough. Carry more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear. Don't be that person collapsed by the side of the Los Gatos Creek Trail, waiting for a medic because you thought you were invincible.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Key
With this heat, you need to pre-hydrate. Start drinking water *before* your run, carry it with you, and re-hydrate afterward. Water isn't optional today; it's survival.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Conditions & Closures

While the air is pristine, and you might think the trails are calling, hold up. If you were thinking of heading up to Máyyan 'Ooyákma – Coyote Ridge, forget it. That preserve is closed today for "resource management" and won't reopen until tomorrow, May 6th. Always, *always* check local park and open space websites before you head out. Santa Clara County Parks, for example, lists various long-term closures like parts of Anderson Lake County Park through 2032 due to dam construction, and intermittent construction on sections of the Alviso Slough Trail. While those aren't directly impacting all San Jose trails today, it's a good reminder that our "urban network" isn't always fully open.

So, weigh your options. If you're going out, be smart about it. Or, you know, consider a treadmill. No shame in self-preservation.
