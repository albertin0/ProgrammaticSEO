### Know Your County Park Closures
Don't just show up to Anderson Lake County Park hoping for a scenic long run. Large sections, including the Serpentine Trail, Dam Crest Woodchoppers Flat, and parts of the Lakeview Trail, are basically a construction zone until *2032* thanks to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Your usual routes there? Probably gone. Same goes for Uvas Canyon County Park, where multiple trails and a bridge are still closed for repairs and safety concerns. You even need a reservation just to get into Uvas these days.
</AlertBox>

If your route takes you through Alviso Marina County Park, be aware there are intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail. They're doing hauling and construction out there, so expect some detours or blocked paths on weekdays.

For those of you who prefer to get your miles in downtown, the good news is the air is great, but watch out for construction closures if you're navigating by foot or bike. Market Street, St. James Street, and Park Avenue have ongoing lane closures that might mess with your urban loop. You won't be running into any wildfires on Park Ave, but you might run into a crane.

So, get out there. The conditions are fantastic for a run, ride, or hike, especially if you're staying local to San Jose proper and away from the major county park construction zones. Just be smart, check specific trail websites if you're heading out to the county parks, and enjoy this brief, glorious window of non-smoky, pleasant weather. It won't last forever, this is California after all.
