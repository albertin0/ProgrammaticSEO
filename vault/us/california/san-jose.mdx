## The Rundown

Alright, San Jose, let's talk. Today's starting off with a nice, cool 67.5°F (19.7°C) under some overcast skies, which sounds like perfect running weather to some of you masochists. But don't get too comfortable. That marine layer is going to burn off, and we're looking at highs creeping up to a respectable 89°F (32°C) later in the day. So, if you're planning a lunch run, you're going to feel it.

The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 25, with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant. That's fantastic for your lungs, especially after the fire warnings we've seen lately. However, yesterday, a new wildfire, the Duvall Fire, was reported in Santa Clara County. While the smoke hasn't apparently choked us out yet, critically dry conditions across California mean we can't afford to be complacent. Wildfires are like a bad ex: they can flare up when you least expect it and ruin your day. Keep an eye on the sky and check those real-time air quality maps if you're out for an extended period.

Pollen's still lingering at a moderate level, which for some of you means a runny nose and itchy eyes. For others, it's a good excuse to call it a day and hit the couch. Either way, be aware.
