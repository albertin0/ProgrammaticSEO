Seriously, folks, stepping out onto the Los Gatos Creek Trail right now for anything more than a leisurely stroll with plenty of water is just asking for trouble. We had heat advisories plastered all over the Bay Area just a couple of weeks ago, warning of "moderate risk of heat-related illness" when it was in the low 90s. We're not far off that today, and your body isn't acclimated to this kind of March heat.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors!
With the mercury hitting 31.0°C (87.8°F), outdoor strenuous activity is highly dangerous. San Jose has been experiencing record-high temperatures this March. This is the kind of heat where you need to take "common-sense precautions" like drinking water regularly and avoiding strenuous activity between 10 AM and 5 PM. Your run can wait.
</AlertBox>
