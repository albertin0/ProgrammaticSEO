## The Heat Is On (Again)

Look, I know we live in the Bay Area, and sometimes we get that "perfect 70-degree" weather. Today isn't one of those days, at least not for long. The temperature is already hitting 31.0°C (that's 88°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit). While our AQI is a respectable 39, meaning you won't be choking on anything nasty in the air, that heat is no joke. A few days ago, the Santa Clara Valley was under a heat advisory with temps expected to hit the mid-90s to low 100s, and while today isn't *that* extreme, it's a reminder that this time of year, you need to be smart. Hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does when you're pushing it outside), and if you're hitting the pavement, stick to the shaded routes or suffer the consequences of asphalt radiating heat back at you.

## Know Your Trails: Closures and Detours

This is where things get a bit annoying if you don't check ahead. Our beloved trail network is under various stages of "improvement" and "maintenance," which translates to detours and outright closures.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Heading out near Skyport Drive? Be aware that the lower trail south of Skyport Drive and the upper trail north of Skyport Drive are both experiencing weekday closures until 3:00 PM today, August 14th. Plan an alternate route or just wait until later.
</AlertBox>

If the Los Alamitos Creek Trail is your go-to, particularly around Mazzone Drive, expect to see crews out doing vegetation removal. The good news is the trail remains open, but you'll encounter flaggers guiding you. Pay attention to them; they're trying to keep you safe from heavy equipment, not just waving hello.

And for those who venture further south into the Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District, specifically Bear Creek Redwoods Preserve, several trails like Toad Hollow, Myotis, and Lower Pete Siemens are closed until September 2026. This is for some significant construction, including a new parking area and a new accessible loop trail. Don't try to sneak through; these closures are for your safety.

<AlertBox type="info">
  Always check the official Santa Clara Valley Water District and Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District websites before you head out, especially if you're exploring new routes or haven't been on a specific trail recently. Construction schedules can shift faster than your pacing on mile 20.
</AlertBox>

Pavement improvements on The Alameda are also on the horizon, so if you run or bike through that area, keep an eye out for future impacts on major streets, not just trails.

## Pollen Outlook

Your sinuses get a moderate break today. The pollen level is "Moderate," so it's not likely to completely derail your run unless you're extremely sensitive. A little congestion or a few sneezes, maybe, but nothing like peak allergy season.

All in all, today's workout is manageable if you respect the heat and the current trail disruptions. Get out there, but be smart about it.
