### Heat Hazard: Seriously, Stay Indoors During Peak Hours
Look, I get it, you want to get your miles in. But with the temperature hovering around 31°C, you're not proving anything by risking heat stroke. The City of San Jose's own safety guidelines tell you to limit physical activity and avoid strenuous efforts between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m.. This isn't a suggestion; it's basic self-preservation. If you absolutely MUST be outside, go before the sun has a chance to fully weaponize itself, or wait until dusk. Carry more water than you think you need, and then add another bottle. Dehydration can creep up on you faster than a runner cutting you off at mile 20.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Pollen: A Double Whammy for Your Lungs
An AQI of 55 with ozone as the main culprit, combined with moderate pollen, means your respiratory system is working overtime today. While not a full-blown "Spare the Air Alert" for ozone, those conditions usually get triggered in these warmer months. And for the love of all that is holy, take an antihistamine if you're prone to seasonal sniffles; you'll already be breathing hard enough.
