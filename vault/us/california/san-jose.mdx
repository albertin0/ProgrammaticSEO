#### Extreme Heat Warning (Seriously, Don't Be Dumb)

The 31°C temperature is no joke. The NWS has a *Heat Advisory* in effect for Santa Clara Valley, including San Jose, all day today. This isn't just "uncomfortable" heat; it's "can land you in the ER" heat if you push it. Heat illness is a serious and potentially deadly hazard. If you absolutely *must* get your miles in, do it before the sun fully commits to baking us, or wait until after sunset. Carry way more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, loose clothing, and know the signs of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Your PR can wait. Your health cannot.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk

Our local trails are generally open, but there's always something. The Santa Clara Valley Water District is doing vegetation removal along sections of the Guadalupe River Trail and Los Alamitos Creek Trail through early September. You'll see flaggers, so pay attention and don't assume you own the path. These crews are out there working, even in this heat, so give them space. You might hit small delays, but nothing major that should derail your (shortened, easy) workout.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Hydration is Your Best Friend

Given the heat, proper hydration isn't just a suggestion; it's a command. Start hydrating well before you head out, and continue throughout your activity. Electrolytes aren't just for marketing; they actually help in these conditions. Consider a vest or a handheld bottle with a decent capacity. Also, tell someone where you're going and when you expect to be back. Seriously.
</AlertBox>
