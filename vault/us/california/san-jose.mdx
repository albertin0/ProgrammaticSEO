If you're out on two wheels, or even just hoofing it, keep an eye out. It's not a race, but these delays will certainly eat into your PR attempt.

Winds are picking up a bit today, too. Northwest winds could gust up to 21 mph this afternoon. For my fellow marathoners, that's enough to feel it on exposed sections, especially if you're cycling. It's not a gale, but it's not nothing. Keep your sunglasses on tight and your hat from flying off into the Guadalupe River.

On the bright side, the air quality is officially "Good" with an AQI of 25. No wildfire smoke to worry about choking on today, which is a rare treat these days.
