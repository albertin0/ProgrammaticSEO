## Weather & Trail Conditions

It’s clear skies all day, which is great for visibility. We're starting cool at 17.2°C (around 63°F) this morning, but it's going to warm up significantly, with afternoon highs expected to reach 29°C (84°F). Yesterday had a heat advisory for inland areas, including San Jose, warning about temperatures in the 90s, so while today isn't *as* extreme, it's a good reminder that summer heat is here.

<AlertBox type="info">
Head out early if you're planning a longer run or a strenuous hike. By midday, things will be toasty. Seriously, bring water. Dehydration will absolutely tank your performance and is just plain stupid.
</AlertBox>

## Local Hazards

Beyond the daily grind, there aren't any *new* significant, widespread hazards to report in the last 24 hours. No sudden floods, no major wildfires choking our air (AQI is solid, remember?), and no crazy high winds to battle on the Hellyer Park trails.

However, the ongoing Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project continues to impact runners and hikers around **Anderson Lake County Park**. Significant portions of the park, including the Serpentine Trail and sections of the Lakeview Trail, are closed until approximately 2032. Don't be that person who ignores the signs; park rangers *will* cite you.

<AlertBox type="danger">
DO NOT attempt to bypass closures at Anderson Lake County Park. The dam project is serious business, and closed trails are closed for a reason—your safety and the progress of critical infrastructure. Find an alternative route or a different park for your workout.
</AlertBox>

The Five Wounds Trail project is still caught in planning limbo over a historic trestle, so while progress is slow, it's not causing new closures today.
