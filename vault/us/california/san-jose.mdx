<AlertBox type="warning">
**Extreme Heat Warning (Still Applies in Spirit):** While the official "Excessive Heat Watch" from last week for inland valleys like ours might have wrapped up, the National Weather Service still advises limiting strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day, typically 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Drink more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, wear a hat. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on fast.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">

Plan for self-sufficiency.
</AlertBox>

Look, the air's good, but the heat and holiday closures make it a less-than-ideal day for an ambitious outdoor session. Keep it mellow, stay hydrated, and for once, consider that treadmill in your air-conditioned living room. Your future self (and joints) will thank you.
