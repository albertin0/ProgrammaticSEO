Look, I know some of you saw the early reports hinting at scorching heat, but forget that noise. The current read for San Jose this Friday, May 29th, says "Partly cloudy" with the temp sitting at a breezy 55°F (13°C) and a high of around 69°F (21°C) expected for the day. That's practically ideal for pounding the pavement or hitting the dirt. No excuses about it being too hot today!

Even with cooler temps, hydration is your friend. Don't be a hero, carry water, especially if you're planning a longer session. You'll perform better and feel less like a dried-out prune later.

## Trail Talk & Construction Chaos

Good news on the construction front: VTA reports no scheduled daytime or overnight construction activities between May 25-29 along the I-280/Wolfe Road Interchange project. So that's one less headache.

However, not all trails are in the clear. If you're heading south, be aware that the **West Little Llagas Creek Trail in Morgan Hill** is shut down on both sides of the creek from Watsonville Road to Edes Court, and will be for the rest of the year. They're doing creek widening, so stay out unless you want to become part of the heavy machinery. Similar story up at **Coal Creek Preserve**, where the Meadow Trail is closed until August 20th for drainage and improvements.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch for Ongoing Construction
While specific VTA project closures are clear for today, general construction in the city is always a thing. The West San Carlos Urban Village Streetscape Improvement Project, for example, had a scheduled completion of "spring 2026" but those things rarely hit deadlines. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours on city streets.
</AlertBox>
