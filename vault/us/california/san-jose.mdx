<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Risk

Running or cycling in 31°C (88°F) conditions significantly increases your risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. This isn't just "uncomfortable"; it's dangerous, especially if you're pushing intensity or duration.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards to Watch For

**Downtown Delays:** If your route takes you anywhere near the heart of San Jose, particularly around Market Street between San Fernando Street and Park Avenue, expect delays and lane closures due to ongoing construction. Same goes for Park Avenue westbound between Woz Way and S. Almaden Boulevard. Navigating these urban obstacles adds unexpected twists to your workout and can force you into traffic if you're not paying attention.

**Trail Troubles at Alviso Marina:** Heading north to Alviso Marina County Park? Portions of the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail are still seeing intermittent closures due to construction. Don't assume your favorite stretch is open. Check local park alerts before you go, or better yet, plan an alternative if those are part of your regular loop.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Pedestrian & Cyclist Alert: Increased Traffic Vigilance

With Daylight Saving Time ending recently, the city's DOT has deployed electronic message boards on routes like Santa Teresa Boulevard, Saratoga Avenue, and Alum Rock Avenue to remind drivers to slow down and watch for pedestrians. Traffic fatalities have been on the rise this year, so assume drivers don't see you, especially during morning or evening hours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Hydration is NOT Optional

Given the scorching temperatures, you need to pre-hydrate, hydrate during, and re-hydrate after your workout. Seriously, carry more water than you think you need. Consider an electrolyte drink, too, especially if you're prone to cramping. And if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, cut it short and get inside. No PR is worth a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, or just hit the gym, you know?
