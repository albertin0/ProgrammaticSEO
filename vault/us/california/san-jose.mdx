Today’s forecast for San Jose is looking pretty good on paper. That AQI of 25 is about as good as it gets, well within the "Good" range according to Weather Underground and AccuWeather. There are no significant air quality alerts in effect for the Bay Area or San Jose, so you can ditch the N95 for your run today.

However, clear skies and warm temperatures come with a catch: the heat. While it’s "Mainly Clear" and perfect for visibility, the mercury is set to hit 31°C (88°F) this afternoon. If you're planning a workout around lunchtime or early afternoon, you need to be smart. The City of San Jose even advises limiting physical activity during the hottest part of the day, typically between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. No one wants to end up with heat exhaustion trying to PR on the Guadalupe River Trail.

Speaking of which, the Guadalupe River, which has seen its share of drama with past flooding events, is currently flowing without any watches, warnings, or advisories. So, while it's nice to know you won't be navigating submerged paths like in previous years, the current mild conditions are why we should be extra vigilant about the heat.

Wildfire risk is high in the general region, and CAL FIRE notes a "High Risk of Fire" for Thursday and Friday in San Jose, but thankfully, there are no active wildfires close enough to blanket us in smoke right now. Always good to keep an eye on, though, especially with these dry conditions. No news of construction closing down major trails or events blocking routes like that recent situation near Valley Fair from last week.

The temperature will be a real factor from late morning through the afternoon. The NWS indicates highs in the lower to mid-80s, but our local data pegs it closer to 31°C (88°F). This isn't "extreme" heat warning territory, but it's enough to cause problems if you’re not prepared. Limit strenuous activity during peak heat hours (10 AM - 3 PM).

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration and Timing are Key
Drink plenty of water before, during, and after your workout. If you absolutely *must* exercise in the afternoon, stick to shaded routes, take frequent breaks, and consider moving your longer efforts to earlier in the morning or later in the evening. Keep those furry running buddies home if it's too hot for them – their paws burn easily.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Pollen for Sensitive Lungs
With moderate pollen levels, those with allergies or asthma might experience some irritation. Consider taking your meds pre-run and be aware of your symptoms. It's not a reason to stay indoors for everyone, but listen to your body.
