### Don't Get Cooked: Midday Heat Advisory

Look, it's not August, but those afternoon highs in the 80s are no joke, especially if you're pushing hard. The City of San José and county officials regularly warn about limiting activity between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. during hot spells. Hydrate *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Seriously, chug some water. Consider shifting your long runs to early morning or late evening if you want to avoid feeling like a baked potato.
</AlertBox>

Plan your routes accordingly, folks.

### Illegal Fireworks Warning

San Jose Fire Department Chief Robert Sapien, Jr. explicitly stated that all fireworks are illegal in San José, "other than those done by professionals with permits." They've already responded to over 700 vegetation fires this early fire season in 2026, and the heavy rains last winter led to a lot of dry grass, meaning a high fire hazard risk.
