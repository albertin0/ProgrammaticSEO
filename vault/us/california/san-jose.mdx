### Trail Conditions & Local Shenanigans

The good news: No wildfire smoke choking us out, no weird extreme heat advisory in effect for *today*, and our creeks aren't overflowing their banks. The bad news (or at least, the slightly annoying news): construction is still a thing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Guadalupe River Trail!**
Valley Water is still doing levee repairs along parts of the Guadalupe River south of Tasman Drive. While the official word for this weekend is generally "open when crews aren't working," these projects mean heavy machinery and potential detours during the week. Even on a Saturday, be vigilant for uneven surfaces or lingering equipment. Don't assume everything's smooth sailing just because it's the weekend. Your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Steer Clear of Anderson Lake County Park if You Were Planning a Long Trek.**
Seriously, a huge chunk of Anderson Lake County Park is effectively closed until *2032* due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Don't even bother trying to navigate that mess. Find a different adventure. There are plenty of other spots in Santa Clara County that aren't a construction zone for the next six years.
</AlertBox>

If you're in the downtown core, particularly around **Sharks Way**, be aware that March Madness events are scheduled for today. While this isn't a direct "workout hazard," it means more foot traffic and potential congestion if you're trying to zip through on a run. Just something to keep in mind, unless you enjoy dodging slow-moving crowds.
