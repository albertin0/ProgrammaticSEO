## The Rundown: Don't Melt Your Marrow

Let's be real, the sun is blazing at 31°C (87.8°F) and it's barely past noon. Forget setting any PRs unless "personal record for most sweat produced" is your goal. While we dodged any official *Heat Advisories* for today (those expired earlier in the week), 31°C isn't exactly prime running weather. Especially if you're hitting the exposed stretches of the Bay Trail or some of the less shaded trails near Alum Rock Park. Hydration isn't a suggestion; it's the only reason you won't end up as a puddle on the pavement.

Good news for your lungs, though: the Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 25, with Ozone as the dominant pollutant. So, you won't be huffing smog, just hot air. Small victories, right?

## Local Hazards & Head-Ups

### Street Party Ahead: Viva CalleSJ
<AlertBox type="info">
Heads up, San Jose! Today is Viva CalleSJ, meaning a whole bunch of city streets are closed to cars and open for bikes, skates, and good old-fashioned foot traffic. Martial Cottle Park is one of the activity hubs. If you're looking for a long, car-free stretch, this is your day. But also expect crowds. If weaving through throngs of happy people isn't your idea of a zen workout, find your solitude elsewhere.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: What's Open, What's Not (and What's Delayed)
<AlertBox type="warning">
The southern portion of the Five Wounds Trail project is currently paused indefinitely. Apparently, a historic railroad trestle is causing all sorts of preservation headaches. So don't plan on that new connection any time soon. We runners always get the short end of the stick when it comes to infrastructure, don't we?
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Starting tomorrow, September 14, the Guadalupe River Trail from Trimble Road to Airport Parkway will be closed Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, for sediment removal. So, you've got today if you want to hit that section before the heavy machinery moves in. Plan your Monday morning routes now, folks.
</AlertBox>

No massive wildfires are directly impacting our air quality today, which is a relief. Just the usual statewide scattered blazes. No construction blocking major trails *today* other than the ongoing minor work on Los Alamitos Creek. VTA's US 101/SR 25 interchange work is also kicking off daytime operations tomorrow, so if that's near your commute to a workout spot, expect some delays.

Bottom line: It's hot, but the air is clear. Get out there early or late, stay hydrated, and enjoy the car-free streets if you're into the Viva CalleSJ vibe. Otherwise, stick to your usual, well-shaded routes and grumble about the heat.
