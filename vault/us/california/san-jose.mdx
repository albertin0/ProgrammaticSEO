<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat Is No Joke – Seriously
With temperatures at 31.0°C and a "Mainly Clear" sky, the sun is relentless. Heat illness is a real danger, especially if you're pushing it during peak hours. Stick to the shade, bring more water than you think you need, and don't be afraid to cut your workout short. The asphalt around downtown or along the Guadalupe River Trail will be radiating heat like a beast. The National Weather Service has been talking about these kinds of temps for a while now, hitting the 90s and even triple digits in parts of the region. Your 10 AM run is probably a bad idea; get out at dawn or wait for sunset.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures and Events
If you had plans for a long ride or run around **Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve**, know that construction for accessibility improvements is ongoing. This means the equestrian lot is closed, and if you're on a bike, the Arrowhead Trail is one-way, with the direction rotating monthly. Don't show up and get surprised. Also, the "Bay Area Fit Fest" is happening today and tomorrow (July 11-12). While it's a good thing people are moving, it means more folks are out and about. Expect potential congestion around popular fitness spots, and don't be surprised by some extra traffic or difficulty parking if you're near an event venue.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Smart Out There
Good air quality means no smoke to worry about from those distant Southern California wildfires like the Summit fire near Llano. So, breathe easy, but sweat harder... or, you know, just sweat. Drink plenty of water before, during, and after. Wear light-colored, loose clothing. If your head starts feeling woozy or your muscles cramp up, it's not "hitting the wall," it's your body telling you to stop. Listen to it.
</AlertBox>
