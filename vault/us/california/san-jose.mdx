### So, You Wanna Run Anyway?

Look, I get it. The miles call. But today's a day for smart choices, not heroics. That 31.0°C heat, even if the AQI is "good," is going to tax your system significantly. As a physical therapist who's seen too many runners push it, I'm telling you: you don't earn extra points for heatstroke.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** The Heat Advisory is no joke. Temperatures are pushing towards the mid-90s, and high humidity makes it feel even worse. Limit outdoor activities to the very early morning or late evening. If you MUST run, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after*. Carry water, or know your water stops. Don't wait until you're thirsty; that's already dehydration. Watch for signs of heat exhaustion: dizziness, nausea, heavy sweating, muscle cramps. Call it quits immediately if you feel any of these.
</AlertBox>

**Ozone Outlook:** While today's AQI is currently good, the Bay Area experienced a "Spare the Air" alert for unhealthy ozone yesterday due to similar hot, stagnant conditions. Ozone levels tend to peak in the afternoon. If you have respiratory issues, consider exercising indoors during peak heat hours.
</AlertBox>
