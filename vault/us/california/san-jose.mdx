## Local Hazards & Hot Spots

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat & UV Alert!
Seriously, avoid prolonged outdoor activity today, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. That 43.5°C heat index? That's not a suggestion; it's a warning. Stick to indoor workouts or hit it *early* this morning if you absolutely have to be outside. Wear a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen. Stay hydrated, and for the love of all that's holy, don't leave kids or pets in a car.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Construction
If you were thinking of hitting up **Anderson Lake County Park**, think again. Large portions of it are still closed until roughly 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That includes most boating and fishing areas, parts of Coyote Road, and sections of the Lakeview Trail. Don't be that person ignoring closure signs. Other parks listed by the Santa Clara Valley Bird Alliance, like Guadalupe Oak Grove Park and Lake Almaden, might also have ongoing, less publicized, closures, so check their sites if you're venturing out. Also, watch out for construction around the US 101/SR 25 interchange; VTA says the #1 lane is closed along the median this week. It's for cars, sure, but construction zones often mean detours or less safe conditions for pedestrians and cyclists nearby.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Your Only Friend
Drink more water than you think you need. Seriously, carry a bigger bottle. If you feel any signs of heat exhaustion (dizziness, nausea, heavy sweating), get inside and cool down immediately. Don't push through it. Your body will thank you later by not collapsing on the Guadalupe River Trail.
</AlertBox>
