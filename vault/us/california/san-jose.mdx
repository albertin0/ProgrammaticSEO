Keep your situational awareness high and be prepared for potential detours or uneven ground if you're planning a route through there.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you're out near the Coyote Creek Trestle, north of Kelley Park, know that its future is a hot topic. While it's not an *active* closure today, it's a reminder that our urban trails are always evolving.
</AlertBox>

For those who venture north, the **Alviso Marina County Park** area has some intermittent closures. Specifically, expect possible disruptions on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing hauling and construction. The good news is these are often opened during weekends when active hauling isn't happening, so you might get lucky today being a Saturday. But if you hit a closure, respect it. There's plenty more trail out there.

Beyond these specific spots, Santa Clara County Parks generally reports no major alerts or closures for today, which is a win. Just remember that long-term closures like those at Anderson Lake County Park for the dam retrofit are still in effect and will be for years to come. Plan your long runs accordingly, folks.
