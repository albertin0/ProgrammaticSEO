## Your Body Isn't Ready for This: The March Heat Wave Hits San Jose

Let's be brutally honest: Mother Nature is playing a cruel joke on us this week. We're barely out of winter, and San Jose is already hitting record-shattering, unseasonable highs. The temperature outside might be a pleasant 18°C right now, but don't let that fool you. We're staring down the barrel of low 90s Fahrenheit (32°C+) by this afternoon, a first for March in recorded history for our city.

This isn't just "a bit warm." The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for the entire Bay Area, active from yesterday, March 16th, straight through Friday, March 20th. Temperatures are a solid 20 to 30 degrees above average, and overnight lows won't offer much relief. If you thought you could sneak in a decent long run on the Los Gatos Creek Trail, you're going to be seriously regretting it by mile two. This kind of heat, especially so early in the year, puts immense stress on your cardiovascular system and dramatically increases your risk of heat exhaustion or worse.

### Pollen and Air Quality: The Lesser Evils

On the bright side, our Air Quality Index is a pristine 25, so your lungs can breathe easy – assuming you're not gasping from heatstroke. The dominant pollutant is listed as "Ozone/Other," which at this level is practically background noise. However, we are dealing with "Moderate" pollen levels. For those of you with seasonal allergies, that means runny noses, itchy eyes, and generally feeling like crud. Not debilitating, but definitely an unwelcome companion to the oppressive heat.

## Navigating the Urban Jungle and Beyond

If you're typically a downtown runner, forget it. The NVIDIA GTC 2026 AI Conference is in full swing at the San Jose Convention Center this week, running from March 16-19. Expect major street closures around San Carlos Street, Market Street, Park Avenue, and Viola Avenue. Unless dodging crowds and navigating detours is your preferred workout, stay far away.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Today's temperatures are dangerously high for outdoor exercise. Dehydration and heatstroke are real threats. Adjust your workout plans, run indoors, or postpone. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures at Anderson Lake County Park:** Many areas of Anderson Lake County Park, including sections of the Serpentine Trail, Dam Crest Woodchoppers Flat, and parts of Lakeview Trail, remain closed until approximately 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Don't plan on using these routes. Check the Santa Clara County Parks website for the latest on trail status before you head out, especially for areas like Alviso Marina County Park, which has intermittent closures due to construction.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable:** If you *must* venture out, carry significantly more water than you think you need. Pre-hydrate, hydrate during, and rehydrate afterward. Electrolytes are your friend. Early morning is your *only* safe bet for an outdoor workout.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. You want to train. But pushing through record-setting heat isn't tough, it's just stupid. Take an indoor day, hit the gym, or grab a really long foam rolling session. Your body will thank you, and it'll still be around to run when it's not trying to melt into the pavement.
