### Joints: Mind the Heat and the Hazards

At a balmy 31°C (87.8°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, it's going to feel like summer decided to stick around for one last gasp. This isn't quite "extreme heat advisory" territory like we've seen earlier in the season, but it's still hot enough to dehydrate you faster than you can say "Guadalupe River Trail." The City of San José's own safety tips recommend avoiding strenuous activity between 10 AM and 3 PM. If you're out there during those hours, slow down, hydrate, and for the love of all that is holy, wear a hat.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory for Your Body, Not Necessarily for the City:** While there's no official *city-wide* heat advisory today, 88°F is no joke for physical exertion. Listen to your body and cut your workout short if you feel lightheaded or excessively tired.
</AlertBox>

And let's talk about getting around this sprawling city. The saga of **Ewert Road** near the San Jose Mineta International Airport continues to be a thorn in the side of anyone on two wheels. While the full permanent closure plans have been delayed, it's an "essential conduit" to the Guadalupe River Trail, and its uncertain access can force cyclists into less safe traffic. Even if the trail section near Highway 101 was open by end of day yesterday after sediment removal, the underlying Ewert Road issue isn't going anywhere. Factor in ongoing city construction on major arteries like Monterey Road, Market Street, and West Julian Street, and you're looking at potential detours and frustrating delays.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Cyclists, Be Extra Aware!** Just yesterday, a bicyclist was tragically killed in a collision on Capitol Expressway. That's the 37th traffic-related fatality this year. This isn't just a number; it's a stark reminder that even on a clear day, San Jose's roads can be dangerous for those of us not encased in steel. Stay alert, assume drivers don't see you, and choose routes with protected lanes where possible.
</AlertBox>
