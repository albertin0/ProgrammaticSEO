While San Jose itself is enjoying some stellar air, don't get complacent. We've got numerous lightning complex fires burning up acres in neighboring counties like Santa Clara, San Mateo, and Monterey. While the winds are holding steady for us right now, keeping our AQI in the "Good" zone, a shift could easily push smoke our way. Always check before you head out, especially if you're planning a longer session. That good air quality can change faster than a politician's promise.

On the ground, you might encounter some minor disruptions. If you're hitting the **Los Alamitos Creek Trail**, know that Valley Water crews are still out doing vegetation removal through early September. You'll likely see flaggers, so pay attention and don't be *that* runner who ignores their directions. It's for your safety, not just to annoy you. The trail itself should remain open.

Also, a heads-up for later this week: the east bank of the Guadalupe River Trail will be closed on Wednesdays and Thursdays, starting August 19th and running through September 17th. They're clearing vegetation for the Coyote Creek Flood Protection Project. Plan to use the west bank on those days. Consider this your official warning.
