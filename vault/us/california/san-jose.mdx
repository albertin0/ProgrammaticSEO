## The Rundown

Alright, San Jose, today's a solid day to earn those miles, but don't just blindly hit your usual route. The air quality is sitting pretty at a "Good" AQI of 42, which means most of you can breathe easy. However, this isn't a free pass for everyone. With moderate pollen levels, if you're one of the many seasonal sniffers, expect some minor irritation. Don't be a hero; take your meds *before* you lace up.

The weather is actually quite agreeable: cool and overcast. You won't be battling a scorching sun or oppressive humidity, which is a major bonus for longer efforts. Your joints will appreciate the moderate temperatures, allowing for better performance and less risk of overheating.

### Local Hazards & Annoyances

<AlertBox type="warning">

**Downtown San Jose: Event Hangover**

If you're planning on running through downtown, be advised that the "Soccer Celebration" at San Pedro Square Market is wrapping up today, July 19. That means those extra screens and associated street closures between St. John and Santa Clara streets are still in effect. Don't expect a clear path; plan to navigate some crowds and detours if your route goes through that area. It's not a major hazard, but it'll definitely slow your pace and test your patience.

</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">

**Trail Maintenance on the Guadalupe River Trail**

For those of you who frequent the Guadalupe River Trail or Los Alamitos Creek Trail, be aware that vegetation removal projects are ongoing through early September. Crews are out there between 7 AM and 3 PM. The trails are technically open, but expect flaggers and potential narrowings. Seriously, follow their directions and stay out of the work zones. It's not worth a twisted ankle because you couldn't wait five seconds.

</AlertBox>

While there aren't any immediate wildfire smoke advisories directly impacting San Jose's AQI today, the region always carries a "High Risk of Fire" this time of year. Just yesterday, the Morgan Fire near Mount Diablo had its forward progress stopped, a reminder that fire season is always simmering. Keep an eye on regional news; conditions can change faster than a runner's diet.

<AlertBox type="warning">

**Regional Trail Closures**

Remember that Anderson Lake County Park still has significant areas closed until 2032 due to dam construction, which impacts some trails. Also, if you venture out to Midpeninsula preserves, the Bear Creek Redwoods Preserve has several trails (Toad Hollow, Myotis, Lower Pete Siemens, parts of Beaver and Oak Knoll) closed until September for parking and new trail construction. Always check specific park websites before heading to an unfamiliar trail.

</AlertBox>

So, grab your trainers, maybe a tissue or two, and hit the road or the open parts of the trails. Just stay alert, respect any construction, and don't assume every path is clear. Common sense, people.
