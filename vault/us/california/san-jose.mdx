## The Real Danger: Heat

This is the big one, folks. 31.0°C (87.8°F) is no joke, especially if you're pushing yourself. This kind of heat saps your energy, stresses your cardiovascular system, and significantly increases your risk of dehydration, heat cramps, heat exhaustion, and even heat stroke.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: 31.0°C (87.8°F)!** This temperature is dangerous for strenuous outdoor activity. Limit your runs to early morning or late evening. Hydrate excessively, wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and LISTEN TO YOUR BODY. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, stop immediately and cool down. This is not a drill.
</AlertBox>

My professional opinion, as someone who's seen the aftermath: reschedule that midday tempo run. Stick to a short, easy jog at dawn or dusk, or take it indoors. There's no medal for getting heatstroke.
