## The Local Lowdown: Don't Get Caught Off Guard

If your usual morning tempo run takes you along the **Guadalupe River Trail**, specifically the east side between Trimble Road and Airport Parkway, listen up. Valley Water is still doing sediment removal, and that section is *closed* Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM. Yeah, I know, it’s a pain, but they gotta keep the river flowing eventually. Plan your detour now or face the wrath of a frustrated trail worker. Don't be "that guy" trying to sneak through.

### Downtown San Jose: Expect Delays and Obstacles
Ongoing construction projects across downtown, especially around Market Street, Park Avenue, and East San Fernando Street, mean frequent lane and sidewalk closures. Watch for sudden changes in pedestrian access and be extra vigilant for equipment. This isn't the time to zone out to your podcast.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Anderson Lake County Park: Still a Ghost Town
If you were thinking of hitting the trails at Anderson Lake County Park, forget about it. Large sections, including many trails and boating areas, are closed until approximately 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Find a new stomping ground; this one's out of commission for the foreseeable future.
</AlertBox>

### Dress for the Morning Chill
While the day promises to be clear, that 7.4°C start is no joke. Dress in layers you can shed. Your muscles and joints will thank you for being smart, preventing those early-morning aches that turn into mid-run regrets.
