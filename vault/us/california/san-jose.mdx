## Local Trail Annoyances: Persistent, Not New

Look, not every "hazard" is breaking news. Sometimes it's just the steady drip of bureaucracy and construction.

You're still out of luck for the southern stretch of the planned **Five Wounds Trail** in East San Jose. Parks officials have paused plans for that portion, thanks to a historic railroad trestle (the Coyote Creek Trestle) that preservation advocates want to protect. They're focusing on the central section now, which runs from Highway 280 North to Santa Clara Street. So, don't expect to run the full vision of that trail anytime soon.

Similarly, if you're a regular at **Anderson Lake County Park**, remember that large sections are still closed until approximately 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That includes the Serpentine Trail and pretty much all boating and fishing access. Check the Santa Clara County Parks website before you go to avoid driving all the way out there for nothing.
