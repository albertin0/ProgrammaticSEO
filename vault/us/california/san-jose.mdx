## The Heat Is On (Literally)

Look, I know we're Bay Area tough, but 31.0°C for May 9th? That's not your usual spring breeze. The National Weather Service has been talking about early-season heat waves, and while other parts of California are getting blasted even worse (hello, Imperial County at 112 degrees), San Jose is still looking at temps in the high 80s today, climbing to the low 90s by Monday. Don't get caught off guard just because it's not August. Your body hasn't acclimatized yet.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger: Early Season Heat Stress.**
Today's 31.0°C (87.8°F) is no joke. Reschedule your long runs or intense workouts for the early morning, before 9 AM, or after 7 PM. Stay out of the sun during peak heating hours (roughly 10 AM to 3 PM). Dehydration and heat exhaustion can sneak up on you faster than a cyclist on the Guadalupe River Trail.
</AlertBox>

## Air Quality & Allergies

The good news is our air quality is holding steady in the "Good" range (AQI 42), with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant, but at low levels. You might remember that Curry Fire near Bernal Road and Heaton Moor Drive just a couple of days ago; it burned 19 acres but is now 90% contained. So, thankfully, no lingering smoke issues messing with your breathing today.

However, that "Moderate" pollen level means those with seasonal allergies might still be feeling it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate.**
With the heat, drinking water isn't just a suggestion, it's mandatory. Guzzle that H2O before, during, and after your workout. And for the love of all that's holy, leave the sugary sports drinks for ultra-marathons; plain water is your friend for most sessions.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Closures You Need to Know

While most of the San Jose trail network is open, there are a couple of significant, ongoing closures that could affect your plans. If you're heading south, be aware that the **West Little Llagas Creek Trail in Morgan Hill** is completely shut down from Watsonville Road to Edes Court, and later this summer, Edes Court to Spring Avenue, for creek widening and deepening. This is a long-term thing, running through the end of 2026, so find an alternate route.

Also, a big chunk of **Anderson Lake County Park** remains closed until approximately 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That's a massive undertaking, so don't plan on using those trails anytime soon.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Caution: Share the Road & Trail.**
May is National Bicycle Safety Month, and the San Jose Police Department is stepping up enforcement on dangerous driving behaviors that put pedestrians and cyclists at risk. Whether you're running on the streets or sharing multi-use trails, stay alert, be predictable, and make eye contact. Drivers are often distracted, and it's always better to be safe than sorry.
</AlertBox>

Ultimately, it's a decent day if you manage the heat properly. Don't be a hero; listen to your body, slow down, and enjoy the relatively clean air.
