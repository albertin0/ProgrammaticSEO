## The Heat Is On (And It's Not a Good Thing)

Seriously, 93 degrees in March? Welcome to California, I guess. The National Weather Service isn't messing around; they've issued a Heat Advisory for our neck of the woods until Friday night. This isn't your average sunny day run. This is the kind of heat where sidewalks radiate, and even the shade feels like a warm hug from a furnace. Your internal thermostat isn't ready for this, and neither are your joints if you plan on pounding pavement when it's this hot. Cal/OSHA is even telling employers to protect workers from heat illness, so take a hint.

If you were planning to log some serious miles on the Guadalupe River Trail, think again. While the river itself isn't currently posing any flood risks, the surrounding concrete and lack of substantial shade will amplify the heat. Save those long runs for when it's, you know, reasonable.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning!** Temperatures are forecast to hit the mid-90s today. Limit outdoor exposure, especially during peak afternoon hours. If you *must* be outside, wear light clothing, take frequent breaks, stay hydrated with plenty of water, and listen to your body. Heat exhaustion and heatstroke are serious.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Tidbits & Other Annoyances

Beyond the oppressive heat, don't forget about the usual San Jose quirks. Don't show up with your fishing gear expecting a tranquil afternoon, because you'll just be turning right back around.

Over at Alviso Marina County Park, those intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail due to hauling and construction might still pop up. Always have a backup plan or call ahead if you're relying on specific sections of those baylands trails.
