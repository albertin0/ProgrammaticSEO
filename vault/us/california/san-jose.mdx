## Today's Outdoor Conditions: The Breakdown

It's clear, alright. Mainly clear and *blisteringly hot*. While the air quality (AQI 49) technically falls into the "Good" category, don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. The big issue today is the heat. Cal/OSHA has been reminding everyone to protect workers from heat illness, and that applies just as much to your lunchtime run as it does to any job site.

### DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, 31°C is not running weather. The National Weather Service issued a heat advisory for the region, warning of a "moderate risk of heat-related illness". You're risking dehydration, heat exhaustion, and even heatstroke. If you absolutely MUST be outside, go before 8 AM or after 7 PM, and for the love of all that is holy, stick to shaded areas and carry more water than you think you'll need. This kind of heat is "unprecedented" for March in San Jose, so don't treat it like a normal warm spring day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Allergy Alert: Moderate Pollen
With moderate pollen levels, those of us prone to seasonal allergies will be feeling it. Keep your antihistamines handy if you're hitting any trails, especially those with significant tree cover. Sneezing and a runny nose are bad enough without adding labored breathing from pushing yourself in the heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Trail Conditions & Closures
While there aren't massive city-wide closures, be aware of localized issues. For instance, sections of the Guadalupe River Trail south of Tasman Drive have seen intermittent closures due to levee repairs. Always check the City of San José's parks and trails website or Santa Clara County Parks for the latest specific alerts before heading out. Don't assume your usual route is clear, and don't ignore "Trail Closed" signs, especially with construction or maintenance crews operating heavy machinery.
</AlertBox>
