## Today's Vitals: A Sweat Fest and Sneezing Risk

Let's not sugarcoat it: 31°C is not "ideal" for breaking a sweat outside, unless your goal is voluntary dehydration. Your lungs are going to work overtime trying to cool you down, and your joints? They'll be stressed from the increased core temperature and potential electrolyte imbalances. I've seen enough heat exhaustion cases to know it's not a badge of honor, it's just stupid. Go early, go late, or go indoors. Preferably the latter.

Now, the air quality (AQI 25) is technically stellar, meaning no smoke or industrial haze to contend with, which is a rare win for the Bay Area. But that's like saying your car has fresh oil when the engine's on fire. The heat nullifies a lot of that benefit. And yes, "moderate" pollen is still enough to make you sniffle and cough if you're sensitive. So, if you're hitting the Almaden Lake Park trails, don't be surprised if you finish looking like you just ran through a dust storm.

## The PT's Plea: Don't Be A Hero

You want to improve your cardiovascular fitness, not end up with a nasty case of heat stroke. I'm a runner, I get it. But pushing through this kind of heat isn't tough, it's reckless. Your heart rate will be artificially elevated, your perceived effort will be through the roof, and your performance will tank. It's a recipe for injury, or worse.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** 31°C (87.8°F) is dangerous for sustained outdoor activity. Prioritize hydration *before*, *during*, and *after* any workout. Consider moving your workout indoors or to the very early morning/late evening.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Detours Ahead:** If your route involves San Fernando Street or other bikeways downtown, expect ongoing construction closures and diversions throughout March 2026. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Key:** Water isn't enough when you're sweating this much. Replenish electrolytes. Seriously. And wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear. No, that old cotton tee isn't doing you any favors.
</AlertBox>

Look, I love our San Jose trails as much as the next masochist, but some days the weather just isn't worth the suffering. Find a gym, hit the pool, or reorganize your sock drawer. Your body will thank you, and you won't be calling me for a PT session for heat-related cramping later this week.
