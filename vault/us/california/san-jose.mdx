### Watch Your Step (and Your Sniffles)

Look, an AQI of 25 is nothing to scoff at. Your lungs will largely thank you for getting outside. But that "Moderate" pollen? That's where some of you are going to feel it. If you're an allergy sufferer, pre-dose your meds, or stick to indoor workouts if a runny nose and itchy eyes derail your groove. Nobody needs a mid-run sneeze attack when you're trying to crush a PR.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Foggy Outlook:** Visibility is reduced out there with the fog. This is especially true on less-maintained trails or paths near creeks and rivers like sections of the Guadalupe River Trail. If you're out early, wear bright colors or reflective gear. Don't be that person in all black suddenly appearing out of the mist.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Coyote Ridge & East San Jose Headaches

Heads up if your go-to is **Coyote Ridge Open Space Preserve**: the Habitat Protection Area, which includes the Serpentine Spring, Tule Elk, and Bay Checkerspot trails, is typically closed on Mondays and Tuesdays for scientific research. Since today's Tuesday, plan for those specific trails to be off-limits. Don't be a scofflaw and mess with ongoing research; there are plenty of other spots to explore. Also, if you plan on bringing a horse trailer to Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve, remember that reservations are now required as of March 1, 2026.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Navigation:** If your route takes you through East San Jose, be mindful of the ongoing VTA Eastridge to BART Regional Connector light rail extension. Construction can pop up without much warning, rerouting pedestrians and cyclists. Keep an eye out for signage and be prepared to take an unplanned detour.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a fine day to get moving, just remember to be smart, be visible, and maybe pack some tissues.
