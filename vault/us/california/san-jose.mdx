Look, you can't beat San Jose on a clear day like this. The air quality is pristine, sitting at a lovely 25 AQI with nothing nasty floating around. Seriously, that's runner's gold. Your lungs are getting a treat today, no complaints there.

Downtown is throwing a massive party, and you're not invited to run through it unimpeded.
