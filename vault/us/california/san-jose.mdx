## Today's Vibe: Oven-Baked Runner

Look, I love hitting the pavement or dirt as much as the next masochist, but today isn't the day to try and PR your long run outside. The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for the Santa Clara Valley, which means our typically pleasant San Jose weather has decided to impersonate a desert. We're talking mid-90s to potentially cracking triple digits.

This isn't just about feeling sweaty; this kind of heat puts serious strain on your cardiovascular system and can lead to rapid dehydration, heat exhaustion, or worse, heatstroke. Your lungs will be working overtime just to keep you cool, and your joints, while not directly impacted by heat in the same way, will suffer from decreased performance and increased fatigue. That "other" dominant pollutant in our AQI? That's likely ozone, which tends to spike with heat and sunshine, especially in the afternoon. While our overall AQI is "Good" at 25 today, combine that ozone with high temps and a moderate pollen count, and you've got a recipe for a miserable, not a marvelous, workout.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Seriously, Stay Hydrated (Or Indoors)

A Heat Advisory means business. If you absolutely MUST be outside, limit your activity to the early morning hours, before 10 AM, or wait until after 7 PM. And even then, significantly reduce your intensity and duration. Drink more water than you think you need, even if you’re not thirsty. Water, not that sugary sports drink you swear by, unless you're out there for more than an hour.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Watch Your Step (and the Closures)

While the Almaden Hills Trail Run happened yesterday out at Almaden Quicksilver Park, if you're thinking of hitting those exposed hills today, prepare to get cooked. Those trails offer next to no shade, and temperatures on the ridge climb significantly higher than down in the valley.

Beyond the heat, you'll still find some ongoing work. The Santa Clara Valley Water District is still doing vegetation removal along the Los Alamitos Creek Trail through early September, so expect flaggers and some minor disruptions there. Further south, the West Little Llagas Creek trail in Morgan Hill is closed entirely for creek widening. Also, if you regularly run the Coyote Creek Trail near Skyport Drive, remember the upper trail still has occasional closures for construction through September. Don't be that person ignoring the signs; those closures are for your safety.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Detours

Before you head out, especially if you're aiming for popular routes like Coyote Creek Trail or Los Alamitos Creek Trail, check for specific closure information. These aren't always well-marked until you're already there, turning your run into an unplanned scavenger hunt for an open path. Better to know before you lace up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pro Tip for Today: Gym, Baby, Gym

Seriously, the AC is your friend. Hit up your local gym, or if you absolutely need fresh air, consider a short walk in a heavily shaded park like Kelley Park in the earliest morning hours. Save the epic outdoor adventures for when San Jose isn't trying to melt you.
</AlertBox>
