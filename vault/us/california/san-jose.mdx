It's August 5th, San Jose, and while the skies look clear *right now*, don't let that perfectly blue facade fool you; today's a day for strategic outdoor moves or staying inside.

*   Moderate pollen levels mean the usual suspects are going to be feeling a bit congested. Nothing catastrophic, but certainly annoying if you're trying to push a hard pace.

### Today's Trail Lowdown

If you're planning a run or ride along the **Coyote Creek Trail** today, you need to be smart. This path gets a lot of sun exposure, and with temps pushing near 90 degrees, you'll feel every single ray. There's not a ton of natural shade, so those exposed stretches become real heat traps.

Also, a heads-up for those who use the Coyote Creek Trail to connect to the broader network: plans for the southernmost portion of the Five Wounds Trail in East San Jose, which was set to link up, are currently stalled due to a historic railroad trestle. It's not a closure for today, but it means that seamless connectivity is still a ways off if you're planning long-distance routes.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Brutal Heat Warning
Seriously, it's hot. The National Weather Service issued a heat advisory for the Santa Clara Valley. Limit physical activity, especially between 10 AM and 3 PM. If you must go out, drink way more water than you think you need, wear light, loose clothing, and seek shade constantly. Your body isn't an oven; don't bake it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Smoke on the Horizon
Even if it's clear now, an air quality advisory is active for today due to wildfire smoke. This means conditions can change fast. If you start seeing hazy skies or smell smoke, cut your workout short and head indoors. Smoke contains nasty particulate matter that's not good for your lungs, even in short bursts.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Your Action Plan
*   **Early Bird or Night Owl:** Hit the trails at dawn or dusk if you're determined to get those miles in. Any time between late morning and late afternoon is pushing it.
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Carry more water than you think you'll need. Add electrolytes if you're sweating buckets.
*   **Monitor AQI:** Keep an eye on real-time air quality updates throughout the day. Your current app might say "Good," but the advisory means things could shift. The EPA's Fire and Smoke Map is your friend here.
*   **Listen to Your Body:** If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, stop. Find a cool place. This isn't the day to push through.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, San Jose. Don't let a "clear" morning fool you into a bad decision.
