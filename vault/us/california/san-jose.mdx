## The Air You Breathe (or Try To)

Don't let the mild 15.7°C (around 60°F) and overcast skies fool you into thinking it's pristine out there. We've got an Air Quality Advisory due to wildfire smoke drifting up from the Timber and Plaskett fires near Big Sur. While much of that smoke might be hanging out higher up, it can definitely settle into our valleys and foothills, pushing that "moderate" AQI of 51 into "unhealthy for sensitive groups" territory without much warning. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which is exactly the kind of microscopic junk you don't want filling your lungs during a hard effort.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Smoke Advisory:** An Air Quality Advisory is in effect for the Bay Area through today due to wildfire smoke. Keep an eye on local air quality updates, and if you start smelling smoke or feeling respiratory irritation, cut your workout short. This is especially crucial for anyone with asthma, COPD, or other lung conditions.
</AlertBox>

## Trail & Road Conditions: Mind Your Surroundings

It wouldn't be San Jose without some form of perpetual construction, right? This time, Valley Water is busy with vegetation removal along sections of the **Los Alamitos Creek Trail near Mazzone Drive** through early September. The good news is the trails are staying open, but expect to see crews and possibly flaggers. Pay attention and don't assume you have the right-of-way over heavy equipment.

Beyond the trails, the "Repave San Jose" initiative is in full swing, with 205 miles of streets slated for paving this year. While the big closures typically come with advance notice, expect minor disruptions, detours, and uneven surfaces on neighborhood streets as they do preparatory work like concrete upgrades. Stay vigilant, especially if you're running on roads that aren't typically car-free.
