Forget your delusions of a "perfect" outdoor workout today, San Jose. It's a furnace out there, and staying safe means ditching your long runs for something a lot chiller.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

## Why today is *not* the day to push it outdoors

<BulletList>
*   **Serious Heat Advisory:** A Heat Advisory is in effect for the Santa Clara Valley, including our fair city, from noon today until Friday evening. Temperatures are expected to climb into the mid-90s Fahrenheit (around 35°C) in San Jose, with inland valleys potentially hitting the low 100s. That's not "sweat it out" weather; that's "risk heat stroke" weather.
*   **Moderate Pollen:** While the air quality for pollutants is generally good, pollen is hanging around at moderate levels. Your lungs might feel fine, but if you're prone to allergies, you'll be sniffling through your effort.
*   **Elevated Fire Risk Nearby:** Hot, dry conditions are creating elevated fire weather, with a Red Flag Warning for some North Bay and East Bay Hills. While not directly over San Jose, mountain areas in Santa Clara County could also experience fire-prone conditions. Keep an eye on the hills, especially if your workout takes you toward the urban-wildland interface.
</BulletList>

## Local Hazards to Sidestep

If you absolutely *must* get out there, avoid the exposed sections of trails like the **Coyote Creek Trail** during peak heat hours. You're just asking for trouble when there's no shade and the sun is beating down on asphalt or dusty paths.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is a Real Threat Today
With temperatures soaring into the 90s, the risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke is significantly increased. Reschedule any strenuous outdoor activity for early morning or late evening, or better yet, take it indoors. Drink way more water than you think you need, and don't ignore dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating. Your body isn't an oven; don't treat it like one.
</AlertBox>

### Current Air Quality & Pollen Breakdown

The overall Air Quality Index (AQI) in San Jose is currently quite good at 25. However, sensitive groups might experience minor symptoms from ground-level ozone, which is at a "Fair" level (AQI 36) in some areas. AccuWeather's forecast also indicates a "Poor" AQI for Thursday and Friday for sensitive groups, so don't assume the good number applies to everyone or all parts of the day.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ongoing Trail Construction
Keep an eye out for existing trail closures. The Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project, for instance, has many areas of **Anderson Lake County Park** closed until approximately 2032. You'll also find intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing construction. Always check county parks websites before heading out, unless you enjoy finding a locked gate a mile into your run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
Seriously, carry more water than you think you need. Or just stay home and hit the treadmill. Nobody cares if you PR in the heat, only that you don't end up in an ambulance. If you are outside, stick to shaded routes and listen to your body – it's telling you to slow down or stop when it gets too hot.
</AlertBox>
