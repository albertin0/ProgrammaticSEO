## The Heat is On (Again)

San Jose is heating up, with temperatures expected to hit around 88°F (31°C) and clear skies. While not quite the "triple-digit advisory" many parts of the Bay Area have seen this week, it's still plenty hot enough to cause trouble. The National Weather Service and the City of San José have been pretty clear: avoid strenuous activity during the peak heat, typically 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. If you're out there, drink more water than you think you need, wear loose-fitting, light-colored clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, *do not* leave kids or pets in a car. That's just asking for a bad time.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Extreme Heat Caution:** Temperatures are high. Plan your outdoor workouts for early morning or late evening. Hydrate aggressively, wear appropriate gear, and listen to your body. Heat exhaustion and stroke are real, folks.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk: Obstacles & Occupied Zones

Good news for the most part: no major trail *closures* due to wildfires or flooding right within the city right now. However, wildfires are burning across other parts of California, and dry conditions persist, meaning the fire risk is generally elevated.

What you *do* need to watch out for is ongoing maintenance. Santa Clara Valley Water is doing vegetation removal along the **Guadalupe River Trail** and the Los Alamitos Creek Trail until early September. This means you might encounter flaggers or active work zones. Keep your head up, obey any instructions, and don't assume they see you. It's their job, sure, but your safety is your responsibility first and foremost.

Also, if you're planning a run through downtown, be aware that the San Jose Jazz Summer Fest kicks off today and runs through Sunday at **Plaza Park**. Expect more crowds and potential road adjustments in that immediate area.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail & Downtown Navigation:** Expect potential delays or minor detours on the Guadalupe River Trail due to vegetation removal. Keep an eye out for increased foot traffic around Plaza Park due to the San Jose Jazz Summer Fest.
</AlertBox>
