### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors or Exercise During Coolest Parts of the Day!
With the mercury hitting 31°C (88°F), the risk of heat-related illness is **severe**. Even if you think you're tough, your body is working overtime to keep cool, increasing the strain on your cardiovascular system and joints. The National Weather Service in our broader Bay Area region has issued warnings for similar conditions, emphasizing limiting strenuous activity to early mornings or evenings.
</AlertBox>

On top of the heat, we've got "moderate" pollen levels. For some of you, that's a sniffle and a sneeze. For others, it's an asthma attack waiting to happen, especially when you're sucking wind on a run. Check your usual triggers before you head out.

And if you were planning a nice long ride or run along the Guadalupe River Trail south of Tasman Drive, think again, at least during prime work hours. Valley Water is still doing levee repairs. That means the west levee/maintenance road section is closed on weekdays from 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. through March 20th. You'll be forced onto some less-than-ideal detours, which are probably even hotter and less shaded. Plan accordingly, or better yet, find somewhere else.
