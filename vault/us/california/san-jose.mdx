### Today's Takeaway: Get Out There, But Stay Alert

The air quality around the South Bay is looking good, thankfully. No major smoke plumes darkening the horizon, which is always a win in California. That low AQI of 25 means you won't be huffing anything nasty, though "Ozone/Other" being the dominant pollutant is a classic Silicon Valley special – thanks, cars. The overcast, cool conditions are pretty much ideal for hammering out miles, whether you're cruising the Guadalupe River Trail or tackling some hills in Almaden Quicksilver County Park.

Speaking of Almaden Quicksilver, those Quicksilver Endurance Runs just wrapped up yesterday. While the main event is over, expect a few stragglers or some lingering course markings. It's always a good idea to remember that events can leave trails a bit churned up or crowded, even after the official finish line is packed away. Another event, the "Trailquake" in Saratoga, is happening today, so if you're venturing further south into Sanborn County Park, anticipate some extra foot traffic there.
