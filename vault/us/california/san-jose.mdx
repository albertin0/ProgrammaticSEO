While the fire was battled and contained yesterday, the remnants of that smoke can linger, especially for those in the immediate vicinity. Keep your senses tuned.

## Trail Talk: Obstacles & Openings

Okay, now for the fun part: what's actually open and what's not?

**Almaden Quicksilver County Park:** Good news, maybe? The Hacienda and Mockingbird Hill parking lots, along with the Mine Hill and Hacienda trails, which were closed due to a fire mop-up, are *reopening today, July 8th.* However, "reopening" doesn't mean "pristine." There might still be crews, residual smoke odors, or unstable ground. Proceed with caution and pay attention to any last-minute signage. Just because the gate is open doesn't mean it's business as usual.

**Anderson Lake County Park:** Still largely off-limits for the foreseeable future. If your usual route involves the Toyon Group picnic site, Serpentine Trail, or anything near the dam, remember those closures are in effect until roughly 2032 due to the seismic retrofit project. Find a different playground.

**Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve:** Expect ongoing construction. The equestrian lot has been closed since June 22, 2026, for public access improvements. While they're trying to minimize closures, keep an eye out for active work zones.
