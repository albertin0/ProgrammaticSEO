### Local Lowdown & Hazards

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up for Cyclists and Lightweight Runners on Exposed Trails!**
Those northwest gusts aren't a joke. If you're hitting an open stretch, like parts of the Guadalupe River Trail, be aware of crosswinds. It can throw off your balance and make you work harder than you planned. It’s also just generally annoying.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the Guadalupe River Trail, good news for your Saturday long run: the sediment removal project between Trimble Road and the Airport Parkway is usually a weekday headache (Monday-Friday, 7:30 AM - 3:00 PM). Since it’s Saturday, the trail *should* be open and clear for your weekend miles. However, always keep an eye out for lingering equipment or changed conditions. Don't assume anything.

While our AQI is in the "good" zone today, let's not get complacent. We've seen smoke advisories from Big Sur wildfires and Pacific Northwest fires just recently, and you know how quickly that can change. Always check the current conditions before you head out, because "clear" can turn "hazy" faster than you can say "Spare the Air."

**Pedestrian Safety Always a Concern.**
Unfortunately, we saw another traffic fatality involving a pedestrian on September 10th near Rigoletto Drive and S. King Road. This is a grim reminder for all runners and walkers: stay visible, follow traffic laws, and assume no one sees you. Your PR isn't worth your life.
</AlertBox>

Looking ahead to tomorrow, Sunday, September 13th, don't be surprised if some roads are closed. It's Viva CalleSJ, where they shut down miles of streets for walking, biking, and skating. If you're planning a long run on city streets, check the route map beforehand so you don't get stuck. Or, embrace it and join the party!
