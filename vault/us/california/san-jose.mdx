### Why today is NOT the day for PRs

<BulletList>
*   **The Heat is Back (Even if the Advisory Isn't):** While the official Bay Area heat advisory from last week (March 16-20) has technically passed, San Jose is still cooking. We're looking at a scorching 31 degrees Celsius (that's 87.8°F, for those of you not on the metric system yet). That's not just "warm," that's "stupid hot" for any serious mid-day effort. Your body isn't fully acclimated to this kind of March madness yet, so don't push it.
*   **Moderate Pollen:** Oaks and cedars are doing their thing, so if your nose is already running like a leaky faucet, today won't be your best friend. It's enough to make those deep breaths during a tough climb feel like sucking air through a coffee filter.
*   **Trail Blockages & Maintenance:** Several spots around the county have ongoing closures. If you were planning a long run through Sanborn County Park, forget about Summit Rock—it's closed for peregrine falcon nesting season until Labor Day. Plus, significant trail construction has the Costanoan parking lot and picnic area shut down through the end of March. Don't be that person ignoring the signs; you'll just end up adding frustration to your workout.
</BulletList>

### DANGER: Extreme Heat
With the mercury hitting 31°C, even for a "clear" day, this is dangerous territory for sustained outdoor activity. Heat stroke and exhaustion are real. Keep your serious workouts indoors, or limit them to the absolute coolest parts of the day (think pre-dawn, not mid-afternoon). Hydrate constantly, and wear light, loose-fitting clothing. Don't be a hero; nobody wants to peel you off the asphalt on Guadalupe River Trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Moderate Pollen Count
If you suffer from seasonal allergies, today's moderate pollen levels, particularly from oak and cedar, might make outdoor exercise less enjoyable. Consider an antihistamine or sticking to indoor alternatives if you want to avoid feeling like you ran through a pollen cloud.
</AlertBox>
