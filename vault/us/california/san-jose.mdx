## Trail & Local Hazards

While there are no major *new* trail closures for San Jose specifically in the last 24 hours, don't think you're entirely in the clear. Valley Water is still doing its thing with vegetation removal along sections of the Guadalupe River Trail and Los Alamitos Creek Trail. They've got flaggers out there, which means you need to pay attention, follow instructions, and absolutely, positively, stay out of the work zones. Those crews aren't there to watch you trip over their equipment because you're too busy staring at your watch.

There's also a Coastal Flood Advisory for the broader Bay Area and Pacific Coast through early Wednesday morning due to higher than normal astronomical tides. While the Guadalupe River itself isn't under a *specific* flood warning for today, low-lying areas near shorelines could still see some minor flooding. So, if your usual route hugs the bay or a river, keep an eye out for standing water.
