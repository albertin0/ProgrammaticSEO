## Today's Workout Reality Check

Look, I get it. You live in San Jose, the weather is usually pristine, and you want to get out there. But 31 degrees Celsius with full sun? That’s not a workout; that’s an invitation to heat exhaustion. The city itself is warning folks to limit physical activity and avoid the hottest parts of the day, which is basically *all day* at this temperature.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, this heat is no joke. The risk of heat-related illness like heat cramps, exhaustion, and even stroke is high. If you absolutely *must* exercise outdoors, do it at dawn, stay in the shade, and hydrate constantly. But my professional advice as someone who's seen too many runners get carted off: just don't.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Local Hazards

Beyond the obvious heat dome, let's talk about our local trails. If you were planning a long run or hike, remember that parts of **Anderson Lake County Park** are still shut down until around 2032 for the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That's not a typo, folks, *2032*. So don't plan on that loop anytime soon. Similarly, Alviso Marina County Park has intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and parts of the Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing construction and hauling. Even at Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve, weekend hikes require pre-booking, and some trails are closed for research on Mondays and Tuesdays.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Hydration is NOT Optional
If you ignore all warnings and decide to brave the heat, you better be carrying more water than you think you need. Dehydration sets in fast at these temperatures. Drink fluids continuously, not just when you feel thirsty. Avoid sugary or alcoholic drinks, they'll just make it worse.
</AlertBox>

Your body isn't a corporate wellness blog, it's a finely tuned machine. Treat it with respect. A treadmill or a spin class in an air-conditioned gym sounds pretty appealing right about now, doesn't it? Save the outdoor heroics for a day when the air isn't trying to cook you from the inside out.
