Looks, it's Halloween, but don't let the ghosts and goblins distract you from the actual hazards out there; today's a decent day to hit the pavement or trails, provided you plan around the crowds.

<BulletList>
*   The air quality is pretty stellar, which is always a win for your lungs, especially after some of the haze we've seen this year.
*   Temperatures are crisp but manageable at 9.1°C (that's about 48°F for you Celsius-challenged folks), and the skies are mainly clear, so no excuses about bad weather.
*   However, downtown San Jose is a construction zone carnival, and with multiple events, you'll be dodging more than just zombies.
</BulletList>

Today's one of those days where your respiratory system will thank you, but your joints (and patience) might get a workout navigating the urban chaos. The AQI is a beautiful 25, meaning you can breathe easy without choking on whatever industrial-grade pollutants usually waft our way. The temperature is cool, definitely jacket-weather, but perfect for generating some body heat without immediately dissolving into a sweaty mess.

The biggest hurdle today isn't environmental, it's *people*. It's Halloween, folks, and San Jose is leaning into it. You've got the Gardner Health Services Día de los Muertos Run-Walk kicking off at 8:30 AM along the Guadalupe River Trail. If you're planning a scenic long run there, expect company – lots of it, probably in costume. Same goes for the Almaden Hills Trail Run happening today out in Almaden Quicksilver Park, where you'll be sharing those hills with other enthusiastic (or masochistic) trail runners. So, if solitude is your jam, you might want to wait until tomorrow or find a lesser-known dirt path.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Dodgeball: Construction & Crawls**

If you're anywhere near the urban core, especially around Market Street, Park Avenue, or San Fernando Street, be ready for a gauntlet of construction. There are ongoing lane and sidewalk closures, and a Halloween bar crawl is slated to infest downtown from 4 PM to 10 PM. This means increased pedestrian traffic (the "costume-impaired" kind) and potential road disruptions. Keep your head on a swivel. Your PT will thank you for avoiding an ankle twist.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for Success (and Survival)**

It's cool out, and while clear now, morning fog isn't unheard of this time of year. Layers are your friend. Also, with Daylight Saving Time ending soon, remind yourself that the evenings are getting darker earlier. Drivers are already doing a bang-up job hitting folks; don't give them an easier target. Wear reflective gear if you're out past sunset.
</AlertBox>
