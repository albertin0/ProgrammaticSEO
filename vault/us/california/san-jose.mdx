## Trail Talk: Mud, Closures, and Construction

This is where it gets a little more interesting, especially if you're a creature of habit with your routes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  If you're an equestrian or a mountain biker, hold off on hitting certain dirt trails today. Many trails, including portions of **Coyote Creek Parkway** and those within Ed R. Levin County Park, are currently closed to equestrians and bicyclists due to wet conditions. Pounding wet trails just ruins them for everyone, so respect the closures.
</AlertBox>

There's ongoing construction that can mess with your commutes to popular spots. For instance, the perennial West San Carlos Street streetscape improvement project is still chugging along, with expected completion in "spring 2026." While it's not a trail closure, it means potential traffic snags if you're trying to get to a starting point downtown. Also, the Calero Boat Launch is undergoing renovations and is closed until April 29th, so no pre-run paddle today.

No major events are actively blocking large sections of trails today, like a Viva CalleSJ route (the last one was on the 19th). So, you won't have to reroute around a sea of cyclists doing the open streets thing.

Bottom line: It's a decent day for a run or walk, especially if you don't mind a little drizzle and can navigate around some of the more sensitive wet-weather trail closures. Just be smart about your footing and your gear.
