## Today's Outdoor Grind: Good Air, Grumpy Sun

Look, the air quality today in San Jose is damn near perfect at an AQI of 25. That's a huge win for your respiratory system, especially when you consider what other parts of the country are dealing with right now (looking at you, East Coast wildfire smoke zones). So, if clean air is your main concern, lace up.

However, "clean air" doesn't mean "easy running." We're staring down 31.0°C (that's 87.8°F for you Fahrenheit die-hards) under a mainly clear sky. That's *hot*. Your joints might be fine, but your entire system will be working overtime to keep cool. Overheating is a real thing, and it sneaks up on you, especially on those exposed sections of trail without a lick of shade.

## Trail Updates & Warnings

Don't just assume your favorite path is clear sailing. We've got a few things brewing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Heat Hazard: Early Bird or Night Owl Only**
With temperatures hitting 31.0°C, prime midday running is just dumb. Stick to early mornings or late evenings if you're heading out for anything strenuous. Hydration is key, and I don't mean a tiny water bottle for a 10-miler. Bring enough water, then bring some more. Electrolytes wouldn't hurt either.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Coyote Valley Construction & Trail Changes**
If you frequent the **Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve**, be aware that construction for accessibility improvements started back on June 22nd. The equestrian lot is closed, and while they're trying to minimize full closures, always check for posted notices at the preserve. Also, the Arrowhead Trail is one-way for bikes, and that direction rotates monthly, so don't be *that* person going the wrong way.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Guadalupe River Trail & Alviso Marina Park**
Closer to home, the **Guadalupe River Trail** and the Los Alamitos Creek Trail are seeing vegetation removal between 7 AM and 3 PM through early September. The trails are *open*, but you'll encounter flaggers and active work. Pay attention and stay out of the work zones. Similarly, Alviso Marina County Park has intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing hauling and construction. Don't be surprised if your waterfront run has a detour.
</AlertBox>

The bottom line? Be smart. Enjoy the good air, but respect that heat. Your body will thank you, and you won't end up a heat-stroked mess on the side of a trail, waiting for me to pull you out.
