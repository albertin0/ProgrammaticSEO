Today? Unless you're chasing a PR in a sauna, you might want to adjust your plans.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
*   **Heat is the Main Event:** Forget the CrossFit Games in downtown, the real test of endurance today is Mother Nature's oven. At 31°C (that's pushing 88°F for you non-metric types), you're looking at a sweaty, potentially dangerous, workout, especially as the day heats up.
*   **Pollen's Mild Annoyance:** Moderate pollen levels mean most of you won't be completely sidelined, but if you're sensitive, you might feel that familiar tickle in your throat.
*   **Good Air, But Watch for Crowds:** The air quality in San Jose is actually pretty decent (AQI 25), so no wildfire smoke to deal with here, thankfully. However, downtown might still be a bit of a maze with the tail end of the CrossFit Games and FIFA World Cup watch parties wrapping up.
</BulletList>

Look, I love hitting the pavement or the trails as much as the next masochist, but 31 degrees Celsius before noon is no joke. The City of San José itself recommends limiting strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day, typically between 10 AM and 3 PM. And today, that "hottest part" is going to feel like a full-body hot compress.

If you absolutely *must* get out there, keep it short, keep it early (or late), and for the love of all that is holy, hydrate like you're a camel preparing for a desert crossing. Bring more water than you think you need, then double it. Electrolytes wouldn't hurt either, unless you enjoy cramping up halfway through your run on the Guadalupe River Trail.

There's also ongoing vegetation removal along the Guadalupe River Trail, as well as the Los Alamitos Creek Trail, through early September. While the trails remain open, expect to see flaggers, and don't be a hero – follow their directions. No, you don't get bonus points for ignoring the construction crew. You just get yourself or someone else hurt. The West Little Llagas Creek Trail down in Morgan Hill is fully closed through the end of the year for creek work, so don't bother heading that way.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Risk (Especially Mid-Day):** While San Jose isn't under an "Extreme Heat Warning" like some of our Southern California neighbors, 31°C is still dangerously hot for sustained outdoor exercise. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real. Limit your time outdoors, seek shade, and stay hydrated. Your PR can wait.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Lingers:** The CrossFit Games and other events in downtown San Jose are wrapping up today. Expect some lingering crowds or road impacts, particularly around the SAP Center and San Pedro Square. If you're running downtown, be aware of your surroundings and potential detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Precaution:** Moderate pollen means those with allergies or asthma should consider taking their medication beforehand. It's not a showstopper, but an ounce of prevention, right?
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. It's Monday. Take it easy. There's always tomorrow when it's hopefully not quite so sticky out there.
