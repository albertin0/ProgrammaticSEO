But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a perfect day for a PR. The temperature is hovering around 31°C (88°F) and climbing, which is prime heat exhaustion territory. <AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat
With temperatures hitting 31°C (88°F), heat-related illness is a serious risk. Stay hydrated, seek shade, and consider rescheduling intense outdoor workouts for cooler parts of the day. The National Weather Service frequently issues heat advisories for the Bay Area when temperatures climb like this.
</AlertBox>
