<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat: Don't Be a Hero, Hydrate!
With temperatures pushing 31C (88F), you need to be smart. This isn't just "sweaty workout" weather; this is "potential heat exhaustion" weather if you push too hard without fluids. Stick to shaded routes if you can, like early mornings on the Los Gatos Creek Trail before the sun bakes it, or just plain old reschedule your run for later this evening. Carry water, electrolytes, and for crying out loud, listen to your body. If you feel lightheaded, stop.
</AlertBox>
