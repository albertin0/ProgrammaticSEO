## Workout Hazards & Local Impact

### Coastal Flood Advisory for Bay Area Shorelines

If your usual route takes you anywhere near the water, pay attention. The National Weather Service has issued a Coastal Flood Advisory for shoreline areas around San Francisco Bay, which includes parts of San Jose. This advisory is in effect through Thursday, October 29. We're talking minor flooding in low-lying spots, but "minor" can still mean ankle-deep water or closed access on paths you usually cruise. Don't be that person trying to splash through unknown depths. Just turn around.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Coastal Flood Advisory:** Avoid low-lying shoreline trails, especially around Alviso. Minor flooding and road closures are possible. Don't risk it if you see standing water.
</AlertBox>

Specifically, if you're thinking of hitting the trails around **Alviso Marina County Park**, be aware that intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail might be in effect due to ongoing hauling and construction. Since it's a Monday, active work is a real possibility. Don't get caught off guard.

### Early Morning Fog & Brisk Winds

The day starts with some patchy fog before 8 AM, especially closer to the coast and low-lying areas. While it usually burns off quickly, visibility might be reduced for early birds. Combine that with those 20 mph northwest gusts predicted, and you'll feel every bit of that 49-degree Fahrenheit start.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress in Layers & Be Visible:** With patchy morning fog and brisker winds, layers are your friend. For early runs, reflective gear and lights aren't optional – they're smart.
</AlertBox>

### Downtown Detours & Trail Status

For you urban runners, keep an eye out for ongoing construction downtown. Various projects are causing lane and sidewalk closures on streets like Market St., Park Ave., and E San Fernando St. It might mean a few unexpected detours if your route involves a lot of city pavement.

Good news for cyclists near San Jose Mineta International Airport: the planned October 1 closure of Ewert Road, an essential conduit to the Guadalupe River Trail, has been *deferred*. So, that particular headache is on hold, at least for now. However, while the recent sediment removal work on the Guadalupe River (east) Trail near Highway 101 concluded on October 9, always stay alert for unexpected changes or lingering conditions on any trail, especially after periods of work.

All in all, today offers some solid running weather, but a little local knowledge goes a long way. Stay safe out there.
