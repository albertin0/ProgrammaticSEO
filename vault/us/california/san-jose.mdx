### Trail Talk & Construction Chaos

Now, for the real fun: navigating our urban jungle. If you're planning a ride or run through downtown, be aware that the **San Fernando Better Bikeway Project** is still underway, upgrading that protected bikeway from plastic posts to concrete. This means potential detours and traffic signal modifications. Similarly, the Balbach Park Transportation Improvements are impacting areas around Auzerais Avenue, Woz Way, and Almaden Boulevard with sidewalk and bike lane upgrades. Expect delays and sudden shifts in your route.

For those escaping the concrete, several popular county park trails are experiencing closures or restrictions:

<BulletList>
*   **Anderson Lake County Park:** Mostly closed until about 2032 due to the dam seismic retrofit project. Find another spot.
*   **Joseph D. Grant County Park:** Equestrians and bicyclists are still facing trail closures south of Mt. Hamilton Road due to "current weather/trail conditions."
*   **Sanborn County Park:** The Ohlone picnic area and adjacent trails are closed through the end of April due to significant trail construction.

Look, the City of San Jose boasts one of the largest urban trail networks, but you gotta know what's open and what's essentially a construction zone.
