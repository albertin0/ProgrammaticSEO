<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Wind, Watch for Smoke
Those 10-20 mph northwest winds with gusts up to 40 mph aren't just going to make your run feel like you're fighting an invisible wall, they're also a major factor in spreading any smoke from the Uvas Fire. Even if the AQI is low right now, keep an eye on real-time conditions. If you start smelling smoke, cut your run short. And remember, that wind makes staying hydrated even harder.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures: Anderson Lake Still Out
If you were planning on a long haul around Anderson Lake County Park, forget it. That place is pretty much locked down until *2032* for the dam retrofit project. Seriously, plan alternate routes now, because that's not changing anytime soon. Also, if you're near Alviso Marina, they've got intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough and Alviso Slough Trails due to ongoing construction. Don't get caught out there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Your Only Friend
With temps nearing 90°F and those strong winds, dehydration is a real risk. The City of San José recommends limiting activity between 10 AM and 3 PM, which is basically now. If you're out there, carry more water than you think you need, hit the shade frequently, and don't pretend you're invincible. Your kidneys will thank you later.
</AlertBox>
