## Today's Workout Hazards & Hot Takes

Look, I get it. The sun's out, and sometimes you just gotta move. But today, "moving" should probably involve less asphalt-pounding at noon and more shaded trail walking at dawn or dusk.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### The Heat is Still On!
Despite some heat advisories *technically* expiring yesterday, we're still sitting at a balmy 31.0°C (that's almost 88°F for you old-schoolers). The Bay Area has been under an "Extreme Heat Warning" recently, with temperatures hitting the mid-90s to low 100s, and limited overnight relief. You're gonna sweat. A lot. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light colors, and for the love of all that is holy, don't leave your dog or kid in the car. Seriously, don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures Galore
If your usual loop is the **Guadalupe River (east) Trail**, specifically between Trimble Road and the Airport Green Parking Lot, today is your last day to find it closed during the workday! Sediment removal means it's been shut down Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, and that wraps up *today*, October 9th. Plan accordingly, or you'll be doing an impromptu urban detour, which let's be honest, is never as scenic. Beyond that, the Farm Bypass Trail at the popular Rancho San Antonio is also closed through today for repairs, and again next week, October 13th-16th. Check park websites before you head out, because Santa Clara County has a fair few long-term closures too, like portions of the Adobe Creek Loop Trail in Palo Alto until the end of October.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Watch Out for E-Bikes (and their riders)
This isn't just an "old man yells at cloud" moment. E-bikes are flying around, and some of the folks riding them, especially the younger crowd, are not exactly following the rules. A 2025 study from San Jose State University found that many e-devices don't even meet legal power levels, with some having as much as eight times the normal power. Accident rates are through the roof, causing types of fractures orthopedic surgeons haven't seen before. Be extra aware of your surroundings, especially on shared-use paths. They're fast, silent, and sometimes operated by someone who thinks the trail is their personal racetrack.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### What About Smoke?
Good news: our AQI is clear today at 25. While there are some distant wildfires and a prescribed burn planned later this month for Rancho Cañada del Oro Open Space Preserve near Gilroy, neither are impacting San Jose's air quality right now. Keep an eye on local news for updates on the prescribed burn dates from the Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority, but for today, the air itself isn't a problem.
</AlertBox>
