<BulletList>

Forget a leisurely afternoon jog; this is prime "melt on the pavement" weather. Plan your outdoor sessions for the crack of dawn, or you'll be chugging water bottles like they're going out of style.

### **Extreme Heat Ahead – Seriously, Hydrate**
With temperatures reaching 31°C (88°F) and set to climb, you're looking at a moderate HeatRisk for inland Bay Area, including San Jose. This isn't just "uncomfortable," it's "potential for heat exhaustion" territory. Stick to shady routes like those along the Los Gatos Creek Trail if you absolutely must run during peak heat, and carry more water than you think you need. Or, you know, just don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Ewert Road Closure: Still a Cloud Hanging Over the Guadalupe River Trail**
While the immediate October 1st closure of Ewert Road, a vital access point to the Guadalupe River Trail, has been delayed due to forecasted weather (because apparently, a potential deluge is better than a direct access point), this issue isn't going away. Cyclists and runners need to stay vigilant about this. It's a constant battle for safe routes, and this one is a prime example of bureaucratic fumbling impacting our ability to get around.
</AlertBox>

While today is the 29th, it's always a good idea to check the City of San Jose calendar before you map out your run, especially downtown or in neighborhoods where these events typically take place. You might find a car-free paradise, or just a blocked route.
