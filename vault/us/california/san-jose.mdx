## Current Conditions & Hazards

It's clear out there right now, which means absolutely no shade from that scorching sun. While our trusty local San Jose Fire Department quickly squashed a vegetation fire near the Highway 280/87 interchange yesterday, don't mistake the lack of smoke for a green light. The real danger today isn't smoke, it's the mercury climbing high enough to fry an egg on the pavement.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!
  Seriously, folks. The National Weather Service has slapped a Heat Advisory on us until Thursday evening. Temperatures are projected to soar into the mid-90s and even touch 105°F in some areas. Even mild overnight temperatures won't give you much relief. This isn't just "uncomfortable hot"; this is "your body can't regulate its temperature" hot. Hydration is critical. Get medical help if you experience symptoms of heat exhaustion or stroke.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk

If you absolutely insist on getting your miles in, be aware of a few things. The Bay Trail near East Caribbean Drive has some temporary lane closures and detours today (7 AM to 5 PM) due to soil delivery for a restoration project. Flaggers will be out there, so pay attention. Also, don't even think about hitting Anderson Lake County Park for any serious trail runs; most of it remains closed until 2032 for the dam seismic retrofit, so your route will be seriously curtailed. Intermittent closures also affect sections of the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern Alviso Slough Trail because of ongoing hauling and construction.
