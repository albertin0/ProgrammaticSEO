**Extreme Heat Warning: Ditch the Mid-Day Grind!**
With temperatures pushing 31.0°C (87.8°F), any intense outdoor activity between 10 AM and 3 PM is asking for trouble. Seriously, reschedule that long run. If you *must* be out, hydrate constantly, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, find some shade and rest. Your body can't cool itself effectively in this kind of heat, and heat stroke is a fast track to the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Alum Rock Park Drill in Progress!**
Heads up if Alum Rock Park is your usual stomping ground. Santa Clara County is running its annual wildland drill there from June 8-12. Expect increased activity, potential access restrictions, and generally not the serene nature experience you might be seeking. Find another spot for your trail run this week.
</AlertBox>
