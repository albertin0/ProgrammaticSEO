## Mind Your Step (and Your Wheels)

While the weather's cooperating, the city is still in a constant state of improvement (read: construction). If your route takes you near major thoroughfares or certain county parks, you need to be aware.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail & Bikeway Blockades:** The **San Fernando Better Bikeway Project** kicked off in March and will be messing with San Fernando Street until March 2027. Expect detours and potential slowdowns if that's part of your urban route. Likewise, the **Balbach Park Transportation Improvements** are ongoing through spring 2026, impacting parts of Auzerais Avenue, Woz Way, and Almaden Boulevard.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Long-Term Park Closures:** Don't plan on adventuring too deep into **Anderson Lake County Park** anytime soon. Many areas, including sections of the Serpentine Trail and Lakeview Trail, are closed until approximately *2032* due to seismic retrofitting on the dam. Seriously, 2032. Plan alternative routes now. Also, **Sanborn County Park's Ohlone picnic area** and related trail construction will keep you out through the end of April.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Guadalupe River Trail:** While no immediate flooding warnings are in effect for today, remember that the Guadalupe River Trail is notorious for seasonal closures at its low points. The city is working on a "Bike Connections Plan" to help navigate these, but always check local park alerts before you go, especially after any significant rain. Yesterday had some rain in the Bay Area, so while San Jose got minimal, some sections might still be damp.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's conditions are favorable for outdoor activity, provided you account for the moderate pollen and the various construction-related headaches around town. Pick your paths wisely, and enjoy the relatively calm weather before the next Bay Area storm rolls in.
