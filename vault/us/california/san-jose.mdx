## Workout Hazards to Watch For

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Seriously, It's Hot
With the temperature clocking in at 31°C, this is a dangerous day for intense outdoor activity, especially during peak sun hours. San Jose has seen a lot of abnormally warm days this year already, and while earlier in October we had official heat advisories, today's temp is still exceptionally high for late autumn. Your body will struggle to regulate its temperature. This isn't the day to try for a PR on the Hellyer Park loop unless you're starting at dawn.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Continues to Block Your Path
If your workout involves navigating Downtown, prepare for detours. Projects on Market St, Park Ave, William St, and E San Fernando St mean lane and sidewalk closures are the norm. Cyclists, pedestrians, and runners need to stay vigilant. Even the "Repave San Jose" initiative means ongoing street work everywhere, from major arteries to local neighborhood roads, complete with temporary closures and parking restrictions. Don't assume your usual route is clear; check the City's road closure maps before you head out.

And speaking of frustrating closures, that looming threat to **Ewert Road** by San Jose Mineta International Airport is still hanging over the heads of our cycling and pedestrian community. This road is a crucial link to the **Guadalupe River Trail**, especially when that Highway 101 underpass inevitably floods later in the season. While the airport "deferred" the planned October 1st closure due to anticipated El Niño weather, the intent to permanently block this essential path for bureaucratic convenience, not actual safety, remains. Plan alternate routes if you rely on this stretch, because it won't stay open forever. Also, if you're planning a trip further afield, many areas of **Anderson Lake County Park** are closed until 2032 for the dam seismic retrofit project, so check Santa Clara County Parks for specific trail impacts there too.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Smart, Stay Hydrated
*   **Timing is Everything:** If you must be outside, go early morning or late evening. Avoid the 10 AM to 4 PM window like it's the plague.
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Drink water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. If you're thirsty, you're already behind. Electrolytes aren't a bad idea today either.
*   **Dress for Success:** Light-colored, loose-fitting, moisture-wicking clothing is your uniform today. And for goodness sake, wear a hat.
*   **Listen to Your Body:** If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively fatigued, stop. Find shade, cool down, and get fluids. Don't push through heat symptoms; it's not worth it.
*   **Be Aware of E-Bikes:** New state laws are attempting to curb unsafe e-bike use, especially concerning speed on shared paths. Keep an eye out for faster-moving e-bikes if you're on a multi-use trail.
</AlertBox>
