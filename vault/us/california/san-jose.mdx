## Trail Closures & Local Headaches

Don't go thinking it's all sunshine and PRs just because the weather's cooperating. San Jose always finds a way to throw a wrench in your plans.

### Guadalupe River Trail: Watch for Crews

If you're hitting the Guadalupe River Trail today, be advised that Santa Clara Valley Water District crews are out doing vegetation removal. They'll be at it until early September, usually between 7 AM and 3 PM. The good news is the trails *should* remain open, with flaggers directing traffic.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up on Guadalupe River Trail:** Expect vegetation removal crews and flaggers between 7 AM and 3 PM. Pay attention to their directions; don't wander into a worksite.
</AlertBox>

### Five Wounds Trail: Southern Section Still a No-Go

Remember that Five Wounds Trail project? Yeah, the southernmost portion is still stalled. Turns out, preserving a historic railroad trestle (the Coyote Creek Trestle) is more complicated than building a bike path. So, if you were planning to run that stretch, find an alternative. They're focusing on the central part for now.

### West Little Llagas Creek Trail: Closed for the Long Haul

Further south in Morgan Hill, the West Little Llagas Creek Trail is closed from Watsonville Road to Edes Court, and sections north of that will follow later this summer. This closure is for creek widening and deepening, and it's set to last through the end of the year. Don't try to sneak in there; Valley Water specifically says "DO NOT enter the closed trail sections, as they and the surrounding area could be unsafe." They're rebuilding the trails eventually, but until then, stay away.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: West Little Llagas Creek Trail is CLOSED.** Do not enter closed sections; they are unsafe due to ongoing creek work.
</AlertBox>

### Anderson Lake County Park: Still mostly closed

Just a reminder that large parts of Anderson Lake County Park are still closed, and will be until about 2032, thanks to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. This means no boating, no fishing along the shoreline, and certain trail sections like parts of Coyote Road and Lakeview Trail are off-limits. Plan your long runs accordingly.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip: Stay hydrated.** Even though the temps are mild, it's still July in California. Don't be that person cramping up at mile three.
</AlertBox>
