## The Lowdown on Running San Jose Today

Look, San Jose, we've had worse. Much worse. Today isn't one of those days where you debate whether your lungs will melt or your joints will fuse. The weather's actually quite pleasant for August, a rare gift we should probably question.

### Air Quality: Don't Trust the Initial Glance

So your app says AQI 25 and you're thinking, "Great, full send!" Not so fast, champ. The Bay Area Air District issued an advisory because wildfire smoke from up north is still trying to sneak into the region. While it might not register as a red alert on the monitors, ground-level smoke can be unpredictable and hit sensitive folks harder.
<AlertBox type="warning">
**Regional Wildfire Smoke Advisory:** Even with a good current AQI, an advisory is in effect for wildfire smoke from the Pacific Northwest, which *could* lead to hazy skies and potential ground-level smoke, especially for sensitive groups. If you start smelling smoke or feeling any irritation, cut your workout short and head indoors. No PR is worth a trip to the urgent care.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Watch for the Orange Cones

Most of our beloved routes are clear, but some spots always have something going on. If you're planning a long run down the **Guadalupe River Trail**, be aware that Valley Water is doing vegetation removal through early September. They'll have flaggers, so just follow directions and don't be that hero who runs through an active worksite. Your PT won't appreciate it, and neither will the heavy machinery.

Additionally, for those who venture into the Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District, specifically at Bear Creek Redwoods Preserve, several trails like Toad Hollow, Myotis, and Lower Pete Siemens are closed until September for new parking and trail construction. Always check the specific preserve pages if you're heading deep into the wilderness, because nothing sucks more than a surprise detour mid-run.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Always Check Trail Status:** Construction and maintenance are ongoing in various parks. If you're hitting county or regional trails, make a quick call to the Santa Clara County Parks trail hotline or check their website. Better safe than turning your long run into an unexpected bushwhacking adventure.
</AlertBox>

Overall, go enjoy the mild weather while it lasts. Just use your brain about the air, and don't assume every path is perfectly clear.
