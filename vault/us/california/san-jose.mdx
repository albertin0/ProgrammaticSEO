## The Heat is On (and On, and On)

Look, I love a good run as much as the next masochist, but 31 degrees Celsius (that's pushing 88°F for you Fahrenheit loyalists) is not "brisk." It's "borderline dangerous if you don't play it smart." San Jose is expected to see highs in the 90s for the rest of the week. While our AQI is a perfectly respectable 25 with no significant wildfire smoke currently hanging around, that just means you'll be breathing *clean* hot air, which, newsflash, is still hot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours!
Seriously, avoid any strenuous outdoor activity between 10 AM and 4 PM. The city itself warns against it. If you *must* be outside, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does) and take frequent breaks in the shade. Overdoing it in this kind of heat is how you end up looking like a melted popsicle, or worse, in the ER with heat stroke.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Troubles: More Than Just the Heat

Even if you're a glutton for punishment and decide to brave the heat, you might hit some roadblocks. If your usual stomping ground is **Alum Rock Park**, be aware that the Penitencia Creek Trail is closed at the Trestle Bridge, forcing you onto the park road as a detour. Plus, the Mineral Springs Trail is shut down due to erosion. So much for a scenic, uninterrupted suffer-fest.

Over on the **Guadalupe River Trail**, there's been ongoing maintenance, especially near the airport. You might encounter flaggers holding up traffic for five minutes at a time, and some runners have reported glass on the trail, particularly around Hedding and Berryessa. Nothing says "great workout" like dodging broken bottles.

And if you were hoping for more of the Five Wounds Trail, forget about the southernmost portion for now. That project is paused thanks to a historic railroad trestle getting in the way, with no timeline for construction on that section.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Hazards
Check local park websites before you head out. Alum Rock Park has active closures, and parts of the Guadalupe River Trail have ongoing maintenance and reported debris. Don't assume your usual route is clear sailing.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Escape
If you insist on going out, go early, or go late. Carry more water than you think you need. Tell someone your route. And for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. That "push through it" mentality is great for a race, not for preventing heat exhaustion on a random Thursday.
</AlertBox>
