**Extreme Heat Warning:** With San Jose facing temperatures of 31°C (87.8°F) and a heat advisory for the Santa Clara Valley, prolonged outdoor activity can quickly lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke. Limit your time outside, especially between 10 AM and 3 PM. Hydrate constantly, and listen to your body – it's not trying to kill you, unlike this heat.

Seriously, save your PR attempts for a cooler day. The city of San Jose itself advises limiting physical activity during the hottest parts of the day, suggesting frequent breaks and staying in shaded areas. If you insist on getting those miles in, hit the pavement before 8 AM or after 6 PM. Even then, dial back the intensity. Your lungs might be okay with the air, but your cardiovascular system will be working overtime trying to cool you down in this soup.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Guadalupe River Trail Closures:** Heads up, runners and cyclists! The Guadalupe River Trail is partially closed for sediment removal between Trimble Road and Airport Parkway. This closure is effective Monday through Friday, from 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, until September 25th. You'll need to find an alternate route or adjust your schedule.

And it's not just the Guadalupe River Trail. We've got ongoing construction and maintenance popping up all over Santa Clara County. The US 101/SR 25 interchange is seeing daytime work this week, meaning heavy equipment and construction crews near roadways – stay alert if your route takes you anywhere near there.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** In this heat, you're losing fluids faster than a politician loses promises. Drink more water than you think you need, before, during, and after your workout. Electrolyte drinks can help too. Leave the sugary sodas for a celebration, not for rehydration.
</AlertBox>

Don't be a hero. This isn't the day to push it. Find an indoor gym, hit the pool, or just take a rest day. Your body (and your joints, and maybe even your lungs if those higher AQI numbers are accurate for your block) will thank you.
