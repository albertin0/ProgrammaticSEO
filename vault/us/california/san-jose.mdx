### Air Quality & Allergies

Let's talk lungs first. Our AQI is sitting pretty at a lovely 25, which means you can breathe deeply without wondering if you're accidentally chain-smoking. That haze we had earlier in the week from the Angel Island prescribed burn? Gone. So, for once, your respiratory system gets a break.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pollen Update
Pollen levels are moderate today. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, pop an antihistamine before you head out, and maybe stash some tissues in your running belt. Nobody wants snot rockets on the Los Gatos Creek Trail.
</AlertBox>

### Weather on the Wane (or Wax?)

It's 14.4°C (a cool 58°F) and overcast right now, which is perfect for avoiding that harsh morning sun. The forecast says it'll clear up and get to a pleasant 22°C (71°F) later, with only light winds. Basically, it's a Goldilocks day for temperature – not too hot, not too cold. Just right.

### Local Trail & Road Chaos

Now for the real talk. It's Saturday, which means people are out. And some of those people are *racing*.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Sanborn County Park is a Zoo!
Forget a peaceful run in the redwoods today if you were planning on hitting Sanborn County Park. The Spartan Trail Challenge is happening, which means 50K, Half Marathon, and 10K races. Expect crowds, congestion, and limited parking. If you're not running the race, find another park or wait until tomorrow. Don't be "that guy" trying to weave through a race course.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the race scene, the city's usual construction ballet continues. You'll find ongoing utility work affecting major thoroughfares like Monterey Road, Market Street, and West Julian Street, so if your route involves those, be prepared for lane closures and general road-rage-inducing delays. These aren't typically trail issues, but if you're biking to a trail, factor it in.

And speaking of trails, remember the Ewert Road situation near the San Jose Mineta International Airport? That crucial bike path connecting to the Guadalupe River Trail was slated for an October 1st closure, but thankfully, they've deferred it. Still, keep an eye out, as the future of that route is still a bit up in the air, especially with El Niño concerns looming.

<AlertBox type="info">
### El Niño Watch
Governor Newsom declared a statewide emergency earlier this week in anticipation of a strong El Niño this fall and winter. While we're not seeing heavy rain or flooding today, keep this in mind for future workouts. "Heavy rainfall, strong winds, mountain snow, and high waves" are on the menu for later in the year, potentially causing "flooding, landslides, debris flows, and coastal damage." Stay updated on weather advisories before longer runs or rides in areas prone to slides or washouts.
</AlertBox>

So, get out there, enjoy the great air and decent weather, but be mindful of your surroundings – especially around Sanborn County Park.
