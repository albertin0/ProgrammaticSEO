<AlertBox type="danger">
### Danger: Extreme Heat is No Joke
With temperatures hovering around 31.0°C (87.8°F), heat-related illness is a serious risk. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 3 PM. If you MUST be out, hydrate constantly, wear light-colored, loose clothing, and know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. This isn't the time to hit your PR on the Los Gatos Creek Trail. Your body is already struggling to regulate its core temperature.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Warning: Breathe Carefully
The "Moderate" AQI from PM2.5 and that ongoing Mines Fire are not a good combo for your lungs. Even if you're not in a "sensitive group," prolonged outdoor exertion can lead to respiratory irritation. If you've got asthma or other lung issues, seriously consider an indoor workout or just taking a rest day. Don't forget that moderate pollen count adding to the misery.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### PSA: Community Centers Closed Today
Just a heads-up for those of you hoping for an indoor option: All City of San Jose Community Centers are closed today, October 12th, for a holiday. Don't get caught out in the heat just to find a locked door. Check facility schedules before you head out.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe, San Jose. There's always tomorrow for that killer workout, assuming we don't spontaneously combust.
