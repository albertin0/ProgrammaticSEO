## The Heat Is On (and it's a problem)

Look, I love pounding the pavement as much as the next masochist, but 31 degrees Celsius – that's 88 degrees Fahrenheit for those still clinging to imperial, and the forecast says we're hitting 90 in San Jose today – is not prime running weather. The National Weather Service has slapped a heat advisory on the entire Bay Area, effective from 10 AM today until Friday evening. They're not doing it for kicks; there's a moderate risk of heat-related illness for anyone sensitive to this kind of oven-like warmth.

If you absolutely *must* get out there, especially on exposed stretches like the Los Gatos Creek Trail where shade is a premium, you need a plan. And that plan better involve more water than you think you need, and timing your torture session to the coolest parts of the day. Which, let's be honest, aren't exactly "cool" today.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
San Jose is forecasted to hit 90°F today, with Tuesday looking even hotter at 94°F. This is dangerously hot. Limit outdoor strenuous activities to early morning or late evening. Drink copious amounts of fluids and find air-conditioned spaces if possible. Your body isn't designed to run a marathon in a convection oven.
</AlertBox>

## Air Quality & Allergies

The good news, I guess, is that our Air Quality Index is currently sitting pretty at a "Good" 25. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, which isn't ideal, but it's not the choking smoke we sometimes deal with. So your lungs, in theory, won't be actively protesting pollutants, just the sheer volume of hot air you're trying to push through them.

Pollen, however, is being a mild annoyance.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
With temperatures soaring, dehydration is a real threat. Don't just sip water; actively chug it. Consider electrolytes if you're going to push it, and know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. That headache and dizziness aren't just you being out of shape; they're warning signs.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. Be smart. This isn't the day to try for a PR. This is the day to consider a treadmill, or at the very least, a very, very short walk at dawn or dusk.
