## Get Out There (Carefully)

Alright, San Jose, let's talk about hitting the pavement today. You've got options, but you've also got some serious heat to contend with. The fact that high temperatures around 100°F (38°C) were "possible for San Jose and Santa Clara" just yesterday, June 13, means we're still deep in this warm spell. Even if the official heat advisory for the Santa Clara Valley expired last night, the air isn't magically cooler.

### Heat Is No Joke

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** With temperatures pushing close to 90°F, heatstroke and heat exhaustion are serious risks. Don't be a hero. Hydrate aggressively *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Schedule your runs for the early morning or late evening. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or get muscle cramps, stop immediately and find shade. This isn't just discomfort; it's a medical emergency waiting to happen.
</AlertBox>

### Your Lungs & Allergies

The AQI at 56 is "Fair," meaning the air quality is generally acceptable for most folks. However, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, those of us with sensitive lungs might still experience some minor irritation or moderate symptoms from prolonged exposure. Couple that with moderate pollen, and it's a recipe for a slightly congested run. Check in with your body; if you're wheezing more than usual, cut it short.

### Embrace the Open Streets!

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Viva CalleSJ Crowds:** While amazing, Viva CalleSJ turns major streets into a shared space. Expect large crowds, especially in popular areas like around Japantown. Keep your head up, be mindful of others, and remember it's not a race. You're sharing the road with strollers, skateboards, and casual walkers. Maintain a safe speed and be aware of your surroundings.
</AlertBox>

Today is your chance to experience San Jose's streets in a totally different way thanks to Viva CalleSJ. Miles of roadways are closed to cars, giving you a unique opportunity to run, bike, or walk through the city without dodging traffic. It's an awesome community event, so if you're looking for a change of scenery from your usual Guadalupe River Trail loops, this is it. Just remember to share the space.

### Final Word

Don't let the heat or the crowds deter you, but do respect them. Grab your water, slather on the sunscreen, and enjoy the car-free streets while you can. Or, if you prefer solitude, hit one of the quieter trails super early. Just be safe out there.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Traffic:** While Viva CalleSJ offers car-free zones, keep in mind there are ongoing VTA road closures and "Repave San Jose" projects across the city. These won't impact the Viva CalleSJ route itself, but they might make driving to and from different parts of the city a headache. Consider public transport or biking to the Viva CalleSJ route.
