If you're looking to crush a PR today, you might just crush yourself instead. Today is *not* the day to push it outdoors in San Jose unless you've got a serious hydration plan and an early alarm clock.

<BulletList>
*   The Bay Area is getting cooked. Expect record-breaking heat for March, with San Jose hitting a scorching 31°C (88°F) and pushing towards the 90s. Your body isn't acclimated to this.
*   "Moderate" pollen is a minor inconvenience compared to the furnace outside, but if you're sensitive, your lungs will feel it on top of the heat stress.
*   The San Jose Shamrock Run is happening downtown around San Pedro Square Market, which means traffic, crowds, and potential detours if you're trying to navigate that area this morning.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. The sun's out, the sky is clear, and the AQI is practically sparkling at 25. Normally, I'd be telling you to hit the Los Gatos Creek Trail and enjoy the fresh air. But "Mainly Clear" at 31°C in March? That's not a blessing, it's a trap. We're talking about unseasonably hot conditions that are a "moderate risk of heat-related illnesses" because your body hasn't had time to adjust to this kind of punishment.

If you absolutely *must* get out there, forget anything strenuous. This isn't the day for speed work or that long run you've been planning. Think short, think shaded, and think early or late. Forget peak sun hours entirely. Cal/OSHA is even putting out advisories for outdoor workers, so if they're worried, you should be too.

The air quality might be good, but don't let that fool you into thinking it's a free pass. Heat stress will hit you faster and harder than you expect. Those of you who usually cruise along the Guadalupe River Trail in the afternoons should know that there have been weekday closures for maintenance until 3:30 p.m. this month, so be ready for detours or just pick another spot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Temperatures are dangerously high for this time of year. Dehydration and heat illness are serious risks. Limit outdoor activity, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM. Drink more water than you think you need. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Shamrock Run Alert:** If you're anywhere near downtown San Jose today, especially around San Pedro Square Market, expect road closures and a significant increase in foot traffic due to the Shamrock Run. Plan alternative routes if you're trying to run or commute through that area.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen Present:** While the big concern is heat, moderate pollen can still trigger allergies for some. If you're prone to seasonal sniffles or asthma, consider an antihistamine before heading out.
</AlertBox>

Frankly, today's a perfect day to discover the joys of an air-conditioned treadmill or a nice, cool yoga studio. Your lungs and joints (and common sense) will thank you.
