## The Heat Is On (and it's not a good thing)

Let's cut to the chase: 31 degrees Celsius, or about 88 degrees Fahrenheit, is not "pleasant for an outdoor workout." It's "hydration-is-your-new-best-friend" weather, especially when the sun's beating down in a "Mainly Clear" sky. You might think "clear skies, great!" but that just means direct solar radiation turning you into a slow-cooked meal. This isn't the kind of "good heat training" they talk about for Western States; this is the kind that puts you in the ER. Remember those warnings from a few years back about temps hitting triple digits? Today might not be *that* extreme, but it's well above our average May comfort zone.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: Hydrate or Die-drate!**
With temperatures pushing 88°F, heatstroke and heat exhaustion are real risks. Seriously, scale back your intensity, shorten your run, and for the love of all that is holy, drink more water than you think you need. The City of San José and Santa Clara County officials consistently advise limiting activity during the hottest part of the day, typically 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. If you're out there now, you're in that window.
</AlertBox>

Today, May 2nd, the Hacienda Hills Run is taking over, with everything from 5Ks to 50Ks kicking off early this morning. That means crowded paths, bottlenecking, and a distinct lack of tranquility. Same goes for the MAYHEM Fitness Race happening elsewhere in San Jose. You'll be dodging competitors and probably feeling some serious FOMO if you're not part of the event.
