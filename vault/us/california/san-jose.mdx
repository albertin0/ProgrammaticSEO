## Hazards to Watch For

### Construction Chaos on the VTA Line

<AlertBox type="danger">
The VTA light rail extension is causing significant headaches (and literal vibrations) for residents and anyone trying to navigate the area from East Ridge Mall towards BART. Expect noise, potential detours, and uneven surfaces if you choose to run through this zone. This isn't just inconvenient; it's a legitimate safety concern with ground vibrations impacting structural integrity of nearby homes. Choose an alternate route if possible, like heading towards the trails around **Rancho Cañada del Oro Open Space Preserve** for a clearer path.
</AlertBox>

### A Brief Reprieve from the Heat

<AlertBox type="info">
While our current 12.5°C and overcast conditions are quite pleasant, don't let your guard down. Just yesterday, San Jose was hitting 81°F (27.2°C), tying daily records, and more widespread warming is expected next week. This cool snap is temporary. Stay hydrated and acclimated, because California's forecast is still wild, with Cal/OSHA already reminding employers about heat illness prevention. Enjoy the mild weather while it lasts, but remember that rapid temperature changes can be tough on your system if you're not prepared for the eventual return of the heat.
</AlertBox>

### Moderate Air Quality and Pollen

<AlertBox type="warning">
With a moderate AQI of 54 (PM2.5 dominant) and moderate pollen levels, sensitive individuals might experience some respiratory irritation or allergy symptoms. It's not a "stay inside" day, but if you're prone to seasonal allergies or have lung issues, consider an earlier or later workout, or stick to areas with less exposure to potential pollutants.
</AlertBox>

### Long-Term Trail Closures

A heads-up if you're thinking of venturing further south: several areas within **Anderson Lake County Park** remain closed due to the ongoing Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project, and this isn't changing anytime soon – closures are expected until approximately 2032. Always check park websites before heading out to avoid wasted trips.
