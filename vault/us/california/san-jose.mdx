## The Lowdown on Lungs & Joints

Alright, let's break it down. Your lungs are actually getting a pretty sweet deal today. The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 25, with no nasty dominant pollutants to speak of. Seriously, enjoy it while it lasts; we all know how quickly that can change around here. So, breathe easy, literally.

Now, your joints (and every other cell in your body trying to regulate temperature) are going to hate you if you go out unprepared. That 31.0°C (87.8°F) combined with the Heat Advisory means heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real possibilities, even for seasoned runners. This isn't just about being uncomfortable; it's about pushing your cardiovascular system into overdrive and risking serious medical trouble.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, I'm not kidding about this heat. When temperatures push into the 90s and low 100s, it's not the time for your personal best. Reduce your mileage, significantly slow your pace, or better yet, take it indoors. Hydrate *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. And for the love of all that is holy, if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or get a headache, STOP. Get to shade, get water, and cool down. Your PR isn't worth a trip to Kaiser.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures & Erosion
The City of San Jose isn't closing trails in Alum Rock Park just for fun. Erosion is a legitimate hazard, especially after previous weather events. Trying to navigate a closed trail can lead to twisted ankles, falls, or worse. Respect the closures on Penitencia Creek and Mineral Springs trails – find an alternate route or choose a different park entirely.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated, Smarty Pants
It might seem obvious, but if you're going to ignore my advice and run in this heat, at least drink some water. And I don't mean a few sips from the fountain at Kelley Park. Electrolytes are your friend. A little salt goes a long way. Or just, you know, run at dawn or dusk when it's not a literal oven outside.
</AlertBox>

In short: the air is clear, which is a win, but the heat is a colossal L. Combine that with some ongoing trail issues, and it's a day for conservative choices or a date with your treadmill.
