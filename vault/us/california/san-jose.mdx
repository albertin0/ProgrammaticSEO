Look, I love hitting the pavement or trails as much as the next maniac, but today requires some common sense. That thermometer isn't lying. A 31°C day in San Jose, even with "Mainly Clear" skies, is prime territory for overheating. Your lungs might be good with an AQI of 25, which is fantastic, but your cardiovascular system and joints are going to be screaming if you don't take precautions.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Heat Advisory: Not a Joke
  The city of San Jose itself warns against intense physical activity during the hottest parts of the day. This isn't just about feeling uncomfortable; we're talking about real risks like heat exhaustion and heat stroke. If you *must* run, do it at dawn, not at high noon. Seriously, hydrate, hydrate, hydrate, and consider carrying a water bottle with electrolytes. Don't be that person collapsing on the Guadalupe River Trail.
</AlertBox>

Pollen's sitting at "Moderate" too, which, while not a total shutdown, means anyone prone to allergies is going to feel it. Combine that with the heat, and your lungs are working overtime. Maybe reconsider that hill repeat session if you're already feeling sniffly.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Construction Zone Ahead
  Don't be surprised if your usual route takes an unexpected turn. There's significant infrastructure work going on, including a 12-mile underground power line project impacting Monterey Road. Pay attention to signage, be aware of increased vehicle traffic around work zones, and for Pete's sake, give construction crews some space. Your workout isn't worth getting in the way of heavy machinery or getting clipped by a frustrated driver.
</AlertBox>

Also, while the Air Quality Index is currently a nice, low 25, remember that during these warmer months, ground-level ozone can build up. The Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD) initiates "Spare the Air" smog season from April to October. While there's no alert today, it's always good practice to keep an eye on the forecast, especially if you're prone to respiratory issues.

My advice? If you can swing it, hit the gym today or pick a heavily shaded route, keep it short, and go slow. Your body will thank you, and you won't end up a heat casualty.
