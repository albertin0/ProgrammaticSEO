Look, the air quality (AQI 25) is technically stellar, so your lungs aren't going to be screaming from smog for once. That's the good news. The bad news? Your entire circulatory system will be screaming from the heat if you push it. We're looking at temperatures hitting at least 86°F today, and frankly, it feels worse when the sun is beating down on you, clear skies and all.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heatstroke is No Joke
Seriously, it's hot. Avoid strenuous activity between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. If you insist on going out, go early, go short, and carry enough water to float a small canoe. You should be drinking more than you think you need. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, ugly possibilities when it's this warm. Don't end up in the ER because you "needed" that outdoor workout.
</AlertBox>
