### South Bay Trail Updates

If your usual grind takes you along the **Guadalupe River Trail**, you might encounter some vegetation removal crews between 7 AM and 3 PM. They're working adjacent to the trail, so expect flaggers to guide you. Don't be "that guy" who ignores the signs and stomps through a worksite. The trail itself *should* remain open, but keep your head up.

For those who venture south, parts of **Alviso Marina County Park** are still seeing intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing construction. And let's not forget the grand daddy of all long-term projects: much of **Anderson Lake County Park** is effectively a no-go zone until roughly 2032 because of the seismic retrofit project. Plan your long runs accordingly, because that dam isn't fixing itself overnight.

<AlertBox type="warning">
The southernmost section of the planned Five Wounds Trail in East San Jose is currently paused. Turns out, a historic railroad trestle is putting a wrench in those plans, so don't expect to connect Story Road and Highway 101 there anytime soon. They're focusing on the central part, so if you're exploring new routes, verify before you go.
</AlertBox>

Finally, while not an immediate hazard today, some of our county parks like Calero, Joseph D. Grant, and Stevens Creek have "scheduled prescribed burn piles" on the docket for 2026. If the wind shifts when those are active, you might catch a whiff of smoke, so be aware.

Overall, it's a pretty sweet day for San Jose runners. Just be smart about where you're going and pay attention to your surroundings.
