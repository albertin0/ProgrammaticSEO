### Weather & Air Quality Rundown

We're looking at "Mainly Clear" skies and a toasty 31°C (88°F). While it's not quite "Red Flag Warning" heat for *our* specific forecast, this isn't exactly prime running weather, especially for long distances or intense efforts. Regionally, the Bay Area has seen heat advisories recently, and it's always good to remember those inland temps can soar.

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting pretty at a "Good" 25, with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant. No "Spare the Air" alerts or wildfire smoke advisories currently in effect, which is a relief given what we've seen in recent years. However, there was a brush fire near Communications Hill just a few days ago, on June 26th. It burned about 7 acres adjacent to Chateau La Salle mobile home park but was contained quickly. While it's out, CAL FIRE is still forecasting above-normal fire activity for Northern California through July. So, if you're out near the hills, stay vigilant.

Pollen is at a "Moderate" level. For many, this is just an annoyance, but if you're prone to seasonal allergies, be prepared for sneezing and itchy eyes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Hydration is NOT Optional in This Heat.** Seriously. If you're heading out for anything more than a quick jaunt around your block, carry water. And no, that sad little water fountain at the Guadalupe River Trail isn't going to cut it for a 10-miler in this kind of weather. Plan your route around known water sources or carry enough for your entire activity. Electrolytes aren't a bad idea either.
</AlertBox>
