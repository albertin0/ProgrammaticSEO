Save your PR attempts for a cooler day.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Ahead: Stay Hydrated or Stay Home
Running in nearly 88-degree heat is no joke. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious. If you absolutely *must* get out there, go before 9 AM or after 6 PM. Drink water like it's your job, and don't wait until you're thirsty. That's too late. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and maybe even a hat. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or unusually fatigued, stop immediately. Find shade, hydrate, and cool down. Your PR isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>
