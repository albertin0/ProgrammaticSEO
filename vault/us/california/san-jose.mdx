## The Breakdown: What You Need to Know

### Weather & Air Quality

Your lungs are getting a relative break today. With an AQI of 25 and "Ozone/Other" as the dominant pollutant, the air quality is in the "Good" category, meaning most of us can enjoy outdoor activities without issue. There was an air quality advisory issued earlier in the week due to smoke from the Little Fire near Pleasanton, but thankfully, pollution levels weren't expected to exceed health standards here in San Jose. So, no Spare the Air Alert for us today.

The big one, however, is the heat. That 31°C is no joke, especially as the day progresses. The National Weather Service has been pushing out heat advisories for interior Bay Area spots, and while you might think San Jose's not *that* inland, trust me, the sun beats down.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Don't Be a Hero, Hydrate and Time Your Efforts!** Running in nearly 90-degree weather is a recipe for heat exhaustion or worse. Drink plenty of water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. Stick to early mornings or late evenings. Avoid peak sun hours between 10 AM and 4 PM like it's a plague. Wear light, loose clothing. Nobody needs to see your heatstroke dance on the Guadalupe River Trail.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards & Updates

*   **Trail Construction on Guadalupe River Trail:** Valley Water is doing some vegetation removal along sections of the Guadalupe River Trail and the Los Alamitos Creek Trail through early September. The trails are staying open, but expect flaggers. Pay attention to them; they're trying to keep your clumsy self safe.
*   **Anderson Lake County Park Closed:** Just a reminder for the long haul: large parts of Anderson Lake County Park are shut down until around 2032 for the seismic retrofit project on the dam. Don't plan your long runs there anytime soon.
*   **Five Wounds Trail Delays:** The southern section of the planned Five Wounds Trail in East San Jose is facing delays due to a historic railroad trestle. Work is focusing on the central portion for now, so plan accordingly if that's your usual stomping ground.
*   **VTA Overnight Work on S. Capitol Ave:** Northbound S. Capitol Avenue (between Capitol Expressway and Westboro Drive) was closed overnight until 5:30 AM today for concrete pouring for the Eastridge to BART Regional Connector. If you're an early bird runner or cyclist, you might still encounter some residual impacts or detours, and pedestrians might be escorted through.
*   **CrossFit Games Downtown:** The SAP Center is hosting the 2026 CrossFit Games from today through Sunday. This means downtown San Jose, especially around Santa Clara Street, will likely see increased traffic, crowds, and general fitness-bro energy. If you're running downtown, expect detours or just more people.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route (and Your Bailout).** Given the heat and potential for localized slowdowns, know your chosen route, carry enough water, and tell someone where you're going. Don't be that person needing a rescue because you thought you could "power through" without a plan.
</AlertBox>
