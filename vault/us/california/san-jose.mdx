### Heat Hazard: Seriously, Stay Hydrated

That 31.0°C isn't playing around. Even if it feels fine when you start, sustained exertion in this kind of heat, especially around midday, can lead to heat exhaustion or worse. Remember that a few days ago, forecasters were warning about temperatures soaring in the Santa Clara Valley.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger: Extreme Heat Risk.** The official San Jose advice isn't just a suggestion: "Avoid physical activity during the hottest time of the day, typically between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m." If you insist on getting out, go early, go short, and listen to your body. Or just hit the treadmill.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Closures

The good news is we aren't seeing any immediate major trail closures due to flooding, though the Guadalupe River Trail is always prone to seasonal issues at its low points. No reports of high winds or new wildfires sending smoke our way.

However, if you're heading south, be mindful of ongoing, long-term closures at **Anderson Lake County Park**. Significant portions, including parts of the Serpentine Trail and Lakeview Trail, are shut down until roughly 2032 for the seismic retrofit project on the dam. Don't be that person ignoring the signs and wandering into a construction zone or an unstable area. Check the Santa Clara County Parks website before you head out.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up: Road Work Ahead.** VTA has traffic advisories for the I-280/Wolfe Rd Interchange construction from today until July 10th. While not a trail closure, expect potential traffic delays if you're driving to a trailhead in that vicinity. Plan accordingly.
</AlertBox>

## Your PT's Two Cents

Look, I love running. But I also love not treating heat stroke in the emergency room. Today is one of those days where a little common sense goes a long way. If you absolutely *have* to get your miles in, do it at sunrise, or maybe just call it a rest day. Your joints, and especially your cardiovascular system, will thank you. Chasing that tan isn't worth passing out on a trail.
