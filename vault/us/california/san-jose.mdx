Alright, San Jose, time to talk about hitting the trails or dodging the sun. Today is decidedly *not* a day for leisurely midday strolls unless you enjoy feeling like a baked potato.

<BulletList>
*   **That Oven-Like Heat:** Temperatures are pushing 31°C (87.8°F) already, and they're only climbing towards the low 90s (Fahrenheit) inland. This isn't just "warm," it's a "moderate risk of heat-related illness" kind of day, especially if you're out there during peak afternoon. Your joints are going to feel every pound of impact if they're not adequately hydrated, and your lungs will be working overtime.
*   **Alum Rock Park is a Minefield of Closures:** Seriously, don't just wander into Alum Rock Park thinking it's business as usual. The San José Fire Department is running training exercises today (June 10th and also June 12th) primarily near the Mineral Springs Parking Lot. This means temporary closures on Penitencia Creek Road and parts of the North Rim Trail. On top of that, sections of Penitencia Creek Trail and Mineral Springs Trail are already shut down due to existing conditions and erosion. Plan your routes *carefully* or pick another spot.
*   **Windy with a Chance of Fire:** We're looking at breezy afternoons, with gusts hitting the 20s mph in the valley, and up to 35-40 mph in exposed spots like bayfronts and hill gaps. Combine that with dry conditions, and the fire weather concerns are elevated in the wider Bay Area, especially the Diablo Range. So, no, don't flick your cigarette butts into the bushes, you heathens.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke is No Joke
With inland temperatures pushing into the 90s, especially between 10 AM and 6 PM, heatstroke is a real danger. Seriously, if you're out, go early, go late, and for the love of all that is holy, hydrate aggressively. This isn't the day to try for a PR in direct sun. Look for cooling centers if you need them.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures at Alum Rock Park
Don't get caught off guard. Fire training means restricted access in parts of Alum Rock Park today. Check the San Jose City Parks website for the latest updates before you head out, specifically if your route includes Penitencia Creek Road or North Rim Trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Alternate Routes and Indoor Options
If you're dead set on getting your miles in, consider the Guadalupe River Trail for flatter, more open (but still hot) stretches, or hit up an air-conditioned gym. I've heard the 24 Hour Fitness at Parkmoor Plaza has... well, it *had* issues with paper towels and broken equipment recently, so maybe call ahead, or better yet, find a better gym. Honestly, for today, the smart money is on the treadmill, or a very, very early morning run.
</AlertBox>
