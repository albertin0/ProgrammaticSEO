Look, we finally got a break from the general "is the sky falling or just burning?" anxiety that's been lingering. The air is clean, the morning is gorgeous. This is prime time to hit the **Los Gatos Creek Trail** or get some miles in around **Lake Cunningham** before the sun decides to turn up the heat.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Afternoon Heat Spike!
Temperatures are predicted to jump significantly by the afternoon, hitting the mid-80s. If you're planning an afternoon workout, seriously reconsider. If you absolutely *must*, load up on water, wear light clothing, and pick a shaded route. Heat stroke is not a fun souvenir from your workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind Your Allergies
While not extreme, moderate pollen can still wreak havoc for some. If you're prone to seasonal sniffles, pop an antihistamine before you head out. Nobody wants a snot-soaked sprint.
</AlertBox>
