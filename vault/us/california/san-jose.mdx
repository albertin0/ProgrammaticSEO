### Hydration is Your Only True Companion

Look, I get it. We love our trails, whether it's the Guadalupe River Trail or pushing up through Almaden Quicksilver. But today, the elements are stacked against serious outdoor effort. While there are no *official* heat advisories currently active *for San Jose itself*, the city's own hot weather safety tips are loud and clear: limit physical activity, especially between 10 AM and 3 PM. And trust me, 88 degrees in the sun *feels* a lot hotter when you're exerting yourself.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Fire Hazard is Real & Present!**
The high fire risk is no joke. Think twice about any trail run that goes near dry brush, and if you see any illegal fireworks, report them. Seriously. We don't need a single spark turning our beautiful hills into an inferno this holiday weekend.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen's a Moderate Annoyance**
If you're prone to seasonal sniffles, "moderate" pollen levels mean you might feel it. Keep your antihistamines handy, but it's the least of your worries today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Closures (Minor Impact for Trails)**
Most VTA construction activities (like those near US 101/SR 25 in Gilroy or Capitol Expressway) are road-related and are expected to be reduced or suspended for the holiday weekend, though barriers might remain. For trails, Santa Clara County Parks reports no new closures for places like Almaden Quicksilver, but Anderson Lake County Park has long-term closures due to the dam project until around 2032. Always double-check park-specific websites if you're venturing far.
</AlertBox>

So, if you absolutely *must* get out, do it early, stick to shaded areas, hydrate like it's your job, and keep an eye on your body. Otherwise, use that great AQI to breathe easy on your couch.
