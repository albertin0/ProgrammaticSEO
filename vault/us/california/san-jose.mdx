## The Rundown: Don't Melt Out There

Alright, San Jose, let's talk turkey. The official data says our Air Quality Index is a breezy 25 (Ozone/Other is the dominant pollutant) and "Mainly Clear" out there, which sounds lovely for your lungs. The pollen's at a "Moderate" level, so most of you should be fine, but the sensitive folks might want to pop an antihistamine.

However, don't let that clear sky fool you into thinking it's a perfect day for a personal best. That "Mainly Clear" means the sun is beating down, pushing temperatures to a scorching 31.0°C (88°F) before noon. We’ve seen this movie before this month; temperatures in this range have triggered heat advisories recently in the Bay Area, including San Jose, and it's no joke for runners. You need to hydrate like your life depends on it – because honestly, when it's this hot, it kind of does.

### Local Hazards & Trail Disruptions

If your usual loop takes you through Joseph D. Grant County Park, listen up: the south side of the park is *closed today* due to a prescribed fire scheduled from June 22-26. You don't want to be running into that kind of smoke, regardless of the overall AQI. Your lungs, even if they're usually bulletproof, will thank you for finding an alternate route.

Speaking of alternate routes, construction continues to be a party pooper across the city. If you're heading toward the Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve, be aware that the equestrian lot is closed for public access improvement construction. The eastern portion of Sierra Vista Open Space Preserve is completely shut down until July 5th, so scratch that off your list. Planning to hit the Mallard Slough Trail or eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail? Expect intermittent closures there too for hauling and construction. The roads aren't entirely clear either; while a major concrete pour on Northbound Capitol Expressway wrapped up this morning, VTA warns that pedestrians and cyclists might still be asked to wait and be escorted around ongoing work. Don't assume the path is totally clear just because the main closure passed.
