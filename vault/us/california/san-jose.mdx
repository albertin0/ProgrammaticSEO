<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Danger: Heat Wave is REAL. Stay Smart, Or Stay Inside.**
Look, I get it, you want to get your miles in. But with highs pushing into the low 90s across San Jose today, pushing yourself during peak heat (think 10 AM to 3 PM) isn't "tough," it's just plain stupid. The City of San José is explicitly warning folks to limit physical activity and stay hydrated. Your 20-mile long run can wait until cooler weather, or at least until the sun starts thinking about setting. If you *must* run, do it at the crack of dawn, load up on water before and during, and wear light, loose clothing. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are no joke, and emergency services don't need to be dealing with your self-induced crisis when there are real emergencies out there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Pollen's Mild Annoyance**
"Moderate" isn't terrible, but it's enough to make your nose run or your eyes itch if you're prone to seasonal allergies. Grab some antihistamines if you plan on hitting the **Guadalupe River Trail** for those flat, fast miles, especially if you're pushing the pace. Don't let a runny nose ruin your stride, or worse, make you think you're getting sick when it's just Mother Nature being a mild jerk.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Trail Talk: What's Open, What's Not**
While a couple of wildfires (Mines Fire, Woodside Fire) in the broader Bay Area have been largely contained and the air quality has improved, there's always a lingering possibility of localized smoke if winds shift, especially near the Santa Clara County hills. On the bright side, no major trail-blocking events are messing up your run plans for today. However, keep in mind that **Anderson Lake County Park** still has significant areas closed due to the ongoing dam retrofit project, and that's not changing until 2032. Don't be that person ignoring the signs, because those rangers *will* cite you.
