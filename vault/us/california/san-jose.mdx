## Trail and Road Hazards: Just Another Tuesday in San Jose

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Complete Closure: Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve**
For those who planned to hit the trails, forget about the Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve for a while. The entire preserve is closed today, September 8th, and will remain shut down through September 18th for "accessibility and public-access improvement" construction. That also includes the equestrian lot. If you try to sneak in, you're just asking for trouble (and a citation). On top of that, the Habitat Protection Area within the preserve is consistently closed on Mondays and Tuesdays for scientific research. So, yeah, stay out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues**
Driving, biking, or running near major construction? Be aware. VTA's I-280/Wolfe Road Interchange project is a beast. Expect lane closures, overnight work, and traffic changes between now and September 18th. They're even excavating for a new bridge support on the I-280 median overnight for weeks. More local headaches include utility work on Monterey Road, Market Street, and West Julian Street, causing partial lane closures during working hours. And don't forget the rebar installation closing lanes on S. Capitol Avenue between Capitol Expressway and Westboro Drive through September 17th. Pedestrians and cyclists in that area might get asked to wait and be escorted. Keep your head up and your phone down.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Upcoming Heat Wave**
While today starts mild, it's a ramp-up to serious heat. The forecast shows temperatures hitting 33°C (92°F) today and 36°C (96°F) tomorrow. An "Excessive Heat Watch" was in effect through yesterday, but the heat isn't quite done with us. Plan your longer efforts for the early morning to beat the afternoon bake.
</AlertBox>

Paved trails like the Los Gatos Creek Trail and Coyote Creek Trail should be clear for now, assuming no surprise afternoon thunderstorms. Just use common sense, especially with the climbing temps.
