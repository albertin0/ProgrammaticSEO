## The Rundown: Don't Melt, Don't Get Lost, Don't Breathe Too Much Pollen

Look, the AQI is sitting pretty at 25. That's "Good," for those keeping score. You can actually fill those lungs with something other than particulate matter today, which is a pleasant change from wildfire season. So, that's a plus for your respiratory system.

However, Mother Nature apparently forgot it's mid-September and decided to turn up the dial to 31.0°C (that's 87.8°F for you Fahrenheit die-hards). It's "Mainly Clear," which means plenty of sun to bake you. This isn't "extreme heat warning" territory *today*, but Cal/OSHA has been on about protecting workers in high heat this month, and you're working out, not just sitting on a patio. Hydration isn't optional; it's survival. Your joints might be okay, but your brain is going to be screaming at you to find some AC.

## Local Headaches

### Guadalupe River Trail Closure

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Active Trail Closure Alert: Guadalupe River Trail (Trimble Road to Airport Parkway)**
Heads up, trail runners and cyclists! The Santa Clara Valley Water District is doing sediment removal along the Guadalupe River from Trimble Road to the Airport Green Parking Lot. This means the trail section from Trimble Road to Airport Parkway is **closed Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM**, through September 25th. Today's a Wednesday, so that closure is active. You can still use the west bank trail, but plan your route accordingly. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the signs.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, people, these trail closures are for your safety (and the workers'). Don't try to sneak around. There are flaggers, and they're not there to cheer you on.

### City Street Construction

<AlertBox type="info">
**Mind the Roadwork:**
Expect ongoing utility work in various San Jose city streets, including sections of Monterey Road, Market Street, and West Julian Street. Lane closures are probable during working hours. If your workout takes you through these areas, stay vigilant. Don't assume drivers see you, and certainly don't assume construction crews are looking out for your Strava segment.
</AlertBox>

You know the drill. Construction means heavy equipment, distracted drivers, and generally less-than-ideal conditions for running or cycling on the road. Just be aware of your surroundings, especially around the US 101/SR 25 interchange where VTA has been doing daytime work.

### Overall: Heat is the Biggest Foe

While the air is surprisingly good and widespread wildfire smoke isn't an issue, that mid-day sun hitting 88 degrees is the real hazard today. Those of us who've had heatstroke before know it's no joke. Don't push it. Shorten your distance, slow your pace, or move your workout to early morning or late evening. And if you're running Alum Rock Park, maybe stick to the shadier sections.
