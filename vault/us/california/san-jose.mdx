## The Details: Sweat, Snot, and Some Seriously Closed Paths

Let's not sugarcoat it: today is hot. The kind of hot where even your sweat is sweating. You might not see a big, flashy "Excessive Heat Warning" sign stapled to every lamppost, but remember those advisories from Tuesday and Wednesday predicting temps in the 90s and low 100s for our valley? That residual heat isn't just going to magically disappear. Your body doesn't care if it's "officially" a warning day or not – 31°C is plenty to put you in the danger zone for heat exhaustion or worse.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Risk!**
Do NOT attempt strenuous outdoor activity between 10 AM and 3 PM. If you must be outside, keep it short, stay to shaded areas, and hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). Seriously, nobody wants to scrape you off the pavement near the San Jose State campus.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the air quality is fantastic with an AQI of 25. So, if you manage to avoid spontaneous combustion from the heat, your lungs will be breathing some of the cleanest air we've seen in a while. Wildfires are popping up across the state (looking at you, San Diego and Riverside Counties), and the San Jose Fire Department has already responded to hundreds of vegetation fires this year, but thankfully, no significant smoke impacts are hitting our air quality right now.

Pollen, however, is being its usual moderate self. If you usually suffer, you'll still suffer. Pop those antihistamines before you head out, or you'll be running a snotty, itchy-eyed mess.

### Local Trail Annoyances

Expect some detours and closures. The Santa Clara Valley Water District is doing vegetation removal on sections of the Guadalupe River Trail and Los Alamitos Creek Trail through early September. They'll have flaggers, which is nice, but it still means navigating around crews and equipment. And if you were planning a long run around **Anderson Lake Park**, forget about it. Many areas are closed until roughly 2032 for the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That's not a temporary inconvenience; that's a decade-long commitment to rerouting your run.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Construction Ahead:**
Keep an eye out for work crews and follow all posted signage, especially on the Guadalupe River Trail and around Alviso Marina County Park, where intermittent construction closures are ongoing. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and ends up in a ditch or, worse, delaying workers trying to get stuff done.
</AlertBox>
