### Don't Be a Heatstroke Hero
While an official "extreme heat watch" is primarily for Southern California, San Jose is still seeing high temperatures today. You *will* get hot. Seriously, hydrate like it's your job, wear light colors, and for the love of all that is holy, don't ignore dizziness or nausea. That's your body telling you to stop being a dumbass and find some shade. Central San Jose is projected to have 7 days above 93°F this year, so consider this a preview.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: All Clear (Mostly)
Good news on the trail front – nothing major seems to be shut down *within* San Jose that would affect your usual routes. The perennial construction on the San Jose Creek Multipurpose Path is ongoing, but that’s up in Goleta, not our neck of the woods. Same goes for the West Little Llagas Creek Trail closure down in Morgan Hill for creek work. So, your local haunts should be open. Just keep an eye out for any pop-up maintenance or unexpected debris, which, let's be honest, can happen anywhere, anytime.

### Plan Your Escape
If you are planning a longer run or ride, carry more water than you think you need. Consider stashing extra bottles along your route if possible. And seriously, let someone know your plans. Getting cooked out on some forgotten stretch of trail isn't anyone's idea of a fun Friday.
</AlertBox>

### The Bottom Line
Today is fine, *if* you're smart. Get your workout in early or wait until the sun starts to dip. The air is beautiful, so enjoy that. Just remember, San Jose summer heat is no joke. Hydrate, stay aware, and don't push it.
