## Today's Local Workout Hazards

### The Heat is On

Look, 31°C (88°F) might sound like a nice beach day, but when you're pounding pavement or pushing hills, it's a recipe for disaster if you're not prepared. San Jose has been seeing these "sizzling" temperatures this week, so this isn't a surprise pop-up heatwave.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**HIGH HEAT RISK.** Hydrate relentlessly. Seriously, start before you even think about stepping out, and keep it up. Plan your runs for early morning or late evening. If you're out there between 10 AM and 4 PM, you're just asking for trouble. Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. This isn't about pushing through; it's about survival. The National Weather Service has been on this, urging folks to limit outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day. Don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

### Wildfires (Still a Concern)

While the AQI is holding steady at "Excellent" (25 AQI) today, which is a rare treat, don't forget we had a slow-moving vegetation fire just two days ago near Alum Rock in the east foothills. It was only about five acres, and they got the forward progress stopped, but Sierra Road was closed. We also had another one, the Hicks Fire, reported in Santa Clara County just yesterday. California's already seen over 1,700 wildfires this year, scorching a massive amount of land. Just because the air is clear *right now* doesn't mean things can't change fast.

<AlertBox type="info">
Keep an eye on air quality apps, especially if you're planning a longer outing. Wind shifts happen. If you smell smoke, cut your run short and get inside. Don't risk your lungs for a PR.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures & Construction Headaches

If your go-to is anywhere near Anderson Lake County Park, you're probably out of luck. Large sections, including various picnic sites, parts of the Serpentine Trail, and the Lakeview Trail, are closed until *approximately 2032* due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Yes, you read that right, 2032.

Over at Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve, expect some new annoyances. Construction for public access improvements kicked off on June 22nd, leading to the equestrian lot being closed. And if you usually hit the Eastern portion of Sierra Vista Open Space Preserve, forget about it; that's closed until July 5th.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**PLAN YOUR ROUTE. SERIOUSLY.** Don't just show up expecting your favorite trail to be open, especially if it's in a regional park. The closures at Anderson Lake County Park and Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve are significant. Hitting a dead end after you've already logged miles in the heat is a major buzzkill and a risk if you don't have enough water. Check the county parks websites before you head out.
</AlertBox>

### Events Blocking Your Path

It's Saturday, so expect crowds. The Fountain Blues & Brews Festival is happening today and tomorrow at Plaza Park. There's also a Global Soccer Watch Party over near Little Italy at Arena Green East. And America's Block Party is going on at the Grand Century Shopping Mall. All of these mean more traffic, more people, and potentially blocked routes if you're trying to navigate downtown or surrounding areas.

<AlertBox type="info">
If your route takes you anywhere near downtown San Jose, especially around Plaza Park or Little Italy, consider an alternative. Or, embrace the chaos and factor in some dodging and weaving into your workout.
</AlertBox>
