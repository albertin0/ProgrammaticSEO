### Trail Closures are a Thing – Plan Your Route!
Several of our beloved trails are still impacted by long-term projects. If your usual loop involves **Anderson Lake County Park**, be advised that significant portions, including the Serpentine Trail and sections near the dam, are *closed until approximately 2032* for seismic retrofitting. Don't try to sneak around it; those park rangers don't mess around, and frankly, it's unsafe. Same goes for those intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern parts of the Alviso Slough Trail due to ongoing construction and hauling.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind Your Local Events
If you're in the South Bay, remember there's a "VIVA PARKS" event happening at **William Lewis Manly Park** from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM today. While it's great for community, it could mean more foot traffic or temporary access changes in that specific area. Just be aware and share the space.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye on Bear Creek Redwoods if You're Up That Way
Farther afield, but still good to know for those who range, sections of Bear Creek Redwoods Preserve, like Toad Hollow and Myotis trails, are shut down for construction until September 2026. Construction zones and runners don't mix, so respect the closures.
</AlertBox>
