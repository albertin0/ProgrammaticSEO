### Construction & Closures: Your Favorite Trails Are Still a Mess

Now, for the bad news. If you had plans to hit some of our local gems, you might need a backup. The big one still kicking us in the shins is **Anderson Lake County Park**. Much of that entire area is basically a no-go zone until *2032* because of the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. That means popular spots like the Serpentine Trail, Dam Crest, Woodchoppers Flat, and parts of the Lakeview Trail are off-limits. Seriously, who plans construction for *seven years*?

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Trail Closures at Anderson Lake County Park:** Don't bother heading to Anderson Lake for your run or hike today. Large sections, including Serpentine Trail and parts of Lakeview Trail, are closed until roughly 2032. Seriously, check the county parks website before you drive all the way out there just to be greeted by a "CLOSED" sign.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just Anderson Lake. The West Little Llagas Creek Trail down in Morgan Hill is also shut down for creek work through the end of the year. Even closer to home, Alviso Marina County Park has "intermittent closures" on sections like the Mallard Slough Trail due to ongoing construction. If you're a regular there, be prepared for detours.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip: Check Before You Trek:** With all the development and upgrades happening around San Jose, always check the Santa Clara County Parks website or specific park pages for the latest closure information. A quick check can save you a lot of frustration (and wasted gas money).
</AlertBox>
