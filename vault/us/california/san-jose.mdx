## Afternoon & Evening: Just Don't.

Seriously, if you're thinking about an outdoor workout later, reconsider. The air quality warnings for tonight are legit. Personal fireworks, which are illegal in San Jose anyway, are notorious for jacking up particulate matter to dangerous levels. Even if you're not planning to light them yourself, you'll be breathing everyone else's questionable choices.
