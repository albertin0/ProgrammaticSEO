## Today's Outdoor Workout Reality Check: San Jose

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next sane person, but today is not the day to push it. It's a prime example of why you can't just glance at a sunny forecast and lace up your shoes. We've got a potent combo of scorching heat and crummy air that'll leave you gasping, literally.

The National Weather Service slapped a **Heat Advisory** on the Bay Area, and that includes our little slice of Silicon Valley, running from this morning until 10 PM on Thursday. They're talking 90s to a blistering 105 degrees in inland spots. Your body is already working overtime just to keep cool, adding a run to that is asking for trouble. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on faster than you think.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning:** Seriously, hydrate *now* and keep hydrating. Limit strenuous outdoor activity to the early morning or late evening, if at all. Don't be a hero; your PR can wait. Never, ever leave pets or kids in a car.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the air. While the overall AQI for San Jose is sitting pretty at 25 (which is "Good" on paper), we're also under a **Spare the Air Alert for ozone** today. That means that "Mainly Clear" sky isn't as innocent as it looks. Ozone is a lung irritant, and when levels are high, even healthy people can experience respiratory issues. If you've got asthma or other lung conditions, consider today a hard "no" for anything more than a gentle stroll.

### Trail Talk

If you absolutely *must* hit a trail, be aware that there's ongoing vegetation removal along parts of the **Los Alamitos Creek Trail** near Mazzone Drive. They'll have flaggers, so pay attention and don't wander into a worksite. It's usually a nice shaded option, but even that won't save you from the heat and ozone today. The Guadalupe River Trail also has closures coming up next week, but not today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone Alert:** Even with a low general AQI, the Spare the Air alert for ozone means you need to be cautious. If your chest feels tight or you're coughing more than usual, get inside.
</AlertBox>

My advice? Take a rest day. Hit the gym, swim indoors, or just stretch on your living room floor. Today's conditions are stacked against you, and pushing through them isn't going to make you stronger, just hotter and maybe sicker. Save that grit for a day when the air isn't trying to actively suffocate you.
