## The Heat is Still On (Mostly)

Alright, the National Weather Service heat advisory for the Bay Area and Central Coast was set to expire Thursday evening, but don't let that lull you into a false sense of cool security. The mercury is still climbing to a toasty 31.0°C (87.8°F) today. San Jose was expected to hit 99°F (37.2°C) during that heat wave earlier this week, and while today might be a *tad* cooler, it's still plenty warm enough to get yourself into trouble if you're not careful.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Dangerously Warm Conditions:** Plan your workouts for early morning or late evening. If you must be out midday, stick to shaded areas and keep it light. Hydration is non-negotiable. Seriously, drink water. Before, during, and after. Dehydration isn't a badge of honor; it's how you end up on a gurney.
</AlertBox>
