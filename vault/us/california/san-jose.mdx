### Watch Your Step: Trail Hazards

While the weather is practically begging you to hit the pavement or dirt, several parts of our local trail network are still a bit of a dumpster fire.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Long-Term Trail Closures:** If your usual long run takes you near **Anderson Lake County Park**, remember large sections are closed until approximately 2032 due due to the ongoing seismic retrofit project on the dam. Don't be "that runner" who ignores the signs; they're there for your safety, not to annoy you. Similarly, for those venturing south, the **West Little Llagas Creek Trail** in Morgan Hill is closed through the end of the year for creek widening.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Urban Trail Realities on Guadalupe River Trail:** An August 2026 review called out persistent issues on the Guadalupe River Trail, specifically regarding homeless encampments and safety concerns at undercrossings. While past construction for sediment removal on the east trail near Trimble Road might be finished as of early October, the general advice to be vigilant or choose alternative routes still stands if you're not comfortable navigating those areas. It's always a good idea to run with a buddy or stick to busier sections if you're heading out there.
</AlertBox>

It's Tuesday, so you won't have the weekend crowds on the Los Gatos Creek Trail, which is usually a positive. However, we're looking at a chance of showers after midnight tonight and "showers likely" tomorrow, which means trails could get muddy quickly. Stick to paved paths or well-maintained fire roads if you want to avoid a slip-and-slide situation in the coming days.

All in all, today's a solid day for a workout if you know where you're going and what to avoid. Just don't expect pristine conditions everywhere.
