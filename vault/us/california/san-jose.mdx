Another Saturday, another chance to hit the pavement or dirt, and honestly, if you're not out there by now, you're missing out on some of the best running weather we've had all season.

Look, the air quality today is clocking in at a fantastic 28 AQI, and it's mainly clear with a cool 14.5°C (about 58°F). Your lungs are going to love you. But because this is San Jose in August, it's not all sunshine and PRs. We've got our usual mix of trail closures and a major race that'll make your usual routes a bit of a cluster.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Almaden Hills Trail Run Today!
  If you were planning a peaceful long run through **Almaden Quicksilver County Park** this morning, forget about it. The Almaden Hills Trail Run - Summer Edition is happening *today*, Saturday, August 15, 2026. Expect significantly elevated foot traffic and parking mayhem around the Mockingbird lot, especially if you're not one of the pre-dawn headlamp crew. These trails are exposed, and even with cooler temps, that sun still beats down later in the day. Get your hill repeats in early or find a different park.
</AlertBox>

Pollen's moderate, so if you're an allergy sufferer, you know the drill: pop your meds before you head out, and maybe don't stop to smell *all* the wildflowers.

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Construction Continues to Reign Supreme
  The city's never-ending quest to... well, do something, means more trail headaches.
  *   **Guadalupe River Trail:** The upper trail north of Skyport Drive has "occasional closures" through September from 7:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.. While the specific weekday closure on the west bank ended yesterday, always keep an eye out for work crews here. The east bank closures kick in next week, so enjoy the full path while you can.
  *   **Los Alamitos Creek Trail (near Mazzone Drive):** Vegetation removal is ongoing through early September, 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Expect flaggers and don't be *that* runner who ignores them.
  *   **Coyote Creek Parkway:** Big one here. The bridge at Metcalf Road and Monterey Road is closed for renovation from the week of August 10 through August 24. Plan your detours now, folks.
  *   **Anderson Lake County Park:** Still largely shut down until around 2032 due to the dam retrofit. Seriously, plan for this to be a no-go for a while.
</AlertBox>

No major wildfires are directly choking our air *today*, which is a miracle in itself for August in California. So enjoy the crisp air while it lasts. This is truly optimal weather for a summer long run, just make sure you’re not running into a fence or a bunch of cones.
