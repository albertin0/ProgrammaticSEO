## Local Lowdown & What to Watch For

Alright, let's get to the real dirt. While the air and weather are playing nice, a few bits of local bureaucracy and concrete work might throw a wrench in your morning plans if you're not paying attention.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**VTA Construction on Capitol Expressway & Ocala Avenue:** If you're commuting or planning a route through East San Jose this morning, be advised that the Eastridge to BART Regional Connector (EBRC) project had intersection closures at **Ocala Avenue and Capitol Expressway** scheduled until 9:30 AM today. Pedestrians and cyclists *might* still be directed or even escorted through the area, so expect potential delays and keep your head on a swivel. This isn't the scenic route for your run anyway, but good to know if you're navigating around it.
</AlertBox>

If you’re a fan of our awesome creek trails, here’s a heads up:

<AlertBox type="info">
**Guadalupe River Trail & Los Alamitos Creek Trail Vegetation Removal:** Crews are out there doing vegetation removal along sections of the **Guadalupe River Trail** (near the airport bridge) and the **Los Alamitos Creek Trail** through early September. They're working from 7 AM to 3 PM, and while the trails are technically open, expect flaggers and *do not* enter their worksite. It’s not a full closure, but it means you need to stay alert and follow directions. No heroics, just common sense.
</AlertBox>

For those looking for a longer escape, remember that **Anderson Lake County Park** is still largely shut down. Much of it, including the Serpentine Trail, Dam Crest Woodchoppers Flat, and parts of the Lakeview Trail, are closed until about 2032 due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. So, if that's your usual stomping ground, you'll need to find an alternative for a *long* time.

There are no major wildfire smoke concerns for San Jose right now, and no high wind, flooding, or extreme temperature warnings to speak of. So, other than some localized construction and maintenance, it's pretty much a green light for your workout. Get out there and enjoy it before August really hits us with the heat.
