### Today's Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat is NO JOKE.** The National Weather Service has put out a **Heat Advisory** for San Jose and the entire Santa Clara Valley. Daytime highs are projected to hit the mid-90s to low 100s, making strenuous outdoor activity incredibly risky, especially between 10 AM and 4 PM. You think you're tough? Heat stroke doesn't care. Hydrate constantly, find shade, and seriously, rethink that midday long run on the Los Gatos Creek Trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality is Meh.** An AQI of 57 might not sound terrible, but combined with the heat and the fact that we were dealing with wildfire smoke through yesterday, it's not ideal. If you have any respiratory issues, or just want to keep your lungs happy, consider an indoor workout. Or at least stick to the cooler, earlier hours and keep an eye on the official air quality maps if you're venturing out. Near-critical fire weather conditions also persist across the interior due to the hot, dry weather.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Construction on Guadalupe River Trail:** If you're planning a run or ride along the Guadalupe River Trail, specifically near the airport bridge, be aware that Valley Water crews are doing vegetation removal through early September. The trail *will* remain open, but expect to see flaggers guiding users. Pay attention, follow their directions, and don't be "that runner" who ignores safety personnel. Also, remember the Lower Calaveras Fault Trail in Sierra Vista Open Space Preserve is still closed due to past storm damage.
</AlertBox>
