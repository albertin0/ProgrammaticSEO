## The Lowdown for Your Legs and Lungs

Alright, San Jose, let's talk turkey about today's outdoor grind. We've got clear skies and temperatures hitting a pleasant 12.5°C (54.5°F) rising to a comfortable 23°C (74°F) later on. Winds are light at 5 to 10 mph, so no fighting a headwind both ways today, thankfully. Seriously, enjoy this weather; it's practically tailor-made for a decent long run or a solid bike ride.

The Air Quality Index is hovering at 53, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This puts us squarely in the "Moderate" category. It's not ideal, but it's not going to kill you either. If you've got super sensitive lungs, maybe dial back the intensity on your workout, but for the majority, you're good to go. There are no active wildfire smoke plumes impacting us directly right now, which is a relief. That "Moderate" AQI is just the standard urban funk.

Now, for those of us whose bodies try to reject spring every year, pollen is at a moderate level. You know the drill: runny nose, itchy eyes, the whole shebang. If you're susceptible, pre-medicate and maybe consider a post-workout shower to get that yellow stuff off.

### Trail Talk & Local Headaches

No major flooding or crazy high winds impacting our trails *today*. The Guadalupe River isn't acting up, and there are no warnings in effect. However, always be mindful on trails after any rain, as under-crossings can still be dodgy with standing water and debris.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Long-Term Trail Closures You Still Need to Know About:**

*   **Anderson Lake County Park:** Heads up if you're a regular here. Large sections, including boating, fishing, and parts of Coyote Road and Lakeview Trail, are **CLOSED until approximately 2032** due to the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project. Seriously, check their website before you plan anything.
*   **Alviso Marina County Park:** Expect intermittent closures on the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail. This is all due to ongoing hauling and construction, so be prepared for detours.
*   **Alum Rock Park:** Our beloved Alum Rock still has some public restrooms closed for maintenance and repairs, specifically near Mineral Springs, Quail Hollow, and Rustic Lands. Plan accordingly, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up, Commuters:** While not a current "hazard," remember that the Five Wounds Trail expansion project is still kicking around the idea of demolishing or replacing the historic Coyote Creek Trestle north of Kelley Park. No final decision, but if you run or bike that way, stay informed.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's shaping up to be a decent one for getting out there. Just be smart about your specific route, especially if you're near any of those long-term construction zones.
