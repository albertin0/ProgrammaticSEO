<AlertBox type="danger">
  ## DANGER: It's a Hot One, Folks!
  Yes, that 31.0°C (87.8°F) isn't just a number; it's a recipe for heat exhaustion if you're not smart. The Bay Area has seen its share of triple-digit temperatures this week, and while today might "only" be in the upper 80s, that's still plenty hot to cause problems. Your core temperature is going to climb faster than housing prices in Willow Glen. Seriously, if you're planning a long run or a high-intensity session, cut your pace, shorten your distance, and consider pushing it to the much cooler early morning or late evening hours. Hydrate constantly, before, during, and after. Drink more than you think you need. Wear light-colored, moisture-wicking gear. And for the love of all that is holy, find shade whenever you can, especially if you're hitting the exposed sections of trails like the Los Gatos Creek Trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ## Trail Work on the Guadalupe River Trail
  If you're out enjoying the urban oasis that is the Guadalupe River Trail, be aware that Valley Water is doing vegetation removal through early September. This means you might hit sections with crews and flaggers between 7:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m.. The trails are supposed to remain open, but always follow their directions. Don't be "that runner" who barrels through a work zone. Patience, people. It's for public safety, and frankly, some of those overgrown sections could use a trim.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ## Wildfire Risk is High
  Look, our current Air Quality Index is a comfortable 47, which is "Good" by the numbers. But don't get complacent. San Jose is always susceptible to wildfire smoke, even from fires miles away in the Santa Cruz Mountains or Diablo Range. Cal Fire is reporting a "High Risk of Fire" for Friday and Saturday in the region. While there are no active smoke advisories for today, the risk is always there during summer. Keep an eye on the air quality map, especially if you're heading out for an afternoon session, as wind patterns can shift quickly. If you see smoke, it's time to take your workout indoors. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>
