### **Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!**
Temperatures are forecast to soar to the mid-90s to well over 100°F (35-39°C) in inland areas like ours. This isn't just "a bit warm," this is "heat exhaustion and heat stroke are very real possibilities" territory. Hydration is critical, but honestly, consider moving your workout indoors during peak afternoon hours. Overnight lows won't offer much relief either, staying sticky in the upper 60s and 70s.

And let's not forget about the air. While the AQI of 68 is technically "Moderate," we've got a Spare the Air Alert for ozone. Ozone is that nasty pollutant that forms on hot, sunny days, and it's a real lung irritant. Combine that with PM2.5, which can irritate your respiratory system and impact your heart, and you're essentially signing up for a tougher workout than necessary. Even at "moderate" levels, air pollution can mess with your performance and health, especially during prolonged or intense exercise.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Don't Forget About Your Lungs!**
The AQI is creeping up, and with a Spare the Air Alert due to ozone, your lungs are going to be working harder than usual. Ozone can trigger chest tightness, coughing, and shortness of breath. If you have asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory issues, today is NOT the day to push it outdoors. You're just asking for trouble.
</AlertBox>
