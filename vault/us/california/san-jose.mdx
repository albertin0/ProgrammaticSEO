## Today's Outdoor Grind: Think Twice, Hydrate Thrice

Alright, San Jose, let's talk turkey. It's Friday, and the urge to blow off some steam after a long week is real. But before you lace up those Hokas and head out the door, take a deep breath – and maybe immediately regret it if you're one of the unfortunate souls sensitive to this "moderate" pollen. Seriously, folks, your lungs might be getting a break from wildfire smoke today, with the AQI looking pretty good at 25, but that doesn't mean it's a free pass.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory**
With temperatures pushing 31°C (that's 88°F for those still in Fahrenheit-land), heatstroke isn't just a suggestion; it's a real possibility. The City of San José officially recommends limiting physical activity between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. If you're out there during those hours, you're practically asking for trouble. Drink more water than you think you need. Like, a lot more. And for the love of all that is holy, don't forget your pets when you're hydrating. They cook faster than you do.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Roadblocks

Planning a scenic run through Sierra Vista Open Space Preserve? Well, tough luck for the eastern portion, because that's shut down until Sunday, June 21st. Always check those trail alerts before you drive all the way out there, only to find a "CLOSED" sign mocking your efforts. And don't even get me started on Anderson Lake County Park; that place is basically a construction zone for the next *six years* thanks to the dam retrofit. Find another spot for your long runs, people.

If you're more of a city runner or cyclist, keep an eye on the roadwork. That northbound Capitol Expressway closure for VTA construction is still in effect until July 10th. Detours are annoying, especially when you're trying to hit a specific pace.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip: Early Bird (or Late Night Owl) Gets the Workout**
If you absolutely *must* run today, get out there before the sun truly commits to baking us, or wait until well after sunset. The asphalt on our local streets, like those around the Willow Glen neighborhood, will absorb and radiate that heat long after the sun dips, making evening runs feel like a sauna. Stay on shaded trails if you can find them, but again, check those closures first.
</AlertBox>
