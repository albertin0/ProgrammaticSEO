<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning
Seriously, don't mess with 31.0°C (87.8°F). Heat exhaustion and heatstroke are real, and they come on fast. The city's own hot weather safety tips advise limiting physical activity during the hottest part of the day, which, let's be honest, is most of the day when it's this warm. If you *must* be outside, stick to early morning or late evening, find shade, hydrate constantly, and listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Downtown is Carnival Central
Forget your leisurely jog through downtown today. The San Jose Diwali Carnival is happening at Plaza de Cesar Chavez, and it's going to be packed. This isn't the day to try and weave through throngs of people for your workout. Plan an alternate route far from the festivities, or better yet, stay home and do some push-ups.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Access Blocked Near Airport
For those of you who rely on Ewert Road to get to the Guadalupe River Trail near the airport, be aware that a planned road closure there is creating safety risks for cyclists and pedestrians. It's an essential connector, and its closure means you'll need to find another way to access the trail, or just pick a different spot entirely. Don't try to navigate unsafe detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### General Trail & Road Construction
While not all directly impacting *today*, it's always good to be mindful of ongoing projects. Santa Clara Valley Water District has had intermittent closures on sections of the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern Alviso Slough Trail due to construction. Also, Anderson Lake County Park has vast areas closed until approximately 2032 for seismic retrofit work, so don't plan on hitting those trails anytime soon. Downtown San Jose also has multiple ongoing construction projects causing lane closures on streets like Market St., Park Ave., and E San Fernando St. Keep an eye on local city and park websites before heading out for the latest on your specific route.
</AlertBox>

Today's not the day to push your limits outdoors, unless those limits involve tolerating a whole lot of heat and navigating around large crowds or closures. Seriously, grab a cold drink and stay safe.
