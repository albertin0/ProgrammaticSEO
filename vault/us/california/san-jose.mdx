Now, onto the actual ground you’ll be covering. No major, sudden trail closures or natural disasters to report in the last 24 hours, which is always a win in this state. No sudden flooding on the Guadalupe River Trail, no unexpected gusts trying to push you into traffic on Stevens Creek.

However, if you had grand plans for a full run on the **Five Wounds Trail** in East San Jose, you're still out of luck on that southern stretch. The whole "historic railroad trestle versus new trail" saga at the Coyote Creek Trestle is still ongoing. City officials paused plans for the southernmost portion back in July, so don't expect to connect Story Road and Highway 101 there anytime soon. They're focused on the central part between Highway 280 North and Santa Clara Street instead. It's an inconvenience, sure, but at least it's a *known* inconvenience. Adapt, people. That's what we do.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Persistent Trail Annoyance: Five Wounds Trail
That planned southern extension of the Five Wounds Trail near the Coyote Creek Trestle? Still stalled. Don't plan your long runs expecting to hit that segment. You'll be rerouting.
</AlertBox>

Other trail notes: places like Almaden Quicksilver County Park are reporting no closures, so if you want some hill work with fewer headaches, that's still a solid option. Just keep an eye out for maintenance on the broader Santa Clara County park system, as those things pop up.

Bottom line: Today's a decent day for a workout. The air won't kill you, the weather won't drown you, and the major hazards are mostly known quantities. Just be smart about your allergies and adjust your route if you were banking on a complete run through the *entire* Five Wounds Trail.
