## Today's Pain Report: You're Gonna Feel It

Let's just be blunt: Today is a rough one for anyone trying to log miles or hit the trails outside. We've got a **Heat Advisory** plastered across the Santa Clara Valley, warning of temps in the mid-90s to potentially near 103°F. And yeah, that advisory is in effect right now, through Monday evening. Your body isn't going to recover overnight with those mild lows in the upper 60s and 70s.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: Hydrate or Die-drate.**
Seriously, this isn't a joke. Temps are 90°F+, with a "RealFeel" of 94°F. Heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and dehydration are *real* risks. If you're out there between 10 AM and 3 PM, you're actively choosing to flirt with disaster. Drink water until you feel like you're going to float away, and then drink some more.
</AlertBox>

### Local Trail & Road Traps

If you *insist* on getting out there, at least know what you're up against.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Almaden Quicksilver County Park is a Zoo Today.**
If your plan was a peaceful trail run through Almaden Quicksilver, forget about it. The **Quicksilver Trail Challenge** is happening right now, with a Half Marathon, 10K, and even a kids' race. Expect crowds, slower traffic on those single tracks and fire roads, and probably a few more inexperienced trail runners than usual. If you're looking for solitude, this ain't it.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the hills, downtown is still a maze. The San Jose Downtown Association is reporting continuous construction closures on Market Street, Park Avenue, William Street, and East San Fernando Street. If you're cycling or running through the urban core, factor in detours and impatient drivers.

Also, over at **Alviso Marina County Park**, the parking lot is completely shut down through today for the "Day on the Bay" event. While the event itself sounds nice, good luck finding a spot if you were planning to hit the trails out there. Plus, the Mallard Slough Trail and eastern sections of the Alviso Slough Trail still have intermittent closures due to ongoing construction.
