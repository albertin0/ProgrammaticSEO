### Trail Talk & Road Rage

Beyond the weather, navigating the city for a workout is its own challenge. If you're typically hitting the trails, be aware that many areas of **Anderson Lake County Park** are basically a no-go zone until the next decade thanks to the dam project. It's frustrating, I know, but park rangers *will* cite you for entering closed areas, so don't be that person.

For you urban runners, San Carlos Street remains a mess of construction, leading to traffic confusion and congestion. And the VTA's light rail extension project is causing its own brand of chaos with various lane and road closures, particularly impacting areas around Capitol Avenue. Factor in extra time and be ready to improvise your route.
