Today's the kind of day where the Guadalupe River Trail is practically begging for you to lace up. It's clear out, sitting at a mild 13.3°C (that's about 56°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) right now, heading up to a comfortable 25°C (77°F) later on. Perfect running weather, honestly. The air quality is sitting at a stellar 25 AQI, with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant, which is essentially a non-issue. So your lungs are good to go.

However, don't get too complacent. While the skies are clear, the city is still, well, a city.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Out for Gusty Winds
Expect light northwest winds to pick up this afternoon, with gusts potentially hitting up to 21 mph. If you're planning a bike ride, especially on exposed sections, be ready for some headwind resistance and don't get knocked off balance. Runners, you might just get a nice tailwind assist if you're lucky, but hold onto your hats.
</AlertBox>

Pollen levels are moderate, so if you're one of those unlucky folks who gets sniffly just looking at a flower, take your meds before you head out. It's not a "stay indoors at all costs" day, but don't assume you're immune to nature's sneezes.

Now for the real pain: traffic and construction. The "Power The Bay" project has utility work ongoing right through September, impacting several key roads in San Jose.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Road Work Means Road Blocks
If your usual route involves Monterey Road (between Metcalf and Capitol Expressway, or Capitol Expressway and I-280), Market Street (between Santa Clara and West St. James), or West Julian Street (between SR-87 and Terraine Street), be prepared for partial lane closures during working hours. These aren't minor detours; they can turn a pleasant run into a frustrating dodge-and-weave session with traffic. Consider alternate routes or sticking to dedicated trails to avoid these areas.
</AlertBox>

Beyond city limits, Caltrans is also performing 55-hour lane closures on US-101 from the San Mateo/Santa Clara County line up to Whipple Ave, impacting northbound and southbound Express Lanes. While not directly *in* San Jose, this is something to be aware of if your workout involves driving north or south to a starting point. That started Friday night and goes until Monday morning, September 9th.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Ahead & Stay Hydrated
No extreme heat warning for San Jose today, but temps hitting 77°F means you'll still be sweating. Carry water, even for shorter distances. Also, while tomorrow is Labor Day, and many recreational facilities will be closed, today is Sunday, so most parks and trails are open. Always double-check specific park websites if you have a niche destination.
</AlertBox>

No major wildfires or smoke to report in the immediate area, which is a blessing, and no flooding to worry about with these clear skies. So, go on, get out there and enjoy this relatively hazard-free Sunday. Just watch for traffic and those afternoon gusts.
