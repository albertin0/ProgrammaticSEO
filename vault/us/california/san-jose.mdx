## Today's Outdoor Rundown: Sweat it Out, But Smart

Let's cut to the chase: the biggest factor for your outdoor workout today, August 19th, is the heat. We're looking at a high of 31.0°C (that's about 87.8°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, which means the sun is going to be beating down hard. While San Jose isn't under the extreme 105°+ heat warnings that some other regions are experiencing, this is still plenty hot for a run or an intense bike ride, especially if you're out past 9 AM. Earlier this month, the Santa Clara Valley *was* under heat advisories with temperatures in the 90s to low 100s, reminding us that this kind of weather isn't a joke around here.

On the bright side, your lungs are getting a break. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is fantastic at 25, so no smoky haze to worry about today, despite ongoing wildfires across California. Pollen, while moderate, isn't going to be the biggest hurdle.

### Trail Troubles & Local Headaches

Now for the real-world stuff you need to know:

<AlertBox type="warning">
Heads up, trail runners and cyclists: The **Guadalupe River Trail** has a significant closure today. Starting August 19th and running through September 17th, the *east bank* of the trail between Airport Parkway and the Airport Green Lot Access will be closed on Wednesdays and Thursdays for vegetation removal. If you're planning on using this section today, Wednesday, you'll need to use the trail on the west bank instead. Don't get caught out there by unexpected machinery. Also, there's a Mid-Week Cleanup happening on the Guadalupe River at Autumn Parkway today, which might mean more people and potential minor disruptions.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
Over on the **Los Alamitos Creek Trail**, expect ongoing vegetation removal through early September. Work is happening between 7:00 AM and 3:00 PM. The trails are *supposed* to remain open, but flaggers will be present to guide users. Always be alert and follow their directions for your safety.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the immediate hazards, keep an eye on local news regarding city services. San Jose public workers recently voted to authorize a strike. While no strike is currently in effect for today, this could impact city-maintained facilities and services in the near future if negotiations stall.
