## The Full Breakdown

### Temperature Trouble

The main takeaway here is the heat. Seriously, people, 31°C is for lounging by a pool with a cold one, not for hammering out miles on the Los Gatos Creek Trail. Your body's ability to cool itself effectively starts dropping off significantly when it gets this hot, leading to dehydration, heat exhaustion, and worse. Hydrate *aggressively* if you insist on being out there. And I mean before, during, and after.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **DANGER: Extreme Heat!** With temperatures hitting 31.0°C, the risk of heatstroke and severe dehydration is HIGH. Avoid peak sun hours (10 AM - 4 PM) like the plague. If you're feeling dizzy, nauseous, or cramping, get inside and cool down immediately. Your PR isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>
