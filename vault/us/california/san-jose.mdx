## The Rundown

Alright, San Jose, let's talk about hitting the trails or pounding the pavement today. Your Ambee data says the air quality is stellar with an AQI of 25, which is fantastic news for your lungs. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, so at least we're not dealing with thick particulate matter from raging wildfires *in our immediate vicinity*. But let's be real, it's the morning after the biggest fireworks day of the year. While the big numbers look good, keep an eye (and nose) out for any localized smoke, especially if you're running near areas that tend to get a little... enthusiastic with their pyrotechnics. San Jose police were even deploying drones to catch illegal activity, so you know the concern was real.

The biggest factor to consider today is that lovely 31°C (87.8°F) temperature. It might feel nice for a morning stroll, but once the sun climbs higher, it’s going to be a sweaty grind out there. Hydration is absolutely key. Plan your longer runs for early morning or late evening, and don't be a hero; carry water. The Bay Area has been experiencing a heat wave this summer, and while 87.8°F might not be the triple-digit extremes some inland areas hit, it's still plenty hot for San Jose.

### Hot Weather + Fire Risk = Double Whammy
Even with decent air quality, the combination of high temperatures and extremely dry vegetation around Santa Clara County means the fire risk is still elevated. Fire chiefs were stressing this hard for the Fourth of July weekend. If you see smoke, report it. If you're running trails, stick to well-trafficked, clear paths.

### Stay Smart Out There
This isn't the day to try a new 20-mile route without adequate water. Seriously, bring more water than you think you need. Consider electrolyte tabs if you're sweating a lot, and for goodness sake, wear sunscreen and a hat. Your skin will thank you, and so will your physical therapist (me).
