Today in San Jose, you *can* get your miles in, but you'd better be smart about it, because Mother Nature (and a few wildfires) aren't making it easy.

## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>Our local AQI is showing a "Good" 25, which looks pristine on paper, but don't let that fool you entirely. The Bay Area Air District issued an advisory through today, Wednesday, due to smoke from the Timber Fire near Big Sur drifting into the South Bay. The fire's still only 5% contained. That means hazy skies and the *smell* of smoke are entirely possible, even if the fine particulate matter isn't officially spiking the monitors right now. Your lungs will know the difference.</li>
  <li>It's a hot one, clocking in at 88°F (31°C) with mainly clear skies. While not the "near 100 degrees" some forecasts hinted at yesterday, it's still plenty warm enough to get yourself into trouble if you're not careful. The sun is out, folks.</li>
  <li>If you're hitting the dirt, expect some detours. Valley Water crews are doing vegetation removal along sections of the Guadalupe River Trail and Los Alamitos Creek Trail through early September. We're talking flaggers, folks. Listen to them. Also, out at Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve, there's ongoing construction for access improvements. Your equestrian friends already know the trailer parking is a no-go there.</li>
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Beat the Heat, Seriously
  That 88°F isn't a joke, especially with clear skies. Schedule your run for first thing in the morning or late evening. Midday is for chumps or pool-dwellers. If you insist on pushing through the heat, stay hydrated and listen to your body. Heat exhaustion and stroke are real, and no Strava segment is worth that trip to the ER. The City of San José isn't wrong when they say avoid strenuous activity between 10 AM and 3 PM. If you're planning a long haul in Almaden Quicksilver County Park, remember how exposed those climbs are. Pack extra water, or better yet, save the big efforts for a cooler day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Don't Breathe Smoke if You Don't Have To
  Even though the AQI is looking good in San Jose today, the Bay Area Air District issued an advisory for wildfire smoke impacting the South Bay. If you smell smoke, see haze, or feel any irritation in your throat or eyes, get inside. It's fine particulate matter, and it messes with your lungs, especially if you've got any kind of respiratory issue. Check fire.airnow.gov for real-time updates in your specific neighborhood before you head out. Trust your nose and your body over a single low AQI number.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Trail Etiquette: Respect the Cones
  You know the drill. If you're on the Guadalupe River Trail and see those bright orange cones or a flagger, slow down, be polite, and follow their instructions. They're trying to keep things safe and the trails clear, even if it feels like they're just getting in your way. It's temporary, unlike that shin splint you'll get if you trip over a rogue branch they were about to clear.
</AlertBox>
