Look, if you were planning an epic long run today, April 21st, 2026, you might want to reconsider. San Jose is getting a proper drenching, with lightning as a potential bonus.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

<BulletList>
  - **Wet, Slippery, and Thunderous Trails:** Forget those "Mainly Clear" fantasy forecasts you might have seen. The Bay Area is getting hit with steady rain, and San Jose is right in the thick of it. We're talking showers, potentially intense downpours, and even thunderstorms with lightning possible today. When thunder roars, you *must* go indoors, people. Nobody wants to be the local lightning rod on the Los Gatos Creek Trail.
  - **Ponding and Blocked Drains:** All this rain means standing water is going to be an issue. Roads, underpasses, and naturally, our beloved trails are going to have sections of deep puddles, or worse. Don't be that hero trying to splash through unknown depths; you'll just end up with soggy shoes and a twisted ankle from hidden debris. Your local PT (that's me) will thank you for *not* creating extra work.
  - **Moderate Pollen:** While the rain might wash some of it down, we're still sitting at a moderate pollen level. If you're sensitive, this is just another layer of 'ick' on top of the 'wet.'
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Lightning Risk is Real:** Seriously, with thunderstorms in the forecast for today, stay off exposed trails and out of open areas. A little rain is one thing, but getting zapped is a whole different ballgame. Your Strava segment isn't worth it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Coyote Creek Trail Cleanup & Hazards:** Heads up if you're usually on the Coyote Creek Trail near Happy Hollow Park & Zoo. The city has been clearing a large homeless encampment ("the Jungle") there since mid-April, which involves significant trash and debris removal. While this is necessary, it means ongoing activity and potential uneven surfaces. Also, that historic Coyote Creek Trestle is still up in the air regarding its future and structural integrity. Expect the unexpected on that stretch.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Reschedule or Hit the Gym:** With the current conditions, your best bet is to reschedule your outdoor workout for later in the week when things dry out, or stick to an indoor facility. If you absolutely *must* run outside, stick to known routes, wear bright, reflective gear, and assume every puddle is a potential hazard. And for the love of all that is holy, bring a towel.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Check Before You Go:** For any specific trail closures not due to immediate weather, Santa Clara County Parks lists ongoing issues, like areas of Anderson Lake County Park being closed until 2032 due to the dam retrofit. Always hit up their website or give them a call if you're venturing further afield.
</AlertBox>

The AQI is currently fantastic at 36, which is great for your lungs. But frankly, with the downpour and potential for lightning, your joints and general well-being are taking a hit today. Stay safe, stay dry, and don't tempt fate with Mother Nature.
