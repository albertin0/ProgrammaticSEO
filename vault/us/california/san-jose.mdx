## The Sweat Report: Boiling Hot, Air's Clean, Trails Are Clear(ish)

Alright, San Jose, let's talk about it. The air quality is pristine, a truly rare gift in the summer months. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a perfect day for a PR on the Los Gatos Creek Trail. That sun is no joke, and temperatures are easily hitting the mid to upper 90s Fahrenheit, making anything more than a gentle stroll feel like an internal combustion engine workout.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat Warning: Don't Be a Hero, Be Hydrated**
Seriously, folks, it's hot enough to fry an egg on the asphalt of West Santa Clara Street. We're under a moderate heat advisory. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real, and they come on faster than you think, especially when you're exerting yourself. Take frequent breaks, seek shade like it's a winning lottery ticket, and if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or lightheaded, STOP. Get somewhere cool, rehydrate, and don't push it. Your body isn't an oven, so don't treat it like one.
</AlertBox>

### **Trail Talk: Watch Out for Vegetation Removal**
If you're hitting the Guadalupe River Trail today, be aware that Valley Water crews are performing vegetation removal adjacent to the trail through early September. The trail remains open, but expect flaggers in some areas. Pay attention to their directions and don't wander into active work zones. It's not a race, and a few minutes of patience won't kill your workout, but a rogue branch might.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **PT's Pro Tip: Time It Right & Hydrate Aggressively**
As a physical therapist who's logged more miles than I care to admit on these very streets, my advice is simple: Go early, go late, or go inside. Midday is for the birds (the ones that can handle the heat, anyway). Plan your outdoor activity for before 8 AM or after 7 PM. And I'm not talking about a sip of water now and then. Before you even head out, down a big glass of water. Take a water bottle with you, and actually drink from it. Consider an electrolyte drink if you're going for more than 45 minutes or sweating heavily. Your joints and muscles will thank you, and you'll lessen your chances of ending up a heat casualty. And remember that the city is actively repaving 205 miles of streets this year, so if you're running on roads, keep an eye out for potential temporary closures or construction zones.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe out there, San Jose. It's a beautiful day for clean air, but a treacherous one for ignoring the sun.
