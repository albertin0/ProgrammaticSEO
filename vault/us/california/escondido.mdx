## The Rundown: March 19, 2026

Alright, fellow Escondido runners, cyclists, and walkers, let's talk about hitting the pavement or dirt today. The morning is looking clear and relatively pleasant, but don't let that fool you. ### Escondido Creek Trail: Still a Construction Zone

Our beloved Escondido Creek Trail is still very much a work in progress. Expect "temporary day-to-day closures" and "active work zones" as improvements continue. There's a persistent "long-term closure at Date Street" on both sides of the creek you'll need to navigate around. Construction crews are usually out Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. So, if you're trying to get a run in during these hours, be prepared for detours or plan an alternate route. You might see new striping, art installations, and underground irrigation work, which will eventually be great, but right now it means paying attention to signage and flaggers.
