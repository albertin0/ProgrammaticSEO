### Allergy Alert: Pollen is Everywhere
That "moderate" pollen forecast is no joke, folks. Oak pollen, a major allergen in Northern California, is in full swing, and mulberry trees are cranking out pollen like it's going out of style. We're talking about pollen that can travel up to 400 miles on wind currents, so even if you don't see the trees, they see you. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, take your meds *before* you head out, and maybe consider an extra shower when you get back to rinse off the invisible yellow dust. Your respiratory system will thank you.
</AlertBox>

Always factor in extra time if your route takes you anywhere near the I-5 to Watt Avenue stretch.
