## Garden Grove's Daily Grind: Don't Get Blown Away

Look, I'm not going to sugarcoat it. It's Friday, March 6, 2026, and the weather gurus are calling for clear skies and a high around 75 degrees Fahrenheit (that's 24°C for those of you not stuck in the past). Perfect, right? Sun's out, temperature's agreeable. You might think about hitting the pavement or taking a cruise down the Medal of Honor Bike and Pedestrian Trail. And you could. But you'd be ignoring the elephant in the room: the wind.

The National Weather Service has slapped us with a Wind Advisory until 4 PM today. While it might start out mellow, expect gusts up to 20 mph. Then, as the evening rolls in, those winds could ramp up to 30 mph with gusts potentially hitting 45 mph overnight. So, if you're planning a sunset run or an early evening stroll, prepare to feel like you're fighting a small, invisible bully every other block. It's not just annoying; strong crosswinds can throw off your gait and make you work harder than necessary, leading to muscle fatigue you didn't sign up for. Keep that core tight, people.

### Pollen and Air Quality: The Usual Suspects

Beyond the bluster, your lungs have a couple of other minor nuisances to contend with. Pollen is at a moderate level today, with alder and cedar/juniper taking center stage. If you usually turn into a snot-nosed mess this time of year, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out, not after you're already hacking. And while the Air Quality Index of 55 isn't terrible, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, anyone with respiratory sensitivities might feel it a bit more than usual, especially when combined with the pollen. No, it's not Irvine-level smog, but it's not crisp mountain air either.
