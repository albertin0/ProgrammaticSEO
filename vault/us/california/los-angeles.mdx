## What's Cooking (and it's not dinner)

Let's cut to the chase: it's too damn hot out there for anything strenuous. We're talking 20-30 degrees above normal for March, with an **Extreme Heat Warning** plastered across most of Los Angeles County, including our beloved Santa Monica Mountains and pretty much anywhere inland. Even coastal areas are seeing temps in the mid-80s to low-90s, which for us, feels like a slap in the face this early in the year. This isn't just "uncomfortable" heat; this is the kind of heat that can sneak up on you and leave you looking like a raisin, or worse, in the ER with heat exhaustion. Hydration isn't a suggestion today; it's a lifeline.

The good news, if you can call it that when you're roasting, is our Air Quality Index (AQI) is fantastic at 18. The dominant pollutant, ozone, is at healthy levels, so your lungs are getting a break from the smog, even if your whole body is not. But honestly, a clear sky at 95 degrees inland still means your workout needs to be severely curtailed.

## Local Hazards & Warnings

<AlertBox type="danger">
**EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT!** Seriously, folks. Temperatures are dangerous for outdoor activity from Tuesday, March 17th, through Friday, March 20th. Avoid strenuous workouts outside during peak heat hours (10 AM - 6 PM). Seek shade, wear light clothing, and drink copious amounts of water. Check on your neighbors, especially the vulnerable, and for the love of all that is holy, do NOT leave kids or pets in cars.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures in Angeles National Forest:** Still dealing with multiple closures. Specifically, trails around the **Mount Baldy** area (Mount San Antonio) within the San Gabriel Mountains National Monument are closed for public safety through at least March 20, 2026. Don't just show up hoping for the best; check the Angeles National Forest website before you go. The Eaton Fire area also has long-term closures affecting places like the Mount Wilson Toll Road and Eaton Canyon Natural Area.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Rethink Your Route:** If you absolutely *must* get out there, stick to shaded, flat routes. The Marvin Braude Bike Trail along the coast might offer a slight reprieve from the insane inland heat, but even there, expect it to be unseasonably warm. Go early, go short, and listen to your body. Or better yet, embrace the gym for a few days.
</AlertBox>
