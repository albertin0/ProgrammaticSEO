<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ongoing Road Work:** Caltrans has ongoing lane closures and parking restrictions on State Route 2 (SR-2) in West LA, Hollywood, and Echo Park. ### Persistent Trail Closures

<AlertBox type="info">

Don't just show up hoping for the best; check the county parks and forest service websites before you head out to avoid disappointment or, worse, running into an unsafe area.
