<AlertBox type="danger">
### High Wind Warning in Effect!
The National Weather Service has issued a High Wind Warning for parts of Los Angeles County, specifically targeting the Santa Susana Mountains and the western San Gabriel Mountains, including the Highway 14 corridor. Expect northeast winds of 25 to 40 mph, with gusts reaching up to 60-65 mph possible through 6:00 PM PDT today. These aren't just breezy conditions; damaging winds could bring down trees and power lines. If your usual route takes you through exposed canyons or higher elevations in these areas, like some of the trails near Acton or Malibu Bowl, seriously reconsider. Falling debris and tree limbs are a real threat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Long-Term Trail Closures Remain
For those planning to hit the dirt, remember that many popular spots are still off-limits. Trails within Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas, which got scorched by the January 2025 Eaton Fire, remain closed until the end of 2027. This isn't just a suggestion; it's for public safety and to let the ecosystem recover. This includes portions of the Altadena Crest Trail and the main Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Even if you're a regular, check official sources before you lace up, especially if you're venturing into the Angeles National Forest. Additionally, Chaney Trail Road, which leads into Millard Canyon, is still closed to vehicles until Spring 2026 for guardrail repairs, though you might be able to hike or bike in.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Gear Up for the Gusts
If you absolutely must head out where it's windy, dress appropriately. Wind-resistant layers are a must to avoid hypothermia, even on a clear day. Consider trail conditions carefully if you're near trees; branches can come down without warning. On the bright side, the AQI is a beautiful 18, so your lungs will thank you if you find a sheltered route.
</AlertBox>
