<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beach Bum Beware: Ocean Water Advisory!**
If your workout involves hitting the sand and surf, listen up. The Los Angeles County Public Health Department has issued an advisory to **avoid all contact with ocean water** at all LA County beaches. Recent rains mean bacteria, chemicals, and all sorts of delightful street runoff could be lurking. This warning is in effect until 8 AM Wednesday, April 15. So, maybe stick to running *next* to the waves, not *in* them, unless you're aiming for a new bacterial infection PR.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Talk & Road Report

Looks like we've dodged any major wildfires or intense Santa Ana winds in the last 24 hours, which is always a win in this city. CAL FIRE reports no active significant incidents for LA, so no smoke-choked runs today.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Know Before You Go: Ongoing Trail Closures**
Just because the air is clear doesn't mean all trails are open. For instance, the **West Trail at Runyon Canyon** is still closed. Don't be that person hopping fences for an Instagram photo; it's annoying, dangerous, and makes more work for park rangers who'd rather be doing literally anything else. Check the LA County Department of Parks and Recreation website for other potential closures, including intermittent ones at Marshall Canyon and sections of Eaton Canyon.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Traffic Shenanigans

While no workout-specific construction hit the headlines in the last day, remember that Los Angeles is perpetually under construction. If you're driving to your chosen workout spot, especially around the Sepulveda Pass area, expect potential delays on the I-405 due to ongoing pavement rehabilitation. Also, Dodger games are running through April 15, so traffic around Dodger Stadium will be a mess. Plan your routes and your sanity accordingly.

The good news? The much-hyped (and much-delayed) LAX Automated People Mover is starting reliability testing next week, but that's not affecting *your* run today, just future airport woes. And no, the LA Marathon isn't today; that was back on March 8th, so those street closures are long gone. Don't worry, you didn't miss it. Or maybe you did, and now you have a year to train.

Overall, it's a decent day to get out and move your body. Just be smart, stay hydrated, and don't drink the ocean. You're welcome.
