### Workout Hazards: The Usual Suspects, Plus Some Perpetual Closures

It's Monday, not the peak of fire season (yet), and there aren't any current extreme weather warnings or fresh wildfire smoke advisories for the LA area from the last 24 hours. So, no excuses there.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### PERMANENTLY CLOSED (Until Further Notice, or Like, Forever)

Seriously, if you were planning to hit some of the higher elevation trails, check before you go. The **Eaton Canyon** area, and much of the **Angeles National Forest**, still have extensive closures in place from the January 2025 Eaton Fire, which aren't expected to reopen until December 31, 2027, in some sections. This includes parts of the Altadena Crest Trail and many roads like the Mount Wilson Toll Road for vehicles (until Fall 2026). Don't be that person ignoring the signs, getting rescued, and wasting taxpayer money because you wanted to "explore." Also, the **West Trail at Runyon Canyon** is still shut down. Get over it, there are other trails.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch Out for Wanderlust-Induced Weeping (aka Allergies)

With moderate pollen, some of you are going to be sneezing more than a cat in a pepper factory. Take your antihistamines. Being congested when you're trying to push pace is a special kind of hell.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### A Glimpse into the Smoggy Future

While today's AQI is golden, the LA Times recently dropped a report that 2026 has been a particularly bad year for ozone pollution so far, especially in the valleys. This is an ongoing issue, not a daily hazard right now, but it's something to keep in mind as summer really heats up. Your lungs will thank you for paying attention to the daily AQI, not just the weather.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, grab your water, and maybe a map to double-check those trail closures. It's a solid day for an outdoor workout, just don't go trying to run through a protected burn zone.
