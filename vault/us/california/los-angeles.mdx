Alright, LA, Thursday is looking *mostly* clear and a comfortable 16.5°C (61.7°F), which usually screams "get outside." But before you lace up and blast out the door, let's talk about the real world beyond your window pane.

First off, the elephant in the room that isn't really in a room: **wildfires**. Multiple new blazes popped up in Los Angeles County just yesterday, June 3rd, and for some, containment is still a big fat question mark. Your current AQI is sitting at 65, which is "Moderate," with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. That's *fine* for most, but PM2.5 is the nasty stuff that comes from smoke, and "moderate" can turn "unhealthy for sensitive groups" real quick if the winds shift or a fire picks up. Don't be a hero; if you taste smoke, get inside.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Wildfire Smoke & Air Quality:** While the overall AQI is moderate, new wildfires in the county mean localized smoke impacts are possible. Pay attention to how you feel, and consider an indoor workout if you detect smoke or have respiratory sensitivities.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the ongoing saga of construction. The city's always building something, and today's no different. If your route takes you anywhere near Westwood, be advised that the UCLA Station construction is still chugging along, causing potential lane closures on Wilshire Blvd and surrounding streets through June. Caltrans also has I-5 in Castaic and parts of US-101 in Hollywood and I-210 in Sunland undergoing rehabilitation work, which means various lane and ramp closures, especially overnight.

But the real kicker for us trail runners and cyclists? **Agoura Road in Agoura Hills is fully closed** between Rondell Street and Hydepark Drive on weekdays from 7 AM to 5 PM until July 1st for the Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing. And I mean *fully* closed – that includes pedestrians and cyclists during those hours. So if you were planning on taking your mountain bike through there, think again or go during off-hours.

Speaking of trails, the **Eaton Canyon area** and parts of the Angeles National Forest are still a minefield of closures thanks to the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Trails like Eaton Canyon Trail, Altadena Crest Trail, and sections of Chaney Trail are off-limits, and these closures are set to last all the way through December 31, 2027. Even roads like Chaney Trail Road are closed to cars until Fall 2026, meaning if you want to access some of the *reopened* trails in the vicinity, you'll be hiking or biking in for a while. Also, don't forget Marshall Canyon Trail has intermittent weekday closures for oak tree pest management.
