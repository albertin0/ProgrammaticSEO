## Current Conditions: Clearing Skies, Lingering Headaches

The skies above Los Angeles are "Mainly Clear" today with a comfortable 16.5°C (that's about 62°F for the folks still clinging to Fahrenheit). That's prime running weather if you're sticking to the pavement. Our Air Quality Index is a sweet 18, which is in the "Good" range, so take a deep breath – unless you're one of the many Angelinos whose nose is already running thanks to that "Moderate" pollen count.

Speaking of breathing easy, the worst of those Santa Ana winds and associated fire dangers that had the Santa Monica Mountains on high alert earlier in the week seem to have died down. The National Weather Service even noted a shift to weakly onshore flow yesterday evening. Good news for not getting blown off a ridge, but it doesn't magically reopen all our trails.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures in Effect!
Don't just assume your favorite mountain escape is open for business. Due to the 2025 Eaton Fire, large sections of Eaton Canyon and surrounding trails, including the Altadena Crest Trail and parts of the Mount Wilson Toll Road, are closed clear through December 31, 2027. Additionally, as of today, October 20, Glendora Mountain Road and Glendora Ridge Road in the Angeles National Forest are closed due to "large amounts of debris on the road." If you were planning on a suffer-fest climb out there, find a new route or prepare for a detour.
</AlertBox>

## Construction & Commute Chaos (Because, LA)

If you're driving to your workout spot, plan for delays. Caltrans has a sign rehabilitation project on State Route 91 from Cerritos to Gardena, meaning overnight lane, connector, and ramp closures through Friday, October 23. Heading near the airport? Expect lane restrictions and closures around LAX on Century Blvd through mid-November for ongoing roadway construction. And if you're in Beverly Hills, the Metro D Line Extension construction continues to chew up Wilshire Blvd and La Cienega with intermittent lane and sidewalk closures.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pro Tip: Embrace the Neighborhood Run
With mountain trails impacted by past fires and current debris, and widespread construction, today might be the day to explore your local streets or a less remote park. Griffith Park still has plenty of open space, but be aware of localized construction or event impacts before you head out. Check the city or county parks websites for the latest on specific closures.
</AlertBox>

The coastal flooding from Tropical Storm Rachel earlier this month seems to have largely subsided, with officials suggesting the worst was over as of early last week. However, a "pretty potent storm" is brewing for Wednesday and Thursday, bringing widespread rain and a chance of thunderstorms. So enjoy today's relative calm, because things are about to get wet.
