## Today's Vitals: Breathe Deep (Mostly)

Let's cut to the chase: your lungs are getting a relative break today. The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a low 18, with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant. That's practically pristine for Los Angeles. Combined with a mild 16.5°C (around 62°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, it sounds like a runner's dream.

But before you sprint out the door feeling invincible, remember it's still March. That means **moderate pollen levels** are hanging around. If you're one of the lucky ones who gets hit by seasonal allergies, you might be sniffing and sneezing your way through that otherwise clear air. Don't forget your antihistamines, or you'll be regretting that run along the Arroyo Seco.

## Watch Your Step: Trail Snafus & Urban Obstacles

While the air might be agreeable, the ground beneath your feet could be another story.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Ongoing Trail Closures
  Look, the January 2025 wildfires were no joke, and their aftermath still lingers. If you're planning a mountain trek, several areas are still off-limits. The **Eaton Fire Area Closure** in the Angeles National Forest remains in effect through December 31, 2027, impacting popular spots like sections of the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Don't try to be a hero and circumvent these. They're closed for your safety and for the land to recover. Also, parts of the **Mount Wilson Toll Road** are still closed to vehicular traffic until "Spring 2026," so be aware if you're attempting to access anything via that route. Check the Angeles National Forest alerts before you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Construction in Century City
  If your workout takes you through the urban jungle, particularly around Century City, be advised. The Metro D Line Extension is still chugging along. **Constellation Boulevard**, from Century Park East to just west of Avenue of the Stars, is seeing deck panel removal and street restoration. This means ongoing traffic control, potential nighttime sidewalk closures, and single-lane operations. If you usually run that stretch, expect detours and keep your head on a swivel for machinery and temporary barriers. It's not a leisurely stroll right now.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today’s a solid 7. Great air and temps, but the usual LA annoyances of pollen, lingering fire closures, and relentless construction are out there. Pick your path wisely and stay safe.
