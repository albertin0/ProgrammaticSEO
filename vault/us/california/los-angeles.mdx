<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory for Inland Areas!** While it's relatively cool in central LA, a Heat Advisory is still in effect for portions of southwest California until Thursday, August 20th, at 8:00 PM PDT. Expect temperatures ranging from 95 to 105 degrees in the foothills and mountains. If you're planning on tackling anything beyond the immediate city limits, especially in areas like the San Gabriel Mountains or closer to the valleys, adjust your plans. ### Your Lungs Get a Break, Mostly

Good news on the air front: our general Air Quality Index (AQI) is a very respectable 18, which is in the "Good" category. However, the dominant pollutant is still ozone.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Ozone Awareness for Sensitive Folks:** While the overall AQI is good, AccuWeather has flagged Los Angeles air quality for Wednesday, August 19th, as "Poor" for sensitive groups, primarily due to ozone. If you have respiratory issues like asthma, be mindful that even lower concentrations of ground-level ozone can cause throat irritation or breathing difficulties.
</AlertBox>

### Runyon Canyon's West Trail Remains Stubbornly Closed

For the love of all that is holy, pay attention to the signs. The **Runyon Canyon West Trail** is *still* closed. It's been fenced off since the January 2025 Sunset Fire ravaged the area, and despite a brief, confusing reopening in February 2026, it was quickly re-flagged as inaccessible by March. They're still doing habitat rehabilitation and erosion control. If you ignore the closure signs, you're not just being a rebel; you're risking arrest. So, if your usual loop involves the West Trail, pick another route, or maybe actually read a map.

Speaking of trail closures, don't forget that many areas within the Angeles National Forest, particularly around Eaton Canyon, are still closed from the 2025 Eaton Fire. These closures are in effect until December 31, 2027, to allow the burned wildlands to recover and for public safety. And yes, fire danger is still elevated to "VERY HIGH" as of late July.
