Today's a classic LA mixed bag: The temperature is a mild 21.4°C (about 70.5°F) and the skies are clear, which sounds lovely on paper. But for those of us who actually push miles, "clear" doesn't automatically mean "clean." That AQI of 73 is squarely in the moderate category, and coupled with moderate pollen, it's enough to make your airways work a little harder. This isn't the day to try for a PR if you're sensitive to air quality.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch for Lingering Heat & Fire Aftermath
A Heat Advisory for parts of LA County (think San Fernando, San Gabriel, Santa Clarita valleys) technically expired last night at 8 PM PDT. However, it's still August, and those interior valleys can still heat up quickly, especially by midday. Drink more water than you think you need, even with today's milder general forecast. Also, while not widespread, new wildfires were reported in LA County yesterday. Stay vigilant for any visible smoke or changes in air quality, especially if you're in hillside areas or on canyon trails.
</AlertBox>

Let's talk about getting out there. While parts of *Griffith Park* are prime for a run or hike, remember that some popular spots aren't always open. Case in point: the West Trail at *Runyon Canyon* is currently listed as closed. Always check your specific route before you head out, because nothing sucks more than driving across town only to hit a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign. And if you're usually hitting up the *San Gabriel River trail*, be aware that the Lario Park staging area is still closed and being used as a hazardous debris dump from a fire back in 2025. Plan your long runs accordingly.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Know Your Air & Body
The dominant pollutant today is PM2.5, which are those tiny particles that can really irritate your respiratory system. If you have asthma or other lung conditions, consider scaling back the intensity of your outdoor workout, or moving it indoors. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

For those of you running or cycling near the airport, be aware that LAX had full closures on westbound Century Blvd during the early mornings yesterday and scheduled for tomorrow as well. While mostly overnight, it's a reminder of the constant construction hustle in this city that can impact traffic and routes.
