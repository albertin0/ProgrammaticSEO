## Today's Rundown: Cloudy with a Chance of Detours and Smoke

Alright, Angelenos, it's June 30th, and the weather gods have blessed us with a reprieve from the heat. We've got that classic overcast vibe, hovering around 17.7°C. That's prime running weather for keeping your core temp down and your stride long. Your knees and ankles, especially, will appreciate not pounding pavement in 90-degree heat, which means less compensatory strain on those overworked tendons.

Now, about the air. The overall Air Quality Index is a pristine 18, which is great news for your lungs. The dominant pollutant is listed as "Ozone/Other," but at that level, you're breathing cleaner than you will be come August. However, this is L.A., and there's always a caveat.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Localized Smoke Advisory Still Active:** Even with good overall AQI, a smoke advisory remains in place for the Boyle Heights area due to the recent Storage Facility Fire. While the shelter-in-place has been lifted, if you're a runner with asthma, COPD, or other respiratory sensitivities, you should still monitor air quality updates from AQMD and consider rerouting your workout away from the immediate vicinity. Don't risk a flare-up for a casual jog.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Blockages: Your Biggest Headache Today

This is where your run might go sideways. While the weather's cooperating, the city is constantly under construction, and trails have their own issues.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Marshall Canyon Trail - Intermittent Closures:** If you're heading east, a portion of the Northern Segment of Marshall Canyon Trail is still seeing intermittent weekday closures through June 2026. This is due to Goldspotted Oak Borer (GSOB) mitigation efforts and tree work. Don't waste your time driving out there only to hit a "trail closed" sign. Check the Trails LA County website before you go.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just Marshall Canyon. Popular spots like **Runyon Canyon's West Trail** and Wattles Garden Park are still closed until further notice. Also, remember that much of **Eaton Canyon** and surrounding Angeles National Forest trails remain closed until December 31, 2027, recovering from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. So, if your usual haunt is one of these, you need a new plan.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Everywhere:** Caltrans has overnight lane and connector closures on SR-91 (Cerritos to Gardena) until July 1, and significant overnight I-405 closures through Sepulveda Pass wrapping up July 2. While these are mostly late-night driving concerns, there are also 40 various road closures in unincorporated areas of LA County, some impacting daytime travel and potentially pedestrian/bike access for things like waterline installation. Be aware of your surroundings and expect delays if you're navigating urban routes.
</AlertBox>
