### What to Watch Out For

Those lingering trail closures are a real pain. If your usual route takes you through **Eaton Canyon Natural Area** or any of the higher elevations in the **Angeles National Forest** around Mt. Baldy, know that many trails are still explicitly closed through at least March 20th due to last year's fire damage. Seriously, unstable terrain and hidden hazards are no joke. Check the official county and forest service websites before you drive all the way out there only to hit a "CLOSED" sign.

There's also some ongoing Metro construction causing lane closures on Wilshire Boulevard between Spaulding Avenue and South Stanley Avenue. Not a trail hazard, but if you're commuting to a run in that area, expect traffic headaches.

So, get out there and enjoy the decent weather while it lasts. Just pick your path wisely, and mentally prepare for the oven that's coming next week.
