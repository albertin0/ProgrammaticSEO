## The Lowdown for Today's Grind

Look, I love hitting the pavement or trails as much as the next masochist, but you need to be smart today. That "Mainly Clear" sky isn't the whole story. The National Weather Service and LA County Public Health are practically screaming about extreme heat inland. If your usual long run takes you anywhere near the San Gabriel Mountains, the Antelope Valley, or the Santa Susana Mountains, think twice. Seriously, switch to an indoor workout, or at least shift your outdoor activity to the very early morning or late evening, and stick to shaded routes near the coast if you can. Hydration isn't optional; it's survival.
