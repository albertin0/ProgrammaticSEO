## The Rundown: Your Body vs. Friday, October 2nd

It's officially fall, but someone forgot to tell Mother Nature. We're staring down the barrel of what the National Weather Service is calling an *extreme heat warning* for the greater Los Angeles area, with temperatures expected to climb into the mid-90s and even triple digits today. And guess what? It’s not just today; they're saying this heatwave could stick around well into next week. So, that perfectly clear morning sky? Don't let it fool you. The pavement will be radiating heat, and staying hydrated will be a full-time job.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning in Effect.** Running or intense outdoor activity during peak heat could lead to heatstroke, dehydration, and serious medical issues. If you *must* go out, do it at dawn, keep it short, and hydrate obsessively. Better yet, hit the gym or reschedule.
</AlertBox>

On the air quality front, we're sitting at a "moderate" AQI of 76, which sounds okay until you notice PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant. Particles from lingering issues, maybe even residual wildfire effects, can be a real lung-irritant, especially for those of us with sensitive airways. Just yesterday, some sources were already calling the air "poor" for sensitive groups. Your lungs are not a filter, people. Listen to them.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: PM2.5 Dominant Pollutant.** Even "moderate" air quality with fine particulate matter can cause respiratory irritation, especially for sensitive individuals. If you start coughing or feel tightness, cut your workout short.
</AlertBox>

If you had plans to hit some of our gorgeous trails, check your maps. A good chunk of the Angeles National Forest, particularly around Eaton Canyon, remains closed until at least December 2027 due to the 2025 Eaton Fire. That means popular spots like Altadena Crest Trail and Idlehour Trail are no-go zones. Don't be "that guy" who ignores the signs and risks getting lost or injured on an unstable path. Also, if you're planning to be anywhere near Loma Alta Park in Altadena tomorrow morning, be aware: the Angeles Crest 100 Mile Endurance Run is kicking off, meaning extra traffic and congestion.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Scope Out Cooling Centers.** With this heatwave expected to last, know where your nearest cooling centers are. Many libraries and rec centers can offer a cool refuge.
</AlertBox>

Honestly, today is a day to seriously consider moving your workout indoors or keeping it extremely low-impact and short. Your body will thank you.
