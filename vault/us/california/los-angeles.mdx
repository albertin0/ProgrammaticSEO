## What's Hitting the Streets and Trails Today

Look, if you're anywhere near the beach cities this afternoon, *do not* plan on a leisurely jog along the coast. The **Nike After Dark Tour Half Marathon** is rolling through Redondo Beach, Torrance, and Hermosa Beach, kicking off at 5:00 p.m.. This isn't just a few cones on a bike path; we're talking significant traffic and parking impacts, with multiple roadway closures and lane restrictions around King Harbor and along the race route. If you try to run through that, you're either going to get run over or seriously annoy thousands of runners who actually registered. Stay out of their way.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**South Bay Shutdown for Nike Half Marathon!** Avoid King Harbor, Redondo Beach, Torrance, and Hermosa Beach from 5 p.m. onwards today. Seriously, find another spot.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the South Bay, construction continues to be a persistent nuisance. If your route takes you anywhere near Wilshire Boulevard or La Cienega, especially in Beverly Hills, expect disruptions. The Metro D Line Extension project is still chugging along, meaning intermittent lane closures, sidewalk detours, and general mayhem are par for the course. Planning on a quick jaunt down State Route 2 in West LA, Hollywood, or Echo Park? Caltrans has ongoing pavement work with lane closures and 24/7 parking restrictions through today. Your car might get towed, and you definitely won't be setting any PRs dodging cones.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Central LA Construction Chaos:** Metro D Line work on Wilshire/La Cienega and Caltrans paving on SR-2 mean unpredictable closures and detours. Check your route before you head out, or you'll be doing an impromptu obstacle course.
</AlertBox>

## Breathing Easy, But Mind the Green Stuff

On the bright side, the air quality today is honestly fantastic, sitting at a "Good" AQI of 18. You'd be hard-pressed to find a better day to fill those lungs. The clear skies and cool 14.3°C (around 58°F) are absolutely prime conditions for any outdoor activity. Forget the usual smog, today is a gift.

However, don't get too cocky if you're prone to seasonal sniffles. We're looking at moderate pollen levels across the city. So, if you're one of the unfortunate souls who feels every pollen grain, maybe pop an antihistamine before you head out, or stick to a treadmill if your eyes and nose are already staging a revolt.

## Trail Time or Trail Travesty?

For our trail runners, parts of the Angeles National Forest are still nursing wounds from past fires, meaning long-standing closures are very much in effect. Don't waste your gas driving up only to find a locked gate and a "Closed Until December 31, 2027" sign. Iconic trails like the Sam Merrill Trail, Echo Mountain, Mount Lowe, and the popular Eaton Canyon Natural Area are still off-limits. Seriously, these aren't suggestions; they're *closures* for public safety and ecosystem recovery. Respect them.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Advisory:** Moderate pollen today. Allergy sufferers, take precautions or you'll be trading that runner's high for a sneezing fit.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Angeles National Forest Trail Closures Persist:** Many trails, including Sam Merrill and Eaton Canyon, remain closed until late 2027 due to fire recovery. Plan alternatives to avoid disappointment (and fines).
</AlertBox>

Overall, the weather is beautiful, and the air is clean. Just be smart, know your specific route, and don't assume every path or road is open. L.A. always keeps you on your toes.
