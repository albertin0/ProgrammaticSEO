Enjoy today, you heathens, because starting tomorrow, Los Angeles is going to feel like the inside of a pizza oven. Seriously, get your miles in while the getting's good, because the next week is looking brutal.

<BulletList>
*   **Today's a gift:** We're sitting pretty with mild temps at 16.5°C (that's 61.7°F for the Fahrenheit crowd) and "Mainly Clear" skies. The Air Quality Index is a pristine 18, so your lungs can actually celebrate a bit. Breathe it in while you can.
*   **The Calm Before the Storm (or Oven):** Don't let today's pleasantness fool you. A significant heat advisory kicks in tomorrow, Wednesday, July 22, and stretches all the way through Monday, July 27. Expect triple-digit temps inland and seriously warm overnight lows, meaning your body won't get much relief. This isn't just "warm," it's "dangerous heat" territory.
*   **Ocean's a No-Go (for some):** If your workout involves splashing in the Pacific, think twice. Tropical Storm Elida is still sending some gnarly surf our way, with waves hitting 4 to 8 feet at south-facing beaches. Those conditions are expected to stick around through at least today, Tuesday, so if you're not a seasoned pro, maybe stick to dry land.
*   **Same Old Trail Closures:** Still can't hit a lot of our favorite spots. Eaton Canyon Natural Area and its surrounding trails, like the Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail, are still locked down tighter than a drum. These closures are due to the January 2025 Eaton Fire and are slated to last until at least December 31, 2027. Some roads are also closed to vehicles until Fall 2026. Don't be that idiot hopping fences; it's closed for a reason, and you'll get fined.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Impending Heat Wave - Reschedule or Die (Not literally, but seriously, be smart)
Starting *tomorrow*, Wednesday, July 22, and lasting through Monday, July 27, an extensive heat advisory is in effect for pretty much all of Los Angeles County. Temperatures are going to skyrocket, especially in the Santa Clarita, San Fernando, and San Gabriel Valleys, and our mountain areas. Overnight lows won't offer much relief, exacerbating heat stress. This means you need to rethink any strenuous outdoor activity for the rest of the week. If you absolutely *must* be outside, do it before the sun fully rises, and be done by 8 AM. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light colors, and know the signs of heat exhaustion. Don't push it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Closures Remain
The Eaton Fire did a number on a huge chunk of our beloved trails. Eaton Canyon Natural Area and its connecting routes, including parts of the Altadena Crest Trail, are still closed indefinitely, likely through 2027. Seriously, respect the barriers. These aren't just suggestions; they're there for your safety and to let the ecosystem heal. Trying to sneak through can put you, and the first responders who'd have to rescue your dumb ass, at risk. Plan alternate routes in areas that are actually open.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Construction Commutes
While not directly impacting your run, heavy overnight construction on SR-2 in West LA, Hollywood, and Echo Park, for curb, gutter, and sidewalk work, is ongoing from Sunday, July 19 to Saturday, July 25. If your early morning run involves driving through these areas to get to a starting point, factor in potential delays.
</AlertBox>
