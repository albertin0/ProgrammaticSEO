## Today's Workout Reality Check

Look, the temperature at 16.1°C (61°F) is damn near perfect. That's the one bright spot. But you can't just slap on your running shoes and head out blindly today. Especially not if your lungs decide to throw a fit or you're planning on hitting the dirt.

### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Deep, Don't Breathe Often

With a "Moderate" AQI and PM2.5 running the show, your lungs are doing extra work filtering out crud. If you're sensitive to air quality, have asthma, or just generally don't enjoy hacking up a lung on your tempo run, you might want to consider indoors. The "Moderate" pollen level isn't helping either.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Sensitive Lungs, Beware!** Today's air quality, while not "Unhealthy," is definitely not "Good," especially with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. If you're prone to respiratory issues or allergies (hello, moderate pollen), keep your workout shorter or indoors. Listen to your body – that cough isn't just a "good workout" cough.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Status: Ignorance is Not Bliss (It's Trespassing)

For those of you eyeing a scenic route through the mountains, a huge chunk of the Angeles National Forest and local county trails remain closed indefinitely due to the January 2025 Eaton Fire. This isn't just a suggestion; it's an official closure for public safety and land recovery. Trying to "just sneak through" not only puts you at risk of unstable ground, landslides, or falling debris, but it also hinders the recovery efforts.

Chaney Trail Road, which feeds into areas like Millard Canyon, is closed to cars until Fall 2026, and while the Millard Canyon *trails* themselves might technically be open for foot traffic, don't expect restrooms or water at the campground. So if you're planning a long run, hydrate beforehand and pack it in.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Stay OFF Closed Trails!** Numerous trails within the Angeles National Forest and Eaton Canyon remain closed until at least December 31, 2027. This includes popular spots like Altadena Crest Trail and the Mount Wilson Toll Road. These closures are for your safety. Respect them.
</AlertBox>

It's a clear day, which is nice for visibility, but the winds are kicking up. If you're on exposed areas, be mindful of gusts. Also, keep an eye on traffic today if you're anywhere near SoFi Stadium later, as there's a concert causing delays from 5 PM to 11 PM. Nobody wants to add dodging bad drivers to their workout.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Always Check Local Updates:** Before you head out, especially if you're venturing off paved roads, do your due diligence. Check LA County Public Works for road closures and the Angeles National Forest Alerts for trail status. Things change faster than a marathoner's pace.
