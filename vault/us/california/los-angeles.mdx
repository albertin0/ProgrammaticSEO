## The Good, The Bad, and The Permanently Closed

The good news? No immediate, widespread "run for your life" kind of hazards. That small brush fire near Eagle Rock yesterday? Forward progress stopped. The "Hilary Fire" in L.A. County from yesterday? It's 86% contained at a measly 74 acres. So, no choking on smoke while you're trying to PR your next segment.

The bigger issues today are the same old annoyances that just *persist*.

### Your Favorite Trails Are Still a Minefield

If your usual routine involves hitting the trails in **Eaton Canyon** or the **Angeles National Forest**, well, you're still out of luck for a lot of it. Large sections, including the Altadena Crest Trail and parts of the Mount Wilson Toll Road, are *still* shut down from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. And guess what? They're staying that way until at least December 31, 2027. That's not a temporary detour, folks; that's a lifestyle change. Also, the Williamson Rock area and San Dimas Experimental Forest are locked up tighter than a drum. It's all "for public safety and environmental recovery," which is nice and all, but doesn't help your Strava stats today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Coastal Crapshoot for Swimmers & Surfers
Thinking of hitting the water for a beach run or swim? Think again. There's a high chance of large waves and strong rip currents, especially on our south-facing beaches. This is courtesy of those south swells from Tropical System Marie, which is mostly doing its own thing out in the Pacific, thankfully not bringing us rain but certainly messing with our coastline. Unless you're trying out for the Olympics, probably best to stick to the sand. "Increased risk of ocean drowning" is usually a good indicator to stay out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zone Ahead (Literally)
Caltrans is still doing its overnight lane reductions and ramp closures on I-405, State Route 2, and even parts of PCH and Topanga Canyon Boulevard through Friday, September 4th. It's mostly overnight work, so your morning run shouldn't be *too* impacted, but keep an eye out if your route takes you near these areas. Don't be that person who gets clipped by a K-rail.
</AlertBox>
