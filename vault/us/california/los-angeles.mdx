## Trail Troubles & Road Humps

If you're a trail runner, you already know the drill, but it bears repeating: **Eaton Canyon is a no-go.** Seriously. Due to the lingering damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire and the subsequent mudslides, all trails in Eaton Canyon are under a strict closure until at least December 31, 2027. That means no waterfall selfies, no challenging climbs, just closed gates. Don't be "that person" who ignores the signs; trespassing could earn you a hefty fine up to $5,000. Find an alternative. There are plenty of other spots that actually want you there.

<AlertBox type="danger">

**Eaton Canyon is CLOSED.** Seriously, stay out. The trails are unstable and entering can result in significant fines. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a hard closure for public safety and ecological recovery until at least the end of 2027.

</AlertBox>

Over in Runyon Canyon, the West Trail is also closed due to a recent fire, though other sections are open. And if you're thinking about the Angeles National Forest, be aware that several areas are closed due to various issues, including Williamson Rock and parts of Rincon-Redbox Road, plus the overall fire danger level is "Very High" as of late July.

For those who stick to the pavement, watch out for roadwork. The Los Angeles County Sanitation District is doing work on Palos Verdes Drive South from Schooner Drive to the eastern landslide entrance until noon today, so expect some delays if that's part of your cycling or running route.

<AlertBox type="info">

**Hydration is NOT optional today.** With that heat advisory and a high of 32°C on the way, carry more water than you think you need, especially if you're out past the morning fog.

</AlertBox>
