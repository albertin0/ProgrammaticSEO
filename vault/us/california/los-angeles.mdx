### Trail & Road Blocks

This is where your outdoor plans might hit a snag. Old Man Winter (and some pesky bugs) have left their mark.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Marshall Canyon Trail Partially Closed

Heads up, especially if you favor the Northern Segment of Marshall Canyon Trail. There are intermittent weekday closures from April through June 2026 due to oak tree pest management (those Goldspotted Oak Borers are no joke) and other tree work. Don't just show up hoping for the best; check the Trails LA County site before you head out, or you'll be turning around.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Angeles National Forest Still Impacted

If you're planning on hitting the higher elevations, be aware that many parts of the Angeles National Forest are still under long-term closures. Roads and trails like Mount Wilson Toll Road (below the main trail), the Williamson Rock Area, and parts of the West Inspiration Loop and Lower Betty Rogers Trail remain closed due to fire recovery and resource protection efforts. These aren't temporary inconveniences; some closures extend through December 2027. Plan your routes carefully and respect those signs.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Agoura Road Users: Wildlife Crossing Construction

For those of you cycling or running along Agoura Road in Agoura Hills, be aware of ongoing weekday closures (7 a.m. to 5 p.m.) between Rondell Street and Hydepark Drive for the Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing construction. No through traffic – that includes us runners and cyclists. Caltrans has a free shuttle running every 30 minutes, but honestly, consider an alternate route for your workout to avoid the hassle.
</AlertBox>

In short, it's a stellar day to be outside in Los Angeles for the most part, provided you check your specific trail for closures and aren't planning a scenic detour through a construction zone. Your lungs will thank you.
