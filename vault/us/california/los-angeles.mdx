### Air Quality & Weather Reality Check

Look, I get it. The AQI says 18, which *should* be "good." But that's a general number, and it doesn't account for the localized garbage fire smoke that's actively polluting our air in significant parts of the county. The South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) has issued an advisory specifically stating "unhealthy air quality" due to the Bouquet Fire. Always trust the specific advisory over a blanket AQI when there's an active incident.
