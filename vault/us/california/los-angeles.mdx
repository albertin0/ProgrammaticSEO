## The Bad News: Fire, Heat, and Closures

Now for the dose of reality. While your immediate neighborhood might feel breezy and clear, LA County isn't without its issues.

### Recent Fire Activity Near Palos Verdes

Just yesterday, a "fast-moving Palos Fire" caused damage and injuries near Los Angeles. This is no small thing. While specific smoke advisories aren't active from the South Coast AQMD right now, fires can pop up or flare without much warning, and residual ash or localized smoke plumes are always a possibility, especially if you're downwind. Keep an eye out. If you smell smoke or see ash, head inside.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Warning Lingering!** Despite our current cool temps, an extreme heat warning for parts of Los Angeles County (including the Malibu Coast, Palos Verdes Hills, and Santa Monica Mountains) was in effect through 8 PM *tonight*, Thursday, September 17th. Temperatures were expected to hit 92-108°F in these areas. Even if the immediate temperature feels fine where you are, sustained heat dries out trails, makes hillsides unstable, and can leave you dehydrated faster than you think. Don't be a hero; hydrate like it's your job, especially if you're hitting any exposed hills.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble

If your usual go-to is up in the hills, you better check before you go.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Eaton Canyon Closures Continue!** Many trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas are still shut down until December 31, 2027, due to the January 2025 Eaton Fire. We're talking about the Altadena Crest Trail, Eaton Canyon Trail itself, and even Chaney Trail Road being closed to vehicular traffic until Fall 2026. These closures are for public safety and to let the land recover. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and gets a hefty fine for trespassing on unstable terrain.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Runyon Canyon West Trail is STILL Closed!** For those of you hoping for a quick sweat sesh with a view, the West Trail at Runyon Canyon is still closed until further notice. You'd think they'd have fixed it by now, but hey, this is LA. Plan your route accordingly.
</AlertBox>
