<AlertBox type="danger">
**Ozone Alert, People!** The "Poor" air quality is driven by ground-level ozone. This stuff makes your lungs work harder and can cause irritation. If you have asthma or any respiratory issues, stay indoors or keep your workout super light and brief. Even if you don't, you'll still feel it on a long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Still Lingers:** Despite any localized cool spots, LA County is still under heat advisories and warnings. If you're venturing inland, expect those temperatures to climb rapidly. Hydrate aggressively, plan for early morning or late evening workouts, and don't push it. Heat stroke is a real thing, folks.
</AlertBox>
