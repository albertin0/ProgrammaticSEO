## The Rundown

Look, I love a good overcast day as much as the next runner trying to avoid baking on the Marvin Braude Bike Trail, but we've got some lurking issues. The weather itself is nearly perfect for a long-slog, cool and forgiving. Your lungs are getting a break with that stellar AQI of 18. That's about as good as it gets in our smoggy basin. But here's the kicker: those new wildfires reported just yesterday in LA County? They're still out there, uncontained, burning on private land. That means even with a good AQI right now, you need to stay vigilant. If you start seeing or smelling smoke, get indoors. Don't be a hero. Your Strava segment isn't worth an ER visit.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Wildfire Smoke is a Real Threat.** Even if the official AQI is low, local smoke plumes can pop up fast, especially with uncontained fires burning in the county. If you see or smell smoke, cut your workout short. Protect your lungs; they're the only pair you've got. The Angeles National Forest also bumped its fire danger to "HIGH" earlier this month, so be extra cautious if you're venturing near any wildland areas.
</AlertBox>

On the trail front, if you were planning on tackling anything in **Eaton Canyon** or the surrounding areas, just turn around. Those trails are still shut down until December 31, 2027, thanks to the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Yeah, over two years. That fire did a number, and the county is prioritizing public safety and natural recovery. So, no scrambling up to the waterfalls for now. This also includes parts of the Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail.

We also have some localized construction to be aware of. Caltrans has intermittent closures on North Access Road in the Port of LA area starting today for resurfacing work. While primarily overnight, some limited daytime work between 9 AM and 3 PM is possible, so if you're running near Wilmington, keep an eye out for reroutes and heavy equipment. And if you were eyeing the northern segment of Marshall Canyon Trail, today, June 29, is supposedly the last day for those intermittent weekday closures due to oak tree pest management. Always worth a quick check before you head out.

So, while the air is crisp (for now) and the marine layer is doing its job keeping us cool, exercise a little extra caution today. Don't be that person caught off guard.
