Look, the weather today is basically perfect. 16.5°C and "Mainly Clear" is what we dream of, a far cry from the scorching heat we've been dealing with or the persistent rain we sometimes get. It's the kind of day that makes you want to hit the pavement or the dirt. But before you lace up, let's talk about the real world, not some corporate wellness blog fantasy.

First off, that wildfire reported yesterday in Los Angeles County? Yeah, that's a thing. While the AQI is currently a lovely 18, meaning the air is generally pretty clean right now, fires are unpredictable. A shift in wind, and suddenly your lungs are regretting every decision you made. Always keep an eye on real-time air quality maps before you head out, especially if you're running near the foothills or any other potentially affected areas. It's not worth compromising your respiratory health over a single run. The smoke might not be *here* right now, but a new, uncontained fire is always a potential game-changer.

Then there are the trails. I swear, sometimes it feels like they're actively trying to keep us off them. If your usual loop involves anything in Eaton Canyon, I'm here to tell you, again, *it's still closed*. Damage from that January 2025 fire means those trails, including the Altadena Crest Trail, are locked down until at least the end of 2027. So much for that scenic workout. And if you're hitting Marshall Canyon, be prepared for some midweek detours. They're doing work for some oak borer bug, so parts of that northern segment are intermittently closed on weekdays through June. Nothing like a good trail run interrupted by a "Do Not Enter" sign.
