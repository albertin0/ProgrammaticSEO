## The Full Rundown: July 16, 2026

Alright, Angelenos, let's talk brass tacks for your workout today. The good news: that "Extreme Heat Warning" for the inland valleys and mountains, which had temperatures soaring into the 90s and 100s, officially expires at 8 PM tonight. So if you're out there now, especially east of the 405 or anywhere near the foothills, you better be wrapping it up or already drenched in sweat and electrolytes. For us closer to the coast, like around Santa Monica or even DTLA, it’s a much milder 70s-80s, but don't get complacent. Heat illness is no joke, especially when humidity decides to join the party.

### Air Quality & Wildfire Woes (Still)

The AQI number looks pretty, sitting at a "Good" 18. Dominant pollutant is Ozone/Other, which is standard LA summer fare. However, don't let that fool you entirely. There's still a *smoke advisory* active due to that Boyle Heights storage facility fire from last week. While the Summit Fire in LA County is 86% contained, that remaining 14% is still sending particulate matter into the air. If you’ve got asthma, COPD, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, take heed. Maybe stick to an indoor session or a walk instead of crushing those intervals. Pollen is moderate today, so if you're usually sniffling and sneezing, your allergies might make an appearance.

### Trail Closures & Obstacles

This is where things get a bit more frustrating for those of us who prefer dirt over asphalt. The Angeles National Forest and the areas around Eaton Canyon are still dealing with the fallout from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Don't even think about hitting the Altadena Crest Trail, parts of the Eaton Canyon Trail, or Chaney Trail itself – they're all still closed for public safety until December 31, 2027. Seriously, respect the closure signs; they're there for a reason, and there are fines involved. Also, a heads-up that the West Trail at Runyon Canyon is currently closed. If your usual route is up there, find a different hill to conquer.
