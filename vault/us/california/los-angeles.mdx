### Where You CAN'T Go (or should be careful)

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Trail Closures: The Saga Continues**
If you were hoping to explore some of the more scenic spots, you might be out of luck. The **Eaton Canyon** trails, along with several others in the Angeles National Forest like the Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail (vehicular traffic is a no-go until Fall 2026), are still shut down. This isn't a new thing; they've been closed since the January 2025 Eaton Fire and aren't expected to reopen until late 2027.

And don't even think about **Stough Canyon or Wildwood Canyon** hiking trails today. They're closed indefinitely due to a nearby fire. Plus, a section of **Runyon Canyon's West Trail** is still taped off. Before you hit any specific trail, do yourself a favor and double-check its status. Nothing worse than driving all the way there just to stare at a "CLOSED" sign.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Keep Hydrated, Especially if You're Working Hard**
While it's not "extreme heat," forecasts suggest temperatures pushing into the upper 70s Fahrenheit. If you're tackling any hills or longer distances, remember your water bottle. Dehydration sneaks up on you, even on seemingly mild days.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **A Note on the Smoke**
The local AQI is fantastic right now, but a "California Weather Update" from yesterday mentioned some fires, including the "Bug Fire" near the CA-NV border, are still producing smoke. There's also some "elevated fire risk" further north and "isolated dry thunderstorm potential" that could spark new blazes. Just something to be aware of if you're out there, and definitely keep an eye on the horizon. If you see or smell smoke, trust your gut and move your workout indoors.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a decent day to get outside, especially if you stick to the flat stuff or know your usual routes are open. Just be mindful of those long-standing trail closures and the moderate pollen.
