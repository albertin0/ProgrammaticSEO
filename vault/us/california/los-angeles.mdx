## Your Lungs are Not a Smoke Filter

Seriously, folks. While the overall AQI might look deceptively low at 18 right now, multiple wildfires are still active around the Southland. We're talking the Sandy Fire in Ventura County, and new fires cropping up in Los Angeles County just last night. This means localized smoke plumes can drift in and out, especially if you're near canyons or up in the hills. Don't rely solely on a single number. Check hyper-local air quality apps before you lace up, especially if you're planning a run near the Angeles National Forest or even on a high point in Griffith Park. What seems clear from your window might be a hazy mess a few miles away, and breathing that garbage in while exerting yourself is a fast track to lung irritation, or worse.

### Trail Closures & Detours

Remember that massive Eaton Fire? The trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas are still recovering and remain closed, some possibly through 2026 or even 2027. Same goes for certain sections of the Santa Monica Mountains, which have been hit by various blazes over the years. Before you commit to your usual route, double-check local park websites for current closure maps. Getting halfway up a climb only to hit a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign is annoying, but much worse is ignoring it and potentially putting yourself or rescue workers at risk.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Wildfire Smoke Hazard!** Even with "Mainly Clear" conditions, active wildfires mean smoke can appear suddenly, especially near canyons or higher elevations. If you smell smoke or see haze, cut your outdoor workout short and head inside. Your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate Pollen Alert:** For those with allergies, today's moderate pollen levels could lead to sneezing, runny nose, and itchy eyes, which won't be helped by any lingering smoke. Consider an antihistamine or an indoor workout.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Events

It's also LA Fleet Week from May 22-25. If your route takes you anywhere near the ports or downtown, expect potential traffic snarls and increased pedestrian activity. Also, be mindful of ongoing roadwork, like the I-5 Burbank Pavement Rehabilitation Project. While the "extended weekend" lane closures for that project might be winding down by today (May 22 is a Friday), there are still regular overnight lane closures. Always plan your route with potential detours in mind, especially if you're cycling.
