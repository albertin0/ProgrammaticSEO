<AlertBox type="danger">
### Coastal Flood Advisory: Stay Off the Sand and Paths Near the Water
Seriously, if you had plans for a beach run or a stroll along the Santa Monica Beach Path right at the water's edge, rethink it. A Coastal Flood Advisory means high tides and surf could bring significant flooding to low-lying coastal areas. Not only is it dangerous to be in the water, but debris and unstable footing on flooded paths are an injury waiting to happen. Give the ocean some space today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Risk Lingers – Especially Inland
Even though the immediate Red Flag Warning from last week has expired, we're still in October, and it's Southern California. Dry brush, warm temperatures hitting the 80s, and any potential for shifting winds (even if currently "light and variable") mean fire danger is a constant companion. Several smaller fires have been reported recently, so stay vigilant, especially if you're hitting trails near the Angeles National Forest. If you smell smoke or see anything suspicious, report it immediately. Know your exit routes, just in case.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind the Metro & Mountain Closures
If your workout involves navigating the concrete jungle, be aware that Metro's Purple Line extension construction is still chewing up Wilshire Boulevard. Expect sidewalk detours and lane reductions, especially around Beverly Hills. On the flip side, if you're a mountain goat, remember many popular trails in the Altadena front country and parts of Eaton Canyon remain closed until December 31, 2027, due to the 2025 Eaton Fire. Don't be "that guy" ignoring the closure signs. Find an open route – Chantry Flat and Switzer are good options for the most part. Also, Glendora Mountain Road and Glendora Ridge Road were closed until noon today due to mudslide concerns after recent storms, so verify their status before you head out there.
</AlertBox>

Enjoy the mostly clear skies, but keep your head on a swivel.
