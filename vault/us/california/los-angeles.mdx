Forget what your phone's current reading says; today in Los Angeles is a deceptively tricky day to push it outside, thanks to an unseasonably persistent heat that's still lingering and some old wounds on our trails.

<BulletList>
*   **Heatwave Hangover:** While the sun might feel "mainy clear" now at 16.5°C, don't let that fool you. We've been roasting the last couple of days with temperatures well into the 90s, a solid 20-30 degrees above normal for mid-March. The heat advisory for LA County was just in effect through yesterday, but the "prolonged March heatwave" isn't magically gone just because the sun dipped. Expect those temps to climb back up, especially inland, turning your casual jog into a full-on furnace workout.
*   **Pollen's Mild Annoyance:** Pollen levels are moderate. Not catastrophic, but enough to make those with sensitivities a bit sniffly.
*   **Persistent Trail Closures:** If you're hoping for a serene long run in the mountains, think again. Various sections of the Angeles National Forest, particularly around the areas hit by the Eaton Fire, are still shut down. We're talking about beloved spots like the Sam Merrill Trail and the Echo Mountain Trail, closed through *December 31, 2027*, for public safety and resource recovery. Don't try to sneak through; those closures are serious.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Unseasonable Heat Advisory in Effect (Unofficially)
Look, the weather data says 16.5°C, and maybe that's what it is *right this second* near the coast. But every meteorologist in town has been screaming about record-breaking March heat through yesterday and today, with inland areas hitting 90-100 degrees Fahrenheit. Seriously, "officials opened cooling centers and urged residents to drink a lot of water and avoid outdoor activities during the daytime." That's not for fun. Don't be a hero. Stay hydrated, plan your workout for the very early morning or late evening, and avoid peak sun hours. Your body will thank you later when you're not in the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Trails, Seriously
The Angeles National Forest is still recovering from the Eaton Fire. Many trails, including key segments of the Sam Merrill Trail and Echo Mountain Trail, are closed until late 2027. Seriously, don't ignore the signs or try to find a "shortcut." These closures are there to protect you and allow the ecosystem to heal. Find an open route or hit the pavement instead. The Marshall Canyon Trail also has temporary closures.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Check Local Construction Ahead of Time
While there aren't massive new city-wide construction alerts blocking major running routes in the last 24 hours, remember that LA is *always* under construction. If your route takes you near LAX or through specific street projects listed by LA County Public Works, do a quick check. Nobody wants to add dodging cones and detours to their sprint workout.
</AlertBox>
