<AlertBox type="warning">
Don't let the nice weather lull you into a false sense of security about all our local trails. Some of your favorite spots are *still* closed. Seriously, stop trying to sneak onto closed trails. You're just making more work for the rangers and potentially putting yourself in danger. Case in point: **Eaton Canyon Natural Area and its trails are still closed** due to fire recovery, and they're not scheduled to fully reopen until the end of 2027. And if you were planning to head out on Chaney Trail, which leads to Millard Canyon, that sucker is also still shut down for guardrail repairs from the Eaton fire. They say "this spring" for reopening, but we all know how LA County timelines work. Don't be shocked when you find a locked gate and have to turn your run into a very frustrated walk.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Construction is always a thing in this city, and it hasn't let up. If your usual urban routes take you anywhere near major Metro projects, especially around **Wilshire/La Brea or Wilshire/Fairfax**, expect detours and potential bottlenecks. These aren't just for cars; pedestrian access can be impacted too. Stay alert, watch for uneven surfaces, and assume no one in a vehicle sees you. Your morning jog shouldn't end with a run-in with a backhoe.
</AlertBox>
