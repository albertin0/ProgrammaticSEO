#### Heat Hangover is Real, People.
Even if today's temps are lower, the ground is dry, your body is likely still dehydrated from the "dangerously hot conditions" of the past week, and the "sustained hot and dry conditions threaten to accelerate wildfire risk." Don't try to make up for lost time with a personal best. Ease into it. Hydrate *before*, *during*, and *after*. And seriously, if you feel dizzy or nauseous, stop. Your ego isn't worth a trip to Cedars-Sinai.
</AlertBox>

#### Watch Out for the Sun
It's "Mainly Clear" out there, which means the sun isn't playing hide-and-seek. Even with cooler air, that L.A. sun still packs a punch. Wear a hat, slather on that SPF, and remember the advice to "avoid outdoor work between 10 a.m. and 5 p.m." unless you're actively trying to become beef jerky.
</AlertBox>

#### Pick Your Moments Wisely
If you're heading out to the trails, like the popular ones winding through Griffith Park, go early. And I mean *early*. Or wait until the sun is dipping low. You'll avoid the worst of the sun, and you'll likely still feel the effects of that lingering heatwave less acutely. Bring plenty of water – more than you think you need.
</AlertBox>
