## Your Lungs are Not a Smoke Filter

Today, the main health hazard isn't the sun, it's that damn smoke from Boyle Heights. What started days ago is still smoldering, and the advisory specifically warns against vigorous physical activity if you're in the impacted areas. "Stay indoors with windows and doors closed," they say. Don't be a hero trying to beat your PR through visible ash. It's not worth the bronchitis.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Smoke & Ash Advisory: Boyle Heights / East LA
A particle pollution advisory is active until at least 12:30 PM today for Boyle Heights, East Los Angeles, and potentially nearby foothill communities. Smoke and ash from a structure fire are present. **Avoid vigorous outdoor physical activity in these areas.**
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures? Still a Thing.

Thinking of escaping the urban haze for a mountain escape? Double-check before you go. The closures from past fires are still impacting our favorite trails.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Significant Trail Closures Remain
*   **Eaton Canyon and adjacent Angeles National Forest trails** are officially closed until at least December 31, 2027. This isn't just about fire recovery; there's also that charming "poodle-dog bush" that causes nasty skin reactions. Find somewhere else to hike.
*   **Marshall Canyon Trail** has intermittent weekday closures through June 2026 for oak tree pest management. Check their site before you head out, or you'll be doing an impromptu U-turn.
*   Parts of the **Santa Monica Mountains**, particularly the Topanga-area MRCA parks like Temescal Gateway Park, Tuna Canyon Park, and Sara Wan Trailhead at Corral Canyon, are still fully closed due to the Palisades Fire. The western parts, like around Agoura and Point Mugu, are generally open.
</AlertBox>

## Events Today: Run Smart

Despite the localized air issues, it's a big day for running events! The Run Juneteenth Day 5K/10K/13.1 is kicking off from Palisades Park in Santa Monica this morning, with waves starting early. <AlertBox type="info">
### Pollen & General Tips
Pollen levels are moderate. If you're sensitive, consider an antihistamine or stick to indoor workouts, especially with the added smoke. Always tell someone your route and expected return time, especially if you're venturing onto trails. And bring plenty of water, even on cooler days.
</AlertBox>
