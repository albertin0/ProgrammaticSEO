Today is a tale of two L.A.s, so choose your workout location wisely, or you might end up cooked.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

<BulletList>
*   **Heat Advisory for Inland Areas:** Don't let the coastal chill fool you; significant heat advisories are in effect for the valleys, mountains, and even inland coastal areas through Friday, with temperatures hitting up to 105°F in some spots.
*   **New Wildfire in LA County:** A fresh wildfire was reported yesterday in Los Angeles County, currently uncontained, so keep an eye out for localized smoke or official alerts if you're hitting the trails further inland.
*   **Moderate Pollen:** Allergy sufferers, you know the drill. It's not terrible, but it's enough to make you sniffle through those last few miles.
</BulletList>

Look, the ocean breeze might be kissing your face in Santa Monica right now, making that 16.5°C (61.7°F) feel glorious. But step away from the immediate coast, especially if you're headed to the San Fernando Valley or up towards the Angeles National Forest, and you're looking at a completely different beast. A heat advisory is in full swing for those inland regions, with the National Weather Service forecasting potentially dangerous heat, peaking today and tomorrow. They're talking triple digits out there, folks, which is a recipe for heat stroke, not PRs.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning (Inland L.A.):** If your workout takes you away from the immediate coast, be extremely cautious. Temperatures in the Santa Clarita, San Fernando, and San Gabriel valleys, as well as the San Gabriel Mountains, are expected to hit up to 105 degrees Fahrenheit through Friday. The risk of heat illness for anyone active outdoors is high.
</AlertBox>

On top of that, there was a new wildfire reported in Los Angeles County just yesterday. While the AQI is currently a pleasant 18, meaning the air quality isn't generally terrible, "uncontained" is not a word you want associated with a wildfire near your running route. Stay vigilant. If you smell smoke or see ash, turn around. Your lungs aren't designed to filter wildfire particulate matter, no matter how good your stride is.

And for those of you who were planning a long trek into the mountains, remember that a chunk of the Angeles National Forest, including many trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas, remains closed until December 31, 2027, due to the Eaton Fire. Don't be "that guy" ignoring closure signs for a selfie. It's for your safety, and frankly, to let the ecosystem heal.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ocean Water Warning:** If you're thinking of a post-run dip, remember that there's an Ocean Water Use Warning for several Los Angeles County beaches due to elevated bacteria levels, issued July 1st. Maybe just admire the waves from a distance for now.
</AlertBox>

Construction-wise, nothing major directly impacting trails today. Road closures are happening on SR-91 further east and around LAX later this month, but those are more a headache for your commute than your morning jog. But seriously, inland runners, stay hydrated, dress light, and if you feel woozy, stop. No workout is worth ending up in the ER.
