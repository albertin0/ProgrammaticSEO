## The Rundown: Don't Be a Hero, It's Just Monday

Look, I get it. We love our sunshine. But today's one of those days where the sun is trying to kill your workout. The National Weather Service isn't messing around with these Heat Advisories, and neither should you. If you're out there between 10 AM and 8 PM, you're looking at conditions that increase your risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Hydration is non-negotiable, and seriously, consider moving your strenuous activity to the early morning or evening. Or, you know, hit the gym with AC. Nobody's judging your treadmill miles today.

### Local Trail & Road Issues

Good news on the major freeway front: the 405 Freeway repaving through the Sepulveda Pass officially concluded this morning. So, at least your drive *to* the heat might be slightly smoother. However, the saga continues on other routes. If you're heading east, expect overnight connector and lane closures on SR-91 from Cerritos to Gardena starting today, Monday, July 27th, through Friday, July 31st, for sign rehabilitation. Plan accordingly if your commute involves that stretch.

Now, for those of you who prefer dirt over asphalt, some bad news, though it's not new. Trails in Eaton Canyon and the surrounding areas are *still* closed. We're talking the Altadena Crest Trail, Chaney Trail, and Eaton Canyon Trail itself, all shut down until December 31, 2027, to allow the land to recover from that January 2025 fire. Chaney Trail Road is also closed to vehicles until Fall 2026. So, if your usual scenic hike involves that area, find a new route or prepare for a lengthy bike-in.
