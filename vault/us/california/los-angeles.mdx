## The City's on Edge, and So Should You Be

Seriously, this isn't some casual breezy day for a run along the Backbone Trail. The first major Santa Ana wind event of the season is upon us, and it’s cranking up the fire risk to "critical." Experts are warning that any new fire could spread rapidly. Rapidly. Think about that. You might start your run enjoying the view, and end it in a mandatory evacuation zone.

### What's Closed? Basically Anywhere You'd Want to Go.

Due to the extreme fire weather, if you were planning on hitting any of the amazing spots in the Santa Monica Mountains, like Franklin Canyon Park or Will Rogers State Historic Park, forget about it. They're closed. And this isn't some temporary inconvenience for a film shoot; this is about public safety and preventing a massive inferno. The Puente Hills Habitat Authority Preserve is also locked down, so don't even try those trails.

### Other Random Headaches

While the air quality is currently "Good" with an AQI of 18, don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. If a fire breaks out, that number will shoot through the roof faster than you can say "mandatory evacuation." Southern California Edison is even talking about Public Safety Power Shutoffs (PSPS) for hundreds of thousands of customers, which means traffic lights could go out and communication might be spotty. Not ideal for finding your way home after getting lost on a closed trail.

Also, just a heads-up if you're in the Los Feliz area: a sinkhole swallowed a Spectrum van on Normandie Avenue yesterday due to a water main break. It's localized, but just another reminder that LA always has something trying to trip you up. And downtown? Not a hazard, but definitely a disruption if you were planning a downtown stroll.
