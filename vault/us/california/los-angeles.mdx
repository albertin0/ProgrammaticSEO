Today, July 29th, is a bit of a Jekyll and Hyde situation for outdoor workouts. This morning, we're enjoying some blessedly clear skies and a comfortable 16.5°C (61.7°F). It's almost *nice*. Almost. Your lungs are getting a break with an AQI of 18, so no excuses about the air being thick with... whatever it usually is. Just keep in mind that "Ozone/Other" is still the dominant pollutant, even at low levels. Don't go sucking down lungfuls like it's mountain air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Looming Heat Wave & Coastal Calamity
Look, today's cool weather is a fleeting dream. An Extreme Heat Warning kicks in Friday and runs through Sunday for much of southwest California, including our coastal ranges and valleys. We're talking 95 to 110 degrees Fahrenheit. If you're planning a long run or hike Friday through Sunday, reschedule. Seriously. Hydration won't save you if you're out there cooking.
</AlertBox>

If you were thinking of swapping pavement for waves, think again. Our lifeguards had a wild weekend, pulling nearly 2,000 people out of the ocean. Rip currents and powerful shorebreak waves are still a thing through today. So, unless you're an actual fish or have a death wish, maybe skip the ocean swim at Santa Monica Bay this afternoon. Stick to the sand, if you must be near the water.

Now, let's talk trails. **Griffith Park** is mostly open for business, offering its usual mix of paved roads and fire trails. Remember, no smoking, no open fires, and keep your dogs on a leash, because yes, there are coyotes. But if you’re thinking about those more rugged climbs, know that the Angeles National Forest has elevated its fire danger level to "VERY HIGH" as of July 24th. It's not just a sign, people; it's a reminder of what this dry heat can do.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Trail Closures & Fire Danger
If Eaton Canyon is your go-to, you're still out of luck. Much of it, including the Altadena Crest Trail and the main Eaton Canyon Trail, remains closed for fire recovery until at least December 31, 2027, thanks to the 2025 fire. And in the Angeles National Forest, areas like Williamson Rock and the San Dimas Experimental Forest are also shut down. Don't be *that* person who blows past closure signs. You're not just risking a fine, you're risking a lot more than that in these conditions.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the moderate pollen means you won't be completely drowning in sneezes, but for those with allergies, consider your usual meds before you head out.
