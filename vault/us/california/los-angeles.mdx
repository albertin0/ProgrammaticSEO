### Stay Safe Out There (Or In Here)

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Ozone Alert for Valleys:** If you're anywhere in the San Fernando, Santa Clarita, or San Gabriel Valleys, the ozone levels are no joke today. Skip any vigorous outdoor activity, especially in the afternoon and evening. Your respiratory system will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Advisory:** Despite the seemingly mild early morning, temperatures will climb significantly. The National Weather Service is urging folks to limit outdoor activity to the cooler parts of the day. This isn't just about feeling sweaty; heat-related illness is a serious risk in LA County, accounting for a chunk of ER visits on hot days.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Trail Closures:** Don't even think about hitting the trails at Stough Canyon or Wildwood Canyon today. They are officially closed due to excessive heat. Respect the closures; they're there for your safety (and to keep first responders from having to drag you off a mountain). Also, the Angeles National Forest still has a "VERY HIGH" fire danger level.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Chaos:** Keep an eye on local alerts for specific construction zones. Metro's D Line extension is still a thing around Wilshire and La Cienega, with potential lane and even full road closures this month. If you usually run or bike near LAX, expect snarls on Century Blvd, Sepulveda Blvd, and 98th St due to ongoing roadway work. Plan alternative routes if you can't be bothered with dodging cones.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Personal Safety:** A woman was recently assaulted in Valley Village after being followed home from a gym. This is a grim reminder to always be aware of your surroundings, especially when running or walking alone. Consider varying your routes and times, and if something feels off, trust your gut.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, today is a prime candidate for an indoor workout, or at least a very gentle stroll in a well-ventilated, air-conditioned space. The mountains aren't going anywhere, and neither are your lungs, hopefully.
