## Your Daily Dose of Reality: LA Running Edition

Alright, let's cut to the chase. The air quality in *your immediate bubble* is stellar, sitting pretty at an AQI of 18 with no major pollutants causing a fuss. That 16.5°C you're feeling? Pure bliss. Go enjoy it, but don't be naive. We're still in Los Angeles, which means hazards are always lurking, usually just over the next ridge or freeway.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfires Are Raging in LA County
Just because your backyard isn't smoking doesn't mean the county is clear. Multiple new wildfires were reported in Los Angeles County on June 12, 2026, with no known containment status yet. We're heading into what's predicted to be a high wildfire risk period for the rest of June. If you're planning on hitting trails in the Santa Monica Mountains or further inland, keep your eyes on the horizon and check fire maps before you leave. Smoke travels, even if it hasn't smacked your local air monitor yet.
</AlertBox>

### Know Your Local Hot Spots (Literally)
While your local thermostat might be showing a runner's dream temperature, large swaths of Southern California are still dealing with a significant heatwave. Yesterday, LA reportedly hit a record-breaking 110°F in some areas. Even today, the National Weather Service forecasts inland valleys hitting up to 93°F and deserts well over 100°F. So, if your long run takes you from the coast to, say, the Valley or beyond, pack extra water and adjust your expectations. This city is a microclimate maze, and what feels good by the beach can be brutal inland.

### Trail Closures & City Bustle
Speaking of trails, several are still giving us headaches. The Marshall Canyon Trail has intermittent closures for pest management, and Eaton Canyon Natural Area and its trails are still shut down for environmental recovery. Also, parts of the Rancho Palos Verdes Nature Preserve trails, including segments of the Catalina Trail and the Abalone Cove Reserve, are closed due to ongoing landslide activity. Don't just show up expecting open gates; do your homework.

And if you're anywhere near the Third Street Promenade, be advised: the Santa Monica Block Fest is in full swing today, June 13, 2026, transforming those blocks into a massive party. Planning a run through downtown Santa Monica? Expect crowds, detours, and general festive chaos. Good luck weaving through that.
