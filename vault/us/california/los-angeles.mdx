### Environmental Rundown: No Excuses, Mostly

Look, it's L.A. We usually get served up a cocktail of smog and sun. Today, though? The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 18, which is basically "go outside and don't die" territory. The dominant pollutant is listed as "Ozone/Other," which sounds vague, but at this level, it's not going to melt your face off. You've got a comfortable 16.5°C (that's 62°F for the Fahrenheit fanatics) to start your day, warming up to a lovely 28°C (83°F) later. Perfect weather for getting those miles in without feeling like you're running through a hot yoga studio.

However, it's not all sunshine and rainbows. We've got "Moderate" pollen levels, which for some of you means a runny nose and itchy eyes. My fellow hay fever sufferers, you know the drill – pre-dose your meds or stick to the treadmill. And for anyone doing their morning miles on the east side, specifically near **Boyle Heights**, there's still a smoke advisory in effect from that storage facility fire back in June. Your lungs might not appreciate a deep breath of that.

### Local Hazards: Rock N' Roll and Closed Gates

Now for the real local intel. If your Saturday morning tradition involves circling the **Rose Bowl Recreational Loop** in Pasadena, abandon ship. Guns N' Roses is in town, and they've shut down the Recreational Loop from 9:30 AM today until 1 AM Sunday for their concert. So, unless you fancy dodging roadies and concert-goers, find another route. The Equestrian Loop is apparently still open, but good luck navigating that with an angry Axl Rose soundtrack.

**Rose Bowl Recreational Loop CLOSED!** Don't even think about it. Find an alternate route today, or prepare for disappointment and potential security pat-downs.

On the park front, while many of our beloved spots like Runyon Canyon are open again, a few remain stubbornly shut. **George Wolfberg Park at Potrero Canyon**, **Palisades Park & Recreation Center**, and **Wattles Garden Park** are still closed until further notice by the City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks. Always check the city's parks department site before heading out, unless you enjoy running into locked gates.

<AlertBox type="info">

And for those beach runners, a heads-up that dangerous beach conditions were noted for the Labor Day weekend period, so keep an eye on current surf reports if you're hitting the sand.
</AlertBox>
