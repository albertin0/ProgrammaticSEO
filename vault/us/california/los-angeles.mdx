## Today's Rundown: Sweat, Snot, and Closed Trails

Seriously, Los Angeles, it's March 12th, and we're already talking about temperatures pushing into the 90s in places like the San Gabriel Valley. This isn't your cool spring morning jog weather; this is "bake an egg on the sidewalk" territory. The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory, and Cal/OSHA is already telling employers to protect workers from heat illness. That means *you* need to protect yourself, because your boss probably isn't watching your 5k.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory
Temperatures are forecast to reach the 90s inland and mid-80s near the coast today. This isn't just uncomfortable, it's dangerous. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, consider moving your workout indoors or to the very early morning/late evening. "Pace yourself" isn't a suggestion, it's a command.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, our air quality is actually quite good today, hovering at an AQI of 18, with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant. So at least you won't be choking on smog *and* heat. [cite: The provided data] However, pollen is sitting at a moderate level, so if you're prone to seasonal allergies, you might feel some nasal drip or itchy eyes. Keep those tissues handy.

### WARNING: Persistent Trail Closures
Don't plan on hitting some of your favorite mountain trails without checking first. Many areas, particularly those affected by the 2025 Eaton and Palisades fires, remain closed for public safety and environmental recovery. Specifically, portions of Eaton Canyon Trail, Altadena Crest Trail, and roads leading to them (like Chaney Trail Road for vehicles until spring 2026), are still off-limits. Mount Baldy trails also have extended closures through March 20, 2026. Seriously, respect the signs – that terrain is no joke, and rescues are a drain on resources.
</AlertBox>
