## The Good, The Bad, and The Closed

Look, Los Angeles has some killer spots, and the weather today is doing us a solid. We're looking at a high of 24°C (75°F) and clear skies, no rain in sight. Ideal, right? Except, as usual, you gotta know where you're going and what to avoid before you lace up those shoes.

### Mind Your Lungs

The air quality, generally, is decent, sitting at a "Good" AQI of 18. However, for those of you with respiratory issues, particularly if you're sensitive to ozone, there's a minor note of caution. The South Coast Air Quality Management District sometimes flags an "acceptable" AQI as still having "moderate health concern for a very small number of people who are unusually sensitive to air pollution" when ozone or other pollutants are dominant. So, if you're prone to lung irritation, listen to your body and maybe stick to lower-intensity stuff, or keep it shorter than usual.

Also, "Moderate" pollen isn't going to kill anyone, but it's enough to make a good run miserable if your allergies are flaring. You know who you are. Pre-medicate, or stick to a treadmill if your nose decides to stage a revolt.

### Trail Updates & Roadblocks

This is where things get tricky, because while the skies are open, a lot of our favorite trails simply aren't.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Angeles National Forest & Eaton Canyon Closures: Seriously, don't ignore these.**
Many trails in Eaton Canyon and the surrounding Angeles National Forest remain closed until **December 31, 2027**, due to damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. This isn't some minor detour; we're talking about extensive closures that include the Altadena Crest Trail, Altadena Crest Trail Connector, Chaney Trail, and the main Eaton Canyon Trail. The Sam Merrill, Echo Mountain, Mount Lowe, Idlehour, and Castle Canyon trails are also out of bounds. Henninger Flats is closed too. Trying to skirt these closures is dumb, dangerous, and can land you a hefty fine. Just stay out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Bike Path Construction Near Whittier Narrows Dam**
If you're a cyclist who regularly uses the San Gabriel River or Rio Hondo Bike Paths, be aware that sections are closed for *years* due to "high urgency" renovations on the Whittier Narrows Dam. These closures started around October 1st and are expected to last until 2033. Finding clear detours can be a pain, so plan your ride well in advance or pick an entirely different route.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Agoura Road Wildlife Crossing Project**
Cyclists and pedestrians in the Agoura Hills area, specifically near the 101 Freeway, need to know about the ongoing closure of Agoura Road between Rondell Street and Hydepark Drive. This closure is for the Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing construction and restricts all through traffic (motorists, cyclists, pedestrians) Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM, until November 30, 2026. If you're used to using this stretch, you'll need to find an alternative.
</AlertBox>

No active Red Flag Warnings for the Santa Monica Mountains are currently in effect, which means those areas should generally be open, but always double-check with the managing agency. Remember, they enacted a new coordinated closure protocol for high fire risk days after the 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires.

In short: great weather for a run, but if your usual stomping grounds are in the hills or along the rivers, do your homework first. Stick to open routes, be smart about your breathing, and enjoy this clear-skied day.
