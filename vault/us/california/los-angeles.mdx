### But Wait, There's Always a Catch (or Two)

While the air is great and the weather is playing nice, it wouldn't be LA without a few caveats.

First up, that "Moderate" pollen level. It's not going to ruin your run for most folks, but if you're one of the many who sniffle and sneeze their way through spring, be prepared. Pop an antihistamine if you're prone to it, or stick to shorter routes where you can bail if your allergies decide to make a dramatic appearance.

Second, wildfires. We saw reports of new wildfires breaking out in Los Angeles County as recently as May 27th, including the Jaspar Fire burning on National Park Service land. While the air quality readings today indicate no widespread smoke impact right now, these fires are fresh. Always keep an eye on local news, especially if you're heading out to trails closer to federal or private land where these blazes were reported. Fire season is always a gamble out here.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Recent Wildfires:** New fires were reported in LA County yesterday. While currently not impacting widespread air quality, stay vigilant, especially if you're venturing near areas like the Santa Monica Mountains where past fires have led to extensive trail closures.
</AlertBox>

Finally, let's talk about where you're actually going to run. Some of our favorite spots are still a mess from past events or ongoing projects. Don't expect to cruise along the entire **LA River Path** through Elysian Valley without hitting a detour. There's ongoing construction for new bridges and the much-hyped "gap closure" project has been delayed indefinitely. Check LADOT's site for specific closures if that's your usual route. Also, **Eaton Canyon Natural Area and Trails** remain closed for public safety and environmental recovery, so don't plan on heading up there today either.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Trail Closures:** Multiple segments of the LA River Path are currently closed for construction. Eaton Canyon Natural Area and Trails are also closed. Plan your route accordingly and check for current detour information before you leave the house.
</AlertBox>
