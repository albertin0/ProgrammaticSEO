## The Rundown: Don't Trip, LA

Look, it's Los Angeles, so there's always *something* trying to mess with your workout. Today, the biggest headaches are, as usual, traffic and ongoing closures, rather than mother nature herself.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Watch Your Step on Santa Monica Blvd!
  If your route takes you through West LA, specifically **Santa Monica Boulevard near Wellesley Avenue**, be warned. Caltrans is doing bus pad work, which means eastbound and westbound lanes will be reduced to one lane starting tonight (Saturday, May 2) at 9 p.m. and running until 11 a.m. Sunday, May 3. Expect delays and definitely no street parking in that immediate area. This is part of a larger, multi-segment project, so if you regularly run or bike along SR-2 between Santa Monica and Echo Park, keep checking for updates.
</AlertBox>

### Freeway Frustrations

If you're driving to your favorite trail, the I-405 near Sepulveda Pass is still a mess. The northbound freeway is down to three lanes between Skirball Center Drive and Ventura Boulevard until Monday morning. And if you're hoping for clear sailing through Beverly Hills or Century City, remember the Metro D Line Subway extension is still under construction in those areas, so anticipate potential lane reductions and sidewalk changes.

### Trail Talk: Still Closed

Don't even think about hitting those familiar trails in **Eaton Canyon** or parts of the Angeles National Forest. Areas around Eaton Canyon, including the Altadena Crest Trail and the paved/unpaved sections of Chaney Trail, remain closed until the end of 2027 due to the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Seriously, stay out. They're closed for a reason—public safety and ecosystem recovery. Similarly, the Williamson Rock Area in the Angeles National Forest is also off-limits through December 2026. Find another loop, folks.
