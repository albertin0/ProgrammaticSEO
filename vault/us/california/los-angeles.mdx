Unless you're planning a very short, very early splash in a pool, today is a hard pass for any serious outdoor sweat session in Los Angeles.

Look, I get it. We live in a city that practically demands you be outside. But today, July 6th, the city's basically a giant convection oven. We're looking at temperatures hitting 95°F (35°C) in much of the city, and if you're out in the San Fernando or San Gabriel Valleys, expect that mercury to kiss 100°F (38°C). Inland areas like Riverside and San Bernardino are projected to be even hotter, with Palm Springs possibly seeing 113°F (45°C). And don't even think about catching a break tonight; those temps aren't dropping much past 70°F (21°C), making recovery a joke. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's straight-up dangerous for your heart, your muscles, and your brain. Dehydration isn't a "maybe" today; it's a "when and how bad."

The environmental data shows a moderate pollen count, which is annoying but not the main event. What's more critical is the Air Quality Index. While the AQI currently sits at a "good" 18, and the dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, remember that yesterday's Fourth of July fireworks combined with this oppressive heat were already making for "hazardous levels" of air pollution in parts of the county. Heat also kicks ozone production into high gear. So, that "good" reading could be misleading depending on your exact location and what time you're out there huffing and puffing. Add to that the elevated wildfire risk we're facing with these dry, scorching conditions, and it means the air could turn south quickly.

And for those of you eyeing a scenic run, think again. Much of Eaton Canyon and its surrounding trails, which were ravaged by the 2025 fire, are still closed through at least December 2027 for recovery. That means no relief in the shade of those canyons. Even closer to the city, the West Trail of Runyon Canyon is also closed indefinitely. So, your usual go-to spots might be less available than you think.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Advisory: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours!**
Temperatures are hitting dangerous highs today, especially away from the immediate coast. Seriously, pushing yourself outside in this heat is a recipe for heatstroke. Hydrate *religiously* if you absolutely must be out, but better yet, find an air-conditioned gym or a pool. This is not the day to PR your long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Localized Air Quality Dips & Fire Danger**
Even with a decent current AQI, the combination of post-fireworks residue, ozone from the heat, and elevated wildfire risk means air quality can be volatile. If you smell smoke or feel any respiratory irritation, cut your workout short. Keep an eye on local alerts, especially if you're near brush.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Trail Closures are Ongoing**
Don't waste gas driving to Eaton Canyon expecting a trail run. Significant portions are closed for rehabilitation until 2027. The West Trail at Runyon Canyon is also closed. Check official park websites before you head out to avoid disappointment and unsafe conditions.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, Angelenos, listen to your body today. Your joints and lungs will thank you for taking it easy, or at least taking it indoors.
