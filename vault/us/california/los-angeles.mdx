<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heat Wave Incoming – Act Like It's Serious
The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning out, starting today and going through Monday evening for much of Southern California. While our current 16.5°C (61.7°F) feels lovely, those inland areas are going to bake, with a moderate heat risk developing. This means your usual mid-day run in the Valley or even heading out past 9 AM could turn into a serious medical emergency if you're not prepared. Heat illness is no joke, and emergency services are already warning about it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Agoura Road Shut Down for Wildlife (and You)
If your route includes Agoura Road between Rondell Street and Hydepark Drive in Agoura Hills, tough luck. That stretch is fully closed to *all* traffic – motorists, cyclists, and pedestrians – weekdays from 7 AM to 5 PM until July 1, 2026, for the Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing construction. They're building a bridge for mountain lions, which is cool, but it means your morning ride or walk is officially rerouted. Don't even think about trying to "sneak through."
</AlertBox>

### Persistent Trail Closures

Remember that Eaton Fire from way back when? The closures around that burn scar in the Angeles National Forest, including parts of **Eaton Canyon Trail**, Altadena Crest Trail, and sections of the Mt. Wilson Toll Road, are *still* in effect until December 31, 2027. Don't be that person ignoring the "CLOSED" signs and risking a rescue. Those areas need to heal, and you need to stay safe. Also, Chaney Trail Road leading into Millard Canyon is still closed for guardrail repairs until "Spring 2026." "Spring" can be a flexible term in government-speak, so assume it's still messed up.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Game Plan for Today: Be Smart, Not Just Enthusiastic
1.  **Embrace the Early Bird:** Seriously, get out there before the sun really starts doing its thing. If you're not done by 9 AM, you're asking for trouble, especially inland.
2.  **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** And then hydrate some more. Carry more water than you think you need.
3.  **Stick to the Coast (If Possible):** If you absolutely must work out later in the day, the beaches and coastal areas will be significantly cooler thanks to the marine layer.
4.  **Know Your Route:** Check for current closures before you head out. A quick search on LA County Public Works or trail-specific sites can save you a wasted trip and potential fines.
5.  **Listen to Your Body:** If you feel lightheaded, dizzy, or excessively tired, STOP. It's not weakness, it's common sense.
</AlertBox>

Bottom line: Today is great for your lungs, but potentially dangerous for your core body temperature. Pick your battles and respect the heat.
