The overall AQI for Los Angeles today is 18, which is in the "Good" range. The dominant pollutant listed is Ozone/Other. This sounds great, right? Almost too good. Here's the catch: While the general AQI is low, we've had reports of new wildfires kicking off in Los Angeles County as recently as yesterday, May 22nd. Plus, a smoke advisory was just lifted, indicating earlier problems. So, while the broad numbers are fine, keep your eyes peeled for any local haze or the smell of smoke, especially if you're closer to the hills or the newer burn areas.

<AlertBox type="warning">

This is where things get tricky, folks.

### Road and Trail Closures

It's the Los Angeles Marathon today! That means significant freeway ramp closures and road closures downtown, impacting anyone trying to navigate or run through those areas, especially earlier this morning. Good luck trying to get around that mess if you didn't plan ahead.

Beyond the marathon chaos, LA County Public Works is reporting a whopping 38 road closures, with 10 more planned. Roads like East Fork and Glendora Mountain Road are closed until May 26th due to "Public Safety - Holiday Weekend". That's right, bureaucracy keeping you off the good stuff.
