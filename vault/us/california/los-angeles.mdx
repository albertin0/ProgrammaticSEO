## Current Workout Hazards

### Heat

<AlertBox type="warning">
  While the extreme heat warnings from this past weekend have officially expired for much of Southern California, including the coastal areas, our inland valleys and desert communities might still be feeling the residual heat. Even if the temps dropped, a multi-day heat event takes a toll. Stay hydrated, even if it feels cool, and watch for signs of heat exhaustion, especially if you're pushing yourself harder than usual after a few days off.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures & Fire Danger

The Angeles National Forest is still under a "VERY HIGH" fire danger level. This means dry brush is primed to burn. While Stough Canyon and Wildwood Canyon trails in Burbank were specifically closed due to excessive heat over the weekend, and those specific closures *may* have lifted, the underlying fire risk is real.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  The Angeles National Forest has ongoing closures. The Rincon-Redbox Road is shut down due to an impassable rock slide, and the Williamson Rock area is closed through the end of 2026 for resource protection. Don't be "that guy" who ignores closure signs. A rock slide isn't going to care how many miles you've logged this week.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Carnage

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Major construction is kicking off, so if your route involves main arteries, adjust. Beyond the I-405 Sepulveda Pass chaos, you've got overnight lane closures on SR-91 from Cerritos to Gardena this week, and significant roadway work around LAX on Century Blvd. Pedestrians and cyclists, this means detours and extra vigilance, especially during those early morning dark hours.
</AlertBox>
