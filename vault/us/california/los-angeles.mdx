Look, I know 15.5°C (60°F) and overcast skies feel like a gift from the running gods in LA. And yeah, an AQI of 18 is practically unheard of. Breathe deep, runners, because your lungs are about to have a fantastic day. But don't get complacent. This isn't your average "just throw on some shorts" kind of morning.

### Coastal Runs? Think Twice.

If your usual route involves the sand or the bike path down by the **Santa Monica Beach Path**, you might want to reroute. We're in the thick of king tides right now, and the water levels are, frankly, ridiculous. Preliminary data from NOAA shows LA experiencing some of the highest coastal water levels in over a century this week, with the San Pedro gauge reporting an all-time record of 8.02 feet recently. That means your picturesque beach run could turn into an impromptu swim, or at the very least, a muddy, slippery slog through standing seawater.

<AlertBox type="danger">
    **Coastal Flood Warning!** Avoid low-lying beach areas, bike paths, and harbor walkways. Significant coastal flooding is happening today and through Friday, leading to inundated areas and potential beach erosion. Stay away from the water's edge.
</AlertBox>

### What About the Trails?

For those of you looking to hit the hills, the air quality is superb, which is great. However, if you're thinking of a peaceful hike in **Eaton Canyon** or parts of the Angeles National Forest, remember that many areas remain closed through December 31, 2027, due to the 2025 Eaton Fire. This includes popular spots like the Sam Merrill Trail, Echo Mountain Trail, and Mount Lowe Trails. Don't be that person ignoring the closure signs. They're there for a reason – resource recovery and your safety.

<AlertBox type="warning">
    **Watch Your Step – Rain is Coming!** Rain chances increase tonight and through the weekend, especially for areas like Pasadena. Wet leaves, loose dirt, and slick pavement are a recipe for ankle sprains and nasty falls. Choose your footwear wisely and reduce your pace.
</AlertBox>

And if you're driving to your trailhead, be aware of the perpetual construction. There's an overnight closure on the I-5 North and 134 West interchange near Griffith Park Ferraro Fields tonight, which will impact access. Plus, Metro's D Line extension continues to snarl traffic and sidewalks on Wilshire, specifically between Stanley Drive and San Vicente Boulevard. Plan for delays.

### The Good News (and a History Lesson)

Your lungs, as mentioned, are getting a break from the usual LA crud. The dominant pollutant is "Ozone/Other," but with an AQI of 18, it's barely a blip. Enjoy the clean air while it lasts.

<AlertBox type="info">
    **Pollen Report:** While coastal flooding is the headline, if you've got seasonal allergies, remember pollen levels are moderate. Keep your meds handy, especially for longer outdoor sessions.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of air quality, a new study dropped today revealing that the 2025 Eaton Fire in Altadena exposed millions to alarmingly high levels of toxic metals like lead and arsenic in the smoke plume. While this isn't an *active* fire issue today, it's a stark reminder that "wildfire smoke" isn't just irritating; urban fires burning structures release a whole different cocktail of nasty stuff. Something to keep in the back of your mind as fire season eventually returns.
