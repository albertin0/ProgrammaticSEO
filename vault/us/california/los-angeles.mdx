## Today's Breakdown: Get Out, But Be Smart

Temperature-wise, we're looking at a mild 16.1°C (about 61°F) under partly cloudy skies, which is pretty comfortable for getting some miles in. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a completely clear day for all activities.

### Air Quality Concerns

The reported AQI of 18 sounds great on paper, but a Wind Advisory was in effect for Southern California recently, bringing with it the risk of elevated dust and dirt. More importantly, with several new wildfires reported across Los Angeles County over the last 24 hours, the South Coast Air Quality Management District (AQMD) has a standing advisory: if you smell smoke or see ash, take precautions, even if the general AQI map shows "green." Those invisible-to-the-sensor particles are still rough on your lungs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Wildfire Smoke & Ash Risk:** While the general AQI is low, active wildfires in Los Angeles County mean localized smoke and windblown ash are possible. If you smell smoke or see ash, limit your outdoor exertion, especially if you have respiratory sensitivities.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures in Effect

Planning a run or hike through the canyons? Think again. The 2025 Eaton Fire left its mark, and many beloved routes within Eaton Canyon and the Angeles National Forest remain closed for public safety and environmental recovery. This includes trails around the Altadena area like sections of the Altadena Crest Trail and the unpaved Chaney Trail, some closures extending through December 31, 2027. Seriously, respect the barriers. They're there for a reason. Williamson Rock area in the Angeles National Forest is also closed until December 2026 for resource protection.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Long-Term Trail Closures:** Multiple trails and roads in Eaton Canyon and Angeles National Forest, including Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail, are closed due to past wildfire damage. Attempting to access these areas is unsafe and could result in fines. Check official LA County Parks and Forest Service alerts before you head out.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen and the Rest

Pollen is at a moderate level, which for many means a bit of congestion but shouldn't derail your entire workout. Hydrate well, and maybe keep an antihistamine handy if you're prone to seasonal allergies.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Commute:** Metrolink service is suspended on the Orange County and Inland Empire-Orange County lines today for track work. If you rely on it to get to your workout spot, plan alternate transportation.
</AlertBox>

In short: Stick to known-open paths, keep an eye (and nose) out for smoke, and enjoy the mild weather responsibly.
