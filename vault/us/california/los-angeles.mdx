## Trail Talk: Where You Can (And Can't) Legally Bust Your Ass

Okay, let's talk trails, because this is where a lot of you go wrong. Just because it's sunny doesn't mean the trail fairies magically reopened everything.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER ZONE: Eaton Canyon & Angeles National Forest Closures**

Listen up. Huge sections of Eaton Canyon and the Angeles National Forest are *still* closed. We're talking trails damaged by the January 2025 Eaton Fire. These aren't temporary "oops, a little mud" closures; they're in effect until **December 31, 2027**, for public safety and ecological recovery. Don't be a hero. Stay off the Altadena Crest Trail, the main Eaton Canyon Trail, Mount Wilson Toll Road, and many others in that burn scar. They mean it. There are active dangers from unstable ground, rockfalls, and erosion. You could get seriously hurt, and then someone has to risk *their* neck to get your dumb ass out.
</AlertBox>

Want to know which roads are closed for *vehicular traffic* but open to hikers/bikers in that area? Chaney Trail Road, which leads to Sunset Ridge Trail and Millard Canyon, is one such example, with closures lasting into Fall 2026 for some segments. Know before you go, and plan to hoof it if you want to access those parts.

Now, for happier news. If you're looking for open spaces with minimal drama, Griffith Park is generally looking good. Sierra Club even has night conditioning hikes scheduled there tonight. Just watch out for a few overgrown spots or ruts from past rains, which are typical. But generally, the dirt and fire roads drain quickly, making for solid footing.

## Commute to Your Workout? Expect Delays.

If your workout spot involves driving, especially on a freeway, you might hit some snags. Caltrans is doing a bunch of overnight pavement rehabilitation work on I-5 in Castaic, I-605, and State Route 2 (Santa Monica Boulevard). These closures are happening from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m. in some areas, so if you're an early bird or a night owl, check your route. Also, keep an eye on LA County Public Works for various construction closures on smaller roads, like Piuma Road for pole replacement today until 4 p.m. And don't even get me started on the ongoing mess around LAX; if you're running near Century Blvd, expect lane restrictions through mid-November.

Bottom line: The air is good, the temps are great. Go run, ride, or hike. Just for the love of all that's holy, respect the damn trail closures and watch out for traffic cones if you're driving to get there.
