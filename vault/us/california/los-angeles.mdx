## The Rundown: What's Out There Today

### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

While it's not "apocalyptic red sky" bad, that AQI of 67 is firmly in the moderate zone. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which means tiny particles are floating around. Think about those lung-burning particulates you get from vehicle exhaust mixed with a whisper of distant wildfire smoke. Speaking of, CAL FIRE reported a new wildfire in Los Angeles County today, September 20th. Plus, the "Palos" wildfire, which started back on September 14th in LA County, is still 80% contained. It's not a raging inferno, but any active fire adds to the atmospheric soup. If you're planning an intense session, maybe dial it back or opt for an indoor track. Your lungs will thank you.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **AQI is Moderate (67 PM2.5).** If you're sensitive or have respiratory issues, consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. A new wildfire was reported in LA County today, adding to potential smoke.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Conditions & Closures: Still Dealing with Old Scars

If your usual Sunday long run takes you up into the foothills, listen up. The trails around **Eaton Canyon** and parts of the Angeles National Forest are *still* a mess from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. I'm talking indefinite closures for public safety and environmental recovery, lasting until December 31, 2027. This includes popular routes like the Altadena Crest Trail and Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Don't be that person hopping fences for an Instagram pic. It's dangerous, and frankly, you're disrespecting the recovery efforts.

Also, for those who frequent the Whittier Narrows Recreation Area, ongoing construction for the Whittier Narrows Dam safety project is expected to cause trail and park closures until 2033. Always check local park and trail websites *before* you head out.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Eaton Canyon & Angeles National Forest Trail Closures:** Trails like Altadena Crest Trail and Eaton Canyon Trail remain closed until at least December 31, 2027, due to the 2025 Eaton Fire damage. Fines can be steep for ignoring these.
</AlertBox>

### Weather: Mellow for Now, but Don't Get Complacent

The overcast conditions and 19°C (66°F) temperature are pretty decent for a run. However, don't forget it's still September in LA. While today is mild, the region has seen heat advisories and warnings recently, with forecasts for the LAX area today still expecting highs around 76-77°F (24-25°C). Even on an "overcast" day, UV can be a factor.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Even with the mild weather, it's still Los Angeles. Carry water, especially if you're doing longer distances or pushing the pace.
</AlertBox>
