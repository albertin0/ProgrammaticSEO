## Today's Local Grind

Look, Los Angeles rarely gives us a completely clear run, and today is no different. While the big picture looks good, some very localized issues might throw a wrench in your plans.

### Traffic & Construction Headaches

There are always road closures in this city, and yesterday brought a fresh crop. A water main break on **San Vicente Boulevard** between Sixth Street and Lindenhurst Avenue in Beverly Grove is causing localized flooding and closures. Seriously, watch out for that. You don't want to turn your morning jog into an impromptu swim.

Caltrans is also still at it with various overnight and ongoing lane closures, notably along **Topanga Canyon Boulevard** for Palisades Fire repairs through June 14th, so expect delays there if you're heading that way. Same goes for stretches of SR-91, I-5, and I-405, mostly during evening and overnight hours, but that can spill over into your early morning commute to a trail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Localized Flooding & Road Closures:** Avoid San Vicente Boulevard in Beverly Grove due to a recent water main break. Check Caltrans and LA County Public Works sites for ongoing lane closures on major routes and local streets, especially Topanga Canyon Blvd.
</AlertBox>

### What's Up at Griffith Park?

If you were planning to cruise through the **Griffith Park Recreation Center** area, be aware that barricades were spotted yesterday morning behind the tennis courts. Apparently, it's not a film shoot, but a "big event for Nike for World Cup," according to local chatter. So, expect some congestion or detours around there.

### Trail Conditions

Remember those pesky fires from January? The **Eaton Canyon** trails, along with parts of the Angeles National Forest, are still closed for public safety and environmental recovery, likely through the end of 2026. Don't be that person trying to sneak in; the fines are steep, and frankly, the trails need time to heal. The Macy Fire in LA County did cross into Kern County yesterday, but forward progress was stopped and evacuation warnings lifted, and our current air quality indicates no widespread smoke issues today.
