## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Heat Advisory in Full Swing:** Forget those perfectly clear skies, folks. A Heat Advisory for most of Southern California, including our neck of the woods, is in effect from 10 AM today through Thursday evening. We're talking widespread highs in the 90s, pushing into the low 100s for places like the Western San Fernando Valley and Woodland Hills, with heat index values feeling like 113°F in Downtown Los Angeles thanks to some "miserably humid air." Overnight lows won't offer much relief either, hovering in the low 70s.
*   **The Ocean is Not Your Friend:** If you were thinking a dip in the Pacific would cool you off, think again. Public health officials have issued an ocean water quality advisory for all LA County beaches until at least 2 PM Wednesday due to bacteria spikes from the recent Tropical Storm Marie rain. Beyond that, dangerous rip currents and high surf are still battering our south-facing beaches today. Lifeguards have been busy, and places like Zuma Beach saw 10-foot waves and numerous rescues over the weekend.
*   **Inland Air Quality is Decent (for now):** The good news? Our Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 18, despite a new wildfire being reported in Los Angeles County today. So, your lungs are getting a break from pollutants, but don't mistake good air for good workout conditions in this heat.
</BulletList>

## Local Lowdown

For those of you stubborn enough to head out, even a classic spot like **Griffith Park** demands respect for the heat. While running and hiking conditions there are often good, today's rising temperatures mean you should adjust your expectations. Go early, stay on shaded paths where possible, and carry way more water than you think you need. The Sierra Club still has their "Griffith Park Night Conditioning Hikes" on for tonight, which might be a better option after the sun goes down, but still, hydrate!

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heatstroke is No Joke!
With temperatures hitting 90s to 100s and high humidity, heat illness is a serious risk. Stay indoors if you can, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. If you must be outside, shift workouts to before 9 AM, drink water consistently, and know the signs of heat exhaustion. Cooling centers are available by calling 211.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Stay Out of the Ocean!
The coastal flood and high surf advisories are still active for LA County beaches through tonight. More importantly, avoid ocean water contact for at least 72 hours after rainfall due to potential bacteria from runoff. That advisory lasts until at least Wednesday afternoon. No, that refreshing ocean dip won't be refreshing, it'll be disgusting.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Trail Closures Persist East of Pasadena
If you're thinking of hitting some trails in the Angeles National Forest or areas like Eaton Canyon, remember that many trails remain closed due to the 2025 Eaton Fire damage. These closures, affecting trails like the Altadena Crest Trail and Eaton Canyon Trail, are in effect until December 31, 2027, for public safety and environmental recovery. Check the LA County Public Works website for the latest on other road and trail closures before you head out.
</AlertBox>
