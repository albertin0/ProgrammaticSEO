### Fire Watch and Trail Frustrations

That new wildfire reported yesterday in Los Angeles County is a grim reminder of what this dry heat brings. While the AQI hasn't taken a hit *yet*, things can change fast. Keep an eye on local news before heading anywhere near brush or open spaces.

Speaking of open spaces, if your go-to is Eaton Canyon, tough luck. Portions of the trails and surrounding areas are still closed from the January 2025 Eaton Fire, with closures now extended through December 31, 2027, to allow for recovery and public safety. That includes parts of the Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail. The San Dimas Experimental Forest is also off-limits. So, plan accordingly or risk a fine and a grumpy park ranger.
