### Today's Brutal Truth: "Early Summer" Arrived Three Months Early

Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in. But if you're planning on hitting the pavement or trails in Los Angeles today, March 20th, you need to recalibrate your expectations or stay indoors. We're in the middle of a historic heatwave that's setting records and has the National Weather Service slapping "Extreme Heat Warnings" all over the place. Temperatures are running a good 25-35 degrees above normal, with inland areas like the San Fernando Valley looking at triple digits. Even the coastal spots are under an advisory.

Your AQI is looking good at a crisp 18, so at least you won't be choking on smog or fire smoke, thank god. But that doesn't mean your lungs (or the rest of you) are safe. This heat is dangerous, plain and simple. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real risks, especially if you're pushing it outside.

### Construction & Coastal Concerns

If you're anywhere near the **Sepulveda Pass**, get ready for a mess that might make even driving a hazard, let alone running or cycling nearby. Starting at 10 PM tonight, the 405 Freeway is getting slammed with major construction, lane reductions, and ramp closures through the weekend. Expect noise, vibrations, and dust in the vicinity. It's not a direct workout hazard unless you're running directly on the freeway (please don't), but it adds to the general unpleasantness of being outdoors in that area.

As for our beaches, while the air's clear, previous reports indicated high bacteria levels at some spots like the **Santa Monica Pier** and Mothers Beach in Marina del Rey. Best to check local advisories if you're planning a beach run and want to dip your toes, just to be safe.
