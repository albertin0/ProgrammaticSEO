## Today's Rundown: Clear Skies, Smoky Concerns, and Closed Trails

The good news first: the weather for your Sunday long run or hike is looking pretty prime. We've got mild temperatures and clear skies, making for some comfortable outdoor exercise. Your lungs should be pretty happy with that AQI sitting at a sweet 18, meaning you're not sucking in all the usual LA crud.

However, this is Los Angeles, so it's never *just* clear skies. Wildfires were reported popping up in Los Angeles County as recently as yesterday, June 6th. While there's no immediate widespread smoke alert reflected in the current AQI, that doesn't mean you shouldn't be hyper-aware. Smoke can drift, and even seemingly distant fires can impact air quality unexpectedly. Always, always check for visible haze or that tell-tale campfire smell. If you can taste it, your lungs are getting a dose of something they don't need.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Watch: Don't Be Complacent
Multiple new wildfires were reported in LA County yesterday, June 6. Keep an eye on the sky and your local news. Even if the official AQI is green, localized smoke plumes can pop up fast. If you see or smell smoke, get your workout indoors. Remember that studies found toxic carcinogens lingering in air around *last year's* LA wildfire cleanup zones, affecting millions. This isn't just about a bad run; it's about long-term health.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Trouble: Eaton Canyon Remains a No-Go

For those of you looking to hit the dirt, be warned: many of our favorite trails are still shut down from past fire damage. Eaton Canyon, a perennial favorite, and its surrounding trails (like Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail) are **closed until December 31, 2027**. And for good reason, folks. These areas need time to recover, and going off-trail or ignoring closures isn't just irresponsible, it's dangerous. Erosion, unstable ground, and hidden hazards are no joke.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Seriously, Stay Off Closed Trails
The Eaton Canyon trails and several spots in Angeles National Forest (like parts of the Mount Wilson Toll Road and Williamson Rock area) are *still* closed. Fines for trespassing can hit $5,000. Don't be that person who needs rescuing because you thought a "closed" sign was optional. There are plenty of other spots to run without risking your neck or further damaging the ecosystem. Runyon Canyon West Trail is also currently closed, so plan accordingly.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of planning, if you're driving anywhere near West Hollywood today, you might hit some traffic. It's WeHo Pride Weekend, and detours are in effect through Monday morning. Also, Dodger games are wrapping up today, so expect some residual traffic around Chavez Ravine. Just add it to the list of things that make getting around this city a special kind of hell.
