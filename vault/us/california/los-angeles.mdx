## Today's Vitals: Don't Be a Hero

Seriously, folks, while you might be looking out your window right now at a crisp 16.5°C (61.7°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies, don't let that tempt you into thinking it's an ideal outdoor workout day. The forecast for Los Angeles County screams *heat advisory* starting at 10 AM, valid through Tuesday morning. We're talking about a "long duration heat event" where downtown could see triple digits and the valleys could hit 100-110 degrees by Tuesday or Wednesday. Even our coastal spots might touch the 90s.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!
A heat advisory is in effect for Los Angeles County from 10 AM today. Temperatures will soar later today and throughout the week, posing a high risk of heat-related illness. Limit outdoor activities to early mornings (like right now) or late evenings, stay hydrated, and for the love of all that is holy, don't leave kids or pets in cars.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality: Good or Ghetto?

Now, the official reading says our AQI is a beautiful 18, which is "Good." However, AccuWeather's daily forecast for August 24 states the air quality is "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups," with Nitrogen Dioxide being a particular concern. This contradiction means you sensitive types (asthma, heart issues, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung) need to be extra vigilant. "Ozone/Other" is listed as the dominant pollutant. Given the impending heat, ozone levels are likely to climb. If you feel any symptoms, bail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Variable Air Quality
While the raw AQI shows "Good" at 18, other forecasts for today indicate "Poor" air quality for sensitive groups. Pay attention to how you feel, especially if you have respiratory issues. It’s not worth it if you’re wheezing more than usual.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures: Still a Mess from Fires and Fixes

If your plan was to get some miles in the hills, be aware. Many popular spots in the Angeles National Forest, particularly around Eaton Canyon, are still dealing with closures from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. These closures are in effect until the end of 2027 to allow for natural recovery. That includes parts of the Altadena Crest Trail and Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Up in the Santa Monica Mountains, the Solstice Canyon Trail is closed Monday through Thursday for repairs, which means today it’s a no-go. Always check local park and forest service websites before heading out, because nobody likes a surprise "TRAIL CLOSED" sign at the trailhead.

## Construction and Traffic Headaches

Driving to your preferred workout spot? Prepare for some overnight chaos if you’re on the westside. Caltrans is shutting down multiple lanes and ramps on both sides of the I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass nightly from 9 PM to 6 AM, all this week. So if you're an early bird getting a pre-dawn workout, you might hit some delays getting there or back. Various Metro bus routes also have construction-related detours and stop closures around the city.
