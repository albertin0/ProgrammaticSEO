## The Daily Rundown for May 10, 2026

Alright, Angelenos, let's cut to the chase. The weather gods are being a bit fickle with us today. While the air *quality index* is currently looking pristine at a mere 18 – which is practically bragging rights in this city – don't let that fool you entirely. The biggest, smokiest elephant in the room is the string of new wildfires reported across Los Angeles County yesterday, May 9th. We're talking multiple small blazes, like the Rock Fire, where containment details are still hazy, much like the air could become if those fires pick up. Your lungs might be happy with the official AQI, but if you're out there and catch even a whiff of smoke or see any haze, pull the plug on your run. Seriously. Breathing in wildfire smoke is a quick way to trash your respiratory system.

### Looming Heat Wave & Closed Trails
Heads up, folks! While today's temp is nice, this weekend and early next week are slated to bring a proper heat wave, pushing temps into the 80s and 90s, possibly even triple digits inland. Hydrate now, plan for early morning workouts if you're hitting the pavement tomorrow or Monday, and don't push it. Heat-related illness is no joke.

Also, for those of you eyeing a scenic trail run, many of our beloved routes are still off-limits. Areas like Eaton Canyon and a good chunk of the Angeles National Forest have been closed since the January 2025 Eaton Fire, and they're not slated to fully reopen until late 2027. Even Chaney Trail Road is closed to cars until this spring. Ignoring these closures isn't just disrespectful to the recovering ecosystem, it's dangerous. Erosion and unstable terrain are real threats.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your Best Bet Today
Given the wildfire situation, stick to urban routes, if possible, where air quality is more predictable, or even better, take it indoors to your gym. If you're running outside, stay vigilant. Keep an eye on the horizon for any smoke plumes, and trust your nose. If it smells like a bonfire, it's time to head in. The ocean breeze might offer some relief near the coast, but even that's not a guarantee against drifting smoke. For those in the Valley, be extra cautious with the impending heat.
</AlertBox>
