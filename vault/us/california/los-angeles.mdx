### **Watch Out for Those Closed Trails!**
Don't get cute and try to sneak onto trails around **Eaton Canyon** or other parts of the Angeles National Forest. A lot of those areas, still reeling from the 2025 Eaton Fire, are shut down until December 31, 2027. We're talking about the Eaton Canyon Trail, Altadena Crest Trail, Echo Mountain Trail, and the Sam Merrill Trail, among others. They're closed for a reason – unstable hillsides and infrastructure that's more wishful thinking than actual trail. Your ankles (and wallet, with fines up to $5,000) will thank you for finding an open route.
</AlertBox>

### **Pre-Season El Niño & Upcoming Heat Alert**
Just a heads-up for your training plans: California has declared a statewide emergency in anticipation of a "super" El Niño season, which means we're bracing for heavy rains, strong winds, and potential flooding later this fall and winter. Also, don't get too comfortable with this mild weather; heat advisories are in effect for Los Angeles County starting Wednesday, transitioning into an *extreme heat warning* through Thursday. We're talking temperatures between 92 and 108 degrees Fahrenheit. So, enjoy today, but plan your mid-week workouts for early mornings, air-conditioned gyms, or the ocean, if it hasn't flooded yet.
</AlertBox>
