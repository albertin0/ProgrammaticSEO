## Local Hazards & Trail Notes

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Eaton Canyon and Angeles National Forest Closures Still in Effect!** Many trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas of the Angeles National Forest remain closed to public access. This isn't new news, but it's important to reiterate: the Eaton Fire Area Closure has been extended through December 31, 2027, for public safety and environmental recovery. This includes popular spots like sections of Mount Wilson Toll Road and various trails such as Idlehour, Castle Canyon, and Sam Merrill. Eaton Canyon Natural Area itself is also still off-limits. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and risks a fine or, worse, an injury. Seriously, find another trail for a while.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up for the Heat Wave!** Enjoy today's mild temperatures, because come Thursday, we're in for an "unprecedented" March heat wave. Forecasters are calling for near-triple-digit temperatures in coastal valleys like the San Gabriel and San Fernando, and low to mid-90s right along the coast. If you're planning a long run or ride for later in the week, adjust your times to avoid the peak heat, drink plenty of water, and don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

Construction is always a thing in LA. If you're usually around the **Westwood/UCLA area**, be aware that construction on the future Metro station is ongoing through March 30, 2026. This means potential temporary lane closures on westbound Wilshire Blvd between Westwood Blvd and Veteran Ave. Pedestrian access is being maintained, but keep an eye out for equipment and changed traffic patterns. Elsewhere, major freeway work at the SR-57/60 interchange in Diamond Bar will have weekend closures this month, so if your route takes you that way, double-check Caltrans.

So, yeah, enjoy the good air and clear skies today. Just don't go trying to run through Eaton Canyon, and start hydrating now for Thursday. You're welcome.
