## The Rundown: Your LA Workout Safety Brief

First off, let's talk about that beautiful, misleading 61.7°F (16.5°C) and "Mainly Clear" sky. Yeah, it *looks* inviting. The AQI is a stellar 18, so your lungs are generally in good shape across the basin. But don't let the pretty facade fool you; there are some genuine hazards out there.

### Coastal Chaos

<AlertBox type="danger">
**WARNING: Stay out of the water at all LA County beaches, including Malibu!** Hurricane Polo, bless its heart, is churning up the Pacific and sending dangerous four-to-seven-foot surf and *strong rip currents* our way. We're talking moderate coastal flooding and seriously hazardous swimming conditions through Tuesday. That morning high tide, peaking around seven feet, is going to amplify the whole mess. Your "refreshing dip" could turn into a rescue mission. Save the beach run for the sand, far from the breaking waves, and keep an extra eye on the kiddos.
</AlertBox>

### Smoke Signals & Moderate Pollen

While the overall Air Quality Index is fantastic, don't assume every corner of the county is breathing easy. There's a new wildfire reported in Los Angeles County today. Location details are sparse, but smoke travels. Additionally, a structure fire in the Westlake neighborhood, specifically near 525 S. Union Ave., produced "thick black smoke" yesterday. It was knocked down, but lingering particles can affect those downwind. If you smell smoke, pivot to an indoor workout or head to a different part of the city. We also have a moderate pollen level today, so if you're one of the sniffly ones, maybe hit the Allegra before your run around the Silver Lake Reservoir.

### Mountain Maneuvers & Construction Commutes

If you're eyeing a trail run, be smart. Many of our favorite spots in the Angeles National Forest are still in recovery mode from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Trails like the Altadena Crest Trail and Eaton Canyon Trail remain closed until December 31, 2027. Chaney Trail Road is closed to vehicles through Fall 2026, though you can still hike or bike in. Red Box Road and sections of Angeles Crest Highway (from Islip Saddle to Vincent Gap) are also shut down. Don't be "that person" who ignores the signs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up for Evening Mountain Workouts:** A Wind Advisory kicks in at 6 PM for some mountainous areas. If you're planning a late afternoon hike or climb, be prepared for strong gusts.
</AlertBox>

For those sticking to urban routes, expect some nighttime disruption. Caltrans has overnight lane and ramp closures planned on the I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass and the I-605 between Santa Fe Springs and Baldwin Park. Also, final paving work on Wilshire Boulevard in Beverly Hills was supposed to wrap up this morning, so traffic and pedestrian detours might still be a mess early on. Your street run might involve more detours than usual.

So, get out there, but know where you're going and what you're getting into. This isn't the day for spontaneity at the beach or a deep dive into unknown mountain trails.
