## Today's Local Grind: Proceed with Caution

That crisp morning air is tempting, I know. My lungs are doing a happy dance looking at that AQI. But don't let it fool you into ignoring the wildfire warnings.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Heading into the hills? The Angeles National Forest has been on "HIGH" fire danger alert since early June, and those local LA Fires have already shut down multiple parks. If your usual route takes you through or near places like Eaton Canyon, check the LA County Parks website *before* you lace up. Being trapped by an unexpected flare-up is a sure-fire way to ruin your PR and maybe your life.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  While the immediate Los Angeles forecast is mild, an inland heat advisory for 90-103°F starts today. If your plan involves trail running deep in the Valley or further east, stay hydrated, go early, and know your limits. That "cool" 60-degree coast isn't indicative of the entire county.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Moderate pollen levels are hanging around. If you're usually sniffling and wheezing after a long run, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out. Don't be that person hacking up a lung on the Marvin Braude Trail.
</AlertBox>

There's also ongoing construction, like the Arroyo Seco Water Reuse Project near San Pascual Park, which is causing a stir among locals due to tree removal and creek alteration. While not a direct trail closure *today*, it's a reminder that our urban green spaces are constantly changing. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours or equipment if you're in that area.

So, get out there, enjoy the rare good air and temperate weather. But stay smart, stay safe, and for the love of all that is holy, check for closures before you drive all the way to a trailhead only to find a "CLOSED" sign.
