Alright, Los Angeles, let's talk about hitting the pavement or the trails today. Frankly, it's a decent day if you plan around the usual LA nonsense.

<LungsJointsScore score={6} />

## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>The weather is actually *nice* for once, which almost feels like a trick. Cool 16.5°C (61.7°F) and clear skies means your sweat won't be evaporating into a sauna.</li>
  <li>Moderate AQI and pollen are the typical Angeleno combo. Not ideal, but it's not "apocalyptic wildfire smoke" levels, either. Just enough to remind you where you live.</li>
  <li>If your usual weekend jam is a long run through Lake Balboa, prepare for company. There's a whole series of races happening, from 5K to half marathon, making parts of Woodley Park a bit of a cluster.</li>
</BulletList>

Today’s 16.5°C temperature and clear conditions are a blessing, especially after the rough air quality we've seen this year. Remember back in June? LA was experiencing the smoggiest first five months in over a decade, with ozone levels that would make your lungs weep. Luckily, we're not dealing with *that* kind of heat and associated smog today.

However, your lungs still aren't getting a completely free pass. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 70 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This isn't critical, but it's enough to bother sensitive individuals, especially coupled with moderate pollen levels. If you're an allergy sufferer, you already know the drill: pre-medicate and maybe consider an indoor option if you're feeling extra cruddy.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro-Tip for Park Runners:** A Summer Day 5k/10k/15k/Half Marathon is happening at Lake Balboa/Woodley Park today. Expect marked loops and increased foot traffic. If you're not running the race, plan your route accordingly or find a quieter spot.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of quieter spots, don't forget that many trails are still recovering from previous fires and closures. Large sections of the Angeles National Forest, particularly around Eaton Canyon, have trails that remain closed until the end of 2027 due to damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. This includes portions of the Altadena Crest Trail and the unpaved Chaney Trail. On the city side, the West Trail at Runyon Canyon is still shut down, along with George Wolfberg Park at Potrero Canyon and Palisades Park & Recreation Center. Don't be "that guy" ignoring closure signs; those rules are there for a reason, mostly to keep you from accidentally tumbling down a charred hillside.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Construction Chaos (Mostly Averted for Today):** While major freeway projects on the 60 Freeway in Diamond Bar (overnight closures) and the 405 Freeway are ongoing, Caltrans suspended most lane closures for the Labor Day weekend. This means you *shouldn't* hit major construction-related traffic delays on the freeways if you're driving to a trailhead, but always double-check before you head out.
</AlertBox>

Also, if you were planning to catch some culture with your cardio, today is the last day for the Griffith Park Free Shakespeare Festival near the old Zoo picnic area. This might mean more people than usual in that specific section of the park. And for those on the westside, the Los Angeles End of Summer Farmers and Craft Market is taking over Pan Pacific Park through today, so expect crowds and limited access if that's your usual stomping ground.

In short: it’s not perfect, but it’s far from the worst. Pick your poison, bring your tissues, and enjoy the relatively cool weather while it lasts.
