### The Run Down on Today's Outdoor Scene

Look, after the oppressive heatwave that baked us earlier this week, today is a breath of *actually* fresh air. Temperatures are sitting pretty at 16.5°C (that’s about 62°F for those still in denial about Celsius) and the sky is mainly clear. Yesterday had an extreme heat warning until 8 PM for a good chunk of L.A. County, with temps pushing 105 in some inland areas. So, seriously, take advantage of this reprieve. Your lungs will appreciate the AQI of 18, which is frankly stellar, and your joints won't feel like they're melting.

But it wouldn't be L.A. without *some* drama.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Recent Wildfire in LA County:** A new wildfire, LAC-249515, was reported just yesterday in Los Angeles County. While our current AQI shows good air quality, indicating minimal immediate smoke impact on the general city area, this is L.A. and fire season is always simmering. Keep an eye on local news, especially if you're running closer to the foothills or canyon areas. The Angeles National Forest always has closures, and an active fire nearby could change things fast.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of closures, don't forget those persistent headaches up in the San Gabriels. Trails in **Eaton Canyon** and surrounding areas damaged by the January 2025 Eaton Fire are still off-limits, with closures extending all the way through December 31, 2027. This includes popular spots like the Altadena Crest Trail and the main Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Chaney Trail Road is also closed to vehicles until Fall 2026. So, plan your mountain runs accordingly, or you'll be doing extra mileage just to get to a legal trailhead.

Then there's the delightful infrastructure news. A massive 110-year-old water main decided to spectacularly erupt on Sunset Boulevard in West Hollywood yesterday, turning parts of the iconic strip into an impromptu river and leaving a sinkhole in its wake. LADWP crews are on it, but road closures are still active, affecting areas like Holloway Drive and Palm Avenue between Sunset and Santa Monica Boulevard. If your morning jog usually takes you through that stretch, maybe reroute unless you're training for an urban triathlon.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Weekend Event Ahead:** Heads up for Sunday! CicLAvia is taking over 6.6 miles of car-free streets, connecting East Hollywood, Hollywood, and West Hollywood. This is an awesome chance to run, bike, or skate without dodging traffic along Santa Monica Boulevard, Highland Avenue, and Hollywood Boulevard. It's a fantastic opportunity if you want to stretch your legs somewhere usually off-limits to pedestrians.
</AlertBox>

Today's actually pretty prime for hitting the pavement or open trails, just make sure the trails are *actually* open. Don't be that person ignoring closure signs; it's there for a reason, usually to keep you from falling into a ravine or getting caught in a burn scar.
