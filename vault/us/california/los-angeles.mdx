## The Nitty Gritty: What to Watch Out For

Let's be real, it's LA, so there's always *something*. While the overall conditions are stellar, a few persistent issues mean you still need to keep your head on a swivel.

### Air Quality & Allergens

The AQI being a sweet 18 means you've got zero excuses about "bad air." It's prime breathing weather out there. Your only real concern is that moderate pollen count. If you're one of the unfortunate souls who swells up like a balloon when the flowers decide to party, maybe pop an antihistamine before you head out. Otherwise, enjoy the clean air.

### Trail Conditions & Closures

This is where things get a bit more frustrating. Don't go planning any epic adventures in the Angeles National Forest without checking your maps. Parts of the forest, particularly around the **Eaton Canyon** area, are still shut down tighter than a drum due to the *Eaton Fire Area Closure* from January 2025. This means trails like the Eaton Canyon Trail, Sam Merrill, Echo Mountain, Mount Lowe, Idlehour, and Castle Canyon are off-limits until at least December 31, 2027. Seriously, don't ignore those signs unless you enjoy a chat with a ranger and a hefty fine.

And if you're a regular in **Griffith Park**, note that the Bee Rock Trail (above the Old Zoo), the Burbank Peak Trail to the “Wisdom Tree,” and the Eileen Getty Trail are still closed. While most of Griffith Park is open, don't be surprised by some overgrown sections or ruts from past rains on other trails. Also, a friendly reminder for mountain bikers: you're relegated to paved paths and roads in Griffith Park; no dirt trails for you.

**WARNING: Long-Term Trail Closures in Effect!** Do NOT attempt to access closed trails in the Angeles National Forest (especially Eaton Canyon area) or specific sections of Griffith Park. These closures are for public safety and resource recovery, and fines will be issued.

### Construction & Traffic

Ah, construction. The eternal soundtrack of Los Angeles. While there are no major events blocking all your usual running routes today, be aware of ongoing road work. The **Colima Road Improvement Project** in Hacienda Heights and Rowland Heights is still underway, meaning temporary sidewalk detours and traffic control on sections of Colima Road. If you run or bike those suburban streets, expect some minor rerouting. Also, Caltrans is doing its thing with lane closures on sections of I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass and State Route 2 in West LA, Hollywood, and Echo Park, mostly overnight, but be mindful of lingering equipment if you're an early riser.

### Impending Weather Shifts & Fire Risk

Today is calm, but enjoy it. The National Weather Service is forecasting "Breezy, Warm, and Dry" conditions from Friday through Sunday, with "Elevated risks for fast-moving fires & heat illness" and "NE Wind Gusts 25-40 mph". The fire danger level in the Angeles National Forest is already "Very High". While it's not today, these conditions can escalate quickly. Keep an eye on local news if you're planning any longer outdoor excursions into the canyons or hills over the weekend.
