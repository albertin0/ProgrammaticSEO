### Trail Troubles & Construction Chaos

If you were eyeing a scenic hike, think again. Much of Eaton Canyon and its surrounding trails, like the Altadena Crest Trail, are still shut down until late 2027 thanks to that massive 2025 fire. Don't even think about hopping a fence – those closures are there for public safety and environmental recovery. Over in the Angeles National Forest, the Williamson Rock Area is also locked up through December 2026. Do yourself a favor and check the official county trails site before you head out.

Meanwhile, if your workout involves getting anywhere near LAX, be aware that the Sepulveda Boulevard area is a mess with ongoing construction for a new pedestrian bridge. While the heaviest closures aren't until next week, expect general traffic shenanigans that might throw off your pre-run commute or cool-down drive if you're in the vicinity.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Afternoon Heat Incoming!**
With forecasted temperatures hitting 90-100°F (32-38°C) this afternoon, postpone any intense outdoor workouts until the evening or move them indoors. Heat illness is no joke and can hit even seasoned runners.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WILDFIRE WATCH: Smoke is a Wild Card!**
A new wildfire is active in LA County. Even with a current "Good" AQI, smoke conditions can change suddenly. If you smell smoke or see haze, cut your outdoor workout short and head indoors. Running intensely in smoky conditions is a direct assault on your lungs.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional**
If you absolutely *must* be outside, hydrate constantly, even if you don't feel thirsty. Drink more water than you think you need. Consider electrolytes if you're sweating a lot, and wear light, loose clothing.
</AlertBox>
