## Today's Brutal Reality: Heat, Heat, and More Heat

Look, I get it. The sky's clear, the AQI is a beautiful 18 (seriously, fantastic air quality if you can ignore the rest), and it *looks* inviting. But don't be a hero. This "unprecedented, potentially deadly heat wave" is still stubbornly baking Southern California. We're talking temperatures in the mid-to-high 90s Fahrenheit in many areas, setting records and putting everyone at a "moderate to major heat risk." Your lungs might appreciate the clean air, but your heart, kidneys, and joints are going to hate you if you try to replicate last week's comfortable long run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours
The National Weather Service has warned against outdoor recreation during afternoon hours due to dangerous, record-breaking heat. Even if it feels "manageable" in the morning, temperatures will spike. Heat-related illness is no joke, and emergency services are already stretched. Don't add to their burden.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Updates and Urban Annoyances

If you thought you'd escape the heat by heading for some shady trails, think again. Much of the Angeles National Forest and Eaton Canyon are still recovering from past fires, with numerous trails, including Chaney Trail, closed through at least 2026. They're doing this for public safety and environmental recovery, which means unstable terrain and closed roads. Respect it. Finding an alternate route means possibly having to hike or bike in just to *reach* a trailhead that might still be closed.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Persistent Trail Closures in Angeles National Forest and Eaton Canyon
Don't rely on your usual routes in the burn areas. Many trails are still closed for public safety and environmental recovery, with some closures extending through 2026 or even 2027. Always check current conditions before heading out, especially for areas like Chaney Trail Road.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget our old friend, construction. Metro's still at it with the Westwood/UCLA Station construction, impacting traffic and pedestrian flow around Wilshire, Westwood, and Veteran Avenue until the end of the month. If you're trying to hit the Marvin Braude Bike Path near Dockweiler Beach, be aware of ongoing repairs there from that crazy 2023 winter storm. They’re slated to finish in May, but you know how these things go.

<AlertBox type="info">
### General Running Tips for This Week
If you *must* get your miles in, stick to early morning or late evening, but even then, be extremely cautious with the heat. Hydrate relentlessly, wear light clothing, and choose routes with easy bail-out points. Or, you know, hit the gym. It's there for a reason.
</AlertBox>
