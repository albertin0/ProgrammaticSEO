## Trail Conditions & Closures: Still a Mess

Alright, listen up, trail runners. Just because it's not actively raining ash over Griffith Park doesn't mean it's smooth sailing everywhere else. The Angeles National Forest is a minefield of ongoing closures, some stretching all the way through 2026 and even 2027. Remember the Eaton Fire? Yeah, trails like those in Eaton Canyon and parts of the Mount Wilson Toll Road are still recovering and remain closed, with hefty fines for those who think "rules don't apply to me." Chaney Trail, leading into Millard Canyon, was also closed for repairs and was *supposed* to reopen this spring, but verify before you go.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Danger Zone: Angeles National Forest Trail Closures.** Many popular trails and roads, including sections related to the Williamson Rock area and the Eaton Fire, are *still closed*. Do your homework. Seriously. Check the Angeles National Forest website or your favorite trail app for current, *verified* status before you even think about driving up. Ignoring these closures isn't just selfish, it's dangerous, both for you and the emergency services who might have to pull your dumb ass out of there.
</AlertBox>

## Construction & Events: The Usual Grind

Los Angeles is always under construction, and today is no different. Metro's I-5 improvements in North LA County are ongoing, so if your route takes you near major freeways, expect the usual traffic headaches and potential detours. It's not usually a direct workout hazard unless you're trying to sprint across a live construction zone, but worth noting for access.

As for events, today isn't looking like a major race day that would shut down large swaths of your regular routes. There's a Mommy & Me Pilates class in Hazeltine Park, and Barnsdall Friday Wine Nights start this evening, but neither should impede your morning run. Good on you for getting out before the rosé crowd, though.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip for Trail Runners:** With moderate pollen and recent wildfire activity (even if minimal smoke right now), carry allergy meds if you're prone to seasonal sniffles. Dehydration hits harder than you think.
