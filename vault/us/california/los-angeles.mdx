Alright, LA, don't let that barely-there morning chill on your patio fool you – today is *not* the day to push your limits outside.

## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>

*   Don't let the pre-dawn temperature of 16.5°C trick you; we are currently smack in the middle of a prolonged, record-shattering March heatwave that's expected to push temps into the upper 90s, possibly even triple digits inland, 20-30 degrees above normal for this time of year.
*   Moderate pollen counts, mostly from oak, cypress, and mulberry, are manageable on their own, but combine them with extreme heat and a little ozone from the afternoon inversion, and your lungs will feel every single one.
*   Plenty of prime mountain trails in the Angeles National Forest, especially around Mount Baldy, remain closed for public safety until at least March 20th. Don't even think about trying to sneak past those signs.

</BulletList>

Look, I get it. The sky is "Mainly Clear," the air quality index (AQI) is a deceptively good 18 this early, but what happens when the sun gets serious? You're going to bake. The National Weather Service has been screaming about this for days, issuing heat advisories and warnings about high risks for heat illness, specifically telling people to limit strenuous outdoor activity between 10 a.m. and 8 p.m. Ignoring that is just asking for trouble, and frankly, some serious chafing.

Speaking of trouble, if you were planning on escaping to higher ground, think again. The Mount Baldy trails, including Mt. Baldy Trail (7W12), Mt. Baldy Bowl Trail (7W02), Devils Backbone Trail (7W05), and Three T's Trail (7W06), are *still* closed. That closure got extended until March 20th, and for good reason – public safety, folks. Some areas of the Angeles National Forest are still recovering from last year's fires, with portions of the Eaton Fire area closure extending all the way through December 2027. So, no heroic trail runs through the San Gabriels today.

### Extreme Heat Advisory: Avoid Strenuous Mid-Day Activity!
Forecasters are warning of record-breaking temperatures reaching into the 90s and potentially 100°F inland today. This is NOT the day for your long run or intense climb. Limit outdoor exertion to very early morning or late evening. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and listen to your body. Heat illness is a real, fast threat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Angeles National Forest Trail Closures!
Multiple trails in the Mount Baldy area and parts of the Eaton Fire burn scar remain closed for public safety. Do not attempt to bypass closure signs. Respect these closures; they're there for a reason.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated and Reschedule
Even with moderate pollen, the real danger is the heat. If you absolutely MUST be outside, keep it short, easy, and near a water source. Better yet, hit the gym or reschedule that workout. Your health isn't worth a March record.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, LA, this isn't a drill. It's March and we're seeing summer heat. Stay smart, stay safe, and for once, maybe skip the "grind." There's always tomorrow (which will probably also be hot, let's be honest).
