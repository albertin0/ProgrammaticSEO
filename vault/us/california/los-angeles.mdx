**Ozone Alert for Your Lungs!**
While the provided AQI is currently 18 (which usually means "Good" for most folks), multiple air quality services are *forecasting* a "Poor" air quality day for Los Angeles, with ground-level ozone being the dominant pollutant. This means if you're prone to respiratory issues, or just want to avoid feeling sluggish, keep your workout indoors or significantly reduce intensity, especially during peak ozone hours (usually afternoon). "Poor" air quality is specifically deemed "unhealthy for sensitive groups" and can lead to throat irritation, headaches, and chest pain. Don't risk it if you feel any symptoms.
