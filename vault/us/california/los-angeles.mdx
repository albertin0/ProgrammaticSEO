## Current Workout Hazards

Alright, let's get down to brass tacks.

### Fire and Smoke

While the city-wide AQI is looking good, that doesn't tell the whole story for everyone. As of today, October 9th, crews are still battling the "Danny Fire" in Los Angeles County near the Grapevine. It started yesterday, has burned 150 acres, and is only 13% contained. Smoke from this one has already shut down sections of Interstate 5 due to poor visibility, so if you're planning a run anywhere near Gorman or up toward the Grapevine, rethink it.

And if you're closer to the city, there's been a multi-day commercial building fire in Boyle Heights and Inglewood sending thick smoke into the air. The Fire Department says they've removed hazardous materials, but "If there is smoke in the area, stay inside." Some folks nearby have reported coughing and chest pain. Don't be a hero; your lungs will thank you.

### Trail Closures & Conditions

This is where your joints, and your patience, are going to take a hit. Many of our prime running and hiking spots are still either completely shut down or severely impacted.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Angeles National Forest and Eaton Canyon remain largely inaccessible.
The damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire is extensive, and several trails in Eaton Canyon and the surrounding Angeles National Forest are *closed until December 31, 2027*. This isn't just a suggestion; it's a critical safety measure to protect both you and the recovering wilderness. This includes popular routes like the Altadena Crest Trail, Chaney Trail, and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Also, Chaney Trail Road (the paved part leading into some areas) is closed to vehicles until Fall 2026, which, you know, is *now*.
</AlertBox>

Angeles Crest Highway (California 2) is another ongoing saga. Segments have been closed since last December due to heavy rain damage, making trailheads like Mt. Waterman nearly impossible to reach easily. Construction is still chugging along, but full completion isn't expected until Summer 2027 for some sections.

### Construction Obstacles

If you're pounding pavement in the concrete jungle, be aware of ongoing Metro subway construction along Wilshire Boulevard. Crews are doing nightly and weekend work near the Westwood/VA Hospital, Beverly Drive, and Century City stations, leading to lane reductions and closures. Beverly Hills is also in on the action, with paving work on San Vicente Blvd and North Santa Monica Blvd resulting in lane closures and traffic headaches throughout October. You might think a little detour isn't a big deal, but when you're in the zone, getting cut off by cones is just plain annoying and potentially unsafe.
