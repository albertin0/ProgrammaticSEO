Look, the weather gods are smiling down on us with a crisp 16.5°C (that's about 61°F for you non-metric folks) and mainly clear skies. This is prime running weather, no arguments there. Your lungs will thank you for getting off the couch and out into this fantastic air, especially with the Air Quality Index sitting at a delightful 18. Honestly, it's about as good as it gets in this city. Moderate pollen levels might make a few of you reach for the Zyrtec, but for most, it's a minor inconvenience on an otherwise stellar day for outdoor activity.

However, don't just lace up and blindly hit your usual spot. We've got some ongoing issues that could throw a wrench in your perfect workout plans.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Closures, Especially in Griffith Park!
Several trails and roads in **Griffith Park** are still shut down for various reasons. The Upper Zoo Trail, for example, is a no-go due to severe erosion. Also, the north end of Griffith Park Drive, from Travel Town to Mount Hollywood Drive, is closed to personal vehicles as part of a pilot program. If you're planning on hiking to the Hollywood Sign, be aware of new weekend and holiday parking restrictions around Lake Hollywood from 8 AM to 6 PM. Don't get towed, people.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Rattlesnake Season is Year-Round, Apparently.
Saw a recent alert about rattlesnakes on the Mt. Hollywood Trail in Griffith Park. They're out there, folks. Keep your eyes peeled, especially on sun-warmed rocks or near brush. Give 'em space. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

And for those of you who love to get out into the wilder parts of our county, remember that many trails in **Eaton Canyon** and throughout the **Angeles National Forest** remain closed. These are long-term closures, some extending all the way to December 2027, as the land recovers from past wildfires. Roads like Chaney Trail Road (leading to Sunset Ridge Trail) are also closed to vehicular traffic until Fall 2026. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and risks getting a fine or, worse, getting injured. Plan alternative routes or explore areas known to be open.

On the bright side, while we've had some brush fires recently, like the one being battled in Rancho Mission Viejo yesterday, our local air quality appears unaffected right now. This is a good day to get those miles in. Just be smart about *where* you're going.
