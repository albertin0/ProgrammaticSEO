## Your Daily Grind, Graded:

The city's meteorological schizophrenia is on full display. We've got clear skies and a pleasant 16.5°C (around 62°F), which normally screams "hit the pavement!" But hold your horses, literally, if you're thinking of a trail run up towards the Angeles National Forest. Those sustained winds and gusts in the mountains are no joke.

### Head for the Hills? Maybe Don't.

If your usual Sunday long run involves anything north of the 101, especially toward the San Gabriel Mountains, seriously reconsider your route today. Gusts up to 50 mph don't just mess with your hair; they can knock down tree limbs and turn a scenic overlook into a dangerous wind tunnel.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Mountain Winds are No Joke!
An updated wind advisory is in effect until 3 PM today for mountain areas, including the San Gabriel Mountains. Expect northeast winds 20-30 mph with gusts up to 50 mph. Loose objects *will* blow around, and tree limbs *could* fall. Stay clear of exposed ridgelines and canyon areas.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Status Check:

The **Runyon Canyon West Trail** remains closed indefinitely due to that past fire. Don't be that person trying to sneak through; find another spot. The rest of Runyon is open, but it's always a zoo, and with moderate pollen, if you're sensitive, you might want to bypass the dusty trails anyway.

Down by the beach, the **Marvin Braude Bike Trail** near the Santa Monica Canyon Channel is still under construction from that Feb 2024 washout. Good news is, they're keeping it open during the work (through December 2025), but expect some minor delays and don't be surprised by temporary no-parking signs or beach access closures in spots. Just keep your head up and watch for crews.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Runyon Canyon West Trail STILL Closed
Seriously, respect the closures. The West Trail at Runyon Canyon is closed until further notice due to a past fire. Find another route and keep yourself and our park rangers safe.
</AlertBox>

### Wildfire Watch:

While the AQI is good, we did have a new wildfire, LAC-171477, reported yesterday in Los Angeles County (no containment info yet), and the Corral Fire (Lancaster area) was 100% contained yesterday but can still be smoldering within the perimeter. Keep an eye on local news throughout the day if you're in areas susceptible to smoke drift, especially with those winds.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Check Local News for Updates
Wildfires can pop up or shift quickly, especially with current winds. Keep an eye on local news sources for any sudden changes in air quality or new closures, particularly if you're near fire-prone areas.
</AlertBox>

Basically, if your workout involves sticking to the flat stuff or protected areas like the Griffith Park roads (away from the wind-blasted peaks), you're probably golden. If you were planning on conquering a mountain ridge, maybe switch it up for an indoor session or a more sheltered urban route. Your lungs will thank you, and your joints won't have to deal with dodging falling branches.
