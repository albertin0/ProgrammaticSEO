## The Rundown: What to Watch Out For

Let’s not get ahead of ourselves. This is LA. There's always *something*.

### Air Quality: Don't Get Complacent

The immediate air quality is fantastic, which is a welcome change. Just a few days ago, pretty much all of Southern California, including our beloved San Fernando Valley and areas out to Arcadia, were under smoke advisories from multiple wildfires like the Sandy and Santa Rosa Island fires. While those advisories are officially lifted, this is a stark reminder of what’s coming. The fire season is getting longer, and we're seeing major blazes earlier in the year.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro-Tip: Bookmark the AQMD:** Seriously, get used to checking the South Coast Air Quality Management District site. Fire season is effectively year-round now, and a clear morning can turn hazy fast.
</AlertBox>

### Local Hazards: Oil, Chemicals, and Perpetual Closures

There's been an oil spill in Los Angeles County near East Cesar E. Chavez Avenue and North Eastern Avenue. It's impacting the Los Angeles River, and while initial air monitoring hasn't found a public health threat, crews are still excavating, so "increased odors may be present." If you're running along the LA River path, particularly downstream from that area, be aware. Road closures are also expected near the incident site for repairs.

Further afield, but close enough to warrant a mention for anyone heading southeast, an ongoing chemical leak in Garden Grove has led to evacuations due to a "highly volatile, toxic, and highly flammable" chemical. While it's in Orange County, these things can be unpredictable. Again, check local news if you're venturing that way.

### Trail Closures: The Eternal Struggle

Remember that Eaton Fire from January 2025? Parts of Eaton Canyon and the surrounding Angeles National Forest are still recovering and remain closed until December 31, 2027. This includes sections of the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Same deal for large portions of Angeles Crest Highway – winter storms wrecked it, and repairs are ongoing with no clear timeline. Don't just show up expecting your favorite routes to be open.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Danger Zone: Respect Closed Signs!** Seriously, folks. Those "Road Closed" and "Trail Closed" signs, especially in the Angeles National Forest, are there for a reason. Whether it's unstable ground, fire recovery, or falling rocks, ignoring them isn't being tough, it's being dumb. It puts you and rescue crews at risk.
</AlertBox>

Overall, enjoy the clear skies and mild weather today. Just stay smart, know your routes, and keep an eye on those local advisories.
