## Today's Rundown

The weather gods are smiling on us today with "mainly clear" skies and a very comfortable 16.5°C. Pair that with an Air Quality Index (AQI) of 18, and your lungs should be doing a happy dance. That's a rare treat in this city, so don't waste it stuck inside.

However, it wouldn't be LA without a catch, would it? We had a few new wildfires pop up in Los Angeles County yesterday, May 30th. While the general AQI reading for the broader area is excellent, these fires are still new, and their containment status is unknown. If you're heading out towards the edges of the county, especially into areas like the Angeles National Forest, stay vigilant. If you see or smell smoke, turn around. Your lungs will thank you. Remember, a good AQI for the city doesn't always reflect hyper-local conditions near an active burn.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Check Your Trails Before You Go

Seriously, don't just show up. Several popular trails are still shut down. For instance, parts of **Eaton Canyon** and many trails within the Angeles National Forest remain closed from the January 2025 Eaton Fire, and these closures are expected to last through December 31, 2027. That includes sections of the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. The Williamson Rock area of the Angeles National Forest also has a temporary closure through December 31, 2026, impacting Pacific Crest Trail hikers. Even Marshall Canyon Trail is seeing intermittent weekday closures through June 2026 for pest management. And let's not forget Runyon Canyon's West Trail—still closed. Ignorance isn't bliss when you're looking at a trespassing fine or, worse, running into unsafe conditions.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind the Long Beach Runners

If your plan involves a scenic waterfront jog in Long Beach, know that the "Blue Moon Run" 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is happening today, May 31st, at Granada Beach. Expect more foot traffic and potential minor detours in that specific area. Plan accordingly if you don't want to get caught in a race crowd.
</AlertBox>
