### Air Quality & Respiratory Risk

While the overall AQI for Los Angeles is a fantastic 18, don't get complacent, especially if you're anywhere near the West Hills. The uncontained Kenneth Fire means localized smoke plumes are a real possibility. Always check hyper-local air quality readings before you head out, particularly if you're an allergy sufferer dealing with the moderate pollen levels.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Active Wildfire in West Hills!** The "Kenneth Fire" is burning in the West Hills area, currently 0% contained and prompting mandatory evacuations. Smoke plumes are unpredictable. If you're in or near West Hills, stay indoors and avoid all outdoor activity.
</AlertBox>

### Temperature & Hydration

A cool 16.5°C feels like a gift, especially when we’re still seeing official Extreme Heat Warnings for broader areas of LA County until this evening. This is classic L.A. microclimates at play. That warning means that while the beach might be breezy, hiking up in the Santa Monica Mountains or out in the San Gabriel Valley could still cook you.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heat Warnings Still Active!** Even with current mild temperatures, an Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for many parts of LA County until 8 PM PDT tonight. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and schedule strenuous workouts for early morning or late evening if you're in areas susceptible to heat spikes.
</AlertBox>

### Road Closures & Trail Conditions

If your route involves pavement, prepare for diversions. Construction is a constant here. For instance, **Wilshire Boulevard** in Beverly Hills continues to see lane reductions and sidewalk impacts due to the D Line project. Further out, expect lane closures on Topanga Canyon Boulevard for guardrail work.

If you're looking to hit the trails, particularly popular spots like those leading into the Angeles National Forest or the Santa Monica Mountains, be aware that various sections remain closed from past fires and ongoing maintenance. For instance, the Solstice Canyon Trail is closed Monday through Thursday for repairs. Don't just show up hoping for the best; check specific trail head conditions online.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction and Trail Updates:** Before you head out, especially for a bike ride on Pacific Coast Highway or a run through areas like Runyon Canyon Park, check for localized street closures and trail updates. Some areas of the Angeles National Forest, like the Eaton Fire Area, are under long-term closure for recovery.
</AlertBox>
