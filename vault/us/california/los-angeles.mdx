## The Grim Reality: Fires, Heat, and Closed Paths

Look, I get it. The sun's out, the sky is mostly clear, and that little voice is telling you to go get after it. But June 6, 2026, is not the day to ignore the warnings. We've got real-deal wildfires burning in our backyard. A new blaze, LAC-195286, sparked just yesterday in Los Angeles County, and another significant fire has 0% containment up by Kern. Forget what the general AQI says for a second (it's "Good" at 18, I know), because "Good" doesn't account for the localized plumes of smoke that can roll through. If you can smell smoke, or even see a slight haze, your lungs are telling you to back off. Pushing hard and breathing deep when there's particulate matter in the air is a recipe for inflammation and long-term lung issues.

Then there's the heat. Yes, it's 16.5°C right now, but that's a deceptive coastal number. The interior valleys are forecasted to be absolutely baking. The San Fernando Valley is expected to hit 100°F+ today. Running in that kind of dry heat is dangerous; you might not feel like you're sweating as much, but dehydration happens fast.
