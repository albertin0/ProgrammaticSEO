## Workout Warnings & Tips

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Trail Closures are Extensive – Plan Ahead or Face Frustration
Seriously, check your route. The closures around Eaton Canyon are still very much in effect, making your usual loops a no-go. This pushes more people onto fewer open trails, meaning more crowded paths and potentially less enjoyable runs or hikes. Know before you go, or you'll be turning around and swearing at Google Maps.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Beach Bum Workout? Check the Water, But You're Probably Clear
Good news for beach runners and ocean swimmers: the recent rain advisory for elevated bacteria levels at LA County beaches expired yesterday morning. So, the water *should* be fine today. But, as always, if it looks murky or smells off near a storm drain, use your head and stay out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Keep an Eye Out for E-bikes on Trails
The city is actively discussing banning e-bikes from multi-use trails due to safety concerns. Whether you're on two feet or two wheels, this is a reminder that trail etiquette is crucial. Stay aware, anticipate faster traffic, and maybe stick to the less crowded fire roads if you're looking for peace. Your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Moderate Pollen Means Manage Your Allergies
For those of us whose lungs decide to stage a protest at the mere mention of pollen, today's "moderate" levels are enough to trigger symptoms. Take your meds *before* you head out, carry tissues, and maybe avoid pushing those intense intervals if you feel congested. This ain't the day for heroics if your airways are already struggling.
</AlertBox>
