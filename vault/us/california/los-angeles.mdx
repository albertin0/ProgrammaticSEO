## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Dangerously High Heat:** Forget the "overcast" forecast you might have seen. Los Angeles is under a Heat Advisory until 8 PM tonight, with actual temperatures soaring to 37°C yesterday and forecasted to hit 34°C today. Even 23.5°C with high humidity can feel oppressive, but we're looking at much worse in the real world. That's heat stroke territory if you're out there pushing it.
*   **Wildfire Threat & Smoke:** This is huge. A Red Flag Warning is plastered across most of Los Angeles and Ventura Counties through Saturday evening, thanks to powerful Santa Ana winds and bone-dry conditions. We've got brush fires burning, like the Bryson Fire in Malibu, which prompted evacuations and is spewing a large cloud of black smoke. There's also the Norte Fire down in San Juan Capistrano. Smoke means terrible air quality, regardless of what a raw AQI number might try to tell you. Don't let a low, general AQI of 18 fool you when fires are actively burning.
*   **Widespread Park & Trail Closures:** If the heat and smoke didn't deter you, the park rangers will. Numerous parks and trails within the Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area, including the Backbone Trail, Temescal Gateway Park, and much of Topanga Canyon, are *closed* through today due to the Red Flag Warning. Even parts of the Angeles National Forest, like Glendora Mountain Road, are shut down. Plus, those trails in Eaton Canyon, damaged by the 2025 Eaton Fire, are still closed until late 2027. You'd be trespassing into dangerous, fire-prone zones.
</BulletList>

#### Active Fire & Extreme Heat Alert!
The Red Flag Warning means a high risk for rapid fire growth, especially with Santa Ana winds gusting up to 50-70 mph in mountain areas. Combined with extreme heat, this creates a dangerous situation for anyone outdoors. Wildfire smoke can severely impact your lungs and overall physical stress. Stay indoors. Period.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail & Park Closures Are Serious
Do not ignore closure signs, especially in the Santa Monica Mountains. These are in place for your safety and to allow emergency services unimpeded access. Violating these closures isn't just illegal; it's foolish and could put you and first responders in unnecessary peril.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### If You Absolutely Must Move (Indoors!)
Opt for an indoor workout today. Seriously, this isn't a day to test your limits against mother nature, especially when she's actively trying to set things on fire. Hydrate constantly, even if you're just inside. Save your outdoor adventures for when the air clears and the heat breaks. You've got time. This isn't your only chance to run on PCH, I promise.
