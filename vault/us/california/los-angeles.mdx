### Local Hazards & Your Knees (and Lungs)

Alright, here's where your meticulously planned long run might hit a snag. While there are no major city-wide events mucking up the streets today (the LA Marathon was *last month*, folks, on March 8th), certain popular trails are still off-limits.

<AlertBox type="danger">
The **Eaton Canyon Trail** and much of the surrounding area, including parts of the **Altadena Crest Trail**, remain closed until **December 31, 2027**, due to damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. Don't be that person ignoring the "CLOSED" signs and potentially getting yourself hurt or worse. Recovery of burned wildlands isn't just for show; it's about unstable terrain and falling debris. Seriously, respect the closures. This isn't a suggestion; it's a safety directive.
</AlertBox>

Other sections of the Angeles National Forest, like the Williamson Rock area, are also under long-term closure through December 31, 2026, for resource protection. So, if your usual mountain escape is up that way, find a new route or prepare for a turn-around.

<AlertBox type="info">
Always check the specific trail status before heading out, especially in burn scar areas. What looks fine from the road can be a disaster waiting to happen on unstable slopes. A quick search for "Angeles National Forest alerts" can save you a world of hurt. And for the love of all that is holy, bring water. It's LA, not the arctic tundra, no matter how "mild" it feels.
</AlertBox>

Today is a good day to get outside and move your body. Just be smart about *where* you're moving it.
