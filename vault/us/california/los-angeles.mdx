Look, I'm usually the first to tell you to just stay on the couch, but today? Today is the day. The marine layer did its job, pulled back, and left us with a crisp 16.5°C (that's about 62°F for the folks who still measure in Fahrenheit) and air so clean it almost feels fake for Los Angeles. The AQI is sitting pretty at 18, which means you're not going to be sucking down a cocktail of car exhaust and whatever else is usually floating around. Your lungs will thank you.

Pollen is "moderate," which means some of you seasonal allergy sufferers might get a little sniffle, but honestly, it's not bad enough to derail a good workout for most. Pop an antihistamine if you need to, but don't let it be an excuse to skip those miles.

<AlertBox type="info">
Head out to Griffith Park early this morning. The trails are going to be quiet, the views clear, and with this temperate weather, it's prime time for a long climb up to the Observatory without baking in the sun or dealing with a smoggy haze.
</AlertBox>

Now, don't get complacent. Just because the air is clean today doesn't mean you can slack on your hydration. Even with mild temps, you're still sweating, especially if you're tackling those inclines around Runyon Canyon or pushing through a long run on the Ballona Creek path.

### Local Hazards Update

Good news, folks: the usual suspects are mostly quiet today. No reports of massive wildfires making the air unbreathable, no unexpected flooding from a phantom rainstorm, and the high winds that love to throw branches at your head seem to be taking a day off. Construction, however, is eternal. While I haven't seen any *new* major trail closures reported in the last 24 hours, always keep an eye out, especially if you're running near downtown or any major road improvement projects. The city never sleeps, and neither do the jackhammers.
