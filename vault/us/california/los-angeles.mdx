## Watch Out For These Local Obstacles

### Trail Closures & Construction
<AlertBox type="warning">
**Solstice Canyon Trail (Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area):** If you were planning a Monday morning hike here, pivot. This trail is closed Monday through Thursday for repairs. You'll need to use the Rising Sun Trail if you want to access the area.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Eaton Canyon and Angeles National Forest:** Just a friendly reminder that many trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas, still recovering from the January 2025 Eaton Fire, remain closed until the end of 2027. This includes popular spots like sections of the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Plan your routes carefully if you're usually in that area.
</AlertBox>

### Waterfront Woes
<AlertBox type="danger">
**Malibu and Other LA County Beaches:** This is a big one. Skip your ocean swim or surf session today. Hazardous conditions, including dangerous rip currents and waves up to 6 feet, are expected along the entire Los Angeles County coastline through this afternoon. It's not worth the risk, folks. Stick to dry land.
</AlertBox>

### Future Headaches
<AlertBox type="info">
**Port of Los Angeles (San Pedro):** While not impacting your run *today*, be aware that significant construction projects are slated to begin this fall around the Port of Los Angeles in San Pedro, gearing up for the 2028 Olympics. This includes work on Harbor Boulevard and a new parking lot. Keep an eye on local news for potential disruptions to your south bay waterfront workouts in the coming months.
</AlertBox>
