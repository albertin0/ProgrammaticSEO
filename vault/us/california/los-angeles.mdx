Alright, Los Angeles, it's a mixed bag out there today, but mostly good, provided you dodge the traffic cones and closed trails.

Let's talk brass tacks. The air quality (AQI 18) is pristine, the temperature is ideal, and it's clear skies. Your lungs are clear for takeoff. The Bouquet Fire in the Angeles National Forest, which caused some localized air quality alerts earlier this week near Santa Clarita, is now 93% contained, and the Danny Fire is 96% contained. So no widespread smoke issues today.

However, if your usual route involves the scenic **Temescal Canyon Trail** in Pacific Palisades, you're out of luck. The Los Angeles Department of Water and Power (LADWP) has temporarily closed the trail to facilitate pump operations while they fix a pretty significant water main leak on Sunset Boulevard. All eastbound lanes and one westbound lane of Sunset between Allenford Avenue and Mandeville Canyon Road are closed. So, if you were planning to run, walk, or bike that stretch, find an alternate. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on detour situation.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Temescal Canyon Trail is CLOSED** due to the Sunset Boulevard water main break. Avoid the area entirely; traffic restrictions are heavy, and the trail is off-limits. Plan an alternate route immediately.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the Westside, don't forget the *eternal* construction in Beverly Hills. Wilshire Boulevard, between N. Santa Monica and the LA Country Club, is seeing lane reductions starting today (October 10th) through early December for sewer and gas utility work. They're even doing weekend work, so expect reduced lanes from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. And if that wasn't enough, San Vicente Boulevard between La Cienega and Wilshire is getting paved this weekend, causing temporary closures and lane reductions there too.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect significant traffic and pedestrian detours around Wilshire Boulevard and San Vicente Boulevard in Beverly Hills due to ongoing construction and paving. If your run takes you through this area, prepare for delays and blocked sidewalks.
</AlertBox>

For the hardcore trail runners among us, remember that trails in Eaton Canyon and surrounding areas are still closed due to damage from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. These closures are expected to last until at least December 31, 2027, to allow for natural recovery. This includes parts of the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Also, a segment of Angeles Crest Highway has been closed since last December due to rain damage, impacting access to some trailheads further up. Don't be "that guy" ignoring closure signs. Fire recovery is serious business, and you don't want to get stuck, or worse, hinder the recovery efforts.

Yesterday's 5.3 magnitude earthquake near the Channel Islands was felt across LA, but thankfully, there are no reports of significant damage or injuries in the city, so no earthquake-related workout hazards today.

So, go run, just stay clear of Sunset, Beverly Hills, and those fire-damaged trails. The rest of the city is calling your name.
