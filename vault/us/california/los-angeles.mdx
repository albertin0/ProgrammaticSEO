## The Rundown

Look, if you're sticking to the beach paths or your local neighborhood loops, today's your day. The marine layer decided to play nice, giving us "Mainly Clear" conditions and a temperature that won't cook you alive. Throw in an AQI that's practically zero, and your respiratory system is in for a treat.

But if your grand plan involved conquering some elevation, you're going to need to adjust. The Angeles National Forest continues to be a patchwork of "closed" signs. Forget about certain stretches of the Mount Wilson Toll Road or any major hiking in the Eaton Fire Area; those trails are locked down through December 2027 for recovery. Same goes for the Williamson Rock Area and Rincon-Redbox Road, which is out of commission due to a rock slide. And if you were planning a scenic drive or ride up Angeles Crest Highway, be aware it's still closed from Islip Saddle to Vincent Gap. Always check the Forest Service website before you head out there, or you'll be turning around grumpy.

### Road Warrior Warnings

If you're a road cyclist or runner, keep your head on a swivel this morning, especially down south. The Los Angeles County Sanitation District is still doing roadwork on Palos Verdes Drive South between Schooner Drive and the landslide area, wrapping up around 11 a.m. today. Expect delays and be extra cautious around the crews. Also, Caltrans has been doing overnight paving work on northbound U.S. Highway 101 in the Cahuenga Pass, specifically between Highland Avenue and State Route 170. Lane and ramp closures were in effect until 6 a.m. today, so if you're an early bird, you might've hit some residual mess.
