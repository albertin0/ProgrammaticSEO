<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures are Widespread and Long-Term:** Seriously, don't just wing it. Check the Angeles National Forest alerts before you head out, especially if you're planning anything near the Eaton Canyon burn scar. Roads like Chaney Trail leading into Millard Canyon are still closed to vehicles for repairs until Spring 2026, meaning a longer trek just to get to *some* open trails. Planning on hitting the Pacific Crest Trail near Williamson Rock? That area's also closed through December 2026 for resource protection. Map your routes carefully, or you'll be turning around.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Overcast and Cool is Your Friend:** Today's 15.7°C (around 60°F) and overcast skies are actually pretty ideal for a run, especially if you're trying to manage the moderate air quality and pollen. Just be mindful if you're in higher elevations, as earlier forecasts mentioned lingering chances of rain from midweek systems. Keep an eye on the sky, and don't forget the layers if you're starting early or finishing late.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Upcoming Rain This Weekend:** While today might be clearing up, forecasts for the weekend (Friday through Sunday) are calling for more rain and even potential thunderstorms in mountain communities. If you're pushing your long run to Saturday or Sunday, be prepared for wet conditions and potentially slick trails.
</AlertBox>

Look, it's L.A., you can always find a place to move your body. Just don't be a hero. Be smart about where you're going and what you're breathing.
