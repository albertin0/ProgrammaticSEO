### Extreme Heat Warning: Seriously, Stay Cool!
An Excessive Heat Warning is in effect for Los Angeles County until 8 p.m. tonight, with the heat index making it feel even hotter than the already brutal 37.3°C (99°F) on the thermometer. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous, leading to a high risk of heat illness. Hydration breaks are not a suggestion, they are a necessity, and avoiding peak sun hours (10 a.m. to 6 p.m.) is strongly advised. If you're out there, wear light clothing, slather on the sunscreen, and for the love of all that is holy, drink more water than you think you need. Or, better yet, hit one of the city's designated cooling centers.
</AlertBox>

### Don't Breathe Too Deeply: Air Quality Alert
An Air Quality Alert for harmful levels of ozone (smog) is also in effect through Friday evening, particularly impacting the South Coast Air Basin. Combine that with an AQI of 94 driven by PM2.5, and you've got a recipe for respiratory misery. AccuWeather is calling our air "Poor" and unhealthy for sensitive groups today, and let's be real, after a few miles in this mess, everyone feels like a sensitive group. Minimize intense outdoor activity, especially if you've got lung conditions.
