### The Heat is On, Seriously

Let's be blunt: it's going to be brutal out there. A heat advisory blankets Southern California from 10 a.m. today through Thursday evening. We're talking up to 105 degrees in places like the San Fernando Valley, San Gabriel Valley, Santa Clarita Valley, and our local mountains. This isn't just "a bit warm"; this is "stay indoors unless your life depends on it" hot. Even coastal areas will see climbing temps. This heat doesn't just mess with your pace; it messes with your health. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real dangers when it's this warm, especially when overnight lows are also staying uncomfortably high.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Advisory: Limit Outdoor Activity!**
  A heat advisory is active across much of LA County. Temperatures could reach 105&deg;F in inland valleys and mountains. Reschedule strenuous outdoor workouts for later in the week or early morning if absolutely necessary. Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and listen to your body – it's not worth risking heat illness.
</AlertBox>

If you've got asthma or any respiratory issues, act like it's a "Moderate" day for your lungs and maybe skip that hill repeat session in the Valley.

### Trail Snafus and Roadblocks

If your go-to is a shaded trail, you're likely out of luck. Many trails within Eaton Canyon and the surrounding Angeles National Forest areas, like the Altadena Crest Trail, are still closed from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. And don't even think about running the West Trail at Runyon Canyon – that's closed too. These aren't just minor detours; they're significant closures protecting both public safety and recovering wildlands, and they're expected to last until at least late 2027 for Eaton Canyon.

Road construction is also ongoing, primarily overnight, but if you're an early bird or late-night exerciser who drives to your spot, be aware. Caltrans has overnight lane and ramp closures on U.S. Highway 101 in the Cahuenga Pass and on I-210 near Sunland Boulevard until Friday for paving and drainage work. Topanga Canyon Boulevard also has closures for guardrail work. So, plan your commute to your workout spot accordingly.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Trail Closures in Effect:**
  Many trails in Eaton Canyon, Angeles National Forest (including Altadena Crest Trail, Chaney Trail, Eaton Canyon Trail), and Runyon Canyon (West Trail) are closed. Check official park websites before you go to avoid disappointment and ensure your safety.
</AlertBox>
