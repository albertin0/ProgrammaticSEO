## Trail & Hazard Update

### Eaton Canyon Closures Continue

Remember that beast of an Eaton Fire from January 2025? Well, the scars are still fresh, and the closures are still firmly in place. Multiple trails in and around Eaton Canyon, including the **Altadena Crest Trail** and the main Eaton Canyon Trail itself, are shut down until at least December 31, 2027. This isn't just about preserving the ecosystem; it's about your safety, too. Burn scars mean unstable ground, falling debris, and a higher risk of mudslides, even without heavy recent rain. Chaney Trail Road is also closed to vehicles until Spring 2026, so even getting close is a trek.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Eaton Canyon and Surrounding Trails: STAY OUT!**
The area impacted by the January 2025 Eaton Fire remains closed until at least December 31, 2027. This includes popular routes like the Altadena Crest Trail and the Eaton Canyon Trail itself. Trespassers risk significant fines and, more importantly, personal injury due to unstable terrain. Find an alternate route.
</AlertBox>

### Angeles National Forest Alerts

Beyond Eaton Canyon, parts of the Angeles National Forest also have ongoing restrictions. The Williamson Rock area, which impacts a section of the Pacific Crest Trail, is closed through December 31, 2026, to protect natural resources. Additionally, the Eaton Fire Area Closure extends into the Angeles National Forest, affecting roads like Mount Wilson Toll Road below Mount Wilson Trail until December 31, 2027. Always check the specific forest service alerts before heading out, especially for longer runs or backpacking trips.
