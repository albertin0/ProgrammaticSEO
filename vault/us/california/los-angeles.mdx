Look, the AQI might technically read "Good" at 18 today, but that's a general metric. The Bouquet Fire in the Angeles National Forest is still burning, and while the dominant pollutant might not be traditional particulate matter across the *entire* basin, localized smoke plumes are a real concern, especially if you're anywhere near the foothills or downwind of Santa Clarita. Plus, that "Ozone/Other" dominant pollutant can still mess with your lungs when paired with this kind of heat.

If you were planning on heading out to Eaton Canyon, forget about it. Those trails are still shut down from the 2025 Eaton Fire damage, and they won't reopen until at least December 31, 2027. Even Chaney Trail Road is closed to cars until next Fall. And let's not even talk about the other closures in the Angeles National Forest like Williamson Rock or Rincon-Redbox Road due to slides or recovery.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Closures and Fire Risk:** Active fires and previous fire damage mean multiple trails, especially in the Angeles National Forest and Eaton Canyon, are closed. Check official sources before you even think about driving to a trailhead. Also, with breezy winds and bone-dry conditions, any spark can turn into a major problem, fast. Keep an eye out and report anything suspicious.
</AlertBox>

Plan your commutes, even if you're just driving to an air-conditioned gym.

Today is not the day to find your inner zen on a sunny trail. It's the day to find your inner couch potato and stay safe. Your lungs, joints, and common sense will thank you.
