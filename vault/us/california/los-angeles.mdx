## Today's Outdoor Workout Rundown

Alright, Angelenos, let's talk about hitting the streets or trails on this Saturday, July 25th. The good news? The air quality is looking pretty decent with an AQI of 18. That's a solid win for your respiratory system. However, don't let that fool you into thinking it's a walk in the park everywhere. We've got a few things to keep in mind if you're planning to sweat it out.

### Heat is On

First and foremost, it's hot out there. A heat advisory is in effect for many parts of Los Angeles County through Monday morning, with inland valleys expected to hit triple digits. Even the coastal areas aren't immune to these unusually warm conditions. This isn't just about feeling a bit sweaty; warm overnight temperatures mean your body gets less recovery time, increasing the risk of heat-related illness. Seriously, people, this isn't the time to skimp on your water intake. If you're out near the coast on the Marvin Braude Bike Trail, remember that even with the ocean breeze, the sun is unforgiving.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Extreme Heat Advisory in Effect!** Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and avoid peak sun hours (10 AM - 4 PM), especially if you're in the valleys or on exposed trails. Listen to your body and don't push it.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Troubles & Fire Frights

If your go-to is a hike in the hills, you need to be extra vigilant. The Angeles National Forest has elevated its fire danger level to "VERY HIGH" as of yesterday. Hot temperatures and dry vegetation are a recipe for disaster. We've already seen recent fires leading to closures. For instance, the Stough Canyon and Wildwood Canyon hiking trails in Burbank are currently closed due to a recent fire.

Speaking of closures, don't just assume your favorite spot is open. Runyon Canyon continues to be a patchwork of accessible and inaccessible areas. The West Trail is still closed indefinitely due to fire damage from way back in January 2025, and the Wattles Garden/Runyon Canyon Road Loop is also currently shut. While the West Ridge Trail reopened a few months ago, check the official park websites before you head out. It's frustrating, I know, but better safe than stuck or, worse, causing more problems.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Elevated Fire Danger!** Be extremely cautious. No smoking anywhere on trails or in parks. Report any suspicious activity immediately. Certain trails in the Angeles National Forest and Burbank canyons are closed due to fire activity or rehabilitation.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Coastal Commute

Beyond the trails, if you're cycling the Marvin Braude Bike Trail near Will Rogers State Beach, be aware of ongoing construction near the Santa Monica Canyon Channel outlet. While the main bike path is supposedly open, expect potential temporary beach access closures and *loud* sheet pile driving as they repair storm damage. Also, some sections of PCH and Topanga Canyon Boulevard will have lane closures this weekend due to Caltrans Palisades Fire recovery work, so plan for delays if your route takes you that way.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Before You Go:** Significant construction is affecting various roads and some sections of the Marvin Braude Bike Trail. Always confirm your route before you leave, especially if you rely on specific access points.
</AlertBox>

### Beach Buzzkill

If you're thinking of cooling off at the beach with a swim, think again, or at least be *very* careful. A Beach Hazard Statement is in effect through Sunday for Los Angeles and Ventura County beaches due to rip currents and elevated surf. That's a fancy way of saying the ocean wants to drag you out to sea. Stick to swimming near lifeguards and steer clear of those rock jetties.

Today's a day for smart choices. If you're going out, go early, go hydrated, and know your route. Don't be that person the paramedics have to scrape off a hot trail.
