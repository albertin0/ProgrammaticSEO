## The Rundown

Look, I get it. The sun's out, the sky is *mostly* clear, and that 16.5°C (61.7°F) start to the day feels pretty good right now. But don't be a rookie. This is Los Angeles in August, and things will heat up fast. The National Weather Service is forecasting daytime highs in the range of 75°F to 91°F (24°C to 33°C) across the region. Those heat advisories for the Antelope Valley, San Gabriel Mountains, and even parts of the LA Inland Coast are no joke. If you’re heading out for a long run, aim for pre-dawn or post-dusk, and for the love of all that is holy, bring more water than you think you need. Dehydration isn't cool, it's a fast track to cramping up on the side of a busy street.

### Air Quality & Respiratory Annoyances

Your overall Air Quality Index is a pristine 18, which is fantastic. But here's the catch: the Ridge Fire near Gorman is still burning and 0% contained as of last night, prompting a localized Wildfire Smoke Advisory. Unless you're running *in* Gorman, you're probably okay, but keep an eye on the wind direction. Then there's the ongoing saga of the Boyle Heights warehouse fire cleanup. The air district is still wrangling with them over "unsanitary and nuisance" conditions, so if you're near that area, expect some nasty smells. And don't forget the moderate pollen count. If you're sniffly, consider an indoor workout or pop an antihistamine before you hit the pavement.
