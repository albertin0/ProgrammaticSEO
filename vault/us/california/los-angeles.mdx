## But don't get complacent, this is LA.

Look, you know the drill. Even on a "good" day, this city's got quirks that can mess with your workout.

### Mountain Mayhem

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Angeles National Forest Fire Danger Remains "Very High"!** Just because the air in the basin is clear doesn't mean the hills are worry-free. The Angeles National Forest's fire danger is still elevated to "Very High." This means if you're hitting those higher elevation trails, be extra vigilant. And for crying out loud, don't be the idiot who starts a fire.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the mountains, if you had plans for a long run up in Eaton Canyon, think again. Those trails, along with areas like the Altadena Crest Trail and Chaney Trail, are still closed from the January 2025 Eaton Fire. They're not reopening until at least December 31, 2027, to allow for recovery. Also, weekend access to Millard Canyon Campground and its Chaney Trail gate in the Angeles National Forest is temporarily locked due to ongoing fire risk.

### Downtown Detours & Freeway Frustrations

If your run takes you near the urban core, be aware that Wilshire Boulevard between MacArthur Park and Lafayette Park is currently shut down for the "Park to Park 2026" event. It's a pedestrian-friendly takeover, so that means *no cars*, which is cool if you planned to run there. But if you're trying to *drive* through, you'll be rerouted. Plan accordingly.

And for those of you who swear by a long effort near the freeways (bless your hearts), the I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass is still undergoing pavement rehabilitation. Expect weekend lane reductions and ramp closures to continue into mid-2026. It's not a full shutdown like the "Carmageddon" days, but it'll add a layer of annoyance to your route planning if you're crossing over or running adjacent.
