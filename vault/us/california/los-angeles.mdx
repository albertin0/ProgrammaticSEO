## The Good, The Bad, and The Allergies

Look, a 16.5°C (that's about 61.7°F for the Fahrenheit folks) and "Mainly Clear" skies? That's about as good as it gets for running in this city without someone trying to film a movie in your lane. Coupled with an AQI of 18, which is practically spa-quality air, your lungs are getting a treat. Enjoy it, because who knows when the next random flare-up or "mystery pollutant" decides to roll in.

Now, for those of us who aren't immune to Mother Nature's microscopic fury, "Moderate" pollen is making its presence known. Expect the usual suspects: sneezing, watery eyes, and that fun post-run throat tickle. If you’re usually a mess during spring, preemptive action is key.

## Trail & Road Woes: The Usual LA Story

While the skies are clear, the ground beneath your feet might be a different story. The Angeles National Forest still has a patchwork of closures, particularly from the Eaton Fire which means trails like the Mount Wilson Toll Road (below Mount Wilson Trail) and the Sam Merrill Trail are still off-limits through December 2027. Don't be that person ignoring the signs, getting rescued, and then blaming the park service. They've got enough on their plate.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Wildfire Risk (Especially Inland)
While the Grand fire in Chino Hills had its evacuation orders lifted yesterday, the National Weather Service is warning of elevated fire risk in inland areas of L.A. County later this week, particularly Saturday and Sunday, due to moderate to strong winds and low humidity. Keep an eye on local news, especially if you're heading out to areas like the Antelope Valley or I-5 Corridor where winds could gust up to 50 mph.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Chaos
Planning a long drive to your favorite trail? Think again. There are ongoing overnight freeway closures on SR-14 in Palmdale and Lancaster, I-5 in Sun Valley, and I-405 through the Sepulveda Pass. Plus, Metro construction for the Westwood/UCLA Station means potential lane closures around Wilshire, Westwood, and Gayley Avenues until June 30, 2026. This isn't just a nuisance; it can add significant time to your travel and create hazardous driving conditions if you're not paying attention.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Event-Related Traffic
Today, May 14th, is the last day for Dodger games that started May 8th, so expect some extra traffic around Dodger Stadium. The LA County Fair is also still running until May 31st, causing delays near the fairgrounds Thursday through Sunday, from 11 AM to 11 PM. If your route takes you anywhere near these spots, adjust your timing or pick an alternate path. Nobody wants to breathe exhaust fumes for their cool-down.
</AlertBox>

## PT's Corner: Don't Be a Hero

With moderate pollen, if you're wheezing, slow down. Seriously. Pushing through an allergic reaction just stresses your respiratory system further. Keep your inhaler handy if you need it. And with all the construction and uneven surfaces around, watch your footing. Ankle sprains and knee tweaks from distracted running on busted pavement or poorly marked detour routes are a real joy to rehabilitate, I promise.
