## Your Lungs are Not a Smoke Filter

Look, I know the temperature is a mild 20.1°C (around 68°F) and it's overcast, which on any other day might sound decent for a workout. But today, September 15, 2026, those clouds are probably mixed with a whole lot of ash and particulate matter from the multiple active wildfires currently tearing through Los Angeles County. Seriously, the news is filled with reports of the Palisades Fire alone scorching over 15,000 acres, and the Eaton Fire in Altadena expanding rapidly. You think an AQI of 18 is reflective of *that*? Local reports are calling the air quality "awful" in and around the fire zones, and common sense says if you can *see* the smoke, you shouldn't be breathing it in while pushing your cardio.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Stay Indoors.** Large wildfires like the ones impacting the Pacific Palisades and Altadena areas mean hazardous smoke and poor air quality are widespread. Breathing this stuff in during strenuous activity is a recipe for respiratory issues, even for healthy individuals. If you absolutely MUST venture outside for any reason, wear an N95 mask.
</AlertBox>

## Trail Closures and Local Hazards

Beyond the obvious, many of our favorite trails could be impacted, either directly by fire, for emergency access, or simply due to dangerous conditions. While our usual spots like Griffith Park might technically be "open" from some perspectives, the smart move is to assume anything in the hills or canyons is a no-go. With strong Santa Ana winds pushing these fires, conditions can change directionally in a heartbeat.
