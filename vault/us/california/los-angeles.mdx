## Today's Hazards

### Extreme Heat: Don't Be a Hero

Look, I get it. You want to feel the burn. But when the county issues an Extreme Heat Warning, it's not for fun. Much of Los Angeles County, including our beloved Santa Monica Mountains, is expecting temperatures between 92 to 108 degrees Fahrenheit today and tomorrow. That's "heatstroke territory" for anyone pushing it too hard, especially between 10 AM and 3 PM.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!**
Temperatures are predicted to reach 92-108°F in many areas of LA County. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during peak heat hours (10 AM - 3 PM). Stay hydrated, wear light clothing, and listen to your body. This isn't the day to PR your long run.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality: Smog and Smoke Combo

Our official AQI might read "Moderate," but that's a broad stroke. The Palos Fire just yesterday was creating significant smoke. You might not see towering plumes from your living room, but that particulate matter floats. If you have asthma, allergies, or just like having functioning lungs, this moderate AQI, coupled with the "Moderate" pollen, is a double whammy. AccuWeather even called today's air quality "unhealthy for sensitive groups" at an AQI of 53. Pick a lane, people!

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Air Quality Concerns**
A Moderate AQI combined with lingering smoke from the recent Palos Fire means sensitive groups should limit outdoor exertion. If your throat tickles or your chest feels tight, head inside.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Blocks: Plan Ahead or Go Home

Getting around for your workout is becoming a puzzle. Effective today, September 16, **Agoura Road** is facing an extended full closure between Rondell Street and Hydepark Drive from 7 AM to 5 PM on weekdays (and this Saturday!) for the Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing construction. This directly impacts pedestrians and cyclists in the Agoura Hills area. They've got shuttles, but seriously, check the map.

Meanwhile, if you're thinking of hitting the trails that burned in the 2025 Eaton Fire, forget it. Large sections of **Eaton Canyon** and surrounding areas in the Angeles National Forest remain closed until December 31, 2027, for habitat recovery. This includes Chaney Trail Road. Similarly, the popular **Temescal Canyon Trail** to the waterfall is still closed due to fire damage creating unsafe conditions. Even **Runyon Canyon's West Trail** had conflicting official closure info for a while, despite being "physically open" after the Sunset Fire. Always double-check current conditions before you drive out.

**Trail & Road Closures Alert:**
Heads up, Agoura Hills runners and riders: Agoura Road is closed to pedestrian and cyclist through traffic (Mon-Fri, 7 AM-5 PM) for construction until November 30. Expect detours. Many fire-damaged trails in Eaton Canyon and Temescal Canyon also remain closed.
</AlertBox>
