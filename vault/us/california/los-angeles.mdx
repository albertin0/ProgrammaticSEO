## The Rundown

Look, if you're thinking of hitting the pavement or some easier paths, today's your day. The weather's cooperating, and the air quality is actually *good* for a change. Forget the Boyle Heights warehouse fire smoke; that drama has thankfully cleared out of the basin. Your lungs can thank the marine layer for scrubbing that particulate matter, though it did cause some gnarly air earlier in the week.

However, if your idea of a good workout involves tackling some serious elevation, you need to be smart and plan ahead. We're well into June, and while our immediate coastal weather is mild, the mountains are still in fire season. The Angeles National Forest has elevated fire danger to "HIGH" since mid-June, and other parts of Southern California, like San Bernardino and Kern counties, are under Red Flag Warnings today with crazy winds and low humidity. While not *in* LA proper, that dry, windy stuff can shift, and it means the hills are tinder.

### Know Your Closures: The Eternal Trail Saga

This is LA, so of course, some of our favorite spots are still recovering from various disasters. If you're eyeing the **Eaton Canyon Natural Area Park** or any of the surrounding Angeles National Forest trails, you're mostly out of luck. Those trails, like Altadena Crest Trail and the main Eaton Canyon Trail, are *still* closed until at least December 31, 2027, thanks to the Eaton Fire back in January 2025. Seriously, two-and-a-half years later, and we're still waiting. It's for public safety and habitat recovery, which, fine, but it sucks for our weekend long runs. Same goes for the San Dimas Experimental Forest and the Williamson Rock Area.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Hunters, Beware:** Always double-check trail status *before* you leave the house. The Mountains Recreation & Conservation Authority still lists several Santa Monica Mountains trails, particularly around Topanga, as fully closed post-Palisades Fire. Don't be that person needing a rescue because you ignored the "Closed" sign.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**City Runners:** If you're sticking to downtown for your workout, be aware of road closures around Union Station for a FIFA Fan Zone event. Streets like Alameda, Los Angeles, and Main are affected through Sunday. Plan your route accordingly, or just embrace the reroute as part of your dynamic warm-up.
</AlertBox>

**Pollen Patrol:** For those of us who feel like a human snot rocket this time of year, remember pollen is at a moderate level. Pop those antihistamines before you head out, and maybe save that long run through a field of wildflowers for another day.

Overall, it's a solid day for a workout here in the city, provided you stick to the open roads or a known open trail and leave the high-altitude heroics for a less fire-prone season.
