### Air Quality & Fire Situation

Look, the weather might be "Mainly Clear" at a cool 16.5°C (that's about 62°F for those of you who still think in Fahrenheit). That sounds lovely, right? But clear skies don't mean clear air when there are multiple fires raging. The Sandy Fire, Bain Fire, and Santa Rosa Island Fire are all still active, some with low containment. Plus, a new wildfire was reported in LA County just yesterday.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Wildfire Smoke Advisory is ACTIVE!** Despite a reported general AQI of 18, the South Coast AQMD has issued an advisory for widespread moderate smoke impacts across the LA Basin. Areas directly downwind of fires, such as the San Fernando Valley, could experience air quality in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" category. If you smell smoke, your lungs are telling you to stay inside, regardless of what an app says.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures & Other Annoyances

Beyond Eaton Canyon being a no-go zone until way past next year, remember that other parts of the Angeles National Forest, like the Williamson Rock area, are also under long-term closure. Do your homework before you head out, or you'll just be wasting gas and aggravating yourself.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Norovirus Alert on the Pacific Crest Trail!** If you're planning a segment hike, especially near Wrightwood, be extremely cautious. Hikers have reported severe illness, likely from a shared water source. Practice impeccable hygiene, and absolutely do not share water or food with anyone, and definitely don't drink from untreated sources.
</AlertBox>
