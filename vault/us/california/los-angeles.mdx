## L.A. Outdoor Workout Report: September 21, 2026

Alright, Angelenos, let's talk turkey about getting your workout in today. The good news is, the air is clean, the mercury's playing nice, and it’s actually overcast for once, so you won't be baking under the usual L.A. sun. That's a triple-threat win for your lungs and joints. With an AQI of 18, you're looking at some of the best air we've seen in a while.

The main fly in the ointment? If your preferred trails take you anywhere near Eaton Canyon or up into the foothills of Altadena, you're still out of luck. The 2025 Eaton Fire did a number on those areas, and many trails, including the Altadena Crest Trail and the Echo Mountain Trail, remain closed until the end of *next year*. I'm talking actual physical barriers and warnings for fines up to $5,000 if you try to pull a fast one. Don't be that person who needs a rescue because you ignored the closure signs. There are plenty of reopened trails, like the Sunset Ridge Trail, if you're willing to adjust your plans. Also, Chaney Trail Road is closed to vehicles until Fall 2026, so plan accordingly if you typically drive that way to access trailheads.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up, Hikers!** Many trails in the Eaton Canyon and Altadena areas, including the Altadena Crest Trail and Echo Mountain Trail, are still closed due to 2025 Eaton Fire damage. These closures are enforced and last through December 31, 2027. Respect the signs, people.
</AlertBox>

Now, for those of you sticking to the urban jungle, expect some construction headaches if you’re trying to cycle or run near LAX. Los Angeles World Airports has roadwork on Century Boulevard and Sepulveda Boulevard with lane restrictions and even sidewalk closures continuing through mid-November. Similar subway construction is still a thing along Wilshire Boulevard, especially if you're anywhere near Beverly Hills or Century City. It's mostly overnight freeway stuff on the 405 and 101, but always stay vigilant for unexpected detours or uneven pavement.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pavement Pounders & Pedalers:** Be aware of ongoing construction around LAX (Century Blvd and Sepulveda Blvd) and along Wilshire Blvd. Sidewalk closures and lane reductions are in effect. Keep your head up, watch for rerouting, and don't assume the pavement's perfectly smooth.
</AlertBox>

Pollen-wise, we're at a "Moderate" level today. For most of you, that's just background noise, but if you're prone to seasonal sniffles, maybe pop an antihistamine before you head out. It's not a killer, but why suffer if you don't have to?

Griffith Park trails are generally open for business, so if you're tired of the Altadena closures, that's a solid option to get some elevation in without going too far. Just remember that traffic can cause road closures near the Observatory, so maybe bus it or go early if you're heading there.

All in all, today's a pretty decent day for a workout. Just know your local conditions, especially if you're planning a serious hike.
