Forget that "April showers bring May flowers" nonsense, today in Madison is just... chilly.

Today's the kind of day where your motivation needs to be stronger than your desire to stay under a blanket. The good news is the air itself is generally clean with an AQI of 29 and clear skies. The bad news? It's hovering just above freezing, so proper layering isn't optional, it's mandatory. Think technical fabrics, wind-resistant outer layers, and definitely a hat and gloves. Your joints will thank you for the extra warmth.

The real kicker for outdoor enthusiasts is the ongoing saga of construction. The City of Madison's Engineering department has been busy, and it's directly impacting some prime running and biking real estate. The John Nolen Drive reconstruction project is in full swing, which means southbound South Broom Street is closed, and westbound North Shore Drive is locked down through April 22.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Trail Alert: Capital City Trail Detour!** Due to the John Nolen Drive reconstruction, the Capital City Trail is currently detoured around Monona Bay. Plan your route accordingly and be prepared for alternative pathways. This isn't a small jog around a puddle; this is a significant reroute.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just the immediate downtown area. The I-39 construction project between US 12/18 (Beltline) and Lien Road resumed on April 6, bringing nighttime lane closures. While this mostly impacts drivers, increased traffic on alternate routes could spill over to pedestrian and cyclist areas.

If you were hoping to hit up some specific trails, be aware that all Dane County Cross-country Ski, Snowmobile, and Equestrian trails are currently closed for the season. Not that you'd be skiing in 34-degree weather, but hey, you never know what some people are into.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Local Park Activity:** While not outright closures, a "Disc Golf Cleanup Day" is happening at Elver Park this morning, and "Bird & Nature Adventures" are scheduled for Owen Conservation Park this afternoon. Expect a few more folks than usual at those spots. Stay alert and share the space.
</AlertBox>

Overall, if you're determined to get outside, you can. Just dress like you're heading to the Arctic (but a clear, moderate-pollen Arctic), navigate the construction detours with the patience of a saint, and you'll probably earn that post-run coffee.
