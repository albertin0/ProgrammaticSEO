## The Smoke Lingers, So Keep it Indoors (or Slow)

Look, I get it. The sun's out, and at 13.4°C (about 56°F) it feels pretty decent out there for a summer morning. But don't let that "Mainly Clear" condition fool you. We're still breathing in that Canadian wildfire junk, and the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources issued a statewide Air Quality Advisory that's in effect until at least noon today. This isn't just a suggestion, folks; your lungs are literally taking a hit out there.

We saw "very unhealthy" air just yesterday, with Milwaukee under a veil of smoke. While it's *supposed* to be improving, an AQI of 109 due to PM2.5 is still classified as "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." If you've got asthma, COPD, heart disease, or even just sensitive lungs, you really need to take this seriously. Even if you're a healthy, iron-lunged beast, prolonged or intense exercise outside is not recommended right now. You don't want to be huffing and puffing that stuff deep into your respiratory system.

### What About the Lakefront?

Thinking of hitting the Lakefront path? While it's usually a go-to, remember the air quality applies everywhere. Additionally, keep an eye on crowds around McKinley Beach and South Shore Park today. The ArtBlaze event is happening there, which means more people and potentially less room to stretch out or get your stride in without dodging folks enjoying the art installations and music. It's not a hazard, but definitely a factor for runners who prefer clear paths.

<AlertBox type="warning">
