## Why today is... well, *damp*:

<BulletList>
*   Heavy, widespread rain is on the menu for Milwaukee today and tonight, with a solid 94% chance of precipitation, meaning wet trails and potential street ponding are almost guaranteed.
*   Moderate ragweed pollen is still hanging around, so even if you brave the rain, your sinuses might still protest.
*   While the air quality is technically "Good" with an AQI of 50, AccuWeather suggests it's merely "Fair" for sensitive groups, so don't expect crisp, clean breaths, just wet ones.
</BulletList>

Look, the weather folks are calling for "Cloudy during the day and Rain showers at night" with temperatures hovering around a comfortable 15.4°C (60°F), but that high chance of rain isn't going anywhere. We're talking 1-3 inches of widespread accumulation expected from tonight through Thursday morning, which means rivers could rise and low-lying areas might see some minor flooding. So, that scenic loop on the **Oak Leaf Trail** might be less "scenic" and more "soggy obstacle course" in spots.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Caution: Wet and Wild Out There!**
Expect slippery surfaces, reduced visibility, and significant water ponding on roads and low-lying trails. Flooding remains a concern on area rivers, so stay clear of swollen banks. Turn around, don't drown – it's not just for cars.
</AlertBox>
