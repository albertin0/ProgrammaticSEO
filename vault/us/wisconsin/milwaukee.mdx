### Construction and Trail Chaos

The biggest headache for runners and cyclists right now continues to be the never-ending I-94 East-West Freeway Project. If you typically rely on the Hank Aaron State Trail, be advised that the River Loop section is shut down through at least October 2026 for the 16th Street bridge replacement. And a whole other chunk of the Hank Aaron Trail around 32nd Street and Canal Street is also closed indefinitely due to I-94 work, forcing you onto a temporary detour on Canal Street itself. So, you'll be dodging more than just geese out there.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up on W. National Avenue:** The reconstruction project on W. National Avenue, particularly between 39th and 27th Streets, is in full swing. Expect limited on-street parking and active construction from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., Monday through Saturday. If you're running that stretch, stick to designated pedestrian paths and be extra vigilant for construction vehicles and uneven surfaces.
</AlertBox>

Don't forget that West Howard Avenue, specifically at the bridge over Miner Creek, is also closed to both vehicular and pedestrian traffic until October 2026 for bridge improvements. Plan your longer runs with this in mind, especially if you're hitting the south side trails.
