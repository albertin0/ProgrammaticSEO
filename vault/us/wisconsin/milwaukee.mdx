### Extreme Heat and Poor Air Quality Alert!
Forget that run along the Lakefront Path today. With heat index values hitting dangerous levels and wildfire smoke making the air "Very Unhealthy," strenuous outdoor activity is strongly discouraged. Children's Wisconsin is already reporting a rise in critical asthma patients and is urging extra precautions. Limit your time outdoors, especially between noon and 8 PM.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* leave the house, make it short, make it early, and keep it mellow. And for the love of all that is holy, drink water. Lots of it. Stay in the shade, wear light clothing, and know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. The city has cooling sites available if you need them.
