The weather today is a bit of a tease. We've got comfortable temperatures hovering around 11.2°C and thankfully, a break from the relentless rain we've seen recently. Humidity is lower too, which is a blessing for your lungs and generally makes a run feel less like slogging through a swamp. However, don't get too cozy. The National Weather Service is calling for strong thunderstorms, capable of delivering damaging winds and minor flooding across southern portions of central Indiana through mid-evening today. That means if you're hitting the trails south of the city, like heading down towards Southwestway Park, you could be in for a rough ride.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Wind and Water!**
Seriously, those potential 60 mph winds aren't a joke, and while widespread flooding might not be city-wide, recent heavy rains mean some rivers and crops are still under water in central Indiana. Even minor flooding can turn a pleasant run into an ankle-twisting disaster. Stay alert for standing water on trails and avoid any routes known to flood.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the never-ending symphony of jackhammers and detour signs. Downtown is a labyrinth. Capitol Avenue and Illinois Street are seeing closures for the Indiana Convention Center expansion and the new Signia by Hilton. Georgia Street, usually a pedestrian-friendly stretch, is currently closed to both cars and foot traffic between Capitol Avenue and Illinois Street. So, if your usual route involves anything near the Circle Centre Mall or the convention center, plan on an obstacle course.

The IndyGo Blue Line construction on Washington Street is another headache. You're looking at various lane restrictions and traffic shifts, especially near White River State Park and the Zoo. Eastbound traffic on Washington Street from Southeastern Avenue to State Avenue is straight-up closed for storm sewer work until summer. That means your commute or casual jog along that stretch is going to be a mess.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Downtown is a Maze!**
Seriously, if you're planning a run through the heart of the city, assume every block has a surprise closure or an unannounced lane shift. Capitol, Illinois, and Georgia Streets are all impacted. Stick to routes you know are clear, or be prepared for some creative navigation.
</AlertBox>

Even some of our beloved greenways aren't entirely free from the construction bug. While the Nickel Plate Trail connection project was supposedly wrapping up last year, there are still "line of sight clearing" and "trip hazard repairs" happening. Always keep an eye out for uneven surfaces, especially after all that rain.
