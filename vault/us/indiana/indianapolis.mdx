## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   **Weather's a Gem (mostly):** The skies are clear, and it's a mild 13.3°C (that's about 56°F for you Fahrenheit die-hards). Perfect running temps if you ask me. No extreme heat, no downpours, just a nice, gentle breeze. Don't expect any precipitation, so no soggy shoes.
*   **Air Quality is a Mixed Bag:** While the general AQI might hover around "Moderate" for most of us, AccuWeather is calling today "Poor" for air quality, especially for sensitive groups, due to ozone and other pollutants. The American Lung Association's latest report wasn't exactly a glowing review of Indiana's air either. So, if your lungs are finicky, pay attention.
*   **Pollen's Doing Its Thing:** It's officially "Moderate" pollen season, which means some of you will be sniffling and sneezing your way around the Monon or any other green space you choose to conquer.
*   **Watch Your Route, Northeast Siders:** If your usual loop takes you anywhere near East 71st Street, you're gonna need a new plan. The 71st Street trail is officially shut down as of yesterday, June 1, for the "Clear Path" project, and it's expected to stay closed for up to 60 days. They're painting, paving, and doing bridge work. Plan your detours now; 75th Street is your friend.
</BulletList>

## The Rundown for Your Run

You've got prime running weather, which is a win. However, it's not all sunshine and PRs out there today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Sensitive Lungs, Take Note
Even though the overall AQI is 53 (Moderate), AccuWeather is flagging today's air quality as "Poor" for sensitive groups, with an AQI around 61 for nitrogen dioxide (NO2). If you have asthma, COPD, or just generally cough your way through bad air days, consider dialing back your intensity or keeping your workout indoors. We're also seeing moderate pollen levels, so if you're an allergy sufferer, pre-dose your meds.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### 71st Street Trail Closure - Major Headache
Seriously, folks, don't just show up expecting to run your usual route on the 71st Street trail. It's closed, effective June 1, for a solid two months. This is part of the I-465 "Clear Path" project, focusing on improvements under I-465 and the overhead bridges. This isn't a small inconvenience; it's a full closure of both the street and the trail. Your official detour is 75th Street. Don't be that person trying to sneak through; it's not safe and it slows down the crews. Find another path for a while, maybe explore a different section of the Monon Trail or head west to Eagle Creek Park.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate, Even in Mild Temps
Just because it's not scorching doesn't mean you can skip the water. You're still sweating, especially if you're pushing it. Stay on top of your hydration. Also, remember to apply sunscreen. Even on clear, mild days, those UV rays still get through.
</AlertBox>

Look, the city isn't shutting down, and neither should you. But be smart. Check that air quality on your preferred app, reroute around the 71st Street mess, and if your allergies are flaring up, maybe stick to the treadmill today. Your body will thank you.
