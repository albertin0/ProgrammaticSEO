## The Rundown: Your Lungs & Joints Edition

  **Pro Tip:** If you're susceptible to pollen, hit the showers immediately after your outdoor workout. Get those allergens off your skin and out of your hair before they make a permanent home in your sinuses. Overcast skies can actually keep pollen counts slightly lower at times, but "Moderate" is still enough to bother sensitive folks.
</AlertBox>

### Weather & Air: The Mixed Bag

The low AQI is fantastic. You can breathe easy, at least from a pollution standpoint. The "Ozone/Other" dominant pollutant is minor at this level. Just remember, despite the current pleasant overcast, those scattered storms the forecast warned about for the weekend could still pop up today. Don't be "that runner" caught miles from home in a sudden downpour or, worse, a severe wind storm. Check the radar one more time before you head out.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Watch the Sky:** Scattered rain and potential thunder are still on the menu. If it starts looking ominous, cut your run short or find cover. Nobody needs to be a lightning rod.
</AlertBox>

### Your Favorite Trails? Probably Not Today.

This is where things get annoying. Indianapolis has a bad habit of closing trails without a lot of advance notice, or the notices just don't stick around.

*   **Monon Trail:** Forget your long run up north. The Monon Trail is **CLOSED between 156th and 169th Streets** for major tunnel construction. This closure is expected to last until the end of the year. Don't try to "just squeeze through." Seriously.
*   **71st Street Trail (under I-465):** Another headache. The 71st Street trail, under I-465, is **closed through at least today, August 2nd, and possibly longer**, for final improvements and cleanup from the Clear Path construction. They're doing painting, paving, and bridge work. Use the detour via 75th Street.
*   **White River Greenway:** If you typically run along the White River downtown, portions are also still a mess. The White River Greenway is **closed between Kentucky Avenue and Washington Street** due to ongoing Henry Street Bridge construction, a closure that's been in place for a while and is expected to last until *fall 2026*. You'll be detoured onto city streets and parts of the Cultural Trail there.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Trail Blockages Ahead:** Major sections of the Monon Trail (156th-169th St), 71st Street Trail (under I-465), and the White River Greenway (near Henry Street Bridge) are currently CLOSED. Plan your route accordingly to avoid frustration and unsafe detours.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, it's a good day to run *if* you know where you're going and you're flexible. Maybe explore some neighborhood streets, or hit the treadmill if dodging construction cones isn't your idea of a good time.
