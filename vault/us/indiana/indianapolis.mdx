### Know Before You Go: Construction & Thunderstorms

Don't let that clear sky fool you entirely, though. While the air feels fresh, the ground you're running on might be a bit... fragmented.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Watch Out for White River Greenway Closures**
The Henry Street Bridge project is still dragging on, meaning a significant chunk of the White River Greenway (aka the White River Trail) is *closed* between Kentucky Avenue and Washington Street. This isn't a quick fix; it’s expected to last until at least Fall 2026. If that's part of your usual route, plan on detours along Washington Street and sections of the Indianapolis Cultural Trail. This is a major inconvenience, so don't get caught off guard staring at a "Trail Closed" sign.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you're hitting up the west side near downtown, double-check your map. It’s a bummer, but you're better off knowing now than finding yourself re-routing mid-run.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Downtown Digs & Interstate Disruptions**
General construction around downtown Indianapolis continues to be a beast. Capitol Avenue and Illinois Street near the convention center are seeing ongoing closures. And don't forget the Morris Street bridge over I-65, which has been closed since Friday, August 21st, for rehabilitation, aiming to reopen in early September. This work will improve pedestrian access with wider sidewalks eventually, but for now, it's another headache for anyone trying to navigate on foot. Keep an eye on sidewalk access if you're jogging through the heart of the city.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Isolated Storms Possible**
The National Weather Service has indicated that isolated thunderstorms are possible today across central Indiana. While severe weather isn't expected, brief heavy rain and lightning are always a risk with any storm. Look up at the sky occasionally, folks. If it starts to look iffy, cut your run short. No Strava segment is worth becoming a lightning rod.
</AlertBox>

So, grab your shoes, enjoy that perfect crisp air and comfortable temperature, but be smart about your route. Our city's always growing, which means our running routes are sometimes in flux. Don't let construction or a surprise sprinkle ruin an otherwise perfect workout.
