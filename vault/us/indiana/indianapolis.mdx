Alright, let's cut to the chase, runners. Today's forecast for Indianapolis is less "ideal long run" and more "sprint to shelter." We're currently seeing light rain and temperatures around 72°F, but it's already feeling like 76°F thanks to that lovely 73% humidity. Don't let that fool you into thinking it's a cool drizzle, though. The National Weather Service is calling for scattered thunderstorms throughout the day, with a 40% chance of rain. The mercury is set to climb to a high of 86°F, and with the humidity cranking up, it's going to *feel* closer to 90°F this weekend. If you're out there pushing it, your body's going to be working overtime just to keep cool.

Now, about the air you're breathing. Your fancy apps might be telling you the Air Quality Index (AQI) is "Good" at 48 today, and yeah, that's technically true. The dominant pollutant is still listed as Ozone/Other. Just two days ago, on Thursday, the Indiana Department of Environmental Management (IDEM) slapped an Air Quality Action Day on Marion County, warning that ozone levels were "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups." Those warnings were tied to warm, dry, and sunny conditions, which, ironically, are what we're heading into, albeit with some rain thrown in. So, while it's "Good" now, keep an eye on it, especially if you're sensitive to that invisible gunk.

And don't forget your allergies. Pollen is sitting at a "Moderate" level, so if you're prone to seasonal sniffles, you'll be feeling it.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Your Step (and Your Head)
Be aware of wet, slick surfaces from the scattered rain and potential thunderstorms. If you hear thunder, get inside. No PR is worth getting struck by lightning. Also, if you're an early bird, dense fog was reported south of I-70 this morning, so visibility could still be an issue in spots.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zone Ahead
Indianapolis is constantly under construction, and 2026 is no different. Indy DPW has a massive $279 million budget for infrastructure projects, including improvements to roads, sidewalks, and *trails*. Specifically, the much-anticipated extension of the Indianapolis Cultural Trail across the White River, near the Henry Street Bridge, is still a work in progress, with delays impacting its original Fall 2026 completion. While some pavers are reportedly laid, don't expect a clear path. You'll likely encounter temporary closures, uneven terrain, or detours on other greenways like the Eagle Creek or Pleasant Run Greenways as well. Always be on the lookout for construction signage.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate!
With those "feels like" temperatures nearing 90°F and high humidity, dehydration and heat-related illnesses are a real risk. Drink more water than you think you need, before, during, and after your workout. Don't be a hero; take breaks and find shade. Your kidneys will thank you.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. Be smart out there. Indy's a great city to run, but not if you end up sidelined by heat stroke or a twisted ankle from dodging a construction cone in a downpour.
