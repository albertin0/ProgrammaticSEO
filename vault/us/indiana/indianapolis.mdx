Alright, Indy, if you're thinking about an outdoor workout today, July 26th, you better be quick and keep your head on a swivel.

<BulletList>
*   **Wildfire Smoke's Back:** Despite what the air quality app might tell you with a low number, IDEM is still forecasting "unhealthy for sensitive groups" for central Indiana due to Canadian wildfire smoke and elevated PM2.5 levels. Your lungs aren't going to appreciate it, especially if you're prone to breathing issues.
*   **Storms Are Brewing:** The National Weather Service is calling for scattered strong to possibly severe storms developing rapidly south of I-70 through 2 PM today. We're talking wind gusts over 60 mph, large hail, and yeah, even a weak tornado isn't off the table. Isolated minor flooding is also a possibility. Don't be that person caught miles out on the Monon Trail when the sky opens up or a funnel cloud drops.
*   **Construction Chaos Continues:** Our fair city is a perpetual construction zone, and today's no different. The 71st Street trail under I-465 is still shut down for improvements until early August. You'll be rerouted to 75th Street, which, let's be honest, is probably just more traffic. Also, Washington Street is a mess with Blue Line BRT work; multiple sections are partially or fully closed with pedestrian detours. If you're trying to walk or run anywhere near there, expect headaches.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Severe Weather Threat: Get Indoors by 2 PM
Seriously, folks, this isn't a drill. Strong winds, large hail, and even a weak tornado are possible. If you're out there, know your escape route. If you see dark clouds, don't risk it for that last mile. Head for cover. Paul Ruster Park might be hosting LARP, but that doesn't mean you want to be live-action role-playing a tornado victim.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Wildfire Smoke for Sensitive Lungs
While the overall AQI might seem "Good" on paper (49, in this case), the state forecast for PM2.5 levels today in central Indiana is predicting "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" due to wildfire smoke. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or even just feel a bit off, keep your workout indoors or significantly reduce intensity. That morning "haze" isn't always just humidity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route Around Construction
With the 71st Street trail closure and the ongoing Blue Line work tearing up Washington Street, don't just head out blindly. Check Indy DPW's updates or just assume your usual path is probably blocked. There are plenty of other options, or just hit the treadmill today. Your sanity (and shins) will thank you.
</AlertBox>

Frankly, with the storm threat and the lingering smoke, today's a great day for indoor cardio or hitting the weights. Save the long runs for tomorrow, assuming those "feels like 100-110" temperatures for Monday don't materialize.
