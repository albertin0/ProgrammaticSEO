## The Rundown: Your Daily Dose of Reality

Look, the weather's pretty sweet today, sitting at a comfortable 16.4°C and "Mainly Clear." No excuses there. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a smooth ride.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Downtown is a Detour Wonderland
Seriously, if your usual route takes you anywhere near Capitol Avenue, Illinois Street, or Georgia Street downtown, plan an alternate. Capitol Avenue between Ohio Street and South Street is closed for through traffic, and Illinois Street between South Street and Georgia Street is also shut down. Georgia Street itself is closed between Capitol Avenue and Illinois Street. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on re-routing challenge for pedestrians and runners. The IndyGo Blue Line construction on Washington Street is also causing lane closures and even full westbound traffic detours around the Rockville Road to Belmont Avenue stretch. Don't get caught off guard; check your maps *before* you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### AQI Discrepancy Alert
While the data provided indicates a low AQI of 34, some forecasts are showing "Poor" air quality for Indianapolis today and the next few days, specifically unhealthy for sensitive groups. If you're sensitive to air pollution or have respiratory issues, maybe give that window a sniff test, or check another local air quality monitor before committing to a long outdoor session. When in doubt, err on the side of caution.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Major Road Construction Continues
Beyond downtown, major arteries like I-65 and I-465 have ongoing long-term construction projects. The I-65 SB Exit Ramp to I-465 is under long-term construction until November, and there are bridge constructions causing shoulder closures on I-865. While these mostly impact drivers, remember that construction often means increased dust, heavy machinery, and unexpected detours even for those on foot or bike, especially if you're near overpasses or interchanges.
</AlertBox>

Enjoy your run, if you can find a clear path.
