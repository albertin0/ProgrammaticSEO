### Today's Rundown

Honestly, if you're planning an outdoor workout in Indianapolis today, you've got a few screws loose. The forecast is screaming "stay home." We're currently sitting at a cool 15.2°C (about 59°F) and it's overcast, which might *sound* pleasant if you ignore the small detail of impending doom. [cite: weather, 2] But don't let that fool you. The National Weather Service in Indianapolis issued a Flood Watch starting this afternoon, with rainfall totals of 1 to 3 inches expected. This isn't just a puddle; this is "your trail disappearing under muddy water" kind of rain. Given that Governor Mike Braun already declared a state of disaster emergency for 63 Indiana counties following "this week's devastating storms" with severe flooding and tornadic activity, the ground is already saturated, and we don't need more.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**IMMINENT DANGER: Severe Storms & Flooding!**
A "Weather Impact Alert Day" is active for Sunday. Severe storms, damaging winds, and isolated tornadoes are possible from 5 PM to midnight. A Flood Watch is also in effect. Do not, under any circumstances, try to "beat the storm" or run through floodwaters. Turn around, don't drown.
</AlertBox>

Even if you manage to dodge the severe weather, which, good luck with that, the trails aren't exactly pristine. If you were thinking of hitting the **71st Street Trail**, forget about it. It's been closed since the beginning of June for final improvements, and that closure is expected to last for about two months. They're painting, paving, and doing overhead bridge work, so unless your workout involves dodging construction equipment, pick another spot.

And speaking of trails, a heads-up if you frequent the **Eagle Creek Trail** near the golf course. There are ongoing issues with golfers using sections of the trail as overflow parking. Yes, you read that right – cars parked on a *trail*. It's a safety hazard for runners, walkers, and cyclists alike. If you do venture out there after the rain (and when the severe weather clears, which won't be today), keep an eye out for confused drivers or rogue car doors. Better yet, stick to paths where vehicles know their place.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Localized Trail Hazards:**
The 71st Street Trail is closed for approximately two months due to construction. Additionally, be aware of vehicles illegally parking on parts of the Eagle Creek Trail near the golf club. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>
