<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Air Quality Advisory: Moderate but Persistent
  Our Air Quality Index is sitting at 79 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. While technically "Moderate," let's not pretend it's mountain air. Indianapolis has a *history* of ranking high in particle pollution year-round. So, if you're one of the many sensitive souls with asthma, allergies, or other respiratory issues, treat this "Moderate" like it's trying to trick you. Take it easy, consider shortening your long runs, and definitely listen to your body.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Downtown is a Demolition Derby (Still)
  If your usual route takes you through the heart of the city, brace yourself. The massive $625 million hotel and convention center project at Pan Am Plaza is still chugging along, aiming for completion this summer. And let's not forget the ongoing "Traction Yards" redevelopment of Circle Centre Mall, which involves literally tearing the roof off. Trying to weave through that mess near Lucas Oil Stadium or the Indiana Convention Center means dodging orange barrels, navigating sudden sidewalk closures, and generally having your zen run shattered. Unless you enjoy the obstacle course vibe, maybe find a different scenery today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Trail Talk: Monon's Your Best Bet
  With the lovely temperatures, hitting a trail is a smart move. While the Nickel Plate Trail's Indy portion should be largely open by now after its planned June 2025 completion, remember they've still got future pedestrian bridges to build, so don't assume a completely pristine path forever. Your safest bet for a clear, less congested run is still likely the Monon Trail. Head north, escape the downtown gridlock, and enjoy that clear-ish sky and comfortable breeze. The Cardinal Greenway also had some I-70 construction detours that have reportedly been *lifted* north of Richmond as of March 11, 2026, so that's good news if you're out that way.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's shaping up to be a pretty comfortable day for a workout, assuming you're prepared for the usual Indianapolis air quality caveats and the downtown construction circus. Pick your poison, or rather, pick your path wisely.
