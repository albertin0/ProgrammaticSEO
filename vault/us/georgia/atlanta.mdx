## The Rundown: Your Body, Your Call

Look, it's not a complete wash-out, but you need to be smart out there.

### Air Quality & Allergies

Let's be real, "moderate" AQI often feels worse when you're pushing your pace. The fact that PM2.5 is the main culprit means those tiny particles can dig deep into your lungs. Pair that with moderate pollen, and it's a double whammy for anyone with respiratory sensitivities or seasonal allergies.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**If you have asthma, allergies, or any lung condition, seriously consider an indoor workout today.** Even at "moderate" AQI, exertion amplifies your exposure to PM2.5. Keep your inhaler handy if you choose to venture out.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Navigate the Beltline Battlefield

The Beltline is fantastic, but right now, it's a project in progress. That construction on Moreland Avenue impacting the Eastside Trolley Trail is no joke; weaving into vehicle traffic is never ideal for runners or cyclists. And that train wreck on the westside means you might encounter emergency personnel, curious onlookers, or unexpected detours if you're on or near the Westside Beltline Trail around Lee & White.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Exercise extreme caution on the Atlanta Beltline today, especially near active construction zones on the Eastside and the recent train collision site on the Westside.** Stick to sections you know are clear, or find an alternate route away from traffic and potential gawkers.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Window: A Brief Reprieve

Today's temperature is pretty much perfect. Take advantage of it for a brisk walk or a short run before the sauna-like conditions set in. Remember that prolonged exposure to the upcoming high heat index can be dangerous.
