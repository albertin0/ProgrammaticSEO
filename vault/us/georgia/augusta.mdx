## Today's Rundown

Good news, folks, it looks like Augusta is giving us a pretty decent day to stretch our legs outside. Forget the heat domes roasting other parts of the country; we're sitting pretty with mild temperatures and mostly clear skies. Your lungs should be happy, as the Air Quality Index is a low 38, firmly in the "Good" category. That means you can push the pace without worrying about sucking down anything nasty.

However, if you're one of the many Augustans who starts itching at the mere mention of fall, consider yourself warned: pollen is at a moderate level today. We're talking ragweed, grass, and chenopods making their presence known. So, before you hit the pavement or the trails, make sure you've taken your allergy meds. There's nothing worse than a runny nose and itchy eyes halfway through a tempo run by the Savannah River.

Speaking of trails, the Augusta Canal Towpath is a local gem, and officially, the Augusta Canal Authority states the Towpath trail is now open from the Headgates to the I-20 Bridge. However, this trail system has a history of closures due to maintenance and hurricane damage, like what happened with Hurricane Helene a couple of years back. While the latest official word is "open," local conditions can change fast, especially with ongoing projects or minor issues that don't make headline news. Always stay vigilant.
