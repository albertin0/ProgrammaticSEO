Look, I get it. The sun's out, it's "mainly clear," and you've got that prerun buzz. But let's apply some common sense, shall we? This isn't just about feeling a little sweaty. The National Weather Service Memphis has issued a Heat Advisory until Saturday evening, warning of high temperatures and those lovely triple-digit heat index values. That's prime territory for heat exhaustion and heat stroke, which, last I checked, don't exactly improve your PR. We're talking about unseasonable, record-breaking warmth for mid-September.

And while you're out there cooking your internal organs, don't forget to take a deep breath of that Code Orange air. The Shelby County Health Department, along with other agencies, has put out an Air Quality Alert. "Unhealthy for sensitive groups" means anyone with asthma, COPD, or even just regular human lungs will be working harder than they should just to pull in enough oxygen. It’s primarily due to elevated ozone levels, which aren't exactly lung-friendly.

You want to hit the trails around **Overton Park**? Be smart. The "Third Thursday Treks" later today might tempt you, but remember what you're breathing. Even if you're feeling good, your body is under stress you might not immediately recognize.
