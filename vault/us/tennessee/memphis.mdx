## The Rundown for Runners

You're looking at muggy, overcast conditions with a 35% chance of scattered thunderstorms during the day. The temperature is hovering around 20.1°C (68°F), which isn't terrible, but combined with the humidity and rain, it's going to feel heavier than you'd like.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Flash Flood Risk is Real!** The National Weather Service in Memphis has warned of an isolated flash flooding risk daily through the next week. If you see standing water, especially on trails, for the love of your knees (and your life), *turn around*. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just for cars. Muddy conditions have also been reported on parts of the Wolf River trails after heavy rains, so expect that to be amplified.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown is a Race Zone.** If your usual route takes you along the Mississippi River or through downtown, know that the Memphis in May Great American River Run is monopolizing those streets and pathways this morning. Plan an alternative, or simply cheer from the sidelines. Also, watch out for the ongoing MLGW utility construction closing parts of North Parkway and Manassa Street. This isn't just an inconvenience; it can be a hazard for pedestrians near equipment and unexpected detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Moderate Pollen, Heavy Hydration.** While not a "high" day, moderate pollen can still irritate. If you're sensitive, consider an antihistamine before you head out, and keep those eyes covered. With the muggy conditions and potential for rain, staying hydrated is crucial even if it doesn't feel scorching hot.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, there's a new storybook trail that just opened at Tom Lee Park today, which is pretty cool for families. Just be mindful of the race crowds if you're planning to check it out this morning.
