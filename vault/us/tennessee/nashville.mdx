Let's cut to the chase: your lungs are going to hate you if you try to crush it outside today. While the official "pollen" level is just moderate, the Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a less-than-stellar 116. More concerning, some local forecasts are calling today's air quality "Poor" and explicitly "unhealthy for sensitive groups," with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That particulate matter is insidious – it gets deep into your lungs and can cause irritation, coughing, and even exacerbate existing respiratory or heart conditions. So, unless you enjoy feeling like you've been chain-smoking after a gentle jog, maybe dial back the intensity or move your workout indoors.

Then there's the heat. It's October 25th, and we're looking at a ridiculous 28.3°C (83°F) with "mainly clear" skies, meaning that sun is going to be beating down on you. We've seen plenty of heat advisories this year, and while today isn't officially one, pushing hard in this kind of weather is just asking for trouble. Dehydration, heat exhaustion, and generally feeling like warmed-over garbage are all on the menu. Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate, or better yet, move that workout to the treadmill where the AC actually works.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### AQI & Heat Warning
With an AQI of 116 (and some reports indicating "Poor" air quality for sensitive groups) combined with 28.3°C (83°F) temperatures, strenuous outdoor activity is highly discouraged. Reduce time spent outside and listen to your body – especially if you have respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if you manage to brave the air and the heat, good luck navigating your usual routes. Metro Parks has a habit of closing things down for necessary work, and this week is no exception. The Richland Creek Greenway, a favorite for many, still has its boardwalk from Colorado Avenue to the 51st Avenue area closed for replacement, a project expected to last for several more weeks. Over at **Shelby Bottoms Greenway**, if you were planning on a peaceful long run, think again. The Bendelow Lane Bridge in Shelby Park is undergoing a four-month replacement, and the boardwalk near the observation deck on the greenway is also closed for repairs. That means detours, unexpected traffic, and potentially less-than-ideal surfaces when you're trying to stay focused.
