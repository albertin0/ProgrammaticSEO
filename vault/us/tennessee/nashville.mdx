Look, I get it. The temperature today is a comfortable 20.8°C (around 70°F), and "clear" conditions usually scream "get out there!" But that AQI of 116 for PM2.5 means fine particulate matter is floating around, ready to get into your lungs and bloodstream. If you're someone with asthma, heart disease, or are otherwise a "sensitive group" (which, let's be honest, applies to most of us if we're pushing hard), you should seriously consider scaling back. This isn't the ozone we typically complain about in summer, but PM2.5 can still originate from vehicle exhaust, construction sites, and even distant wildfire smoke. While no active wildfires in the immediate area were reported in the last 24 hours, Nashville *did* get an F grade for ozone pollution in the 2026 American Lung Association report, so our air isn't exactly pristine even on a good day.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Unhealthy Air Quality!** With an AQI of 116 driven by PM2.5, sensitive groups should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Healthy individuals might also experience respiratory symptoms. This isn't just about feeling a little winded; PM2.5 can have serious health implications for your heart and lungs.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you were planning a long run down the Richland Creek Greenway, know that the segment including the trailhead and parking lot at 22 White Bridge Road is closed until November 6th. The boardwalk from Colorado Avenue to the 51st Avenue area is also out of commission for several weeks. Don't be that person trying to hop barriers or create your own trail through someone's backyard. Just don't. And speaking of closures, the north side of Centennial Park is under construction through Fall 2026, so adjust your routes there too.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Greenway Closures & Construction:** Expect detours and blocked paths on sections of the Richland Creek Greenway (near 22 White Bridge Road and the boardwalk from Colorado Ave to 51st Ave) and the north side of Centennial Park.
