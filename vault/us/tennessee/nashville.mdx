Look, I'm usually the first one to tell you to suck it up and embrace the swamp-like conditions of a Nashville summer. But today, June 25, 2026, is a gift from the weather gods. We're talking clear skies and a temperature that won't make you question every life choice you've ever made. The air quality index is a beautiful 31, which means your lungs won't feel like they're trying to filter concrete dust.

However, don't let this glorious weather lull you into a false sense of security regarding your route. While the sky is clear, some of our favorite spots are a hot mess of construction and repairs. If your usual loop takes you through **Centennial Park**, you're going to hit some snags. The parking lot across from the Sportsplex is shut down until June 27, and a section of the trail near the Bandshell is closed for maintenance for the next four days. Oh, and that north side of the Lake Watauga Loop? Still closed from early May. So, adjust your expectations, or just go run some hills in Percy Warner instead.

The moderate pollen count is really the only ding on an otherwise stellar outdoor day. For most, it's just a minor annoyance, but if you're a serious allergy sufferer, you know the drill.
