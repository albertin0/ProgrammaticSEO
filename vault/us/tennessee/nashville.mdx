## Today's Nashville Nuisances: Rain, Rank Air, and Reroutes

Let's just be brutally honest: Mother Nature is not on our side today, Nashville. We're staring down a day of heavy rain with temperatures hovering around a damp 14.3°C (that's about 58°F for those who prefer freedom units). You're going to get wet, and the trails are going to be a slick, muddy mess. Historically, heavy rain in Nashville means rivers and creeks swelling, with flash flood warnings not unheard of. Unless you enjoy running through a car wash, maybe find an indoor option.

And if getting drenched wasn't enough, we've got an Air Quality Index of 116, flagged as "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" due to PM2.5. The American Lung Association already gave our metro an 'F' for ozone pollution in 2026, so adding fine particulate matter to the mix is just rubbing salt in the wound. If you have asthma, allergies, or just a general aversion to coughing up a lung, seriously consider scaling back or staying inside.
