## What's Happening Out There

Lexington woke up to the aftermath of a "wild field storm" that ripped through the area last night, bringing with it tornado watches, heavy rain, hail, and gusty winds. First responders have been busy dealing with damage, with reports of partial wall collapses along Versailles Road from the high winds. While things might seem calm *right now*, consider it the eye of a smaller storm, because more severe weather is expected to "roar back" this afternoon and evening. Mayor Linda Gorton is even holding a press conference.

This isn't just a drizzle we're talking about; we're dealing with the lingering effects of a significant weather event and the potential for a repeat performance. You might think that 14.6°C (around 58°F) and clear skies are perfect running weather, and under normal circumstances, you'd be right. But these aren't normal circumstances. The danger of falling limbs, slick surfaces from yesterday's rain, and the very real threat of rapidly deteriorating conditions make any extended outdoor workout a bad idea.
