## The Good, The Bad, and The Gritty

Look, the air quality (AQI 37) is in the "Good" range, which honestly, after some of our recent years, feels like a minor miracle. That's great news for your lungs. But don't let that lull you into a false sense of security; a recent report from late April highlighted that Louisville's air quality has *worsened* for ozone smog, ranking us 22nd worst in the nation. So, while today's numbers are looking pretty, the underlying issue still lingers.

Temperature-wise, 20.5°C (about 69°F) with clear skies is prime running weather. You can't ask for much better in May. Just be aware that by Sunday morning (May 10th), a Frost Advisory kicks in from 3-9 AM EDT, with temperatures expected to drop into the low to mid-30s. So, don't ditch the layers entirely if you're an early bird tomorrow.

The real headache today, and for the foreseeable future, is the city's relentless construction. If you usually hit the pavement, you're going to have to get creative. The popular Ironman 70.3 Louisville race was straight-up *canceled* for 2026 because ongoing city road construction prevents the necessary closures. That should tell you everything you need to know about the scale of disruption.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos: Waterfront Park and Beyond**
Expect lane and potential sidewalk closures on major arteries. Specifically, the I-264/U.S. 42 (Brownsboro Road) improvement project is in full swing (Phase 4, May-June 2026), with daily/nightly lane closures and weekend ramp closures on I-264 West to U.S. 42. Various lane closures are also impacting I-64 near Cannons Lane and I-264, U.S. 60 (Shelbyville Road) with an extended Gilliland Road detour, and KY 1932 (Chenoweth Lane). Even sections of the Louisville Loop are compromised, with the Riverwalk near Portland Wharf Park closed due to bank erosion and detours in place. Avoid the section of the Loop between Mile Markers 10.75-13.75 on Camp Ground Road as it lacks a pedestrian pathway entirely.
</AlertBox>
