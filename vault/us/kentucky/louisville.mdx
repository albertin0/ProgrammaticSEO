## The Down and Dirty Details

**Air Quality & Your Lungs:**
While the immediate reading is "Good," Louisville Metro Air Pollution Control District (APCD) tracks this stuff, and we live in a zone where ground-level ozone is a known pain, especially this time of year. That "Ozone/Other" dominant pollutant on an otherwise good day is your whisper of caution. If you've got asthma or any other respiratory issues, heed the "Poor" forecast for sensitive groups. Don't be that person hacking up a lung on the *Tarc* after pushing it too hard.

**Weather & Your Joints:**
Look, 68 degrees and clear skies? That's what we dream about during the humid hellscape of August. Your knees, ankles, and everything in between will thank you for getting out. Just remember that sunshine can make you forget to hydrate properly, even when it's not scorching. So, grab that water bottle.

## Local Shenanigans & Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Bourbon & Beyond Festival: Avoid the Kentucky Expo Center Area!
Today kicks off Bourbon & Beyond, and if you thought traffic was bad before, just try getting near the Kentucky Exposition Center. Phillips Lane from Monarch Drive to Freedom Way is *closed* from 8:30 AM to 1 AM *every day* through Sunday. You'll also see Interstate 264 ramp closures during the evenings. Seriously, unless you're going to the festival, just stay away from Phillips Lane and the surrounding roads entirely. There's no "quick way" around thousands of people trying to see Foo Fighters.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Butchertown Greenway & Louisville Loop Closures
Heads up, trail runners and cyclists! A chunk of the Butchertown Greenway is *still* closed between River Road and Story Avenue. They're rebuilding the Beargrass Creek bridge as part of that I-71 widening project, and it won't reopen until Fall 2027. Find an alternate route like Frankfort or Edith Avenue if that's your usual stomping ground. Also, the Ohio River Valley section of the Louisville Loop (near Portland Wharf Park) is closed due to bank erosion. Plan your long runs accordingly, folks. Don't end up on a washed-out path thinking you can jump it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Downtown Construction and Pedestrian Awareness
Expect overnight lane closures on Brook Street between College Street and Broadway through tomorrow. And soon, Jacob Street is getting permanently closed when Broadway reopens under I-65. More generally, September means more pedestrians out and about, especially near schools as kids settle into routines, and with daylight shrinking, visibility drops for both drivers and walkers. Keep your head on a swivel if you're pounding pavement in residential areas.
</AlertBox>
