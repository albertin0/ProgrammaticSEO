Strong to severe thunderstorms, complete with potential damaging winds and localized flash flooding, are no joke when you're caught on the Louisville Loop or out by Cherokee Park. An isolated tornado or two isn't out of the question either. Seriously, plan any outdoor activity for the early morning hours, *before* those storms decide to crash the party.

If you're trying to bike or run through Old Louisville, particularly near the I-65 corridor, be aware of ongoing construction. Plus, Industry Road at 4th Street is completely shut down for concrete work for the next four days. Don't get rerouted into traffic; map your path beforehand.
