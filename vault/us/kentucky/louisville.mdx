### Seriously, Keep Your Lungs Indoors Today
An AQI of 154 means "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," and the dominant pollutant, PM2.5, is nasty stuff that gets deep into your lungs. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or heart conditions, you *need* to stay inside. Even healthy individuals may experience throat irritation and breathing difficulties with prolonged exertion. Don't be a hero; your lungs will thank you.
</AlertBox>
