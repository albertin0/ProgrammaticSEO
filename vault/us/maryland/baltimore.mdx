## Today's Tour de Force: Heat, Haze, and Headaches

Look, it's August in Baltimore. We know what that means: humidity that clings like a wet blanket and temperatures that make you question every life choice that led you to consider an outdoor workout. Today, that's cranked up a notch. We're talking a "feels like" temperature of 94°F. That's not just uncomfortable; it's a legitimate risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke, especially when you're trying to add miles.

Pair that with a "moderate" AQI. While 83 might sound okay on paper, the dominant pollutant is PM2.5 – those tiny particles that love to burrow deep into your lungs and cause irritation. If you've got asthma, allergies, or just generally enjoy breathing, you need to be cautious. Your daily pollen count isn't helping either.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** The heat index is hitting 94°F (34.4°C) today. This isn't a joke, folks. Dehydration happens fast, and heat stroke can sneak up on you quicker than a tourist trying to cut through your long run on the waterfront. Seriously, scale back, hydrate aggressively, or move indoors.
</AlertBox>

### Downtown Detours and Dodgy Drainage

If you're one of those brave (or foolish) souls who enjoys running the urban labyrinth, prepare for a logistical nightmare. The Baltimore Orioles are playing, so expect your usual routes around Camden Yards to be blocked off. Hamburg Street, Camden Street, and the northbound service lane by M&T Bank Stadium will be impacted starting mid-morning. Don't be that runner yelling at traffic cones; they won't move.

And remember that flash flood warning for Baltimore County yesterday? While the city might have dodged the worst of it, don't be surprised if some lower-lying areas, especially parts of the Gwynns Falls Trail that hug stream banks, are still a muddy mess or have some lingering debris. It's always a good idea to check conditions before heading out, particularly after heavy rain.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction & Event Closures:** Avoid running near Oriole Park at Camden Yards today. Road closures for the Orioles game will snarl traffic and block pedestrian access in the immediate vicinity. Consider rerouting or hitting a park further from the downtown core.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional:** With this heat and humidity, you're losing fluids faster than you think. Drink water before, during, and after your workout. Electrolytes are your friend. Don't wait until you're thirsty; that's too late.
</AlertBox>
