## Today's Baltimore Beatdown (or Not)

Look, I get it. You woke up, saw "Mainly Clear" and a comfortable 20.1°C (about 68°F) on your weather app, and thought, "Alright, let's crush some miles." And for a good chunk of the morning, that might be true. But don't get too comfy, because the forecast I'm seeing for the rest of Monday, June 22nd, tells a different story: think cloudy, 77°F (25°C) feeling more like 80°F (26°C), and a 75% chance of rain rolling in. So, that crisp, clear run could turn into a humid, wet slog pretty quickly. You've been warned.

### Air You Breathe, Trails You Tread

Our Air Quality Index is sitting at 83, which puts us squarely in the "Moderate" category with PM2.5 as the main culprit. For most of us iron-lunged runners, that's fine. But if you're prone to a cough, or your asthma kicks up, maybe dial back the intensity a notch, especially if you're planning a long run down the Gwynns Falls Trail or hitting the Inner Harbor loop. It's not "stay indoors and panic" bad, but it's certainly not "breathe deep and feel invigorated" good either.

Pollen is also moderate. Again, not catastrophic, but enough to make your eyes itch and your nose run. Carry some tissues, maybe pop an antihistamine if you're usually affected. You don't want to be halfway up Federal Hill Park, gasping for air because your sinuses decided to stage a protest.

### Construction Zone Woes

The I-695 Beltway construction continues its relentless march through 2028, and while most major lane closures are scheduled for off-peak and overnight hours, always assume there might be something unexpected. Same goes for the Baltimore Harbor Tunnel (I-895) — northbound closures are scheduled from 9 PM to 3 AM through Thursday for maintenance. This isn't a direct hit on your daytime trail run, but it's a reminder that Baltimore's always a work in progress.
