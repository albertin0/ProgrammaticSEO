## Today's Vibe: Treadmill or Tempest?

Look, if you're thinking about a casual jog or a serious long run, the air quality is pretty sweet today. We're talking an AQI of 33, which is firmly in the "Good" category. You won't be huffing anything worse than your own effort, and that's a rare treat sometimes. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, but at this level, it's mostly background noise.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Thunder Roars, Stay Indoors!
The National Weather Service is calling for scattered thunderstorms throughout the area this evening. More importantly, they're flagging a chance for **severe thunderstorms** with damaging winds and large hail, especially west of the city, but these things move fast. Don't be that hero who tries to outrun lightning; reschedule your outdoor session if you hear rumbles or see those ominous clouds rolling in. It's not worth it.
</AlertBox>

Temperature-wise, 23.0°C (about 73°F) under overcast skies is actually quite comfortable for a workout, shielding you from that brutal August sun. It *could* have been hotter; yesterday saw highs near 91°F in some spots. But today, the primary weather concern is definitely those storm cells.

## The Concrete Jungle's Constant Grind

If your route takes you anywhere near the Loop, specifically around the Chicago Riverwalk, prepare for some navigation challenges. The ongoing major rehabilitation of the Lake Street Bascule Bridge means daily bridge house demolition is continuing, and pedestrian access to the Riverside Upper Riverwalk from Lake Street has been closed since January and is expected to stay that way through summer 2026. So if your usual path cuts through there, you're out of luck. Likewise, the State/Lake Elevated Station is still entirely shut down for reconstruction. These aren't just minor detours; they're significant disruptions for anyone trying to get around downtown on foot.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Downtown Gridlock (for your feet)
Avoid trying to run or walk through the direct vicinity of the Lake Street Bridge and the State/Lake CTA station if you're on a tight schedule or prefer uninterrupted flow. These projects are causing significant pedestrian detours and closures. Your zen workout will quickly become a frustrating game of "where's the sidewalk?"
</AlertBox>

There's also some bridge reconstruction continuing on Touhy Avenue between Kedzie and McCormick, though that's more likely to impact local traffic than major running trails. Just be aware of general construction noise and activity if you're in those areas.

## Allergy Report: Moderate Mischief

For those of you with sniffles and sneezes, pollen levels are sitting at a moderate level today. It's not an apocalypse, but if you're usually sensitive, grab an antihistamine before you head out, *especially* before those thunderstorms potentially kick up more particulate matter.
