With the heat ramping up, **hydration is non-negotiable**, even if you're just out for a quick walk this morning. The City of Chicago opens cooling centers during these warnings, so know where your nearest one is. You can call 3-1-1 for locations. Also, if you're hitting the Lakefront, there's a **Small Craft Advisory** from noon to 10 PM for south winds up to 25 knots. Stay safe out there, Chicago.
