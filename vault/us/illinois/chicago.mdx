If you hear thunder, you know the drill: get indoors, or you might end up as a human lightning rod.

## Trail Talk: Obstacles & Annoyances

The Forest Preserves of Cook County are a mess of construction and storm cleanup. For instance, a segment of the **Poplar Creek Trail System's Red Paved Loop near Bode East** is temporarily closed for IDOT construction. You'll need to detour via the east side of Barrington Road if you're trying to pass through there. And don't even think about the Thorn Creek Trail System; it's still recovering from recent storms, and crews are advising people to stay off while they clear debris.

Even in the city, the fun continues. Access to the Riverside Upper Riverwalk near Lake Street is still closed thanks to ongoing construction, expected to last until late summer. If you're downtown, be aware that the State/Lake Loop Elevated Station is shut down for a full reconstruction, which might impact surrounding pedestrian traffic.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Widespread Construction & Trail Issues:** Expect detours and potential hazards on many popular routes, especially in the Cook County Forest Preserves and downtown areas. Check specific park district alerts before you head out. Uneven surfaces, heavy vehicles, and blocked paths are a real possibility.
</AlertBox>

So, can you work out outside today? Yeah, sure, if you're prepared for the humidity to soak you through, your allergies to act up, and potentially needing to reroute your run thanks to construction or storm damage. Just don't say I didn't warn you.
