## Today's Vitals: Clear Skies, Crowded Parks

Alright, Chicago, Mother Nature is throwing us a bone today. After a flirtation with some warmer temperatures yesterday, we've landed squarely in the sweet spot: a crisp 15.3°C (about 60°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies. That's prime running weather if I ever saw it. But before you lace up and blast out the door, let's talk about the usual urban hurdles.

First, let's address the elephant in Grant Park: El Grito Food and Music Festival. It's still going strong today, Sunday, September 13th, from noon to 10 p.m. in Butler Field. So, unless your idea of a good workout involves dodging mariachi bands and food vendors, you might want to find an alternate route for your long run. This isn't just about the main event either; expect increased traffic and potential street closures in the Central Business District due to Mexican Independence Day celebrations. The Marathon course build-out for October 11th's race isn't officially impacting the Lakefront Trail yet, so you're mostly clear there, but downtown streets are always a mess this time of year.

Air quality is sitting at a "Moderate" AQI of 55, with Ozone/Other listed as the dominant pollutant. While that's generally acceptable for most people, remember those reports from earlier in the week about thick wildfire smoke drifting in from Canada and the West Coast? Plus, there was a rather large fire at a recycling center or junkyard near Cicero and Division on Friday that sent black smoke billowing across the West Side. What exactly constitutes "Other" in that AQI reading can be a bit of a mystery, but don't be shocked if the air doesn't feel perfectly pristine, especially if you're downwind from any lingering local funk.
