## Today's Rundown: Hydration, Obstacles, and Dodging Tourists

So, the air itself isn't trying to kill you today, which is a nice change of pace after the last few sweltering days. Remember that extreme heat warning that was just lifted? Yeah, we collectively survived that. So enjoy the cooler temps, but don't get complacent.

### Watch Your Step (Literally)

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOOD ADVISORY IN EFFECT:** This is not a drill, people. With the Flood Advisory active, those underpasses along the Lakefront Trail — particularly south of Belmont and north of McCormick Place — are prime candidates for becoming impromptu swimming pools. Don't be that hero who tries to run through ankle-deep water. You don't know what's underneath, and a twisted ankle or worse will put you on my PT table. Go around, find an alternate route, or hit the streets. Your ligaments are not meant for submerged parkour.
</AlertBox>

### Weekend Warriors and Trail Traffic

It's the start of a long holiday weekend, which means more casual runners, cyclists, and families are going to be out. The Lakefront Trail will be congested. If you're looking for a PR today, you're probably going to be weaving more than running. Consider hitting a quieter neighborhood route or an inland trail if you want to avoid the crowds. Remember, that virtual 4th of July run isn't blocking any physical paths, so that's not your issue today.
