<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heads Up: River Trails Still Questionable!
Despite the official flood warning expiring this morning, the Des Plaines River and its tributaries have seen significant rainfall and rising water levels recently. DO NOT assume trails along these waterways, particularly sections of the Des Plaines River Trail south of the new Lawrence Avenue bridge, are clear or safe. Flooding can leave behind slick mud, debris, and damaged surfaces. Turn around if you encounter standing water or closed signs. Your PR isn't worth a broken ankle or a dip in the Des Plaines.
</AlertBox>

### What to Expect on Your Run/Walk:

**Lakefront Trail:**
The Lakefront Trail is generally clear, but expect a biting wind off the lake given the cold temperatures. Don't be a hero; gloves and a hat are your friends today.

**General City Running:**
Construction is always a thing in Chicago. While no major widespread running trail closures were reported in the last 24 hours, smaller localized projects are always popping up. For instance, the Salt Creek Trail under I-294 remains closed for tollway work, with a tentative reopening in early June. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours, especially if you're venturing off the main arteries.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Pro Tip: Layer, Hydrate, and Check Your Routes
Even if it's cold, you'll sweat. Dress in layers you can shed, and don't forget to hydrate. If your usual route takes you near a river, strongly consider an alternative today or be prepared for an impromptu reroute. Check local park district updates if you're heading into specific forest preserves. Also, the Chicago Park District is doing online registration for summer programs today for parks east of California Avenue, so expect a bit more activity around those facilities.
</AlertBox>
