## The Fine Print (Because Nothing's Perfect)

Let's be real, it's May, and 6.9°C (that's about 44°F for those of you still refusing the metric system) is *unseasonably* chilly. You're not going to spontaneously combust, but you're definitely going to want more than a flimsy windbreaker, especially if you're hitting the Lakefront Trail where that breeze off Lake Michigan loves to cut straight to your bones. I saw a few folks out there yesterday looking like they were auditioning for a winter expedition, and honestly, they probably had the right idea. The National Weather Service even mentioned a "Limited Frost Risk" for interior northern Illinois, which means our early morning runners might see a bit of crispness out there.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Allergy Alert:** Moderate pollen means those with seasonal allergies might feel a bit itchy or sniffly. If you’re prone to it, pop that antihistamine *before* you head out. Nothing ruins a good tempo run faster than sneezing uncontrollably into a headwind.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for Success (and Warmth):** Layer, layer, layer! Start with a moisture-wicking base, add an insulating layer, and top it with a wind-resistant shell. Gloves and a hat are non-negotiable for early risers. You can always shed layers, but you can't magically conjure a fleece mid-run. Your joints will thank you for keeping them warm in these cooler temps. Nobody needs a knee flare-up because they thought they were tough enough for shorts today.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today’s a pretty solid opportunity to get some quality outdoor time in before the real summer humidity decides to punch us in the face. Just remember to respect that chill, embrace your inner onion with those layers, and maybe carry a tissue or two.
