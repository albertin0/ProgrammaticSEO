### Weather Woes & What to Watch For

Our beloved city is looking at a temperature of about 16°C (61°F) under overcast skies, which sounds pleasant enough until you dig into the forecast. There's a 20% chance of rain during the day, but that jumps to a 55% chance of light rain tonight. More importantly, the National Weather Service is predicting a "corridor of torrential rainfall" across northeastern Illinois, including us, this afternoon and evening. That means saturated soils and a high potential for flash flooding. Seriously, don't tempt fate trying to run through what looks like a new river.

### Trail Talk: The Lakefront & Grant Park

If you were planning on a nice long run on the Lakefront Trail, think again, or at least be prepared to improvise. The Chicago Park District has an ongoing maintenance alert, meaning repairs are happening in sections, moving from south to north. Expect signage and active work zones. This isn't just an inconvenience; it's a legitimate tripping hazard if you're not paying attention.

Also, for those hoping to get some speed work in around Grant Park, remember that the Chicago Marathon might be over, but the cleanup isn't instantaneous. Balbo Drive, specifically from Columbus Drive to DuSable Lake Shore Drive, is still closed until Friday, October 16th, at 3 PM for event breakdown. Factor that into your route planning, unless you enjoy scaling barricades (don't do that).

<AlertBox type="warning">
Lakefront Trail Construction: Ongoing maintenance means detours and active work zones. Keep your head up and eyes peeled for obstacles, especially on the south end.
</AlertBox>
