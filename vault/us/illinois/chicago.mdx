If your usual long run involves the Des Plaines River Trail, you're in for a rude awakening. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a full-on reroute or bust situation. Same goes for parts of the North Branch Trail near Touhy Avenue where IDOT is doing bridge replacement, so be prepared to slow down and stay vigilant. Also, expect equipment on the Busse Forest Trails Red Paved Loop for maintenance pavement repairs through September.
