## Today's Chicago Grind Breakdown

### Weather Woes & What to Watch

It's 19.6°C (about 67°F) out there right now, which is pretty decent, but that fog is the real kicker. It means things are damp, and surfaces might be slick. Visibility on the Riverwalk, especially under the bridges, will be cut down, making it harder to dodge oblivious tourists or rogue e-bikers. Yesterday we were sweating through a Heat Advisory with a 102-degree heat index and dodging severe storms, so at least we're not dealing with that particular hell today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Beach Hazard Still Kicking
The National Weather Service issued a Beach Hazard Effect that's still in play until Wednesday morning. That means dangerous currents and waves up to 9 feet high. So, if you were planning some open water swimming off North Avenue Beach, maybe rethink that unless you enjoy being a human-sized bobber.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Closures: The Eternal Chicago Story

Remember that never-ending State/Lake Station reconstruction? Yeah, part of that deal is a long-term southbound closure of State Street between Wacker Drive and Randolph Street. So, if your route takes you through the Loop, be prepared for some detours or a battle with traffic if you're on two wheels.

Also, don't forget the Lake Street Bridge rehabilitation. Pedestrian access from Lake Street to the 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk is still closed, and it's staying that way until "later in summer 2026." Another one of those "temporary" closures that feels permanent. Plan your Riverwalk loops accordingly.
