The iconic **Lakefront Trail** is looking like your best bet today. The weather is fantastic for cruising from Hollywood Beach down to South Shore, and I haven't seen any reports of major events or closures gumming up the works directly on the main path today. Enjoy those lake breezes before it gets too damn hot.

However, if you're venturing further inland onto some of our less-beaten paths, keep your eyes peeled.

Always check local park district updates if your run involves these less-maintained routes. You don't want to hit a "TRAIL CLOSED" sign mid-stride.
