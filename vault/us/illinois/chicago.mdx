## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy Out There

Alright, let's talk brass tacks. It's March 27th, and the weather is playing typical Chicago games. The thermometer says -0.4°C (that’s 31 degrees Fahrenheit for you imperial system holdouts), and it’s a grey, overcast morning. While the air quality is actually quite good with an AQI of 43, that near-freezing temp is your primary concern.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Skimp on Layers for Your Lakefront Run
The wind off the lake can turn a 31-degree morning into something far more brutal, making it feel closer to 20°F with a decent breeze. You remember those extreme cold warnings from January, right? While not *that* bad, exposed skin can still get irritated fast. Cover those ears, hands, and any skin not already bundled. Your lungs will appreciate a buff or mask too. If you start out comfortable, you’re probably dressed too warm, but today, that's not a bad problem to have compared to being underdressed.
</AlertBox>

## Watch Your Step: Trail & Road Hazards

Remember yesterday's severe thunderstorm watch? While the worst of it weakened, heavy downpours were expected, which means low-lying areas, especially near our waterways, might still be damp or even a bit slick with residual ice if shaded.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Zone Ahead: Avoid the Loop Unless You Love Detours
If you're planning a run through the heart of the city, especially near the State/Lake 'L' station, be prepared for significant disruptions. That massive $180 million overhaul is still ongoing, and pedestrian access is absolutely impacted. The Lake Street Bridge is also undergoing major rehab, with closures affecting pedestrian access to the Upper Riverwalk until summer. Stick to paths further south or north on the Lakefront Trail if you want uninterrupted mileage.
</AlertBox>

And for my suburban runners, the **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294 is *still* closed. Don't plan on that shortcut until at least early June. There's also construction starting on the Rand Road bridge over the Des Plaines River, which will eventually include a new multi-use path for the Des Plaines River Trail, but for now, expect some detours there too.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Planning Your Weekend? Watch for Marches
Just a heads up for tomorrow, Saturday, March 28th: there's a "No Kings Rally and March" planned in Grant Park. Expect rolling street closures downtown and in the Grant Park area from 1:30 p.m. onwards. Your usual Saturday long run around the park might need an alternate route or an earlier start. Check the city's OEMC alerts before you head out.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're heading out, layer up, watch your footing for any lingering wet or icy patches, and definitely steer clear of downtown if you're looking for an uninterrupted run. The Lakefront Trail north of the Loop might be your best bet, but even there, dress appropriately for the wind off the lake.
