Look, the official data says 19.8°C and "Mainly Clear," but anyone who actually lives and runs here knows that's a fleeting moment at best. Yesterday brought heavy rain and damaging winds, with warnings for severe weather, including potential tornadoes and street flooding. Today's forecast calls for light rain and mostly cloudy skies with a 70% chance of getting wet, and temperatures hovering closer to 8-17°C (47-62°F). So, yeah, dress in layers and assume it'll change its mind twice before your long run is over.

Pollen is "Moderate," so if you're one of the sniffly ones, maybe pre-dose your meds. But honestly, the bigger concern is what's on the ground.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Watch for Lingering Flooding and Debris:** After yesterday's torrential downpours and high winds, certain underpasses and low-lying sections of trails, like parts of the Lakefront Trail or the Des Plaines River Trail, could still have standing water or scattered branches. An 80 mph gust at O'Hare isn't just a number; it means things flew. Keep your eyes peeled and don't assume your usual route is clear. If it looks sketchy, turn back. Your PR isn't worth a twisted ankle or worse.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of trails, if your usual haunt is the **Des Plaines River Trail**, know that the section just south of the new Lawrence Avenue bridge is still a hot mess from ongoing construction, with work lingering into 2026. Expect detours and maybe a new boardwalk to navigate. Same goes for the **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294; it's shut down until early June for tollway construction. These aren't minor inconveniences; they're legitimate reroutes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Season is in Full Swing:** Metra has a massive construction program for 2026, upgrading stations, tracks, and bridges. If your run takes you anywhere near commuter rail lines, be aware of increased vehicle traffic around construction zones and potential sidewalk/path closures. The $337 million project to rebuild 11 aging bridges on the Union Pacific North Line between Fullerton Avenue and Addison Street, for example, is no small undertaking and affects major corridors. Road improvements, like the ongoing I-294 construction, also mean delays and shifting patterns, even if you're not directly on the highway.
</AlertBox>

The air quality, sitting pretty at an AQI of 42 with Ozone/Other as the dominant pollutant, is about the only thing *not* trying to kill your workout today. That's a green light for your lungs. Just make sure the rest of you is ready for the classic Chicago spring gamble.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Layer Up and Stay Visible:** Given the unpredictable nature of today's weather – from light rain to potential clearer skies – layers are your best friend. And with overcast conditions and potential construction zones, consider bright colors or reflective gear, especially if you're out near road traffic.
