### Trail & Street Snarls:

Don't let the good weather distract you from the city's usual shenanigans.

*   If you're usually navigating the Loop on foot, be aware that southbound **State Street between Wacker Drive and Randolph Street** is shut down for emergency sewer repairs. This started yesterday and isn't going away anytime soon. Avoid it unless you enjoy detours through construction dust.
*   Another spot to watch out for is near the river downtown. Pedestrian access from Lake Street to the **150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk** is closed and will be through the summer, thanks to ongoing rehab work on the Lake Street Bascule Bridge. If your route takes you that way, find an alternate.
*   Out west, a section of the **Des Plaines River Trail** south of the Lawrence Avenue bridge has been dealing with "lingering" closures into 2026. Always check local Forest Preserve alerts before you head out there. Nothing ruins a long run like a surprise barricade.
