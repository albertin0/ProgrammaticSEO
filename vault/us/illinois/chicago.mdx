Look, the weather is finally playing nice after a week of on-again, off-again heat advisories that had Chicago Public Schools canceling outdoor athletics just days ago. Today, it’s a crisp 21.9°C and clear, which for September, is practically a gift from the running gods. However, those lungs still need a bit of consideration. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 69, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the main culprit. While this isn't the "hazardous" level we experienced during some of those particularly nasty wildfire smoke days in July, it’s still not pristine lake air. Folks with asthma or other respiratory issues should probably lean towards an indoor workout or keep it light.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Your Step (and Your Route!)
There's a fair bit of ongoing construction around the city that could mess with your flow. Specifically, if you're heading out to the Forest Preserves, expect some disruptions. Jurgensen Woods has construction activity near its unpaved yellow trail through September 18th, and a segment of the Poplar Creek Trail System's Red Paved Loop is also closed due to IDOT work. Even if you're just commuting downtown, remember that the State/Lake elevated CTA station is closed for reconstruction, and there are ongoing bridge repairs like the Lake Street Bridge closure, which might impact pedestrian flow in the Loop.
</AlertBox>

Pollen is also at a moderate level, so your seasonal allergy sufferers might feel a bit of a sniffle. Combine that with the PM2.5, and you’ve got a recipe for some minor respiratory irritation.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Ahead for Tomorrow
While today's Lakefront Trail should be fine, just a heads-up that tomorrow, Sunday, September 6th, is "Bike The Drive." DuSable Lake Shore Drive will be closed to vehicle traffic from Hyde Park to Edgewater, opening up for cyclists from 6:30 a.m. to around noon. This means that if you're not participating, the Lakefront Trail itself could see heavier than usual traffic from runners and walkers trying to navigate around the event, especially as riders are guided off the Drive onto the trail later in the morning.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's a good day to get out there, especially with those comfortable temps. Just be smart about your air intake and double-check those trail closures before you head out, especially if you're planning a longer run in the preserves.
