The weather itself? Pretty solid for a run or ride. No scorching sun, no biting wind. Just that ideal temp where you don't instantly regret leaving the house. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's smooth sailing.

### Trail Closures & Detours

Listen up, Lakefront Faithful: Sections of the Lakefront Trail, specifically between Fullerton and North Avenue Beach, are still closed from those high waves and winds we had recently. The damage to railings and pavement means you’ll be routed inland. Don't be a hero and try to hop barriers; parks officials are warning against it, and frankly, you're just asking for an ankle injury and a lecture from a very bored EMT. Plan your detours along Stockton Drive and other nearby streets. It's a bummer, I know, but better safe than sorry, especially with repairs expected to drag on through next week.

### Air Quality & Allergens

While the overall Air Quality Index (AQI) is moderate at 51, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, it's right on the edge. For most, this is fine, but if you've got sensitive lungs or are pushing hard, you might notice it. Combine that with a "Moderate" pollen count, and if you're an allergy sufferer, you'll be earning that post-workout shower.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** If you're hitting the Lakefront Trail south of McCormick Place, be aware of ongoing construction around Jackson Park for the Obama Presidential Center. Expect lane closures and pedestrian detours, especially on DuSable Lake Shore Drive and Stony Island Avenue. It’s been going on for a while, so stay vigilant.
</AlertBox>

### Lingering Storm Impacts

Yesterday saw scattered thunderstorms, with reports of lightning strikes causing fires and power outages. While today is calmer, the ground is pretty saturated in parts of Illinois, meaning there's a continued risk of localized flooding in some areas. Stick to paved, well-drained paths, and avoid anything that looks like a new creek bed. And for goodness sake, if you see downed power lines from those storms, stay far away.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Caution:** Bridge construction is still a thing downtown. The Lake Street Bascule Bridge rehabilitation continues, which means some pedestrian access is closed from Lake Street to the Upper Riverwalk at 150 N. Riverside Plaza. This closure is expected to last until "later in summer 2026," so plan your routes accordingly if you're navigating the Loop on foot or bike.
</AlertBox>
