### Construction: The Eternal Chicago Hazard

Now, for the inevitable catch: construction. It's Chicago; if there isn't a crane, there's a pothole, and if there isn't a pothole, they're probably *making* one.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect some detours if your route takes you near the Poplar Creek Trail System. A segment of the Red Paved Loop near Barrington Road and Golf Road is still shut down for IDOT construction, and that project isn't wrapping up until "early fall." So, "early fall" could mean next week, or it could mean Thanksgiving, knowing how these things go. Plan your run or ride accordingly, or you'll be doing some unplanned cross-country.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
If you're heading south, be aware that the Chicago Skyway has a fresh lane closure. Lane 3 from Colfax to Exchange, both westbound and eastbound, is closed starting *today* for overpass joint replacement, and that's expected to last until October 12. This might not directly affect your *run* but will certainly impact your drive to a starting point if you're coming from that direction.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
Keep an eye on the city's street closure updates if your workout takes you downtown, especially near any bridges. While there aren't specific new closures for events today, construction projects, like the ongoing rehabilitation of the Lake Street Bascule Bridge, mean pedestrian access can be unpredictable. Nobody likes a surprise dead end when they're in the zone.
</AlertBox>

Overall, don't let the moderate pollen or the usual construction headaches keep you indoors. Get out there, enjoy the clear skies and comfortable temps, but maybe check a map for those pesky trail and road closures before you lace up.
