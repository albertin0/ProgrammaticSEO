## Local Hazards & Trail Alerts

### Grant Park & Downtown Gridlock

If you thought running the lakefront path near Grant Park was an option today, you're dreaming. Lollapalooza has locked down a huge chunk of downtown. Expect major road and sidewalk closures on Balbo, Jackson, Columbus, and Monroe until early next week. Seriously, just avoid the entire area unless you're actually going to the festival (and even then, bring your rain gear).

### Suburban Trail Troubles

It's not just downtown. If you usually hit the **Des Plaines River Trail**, be aware of ongoing construction. There are full closures between Lawrence Ave and Irving Park Rd, and other sections are impacted by water main projects, particularly from Ditka to Herrick Ave. Look for detour signs near Fullerton Ave, but honestly, it might be less hassle to just pick a different route today. Same goes for parts of the Poplar Creek Trail System near Bode East, which has a segment closed for construction for another 6-8 weeks.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Lake Michigan: Dangerous Waters Today
A Beach Hazard Statement is active for Lake Michigan. High waves and strong rip currents mean staying out of the water entirely. Even walking too close to the edge is asking for trouble. Keep Fido and the kids far from the surf.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Caldwell Woods Parking Lot Reservation
If you're planning on using the Caldwell Woods main parking lot, forget it until 2 PM today. It's reserved for a scheduled event. You might find alternative parking at Bunker Hill Grove 1 or the Whealan Pool Aquatic Center, but be prepared for extra steps just to start your workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Rain Gear is Your Only Friend
With heavy rain in the forecast, invest in decent waterproof gear. A soggy run leads to chafing, blisters, and generally a miserable time. Don't be that person drenched to the bone, complaining about it later.
</AlertBox>
