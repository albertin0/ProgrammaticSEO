### Mind the Gaps (and the Cones) on the Lakefront Trail!

Yeah, I know, I sound like a broken record, but that never-ending "refresh" project on the Lakefront Trail is still happening. They started working their way north from the South end earlier this fall, patching asphalt and re-doing trail graphics. The Chicago Park District put out a heads-up that this is ongoing for "several months," so expect to see construction signage and active work zones. Don't be that idiot who barrels through a coned-off section only to trip over a freshly patched divot. Seriously, pay attention. It's an 18-mile stretch, so if you're hitting it, you're bound to run into some of this mess.
</AlertBox>

### Weather Whining & Wind Advisory Woes

Today's forecast is "Mainly Clear" but don't expect it to stay like that forever. We're in a "transition day" so expect increasing cloudiness later on. Winds are moderate, up to about 24 kph (around 15 mph), which might make it feel a bit cooler, especially near the lake. Layers are your friend, people. The good news is, there are no hazardous wind advisories currently in effect for Illinois, so you shouldn't get blown directly into Lake Michigan. However, if you're planning an early morning jaunt, especially in the northern suburbs, there's a possibility of some locally dense fog that should burn off by mid-morning. Don't be a hero trying to PR in pea-soup conditions.
