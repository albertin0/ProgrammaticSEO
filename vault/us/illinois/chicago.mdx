Look, I get it. You want to earn those miles. But today isn't the day to prove your toughness against Mother Nature's worst. The official weather warnings for Chicago are screaming at us about extreme heat and humidity, with daytime highs pushing 96-99°F (36-37°C). Add in dew points in the upper 70s, and you've got air so thick and oppressive it feels like you're breathing soup. Your lungs are already working overtime just existing out there; don't ask them to handle a tempo run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  <h3>Extreme Heat Advisory: Stay Indoors</h3>
  The combination of scorching temperatures and oppressive humidity creates a dangerously high heat index. Outdoor exercise can quickly lead to heat exhaustion or heatstroke. Prioritize hydration if you *must* be outside for short periods, but seriously, just stay in.
</AlertBox>

And let's not forget the "Critical Weather Day" status that extended into today. We're looking at a moderate risk of severe thunderstorms, not just for central Illinois but with Chicago still very much in the crosshairs. That means damaging winds of 60-70 mph could be ripping through, capable of causing serious harm or knocking out power. There's even talk of a focused tornado threat if atmospheric conditions line up just right. Trying to hit your stride when a microburst could be around the corner is just plain foolish.

Even if you brave the weather, good luck finding a clear path. Grant Park and the Lakefront Trail around Balbo Drive are already dealing with closures for the upcoming NASCAR Street Race. And on the South Side, if you were planning to get some mileage around Washington Park or Jackson Park, the Obama Presidential Center grand opening has many streets like Midway Plaisance and Stony Island shut down until at least 6 AM today, with some extending even longer. Oh, and don't forget the Taste of Randolph festival kicking off today in the West Loop, which will add to downtown street chaos.

Your body's telling you to take a rest day, or at least hit the treadmill. Listen to it.
