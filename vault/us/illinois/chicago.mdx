## The Air You Breathe (and What's in It)

Let's talk about those lungs. While the sky might look "Mainly Clear", your internal air filter might disagree. The Air Quality Index is at a moderate 63, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. That's fine for most, but if you've got asthma or are just generally sensitive, you might feel it. Couple that with "Moderate" pollen levels, and it's a recipe for some post-workout sniffles and itchy eyes for the allergy-prone. Take your meds, or stick to some indoor work if you're feeling extra sensitive.
