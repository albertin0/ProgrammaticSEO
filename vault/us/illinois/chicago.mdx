## Today's Hot Takes & Hazards

Forget about a peaceful jog down the Lakefront Trail today, folks. Seriously, unless your idea of a good time is weaving through throngs of tourists and dodging families setting up camp for the evening's pyrotechnics, find another route. The city's already anticipating "massive traffic gridlock" and is strongly recommending public transport, which tells you everything you need to know about driving anywhere near the action.

If you absolutely *must* get your miles in outside, head to one of the smaller, less-trafficked neighborhood parks or trails away from the main celebrations. Think maybe the North Branch Trail system further out, or some of the smaller pockets of green space like Horner Park, far, far away from the madness. Just be mindful of local parades and events that might pop up and block streets even in residential areas. The City of Chicago itself is warning of "some street closures in effect for larger events to ensure public safety."

And while the air quality is looking good at an AQI of 47, and the dominant pollutant is just "Ozone/Other" (whatever that means today), don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. The moderate pollen still means allergy sufferers are going to be sneezing their way through any outdoor activity.

The area around Navy Pier and the entire Chicago lakefront will be *packed* today for Fourth of July festivities and fireworks.
