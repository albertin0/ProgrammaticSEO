<AlertBox type="danger">
**DO NOT ATTEMPT TO RUN THROUGH GRANT PARK TODAY.**

Seriously, folks. Lollapalooza kicks off today, and **Grant Park** is a complete disaster zone of stages, fencing, and thousands of concert-goers. Street and sidewalk closures are extensive and will last through August 9th. Key arteries like Balbo, Jackson, Columbus, and Monroe are heavily impacted. If you usually run the section of the Lakefront Trail adjacent to Grant Park, expect major detours and crowds. Plan an alternate route or hit the trails further north or south.
</AlertBox>

While the morning looks promising, don't get too complacent. The National Weather Service is calling for scattered thunderstorms to affect parts of the region later today, specifically in the afternoon and early evening. These storms could bring some brief but heavy downpours, gusty winds, and, most importantly, lightning. There's no PR worth getting struck by lightning, so keep an eye on the forecast and the radar if your workout extends into the later hours.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Afternoon Thunderstorms Possible!**

Keep an eye on the sky and check local radar if you're heading out for an afternoon or evening workout. Scattered thunderstorms with gusty winds and lightning are a possibility.

Always check the Cook County Forest Preserves website before you go, or better yet, scope out your chosen trail on a day you're not trying to set a new personal record.
