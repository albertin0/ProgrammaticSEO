### The Lakefront Trail Is a Death Trap Today

Forget your usual long run along the Lakefront Trail. While some stretches might get cleared eventually, with ongoing moderate snow and those brutal winds off the lake, you're looking at a recipe for disaster. Drifting snow will obscure what little path might be visible, and the chances of hitting a patch of black ice are higher than your rent in this city. If you value your teeth, your wrists, or your pride, stay off it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Caution for Outdoor Activity:** The combination of moderate snow, high winds, and freezing temperatures creates hazardous conditions for slips, falls, and potential hypothermia. Near-blizzard conditions are possible with blowing snow. Seriously, reconsider.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Cold Exposure Risk:** With temperatures hovering below freezing and significant wind chill, exposed skin can quickly suffer from frostnip or frostbite. Layer up, cover everything, and don't push it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Indoor Alternatives:** This is a perfect day for the treadmill, elliptical, or hitting the weights. Your local gym loves you, and so do your intact bones. If you *must* get outside, keep it short, stay on cleared sidewalks, and assume every step is a potential wipeout.
</AlertBox>
