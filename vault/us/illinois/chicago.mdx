Today is a great day to get outside, provided you're either running a half marathon or you've planned your route with surgical precision around one.

Look, the weather gods finally decided to play nice. We've got clear skies and a proper chill in the air, perfect for a long run without feeling like you're baking on the pavement. The air quality? Fantastic. Your lungs are basically doing a happy dance today.

However, before you lace up and head straight for your usual route along the lake, check your calendar. Today is the **Life Time Chicago Half Marathon**. And that means that if your morning run involves DuSable Lake Shore Drive between 31st and 67th Streets, or anything near Jackson Park, you're out of luck unless you're wearing a bib.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Major Road & Trail Closures!
The Life Time Chicago Half Marathon has DuSable Lake Shore Drive CLOSED from 31st to 67th Streets starting at 6:00 AM, with other street closures and significant impacts around Jackson Park. Do NOT expect to run or bike freely on these sections of the Lakefront Trail or adjacent roads until well into the afternoon. Spectator access to the Drive itself is a no-go. If you're not running the race, plan a completely different route or stick to your neighborhood streets.
</AlertBox>

For those of us not tackling 13.1 miles in Jackson Park today, the moderate to high ragweed pollen is the main environmental nuisance. So if you're prone to allergy attacks, pop that antihistamine before you head out, especially if your alternative route takes you through parks or grassy areas.

Beyond the race, there's also some ongoing track construction for the Milwaukee District North line, which might cause commuter rail delays. While not directly impacting your run, it's good to know if you're relying on public transport to get to a specific trailhead. Downtown Chicago also has that massive South Shore Line expansion chewing up space around Millennium and Van Buren Street stations, affecting train capacity and causing potential delays through next year. Just another Tuesday for city living, right?

So, if you're hitting the pavement, enjoy the cool air and clean breathing, but for the love of all that is holy, avoid the South Side Lakefront Trail and DuSable Lake Shore Drive. Unless you're trying to set a new personal record, then, by all means, run through the crowds! Just kidding. Don't do that.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Consider Your Gear!
With current temps hovering around 9.7°C (49°F), layers are your friend. A light base layer, a long-sleeve, and maybe some gloves for the start will serve you well. You can always shed layers as you warm up.
</AlertBox>
