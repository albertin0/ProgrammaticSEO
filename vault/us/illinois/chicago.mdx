Look, I get it. We're runners. We tough it out. But today, March 13th, is a day for smart choices, not heroic — or foolish — ones. We've got a High Wind Warning blasting through Central Cook County until late this afternoon, with gusts that could genuinely knock you off your feet, especially near exposed areas like the Lakefront Trail. Think about trying to hold a pace against a 60 mph headwind while simultaneously trying not to face-plant into Lake Michigan. Not exactly peak performance, is it?

The weather report confirms a lovely light drizzle to go with those winds, pushing the "feels like" temperature down to a bracing -1°C. That's a recipe for chilled muscles, stiff joints, and a generally miserable experience. Add to that, all this wetness is going to make any paved paths, boardwalks, or metal grates (looking at you, various bridges) incredibly slick. We've had a lot of rain recently, with significant flooding on Chicago roads just a couple of days ago after severe storms, including parts of Damen Avenue and the Kedzie Avenue viaduct. While today's drizzle isn't a flood risk, it's enough to ensure a greasy layer on everything.

### St. Patrick's Day Chaos & Construction

If you were planning on a casual stroll or bike ride, forget anything near the river this afternoon. The Chicago Riverwalk is officially closed from 11 PM tonight through Sunday morning for the St. Patrick's Day celebrations. And don't even *think* about Hubbard Street if you're looking for peace and quiet; there's a two-day festival kicking off this afternoon. Save yourself the headache, the crowds, and the general shenanigans.
