The air quality is actually looking pretty solid today with an AQI of 35, which is firmly in the "Good" category. Your lungs can breathe easy on that front. However, let's not pretend it's all sunshine and PRs. That moderate pollen level means if you're one of the unfortunate souls who feels it, you might want to pre-dose your meds or stick to an indoor session. Nobody wants to finish their run looking like they wrestled a juniper bush.

The real kick in the pants might be those winds. Head out on the Lakefront Trail, and you'll quickly realize who's boss when those south-southwest gusts hit you head-on near, say, Belmont Harbor. It's not just about pushing harder; the National Weather Service is even mentioning an elevated fire risk due to dry fuels with these kinds of winds. So, no ill-advised bonfires in Lincoln Park, alright?

Speaking of outdoor adventures, if you're planning on really putting in some mileage, you need to be aware of ongoing construction. The Salt Creek Trail under I-294? Still closed. That project's been dragging, and it looks like it'll be early June before you can safely pass through there again. They paused the pavement reconstruction for winter, and it's set to resume when asphalt plants open. Meaning: not today.

And for those of you venturing further out, parts of the Des Plaines River Trail, specifically between Lawrence Avenue and Irving Park Road, are seeing intermittent and occasional full closures due to renovations. They aren't even bothering with an official detour, so expect some creative navigation or just plan a different route. Don't even think about running through construction zones; heavy equipment gives zero damns about your Strava segment.

### Gusty Winds & Fire Risk
Strong south-southwest winds (10-15 mph, gusts to 25-30 mph) are creating an elevated fire risk due to dry fuels. Be extra careful with anything that could spark, and be prepared for challenging conditions on exposed routes like the Lakefront Trail.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Closures and Detours
The **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294 remains closed, with a reopening tentatively scheduled for early June. Expect intermittent and occasional full closures on parts of the **Des Plaines River Trail** between Lawrence Avenue and Irving Park Road due to renovations; no official detours are provided. Plan your long runs accordingly to avoid frustration and unsafe areas.
