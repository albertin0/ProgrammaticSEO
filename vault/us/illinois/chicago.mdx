<AlertBox type="warning">
### Race Day Roadblocks & Trail Traffic
Multiple major events are happening *today*, Sunday, October 4th, which means prime running real estate, particularly on the Lakefront Trail and in neighborhoods like Bucktown, will be swarming or outright closed. The Chicago Fall Classic is touring through Montrose Harbor, and the STRENGTH IN THE CITY Festival is setting up shop right on the Lakefront Trail near Theater on the Lake. If your usual long run involves the Lakefront Trail, you're going to hit some serious bottlenecks and reroutes. Check race maps before you head out, or better yet, plan an inland adventure to avoid the chaos.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Flash Flood Risk Developing!
While it's clear now, don't get caught out unprepared. The National Weather Service is indicating a significant risk for heavy rainfall and flash flooding this afternoon and evening across northeastern Illinois. This isn't just a sprinkle; we're talking about "torrential rainfall" that can make roads impassable. If you're out for a long effort, keep an eye on weather updates and have a bailout plan. "Turn around, don't drown" isn't just a catchy phrase, it's how you avoid a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zones: The Never-Ending Story
Don't forget the ongoing construction on the Lakefront Trail, with repairs happening in sections from 63rd Street all the way up to Foster Avenue. Beyond that, the Foster Avenue Bridge replacement over I-90 is still dragging on, impacting traffic in that area, and various roadworks like those on the Bishop Ford Freeway mean you should expect delays if you're driving to your starting point. Stay alert and assume drivers might not be paying attention in these zones.
</AlertBox>

Remember, a little planning goes a long way. This could be a stellar day for a workout *if* you navigate the urban obstacles and the developing weather.
