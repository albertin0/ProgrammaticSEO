## Navigate the Urban Jungle

Let's talk about the real Chicago hazard: construction. It's perpetual, it's everywhere, and it loves to mess with your meticulously planned routes. While I haven't seen any *new* massive, sudden trail closures pop up in the last 24 hours, the usual suspects are still at it. Expect ongoing impacts from the CTA Red Line Extension project, which means dust, noise, and various street and sidewalk closures south of 95th Street. Metra also has several construction projects causing potential delays on lines like Union Pacific North and Metra Electric, so if your workout involves getting anywhere near those tracks, be aware.

The Lakefront Trail, our city's crown jewel for runners, often sees its fair share of adjacent construction or events. While no specific major event is blocking it today, it's always smart to keep an eye out for smaller localized disruptions, especially around Grant Park or other popular spots. A little detour can turn a great run into a twisted ankle if you're not paying attention.

## Weather & Trail Conditions

Today's weather is pretty prime: 19.8°C (around 68°F) and "Mainly Clear" skies for your morning and midday workouts. However, the forecast does hint at light rain during the day and scattered thunderstorms tonight. Don't be that person wiping out on a damp patch just because you weren't looking.
