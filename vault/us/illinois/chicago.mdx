## Today's Chicago Workout Lowdown: March 21, 2026

Look, I'm all for getting out there, but you gotta be smart. Today is a prime example of why you can't just blindly follow a training plan. We're waking up to a chilly 3.6°C and overcast skies, which is a rude shock to the system for anyone thinking about an early long run. The good news is the forecast says it'll climb to a comfortable 18.3°C (65°F) by afternoon. The bad news is that temperature swing can be tough on your body, and the city's still a minefield of distractions.

### Navigating the Urban Jungle

This weekend is already shaping up to be a headache for downtown runners. The Bank of America Shamrock Shuffle has its "The Mile" event today, meaning **Grant Park** is partially shut down. Even if you're not running the race, general vehicle traffic closures in the park are in effect from 6 a.m. today through Sunday afternoon. So, if your usual Saturday morning route involves cruising through Grant Park or parts of the Lakefront Trail that feed into it, prepare to reroute. Seriously, trying to navigate downtown on a race day is like playing Frogger with angry commuters and tourists.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Downtown Gridlock:** Grant Park is impacted by the Shamrock Shuffle's "The Mile" event today, leading to vehicle closures from 6 AM Saturday through 2 PM Sunday. Plan your routes carefully and expect detours and crowds.

And let's not forget the endless construction. The Kennedy Expressway's reversible express lanes are entirely closed until Monday morning for fiber optics work, along with various associated lane and ramp closures. This isn't just about driving; it often messes with access points and adjacent pedestrian paths. Plus, that Salt Creek Trail under I-294? Still closed until at least early June. So much for scenic, uninterrupted mileage there.
