If your usual route takes you anywhere near Grant Park or the Loop, brace yourself for ongoing headaches. This isn't a quick fix; these marathon-related closures are an ongoing saga that lasts for weeks as they build out the finish-line infrastructure. Don't be that person trying to squeeze through orange cones; just find another way.

### Bridge Work is a Constant
Numerous bridge and viaduct rehabilitation projects are always underway. Keep an eye out for potential pedestrian detours around areas like the Lake Street Bascule Bridge, the Chicago Avenue Bridge, and the State Street Bascule Bridge. The Lake Street bridge rehabilitation, for example, closed pedestrian access from Lake Street to 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk, and that was expected to last through "later in summer 2026," so it very well might still be in effect. Always check the OEMC alerts before heading out on a planned long route.
</AlertBox>
