## Local Hazards & Headaches

### Lakefront Living? Proceed with Caution.

Look, I know the Lakefront Trail is your sanctuary, but those Lake Michigan beaches are looking pretty gnarly today. We're talking "hazardous to dangerous swimming conditions" territory, especially in Illinois. And for good reason: we've got east-northeast winds kicking up, with gusts predicted to hit around 25 mph, particularly from 1 PM to 4 PM. If you're planning any water-based activities, or even just running right along the shore, be aware that you might get more than a refreshing spray.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect strong winds along the Lakefront Trail today, particularly north of the Loop. The lake itself is exhibiting hazardous conditions, so keep your distance from the water's edge and definitely skip the swim.
</AlertBox>

### Grant Park Gridlock (Still)

Remember that Sueños Festival that clogged up Grant Park last weekend? Well, Balbo Drive from Columbus Drive to DuSable Lake Shore Drive is *still* closed until 11:59 PM tonight, May 30th. And some of those sidewalks along DuSable Lake Shore Drive, from Buckingham Fountain down to Roosevelt Road, have also been affected by these lingering closures. This means if your route usually takes you through that section, you're going to need a detour. Plan ahead, don't just blindly follow your usual path, or you'll be doing an impromptu urban obstacle course.

### Millennium Park Mayhem (the Good Kind)

On the flip side, Millennium Park is hosting free summer workouts today – Pilates, Yoga, Cardio Kickboxing, and Zumba on the Great Lawn and Wrigley Square from 8 AM to 11:45 PM. This is a fantastic opportunity if you're looking for a group sweat session, but just know that area is going to be swarming with people. If you're looking for quiet solitude, this ain't it. If you're looking for energy and a crowd, lace up and join in.
