If you're looking to hit the streets or trails in Chicago today, March 5th, you might want to consider the treadmill instead, because it's a foggy, chilly mess out there with some questionable air.

<LungsJointsScore score={4} />

## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Visibility is practically zero, thanks to dense fog.** The National Weather Service issued a Dense Fog Advisory, warning of visibility as low as a quarter-mile, especially near the lakefront through Thursday afternoon. Trying to run or bike in that is just asking for a collision.
*   **Your lungs will hate you.** We're sitting at a Moderate AQI of 75 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Couple that with moderate pollen levels, and anyone with allergies or respiratory issues is going to be wheezing their way through their workout.
*   **It's bone-chillingly damp.** The temperature hovers around 2.9°C (37°F), but with 100% humidity and fog, it feels more like 1°C (33°F). Getting drenched by mist in these temps is a recipe for a nasty head cold, or worse, hypothermia if you're not careful.
*   **Parts of the Lakefront Trail are reportedly still closed.** An alert from late February mentioned portions of the Lakefront Trail from Oak Street to Ohio Street, and Chicago Avenue were closed due to weather conditions. With today's fog and recent dampness, it's wise to assume these closures could persist or pop up elsewhere.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Dense Fog Warning!
Seriously, folks, the fog is no joke today. Visibility near Lake Michigan could be a quarter-mile or less all day. If you *must* be outside, assume no one can see you, even with reflective gear. Stick to well-lit areas, wear bright colors, and for the love of all that is holy, take it slow. This is how you end up introduced to an ambulance.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch Your Breath
With moderate AQI and pollen, your lungs are already working overtime. High-intensity workouts will just push them harder. If you have asthma or seasonal allergies, you’ll feel it. Consider opting for an indoor session or a very low-impact walk if you absolutely need to stretch your legs outside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Construction Continues
Heads up around major intersections and downtown. The Lake Street Bascule Bridge rehabilitation continues, meaning pedestrian access from Lake Street to 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk is shut until summer 2026. Also, expect potential disruptions from the Chicago Street interchange at I-80 construction, which kicked off this week. Always check the Chicago Department of Transportation website before heading out if your route takes you near these areas.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. We Chicagoans are tough. We run in snow, sleet, and questionable temperatures. But today is a trifecta of "why bother?" Between the pea-soup fog making every intersection a game of Russian roulette, air that's actively trying to annoy your respiratory system, and the sheer damp chill, it's just not worth it. Take a rest day, hit the gym, or do some living room yoga. Your future self (and your lungs) will thank you.
