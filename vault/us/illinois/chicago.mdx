### Hazards on the Streets & Shore

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Stay Out of Lake Michigan! Life-Threatening Rip Currents.**
Seriously, folks. Weather reports from yesterday and continuing into today indicate **dangerous, life-threatening rip currents** along the Lake Michigan shoreline, particularly affecting Northern and Central Cook County. Those rough waves and strong currents are no joke, even for experienced swimmers. Stick to dry land.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Grant Park Gridlock for Runners.**
The Bank of America Chicago Marathon isn't until October, but the party's already starting for setup. Expect **ongoing street and park closures within Grant Park** as they build out the finish-line area. If your route takes you through or around the Loop's east side, especially near Balbo Drive, you're going to hit detours. Plan your run to avoid that central area entirely, or at least scope out alternate paths before you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Downtown Construction.**
Beyond the marathon madness, there's specific construction at the **intersection of Washington Street and Lyman Avenue** causing a street closure today from 7 AM to 3 PM. Also, general construction in downtown Chicago is causing service impacts for the South Shore Line near Millennium and Van Buren Street stations, meaning there's likely some related activity or pedestrian disruption in that area. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for the Breeze, Mind the Water.**
With overcast skies and breezy conditions, a light jacket or long sleeves are a good call. Given the rip current warnings, if you're hitting the Lakefront Trail, enjoy the view, but keep a healthy distance from the churning water.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a decent day to get outside if you're flexible with your route and staying far away from the lake's edge.
