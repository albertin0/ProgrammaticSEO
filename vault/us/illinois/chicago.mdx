## The Air: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

You might see an AQI of 48 floating around, which usually screams "go run forever!" But hold your horses. AccuWeather's forecasting "Poor" air quality for today, specifically unhealthy for sensitive groups. Given PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant, which is basically tiny particles that love to burrow into your lungs, I'd trust the warning. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just generally don't enjoy wheezing, today's not your day for a long run along the Lakefront Trail. That brisk wind isn't helping to clear anything out, either.

## Trail Talk & Construction Chaos

Don't even *think* about hitting the **Salt Creek Trail at I-294**. That section is still closed for tollway construction until at least early June. Seriously, they're not kidding, it's been blocked for a few years already. Elsewhere, expect to navigate some active work zones. The Des Plaines River Trail is still undergoing upgrades in Cook County, with some rerouting for flooding. And don't forget the CTA Red Line Extension groundbreaking happening this year on the Far South Side; while not a trail directly, it means heavy construction in the area. Even pedestrian access from Lake Street to the 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk is closed until summer due to bridge rehab. It's a minefield of detours out there.
