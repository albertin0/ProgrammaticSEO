### Air Quality & Allergies

Despite the mild weather, that moderate AQI and pollen count means some of you might feel it in your lungs. While we're thankfully past the worst of the post-fireworks haze from the Fourth of July, the air isn't exactly pristine. The good news is we're not dealing with the same level of Canadian wildfire smoke alerts as in past years, but that background "summer haze" is a constant companion.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you're one of the unlucky souls with seasonal allergies or any kind of respiratory sensitivity, take it easy today. Consider an indoor workout, or at least keep your outdoor activity to earlier in the morning when pollen counts might be slightly lower.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Hazards

This city is always under construction, and your workout routes are no exception.

**Des Plaines River Trail:** Heads up if you planned on hitting the Des Plaines River Trail near Robinson Woods South. While the pedestrian bridge installation over Lawrence Avenue that caused closures yesterday (July 9) should be complete, general trail improvements in that 1.2-mile segment between Bryn Mawr and Lawrence are ongoing until September. They’re even raising sections of the trail to get it out of the floodplain, which is great for future runs but means current disruptions.

**Starved Rock State Park:** If you're heading south for a serious hike, be aware that Starved Rock State Park has significant trail closures. Access from the Visitor Center, Tonti Canyon, and the Tonti bridge are all shut down for construction through the end of July. Don't make the drive only to find your favorite trails blocked.

**I-290/I-88 Interchange:** For those who use the Illinois Prairie Path or roads near the I-290/I-88 interchange, be prepared for headaches. The Illinois Tollway's massive project continues with ramp closures, lane reductions, and ongoing bridge work on surrounding local roads like Vallette Street and Roosevelt Road. Flaggers are on site to help Prairie Path users navigate, so stay vigilant.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect detours and potential uneven surfaces on parts of the Des Plaines River Trail and near major highway interchanges. Always scope out your route beforehand and be ready to adapt.
</AlertBox>

It's the opening day of the Taste of Chicago in Grant Park, running through the weekend. If your usual loop takes you through there, prepare for crowds, food vendors, and general festival chaos. While it’s not exactly blocking trails, weaving through throngs of people isn't exactly a zen workout experience.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Grant Park will be a zoo this weekend for the Taste of Chicago. If you prefer solitude for your run, find an alternate route.
