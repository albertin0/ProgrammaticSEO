### Trail & Road Conditions

No major flooding alerts impacting Chicago trails directly, which is a relief after some past spring soakings. Construction, as always, is a thing in this city. There are numerous road projects across Cook County for 2026, including major expressway work. While these are mostly vehicle-centric, they can mess with your commute to trailheads. Always check local DOT resources if you're driving to a specific starting point, especially if it involves crossing major arteries. Some suburban trails, like sections of the Salt Creek Trail under I-294, remain closed for long-term construction projects.
