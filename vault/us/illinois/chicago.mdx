Look, the weather gods aren't actively trying to kill you today, which, let's be honest, is a win for late August in Chicago. We've got clear skies and temperatures that are actually quite pleasant for logging miles, especially after the sun gets a chance to warm things up a bit from this morning's chill.

However, if you've got lungs that act up, today's AQI of 52 means the air is "Moderate," and the dominant pollutant is Ozone/Other. For most of us, it’s fine for a normal run along the Lakefront Trail, but if you're an older adult, have heart or lung issues, or are out with kids, keep an eye on symptoms like coughing or shortness of breath. You might need to dial back the intensity or shorten your outing.

Speaking of the Lakefront Trail, this past weekend saw the Chicago Triathlon wreak some havoc on downtown street access and even parts of DuSable Lake Shore Drive. While the main events are over, these things always leave a lingering mess of cones, barriers, and clean-up crews. Don't be surprised if your usual route around Grant Park or near Balbo Drive is still a bit... *complicated*. Check the city's OEMC website before you head out, unless you enjoy impromptu urban obstacle courses.

### Downtown Detours Still Possible
The Chicago Triathlon, which included closures on DuSable Lake Shore Drive and surrounding downtown streets, wrapped up yesterday. While the major closures should be lifted, expect residual clean-up, lingering traffic impacts, and potential pedestrian detours around Grant Park and the Lakefront Trail near the Loop. Always check for last-minute updates, especially if you're navigating by bike or foot in that area.

If you're heading out to the Des Plaines River Trail, be aware that construction is ongoing in various sections, particularly south of Lawrence Avenue and near Robinson Woods South, as the Forest Preserves continue to upgrade the trail and route it away from flood-prone areas. It's for a good cause, but it might mean unexpected detours or narrower paths in spots.

Finally, the Chicago Park District is kicking off its "Chicago OnScreen Film Festival" today, running through August 29th. While it's unlikely to shut down major running corridors, if your workout takes you through smaller neighborhood parks that might host an outdoor screening, be prepared for crowds or sections taped off.
