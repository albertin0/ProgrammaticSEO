## Today's Chicago Workout Lowdown: September 22, 2026

The air quality, for once, is playing nice with an AQI of 29. That's "Good" on the scale, meaning your lungs get a slight reprieve. However, it's still officially "moderate" for pollen, so if you're usually wrecked by ragweed or whatever else is floating around, you're not entirely off the hook. AccuWeather calls today's air quality "Fair," which is just polite speak for "if you're super sensitive, maybe reconsider that 10k."

Weather-wise, we're firmly in that "is it fall or just a really long September Tuesday?" zone. It's 12.3°C (around 54°F) and overcast, with breezy conditions hitting the city. While it's certainly not the oppressive heat we saw earlier this month, you won't be basking in any sunshine. Layers, people, layers. This isn't a "dangerous arctic blast," according to the meteorologists, just your typical, slightly gloomy early fall cooldown.

### Local Hazards to Watch For:

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Lakefront Trail Repairs Are Kicking Off!**
Heads up, everyone who relies on the Lakefront Trail for their miles: the Chicago Park District is starting a multi-month repair project. This isn't some quick fix; we're talking asphalt patching, seal-coating, and edge repairs. The work starts down south around 6300 South and will progress north towards Foster Ave beach. Keep an eye out for construction signage and uneven surfaces. Don't be that person who trips over a fresh patch of asphalt because you were glued to your Strava splits.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Lake Michigan: No Swim Zone**
Seriously. The National Weather Service has issued a High Swim Risk for Lake Michigan beaches today and tomorrow due to high waves. That means dangerous swimming conditions. Unless you're trying out for some kind of extreme survival show, stay out of the lake. Stick to dry land, preferably away from the crashing waves.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Construction Continues, Everywhere**
Beyond the Lakefront Trail, Chicago's always in a state of flux. Bally's casino construction is chugging along downtown, aiming for an early 2027 opening. While it's mostly vertical, expect localized traffic impacts around the site. Road resurfacing projects are also happening across the city and suburbs, so if your route takes you off the main trails, be mindful of detours and uneven roads.
</AlertBox>
