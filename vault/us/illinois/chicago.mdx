## The Rundown: Don't Get Caught Off Guard

You see that sunshine and think, "Finally, a perfect run!" And for the most part, your lungs will agree—the air quality in Chicago today is sitting pretty at a "Good" 36 AQI, with Ozone being the dominant pollutant, but nothing to write home about. Forget those July nightmares of wildfire smoke turning our skyline into a hazy, apocalyptic mess; we're in the clear for now.

But before you lace up, let's talk about the obstacles.

### Construction Chaos in Grant Park

First, the annual Bank of America Chicago Marathon isn't for another couple of weeks, but the city's already tearing up Grant Park. Balbo Drive, specifically between Columbus and DuSable Lake Shore Drive, has been shut down since yesterday, September 25th, to build that glorious finish line infrastructure. This isn't a quick fix; expect this closure to last until October 17th. So if your usual long run involves any part of that area, consider it gone. Your PR attempts will have to weave through a construction zone.

**Major Disruption:** Balbo Drive near Grant Park is CLOSED for Chicago Marathon finish line construction, impacting access near the Lakefront Trail. Avoid this area entirely.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Traffic: AIDS Run & Walk Chicago

Adding to the downtown congestion, today is the AIDS Run & Walk Chicago. We're talking 1,000 to 5,000 participants hitting "trails" across the city. While they don't always explicitly state "Lakefront Trail," any major charity run of that size in Chicago that mentions "trail" and "lakefront" means you'll be dodging a lot of people if you're trying to do your own thing on our most popular path. Keep an eye out, especially near any traditional race routes.
