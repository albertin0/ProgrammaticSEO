The weather today is doing us a solid: a cool 16.9°C and clear skies. Your lungs will thank you, with an AQI of a fantastic 37. Even the moderate pollen count isn't enough to put a serious damper on things for most folks.

But let's be real, Chicago. "Clear skies" doesn't mean "clear trails." Our beautiful Lakefront Trail, specifically the stretch between Fullerton and North Avenue Beach, is still wrestling with storm damage. High waves and relentless winds did a number on the railings and pavement, and the detours from last week are still firmly in place. Don't be that person trying to hop barriers for a 'shortcut' – you're risking a twisted ankle, or worse. The Chicago Park District crews are working on it, but repairs take time, and this isn't a minor patch job.

Beyond the lakefront, if you were planning to escape to the sprawling Forest Preserves of Cook County, you need to be aware. The Thorn Creek Trail System is still a wreck from recent storms, riddled with downed trees and debris. Just stay off it. Seriously. And the River Trail Nature Center is temporarily closed for utility and power work.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Lakefront Trail Detours & Damage:** The Lakefront Trail between Fullerton and North Avenue Beach remains impacted by storm damage, with ongoing detours. Expect rough patches and follow posted signage.
</AlertBox>

On top of Mother Nature's little temper tantrum, nearly 200 Teamsters union workers who maintain the Forest Preserves went on strike yesterday. While officials say the preserves will remain open, it's wise to assume that routine maintenance like clearing smaller debris or addressing minor issues might be slower than usual. So, keep an extra eye out for anything unexpected if you're venturing into those areas.

Construction continues to be a factor across the city, with the City of Chicago's website noting various bridge and viaduct projects, including the Chicago Avenue Bridge and the Lake Street Bascule Bridge. These are long-term projects, but always check for local street closures if your route takes you through downtown.
