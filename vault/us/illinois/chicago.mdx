## The Good News (and the Not-So-Bad)

Alright, let's start with the positives. You won't find me complaining about a clear, cool 14.6°C day in June. This is prime running weather, folks. Forget the stifling humidity or the lakefront wind trying to rip your soul out. This is the kind of day that makes those miles just melt away. And with an AQI of 41 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant – which, let's be real, is probably just regular city dust today – your respiratory system can breathe easy. Pollen's moderate, so if you're a sniffler, pop an antihistamine, but it's not grounding anyone.

## Major Local Hazards: Hyde Park Gridlock and General Construction Annoyances

Here’s where you need to pay attention, especially if you live or train on the South Side. The grand opening of the Obama Presidential Center has turned Hyde Park into a veritable obstacle course. From today, June 15th, through June 21st, expect **significant traffic delays and road closures** around the Center itself (that's 6001 S. Stony Island Avenue, for those keeping score) and the entire Midway Plaisance park. Seriously, if you were planning a long run through that area, re-route. UChicago Medicine is even warning patients to avoid Stony Island Avenue and Lake Shore Drive exits between 47th and 63rd Streets. Don't be that person trying to weave through bewildered tourists and blocked-off streets. Your ankles will hate you, and your patience will evaporate faster than a puddle in July.

Beyond the South Side chaos, Mayor Johnson kicked off "Construction Season 2026" back in April, so general infrastructure projects are ongoing across the city. This means unexpected detours, torn-up sidewalks, and uneven surfaces are always a possibility on local streets and even some suburban trails. For instance, the Salt Creek Trail under I-294 was hoping to reopen in early June, but these things tend to drag on. Always keep an eye out for construction barrels and respect those closure signs – they're there for a reason, usually involving heavy machinery or giant holes in the ground.

And if you're hitting downtown, remember that the Millennium Park Summer Music Series starts today. While it's not a "hazard" per se, it does mean increased foot traffic and potential congestion around the Jay Pritzker Pavilion and surrounding areas. Great for people-watching, not so great for hitting your stride without dodging strollers.

## Stay Smart, Chicago

All in all, today's weather is fantastic for getting outside. Just be aware of your surroundings and plan your route carefully. If you’re heading to Hyde Park, pack your patience or, better yet, pick a different neighborhood for your workout. Your local run club isn't going to be thrilled if you show up late because you got stuck behind a motorcade on 59th Street.
