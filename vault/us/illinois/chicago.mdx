<AlertBox type="danger">
### Flash Flood Risk!
The National Weather Service has issued a Flash Flood Watch for northeastern Illinois. With already saturated soils, even moderate rain could quickly turn streets and underpasses into unexpected swimming pools. Do NOT attempt to run or bike through flooded areas. Turn around. Seriously. Your PR isn't worth getting swept away or stalled out.
</AlertBox>

Winds are coming in from the southeast at 10 to 15 mph, which, when combined with cold rain and reduced visibility, is just plain unpleasant. Those wet roads and trails? They're going to be slick. Any quick turns or descents could become an ankle-twisting hazard. My physical therapy license isn't just for show, people. Take care of your joints.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Slippery Surfaces & Low Visibility
Expect wet, slick surfaces on roads, sidewalks, and especially crushed limestone trails. Wear appropriate footwear with good traction. Heavy rain will reduce visibility for both you and drivers. Bright, reflective gear is non-negotiable if you insist on being out there.
</AlertBox>
