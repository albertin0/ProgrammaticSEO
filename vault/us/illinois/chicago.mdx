## The Rundown: Don't Be a Dummy

Look, the temperature's a decent 18°C (around 64°F) and it's clear right now, which is pretty much prime running weather for August in Chicago. But don't get complacent. The forecast for later today hints at "few storms," so keep an eye on the sky. We've had a record-breaking year for severe weather in Illinois, so pop-up storms aren't exactly unheard of.

**Watch Your Step on Thorn Creek:** Seriously, the Cook County Forest Preserves are warning people. The Thorn Creek Trail System has significant storm damage and debris. It's not worth a trip to the ER for a sprained ankle or worse trying to navigate through that mess. Find another route, like the Lakefront Path, which is usually clear, or a treadmill.
</AlertBox>

**Hydration and Health:** While the extreme heat warnings from June are long gone, we've seen a spike in Legionnaires' disease in the Chicago area this year, linked to past rainfall and heat. While not directly a workout hazard, it's a good reminder to stay hydrated and be generally aware of your surroundings and any pneumonia-like symptoms, especially if you've been around stagnant water.
</AlertBox>
