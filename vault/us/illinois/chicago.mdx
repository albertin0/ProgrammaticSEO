### Today's Rundown

It's Friday, September 18th, and while the morning looks partly sunny with temperatures hovering around a pleasant 18-21°C (64-70°F), don't let it fool you. The forecast is calling for heavy rain tonight, with an 85% chance, and those wet conditions are sticking around through the entire weekend. If you're planning a long run or a bike ride, make it quick and do it before the skies really open up. Trails will get slick, and areas, especially north of I-80, could see some flooding.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Rain, Rain, Go Away (But It Won't):** Heavy rain and thunderstorms are expected to roll in tonight and persist through Saturday. This means trails and low-lying areas, particularly along the Lakefront Trail, could become wet, muddy, and potentially flooded. Plan your runs and rides accordingly, and don't be a hero trying to splash through standing water. Turn around.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the Lakefront Trail, your favorite 18-mile stretch is currently a work-in-progress. The Chicago Park District kicked off repairs recently, tackling everything from asphalt patching to seal-coating and edge work. They're starting south, around 6300 South/East of DuSable Lake Shore Drive, and slowly making their way north toward Foster Ave Beach. Keep an eye out for construction signage and active work zones. Don't be "that guy" who runs right into a freshly paved section.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Lakefront Trail Construction:** Expect ongoing asphalt patching, seal-coating, and edge repairs on the Lakefront Trail. Work is moving north from 6300 South/East of DuSable Lake Shore Drive. Be alert, look for signage, and steer clear of active construction areas.
</AlertBox>

Now, for your weekend plans, consider this your early warning: Downtown Chicago is about to become a human-sized sardine can. Mexican Independence Day celebrations, along with other festivals, sporting events, and concerts, are converging, and city officials are already warning that the Loop might see vehicle access restrictions due to hundreds of thousands of people. If you planned to do your long run on the Lakefront Trail this Sunday, know that the "Ready to Run 20 Miler" race is taking over most of the pedestrian path from Montrose Wilson Track all the way down to Jackson Park. That's 18.5 miles of one-way traffic, so unless you're in the race, find another route or prepare for serious crowds and diversions.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Weekend Mayhem Downtown & on Lakefront Trail:** Be warned, this weekend is going to be absolutely packed downtown due to various events. More importantly for us runners, the "Ready to Run 20 Miler" race on Sunday, September 20th, will effectively shut down the pedestrian Lakefront Trail from Montrose Wilson Track to Jackson Park. Avoid this stretch entirely on Sunday morning unless you're participating.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's not bad for a quick, mindful outdoor jaunt, but get it done before dusk. And for the weekend, seriously, look at your maps and have a backup plan.
