### Triathlon Chaos & Weather Woes

Today, August 22nd, marks the SuperSprint and Kids Triathlon events right here in our city. This isn't just a few road cones; we're talking full-blown street closures impacting East Balbo Drive, DuSable Lake Shore Drive, and the Montrose Harbor area from 4 AM to well into the afternoon. The entire Lakefront Trail, particularly around Grant Park and south, is going to be a congested mess or completely shut down for athletes. Your usual routes? Forget 'em.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Triathlon Closures Are REAL:** If you're planning a long run or ride, especially along the Lakefront Trail or DuSable Lake Shore Drive, check specific race routes and closures. You'll be rerouted, stuck, or elbowing tourists.
</AlertBox>

On top of that, don't let the pleasant morning lull you into a false sense of security. The forecast indicates "a couple rounds of rain/storms today," and Chicagoland is under a "Slight Risk (Level 2 of 5)" for severe storms this afternoon and evening. We're talking heavy rain and gusty winds as the main threats, so what starts as a beautiful day could turn into a soaked, windy slog. Nobody wants to be dodging debris on the 606 or slipping on wet pavement, especially with the Lake Street Bridge rehabilitation still making pedestrian access tricky downtown.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Lake Michigan is Angry:** There's a high swim risk for Lake Michigan beaches. If you thought a post-run dip was a good idea, think again. Stay out of the water.
</AlertBox>

Finally, that "Moderate" pollen level? While not debilitating for most, combine it with a potential downpour, and your post-workout sniffles might feel a little worse. Good air quality overall, but Mother Nature always finds a way to throw a wrench in our plans.

My advice? Get out there early if you can, stay flexible with your routes, and keep an eye on the sky. Otherwise, embrace the treadmill and save the outdoor heroics for a less complicated day.
