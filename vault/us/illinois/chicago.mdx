## The Spring Half Marathon: Embrace the Crowds or Avoid the Fiasco

Look, it's a beautiful day, and half the city decided to run 13.1 miles for fun. The Life Time Chicago Spring Half Marathon & 5K is kicking off today, May 17th, with the action centered around Columbus Drive, Grant Park, and the *majority* of the course stretching along our beloved Lakefront Trail.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Race, Major Closures:** Expect significant street closures around Grant Park and Columbus Drive, especially in the early morning. While they're scheduled to reopen by 6 PM, your morning run along the Lakefront Trail south of, say, Oak Street Beach, is going to be a contact sport. The race organizers even admit the course "will remain open to the public and participants should expect general public traffic during the race". Translation: it's a free-for-all, so stay alert.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if you're not pinned a number to your chest, you might want to find an alternative route or just accept you'll be dodging a few hundred high-fives and spilled water cups. Head west to Humboldt Park or over to the North Branch Trail if you want some clear asphalt.

## Air Quality: Breathe Easy, For Now

Our AQI is a sweet 40. That's "Good" in plain English. We've had our fair share of garbage air days (remember those wildfire smoke fiascos?), and even a recent report dinged the Chicago-Naperville metro with an "F" for ozone in 2025. But today? Today is not one of those days. Enjoy it. Your lungs deserve a break.

## Weather: Don't Forget the Wind

While the temperature is spot on, remember this is Chicago. "Mainly Clear" at 19.8°C (67.6°F) sounds dreamy, but don't be shocked if those lake breezes kick up a bit. We saw some forecasts for gusty winds along the I-80 corridor overnight, and while that's not exactly hitting the city center, a southerly wind of 10-20 mph was expected today. It might not be "knock you over" wind, but it's enough to make that last mile a little more spicy.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Check-in:** For those navigating the city, Metra has weekend construction happening *through today* on several lines, including Milwaukee North and Union Pacific West. And the Foster Bridge construction on the Northwest Side is still ongoing. While these are mostly commuter headaches, it's a reminder that getting around Chicago always has its little hurdles.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, watch out for the half marathoners, and enjoy a genuinely decent day to be outside. Just try not to get too close to any new construction on your long run, unless you *want* a detour.
