### Today's Rundown

Today, June 14, 2026, we've got a mixed bag. The temperature is a decent 19.0°C (about 66°F) under an overcast sky, which usually means "go time" for us runners. And hey, the Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 43, so your lungs aren't going to be taking a beating from pollution. That's the good news.

Now, for the reality check.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step: Storm Aftermath & New Threats**
Remember that mess of tornadoes and straight-line winds from a few days ago? Park crews are still out there, clearing debris and trying to get things back to normal in our parks. That means uneven surfaces, downed branches, and general nastiness could be lurking where you least expect it. Keep your head up, literally. On top of that, the Storm Prediction Center has highlighted a risk for strong to severe storms, packing damaging winds and heavy rainfall that could cause isolated flash flooding in the region today. So, while it's mild now, that could change faster than you can say "Chicago summer."
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Lace Up for a Crowd, or Find a Detour**
If your usual route involves the Lakefront Path near Montrose Harbor, be prepared for company – a lot of it. The 16th Annual DASH for Detection 5K is happening there today, supporting pancreatic cancer research. It’s a great cause, but your PR attempt might need to wait unless you're a fan of weaving through a thousand people. Similarly, the Juneteenth Wellness 5K is taking over Garfield Park. These events are fantastic for community, but for a clear run, you'll need to plan around them or explore alternative spots.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen's Pestering Presence**
While the air quality itself is decent, allergy sufferers, beware: pollen levels are moderate today. If you're sensitive, you might still experience some sniffles and sneezes, making that "good" AQI feel a little less glorious for your respiratory system.
</AlertBox>

If you're relying on public transport to get to your favorite starting line, be aware that Metra Electric Line trains are on a Sunday schedule, with some originating and terminating at 18th Street and bus service to Millennium Station. Also, the South Water elevator is closed for reconstruction. South Shore Line riders are also looking at station closures and busing for downtown routes, and they're *not* allowing bikes on the buses. So, getting around might be more of a workout than your actual run.
