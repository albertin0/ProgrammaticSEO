<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Severe Thunderstorms Expected Tonight!
  Do not, I repeat, *do NOT* plan any outdoor workouts after 8 PM. The forecast for late this evening (9 PM-midnight) includes severe thunderstorms with potential damaging winds, large hail, and even a low tornado risk across Chicagoland. Flash flooding is also a concern. This isn't just a little rain; get inside and stay safe.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Watch Out for Construction Obstacles
  While the Chicago Marathon street closures from Sunday are thankfully well behind us, the city's still a construction zone. Expect potential detours around the Lake Street Bridge area due to ongoing CTA Green and Pink Line work (lasting until Oct 25). Also, the massive 400 Lake Shore development is underway, which will eventually extend the Chicago Riverwalk's Founder's Way path, but for now, it's just... construction. Keep your eyes peeled and your head up.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Hydration is Still Key
  Even though it's overcast and not scorching hot, 22°C (72°F) is still plenty warm for October. Humidity is around 72%. If you're out there for more than 30 minutes, especially if you're hitting the pavement for speed work, make sure you're drinking water. Nobody likes cramping up halfway across Lincoln Park.
</AlertBox>

Listen, the daytime window today is fantastic for getting some miles in, particularly down by the lake or in your local park. But the second that sun starts thinking about setting, your outdoor workout window slams shut. Seriously. Don't be that person caught in a downburst just because you wanted to squeeze in one more loop.
