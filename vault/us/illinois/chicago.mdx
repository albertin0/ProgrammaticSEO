### Hazards & Head-Ups

<AlertBox type="danger">
**High Wind Warning in Effect (Again).** Seriously, yesterday was wild with gusts hitting 60 mph, causing damage and outages. Expect more of the same today, with winds up to 60 mph still possible. Running into a headwind like that is going to be brutal on your lungs and joints. Not to mention the risk of falling debris. Keep your eyes peeled and your wits about you, especially near construction or older buildings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Flash Freeze Incoming.** We're hovering around freezing today, and a much colder airmass is on its way. What little rain we might see or any remaining wet spots from last week's floods could turn into treacherous ice patches by tonight or early tomorrow. Don't assume that puddle is just a puddle; it could be a tibia fracture waiting to happen. The forecast is talking about a full-on "flash freeze" after rain today, so be hyper-aware.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Local Events Affecting Your Route.** If you're planning a run near Grant Park this morning, forget about it unless you're actually *in* the Lucky 7K. Grant Park's Arvey Field and surrounding areas will be swarming with runners and spectators. Plus, the Chicago Riverwalk, which was closed through 6 AM today for St. Paddy's festivities, might still be congested or have lingering debris. Check your route carefully if you're hitting the Lakefront Trail, as access points might be impacted by event traffic and crowds, and local parades mean street closures further afield. Don't be that runner trying to weave through a parade route; it's annoying for everyone.
</AlertBox>

### My Two Cents

Look, I love hitting the pavement as much as the next lunatic, but today's a real mixed bag. The air quality is decent, so your lungs won't hate you for *that*. But those winds? They'll strip the joy right out of any long run. And with a colder snap and potential for a flash freeze looming, surfaces could be slick. If you absolutely *must* get out, stick to known, well-maintained paths like the main artery of the Lakefront Trail and keep an eye on official Park District updates (#chiLFTAlert). Better yet, hit the gym or embrace some indoor cross-training. Your joints (and hair) will thank you.
