## Why today is/isn't the day.

*   More importantly, watch out for dense fog in spots, especially during those early morning miles.
*   It's a big weekend for runners with the VIVAVITA Biohacker Run & Walk Festival kicking off today through Sunday, and some Chicago Marathon shakeout runs happening, which means popular routes are going to be busy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Dense Fog Alert for Early Birds!**
If you're out before the sun really gets going, expect patchy, dense fog across parts of northeast Illinois. Visibility can drop suddenly, so be extra cautious, especially on roads or shared paths. Don't be that person who ignores the fog and ends up in a ditch. Or worse, a canal.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Lakefront Trail: Prepare for Company (and Construction)!**
Today marks the start of the VIVAVITA Biohacker Run & Walk Festival, running through October 6th, and various Chicago Marathon shakeout runs are on the schedule. This means high traffic on popular running spots, especially sections of the Lakefront Trail. Be prepared for crowds, and be mindful of other runners, walkers, and cyclists. Also, the Lakefront Trail still has ongoing maintenance in various sections, so stay alert for any detours or uneven surfaces. They've been at it for months, so it's not going away overnight.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Detours Still a Thing**
While not directly impacting running trails for the most part, major reconstruction on the Lake Street Bascule Bridge is still happening, impacting pedestrian access in that downtown area. If your route takes you near there, be aware of potential closures or re-routes. Also, expect general construction delays on major expressways around the city, which might affect your commute *to* a workout spot.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's a solid day for outdoor activity, especially if you get past the morning fog. Just remember that moderate pollen can be a nuisance, and you'll be sharing the path with a lot more folks than usual. Embrace the crowds, or find a quieter, less-trafficked neighborhood loop if you prefer solitude.
