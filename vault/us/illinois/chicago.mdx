<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, Stay Hydrated (or indoors)
An Extreme Heat Warning means what it says: *extreme*. Heat-related illnesses spike during these conditions. If you absolutely MUST be outside, drink way more water than you think you need, seek shade, and keep your damn run short. Public cooling centers are open if your AC is on the fritz.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Buckled Roads
The Illinois Department of Transportation is warning about pavement buckling and blowouts due to the heat. While this is primarily for drivers, be mindful if your route involves less-trafficked roads or older paths. Uneven surfaces are a sprained ankle waiting to happen.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Reroute Around Grant Park
With the Taste of Chicago rolling in, street closures are already affecting Columbus Drive and Ida B. Wells. Forget a straightforward loop; plan on detours or pick a different part of the city entirely.
</AlertBox>
