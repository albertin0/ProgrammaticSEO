## Chicago's Concrete Jungle and Weather Woes

If you had grand plans for a long run along DuSable Lake Shore Drive or a serene pedal through Grant Park this morning, you probably hit a wall. And that wall was probably a police barricade. The Chicago Triathlon is in full swing today, August 25th, and it's brought with it the usual cascade of street closures. Southbound DuSable Lake Shore Drive, from Hollywood all the way down to Randolph, is shut down until noon. Columbus Drive and other key downtown arteries are also choked off.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Lakefront Lockdown:** Forget your usual route. Due to the Chicago Triathlon, significant sections of DuSable Lake Shore Drive and surrounding downtown streets are closed. Plan for major detours or pick a different neighborhood entirely.
</AlertBox>

### Evening Thunder Rumbles

Even if you managed to dodge the triathlon chaos this morning, don't get too comfortable. The National Weather Service is calling for scattered thunderstorms through this evening, with a few potentially severe, packing damaging winds and large hail, especially west of the immediate lakefront. Hydrate, sure, but also consider hydrating *indoors* while watching the storm roll through. Nobody wants to be caught out in a downpour with lightning doing its thing.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Evening Storms Approaching:** Later today, expect scattered thunderstorms with potential for damaging winds and hail. If your workout window is this afternoon or evening, move it indoors or reschedule.
</AlertBox>
