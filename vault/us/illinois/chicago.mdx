## Your Lungs Are Still On Edge

Look, the AQI is 75, which means it's generally okay for most folks to be outside. But let's be real, this is "moderate" after Chicago spent the last week choking on Canadian wildfire smoke that pushed our air quality into the "hazardous" zone, well over 400 AQI. Your body needs time to recover from breathing that in. PM2.5, that nasty fine particulate matter, is *still* the dominant pollutant. So, if you're asthmatic, have other respiratory issues, or just felt that scratchy throat earlier in the week, take it easy. Don't push for a PR today, no matter how good the temperature feels.

## The Calm Before the Swelter

Today's weather is a gift: mainly clear and a perfect 19.8°C. Seriously, go run along the Lakefront Trail, hit up Millennium Park, enjoy it. But enjoy it *today*. Because come Sunday, we're staring down the barrel of highs in the 90s and heat index values near 100 degrees. We're also looking at a Level 1 risk of severe thunderstorms popping up this weekend. Save your long, slow distance for an early morning run tomorrow, or better yet, hit the gym.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Weekend Heat Advisory!
Starting Sunday and extending into Monday and Tuesday, afternoon heat indices are expected to soar to 100-110 degrees. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's dangerous, especially for vulnerable populations and anyone attempting strenuous exercise. Hydrate aggressively, plan workouts for dawn, and don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

## Mind the Obstacles

Our city is always under construction, and this weekend is no different. If you're planning a long run through the Forest Preserves, be aware that sections of the **Des Plaines River Trail System** are still dealing with ongoing water main construction, particularly between Ditka and Herrick Ave, with detours near Fullerton Ave. Similarly, part of the Red Paved Loop on the Poplar Creek Trail System is closed for 6-8 weeks due to construction.

Downtown, weekend work on the State/Lake CTA Station Reconstruction Project means temporary reroutes on the Loop Elevated and closures of several CTA stations, which could impact how you get to your starting point. Plus, with the Gospel Music Festival, WNBA All-Star Game, and Fulton Market Grand Prix happening, expect general street closures and crowds. Plan your routes, check for closures, and consider public transit if you're heading downtown.
