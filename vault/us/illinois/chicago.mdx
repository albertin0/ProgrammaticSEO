## Local Alerts & Construction Zones

### Downtown & Loop Logistics
If you're heading downtown, be aware that while yesterday's El Grito festival is over, construction continues to be a persistent urban hurdle. The Lake Street Bascule Bridge project still has pedestrian access closures from Lake Street to the 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk, a situation that's been ongoing since early 2026. While it was *expected* to be resolved by "later in summer 2026," it's always best to assume delays with these things and check local signage. Also, the State/Lake Elevated CTA station remains completely closed for a full rebuild, affecting train commuters, not necessarily your run directly, but it's a massive ongoing project.

### Neighborhood Nuisances
The German-American Festival in Lincoln Square is still impacting local traffic and bus routes (CTA routes 11 and 49B) through 3 PM today. If your run takes you through the Western/Lincoln/Foster area, expect more foot traffic than usual and plan accordingly.

### Far South Side Developments
Out on the far south side, the CTA Red Line Extension project is in full swing between 95th and 130th Streets, a multi-year endeavor. Expect localized noise, dust, truck traffic, and potential street or sidewalk closures in those zones. This isn't your casual stroll route, but if you're working out in that area, know what you're getting into.
