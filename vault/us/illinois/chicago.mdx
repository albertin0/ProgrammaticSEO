*   The pollen count is also moderate, so if you're prone to seasonal allergies, you might be sneezing your way through your workout.

### Watch Your Route: Construction and Closures

Chicago is always under construction, and today is no different. If your usual route takes you anywhere near the Chicago Skyway, be aware there are ongoing "Overpass Joint Replacement" projects, leading to lane closures between 79th and Dante. These aren't necessarily blocking pedestrian or bike paths, but they mean more traffic, detours, and general chaos in those areas.

There are also various street and bridge rehabs happening city-wide. While a lot of the reported long-term bridge closures (like the Chicago Avenue Bridge) aren't new news, they still impact traffic flow and can make getting to your starting point a headache.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Ahead for Construction.** Check the City of Chicago's ChiStreetWork tool or local news before you head out, especially if you're driving to a trail or running through dense urban areas. No one wants to add an unexpected half-mile detour through a noisy construction zone to their long run.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today could be a decent day for a workout, but you've got to be smart about *when* and *where* you go. Get your miles in early before those storms hit, and don't push it if your lungs feel iffy with that moderate AQI and pollen.
