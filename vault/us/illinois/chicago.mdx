## The Air You Breathe (or Don't)

Today's Air Quality Index (AQI) sits at 64, classified as "Moderate." While a far cry from the truly awful days we've had with wildfire smoke turning the sky orange back in July, "moderate" still means something. If you've got sensitive lungs, asthma, or other respiratory issues, you're going to feel this. It's not a green light to push your hardest; consider dialing back the intensity or shortening your run. According to reports from early this morning, the AQI for PM2.5 was at 48.7, which is definitely in a "cautionary zone". So, maybe don't plan any personal bests on the Lakefront Trail today, unless your personal best involves dodging pedestrians who are also vaguely concerned about the air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Gaps (and the Cranes)

Seriously, construction is rampant across the city. The Stevenson Expressway, the Dan Ryan, I-290, and even the Bishop Ford Freeway are all seeing ongoing work. While you might not be running *on* these, the ripple effect on local streets and access points is real. More relevant to us pavement-pounders and trail-blazers: there's construction affecting various sections of the Forest Preserves of Cook County, including intermittent sewer and outfall repair in Glenwood Woods-North and site improvements at Caldwell Woods. Even the Poplar Creek Trail System has a segment of its Red Paved Loop closed for IDOT construction near the Bode East area. Don't just assume your favorite path is clear. Check the Forest Preserve alerts before you head out, or you'll be adding an unplanned scramble through the bushes to your workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Event Overload: Avoid Downtown (and other places)

It's Labor Day weekend, and Chicago is doing its usual thing: throwing multiple large events simultaneously. Today, September 3rd, marks the start of the Chicago Jazz Festival in Millennium Park and the Chicago Cultural Center. That means Grant Park and the surrounding areas are going to be a zoo. Furthermore, the Pilsen Mexican Independence Day Parade is kicking off on Saturday, with street closures expected along its 18th Street route. The Labor Day Parade & Eddie Fest is also happening on Saturday on the Southeast Side.

And don't even get me started on the looming shadow of the Chicago Marathon. While the race isn't until October, the finish-line infrastructure construction on Balbo Drive, near Grant Park, typically starts *weeks* beforehand and can cause significant disruptions in the area for over three weeks. So, even if you're not running a race, you're running into obstacles.
</AlertBox>
