## The Rundown

Look, let's be real: Chicago weather in May is a fickle beast. We're starting the day a bit brisk at 9.1°C (48.4°F) and partly cloudy. But, don't let that initial chill fool you. The forecast is showing things warming up significantly into the low to mid-70s Fahrenheit (around 21-24°C) by the afternoon. That's prime running weather if you ask me. ## Watch Out For This Stuff

<AlertBox type="info">
**Lakefront Trail Traffic - Get Ready for the Crowds!**

The Life Time Chicago Spring Half Marathon & 5K is hitting the Lakefront Trail this Sunday. While the main event isn't today, expect increased foot and bike traffic, especially around the start/finish lines near Columbus Drive and Maggie Daley Park, as folks scout the course or event staff begins early staging. The good news is the trail remains open to the public during the race, so you're not getting kicked off—just sharing. Be courteous, stick to your lane, and maybe dodge a few lost tourists.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos Continues**

Our city is perpetually under construction, and today is no different. The Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT) is actively resurfacing King Drive from 35th Street to 55th Street, a project that kicked off in late April and isn't set to wrap until July 31st. If your route takes you anywhere near that corridor, expect detours, dust, and general disruption. Also, remember that construction haul roads are being installed for the Red Line Extension, so keep an eye out for heavy equipment and unexpected closures around those areas. If you're heading out to the suburbs for a run, the Des Plaines River Trail, particularly south of the new Lawrence Avenue bridge, still has some lingering closures from flood mitigation work that might impact your long run. And if you're really out there, south of I-80, gusty winds might kick up some blowing dust in rural areas, potentially affecting visibility.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Small Craft Advisory for Lake Michigan**

If you had grand plans of hitting Lake Michigan in a kayak or paddleboard before or after your run, think again. There's a Small Craft Advisory in effect as of early this morning. The lake is no joke, even on a partly cloudy day. Stick to the shore unless you enjoy being tossed around like a rag doll.
</AlertBox>

Overall, it's a perfectly acceptable day to get your miles in, just be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're on popular routes like the Lakefront Trail or near active construction zones. And for crying out loud, hydrate. You're not invincible.
