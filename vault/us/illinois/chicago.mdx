### Navigating the Urban Maze

While the mild, overcast weather at 14.0°C (about 57°F) is otherwise quite pleasant for a run, the urban landscape itself presents the biggest hurdles today. Forget trying to PR through the heart of downtown; you'll be too busy dodging barriers and rerouting. Beyond the Loop, some of our beloved forest preserve trails are also seeing action. The Centennial Trail, for instance, was closed yesterday for sealcoating in parts of DuPage County, which, let's be honest, affects plenty of us who venture just beyond Cook. The Poplar Creek Trail System also has a segment of its Red Paved Loop closed for IDOT construction near Bode East, and that's expected to continue through early fall.
