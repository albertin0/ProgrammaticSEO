### On the Trails: Construction & Closures

Look, this is Chicago. There's always *something* being torn up or rebuilt. While the air's good, don't expect smooth sailing everywhere.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Des Plaines River Trail Users, Heads Up!** If you're planning on hitting the Des Plaines River Trail system, particularly between Ditka and Herrick Ave, be aware of ongoing construction for water main projects. This isn't new news, it's been going on since March and is expected to continue until the end of July. Detour signs *should* be posted, but you know how that goes. Stay alert and don't just blindly follow your GPS into a pile of dirt.
</AlertBox>

Other spots to keep in mind, especially if you venture out to the 'burbs, include Midlothian Meadows, where they're doing pavement maintenance until mid-September, and parts of the Poplar Creek Trail System still have temporary closures. Always check the Forest Preserves of Cook County site before you roll out.

### Downtown Commotion: Not Your Running Path

For you urban warriors, while the glorious weather might tempt you to dart through the Loop, remember it's still a minefield of construction. The CTA's State/Lake Station Reconstruction is a multi-year affair, with State Street southbound still closed between Wacker and Randolph. Plus, certain elevated train stations like Washington/Wabash, Adams/Wabash, and the elevated portion of Clark/Lake were closed this past weekend for track work, and will be again next weekend. While today is Monday, expect residual weirdness and watch out for pedestrian detours if your route takes you near these areas. You're not trying to set a PR by dodging orange cones, are you?

The Halsted Street & Chicago Avenue Bridge project is also still doing its thing, which means noise and dust are part of the daily grind there. Basically, downtown Chicago is still a construction zone, so if you're looking for a peaceful run, maybe stick to the lakefront or a dedicated trail.
