### Air Quality & Pollen: Don't Breathe Too Deep

Look, an AQI of 58 isn't "hazardous," but it's firmly in the "Moderate" zone with PM2.5 as the main culprit. That means if you've got even a hint of lung issues, or you're just generally sensitive, you might feel it. The weather's overcast and a pleasant 23.8°C (around 75°F), which usually means your joints would be singing, but your lungs might be doing a different tune. Add a "Moderate" pollen count to that, and you've got a recipe for a snotty, slightly wheezy workout. AccuWeather even forecast "Poor" air quality for today and tomorrow, so things might get worse before they get better. If you insist on pushing it, consider shorter efforts and maybe stick to an indoor mask if you're really committed.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  With a "Moderate" AQI driven by PM2.5 and "Moderate" pollen, sensitive individuals might experience respiratory irritation. Keep your inhaler close and consider adjusting intensity.
</AlertBox>

Here's where it gets real, folks. Remember those storms from a while back? Yeah, the Lakefront Trail between Fullerton and North Avenue Beach is *still* partially closed thanks to storm damage. City crews are "racing to repair," but "detours will remain into next week." So, if your Sunday ritual involves cruising those miles, you're going to be rerouted inland, dodging traffic on Stockton Drive. It's a bummer, but safety first, I guess. Don't be that idiot climbing over barriers – you'll just end up with a sprained ankle and a ticket.

And speaking of the lakefront, it's the second day of the Chicago Air & Water Show. That means North Avenue Beach and surrounding areas are absolutely *packed*. If you were hoping for a serene run, you picked the wrong weekend. Expect massive crowds, especially between Fullerton and Oak Street. Good luck finding parking, let alone open pavement.

Out in the Forest Preserves, some parking areas, like those at Bode East and Brownell Woods, are closed until today due to storm debris. If you usually start your trail runs from those spots, you'll need to find an alternative access point or pick a different preserve entirely.
