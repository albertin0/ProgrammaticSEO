If you're planning an outdoor workout in Chicago today, March 29th, prepare for some serious detours and a chilly start, because it's going to be a logistical puzzle.

<LungsJointsScore score={5} />

<BulletList>

*   Today is the day of the Shamrock Shuffle, which means much of Grant Park and the Loop are blocked off until at least 2 PM, making your usual running routes a no-go.
*   The air quality is surprisingly good, but a moderate pollen count could still sneak up on sensitive lungs.
*   It's a frosty 3.1°C out there, which is cold enough to make your joints feel it if you don't warm up properly.

</BulletList>

Look, I get it. The sun's out (mostly clear conditions, after all), and you're itching to hit the pavement. But unless your idea of a good run involves dodging barriers and confused tourists, you might want to rethink your morning strategy. The annual Shamrock Shuffle has taken over a huge chunk of downtown, including major thoroughfares and, yes, your beloved Grant Park. According to CBS Chicago, Balbo Drive, from Columbus Drive to DuSable Lake Shore Drive, is shut down until Wednesday, and general vehicle traffic in Grant Park is restricted until about 2 PM today. If you were hoping for a serene long run along the lakefront through the park, forget about it until later this afternoon, or just accept that you'll be sharing the space with thousands of fresh-faced (and possibly green-clad) runners.

### Weather & Air Quality Rundown

Today's temperature is a brisk 3.1°C (that's about 37.6°F for you Fahrenheit folks). While "Mainly Clear" skies sound inviting, that chill factor is no joke. Your lungs might feel the bite of that cold air, especially if you're pushing the pace, and your joints will be screaming for a good, long warm-up before you ask them to pound the pavement.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Layer up! A temperature of 3.1°C can lead to rapid heat loss, especially with any wind chill. Make sure your hands, ears, and feet are well-covered to avoid frostnip or general discomfort.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, our air quality is looking pretty decent with an AQI of 42, which is firmly in the "Good" category. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, but at this level, it shouldn't cause any major respiratory issues for most people. The moderate pollen level, however, means some of you seasonal allergy sufferers might still get a little sniffly.

<AlertBox type="info">
Pollen is moderate today. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, consider an antihistamine before you head out, or stick to indoor workouts if your symptoms are usually severe at this level.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Closures

Beyond the Shamrock Shuffle, keep an eye out for ongoing construction. The rehabilitation of the Lake Street Bascule Bridge is still happening, which means pedestrian access from Lake Street to the 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk is closed until at least summer 2026. And if you're a suburban warrior, remember the Salt Creek Trail under I-294 remains closed due to tollway work, with a tentative reopening not until early June.

<AlertBox type="danger">
Avoid Grant Park and the immediate Loop area for your outdoor workout this morning. The Shamrock Shuffle has caused significant road and park closures until at least 2 PM today. Expect heavy crowds and reroutes.
</AlertBox>
