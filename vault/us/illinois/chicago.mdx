### Weather Woes & What to Wear

Seriously, this isn't the balmy spring day we were promised. With the temperature barely above freezing and those winds ripping off Lake Michigan, hypothermia is a real concern if you're not dressed right. Layer up. Think base layer, insulating mid-layer, and a wind/water-resistant outer shell. Don't forget gloves, a hat, and a neck gaiter. Your body's going to work hard to stay warm, so don't make it harder.

### Trail Traps and Urban Obstacles

If your usual route involves the **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294, forget it. That section is still closed for tollway work and isn't slated to reopen until early June. Seriously, who thought a multi-year closure on a popular trail was a good idea? Meanwhile, if you're trying to navigate downtown, be aware that several **Chicago River bridges** are aging out, with major repairs and closures expected this year for spots like the Northwest South Street Bridge, Michigan, and Grand Avenue bridges. What was once a scenic urban run could easily turn into a game of "dodge the detour sign."

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Muck!
With overcast conditions and potential showers (there's a 30% chance today), trails and sidewalks could be slick. Watch your footing, especially around construction zones where drainage might not be ideal. A sprained ankle in April is a real buzzkill.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Zones Are NOT Your Playground
Seriously, keep your head on a swivel. Major construction is underway on the Eisenhower, Stevenson, Dan Ryan, and Kennedy Expressways. This means increased truck traffic and potential spillover onto adjacent streets and paths. Assume drivers aren't looking for you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Visible, Stay Safe
It's overcast and dull out there. Wear bright colors or reflective gear, even during daylight hours. With all the construction chaos, you want to be as visible as possible to distracted drivers and heavy machinery operators.
</AlertBox>

Look, the desire to get out there after a long winter is real, I get it. Just be smart about it today. Bundle up, watch your step, and maybe choose a familiar, less-obstructed route. Or, you know, there's always the gym.
