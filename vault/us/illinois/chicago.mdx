## Today's Rundown: Brisk, Pollen-Dusted, and Still Under Construction

Let's cut to the chase. The weather gods are being relatively kind for a late April Sunday. We've got clear skies, and while 6.2°C (that's about 43°F for those of you still clinging to the old ways) is brisk, it's not the kind of cold that threatens your extremities. You layer up, you warm up, you'll be fine. My PT brain immediately screams "dynamic warm-up!" before you even think about jumping into that first mile. Don't be a hero; nobody wants to deal with a pulled hamstring this early in the season.

The air quality index (AQI) is sitting pretty at 45, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's "Good" territory, which means your lungs, generally speaking, aren't getting a beating from industrial smog today. Don't get me wrong, we just got an "F" for ozone in 2025, and that's a long-term problem for our respiratory systems, but today's numbers are a temporary reprieve.

### Pollen: The Silent Assassin of Spring Workouts

Now, about that "moderate" pollen. If you're one of the millions who consider spring "allergy season," you already know the drill. While the AQI might be low, a moderate pollen count can still turn your easy run into a snotty, wheezy affair. Plan your route to avoid overly wooded areas if you're sensitive, and for the love of all that is holy, take your meds *before* you head out, not after you're already halfway through your run.

### Hazard Alert: Watch Your Step (and Your Route)

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Salt Creek Trail Underpass at I-294: Still CLOSED!**
Seriously, folks, for those of you who venture out west, the underpass on the Salt Creek Trail at I-294 is *still closed*. It's been that way for a while and isn't expected to reopen until early June. Don't try to be clever and find a "shortcut" around it; people have reported unsafe workarounds. Find an alternative route or just accept it's a no-go for that section.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**River Levels and Upcoming Storms**
Parts of the Rock, Fox, and Des Plaines Rivers are still at "Action" or "Flood Stage." While no major city trail closures were reported for today, it's a good reminder to be aware if your usual route hugs any of these waterways, especially after rain. Speaking of rain, tomorrow (Monday) brings a "slight risk" of severe weather, including strong showers, thunderstorms, and potential flash flooding. Today is mostly dry, but that could change quickly come Monday afternoon.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Season is ON**
Mayor Johnson officially kicked off the 2026 Construction Season this week. While the massive Red Line Extension project on the Far South Side just broke ground, its impact on central running and biking trails isn't immediate. However, expect general construction activity across the city to pick up. Keep an eye out for unexpected detours, torn-up sidewalks, and general road chaos. Always be aware of your surroundings; a distracted runner is an injured runner.
</AlertBox>

So, in short, get out there if you want to. The air's decent, the sun's out, but remember your layers, respect the pollen, and for Pete's sake, avoid that Salt Creek Trail underpass. Nobody needs a construction-induced injury for a Sunday morning workout.
