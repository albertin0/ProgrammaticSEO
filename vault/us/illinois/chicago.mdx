### Trail Updates & Roadblocks

Chicago's always building, improving, or tearing something down. Today's no different.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for the Cold:** Layers are your friend. A good base layer, a mid-layer for insulation, and a wind-resistant outer shell are key. Don't forget gloves, a hat, and perhaps even a buff to protect your face from that brisk wind. Better to overdress and shed than be shivering.

The **Des Plaines River Trail** is a prime example of our city's never-ending infrastructure projects. While a new multi-use path connecting to the trail at Rand Road won't begin construction until March 24, be aware that other sections of the Des Plaines River Trail are undergoing "reconstruction" throughout 2026, particularly south of the new bridge near Lawrence Avenue. Expect potential detours or uneven surfaces. Always stay vigilant for construction signage and equipment.

Major expressway work is also in full swing or about to kick off. The Stevenson Expressway is seeing lane closures starting this week, and the Dan Ryan Expressway is next on March 19. While these are mostly vehicular, the increased congestion means more exhaust, and if your route takes you anywhere near these arteries, expect a less-than-serene experience.

So, get out there if you want to. Just don't be a hero. Look before you leap, dress smart, and remember that Chicago will still be here with more trails and fewer hazards another day.
