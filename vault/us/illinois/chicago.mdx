## Today's Breakdown

The air quality, bless its heart, is actually looking pretty good with an AQI of 28. So your lungs aren't fighting pollution today. They'll just be fighting off rain and moderate pollen. The temperature is a cool 14.0°C (57°F), which normally would be great for a long run, but combined with heavy rain, it just screams "chilly and miserable."

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Lakefront Warning!
Seriously, stay clear of the Lakefront Trail's immediate edge. That Beach Hazards Statement from the National Weather Service isn't a suggestion; it's a warning about dangerous conditions near the water. A good workout isn't worth getting swept away.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Slippery When Wet
With heavy rain on the menu, expect everything to be slick. Sidewalks, bike paths, and especially any unpaved trails are going to be treacherous. Slow down, shorten your stride, and forget about setting any PRs unless it's for "fastest trip to the ER." Watch your footing, particularly around construction zones or areas with poor drainage. While the Chicago Department of Transportation keeps an eye on street closures, a deluge can quickly turn a normal path into a hazard.
</AlertBox>
