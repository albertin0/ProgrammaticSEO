Look, I get it. The sun's trying to peek through that haze, and your running watch is probably judging you. But let's be blunt: today is a terrible day to be pushing it outside. The air quality alert isn't for show; the AQI is expected to exceed 150, which is straight-up unhealthy for *everyone*, not just sensitive groups. You'll be inhaling wildfire smoke particulates, and frankly, your lungs don't need that kind of abuse just to tick off some miles. Areas along the lakefront and in the northern suburbs could be even worse, experiencing "Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy" air quality from dense smoke.

Coupled with that, the forecast for a near 100-degree heat index means you're not just fighting the air, you're fighting the humidity too. Your body's going to struggle to cool itself, making strenuous activity a straight shot to feeling like garbage, or worse, ending up in a cooling center. While the official "Extreme Heat Warning" triggers might not be fully met everywhere today, the conditions are certainly flirting with them.

If you're dead set on getting some movement in, take it indoors. Find a gym, hit the stairs in your building, or finally tackle that P90X DVD your buddy lent you in 2012. Your lungs, joints, and general well-being will thank you.
