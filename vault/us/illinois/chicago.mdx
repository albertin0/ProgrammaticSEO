## Today's Outdoor Grind: Bundle Up or Bust

You looked outside this morning, saw "clear skies," and thought, "Finally, spring!" Then you checked the temp, didn't you? It's May 11th, and we're back to bundling up. The Lakefront Trail might look inviting with that clear view across the lake, but that chill coming off the water is no joke. Today's high is barely cracking 11°C (52°F), and it feels more like 6°C (44°F). Your knees and hips are going to hate you if you skip the dynamic warm-up. Don't be that person limping around tomorrow.

### Air Quality Report: Breathe Easy, Sort Of

Good news, your lungs are getting a free pass today. With an Air Quality Index of 36 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, you're looking at some genuinely decent air for a change. So, while your body might be fighting the cold, at least you won't be sucking in a cocktail of urban crud.

Pop that antihistamine *before* you head out, not halfway through your run when you realize you've turned into a snot-nosed mess.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Chilly Temps Demand Respect.** Temperatures are unseasonably cold for May, hovering around 7-11°C (45-52°F). This isn't January, but it's not shorts weather either. Layer up! Think wicking base layer, a light fleece, and a windbreaker, especially if you're hitting the Lakefront Trail where that lake breeze will cut right through you. Pay extra attention to your hands, ears, and feet. And for the love of all that is holy, give your joints a proper warm-up before you try to hammer out those intervals. Cold muscles and tendons are unhappy muscles and tendons.
