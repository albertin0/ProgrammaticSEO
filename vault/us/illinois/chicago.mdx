## The Rundown: Don't Be a Fair-Weather Friend to Your Fitness

Alright, Chicago, it’s May 19th, and honestly, the weather gods are giving you a break. That 18.5°C and overcast sky is prime for hitting the pavement without getting baked by the sun or drowned in humidity. Seriously, you've got zero excuses on the temperature front today.

The Air Quality Index is a crisp 30, which means whatever "Ozone/Other" pollutants are out there aren't making a significant dent in your lungs today. You can actually fill 'em up without feeling like you're sucking on a tailpipe. This is a far cry from the days when the air looked like a bad Instagram filter. Enjoy it while it lasts.

### Pollen's Still a Pain, Though

Now, don't get too cocky. While the air quality is on your side, the city's flora is still trying to kill your sinuses. Pollen is at a moderate level. So, if you usually sound like a dying goose after a few miles this time of year, pop your meds *before* you head out. Nobody wants to see you hacking up a lung on the Lakefront Trail, even if the air *is* clean.

### Keep an Eye on Your Feet, Especially North

Speaking of trails, while the weekend's Chicago Spring Half Marathon closures in Grant Park should be well cleared by now, construction is always a thing in this city. There's ongoing talk about various bike infrastructure projects across Cook County, including work on segments of the Des Plaines River Trail and the Burnham Greenway as part of the Grand Illinois Trail completion push. While there aren't *new, major* closures announced for today directly impacting the downtown Lakefront, if you're heading to the forest preserves or north/south suburbs, always check local alerts. You don't want to get rerouted onto some sketchy road because a new boardwalk is going in where your trail used to be. The Des Plaines River Trail, for example, has seen ongoing work, with portions south of the Lawrence Avenue bridge still being addressed as of earlier this year. Don't be that person surprised by a "Path Closed" sign.
