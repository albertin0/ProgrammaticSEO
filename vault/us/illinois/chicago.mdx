## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Flood Watch/Warning Hangover:** We've had a solid run of rain, and while the skies are just overcast now, those river levels aren't magically dropping. Expect lingering puddles, trail closures, and definitely some squishy sections. The Des Plaines River Trail, especially the underpasses, is likely to be a mess.
*   **Windy City Lives Up to Its Name:** You're looking at 15-25 mph winds from the WNW, with gusts hitting up to 28 mph. That Lakefront Trail run isn't going to be a gentle breeze; it'll be a full-on headwind for half your journey and a push for the other. Good luck with those PRs.
*   **Moderate Pollen is Back:** Just when you thought you cleared that last hurdle, the moderate pollen count is here to remind your sinuses it's spring. Couple that with the damp air, and you might feel like you're breathing through a wet sponge.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Flooding isn't just an inconvenience, it's a hazard.
Don't be that person trying to power through knee-deep water on a path that's clearly marked as flooded. "Turn around, don't drown" applies to more than just driving. We've seen reports of DuSable Lake Shore Drive underpasses inundated, and if it's bad there, assume any low-lying sections of your favorite trail are compromised. The Des Plaines River is particularly problematic, with near-record levels reported. Parts of Cornish Park in Algonquin are already shut down. You think your waterproof shoes are up to the task? Think again. That water is not pristine, folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route, Check Your Apps, and Maybe Just Hit the Gym.
If you're still determined to get outside, be smart. Check the Chicago Park District's website or social media (hashtag #chiLFT) for real-time Lakefront Trail conditions, especially around the underpasses. Expect slippery surfaces and debris, even in areas that aren't fully submerged. The wind will be a constant companion, so dress in layers that you can adjust. And seriously, if your allergies are acting up, consider whether an indoor workout is the smarter play today. No medals for self-induced misery.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, it's not the worst day ever, but it's not exactly calling your name for a carefree sprint either. Be cautious, be prepared, and don't assume your usual routes are clear.
