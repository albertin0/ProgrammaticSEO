The National Weather Service isn't kidding around with a Level 3 Enhanced Risk for severe weather, particularly this morning and early afternoon. We're talking winds that can knock you off your feet, hail big enough to leave a mark, and the low-but-not-zero chance of a tornado. Those "isolated tornadoes" aren't usually *that* isolated when they decide to drop.
