## Chicago Marathon Chaos & Climbing Temps

Alright, Chicago, let's cut to the chase: Today isn't about *your* training run, it's about the Bank of America Chicago Marathon. Over 55,000 runners are hitting the streets, and that means if you're not one of them, you're pretty much out of luck for hitting your preferred pavement or trail. Street closures started days ago and are now in full swing, turning most of downtown and 29 neighborhoods into a complex maze of detours and spectator zones. Forget trying to get across town easily, and definitely forget doing any kind of speed work along the Lakefront Trail where it's impacted by the course, or anywhere near Grant Park, which is the start and finish line.

### Weather Watch: More Than Just a "Clear" Day

While the sky might look "Clear" right now at 15.2°C (that's about 59°F for the non-metric folk), don't get fooled. The forecast for later today predicts highs pushing 71-74°F (around 22-23°C). For October 11th, that's warm, folks. The marathon organizers even slapped a "yellow alert" on the race, advising runners to slow down. If you *are* brave enough to attempt a workout, remember that even "moderate" temperatures feel a lot hotter when you're exerting yourself, especially in direct sunlight which we're getting plenty of. Hydration isn't just for the marathoners.
