## Workout Hazards & Conditions

This is one of those days where the actual *weather* is pristine, but the *urban landscape* is a minefield of activity. Don't just lace up and sprint; you need a strategy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Downtown & Lakefront Trail Chaos
Today is the final day for a couple of HUGE events. The **Taste of Chicago** is wrapping up in Grant Park, meaning massive street closures around Columbus Drive, Ida B. Wells Drive, and Jackson Drive. Don't even think about running through there. Simultaneously, the **Polka Run 5K** is happening, with its route taking over parts of Lake Shore Drive and the paths along Lake Michigan near McCormick Place. If you were planning a long run on the Lakefront Trail, especially south of Millennium Park, be prepared for significant crowds, detours, and possibly needing to walk entire sections. These areas will be thick with people, so adjust your pace and expectations accordingly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Lincoln Park & North Side Events
Another race, the **Chicago History 5K Run and Walk**, kicks off this morning near the Chicago History Museum (1601 N Clark St). This means routes through Lincoln Park and surrounding streets will be impacted, particularly early in the day. If Lincoln Park is your usual stomping ground, either go early, head further north or west, or be ready to share the path with a lot of enthusiastic (and possibly not-so-speedy) walkers.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Ahead, Or Embrace the Chaos
Seriously, check your routes. Use apps, check local news, or just assume any major park or scenic route might be swarmed. If you don't want to deal with the crowds, explore some of the lesser-known neighborhood trails or stick to side streets. Hydration is always key, but especially when navigating crowds – it's more work than you think!
</AlertBox>
