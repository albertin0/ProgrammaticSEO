## Today's Vibe: Winter's Last Gasp (We Hope)

Look, I get it. It's May. We're all itching to shed the layers and hit the Lakefront Trail in shorts. But today, Friday, May 2nd, the universe decided to prank us with a blast of unseasonably cold air. We're talking frost advisories across much of the area through Saturday morning, with temperatures struggling to get much above freezing early on. Even if the sun peeks out later, those exposed sections of the Lakefront Trail are going to feel a few degrees colder with the northeast wind.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Bundle Up, Seriously.
  With current temperatures at -0.2°C (31.6°F) and the possibility of frost, dressing in layers is non-negotiable. Don't skimp on gloves, a hat, and a good base layer. Hypothermia is no joke, even in May. Pay extra attention to your warm-up to protect those joints from the chill.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen and Air Quality: The Mixed Bag

On the lung front, it's a mixed bag. The Air Quality Index is a beautiful 38, which means the air is clean, and you can breathe easy in that regard. No nasty PM2.5 to contend with today. However, pollen is sitting at a "Moderate" level, so if you're one of the many Chicagoans whose nose decides to stage a protest this time of year, be prepared. Grab your antihistamines before you head out, especially if you're planning a longer session around areas like Lincoln Park, where budding trees and plants are doing their thing.

### Local Trail & Road Impact

As for specific hazards, yesterday was May Day, and while there were various rallies and marches planned around Grant Park and other areas, those events appear to have wrapped up for today. So you shouldn't run into any large-scale event-related street closures or crowd blockages.

However, Metra is undergoing some construction impacting several lines this weekend. If your usual route involves taking the Metra Electric Line, be aware that it's on a Sunday schedule today and no trains are running between Millennium and 18th Street stations, with bus shuttles in place. This might affect your commute to your preferred running spot, so plan accordingly. Also, the Salt Creek Trail under I-294 remains closed, with reopening not expected until early June.
