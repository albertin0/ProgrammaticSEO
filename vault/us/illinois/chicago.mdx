## Today's Rundown

Alright, Chicago, listen up. It's 16.5°C (that's about 62°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit) and partly cloudy. Normally, I'd say "get out there and enjoy it," but today's a little… complicated.

The big news, if you haven't already seen the barricades being set up since Friday, is the **Bank of America Shamrock Shuffle**. Grant Park is basically ground zero for thousands of runners, and a huge swath of downtown streets is shut down. We're talking Columbus Drive, parts of Michigan Avenue, State Street, Wacker Drive – the whole nine yards. These closures started early this morning and won't fully clear until mid-day. If your usual long run takes you through the Loop or along the lakefront near Grant Park, you're either running the race, cheering someone on, or seriously rerouting your workout.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Major Street Closures in Downtown & Grant Park:** The Shamrock Shuffle means many roads are inaccessible until at least 11:30 AM today. Check the official race routes and street closure maps before you head out, especially if you planned to run anywhere near the Loop. Even pedestrian traffic will be redirected in some areas.
</AlertBox>

On the air front, our Air Quality Index is hanging out at 59, which falls into the "Moderate" category. The Illinois Environmental Protection Agency and AirNow confirm this. The primary troublemaker is PM2.5 – those fine particulate matters that can be annoying if you're sensitive. So, while it's not "stay indoors and seal the windows" bad, it's not exactly sparkling mountain air either.

Speaking of annoyances, pollen is at a moderate level. For the allergy sufferers among us, that means potential sniffles and itchy eyes could join you on your run.
