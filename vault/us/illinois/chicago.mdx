*   Forget that early morning clear sky. The National Weather Service is calling for a cold front to roll through this afternoon, bringing with it showers, thunderstorms, and a "level 3 out of 5 threat for severe weather." We're talking large, possibly *very* large hail (over 2 inches in diameter) and damaging winds up to 60 mph. Temperatures could drop by 20+ degrees in an hour. Your "peaceful run" will be anything but.

Today started deceptively mild and clear, but don't let it fool you. While the air quality (AQI 35) is perfectly fine and pollen is only at a "Moderate" level, the afternoon weather forecast is a nasty curveball. That sudden temperature drop, coupled with the threat of severe storms, is a recipe for a miserable (and potentially dangerous) outdoor session. You're going to go from sweating in what felt like spring to shivering while getting pelted by hail faster than you can say "I should have just stayed on the treadmill."
