Look, I know you're dedicated, but today, September 30th, is making a strong case for the treadmill. We've got temperatures hovering around a damp 16-19°C (61-67°F) under overcast skies, which, by itself, isn't terrible. But when you add in the constant threat of a soaking and genuine flood risks, it's just not worth it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Out for Flooding!
Heavy rain means significant ponding on streets and underpasses. Seriously, do *not* try to run through standing water. A water main break near 25th and Claremont already caused knee-high flooding in basements this morning, and the city's expecting more localized issues. Turn around, find another way, or better yet, stay inside. Your ankles (and your insurance company) will thank you.
</AlertBox>

The Chicago River bridges are doing their yearly dance to tuck away sailboats for winter. That means if your usual run takes you across the river downtown, like over the Lake Street Bridge, prepare for some frustrating stops. Speaking of Lake Street, that bridge is already a mess with ongoing rehabilitation and bridge house demolition, so even without the lifts, it's not exactly prime running territory.

And while the full-blown Chicago Marathon chaos isn't until October 11th, the early birds have already started shutting things down. Grant Park is seeing ongoing street and park closures since September 24th for finish-line infrastructure. Balbo Drive, specifically, is a no-go between Columbus and DuSable Lake Shore Drive. So, forget any long scenic stretches through that area.

The air quality, surprisingly, is actually decent, with an AQI of 29. So your lungs might be happy if you were just, say, *breathing* outside, but the rest of your body would be miserable.

The Chicago Department of Transportation's annual fall bridge lifts are active today on the Chicago River. This means periodic stoppages for cars and pedestrians at various downtown crossings. Plan your route accordingly and be prepared to wait or reroute if you're trying to cross.
