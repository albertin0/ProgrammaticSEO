## Today's Vibe: Crisp Air, Cold Everything Else

Today's forecast is "clear" and, well, *cold*. We're looking at temperatures around 3.8°C (that's about 39°F for those of us who still use real measurements) after overnight lows dipped close to 39°F. While the sun's supposed to make an appearance, those northwest winds, shifting to east later, might gust up to 10 mph, making it feel even colder. So, that light jacket you *thought* was enough? Think again. You need long sleeves, gloves, a hat – the whole shebang. Your lungs will thank you for not sucking down icy air, and your joints will appreciate the warmth when you're trying to get those muscles moving.

## Local Hazards to Dodge

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beware the Brrr!** With temperatures hovering around freezing, particularly early in the day, exposed skin can get cold fast. Dress in layers, cover your extremities, and consider shortening your outdoor workout if you're sensitive to the cold.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Salt Creek Trail Closure Continues:** If the **Salt Creek Trail** near I-294 is part of your route, you're out of luck. It's still shut down for Tollway work and isn't expected to reopen until early June. And no, there isn't a "designated alternative route," which is just fantastic. Riders have reported "unsafe workarounds," so seriously, find a different path for now unless you enjoy playing Frogger with construction equipment.
</AlertBox>

Elsewhere around the city, general construction is still Chicago's favorite pastime, though most of it won't directly impact your runs unless you're trying to sprint down Oak Park Avenue in the western suburbs, where water and sewer pipe work has shut down a stretch. On the bright side, the reconstruction of four bridges around Montrose Harbor is "nearly finished" as of yesterday, so at least that section of the Lakefront Trail should be looking good soon.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Looking Ahead:** While not impacting today, keep an eye on the calendar. The Life Time Chicago Spring Half Marathon & 5K is coming up on May 17th near Maggie Daley Park, and the Bank of America Chicago 13.1 on June 7th will mean street closures, especially around Garfield Park, starting June 3rd. Planning your routes around these events will save you a world of frustration.
</AlertBox>

In short, today is manageable if you're smart about your attire and avoid that one notoriously closed trail. Otherwise, enjoy the clear skies before the scattered showers and thunderstorms roll in this weekend.
