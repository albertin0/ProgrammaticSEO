<AlertBox type="danger">
### DuSable Lake Shore Drive and Lakefront Trail are a Warzone this Morning!
Seriously, if your plan was a peaceful long run on the Lakefront Trail between Grant Park and, say, Fullerton, you picked the wrong morning. "Bike The Drive 2026" has DuSable Lake Shore Drive shut down for cars until roughly noon, turning it into a massive bike course. This also means cyclists will be redirected onto the already busy Lakefront Trail as the event winds down, creating major congestion and potential hazards for runners, especially with different speeds and directions of traffic. Avoid the central Lakefront Trail area until well after 12 PM if you value your shins and sanity. Head west or south of 31st Street if you need your lake views, but be prepared for detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Wet Stuff
Even though the skies are clearing, a "Mainly Clear" forecast doesn't erase last night's deluge. The Chicago River Swim was canceled for a reason, folks – combined sewer overflow is no joke, and trails and low-lying areas will be damp, if not outright puddled. Watch for slippery leaves, slick pavement, and unexpected standing water, especially on side streets or less-maintained paths. Your ankles will thank you for taking it easy and sticking to well-drained routes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Construction Zone on the South Side
If you're out near the 95th to 130th Street corridor, remember the ongoing Red Line Extension construction. This multi-year project means various street and sidewalk closures, potential dust, and truck traffic. It's not a new 24-hour hazard, but it's a constant consideration for runners and cyclists navigating that part of the city. Always stay aware of changing traffic patterns and signage, and assume you might need to reroute.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Allergy Alert: Moderate Pollen
For all you snotty-nosed sufferers (you know who you are), pollen levels are moderate today. While it's not a full-blown attack, don't forget your meds if seasonal allergies usually get to you. A good run can feel a lot less exhilarating when you're battling a runny nose and itchy eyes.
</AlertBox>

Today's a mixed bag. Enjoy the stellar temps and clean air, but be smart about where and when you get your miles in. The city is a playground, but sometimes it's also a demolition derby.
