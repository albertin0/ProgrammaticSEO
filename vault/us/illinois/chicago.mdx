Look, I get it. The lakefront usually offers a bit of a breeze, but even along Lake Michigan, the heat index will be brutal today. This isn't just "sweaty workout" heat; this is "medical emergency" heat if you push it. City officials are already telling everyone to stay cool, check on neighbors, and utilize cooling centers for a reason.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat Warning: 100-105°F Heat Index
  An **Extreme Heat Warning** is in effect for Chicago and surrounding Cook County through Wednesday, July 1st. High temperatures in the mid-90s combined with oppressive humidity mean it'll *feel* like 100-105°F. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these conditions. If you absolutely must be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and drink more water than you think you need. But honestly, stay inside. Your lungs and joints (and every other organ) will thank you.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, save the long runs for another day. Even a brisk walk could be dangerous. If you planned on hitting the Lakefront Trail or doing loops in Lincoln Park, reconsider. The ground radiates heat, and shade can be scarce. The city has cooling centers open if your AC is struggling – use them.

Beyond the heat, watch the sky. Those scattered thunderstorms aren't just a nuisance; hail, as noted by the National Weather Service, is a real threat. You don't want to be caught out on an exposed trail when that hits.

And if you were thinking of navigating the crowds, remember the Taste of Chicago is happening. Columbus Drive between Monroe and Balbo, Congress between Michigan and Columbus, and Jackson between Lake Shore Drive and Michigan are all seeing street closures. So even if you ignore the weather, your usual routes around Grant Park are going to be a mess.
