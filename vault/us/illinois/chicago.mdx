<BulletList>
*   **The Air: Pretty Good, But Watch for Pollen.** Our AQI is a respectable 50, which means you won't be tasting Canadian wildfire smoke today, thankfully. That said, pollen is moderate, so if you're one of the sniffly ones, pop that antihistamine before you head out.

## Your Daily Grind Breakdown:

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Lakefront Trail is a Bike Highway, Not Your Running Path This Morning!**
Seriously, if you thought you'd get some uninterrupted miles on the Lakefront Trail between, say, North Avenue Beach and Grant Park, think again. Bike the Drive has turned DuSable Lake Shore Drive into a cycling free-for-all until noon. While the separated pedestrian path might *technically* be open, it's going to be overflowing with spillover from the main event and frustrated runners like you. Diversions and heavy crowds near entry points like those around Navy Pier and Museum Campus are guaranteed.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Alternative Routes & Timing Your Run**
If you still want to get out, consider hitting your usual Lakefront Trail miles *after* noon, when DuSable Lake Shore Drive reopens to cars and the bulk of the cyclists have dispersed. For morning runners, your best bet is to head inland. The North Branch Trail System in the Forest Preserves of Cook County (like near Caldwell Woods or Morton Grove) is a solid option, though some sections still have minor construction alerts, so stay alert for signage. Or, stick to your neighborhood streets; just be aware that the Chicago Waterfront 10K is also happening today, potentially causing minor localized disruptions near the waterfront.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Ongoing Construction Around Navy Pier and Lake Street**
Don't forget the persistent construction headache. The Navy Pier Flyover project is still ongoing, aiming to fix that notoriously congested stretch of the Lakefront Trail from the Chicago River to Navy Pier. And the Lake Street Bascule Bridge rehabilitation might still be impacting pedestrian access to the Riverwalk, so if you're trying to hit those downtown routes, be prepared for detours.
</AlertBox>
