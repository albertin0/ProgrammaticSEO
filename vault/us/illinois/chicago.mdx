## The Rundown for September 21, 2026

Alright, Chicago, let's talk about getting those miles in today. The good news is the air quality index (AQI) is a sweet 33, which is excellent. So, your lungs aren't going to be working overtime fending off pollutants. Temperature's sitting pretty at a comfortable 19.8°C (around 68°F), making it an ideal day for breaking a sweat without melting.

However, it's not all sunshine and PRs.

### Weather Whining & Water Hazards

The National Weather Service reported some bands of heavy rain this morning across northern Illinois and far northwest Indiana. This means you're likely to encounter localized ponding on roads and some low-lying areas might be flooded. If your route takes you near rivers in Lake County, be advised there's a River Flood Warning in effect until Wednesday afternoon. Southern and Northern Will County also had a River Flood Watch that expires tonight. Basically, if it looks wet, find another way around. Don't be "that guy" trying to run through ankle-deep water. That's a surefire way to tweak an ankle or worse.

And for those thinking of a refreshing dip or a paddle on Lake Michigan? Forget about it. There's a "High Swim Risk" and "Hazardous conditions for small craft" in effect for the Lake Michigan beaches, continuing through Wednesday, all thanks to those persistent northeast winds whipping up high waves. Seriously, stay out of the water unless you're actively trying to become a statistic.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Watch for Flooded Streets & Trails:** Recent heavy rains mean standing water is a real possibility, especially on low-lying paths. Plan alternative routes to avoid injuries.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Troubles & Event Eruptions

If your usual go-to is the **Lakefront Trail**, listen up: the Chicago Park District just announced that maintenance work on the entire 18-mile stretch is starting "this fall" and "in the next few weeks." They're talking asphalt patching, seal-coating, new trail graphics, and "trail closures to occur." While they start at 6300 South and work their way north, expect to see crews and signage. Assume delays, detours, and active construction zones. Your morning run along the lake might be less scenic and more obstacle course for the foreseeable future.

Beyond the lakefront, some of the Forest Preserves of Cook County also have ongoing issues. The Red Paved Loop at Orland Grassland Trails is undergoing maintenance, and parts of the Poplar Creek Trail System are closed for IDOT construction. Check the Forest Preserves website before you head out, because getting there only to find a "Trail Closed" sign is peak frustration.

Finally, if you're anywhere near the Douglass Park area, be aware that street closures related to Riot Fest are still in effect today. Farrar Drive from 12th Place to Ogden Avenue is closed until 11:59 PM tonight, and other closures on Sacramento and Farrar Drives continue until Thursday. Don't try to navigate that mess.
