## The Rundown

Look, if you're attempting a downtown run today, you either didn't check the news, or you really like jumping barricades. The 48th Bank of America Chicago Marathon is this Sunday, October 11th, and the city's already in full prep mode. That means you can say goodbye to your usual smooth sailing through **Grant Park** and around Columbus, Jackson, and Congress Plaza Drives. These aren't just minor diversions; we're talking full-on closures that started this morning at 4 AM and won't lift until Monday. Want to visit the Abbott Health and Fitness Expo? Head to McCormick Place, but don't expect to run *to* it easily.

### Trail Trouble Beyond the Loop

It's not just downtown either. If you're usually hitting the longer trails, you're still in for some headaches. The **Lakefront Trail** is undergoing repairs, with asphalt patching, seal-coating, and edge work from 63rd Street north to Foster Avenue Beach. Signs are up, so be smart and avoid those active construction zones unless you fancy a face-off with a paver.

Further out, the **Major Taylor Trail** is completely shut down at Whistler Woods until December 1st for some "improvements." Same goes for parts of the Poplar Creek Trail System near Bode East due to IDOT work, and the Des Plaines River Trail between Fullerton Avenue and North Avenue is also seeing significant construction. Seriously, if you're not checking local trail alerts, you're basically signing up for an impromptu cross-country detour.
