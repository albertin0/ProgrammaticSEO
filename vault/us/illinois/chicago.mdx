## The Rundown: A Soggy & Blocked City

Let's cut to the chase: The weather itself isn't terrible. We've got clear skies and a comfortable 18.5°C (about 65°F), which usually makes for a prime running day. Your lungs will appreciate the "Good" Air Quality Index of 46, with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant, but still well within healthy limits. Pollen's sitting at "Moderate," so if you're usually sensitive, pop an antihistamine, but it's not a deal-breaker.

However, the real hazards today are on the ground. Recent storms have left their mark, and it's not pretty for outdoor enthusiasts.

### Flooding and Trail Carnage

If you're out west, particularly along the East Branch Du Page River, forget it. A Flood Warning was in effect as of yesterday, threatening the DuPage River Greenway Trail near Bolingbrook and other paths in Green Valley Forest Preserve. Minor flooding is *still* occurring, and frankly, that means mud, debris, and potential washouts. Don't be that person who needs rescuing because you thought your waterproof trail shoes could handle a river.

Even closer to home, areas like the Izaak Walton Preserve are still recovering from a "weekend deluge," with standing water and significant flooding. We're talking downed trees across paths and conditions so bad that officials are "strongly recommend[ing] avoiding the trails until conditions improve." Some parts might be closed for *weeks*. Consider yourself warned.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Significant Flooding & Trail Closures!** Do NOT attempt to run or bike on flooded trails, especially along the DuPage River or in preserves still recovering from recent storms. Respect barriers and warning signs. Water hides hazards.
</AlertBox>

### Grant Park Gridlock for Your Run

Downtown, while the river isn't threatening to swallow you whole, the annual Taste of Chicago is slowly eating away at Grant Park. Street closures, including Jackson between Michigan Avenue and Lake Shore Drive, and Columbus from Balbo to Roosevelt, went into full effect yesterday as setup continues through July 12. If your usual route takes you through or around Grant Park, prepare for major diversions and heavy equipment. The Lakefront Trail might be technically open, but getting to it through the maze of closures around Taste of Chicago setup will be a headache.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Taste of Chicago Street Closures.** Expect major disruptions around Grant Park. Avoid running through the festival setup zone; plan alternative routes if you typically use this area.
</AlertBox>

### Ongoing Construction & Smart Choices

Beyond the festivals and immediate flood zones, remember that Chicago is always under construction. While non-emergency closures from the holiday weekend *may* have reopened, ongoing infrastructure projects citywide mean you should always keep an eye out for unexpected lane reductions or sidewalk closures, especially on major thoroughfares.

<AlertBox type="info">
**PRO TIP: Plan Your Route, Scope it Out.** With ongoing construction and localized flooding, check park district websites or local news for specific trail status updates before heading out. If you see standing water, turn back. There's no PR for being pulled from a creek.
</AlertBox>

Today's a day for smart choices. Maybe hit the gym, do some stairs, or stick to known, clear routes. And for the love of your ankles, don't jump into murky water.
