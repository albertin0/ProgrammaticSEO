## Today's Workout Outlook

Currently, it's a mild 11.1°C (52°F) with clear skies, which sounds inviting, right? Wrong. This is Chicago in March, where the weather can pull a bait-and-switch faster than you can say "parking ticket." While it might feel good now, a cold front is pushing through, bringing with it the threat of severe weather later today. Expect temperatures to drop significantly by tomorrow, with Friday feeling much chillier and breezy.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Incoming Storm Risk!** Later today, especially south of I-80, there's a serious chance of strong thunderstorms, large hail, and damaging wind gusts. This isn't the kind of weather you want to be caught in on your long run or bike ride. Keep an eye on the radar and have an indoor backup plan ready.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk & Road Reports

Beyond the immediate weather, don't forget the usual urban chaos. The **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294 remains closed until early June, so if that's part of your route, you're out of luck. And construction continues to be a never-ending saga. The Rand Road bridge over the Des Plaines River is currently undergoing reconstruction, which impacts access to the Des Plaines River Trail.

Even your usual driving routes might be a mess. The Stevenson and Dan Ryan Expressways have ongoing construction projects with lane closures, mostly at night, but expect general traffic headaches. And let's not even start on the Chicago River bridges; more closures are slated for future repairs, with the Lake Street Bridge still out for a couple more years.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Grant Park Vehicle Access Compromised:** If you typically drive to Grant Park for your workout, be aware that portions are still under vehicle access restrictions due to the Shamrock Shuffle earlier this week. Balbo Drive, for example, remains closed until today, and general vehicle traffic to the park is limited until Sunday. Plan on walking, or find an alternate starting point.
</AlertBox>
