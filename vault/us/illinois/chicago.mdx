<AlertBox type="info">

### Watch for Seasonal Surprises
While there are no current reports of major trail closures or surprise flooding in the city proper for today, May is still spring. Keep an eye on the Chicago Park District alerts if you're venturing off the beaten path or onto less-trafficked routes. Even though the big flooding reports from last month around the Des Plaines River are well behind us, unexpected rain can always pop up in the Midwest.
