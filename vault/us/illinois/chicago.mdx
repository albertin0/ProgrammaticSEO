## The Deep Freeze Ain't Over Yet

Look, I get it. It's March in Chicago. We're all desperate for spring. But today, Mother Nature's having a laugh, keeping us squarely in winter's icy grip. We're talking -0.8°C (that's 30.6°F for those still in Fahrenheit land) and partly cloudy. While it's not a blizzard, that temperature is just cold enough to make every step on pavement feel a little harder, especially on your knees and hips. Don't go thinking the Lakefront Trail is magically clear just because the sun's peeking out. Winter conditions often mean sections can close due to freezing, and if there's any residual moisture from previous days, you could be dealing with black ice. Keep your eyes peeled and maybe opt for some trail shoes with better grip if you're venturing out.

## Lung Check: Breathe Easy (Mostly)

Alright, credit where it's due: the air itself isn't actively trying to kill you today. Our Air Quality Index is a comfortable 37, which is "Good," with Ozone/Other being the dominant pollutant. So, while your fingers and toes might be screaming, your actual lungs should be in decent shape.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step Around Grant Park!**
The Shamrock Shuffle wrapped up yesterday, which is great for the city's spirit, but not always great for immediate trail access. Balbo Drive, specifically from Columbus Drive to DuSable Lake Shore Drive, was expected to remain closed until Wednesday. Even if the race route is clear, be aware of any remaining barricades, road debris, or cleanup efforts in the downtown and Grant Park areas. Better safe than tripping over a rogue traffic cone.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Layer Up, Seriously.**
It's tempting to shed layers as we approach April, but with temps hovering around freezing, proper layering is non-negotiable. Think technical base layer, an insulating mid-layer, and a wind-resistant outer shell. Don't forget gloves, a hat, and thick socks. Frostbite on your ears isn't a medal.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Suburban Construction Continues to Be a Headache.**
If you're heading outside the city for your run, be aware that construction season is officially *always* season in Illinois. For instance, construction on the Rand Road bridge over the Des Plaines River is kicking off today, which will impact local multi-use path connections to the Des Plaines River Trail. Also, the Salt Creek Trail under I-294 remains closed, with work tentatively scheduled to finish by early June. Don't assume your favorite suburban routes are clear; check local alerts before you go.
</AlertBox>
