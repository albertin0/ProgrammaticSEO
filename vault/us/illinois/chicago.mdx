## The Elephant (and Reggaeton Festival) in the Park

You planning on a long run down the Lakefront Trail near downtown today? Good luck with that. The **Sueños Chicago Music Festival** is in full swing at Grant Park, and it's basically swallowed half the downtown lakefront. We're talking major street and sidewalk closures on Balbo Drive and Columbus Drive, not to mention a massive influx of people. If your usual route takes you through or around this area, find another way, or just accept you'll be dodging crowds more than actually running. Your patience, and probably your ankles, will thank you.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Downtown Congestion & Closures!** The Sueños Chicago Music Festival is dominating Grant Park today. Expect significant street and sidewalk closures around Balbo Drive, Columbus Drive, and parts of DuSable Lake Shore Drive. Pedestrian traffic will be HEAVY. Avoid this area if you're trying to get a serious workout in.
</AlertBox>

## Weather & Other Headaches

While the air quality is on your side, don't get *too* comfy. There's a "Limited Thunderstorm Risk" for Sunday morning, especially east of I-55, with some locally heavy rain possible. "Limited" usually means "don't plan on severe weather, but also don't be shocked if you get soaked."

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Scattered Showers:** We've got a limited risk of thunderstorms this morning, particularly for areas east of I-55. Nothing severe expected, but a quick downpour could still ruin your day. Keep an eye on the radar.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the downtown mess, keep an eye on other, less central routes. For you suburban cyclists, a section of the **Salt Creek Trail** under I-294 is closed until early June due to Tollway construction. Don't get caught off guard.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Check:** For those with allergies, today's moderate pollen means you might be feeling it. Pre-dose your meds, or stick to indoor workouts if you're usually hit hard.
</AlertBox>
