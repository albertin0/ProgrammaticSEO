## The Air You Breathe (or Huff and Puff Through)

Your lungs are getting a decent break today, all things considered. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a moderate 62, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. Not exactly sparkling clean, but it's nowhere near the "hazardous" levels we saw back in July when that Canadian wildfire smoke decided to camp out over the city. Still, if you're sensitive to ozone, maybe don't push that tempo run too hard, especially late in the afternoon when ground-level ozone tends to peak.

If you're sneezing on your long run, that's probably why.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Lakefront Trail!** Construction is *not* over. Expect ongoing asphalt patching and edge repairs, especially as you head south from Grant Park towards the Museum Campus and beyond. These active work zones will have signage, but stay alert for sudden changes in surface or path closures. This isn't the time to be glued to your watch.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for the Wind Chill.** While the thermometer reads a pleasant 19.8°C, those 30 mph gusts, particularly along the lake, will make it feel significantly cooler. Layer up with a windbreaker if you're planning a longer session, especially if you're running any north-south stretches of the Lakefront Trail. Nobody wants to finish a run feeling hypothermic in mid-November.
</AlertBox>

So, lace 'em up if you're feeling feisty, but don't say I didn't warn you about leaning into that wind. Your quads will remember this tomorrow.
