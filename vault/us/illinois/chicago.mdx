## The Air: Surprisingly Not Awful
Look, Chicago's air quality can be a real crapshoot, especially as we head into summer. But today? We're hovering at an AQI of 45, which is firmly in the "Good" category. You can thank the relatively mild 15.4°C (about 60°F) and partly cloudy skies for keeping things from cooking up too much ground-level ozone. This means you shouldn't be wheezing through your intervals because of airborne particulate matter, which is a win in my book.

However, and this is where my cynicism kicks in, don't get used to it. AccuWeather is already calling for "Poor" AQI for Saturday and Sunday, so if you've got a long run planned for the weekend, consider hitting the treadmill or getting out *very* early.

## Trail & Road Shenanigans: Construction, Always Construction.
If you're heading out for a run or ride, be aware that Chicago is doing what Chicago does best: tearing up roads and paths.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Illinois Prairie Path!** The Salt Creek Trail section under I-294 is still closed, and has been for a while, with no reopening expected until late summer due to the massive Tri-State construction project. Plus, other sections of the Illinois Prairie Path, particularly near I-294 and I-290 in Elmhurst, are experiencing intermittent closures for pedestrians. Flaggers are supposed to be on site, but don't count on them being your personal guides. Be prepared for detours and bring your patience.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, if your usual route involves the Prairie Path out west, check the Illinois Tollway website before you head out, or just assume there will be some fresh hell waiting for you.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Road Closures (Traffic, Not Trails):** The Ohio Street ramp between the Kennedy Expressway and Orleans Street is fully closing tonight at 11 p.m. and won't reopen until Monday morning. This isn't a trail issue, but if you're driving to a workout spot in that area, you're going to hit delays.
</AlertBox>

## Weather Whining
Yesterday saw some scattered storms, wind damage, and even a tornado or two in the region, which is always a joy. While Chicago O'Hare's forecast for today is mostly sunny and milder, the National Weather Service is still flagging a "Threat for Severe Weather and Flash Flooding Today" for the broader area. Keep an eye on the sky, especially if you're heading further afield.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Stay Weather-Aware:** Even with a partly cloudy start, pop-up storms can happen. Don't be that person caught miles from shelter when the skies open up. Check a reliable weather app before you commit to a long outdoor session.
</AlertBox>

All in all, today's a decent day for a run, provided you're flexible with your route and keep an eye on those construction zones and the skies. Your lungs will thank you for the good air, even if your joints might curse the uneven pavement or detours.
