### Don't Breathe Too Deeply (or do, if you're a masochist)
That moderate Air Quality Index (AQI 62, dominant pollutant PM2.5) is just high enough to be annoying, especially if you've got sensitive airways. Combine it with the moderate pollen count, and you might find yourself doing a surprising amount of unplanned interval training (sprinting between sneezes). If you're prone to asthma or allergies, maybe scale back the intensity or stick to an indoor session today. No one needs a post-run coughing fit that sounds like a broken accordion.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### The Loop is a Maze: Lake Street Bridge Still a Mess
Heads up if your route takes you anywhere near the Loop, especially on the west side. The Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT) announced that the major replacement of the **Lake Street Bridge** has been extended until 4 a.m. Wednesday, October 28. This isn't just a CTA problem; it means ongoing traffic snarls, potential detours, and general chaos for pedestrians and cyclists trying to navigate that area. Don't assume your usual path across the river is clear. Also, the State/Lake elevated station reconstruction is still an ongoing headache, impacting pedestrian flow. Planning a run along the Chicago Riverwalk? Access from Lake Street to 150 N. Riverside Plaza Upper Riverwalk is closed, expected to last until summer 2026. Find an alternate access point, or just avoid that section.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Post-Event Lingering & General Urban Grit
While yesterday's Haunted Halloween Half Marathon in Lincoln Park is over, expect some residual crowd energy or maybe even a stray orange cone or two if you're hitting the Lakefront Trail up there. You know how it is. Also, various infrastructure upgrades in neighborhoods like Pilsen (W 17th St, W Cullerton Ave) mean sidewalk work and temporary "No Parking" zones, which can mess with your neighborhood loops. Always keep an eye out for unexpected street furniture or hastily placed barriers. Chicago's always under construction, somewhere.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, pick your poison. The weather's decent for moving, but the air quality and persistent urban archaeology (we call it "construction") are doing their best to derail your zen.
