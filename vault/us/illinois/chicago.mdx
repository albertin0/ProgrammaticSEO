<AlertBox type="danger">
### Humboldt Park is a No-Go Zone for Serious Runs
Forget about hitting the trails in **Humboldt Park** today. The Puerto Rican Festival and People's Parade is in full swing from June 11-14, and today is smack in the middle of it. Unless your workout involves navigating dense crowds, dodging food vendors, and performing impromptu high-knees over stray confetti, you’ll want to find an alternate route. This isn't just "some people walking around" – this is a major cultural celebration, and it means significant portions of and access to Humboldt Park will be gridlocked with festivities. Plan on serious delays or just picking another park entirely.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Old Town Art Fair Means Foot Traffic Mayhem
If you're typically pounding the pavement or just trying to get some steps in around the **Old Town Triangle** neighborhood, be aware that the Old Town Art Fair is happening today and tomorrow. While it's a fantastic event for art lovers, it turns quaint streets into a pedestrian labyrinth. Expect street closures and heavy foot traffic, especially on sidewalks. Keep your head on a swivel if you're trying to weave through, or better yet, just avoid the area until Sunday evening. No one wants to sprain an ankle on a pottery shard.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Lakefront Path is Your Best Bet (with a caveat)
Given the lovely weather and clear air, the Lakefront Path is probably going to be busier than usual, with smart folks ditching the festival crowds for open air. Enjoy the smooth pavement and skyline views, but don't expect solitude, especially around popular access points like North Avenue Beach or Museum Campus. Always practice good path etiquette: stick to the right, pass on the left, and announce yourself. Also, keep an eye out for any specific localized construction near high-rises along the path, though no major *trail-blocking* work has been reported in the last 24 hours. The "Redefine the Drive" project concerning DuSable Lake Shore Drive is still in a planning/analysis phase, so no active trail impacts from that today, but it's a good reminder to always be aware of long-term projects near our cherished lakefront.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, hydrate, and if you're not into parades, hit the Lakefront early. Otherwise, embrace the organized chaos or stay home and stretch.
