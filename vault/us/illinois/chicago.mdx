## The Chilly Reality & Warming Lies

Alright, let's talk brass tacks. You looked outside this morning and thought, "Oh, it's just overcast." Don't let that fool you. We're sitting at a balmy 0.6&deg;C (that's 33&deg;F for the rest of us) right now. Your joints are going to scream if you try to sprint out the door in shorts. Yesterday was even colder with wind chills down to 20&deg;F. This isn't the kind of "refreshing" cold, it's the kind that makes your lungs sting and your hamstrings pull.

However, the city's weather is playing mind games. We're expecting a dramatic swing today, with temps climbing into the mid-60s (Fahrenheit) by the afternoon. So, if you're an afternoon runner, you might actually get some decent mileage in. But for you morning warriors, prepare for a slow warm-up, and dress in layers you can shed faster than a bad habit.

### Air Quality & Allergy Report

Good news, at least your lungs won't be fighting pollution today. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is a very pleasant 35, which is firmly in the "Good" category. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, but at this level, it's nothing to worry about.

Now, for those of you with sniffles and sneezes, the pollen forecast is "Moderate." Trees are definitely starting to show off, so if you're prone to seasonal allergies, you might want to pop an antihistamine before you head out, especially later in the day when the air warms up.

### Don't Ignore the Trail Closures, Seriously.

While the Lakefront Trail is likely clear, if you venture further afield, be aware. That section of the Des Plaines River Trail south of the new Lawrence Avenue bridge? Still closed. They've been "working" on it, or "lingering," since the beginning of the year. Don't assume it's magically open because the weather is improving. Turn around if you see the signs, or better yet, check the Cook County Forest Preserves site before you go. Nobody wants to add an unexpected off-road adventure (or a ticket) to their run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate, Even When It's Cold

With the temperature fluctuation today, your body will be working harder than usual to regulate. Even though it's cold this morning, you'll still be losing fluids. Keep sipping water before, during (if you're out for a long one), and after your workout. Don't wait until you're thirsty.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Frostbite is No Joke

Even at 0.6°C, especially with any residual wind, exposed skin is at risk for frostnip or even frostbite if you're out long enough and not dressed properly. Fingers, toes, ears, and nose are particularly vulnerable. Wear gloves, a hat, and consider a neck gaiter or balaclava for those early morning miles.
</AlertBox>
