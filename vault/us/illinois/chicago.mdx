*   **Pollen's Mild Annoyance:** Just to add insult to injury, pollen levels are moderate. Not the main event, but enough to make you sniffle through the haze.
*   **Pop-Up Thunderstorms:** Scattered, slow-moving thunderstorms capable of torrential rainfall and localized ponding are still possible this afternoon, particularly south of the Chicago metro. You don't want to get caught in one of those, especially if you're already pushing it in the heat.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### EXTREME HEAT INDEX ALERT!
Seriously, Chicago is forecasted to feel like 100°F in many areas today. This is prime heat exhaustion and heat stroke territory. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like it's your job. The Lakefront Trail might offer some lake breeze, but inland areas are going to be brutal. Best advice? Hit the gym's AC or just plan your run for another day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### WILDFIRE SMOKE HAZARD
Canadian wildfire smoke is drifting through, creating hazy conditions and potentially worsening air quality. Even if the AQI isn't screaming "DANGER" right now, sensitive individuals – especially those with respiratory issues – should limit prolonged outdoor exertion. If you can smell smoke, your lungs are probably feeling it.
</AlertBox>
