Alright, listen up, because this is where things get annoying. Construction is the never-ending story in Chicago, and today is no different. ### Water Hazards

If your workout involves the lakefront, pay attention. The U.S. National Weather Service issued a Beach Hazard Statement for Chicago, which was in effect from yesterday through 10 AM today. High wave action and dangerous currents are expected. So, no open water swimming, and be cautious even near the shore if you're doing beach workouts or just admiring the view. These aren't playful waves; they're dangerous.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Lakefront Warning:** Seriously, stay out of the water today. Those currents are no joke, and a "refreshing dip" can turn deadly fast. Stick to the paved paths and keep an eye on the waves.
</AlertBox>
