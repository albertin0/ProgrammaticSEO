Alright, Chicago. Get out there. The weather today is absolutely stellar for getting some miles in. We're looking at a cool 13.2°C (that's about 56°F for those still stuck in Fahrenheit land) and crystal clear skies. No gnarly wind warnings, no oppressive humidity, just solid, clear running weather. You won't find better conditions for pounding the pavement or hitting the soft paths this time of year without having to wake up before the sun.

The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at a "Good" 36, with Ozone/Other listed as the dominant pollutant. That's about as good as it gets, meaning most of you can breathe easy. However, the Chicago Department of Public Health (CDPH) did remind us earlier this week to "Keep an Eye on Air Quality" as warmer temperatures can elevate ozone and pose risks for sensitive groups. While today's AQI is fantastic, it's a good reminder that summer air can turn on a dime, so always check before you head out.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Pollen's on the Prowl
Your environmental data shows moderate pollen levels. If you're one of the unfortunate souls whose immune system declares war on spring, take your meds *before* you lace up. You don don't want to be halfway down the Lakefront Trail, hacking your lungs out while everyone else is enjoying the breeze.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the Lakefront Trail, it's generally good to go. There was a Beach Hazard Statement in effect from yesterday through 7 AM this morning, but that's cleared up now. So, no crazy waves to worry about splashing you on the path (unless some rogue speedboats are out there, but that's a different problem). This iconic 18-mile stretch is always a favorite, and with these temps, it's going to be busy. Seriously busy. Expect cyclists, walkers, fellow runners, and tourists all vying for space.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Lakefront Trail Crowds: The Eternal Struggle
The Lakefront Trail is fantastic, but it's also Chicago's busiest outdoor gym. Be aware of your surroundings, stick to your side of the path, and for the love of all that is holy, don't stop dead in the middle of the trail to check your Strava. Step off to the side. Thank you.
</AlertBox>

Now, if you're looking to escape the downtown hustle and explore some of the wider region, be mindful of construction. The Illinois Prairie Path, for instance, has "intermittent closures" around the I-294 and I-290 interchange in Elmhurst due to the Tollway's project, with flaggers on site to guide you. Similar intermittent construction might affect areas of the Thorn Creek Trail System north of Glenwood Woods in Chicago Heights. These aren't downtown Chicago, but for our suburban trail runners, it's worth checking local forest preserve websites before you roll out. No major events are blocking central city trails today, like next week's Chicago 13.1 street closures on the West Side.

All in all, today's shaping up to be a prime opportunity to rack up some miles. Just come prepared for the moderate pollen, and remember that even on a beautiful day, Chicago's trails are a shared space.
