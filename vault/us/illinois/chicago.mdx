## Environmental Check-In

### Air Quality Index (AQI):

The AQI is currently 55, categorized as "Moderate." While generally acceptable, the dominant pollutant is ozone. Keep in mind that Greater Chicago was ranked 15th worst in the nation for ozone pollution in a recent report. So, if you're prone to respiratory issues, maybe dial back the intensity or stick to areas away from heavy traffic where ozone concentrations can be higher.

## Local Hazards & Trail Alerts

### Flooding Along Our Waterways

Yes, it's clear out, but those river levels don't magically drop overnight. The Des Plaines River and the Fox River have been under flood warnings, and the city's aging stormwater infrastructure isn't helping. Do not, I repeat, *do not* try to power through standing water on a trail. You don't know what's underneath, and a sprained ankle is a real buzzkill. Stick to higher ground or known clear routes, especially around the **Des Plaines River Trail**. Many sections south of the new Lawrence Avenue bridge are still impacted by lingering construction and flood-prone areas.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**FLOODING WARNING:** Parts of the Des Plaines River and Fox River trails are still subject to flooding. Turn around, don't drown or injure yourself. Your run isn't worth a trip to the ER.
</AlertBox>

### Construction: The Chicago Constant

Construction season is officially in full swing, which means unexpected detours and blocked paths are a given. Mayor Johnson kicked off numerous infrastructure projects on April 20th, from street resurfacing to sewer replacements. Even if you're not on a major trail, assume your usual sidewalk or road shoulder might be a construction zone.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**TRAIL CLOSURES:** The Salt Creek Trail under I-294 is closed until early June, and people are taking unsafe detours. Stick to marked routes or find an alternative trail entirely. Don't add to the problem by trying to bypass official closures.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**PLAN AHEAD:** Before you commit to a long run, check the status of your preferred trails or routes. The City of Chicago's ChiStreetWork tool and OEMC alerts can provide updates on street closures and construction.
</AlertBox>

Go get those miles, but don't be stupid about it.
