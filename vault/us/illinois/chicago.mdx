That "Mainly Clear" weather condition sounds inviting, right? Wrong. It’s a trick. The temperature is hovering right at freezing, and if you were out yesterday, you know those wind chills were gnarly. Your body’s gonna take a beating if you don't dress for actual winter running, not some fantasy spring. Think thermal layers, a good hat, gloves, and maybe even a face covering to protect those airways.

Don't expect to set any PRs today if you're fighting for every breath.
