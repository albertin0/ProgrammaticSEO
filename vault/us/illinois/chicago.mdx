### The Real Hazards Today: Events & Conditions

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Severe Weather Threat
The National Weather Service Chicago is issuing a "Severe Weather Threat Today" and warns of "Heavy Rain at Times Today; Localized Flooding Possible" through the early-to-mid morning, along with "Very Windy Today." If you're out on the Lakefront Trail or anywhere near the river, expect slick surfaces, reduced visibility, and potential debris. Underpasses along trails like the Des Plaines River Trail can also flood quickly, so check local status maps before heading out, even if that one is a bit outside the city.
</AlertBox>

#### Race & Festival Congestion
The "Race Against Gun Violence" (RAGV) is happening this evening, June 4th, starting at 6:00 PM CDT. This race, including 8K, 5K, and 2-Mile Walk events, will bring significant pedestrian and road closures around the city. Simultaneously, the Chicago Blues Festival kicks off today at Millennium Park and other venues. Trying to squeeze in a run through these areas will be a nightmare of dodging people, barricades, and probably a few rogue harmonicas. Seriously, just don't.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Avoid Downtown & Lakefront South
If you absolutely *must* run today, steer clear of the Loop and the central sections of the Lakefront Trail, especially around Grant Park and Millennium Park, due to the sheer volume of people for both events. Opt for quieter neighborhood routes, but remain hyper-aware of weather conditions and potential localized flooding. And for crying out loud, hold onto your hats.
</AlertBox>
