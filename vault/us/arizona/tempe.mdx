<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Hydration is STILL King (or Queen)
  Look, just because it's overcast and not 110 degrees, doesn't mean you get a pass on hydration. This is Tempe, not Tacoma. You're still losing fluid, especially if you're pushing it along the <a href="https://www.tempe.gov/government/parks-and-recreation/tempe-town-lake">Tempe Town Lake</a> path. Carry water. Drink water. Pretend you're a saguaro – constantly sucking up moisture. Don't be that person getting rescued by Tempe Fire because you thought 83 degrees was "cool."
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Moderate Air Quality & Pollen: Know Your Limits
  While the AQI is moderate, and pollen is moderate, combined they can still be an irritant. If you're asthmatic or have severe allergies, pay attention to your body. Today isn't the day to try for a PR if you're feeling sniffly or a little chesty. Maybe dial it back a notch on those sprints up "A" Mountain.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Don't Be Another Statistic: ASU's Warning Still Stands
  Remember what happened with the ASU hockey player back in August? Hospitalized after an outdoor workout. While the exact conditions that day aren't our current forecast, it's a stark reminder that even college athletes push too hard. Heat illness isn't just for 115-degree days. Know your body, respect the conditions, and don't try to be a hero. Especially when it's still warm enough to get into trouble.
</AlertBox>
