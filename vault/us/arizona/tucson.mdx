### Local Hazards & Closures

Alright, here's where the good vibes hit a few speed bumps. While the weather is playing nice, the city isn't slowing down for your long run.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**"A" Mountain & Downtown Closures:** Forget your evening run around "A" Mountain (Sentinel Peak Park) tonight. The park and its access roads have been closed since yesterday evening and won't fully reopen until Sunday morning for vehicles, and sunrise for pedestrians and cyclists. Expect heavy traffic and road closures on Mission Road, Bella Vista Road, and parts of the I-10 frontage roads around the mountain for the fireworks display. If you're heading downtown, also note that the intersection of Church Avenue and Franklin Street is closed for construction through early July.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**The Loop — Know Your Sections:** Our beloved Chuck Huckelberry Loop has some ongoing construction and closures. The north lane under the Sunset Links Bridge should be reopening today, July 4, after artwork installation, which is a small win. However, parts of the south bank of the Rillito are closed for seal coating (expected to be done by early July), and the Julian Wash section from Wilmot to Via Bombachas is closed until July 13 with a detour in place. There are also intermittent closures on the Santa Cruz section northwest of Grant and I-10 through August. Don't just blindly hit the trail; check the Pima County notices before you head out, or you'll be doing an unexpected obstacle course.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Wildfire Watch:** While our local weather is unusually cool, fire danger remains a concern in Arizona. The Pocket Fire north of Sedona is still active (23,451 acres, 25% contained as of July 3), and there were recent smaller fires in the Santa Catalina Mountains (Ventana and Table Mountain fires) and Saguaro National Park (Box Fire), though no significant smoke impacts are reported directly in Tucson today. The Box Fire in Saguaro National Park has specific trail closures, including those originating from Javelina Picnic Area and Loma Alta Trailhead. Keep an eye on the skies, but for now, no immediate smoke warnings for your run.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, with this freakishly cool weather, you'd be crazy to stay inside. Just make sure your planned route isn't a fireworks viewing area or a construction zone. Happy running, Tucson.
