Listen up, Phoenix. Trying to get your miles in outside today is a spectacularly bad idea.

## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  - **Heat stroke is a very real threat.** The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning slapped on us until 8 PM MST tonight, and for good reason. Temperatures are predicted to hit a blistering 109°F, easily 6-8 degrees above our *normal* scorching average for this time of year. That ain't just "warm," folks; that's "your internal organs are cooking" hot.
  - **Your favorite trails are literally shut down.** The City of Phoenix isn't playing games with your life (or their first responders'). Due to the extreme heat, popular spots like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain are closed from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. If you try to sneak in, you're not just risking a rescue, you're being a public menace.
  - **Don't breathe that in.** While some reports might suggest otherwise, we've got "Moderate" air quality with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. AccuWeather even called today's AQI "Poor" and unhealthy for sensitive groups. Factor in the "Extreme Risk of Fire" across the region and the lingering smoke from ongoing Arizona wildfires, like the Sycamore Fire still burning over 8,000 acres in the Tonto National Forest, and your lungs are going to hate you. Plus, pollen is still hanging around at a moderate level, just to add insult to injury.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **DO NOT ATTEMPT MAJOR HIKES OR LONG OUTDOOR RUNS.** The combination of extreme heat, trail closures, and questionable air quality is a recipe for disaster. This isn't just about feeling a bit warm; this is about heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and becoming another statistic for our already overworked first responders.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  If you *must* be outside, keep it short, keep it light, and stick to shaded areas. Think a quick walk around your block at dawn or dusk, NOT hitting the trails or pushing a long run during peak heat. Hydrate aggressively, even if you don't feel thirsty. And seriously, don't even *think* about trying to defy the trail closures on Camelback Mountain – they're there for your safety, not to cramp your style.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  This is a perfect day for indoor workouts. Hit the gym, do some bodyweight stuff at home, or finally tackle that yoga video you've been putting off. Your lungs and joints (and the paramedics) will thank you.
