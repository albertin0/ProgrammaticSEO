<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Alert: Watch for Dust and Ozone**
Our current AQI might look "Moderate" at 58 for PM2.5, but ADEQ issued High Pollution Advisories for PM10 (dust) and Ozone. That PM10 advisory means the air is "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups," and even for healthy folks, prolonged exposure can cause breathing issues. The AccuWeather forecast for today even has our AQI at 109, which is straight-up Unhealthy. Save your lungs, seriously.
</AlertBox>

**My Two Cents: When to Actually Run (If You Must)**
If you absolutely, positively *must* get some miles in, stick to early morning (and I mean *early*, before the sun even thinks about it) or late evening.
