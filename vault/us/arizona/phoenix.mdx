<AlertBox type="info">
If you absolutely *must* get your workout in, do it indoors, or stick to shaded, air-conditioned environments. If you are forced to be outside, reschedule strenuous activities to the early morning or after sunset, if at all possible. Hydrate *constantly*, wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing, and recognize the signs of heat exhaustion. Seriously, chug water like it's going out of style. Also, consider the elevated ozone levels, especially in the afternoon. If you have respiratory issues, staying indoors is your best bet.
</AlertBox>

Stay safe, Phoenix. This heat isn't playing around.
