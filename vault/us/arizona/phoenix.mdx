## Heat Hazards & Trail Closures

That 38.7°C temperature makes trail running a serious gamble. While official city trail closures for extreme heat usually kick in with an NWS Extreme Heat Warning, which forecasts temps of 105°F or higher, we're right on that edge. When those warnings *are* active, popular spots like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain typically shut down from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Do you really want to be the reason Phoenix Fire has to deploy a helicopter crew in this heat? No, you don't. They average hundreds of heat-related rescues a year.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Risk!** At 101.7°F (38.7°C), your risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke is critically high. Limit outdoor activity to early morning or late evening, if at all. Never leave children or pets in a parked car.
</AlertBox>
