## The Full Breakdown

Let's be real, Phoenix, May is when the oven officially turns on. Today's "Clear" skies are a welcome sight, but don't let that fool you. The mercury is going to rocket up to 34°C (93°F) later in the day, setting the stage for what’s to come. We've already got forecasts hitting triple digits next week, so start adjusting your internal clock to "pre-dawn or post-dusk" if you haven't already.

Air quality is sitting at a "Fair" 50 AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's not terrible, but combine it with what’s floating around in the air from our lovely desert flora, and you might feel it. Speaking of which, the pollen situation is a mixed bag depending on who you ask, but the bottom line is: it's out there. While the overall Ambee report says "Moderate," some sources are flagging grass pollen as "Very High" for today. If you're sneezing and rubbing your eyes on your run down Indian School Road, now you know why.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Heat Ahead:** While it's not quite "Extreme Heat Warning" territory *today*, the City of Phoenix has already implemented its Trail Heat Safety Program. This means popular spots like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and certain South Mountain trails (like Holbert and Mormon) will shut down from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on extreme heat days. Get your mountain climbs in early, folks.
</AlertBox>
