## Today's Rundown for Your Run

You finally get a break from the oven-like conditions that have us all questioning our life choices for most of the year. Today's 17°C weather is practically perfect for hitting the trails without melting into a puddle or risking heat stroke. So, if you've been eyeing that long run up the **South Mountain Park and Preserve** or finally tackling a section of the Arizona Trail, today's your day for temperature comfort.

However, "perfect" rarely applies to *all* of Phoenix, does it? While yesterday saw some serious thunderstorm and flash flood warnings up in areas like Greenlee and Graham counties, and even some hail in north Phoenix, the immediate severe weather threat for the Valley has largely passed as of Friday morning. Don't expect any trail closures due to extreme heat today, because, well, it's not actually extreme heat. Small victories, people.

### What's Up With Your Lungs?

This is where things get a bit murky. We're sitting at a "Moderate" AQI, which usually means "mostly okay for most people." But then you look a little closer and see PM2.5 is the main culprit, those tiny particles that love to burrow deep into your lungs. AccuWeather, bless their dramatic hearts, is straight-up calling today's air quality "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups". My professional opinion? If you're someone who feels every little sniffle or wheeze, take this seriously.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Alert for Sensitive Souls:** If you have asthma, COPD, or just generally hate coughing up a lung after a workout, keep your eye on that AQI. "Moderate" can still feel "Poor" when PM2.5 is the dominant pollutant. Consider a shorter route or an indoor option if you're feeling it.
</AlertBox>
