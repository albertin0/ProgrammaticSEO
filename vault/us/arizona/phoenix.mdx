### Why today is absolutely *not* the day for that PR:

<BulletList>
*   **Dangerously Hot:** We're staring down 101.7°F (38.7°C), which is well past "uncomfortable" and deep into "straight-up dangerous" territory. Forget setting records, you'll be setting yourself up for heatstroke.
*   **Questionable Air Quality:** While the overall AQI is "Moderate" at 59, AccuWeather reports the air as "Poor" today and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." Combine that with a decent dose of pollen, and your lungs are going to feel like you've been running through a sandpaper factory. ADEQ's own forecast for Tuesday, April 7, shows ozone at 71 AQI, meaning some people might start feeling it.
*   **Trail Restrictions:** The City of Phoenix has a heat safety program. When the National Weather Service issues an Extreme Heat Warning, popular trails at places like Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon, Cholla Trails) and the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail are restricted from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Even sections of South Mountain Park and Preserve, like the Holbert Trail and access to the National Trail from Pima Canyon Trailhead, get shut down. While the NWS might not have an *active* warning for *this very second*, the temperature is absolutely in the danger zone that triggers these. Don't be "that guy" needing a rescue crew in this heat.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: 101.7°F is NOT a joke.**
This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely life-threatening for sustained outdoor activity. Heat is the deadliest weather phenomenon in Arizona. Symptoms like confusion, throbbing headache, or a rapid, weak pulse mean you need to stop, find a cool place, and get help. Your PR isn't worth an ambulance ride or worse.
</AlertBox>
