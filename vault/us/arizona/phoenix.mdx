### Your PT's Unsolicited Advice:
*   **Hydrate aggressively.** We're not talking a bottle of water. We're talking gallons. And electrolytes.
*   **Move your workout indoors.** Hit the gym. Swim laps. Do some yoga in your air-conditioned living room. Your body will thank you, and I won't have to read about another preventable heat exhaustion case.
*   **If you *must* be outside for a minute, do it before sunrise.** And even then, be extremely cautious. The ground holds onto heat like a spiteful ex, and those overnight lows aren't giving us much relief.
