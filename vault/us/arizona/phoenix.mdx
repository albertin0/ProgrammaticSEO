## Air Quality: Not Exactly "Fresh"

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at a nice, unremarkable 55 today, officially "Moderate." The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, which are those tiny particles that love to get deep into your lungs and cause trouble. Meanwhile, the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality has been flagging ozone as an issue, and it's expected to stay in the upper Moderate range today. So, while it's not "hazardous," combining even moderate lung irritants with extreme heat is a recipe for a bad time. Don't push it, especially if you're already prone to respiratory issues. Your lungs have enough work to do just trying to cool your body.

## Weather: A Brutal Oven

Let's not sugarcoat it: today is brutally hot. The temperature sits at 38.7°C (a cool 101.7°F) right now, but we're staring down afternoon highs between 109 and 117°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect until 8 PM tonight.

What makes it worse? The overnight lows aren't providing much relief, hanging in the upper 80s and low 90s. Your body just doesn't get a chance to recover, ramping up the risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. People have died out here from this kind of heat. This isn't a drill.
