## What You *Really* Need to Know Today

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **FLOODING HAZARD: Salt River & Washes are NOT Dry!**
Listen up, because this is important. Even though the rain has stopped, weekend storms caused significant runoff. SRP is actively releasing water from dams into the Salt and Verde rivers. This means those "dry" riverbeds and washes you usually cruise through are *flowing*, and some roads built within the Salt River riverbed are closed. Tempe Town Lake is also temporarily closed to all on-water recreation due to increased flow and potential debris. Don't be a hero; avoid these areas completely. You saw the news about Phoenix Fire Technical Rescue pulling people out of washes just Friday near 40th Street and Camelback Road. Don't become a statistic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **HAZMAT ALERT: Shamrock Farms Area - AVOID!**
As of this morning, hazmat crews are on-site dealing with an ammonia leak at Shamrock Farms. This is located near I-17 and Encanto Boulevard. Roads in that immediate vicinity are blocked, and authorities are explicitly asking the public to avoid the area. This isn't just a traffic delay; it's a hazardous materials incident. Stay clear until further notice. This operation is expected to be active until at least this afternoon.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Construction Zone Lingers**
Those weekend freeway closures for widening and pavement improvements (looking at you, Loop 101, Loop 202, SR 51) officially lifted this morning. However, expect residual impacts, shifted lanes, and possibly lingering debris around those construction zones. If your route takes you near one of these major arteries, stay extra vigilant.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, enjoy that glorious weather. But for the love of all that is holy, don't ignore the very real, very present hazards out there. Your run isn't worth a rescue or, worse, an emergency room visit.
