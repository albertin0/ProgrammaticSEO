Today is absolutely *not* the day to push yourself outside in Phoenix. Seriously, just don't.

<LungsJointsScore score={3} />

<BulletList>
*   **It's a genuine oven out there.** An Excessive Heat Warning is plastered all over Maricopa County, with temperatures pushing over 100°F and expected to reach up to 115°F. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's straight-up dangerous for your heart and lungs.
*   **Your favorite trails are likely off-limits.** The City of Phoenix has restricted access to popular hiking trails like those on Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails, for example) from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. due to the extreme heat. The Fire Department is already seeing an upward trend in trail rescues, and believe me, you don't want to be one of those statistics.
*   **Moderate air quality and pollen just add insult to injury.** With an AQI of 59 and PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, your lungs are already doing extra work. Throw in moderate pollen levels, and any strenuous outdoor activity becomes a recipe for a miserable (and potentially dangerous) time.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat and Trail Closures Are No Joke**
An Excessive Heat Warning is in effect for Maricopa County until at least June 12th. This means afternoon highs are expected to be between 103°F and 109°F, possibly even hitting 115°F. Do not underestimate this heat. Phoenix hiking trails, including the main routes up Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak, are closed from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The city "strongly discourages" hiking on *any* other city trails during these hours too. Seriously, stay off the trails. You're putting yourself and our first responders at risk if you ignore these warnings.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### **Moderate Air & Allergens**
The Air Quality Index is sitting at a moderate 59, with PM2.5 being the primary pollutant. This, combined with moderate pollen, means if you *must* be outside, your respiratory system will be working harder. Headaches, coughing, or shortness of breath are not badges of honor; they're signs to get inside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### **What To Do Instead**
Look, I get it. We runners are antsy. But today, "toughing it out" is just plain stupid. Hit the treadmill, find an indoor gym with A/C, or swim laps at a public pool. If you absolutely *have* to get outside, limit it to very early morning (before 7 a.m.) or late evening (after 7 p.m.) and stick to flat, shaded areas. Even then, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). This heat isn't a game.
</AlertBox>
