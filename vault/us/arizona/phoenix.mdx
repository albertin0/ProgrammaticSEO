## The Rundown: Breathe Easy (Mostly) & Watch Your Step

Look, we live in Phoenix, so "perfect" usually means "not actively trying to kill you with heat." Today, Mother Nature is being reasonably kind. The air is "Moderate" on the Air Quality Index, which means most of us will be fine, but if you've got asthma or any other respiratory shenanigans going on, maybe don't go for that PR today. Those moderate pollen counts aren't helping either; blame it on everything suddenly blooming in our desert spring.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate Air Quality & Pollen Alert
With an AQI of 60 driven by PM2.5 and moderate pollen levels, sensitive individuals might experience some irritation. Consider keeping your longer, higher-intensity workouts indoors if you're prone to respiratory issues or allergies.
</AlertBox>

Weather-wise, you've got no excuses. A comfortable 16.4°C and clear skies? That's what we call a gift in Phoenix. You can actually enjoy your run without feeling like you're baking in an oven or slogging through a monsoon.

### Construction Zone Headaches

Now, onto the usual Phoenix obstacle course: construction. If your route takes you anywhere near the west side of town, specifically around **Loop 101** at 75th Avenue, be aware. The eastbound on-ramp there just reopened, but the westbound off-ramp at 75th is closing for 60 days starting last night. So, plan your drive to your favorite trail accordingly, or be prepared for some detours if you're driving to places like Thunderbird Mountain or the Glendale portion of the Grand Canal Linear Park. Other Loop 101 closures are coming later this month too, further east. And if you're downtown, there's also a partial street closure on 13th Ave between Adams St and Washington St for private development that's ongoing this week.

<AlertBox type="info">
### Traffic & Access Impact
Major freeway construction on Loop 101, especially near 75th Avenue, will impact travel. Check ADOT's site before heading out if your workout destination requires freeway access in the Northwest Valley. Local street closures for private development are also in effect downtown.
</AlertBox>

Finally, while it's not a direct hazard in Phoenix, some small "demonstration fires" are being lit in the Bradshaw Ranger District (Prescott National Forest) for training this week. Smoke *may* be visible from various locations, so don't freak out if you catch a whiff of it.
