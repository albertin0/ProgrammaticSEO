## Don't Be a Statistic: The Heat is No Joke

Look, I love a good long run as much as the next masochist, but today? Today is a day to re-evaluate your life choices if your workout plan involves direct sun exposure between 8 AM and 5 PM. The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect until 8 PM MST tonight, with forecasted temperatures hitting a dangerous 103-106°F. That's not "warm," that's "your internal organs are cooking" territory.

<AlertBox type="danger">
An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect until 8 PM MST. Afternoon temperatures are expected to reach 103-106°F (40-41°C). This is a Major Heat Risk.
</AlertBox>

The City of Phoenix isn't messing around either. When an Extreme Heat Warning hits, popular trails like the Echo and Cholla Trails on Camelback Mountain, as well as the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, are automatically restricted from 8 AM to 5 PM. And for good reason. People end up in the ER, or worse, and it puts our already strained first responders at risk. If you insist on hitting any trail, even the "open" ones, do it at dawn or dusk, and bring more water than you think you need. Then bring more.

## Breathe Easy? Not So Fast.

Yesterday, Maricopa County was under an Ozone High Pollution Advisory because levels were expected to exceed federal health standards. Today's AQI is technically 51, which is "Moderate," but the dominant pollutant is still Ozone/Other, and the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) noted that Thursday would be "only slightly better" and "just shy of Advisory thresholds" for ozone.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Elevated ozone levels are present today, following yesterday's High Pollution Advisory. Ozone is harmful to lungs and can worsen conditions for those with asthma or other respiratory issues.
</AlertBox>

Ozone at ground level is nasty stuff for your lungs, especially if you're a kid, an older adult, or someone with asthma. It's like giving your respiratory system a workout it didn't ask for, but without the fitness benefits. Factor in the "Moderate" pollen levels, and your lungs might feel like they're trying to clear a dust storm while running through a flower shop.
