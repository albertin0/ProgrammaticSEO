### Construction & Trail Updates

Good news, I guess? No major news reports about widespread trail closures due to wildfires, flooding, or construction blocking major running routes in the last 24 hours. Given the heat, though, some smaller parks might opt to close amenities or recommend against use during peak sun hours. Always check the specific park's website before you head out. Don't assume that because the main trails are "open" they are *safe* to use.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you *must* run outside today, stick to shaded areas, go before sunrise, and for the love of all that is holy, hydrate like you're a camel preparing for a desert crossing. Wear light, loose-fitting clothing, and tell someone where you're going. Carry your phone. Maybe even an emergency beacon if you're feeling adventurous (and stupid). Consider hitting the treadmill or the pool. Your body will thank you, and your PT won't have to explain basic physiology to your emergency contact.
