### Today's Rundown

Look, if you're hitting the trails today, you picked a decent day temperature-wise. This 22.4°C (72°F) and overcast situation is about as good as it gets before we plunge headfirst into the inferno. The fact that you won't be immediately scorched off the asphalt is a win in itself.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Watch Your Lungs, Seriously:** While the official AQI is clocking in at 55, placing us in the "Moderate" category, don't get complacent. Phoenix has a notorious track record for air quality, consistently ranking among the worst for both ozone and particle pollution. Our "hot, dry climate plays a role in particle pollution as well," with "drought, catastrophic wildfires, dust from construction — all of that also contributes to our particle pollution burden." The forecast for today specifically mentions PM2.5 levels, and with increased breezes expected this afternoon and Sunday, you can bet that dust from construction sites and general desert nastiness will be swirling around. If you're sensitive, maybe keep it indoors or shorten that long run.
</AlertBox>

Pollen levels are sitting at a moderate to high range, particularly if you're allergic to Mulberry, Ash, or Poplar. So, if your nose is already running like you just finished a speed workout, consider that your body telling you to take it easy.

No city-wide trail closures due to extreme heat today, which is a relief. However, remember that the City of Phoenix has a "Trail Heat Safety Program" that restricts access to popular trails like those on Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak, and parts of South Mountain, when the National Weather Service issues an Extreme Heat Warning. They've even started these closures earlier in the year as our summers get hotter. For instance, the Echo Canyon Trail on Camelback Mountain has been closed since late last year due to unstable boulders from erosion, and there's no definite reopening date yet. So, check the specific trail you're planning to tackle before you head out, especially if you had your heart set on hitting the challenging routes at South Mountain.

Oh, and there are a few local races happening today, like the Chocolate Bunny 5K in Phoenix. If you're not participating, be aware of potential road or path congestion in those areas.
