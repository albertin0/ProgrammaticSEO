The heat is no joke, folks. The National Weather Service isn't messing around with an "Extreme Heat Warning". This isn't just "uncomfortable," this is "heat stroke is a real possibility" territory. The City of Phoenix is closing popular trails like those on Camelback Mountain between 8 AM and 5 PM when these warnings are active, and for good reason. That closure program has even expanded to South Mountain Park and Preserve for user and first responder safety. Don't be that person who needs rescuing because you thought you could outrun a Phoenix summer day.
