## Heat: "Modest Cooldown" Still Means Danger

Let's be real, "modest cooldown" in Phoenix in May usually just means it's slightly less murderous than yesterday. The National Weather Service had an Extreme Heat Warning in place yesterday, with highs near 106-110°F. Today's forecast for highs in the upper 90s might sound like a reprieve, but it's still pushing 37°C. That's not "comfortable" running weather; it's still prime time for heat-related illnesses if you're not careful. The overnight lows aren't providing much recovery either, so everything is already starting warm.

<AlertBox type="warning">
The afternoon temperatures are still plenty capable of making you a heat-stroke statistic. The City of Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program restricts access to popular, strenuous trails like those on Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla Trails) from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. when an Extreme Heat Warning is issued. Always check official city sites before heading out, and seriously, just hike early or stay indoors for intense workouts.
</AlertBox>
