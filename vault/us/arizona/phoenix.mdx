## Today's Hot Mess of a Workout Forecast

Look, I'm all for getting after it, but today is not the day to play hero in the desert sun. We're staring down forecasted highs around 97°F (36°C). That's "dangerously hot" territory, according to weather experts, even with the sun shining clear. The National Weather Service Phoenix Heat Page even notes that heat-associated deaths can occur with temperatures in the mid-80s and hotter.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat: It's Not a Game, Especially on Trails.**
Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program automatically kicks in when the National Weather Service issues an Extreme Heat Warning. This means your beloved **Camelback Mountain** (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails), **Piestewa Peak Summit trail**, and several trails at **South Mountain Park and Preserve** (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and the National Trail access from Pima Canyon Trailhead) are likely *closed* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Trying to hike these during a restriction can lead to fines, not to mention a serious trip to the emergency room. Just last week, we heard about a student athlete in Tempe who ended up on life support due to exertional heat stroke during practice when temps were "approaching 90 degrees Fahrenheit." Don't be that guy.
</AlertBox>

### AQI and Pollen: The Lesser Evils

Your lungs might not be screaming from smoke today, but the moderate AQI of 55, dominated by PM2.5, isn't exactly fresh mountain air. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you'll feel this, especially paired with the heat. Pollen's also hanging out at a moderate level. For most, this won't be a deal-breaker, but if you're sniffly, it'll make that run even less pleasant.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Overnight Construction:**
While not a daytime workout hazard, if you're driving to an early morning cool-down spot tomorrow, be aware of overnight lane restrictions on I-10 through the Wild Horse Pass Corridor. They're doing earthwork and barrier placement from 8 p.m. to 5 a.m. daily through Friday, Oct. 9. Plan your routes.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Stick to the Shaded Paths or Treadmill.**
If you absolutely *must* get a workout in outside, go before the sun really gets going or wait until after it drops significantly. Stay on paved, shaded paths, or better yet, hit the gym. Hydrate like it's your job, and carry more water than you think you need. Seriously, *more*.
</AlertBox>
