Look, I love hitting the trails around South Mountain as much as the next masochist, but today's a hard pass for anything longer than a quick walk to the mailbox. The heat is the absolute killer here. People underestimate how quickly the desert sun turns a pleasant morning into a life-threatening oven. The "Mainly Clear" skies just mean the sun has a direct shot at you, no cloud cover reprieve.

If you absolutely *must* get your miles in, stick to early morning, *before* sunrise, or very late evening after the sun has truly set, and even then, be smart. Hydrate like it's your job, wear light clothing, and for the love of all that is holy, tell someone where you're going. Don't be that person they have to send Mountain Rescue out for. We see it every year.

### Hazards to Note
