## The Rundown

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heat: It's Not a Suggestion, It's a Threat.
The provided temperature of 38.7°C (101.6°F) is no joke. While the NWS Phoenix site might not have an "Extreme Heat Warning" up *today* for automatic trail closures, that doesn't mean the heat isn't deadly. A hiker needed rescuing from Camelback Mountain just two days ago due to overheating. Stick to your early morning or late evening workouts. Anything between 8 AM and 5 PM on exposed trails like Echo Canyon is just asking for a helicopter ride.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Monsoon Mood Swings
Yesterday saw storm chances and potential severe wind gusts, with flash flood risks forecast to increase through today. Even with a "Mainly Clear" sky now, Phoenix weather can turn on a dime. Remember those sandbags the City just made available? That wasn't for fun. If you hear thunder or see ominous clouds, bail. No PR is worth getting swept into a wash or struck by lightning.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Construction Zone Blues at Papago Park
If you're planning on a run through Papago Park, be aware that there's ongoing construction for the Grand Canal relocation. They're doing multi-use path improvements along the canal, which means detours, uneven surfaces, and heavy equipment. Keep your head on a swivel, because that dump truck driver probably isn't looking out for your Strava segment.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Just Stay Smart, Phoenix
If you absolutely *must* be outside:
*   **Hydrate aggressively.** And then hydrate some more. Water, electrolytes. Not that lukewarm stuff you left in the car.
*   **Dress like you're going to the beach, not a fashion show.** Light-colored, loose-fitting, moisture-wicking gear.
*   **Listen to your body.** Nausea, dizziness, confusion? That's your body telling you to stop. Immediately.
*   **Tell someone where you're going.** Seriously. Especially if you're hitting any of the less-trafficked trails.
</AlertBox>
