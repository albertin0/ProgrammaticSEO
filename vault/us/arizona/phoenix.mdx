<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning & Trail Closures
The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect for Phoenix. This means trails like Camelback Mountain's Echo Canyon and Cholla, Piestewa Peak Summit, and specific routes at South Mountain Park (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to National Trail from Pima Canyon) are CLOSED from 8 AM to 5 PM. This isn't a suggestion; it's to keep you and our overworked first responders safe. Don't attempt to circumvent these closures.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Double Whammy Air Quality
Your air quality is a cocktail of Moderate PM2.5, a high ozone warning, and blowing dust. If you have any respiratory sensitivities, or even if you don't, seriously consider keeping your workout indoors today. Minimize outdoor activity between 10 AM and 6 PM.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Drive Safely, if you must venture out
ADOT has freeway closures and restrictions this weekend (June 26-29) on SR 51, Loop 101, Loop 202, and US 60 for various improvement projects. If you insist on going anywhere, factor in extra time and be aware of construction detours. And watch out for blowing dust on the roads, which can cause sudden drops in visibility.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, it's June in Phoenix. The heat is here, and it's lethal. Hydrate, stay inside, and maybe hit the gym's air-conditioned treadmill. Your vanity isn't worth a mountain rescue, and neither is being a statistic.
