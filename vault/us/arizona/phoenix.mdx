Today's a classic Phoenix "feels good, but don't be stupid" day. The temperature is hovering around 74 degrees Fahrenheit, which for early May is practically a miracle, or a cruel tease before the inferno. It's clear outside, so you won't be navigating any dust storms or surprise flash floods like we've seen in the past. That's a win.

However, the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality has slapped us with an Ozone High Pollution Advisory, extending through Saturday. While your app might say "Moderate" AQI at 56 (with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant), ADEQ explicitly expects ozone to hit "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels. This means if you've got asthma, other respiratory issues, or you're just generally prone to feeling crappy from bad air, today's long runs or intense sessions are probably best moved indoors. Or at least keep them short and easy.

Pollen is holding steady at a moderate level. For some of us, that's business as usual; for others, it means itchy eyes and a runny nose mid-stride. Factor that into your planning and carry tissues.

And speaking of planning, if you're thinking of hitting South Mountain Park and Preserve, remember that the annual fire ban for Phoenix preserves just kicked in yesterday, May 1st. No campfires or charcoal grills. This is also Wildfire Awareness Month, and given our record warm winter and low precipitation, officials are already bracing for a "wild wildfire season." Don't be the spark.
