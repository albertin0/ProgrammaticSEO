<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Ozone High Pollution Advisory IN EFFECT for Phoenix!

The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality has issued an Ozone High Pollution Advisory for today, August 11th. This means ground-level ozone is expected to be at unhealthy levels. Avoid strenuous outdoor activity, especially between noon and sunset when ozone peaks. Kids, seniors, and anyone with respiratory issues (looking at you, asthmatics) should stay indoors.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Heat Hangover & Dust

Even with "overcast" conditions, temperatures are still in the 90s, and the humidity makes it feel worse. If you absolutely *must* go out, stay hydrated like your life depends on it (because it does), and keep your workout short and easy. Blowing dust continues to be a risk in the evenings from gusty winds. You don't want those fine particulate matters deep in your lungs.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Avoid Peak Hours

Seriously, if you insist on a run, do it at sunrise. Any later, and you're battling the triple threat of heat, rising ozone, and potential dust. Maybe hit the gym or find a pool. The trails, like those at South Mountain Park, are best left alone during these conditions.
</AlertBox>
