### Trail Closure: Camelback Mountain's Echo Canyon Trail

Seriously, folks. The Echo Canyon Trail is CLOSED for improvements like new gates and trail posts through September 18th. Don't be "that person" who ignores the signs and makes park rangers' lives harder. The Cholla Trail *is* open if you still need that Camelback fix.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Heat: Still a Factor, Even Under Clouds

While the recent *Extreme Heat Warnings* that saw temperatures soaring well over 100°F expired last week (around September 10th or 11th), 90°F is no joke. The City of Phoenix has a Trail Heat Safety Program for a reason, restricting popular trails like those at South Mountain Park and Piestewa Peak when it gets truly dangerous. Even with "overcast" conditions, the heat index can climb, and you can still absolutely overheat. Remember, our bodies aren't designed for sustained exertion in this kind of desert oven. Hydration needs to start *yesterday*, not five minutes before you leave.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Lungs & Allergy Alert

With moderate AQI and pollen, if you've got asthma, COPD, or just a generally sensitive respiratory system, you might want to consider an indoor workout today. Or, at the very least, keep your outdoor session shorter and less intense. Ozone, the dominant pollutant today, forms when our lovely Phoenix sun reacts with vehicle emissions. It's why afternoon pollution advisories are common from May through September.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Updates

On a related note, keep in mind that North Mountain Park is slated for major renovations starting late this year (2026), which will likely mean closures. Always check the City of Phoenix Parks and Rec website if you're heading to any of our preserves for construction or heat-related restrictions.

Look, I get it. We live here because we love the outdoors. But a truly local runner knows when to push it and when to pull back. Today's a day for smart choices. If you're going out, go early, hydrate aggressively, listen to your body, and for Pete's sake, pick a trail that's actually open.
