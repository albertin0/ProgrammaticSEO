## The Rundown

Seriously, do not mess with Phoenix in August, especially not today. The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning pasted all over the region, and they're not exaggerating. We're talking about afternoon highs forecasted at a brutal 116°F here in Phoenix. That's "oven mitts required to touch your car door" hot, not "let's go for a casual jog" hot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat is a Killer.** With "feels like" temperatures pushing past 120°F, heat-related illnesses like cramps, exhaustion, and stroke are not just possibilities, they're probabilities if you're not careful. Limit outdoor activity to *before sunrise* if you absolutely must. Reschedule everything else.
</AlertBox>

If you had plans to hike up Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, or tackle trails like Holbert or Mormon in South Mountain Park and Preserve, forget about it during the day. Those trails are under mandatory closure from 8 AM to 5 PM. And frankly, all other city trails are strongly discouraged too. There's a reason for this: to prevent mountain rescues and save lives.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Dust Storms and Isolated Flash Flooding Tonight.** Keep an eye on the sky and your local weather app. We've seen significant dust storms in the past 24-48 hours, and there's a chance for more high winds and blowing dust later this evening, with isolated heavy rain and flash flood potential. If you see a dust wall approaching, get inside or pull off the road safely.
</AlertBox>

Your lungs might feel okay with the current moderate AQI, but any dust events will quickly change that. If you're a runner, this is a treadmill day. If you're a hiker, this is a library or mall walking day. Hydrate like it's your job, and prioritize staying cool indoors. It's just not worth the risk out there today.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is Non-Negotiable.** Even if you're mostly indoors, drink more water than usual. If you *must* be outside, carry ample water (more than you think you need), wear light-colored, loose clothing, and seek shade frequently. No sugary drinks, alcohol, or excessive caffeine.
</AlertBox>
