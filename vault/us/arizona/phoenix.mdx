### **EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT: Seriously, stay indoors.**
The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for the Phoenix metro area, valid all week. Temperatures will be in the 109-112°F range, with little relief overnight. Heat-related illnesses spike significantly during these periods. Don't be a statistic. Reschedule any outdoor activities for very early morning, or better yet, take it to the gym today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### **TRAIL CLOSURES: Respect the Restrictions.**
Due to the dangerous heat, the City of Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program has kicked in. This means popular trails like Echo Canyon and Cholla on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, and specific trails in South Mountain Park are *closed* between 8 AM and 5 PM. These closures are to protect *you* and the first responders who have to come scrape you off the mountain.
</AlertBox>

The forecasted ozone at 93 AQI is nothing to sneeze at, especially when your respiratory system is already working overtime to cool you down in this inferno. That's a double whammy for your lungs.
