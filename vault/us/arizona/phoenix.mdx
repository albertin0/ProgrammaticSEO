### DANGER: Extreme Heat Is No Joke
An Extreme Heat Warning means exactly what it sounds like. Afternoon temperatures are forecast to hit a brutal 109-114°F. This isn't "push through it" weather; this is "stay inside, hydrated, and preferably under air conditioning" weather. Heat-related illnesses increase significantly during these events. Overexposure can lead to heat cramps, heat exhaustion, and ultimately, heat stroke. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get immediate help. Call 911.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### TRAIL CLOSURES: Respect the Restrictions
The City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation Department has activated its Trail Heat Safety Program. This means specific trails, including the infamous Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain, the Piestewa Peak Summit trail, and several key routes on South Mountain (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to the National Trail from Pima Canyon Trailhead) are *restricted* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Ignoring these closures puts both your life and the lives of our first responders at risk. You might think you're tough, but the desert doesn't care about your ego.
</AlertBox>

### LOCALIZED CONSTRUCTION: Mind Your Path
If you're determined to get some steps in *very* early or *very* late, be aware of ongoing localized construction. These are multi-month projects impacting pedestrian and bicycle infrastructure. Always stay vigilant for detours or uneven surfaces.
