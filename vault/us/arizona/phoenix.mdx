## The Heat is No Joke, People

Look, I love a good long run as much as the next crazy person who lives in Phoenix. But today, Mother Nature is actively trying to stop you, and you should listen. We’re in peak summer, and these temperatures aren't just uncomfortable; they're dangerous. We've got an Extreme Heat Warning that means heat cramps and heat exhaustion are a real possibility, even if you think you're "used to it." Your body doesn't adapt to 100+ degrees Fahrenheit, it just tries to survive it. Overnight lows aren't providing much relief either, hovering around the upper 80s and even 90 degrees in some spots, which means your body isn't getting a chance to fully recover from the daytime oven.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning & Trail Closures!
Today's 38.7°C (101.7°F) temperatures are dangerous. National Weather Service advisories mean popular trails like those on Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak are *CLOSED* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Do not ignore these warnings. Heat stroke is a medical emergency. If you're out and feel dizzy, confused, nauseous, or have a rapid pulse, get help immediately (call 911).
</AlertBox>

### So, What Can You Do?

If you absolutely *must* get a workout in, stay indoors. Hit the gym, use your home equipment, or find a community center with air conditioning. If you insist on outdoor activity, seriously, keep it to the pre-dawn hours, like before 6 AM, and keep it short. We're talking a quick walk around your neighborhood, not a long session on the canal path.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Morning Commute Construction
If you're driving to an indoor gym or early morning outdoor spot, be aware of overnight lane restrictions on northbound and southbound SR 51 between Northern Avenue and Shea Boulevard, which will be in place nightly from 9 p.m. to 5 a.m. starting today. Litchfield Road also has overnight closures for paving from 8 p.m. to 4 a.m. Plan accordingly.
</AlertBox>

Hydration is crucial, even indoors. Drink water constantly. And for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. That little voice telling you to stop? It's not weakness, it's self-preservation.
