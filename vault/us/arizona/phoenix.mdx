**Extreme Heat Alert!**
Phoenix is forecasted to hit a scorching 100°F (38°C) today, well above average for October 1st. The City of Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program likely has popular trails like Camelback Mountain’s Echo Canyon and Cholla trails, as well as the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, *closed* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Do NOT risk it. Heat-related emergencies are no joke, and our first responders have enough to do. Stay off those trails during the hottest parts of the day, period.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead!**
If you're opting for city streets instead of trails, be aware that ADOT has ongoing construction projects all over the Valley. Lane restrictions and partial closures are impacting areas along I-10 (especially around 7th Street), Loop 101, Loop 202, and Loop 303. Don't assume your usual route is clear, and definitely don't run into a construction zone just because you're in a hurry. Check the City of Phoenix and ADOT websites for specific street closures before you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional.**
Even if you manage to snag an early morning or late evening workout, you're still in Phoenix, and it's still hot. You need more water than you think. And no, that sugary sports drink isn't cutting it for pure hydration. Drink plain water, and then drink some more. Consider electrolyte tabs if you’re actually putting in serious work, not just for a casual stroll. This isn't a suggestion; it's how you avoid becoming a statistic.
