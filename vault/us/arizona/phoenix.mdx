### Ozone: Your Lungs' Worst Enemy Today

That "Ozone/Other" pollutant in your data? Yeah, it's not a friend. An Ozone High Pollution Advisory means ground-level ozone is high enough to exceed federal health standards. Active kids, adults, and anyone with lung conditions like asthma should absolutely reduce or avoid prolonged outdoor exertion, especially in the afternoon and early evening when ozone peaks. Even healthy individuals can feel it.
