### What to Watch Out For

Tomorrow and Thursday, Phoenix is expecting some "First Alert Weather Days" with potential thunderstorms, damaging winds, and even some hail, which could lead to localized flooding. So, if you're planning a workout for later in the week, keep a very close eye on the forecast, especially if your route takes you anywhere near washes or low-lying areas in North Phoenix or beyond. We've seen heavy rain cause road closures before.

For today, the primary concerns are the moderate pollen and AQI, plus the rising temps. If you have asthma or allergies, pre-medicate or stick to an indoor session. Nobody wants to wheeze their way through Papago Park.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Even with clear skies, a moderate AQI means some folks might experience respiratory symptoms. Pay attention to your body. If you feel any irritation or difficulty breathing, cut your workout short. This isn't about pushing through; it's about not ending up in urgent care.
</AlertBox>

The City of Phoenix has ongoing construction, so if you're running or biking on city streets, keep an eye out for potential traffic restrictions and closures. Check the city's Street Transportation Department resources if your route involves major thoroughfares.

Otherwise, it's a perfectly runnable day – just don't be a hero with your hydration.
