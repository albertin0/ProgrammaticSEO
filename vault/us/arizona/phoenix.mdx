<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Air You Breathe
With moderate AQI, moderate pollen, and lingering smoke from recent structure fires, your respiratory system is getting a workout whether you want it to or not. If you're sensitive, have asthma, or just don't like breathing in other people's problems, consider an indoor workout today. If you *must* run outside, stick to areas away from major intersections where pollutants concentrate, and ease up on the intensity. This isn't the day for PRs if your lungs are already irritated.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Construction Zones & Event Crowds Are No Joke
Seriously, people, ADOT isn't messing around this weekend. Navigating the Valley for your run or ride could add serious time and frustration. Plus, the Arizona State Fair guarantees extra vehicles and pedestrians, especially around the Fairgrounds. If you're heading to, say, Papago Park, be prepared for increased traffic on McDowell Road. Allow *ample* extra time, check ADOT's alerts before you leave, and know your alternate routes. Don't let road rage ruin your Zen.
</AlertBox>

### Plan Smart, Stay Hydrated
Even with cooler temps, this is Phoenix. Clear skies mean the sun still beats down. Drink water *before* you go, carry more than you think you need, and don't wait until you're parched.
