### The Heat Will End You (or at least your run)
Look, it's 101.7°F. That's a triple-digit death wish for most outdoor activity after about 7 AM. Heat is the deadliest weather in Arizona, and our emergency rooms see plenty of heat-related illnesses even on "moderate risk" days like today. Don't be a statistic. If you absolutely *must* run, do it before the sun truly wakes up, carry more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, listen to your body. That "push through it" mentality will land you in an ambulance, or worse, arguing with an EMT on a mountain trail that was clearly closed. Phoenix Fire Captain DJ Lee reminds us you can lose 1-2 liters of water *per hour* in these temps. This isn't a game.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Roadwork & Cyclist Safety
For those of you trying to squeeze in an early morning ride or run on the streets, be aware that some significant freeway construction projects on SR 51, Loop 202, and Loop 101 were scheduled to wrap up this morning. While they should be clear by now, always stay vigilant for lingering traffic impacts or unexpected lane changes. On a more somber note, a report released *today* highlights a disturbing trend: fatal bicycle hit-and-runs are on the rise nationally, with cyclists often left vulnerable. If you're on two wheels, assume drivers don't see you, especially at dawn or dusk, and stay hyper-aware.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Your PT's Unsolicited Advice:
If you insist on an outdoor workout:
*   **Time it:** Before 6 AM, or after 8 PM. Anything in between is flirting with disaster.
*   **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** Start drinking water now. Not just when you feel thirsty.
*   **Dress for success:** Light-colored, loose-fitting, moisture-wicking clothes are your friends.
*   **Know the signs:** Dizziness, nausea, rapid pulse, confusion? Get to shade, drink water, and call for help if things don't improve. That's your body telling you to stop being an idiot.
*   **Trails:** Again, avoid the mountain trails like Piestewa Peak or those restricted sections of South Mountain. Stick to flat, shaded, or well-populated areas where help is easier to reach if you get into trouble.
</AlertBox>
