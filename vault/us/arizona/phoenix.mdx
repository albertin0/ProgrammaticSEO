## Phoenix Heat: It's a Real Killer

Look, I get it. We live in Phoenix. We're supposed to be tough. But there's a difference between being tough and being stupid. Today is firmly in the "stupid" category if you plan to hit those trails during peak sun hours. The mercury is already pushing 38.7°C (that's over 101°F for you Fahrenheit fundamentalists) and it's only going up, with forecasts predicting afternoon highs well into the 108-110°F range. This isn't just "hot," it's "your internal organs are cooking" hot.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  An Excessive Heat Warning is in effect for the entire Phoenix metro area. Seriously, if you're out running between 10 AM and 6 PM, you're rolling dice with heatstroke, exhaustion, and worse. People die out here every summer from this. Don't be a statistic.
</AlertBox>

I'm talking specifically about places like Camelback Mountain. Park officials and rescue teams are practically begging people to stay off those exposed trails from late morning through late afternoon. They might as well put up a sign that says "Free ER Visit for Idiots." Your best bet is to hit the pavement or the dirt before 7 AM, or wait until after 7 PM, and even then, be extremely cautious. Pack more water than you think you need, and then double it. Electrolytes are your friend.

### Lungs & Lingering Grime

Our air quality is sitting at a "moderate" AQI of 64, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. That's fine particulate matter, the kind that irritates your lungs and can mess with your breathing, especially when you're exerting yourself. While it's not "hazardous" by official standards, combine it with this hellish heat, and your respiratory system is working overtime just to keep you upright. Don't add unnecessary strain.
