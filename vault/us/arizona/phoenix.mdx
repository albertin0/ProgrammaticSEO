<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat: Shut it Down!
This isn't a drill. 101.7°F is dangerous for *any* prolonged outdoor activity. Hiking the Echo Trail on Camelback Mountain today is a recipe for heatstroke, not an achievement. Phoenix often closes trails like this above 100 degrees for a reason: to keep you and the first responders safe. Don't ignore the city's lead, even if you don't see the big red warning signs out yet. Hydrate all you want, but your body just can't dissipate heat efficiently when it's this hot.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality & Respiratory Risk
While the official AQI is "Moderate" at 57, AccuWeather forecasts "Poor" for Phoenix today, warning that the air "has reached a high level of pollution and is unhealthy for sensitive groups." If you've got asthma, COPD, or just dislike feeling like you're sucking on a tailpipe, keep your hard workouts inside. The heat only exacerbates respiratory stress.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Escape (Routes)
If you *must* be out, know your limits. This isn't the day to try for a PR. Stick to shaded routes if you can find them (good luck with that in Phoenix today), carry way more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that's holy, tell someone exactly where you're going and when you expect to be back. And remember, "heat-related illness was observed even on 'Low' days". Every day in Phoenix summer-like heat carries risk, regardless of official alerts.
</AlertBox>
