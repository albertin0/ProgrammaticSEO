## The Rundown for Monday, April 6, 2026

Alright, desert dwellers, you got a bit of a reprieve today. That 25.9°C temperature, coupled with overcast skies, is practically a gift from the heavens for anyone looking to get outside. This is prime weather for a long run on the Central Canal or a hike up Telegraph Pass in South Mountain Park without feeling like you're running on the surface of the sun. The City of Phoenix has been on high alert with trail closures due to extreme heat warnings as early as March this year, but with today's mild forecast, you won't have those restrictions cramping your style.

However, it wouldn't be Phoenix without *some* environmental snags.

### Air Quality & Pollen

While the overall Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting pretty at 45, mostly due to PM2.5, keep in mind that the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) has warned about potential gusty easterly winds today. These winds love to whip up dust, which means that PM10 and PM2.5 levels could shift into the "moderate" range. So, that "good" AQI could degrade quickly if the wind picks up.

And then there's the pollen. It's moderate, which means your sinuses might decide to stage a tiny protest.

Always carry more water than you think you need, especially on longer routes or when tackling elevation. It’s April; the real heat is just around the corner, and getting acclimated now is smart.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Trail Access

Thankfully, there aren't any major events blocking popular running routes today. The Women's Final Four events downtown wrapped up yesterday. However, always be mindful of ongoing city projects. For instance, North Mountain Park is in the design phase for major renovations, with construction expected to begin in late 2026. While not impacting your run today, it's a heads-up for future trail disruptions. Similarly, ADOT is working on various freeway widening projects across the Valley, which might impact your drive *to* the trails, but generally don't block the trails themselves.

Today's a solid opportunity to get out there and enjoy Phoenix before the true inferno of summer descends. Just be smart about the dust and hydrate like your life depends on it – because sometimes, out here, it does.
