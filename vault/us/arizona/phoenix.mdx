## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  - **The Heat is Relentless:** We’re looking at 38.7°C (101.7°F) with mainly clear skies, meaning direct sun is a killer. The National Weather Service still indicates a major heat risk, and frankly, it feels like an oven outside.
  - **Trails Are Shut Down (and for good reason):** Due to the extreme heat, iconic spots like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain are closed from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Don't be "that guy" who thinks they're tougher than Phoenix summer. You're not.
  - **Air Quality is Just Okay:** While not outright terrible, an overall AQI of 55 isn't doing your lungs any favors, especially when combined with this heat. Your lungs are already working overtime just to keep you cool.
</BulletList>

Look, I get it. We live here for the sun, but September in Phoenix is still peak summer. Thinking about hitting the trails around lunch? Forget it. The city’s not closing Camelback Mountain trails during the day for fun. They do it because people *literally die* out there from heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Do you want to be the reason some poor first responder is lugging your dehydrated carcass off a mountain? Didn't think so.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Extreme Heat: Your Biggest Enemy Today
  With temperatures pushing 38.7°C (101.7°F), the risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke is critically high. Hydrate *constantly*, avoid peak sun hours (roughly 8 AM to 5 PM), and for the love of all that is holy, stick to indoor workouts if you can. Even an early morning run means you're starting hot and finishing hotter.
</AlertBox>
