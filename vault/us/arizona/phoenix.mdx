## **North Mountain Park: Get Ready for a Year-Long Shutdown**

Here’s the real kicker for North Central runners: North Mountain Park, that crucial access point at 7th Street and Peoria, is slated for a *year-long closure* for construction starting this fall. "This fall" means any day now, folks. The city council greenlit the funding for a $30 million project back in December 2025, and they're aiming for a full closure from late 2026 through late 2027. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a complete shut-down of a major trailhead just as prime hiking season kicks off. Don't be that person showing up with your CamelBak next month, only to find a fence. Plan your routes, especially if Trail 44 is your go-to.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Status & Local Lowdown

Today, with temps hovering around a comfortable 80 degrees and overcast skies, you're not battling the usual September inferno. This means the infamous "Extreme Heat Warning" restrictions that shut down trails like Echo Canyon and Cholla on Camelback Mountain, or the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, are highly unlikely to be in effect. So, your favorite mountain climbs should be fair game.

Speaking of Piestewa Peak, remember those extended summer hours? They're still active until September 30th, keeping the trailhead gate open until 9 p.m., an hour later than usual. This is great for an evening climb when the air is cooler and the views are, frankly, less blinding. Just remember the year-round rule for both Camelback and Piestewa: **no dogs** on the Summit Trail. Seriously, leave Fido at home for those particular ascents.

## **Heads Up: Freeway Construction and Local Street Closures**

While your trails might be clear of specific "workout events" today, the Valley's a perpetual construction zone. There are multiple freeway projects underway or kicking off in 2026 on Loop 101, Loop 202, Loop 303, and I-10. These are major projects. They might not be *on* your running path, but they will absolutely mess with your commute to trailheads. Check ADOT for the latest closures before you head out. Also, don't be surprised by localized street closures for various projects across the city, like on 101st Ave or 10th Ave. It's Phoenix, it's always something.
</AlertBox>

So, go enjoy the almost-fall weather, but stay aware of that looming North Mountain closure, and as always, hydrate like it's your job.
