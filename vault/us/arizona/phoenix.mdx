<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Off Restricted Trails!
This isn't just a suggestion; it's a public safety program. With temperatures well over 100°F, heat stroke is a very real, very dangerous possibility. The trails at Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and parts of South Mountain (like Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and Pima Canyon access to the National Trail) are officially restricted during the hottest parts of the day. These aren't just for your safety, but for the safety of the fire crews who have to rescue your cooked self. Seriously, nobody wants to be a statistic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Moderate AQI & Pollen: A Double Whammy for Your Respiratory System
An AQI of 51 is "Moderate," meaning sensitive groups could experience respiratory symptoms. Throw in that moderate pollen count, and you've got a recipe for wheezing and sneezing, even if you don't typically struggle. Your lungs are working overtime to deal with the heat already; don't make them fight pollution and allergens too.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### When to Actually Get Out There
If you absolutely *must* run or hike today, aim for before sunrise or after sunset. That means before 5:30 AM or after 7:00 PM if you want to avoid the worst of the sun. Even then, the ambient temperature will still be brutally high. Hydrate aggressively, wear light, loose clothing, and listen to your body. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or crampy, turn around. Seriously, that PR can wait. Your life can't.
</AlertBox>
