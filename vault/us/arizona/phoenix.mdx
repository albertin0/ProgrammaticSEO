## Air Quality Report

Our AQI is sitting at a "Moderate" 55 right now, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Don't let that fool you into thinking it's fine. The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality has slapped us with an Ozone High Pollution Advisory for today, which means ground-level ozone is expected to be near unhealthy levels. Combine that with moderate pollen and you've got a recipe for a good cough, especially if you're pushing it outside. Sensitive groups, you already know the drill: stay inside.

## Weather Watch

It's 40.7°C (a cool 105°F for those keeping score in Fahrenheit), and surprise, surprise, it's overcast. Don't think for a second that an overcast sky means you're safe from the heat. This is Phoenix in late August, and that blanket of clouds is barely making a dent in the insane temperatures. An Extreme Heat Warning is in full effect until Saturday evening because we're looking at well-above-normal temperatures that could break records. This isn't just "warm," it's "dangerously hot."

### EXTREME HEAT WARNING: Stay Off Trails!
Seriously, I'm not kidding. The City of Phoenix has officially *restricted* access to popular hiking spots like Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails), Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, and sections of South Mountain Park and Preserve from 8 AM to 5 PM. This isn't a suggestion; it's a public safety measure. Phoenix Fire Department crews are still pulling overheated hikers off mountains, putting their lives at risk because someone "really needed that sunrise selfie." Just don't do it.

With an AQI of 55 and PM2.5 dominant, your lungs are already doing extra work. This isn't the day for speed work or anything that makes you breathe heavily.
</AlertBox>
