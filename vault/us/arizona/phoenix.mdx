## Hazards & Recommendations

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat — Seriously, Stay Inside
Look, 22.4°C (72.3°F) is what people in San Diego call "a little breezy." This is Phoenix in June. The *actual* forecast for your outdoor workout window is pushing triple digits, 98-103°F, with an extreme UV index. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's a direct threat to your health. The City of Phoenix has a standing "Trail Heat Safety Program" that restricts trails like Echo Canyon and Cholla on Camelback Mountain from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. when an Extreme Heat Warning is issued. Even if there's no official *new* warning issued for this specific morning within the last 24 hours, these temperatures warrant extreme caution. Do your heavy lifting before 8 AM or after 7 PM, hydrate constantly, and pay attention to how your body feels. Seriously, don't be a statistic. Maricopa County already confirmed its first heat-related death of 2026 back in April.
</AlertBox>

### Moderate Pollen Alert
With tree pollen at moderate levels, your lungs might not be thrilled with a strenuous outdoor session. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out. Otherwise, expect a bit of extra sniffle and maybe a slightly heavier breath than usual. AQI is thankfully good today at 36, so at least you're not battling smoke *and* pollen.
</AlertBox>
