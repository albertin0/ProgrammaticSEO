Seriously, if you're even *thinking* about a serious outdoor workout in Phoenix today, you need to re-evaluate your life choices.

## Why today is decidedly NOT the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>**Scorching Heat Advisory:** It's a brutal 38.7°C (101.7°F) out there. This isn't just "warm," this is "fry an egg on the pavement and possibly yourself" territory. The City of Phoenix has a Trail Heat Safety Program for a reason, and you're squarely in the danger zone.</li>
  <li>**Moderate Air Quality & Pollen:** While not the main killer today, your lungs still aren't getting a free pass. We've got moderate pollen and an AQI of 55 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Enough to make you wheeze even if you weren't boiling alive.</li>
  <li>**Freeway Frustrations:** ADOT is doing its thing with weekend freeway closures on SR 51, Loop 101, and Loop 202 through Monday morning. If you were planning to drive to some far-flung trailhead, expect delays and a lot of extra time stewing in your oven-on-wheels.</li>
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### **Extreme Heat: Trails are NOT your friend right now.**
  With temperatures hitting 101.7°F, you are at serious risk of heatstroke, dehydration, and exhaustion. The City of Phoenix implements trail restrictions when extreme heat warnings are in effect, typically from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. This includes popular and challenging spots like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on **Camelback Mountain**, as well as the Piestewa Peak Summit trail and several paths on South Mountain. Ignoring these warnings is not only reckless for your own health but also puts our first responders at unnecessary risk. They've already seen heat-related emergency calls on trails this year, even in what used to be considered "early season" for this kind of heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### **Lungs Under Duress: Smoke, Dust, and Allergens**
  While we're "Mainly Clear" today, don't ignore the moderate AQI and pollen count. There have been some small, localized wildfires in the broader Phoenix dispatch area recently, and larger fires in Southern Arizona are causing trail closures elsewhere. While not directly impacting our air quality with heavy smoke *today*, it's a reminder that this is fire season. Pair that with moderate pollen, and your respiratory system is already working harder than it should. If you must be outside, keep it mellow and keep an eye on how you're feeling.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### **Commute Chaos: Plan Ahead if You're Driving**
  ADOT's got a whole slate of freeway closures for improvement projects this weekend, running through Monday morning. Expect significant delays if you're trying to navigate the metro area, especially on northbound SR 51 between Indian School Road and Glendale Avenue, or parts of Loop 101 and Loop 202. Local utility work is also causing partial street closures, such as on 15th Street. Factor this into your travel time, or better yet, stay local and move your workout indoors.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. We live in Phoenix, we're tough. But ignoring 100+ degree heat, especially when your body isn't fully acclimated, is just plain stupid. Go hit the gym, swim laps indoors, or at the very least, wait until the sun drops significantly and stick to a shaded, flat path. And for the love of all that is holy, bring more water than you think you need.
