## Hazard Highlights

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat = Extreme Danger

Look, the City of Phoenix has a Trail Heat Safety Program for a reason. When temperatures hit these levels, popular trails like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain are *restricted* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. This isn't a suggestion; it's to keep you and the first responders, who will inevitably have to drag you off the mountain, safe. Heat is the leading weather-related killer in the U.S., and Phoenix has seen its share of tragic heat-related deaths this year. Seriously, don't be that person.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Push It on the Pavement

While ADOT's major weekend freeway construction projects wrapped up yesterday, leaving the Loop 101, I-17, and Loop 202 clear of *planned* restrictions, surface street paving is in full swing across the city through the fall. If you're running or cycling on city streets, be aware of potential detours, rough patches, and fresh asphalt, which gets even hotter under this sun.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Early Birds (or Night Owls) Get the Worm

If you absolutely *must* get your miles in today, make it happen before the sun truly gets going, or wait until well after it sets. And I mean *well after* it sets, because the pavement holds heat like a stubborn memory. Hydrate like it's your job, and carry more water than you think you need. Consider an indoor workout, or at least a dip in a pool. Your lungs and joints (and literally every other organ) will thank you.
</AlertBox>
