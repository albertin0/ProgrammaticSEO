<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat: Your Body's Not a Radiator!
With temperatures soaring to 38.7°C (101.7°F), this isn't just "warm" – it's dangerously hot. The National Weather Service confirms heat-related deaths can occur even in the mid-80s here, and that's not even counting the 100+ degree days. The City of Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program typically restricts access to popular spots like **Camelback Mountain** and the Piestewa Peak Summit trail when extreme heat warnings are in effect from 8 AM to 5 PM. While I don't have an explicit warning issued for *today*, this temperature should be your warning. Seriously, avoid strenuous outdoor activity during peak heat hours. Reschedule that long run for another day, or at least for the pre-dawn hours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Dust Storms & Gusty Winds: Breathe Easy? Not Today.
Hold onto your hats, literally. Strong winds are rolling through Phoenix starting this evening and into tomorrow morning, with gusts potentially hitting 25-55 mph. These aren't just breezy; they're strong enough to whip up significant blowing dust, which can drastically reduce visibility and make breathing a nightmare, especially for those of us with sensitive lungs or allergies. If you absolutely *must* be outside, be prepared for dusty conditions and keep an eye on rapidly changing visibility, particularly if you're driving to a trailhead.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Hydrated and Stay Smart
If you absolutely insist on getting some miles in, go early. And by "early," I mean before the sun even thinks about cracking the horizon. Carry more water than you think you need. Consider moving your workout indoors to a gym, or hitting the pool instead. There are no major wildfire smoke events impacting Phoenix directly today, and no big race closures affecting your usual routes right within the city, but the weather itself is the biggest hazard.
</AlertBox>
