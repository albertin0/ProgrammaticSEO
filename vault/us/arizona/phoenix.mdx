## Run at Your Own Risk (No, Seriously)

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next runner, but today is a hard pass for anything strenuous during daylight hours. The combination of scorching heat and a high pollution advisory for ozone means your body will be fighting a losing battle. The Phoenix Fire Department has already been busy with an alarming spike in mountain rescues this year, many due to the heat. They've even got ice machines at trailheads like the Echo Canyon at Camelback Mountain, which should tell you something about how serious this is.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Hazard:** Temperatures are forecast to hit 106°F (41°C) today, with a Moderate HeatRisk. Heat-related illness is a very real threat. If you absolutely *must* be outside, go before the sun is fully up, carry more water than you think you need, and for the love of all that is holy, know your limits. South Mountain Park and Preserve, along with other popular mountain trails, sees a significant number of heat-related rescues. Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program closes certain trails, like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails at Camelback, and specific routes on South Mountain, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. when an Extreme Heat Warning is issued. Stay off those trails during peak heat.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone Pollution Advisory:** An Ozone High Pollution Advisory is in effect for the Phoenix Metro Area. This means breathing will be harder, especially for children, older adults, and anyone with respiratory issues. Outdoor physical activity is explicitly not recommended.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Trail Detours:** If you're planning a long ride or run on the multi-use paths, be aware that freeway closures are happening this weekend for improvement projects, which could impact travel to trailheads. Additionally, annual concrete repairs on trails in Gilbert, including sections of the L202 trail, will begin Monday, June 8th, so expect detours if you head that way next week. For today, though, the air and heat are your biggest obstacles.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, consider an indoor workout today. Or just kick back with some AC and hydrate. Your body will thank you.
