Look, October in Phoenix can be a crapshoot, but today you've got a solid window to hit those trails or get some road miles in. The air quality forecast from ADEQ for today, October 28th, shows ozone, PM10, and PM2.5 all hovering in the low 50s for AQI, which the feds classify as "Moderate." It's not pristine mountain air, but it’s definitely not the High Pollution Advisory levels we saw earlier this month.

What *could* throw a wrench in your plans is the usual Phoenix traffic ballet. If you're trying to get to a good trailhead, like something on the west side heading out towards the Estrella Mountains, be aware of the ongoing I-10 frontage road closure at the Loop 101 interchange. That started Monday, October 26th, and it's sticking around until November 9th. McDowell Road is your suggested alternate, which, let's be real, is probably already a nightmare. Plan your route to Papago Park or the Arizona Canal Trail if you want to avoid that specific headache. You don't want to spend more time fuming in traffic trying to *get* to your workout than actually working out. That's a bad mental health day right there.
