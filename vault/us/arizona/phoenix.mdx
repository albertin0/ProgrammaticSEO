### Today's Rundown: The Heat is On (Again)

Look, I get it. You've got that running bug. But seriously, today's a prime example of why Phoenix summers aren't for the faint of heart, or the foolish. While the official NWS Extreme Heat Warning that dominated our week just expired (it ran through Thursday evening), don't let that lull you into a false sense of security. We're still hitting triple digits, flirting with 106°F, and that’s not just uncomfortable, it’s dangerous. Maricopa County has already seen a worrying uptick in heat-linked deaths this year, outpacing last year's numbers.

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Trail Closures are Your Friend, Not a Suggestion
The City of Phoenix Trail Heat Safety Program is likely active, meaning popular trails at places like Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla) and specific routes in South Mountain Park and Preserve (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and Pima Canyon access to National Trail) are restricted from 8 AM to 5 PM. These aren't just suggestions; they're in place to keep *you* out of the ER and our first responders from hauling your heat-exhausted butt off a mountain. Respect the closures, people.
</AlertBox>

### Air You Can See (Maybe)

The Air Quality Index is currently moderate at 65 AQI, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality had a PM10 High Pollution Advisory for Friday, kicked up by dust from thunderstorms. They're forecasting mid-Moderate levels for PM10 throughout the weekend, so you might be breathing in a bit of that desert grit, especially with evening dust potential from more storms. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, this is your cue to keep it indoors.

### Monsoon Mayhem on the Horizon

Welcome to peak monsoon season! Forecasts are leaning towards a wetter-than-normal season, which sounds great for our parched desert, but also means we get all the fun trimmings: high winds, lightning, and flash floods. There’s a decent chance of thunderstorms rolling in after 5 PM today, so if you're out past lunch, keep an eye on the sky for those ominous brown clouds. A haboob rolling through while you're on a long run down Central Avenue is not a fun surprise, trust me.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch for Dust Storms & Lightning
Monsoon storms bring unpredictable dust (haboobs) and lightning. If you see dust walls approaching or hear thunder, get indoors immediately. No PR is worth getting struck by lightning or losing visibility on a busy street.
</AlertBox>
