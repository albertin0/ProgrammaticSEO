Look, I love our desert trails as much as the next maniac who wakes up at 4 AM to beat the sun, but today is not the day to play hero. An *Extreme Heat Warning* is not a suggestion, it's a declaration from the National Weather Service that your body will revolt if you push it. We're talking 105-112 degrees Fahrenheit in the afternoons. That’s not "challenging," that's "emergency room visit" territory, especially if you're out there between 8 AM and 5 PM.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Warning & Trail Closures!**
The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for the Phoenix metropolitan area, active from May 8 through May 11. This means multiple popular trails, including Echo Canyon and Cholla Trails on Camelback Mountain, the Piestewa Peak Summit trail, and several key routes on South Mountain (like Holbert and Mormon Trails), are *restricted* from 8 AM to 5 PM. Do not risk it. First responders are busy enough.
</AlertBox>

And let's talk about the air. Your lungs are already fighting a losing battle. The AQI might be sitting at a "Moderate" 56 for PM2.5, but AccuWeather says "Poor" and the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) has slapped us with an Ozone High Pollution Advisory for today and tomorrow. That means elevated ozone is hanging around in the "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" range. On top of that, we're still dealing with potential smoke impacts from the Hazen Fire near Buckeye, especially in the West Valley. Strong afternoon winds (up to 30 mph!) could stir that smoke up even more. So, you're not just baking, you're breathing in a delicious cocktail of heat, ozone, and particulate matter. Yum.
