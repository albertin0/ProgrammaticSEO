## Today's Vibe: Oven-Baked Runner, Not Well Done

Look, I get it. We live in Phoenix. We're tough. We *do* run when it's hot. But today? It’s not just "hot," it’s "don’t be an idiot" hot. We're looking at a high of 38.7°C (101.7°F) according to your data, which tracks perfectly with the "above normal" temperatures the National Weather Service is still talking about. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely hazardous, especially for prolonged exercise.

You've got that "Mainly Clear" sky, which means the sun is going to be relentlessly beating down on you, adding insult to injury to those already scorching temps. We've also got some "fairly widespread breezy conditions" today, which might *feel* nice for a second, but it just helps suck the moisture right out of you faster. That means faster dehydration.

### Trail Closures & Common Sense

If you're still considering hitting the trails, let me remind you about the Phoenix Trail Heat Safety Program. When we hit these extreme temperatures, popular and challenging trails like Echo Canyon and Cholla on Camelback Mountain, the Piestewa Peak Summit trail, and certain parts of South Mountain (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to the National Trail from Pima Canyon) are *restricted* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. These aren't suggestions; they're for your own good. The city closes them because people keep needing to be rescued. In 2024, Phoenix had 45 days of trail closures due to extreme heat, and 121 days over 105°F. Don't be a statistic.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat is NO JOKE!** Temperatures over 100°F put you at serious risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke, which can be fatal. Listen to your body and err on the side of caution.
</AlertBox>
