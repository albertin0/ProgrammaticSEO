### Trail Closures Today (8 AM - 5 PM)

If you had grand plans to conquer a mountain, put those shoes back in the closet. The following popular trails are OFF-LIMITS during the hottest part of the day, from 8 AM to 5 PM:

*   **Camelback Mountain Preserve:** Echo Canyon and Cholla trails.
*   **Phoenix Mountains Preserve:** Piestewa Peak Summit trail and its associated routes.
*   **South Mountain Park and Preserve:** Holbert Trail, Mormon Trail, Hau'pal Loop Trail, and the access point to the National Trail from the Pima Canyon Trailhead.

Don't be that person requiring a mountain rescue. Phoenix Fire Department has enough on its plate without having to haul you off a blazing hot trail. They even have a specific "Take a Hike. Do it Right." safety program for a reason.
