## The Lowdown on Your Monday Miles

Look, the real story here is the weather. Usually by now, we're already cooking in Phoenix, so this temperate start to the week is a gift. The National Weather Service isn't throwing any extreme heat warnings our way today, which means your favorite trails, including the often-restricted ones at South Mountain Park and Preserve, should be open and accessible during daylight hours. No 8 AM to 5 PM heat-related closures on Holbert, Mormon, or Hau'pal Loop trails today.

However, just because it's not scorching doesn't mean the air is perfectly crisp. The Air Quality Index is hovering at a "Moderate" 86 due to PM2.5. While the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) forecasted PM2.5 at 42 and Ozone at 74 for today (both moderate), the current reading is a bit higher. Sensitive groups – that's you, asthmatics, folks with respiratory issues, young kids, and older adults – should still consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. We've had high pollution advisories for ozone just recently, so keeping an eye on how you feel is key.

Pollen is still at a moderate level, so if you're one of the many who start running a snot factory this time of year, grab your meds before you head out.

### Fire Danger Update

While the temperature is pleasant, a recent report from this past weekend highlighted critical fire danger for much of Arizona due to high winds and low humidity. There's a city-wide fire ban in preserves that kicked in May 1, 2026. While no active fires are specifically impacting Phoenix workout areas today, always be aware of your surroundings, especially if you're hitting any dry, brushy areas.
