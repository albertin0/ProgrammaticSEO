## The Grim Reality of a Phoenix Summer Sunday

Look, it's July in Phoenix. If you're not up before the sun, you're flirting with heatstroke. Today, even with a little cloud cover, is no exception. We're on the cusp of truly brutal temperatures, with an "Extreme Heat Watch" already issued by the National Weather Service Phoenix AZ for Tuesday through Thursday, promising afternoon highs between 109 to 117 degrees in some areas. You think 108°F today is much different? Think again. Your body doesn't care about a couple of degrees when it's already fighting to cool itself.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Heat Hazard: It's Already Too Hot for Peak Hours.**
  The City of Phoenix already implements trail restrictions on popular spots like Camelback Mountain from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. when extreme heat warnings are in effect. While an *official* Extreme Heat Warning might not be active *right this second* for today's highest temps, the advice stands: stay off the trails during those hours. Rescues are up, and you don't want to be another statistic slowing down our already-stretched first responders.
</AlertBox>
