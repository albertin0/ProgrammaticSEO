Honestly, today is not the day to push it outside. While the early morning might feel "overcast" and a relatively mild 27.5°C (81.5°F) for about five minutes, that's just Phoenix lulling you into a false sense of security. The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning that runs through at least Friday, with afternoon highs expected to rocket into the 106 to 114°F (41-45°C) range. This isn't just "hot," it's "major heat risk" hot, where heat-related illnesses spike dramatically.

If you absolutely *must* get out there, forget anything involving a climb. The City of Phoenix has put its Trail Heat Safety Program into action, which means several of our go-to spots are restricted. Don't even think about hitting the Echo Canyon or Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. They're closed for a reason, people, and it's to keep you and the first responders from ending up in an ambulance. Other major trails in the Phoenix Mountains Preserve (like Piestewa Peak Summit) and South Mountain Park and Preserve are also on the restricted list during those hours.

Beyond the furnace-like temperatures, your lungs are also getting a raw deal. The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality has slapped us with an Ozone High Pollution Advisory for both Wednesday and Thursday. This means ground-level ozone is high enough to cause breathing difficulties, especially for kids, older adults, and anyone with respiratory issues like asthma. Our overall Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 61, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. The moderate pollen level (see table below) is just icing on this less-than-appetizing cake.

Remember those dust storms and thunderstorms that were kicking up yesterday evening in the southeast Valley? There's still a chance of lingering dust and even more isolated storms today, which can further degrade air quality and create hazardous conditions. We've had Flash Flood Warnings and Flood Advisories in nearby Pinal and Gila counties too, so keep an eye on the sky and stay clear of washes.
