## Dive into the Danger

Look, I love our Phoenix mountains as much as the next runner, but seriously, stay off the major peaks today. The National Weather Service has an **Extreme Heat Warning** in effect for the Phoenix metro area through Monday, with temperatures expected to soar. This has triggered the city's trail heat safety program.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Trail Closures Due to Extreme Heat!**
Popular and difficult trails are *closed* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. today. This includes **Camelback Mountain** (Echo Canyon and Cholla Trails), the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, and several trails on South Mountain (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to the National Trail from the Pima Canyon Trailhead). Don't be that person requiring a mountain rescue; it puts everyone at risk.
</AlertBox>

On top of the heat, the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) has issued an **Ozone High Pollution Advisory** for Maricopa County, which includes us. Ozone levels are predicted to be unhealthy for sensitive groups. This means if you're out there pushing it, you're not just cooking your core temperature, you're actively irritating your respiratory system. The air is expected to worsen in the afternoon.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Air Quality "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups"**
Ozone is the dominant pollutant today, expected to reach "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" levels. If you have asthma, emphysema, or are just generally not a fan of coughing, keep your cardio indoors, especially during peak afternoon hours.
</AlertBox>

And for my fellow allergy sufferers: it's a rough one. Grass pollen is "Very High," primarily Bermuda grass, and tree pollen is "Moderate." If you're heading outside, expect the full symphony of sneezes, sniffles, and itchy eyes.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: High Pollen Counts**
With very high grass pollen and moderate tree pollen, allergy symptoms are likely to be strong today. Pre-medicate if you must venture out, and consider a shorter, less intense workout.
</AlertBox>

## Getting Around

If you're trying to get to a less restricted trailhead or just navigate the city, be aware of some ongoing road work. ADOT has weekend freeway restrictions, including parts of SR 51 North and I-17 South, through this morning and into Monday morning. Factor in extra time and be patient.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up: Weekend Freeway Restrictions**
Portions of SR 51 North and I-17 South have closures or lane restrictions this weekend for maintenance. Plan your routes and expect delays, especially if you're trying to reach a different side of the valley.
</AlertBox>
