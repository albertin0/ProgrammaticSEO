## Today's Rundown: A Brief Respite from the Inferno

Alright, listen up. For once, we're not starting this with a fire and brimstone warning about impending heat death. That glorious 20.9°C and those actual *clouds* are practically a holiday in May for us desert dwellers. Take advantage of it. Usually, by now, the City of Phoenix is already shutting down trails like Echo Canyon and Cholla on Camelback Mountain, or restricting access at Piestewa Peak and South Mountain, all thanks to those lovely Extreme Heat Warnings the NWS likes to drop on us when temps hit 105°F+. Today? No NWS extreme heat warning, no automatic trail closures. So, go enjoy South Mountain Park and Preserve without fear of being a rescue statistic due to overheating.

However, just because it's not actively trying to kill you via heat stroke doesn't mean it's a free-for-all.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe Too Deeply (If You're Sensitive)

That AQI of 54, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, is still in the "Moderate" zone. This isn't "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" bad, but it means if you're an active adult, a kid, or someone with a respiratory issue like asthma, you might feel it. "Moderate" is just a polite way of saying "some of you will get a cough, the rest of you probably won't notice until you're huffing it up a hill." Think about cutting back on intense, long efforts, especially if you're usually affected.
</AlertBox>

### Pollen's Perpetual Presence

"Moderate" pollen. What else is new? It's Phoenix. Something's always trying to get into your sinuses. If you're one of the unlucky ones, you know the drill: pre-dose your meds, wear sunglasses, and maybe consider an extra shower when you get back.

## Construction and Other Non-Weather Bummers

Thankfully, there aren't any major wildfire smoke alerts or high wind advisories making headlines for *today*. We saw some high fire danger and gusty winds last week, but the forecast for today is just "light afternoon breezes."

As for construction, ADOT kicked off a big Loop 101/I-10 interchange project recently, which means freeway closures and detours. While this is a headache for drivers, it's not directly blocking your favorite trail loops unless you're literally running along the shoulder of the 101, which, let's be honest, you shouldn't be doing anyway. No reports of trail-specific construction closures or events blocking access to your usual running spots.

<AlertBox type="info">
### PSA: Hydration is Always King (Even When It's Not 110)

Overcast doesn't mean you can slack on water. This is Phoenix. Always, always bring more water than you think you need. Even at 70 degrees, you're still in the desert, and your body is a leaky sieve.
</AlertBox>

So, lace up, watch your breathing, and enjoy this slightly-less-infernal-than-usual day. Don't take it for granted, because the 100s are just around the corner.
