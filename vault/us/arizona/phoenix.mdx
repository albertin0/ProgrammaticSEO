### Local Trail Closures Are Real

As a physical therapist who's seen too many heat-related rescues, let me be crystal clear: **The City of Phoenix has a Trail Heat Safety Program for a reason.** When an Extreme Heat Warning is in effect (which it is for the coming days, and temperatures today are climbing into that danger zone), popular trails like those at **Camelback Mountain** (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails), Piestewa Peak, and portions of South Mountain Park and Preserve are *restricted from 8 AM to 5 PM*. Ignore these at your peril, and don't expect a quick rescue if you get into trouble. Our first responders are stretched thin enough without folks playing hero in triple-digit heat.
