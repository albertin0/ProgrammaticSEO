Phoenix, if you're thinking of a midday long run or a strenuous hike, just don't. Today is officially a hard pass for anything more than an early morning shuffle around your air-conditioned gym.

## Why today is decidedly *not* the day

<BulletList>
*   **Record-Smashing Heat:** We're talking 105 to 108 degrees Fahrenheit (40.6-42.2°C) by afternoon, which is absolutely absurd for March. This isn't just "warm," it's historically dangerous, and Phoenix has already broken its earliest 105°F day record this week.
*   **Mandatory Trail Closures:** The City of Phoenix isn't messing around; they've closed popular trails like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. through Sunday due to this extreme heat. Ignoring these isn't just stupid, it's putting our already-strained first responders at risk.
*   **Heat-Related Rescues Already Piling Up:** Our fire department has been busy, pulling multiple hikers off trails in the last 24-72 hours. Six rescues in a single day is a trend, not an anomaly, when temps hit these levels, especially for folks not acclimated to this kind of early-season furnace.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours!
An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for Phoenix through Sunday. Temperatures are forecast to hit a blistering 105-108°F today, which is unprecedented for March. The city is closing major trails from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., including those on Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak. Do NOT attempt to hike or do strenuous outdoor activity during these hours. You risk heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and putting emergency services in harm's way.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Push It in the Early Morning or Late Evening
Even outside the mandated closure times, the temperatures will climb rapidly. If you absolutely *must* get outside, make it a short, low-intensity activity before the sun really gets going or after it sets, when it's still warm but not quite as lethal. Hydrate like your life depends on it, because it might. Bring more water than you think you need, wear light-colored, loose clothing, and listen to your body – if something feels off, stop immediately.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Embrace the Indoors (or the Water!)
This is the perfect day for a gym workout, laps at an indoor pool, or any activity that keeps you out of the direct sun. Phoenix has opened splash pads early due to the heat, which might be a good option for cooling down. Your ego can handle missing a sunny trail run today; your body might not.
</AlertBox>
