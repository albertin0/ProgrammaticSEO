## The Air: Not Horrible, But Not Heroic Either

The official word is an AQI of 70, with Ozone and "Other" as the main culprits. That's "Moderate." Now, the ADEQ forecast for today actually leaned more towards "Good" for all pollutants, but if your app says 70, trust your app and your own lungs. For most of us, it's fine. For anyone with even a touch of asthma or other respiratory issues, you might feel that familiar tickle, that slight drag. Ozone, even at moderate levels, can be a pain in the chest, making breathing feel a little heavier than it should. Don't be a hero. Listen to your body, not just the numbers.

## Trail Talk: No Heat Closures (Thank Goodness)

Remember a few weeks ago, in mid-March, when we hit triple-digits *way* too early and Phoenix Parks closed trails like Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.? Yeah, today isn't that. With the temperature sitting at a reasonable 20°C, you won't have the City of Phoenix actively trying to save you from yourself on the trails. Feel free to hit South Mountain, but still, go early or late, especially if you're doing something strenuous. It's April, not July, but the sun here still means business.

Even though the temperature isn't screaming "heatstroke," our desert air is dry. Carry more water than you think you need, especially if you're out for more than an hour. Dehydration sneaks up on you, even on a "nice" day.
