That "overcast" you see might just be a lingering haboob, not actual clouds.
*   **Monsoon Mischief:** It's monsoon season, folks. Maricopa County is on high alert for sudden, heavy rain, high winds, and the flash flooding that inevitably follows, especially in our washes and low-lying areas. Dust storms often precede or accompany these microbursts.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat: Stay Indoors During Peak Hours!** With temperatures soaring, hiking on restricted trails like those on Camelback Mountain between 8 AM and 5 PM is prohibited. The city closes them for a reason: to prevent you from becoming a statistic. If you *must* be outside, stick to shaded areas and go before the sun really gets cooking.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Dust Advisory: Breathe Easy (or Don't)!** Air quality is compromised by particulate matter from recent dust storms. If you have asthma, COPD, or just value being able to take a full breath, consider an indoor workout today. That grey haze isn't a cloud, it's pulverized dirt.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** This isn't just a suggestion; it's survival in August. Drink water before, during, and after any activity, even if it's just walking to your car. Carry more water than you think you'll need.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check for Localized Flooding:** Monsoon storms are unpredictable. Even a brief downpour miles away can turn a dry wash into a raging river. Don't be "that guy" who drives into standing water. You will drown.
</AlertBox>

On a related note, expect some major traffic headaches this weekend if you're driving to any trailhead or gym in the west Valley. ADOT has eastbound I-10 closed between 99th and 75th avenues and westbound Loop 101 shut down between 51st and 75th avenues for "improvement projects." Just what we needed.
