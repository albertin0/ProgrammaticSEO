## Don't Get Swept Away: Our Washes Aren't Playgrounds

Alright, listen up, because this isn't some corporate wellness blog telling you to "embrace the day." We had some serious rain rip through the Valley over the weekend, and while your car isn't floating down Central Avenue anymore, that doesn't mean our washes are suddenly safe for your morning jog. The National Weather Service in Phoenix is still reporting elevated flows across south-central Arizona waterways. Just a few days ago, Phoenix Fire Technical Rescue was pulling six people out of a wash near 40th Street and Camelback Road. Six people. In a *wash*. That's not a suggestion to turn around; that's a blatant instruction.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: DO NOT ENTER FLOODED OR FLOWING WASHES/LOW WATER CROSSINGS.** Even a few inches of water can sweep you off your feet or worse, hide dangerous debris and unstable ground. The Salt River, New River, and Verde River were all under flood warnings as of yesterday and this morning, and while some may be lifting, the danger of elevated flows persists. Stay on paved roads and established trails, far from any moving water.
</AlertBox>

## Breathing Room (or Lack Thereof)

The air quality is sitting at an AQI of 68, with PM2.5 being the main culprit. That's "Moderate," which, to translate from bureaucratese to human, means if you've got asthma, COPD, or just generally cranky lungs, you might feel it. It's not the apocalypse, but it's not pristine mountain air either. Combine that with "Moderate" pollen levels, and your morning tempo run could easily turn into a snot-filled struggle for some folks.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Moderate AQI & Pollen.** If you're in a sensitive group for respiratory issues, consider moving your longer outdoor workouts indoors or significantly reducing your intensity and duration. Allergy sufferers, load up on your meds if you plan to hit the trails around South Mountain Park and Preserve.
</AlertBox>

## Temper Your Expectations (And Your Joints)

Temperature-wise, 14°C (58°F) and clear skies make for a gorgeous morning, with highs expected around 22-23°C (72-74°F). Your joints will thank you for not pounding them in 110-degree heat. This is prime running weather *if* you avoid the other hazards. So, enjoy the cool air, but keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Check Your Route Twice.** Construction is always a factor in Phoenix. The "Pave Phoenix" initiative and freeway projects (like the I-17 North Expansion and Loop 101 Pima Freeway improvements) mean unexpected closures or detours are a constant. Always check the City of Phoenix and ADOT websites before you head out, even for a familiar route.
</AlertBox>
