<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step & Your Route: Construction Ahead**
While the weather's playing nice, the city's still in a perpetual state of "improvement," which often means tearing up roads. This weekend saw a whole lot of freeway closures for pavement work and widening projects across I-10, I-17, Loop 101, Loop 202, Loop 303, SR 51, and SR 347. Many of these were supposed to wrap up early this morning, but you know how that goes. More significantly, the westbound I-10 frontage road at the Loop 101 interchange is completely closed until November 9th. If your usual running or cycling path takes you anywhere near these major arteries, especially near **Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport** or the wider I-10 corridor, assume there are detours.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, don't just blindly follow your old GPS route. A quick check of ADOT's alerts before you head out could save you from an unexpected detour into a construction zone. What was a minor inconvenience Friday could still be a headache for your Monday morning miles.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Don't Forget the Basics**
Even with ideal temps, carry water. It's still Phoenix, and dehydration is always just around the corner if you're not paying attention. Also, remember that "Extreme Heat Warnings" are not in effect today, so the city's usual trail restrictions on spots like Camelback Mountain aren't active. But that doesn't mean you can't still make bad decisions. Pace yourself, listen to your body, and don't try to PR on a new, unfamiliar route because your usual one is blocked by a barricade.
</AlertBox>

Go get after it, but stay smart out there.
