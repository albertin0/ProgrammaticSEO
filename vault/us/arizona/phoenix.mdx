## Current Conditions & What to Expect

Today's forecast is pretty much a runner's dream. We're looking at clear skies and a lovely 14.1°C (about 57.4°F) right now, climbing to the mid-80s by the afternoon. That's prime time for getting your miles in, whether you prefer the sunrise chill or the midday warmth. Winds are light, and there's not a cloud in the sky. Don't get used to it though; rain's on the menu starting Thursday night and through the weekend, so capitalize on this while you can.

## Local Hazards & Headaches

While the weather's being cooperative, the Valley's eternal construction isn't taking a break.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch out for I-10 Construction near Loop 101.** The westbound I-10 frontage road at the Loop 101 interchange is shut down for two weeks, having started yesterday, October 26. If your route involves crossing under or over that interchange, especially on a bike, expect detours and increased traffic congestion. These aren't just minor inconveniences; they can force you into less safe areas if you're not paying attention.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of recent nonsense, remember that massive downtown fire near 19th Avenue and McDowell Road on Saturday? It sent a thick plume of smoke over the Valley. While the AQI is good now, if you're running any urban routes around that area, keep an eye (and nose) out for any lingering industrial smells or haze. Our desert air is usually too efficient at clearing things out for it to be a major *breathing* issue days later, but it's Phoenix, so you never really know what's in the air until you taste it.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is always key, even when it's "cool."** Mid-80s in the afternoon is still plenty warm for dehydration to sneak up on you, especially if you're pushing distance. Don't be that person getting rescued off a trail because you thought a single water bottle was enough for your ego trip.
</AlertBox>

Today is genuinely one of the better days we've had in a while for outdoor activity. The heat's not trying to kill you, the air is mostly clear, and aside from the usual roadwork shenanigans, you're pretty much in the clear. Get out there, but don't be stupid.
