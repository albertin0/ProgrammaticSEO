Look, I've run these trails for years, and every May it's the same song and dance: "It's not *that* hot yet," then BAM, you're looking at heat stroke. Today's 71 degrees is a gift, but it's a fleeting one. The National Weather Service is forecasting triple digits for the weekend, so if you're planning a long run or hike, do it at dawn or dusk. Seriously, those midday hours are for pool lounging, not pounding pavement.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Out for the Buzz Killers

Remember those poor souls on Camelback Mountain earlier this week? Swarms of Africanized honey bees are active, especially on our popular mountain trails like the Cholla and Echo Canyon trails. If you hear an aggressive buzzing, freeze, cover your head and neck, and get out of the area as quickly as possible without flailing around like a lunatic. They're drawn to movement. Fire officials specifically mentioned this, so they're not messing around.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### The Heat is Coming, Phoenix!

Today might be forgiving, but the weekend is going to be brutal. Expect temperatures to hit around 98-100°F through Memorial Day. That means if an Extreme Heat Warning is issued, popular trails like those on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and parts of South Mountain will be *closed* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. This isn't just about avoiding a rescue; it's about not becoming a statistic. The City of Phoenix Parks Board has been expanding these trail closure programs because too many people ignore the warnings. Hydrate like your life depends on it – because it does. Drink water *before*, *during*, and *after* your workout. If you start feeling dizzy, nauseous, or stop sweating, get to shade and cool down immediately.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Fire Restrictions are ON

Due to the bone-dry conditions and increasing wildfire activity, seasonal fire restrictions were implemented yesterday, May 21st, across central and western Arizona, including right here in Maricopa County. This means no building open fires or campfires with solid fuels, no smoking outside enclosed vehicles, and no welding on BLM-managed lands. Be smart out there; we don't need another fire to deal with on top of everything else.
</AlertBox>
