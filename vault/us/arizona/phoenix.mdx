Alright, Phoenix. If you're thinking about that midday run today, March 22nd, 2026, you've officially lost your damn mind.

## Why today is *definitely* not the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Extreme Heat Warning is Active, Trails are Closed:** The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning plastering the Valley until 8 PM MST tonight. This isn't your grandma's "warm March day"; we're talking forecasted temperatures hitting between 94 to 108 degrees Fahrenheit, possibly even 109 degrees, which is unheard of for March.
*   **Mandatory Trail Closures in Effect:** Because it's so ridiculously hot, the City of Phoenix has shut down major trails from 8 AM to 5 PM today. This includes iconic spots like Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain, the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, and several routes in South Mountain Park and Preserve. Ignore these at your own peril – or, more accurately, at the peril of our already overworked rescue teams. Phoenix Fire has already been busy this week.
*   **Air Quality is Borderline Nasty:** While the official AQI reads "Moderate" at 51, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, AccuWeather is calling our air "Poor" for today, indicating unhealthy levels for sensitive groups. Combine that with the heat, and you're essentially breathing hot, mildly polluted soup.
</BulletList>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Danger: Extreme Heat & Closed Trails!
Seriously, people. This isn't the time to prove how tough you are. We're in an *Extreme Heat Warning* with record-breaking temperatures for March. Trails on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and South Mountain are *closed* from 8 AM to 5 PM. If you try to hike these, you're not only risking heat stroke for yourself but also potentially putting our emergency responders in harm's way when they inevitably have to come get you. The city *strongly discourages* hiking on *any* city trails during these hours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality & Pollen Aren't Helping
The air quality is hovering on the edge of "unhealthy for sensitive groups," primarily due to PM2.5. If you have asthma, allergies, or other respiratory issues, this heat combined with the air pollution is a recipe for disaster. On top of that, pollen is at a "Moderate" level. Your lungs don't need this kind of workout.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan B: Hydrate, Stay Indoors, or Go REALLY Early/Late
If you absolutely *must* get out, consider indoor options. Or, if you're a glutton for punishment, hit the streets (away from trails) *before* 7 AM or *after* 7 PM. Even then, hydrate like your life depends on it (because it does), wear light-colored, loose clothing, and listen to your body. There's an Arizona Aloha Festival at Tempe Town Lake today, which means more crowds, so maybe skip the lake path too. Also, ADOT has freeway closures on I-10 and SR 143 this weekend, so plan your commute to the gym accordingly.
</AlertBox>
