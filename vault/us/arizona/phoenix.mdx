### So, What Can You Do Without Being a Moron?

If you *must* get out there, consider an early morning or late evening run on something flat like the Arizona Canal Path near Steele Indian School Park. But even then, listen to your body. Today's "feels like" temperature is no joke, and a 7 Max UV Index is going to fry you if you're not careful.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate.** No, really. Drink water like it's your job, and start *before* you even think about stepping outside. The Phoenix Fire Captain recommends hydrating the night before a hike and drinking water every 20 minutes, even if you don't feel thirsty.
</AlertBox>
