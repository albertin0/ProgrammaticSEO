## The Rundown: Don't Get Complacent Just Because It's Not 100 Degrees

Look, I know we've been spoiled with some brutally early heat this year. We saw triple-digit temperatures and mandatory trail closures on the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain back in March. That was *March*, people! Today's cool-down is a gift, but it's not an excuse to completely forget where you live.

**Today's Pro-Tip: Enjoy the Mornings (and Evenings Too)**

While the temps are pleasant today, Phoenix transitions fast. If you're hitting the trails, especially on those exposed mountain paths around South Mountain Park and Preserve, aim for early morning or late evening. The city’s "Trail Heat Safety Program" exists for a reason, even if it's not active today. Those closures save lives and first responders a lot of grief when the mercury inevitably spikes again.

</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">

**Wildfire Watch: It's Already Getting Spicy Out There**

Don't let the current calm fool you. Arizona is looking at an elevated wildfire risk through June, thanks to a dry winter. We've already had 53 wildfires in the Phoenix area this year, with 96% being human-caused. That's on *us*. Even a seemingly small gust of wind can spark a disaster. Be extra mindful of anything that could start a fire, and if you see smoke, report it. Your leisurely run shouldn't turn into a rescue mission for someone else.

</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">

**Construction Zones: Mind the Detours**

ADOT is still plugging away on various freeway projects across the Valley, like the Loop 101, 202, and 303 widenings. While these are mostly highway-focused, they can affect your commute to trailheads or create unexpected dusty patches near major arteries. Stay aware of local traffic updates before you head out, especially if your usual route takes you near one of these construction zones.

</AlertBox>

So, lace up, hydrate, and for the love of all that is holy, don't be that person needing a helicopter off Piestewa Peak. Today's a decent day to get out, but always keep your wits about you.
