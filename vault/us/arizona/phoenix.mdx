Look, I run in Phoenix. I get it. We pride ourselves on being tough. But today, the city itself is telling you to back off. The National Weather Service Phoenix office has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for dangerously hot conditions, with afternoon temperatures expected to hit between 109 and 116 degrees Fahrenheit today. This isn't just "hot for Phoenix," this is "heat-stroke-is-a-real-risk" territory, and it's sticking around until at least Wednesday.

Case in point: a 61-year-old man was found dead on a South Mountain trail this past Saturday after going for a hike in the early morning. They found his body 12 hours later. Let me repeat: he went out *early*. And still, the heat got him. The City of Phoenix has even shut down trails on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and specific routes in South Mountain Park (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to National Trail from Pima Canyon Trailhead) from 8 AM to 5 PM because of this. If the city is closing trails, it’s not a suggestion, it's a desperate plea for you to stay inside.

Your joints aren't going to love the pavement when it's hot enough to fry an egg, and your lungs won't appreciate the extra strain.
