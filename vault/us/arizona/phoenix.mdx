## The Phoenix Heat: Not for the Faint of Heart (or the Foolish)

Seriously, folks. It's April 14th, and Phoenix is already cranking out triple-digit temperatures. This isn't "push through it" weather. This is "stay inside or swim" weather. The city and Maricopa County issue heat-safety guidance for a reason, even for outdoor workers, advising extra water and frequent shade breaks. If you're out there for recreation in this, you're actively choosing to ignore common sense.

You might have heard about the extreme heat warnings that triggered trail closures earlier this spring, setting a record for how early they went into effect. Well, today's temperature pushes us right back into that danger zone. The official policy is clear: if there's an Extreme Heat Warning, those peak trails are off-limits during the hottest parts of the day. This isn't a suggestion; it's a mandate designed to keep you from becoming another heat-related rescue statistic. The Phoenix Fire Department has already responded to heat-related emergency calls on trails this week during similar conditions.
