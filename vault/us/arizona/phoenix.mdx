## Today's Phoenix Forecast: Just Say No to That Afternoon Run

Look, I get it. Holiday weekend, maybe you've got some extra time. But today, Friday, July 4, 2026, Phoenix is a prime example of why sometimes the best workout is no outdoor workout. We're staring down 107°F, which, let's be real, feels like 115°F when you're hoofing it on blacktop or climbing a rock face. The National Weather Service is calling for "moderate heat risk," and that's not a suggestion, it's a warning to stay smart.

The City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation Department has already shut down trails on Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails), Piestewa Peak Summit Trail, and several at South Mountain Park and Preserve from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. today. Why? Because people keep pushing it and ending up needing a rescue, tying up resources that are already stretched thin. If you ignore those signs and still try to tackle something like the Mormon Trail at South Mountain in this heat, you’re not a hero, you're a liability.

### Air Quality: Don't Breathe That In

Beyond the oven-like temperatures, our air quality is taking a hit. The AQI is currently 55, deemed "Moderate" by Ambee, but local reports indicate Phoenix is under an ozone high pollution advisory. This is partly due to the Pocket Fire burning up north near Sedona, sending smoke our way, and partly just because, well, it's Phoenix in July. PM2.5 levels, the tiny particles that mess with your lungs, are forecast to rise to an AQI of 72 tonight because of all the Independence Day fireworks. That's "Moderate" but still not ideal for sustained exercise, especially for sensitive groups. AccuWeather even calls today's AQI "Poor" for sensitive groups.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Extreme Heat Warning: Trail Closures in Effect!**
Popular trails like those on Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, and South Mountain are *closed* from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. due to the extreme heat. Do not attempt to hike them. Rescues are up, and you're risking your life and the lives of first responders.
</AlertBox>

**Hydration and Safety for the 4th**
If you absolutely *must* exercise outdoors, go before sunrise, drink more water than you think you need, and wear light clothing. The City of Phoenix is under Stage 1 Fire Restrictions, prohibiting all fireworks within one mile of a desert or regional park. Leave the pyrotechnics to the professionals.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks. It's a bad day to push your limits outside. Find an indoor gym, go for a swim (responsibly, not in an electrocution hot tub, obviously), or just enjoy the holiday from the comfort of your air-conditioned home. Your lungs and joints will thank you.
