Okay, "Moderate" AQI of 55 sounds benign on paper. But let's be real. Phoenix just had a massive dust storm roll through, dropping visibility to near zero and likely elevating PM10 concentrations to absurd levels yesterday. The current AQI is probably just the after-party settling, but with ongoing winds, don't be surprised if your "mainly clear" skies suddenly get hazy with more stirred-up crud. That PM2.5, even at moderate levels, isn't doing your cardiovascular system any favors when you're already fighting triple-digit heat.

## Current Hazards & What to Do

### Extreme Heat - Still a Killer

<AlertBox type="danger">
The temperature is 101.7°F. This isn't just "warm," it's dangerously hot. Exercising in this kind of heat puts you at severe risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. The National Weather Service issued Extreme Heat Warnings earlier this week, and while they've technically expired for the whole metro area, the *danger* has not. Even a "Minor" HeatRisk carries risks for sensitive individuals.
</AlertBox>

### Blowing Dust & Wind

<AlertBox type="warning">
Yesterday's haboob was no joke, and a Wind Advisory is active for the Valley today until 5 PM, with gusts up to 40 mph. This means reduced visibility and more dust in the air is a real possibility, especially on open roads or trails. Driving conditions could be difficult, and light objects may become airborne.
</AlertBox>

### Construction Chaos

<AlertBox type="info">
If you're driving to any trailhead, plan for delays. ADOT has freeway closures and restrictions on Loops 101 and 202, and northbound SR 51, continuing through Monday morning. That stretch of northbound SR 51 from Indian School Road to Glendale Avenue is shut down, for instance. Don't be that person stuck in traffic before you even get to melt on a trail.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, folks, today is a fantastic day for indoor workouts, maybe an early morning swim, or just staying in with the AC cranked. Your lungs, joints, and frankly, common sense, will thank you.
