## The Nitty-Gritty

Right now, it's sitting at a balmy 31°C (87.8°F) with clear skies. Sounds nice, right? Wrong. That's just a warm-up act for the oven-like conditions coming later. The air quality is currently moderate at 68 AQI, primarily due to PM2.5. However, the winds are kicking up, and with Red Flag Warnings in effect for parts of the region due to critical fire weather, there's a real risk of blowing dust and potential smoke from ongoing wildfires in northern Arizona impacting air quality. We've seen smoke from Arizona fires drift hundreds of miles before. So even if you don't see flames, your lungs might feel it.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Kills. Seriously.
With highs pushing 106°F (41°C), heat exhaustion and heat stroke are not just possibilities; they are very real threats. Many of Phoenix's most popular hiking trails, including those on Camelback Mountain, are likely closed between 8 AM and 5 PM to prevent heat-related emergencies and mountain rescues. Respect these closures. Do not be "that guy" who needs to be rescued.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe Dust
Gusty winds mean dust. Lots of it. If you're out running near any of our lovely unpaved areas or construction sites, you're going to get a mouthful. PM2.5 is already the dominant pollutant, and dust just adds to the fun, especially in the afternoons. If you have any respiratory issues, stay indoors.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### If You *Must* Get Out
Hydrate like it's your job. Seriously, pre-hydrate, hydrate during, and post-hydrate. Water, electrolytes – all of it. If you absolutely have to work out outside, make it a quick, easy one, and get it done before the sun has a chance to properly scorch the pavement, ideally before 7 AM. Keep it to flat, shaded areas if possible. Deer Valley Park has an "After Dark in the Park" event tonight, which is great for evenings, but don't think you can escape the daytime heat there. Your body is not an air conditioner; it's a meat suit that will cook in this heat.
</AlertBox>
