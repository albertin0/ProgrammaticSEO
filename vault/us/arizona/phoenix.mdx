<AlertBox type="danger">
### **Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!**

Seriously, the National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning in effect for the entire Phoenix metro area. Temperatures are forecasted to be dangerously high, and they're not letting up anytime soon. You can find plenty of "heat relief stations" across the Valley if you need them. Take advantage of air conditioning.
</AlertBox>

Let's be blunt: attempting any strenuous outdoor activity between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. is not just ill-advised, it's straight-up dangerous. The city has had these trail closures at places like Camelback Mountain for a reason – to prevent you from getting heat stroke and to keep our first responders from having to haul your dehydrated carcass off a mountain. It's not a suggestion; it's a mandate for your own safety and for the safety of those who would have to rescue you.

You might look outside and see "overcast" and think, "Oh, a reprieve!" Don't fall for that desert mirage. Overcast just means the sun isn't directly baking you, but the ambient temperature is still going to cook you alive. The humidity is also a factor with lingering monsoonal moisture, making it feel even worse.

If you absolutely *must* move, stick to indoor gyms with A/C that works. Your lungs will appreciate not trying to suck in moderate AQI air mixed with moderate pollen in this furnace. And your joints, well, they'll thank you for not pushing them when your body is already fighting to regulate its core temperature.

<AlertBox type="info">
### **Construction Alert: SR 51**

Heads up if you're driving to any *indoor* workout spots: Northbound SR 51, specifically between Highland Avenue and Camelback Road, is down to two lanes for pavement and drainage repairs. This is an around-the-clock thing, started yesterday, and it's expected to last about seven weeks. Expect delays and plan alternative routes like I-17 if you need to go north. It won't directly impact your run on a trail (because you shouldn't be on a trail), but it'll make getting around a pain.
</AlertBox>

Today is a prime example of why being a year-round runner in Phoenix requires brains, not just brawn. Save the heroics for cooler days. Today is about survival.
