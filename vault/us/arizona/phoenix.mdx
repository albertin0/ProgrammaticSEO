## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  <li>**Extreme Heat Warning in Full Swing:** We're staring down the barrel of 38.7°C (a cool 101.7°F) right now, but the forecast is pushing us towards a blistering 107-110°F (42-43°C) and beyond today and through the week. The National Weather Service has slapped an Extreme Heat Warning on parts of the area from Monday through Tuesday evening, and Extreme Heat Watches are posted for most of us for the middle of the week. Seriously, your body isn't designed to run in an oven.</li>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Pro-Tip: Just Stay Inside
  Seriously, this isn't rocket science. Do your workout indoors today. Hit the gym, do some yoga in your living room, or swim laps at an indoor pool. If you absolutely *must* be outside for some reason (and I'm judging you), do it before sunrise, keep it short, stay to shaded, flat paved paths, and carry more water than you think you need. Reschedule your long runs or mountain hikes for a day when it's not trying to kill you. Don't be a hero; be smart.
