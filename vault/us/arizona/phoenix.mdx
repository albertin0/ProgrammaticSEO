## The Rundown: October 6, 2026

If you're looking for an excuse to skip your workout, today's not it, unless you're *really* sensitive to moderate pollen. The mercury is sitting comfortably below "surface of the sun" levels at 25.4°C (77.7°F), which means those early morning or late evening runs won't feel like a death march. It's clear out there, so enjoy some vitamin D, just remember your sunscreen.

The Air Quality Index is hovering at a "Moderate" 55, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. Honestly, for Phoenix, that's not terrible. But if you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, you might feel a bit of a tickle. The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality's forecast for today actually pegged PM2.5 a little lower at 32 AQI, but Ozone was higher at 77 AQI. So, it's a mixed bag of moderate pollutants. Combine that with a "Moderate" pollen level, and your sinuses might be putting up a fight.

### Trail Talk: South Mountain & City Streets

For those of you hitting the dirt, places like South Mountain Park and Preserve are wide open today. There are no extreme heat warnings (thank the running gods!), so the usual summer trail restrictions on popular routes like the Holbert Trail aren't in effect. Go get those vert miles in, but don't forget your water. It's not *hot*, but it's still Phoenix.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step (or Wheel) Downtown!**
If your workout involves anything near downtown Phoenix, be aware of the ongoing urban sprawl. There are multiple utility and private development projects causing street closures. For example, parts of 10th Ave, 10th St, and 11th Ave have partial or full closures that are still active today and extend into the coming weeks. These aren't necessarily "hazards" in the sense of danger, but they are a massive pain in the butt for your planned routes. If you're cycling or running through the city grid, check the City of Phoenix's traffic restrictions before you head out, or just accept that you'll be adding some unplanned mileage.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Lungs and Allergies:**
With moderate AQI and pollen, this is a day for you to pay attention to your body. If you feel any chest tightness or unusual shortness of breath, back off. I've seen too many runners push through thinking it's just "getting in shape" when it's really an environmental trigger. It's not worth an ER trip, especially when a good PT would tell you to listen to your damn body.
</AlertBox>

There aren't any major wildfires spewing smoke over Maricopa County right now, which is a blessing, so your breathing issues today are likely localized to pollen and general urban funk. So, grab your water, maybe an inhaler if you need it, and get out there before the real heat inevitably returns.
