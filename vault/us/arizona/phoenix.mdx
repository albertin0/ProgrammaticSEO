## Phoenix, You’re a Wild Card Today

It's 81 degrees Fahrenheit and "clear" right now, which sounds inviting. But trust me, in Phoenix, that’s just the desert lulling you into a false sense of security before it throws a curveball. That curveball today? Wind and rain, potentially at the same time, with a side of dust.

### Mind the Air, and the Wind Pre-Game
With a moderate AQI of 53 driven by PM2.5, sensitive groups should reduce time spent outdoors. Combine that with moderate pollen and the *imminent* strong winds expected to kick up dust across the Valley, and your respiratory system is in for a bad time. Think twice before tackling a long-distance run or any strenuous activity where you’ll be sucking in that lovely desert grit.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: South Mountain Park & Preserve

I love South Mountain as much as the next masochist, but today might not be its shining moment. While the city typically restricts trails like Holbert and Mormon from 8 AM to 5 PM during *Extreme Heat Warnings* (which we don't have today, thank goodness), the incoming wind advisory for Sunday explicitly mentions areas like South Mountain/Ahwatukee. The last thing you want is to be caught on a exposed ridge when those 50 mph gusts hit, or worse, scrambling down a muddy trail in a sudden downpour. Save the elevation gain for a calmer day.

## Construction Chaos

If you're driving to any of your workouts, brace yourself for the usual weekend fun. ADOT's got freeway closures and restrictions on Loops 101, 202, and SR 51 this weekend for widening projects. The I-10 widening project between Phoenix and Casa Grande is also ongoing, so expect delays and reduced speeds if you're heading south. Factor in extra commute time, or better yet, stay local and reconsider your outdoor plans.
