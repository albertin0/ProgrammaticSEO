## The Scorch Report: Today's Outdoor Debacle

Look, I get it. You want to stay fit. But pushing it outside today, July 6th, 2026, is less about fitness and more about tempting fate. The National Weather Service in Phoenix has been broadcasting an Extreme Heat Warning, effective from Tuesday through Thursday, with afternoon temperatures expected to soar between a brutal 109°F and 117°F. And while "officially" it starts tomorrow, today's forecast high is a blistering 111°F (44°C). That’s a Major Heat Risk, folks.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat: Stay Indoors, Seriously.
Afternoon temperatures are forecast to hit 111°F (44°C) today, leading into an Extreme Heat Warning for the rest of the week. This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous. Heat-related illnesses can escalate quickly to heat stroke. Reschedule any strenuous outdoor activities for early morning, *if* you must, but honestly, stay inside. Your air conditioning is your friend.

Their Trail Heat Safety Program means popular spots, including sections of South Mountain Park and Preserve like Holbert and Mormon Trails, are restricted or outright closed from 8 AM to 5 PM. This isn't for fun; it's because people keep underestimating the desert and emergency services are tired of playing rescue squad.
