## The Daily Grind: Phoenix Edition

Alright, Phoenix runners, let's talk turkey. It's June 19th, and if you think that 27.9°C (82°F) at 4 AM is a green light for an afternoon jaunt around Tempe Town Lake, you've got another thing coming. The forecast for today is a blistering 110°F (43°C) by mid-afternoon. That's not "warm," that's "your-sweat-evaporates-before-it-even-hits-the-ground" hot. We're not getting any breaks from the heat this week; expect similar temperatures through the weekend.

Seriously, if you're planning a long run, you should've been out the door an hour ago. Or maybe even two. If you're reading this anytime after 7 AM, you're already flirting with some serious dehydration and heat exhaustion if you're trying to hit those longer distances.

It means there's enough particulate matter floating around to make a difference, especially when you're sucking wind on a trail run. Combine that with a moderate pollen count, and anyone prone to seasonal sniffles or asthma might find themselves wheezing a bit more than usual.
