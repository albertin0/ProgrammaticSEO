### EXTREME HEAT WARNING IN EFFECT!
Seriously, Phoenix is under an Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM tonight, August 3rd. Yesterday saw record highs, and the forecast for today is still well over 100°F. This isn't "hot for Phoenix"; this is *dangerously hot*. Limit strenuous outdoor activity to the absolute earliest hours, if at all. Hydration is non-negotiable, but even then, your body can struggle to cool itself. Heat-related illnesses are no joke.

### Trail Closures on Popular Peaks!
Don't even think about hitting Camelback Mountain's Echo Canyon or Cholla trails between 8 AM and 5 PM today. The City of Phoenix's Trail Heat Safety Program has those, along with Piestewa Peak Summit and specific South Mountain trails, locked down. These closures are active through today. Save yourself the ticket and the potential emergency rescue.
</AlertBox>
