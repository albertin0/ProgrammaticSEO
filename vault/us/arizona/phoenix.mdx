## Local Hazards & Your "Brilliant" Workout Plans

Look, I get it. The temperature at 23.2°C (about 74°F) and overcast skies might *seem* like a break from the usual inferno. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a good day to hit the trails at, say, **South Mountain Park and Preserve**. The ground is likely to be slick and muddy, making those rocky climbs and descents extra treacherous. Plus, with a Flood Watch in effect, those desert washes you usually hop over could be raging torrents. Getting stranded or swept away is not a good look, nor is it good for your joints.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **FLOOD WATCH & SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS!** The National Weather Service has issued a Flood Watch for the Phoenix metro area until Thursday, 5 PM MST. Expect scattered thunderstorms throughout the day with risks of large hail, damaging winds, and flash flooding. Stay out of washes and low-lying areas!
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Slick Trails & Roads:** With a 75% chance of rain, trails, especially those with rocky or loose gravel sections, will be extremely slippery. Roads could also be slick, increasing the risk for cyclists and runners near traffic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Consider an Indoor Alternative:** Seriously, today's a great day to hit the gym, do some cross-training, or just enjoy a rest day. Your lungs, joints, and frankly, your life, will thank you.
</AlertBox>

There's also some partial street closures for utility work on 107th Ave between Highland and Roma Avenues, which might impact your neighborhood routes if you're out that way. Just another reason to maybe rethink the outdoor excursion.
