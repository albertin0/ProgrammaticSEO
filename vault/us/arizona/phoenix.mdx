## The Breakdown

It’s October, but Phoenix clearly didn't get the memo about autumn. At 38.7°C, we're staring down temperatures that scream "stay in your air-conditioned box." City officials have been pretty clear on this in the past, implementing trail closures on days with extreme heat warnings. These restrictions typically hit places like Camelback Mountain and parts of South Mountain Park, keeping you off the trails from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Ignoring these isn't just dumb, it puts our first responders at risk.

Then there's the air. A moderate AQI of 55 for PM2.5 is one thing, but throw in a PM10 High Pollution Advisory for the day, and you've got a recipe for irritated airways. Winds earlier today were strong enough to loft dust, and the ADEQ even raised its PM10 forecast because of it. And just for a little extra spice, there's a planned prescribed burn at Tres Rios Wetlands in southwest Phoenix, expected to create a noticeable smoke plume. So, you're looking at a mix of dust, fine particulates, and local smoke.
