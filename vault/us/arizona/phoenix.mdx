<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!
This isn't a suggestion; it's a direct threat to your well-being. The National Weather Service Phoenix issued an Extreme Heat Warning effective until August 21st. Your body is not designed to perform strenuous activity in 110+ degree weather. You *will* get heat exhaustion, and it can quickly escalate to heat stroke, which is a medical emergency. Seriously, stay indoors during peak heat.
</AlertBox>

Look, I get it. You want to get your miles in. But Phoenix summers are a different beast. That 91°F "morning" temp quickly becomes a deadly oven. Don't be that person rescuers have to carry down, soaking up valuable resources because you thought you were tougher than the Sonoran sun. Our first responders have enough to deal with without fishing dehydrated runners off the side of Piestewa Peak.

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Trail Restrictions in Full Effect
The City of Phoenix Trail Heat Safety Program has implemented mandatory closures on the most popular trails, including Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla trails) and Piestewa Peak's Summit Trail, from 8 AM to 5 PM. These aren't just polite suggestions; they're in place to save lives. Respect them. Also, a heads-up, Cholla Trail on Camelback is fully closing for construction *tomorrow*, August 17th, for a couple of weeks, so plan accordingly for future outings.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *must* move your body outside, do it before sunrise, keep it short, and stick to flat, shaded areas like around Papago Park, but even then, hydrate relentlessly. Seriously, hydrate until you float. And for the love of all that is holy, tell someone where you're going and when you expect to be back.

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Hydration is Non-Negotiable
Even if you're just walking to your car, you're losing fluids. For any outdoor activity, carry more water than you think you need. Dehydration can creep up fast and lead to serious heat-related injuries. Think about pre-hydrating too. It's not a magic bullet, but it helps.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, find an indoor gym today. Or just embrace the rest day. Your lungs and joints (and literally every other organ) will thank you.
