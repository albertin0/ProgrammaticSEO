Look, I'm usually the first to tell you to grab a treadmill when the air is thick enough to chew, but 23.7°C (that’s about 74.6°F for those still in the dark ages) and overcast skies? You won't see many of these days until November. Get out to South Mountain Park and Preserve, hit those lower trails, and enjoy the reprieve from the oppressive sun that’s usually trying to melt your face off by now.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Your lungs will be working double time. Even though the overall AQI is "Moderate" at 51 with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant, moderate pollen levels are piling on top of that. If you're sensitive to allergens or have any respiratory issues, consider scaling back your intensity or sticking to shorter routes. Phoenix air quality in general is often a struggle, with our metro area sometimes ranking pretty poorly for ozone pollution.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
The City of Phoenix Parks Board expanded its trail closure program to South Mountain Park and Preserve to include all trails when temperatures are forecast to exceed 105°F, with closures starting at 8 a.m. While today's temperature is far below that, keep this in mind as we inevitably creep towards our usual triple-digit inferno. Always check the city's parks website before heading out during the summer months. It's not just for your safety, it's for the first responders who have to haul your dehydrated carcass off a mountain.
</AlertBox>

As for wildfires, a few tiny blazes were spotted in the Phoenix Dispatch Area around April 12-14, but nothing major that's currently spewing smoke over the Valley and impacting our *already* moderate AQI. Still, this is Arizona, so keep an eye out; fire season usually ramps up about now.

Overall, today is a decent shot at enjoying the desert without feeling like you're actively battling the elements. Just don't forget the allergy meds and maybe keep that workout a little less "race day" and a little more "enjoy the weird weather."
