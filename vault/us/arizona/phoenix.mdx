### My Professional Advice (as someone who actually runs here)

Look, I get it. You want to get out there. But pushing through this kind of heat isn't tough, it's just plain stupid. Your body needs 7-14 days to properly acclimate to extreme heat, and that's *if* you're gradually exposing yourself. We're not even talking about "gradual" today, we're talking about a furnace.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Smart Moves for a Scorcher**
If you absolutely *must* move your body outdoors, do it before 6 AM or after 7 PM. Stay on flat, shaded routes, or hit the treadmill. Drink water constantly, even if you don't feel thirsty, and avoid sugary drinks, alcohol, and caffeine. Wear light-colored, loose clothing and a wide-brimmed hat. And for the love of all that is holy, know the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or excessively sweaty, stop, get to shade, and hydrate.
</AlertBox>

Don't be that person the Phoenix Fire Department has to rescue from Piestewa Peak because you thought you could "power through." Your health isn't worth bragging rights or a few extra miles.
