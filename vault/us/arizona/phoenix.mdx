## IMMEDIATE DANGER: Extreme Heat!
The National Weather Service has issued an **Extreme Heat Warning** for Maricopa County. Temperatures will be dangerously high. Outdoor exertion is highly discouraged, especially between 10 AM and 8 PM. Heat-related illnesses like heat cramps, heat exhaustion, and heat stroke are a real threat. If you *must* be outside, hydrate constantly, wear lightweight clothing, and seek shade immediately if you feel any symptoms.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
## Trail Closures in Effect
Don't be that person needing a rescue. Key trails, including those on **Camelback Mountain** (Echo Canyon and Cholla), Piestewa Peak, and specific routes at South Mountain Park and Preserve (Holbert, Mormon, Hau'pal Loop, and access to National Trail from Pima Canyon Trailhead), are **CLOSED from 8 AM to 5 PM**. These closures are a direct response to the excessive heat and are designed to keep you and our first responders safe. Find an air-conditioned gym, or stick to a cool pool.
</AlertBox>
