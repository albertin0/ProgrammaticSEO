## Why today is *not* the day to push it:

<BulletList>
*   **Extreme Heat Warning in Full Effect:** The National Weather Service has an Extreme Heat Warning plastered across Phoenix until 8 PM tonight. We're looking at afternoon temperatures hitting anywhere from 102 to 112 degrees Fahrenheit, making for a "Major Heat Risk." This isn't just uncomfortable; it's genuinely dangerous.

Due to this oppressive heat, popular spots like Camelback Mountain (both Echo Canyon and Cholla Trails) and the Piestewa Peak Summit Trail are shut down from 8 AM to 5 PM. An ASU hockey player recently landed in the hospital with exertional heat stroke after an outdoor workout in "roughly 90-degree temperatures" last month. We're talking 10-20 degrees hotter than that, easily, this afternoon. Your lungs and joints (and every other organ) will thank you for finding an alternative.

The Phoenix Trail Heat Safety Program is active. This means specific, high-exposure trails like the Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain are CLOSED from 8 AM to 5 PM today. Hiking is PROHIBITED. Do not ignore these closures. Park rangers are out there for a reason, and they don't appreciate having to rescue people who thought they knew better.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Extreme Heat = Extreme Danger
Temperatures are predicted to be between 102-112°F this afternoon. This is not just hot, it's dangerously hot. Heat-related illnesses like cramps, exhaustion, and stroke are very real risks. If you must be outside, keep it to the early morning or late evening.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydration, Hydration, Hydration (and Shade)
If you absolutely *have* to get moving outdoors today, do it before the sun really gets cooking. We're talking pre-7 AM, or wait until after 7 PM. Stay on flat, shaded routes if possible, carry way more water than you think you need, and don't be afraid to cut your workout short. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing. Your personal record isn't worth a trip to St. Joe's.
