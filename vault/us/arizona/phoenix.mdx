## Why today is decidedly *not* the day:

<BulletList>
  - **Record-Shattering Heat:** Phoenix is staring down an unprecedented March heat wave, with forecast highs hitting 104-108°F today, Friday, March 20th, potentially breaking records by a ridiculous 10 degrees.   - **Trail Closures & Rescues:** Several popular trails, including the infamous Echo Canyon and Cholla trails on Camelback Mountain, are **closed** from 8 AM to 5 PM. The Fire Department has already been busy with multiple heat-related rescues this week. Don't make them add you to the tally.
  - **"Moderate" Air Quality & Pollen:** On top of everything else, the air quality isn't doing you any favors. ### Phoenix Heat: It's Not Even Summer Yet

I've lived and run here long enough to know when the desert is trying to kill you, and this week, it's pulling out all the stops, even for March. We're talking June/July temperatures in late March, which means your body hasn't had a chance to acclimate. That "cool" 77°F this morning at midnight quickly ramps up to triple digits by late morning, and it stays there. Tourists, especially, get caught off guard, thinking "dry heat" means it's fine. It's not.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Extreme Heat Warning in Effect!** Do not hike popular trails between 8 AM and 5 PM. Many are officially closed, and the city *strongly discourages* all other outdoor activity during these hours. The Phoenix Fire Department is already responding to heat-related emergencies.
</AlertBox>

### So, what *can* you do?

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate:** If you absolutely *must* be outside, do it before 8 AM or after 5 PM, but even then, temperatures will still be high. Drink way more water than you think you need. Seriously, gallons.
