## Today's Real Talk:

Look, I love hitting the trails as much as the next masochist, but today is not the day to prove anything to yourself or anyone else. We've got an **Extreme Heat Warning** slapping us in the face until at least Saturday evening, with afternoon temperatures projected to soar into the high 100s. And let's not forget how little relief we're getting after dark, with overnight lows still hanging out in the 90s. Your body simply cannot recover and cool down adequately in these conditions. Heat stroke isn't a joke, and our emergency services are already stretched thin dealing with folks who underestimated the desert.

**Extreme Heat Warning in Effect:** The National Weather Service has issued an Extreme Heat Warning for the Phoenix area until 8 PM MST on Saturday, August 29th. On top of the inferno, we also have an **Ozone High Pollution Advisory** for Monday. While the general AQI might be sitting at "Moderate" from PM2.5, the *ozone* levels are forecast to be unhealthy for many. Ozone is a lung irritant, especially for kids, older adults, and anyone with respiratory issues like asthma. So, even if you manage to drag yourself out before the sun gets too high, you're still sucking down lung-damaging air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Ozone High Pollution Advisory:** An Ozone High Pollution Advisory is in effect for Maricopa County today, August 24th, with ozone levels expected to exceed federal health standards (forecast AQI 112). Limit prolonged outdoor exertion, especially if you have respiratory sensitivities.

And guess what? City officials are actually making smart decisions for once. Extreme heat warnings trigger automatic daytime closures of trails at places like **South Mountain Park and Preserve**, Camelback Mountain, and Piestewa Peak between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. This isn't to inconvenience you; it's to prevent yet another heat rescue that puts both hikers and first responders at risk. We've already had emergency crews responding to heat-related distress calls on South Mountain's Pima Canyon Trail this week.
