<AlertBox type="danger">
## Trail Closures on Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak!
Don't even think about hitting the popular trails during the hottest part of the day. The City of Phoenix has a standing policy to close trails like those on Camelback Mountain (Echo Canyon and Cholla Trails) and the Piestewa Peak Summit trail from 8 AM to 5 PM when Extreme Heat Warnings are in effect. Given today's forecast, assume these are locked down. Trying to "power through" is how you end up in a helicopter, dehydrating our first responders.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Breathe Deeply (Seriously)
With the Ozone High Pollution Advisory, children, active adults, and anyone with respiratory issues should seriously restrict outdoor activities. If you absolutely *must* be outside, keep it short, keep it easy, and listen to your lungs. This isn't the day to push it. Wildfire smoke isn't an issue, thankfully, but that ground-level ozone is.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate or Die-drate (Seriously)
If you're out there, even for a short urban jog, guzzle water. Avoid sugary drinks, alcohol, and caffeine. Carry more water than you think you need. Your body is already working overtime just trying to keep you from spontaneously combusting.
</AlertBox>
