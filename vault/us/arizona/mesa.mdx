### DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!

Seriously, do not mess with 108°F. The National Weather Service is forecasting highs around this mark, and cities like Mesa consistently advise extreme caution. If you must be outside, finish your activity by 7 AM. After that, the pavement on places like the Arizona Canal Trail will start cooking, and you'll be cooking along with it. <AlertBox type="warning">
### WARNING: Watch Your Lungs and the Landscape

While an AQI of 57 is "Moderate," it's on the higher side of moderate and still means sensitive groups might experience symptoms from prolonged exposure. Combine that with the "Extreme Risk of Fire", and it means dust and potential smoke are real concerns. Even if there isn't an active fire nearby causing visible smoke, the conditions are ripe for one to start and spread quickly. If you're out on the trails at Usery Mountain Regional Park, be extra vigilant for any signs of smoke and check local alerts before you head out.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Tip: Hydration is Your Only Friend Today

You need to be a camel today, folks. Hydrate before, during, and after any outdoor activity. Don't wait until you're thirsty – by then, you're already behind. Councilmember Alicia Goforth's newsletter also reminds us to check on seniors, neighbors, and anyone vulnerable during this extreme heat. Consider an indoor workout at a gym or one of Mesa's many recreation centers instead. Your body will thank you.
