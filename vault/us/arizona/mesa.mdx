## Why today is/isn't the day

<BulletList>
*   Today's "pleasant" early morning temperature of 26.9°C (80.4°F) is a straight-up mirage. The National Weather Service expects temperatures to be near normal for the weekend, but AccuWeather is still forecasting a high of 102°F (39°C) for July 19th. So, unless you're a lizard, expect the mercury to climb well past comfortable, so get out *early* or wait until after sunset, and hydrate like your life depends on it – because out here, it just might.
*   Your lungs might have an opinion about today too. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a "Moderate" 57, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Add a moderate pollen count to that, and it's not exactly prime breathing air, especially if you're sensitive.
*   If your route takes you along the Eastern Canal Shared Use Path, be aware of ongoing construction. While one section (Brown Road to Broadway Road) was slated for completion around now, other parts are still gearing up for or undergoing work. Expect potential detours, uneven surfaces, or recently finished sections that might still be a bit rough.

</BulletList>

Keep an eye out for construction equipment, unfinished surfaces, and unexpected reroutes. Your ankles will thank you.

Always be ready for sudden changes in weather if you're hitting the trails.
