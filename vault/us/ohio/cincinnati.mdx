## Seriously, It's a Sauna Out There

Look, I love pounding the pavement as much as the next runner, but some days, Mother Nature actively tries to take us out. Today is one of those days. The National Weather Service has slapped us with a Heat Advisory for Hamilton County, effective from this afternoon through Wednesday evening. We're looking at actual temperatures climbing into the mid-90s, and with the humidity, that "feels like" temperature, or heat index, could be hitting a disgusting 104°F.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Advisory!** A Heat Advisory means the combination of hot temperatures and high humidity will create a situation in which heat illnesses are possible. For real. Don't mess with this. Slow down, stay hydrated, or better yet, just reschedule that long run for another day.
</AlertBox>

Running the exposed stretches of the **Lunken Airport Trail** today would be a special kind of masochism. There's almost no shade out there, and that heat will radiate off the asphalt like a grill. If you *must* get out, stick to early morning, find some serious tree cover, and keep it short. Hydrate like it's your job, and I'm not talking about that fancy electrolyte drink you saw on Instagram. Water. Lots of it.

### Your Lungs Might Be Feeling It Too

The air quality is a bit of a mixed bag, officially sitting at an AQI of 53, which is "Moderate." However, some forecasts are leaning towards "Poor" and warning that it's "unhealthy for sensitive groups." The dominant pollutant floating around is Ozone or "Other," whatever that means for your particular respiratory system. If you're someone who feels chest tightness or irritation when the air gets thick, consider this your permission slip to bail on outdoor cardio. It’s not worth triggering an asthma attack for a Strava segment.

### Local Obstacles (Minor League Edition)

Beyond the heat, there are a few local snags. Over at the **Cincinnati Nature Center**, the Fox Rock overlook is still temporarily closed due to maintenance. And if you're a fan of **Burnet Woods**, a lower section of the Bard Owl Trail is closed because of some serious sinkholes. The valley in Burnet Woods is also prone to flooding from Combined Sewer Overflows after rain, so keep an eye out for closure signs there. These aren't huge deal-breakers for most, but good to know if they're on your regular route.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip for the Week:** With this heat advisory sticking around, consider moving your workouts to the early morning hours (like, before 7 AM) or late evening (after 8 PM). Even then, keep an eye on the heat index and listen to your body. Or just hit the gym. No shame in the air-conditioned game.
</AlertBox>
