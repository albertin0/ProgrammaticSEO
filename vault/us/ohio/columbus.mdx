### Hydration is Your Best Friend, Not an Afterthought

Seriously, I see too many of you out there looking like you're about to spontaneously combust. It's March, but it feels like June out there. Your body isn't used to this kind of heat this early in the season, so it's going to work harder to cool itself down. If you're planning a long run along the **Olentangy Trail**, don't just carry water, *pre-hydrate*. Drink up before you even step out, and keep sipping throughout your workout. And for the love of all that is holy, if you start feeling light-headed or dizzy, cut it short. It's not weakness, it's common sense. No PR is worth a heat-related illness.

### Wet Trails & Hidden Hazards

The heavy rainfall earlier this week, with some areas seeing up to three inches, has left our local waterways and low-lying areas swollen. While the official "Flood Watch" for central and southwest Ohio expired yesterday, the residual effects are still present. Even if your favorite section of the Scioto Trail isn't completely submerged, you're likely to encounter standing water, mud, and hidden debris. A minor flood warning for the Hocking River area might be a bit south, but it gives you an idea of how much water is still moving through the region.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Conditions Could Be Sketchy.** Be prepared for muddy, slick surfaces and potential debris on shared-use paths, especially near riverbanks and underpasses. You might even find some sections closed if crews are still assessing safety from the recent rains. Best to check the Columbus Recreation and Parks Department alerts before you go.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Congestion

Speaking of the Olentangy and Scioto Trails, listen up: major improvement projects are planned. While full construction isn't kicking off until summer, they're in the planning and public comment phase right now. This means crews are likely doing initial assessments, and you might see some preliminary activity. Beyond that, the Heritage Trail in Hilliard has AEP crews clearing powerline easements, meaning equipment and flaggers. Several Metro Parks are also undergoing construction on playgrounds and boat launches, which, while not directly impacting trails, can mean localized closures or hazards you need to avoid.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route (and an Escape Route).** With various events happening today – from Metro Parks walks to a "Pop-Up Protest" in Clintonville near North High Street and East North Broadway – some areas might be busier or have unexpected detours. Don't just blindly follow your usual path; be ready to reroute.
</AlertBox>

### Listen to Your Body, Not Just Your Playlist

I'm a PT, remember? So let me drop some knowledge: "No pain, no gain" is the dumbest mantra out there if you don't understand the difference between effort and injury. Pushing through persistent soreness or sharp pains is how you end up in my office. Today, with the heat, the humidity, and potentially uneven ground, you're at a higher risk for joint sprains or muscle strains. Ease into it, keep an eye on your footing, and hydrate, hydrate, hydrate. If something feels off, back off. There's always tomorrow.
