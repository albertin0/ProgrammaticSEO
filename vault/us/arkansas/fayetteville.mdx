That's practically a spa day for your respiratory system. So, lace up, grab your water bottle, and hit the pavement or the dirt.

But before you sprint out like a spring chicken, let's talk about the hangover from yesterday's weather. The western half of Arkansas, including our neck of the woods, had scattered severe thunderstorms roll through late Friday night. While Fayetteville itself might not have gotten hammered, widespread rainfall is expected across the state over the next week, and Southwest Arkansas was looking at 4-6 inches of rain through March 12.

Don't be "that guy" who tries to power through a flooded section and ends up needing a rescue or, even worse, tracking mud all over the paved path. Respect the trails.
