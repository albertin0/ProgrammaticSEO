### Air Quality & Allergies

Your lungs are getting a moderate workout just breathing this air. Our AQI is sitting at a "Fair" 60, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most of you, it's probably fine, but if you're one of the "sensitive groups" (you know who you are, wheezers and sneezers), you might experience some minor irritation. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and it's a double whammy for your respiratory system. Don't be surprised if you're sniffing like a bloodhound by the end of your route.

If you're heading out for a longer effort, especially in the afternoon or evening when the precipitation chances are highest, consider bringing a hat or visor to keep the wet stuff out of your eyes. And maybe a dry shirt for when you finish.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Local Hazards

Remember that Rochester Trail Restoration Project on the Erie Canalway Trail between Lyell Ave and Buffalo Road? Yeah, that's still a full closure with a marked detour. Don't be "that guy" trying to sneak through it. It's closed for a reason, likely involving heavy machinery and a complete lack of trail. Use the detour, or find a different path entirely.

And speaking of detours, construction on the Genesee Riverway Trail, specifically related to the Vacuum Oil Brownfield Cleanup Projects between Violetta Street and South Plymouth Avenue, means you'll hit some snags. Expect temporary closures and increased construction traffic. Pay attention to signs. Don't rely on muscle memory right now; things change.
