<AlertBox type="danger">
### Extreme Heat Warning!
Seriously, avoid peak sun hours (10 AM - 4 PM) entirely. If you MUST be outside, keep it short, stay in the shade, and hydrate like your life depends on it (because it kind of does). We're talking 35°C/95°F heat with high humidity. Heatstroke is a medical emergency – know the signs: confusion, dizziness, nausea, hot/red/dry skin, or a very high body temperature. Call 911 if you suspect it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality Alert for Ozone!
With elevated ozone levels, strenuous outdoor activity is highly discouraged, especially this afternoon. If you're in a sensitive group, limit your time outside. Even if you're not, listen to your body and cut your workout short if you feel any discomfort or difficulty breathing. This isn't the day to push it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Stay Smart, Stay Safe
Consider moving your workout indoors today. Hit the gym, find a cool community center, or crank up that fan for some at-home bodyweight circuits. If you absolutely can't resist the outdoors, go during the very early morning before the heat and ozone have fully built up. Drink more water than you think you need, wear light, loose clothing, and don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>
