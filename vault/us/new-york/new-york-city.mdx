## The Air: Moderate Means "Mind Your Business" (Your Lungs' Business, That Is)

Your fancy health apps might tell you the AQI is 53 and that's "moderate," and technically, they're not wrong. For most of us iron lungs, it’s fine for a run, but if you've got asthma or any other respiratory quirks, you're in the "sensitive group" today. That means prolonged exertion could lead to some minor-to-moderate symptoms. We're not in Canadian wildfire territory like we were last year, thankfully, but it's not pristine mountain air either. The dominant pollutant is PM2.5, those tiny particles that love to get all up in your lungs. Pay attention to how you feel, and don't push through any discomfort.

## The Roads & Trails: Dodge the Diggers, Detours, and Dedicated Dashers

The weather's overcast and a pleasant 21.5°C (around 71°F), so physically, your joints will be happy. No crazy winds or unexpected downpours forecasted for today, which is a win in itself. However, navigating the city for a workout is, as always, a logistical puzzle.

The **Hudson River Park** segment of the Empire State Trail, specifically between W. 29th and W. 30th Street, still has that Manhattan Tunnel Project work going on. The bikeway and walkway are narrowed, and you might encounter construction trucks crossing. Keep your head up and eyes peeled; those workers aren't looking for PRs, they're moving heavy equipment.

And if you were hoping for a pristine track workout in Brooklyn, tough luck. The **Red Hook Track** is still under construction, delayed by a defaulting contractor and some gnarly Department of Environmental Protection stormwater line issues. They're hoping for "late summer 2026" completion, which, let's be real, in NYC construction time, means "eventually."

<AlertBox type="warning">
  The NYC DOT has a construction embargo in effect through Sunday, June 28th, for the **NYC Pride March** in specific Manhattan areas (around 5th Ave between 33rd & 25th St). While this means less construction *in those areas*, expect significant road closures and crowds if your route takes you anywhere near the march. Plan alternate routes or prepare for major detours.
</AlertBox>

On the flip side, if you're looking for some organized fun, the **NYRR Open Run: Shore Road Park** has a free 5K this morning in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, from 8:45 AM to 11:00 AM. It's a community run, so expect company and a good vibe if that's your scene.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  This city is a "Gridlock Alert Day" today for a World Cup match (Panama vs. England), meaning heavy traffic in Midtown and around MetLife Stadium. If you're driving to a running spot, factor in serious delays. Public transport is always your best bet on these days, but even that can get bogged down.
</AlertBox>

Finally, a heads-up for next week: a "major" to "extreme" heat risk is setting up for the eastern US, including NYC, with "feels-like" temperatures possibly hitting 100-110 degrees. Today's overcast skies and 70s are a pleasant anomaly. Start hydrating now, because next week is going to be brutal.
