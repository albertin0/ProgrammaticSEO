Alright, listen up, New Yorkers. Today's not terrible for a sweat session, but it's definitely not a "set a new PR" kind of day. Expect to be battling the elements and some serious event-related headaches.

<BulletList>
-   **Gusty Winds:** Don't let the partly cloudy skies fool you. There are some strong, gusty winds whipping through the city today, particularly later on, that'll make that 13.2°C feel a lot colder than it is.
-   **NYC Half Marathon Mayhem:** The United Airlines NYC Half Marathon is this weekend, and if you thought the city waits until race day to shut things down, you clearly haven't lived here long enough. Construction embargoes and course prep are in full swing, especially in major running hubs.
-   **Moderate Air Quality:** The AQI is clocking in at 58, which is "moderate" with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Mix that with moderate pollen levels, and anyone with sensitive lungs or allergies might feel it.
</BulletList>

You know I'm usually all about hitting those miles, but today demands a bit of caution, especially if your usual route takes you through prime race real estate.

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Avoid the NYC Half Marathon Route in Central Park

Seriously, stay away. The United Airlines NYC Half Marathon course is under a full construction embargo from Friday, March 6th through Sunday, March 15th. This means the East Drive and West Drive in Central Park, along with other sections of the route, are likely to be a mess with setup, barriers, and crews. Expect restricted access, detours, and general chaos. Trying to run your usual loop through Central Park today will be a frustrating exercise in dodging cones and irritated race staff.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Wind, Especially on Bridges and Waterfronts

While the severe thunderstorms from yesterday are mostly clearing out, today's going to be "very cold and windy" with northwest winds reaching up to 30 mph, and gusts potentially hitting 35-40 mph, especially out on Long Island. That means exposed areas like the Brooklyn Bridge pedestrian path, the Hudson River Greenway, or the Shore Road Promenade in Bay Ridge are going to be a wind tunnel. If you're out there, brace yourself, hold onto your hat, and watch out for any stray debris.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Moderate Pollen & AQI – Know Your Body

The air quality is moderate, and we've got moderate pollen levels in the mix too. For most of you, this is just background noise, but if you're prone to seasonal allergies or have any respiratory sensitivities, be smart. Maybe opt for an indoor workout or keep your outdoor efforts shorter than usual. There's no medal for pushing through an asthma attack.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're determined to get out there, pick your battleground wisely. Stick to less exposed, less congested routes, be aware of your surroundings, and don't be a hero trying to run through a closed-off race course.
