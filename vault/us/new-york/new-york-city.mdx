## Local Hazard Breakdown

**Hudson River Park Greenway / Empire State Trail Detour**

If your usual route takes you along the west side, specifically the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail, be aware of ongoing "Manhattan Tunnel Project" work. Between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street, the bikeway and walkway are significantly narrowed. Expect construction trucks to be crossing the trail regularly. This project kicked off in March and is slated to run for about six months. Don't just zone out listening to your podcast; keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: Hudson River Park Detour!**
The Empire State Trail between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets in Hudson River Park is narrowed due to construction. Watch out for reduced path width and heavy machinery crossing the trail.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Allergy Alert: Moderate Pollen**
If you suffer from seasonal allergies, today's moderate pollen count could trigger symptoms. Consider taking antihistamines before heading out and maybe keep your workouts to shorter durations or lower intensity if you're feeling particularly sensitive.
</AlertBox>
