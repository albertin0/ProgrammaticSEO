## Today's Outdoor Workout Reality Check: Don't Be a Hero

Look, if you absolutely *must* get outside, make it quick and stay hyper-aware of the skies. We've got a "First Alert Weather Day" brewing, which is code for "don't even think about a long run when you could get swept into a storm drain."

The forecast calls for scattered thunderstorms, isolated downpours, and scattered flash flooding throughout the day, with a 35% chance of rain during daylight hours that jumps to a 65% chance tonight. That's not "a little sprinkle," folks. That's "stay home and foam roll" weather.
