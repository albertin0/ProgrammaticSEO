### Today's Rundown

Today, August 21, 2026, is a pretty solid day to lace up. That overcast sky means you won't be squinting into the sun or worrying about heat stroke, unlike a few weeks back when Mayor Mamdani was practically begging everyone to stay indoors during the July heatwaves. We're looking at prime running temperatures that won't make your lungs feel like they're working overtime just to keep you upright.

The air quality, while not sparkling clean, is far from "unhealthy." A moderate 55 AQI means most of you won't notice a thing. If you're an asthmatic or have sensitive lungs, you might feel a slight tickle from the ozone, so listen to your body, as always. But honestly, compared to what we've seen this summer, this is a breath of fresh air—literally.

Pollen is at a moderate level, so if you're one of the unfortunate souls who turns into a snotty, watery-eyed mess, pop an antihistamine before you head out. It's not going to ruin your run, but it might make you regret that post-run outdoor coffee.
