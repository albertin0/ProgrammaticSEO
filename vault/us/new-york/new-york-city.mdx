### Today's Rundown: Fog, Gusts, and Dodgy Footing

Look, the AQI is holding steady at a "Good" 45 today, so your lungs are mostly in the clear. But let's be real, that's about the only "good" news on the environmental front. We're starting the day shrouded in fog, particularly around the East River and the harbor. Early birds, consider yourselves warned: visibility will be low, making those morning miles a gamble. Don't be surprised if your usual Central Park loop feels less like a serene escape and more like a game of Marco Polo with other runners.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Coastal Flooding Advisory!** Minor coastal flooding is expected this morning along many shorelines due to high tides. If your route typically hugs the water, especially around areas like the Hudson River Park Greenway, expect some standing water. Move your car if you parked in one of those low-lying waterfront spots overnight.

By afternoon, that fog might lift, but don't get too excited. We're looking at a 65% chance of rain. Coupled with northwest winds gusting 20-25 mph, you'll be battling not just precipitation, but a solid headwind too. ### Trail Updates & City Obstacles

If you're planning a run along the Hudson River Park, be aware of ongoing construction from the Manhattan Tunnel Project. Between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets, the bikeway and walkway are narrowed and shifted. Expect construction trucks and general chaos. This isn't a new issue, but it's a persistent annoyance for anyone trying to get some uninterrupted mileage in.

<AlertBox type="info">
