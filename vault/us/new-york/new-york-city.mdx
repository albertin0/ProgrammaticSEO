## Today's Vibe: Wet and Wild (Not the Fun Kind)

Look, I love hitting the pavement as much as the next maniac, but today is a hard pass for anything longer than a quick dash to the bodega. We're talking a 70-75% chance of rain all day and night, with temperatures barely crawling into the high 40s (around 7.6°C if you're keeping track). That's not "brisk," that's "miserable and a recipe for chafing." The CBS News New York First Alert Weather Team has even issued "First Alert Weather Days" for the weekend, emphasizing the rain. So, unless your idea of a good time involves shivering through every mile, maybe consider the treadmill. Yes, I said it. The T-word. Sometimes you gotta know when to fold 'em.

The good news, if you can call it that, is our Air Quality Index is a breezy 35, which is firmly in the "Good" category. So, you won't be choking on smog, just rain. Small victories, right?

<AlertBox type="danger">
  ### Rain, Rain, Go Away (Seriously)
  Today's forecast calls for heavy rain throughout the day. Wet conditions mean slippery surfaces on sidewalks, trails, and especially stairs (looking at you, *Brooklyn Bridge* pedestrian path). Visibility will be low, and hypothermia is a real risk in these chilly, damp conditions. Dress in layers, wear waterproof gear, and seriously consider postponing that long run.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
  ### Watch for Unexpected Closures and Events
  Today is Car-Free Earth Day, meaning some streets will be closed for festivities. Specifically, areas like East 188th Street in Fordham Heights will be hosting events and performances. The East River Esplanade is reportedly closed between East 93rd and East 94th Streets, which is a perennial pain in the ass for East Side runners. Also, yesterday morning, the Van Cortlandt Track was locked up, so check local park alerts before you head out. Don't assume your usual route is clear.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  ### Gear Up, Or Don't Go Out
  If you absolutely *must* get your miles in, invest in proper waterproof running gear: jacket, hat, gloves, and shoes with good traction. Reflective clothing is also a must with the overcast skies and reduced visibility. And for the love of all that is holy, tell someone where you're going and when you expect to be back.
</AlertBox>
