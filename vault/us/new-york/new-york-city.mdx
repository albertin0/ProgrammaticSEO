### Smoke Advisory in Effect!
Seriously, folks, the wildfire smoke from New Jersey is no joke. The Air Quality Health Advisory specifically warns that PM2.5 can be harmful, particularly for children, seniors, and those with asthma or heart/lung conditions. Health officials are urging sensitive groups to limit outdoor time and avoid strenuous activity. Even if you're not in a "sensitive group," those tiny particles can irritate your respiratory system. Consider an indoor workout today, or at least keep it light and short around Central Park, and pay attention to how you feel.
</AlertBox>
