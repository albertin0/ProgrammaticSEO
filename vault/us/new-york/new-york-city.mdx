Alright, listen up, runners. Today, April 5th, is one of those days where the weather gods aren't actively trying to kill you, which for New York City, is practically a miracle. Six degrees Celsius and overcast means no scorching sun beating down and no ice patches trying to trip you into an ER visit. It’s actually pretty solid for a long run or getting some speed work in without turning into a sweaty mess.

The AQI is sitting pretty at 37, which is "Good," so your lungs won't be doing extra duty filtering out god-knows-what from the city air. However, those of us with sniffles know that "Moderate" pollen is still enough to make you feel like you've run through a field of sneezes. So, if you're prone to seasonal allergies, don't be a hero; take your antihistamine. Your post-run shower will thank you, too.

Now, for the actual *hazards* – because it wouldn't be New York without some urban obstacle course action. If you were planning a leisurely trot over to the American Veteran Memorial Pier area today, especially near the Verrazano Bridge, you might want to rethink your route. The **Verrazano 10K & 5K Races** are happening, kicking off at 9:00 AM. That means roads around there are going to be a nightmare for anyone not wearing a race bib. Don't be "that guy" trying to weave through thousands of runners; find another path or cheer them on. Seriously, no one wants to dodge your clueless sprint when they’re trying to PR. There's also an NYRR Open Run 5K weekly event happening in Cunningham Park if you're in Queens. Keep an eye out for smaller crowds there too.
