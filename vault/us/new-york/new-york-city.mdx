## Today's Urban Gauntlet & Regional Headaches

Look, it's New York. There's always *something*. While the skies might be smiling on your run, getting to your favorite spot might be another story.

### Roadblocks & Detours

<AlertBox type="warning">
Travelers, especially those trying to escape Manhattan or Brooklyn via major crossings, listen up: The Port Authority has lane closures and construction affecting your commute. Specifically, the south tube of the Holland Tunnel is closed today, March 27. Expect delays around the George Washington Bridge and Lincoln Tunnel too. So, if your workout involves getting across the river, plan on extra time or just run in your borough, you lazy bum.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Talk: Mind the Rocks!

If you're thinking of a longer haul out of the city for some real trail running, remember that the Giant Stairs Section in Palisades Interstate Park has been closed since January due to a major rockfall. It's still closed. Don't go trying to become a search-and-rescue statistic because you thought you could "just squeeze by." It's not worth the broken ankle, trust me. There are plenty of other trails; use your head.

<AlertBox type="info">
With moderate pollen floating around, if you're hitting spots like Prospect Park or Central Park, consider wearing sunglasses to keep the allergens out of your eyes. And maybe rinse your gear and yourself when you get home; no need to bring the sneeze-factory indoors.
</AlertBox>
