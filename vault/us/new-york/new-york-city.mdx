### Construction Zone: The NYC Runner's Natural Habitat

Seriously, is anything ever *not* under construction in this city? We've got a couple of major headaches for runners to keep in mind:

<AlertBox type="danger">
### East River Greenway: Still a Hot Mess
The East Side Coastal Resiliency (ESCR) project means the **East River Greenway** between Montgomery Street and Stuyvesant Cove Park is completely closed, 24/7. If you usually run this stretch, you need to find an alternative. There's also ongoing overnight work in the northern section between E. Houston Street and E. 14th Street that can be loud and disruptive. Don't expect a peaceful waterfront stride there.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Hudson River Park Detour
If you prefer the west side, be aware that the Manhattan Tunnel Project kicked off in March and has narrowed sections of the **Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail** between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street. You'll encounter construction trucks crossing the trail, so keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>
