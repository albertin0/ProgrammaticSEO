### What's in the Air?

The air quality index (AQI) is sitting at a moderate 58, which is generally acceptable. However, it’s still PM2.5 that's the dominant pollutant, meaning those fine particulate matters from vehicle emissions are floating around. A recent city report, in its infinite wisdom, confirmed that congestion pricing hasn't exactly scrubbed the air clean, with traffic still a notable contributor to PM2.5 levels. If you've got asthma or other respiratory issues, "acceptable" might still mean "a bit annoying."
