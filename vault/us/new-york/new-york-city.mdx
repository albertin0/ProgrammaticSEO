### Trail Troubles & Urban Obstacles

If your usual route involves the **Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail** between W. 29th and W. 30th Street, prepare for some real estate adjustment. Construction for the Manhattan Tunnel Project means the bikeway and walkway are both narrowed, and you might even have to dodge some construction trucks. This isn't a quick fix either; expect these detours to be in place for about six months. It's enough to make you wish you had a personal drone to scout your path.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Heads up, waterfront runners! That construction along the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail means narrowed paths and potential construction vehicle crossings. Stay alert and watch your step.
</AlertBox>

General construction is always a thing in this city, and with over 1,500 Department of Buildings violations issued in the first two weeks of March, it's clear there's plenty of active work out there. So, expect the usual urban obstacle course, even if it's not a named "hazard."

<AlertBox type="danger">

Bottom line? Today isn't a write-off, but it's not a day for setting PRs unless you're an actual polar bear with no allergies. Be smart, stay safe, and maybe stick to laps around your living room if the wind sounds too much like a banshee.
