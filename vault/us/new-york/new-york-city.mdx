### Construction Chaos & Trail Trouble

It wouldn't be New York without some major disruptions. If your route takes you anywhere near the Lower East Side, specifically around **Corlears Hook Park**, you're gonna hit a snag. The Parallel Conveyance project is still going strong, meaning sewer installations and related construction are causing "intermittent roadway closures, traffic detours, and parking limitations" on Jackson Street between South and Water Streets, affecting the southern limits of the park. Don't assume your usual path is clear there.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Runners and cyclists on the Hudson River Greenway: be advised that the bike path is *still* temporarily re-routed around W 29th and W 30th Streets due to the Gateway Tunnel Project. Stick to the marked detours and stay off the pedestrian walkway. We don't need any more human-bike collisions.
</AlertBox>

Over in the Bronx, if you're planning a lunch-hour jog around 625 Brook Ave, prepare for a slowdown. While not a direct threat to your workout, it could mean localized crowds and traffic, so plan accordingly.

If you're an extreme early bird or a late-night pavement pounder, be aware of some overnight lane closures on the George Washington Bridge ramps connecting to the Henry Hudson Parkway, mostly between midnight and 5:30 AM.

### Final Word

Temperatures are perfect, so enjoy the breeze, but stay aware of your surroundings. Check for local street closures before you head out, hydrate, and maybe grab a tissue for that moderate pollen count. And seriously, don't bike on the walkway.
