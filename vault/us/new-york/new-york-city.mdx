### Your NYC Workout Today

Look, if you absolutely *must* get out there, I get it. But don't be a hero. Yesterday's weather left its mark. Those strong winds and heavy downpours mean more than just a damp path. You're looking at potential debris on sidewalks and even some downed branches in parks like Central Park. Watch your step, particularly if you're taking the bridle paths. That mud can hide all sorts of ankle-twisting surprises.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **Wind Chill is Real:** Even if the wind isn't as fierce as yesterday's 50 mph gusts, that 30°F "feels like" temperature is no joke. Exposed skin is going to get cold fast. Consider a windbreaker and cover your ears. No one wants an ear infection from a "brisk run."
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Trail Conditions are Suspect:** If your usual route involves anything unpaved, like sections of Van Cortlandt Park or even the less-trodden paths in Prospect Park, assume it's wet and muddy. Plan for extra traction or just stick to the sidewalks today. Your shoes (and your laundromat bill) will thank you.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
  **Watch for Debris:** Post-storm, high-wind conditions always mean unexpected obstacles. Keep your eyes peeled for fallen twigs, branches, and even displaced trash. A twisted ankle because you weren't looking down is a rookie mistake. Don't be that person.
</AlertBox>
