## The Rundown: May 31, 2026

So, it's a Sunday morning, and typically, you'd expect to be sweating through your shirt by now. But no, Mother Nature decided we needed a little throwback to early spring. The good news? The air is pristine, which is always a relief after some of the gunk we've breathed in this city. An AQI of 26 is practically a unicorn. Seriously, get out there and appreciate that clean air while it lasts. Your lungs will thank you for skipping the treadmill today.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress in layers.** Seriously, for 7.5°C, you'll want a base layer, maybe a light long-sleeve, and definitely a windbreaker if you're running along the waterfront or anywhere exposed. Don't forget gloves and a headband if you're prone to cold ears. It's not February, but it's not beach weather either.
</AlertBox>

Now, about those joints. When the temperature drops unexpectedly, your muscles and connective tissues can be a bit stiffer. Don't just blast out the door and expect a PR on the **Central Park Loop** without some serious dynamic stretching first. We're talking leg swings, high knees, butt kicks – the whole nine yards. A proper warm-up isn't just for avoiding injury; it's for actually enjoying your run instead of feeling like a rusty robot. If you're planning a long one, consider starting with a brisk walk to get the blood flowing before you ramp up the pace.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Pollen on the Prowl:** "Moderate" pollen might not sound alarming, but for those with seasonal allergies, it's enough to trigger symptoms. If you typically react, take your antihistamines *before* you head out. A runny nose and itchy eyes can turn a good run into a miserable slog, especially when you're trying to breathe deeply. Don't push through it and regret it later.
</AlertBox>

There aren't any reports of surprise construction blocking off the West Side Highway path or any last-minute street closures for obscure parades today. So, your usual routes should be clear. Just watch out for the usual weekend warrior crowd if you're heading to popular spots. Overall, it's a decent day for a run, provided you respect the chill and prep for the pollen.
