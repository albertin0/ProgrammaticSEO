## The Breakdown

### Weather Woes (and Wins)

Right now, it's pretty decent out there. We've got a comfortable 16.9°C (that's about 62.4°F for you non-metric types) and clear skies. Perfect for a lunchtime jog or an afternoon stroll through Central Park. But don't get too comfortable. NYC Emergency Management is warning about strong thunderstorms hitting the city this evening, bringing heavy rain and wind gusts that could snap tree branches. If you're planning a run or ride after work, pivot to the gym, or you'll regret it. The worst of it is expected between 6 PM and 9 PM, with western parts of the city seeing the highest chance of severe weather. And if you thought Saturday morning would be a fresh start, think again; expect lingering drizzle and gusty northwest winds up to 35 mph, plus much cooler temps.

**Evening Storms inbound!** Strong thunderstorms with wind gusts of 40-60 mph are forecast for Friday evening (6 PM - 9 PM). Stay indoors during the storm to avoid falling branches and localized ponding from clogged storm drains.

### Obstacle Course NYC

If you're eyeing a long run this weekend, let me be blunt: good luck. The TCS New York City Marathon is on Sunday, November 1st, and the city is already gearing up for it. This means extensive road and bridge closures starting *tomorrow*, Saturday, October 31st, and running through Sunday. Forget your usual routes through Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, or Manhattan's First Avenue and Central Park West. Bridges like the Verrazzano-Narrows, Queensboro, Pulaski, Willis Avenue, and Madison Avenue will be locked down. Seriously, if you're not running the marathon, stay off the affected streets. It's not worth the headache.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**NYC Marathon Chaos this Weekend!** Widespread street and bridge closures will be in effect across all five boroughs, starting Saturday, October 31st, through Sunday, November 1st. Avoid running or driving on or near the marathon route. Check DOT advisories if you must travel.
</AlertBox>

And because this city never sleeps, construction continues. Over in Brooklyn Bridge Park, they're building the seasonal ice rink at Emily Warren Roebling Plaza, so expect detours there until November 13th. Plus, the Empire Fulton Ferry Lawn is closed for renovations until Spring 2027. Marine maintenance work at Fulton Ferry Landing is ongoing until December. And don't forget the various Con Edison utility work and building inspections causing scattered street closures throughout the weekend, some ending today, some extending into November. Just assume if you're going anywhere, there's a detour, a closure, or a crane involved.
