### Your Daily Dose of NYC Trail Annoyances

Alright, let's talk real talk for runners. While the weather gods are smiling, the city's perpetual state of "under construction" is still very much a thing. If you're planning on hitting the East River Park, well, I hope you like looking at fences. The northern stretch, from Houston Street all the way up, including the running track and most access points like those at 6th and 10th Streets, has been fully closed since September 2025 as part of the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. And don't hold your breath, because it's staying that way until the end of 2026. Yes, they took our track, the one they just "re-made" a few years ago. It's a real kick in the teeth.

Similarly, if the Hudson River Greenway is your jam, be aware of a detour between West 29th and West 30th Streets. The path is narrowed, and you'll be sharing space with construction vehicles from the Manhattan Tunnel Project. Keep your head up and your earbuds low; a delivery truck isn't going to stop for your PR attempt.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**East River Park is STILL a Ghost Town!**
The northern section of East River Park and its beloved running track remains completely closed for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, and will be for the foreseeable future (think late 2026). If you were hoping for loops there, you're out of luck. Adjust your route or head further south for what little remains open.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Zone Survival Guide:**
You're going to encounter detours. Don't fight them. Use common sense, stay alert for vehicles, and respect the workers. The city's a constant build site, so a little flexibility (and possibly a slightly longer route) is just part of the deal.
</AlertBox>
