## Today's Vibe: Overcast & Questionable Air

It's a balmy 19.5°C (that's pushing 67°F, for those of us still converting) under an overcast sky, which usually means prime running weather, right? Not so fast, hotshot. While the temperature is decent, the air quality isn't exactly crisp. We're sitting at an AQI of 51, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most folks, that's just "acceptable," but AccuWeather is reporting a 63 AQI, classifying it as "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." If you've got any respiratory issues, consider this your heads-up. Add moderate pollen to the mix, and you might be better off on a treadmill or, dare I say, doing some strength work indoors.

## Trail Torture: East River Park is Still a No-Go Zone

Remember East River Park? Yeah, the northern section, from Houston Street stretching northward, is still effectively a massive construction site. That means your usual loops around the running track, riverside strolls, and access points are shut down until the *end of 2026*. Don't make the trek thinking you'll find a secret shortcut; you'll just hit a fence and a lot of heavy machinery. The East River Greenway in that area is also closed 24/7 with detours. Find an alternative route, seriously.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**East River Park's Northern Section is CLOSED:** I cannot stress this enough. The northern stretch of East River Park, including the popular running track and esplanade, is completely inaccessible for the foreseeable future due to the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. Plan your runs elsewhere.
</AlertBox>

Further uptown, if you're hitting the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail, be aware of some narrowing between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. The Manhattan Tunnel Project is causing detours, and you might encounter construction trucks. Keep your head on a swivel. Also, if your route takes you anywhere near the Grand Central Parkway or Van Wyck Expressway in Queens, expect ongoing road construction impacting ramps until at least April 23rd or 24th.
