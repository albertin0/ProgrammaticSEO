<AlertBox type="danger">
### Flash Flood Warning & Hazardous Conditions!
Do NOT run through standing water, no matter how shallow it looks. Floodwaters can hide potholes, open manholes, and debris, not to mention being electrically charged. New York City Emergency Management has issued a travel advisory, and the National Weather Service warns of life-threatening flash flooding potential. Your run isn't worth an ER visit or worse.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Slippery Surfaces and Construction Ahead
Between the constant rain and ongoing city projects, expect slick surfaces everywhere. If you absolutely *must* get out, be extra cautious, especially near construction zones. Speaking of which, the northern stretch of **East River Park**, including the beloved running track, remains entirely closed through the end of 2026 for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. Don't even try it; find another route.
</AlertBox>
