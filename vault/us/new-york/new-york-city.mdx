### Smoke & Your Lungs: Don't Be a Hero
With the moderate AQI and smoke advisory, your lungs are working harder than usual, even if you can't see the haze. If you have asthma, allergies, or any other respiratory sensitivities, consider sticking to an indoor workout today. If you're heading out, keep your intensity down and listen to your body. No PR is worth a coughing fit.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Watch Your Step on the Hudson River Greenway
The construction on the Hudson River Greenway near the 29th-30th Street area is more than just an inconvenience; it's a hazard. The bike path is narrowed and rerouted, and you might encounter trucks. Keep your head up, ditch the headphones for this stretch, and be acutely aware of your surroundings. A surprise pothole or an unexpected swerve from another runner or cyclist could lead to a quick trip to the urgent care clinic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Bethesda Fountain: Under Construction, But the Terrace is Open
While not a direct workout hazard, a heads-up for those running through Central Park: the iconic Bethesda Fountain is undergoing a year-long restoration starting this month. It'll be turned off, but don't worry, Bethesda Terrace itself remains open for you to soak in the atmosphere (just not the fountain's spray).
</AlertBox>
