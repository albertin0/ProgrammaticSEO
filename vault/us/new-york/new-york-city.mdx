## Today's Rundown

That beautiful, crisp air? It's a gift. Grab it while you can. We're looking at clear skies and a comfortable 9°C, which is practically perfect for any outdoor activity, whether you're logging serious mileage or just taking a stroll through Central Park. Your lungs will thank you. The Air Quality Index is sitting pretty at 37, which is firmly in the "Good" category, with ozone and other pollutants at minimal levels. So, no smoky haze or gritty air to contend with today.

However, it wouldn't be New York without *some* kind of organized chaos. It's October, which means Halloween festivities are in full swing. The city's "Trick or Streets" initiative has turned numerous streets and plazas into car-free zones for various events. For instance, today, Thursday, October 22nd, Hillel Plaza on Hillel Place in Brooklyn is an Open Street from 2 PM to 6 PM. While great for trick-or-treaters and community fun, it means that if Hillel Place is part of your usual Brooklyn route, you're going to be dodging tiny superheroes and princesses. Check your specific neighborhood's DOT alerts before you get out there.

The Bronx is currently under a construction embargo until November 1st, specifically for the marathon route from East/West 169th Street down to East 138th Street, encompassing the area near the Grand Concourse and Harlem River. While this means no *new* heavy construction, existing work or lingering equipment might still be a factor for your run, especially with the minor course changes announced this year. Best to stick to known clear paths or scope out your planned route beforehand.
