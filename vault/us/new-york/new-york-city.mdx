Alright, NYC, listen up. Today's actually not a bad day to get out there, especially after the haze we've been choking on.

<LungsJointsScore score={7} />

<BulletList>
  <li>The mercury is sitting pretty at 15.1°C (about 59°F) with clear skies. Finally, some proper running weather that doesn't feel like a sauna or a freezer.</li>
  <li>Air Quality Index is currently moderate at 61, a welcome change after last week's Canadian wildfire smoke made us all feel like we were living in a poorly managed campfire. Just yesterday, the AQI was still hovering around 78, with warnings of spikes.</li>
  <li>Still dealing with some localized construction and closures, because, well, this is New York, and something's always under construction.</li>
</BulletList>

You’d be forgiven for still eyeing the sky with suspicion after the past week. Just a few days ago, the city was under an Air Quality Health Advisory, with AQI numbers for our region spiking into the "Very Unhealthy" range, touching 289 on Thursday. NYC Emergency Management was practically handing out KN95 masks like they were free bagels. The good news is those Canadian wildfires seem to be giving us a break, at least for now. Our current AQI of 61 is a definite improvement, but let's not get complacent. PM2.5 is still the dominant pollutant, so if you've got sensitive lungs, take it easy or stick to indoor workouts if you're feeling anything less than 100%. Don't be a hero; your lungs will thank you.

<AlertBox type="info">
  **Pro Tip for Air Quality:** Even when the general AQI looks good, local conditions can change fast, especially with PM2.5. If you start feeling any irritation, cough, or shortness of breath, cut your workout short. Your PR isn't worth an ER visit.
</AlertBox>

Now, about those trails. If your usual loop involves the **Hudson River Greenway**, be warned that the "Manhattan Tunnel Project" is still making a mess of things between West 29th and West 30th Streets. The bike path is narrowed and rerouted, and you might encounter construction trucks crossing the trail. Pay attention to those signs, for crying out loud. Don't go blindly flying through there just to shave a few seconds off your segment time. Your face hitting a jersey barrier will be way more inconvenient than a slight detour. Plus, Pier 40 Finger Pier and the Picnic House are still temporarily closed. And if you heard about that building near Grand Central with the buckling beams from a couple weeks ago, fear not for your run – that mess was thankfully stabilized quickly and isn't impacting our routes today, but it’s a good reminder that things can shift in a minute in this city.

Meanwhile, up in Central Park, the eastern section of Heckscher Playground is closed for construction starting *today*, July 20. Also, if you were planning on fishing in the Harlem Meer, forget about it—harmful algal blooms have suspended that program. It might not impact your run directly, but it's another sign that not all is perfectly pristine out there.

Overall, it's a decent day, but keep your wits about you. We've ridden out worse, and we'll ride out worse again, but for today, enjoy the clear skies and mild temps, just don't assume every path is perfectly clear.
