## Urban Gauntlet: Construction, Crowds, and Celestial Events

Here's where it gets interesting, or rather, infuriating, for those of us trying to get a continuous stride in.

<AlertBox type="danger">
The northern stretch of **East River Park**, from Houston Street all the way up, including the beloved running track, is completely off-limits until the end of 2026 due to the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. That's right, your scenic riverside loops are a pipe dream for another year and a half. Find an alternate route or prepare for disappointment.
</AlertBox>

And it's not just East River Park. The **Red Hook Track** is still bogged down in construction delays with no clear end in sight. So much for finding a smooth, uninterrupted surface to log your miles.

Beyond the specific track closures, the city is still under a general construction embargo for the FIFA World Cup 2026. This means various streets in Manhattan and Brooklyn have suspended permits, and you can expect unexpected closures and diversions, especially around key areas like Lexington Avenue and parts of the Hudson River Greenway. If you're running in Midtown, remember that building on East 42nd Street near Grand Central that was at risk of collapse a few days ago? Streets around there are still closed for safety.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect crowds! Today is July 12, 2026, and it's the day for the "half sun" **Manhattanhenge** viewing around 8:21 PM ET. People will be flocking to west-facing cross streets (think 14th, 23rd, 34th, 42nd, 57th) to snap pictures of the sunset perfectly aligning with the street grid. If your evening run takes you through these areas, prepare for throngs of gawkers and potentially blocked sidewalks. Stick to the parks if you want an unobstructed path.
</AlertBox>

Also, keep an eye out for **"NYC DOT Soccer Streets: Block Party"** events happening today. These pop-up soccer zones transform car-free streets, which is great for the kids but not so much for your planned route. They're likely widespread, so check local announcements before heading out.
