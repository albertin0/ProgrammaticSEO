## Today's NYC Outdoor Workout Reality Check

Look, the calendar says October, and the thermometer agrees: 20.6°C and partly cloudy sounds like a dream for hitting the pavement or trails. The issue? Air quality and our city's never-ending glow-up (read: construction).

Your environmental data gives us a "Moderate" AQI of 69, with PM2.5 as the main culprit. But let's be real, "moderate" can be a sliding scale. AccuWeather's showing an AQI of 70 for today and tomorrow, which they're straight-up labeling "Poor," with a warning for sensitive groups to limit outdoor time. Fine particulate matter, that PM2.5, is the nasty stuff that digs deep into your lungs. Even if there's no visible smoke from Canada today, those particles can linger or drift from far-off sources. If you've got asthma, COPD, or just feel a bit off on hazy days, listen to your body and maybe take it indoors.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  **AQI is borderline "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups."** If you're in a high-risk category (kids, older adults, anyone with lung or heart issues), seriously consider dialing back outdoor intensity or sticking to a filtered indoor environment. NYC's air quality can spike hour-to-hour.
</AlertBox>

And then there's the delightful dance around construction. If East River Park is your usual stomping ground, prepare to be disappointed (again). The northern section, including that beloved running track, has been shuttered since September 2025 for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project and won't be back until the tail end of 2026. That's a long "until further notice," folks.

Down on the Manhattan stretch of the Empire State Trail, near Hudson River Park between W. 29th and W. 30th, you'll find a narrowed path and construction trucks playing chicken with runners and cyclists through November. Keep your head on a swivel there. And if you're an early bird or night owl near the Third Street Bridge over the Gowanus Canal, be aware of full bridge closures happening from 11 pm to 5 am on Thursday morning for repairs.

Even Van Cortlandt Park in the Bronx has "Trail Work Thursdays" today from 9 AM to 12 PM. While it's great they're keeping the trails tidy, it means you might encounter volunteers and equipment. Not a major hazard, but something to be aware of if you like to zone out on your run.
