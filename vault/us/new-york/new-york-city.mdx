### Today's Urban Gauntlet

Look, I love this city, but sometimes it feels like it's actively trying to prevent you from getting a decent long run in. Today is one of those days. While the skies are clear and the air is crisp, navigating the concrete jungle will require some serious forethought.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Museum Mile Festival - Fifth Avenue Shutdown!** If you planned on cruising down Fifth Avenue, forget it. From 6 PM to 9 PM tonight, Fifth Avenue between 82nd and 110th Streets is completely shut down for the Museum Mile Festival. It's a pedestrian block party, which means thousands of people will be where you usually run. Find an alternate route, or enjoy the culture (from a distance, if you're trying to hit pace).
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Brooklyn Bridge Park Pride Run & FIFA Fan Fest.** Headed to Brooklyn? Good luck. Today is the Brooklyn Bridge Park Pride Run, which means parts of Brooklyn Bridge Park will be swarming with runners and spectators. Combine that with the ongoing FIFA World Cup Fan Fest and viewing events on Water Street between Dock Street and Old Fulton Street (running through tomorrow), and you've got a recipe for serious congestion. Consider DUMBO or other waterfront areas further away, or brace for a stop-and-go session.
</AlertBox>

Beyond today's big events, we've still got the usual NYC special sauce of construction and residual mess.

Expect narrowed paths and construction trucks. Plus, Hudson River Park has some temporary closures, including Pier 40 Finger Pier and its rooftop field, so be mindful of signage.
