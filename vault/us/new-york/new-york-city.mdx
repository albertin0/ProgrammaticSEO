### Know Your Obstacles: Events and Construction

Central Park, bless its crowded heart, is hosting the 41st Annual New York City Duathlon today. Starting at 7 AM near the Central Park Boat House, runners will be hitting the east side paths and cyclists will be tearing around Central Park Drive, including good ol' Harlem Hill. The park doesn't close for these things, so be hyper-aware of your surroundings. Sharing is caring, people.
<AlertBox type="info">
  **Central Park Duathlon Today!** Expect heavy traffic on running paths (east side) and cycling routes (Central Park Drive) this morning. Stay alert, share the road (or path), and don't be *that* person cutting off a racer.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the Duathlon, the NYRR Open Run program is also holding free 5K community runs in Marine Park, Conference House Park, and Cunningham Park today. These are great, but again, mean more foot traffic in those areas.

Now for the perpetual pain in our collective asphalt: construction. The East Side Coastal Resiliency Project (ESCR) continues its seemingly endless saga, with the northern section of **East River Park, including the track and field area and esplanade, completely closed**. And yes, the Greenway through that section is also shut down 24/7. You'll need to use detours via the Delancey Street or Corlears Hook Bridges to access any open southern amenities. They're doing night work too, so if you're an insomniac runner, expect some noise and disruption.
<AlertBox type="danger">
  **East River Park Greenway is CLOSED (Northern Section):** Seriously, don't even try. The Greenway from Montgomery Street to Stuyvesant Cove Park is shut down 24/7 due to the ESCR project. Plan for significant detours or pick a different route entirely.
</AlertBox>

Over on the west side, the Hudson River Greenway bike path is still re-routed between W 29th and W 30th streets due to the Gateway Tunnel Project. Cyclists, pay attention and stick to the marked detours; don't be a hero trying to weave through construction zones. Also, Tennis Court #1 at Hudson River Park is closed until 10 AM today.

Even Central Park itself has its share of ongoing repairs. The Drives north of East 90th Street are getting repaved on weeknights, and while they stay open, cyclists and pedestrians should use extra caution. The Great Lawn Oval and North Ballfields are also closed due to weather, and various playgrounds and paths are undergoing restoration.

In short: Today's workout requires situational awareness. Watch your step, watch for others, and keep an eye on those skies.
