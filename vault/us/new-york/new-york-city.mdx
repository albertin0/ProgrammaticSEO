The provided AQI for PM2.5 is moderate at 57. However, the critical issue today is the *Air Quality Health Advisory* specifically for **ground-level ozone**, which is expected to push the AQI over 100. This isn't visible smog like PM2.5, but it's a colorless gas that's particularly bad for your lungs, especially during strenuous activity in the afternoon and early evening when it's most concentrated. If you've got asthma, heart conditions, or are just a normal human who prefers to breathe without wheezing, stay inside during peak heat and ozone.

### Trail Conditions

For those of you stubborn enough to head out, expect some detours and caution, especially along popular routes. The **Hudson River Greenway** is a perennial favorite, but right now, the segment between W. 29th and W. 30th Street has narrowed bikeways and walkways due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. Watch out for construction trucks. Further north, the Fort Washington Park Bridge on the same greenway near W. 180th Street is in disrepair, and while "immediate repairs" are planned, the full replacement has been "17 years in the making." So, watch your step up there.
