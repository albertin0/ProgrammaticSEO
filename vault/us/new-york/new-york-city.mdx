### Watch Your Step (and Your Head)

Our fair city is always under construction, and today is no different. The NYC Department of Transportation is swamped with crane operations, sewer work, and building inspections all over town, leading to a smattering of street closures across all five boroughs.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Specifically, anyone hitting the **Hudson River Greenway** needs to be extra cautious. Between West 29th and West 30th Streets, the bike path is temporarily re-routed and narrowed to accommodate the Gateway Tunnel Project. The walkway is also squeezed down. Expect construction trucks to be crossing the path, so keep your head on a swivel. This isn't just an inconvenience; it's a legitimate hazard if you're not paying attention.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the major projects, don't forget the localized stuff. The East Side Coastal Resiliency Project also has ongoing closures to contend with. Always check the NYC DOT advisories if you're heading out for a long run on a known route.

### Damp Conditions and Lingering Nuisances

Remember those heavy rains and flash flood warnings from earlier this week? While the immediate flood threat has passed, the ground is still likely damp in many spots, and we've got a 25% chance of more light rain today and a 40% chance tonight. This means slick spots on pavement, potentially muddy sections on trails, and generally less-than-ideal footing. If you're planning a trail run in, say, Forest Park, be aware there are "Restoration Fridays" happening, meaning workers and potential trail disruptions too.

<AlertBox type="info">
Wear appropriate footwear with good traction. No one wants to end up with a sprained ankle just because you thought your old trainers could handle a wet patch.
</AlertBox>

Also, if you're a cyclist, be mindful of ongoing events. The Harlem Skyscraper Cycling Classic and Festival, which runs through today, means closures around Madison Avenue and 124th Street. You don't want to accidentally roll into a race route.

Stay safe out there, folks. This city will always try to get you, so be smarter than it.
