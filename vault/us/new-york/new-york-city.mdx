## The Air: Don't Be a Hero

Seriously, that Air Quality Health Advisory isn't just for asthmatics. When the AQI for PM2.5 is predicted to be over 100, your lungs are doing extra work to filter out tiny crap. If you're hammering out intervals or logging serious mileage, you're just sucking down more pollution. The weather report says it's clear, but "clear" doesn't mean "clean."

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Air Quality Health Advisory in Effect!** The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation warns of unhealthy air for sensitive groups due to fine particulates (PM2.5), with AQI predicted above 100. Reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors.
</AlertBox>
