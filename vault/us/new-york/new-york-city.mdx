<AlertBox type="warning">
  You're not imagining things if your nose is a bit stuffed or your eyes are itchy. We're in "Moderate" pollen territory. If you're sensitive, pop an antihistamine *before* you head out, not after you've already started crying on the West Side Highway.

While the air quality itself is stellar, don't forget we're in a city that's perpetually under construction. The Hudson River Greenway, specifically around West 29th and West 30th Streets, is still dealing with re-routes due to that Gateway Tunnel Project. They've moved the bike path, and trust me, you don't want to be one of those folks who ignores the signs and ends up playing chicken with a concrete mixer. Stick to the marked detours; it's not worth shaving thirty seconds off your run to potentially get pancaked. And speaking of the Hudson, remember Pier 51 is totally shut down for playground reconstruction. So if your routine involved lunges on the pier, find a new spot for a bit.

Overall, it's a solid day to rack up some miles. Just be smart about it, and don't assume every path you've always run is clear. This city changes faster than my splits on a good day.
