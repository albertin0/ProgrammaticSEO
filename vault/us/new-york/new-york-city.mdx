### Major Obstacles & Annoyances

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### World Cup Chaos: Gridlock Alert!
Today, June 25th, is officially a "Gridlock Alert Day" due to the ongoing FIFA World Cup. This isn't just about car traffic; it means significant street closures and a massive influx of pedestrians, especially in areas like Midtown Manhattan. If your usual route involves navigating busy avenues or crossing major intersections, reconsider. You're going to be dodging more than just slow walkers. Plan your workout for less congested parks or trails, or go early to beat the crowds and closures.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Hudson River Greenway Headache: Construction Zone Ahead
If you're a regular on the Hudson River Greenway, be warned: that Gateway Tunnel Project work between W 29th and W 30th Streets is still active. The bike path is rerouted, and both the bikeway and pedestrian paths are significantly narrowed. This has been going on for a while and shows no signs of letting up soon. Expect tight squeezes and keep an eye out for construction vehicles. It's not exactly the scenic, stress-free run you might be hoping for. Consider jumping onto the path further uptown or exploring Central Park to avoid this bottleneck, though Central Park will likely see increased foot traffic today with many main roads possibly closed or rerouted to avoid World Cup traffic.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### General Tips for Navigating NYC Today
Given the moderate pollen and the pleasant, overcast weather, it's a great day to get some miles in, *if* you can avoid the urban obstacle courses. Stay hydrated, as always. If you're heading to Van Cortlandt Park, remember that today is "Trail Work Thursday" until noon, so parts of the trails might have active maintenance or debris being cleared. Your best bet for a smooth run is likely an early morning outing or sticking to less trafficked loops in Prospect Park or the outer edges of Central Park, far from any major thoroughfares or event zones.
</AlertBox>
