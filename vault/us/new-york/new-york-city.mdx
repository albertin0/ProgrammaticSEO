### Air Quality: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

While it's not "apocalypse smoke from Canada" bad, an AQI of 74 is firmly in the "Moderate" category, and critical sources are calling it "Poor" for sensitive groups. That means people with respiratory conditions like asthma, or those with heart disease, should seriously reconsider intense or prolonged outdoor exercise today. PM2.5, the tiny particulate matter, is the main culprit, and that stuff gets deep into your lungs. It’s why the state issued an advisory, not just a suggestion.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Health Advisory in Effect!** Active children, adults, and especially those with lung diseases (asthma, COPD) should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Your lungs are not bulletproof.
</AlertBox>

### Construction: The City Never Sleeps (or Stops Digging)

You can't swing a dead cat in this city without hitting some form of ongoing construction. For runners, this means unexpected detours and blocked sidewalks. Be particularly aware if your route takes you anywhere near the lower Manhattan courthouses, where the NYC Borough-Based Jail Program is causing noise and material transport with various street closures through November 8th. Also, if you're trying to get around during off-peak hours, remember that ramp closures and detours are in effect for the Lincoln Tunnel Expressway and Roosevelt Avenue Bridge.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route!** Construction closures and detours are abundant today. Check the NYC DOT's advisories before you head out, especially if you rely on specific bridges or tunnels. The Manhattan perimeter paths can be affected by various projects, so stay alert.
</AlertBox>
