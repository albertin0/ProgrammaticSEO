Alright, listen up, because while the air is decent, the ground game is a mess in some spots.

#### Midtown Structural Failure – Avoid 42nd Street Area!
As of yesterday, structural columns buckled on the 21st floor of a major construction site at 235 East 42nd Street, leading to evacuations and ongoing street closures around Grand Central. This area is currently considered unstable. If your route takes you anywhere near here, reroute. Seriously, cross the street, go another block, do whatever you need to do to steer clear. This isn't just about traffic; it's about structural integrity.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### East River Park & Other Waterfront Work – Expect Closures and Detours
The East Side Coastal Resiliency Project is still chugging along, and that means the section of **East River Park south of Stanton Street is closed** until further notice. Same goes for the east end of Corlears Hook Park. If your usual waterfront long run involves these areas, you'll be detoured. Also, be mindful of lingering moisture and debris on other parts of the East River Greenway from yesterday's significant rainfall. Flood mitigation construction also means nightly closures (Sunday-Friday, 10 PM - 5 AM) for the Battery Park Underpass until the end of July.
</AlertBox>
