## The Rundown: Your Saturday Morning Grind

Look, I get it. It's Saturday, and you're itching to hit the pavement. The 7.7°C (about 46°F) overcast skies mean it's cool enough for a good sweat without baking, which is a blessing this time of year. But don't just blindly head out the door, especially if your usual haunt is in Brooklyn.

### Prospect Park Pandemonium

Today, **Prospect Park** is basically a giant treadmill for a few thousand people. The Brooklyn Spring Half, 10K, and 5K races are kicking off this morning, starting around 9:30 AM. Unless you paid the exorbitant registration fee, you're better off finding another spot or waiting until the afternoon. Expect crowds, blocked paths, and general runner chaos. If you're looking for peace and quiet, this ain't it. Save the scenic loops for another day. Seriously, don't be *that* person trying to weave through a race pack.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Avoid Prospect Park this morning.** The Brooklyn Spring Half, 10K, and 5K races are underway, causing significant congestion and closures.
</AlertBox>

### Air & Allergies

The good news is the air isn't trying to actively kill you today. Our Air Quality Index is at a moderate 53, with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This means it's generally okay for most folks, though if you're sensitive, you might still feel it a bit. Historically, the AQI has been in the good range recently, hitting about 44 a couple of days ago.

The bad news for many of us is the "Moderate" pollen level. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, you know the drill: take your meds, carry tissues, and maybe rinse off immediately after your run to avoid tracking those microscopic irritants everywhere. Nothing ruins a PR like a sneezing fit halfway up a hill.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress in layers.** While "Overcast" and 7.7°C isn't freezing, it's definitely brisk. You'll warm up, but those first few miles can be chilly.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Troubles: Construction Edition

If you're usually cruising along the Hudson River Park section of the Empire State Trail, be aware of some ongoing disruptions. Starting this month, the Manhattan Tunnel Project is causing detours between W. 29th and W. 30th Street. This means the bikeway and walkway are narrowed, and you might encounter construction vehicles. Keep your eyes peeled, especially if you're flying on two wheels. No need to end up in a staring contest with a cement mixer.

### What Else You Missed (or Missed Avoiding)

Just a quick heads-up, in case you were still feeling the phantom pain of yesterday's half-marathon or were planning to cheer on a parade: The United Airlines NYC Half Marathon already wreaked havoc on March 15th, and the St. Patrick's Day Parade marched through Midtown on March 17th. Both caused major street closures, but they're in the rearview mirror now. Today's main headache is the Brooklyn Spring race.

Stay safe out there, try not to collide with any wannabe Olympians, and enjoy the relatively mild weather.
