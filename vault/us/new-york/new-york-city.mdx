## Hazards & Hot Spots

### Afternoon Storms & Flash Flooding: Seriously, Stay Inside

Look, this isn't some gentle summer shower. The New York City Emergency Management Department (NYCEM) is warning us about thunderstorms, heavy rainfall, and potential localized flash flooding kicking off this afternoon and going into the evening. We're talking multiple rounds of storms most likely between 4 p.m. and 11 p.m., with rainfall rates that could hit 1.5 to 2 inches per hour. That's enough to turn your favorite underpass into a muddy river. Remember Saturday's flooding? Expect a repeat, possibly worse.

<AlertBox type="danger">
  Severe thunderstorms, damaging winds (40-60 mph!), and flash flooding are highly likely this afternoon and evening. Seriously, if you hear thunder, you need to be indoors. This isn't just about getting wet; it's about getting swept away or having a tree branch land on your head.
</AlertBox>

### Air Quality & Pollen: Breathe Easy? Maybe Not.

While we're not reliving last week's Canadian wildfire smoke debacle (thank god, that AQI of 289 was no joke), the air is still sitting at a "moderate" 64 AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. Couple that with moderate pollen, and anyone with a set of lungs that aren't factory fresh might feel it. Your usual sprint along the Hudson River Greenway might feel a bit heavier today.

<AlertBox type="warning">
  Moderate air quality (AQI 64, PM2.5) combined with moderate pollen levels means sensitive individuals might experience respiratory irritation. If you've got asthma or other lung issues, scale back your outdoor efforts, especially with the impending rain stirring things up.
</AlertBox>

### Trail Troubles: Construction & Events

It's NYC, so of course, something's always being built or celebrated. The Hudson River Greenway is still dealing with its Gateway Tunnel Project construction between W 29th and W 30th streets, narrowing both the bike path and walkway. Watch out for those construction trucks. Over in Central Park, the CRCA Central Park Racing Series is still happening on East Drive during early morning hours through July 25th, so if you're out there, expect company and potential closures. And don't forget the FIFA World Cup Final Viewing Party on the Great Lawn is wrapping up today by 6 PM, which means more crowds and potential obstacles around Central Park West.

Meanwhile, if you were hoping for a run on the East River Park track, you're still out of luck — it's closed through 2026 for rebuilding. And the Red Hook track? Still a mess, "delayed indefinitely" because the contractor bailed and they found some unexpected sewer issues under the turf. Don't hold your breath for that one.

<AlertBox type="info">
  Morning workout warriors: If you're hitting Central Park, be aware of the CRCA Racing Series. Expect congestion and potential detours on the East Drive. Also, construction continues to narrow sections of the Hudson River Greenway; stay vigilant.
</AlertBox>
