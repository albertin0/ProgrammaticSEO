Today's the kind of day you wish every August day was in this city. Forget that oppressive heat we usually get; we've got a cool, partly cloudy morning that's practically begging you to hit the pavement or the trails. The air quality is stellar, and while pollen is at a moderate level, it's nothing a good dose of Zyrtec or a tissue won't handle if you're one of the sniffly types.

<AlertBox type="info">
Don't forget your hydration, even on cooler days. It's still August in New York, and that sun can sneak up on you between the clouds.
</AlertBox>

However, this is New York, and we always have to deal with the perpetual state of "under construction." If you're planning on a scenic run through East River Park, remember that chunks of it are still locked down north of Stanton Street for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. It's a necessary evil for flood protection, but it means detours are a way of life down there. Always check the NYC Parks website before heading out if you have a specific route in mind near any major park.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Expect some localized damp spots and potentially muddy patches on unpaved trails, especially after yesterday's significant rainfall and flash flood warnings. Watch your footing to avoid an unexpected face-plant.
</AlertBox>

And if your workout plan involves traveling any significant distance by subway, particularly on a weekend, you'd best plan ahead. The MTA is in the middle of a multi-weekend construction blitz on the 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 lines, making Lower Manhattan particularly tricky to access. Today is Tuesday, so weekday service *should* be less affected, but it's always worth a quick check. Nobody wants to miss their morning loop because they're stuck underground.

Lastly, while today isn't one of the designated car-free Summer Streets events (those are on August 1st, 8th, and 15th for Manhattan), there's always ongoing street work. For instance, the Battery Park Underpass is seeing full tube closures overnight during the week for flood mitigation, which won't impact daytime runs but highlights the constant churn of city infrastructure. Park Avenue Viaduct-Northbound is also fully closed 24/7 through August 28th between E 128th and E 131st Street.
