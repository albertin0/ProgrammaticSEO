### The Rundown

Alright, so the mercury's sitting at a cool 11.7°C (53°F) under an overcast sky, which usually means prime running weather for us non-sun worshipers. But don't get too cozy. The air quality is clocking in at a moderate 59, with ozone being the main troublemaker. This isn't full-on "apocalypse haze" like we sometimes get, but it's enough to consider if you've got sensitive lungs or a history of respiratory issues. Ozone, as we all know from that recent "dirtiest air east of Texas" report, is a byproduct of pollution and sunshine, and it's a real lung irritant. Factor in a moderate pollen count, and you've got a decent recipe for post-workout sniffles and a persistent cough.

<AlertBox type="warning">

You'll be sharing space with construction vehicles and dealing with a reduced path, so keep your head up and your pace controlled. This isn't the time to zone out.
</AlertBox>

The Park Avenue Tunnel, for example, has daily lane closures this week. While you're probably not running *through* the tunnel, it's a good reminder that street surfaces can be unpredictable and detours are a constant reality. If your route involves dodging traffic cones or unexpected pedestrian reroutes, stay vigilant.
