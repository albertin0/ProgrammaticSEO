However, if your idea of "getting out there" involves actual *trails* rather than just pounding pavement, you've got to pay attention. If you're heading up to the Bronx, specifically **Van Cortlandt Park**, be aware that today is "Trail Work Thursday." That's right, between 9:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m., there are folks out there clearing debris, battling overgrowth, and doing "desire line closures." What does that mean for you? Potential detours, active work zones, and maybe a volunteer or two giving you the side-eye for bombing through their hard work. It's not worth a twisted ankle just to "power through." They're making the trails better, so respect the process and find an alternative route if you see signs of work.

Speaking of trails, for those who like to escape the concrete jungle entirely and head for the real woods, a section of the Highlands Trail, specifically Section #5 in Byram, New Jersey, is closed from July 2026 to July 2027. It's an EPA contamination cleanup around a Superfund site. Yes, you read that right: *Superfund*. So maybe don't try to "find a way around" that one. It’s outside our immediate NYC bubble, but a lot of us venture there, and getting caught in a hazmat zone isn't exactly peak performance training.
