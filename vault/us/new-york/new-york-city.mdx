### Local Rundown

Alright, folks, listen up. Word on the street is they’re still doing some work on the **Brooklyn Bridge pedestrian path**. You know, the usual slow-as-molasses city project that's been going on for ages. Expect some bottlenecks, even more than usual with the tourists trying to snap that perfect, completely unoriginal skyline photo. If you're trying to set a PR on the bridge, forget it. You'll be dodging selfie sticks and construction cones more than actual runners. It's a real joy.

Also, a heads-up for anyone thinking of doing laps around Central Park. There's some minor asphalt repair happening near the **East Drive entrance at 72nd Street**. Nothing major, but watch your footing. Last thing you need is to trip on a loose patch and explain to your boss why you're hobbling in with a sprained ankle for the third time this month.
