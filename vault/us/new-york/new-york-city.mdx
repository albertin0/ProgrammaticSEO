## The Air: Moderate Means Monitor

Don't let the clear skies fool you into thinking the air is sparkling fresh. The Air Quality Index is sitting at a "moderate" 77, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. For most of us, that's just a bit annoying, maybe a little scratch in the back of your throat. But if you're asthmatic or have sensitive lungs, consider scaling back that interval workout or sticking to the treadmill. This isn't the day to try for a PR.

## On the Ground: Mind the Mayhem

### Construction Chaos Continues

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Trail Narrowing & Detours on Empire State Trail:** Expect disruptions and narrowed paths on the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. Watch out for construction trucks crossing the trail.
</AlertBox>

Seriously, sometimes I think the city just *enjoys* making runners navigate an obstacle course. If your route takes you along the Hudson River Park Greenway, specifically the stretch between 29th and 30th Street, be ready to merge and dodge. That bikeway is narrowed to 12 feet, and the pedestrian path to a meager 8 feet. It's tight, and you'll be sharing space with construction vehicles. Keep your head on a swivel.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Major Sewer Work & Road Closures Near Corlears Hook Park:** Extensive sewer installation and deep excavation are ongoing around Jackson Street, Water Street, and Delancey Street. This includes daytime road closures on Jackson Street (Mon-Fri, 7 AM - 4 PM) and potential intermittent roadway closures and traffic detours impacting access to Corlears Hook Park. Follow flaggers and posted signs.
</AlertBox>

And for those of you further downtown, especially if you like winding through the Lower East Side, steer clear of the Corlears Hook Park area. There's some serious sewer installation happening, meaning road closures, detours, and general pandemonium around Jackson Street and Water Street, continuing well into the future. You'll be dodging more than just slow tourists there. Find another loop if that's your usual stomping ground.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Bridge Work Mostly Overnight:** While there's ongoing rehabilitation on the Pulaski Bridge, Hutchinson River Parkway Bridge, and flood mitigation at the Battery Park Underpass, the primary closures are occurring overnight or during off-peak hours this week. Check DOT advisories if you're planning a very early or late session that involves these crossings.
</AlertBox>

It's a "get it done" kind of day. The weather's cooperating, but the air quality isn't pristine, and the city's perpetual state of construction is alive and well. Pick your path wisely, stay alert, and don't forget to stretch those hamstrings; navigating detours counts as extra agility work.
