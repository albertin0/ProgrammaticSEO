## Today's Lung & Joint Lowdown

Look, you could do a lot worse. The mercury's sitting pretty, and the sun's out. That 18.9°C is prime running weather, assuming you don't mind a little company. But this is New York, so "good weather" just means a different set of obstacles.

That moderate AQI isn't going to kill you, but if you've got any kind of respiratory issues, keep it in mind. It's not the wildfire smoke we've dealt with, but it's not mountain air either. Same goes for the moderate pollen. If you’re one of the unfortunate souls whose nose hates autumn, take your meds *before* you hit the pavement.

## Navigating the Urban Jungle

This month, the city's gone all out with "Trick-or-Streets," converting a whopping 160 streets and plazas into car-free zones for Halloween festivities. Sounds great for pedestrians, right? Until you're trying to maintain a pace on what used to be your quiet side street, and now it's a gauntlet of face-painting stations and enthusiastic kids. Expect major crowd congestion in areas designated for these events, especially in neighborhoods like the East Village (around Tompkins Square Park, where Avenue B just got its Open Street upgrades) and various spots across Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx. Your casual run might turn into an accidental urban obstacle course.
