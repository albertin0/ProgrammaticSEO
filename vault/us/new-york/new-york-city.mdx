Forget about hitting the pavement today, folks. New York City Emergency Management has already issued a travel advisory for today, June 22, with forecasters predicting heavy rain and thunderstorms for Monday afternoon and into Tuesday morning. We're talking widespread rainfall of 0.75 to 2 inches, with some localized downpours potentially hitting 4 inches. Rainfall rates could easily reach 1.5 to 2 inches per hour during the strongest storms. That's enough to turn your favorite running routes into impromptu swimming lanes, fast.
