## The Rundown for Sunday, August 9, 2026

You dodged a bullet yesterday, literally. Saturday, August 8th, saw an Air Quality Health Advisory for ozone, with the AQI forecast to hit Level Orange, making it "unhealthy for sensitive groups." The city was also under a heat advisory with heat index values threatening 104 degrees. Today, the air is looking better with an AQI of 69 for PM2.5, which is generally considered acceptable for most. But don't think you're completely in the clear.

We're still dealing with moderate pollen levels, which means your morning run might still come with a side of sneezes and watery eyes if you're sensitive. Also, while the official heat advisory for extreme heat expired last night, it's still August in New York, and it's going to be warm. Expect highs in the upper 80s (around 31°C) with continued humidity, even if slightly less oppressive than Saturday.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Chaos on Hudson River Park:** If you're planning a scenic run or ride along the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail, be aware of ongoing construction from the Manhattan Tunnel Project between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. The bikeway and walkway are narrowed, and you might encounter construction trucks. This isn't a quick fix either; it's slated to continue through November 2026. Plan for tight squeezes and potential delays.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Battery Park Underpass Closures:** For those of you running or cycling in Lower Manhattan, the Battery Park Underpass will see full northbound and southbound tube closures (not concurrently) nightly from Sunday 10 PM to Friday 5 AM for flood mitigation work. This is a recurring headache through August 28th, 2026. If you're out late, be ready for detours.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydrate or Die:** It's still hot out there. Your body doesn't care if the "advisory" lifted; it cares about staying cool. Drink more water than you think you need, especially before and after your workout. And for the love of all that is holy, don't run a marathon at noon today unless you're packing a camelback and have a support crew with ice baths.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Check Transit for Your Trek:** If you're relying on public transport to get to your favorite workout spot, double-check those MTA and LIRR schedules. August is a mess of service disruptions due to maintenance and construction across several subway lines and the LIRR, particularly near Elmont-UBS Arena. Don't get stuck doing an unplanned warm-up sprint to a shuttle bus.
