## The Air: Breathe It In (Mostly)

Let's talk lungs first. That AQI sitting at 64, with PM2.5 being the main culprit, is "moderate." It's not a full-blown "stay inside unless you enjoy coughing up a lung" kind of day like we've seen earlier this month with those Canadian wildfire plumes, but it's certainly not pristine alpine air either. If you’ve got asthma, allergies, or just generally aren't a fan of microscopic particles making their way into your respiratory system, you'll feel this one. Keep your inhaler handy and maybe stick to a less intense pace if you're sensitive.

The good news is the major smoke issues that plagued us mid-July seem to have cleared out. Yesterday's forecast even called for "Good" air quality, so things have slipped a little, but nothing catastrophic.

## The Roads: Run With the Masses

Now, about those joints. Today is **Summer Streets** day in Queens and Staten Island, specifically along **Vernon Boulevard** in Long Island City from 9 AM to 5 PM. This means a glorious, car-free stretch of asphalt where you can run, bike, or even rollerblade without fearing an SUV turning into you. Sounds amazing, right? Except everyone else in Queens and probably half of Brooklyn is also thinking the same thing.

The New York Road Runners are even hosting a free Start Line Series event right there on Vernon Boulevard, so expect extra crowds of enthusiastic beginners alongside the usual morning warriors. And let's not forget the "Run for Donuts" race happening elsewhere in the city today. Basically, if you're looking for solitude, go hit the treadmill in your air-purified basement. If you want to experience the "energy of the city," be prepared to dodge strollers, slow walkers, and people taking selfies.

<AlertBox type="info">
If you're heading to Vernon Boulevard in Queens for Summer Streets, arrive early to beat the biggest crowds, or embrace the collective chaos. Plan your route with foot traffic in mind.
</AlertBox>

At 16.6°C (that's about 62°F for those of you still clinging to Fahrenheit), and clear skies, the actual running conditions are *chef's kiss*. Low humidity, light winds – it’s really hard to complain about the temperature. This is the kind of weather where you PR if your lungs and the crowds cooperate. Don't let the moderate air quality or the sheer volume of humanity ruin what is otherwise a perfect day for breaking a sweat outdoors.
