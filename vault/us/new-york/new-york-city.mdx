## What's Happening Out There (Beyond Your Own Head)

Looks like April is bringing more than just showers; it's bringing some good old NYC construction chaos and a thick blanket of morning fog.

The weather today is overcast with a mild 13.8°C (about 57°F), which is pretty decent for a run. However, if you're an early bird, a Dense Fog Advisory is in effect until noon today for our coastal areas, meaning visibility is going to be low. That's not ideal for dodging tourists or rogue delivery bikes, so be extra cautious if you're out near the water.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Morning Fog Hazard!
If you're out before noon, especially near the water or along the Hudson River Greenway, expect reduced visibility due to dense fog. Wear bright colors and stay alert.
</AlertBox>

Speaking of the **Hudson River Greenway**, prepare for some navigational gymnastics between W. 29th and W. 30th Street. There's a Manhattan Tunnel Project detour narrowing both the bike and pedestrian paths, and you might encounter construction trucks crossing the trail. This isn't a new problem – it started in March and is expected to last six months – but it's still a pain in the ass if you're trying to hit a stride.

And if your weekend plans included a run or ride over the **Williamsburg Bridge**, adjust your schedule. The Brooklyn-bound Outer Roadway is completely closed from 10 PM Friday until 5 AM Monday for rehabilitation work. Cyclists and pedestrians will still have access, but expect it to be a bit more congested than usual.
