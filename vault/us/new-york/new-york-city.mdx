Look, the air quality index is a respectable 47 today, which for New York City is basically pristine. Enjoy breathing deeply without tasting exhaust fumes for once. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a smooth ride out there.

We're staring down the barrel of a weekend absolutely riddled with street closures and event-related chaos. The NYC DOT's got a laundry list of temporary shutdowns for everything from building inspections and crane operations to utility work across Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Staten Island. You want to run down West 11th Street? Forget about it. East 13th? Nope. Even Battery Park Underpass is getting in on the fun with ongoing flood mitigation construction, closing tubes from 10 PM Sunday to 5 AM Friday. So, if your workout involves dodging heavy machinery, you're golden.

If you were planning a nice, uninterrupted long run along the Hudson, think again. The Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail between West 29th Street and West 30th Street has a "small detour" due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. "Small detour" in NYC-speak usually means "prepare for a fun adventure involving narrowed pathways, bollards, and construction trucks playing chicken with pedestrians." This gem is sticking around through November, so get used to it.

And because it's fall, we've got races. While the big Marathon isn't until November 1st, today we've got the Alpha Win Big Apple Triathlon kicking off, which means parts of Riverside Park, particularly around the Esplanade between 72nd and 83rd Streets, are going to be a congested mess with athletes and spectators. Plus, the Bike MS NYC event, scheduled for tomorrow, already has construction embargoes in effect today (October 16-18), impacting areas like Madison Avenue and Fifth Avenue. So, even if you're not participating, you're dealing with the fallout.
