### East River Park: Closed for Business (Again)

If you had plans to hit the running track or enjoy the waterfront north of Houston Street in **East River Park**, you can scrap 'em. As of today, September 8th, the entire northern section, including the running track, is completely closed. This isn't some short-term inconvenience, folks. This is for the multi-billion-dollar East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, and the northern section isn't expected to reopen until late 2027. So, that year-long renovation in 2017-18 to upgrade the track? Yeah, enjoy the memory. You want waterfront? You'll have to head downtown towards the reopened Corlears Hook Pedestrian Bridge and its newly upgraded amenities.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**East River Park Closure Alert:** The entire northern section of East River Park, from Houston Street north, is closed as of September 8, 2026, for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. This includes the running track and most access points. Expect major detours and plan alternative routes.
</AlertBox>

### Hudson River Greenway: Mind the Gap

Over on the west side, the Hudson River Greenway bike path has its own little adventure for you. There's a temporary rerouting between W 29th Street and W 30th Street due to the ongoing Gateway Tunnel Project. The path is narrowed, and you might encounter construction trucks. So, keep your head on a swivel, don't assume the right-of-way, and maybe don't try to beat a cement mixer. Your PT knows what happens when you lose that particular game.

### City-Wide Obstacles

Beyond the usual construction ballet, the **UN General Assembly** is in town, which means the already-fun traffic on FDR Drive is going to be even more... *New York*. Expect street closures and general gridlock. If your route involves getting anywhere near the East Side, adjust your commute or better yet, just walk. Seriously.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**UN General Assembly Traffic Impact:** Expect significant street closures and traffic delays on FDR Drive today, September 8, 2026, due to the UN General Assembly. Plan extra travel time or use public transport.
</AlertBox>
