<AlertBox type="warning">
### Extreme Heat Wave Imminent!
Don't let today's pleasant 21.7°C (71°F) fool you. New York City, Long Island, and the lower Hudson Valley are under an Extreme Heat Watch starting July 1st and lasting through July 3rd. We're talking heat index values that could hit a brutal 41-43°C (105-110°F). The National Weather Service is literally telling people to consider canceling outdoor activities if you're in an Extreme Heat Risk area, which we will be. This isn't just "uncomfortable" — it's dangerous, with the potential for heat-related illnesses like heat stroke. NYC Health Commissioner Alister Martin explicitly warned about heat stroke being "fast, deadly, and almost always preventable." Start hydrating *now* and plan on hitting the treadmill or a cooling center later this week.
</AlertBox>

It's not a full closure, but it's a tight squeeze and requires extra vigilance.
