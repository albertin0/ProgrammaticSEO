## Today's NYC Workout Debrief: Run with a Plan (or Don't Run at All)

Let's cut to the chase: the weather gods have blessed us with a phenomenal day to get outside. We've got clear skies and a comfortable 15.1°C. That's practically unheard of for early October in this city, so don't even *think* about complaining about the temperature. Your lungs will thank you for the fresh, if slightly pollen-laden, air. Speaking of, pollen is moderate, and our AQI is sitting at 51, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. For most of you, that's fine. If you’ve got serious asthma or other respiratory issues, maybe keep your intense intervals on the treadmill today, but for the rest of us, it's a green light, mostly.

### Roadblocks and Runners Galore

Here's where it gets complicated. Today is apparently "Let's Close All The Streets" Day in NYC. If you were hoping for a serene long run, tough luck.

Expect significant street closures and serious foot traffic in that area of Washington Heights. Your usual route around Inwood Hill Park might be a nightmare to get to or navigate around.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Brooklyn Runners, don't even think about a casual stroll down Shore Road Promenade.** The Bay Ridge Half Marathon is happening today, starting at 8:00 AM. That means large sections of that beautiful, flat course will be packed with racers. Plan for major detours or join in the cheering squad if you want to avoid being part of the pack.

For example, West 3rd and West 4th Streets between Mercer and Broadway are closed from 7 AM to 2 PM, and East 15th Street near Union Square East is shut down for gas system enhancements. This kind of stuff means unexpected detours and a constant need to check crosswalks.
