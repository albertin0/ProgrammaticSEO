<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Seriously, Stay Indoors Today.
This isn't a drill. The combined force of extreme heat (with a heat index well into the 100s) and compromised air quality makes today genuinely dangerous for outdoor workouts. NYC Emergency Management is telling everyone to limit strenuous outdoor activity, and that means you, too, Marathon Maniac. If you don't have AC, head to a cooling center. Your PR can wait.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Watch Your Breathing and Your Path
Even if you're just doing a quick walk, be aware of the air quality, especially if you're susceptible to respiratory issues. The moderate AQI, coupled with the potential for wildfire smoke adding more PM2.5, means your lungs are working overtime. Also, if you're usually on the **Hudson River Greenway** down by West 29th and 30th Streets, know there's a detour for the Gateway Tunnel Project, narrowing the path. Keep an eye out for construction trucks and don't assume the way is clear.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Consider a Treadmill or Early Morning Stroll (if you must)
If you absolutely *have* to get some steps in, aim for the earliest possible morning hours when temperatures might be *slightly* less oppressive. Even then, keep it short, keep it light, and hydrate like it's your job. Sticking to a climate-controlled gym, however, is the smartest move today. Remember, your body isn't an oven, and Central Park isn't a sauna you want to sweat out a fever in.
</AlertBox>
