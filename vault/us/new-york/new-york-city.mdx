Today's actually pretty decent for getting some miles in, temperature-wise. We're looking at a clear 18.1°C, which is about as perfect as it gets for a September run in this city. Enjoy it while it lasts, because we all know how fast that can change.

But let's talk about what's *always* trying to trip you up: the air. Your general AQI might be sitting at a "moderate" 57, with PM2.5 being the main culprit, meaning most of you won't even notice. However, for those of us with actual respiratory systems that aren't forged from steel and spite, AccuWeather is calling the air "poor" for sensitive groups, particularly due to Nitrogen Dioxide levels. So, if you're prone to wheezing after a brisk walk to the bagel shop, maybe take it easy on that tempo run along the FDR. No one wants to see you gasping for air by the East River.
