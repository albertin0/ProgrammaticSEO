Multiple sections, including River Avenue and East 161st Street, are under a "special event construction embargo" today. That means they're "not available." Not "slightly inconvenient," not "a bit of a detour"—they're just *gone* for your Saturday morning run. So much for that long, straight stretch. Don't bother arguing with the traffic cops; they've heard it all before.

And while today, September 19th, isn't an official "Gridlock Alert Day" for the UN General Assembly, the preparations are well underway. Expect tightening security and rolling closures to start popping up around Midtown Manhattan, especially near **First Avenue** from 41st to 48th Streets. Even if it's not fully locked down, you're looking at increased pedestrian traffic and potential detours that'll turn your tempo run into a frustrated stop-and-go shuffle. Save your Midtown sprints for another day, unless weaving through crowds is your new HIIT workout.
