### Navigating the Concrete Jungle's Obstacles

Here's the real kicker for many of you Manhattan runners and cyclists: construction. The Empire State Trail, specifically the Hudson River Park segment between West 29th Street and West 30th Street, is still impacted by the Manhattan Tunnel Project. They've shifted and narrowed the bikeway to a measly 12 feet and the pedestrian walkway to 8 feet. Expect construction trucks crossing the trail. This isn't just a minor inconvenience; it's a legitimate hazard.
