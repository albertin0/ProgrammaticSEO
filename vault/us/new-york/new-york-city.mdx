## The Cold, Hard Truth

Look, the morning started off overcast and chilly, hovering around 6.2°C (43°F), with a "feels like" temp of 5°C (41°F). It's going to warm up nicely into the 50s and even mid-60s by the afternoon, which sounds lovely, right? Well, don't pack away your waterproof gear just yet. There's a 10% chance of light rain during the day, which escalates to a 75% chance of rain showers tonight. Couple that with southerly winds around 10-15 mph early, shifting southwest, then north as a cold front barrels in, dropping those temps again by evening. So, if you're out for an evening run, you might just get to experience all four seasons in one workout.

On the bright side, your lungs are relatively safe today. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is a decent 43, putting us firmly in the "Good" category. The dominant pollutant is listed as Ozone/Other, but at this level, it’s not something most people need to stress over.

### Major Road Closures & Construction Ahead!
Today, March 26, is the last day of extensive construction embargoes related to "Yankee Stadium Opening Day." These aren't just minor annoyances; we're talking about significant parts of Manhattan, Queens, and even parts of Brooklyn being affected until midnight. Specifically, expect major disruptions on sections of the FDR Drive, 42nd Street, and 7th Avenue. If your usual Central Park perimeter loop involves anything south of 59th Street or any approach via 7th Avenue, you're going to hit a wall of cones and confused tourists. Plan your routes carefully, or better yet, opt for an inner park loop if you're dead set on Central Park. The Brooklyn Bridge-Montgomery Coastal Resilience project also means ongoing utility work and floodwall construction near the FDR Drive and the esplanade.
</AlertBox>
