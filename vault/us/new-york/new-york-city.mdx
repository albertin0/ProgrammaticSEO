## Local Hazards & Head-Ups

### East River Park: Still a Battlefield

Look, I'm going to keep saying it until they actually finish the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, but if you're planning a run through East River Park, be prepared for a fragmented experience. The northern section, including the track and field area and esplanade, is *closed* until further notice due to ongoing construction. You'll find limited access points and partial FDR Drive lane closures. They're even doing nightwork, which can be loud and disruptive, so if you're an early bird trying to beat the heat (not an issue today, thankfully), you might still be greeted by heavy machinery. The section south of Stanton Street is also temporarily out of commission. Stick to the open areas north of Stanton if you insist on that view, or better yet, plan an alternate route.

### Hudson River Park: Mind the Narrowing

If you're a West Side runner, the Hudson River Park Greenway is still undergoing its Manhattan Tunnel Project facelift between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. The bikeway and walkway are narrowed, so expect slower traffic and keep an eye out for construction trucks. Also, Pier 40's Finger Pier, Rooftop Field #1, and Picnic House are all temporarily closed for maintenance or construction. Don't be that person trying to sneak through; respect the closures.

### Queens Parks: Race Day Alert!

Good news for the organized types, but a heads-up for the casual jogger: Flushing Meadows Corona Park is hosting an NYRR Open Run 5K this evening from 6:45 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.. And over in Astoria Park, the W!se 5K Fun Run/Walk is also happening today. If you're not part of these events, expect larger crowds and potential detours around the race routes.

### Pollen's Low-Grade Annoyance

While the air quality itself is stellar, don't forget about that "Moderate" pollen level. If you're prone to seasonal allergies, you know the drill. It's not going to put you in the ER, but a runny nose and itchy eyes can certainly make a long run feel a lot longer.
