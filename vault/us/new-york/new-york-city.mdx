### Navigating the Urban Jungle

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step on the Hudson River Park Greenway!**
Starting this month, the Manhattan Tunnel Project is causing headaches for runners and cyclists on the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail between West 29th Street and West 30th Street. Expect narrowed paths – the bikeway is down to 12 feet, and the walkway to a measly 8. And yes, you'll be sharing that cramped space with construction trucks. Assume they don't see you. This mess is sticking around for about six months, so learn the detours now.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Heads Up for Tomorrow: NYC Half Marathon Embargo**
While it's not *today*, if you're planning a weekend long run, know that the construction embargo for the United Airlines NYC Half Marathon kicks in tomorrow, March 6th, and runs through the 15th. This will affect areas in Brooklyn and Manhattan, including significant portions of **Central Park**'s East and West Drives, Columbus Circle, and other routes. Plan your long runs accordingly.
</AlertBox>

Overall, today's conditions are less than ideal. The combination of damp, chilly air, moderate air quality, and active construction means you need to be extra vigilant. If you're hitting the pavement, stick to well-lit, familiar routes and assume every surface is slick. Good luck out there, you brave (or foolhardy) souls.
