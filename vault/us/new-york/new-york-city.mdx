<AlertBox type="danger">
### Heatstroke and Thunderstorms Aren't a Joke, People!
Seriously, the heat index is no joke. Running in this kind of heat and humidity significantly increases your risk of heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Couple that with the forecast for severe thunderstorms and potential flash flooding this afternoon and evening, and you're actively asking for trouble. Torrential downpours can turn a peaceful jog into a dangerous swim in seconds. Stay inside during peak heat and when those storms roll in.
</AlertBox>

### What to Expect on Your Route (If You Insist)

If you absolutely *must* get out there, be aware that your usual paths might be a bit of a cluster. Beyond the weather, construction projects are still in full swing. For instance, the High Line Western Rail Yards Reconstruction Project is still impacting the area around West Street, closing northbound lanes. So, if your beloved Hudson River Greenway loop takes you through there, expect detours and narrower spaces. Same goes for parts of the FDR Drive, which is seeing lane closures for median reconstruction and the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project. These aren't just traffic headaches; they can snarl pedestrian and bike paths too.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Mind the Construction, Even When It's Not Pouring
Even if you manage to squeeze in a run before the storms, remember the city's a constant construction zone. Keep an eye out for unexpected barriers or narrowed pathways, especially on major routes like the FDR Drive or near the Battery Park Underpass, where flood mitigation work is ongoing. A quick check of DOT advisories before you head out isn't a bad idea.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Hydrate, Adjust, or Just Stay Home!
Your best bet today is an indoor workout. Hit the gym, find a spin class, or literally just walk laps in your air-conditioned apartment. If you absolutely *have* to be outside, keep it short, stick to the shade, go early (like, *really* early), and hydrate like it's your job. Wear light, loose clothing. And if you feel any dizziness, nausea, or excessive fatigue, stop immediately. It’s not worth it.
</AlertBox>
