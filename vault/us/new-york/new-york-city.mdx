### East River Park: Plan a New Route, Seriously.
The East Side Coastal Resiliency Project is a marathon, not a sprint, and it's still eating up huge chunks of prime running real estate. The northern half of **East River Park** is a no-go zone, and the greenway is completely closed 24/7. If you usually run this stretch, find an alternate. Your joints will thank you for not navigating endless detours and questionable temporary paths, and your lungs will appreciate not breathing construction dust during "utility work, median demolition, and floodwall construction" that's happening overnight. If you're heading to the southern amenities, you'll need to use the Delancey Street Bridge or the Corlears Hook Bridge. Just assume it's a mess and go somewhere else.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Moderate Air & Allergens: Listen to Your Body
With a moderate AQI of 65 and moderate pollen, this isn't the day to try for a PR if you're sensitive. While it's certainly better than the wildfire apocalypse we dealt with back in July, active children, adults, and people with lung conditions like asthma should still consider reducing prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. If your throat feels scratchy or your nose is running before you even hit the first mile, ease up. Hydrate, and maybe stick to a recovery pace.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Recreation Center Closures
If you were hoping to hit the track or pool at the Chelsea Recreation Center today, tough luck. It's closed for scheduled repairs and will be closed tomorrow for Labor Day. It reopens on Tuesday, September 8th. Always check your local rec center's status before you trek across town.
</AlertBox>

So, if you're heading out, enjoy the cool temps, but remember to be smart about where you're putting those miles in. This city's always got something to throw at you.
