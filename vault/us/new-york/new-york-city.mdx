#### Air Quality Health Advisory
An Air Quality Health Advisory for ground-level ozone is in effect. While the initial data showed a good AQI, official advisories from the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation indicate levels are expected to exceed 100. If you have asthma, other respiratory issues, or are generally sensitive, your lungs will be working harder. Pair this with the heat, and you've got a recipe for misery.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Stay Hydrated (Indoors) and Stay Cool (Period)
If you absolutely *must* go outside for any reason, do it in the early morning or late evening, but honestly, just don't. Hydrate constantly (water, not booze, even though it's a holiday). Seek out air-conditioned spaces. The city has cooling centers open; use them. And forget that long run along the Hudson River Greenway this afternoon; NYPD closures and restrictions are in effect for the America's 250th Celebration.
</AlertBox>

Today is not the day to prove your toughness. It's the day to be smart and stay safe indoors. Your lungs and joints (and everything else) will thank you.
