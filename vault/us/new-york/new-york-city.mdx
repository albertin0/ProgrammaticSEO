### Trail & Road Hazards

This is where things get... *New York*. Don't expect a clear shot if your usual running grounds are near the water or downtown.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Heads up, waterfront warriors! The **East River Greenway** is still a mess thanks to the Brooklyn Bridge – Montgomery Coastal Resilience (BMCR) project. You'll find temporary esplanade width reductions, traffic shifts at South Street and Robert F. Wagner Sr. Place, and outright closures of the bike path between Dover Street and Catherine Slip. There are detours, but they're not always intuitive. Don't go on autopilot.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
Up on the west side, if you're hitting the **Hudson River Park** segment of the Empire State Trail, be aware of the Manhattan Tunnel Project. Starting this month, you're looking at detours, narrowed bikeways and walkways between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. Expect construction trucks to be crossing, so keep your head up and not buried in your Strava splits. This ain't a six-month commitment for nothing.
</AlertBox>

And for those of you navigating the Lower East Side streets, the Parallel Conveyance (PC) project is still chugging along. Expect road closures on spots like Jackson Street between Water Street and South Street during work hours. You might get some extra noise and dust with deep sewer excavation, too. Basically, assume every path has a detour or an obstacle and you won't be disappointed.
