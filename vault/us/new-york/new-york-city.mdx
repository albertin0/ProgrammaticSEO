## The Rundown: Rain, Chill, and Sneaky Hazards

Look, I love running in this city as much as the next masochist, but today? Today is a slog. We're in for rounds of rain all day, which will be light to moderate for the most part, but with periods of heavier stuff, especially into Monday morning. Combine that with a stiff breeze that could gust up to 30 mph, especially near the water, and you’re looking at a raw, damp experience. The good news is, widespread flash flooding isn't expected, but localized ponding on roads and paths is definitely on the menu. Remember those insane floods from just a few days ago that turned Queens streets into rivers? Our sewer system got overwhelmed. While we're not expecting that level of chaos today, it means there could be lingering slick spots or debris from previous storms.

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Watch Your Step, Especially on the Hudson River Greenway
Expect slippery conditions everywhere, from sidewalks to park paths. Areas like the Hudson River Greenway will be particularly exposed to the wind and rain, making the smooth pavement a slip-and-slide if you're not careful. Stay alert for standing water, wet leaves, and even slick manhole covers.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Stay Away From Ocean Beaches!
The New York City Office of Emergency Management has issued a **beach warning for life-threatening rip currents** all Memorial Day Weekend. A High Surf Advisory is also in effect for Brooklyn, Queens, and Long Island, with waves of 5-10 feet expected today. This is not the weekend for a beach run. Seriously.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Gear Up or Stay In
If you *must* venture out, layer up with waterproof and wind-resistant gear. A hat or visor will help keep the rain out of your eyes. Proper traction on your shoes is non-negotiable. Consider a high-visibility vest; the grey, rainy conditions reduce visibility for drivers and cyclists. Hydrate, even if it doesn't feel hot. And honestly, a nice indoor workout might be your best bet today.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction & Closures Continue
While the Port Authority paused *some* non-emergency construction for the holiday weekend, general road and bridge work around the city is still ongoing. Check the NYC DOT website if your route takes you near major bridges or through busy streets; you might encounter unexpected detours or narrowed paths.
</AlertBox>
