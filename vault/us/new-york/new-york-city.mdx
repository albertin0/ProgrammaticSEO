## Why today is/isn't the day:

<BulletList>
*   **Air Quality is Sketchy for Some:** While the skies are clear and the temperature is mild at 11.8°C (around 53°F), the Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 55 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant. This might seem "moderate" on some scales, but AccuWeather's forecast for today explicitly states the air is "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." So, if you've got asthma or any respiratory sensitivities, you're rolling the dice.
*   **Pollen is Hanging Around:** We've got a "Moderate" pollen level, which isn't going to kill you, but combined with the iffy AQI, it's just another irritant your respiratory system has to contend with.
*   **Construction Chaos Continues:** Our beloved city is always under construction, and today is no exception. Major projects like the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project mean heavy machinery, potential noise, and significant closures along the East River Park. If you were planning to cruise the East River Greenway, be ready for substantial detours and possibly loud activities, even overnight.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI Warning for Sensitive Groups:** Today's AQI of 55, with PM2.5 as the main culprit, is considered "Poor" and "unhealthy for sensitive groups." This means if you have respiratory issues, heart conditions, or are prone to allergies, you should seriously limit your outdoor exertion. Short-term exposure to PM2.5 can aggravate asthma and cause difficulty breathing or throat irritation. Don't be a hero; take it inside.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step (and Your Route) Near the Waterfront:** Construction is impacting multiple popular running routes. The East River Greenway through East River Park has been closed 24/7 since last September due to the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, forcing detours. Similarly, the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street has a narrowed bikeway (12 feet) and walkway (8 feet) due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project, with construction trucks crossing the trail. You'll encounter barriers and bollards, so pay attention and don't assume your usual path is clear.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Dress for Success:** Despite the clear skies, it's only 11.8°C (53°F). That's prime running weather if you're layering right, but don't head out in just a tank top unless you're built different. A light long-sleeve and a windbreaker will serve you well, especially near the water.
</AlertBox>
