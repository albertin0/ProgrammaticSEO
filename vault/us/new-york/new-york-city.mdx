**Construction Chaos: Brooklyn Bridge & Hudson River Park**

Don't expect smooth sailing if your route takes you over the Brooklyn Bridge or along the Hudson River Greenway. The DOT’s weekly advisory has the bikeway and a Manhattan-bound lane on the Brooklyn Bridge closed nightly from 9 PM to 5 AM until September 3rd. Cyclists, you're being shunted to the promenade, which means it’s a shared path. Translation: be extra aware, both for yourself and the oblivious tourists. Further north, the Empire State Trail in Hudson River Park between West 29th and West 30th Streets is narrowed, and you might have construction trucks crossing your path. Your commute, run, or ride is going to involve more dodging than usual. Keep your head up, your music down, and remember that orange cones mean "don't be an idiot."
</AlertBox>

**City Hall Park Protest Today**

If your workout takes you through that area around rush hour, plan for potential delays and re-route if you want to avoid the crowds. Show solidarity if you can, but know it's happening.
