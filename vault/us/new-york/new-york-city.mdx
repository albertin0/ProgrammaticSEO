## Air Quality Breakdown: Don't Breathe Too Deeply

The good ol' AQI (Air Quality Index) is showing 65 for PM2.5, which usually just means "moderate." But here’s the kicker: The advisory for today, June 5th, is specifically for ground-level ozone, and that sucker is expected to push the AQI over 100 during the bulk of the day. Ozone is particularly nasty, forming from car exhaust and industrial emissions cooking in the sun. It’s not as visible as wildfire smoke, but it’ll make your lungs work overtime.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Ozone Alert in Effect!** For anyone with respiratory issues (asthma, COPD), heart conditions, young kids, or the elderly, **DO NOT** engage in strenuous outdoor activity between 11 AM and 11 PM. Even healthy individuals should significantly reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors. Seriously, move that workout indoors.
</AlertBox>
