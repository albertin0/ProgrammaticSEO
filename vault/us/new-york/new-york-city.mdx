*   **Goldilocks Weather:** Temps are perfect at 17.5°C (63.5°F) and it's clear out. A genuinely pleasant day to log some miles without sweating through your socks by mile two or freezing your digits off.
*   **Air's Fair, Mostly:** While the overall AQI is moderate at 60, the detailed breakdown shows PM2.5, PM10, and most other pollutants are actually "good." Ozone peaks around 1 PM, so maybe push that lunchtime sprint to later if you're sensitive.
*   **Construction Chaos (Still):** NYC never sleeps, and neither does its construction. Expect detours and closures, especially if you're an early bird or night owl tackling some major routes.

Look, the city isn't trying to stop you from hitting your stride, but it certainly isn't making it easy in some spots. While the skies are mostly clear and the temperature is mild for late August, don't just blindly follow your usual route, especially if you're on two wheels. The Department of Transportation is doing its annual dance of closing off vital arteries for various projects, and today is no different.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Brooklyn Bridge Bike Path Detour:** Attention all cyclists and early morning runners! The Brooklyn Bridge bikeway and the left Manhattan-bound lane are closed from 9 PM to 5 AM through September 3rd. If you’re planning an overnight or pre-dawn workout across the bridge, you'll be directed to a *shared* promenade. Keep your head on a swivel; pedestrians and cyclists don't always mix well, and nobody wants a collision before sunrise.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the bridges, the **Hudson River Greenway** from West 29th to West 30th streets has its bike path temporarily re-routed due to the Gateway Tunnel Project. Stick to the designated path, and for the love of all that is holy, don't try to bike or scooter on the esplanades or piers. Those rules exist for a reason, and it’s not just to annoy you. Similarly, down on the Lower East Side, the area around **Corlears Hook Park** and Jackson Street is seeing ongoing sewer installation work. This means intermittent road closures, traffic detours, and parking limitations. If you usually run or cycle through there, be prepared to navigate around flaggers and construction equipment.

The good news? The major Summer Streets closures wrapped up on Saturday, so those routes are generally clear now, albeit back to normal vehicle traffic.

As for the air, your lungs should be mostly okay today. The AQI of 60 puts us squarely in the "Moderate" category, which is generally acceptable for most folks. However, if you're one of those sensitive types – maybe you've got some underlying respiratory issues, or you just prefer your air crisp and pristine – keep an eye on that ozone, which is expected to peak mid-day.
