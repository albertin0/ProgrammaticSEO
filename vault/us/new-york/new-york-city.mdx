## Today's NYC Grind: More Gumbo, Less Glide

Look, I'm all for dedication, but sometimes the city just isn't cooperating. Today is one of those days. The weather forecast for New York City is basically a broken record of "rain, rain, and more rain," with a stiff breeze thrown in for good measure. It's not exactly inspiring that sunrise run on the Hudson River Greenway when you know you're going to be battling wind and a constant drizzle.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Ponding & Poor Visibility:** With continuous rain, expect significant ponding on roadways and trails, particularly underpasses and low-lying areas. Visibility will also be reduced, making early morning or evening runs trickier, especially with city traffic.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & Closures: The Usual Obstacles

As usual, construction is a never-ending saga in this city. While Governor Hochul announced a suspension of non-emergency road and bridge construction on *state* highways for Memorial Day weekend to ease travel, the city's streets are still very much a minefield. The NYC Department of Transportation’s advisories show a healthy dose of street and lane closures for various projects across Manhattan and Brooklyn.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Plan Your Route (and Your Gear) Carefully:** If you’re determined to get out, wear waterproof layers, reflective gear, and shoes with good grip. Consider sticking to routes you know well to avoid unexpected detours into construction zones or deeper puddles. The Southbound Henry Hudson Parkway exit ramp to West 79th Street, for example, is facing closures that could impact access to the Greenway.
</AlertBox>

Your knees will thank you for taking it easy today, or at least opting for that "Cardio Circuit Blast" class at Thomas Jefferson Recreation Center instead of a long, miserable slog through the elements.
