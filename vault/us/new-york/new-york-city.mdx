### Air Quality & Allergens

The AQI sits at a "moderate" 79 today, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) being the dominant pollutant. The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation has issued an Air Quality Health Advisory, specifically cautioning "active children, adults, and people with lung diseases such as asthma" to "reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors." So, if you're planning a long run, or if your lungs are already feeling a bit off, listen to that advisory. Pair that with moderate pollen levels, and you've got a recipe for sniffles and a tight chest.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality Health Advisory for Sensitive Groups:** While the general public might not feel much, if you have asthma or other respiratory conditions, consider modifying or moving your workout indoors. PM2.5 can irritate your lungs and airways.
</AlertBox>
