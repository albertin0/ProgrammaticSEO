### Don't Breathe Too Deeply

Look, 18 degrees Celsius and clear skies? That's about as good as it gets for running in this city. But before you lace up and decide to PR, remember those lungs. The AQI is clocking in at 51, which is "Moderate" and means "acceptable, but moderate health concern for very small number of people who are unusually sensitive." And if you're one of the many New Yorkers who treats pollen like a personal enemy, today's "Moderate" levels are just another reason to keep your inhaler handy. It's not a full-blown "stay inside and hermetically seal yourself" situation like we've seen on worse PM2.5 days earlier this year, but it's enough to irritate if you're pushing hard.

### Obstacle Course NYC Edition

Now, about those "clear skies." Clear doesn't mean clear *paths*. On a Saturday like today, May 17th, you've got a couple of things to watch out for if you're pounding the pavement or trying to enjoy a long ride.

First off, the northern stretch of **East River Park** is still an absolute war zone, completely closed from Houston Street upwards for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, and that includes the running track. They're not reopening that until the end of 2026, so if you were hoping for loops there, tough luck. You can still access some southern sections below Houston Street, and the Corlears Hook Pedestrian Bridge is open if you want to brave that route.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Construction Zone Ahead (and Everywhere):** The city's perpetual state of improvement means closures. Beyond East River Park, expect detours along the **Hudson River Park** segment of the Empire State Trail between West 29th and West 30th Streets due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. The path is narrowed, and you might even encounter construction trucks crossing. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

Then there's the planned event chaos. While the massive RBC Brooklyn Half was yesterday (and apparently had some temperature swings itself, peaking warmer at the finish line), there's still the 24th Annual NYPD Memorial 5K Run/Walk kicking off this morning at Liberty Street and West Street. That means localized disruptions in the Battery Park City area, especially near the Esplanade where the memorial service is happening. If you're down there, expect crowds and potentially blocked access.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Early Bird Alert:** If you're an extreme early riser, the **FDR Drive Northbound** between East 61st and East 96th Streets was fully closed until 7 AM this morning for median reconstruction. Hope you planned your long runs around that.
</AlertBox>

So, yeah, enjoy the mild weather, but stay aware. This city gives and takes.
