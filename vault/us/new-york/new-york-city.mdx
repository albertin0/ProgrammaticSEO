## Hazards & Headaches

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Air Quality Advisory for Sensitive Groups
Despite the "Good" AQI of 49, the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation has issued an **Air Quality Health Advisory for New York City**. What does that mean for you? If you're an active kid, an adult with a lung disease like asthma, or just generally sensitive, you need to "reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors." Don't shrug it off. Even if the air *looks* clear, there might be microscopic crud floating around that'll irritate your airways.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Chaos on the Waterfront
Thinking of a nice, uninterrupted run along the water? Think again. The **East River Greenway** is still a piecemeal mess. There's a persistent closure around 74th Street, forcing runners to detour off the path from 71st to 78th Street. And don't even get me started on the lower section of **East River Park** south of Stanton Street – it's completely closed for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project.

Over on the west side, the **Hudson River Park** segment of the **Empire State Trail** between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street is narrowed, and you might be dodging construction trucks. Keep your head on a swivel. Your PR isn't worth a fender bender with a dump truck.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Plan Your Route, Especially Today
With these detours and advisories, blindly heading out the door is a recipe for frustration (or worse, an asthma attack). If you're planning a long run, check the NYC DOT's advisories for street closures – there are several around Manhattan for crane operations and utility work that might impact sidewalk access. Better yet, hit one of the many organized runs, like the "Forest Fitness" in **Fort Tryon Park** or the NYRR Open Runs at Canarsie Park or Inwood Hill Park. At least then you know someone else has hopefully scoped out the path.
</AlertBox>
