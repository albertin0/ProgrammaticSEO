## The Rundown

Today's shaping up to be one of those near-perfect New York days for outdoor activity. The air is ridiculously clean, and the temperature is just right for everything from a leisurely stroll to pushing the pace on your long run. Go enjoy it, but don't just blindly follow your usual route, especially if you're near the water or Midtown.

### Navigating the Concrete Jungle

While the air is crisp, there are still a few urban hurdles to be aware of. The Hudson River Greenway is still dealing with that Gateway Tunnel Project nonsense between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. The bike path is narrowed to a meager 12 feet, and the pedestrian path isn't much better at 8 feet. Don't be surprised if you're dodging a construction truck or two. It's a bottleneck, so keep your head up and don't assume everyone sees you.

Speaking of crowded areas, if you're planning on being anywhere near Times Square today, be advised that "Solstice in Times Square: Mind Over Madness Yoga" is taking over the Broadway pedestrian plazas. While good for the soul, it's not ideal for setting a PR. Find an alternate route or just embrace the zen and walk through.

And heads up for tomorrow: Monday, June 22nd, is a World Cup Gridlock Alert Day. While today is Sunday, expect traffic to start getting funky in anticipation, especially around Midtown. Plan your commutes to and from your workout spots accordingly. Don't let your perfect run get ruined by a bus re-route or an unexpected street closure.
