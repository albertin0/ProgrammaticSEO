## The City's a Washout: Stay Indoors

Look, I get it. You've got miles to log, gains to make, or just need to clear your head. But today, March 16, 2026, Mother Nature is actively trying to thwart your efforts. We're looking at moderate rain that's going to get heavier, turning into thunderstorms, especially later this afternoon and evening. And don't even think about that scenic sprint over the Brooklyn Bridge; a Wind Advisory is active from 2 PM today through tomorrow morning, with wind gusts possibly exceeding 60 mph. Trying to hold a pace against that kind of headwind isn't dedication, it's just asking for an ankle sprain or worse.

The New York City Emergency Management Department (NYCEM) has even issued a Weather Alert and activated the City's Flash Flood Emergency Plan. Translation: low-lying areas, underpasses, and anywhere with poor drainage is going to be a soupy mess. And while the United Airlines NYC Half Marathon was yesterday, Sunday, March 15th, taking over parts of Brooklyn and Central Park, today's weather would've made that race a nightmare. Just imagine those conditions if you're trying to navigate the Hudson River Greenway with strong winds trying to push you into the water.

### Severe Weather Alert: High Winds & Flash Flooding Expected
Seriously strong winds, heavy downpours, severe thunderstorms, and the risk of flash flooding are all on the menu today. Forget PRs, focus on not getting knocked off your feet or submerged. Avoid exposed areas like bridges, waterfronts, and park paths.

While major marathon-related closures from yesterday's NYC Half are likely cleared, ongoing construction for things like the Queensboro Bridge steel repair and various utility installations mean you might still encounter unexpected detours and uneven surfaces. Add heavy rain to that, and every curb cut becomes a slip-and-fall waiting to happen.
