<AlertBox type="danger">
### UNHEALTHY AIR QUALITY FOR ALL NEW YORKERS
The Air Quality Index (AQI) is at 200 for fine particulate matter (PM2.5) across the NYC Metro region, classified as "Unhealthy." This is due to Canadian wildfire smoke and high temperatures. Everyone is advised to limit strenuous and prolonged outdoor activities. Sensitive groups should avoid outdoor activity altogether.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mask Up if You Must Go Out
If you absolutely *have* to be outside for more than a quick dash, wear a well-fitting KN95 or N95 mask. No, your cloth mask won't cut it against PM2.5. Keep those windows closed and set your AC to recirculate indoor air.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Construction Zone Ahead (As Always)
While not directly related to air quality, remember ongoing construction projects like the Roosevelt Avenue Bridge rehabilitation or the Battery Park Underpass flood mitigation could add localized dust to the already poor air. Keep an eye out for detours and potential hazards if you're navigating the city.
</AlertBox>
