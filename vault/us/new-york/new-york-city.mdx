## Local Hazards & Headaches

### Construction Chaos Continues
Just because the sky is clear doesn't mean the city stopped tearing itself apart. Central Park, bless its sprawling heart, is still a minefield of restoration and repaving. Grand Army Plaza South and the West 85th Street Playground are currently closed for restoration, and don't forget NYC DOT has been milling and repaving the Drives north of East 90th Street on weeknights. Tread lightly, especially if you're out early, and watch for those uneven surfaces that will absolutely wreck your stride and potentially your ankles.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch Your Step in Central Park:** Construction on the Central Park Drives (north of East 90th Street) is still a thing. Cyclists and pedestrians need to exercise extra caution. Also, Grand Army Plaza South is still closed for restoration, meaning detours are your friend. Don't be that person who ignores the signs and ends up tripping over rebar.
</AlertBox>

Over in the East Village, the East Side Coastal Resiliency (ESCR) project continues its reign. The east end of Corlears Hook Park remains closed for construction, so expect detours there too. It's all for a good cause, eventually, but right now, it's just more obstacles for your workout.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Post-Storm Slick Spots:** Remember those "localized flash flood threats" from Thursday and Friday? Even with clear skies today, some low-lying areas or unpaved trails might still be wet, muddy, or have lingering debris. Keep an eye out for slick spots and puddles, especially if you're trail running or hitting less-maintained paths.
</AlertBox>

## My Two Cents
Today's a genuinely good day for getting outside, especially compared to the last few. The air is clean, the temperature is fantastic, and you won't melt into the asphalt. Just don't let the pleasant weather lull you into complacency. Pay attention to your body, especially if pollen usually gets to you, and watch out for those endless construction zones that New York City seems to love more than a good bagel. Enjoy it while it lasts, because who knows what tomorrow brings.
