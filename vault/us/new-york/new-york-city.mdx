<AlertBox type="warning">
### Don't Trust That Low AQI Reading
While some general reports might show a low AQI, there's a specific Air Quality Advisory for New York City today due to elevated ground-level ozone. This means if you have asthma, other respiratory conditions, or are in a sensitive group (children, elderly, pregnant individuals, outdoor workers), you should seriously consider reducing strenuous outdoor activity. That "clear" sky is cooking up some lung irritants. Take breaks, listen to your body, and maybe reschedule that long run for another day.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Pollen's Annoying, Not Debilitating
Moderate pollen means your standard allergy meds should keep you mostly in the clear. Still, if you're prone to seasonal sniffles, maybe opt for routes with less greenery today, or wear some sunglasses to keep the pollen out of your eyes.
</AlertBox>

### Trail & Road Hazards

<AlertBox type="danger">
### Hudson River Park Closures & Construction
Runners and cyclists on the West Side should be aware that Pier 40's Finger Pier, Rooftop Field #1, and Picnic House are all temporarily closed. More significantly, there's ongoing landscape and esplanade design construction between W 29th and W 44th Street. This isn't just a minor detour; it's a big project in a heavily used section of the park. Expect disruptions and plan alternative routes if this is your usual stomping ground. Don't be that person trying to muscle past construction cones.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Citywide Construction Continues
NYC DOT has issued a preliminary weekend traffic advisory (which includes today, Friday) listing numerous street closures across Manhattan for things like crane operations, building inspections, and water main work. While these aren't trail closures, they can impact your approach to major parks or create unexpected detours on city streets. Stay vigilant, look for signage, and don't assume your usual path is clear.
</AlertBox>
