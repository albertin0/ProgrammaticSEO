Today is one of those rare New York summer days where you don't immediately regret stepping outside. The air is clean, the temperature is mild – a welcome respite from the usual July swamp. But this is NYC, so of course, there are still a few urban hurdles to navigate.

### Air Quality & Allergies

The air quality index is a solid 50, labeled as "Good," which means your lungs get a break today. We've had a few dicey days earlier this month with wildfire smoke rolling in from Canada, making visibility hazy and breathing tougher, but for July 23rd, those conditions have cleared. Don't get complacent, though; always check before you head out.

### Trail Talk: Hudson River Greenway & Beyond

For those of you hitting the **Hudson River Greenway**, be advised: the never-ending construction saga continues. There are active re-routes and narrowed sections between W 29th and W 30th streets due to the Gateway Tunnel Project and other ongoing work. The bikeway has been squeezed to 12 feet and the walkway to 8 feet, which, let's be real, is just enough space for two people and a very determined pigeon. You might also encounter construction vehicles crossing the trail, so keep your head on a swivel and don't assume right-of-way. Just north of that, the Pier 40 Finger Pier and Picnic House are also closed.

If you're heading downtown, the Battery Park Underpass is undergoing flood mitigation construction. While pedestrian and bike paths remain open, you're advised to use caution, especially if you're traveling during the late-night/early morning weekday hours when vehicular tube closures are in effect.
