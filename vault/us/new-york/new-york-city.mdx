### Joints: Mind Your Steps (and the Horses)

Weather-wise, it's clear and sitting at a comfortable 20.2°C (around 68°F) to start, with highs expected to hit 22-28°C later. A nice break after yesterday's scorcher, which saw "feels like" temperatures of 105°F. But don't let the pleasant morning fool you.

Now, for the fun part: dodging literal hazards. If your usual route takes you through **Central Park**, listen up. There have been *multiple* horse-related incidents recently, including a fatal one just last month near 72nd Street and West Drive, where a bolted horse collided with another carriage. That's not exactly conducive to a zen run. Keep your head on a swivel, people. Also, watch for overnight repaving on the Central Park Drives north of East 90th Street; they're open during the day, but caution is still a good idea.

Down south, the northern stretch of **East River Park** (from Houston Street to 10th Street) is still completely shut down for the East Side Coastal Resiliency Project, and it's not reopening until the end of 2026. That means no loops on that running track for a long, long time. Plan alternate routes if that was your jam.

Elsewhere, the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail has some narrowing and detours between West 29th and West 30th Streets due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. And while the big Midtown building scare at 235 East 42nd Street that caused evacuations last week is mostly contained to a couple of streets, it's a reminder that construction is *everywhere*. Good thing the FIFA World Cup embargo means some non-emergency construction is on hold in parts of Manhattan until July 20th. Small mercies.
