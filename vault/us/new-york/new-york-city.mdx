### Construction & Events: The Usual NYC Obstacle Course

If you're planning a run along the Hudson River Park, specifically between W. 29th and W. 30th Street, be aware that the Manhattan Tunnel Project is causing detours and narrowing the Empire State Trail starting this month. You'll be dealing with shifted bikeways, narrowed walkways, and potentially construction trucks crossing the path. Use caution there, folks.

Down in the Lower East Side, if Corlears Hook Park is part of your route, know that the Parallel Conveyance project is still doing its thing. Expect sewer work, road closures (like Jackson Street between Water Street and South Street during work hours), noise, and dust. Flaggers are usually on site, but stay alert.
