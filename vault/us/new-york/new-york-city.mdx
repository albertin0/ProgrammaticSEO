### Look out for what's coming

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### Heads up: Storms and Flooding Incoming!
Don't get complacent with today's calm. Starting late tonight and rolling hard into Tuesday, we're looking at "First Alert Weather Day" conditions. Expect heavy rain, strong-to-severe thunderstorms, and a very real threat of flash flooding. We're talking 1-2 inches of rain *per hour* in some spots. If you were planning a long run on the East River path for tomorrow, consider the treadmill. Seriously, don't be that person caught in a downpour trying to outrun a flash flood. The ground is already saturated from past storms, which will only make the flooding worse.
</AlertBox>

### Construction & City Shenanigans

Beyond the weather, it's still New York City, which means constant construction and a delightful game of "dodge the detour."

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Mind the Construction Zones
Various street closures are in effect around the city. For example, East 10th Street between Avenue D and FDR Service Road is closed for bridge construction until mid-August. The Battery Park Underpass is seeing weeknight full tube closures for flood mitigation work through the end of the month. Always check the DOT's weekly advisories before you head out, especially if your route takes you through lower Manhattan or Brooklyn. You don't want to find yourself rerouting mid-run because a crane decided to take up your favorite sidewalk.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Transit Troubles
If you rely on public transport to get to your favorite running spot, be aware of the MTA's weekend advisories (even though it's Monday, these things linger). The 1 train isn't running between 14 St and South Ferry, and the 2 train has no service between 34 St-Penn Station and Brooklyn. Plan your routes carefully, or you'll be adding extra miles before you even start your actual workout.
</AlertBox>

Today is your chance to get those miles in with relatively good weather. But be smart about it, stay hydrated, and for the love of all that is holy, check the forecast *again* for Tuesday. Your joints (and sanity) will thank you.
