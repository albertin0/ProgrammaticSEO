### Lungs: Prepare for the Usual City Grime & Flora

Look, it's New York. Our air is rarely pristine, and today's AQI of 60, dominated by PM2.5, is squarely in the "Moderate" zone. That means if you've got any kind of respiratory issue – asthma, allergies, or just a general aversion to breathing in tiny particulate matter – you might notice it. Couple that with a "Moderate" pollen count, and you've got a recipe for some sniffles and maybe a bit of a throat tickle. It's not "stay indoors and seal the windows" bad, but it's definitely not "take a deep, refreshing breath of mountain air" good either. If you're planning a long run, especially anywhere near high traffic, consider scaling back or sticking to less exposed routes.
