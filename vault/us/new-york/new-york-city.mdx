### Today’s Workout Vibe

The air quality, at a lovely 30 AQI with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant, is about as good as it gets in this concrete jungle. You can breathe easy, literally. The clear skies and temperate 18.5°C mean you won't be baking or shivering out there. It’s pretty much perfect weather for a long run or a solid bike ride through **Central Park** or along the waterfront.

However, don't just blindly follow that perfect weather report. Today's "Moderate" pollen levels, driven by pesky grass, are still enough to mess with your allergies. If you’re one of the many New Yorkers whose eyes itch and nose runs at the mere mention of a blade of grass, take your meds *before* you head out. Don’t wait until you’re halfway across the Brooklyn Bridge, wondering if you’re coming down with something or just experiencing spring in full, allergenic glory.

### Local Hazards & Annoyances

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Grass Pollen is Active:** Despite the overall "none" reported by one source, others indicate moderate to high grass pollen today. This is peak season for it. If you're running through Prospect Park's meadows or any other grassy areas, be prepared. Grass pollen can reach high concentrations in parks and residential areas during June and July.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hudson River Greenway Detour:** If your route takes you along the Hudson River Park segment of the Empire State Trail between W. 29th and W. 30th Street, be aware that the bikeway is narrowed to 12 feet and the walkway to 8 feet due to ongoing tunnel project work. You might also encounter construction trucks crossing the trail, so stay alert. This has been going on since March and is expected to last about six months.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**East River Park Track Closure:** For those who rely on the East River Park track, a heads-up: it's slated to be closed through 2026 for rebuilding. Plan your track workouts elsewhere for the foreseeable future.
