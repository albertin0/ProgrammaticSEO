## The Rundown for Your Run:

Look, the sun's out and it's clear, which is a rare enough treat this time of year. But "clear" doesn't mean "perfect."

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Beware the Morning Chill:** If you're an early bird, those lower 40s (or colder, upstate-style temps in the northern 'burbs) mean proper layers are non-negotiable. Your hamstrings and calves will thank you. Think warm-up, dynamic stretching, and don't skimp on the long sleeves. You'll shed layers mid-run, but starting cold is a recipe for pulls and strains.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Governors Island and Hudson River Park Are Hotbeds (of people):** If your go-to long run takes you through Governors Island, understand that the NYCRUNS Haunted Island 5K means those paths are going to be packed this morning. Same goes for the Shatterproof Walk participants around Pier 76 in Hudson River Park. Find an alternate route or just accept you'll be dodging a lot of folks.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Air Quality & Lung Love:** An AQI of 94 with PM2.5 is something to acknowledge, especially if you're pushing serious mileage. If you've got asthma or are just feeling a bit congested, maybe scale back the intensity or stick to shorter intervals today. Listen to your lungs; they're not asking for much, just a little respect.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction Chaos Continues (mostly at night):** While not impacting daytime runs much, remember that city-wide resurfacing and various smaller closures are always happening. Just stay alert, keep your head up, and try not to trip over a stray orange cone.
