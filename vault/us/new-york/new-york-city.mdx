### On the Trails & Streets: More of the Usual Gridlock

If you're planning a run along the **Hudson River Greenway**, specifically between West 29th and West 30th Streets, prepare for a squeeze. The "Manhattan Tunnel Project" is still at it, narrowing both the bikeway and walkway. You might even catch a delightful view of construction trucks crossing the trail. This isn't a new development – it's been going on since March and is slated for six months – but it's a constant reminder that even our dedicated running paths aren't sacred ground from the city's endless building spree.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Heads Up on the Hudson River Greenway:** Watch your step (and your bike handlebars) between W. 29th and W. 30th Streets. Construction for the Manhattan Tunnel Project means narrower paths and potential truck crossings. Keep your head on a swivel.
</AlertBox>

Elsewhere, the city's general construction grind continues. While most of the recent NYC DOT advisories are for vehicular traffic (think lane closures on the Henry Hudson Parkway or the Park Avenue Tunnel), it's a good reminder that street-level running always requires extra vigilance.

No major running events are locking down entire boroughs today, thankfully. The big Brooklyn Half is tomorrow, so our streets aren't quite as chaotic yet. And don't bother showing up for "Restoration Fridays at Highland Park" – that got cancelled. Probably for the best; less chance of accidentally becoming part of a "native plant restoration."

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pollen Precaution:** With moderate pollen levels, sensitive individuals should consider taking allergy meds before heading out, especially if you're planning a longer session. A shower afterward might help too.
</AlertBox>

Overall, pull on those running shoes. Just remember to dodge the cones and occasionally wave at a construction worker. It's New York, after all.
