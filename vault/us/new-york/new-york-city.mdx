## Today's NYC Running Rundown

Look, if you're not planning on running straight up 5th Avenue, you're in for a decent outdoor workout today. That moderate pollen might try to mess with your sinuses, but the "good" air quality (AQI 36) means your lungs will generally be happy. The clear, cool weather is practically begging you to get outside.

However, a word to the wise: the NYC DOT's got a whole laundry list of street and lane closures this week. Beyond the parade, there's ongoing median reconstruction on the FDR Drive, so if you're usually flying down that, be prepared for potential single and double-lane closures, especially late night and early morning. And if you were eyeing the Hudson River Greenway, remember that construction from last year might still be forcing pedestrians into the bike lane in some sections. It's a disaster waiting to happen if you're not paying attention.

### Construction Congestion & Parade Prep

Seriously, the Department of Transportation's weekly advisory is thicker than a cheap protein shake. From the West 55th Street Bridge to the Park Avenue Tunnel, there's always something getting torn up or put back together. So, even if you avoid the parade's specific route around 5th Avenue between 55th Street and 74th Street, be aware that side streets and major arteries could have unexpected detours. Don't be that runner who gets surprised by a giant crane or a blocked sidewalk. Plan your route, check for local alerts before you head out, and for the love of all that is holy, pay attention to your surroundings.
