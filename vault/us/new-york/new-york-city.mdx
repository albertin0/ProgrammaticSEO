## Today's Rundown

The air quality is genuinely fantastic, so no excuses there. That 37 AQI means your respiratory system is getting a break, which is more than we can usually say for this city. So, if you manage to avoid freezing your earlobes off, your lungs will thank you.

But let's talk about that temperature. We're hovering around freezing, and while it's clear, that doesn't make it warm. It's a day for layers, gloves, and probably a buff over your face if you're out for anything more than a brisk walk. Remember that brutal winter we just "finished"? Don't get caught out thinking it's suddenly shorts weather.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Chilly Temps Mean Smart Choices:** At 1.7°C (35°F), hypothermia is a real risk if you're unprepared, especially on longer runs or if you get sweaty and then stop. Dress in moisture-wicking layers, cover exposed skin, and don't underestimate how quickly the cold can sneak up on you, even with "clear" skies.
</AlertBox>

Pollen's moderate, so for the allergy sufferers among us, take your usual meds and you should be fine. It's not going to derail your workout, but don't be surprised if you get a little sniffly.

## The Bronx: Construction Central

Now, for the real hazards. If your usual route takes you anywhere near Yankee Stadium, particularly around 161st Street, you're going to want to reroute or embrace the chaos. The city just broke ground on a "major overhaul" for a new bus lane on 161st Street, a project expected to run *through 2028*. That's a long haul, folks. Expect significant disruptions, especially if you're trying to cross or run along that corridor between Concourse West Village and River Avenue.

And to top it off, there's a "Special Event Construction Embargo" in effect for Yankee Stadium Opening Day until Thursday, March 26th. This means only emergency work is permitted, but it also signals that the area is already a mess of traffic and pedestrian detours. Don't expect smooth sailing.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**Yankee Stadium Area Gridlock (161st Street):** Avoid running or cycling near 161st Street if possible. Major construction for a new bus lane just started yesterday and will impact the area significantly. Coupled with an active construction embargo for Opening Day, expect heavy congestion, detours, and potential unexpected closures.
</AlertBox>

Elsewhere, there's ongoing coastal resilience construction impacting the esplanade and South Street areas from the Brooklyn Bridge down to Montgomery Street. While not as immediate a "stop you in your tracks" hazard as the Yankee Stadium situation today, it means less space and more obstacles on those routes. Keep an eye out.

**Keep Hydrated, Even When Cold:** It's easy to forget to drink water when you're not sweating buckets, but hydration is crucial even in cold weather. Your body still loses fluids.
