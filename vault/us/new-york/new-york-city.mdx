*   **Air Quality is Meh, Not Great:** While we're not under a Code Orange advisory *today* like earlier this week, an AQI of 59 with PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant still lands us in the "moderate" zone. Just a couple of days ago, the entire NYC Metro region was under an Air Quality Health Advisory for ozone, with levels expected to hit unhealthy highs for sensitive groups due to that stagnant, hot air. So, if your lungs are touchy, keep it in mind, because what goes up tends to linger.
*   **Pollen is Doing Its Thing:** "Moderate" pollen levels mean your nose might still decide to run its own marathon, even if you don't. Stock up on tissues and maybe pre-run some antihistamines if you’re prone to the sniffles.
*   **Construction Never Sleeps:** Seriously, is anything ever *not* under construction in this city? We've got ongoing closures and detours in East River Park thanks to the Coastal Resiliency project, which means your usual scenic waterfront run is still a labyrinth until late 2026. Plan your routes accordingly, or face a sudden dead end and a good dose of existential dread. Plus, various bridge and tunnel lanes are getting shut down overnight this week for "inspections and construction," which could complicate getting to your preferred running spot if you drive.

## Hazards & Your Run

<AlertBox type="warning">
**East River Park: Still a Maze.** The East River Park Esplanade remains significantly impacted by the Coastal Resiliency project. Don't expect a clear, uninterrupted run south of 14th Street. You'll be rerouted, you'll hit barriers, and you'll likely question every life choice that led you to run there today. Seriously, find another route or embrace the urban obstacle course.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
**Keep an Eye on That AQI (Even When It's "Moderate").** Just because the Air Quality Advisory for ozone lifted, doesn't mean the air is suddenly pristine. Your body's got a memory, especially your lungs. If you felt it earlier in the week, take it easy today, especially during peak traffic hours when pollution tends to spike. And remember, "moderate" means "acceptable, but there may be a risk for some people, such as those who are sensitive to air pollution".
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate Pollen is Still Pollen.** Don't pretend you're invincible. If you've got seasonal allergies, now's the time to pop those meds *before* you head out. A snot-filled run is a miserable run, and nobody wants that for you.
</AlertBox>

Temperature at a comfortable 23.7°C (about 75°F) with clear skies is a gift, so take advantage. Just be smart about where you're putting your feet and what you're breathing. And maybe bring a map, because East River Park isn't doing anyone any favors these days.
