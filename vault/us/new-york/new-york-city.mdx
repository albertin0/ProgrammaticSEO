## Today's NYC Outdoor Workout Report: October 23, 2026

Alright, New Yorkers, today isn't one of those apocalyptic "stay inside or grow a third lung" days, which is a win in itself. The weather's cooperating, which is a rare treat. We're looking at clear skies and a cool 8.8°C (around 48°F) to kick things off, warming up slightly through the day. Humidity is reasonable, and there's no rain to speak of. Basically, ideal running temps if you dress right.

### Air Quality & Allergies

The city's Air Quality Index (AQI) is a low 31, which falls firmly in the "Good" category. Dominant pollutants are Ozone/Other, but at this level, your average runner shouldn't be wheezing any more than usual.

<AlertBox type="warning">
Hold your horses, asthmatics and allergy sufferers. Despite the "Good" AQI, the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation has issued an Air Quality Health Advisory for the NYC Metro region. This means if you're an active child, adult with lung disease, or generally sensitive, you should *reduce prolonged or heavy exertion outdoors*. So, that 15-mile long run might be better kept indoors or cut short if you're in the sensitive crowd. Don't push it.
</AlertBox>

Pollen levels are still sitting at "Moderate." Nothing catastrophic, but if you're prone to seasonal sniffles, pop an antihistamine before you head out.

### Construction & Events: The Usual Urban Obstacle Course

This is where your run gets interesting, or annoying, depending on your perspective. NYC never sleeps, and neither does its construction.

**Central Park** is hosting the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon Kids Kickoff today. We're talking a 1.4-mile race for kids aged 12-18, starting at 9 AM. While it's great for the youth, it means parts of the park, especially around the race route, will be crowded and potentially have temporary closures or detours. Bib pickup for this is also ongoing at the NYRR RUNCENTER. If your usual Central Park loop is sacred, you might want to adjust your timing or route to avoid the young speed demons.

Over in **Riverside Park**, it's a hot mess of long-term projects. Drainage reconstruction between 108th and 116th Streets is 38% done and won't finish until May 2027. Plus, adult fitness equipment construction is kicking off at West 71st Street this month. Expect detours and general contractor chaos. The Rotunda above the marina is also undergoing a massive $150 million rehab by NYC DOT, which means ongoing pedestrian and cyclist detours. And if you're thinking of running further afield, path reconstruction is also happening at Carl Schurz Park.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Pro Tip:** Always check the NYC Parks Capital Project Tracker before heading to your favorite green space. What was open yesterday might be a construction zone today.
</AlertBox>

Finally, if your route takes you anywhere near the **Battery Park Underpass**, be aware that full northbound and southbound tubes are closed weekly from Sunday 10 PM to Friday 5 AM for flood mitigation work. This is a recurring pain for those trying to get around Lower Manhattan.

In short, it's a beautiful day, but bring your street smarts and plan for some diversions.
