<AlertBox type="danger">
### Severe Weather Incoming: Saturday Afternoon/Evening
NYC Emergency Management has issued a travel advisory for today, Saturday, June 6, and tomorrow, Sunday, June 7. The National Weather Service is forecasting heavy rain, gusty winds, and potential severe thunderstorms, with the worst expected between 5:00 p.m. Saturday and 2:00 a.m. Sunday. Sustained winds of 10 to 20 mph are expected today, but gusts could reach a whopping 65 mph later on. Think falling branches, difficult running conditions, and slippery everything. Get your workout done early if you must, and then stay inside. Avoid areas prone to nuisance flooding.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
### Ozone Spike Expected
While our current AQI might look okay (64, moderate), the Air Quality Index is *predicted* to climb to 115 today, putting it in the "unhealthy for sensitive groups" category due to ground-level ozone. This means if you have asthma, heart conditions, or are just generally sensitive, you should seriously consider limiting strenuous outdoor activity, especially during the afternoon and early evening when ozone levels peak. This isn't visible smoke, but it's still rough on your respiratory system.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Watch Your Step (and Where You're Going)
Plenty of ongoing construction around the city. For instance, the East Side Coastal Resiliency (ESCR) project means large sections of John V. Lindsay East River Park are closed for construction. Don't try to power through construction zones; find alternative routes or just accept that some areas are off-limits for a while. Also, today is National Trails Day, and there are several NYRR Open Runs happening across various parks, including Inwood Hill Park and Shore Road Park. Expect more foot traffic than usual on those trails, especially this morning before the weather goes south.
</AlertBox>

If you absolutely *have* to get your miles in, do it now, before lunch. Seriously. And watch the sky. If it looks nasty, it probably is. There's always tomorrow (maybe).
