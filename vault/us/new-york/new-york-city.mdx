### Weather & Air Quality Report

You woke up to a chilly but blessedly clear New York morning, a welcome sight after some of the recent gloom. The thermometer's sitting around 6.7°C (44°F) right now, which is definitely "layers-needed" territory for anyone thinking about an early run or ride. Don't be fooled by the sunshine; those temperatures will only creep into the low 50s Fahrenheit by midday.

But here's the kicker: that clear sky is a fleeting moment. We're looking at increasing cloud cover through the morning, with showers expected to roll in from the west this afternoon, specifically between 1 PM and 5 PM. By late afternoon and early evening, expect periods of actual rain, so plan your outdoor activities accordingly unless you enjoy running through a drizzle (some of us do, no judgment). Winds will be light throughout the day, so no need to worry about being blown off your feet.

The good news for your lungs is that the Air Quality Index (AQI) is fantastic today, sitting at a healthy 37, with ozone being the dominant pollutant. No worries there.

### Local Hazards & Annoyances

<AlertBox type="danger">
#### East River Greenway Remains a Construction Zone Nightmare
Look, I'm all for coastal resiliency, but the ongoing work at East River Park continues to be a massive pain for runners and cyclists. The East River Greenway, specifically the section between E. Houston Street and E. 14th Street, is *fully closed 24/7* due to the East Side Coastal Resiliency (ESCR) project. This isn't a partial closure; it's a complete shutdown of a vital stretch. If you normally use this path, you need to find an alternate route. Access to the new southern amenities of East River Park Track requires detours via Delancey Street Bridge or Corlears Hook Bridge. Seriously, check the NYC DOT website or the ESCR project site before you head out, or you'll be doing an unexpected urban adventure.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="warning">
#### Hudson River Park Detour
If you're out on the Hudson River Greenway, specifically between W. 29th Street and W. 30th Street, watch your step. There's a "small detour" in place due to the Manhattan Tunnel Project. The bikeway and walkway are narrowed, and you might encounter construction trucks crossing the trail. Keep your head up and stay aware of your surroundings; don't assume you have the right-of-way blindly.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
#### Layer Up for the Morning, Prepare for Rain
With morning temperatures in the mid-40s and afternoon rain on the horizon, dressing in layers is crucial. Start with a moisture-wicking base, add a light insulating layer, and definitely have a packable, waterproof shell ready to go if you're planning an afternoon or evening workout. Hydration is still key, even when it's cool.
</AlertBox>

Today's not the worst day to get outside, especially if you get your workout in early. Just be prepared for the chill, keep an eye on the sky, and for heaven's sake, don't try to power through a closed trail section. You're a runner, not an urban explorer (unless that's your thing, then carry on, I guess).
