## Air Quality & Pollen Breakdown

Here's where things get a little less Instagram-perfect. The Air Quality Index (AQI) is sitting at 61, with PM2.5 being the dominant pollutant. What does that mean for your lungs? It means it's "moderate." For most of us, it's just a bit annoying. But if you're one of the "sensitive groups"—got asthma, other lung conditions, or you're pushing a stroller with a tiny human in it—you might feel it more. Maybe skip that all-out sprint today and stick to a steady pace.

As for pollen, it's a "moderate" level out there. For those of you who still get the sniffles even as the leaves drop, this could be your late-season nemesis.

## Local Hazards & Head-Ups

While the marathon road closures are thankfully a thing of the past for today, November 4th, New York City construction never sleeps. If your route includes the **Hudson River Greenway**, especially around West 29th and West 30th Streets, know that the bike path is *still* temporarily re-routed due to the Gateway Tunnel Project. Don't be that person biking on the pedestrian walkway; just follow the detours. Also, the Metropolitan Avenue Bridge over Newtown Creek has overnight Queens-bound bike lane closures, though that mostly impacts your late-night rides.
