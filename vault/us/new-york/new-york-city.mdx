<AlertBox type="warning">
### East River Park is Still a Maze

Listen, if your usual route takes you through **East River Park**, you know the drill. The East Side Coastal Resiliency Project is still very much a thing, with the park in various states of being "demolished and rebuilt in phases." That means detours, temporary paths, and generally more thinking than you want to do on your morning run. Check the Parks Department site before you head out, or just assume half your favorite path is closed. Seriously, that seawall better be worth it.
</AlertBox>

<AlertBox type="info">
### Mind the Muck

That "torrential rainfall" we had last Thursday caused "widespread flash flooding" across the city, particularly hitting neighborhoods like Sunset Park and Queens Village. While the immediate crisis has passed, the ground in some areas, especially near the shoreline or older drainage systems, could still be saturated or muddy. Don't be surprised if your usual trail run turns into an unexpected mud obstacle course. Wear appropriate shoes or stick to the pavement in higher elevations for a few more days.
</AlertBox>
