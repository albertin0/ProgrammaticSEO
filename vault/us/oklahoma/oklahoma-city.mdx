## Your Body is Not a Heat-Seeking Missile

Look, the morning might feel decent enough at 24.2°C and clear, but don't let that fool you. By afternoon, the forecast high is near 96°F, and with the humidity we're packing, it’s going to *feel* like a staggering 106°F. The National Weather Service in Norman is even calling a Heat Advisory likely. This isn't just about feeling sweaty; we're talking about a serious risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Strengthening southerly winds aren't going to help much either, just stirring up that humid, hot air.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**DANGER: Extreme Heat Index!** If you must be outside, keep your workout to the absolute earliest morning hours. Shift any intense activity indoors. Hydrate aggressively and listen to your body – dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating are not "pushing through it," they're warning signs. Seriously, don't be a hero.
</AlertBox>

## Lungs & Allergies: A Double Whammy

Now for the air. While the raw AQI reading of 37 might make you think you're in the clear, AccuWeather's forecast for today is calling for "Poor" air quality due to high pollution, specifically ground-level ozone, which is unhealthy for sensitive groups. Combine that with a "Moderate" pollen level, and your respiratory system is in for a fight. Those strengthening winds? They'll just help spread the pollen and whatever crud is in the air. Expect some serious congestion and irritation if you're susceptible.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**WARNING: "Poor" Air Quality & Pollen!** Sensitive groups (asthmatics, allergy sufferers, kids, elderly) should seriously limit outdoor exposure today. Even if you think you're tough, this combination can kick your ass. Consider an indoor workout where the air is filtered.
</AlertBox>

## Navigating the OKC Concrete Jungle (and Trails)

Beyond the weather, getting around our fair city for a workout is, as usual, a game of dodging. The City of OKC is starting overnight closures on the NW Expressway at May Avenue tonight (Sunday, June 21, 8 p.m. to 6 a.m.) for bridge demolition. This means traffic chaos if you're out late or early, and it's a two-year project, so get used to it.

For our trail runners and cyclists, if you usually hit the River Trails, remember there are "rolling closures" on the South River Trail as resurfacing continues, and a detour is still in effect near Wheeler Park on the North River Trail. These aren't new, but they're always a pain to navigate when you're just trying to get some miles in.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Tip: Check Your Route Twice.** Before you head out, especially if you're hitting the River Trails or navigating areas near the NW Expressway, check the OKC Parks trail closures web app or the City of OKC website for the latest updates. Nobody likes a surprise detour when they're already gassed.
</AlertBox>
