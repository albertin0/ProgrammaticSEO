## Today's Local Lowdown: Don't Be a Hero

Seriously, your lungs will thank you for taking it easy today. That AQI of 132 isn't just a number on a screen; it's a real health risk, especially for kids, older folks, and anyone with existing respiratory issues. Exercising hard in poor air quality can exacerbate problems like asthma and decrease lung function. Why willingly invite a coughing fit?

Beyond the air, the weather is just plain nasty for an enjoyable outdoor session. With heavy thunderstorms forecast throughout the day, those seemingly benign overcast skies are just holding back the deluge. Trails around town, like sections of the **Bert Cooper Trails near Lake Hefner**, are already prone to standing water after heavy rain. Plus, today is the "Landrunner Trail Sweep" at Stars & Stripes Park, so even if you brave the weather, you might encounter event participants.
