### Today's Rundown

Looks like Mother Nature decided to keep us on our toes. We're seeing overcast skies and a comfortable 16°C, which usually makes for prime running conditions. However, don't let that fool you into a false sense of security. The chances of rain are hanging around 25% today, with showers and even a few scattered storms possible through the evening. This isn't just a drizzle; we've had reports of heavy rainfall and flash flooding threats recently, and while the worst of *that* might have passed, lingering wetness means slippery surfaces.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Slick Spots and Standing Water:** That "2-3+ inches of rainfall likely" forecast from yesterday means some low-lying areas, especially underpasses or less-trafficked parts of the trails near Lake Overholser, could still be holding water. Don't be that person who learns about hydroplaning the hard way on your morning run. Slow down, especially on turns.
</AlertBox>

Wind is also a factor. We're looking at south winds around 15 mph with gusts hitting up to 22 mph. That's not enough to blow you off the Skydance Bridge, but it's enough to make you work a bit harder on exposed sections and can definitely kick up any remaining debris from the recent storms.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Gear Up for Changing Conditions:** Even if it's just overcast, a light, water-resistant layer isn't a bad call. And seriously, check the tread on your shoes. If they're bald, you're just asking for trouble on wet asphalt.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, the air quality is stellar with an AQI of 23, so you can breathe easy knowing you're not inhaling anything nasty. As for pollen, it's at a moderate level. Nothing to cause widespread panic, but if you're usually bothered, pop an antihistamine before you head out.
