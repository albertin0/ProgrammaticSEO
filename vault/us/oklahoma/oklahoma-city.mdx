Look, I get it. You want to hit the pavement. But seriously, with an AQI like 132, hitting your usual loop around the Bert Cooper Trails (yeah, still calling them Lake Hefner Trails, fight me) is going to feel like running through a dusty, pollen-filled gauntlet. The air is "Mainly Clear" at 18.7°C, which sounds idyllic until you remember what's *in* that clear air.

<AlertBox type="danger">
**AQI is Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups.** With PM2.5 as the dominant pollutant and an AQI of 132, anyone with asthma, lung conditions, heart issues, or who is otherwise sensitive should absolutely avoid outdoor exertion. Even healthy individuals might experience respiratory irritation. This isn't a suggestion; it's a hard stop if you value your lung capacity for tomorrow's run.
</AlertBox>

Yesterday, we had Red Flag Warnings and wind advisories screaming about 50 mph gusts fueling at least 20 active wildfires across the state. Even if the direct smoke isn't suffocating us in the metro today, the residual particulate matter carried by today's still-gusty winds (we're talking gusts up to 25 mph, according to NWS Norman) is likely contributing to that ugly PM2.5 number. So, while the skies *look* clear, don't let them fool you into thinking it's pristine air.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Moderate Pollen Count:** Oak, elm, and cedar are likely doing a number on allergy sufferers. Combine that with the poor air quality, and you're looking at a double whammy for your airways. Consider an indoor workout or double up on your allergy meds if you insist on being outside.
</AlertBox>

On the bright side, we're not dealing with any major flooding today, nor are temperatures extreme. But you still need to keep your eyes peeled for construction. The new Continental Coliseum in downtown is breaking ground, so expect some potential detours if you're trying to navigate that area. Also, a heads-up for anyone using major thoroughfares to get to their favorite running spots: those I-44 ramps at NW 10th St are closed through summer 2026 for a bridge project, and SH-152 near Union City is also a mess. Always check the City's traffic advisories before you head out.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Construction & Winds:** Even if you're not running through a construction zone, strong winds can kick up dust from these sites. Plus, be mindful of any loose debris from yesterday's heavy gusts. Secure your outdoor gear, runners.
</AlertBox>
