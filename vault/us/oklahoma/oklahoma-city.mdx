Alright, OKC. You want to sweat it out today? Better lace up smart, because while the air's okay, the ground beneath your feet might have other plans.

<LungsJointsScore score={7} />

<BulletList>
-   **Pollen's Being a Middle Child:** Moderate pollen means your sinuses might protest a bit, but it's not a full-blown allergy attack. Still, bring the tissues.
-   **Trail Roulette:** Parts of our beloved trail system are currently a hot mess of construction and detours. Specifically, the South River Trail has "rolling closures" for resurfacing, and there's a detour on the North River Trail near Wheeler Park. Don't expect a smooth ride (or run) everywhere.
-   **Lingering Wetness:** We had some severe weather risk over the weekend, including potential heavy rain and urban/small stream flooding. Even if the sky's clear now, some of those lesser-used paths might still be holding onto some slick spots or hidden puddles. Watch your step, or you'll be finding out exactly how many small muscles you have in your foot.
</BulletList>

Today's one of those days where the air quality is actually pretty decent for a change, which is a small miracle for your lungs with an AQI of 44 and no significant wildfire smoke expected. But don't let that fool you into thinking it's a perfect day. While the crazy 110-degree heat index from Saturday has thankfully passed, it's still August, and 27.1°C (around 80.8°F) is plenty warm enough to make you regret that extra mile if you're not hydrated.

My main beef for your joints today, and frankly, my own, is the trail situation. We've got "rolling closures" on the South River Trail and a detour near Wheeler Park on the North River Trail. This isn't just an annoyance for your Strava segment times; it's a hazard. Unexpected detours can lead you onto uneven surfaces, less-maintained patches, or even straight into active construction zones. The city is working on resurfacing "multiple trails" with that Better Streets, Safer City sales tax, which is great for the long run, but a pain in the glutes right now.

<AlertBox type="warning">
**Watch for Unexpected Obstacles on the Oklahoma River Trails.** With ongoing resurfacing and detours, you could encounter loose gravel, temporary barriers, or unexpected changes in elevation. Slow down and scan ahead, especially around blind corners or underpasses. Your ankles will thank you.
</AlertBox>

Beyond the trails, remember our usual OKC road construction circus. If you're running on sidewalks near major arteries, know that the Northwest Expressway over Lake Hefner Parkway still has narrow lanes, and the May Avenue bridge project is causing shifting lanes and ramp changes. Drivers are already distracted; don't give them another reason to look up from their phones.

<AlertBox type="info">
**Hydration is NOT Optional.** Even at 80 degrees, this isn't a "sip water occasionally" kind of day. If you're out for more than 30 minutes, carry fluids. If you feel dizzy or lightheaded, stop. Nobody needs a heat-related PT session on my watch.
</AlertBox>

So, can you get your workout in? Sure. Just be smart about it. Check the specific sections of the Oklahoma River Trails before you head out, be ready to adapt, and don't push it. There's no medal for ending up in the ER.
